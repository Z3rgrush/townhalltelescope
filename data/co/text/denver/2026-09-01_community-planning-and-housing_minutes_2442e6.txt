26-1253 Amends the Denver Revised Municipal Code, Sec.27-150 and Sec. 
27-164 to allow for a Department of Housing Stability to draft and submit a 
one-year continuation plan for 2027. The ordinance also states that HOST 
will bring forward its next long-range strategic plan for approval in 
September 2027 which will go into effect in January 2028.
A motion offered by Council member Torres, duly seconded by Council President 
Romero Campbell, that Council Bill 26-1253 and Council Resolution 26-1254 be 
approved for filing in a block, carried by the following vote:
Aye: Alvidrez, Lewis, Romero Campbell, Vice Chair Sawyer, Torres5 - 
Nay: 0   
Absent: Kashmann, Chair Sandoval2 - 
26-1254 Approves the Department of Housing Stability’s one-year continuation plan 
for 2027 in accordance with the Denver Revised Municipal Code, citywide.
