John Landwehr with Columnar Investments, is representing the owner, Wheatly, LLC, 
in requesting a 12-month expiration date extension for the Wheatlands Subdivision 
Preliminary Plat. 
 
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
Approval timeframe 
The Wheatlands Subdivision Preliminary Plat was approved by the Board of Trustees 
on September 9, 2025. The Town of Frederick Land Use Code states the approval and 
conditional approval of a preliminary plat shall be effective for one (1) year unless 
otherwise approved by the Board of Trustees. If a final plat is not submitted within said 
time limit or an extension has not been granted, a preliminary plat must again be 
submitted and approved before action may be taken on a final plat. The applicant has 
not submitted a final plat to the Town for review.  
 
Request 
The applicant is requesting a 12-month extension of the approval timeframe for the 
preliminary plat. The proposed timeframe would grant approval through September 9, 
2027. The Board of Trustees is the approval authority for this type of request. The 
applicant stated the residential development market has experienced significant 
challenges over the past year, resulting in a slower pace of builder acquisition and land 
transactions than anticipated.  
 
Project Timeline 
2021 - Neighborhood Meeting 
2022 - Application submittal 
2025 - Board of Trustees approval 
2026 - Extension request public meeting 
2027 - Requested new expiration deadline 
 
Project Details 
~124 acres in size 
21.4 acres of open space 
9.4 acres of park space (1 neighborhood park and 3 pocket parks) 
317 single-family lots 
221 future townhomes  
5 plugged and abandoned oil and gas facilities with 25-foot setback 
 
Preliminary Development Plan (PDP) 
The PDP was approved alongside the preliminary plat on September 9, 2025. The 
preliminary development plan does not have an expiration date per the Land Use Code 
that was used to review the project. The updated Land Use Code from 2024 and 2026 
now has provisions requiring every PDP with no approved final development plan to 
convert into a PUD Zoning Document. The PUD Zoning Document will be one final 
document with the rules and regulations for the subdivision vs. having a PDP and FDP. 
 
The Wheatlands project will be required to convert the approved PDP into a PUD 
Page 15 of 94
Zoning Document at the time of final plat submittal, regardless of the preliminary plat 
extension request. The PUD Zoning Document will contain additional information for the 
project that the preliminary development plan did not include. Town staff is currently 
working on developing a list of items and expectations for all projects going through this 
conversion process, specifically, providing direction on which new Land Use Code 
provisions are expected to be met with each project. Again, the preliminary plat 
extension request is a separate item from the future required PUD Zoning Document.  
 
Next steps 
1. The applicant will either submit a final plat or re-submit a new preliminary plat 
(depending on the extension request outcome) 
2. Amend the preliminary development plan into a final PUD zoning document at the 
time of final plat 
  
 
Legal Comments: 
 
The Town of Frederick Attorney's Office has reviewed the request and provided 
comments through the review. All comments have been incorporated into this item.

I move to approve Resolution No. 26-R-43, which grants a 12-month expiration date 
extension for the Wheatlands Preliminary Plat.

N/A

Staff is recommending the Board of Trustees hold a public meeting, receive the 
presentation from staff and the applicant and ultimately consider all the information prior 
to taking any final actions. Staff is recommending the following potential condition of 
approval: 
  
1. Approval of the 12-month expiration date extension shall provide for the re-
Page 16 of 94
negotiation of development standards within the future Wheatlands PUD Zoning 
Document. 
 
Community Impact 
 
Commercial and residential development has slowed down throughout the Front Range 
within the last couple of years. This is evident from the number of building permits 
issued within Frederick and active applications with the Town. Approving or denying 
this request affects if the developer can move forward onto the next step of 
development (PUD Zoning Document and Final Plat) or start from the beginning with a 
new preliminary plat.

Dear	Members	of	Staff	and	Board	of	Trustees,	
Columnar	Land	respectfully	requests	a	twelve-month	extension	to	the	Preliminary	Plat	for	the	Wheatlands	Subdivision	
that	was	approved	by	the	Town	of	Frederick	on	September	9,	2025.	This	extension	will	expire	on	September	8th,	2027.		
Since	receiving	preliminary	approvals,	we	have	continued	to	evaluate	opportunities	to	advance	the	project	toward	
final	platting	and	development.	Our	intent	has	always	been	to	move	the	project	forward	in	a	thoughtful	and	
coordinated	manner	that	positions	the	community	for	long-term	success.	However,	the	residential	development	
market	has	experienced	significant	challenges	over	the	past	year,	resulting	in	a	slower	pace	of	builder	acquisition	and	
land	transactions	than	anticipated.	While	we	have	received	interest	from	several	homebuilders,	market	conditions	
have	limited	the	ability	of	builders	to	commit	to	new	projects	and	execute	purchase	agreements.	
Recognizing	these	challenges,	we	recently	initiated	a	broader	marketing	effort	for	the	Wheatlands	property	to	increase	
exposure	to	additional	qualified	builders	and	developer	partners.	Based	on	current	discussions	and	industry	
timelines,	securing	a	builder	typically	requires	approximately	sixty	days	to	negotiate	a	Purchase	and	Sale	Agreement	
followed	by	an	additional	ninety	days	for	underwriting,	approvals,	and	contractual	commitment.	As	a	result,	additional	
time	is	needed	to	successfully	complete	this	process.	
Our	objective	is	to	secure	a	builder	partner	before	commencing	the	Final	Development	Plan	process.	By	doing	so,	we	
can	ensure	that	the	selected	builder's	product	offering	is	appropriately	integrated	into	the	overall	community	vision,	
resulting	in	a	more	cohesive	and	market-responsive	development.	Proceeding	in	this	manner	will	allow	the	Town,	
future	residents,	and	development	partners	to	benefit	from	a	well-planned	project	that	reflects	current	market	
demands	and	community	goals.	
A	twelve-month	extension	will	provide	the	necessary	flexibility	to	complete	builder	selection,	coordinate	project	
planning	efforts,	and	advance	the	Wheatlands	Subdivision	toward	final	plat	approval	and	construction.	We	believe	this	
timeframe	is	both	reasonable	and	prudent	given	current	market	conditions	and	will	help	avoid	the	need	for	multiple	
extension	requests	while	maintaining	project	momentum.	
Columnar	Land	remains	fully	committed	to	the	Wheatlands	project	and	appreciates	the	Town's	continued	partnership	
and	patience.	We	are	enthusiastic	about	bringing	this	development	to	fruition	and	believe	the	requested	extension	will	
provide	the	best	opportunity	for	a	successful	outcome.	
Thank	you	for	your	consideration.	We	look	forward	to	continuing	to	work	collaboratively	with	Town	staff	and	the	
Board	of	Trustees.

Summary Statement: 
Page 53 of 94
 
The Town of Frederick is proposing to convey approximately 4.7 acres of vacant land 
known as Tract B to Mile High Metal Works Inc. to facilitate development of a new 
facility at 4501 Salazar Lane. The land to be conveyed was originally dedicated to the 
Town in 2006 at no cost. Drainage, utility, and access easements associated with Tract 
B are also proposed to be vacated. 
 
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
Mile High Metal Works, Inc. has submitted applications to the Town to develop a site at 4501 
Salazar Lane. The development proposes an approximately 52,000 square foot manufacturing 
building and accessory outdoor storage. The applicant is proposing to utilize Tract B that was 
previously dedicated to the Town in 2006 for use as open space, drainage and utility easements, 
and shown on the 2006 subdivision plat within the 100-year floodplain. 
 
Through the application review process, Town staff confirmed that, based on updated floodplain 
mapping, Tract B of the subdivision which is owned by the Town, and was previously within the 
approximate 100-year floodplain boundary, is now mostly outside the 100-year boundary. In 
addition, Tract B has not been utilized by the Town for the stated purpose on the plat of open space 
and drainage and utility easements, nor has it been used or held for any governmental purpose, 
pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-15-713(1)(a). Drainage, utility, and access easements associated with Tract 
B are also proposed to be vacated since they are no longer needed, and new easements necessary 
for the Mile High Metal Works development will be dedicated to the Town with the new subdivision 
plat. 
Separately, the Town has entered into an incentive performance agreement with Mile High Metal 
Works, Inc. that would provide reimbursement of a portion of Town fees, subject to Mile High Metal 
Works meeting the Town incentive policy conditions. That request was reviewed under the Town's 
administrative incentive program and approved prior to the Town identifying a potential desire to 
divest its ownership interest in Tract B.   
 
Legal Comments: 
 
The ordinance was reviewed by the Town's Land Use Attorney.

Financial Considerations 
 
Tract B was originally dedicated to the Town as part of the subdivision plat and the 
Town did not invest any public funds acquiring or maintaining Tract B. Conveyance will 
benefit the Town by removing the property from future Town maintenance obligations 
and will return the property to the tax rolls subject to property tax. Separately, the Town 
has entered into an incentive performance agreement with Mile High Metal Works that 
would provide reimbursement of a portion of Town fees as previously adopted by the 
Board, subject to Mile High Metal Works, Inc. meeting the Town incentive policy 
conditions. The incentive agreement is not connected to this conveyance request which 
was initiated by the Town. 
 
Staff Recommendation 
 
Staff recommends approval of the ordinance. 
 
Community Impact 
 
Conveyance of Tract B will not have an adverse community impact. Revised floodplain 
mapping has removed most of Tract B from the floodplain, and the portion that is still 
within the floodplain will include a drainage easement and will not be developable. The 
Town has no short or long-term need or plans for the use of this property. Conveyance 
will benefit the Town by removing the property from future Town maintenance 
obligations and will return the property to the tax rolls subject to property tax.

ORDINANCE NO. 1421 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF FREDERICK, COLORADO, AUTHORIZING 
CONVEYANCE OF A PROPERTY KNOWN AS TRACT B OF DEL CAMINO SOUTH REPLAT 
OF OUTLOT A TO KO INDUSTRIAL LLC, AND  
VACATION OF DRAINAGE, UTILITY, AND ACCESS EASEMENTS ASSOCIATED WITH 
TRACT B IN DEL CAMINO SOUTH REPLAT OF OUTLOT A 
 
 WHEREAS, on November 12, 2025, KO Industrial, LLC and Mile High Metal Works 
submitted applications for a subdivision amendment and site plan at 4501 Salazar Lane in 
the Town of Frederick; and  
 
WHEREAS, through the application process, Town staff confirmed that, based on 
updated floodplain mapping, TRACT B DEL CAMINO SOUTH REPLAT OF OUTLOT A, 
addressed as 4503 Salazar Lane, which is owned by the Town of Frederick, further described 
in Exhibit A1, attached hereto (“Property”), which was previously within the approximate 
100-year floodplain boundary, is now mostly outside of the 100-year boundary; and 
 
WHEREAS, Tract B has not been utilized by the Town for the stated purpose on the 
plat of open space, drainage, and utility easements; and 
 
WHEREAS, Tract B was dedicated to the Town in 2006, and the Town has not 
incurred any acquisition or maintenance costs; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Property has not been used or held for any governmental purpose, 
pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-15-713(1)(a); and 
 
WHEREAS, C.R.S. §§  31-15-713(1)(b) and 38-30-141 provide for conveyance of 
property by municipality by ordinance; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees wishes to convey such surplus property to KO 
Industrial, LLC to facilitate a new development for Mile High Metal Works, including an 
approximately 52,000 square-foot new building; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees also wishes to vacate drainage, utility, and access 
easements associated with Tract B of Del Camino South Replat of Outlot A, as described in 
Exhibits A1 and A2, attached hereto, as the easements to be vacated are not needed by the 
Town in the short or long term, and new easements necessary for the Mile High Metal Works 
development will be dedicated with the subdivision plat. 
 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF 
FREDERICK, AS FOLLOWS: 
 
Section 1. Authorization to Convey. Pursuant to C.R.S. §§ 31-15-713 and 38-30-141, the 
Town of Frederick Board of Trustees hereby authorizes conveyance of the Property 
identified in Exhibit A1 to this Resolution, Tract B of Del Camino South Replat of Outlot A,

attached hereto as Exhibit B and reviewed by the Town Attorney, and authorizes the Mayor
