Transparent & Strategic Governance 
 
• The PROST Commission hosted a brief Work Session for their July Meeting as 
they did not have a quorum for any a ction items. They held their regular meeting 
on August 12, where the Commission heard final updates on the Parks Water 
Management Plan, Golf Course Management Plan,  and Urban Forest ry 
Management Plan. All of those are working towards adoption by PROST this fall. 
The Commission also heard an update on the Department's Asset Management 
Program, to inform an upcoming conversation on the Asset Replacement 
Plan/Schedule, which is one of the most financially impactful documents within the 
Go Outdoors Frederick effort. 
• Water Resources staff recently collaborated with the Town Attorney’s Office and 
the Wyndham Hill Metropolitan District to create a water lease agreement to 
accomplish the first fill of the Wyndham Hill irrigation pond. Upon receiving  
payment, staff anticipate coordinating water delivery in early August.

Summary Statement: 
 
This Amendment to the facility use agreement for the Frederick Firestone Fire District to 
utilize the public works facility for vehicle maintenance allows for the Agreement to 
terminate earlier than expected. 
 
Page 22 of 46
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
A number of years ago, the Town and Fire District had an agreement for the Town to 
maintain Fire District vehicles. That arrangement adjusted to have the Fire District 
maintain their own vehicles, but allowed the use of the public works facility to do so. 
This was intended to prepare for the eventual new public works facility to be built and 
for the Town to transition out of the current facility to the new facility. In that time, the 
Fire District built a new maintenance facility to house their operations. As this new 
maintenance facility is coming online, the need for the use of the Town's public works 
facility is coming to an end. This Amendment will allow the lease terms to terminate 
ahead of schedule, allowing the Fire District to move their operations to their own 
facility and for the Town to prepare to move from the current facility to the new public 
works building as it is completed. This will further allow the Town to prepare for turning 
over the current public works building to Agilent pursuant to the lease agreements and 
property transfer approved prior to the construction of these new facilities. 
 
Legal Comments: 
 
The amendment and resolution have been reviewed by the Town Attorney's Office. 
Allowing for the termination of the lease early is not anticipated to create any liabilities. 
 
Alternatives/Options 
 
The Board can approve the amendment, deny the amendment, or direct staff to make 
adjustments to the amendment as presented. 
 
Financial Considerations 
 
The lease by the Fire District was made for a nominal amount to allow this important 
community function to continue unfettered, as the District residents are also residents of 
the Town. As such, it is not anticipated to create any substantive financial burden or 
loss by terminating the lease agreement early. 
 
Staff Recommendation 
 
Staff recommends approval of the amendment as presented. 
 
Community Impact 
 
The service to the community will not be interrupted or degraded by this action. 
Allowing for the early termination of the agreement will allow the Fire District and the 
Town to move towards their operational efficiencies ahead of schedule, which is a 
benefit to the public generally.
