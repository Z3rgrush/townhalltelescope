Town of Frederick Arts Committee 
Meeting Minutes 
August 11, 2026 
   
Call to Order – Roll Call: At 4:31 pm, the meeting was called to order.  
Present were Chairperson Sumner, Vice Chair Hattel, Committee Member Lewis, 
Committee Member Maxwell, Committee Member Church, and Committee Member 
Nyland.  
Also Present were Deputy Town Clerk Emily Nitcher. 
Pledge of Allegiance:  
Approval of Agenda: No Changes to the Agenda. 
Public Comment: No Public Comment.  
Consent Agenda: Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Chairperson Sumner 
to approve the consent agenda. 
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes:  
Action Agenda:  
Placement of 2026 Wood Carvings and Removal of Old Wood Carvings: Chairperson 
Sumner questioned if the Committee wanted to leave any of the Downtown spots open 
for future carvings.  
Motion by Committee Member Lewis and seconded by Committee Member Church to 
approve the placement of new wood carvings and remove old carvings as presented.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
  
Selection of Arts Committee Poster Contest Winner - Item tabled at June Meeting : 
Motion by Chairperson Sumner and seconded by Vice Chair Hattel to not select a 
winner for the poster contest. 
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Discussion Items:  
Discussion of Engineering Building Mural: The Arts Committee discussed the RFP for 
the Mural.  
Edits to the RFP draft to be brought to next meeting.  
  
Final Summer Concert: Still They Ride, August 12, 2026 6:30pm - 8:30 pm. : 6:00 pm 
meeting time for the Commission for setup of the Concert on 08/12/2026. 
Commissioner Reports: Committee Member Church had a question about the 2027 
budget. 
Other Business: Discussion of planning for next year.  
Possible collaboration with Historic Preservation Committee for Miners day was 
discussed.  
Adjournment: There being no further business of the Arts Committee, the meeting 
adjourned at 5:50 pm.
