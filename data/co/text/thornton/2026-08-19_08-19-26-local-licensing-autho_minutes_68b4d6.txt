A. Consideration of a New Hotel and Restaurant  Liquor License Application 
submitted by WCB Thornton, LLC  DBA Waldo's Chicken and Beer , 9675 
Washington Street.            
 
The Public Hearing was opened at 6:03 p.m. 
 
Mohammed Barry, Owner; and Sean Jennings, Attorney, were present. 
 
Mohammed Barry, Owner, was sworn in by the Licensing Coordinator  and 
stated he is over the age of 21.  Mr. Barry  provided information on his 
business experience, the operation of the business, training, the layout of the 
business, and the needs and desires of the neighborhood. 
 
Mr. Jennings introduced Applicant’s Exhibit B, Business Petitions. 
 
Applicant’s Exhibits B was admitted into evidence. 
 
Mr. Barry provided further information on and responded to question from Mr. 
Jeffery and the Authority regarding the needs and desires of the 
neighborhood, public hearing posting requirements, the petitions , the 
operation of the business, the layout of the business, training, and violations. 
 
Mr. Jeffery introduced City’s Exhibit 1, Preliminary Findings; City’s Exhibit 2, 
Boundary Map; City’s Exhibit 3, Affidavit of Publication; City’s Exhibit 4, 
Photograph of the Posted Public Hearing Signs; City’s Exhibit 5, Memo from 
the City Clerk’s Office regarding t he Petition Results; and City’s Exhibit 6, 
Affidavit of Posting and Log.   
 
City’s Exhibits 1 through 6 were admitted into evidence. 
 
Nobody signed up to speak in support of or in opposition to the application. 
 
Mr. Jennings presented a closing argument in support of the application. 
 
Mr. Jeffery presented a closing argument in support of the application. 
 
The Public Hearing was closed at 6:24 p.m.

Nobody signed up to speak in support of the application.

Laura Beckel, 12515 2nd Street, was sworn in by the Licensing Coordinator

with concerns regarding open containers, barriers, blocked walkways and a

Scarlett O’Malley, 12494 2 nd Street, was sworn in by the Licensing

Darla Ponzio, Owner of Lake Avenue Inn at 2181 Lake Avenue, 14022 Cook
