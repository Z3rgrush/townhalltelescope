7. PUBLIC HEARINGS 
A. Consideration of a New Hotel and Restaurant Liquor License Application submitted
by Love Sushi LLC DBA Love Sushi, 9740 Grant Street, Suite 105.
The Public Hearing was opened at 6:10 p.m. 
Xixi Shen, Certified Interpreter for the Applicant, was sworn in by the Licensing 
Coordinator and provided interpretation throughout the Public Hearing. 
Xiaopeng Zheng, Owner, was present. 
Xiaopeng Zheng, Owner, was sworn in by the Licensing Coordinator and 
stated he is over the age of 21.  Mr. Zheng provided information on and 
responded to questions from Mr. Jeffery and the Authority regarding his other 
location, the operation and nature of the business, training, alcohol storage, 
and staff members experience. 
Member Cardenas arrived at 6:20 p.m. 
Nobody signed up to speak in support of or in opposition to the application. 
Erin McCullough, Oedipus Petitioning, was sworn in by the Licensing 
Coordinator.  She testified with respect to the petition procedures and stated 
that 47 out of 47 businesses and 35 out of 35 residents signed in favor of the 
application based on the needs of the neighborhood and desires of the 
inhabitants.  
Mr. Jeffery introduced Applicant’s Exhibit A, Residential Petitions; Applicant’s 
Exhibit B, Business Petitions; and Applicant’s Exhibit C, Petitioner’s 
Summary Report. 
Applicant’s Exhibits A through C were admitted into evidence. 
Mr. Jeffery introduced City’s Exhibit 1, Preliminary Findings; City’s Exhibit 2, 
Boundary Map; City’s Exhibit 3, Affidavit of Publication; City’s Exhibit 4, 
Photograph of the Posted Public Hearing Signs; City’s Exhibit 5, Memo from 
the City Clerk’s Office regarding the Petition Results; and City’s Exhibit 6, 
Affidavit of Posting and Log.   
City’s Exhibits 1 through 6 were admitted into evidence. 
Mr. Jeffery presented a closing argument in support of the application. 
The Public Hearing was closed at 6:45 p.m.
