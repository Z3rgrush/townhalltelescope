Summary: 
This Agenda Item Summary ("AIS") has been prepared for the proposed zoning map 
amendment to establish Public Facilities (PF) zoning for certain Town-owned properties (the 
"Zoning Map Amendment”). The Properties consist of thirty (29) Town-owned parcels totaling 
approximately 133.28 acres located within the Town of Mead, as more particularly described in 
Exhibit A and depicted in Exhibit B to the Resolution (“Properties”). The Board previously 
approved Ordinance No. 1102, establishing the Public Facilities (PF) zone district, in 
accordance with the recommendation by the Planning Commission. The Resolution implements 
the new PF zone district and rezones certain Town Owned property, with a corresponding 
Zoning Map Amendment (“Rezoning”). 
 
Detail of Request:  
 
Applicant(s):             Town of Mead 
 
Property Owner(s):  Town of Mead 
 
 
 
 
Page 6 of 30
Review Criteria Analysis: 
 
General Rezoning Requirements 
  
General rezoning of the Town is subject to the procedural requirements set forth in Section 16-
3-160(b) of the Mead Municipal Code (“MMC”), which requires that the proposed zoning map 
shall be available for public inspection in the Town Hall during regular business hours for thirty 
(30) days prior to the public hearing on such amendments. MMC Section 16-3-160 also 
requires that the Planning Commission hold a hearing and make a recommendation to the 
Board of Trustees regarding such rezoning, prior to ultimate decision making on same by the 
Board.  
 
The Zoning Map Amendment qualifies as general rezoning as it touches a broad category of 
properties. Staff has published notice of the Planning Commission and Board hearings on the 
Zoning Map Amendment in accordance with MMC Section 16-3-160, and the Zoning 
Amendment Map has been available for public inspection at Town Hall for more than thirty (30) 
days prior to the Planning Commission hearing on October 21, 2026. 
 
Public Facilities (PF) Zoning Map Amendment Approval Criteria 
 
The establishment of zoning is subject to review against the following criteria set forth in 
Section 16-3-160(e) of the MMC. Only one (1) of the following criteria must be met for the 
zoning amendments of the Properties: 
1. To correct a manifest error in an ordinance establishing the zoning for a specific 
property; 
2. To rezone an area or extend the boundary of an existing district because of changed or 
changing conditions in a particular area or in the Town generally;  
3. The land to be rezoned was zoned in error and as presently zoned is inconsistent with 
the policies and goals of the Comprehensive Plan;  
4. The proposed rezoning is necessary to provide land for a community-related use 
that was not anticipated at the time of the adoption of the Comprehensive Plan, 
and the rezoning will be consistent with the policies and goals of the 
Comprehensive Plan;  
5. The area requested for rezoning has changed or is changing to such a degree that it is 
in the public interest to encourage development or redevelopment of the area; or 
6. A rezoning to Planned Unit Development overlay district is requested to encourage 
innovative and creative design and to promote a mix of land uses in the development.  
 
The zoning amendment meets criterion #4 above, shown in bold text.  
 
The proposed Public Facilities (PF) zoning designation is appropriate because the Properties 
are owned and operated by the Town of Mead for governmental, civic, recreational, utility, and 
public service purposes. The proposed zoning will ensure that the Town's zoning map 
accurately reflects the existing and intended use of these Town-owned Properties.

Summary: 
Anadarko E&P Onshore LLC, represented by Peak Development Group (together, the 
“Applicants”) have submitted an application for the proposed Ariet’s Grove Filing No. 1 
Subdivision Preliminary Plat, attached as Exhibit 1 to Resolution No. 09-PC-2026 and 
incorporated herein by this reference (“Preliminary Plat”) for the 135.84-acre property located 
south of State Highway 66 and east of Weld County Road 7 (Third St.), as more particularly

Applicant proposes to subdivide the Property into one hundred and eighty-nine (189) lots and 
twelve (12) tracts. Staff has determined that the application satisfies the relevant requirements 
set forth in the Mead Municipal Code (“MMC”) and recommends that the Planning Commission 
recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat to the Board of Trustees, subject to standard 
conditions.

Applicant: Peak Development Group, represented by Sam Sharp & Bonnie Niziolek on behalf 
of Anadarko E&P Onshore LLC

West: Town of Mead – General Commercial (GC), undeveloped & Single-Family Residential

Overview: 
 
The Applicant has submitted an application for the Preliminary Plat pursuant to MMC Section 
16-4-60. Upon consideration of the application, the Planning Commission shall make a 
recommendation to the Board of Trustees to approve, deny, or approve with conditions the 
Preliminary Plat.  
Page 15 of 30
 
The Preliminary Plat subdivides the Property into one hundred and eighty-nine (189) lots and 
twelve (12) tracts. The Preliminary Plat is the first step towards construction of a mixed-use 
subdivision that will provide commercial opportunities and a mixture of housing types that are 
currently in limited supply or unavailable in Mead. 
 
The Preliminary Plat also proposes the dedication of additional rights-of-way (ROW) for County 
Road 7 (Third St.), in compliance with the Town of Mead’s Transportation Master Plan. In 
addition, the Filing No. 1 preliminary plat proposes to dedicate 23.06 acres of land for parks 
and open space, which is well in excess of the 20% open space dedication requirement of 
13.13 acres for this filing. Parks & open space will be constructed in phases. 
 
Review Criteria - Preliminary Plat: 
 
The following is a detailed analysis of the Preliminary Plat application as it relates to the review 
criteria for preliminary plats set forth in MMC Section 16-4-60(c). 
1. The preliminary plat represents a functional system of land use and is consistent 
with the rationale and criteria set forth in this Code and the Town Comprehensive 
Plan. 
Staff Response: The Preliminary Plat provides a functional system for land use. The 
Preliminary Plat provides adequate access to the roadway network for each proposed lot. 
Proposed lots meet the minimum dimensional standards set forth in the Ariet’s Grove PUD. 
Planned drainage facilities will be maintained in accordance with the final plat, once 
approved. Easements will be provided at final platting for the location of utilities to serve the 
Property. 
2. The land use mix within the project conforms to the Town's Zoning District Map 
and Land Use Map and furthers the goals and policies of the Town 
Comprehensive Plan including: 
a. Proposed residential development adds diversity to the Town’s housing 
supply: 
Staff Response: Ariet’s Grove is proposed to include a range of lot sizes, with this 
plat featuring a variety of lot widths that can accommodate a mixture of single-family 
detached products that will appeal to multiple generations of home buyers. 
  
b. Parks and open space and trails are incorporated into the site design: 
Staff Response: Included with the plat were preliminary landscaping, parks, and 
open space designs for the development. In accordance with the approved PUD, 
these plans include an expansive trail network that connects to the Town’s existing 
trail system where possible, a 1 acre pocket park which includes a play lawn, 
seating area, nature-play playground, and picnic area, and a neighborhood park of 
~18 acres along the development’s southeastern boundary that includes a multi-use 
lawn, gathering nodes, a communal plaza, nature play, overlooks of Foster 
Reservoir, a dog park, and a grove of fruit trees. 
c. The proposed project protects the Town's environmental quality: 
Staff Response: The proposed Ariet’s Grove development protects the Town’s 
environmental quality in the following ways:

1. The existing use of the Property is agricultural, with no known environmental 
contamination requiring mitigation. 
2. The development incorporates drainage facilities designed to protect water 
quality.  
3. The development is not anticipated to cause any disturbance to natural areas 
within the Town of Mead. 
4. The development provides ample open space and pays special attention to 
natural areas, with the site designed to preserve existing vegetation 
surrounding Foster Reservoir. 
5. The development addresses the oil & gas development located on the 
property by buffering proposed lots from the area of development. 
3. The utility and transportation design is adequate, given existing and planned 
capacities of those systems. 
Staff Response: Proposed utility and transportation systems have been reviewed by Town

Traffic Engineering consultant. The proposal complies with the Town’s adopted 
Transportation Plan and the Town’s engineering standards regarding transportation, 
drainage, and utility system design. This infrastructure will be constructed at the time of 
development in order to provide adequate facilities to accommodate the proposed use. 
  
4. Negative impacts on adjacent land uses have been identified and satisfactorily 
mitigated. 
Staff Response: There are no negative impacts on adjacent property that have been 
identified. 
  
5. There is a need or desirability within the community for the applicant's 
development and the development will help achieve a balance of land use and/or 
housing types within the Town of Mead. 
Staff Response: Filing No. 1 of the Ariet’s Grove development will help address the growing 
demand for residential opportunities within the Town of Mead as a part of a larger mixed-
use subdivision. The development will also provide connectivity to the proposed Waterfront 
subdivision to the south, and to the commercial subdivision to the east containing QuikTrip 
and O’Reilly Auto Parts.
