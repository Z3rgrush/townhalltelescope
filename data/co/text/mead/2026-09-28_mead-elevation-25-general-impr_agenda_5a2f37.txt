Subject: Resolution No. 01-E25-2026 - A Resolution of the Elevation 25 
General Improvement District Approving an Intergovernmental 
Agreement Between the Town of Mead, Colorado and the 
Elevation 25 General Improvement District  
Presented by: Marcus McAskin, Town Attorney 
  
Summary: 
The Elevation 25 General Improvement District (the “District”) is a public or quasi-municipal 
corporation of the State of Colorado and a body corporate under Part 6, Article 25, Title 31, 
Colorado Revised Statutes (the “Act”). Pursuant to Section 31-25-607(4)(c) of the Act, the 
Town of Mead (the “Town”) Board of Trustees previously declared the District organized by 
Ordinance No. 1019, dated January 9, 2023.  
 
The District was established to reimburse costs and expenses of installation and construction of 
public improvements within or serving development within the boundaries of the District, and to 
assist with defraying the costs associated with administering the District and operating and 
maintaining said improvements. 
 
Section 12-4-60 of the Mead Municipal Code (“MMC”) requires each general improvement 
district (“GID”) to execute an intergovernmental agreement with the Town to memorialize the 
terms and obligations of the GID petition for organization. Staff recommends that the District 
enter into an intergovernmental agreement with the Town to satisfy the requirement set forth 
Section 12-4-60 of the MMC (the “IGA”).  
 
Resolution 01-E25-2026 (the “Resolution”) approves the IGA and authorizes the GID 
Chairperson to execute the IGA on behalf of the District when in final form.  
Financial Considerations: 
The IGA establishes that District revenue, not Town general revenue, will fund District-related 
costs. Administrative and O&M levy revenue is remitted to the Town to offset its staff, legal, 
administrative, and eligible maintenance costs. Contract-payment and debt-service levy 
revenues must be held in separate Town-administered funds and released only for approved, 
documented payments or reimbursements. 
Staff Recommendation / Actions Required: 
Staff recommends adopting the Resolution approving the IGA. A motion to approve the 
September 28, 2026, consent agenda will approve the Resolution.

motion:
