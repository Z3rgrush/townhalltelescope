September 10, 2026Senior Commission Meeting Minutes - Final
    a. Holiday Project
The commission discussed the holiday project and will purchase mugs, cocoa, candy 
canes, and wrapping to deliver to Conter Estates, Kearney Plaza, and Madonna Plaza 
residents on Thursday, December 10. The commission will meet at Eagle Pointe 
Recreation Center's Anima Conference Room to assemble at 1pm, followed by the 
delivery of gifts, followed by their regular meeting.
    b. 2027 Programming
The commission discussed continuing Lunch & Learns and the Senior Dance, 
Valentine deliveries, and holiday deliveries. The commission discussed potentially 
eliminating their mid-year and/or EOY celebration to allow for more budget going 
towards programming. Staff will bring collected survey data from events this year to the 
October meeting to help determine speakers/topics.
A motion was made by Commissioner Plummer, seconded by Commissioner 
Boyd, to host and participate in a Senior Resource Fair on April 17, 2027.
