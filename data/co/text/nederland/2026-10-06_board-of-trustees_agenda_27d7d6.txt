AGENDA ITEM  
Second Amendment to the Ground Lease between the Town of Nederland and TEENS, Inc. 
 
SUMMARY  
The Board of Trustees is being asked to consider approval of the Second Amendment to the Ground 
Lease between the Town of Nederland and TEENS, Inc. The proposed amendment was prepared by 
the Town Attorney and establishes the terms under which TEENS may pay the $361,582 in water 
and sewer Plant Investment Fees over five years rather than as otherwise required under the 
existing Ground Lease. 
 
BACKGROUND 
In February 2025, the Town and TEENS entered into a Ground Lease for Town-owned property used 
for the development, construction, and operation of the Nederland Early Childhood Center. The 
Ground Lease was approved through Ordinance No. 872. 
Section 3.03 of the Ground Lease requires TEENS to pay all legally required water and sewer tap 
fees associated with the facility. 
Rather than requiring payment of the full amount at once, the proposed Second Amendment would 
allow TEENS to repay the principal over five years. The amendment provides for five annual 
principal payments of $72,316.40, with the first payment due October 31, 2026, and subsequent 
payments due each October 31 through 2030. 
No interest would accrue on the first payment. Beginning November 1, 2026, the remaining unpaid 
principal would accrue interest at 3% annually. 
The proposed amendment does not require TEENS to provide a lien, deed of trust, security interest,
