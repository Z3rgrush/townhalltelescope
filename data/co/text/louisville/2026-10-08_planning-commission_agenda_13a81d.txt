a. Accessory Dwelling Unit Code Amendment – Resolution 2, Series 2026, with a 
recommendation to City Council for an amendment to Louisville Municipal Code Title 
17 for a targeted update to comply with HB 24-1152 related to the minimum size 
allowance for an ADU. 
 
Staff Presentation: 
Staff presented a proposed amendment to the City’s Accessory Dwelling Unit 
(ADU) ordinance to address a state law compliance issue. The proposed 
amendment would explicitly allow a 750-square-foot ADU by right, unless the 
primary dwelling unit is smaller than 750 square feet. The amendment is narrowly 
focused and intended only to bring the City’s ordinance into compliance with 
state law. 
 
Staff Recommendation: 
Staff recommended approval of Resolution 2, Series 2026 recommending City 
Council adopt the ordinance amendment to ensure compliance with state law. 
 
Commissioner Questions of Staff:  
Commissioners asked questions regarding the amendment’s interactions with 
existing size limits for attached and detached ADUs as well as the number of 
ADU permits issued since adoption of the ordinance.  
 
Staff clarified that the amendment primarily overrides the existing 75% size 
limitation when necessary to allow a 750-square-foot ADU in compliance with 
state law and that only a small number of permits—approximately five—had been 
submitted and that staff had not received significant concerns regarding the 
current size limits. 
  
Public Comment:  
A comment was made in support of the amendment. The commentor stated that 
it was a straightforward compliance issue for an important flexible housing option 
for the city.  
 
Commissioner Discussion: 
Commissioners discussed whether the amendment should remain focused on 
state compliance or consider modifying ADU size limitations at this time. 
  
Several commissioners supported adopting the ordinance as drafted to maintain 
compliance with state law while revisiting broader policy considerations during 
the forthcoming development code update. Other commissioners expressed

Agenda Item 5.B.  
 
Subject:  Development Code Update 
Date: October 8, 2026 
Prepared By: 
 
Presented By: 
 
 
Summary:  
The purpose of this agenda item is to provide Planning Commission with a brief update 
on the Development Code Update, including consultant selection, the anticipated scope 
and schedule, and Planning Commission's role in the project. 
 
Staff expects to return to Planning Commission in the near future for a more focused 
project kickoff discussion once the consultant is under contract and onboarded. 
 
Background / Prior Discussions: 
Following a competitive procurement process that included nine proposals and 
interviews with four finalist teams, a City staff team selected Clarion Associates as the 
preferred consultant. Clarion will lead a multidisciplinary consultant team with 
specialized expertise in development codes, housing, sustainability, urban design, 
market analysis, and community engagement.  
 
City Council is scheduled to consider approval of the Clarion contract and proposed 
scope of services on October 6. If approved, staff will begin project initiation with the 
consultant shortly thereafter. The consultant anticipates an approximately 18-month 
process, although the schedule will remain flexible based on the issues identified and 
the amount of review and iteration needed.    
 
The project is a comprehensive update of the City's development regulations, primarily 
Titles 16 and 17 of the Louisville Municipal Code and the City's adopted design 
standards and guidelines, with targeted updates to Title 15 as needed. The update will 
address topics including: 
• Development review procedures and administration; 
• Zoning districts, permitted uses, and use-specific standards; 
• Housing and environmental sustainability regulations and incentives; 
• Development and design standards, including parking, landscaping, site design, 
sustainability, and compatibility; 
• Subdivision regulations; 
• The City's existing PUD/GDP and legacy zoning framework; and 
Page 23 of 25
• Implementation of adopted City policies and applicable state requirements. 
 
The work will build from policy direction established through the Comprehensive Plan, 
Housing Plan, and other adopted City policies rather than reopening those broader 
policy processes. The proposed scope organizes the work around an initial code 
diagnosis and regulatory framework, preparation and iterative review of draft code 
modules and design standards, adoption, and post-adoption implementation. 
 
PLANNING COMMISSION ROLE AND NEXT STEPS 
Planning Commission will have a significant and ongoing advisory role throughout the 
project. The proposed scope includes numerous Planning Commission discussions and 
work sessions beginning with the initial project diagnosis and continuing through 
development of the regulatory framework, review of individual code modules, zoning 
changes, consolidated drafts, and ultimately the adoption process. The process is 
intentionally iterative, with opportunities for Planning Commission, City Council, 
community members, and stakeholders to review and inform the work as it develops.     
 
Community engagement will similarly occur throughout the project and is anticipated to 
include broad community-wide engagement as well as topic-specific focus groups 
involving developers, architects, housing providers, sustainability professionals, City 
boards and commissions, and other stakeholders.     
Following Council consideration of the consultant contract, staff plans to return to 
Planning Commission for a dedicated kickoff discussion. At that time, staff and the 
consultant will provide additional detail regarding the project schedule, major phases 
and decision points, community engagement approach, and how Planning Commission 
and City Council will be involved throughout the process.
