Subject:  Joint Meeting with Historic Museum Advisory Board 
Date: September 22, 2026 
Prepared By: Gigi Yang, Museum Services Supervisor 
Presented By: Gigi Yang, Museum Services Supervisor 
 
Summary:  
This joint meeting between the City Council and the Historical Museum Advisory Board 
(HMAB) is intended to create an open, collaborative discussion about the role of local 
history, preservation, and education in building a vibrant and connected Louisville 
community. 
 
The Historical Museum Advisory Board advises the Historical Museum Division of the 
Cultural Services Department and helps ensure that Louisville’s history is thoughtfully 
preserved and shared in ways that reflect the full, rich story of the community. The 
Museum’s collections are rooted in the foundational stories of Louisville—including coal 
mining, immigration, labor rights, and the people, families, industries, and movements 
that shaped the community from before its founding through the Louisville of today. 
Through this work, the Board supports opportunities for residents to see themselves as 
part of Louisville’s story and to contribute directly to the community’s ever-growing 
historical record. 
 
Central to this work is ensuring that the stories Louisville preserves and shares continue 
to broaden our understanding of the community—reflecting the people, experiences, 
perspectives, and moments that have shaped Louisville across generations. 
 
During the joint meeting, HMAB members will share perspectives on the community’s 
evolving cultural needs, opportunities to strengthen Louisville’s Historical Museum and 
the preservation of local history, alignment with professional standards and best 
practices, and priorities for collections and education. Council will have an opportunity to 
share its priorities and perspectives and discuss how the City can continue fostering a 
strong, inclusive, and sustainable historical record for future generations. 
 
Background / Prior Discussions: 
City Council and the Historical Museum Advisory Board meet jointly each year to 
discuss priorities for history, preservation, and education and to maintain strong 
coordination between Council and the Board. 
 
This year’s joint meeting provides an opportunity for Council and the HMAB to consider

how these efforts can continue to evolve, where opportunities exist to deepen

Louisville’s broader goals for community connection, vibrancy, and quality of life.
