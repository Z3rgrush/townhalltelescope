Subject:  Joint Meeting with Sustainability Advisory Board 
Date: August 11, 2026 
Prepared By: Hannah Miller, Sustainability Manager 
Presented By: Louisville Sustainability Advisory Board 
 
Summary:  
This joint meeting between the Louisville Sustainability Advisory Board and City Council 
is intended to create an open, collaborative discussion about the future of sustainability 
in Louisville. LSAB will share ideas related to climate action, funding, water 
conservation, building electrification, and community engagement, while Council will 
have the opportunity to provide feedback, share priorities, and discuss implementation 
challenges. The goal is to exchange perspectives, identify areas of alignment, and 
strengthen collaboration on the City’s near- and long-term sustainability priorities. 
 
Background / Prior Discussions: 
The Louisville Sustainability Advisory Board (LSAB) and City Council hold a joint 
meeting each year to discuss the City's sustainability priorities and strengthen 
collaboration. This year's discussion builds on recent work, including the City's updated 
greenhouse gas inventory and ICLEI's greenhouse gas wedge analysis, which found 
that Louisville is not currently on track to meet its adopted Climate Action Goals without 
additional implementation efforts. 
 
Following those discussions, City Council approved reallocating the remaining 
Sustainability Action Plan funding from additional planning to implementation-focused 
programs. This joint meeting provides an opportunity for LSAB and City Council to 
discuss future priorities, implementation opportunities, and the City's long-term 
sustainability direction.

Pavement
 Pavement program is a significant component of the City’s Capital fund at
~$5M+ annually.
 Current PCI (Pavement Condition Index) is73/74
 The City conducts an independent scan every 3 years
 Previous Council direction was to achieve a network average of 75 with no
street below 35.
 Staff believes the City can maintain (for a time) current PCI by spending
~$3M annually, but in some years additional funding may be needed due
to harsh winters or arterial needs.
 Council Decision Point – Direct Staff to reduce Paving Program and
direct to Front & Center – Phase 2.
