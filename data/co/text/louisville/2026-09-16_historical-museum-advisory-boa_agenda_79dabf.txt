Memorandum 
To: Historical Museum Advisory Board 
 From: Gigi Yang, Museum Services Supervisor 
 Date:  September 16, 2026 
 Re: Museum Services Supervisor’s Report 
September is Louisville History Month, and we jumped in with back-to-back events during the 
First Friday event, Lens on Louisville opening, Museum’s 40th birthday celebration, and Labor 
Day parade! We have received many appreciative comments from the community about the 
Museum’s work and presence over the decades.  
 
We are looking forward to the Vintage base ball game on September 19. In partnership with 
the Louisville History Foundation, the game is a popular way to bring history to life for the 
entire community. This year, we will be adding a fundraising element for the Foundation to the 
event, as well as additional fun with the Boulder Brassaholics band and a few Living Legends 
historic characters. We will wind up Louisville History Month with an exploration of baseball 
history at the September 30th History Happy Hour event at Louisville Underground with Kurt 
Wells from the Society of American Baseball Records. He will be highlighting Louisville and 
Colorado players of note from early days to the present. 
 
We had successful events celebrating Colorado Day and the Museum’s 40th birthday and 
greatly appreciated the participation of Museum volunteers, HMAB, and the Louisville History 
Foundation in leading activities and engaging with the public at these events. We also enjoyed 
outreach events on the library plaza during the Farmer’s Market where we have had the 
opportunity to share some Louisville trivia, promote upcoming programs and highlight the work 
of HMAB, Louisville History Foundation, and the Historic Preservation Commission. We have 
two more dates scheduled on Sept 12 and Oct 10. 
 
We are finishing up sesquisemiquincentennial reflections with the latest issue of the Louisville 
Historian featuring an article on suffrage by Museum Associate, Eilish Brennan. A new front 
window exhibit will be installed in September highlighting the ideal of “Consent of the 
Governed” and the bumpy road to Colorado statehood and suffrage. Please stop by to 
discover Colorado’s remarkable role in shaping voting rights and equality.

Benefitting the Community
2024 Community Survey ranked as excellent or good: 
• 97% rated overall Museum services
• 96% Museum programs (lectures, walking tours, newsletters, expanded/ new programming)
• 98% rated Museum customer services
Museum services, programs, and staff all contributed to priorities from the community:
• 60% Economic Prosperity - through heritage tourism, tourism, as a visitor destination
• 46% Recreation - through informal and life-long learning experiences, historic interpretation of 
landscape and built environment, walking tours
• 45% Library - through collaborations, preschool programs, and free family-oriented activities
• 56% Public safety - the Museum contributes to a strong community identity and sense of place
• 43% Admin and support services – DEI content, historical knowledge, Historic Preservation research
