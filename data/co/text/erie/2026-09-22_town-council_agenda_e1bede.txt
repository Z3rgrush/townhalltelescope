Domestic Violence Awareness Month 
  
WHEREAS, domestic violence is a pervasive issue that affects individuals and families 
across our nation, and the Town of Erie is not immune to its impact; and 
WHEREAS, the month of October has been designated as Domestic Violence Awareness

services to elevate awareness about domestic violence and contribute to the creation of

Background of Subject Matter:

interest to continue to invest in the CBT project when funds and timing allow.

Council transfer $9,150,000 to Budget Line Number 500-75-110-603500- 267509 (Raw

Background of Subject Matter: 
The Sunset Area is an area that consists of the Erie Singletrack Trail on Sunset Open 
Space, the Sunset West Multi-use Trails, and the surrounding open space properties. 
The Sunset Area Plan is being implemented in phases as funding allows and as 
adjacent development occurs. The goal of the project is to provide concrete trail 
connections to the area as well as enhance the trailhead and bike skills amenities in the 
area. The Phase 1 Trail Connection Project was recently completed in May 2026 and 
connects the Erie Singletrack Trailhead to the Sunset West Multi-use Trails. Phase 2A, 
which is currently being designed with grant funding support from Colorado Parks & 
Wilflife, will connect the west end of Phase 1 to the Coal Creek Regional Trail. 
Completion of this phase will provide a continuous concrete trail from the Coal Creek 
Regional Trail to the Erie Singletrack Trails and beyond. Subdivisions in east Erie, such 
as Sunset and Westerly, will be provided off-street trail connections to the Coal Creek 
Regional Trail.  
 
This grant request will supplement Trails & Natural Areas Fund (TNAF) funding for 
these trail connections. Funding from TNAF is being requested as part of the 2027 
budget and the appropriation has received a favorable recommendation from the Open 
Space and Trails Advisory Board (OSTAB). TNAF has sufficient fund balance for the 
required match. 
• Total Grant Request: $250,000 
• Town Match (2027 Trails & Natural Areas Fund Request): $650,000

Summary/Key Points: 
This agreement allows the Town to retain an on-call consultant for Landscape 
Architectural, Planning and Design services for the next year. 
 
Background of Subject Matter: 
On July 22, 2025, the Town posted a Request for Proposals (RFP) solicitation on the 
Rocky Mountain E-Purchasing website (aka BidNet) for On-Call Landscape 
Architectural, Planning & Design Services.  
Page 52 of 248
 
On-call consultants act as an extension of Town staff and provide specialized 
professional services as project needs arise. The agreements provide services including 
planning, landscape and site design, construction documents, cost estimating, bidding 
assistance, construction administration, engineering, and other related technical 
services. The consultants were carefully selected for their qualifications, experience, 
pricing, and ability to acquire sub-consultants for specialty tasks when required by the 
Town. Projects exceeding the scope of the on-call services are advertised for bid. 
 
On Aug. 15, 2025, the Town received 19 proposals through the competitive process and 
selected four qualified firms: 
• DHM Design Corporation  
• Wenk Associates, Inc.  
• Stream Landscape Architecture + Planning  
• Kimley-Horn and Associates, Inc.  
 
Each individual agreement had an initial 12-month term with the Town with two optional 
12-month renewal terms. The proposed First Amendment exercises the first renewal 
option through Sept. 24, 2027, and establishes a contract amount not to exceed 
$1,500,000. Funds for this contract will come from various projects that utilize funding 
from different capital improvement project (CIP) budgets. These funds will not be 
encumbered up front. Funds will be encumbered as Task Orders are assigned over the 
course of the contract term.
