Employment Agreement 
This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement"), drafted by the Employer, is made 
and entered into as of the 20th day  of October, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and 
between the Town of Erie, a Colorado home rule municipal corporation with an address of 
645 Holbrook Street, P.O. Box 750, Erie, CO 80516 (the "Town"), and Kelli Johnson, an 
individual ("Johnson") (each a "Party" and collectively, the "Parties"). 
Whereas, the Town wishes to employ Johnson as the Town Manager pursuant to 
Section 8.01 of the Erie Home Rule Charter (the "Charter") and the Erie Municipal Code 
(the "Code"); 
Whereas, Johnson is appointed by, reports to, and serves at the pleasure of the 
Town Council; and 
Whereas, Johnson wishes to accept employment as the Town Manager as of 
January 4, 2027, under the terms set forth in this Agreement. 
Now therefore, for the consideration hereinafter set forth, the receipt and 
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
1. Employment. 
a. Pursuant to the terms of this Agreement, the Town agrees to employ 
Johnson as Town Manager, and Johnson accepts such employment.  Such employment 
is "at will." 
b. Johnson's primary function shall be to serve as the Town Manager and to 
perform the functions and duties specified in Section 8.01 of the Charter, the duties as 
prescribed in the Code, as well as the job description, a copy of which is attached hereto 
as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference (and any future amendments thereto 
which are deemed incorporated herein).  Johnson acknowledges and agrees and 
understands that said duties may change from time to time as provided by the Charter, 
the Code, the job description, or as otherwise directed by the Town Council. As the Town 
Manager, Johnson shall be responsible for the organization and efficient administration 
of all administrative departments of the Town, in accordance with applicable law and the 
job description attached hereto. 
c. During the term of this Agreement, Johnson agrees to remain in the 
exclusive employ of the Town and neither to accept nor to become employed by any 
other employer. The term "employed" shall not be construed to include occasional 
teaching, speaking, receiving honoraria, writing, or consulting, as approved by the Town 
Council, performed on Johnson's time off with prior written approval of the Town Council. 
d. Johnson is permitted to exercise a flexible work schedule. However, 
consistent with this flexibility and Johnson's participation in activities out of the office or

e. Johnson acknowledges that the Town Manager role is one which requires 
nonpartisanship. Johnson therefore shall not endorse candidates, make financial

appropriation, shall continue for an indefinite term; provided that nothing in this 
Agreement shall change the at-will nature of Johnson’s employment nor prevent, limit, or

interfere with the right of Johnson to resign at any time, for any reason.  Nothing 
contained in this Agreement shall be deemed or construed as creating any property or 
other right to a continuation of Johnson's employment. For purposes of this Agreement, 
the anniversary date of Johnson's employment with the Town shall be January 4th of 
each year (the "Anniversary Date").

3. Compensation. 
a. For her employment as Town Manager, Johnson shall be compensated at 
an annual base salary of $280,000, which shall be payable in installments at the same 
time and manner as other management employees of the Town are paid.  The Town 
Council may adjust the base salary as the Town Council determines appropriate, in the

services and all hours worked. 
4. Benefits. In addition to the Compensation set forth in Section 3, Johnson shall 
receive the following benefits during her employment as Town Manager: 
a. Insurance. Health, life, vision, and dental insurance shall be provided by 
the Town for Johnson and her applicable dependents in the same manner as for all other 
full-time employees of the Town, which means Johnson will be required to pay the same 
premium or other fees as required by other full -time employees for the same coverage 
for such insurance. 
b. Disability. The Town shall pay for short -term and long -term disability 
benefits for Johnson in the same manner as for all other full-time employees of the Town. 
c. Holidays and Paid Time Off. Johnson shall receive paid Town holidays and 
paid time off (“PTO”) in the same manner as for a full-time employee whose tenure equals 
at least 6 years of service to the Town in accordance with Town policies. The Town will 
offer a one-time preloaded PTO bank of 120 hours. 
d. Retirement. The Town agrees to pay, in installments at the same time as 
other management employees of the Town are paid, (a) an amount equal to 10% of 
Johnson's base salary into Johnson’s Town of Erie 401a Plan; and (b) an amount equal to  
Johnson’s personal contribution into Johnson’s Town of Erie 401a  Plan, not to exceed 5% 
of Johnson's base salary. All of the Town's contributions shall vest immediately on Johnson's 
first day of employment.  If the cumulative amount of the Plan contributions exceeds  the 
then-current annual limit under the Internal Revenue Code for contributions to a deferred 
compensation plan, the remainder of the Town’s contributions shall be paid to Johnson as 
taxable compensation in the applicable pay periods.  Johnson may also elect to direct 
additional portions of her salary into a 457b deferred compensation plan, but the Town will not 
match those contributions.

Job Summary: 
Appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Town Council, the Town Manager is the Town’s chief 
administrative officer and is responsible for providing strategic leadership, overseeing the effective 
administration of municipal operations, and implementing the policies and priorities established by the 
Town Council. 
The Town Manager serves as the Town Council’s principal advisor, ensuring transparent 
communication, sound organizational management, and the efficient delivery of high -quality public 
services. The Town Manager provides executive oversight for the Town’s operations, departments, and 
personnel, fostering a collaborative, high-performing organization that delivers exceptional service to 
the community. The position is responsible for organizational leadership, workforce management, 
budget development and administration, financial planning, risk management, and operational 
accountability while ensuring compliance with applicable laws, ordinances, and Town policies. The 
Town Manager regularly reports to the Town Council on organizational performance, financial 
conditions, strategic initiatives, and community priorities. 
The Town Manager appoints and supervises key members of the executive leadership team and 
establishes an effective organizational structure that supports the Town’s strategic goals. This position 
also develops succession plans for executive leadership, coordinates implementation of Council 
direction across the organization, and performs additional duties as assigned by the Town Council and 
prescribed by Town ordinance. 
All staff are expected to adhere to the Town’s mission, vision and values of TRUE. 
 
Duties and Responsibilities: 
• The Town Manager is a strategic, approachable, and politically astute leader who brings vision, 
sound judgment, and a steady presence to the organization. 
• See the big picture while translating long-term priorities into practical action, balancing 
competing interests with confidence, integrity, and transparency. 
• Thoughtful and decisive, provide candid advice, navigate complex issues with professionalism, 
and remain focused on achieving the Town Council’s vision for Erie. 
• An exceptional relationship builder, putting people first.

As required by Town Code, every five years the Town of Erie engages a consultant to 
review the Town’s capital needs as it relates to infrastructure required due to population 
and commercial growth. The current study is underway.

of the Impact Fee study. He will also review the defined purpose of these funds, uses

• Transportation Impact Fund 
• Parks Improvement Fund 
• Storm Drainage Impact Fund

Background of Subject Matter: 
The Town imposes fees for a variety of infrastructure needs related to the impacts of 
growth. These one-time fees are referred to as Impact Fees. The Town of Erie has 
imposed Impact Fees for over 25 years to help address the effects of growth. They were 
first adopted in 1995 for implementation in 1996. Every five years, the Town engages a 
consultant to review the Town’s capital needs as it relates to infrastructure required due 
to population and commercial growth. This presentation is an opportunity for Council to 
review preliminary results and ask questions of the consultant about the current study 
and process.
