II. Adjournment 
The Town Council's goal is that all meetings be adjourned by 10:30 p.m. An agenda check will 
be conducted at or about 10:00 p.m., and no later than at the end of the first item finished after 
10:00 p.m. Items not completed prior to adjournment will generally be taken up at the next 
regular meeting.
