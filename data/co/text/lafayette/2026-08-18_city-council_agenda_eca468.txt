Council Deliberation 
Acknowledging that there was still work to be done before final passage, Council generally 
supported the code and process, noting that the Planning Commission had recommended 
approval with conditions. There was some concern that too many details remained unresolved 
and that the Land Use Code Working Group did not have an opportunity to weigh in on the final 
draft. 
Council proposed the following: (1) remove the private credit provision that reduces the required 
Public Land Dedication amount; (2) accept the proposed zoning map, with direction that the 
church parcels at Highway 287 and South Boulder Road be zoned consistently with Immaculate 
Conception (NC), and that staff bring a recommendation at second reading; (3) hold a Land Use 
Code Working Group meeting before second reading on September 15; (4) create flowchart 
diagrams illustrating how the Land Use Code operates in practice, including how multiple 
processes can be combined; (5) allow childcare as an approved use and ensure the review 
process is as simple as possible; (6) add Sustainable Design Features (Section 26 5 5(b)) to the 
City Council/Planning Commission workshop agenda; (7) add roof pitch for solar panels to the 
City Council/Planning Commission workshop agenda; and (8) add affordable housing language 
to the intent statement for the Manufactured or Mobile Home Community zoning district. 
A motion was made by Councilor Gianola and seconded by Councilor Jensen to approve on first 
reading Ordinance No. 15, Series 2026, repealing and replacing Chapter 26 of the Code of 
Ordinances with a new Chapter 26 Titled “Land Use Code,” repealing the existing zoning map and 
replacing it with a new zoning map, and rezoning properties within the city thereby; and setting 
an effective date. The motion passed 6-0 with Councilor Gallegos present and Councilor Fridland 
absent for the vote. 
These minutes were amended by motion at the September 15, 2026, City Council meeting to 
replace the above “(2) accept the proposed zoning map, with a request to evaluate consistent 
zoning for churches along Highway 287” with “(2) accept the proposed zoning map, with 
direction that the church parcels at Highway 287 and South Boulder Road be zoned consistently 
with Immaculate Conception (NC), and that staff bring a recommendation at second reading.”
