VI. Presentations 
H. New Online Service Portal 
In September, the City will release a new online service portal where permits, licenses, reported 
concerns, and land use reviews will be received and coordinated. The new portal will replace 
several disparate systems to provide a more collaborative, transparent, and foundational 
approach for staff to engage with the community. 
Ernesto Chavez presented background information and objectives for the project, key features 
and functions, hypercare support, and next steps. 
Council asked about the relationship with the Continuity of Operations (COOP) Plan, user profile 
and account requirements, ease of use for business owners and contractors, efficiency metrics, 
communication to users, integration of AI tools, using the “report a concern” feature, and the 
number of processes migrating to the system.   
VII. Regular Business 
I. First Reading / Ordinance No. 16, Series 2026 / Authorizing the Issuance of City of Lafayette, 
Colorado General Obligation Bonds, Series 2026 
Megan Davis, Heather Balser, and Mattie Prodanovic presented context and timeline 
information regarding the bond issuance, timing and issuance considerations, project status 
and next steps, ordinance parameters and accompanying documents, a market update, 
anticipated financing timeline, and next steps. 
Council asked about the bond sale and bond rating metrics, expected annual trends and 
impacts of elections, the spend-down requirement, balancing and prioritizing community 
feedback related to facility design, development and public engagement requirements for the 
service center project, potential plans for the existing Senior Center, and priority uses of the 
recreation center space. 
A motion was made by Councilor Jensen and seconded by Councilor Gianola to approve on first 
reading Ordinance No. 16, Series 2026, authorizing the issuance of City of Lafayette, Colorado, 
General Obligation Bonds, Series 2026. The motion passed 5-0 with mayor Pro Tem Barnes 
absent for the vote.

Heather Balser reviewed the proposed updated purchasing limits. The changes reflected

VIII. Staff Reports

City Attorney Macsalka reminded Council of the “Colorado Fair Campaign Practices (FCPA) / 
Restrictions on the Use of City Funds and Resources for Campaigning” memo she sent last

L. City Manager’s Report 
A joint workshop with the Planning Commission is scheduled for September 9. The September 
15 meeting will feature presentations on a short-term rental festival license and moratoriums 
on data centers and air mobility and second reading of the Land Use Code. A capital 
improvements tour is scheduled for the September 22 workshop. 
The October 6 meeting will feature first readings of the short-term rental festival license and 
moratorium ordinances and a public hearing on the 2027 budget. City, General Improvement 
District, and Downtown Development Authority budget items are scheduled for October 20. A 
presentation on Flock automatic license plate readers is scheduled for the October 27 
workshop.
