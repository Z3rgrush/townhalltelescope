SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS 
CITY OF LAFAYETTE, COLORADO 
City Council Workshop 
July 28, 2026 
Mayor Tapia Vega opened the July 28, 2026, workshop of the Lafayette City Council at 
5:31pm in the Council Chambers at Lafayette City Hall, 1290 S. Public Road, Lafayette, 
Colorado.  
Those attending in Council Chambers included Mayor Saul Tapia Vega, Mayor Pro Tem Tim 
Barnes, and Councilors Kyle Beaulieu, Crystal Gallegos, and Annmarie Jensen. Councilor 
Adam Gianola attended remotely. Councilor David Fridland was absent. 
City staff included City Manager Kady Doelling, City Attorney Mary Lynn Macsalka, Assistant 
to the City Manager Alex Nelson, City Clerk Lynnette Beck, Deputy City Attorney Erin Poe, 
Public Works Director Jeff Arthur, Senior Civil Engineer Casey Carlson, Engineering and 
Capital Projects Division Manager Leslie Ewy, Community Services Director Melissa Hisel, 
Long Range Planning Manager Phil Kleisler, Principal Transportation Engineer Michelle 
Melonakis, and Planning and Building Director Steven Williams. 
I. Transportation Update  
Jeff Arthur, Leslie Ewy, Casey Carlson, and Michelle Melonakis highlighted existing 
system assets and regional context, guiding documents for transportation priorities, 
asset management, construction and planning projects, regional collaboration, 
missing links, and Waneka Lake Trail modifications. 
Council asked about the timeline for infrastructure construction projects, influencing 
RTD route evaluations, current construction and rehabilitation projects, maintenance 
responsibility for state roads, HAWK signals, PCI (Pavement Condition Index) rating 
cycles, messaging to the public about construction projects, increased neighborhood 
traffic resulting from intersection improvements, Waneka Lake Trail modifications, 
photo radar enforcement, the Safe Routes to School Action Plan, and timing for 
RTD’s BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) service. 
II. Land Use Code Update  
Steven Williams, Phil Kleisler, and Mary Lynn Macsalka presented the project goals 
and steps, code amendment approval process and schedule, Planning Commission 
recommendations, and next steps. 
Council asked about advanced air mobility, regulation of data centers, a joint 
workshop with the Planning Commission, mobile home regulations, combining lots, 
requirements for neighborhood meetings, subarea plans, planned outreach prior to 
the effective date, moratoriums, new zone districts, and notice for the public hearing. 
Council generally supported the proposed schedule and the Planning Commission’s 
recommended changes and was interested in a joint workshop with the Commission. 
Council wanted to discuss rezoning in areas that could potentially result in special 
use and nonconforming use scenarios. Proposals for this discussion will be advanced 
at first reading.

III. Board and Commission Update 
Alex Nelson presented potential updates to the Human Rights Board scope and the 
recruitment timeline for the Planning Commission.  
Council asked about different board and commission structures, rationale for 
changing the schedule of only the Planning Commission recruitment, and moving all 
the boards and commissions to a similar recruitment schedule. 
Council was in agreement about the new scope for the Human Rights Board and 
discussed new recruitment schedules for all boards and commissions and potentially 
requiring a certain amount of time to pass before an individual could serve on a City 
board or commission after having been a member of City Council. 
IV. New Ideas 
Councilor Jensen requested a workshop regarding plugging abandoned wells. Council 
was in agreement. The Mayor and City Manager will discuss a format for the 
discussion. 
The workshop was adjourned at 9:00pm.
