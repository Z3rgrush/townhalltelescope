SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS 
City of Lafayette, Colorado 
City Council Workshop  
Lafayette City Hall, 1290 S. Public Road 
September 22, 2026 
5:30pm 
Bus Tour of City Capital Improvement Projects 
City Council’s September workshop was a bus tour of capital improvement projects. Transportation 
was provided and departed from City Hall at 5:31pm. 
Those in attendance from City Council were Mayor Saul Tapia Vega, Mayor Pro Tem Tim Barnes, 
and Councilors Kyle Beaulieu, Crystal Gallegos, Adam Gianola, and Annmarie Jensen. Councilor 
David Fridland was absent. 
Participating City staff included City Manager Kady Doelling, Deputy City Manager Megan Davis, 
Assistant to the City Manager Alex Nelson, Bilingual Communications Coordinator Sebastian 
Acevedo, Public Works Director Jeff Arthur, Water Resources Senior Engineer Melanie Asquith, 
Chief Financial Officer Heather Balser, Communications Director Erin Frazier, Operations Manager 
Callie Hayden, Community Services Director Melissa Hisel, Water Treatment Manager Scott Pavlik, 
Interim Deputy Recreation Director Morgan Skalla, and Deputy Parks, Recreation, and Golf Director 
Dennis Warrington. 
Members of the public included Roy Johnson, Si Qi Neo, Karen Norback, Frank Phillips, Vicky 
Uhland, Holly Valenta, Haruka Watson, and Mike Watson. 
The tour provided an opportunity to learn more about Lafayette’s capital projects:  
• Lamont Does Park and Great Outdoors Water Park 
• Service Center 
• Water Treatment Plan, Distribution, and Storage 
• West Baseline Road Multimodal Safety Improvements 
• Bob L. Burger Recreation Center 
• Future Civic Center 
The tour concluded at City Hall.
