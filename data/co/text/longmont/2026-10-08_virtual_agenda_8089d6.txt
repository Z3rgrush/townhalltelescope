160 11th Avenue Variance 
Proposal: Replat existing “Outlot B” of the Darling Minor Subdivision into a developable lot for construction 
of a detached single-family home by Habitat for Humanity 
Project Location: 160 11th Avenue (see vicinity map on the reverse side) 
When: October 8, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. 
Where: Via livestream at https://www.youtube.com/@cityoflongmont. There will be an opportunity for 
questions. 
Property Owner: Habitat for Humanity of the St. Vrain Valley Inc.  
Applicant: Habitat for Humanity of the St. Vrain 
Background: This 0.11-acre property is owned by Habitat for Humanity of the St. Vrain Valley, who are 
seeking to build a detached single-family home on the site. This parcel is subject to a 100-foot setback from 
riparian habitat area along Spring Gulch #1 and is located entirely within this setback area. The process for 
obtaining relief from this setback requires completion of a sustainability evaluation, and approval of a 
variance from the required setback by City Council.  
Future Meetings: The Longmont City Council is the decision-making body for variances from required 
riparian habitat setbacks, with the Planning and Zoning Commission making a recommendation to City 
Council. If this project applies for a riparian setback variance and goes through the full development review 
process, public hearings before the Planning and Zoning Commission and City Council will be held at future 
dates to be determined. 
Additional notification of all public hearings before the Planning & Zoning Commission and City Council will 
be provided, as required by City regulations. If you have questions regarding the neighborhood meeting, the 
development review process, code requirements, or other specific items, please contact the staff member 
identified on the reverse side.
