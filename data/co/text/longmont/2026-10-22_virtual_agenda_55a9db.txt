605 – 607 Emery Street Short Term Rental 
Proposal: Conditional Use for Short Term Rental at 607 Emery Street 
Project Location: 605 – 607 Emery Street (see vicinity map on the reverse side) 
When: October 22, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. 
Where: Via livestream at https://www.youtube.com/@cityoflongmont  
Property Owner: Noah Sturm & Taylor Sturm 
Applicant: Noah Sturm 
Background: 605 – 607 Emery Street is an existing duplex. Owner seeks intermittent use of 607 Emery 
Street as a short-term rental. There is an existing short-term rental located at 619 Emery Street. Per the Land 
Development Code, no more than one short-term rental may exist on the same street segment of a block. 
Additional short-term rentals on the same block require conditional use approval, as is the case with this 
property. 
Future Meetings: The Planning and Zoning Commission is the decision-making body on Conditional Uses. If 
this project submits an application and goes through the full development review process, public hearings 
with the Planning and Zoning Commission will be held at a future date to be determined. 
Additional notification of all public hearings before the Planning & Zoning Commission will be provided, as 
required by City regulations. If you have questions regarding the neighborhood meeting, the development 
review process, code requirements, or other specific items, please contact the staff member identified 
below.
