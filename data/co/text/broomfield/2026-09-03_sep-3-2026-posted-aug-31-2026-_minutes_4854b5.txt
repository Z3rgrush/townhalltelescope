6. Strategic Plan and Al Civics - Alison Harvey 
a. Al Civics - We are onboard to help shape this program from Data and Society. 
i. Data and Society is a nonprofit focused on social impacts. 
ii. We are one of 8 libraries in the country on this Al Civics pilot. 
iii. The program manager came and had various meetings, met with staff, etc. 
Data and Society wants to create a toolkit for public libraries to pick up that 
will help people understand governance, outcomes, etc. of Al and Al Queries. 
iv. The program starts with public libraries, and will move to K-12; then to Faith 
institutions in year 3. Meg Young came - researcher; comes from background of 
Al and government. 
v. Site visits were about listening to what the library is actually seeing - what 
does the reference desk see? What does the team need to feel equipped to 
support people? What tools would be helpful re: community engagement on Al? 
vi. Tools - slide decks, scripts, etc that are both internal facing; when is it the 
right time to ask bigger picture questions? 
vii. Community of practice around Al - Google Suite; Gemini, Good Guardrails is the 
aim. 
viii. Reminder that people who don't have internet access at home come to the 
library to access it. 
ix. Second thing they'll work on is a community facing program, historically based. 
It will focus on technology transformations of the past. It will be based around 
literacy, but also ethics. 
x. Tools that we'll pilot test in 2027; also full toolbox when it is done.
