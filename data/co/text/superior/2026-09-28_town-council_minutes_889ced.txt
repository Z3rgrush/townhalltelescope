Presentations 
 
 
 a. Boulder County Co-Responder Program Presentation 
Town Manager Matt Magley introduced Megan Grimes, MSW, Co-Responder 
Supervisor and Molly McDonald, LCSW, Program Manager/Clinical Supervisor 
from the Boulder County Co-Responder Program. Co-Responder's Grimes and 
McDonald presented information on the program and the service provided to 
the Town of Superior since 2021.  Town Council members asked questions 
and thanked the members of the program for their service.
