3. Staff Updates 
a. Q3 2026 budget report 
The CIP Public Art Acquisition budget is as expected at this time. There are two remaining films 
this year so the remaining cost will be in the $900-1000 range, with approximately $600 left in 
the Annual Programming budget. JG will provide monthly budget reports for the rest of the 
year. 
b. Mural festival admin 
Streetwise, Communications, Events team, and JG are each managing different areas of the 
Mural Festival. The murals are currently in progress, and signs have been posted to inform the 
public about the work. The Civic Space is serving as headquarters for the artists. Additional 
volunteers are needed for the event, and CAPS assistance with volunteer recruitment would be 
helpful. A map along with a numbered list of artist names will coincide with large pink feather-
flags with numbers at each mural will be available to help the public self-guide. The artists’ talk 
will take place outdoors on October 1. JG has ordered free Mural Festival tote bags to hand out 
as an incentive to shop. A Happy Hour for CAPS, Town Council, Street Wise, and the artists will 
follow the event.

5. Work Plan Check-In 
a. ARTery 
The ARTery project, which began in 2019, is continuing its expansion through town with 
a plan for various forms of public artworks. For 2026, Claire and Xochi are developing a 
segment that will include Circle Park and the open area behind Safeway. The committee 
is focused on using the remaining 2026 budget effectively, including artwork for utility 
boxes and new art benches. The committee discussed several bench options, including 
custom prefabricated swing benches made from recycled wind-turbines by Noblewins. A 
lava bench was suggested for Circle Park, and Moe Gram was suggested as an artist for a 
turbine bench. The committee is also considering artwork for the 5–8 utility boxes in the 
area, potentially using different artists for groups of boxes.  Because of the cost and 
requirements associated with commissioning a custom piece, the committee 
determined that a custom bench cannot be completed this year. Instead, the focus will 
be on purchasing two already fabricated benches and developing artwork for the utility 
boxes. Information for the Council presentation must be provided to JG by October 19. 
Andrew proposed completing artwork for all eight utility boxes and installing two 
Noblewins benches at approximately $8,000 each plus the cost for shipping and 
concrete pads to be poured, for a grand total of approximately $12,000 each.  
Andrew made a motion that we complete the artwork on the 8 Utility Boxes with the 
proposed art in the plan developed by Claire and Xochi for approximately $12,000 and the 
purchase of two Noblewins swing benches installed at $12,000 each for a grand total of 
$36,000. Claire seconded.  The motion was passed by roll call vote.
