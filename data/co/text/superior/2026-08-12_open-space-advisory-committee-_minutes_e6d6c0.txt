d. Prairie Dog (PD) Management Plan outline review (7:10) 
Amber: A draft update was with OSAC. The original playbook was specific to 
the Zaharias property and needs to be expanded into other areas within 
Superior. Amber is updating the plan and playbook to proactively address 
movements of PDs around town, gathering data on populations, conservation 
and disbursal recommendations, collaboration with BOCO and other agencies, 
private and public considerations, relocation strategies, conflict management, 
involvement of potential vendors to assist with management activities.  
e. Current Prairie Dog management activities (7:20) 
Leslie: Received mapping results from Misty of over 500 PD burrows including 
Coalton Trail head, Coal Creek Crossing, along McCaslin Blvd, and around 
Sagamore. Information will inform plans by town staff for management, 
potential use of barriers, communication with Town, etc. PD information is 
included on the Town website under “Superior Wildlife”

4. Standing Updates (7:50) 
a. Raptor Program - Kara: From July 1- Aug 1st, we logged 44 observations in 
CitSci. The results are as follows: 16 Red Tailed Hawks, 9 American Kestrels, 1 
Swainson’s Hawk, 6 Great Horned Owls, 11 Cooper’s Hawks, and 1 Osprey 
 
● The activity/behavior observed is really interesting… especially with fledgling 
activity… begging calls, etc.  
● Hot summer weather likely explains fewer than normal observations.  
● The Raptor Program will have a booth at Lafayette’s World Migratory Bird Day on 
Sep 13th. Hoping to leverage ideas to improve our own event. 
 
b. Coyote Ridge - Todd: 3 installed Trail counters show 4,200 visits between April- 
August. This number is likely low considering several entry/exits do not currently 
have counters.
