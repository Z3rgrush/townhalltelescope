RE: Request for $30,000 in Conservation Trust Funds – Altitude Community Fitness 
Pool Resurfacing 
 
Dear Mayor and Board of Trustees, 
Custer 2020 dba Altitude Community Fitness respectfully requests $30,000 in 
Conservation Trust Funds from the Town of Silver Cliff to assist with resurfacing our indoor 
swimming pool. 
Altitude Community Fitness has been part of this community for decades, and Custer 2020 
has operated as a nonprofit since 1998 with a mission centered on health, wellness, 
fitness, and recreation. Altitude continues to serve as an important community resource, 
and we greatly appreciate the Town’s support over the years. 
 
Pool Resurfacing Project 
Our swimming pool has not been resurfaced in more than 20 years, and the existing finish 
is significantly deteriorated. 
Current contractor estimates range from approximately $98,000 to $160,000. Altitude is 
currently pursuing a proposal near the lower end of that range, at approximately $100,000. 
Through our Save the Pool fundraiser, our community has contributed $60,506.98. Altitude 
also has $11,022.19 already set aside for the project, giving us $71,529.17 currently 
available. 
We are requesting $30,000 in Conservation Trust Funds to help close the anticipated 
funding gap. 
If awarded, the funds would be applied directly toward preparation/removal of the 
deteriorated pool surface and installation of a new pool finish in accordance with the 
selected contractor’s scope of work. 
 
Community and Recreational Use 
Altitude is a nonprofit community fitness facility located in Silver Cliff, and our indoor pool 
is open to the public. The pool supports: 
• Youth and adult swim instruction  
• Water aerobics and aquatic fitness  
• Lifeguard and water-safety training  
• Free use by the Custer County Dive Team for training  
• Free use by the Custer County High School football team for periodic training  
• The annual Kids Triathlon for local third- through fifth-grade students, including 
several weeks of swim instruction  
• Public swimming, family recreation, and other community programming  
The proposed resurfacing is a capital rehabilitation and maintenance improvement to 
an existing recreational facility, intended to preserve this community asset and extend its 
useful life. 
Because Custer 2020 owns the building and property, we understand there may be a 
question regarding the Conservation Trust Fund definition of a “public site. ” We would 
welcome the opportunity to work with the Town on an appropriate MOU, public-use 
agreement, contractual interest, or other partnership if necessary to satisfy CTF 
requirements. 
With $71,529.17 already secured, we are asking the Town of Silver Cliff to help us close 
the remaining gap and preserve this important health, wellness, and recreational resource 
for our community. 
Thank you for considering our request and for the Town’s continued support of Altitude 
Community Fitness. We would be happy to provide contractor estimates, photographs, 
fundraising documentation, or any additional information the Board may need. 
Sincerely, 
Christy Schram 
Operations Manager 
Custer 2020 dba Altitude Community Fitness 
50 Main Street, Silver Cliff, CO 81252 | 719-783-0750

RESOLUTION 08-2026 
 
A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE TOWN CLERK TO SUBMIT A 
FUNDING REQUEST TO DOLA’S LOCAL PLANNING CAPACITY GRANT 
PROGRAM ON BEHALF OF THE TOWN OF SILVER CLIFF 
 
WHEREAS, the voters of Colorado approved Proposition 123 in 2022 creating 
the State Affordable Housing Fund to make certain funds available to local governments 
as defined by C.R.S. 29-32-104; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff, Colorado is a statutory town duly and 
regularly organized and validly existing as a body corporate and politic under and by 
virtue of the Constitution and laws of the State of Colorado and is eligible for Proposition 
123 funding and programing; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Town, having adopted policies and procedures to expedite the 
development review process for affordable housing, has received confirmation from 
Colorado’s Department of Local Affairs (DOLA) that these policies and procedures meet 
the statutory requirements of as required by C.R.S. 29-32-105; and 
 
WHEREAS, Colorado’s Department of Local Affairs (DOLA) announced that 
early adoption of expedited review would qualify local governments for up to forty-five 
thousand dollars of incentive funding through the Local Planning Capacity (LPC) grant 
program; and  
 
WHEREAS, in order to redeem the incentive award funding, DOLA requires an 
“official action” to be taken by the Town Board of Trustees demonstrating support of the 
proposed project;  
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Town Board of Trustees of 
the Town of Silver Cliff, Colorado that: 
 
Section 1.   The Town Board supports the proposed project entitled “Village at Silver 
Cliff” for use of this incentive funding and confirms acceptance of the terms and 
conditions of DOLA’s Local Planning Capacity (LPC) Grant Program. 
 
Section 2.   The Town Board hereby authorizes Ileen Squire, as Town Clerk, to submit 
the proposed project to DOLA for approval, and to execute the grant agreements 
necessary for the awarded grant on behalf of the Town of Silver Cliff.  
 
Section 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage 
and approval. 
 
 
 
Section 4.   If any portion or provision of this resolution shall for any reason be held to 
be invalid or unenforceable, the invalidity or unenforceability of such portion or 
provision shall not affect any of the remaining provisions of this resolution, the intention 
being that the same are severable.
