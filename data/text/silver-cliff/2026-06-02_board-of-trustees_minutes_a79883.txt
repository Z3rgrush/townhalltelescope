TOWN OF SILVER CLIFF 
ORDINANCE NO. 02-2026 
 
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SEC. 2-2-90 OF THE SILVER CLIFF MUNICIPAL 
CODE TO ALLOW FOR VIRTUAL ATTENDANCE TO COUNT IN CALCULATION OF 
QUORUM 
WHEREAS, the Municipal Code for the Town of Silver Cliff  allows for remote 
participation of members of Town boards and committees, including the Board of Trustees; and, 
WHEREAS, the Code further provides limitations on virtual attendance as it applies to the 
calculation of quorum; and, 
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees for the Town of Silver Cliff wishes to relax those 
stringent limitations on the calculation of quorum, as such limitations have proven to be 
detrimental to the effective participation of some members of such boards and committees; and, 
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees wishes to amend the Municipal Code to allow for 
remote participants in such board or committee meetings to count in the calculation of quorum. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF 
THE TOWN OF SILVER CLIFF AS FOLLOWS: 
Section 1: Sec. 2 -2-90 (d) of The Silver Cliff Municipal Code is hereby amended , as 
follows (double underline indicates addition; strikethrough indicates deletion): 
Sec. 2-2-90. Conduct of meetings; voting. 
(d) Members of the Board of Trustees, Planning and Zoning Commission and other 
Town boards and committees may attend meetings of such bodies  by telephone or other 
electronic means  under the terms and conditions contained in this Section. Members 
wishing to attend virtually must advise the Town Clerk of such intention, and such virtual 
attendance must not cause less than a quorum to remain in physical attendance at such a 
meeting. Virtual attendance shall constitute “presence” for the purposes of determining  a 
quorum under this Section.  Further, the Town Clerk shall take reasonable and practical 
measures to ensure that each such meeting is open to the public at all times (except during 
executive sessions), and information is provided to enable the public to participate in such 
meetings by telephone or other electronic means  to the extent that such members of the 
public would otherwise be allowed to participate in such a meeting in person. In the event 
of an emergency appropriately declared in writing beforehand by the Mayor (or appropriate 
official acting in the Mayor’s absence), any such meeting may be conducted by telephone 
or other electronic means. 
(Ord. No. 23-03, § 1; Ord. No. 26-02) 
 
Section 2: The officials of the Town of Silver Cliff are authorized to take any and all 
actions to effectuate the enactment of these changes to the Municipal Code, including publishing 
in the official copies of the Municipal Code kept at Town Hall and published electronically online.

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SEC. 2-2-90 OF THE SILVER CLIFF MUNICIPAL  
CODE TO ALLOW FOR VIRTUAL ATTENDANCE TO COUNT IN CALCULATION OF  
QUORUM  
WHEREAS, the Municipal Code for the Town of Silver Cliff allows for remote  
participation of members of Town boards and committees, including the Board of Trustees; and,  
WHEREAS, the Code further provides limitations on virtual attendance as it applies to the  
calculation of quorum; and,  
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees for the Town of Silver Cliff affirms that  in-person 
attendance at Board meetings is a critical  duty and responsibility of each Trustee, yet recognizes 
the need to relax the current stringent limitations governing the calculation of quorum, as such 
limitations have proven to be  detrimental to the effective participation of certain members of such 
boards and committees; and,  
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees wishes to amend the Municipal Code to allow for  
remote participants in such board or committee meetings to count in the calculation of quorum.  
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF  
THE TOWN OF SILVER CLIFF AS FOLLOWS:  
Section 1: Sec. 2-2-90 (d) of The Silver Cliff Municipal Code is hereby amended, as  
follows (double underline indicates addition; strikethrough indicates deletion):  
Sec. 2-2-90. Conduct of meetings; voting.  
(d) Members of the Board of Trustees, Planning and Zoning Commission and other  
Town boards and committees may attend meetings of such bodies by telephone or other  
electronic means under the terms and conditions contained in this Section. Members  
wishing to attend virtually must advise the Town Clerk of such intention in advance by 24 
hours. Conditions for attending virtually shall be limited to absence due to out of town travel 
or illness., and such virtual  attendance must not cause less than a quorum to remain in 
physical attendance at such a  meeting. Virtual attendance shall constitute “presence” for 
the purposes of determining a  quorum under this Section. Further, the Town Clerk shall 
take reasonable and practical  measures to ensure that each such meeting is open to the 
public at all times (except during  executive sessions), and information is provided to enable 
the public to participate in such  meetings  by telephone or other electronic means to the 
extent that such members of the  public would otherwise be allowed to participate in such 
a meeting in person. In the event  of an emergency appropriately declared in writing 
beforehand by the Mayor (or appropriate  official acting in the Mayor’s absence), any such 
meeting may be conducted by telephone  or other electronic means.  
(Ord. No. 23-03, § 1; Ord. No. 26-02)  
 
Section 2: The officials of the Town of Silver Cliff are authorized to take any and all  
actions to effectuate the enactment of these changes to the Municipal Code, including publishing  
in the official copies of the Municipal Code kept at Town Hall and published electronically online.

RESOLUTION 05-2026 
 
A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE ACCEPTANCE OF GRANT FUNDING 
FROM THE BLACK HILLS CORPORATION FOUNDATION 
 
 WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff is in need of a making renovations to the 
existing baseball field and other facilities at the Town Park; and 
 
 WHEREAS, Town staff have made application for a grant from the Black Hills 
Corporation Foundation for assistance with such project; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Town has been informed that it has been graciously awarded a 
grant of $25,0.00 from the Black Hills Corporation Foundation; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees wishes to accept such grant funds under the 
terms and conditions outlined by the Black Hills Corporation Foundation  for receipt of 
such grant. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Town Board of Trustees  of the 
Town of Silver Cliff, Colorado that: 
 
 SECTION 1:  The Board of Trustees hereby authorizes the acceptance of grant 
funding from the Black Hills Corporation Foundation for renovations at the Town Park. 
 
 SECTION 2:  The Board further authorizes entry into terms and conditions of said 
grant that may be attached to receipt of such funding, so long as such terms and conditions 
are determined to be standard and normal for such funding by the Town Attorney and

RESOLUTION 06-2026 
 
A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING EXPEDITED REVIEW POLICIES FOR 
AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROJECTS 
 
 WHEREAS, the voters of Colorado approved Proposition 123 in 2022 creating the State 
Affordable Housing Fund to make certain funds available to local governments as defined by 
C.R.S. 29-32-104; and  
 WHEREAS, Town of Silver Cliff is a Colorado statutory town duly and regularly 
organized and validly existing as a body corporate and politic under and by virtue of the 
Constitution and laws of the State of Colorado and is eligible for Proposition 123 funding and 
programing; and  
 WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff recognizes the importance of allowing affordable 
and attainable housing to be developed for its workforce and its residents; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff has set a baseline and commitment to increase 
affordable housing as defined in C.R.S. 29-32-105; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff’s current process for reviewing proposed housing 
development projects already meets the requirement for a 90-calendar day “fast-track approval 
process;” and   
 WHEREAS, the Town of Silver Cliff also creates this policy for expedited review to ensure 
that affordable housing development projects are reviewed and a decision rendered within 90-
calendar days of a complete application in alignment with the requirements of C.R.S. 29-32-105 (2) 
et seq., as determined by the Building and Zoning Official, Planning Commission, and the Board of 
Trustees; and   
 WHEREAS, The Town of Silver Cliff recognizes that an affordable housing 
development project, including mixed-use projects, which have at least 50% of the residential 
units as affordable, as defined in statute (C.R.S. 29-32-101 and C.R.S. 29-32-105 (2)), would be 
eligible for expedited review; and  
 WHEREAS, The Town of Silver Cliff recognizes the following application types which 
require expedited review: Site Plan, Development Plan, Conditional Use Permit, Building 
Permit, Special Use Permit, Variance or Waiver, and Administrative Modifications; and  
 WHEREAS, The Town of Silver Cliff’s expedited review process for affordable housing 
projects does not apply if an applicant chooses to opt-out of the process; and  
 WHEREAS, The Town of Silver Cliff recognizes the allowable extensions in C.R.S. 29-
32-105 (2) (b) and (c), both for applicants and for the Town, and recognizes the circumstances 
under which the expedited review timeline may be extended.  
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Town Trustees of Silver Cliff, Colorado, 
that: 
1. The policy herein is created to implement a system to expedite the development review 
 
 
process for affordable housing.  
2. Any complete application received by the Town for an affordable housing development will 
be placed on the next available agenda of either the Planning Commission or the Board of 
Trustees, as applicable, once proper public notice has been posted, and a decision is intended 
to be rendered on the application within 90-calendar days, to ensure an expedited and timely 
review of the affordable housing project, with the intent to comply with the requirements in 
C.R.S. 29-32-105 (2) et seq.  
3. The review period established herein may be extended for an additional ninety days under the 
following circumstances: (a) at the request of a developer; (b) for compliance with state law 
or court order; or (c) for a review period required by another local government, tribal 
government, or agency, within the Town of Silver Cliff or outside, for any component of the 
application requiring that government’s or agency’s approval. 
4. The review period established herein may be extended to allow for the submission of 
additional information or revisions to an application in response to requests from the Town of 
Silver Cliff. Such extension shall not exceed the amount of time from the request to the 
submission of the applicant’s response plus thirty days. Applicants shall provide such 
information or responses promptly and shall, whenever practicable, provide a response 
within five business days. 
5. Nothing in this policy shall require a developer of affordable housing to utilize this fast-track 
approval process. 
6. Staff of the Town of Silver Cliff are instructed to comply with this policy and to advise 
developers of affordable housing of the existence of this policy.
