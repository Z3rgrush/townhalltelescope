RE: Amendment to SCIG-PL-206 Huerfano County IHOP Strategy Adoption 
 
Dear Ms. Loranger, 
 
Huerfano County requests an amendment to the scope of work for our Strong Communities Grant, 
SCIG-PL-206 for Strategy Adoption for our IHOP program to add design of a greywater reuse 
program to the grant.  Specifically, we request the language in bold below, or language of a similar 
effect approved by your team, be added section 2.2 of the grant agreement: 
 
Huerfano County (Grantee) will hire a qualified consultant to update the land use code, create 
programs for land banking and an affordable housing fund, update the Gardner Public 
Improvement District regulations for water conservation, develop guidelines for a greywater 
reuse program, and assess, refine, and adopt qualifying policy and regulatory strategies for 
affordable housing (as defined locally or aligned with the definition of affordable housing in 
C.R.S. 24-32-133(1)(a)). Grantee will own all resulting documents.  
 
At a time when our region, and the State as a whole, is struggling with severe drought conditions this 
program reflects the water conservation best practice included in the Strong Communities strategy 
list. 
 
If you have any questions please contact Carl Young, Huerfano County Administrator, at 
administrator@huerfano.us. Thank you for considering this request. 
 
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
Karl S. Sporleder 
Chairman, Board of County Commissioners

RE: Extension Request for EIAF 9874 Huerfano County Law Enforcement Center Improvements 
 
Dear Ms. White-Tanabe, 
 
Huerfano County requests a one-year expiration date extension to September 30, 2027 for our Law 
Enforcement Center Improvements Project, EIAF 9874. 
 
This project has been delayed as the County worked to secure a lease purchase agreement to assist 
with project funding.  We expect to begin construction in mid-August and hope to complete work in 
early 2027.  The additional time will help ensure that all construction and any closeout activities are 
concluded within the extension. 
 
Please let me know if you have any questions. 
 
 
Sincerely,
