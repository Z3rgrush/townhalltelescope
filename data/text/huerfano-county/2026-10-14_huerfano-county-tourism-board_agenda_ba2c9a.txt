Sponsorship Program Guidelines 
2025-2026 
Program Purpose & Goals 
The Huerfano County Tourism Board (HCTB) is dedicated to growing travel and tourism and 
strengthening Spanish Peaks Country’s position as a premier destination in Southern 
Colorado. Recognizing events as a powerful tool to achieve these goals, the Sponsorship 
Program provides funding for events that attract overnight visitors, foster industry 
collaboration, and engage the community. 
The program supports tourism-focused experiences that elevate the Spanish Peaks 
Country brand to reach key travel segments. 
 
Funding Overview & Opportunities 
This program is funded by the HCTB through a 2% Lodging assessment generated from 
overnight stays at hotels, motels, RV resorts, and vacation rentals in Huerfano County, and 
reinvested into tourism promotion, marketing. Program awards are structured with 75% of 
funding available prior to the event and the remaining 25% released following the event 
upon completion of required post-event reporting.  
Event Sponsorships ranging from $100 to $1,000 per event are available for events that 
align with HCTB’s tourism goals. 
 
Program Eligibility 
Who Can Apply: Non-profit organizations, businesses, and independent event producers 
are encouraged to apply. Residency in Huerfano County is not required. 
Event Location – All events must take place within Huerfano County 
Target Audience: Priority will be given to events that focus on attracting visitors from more 
than 50 miles away to increase tourism and overnight visitation in Huerfano County. 
Event Timing: While events can be scheduled at any time, there is a particular interest in 
Page 24 of 31
 
 
those planned for the non-peak months of October thru May. This approach supports our 
goal of fostering year-round tourism and aligns with the HCTB strategy to increase 
occupancy in hotels and vacation rentals during these typically quieter months. 
 
Application Dates 
One option is to have one or two application periods for the year 
Another option is to accept applications throughout the year but require a specified lead 
time. 
 
Sponsorship Application Process: 
Applicants seeking event sponsorship support are required to submit their applications 
online/email . Applications must include: 
• Event overview and alignment with HCTB goals. 
• Promotional Strategy – brief description of the target audience and outline of 
planned marketing or promotional efforts. 
• Budget – total event budget, amount requested from HCTB, and 
breakdown of how HCTB funds will be used (e.g., marketing, equipment, permits, 
venue fees), plus any confirmed or anticipated funding sources such as other 
sponsorships, grants, or in-kind support. 
 
Sponsorship Review Process 
Sponsorship applications are reviewed by the HCTB for approval. 
Reviews consider tourism impact, alignment with HCTB goals, and 
use of funds to enhance the event.

Below is a full list of upcoming program opportunities offered by the CTO Destination Development team. 
Colorado Tourism Climate Resilience 
The CTO launched the second year of the Colorado Tourism Climate Resilience Program to help tourism industry
partners address climate-related challenges and strengthen the long-term resilience of Colorado’s tourism economy. This
self-paced program offers three online learning courses, peer networking opportunities, and up to 20 hours of technical
assistance. Designed for partners at any stage of resilience planning, the program supports participants in building
foundational knowledge and advancing priority projects that strengthen local tourism economies. Technical assistance
hours can be used for projects such as zero waste event plans, crisis communications, low-impact travel strategies,
Colorado Green Business Network regional program development, and more.
Register for an informational webinar on Nov. 4 at 12 p.m. to learn more about the program.
Destination Development Mentor Program Applications Close Soon
Applications for the Destination Development Mentor Program close next Thursday, Oct. 15 at 4 p.m. The Destination 
Development Mentor Program provides DMOs and Colorado industry associations 100 hours of technical assistance to 
implement priority tourism projects. Interested partners are encouraged to log in or sign up on CTO Learning Labs to 
review the informational webinar recording, then submit a Letter of Interest (LOI) prior to submitting an application.  
Dark Sky Certification Mentor Program 
The CTO will begin accepting participants for the Dark Sky Certification Mentor Program on Tuesday, Oct. 27. The 
program provides Colorado destinations and state parks pursuing International Dark Sky Place (IDSP) recognition up to 
15 hours of on-demand technical and a series of free webinars on CTO Learning Labs. By helping more Colorado 
communities and parks achieve IDSP certification, the program also expands astrotourism opportunities, attracts visitors, 
and supports local economic development.  
On demand technical assistance will be available on a first come, first serve basis from October 2026 - June 2027. Visit 
the Dark Sky Certification Mentor Program webpage to learn more. 
Support to Champion the Value of Tourism
The Colorado Tourism Office has a tourism subject matter expert that is available to schedule one-hour consultations with 
Colorado DMO partners that are interested in understanding how to leverage research to champion the value of tourism in 
their communities. Up to 7 additional hours of consulting are available if a specific action item or research question 
emerges from the consultation. Submit this form to request a free hour-long consultation. For questions, please reach out 
to Dave Santucci dave.santucci@colorado.com
