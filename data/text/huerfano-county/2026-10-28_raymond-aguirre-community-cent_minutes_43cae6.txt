 a. April 22, 2026 
4. County Report 
 a. Building Upgrades - Sound Dampening 
Lester Berry gives an update on the sound panels. Public works installed a few sound panels. He is 
waiting for screws to install the rest. He would like to order new ceiling fans. He also let the board 
know his crew replaced the plug to concessions and the bus outside.  
 b. Phase 3 - Sports Courts: Sand Volleyball, Bocce Ball, Horseshoe Pits, Multi-sport 
Court 
Lester Berry says he has bought corn hole boards and will start on concrete mold to install in the 
back. he is hoping to have more time this fall to complete most of the sport court. 
 c. Rodeo Grounds 
Lester let the board know the Black Widow bought for Gardner Fairgrounds was taken and used for 
the county fair in La Veta. He said it will be returned this week and will continue to be housed at 
Gardner road and bridge. 
5. Maintenance Review 
6. Old Business 
Mitchell Wardell explains that the state of the trees does not look great. He would like to put in a 
water system before replanting. Mickey asks if the county has thought about hiring a part time 
employee to keep up with watering and cleaning. Lester explains that he has and would like to try to 
budget for that potion in the future but for now he and Celena have a good system in place.   
 
Mitchell also let the board know that there will be a bus barn coming to Walsenburg soon. That 
would help with not having to schedule with Trinidad, offering a smoother system in the future. He 
did let the board know that he does not have an exact timeline for it but it is coming.  
7. New Business 
• Carl Young asks the board how they would like to move forward with renting the community 
center. Different options were presented. Mickey said she would like to have people sign 
contracts that would state they are welcome to have their classes held, as long as they keep 
true to the contract. She also stated she would like to have back-up options for classes in 
case they need to be moved, whether that is a different date or a different location. All 
agreed that we will have classes sign an updated rental agreement and contract. 
 
• Carl Young informs the board of the new ballot measure coming up in November. Mickey 
asks how this would benefit Gardner and explains her worries when it comes to voters on a 
fixed income. Carl ensures Mickey that if this passes, it would help fund the rodeo grounds 
and give the children in the county more options. Myra states that SCCY would have 
benefited from funding from this and emphases that if it passes, the county needs to help 
fund programs similar to help the community.
