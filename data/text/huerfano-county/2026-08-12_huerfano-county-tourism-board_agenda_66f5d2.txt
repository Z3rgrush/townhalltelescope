Colorado's Destination Stewardship
Efforts Earn Global Recognition
The Colorado Tourism Office continues to receive international recognition for its leadership in
destination stewardship. The World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC) recently highlighted Colorado in
its latest destination stewardship report, recognizing the state's long-term, collaborative approach to
tourism management. Colorado's Destination Stewardship Strategic Plan is helping position the state as
a model for balancing a thriving visitor economy with the needs of residents while protecting the places
that make Colorado unique.
This recognition places Colorado alongside leading destinations from around the world and reinforces
the innovative work being done to balance tourism growth with community well-being, environmental
stewardship, and long-term sustainability. Read more in the Travelers Today article highlighting the
WTTC report.

service project. The project will be hosted in partnership with the Friends of Dillon Ranger District and

Leadville Strong: A Story of Community and Resilience

Colorado Passport Program
Welcomes Two New Experiences
The Colorado Tourism Office has launched two new additions to the Colorado Passport Program: the
Scenic Rides Passport and Arts & Culture Passport.
These free mobile passport experiences encourage residents and visitors to explore Colorado through
iconic railroads, scenic byways, gondolas and tramways, as well as museums, galleries, theaters, public
art, cultural attractions and all 37 Colorado Creative Districts.
Participants can sign up for free, check in at participating locations and automatically earn entries for
sweepstakes prizes, including $500 United Airlines travel credits. Every check-in also counts as an entry
for the grand prize of 150,000 United MileagePlus® miles.
Thank you to the destinations, attractions, cultural organizations and industry partners who helped bring
these new experiences to life.
Learn more and sign up at colorado.com/passport.

Colorado Tourism Board Elects New Leadership
The Colorado Tourism Office extends its sincere appreciation to outgoing board chair, Ken Murphy, for
his two years of dedicated service, leadership and unwavering commitment to Colorado's tourism
industry. Throughout his tenure, Murphy brought a valuable outdoor recreation perspective to board
discussions and helped guide initiatives that support destinations and communities across Colorado.
The Colorado Tourism Board also welcomed its new leadership, electing Jennifer Shea as board chair
and Bruce Dalton as vice chair. Shea previously served as vice chair and chair of the International
Promotion Committee, where she helped strengthen Colorado's international tourism initiatives.
Together, Shea and Dalton bring extensive industry experience and a shared commitment to supporting
Colorado's tourism economy. We look forward to their leadership in the year ahead.
Colorado Tourism Leadership Journey Explores
Collaboration in Glenwood Springs
The Colorado Tourism Leadership Journey (CTLJ) Class of 2026 gathered in Glenwood Springs July
22–24 for its fourth leadership experience, Collaboration & Partnerships. Participants explored
strategies for building strong partnerships through local speakers, discussions of “The Culture Code” by
Daniel Coyle, Myers- Briggs collaboration workshops, and Action Learning Project presentations.
The experience concluded with participants reflecting on key leadership lessons and developing
personal leadership action plans as they prepare for their final experience and graduation at the 2026
Colorado Governor's Tourism Conference in September.
