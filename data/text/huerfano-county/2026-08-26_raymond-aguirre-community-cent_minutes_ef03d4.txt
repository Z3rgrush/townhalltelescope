5. County Report 
 
a. GIPD Rules and Regulations 
Carl Young let the bard know that the GIPD rules and regulations have been 
updated. This includes fees to connect to water. Bulk water has also been rewritten. 
He went over resident vs non resident and the price variance. Carl mentions that 
you can find the updates on the county website. Danielle asked if it was possible to 
put out something physical for residents to see. Mickey also requested the updated 
rules.  
 
b. Building Upgrades & Sound Dampening  
Lester Berry said the sound dampening panels will be arriving early June and will be 
delivered to Road & Bridge. Lester will get his team to scrape, remove, and apply 
new panels. Danielle Sewell asked Carl Young if we got the discount for the panels. 
Carl said yes. Lester mentioned that the stove was inspected and the problem was 
found. They discovered that the thermostat was stuck on high. Thermostats, 
gaskets, and new stove door have been ordered and will be replaced. Danielle asked 
if the stove will be fixed by the next meeting. Lester replied it depends on when parts 
arrive but he’s hopeful.  
Commissioner Wardell said he will look at the state of trees after the meeting. 
Lester said he will order a weed screen. He also asked if the board prefers sand, 
rock or decorative rock. Wardell suggests bigger rock. Mickey D’ Ambrosia asked 
what the timeline would be. Lester responded that the weed screen needed to be 
ordered and put in before we have a timeframe.  
 
c. Phase 3 – Sports Courts: Sand Volleyball, Bocce Ball, Horseshoe pits, multi-
Sport Court 
 
Lester Berry asked the board if they would like to replace bocce ball for cornhole. 
Danielle Sewell asked if the bocce ball can double as cornhole. Lester said he 
would look into it.  Mickey D’Ambrosia asked what the price would be. Lester would 
do some research. Danielle asked if it was possible to do all 3. Horseshoe, cornhole, 
and bocce ball. The board agrees. 
 
d. Rodeo Grounds 
 
Lester Berry said the black widow needed for arena would cost $8-9,000. Myra
