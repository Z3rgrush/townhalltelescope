RE: Letter of Support for Blackhawk Ranch Forest Restoration & Wildfire Risk Mitigation Grant Application 
 
Dear Blackhawk Ranch, 
 
 Huerfano County strongly supports Blackhawk Ranch’s application for the 2026 Forest Restoration & 
Wildfire Risk Mitigation (FRWRM) capacity-building grant. This funding is essential to expand their proven, 
community-driven fuel reduction efforts. 
 
Blackhawk Ranch has a demonstrated track record of effective wildfire mitigation. Their established 
chipper program has been instrumental in removing fuels from Home Ignition Zones and improving 
emergency egress and response access. As a long-standing Firewise Community, they consistently execute 
work aligned with their Community Wildfire Protection Plan (CWPP). 
 
Furthermore, Blackhawk Ranch’s collaboration on the forthcoming county-wide CWPP has provided 
invaluable insights into successful community-based risk reduction. We fully endorse this project, as it aligns 
perfectly with the goals of the FRWRM grant and significantly strengthens wildfire resilience within our 
county. 
 
 
Sincerely, 
 
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF HUERFANO COUNTY, COLORADO
