ITEM NAME: Lifting Stage One Fire Restrictions 
SUBMITTED BY: Robert Gilbert, Emergency Manager 
SUMMARY: Following decisions by Pueblo & Custer Counties and the USFS-Pike-
San Isabel National Forest to lift Stage One Fire Restrictions due to 
seasonal weather and increased moisture, the chiefs of Upper Huerfano 
Fire Protection District, Huerfano County Fire Protection District, and La 
Veta Fire Protection District requested that the BOCC also lift Stage One 
Fire Restrictions. The chiefs cited recent precipitation, weather shifts, 
and the opportunity for residents to complete critical fire mitigation 
projects before winter as justification. Huerfano County Emergency 
Management supports this request, encourages responsible fire use, and 
reminds the public to follow the County’s Burn Permit process. 
RECOMMENDATION: Recommend the BOCC move to approve lifting Stage 1 Fire Restrictions. 
BACKGROUND: On March 31st, 2026, by order of the BOCC, Huerfano County moved 
into Stage 1 Fire Restrictions. On June 30, 2026, by order of the BOCC, 
Huerfano County moved into Stage 2 Fire Restrictions. On August 25, 
2026, the Stage 2 Fire Restrictions were lowered back to Stage One. This 
would remove the County from being in a fire-restrictive status.
