4. Old Business 
 a. Public Hearing LU-26-000122 School Creek Gravel Pit CUP 
The Public Hearing opened at 1:34 pm. Carl Young was appointed Hearings Officer.  Carl Young 
started the presentation and then had Contract Planner Sky Tallman go through the Staff Report. 
 There weren't any comments from the Planning Commission for Sky when he finished his staff 
report. The applicants, Tyler Duzenack, Blake Warren and Laurie Erwin were then called to answer 
questions from the Planning Commission. The public was then asked to make comment, to come to 
the podium, speak into the microphone, state their name, and they have three minutes each to 
speak.  After several citizens commented, the applicants were given a chance to respond to those 
comments.  The Public Hearing closed at 3:53pm.
