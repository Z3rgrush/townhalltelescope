HCTB 
Sponsorship Program Guidelines 
2025-2026 
Program Purpose & Goals 
The Huerfano County Tourism Board (HCTB) is dedicated to growing travel and tourism and 
strengthening Spanish Peaks Country’s position as a premier destination in Southern 
Colorado. Recognizing events as a powerful tool to achieve these goals, the Sponsorship 
Program provides funding for events that attract overnight visitors, foster industry 
collaboration, and engage the community. 
The program supports tourism-focused experiences that elevate the Spanish Peaks 
Country brand to reach key travel segments. 
 
Funding Overview & Opportunities 
This program is funded by the HCTB through a 2% Lodging assessment generated from 
overnight stays at hotels, motels, RV resorts, and vacation rentals in Huerfano County, and 
reinvested into tourism promotion, marketing. Program awards are structured with 75% of 
funding available prior to the event and the remaining 25% released following the event 
upon completion of required post-event reporting.  
Event Sponsorships ranging from $100 to $1,000 per event are available for events that 
align with HCTB’s tourism goals. 
 
Program Eligibility 
Who Can Apply: Non-profit organizations, businesses, and independent event producers 
are encouraged to apply. Residency in Huerfano County is not required. 
Event Location – All events must take place within Huerfano County 
Target Audience: Priority will be given to events that focus on attracting visitors from more 
than 50 miles away to increase tourism and overnight visitation in Huerfano County. 
Event Timing: While events can be scheduled at any time, there is a particular interest in 
Page 29 of 32
 
 
those planned for the non-peak months of October thru May. This approach supports our 
goal of fostering year-round tourism and aligns with the HCTB strategy to increase 
occupancy in hotels and vacation rentals during these typically quieter months. 
 
Application Dates 
One option is to have one or two application periods for the year 
Another option is to accept applications throughout the year but require a specified lead 
time. 
 
Sponsorship Application Process: 
Applicants seeking event sponsorship support are required to submit their applications 
online/email . Applications must include: 
• Event overview and alignment with HCTB goals. 
• Promotional Strategy – brief description of the target audience and outline of 
planned marketing or promotional efforts. 
• Budget – total event budget, amount requested from HCTB, and 
breakdown of how HCTB funds will be used (e.g., marketing, equipment, permits, 
venue fees), plus any confirmed or anticipated funding sources such as other 
sponsorships, grants, or in-kind support. 
 
Sponsorship Review Process 
Sponsorship applications are reviewed by the HCTB for approval. 
Reviews consider tourism impact, alignment with HCTB goals, and 
use of funds to enhance the event.

A score of 20 points or higher is recommended for funding consideration. 
 
Marketing & Promotion Requirements:  
All event sponsorship recipients must comply with the HCTB marketing requirements, as 
outlined in the agreement issued upon approval. Requirements include featuring the 
Spanish Peaks Country logo in event marketing materials, linking to the Spanish Peaks 
Country lodging page, tagging Spanish Peaks Country on social media, and fulfilling any 
additional promotional commitments specified in the agreement. Banner?? 
Funding Release Schedule: Once approved, event sponsorship funds are distributed in two 
phases: 
• 75% of the awarded event sponsorship is released prior to the event.

• The remaining 25% is released after the event, following submission of required post 
event reporting. 
 
Reporting & Accountability:  
Sponsorship recipients are required to submit a post-event report within four weeks of the 
event. The report must include: 
• A summary of the event,  estimated total attendance,  and out of town attendance. 
• Event photos and/or videos (high-quality images encouraged for marketing use). 
• A description of how TBID funds were used. 
• Financial documentation itemizing all sponsorship-funded expenditures. 
• A copy of the actual event budget and expenditures.
