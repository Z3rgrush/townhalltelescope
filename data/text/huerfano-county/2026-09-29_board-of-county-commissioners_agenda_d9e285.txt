Each person will have up to three minutes to present public comment. Please address the 
Board as a whole through the Chair. This is an opportunity for people to express their 
views and the Board generally does not engage in back-and-forth discussion or respond to 
questions. When addressing the Board, please state your name for the record prior to 
providing your comments.
