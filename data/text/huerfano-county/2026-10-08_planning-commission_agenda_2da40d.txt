Continuation 10/08/2026 
 
 
 
Planning Commission Options 
The Planning Commission held a public hearing for Application  on September 10,2026. After the 
public hearing was closed, the Planning Commission voted to continue discussion on the application to 
the October 8 meeting.  
  
At this continuation, the Planning Commission has the following options for how to proceed: 
 
1.  Reopen public hearing. The Planning Commission may decide to reopen the public hearing, 
which would mean that new testimony could be received by anyone in attendance. 
 
2. Proceed to a vote without admitting new material received since the Sept. 10 public hearing. 
This means that the recommendation will be made based on information contained in the 
previous packet and received at the public hearing. 
 
3.  Admit new material received since Sept. 10 public hearing. This includes communications 
between the Applicant and the County as well as letters from agencies. Admitting new material 
means that it can be discussed and used in the recommendation.  
 
The decision to admit new material can be made independently of whether or not to reopen the 
public hearing. 
 
The recommendation to the BOCC should be made based on the criteria set forth in LUR Section 11-
18, The recommendation may include any conditions the Commission deems necessary to mitigate 
potential impacts of the proposed project. 
 
The Commission may also consider a recommendation that the BOCC to hold a separate public hearing 
when reviewing the application. 
 
The Commission may take any of the following actions on each of the conditional uses proposed in the 
application. Each conditional use may be approved, conditionally approved, or denied separately and 
independendently of each other:  
1. Approval of any conditional use without any special conditions. 
2. Conditional Approval with a description of the special conditions for each conditional use. 
3. Denial, of specific uses, indicating for the record the reason(s) for such action. 
4. Continuation until a future date to gather more information or obtain clarification or for any other
