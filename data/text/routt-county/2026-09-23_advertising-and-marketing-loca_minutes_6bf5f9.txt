ADVERTISING AND MARKETING LOCAL TOURISM PANEL MEETING 
September 23, 2026
Jennifer Parent called the first meeting of the Advertising and Marketing Local Tourism 
Panel to order. A quorum was established with Panel members Ben Martin, Nancy 
Mucklow, Holly Olson, representing Yampa, and Nicole Seltzer, representing Oak 
Creek, present. Matthew Fredrickson, Assistant County Attorney, and Shawn Sonnkalb, 
Accounting Director, were also present. Jennifer Parent, Staff Assistant, prepared the 
minutes.
EN RE: PANEL OVERVIEW, RESPONSIBILITIES & GOVERNANCE 
Ms. Parent provided a brief overview of the purpose of the Advertising and Marketing 
Local Tourism Panel and her role as staff support to the Panel. She explained that she 
will coordinate meetings, serve as the primary point of contact for Panel members, and 
prepare meeting minutes. 
Mr. Fredrickson reviewed the Panel's responsibilities under the establishing resolution 
and explained that the Panel serves in an advisory capacity to the Board of County 
Commissioners. The Panel is responsible for developing recommendations regarding 
the use of the 10 percent portion of lodging tax revenue designated for advertising and 
marketing local tourism. Final funding decisions are made by the Board of County 
Commissioners. 
Mr. Fredrickson reviewed requirements applicable to the Panel as a local public body, 
including Colorado open meetings and public records requirements and conflict of 
interest considerations. Panel members were advised that discussions involving Panel 
business must take place during properly noticed public meetings and that records and 
communications concerning Panel business may be subject to public disclosure. 
Members were also advised to direct questions through Ms. Parent for coordination with 
County staff and the County Attorney's Office. 
The Panel discussed establishing bylaws or other basic governance procedures to 
provide consistency for current and future members. Members expressed support for 
developing straightforward bylaws addressing officers, meeting procedures, and other 
basic organizational matters. Staff will prepare examples for future Panel consideration. 
The Panel also requested a conflict-of-interest disclosure form and additional guidance 
regarding potential conflicts associated with future funding applications.
The Panel considered the annual Sunshine Resolution establishing the location for 
posting public meeting notices.

MOTION
Ms. Seltzer moved to adopt the Sunshine Resolution.
Ms. Mucklow seconded; the motion carried unanimously, 4-0.
Because the Panel has not yet selected a Chair, members agreed to postpone officer 
selection until bylaws and officer responsibilities have been developed.
EN RE: ADVERTISING AND MARKETING LOCAL TOURISM FUNDING 
FRAMEWORK
Mr. Sonnkalb presented an overview of lodging tax collections and the funding available 
to the Panel. He explained that 10 percent of qualifying lodging tax revenue is allocated 
for advertising and marketing local tourism, with the remaining 90 percent available to 
the Board of County Commissioners for allowable public safety and infrastructure 
purposes.
Mr. Sonnkalb reported that approximately $141,000 in qualifying lodging tax revenue 
had been collected through the first two quarters, making approximately $14,000 
available to the Panel at that time. Based on early collections, annual revenue was 
estimated at approximately $240,000 to $260,000, which could provide approximately 
$24,000 to $26,000 for advertising and marketing purposes. He cautioned that the 
lodging tax is new and collections may fluctuate as additional data becomes available.
The Panel discussed whether funds must be spent during the year in which they are 
collected. Staff explained that available funds may carry forward, allowing the Panel 
flexibility to accumulate funding for larger projects rather than necessarily awarding all 
available funds annually. The Panel was also advised not to commit or earmark future 
lodging tax revenue that has not yet been received.
Discussion then focused on the structure of a future funding program, including whether 
to conduct one or multiple application cycles each year. Members discussed the 
relatively limited amount of funding currently available, the timing of tourism marketing 
activities, and the potential benefit of concentrating available funding rather than 
dividing it among multiple small awards. No determination was made regarding the 
number or timing of annual application cycles.
The Panel discussed applicant eligibility and the types of projects that could be 
considered. Questions included whether funding should be available to individual 
businesses, nonprofit organizations, municipalities, collaborative groups, County 
programs, or projects benefiting broader geographic areas. Members expressed interest 
in establishing guidelines that provide equitable access to funding while emphasizing 
projects that provide broader tourism benefits to unincorporated Routt County and 
municipalities subject to the lodging tax.

Members also discussed whether matching funds should be required, whether funding 
limits should be established, and what information applicants should be required to 
provide. The Panel agreed additional research and discussion are needed before 
establishing eligibility requirements or application parameters.
Staff will gather application materials, guidelines, and examples from other Colorado 
counties and local grant programs that may assist the Panel in developing its funding 
framework. Panel members were encouraged to provide examples from other grant 
programs for inclusion in the resource materials.
EN RE: EVALUATION PROCESS DISCUSSION
The Panel discussed the need to establish clear evaluation criteria and a consistent 
process for reviewing future funding requests. Members expressed interest in 
developing criteria that consider the overall public benefit of a project, its relationship to 
tourism advertising and marketing, geographic benefit, other available funding sources, 
and the extent to which a project supports the goals established by the lodging tax 
resolution.
Members discussed the potential use of a scoring rubric to provide consistency and 
objectivity when comparing applications. The Panel agreed that applicant eligibility and 
funding guidelines should be developed before a scoring methodology is finalized.
The Panel also discussed seeking input from the Board of County Commissioners as 
the framework is developed. Ms. Parent will request Commissioner feedback regarding 
the types of applicants and projects the Commissioners would be interested in 
considering. The Panel expressed interest in ultimately presenting its bylaws, 
application materials, and evaluation criteria to the Commissioners for review and 
feedback before beginning the first funding cycle.
EN RE: NEXT STEPS
The Panel agreed to meet more frequently during the initial organizational period with 
the goal of developing the funding program and associated documents over the coming 
months. Staff and the County Attorney's Office will begin preparing basic bylaws, conflict 
of interest materials, sample applications, grant guidelines, and evaluation resources for 
Panel review.
Ms. Parent will establish a shared location where Panel members can review resource 
materials and examples collected from other organizations. Members were reminded 
that materials may be shared for individual review, but substantive discussion among 
Panel members must occur during a properly noticed public meeting.
