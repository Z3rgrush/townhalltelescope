Subject: 2027 Community Partner and Service Agreements 
 
BACKGROUND INFORMATION: 
The County uses two types of agreements to outline expectations and restrictions for distributing 
County funding to community partners. The County uses community partner agreements when 
funding is under $80,000, and service agreements when funding is $80,000 or more. Staff 
recommend moving the threshold to $100,000 to align with the County Administrator’s purchasing 
threshold and prevent unnecessary administrative burden on smaller partners such as the 
Conservation District and Lecompton Historical Society. 
  
Service agreements differ from community partner agreements in several key ways. Partners 
receiving funding through a community partner agreement receive county funds in two disbursements 
with no reporting requirements, while partners receiving funding through a service agreement must 
submit invoices monthly or quarterly to be reimbursed for services provided. Each invoice must 
include a data report for the corresponding period and be approved by the staff liaison before Finance 
staff processes the payment. In addition, these partners collaborate with their staff liaison to draft 
several exhibits for the agreement, which include: 
 Exhibit A: Scope of Services 
 Exhibit B: Personnel Schedule (if County funds are used to support staff) 
 Exhibit C: Outcomes Report 
 Exhibit D: Additional Cooperation and Reporting Clauses (for behavioral health partners) 
 Exhibit E: Federal Funds Certification Form 
County Administration staff and the County Counselor reviewed the boilerplate community partner 
and service agreements in preparation for drafting 2027 agreements with community partners. This 
review clarified existing practices, enhanced accountability, and aligned agreement language with 
current procedures.  
  
As a result of this review, the following updates were made to the community partner agreement: 
 Clarifications in Sections 1-8, 9, and 10 
 New guidelines in Sections 11-23 
As a result of this review, the following updates were made to the service agreement: 
 Clarifications throughout the agreement, including Exhibit D 
 New guidelines in Sections 12, 14, 15, 17, 19, 24, and 26 
 Exhibit E was added to ensure compliance for federal funding

Subject: 2027 Community Partner and Service Agreements
BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
The County uses two types of agreements to outline expectations and restrictions for distributing 
County funding to community partners. The County uses community partner agreements when 
funding is under $80,000, and service agreements when funding is $80,000 or more. Staff 
recommend moving the threshold to $100,000 to align with the County Administrator’s purchasing 
threshold and prevent unnecessary administrative burden on smaller partners such as the 
Conservation District and Lecompton Historical Society.
 
Service agreements differ from community partner agreements in several key ways. Partners 
receiving funding through a community partner agreement receive county funds in two disbursements 
with no reporting requirements, while partners receiving funding through a service agreement must 
submit invoices monthly or quarterly to be reimbursed for services provided. Each invoice must 
include a data report for the corresponding period and be approved by the staff liaison before Finance 
staff processes the payment. In addition, these partners collaborate with their staff liaison to draft 
several exhibits for the agreement, which include:
Exhibit A: Scope of Services
Exhibit B: Personnel Schedule (if County funds are used to support staff)
Exhibit C: Outcomes Report
Exhibit D: Additional Cooperation and Reporting Clauses (for behavioral health partners)
Exhibit E: Federal Funds Certification Form
County Administration staff and the County Counselor reviewed the boilerplate community partner 
and service agreements in preparation for drafting 2027 agreements with community partners. This 
review clarified existing practices, enhanced accountability, and aligned agreement language with 
current procedures. 
 
As a result of this review, the following updates were made to the community partner agreement:
Clarifications in Sections 1-8, 9, and 10
New guidelines in Sections 11-23
As a result of this review, the following updates were made to the service agreement:
Clarifications throughout the agreement, including Exhibit D
New guidelines in Sections 12, 14, 15, 17, 19, 24, and 26
Exhibit E was added to ensure compliance for federal funding
