WORK SESSION 09-16-26
The Board received an update on the Zoning and Land Use Regulations Diagnostic Review and the implementation of next steps from Tonya Voigt, Director of Zoning and Codes and support staff.
Clarion Associates, a land use planning firm, was selected to provide a zoning diagnostic review to evaluate how effectively the Douglas County land use regulations are functioning.The diagnostic identifies deficiencies and inconsistencies but does not amend the zoning code.The County is entering the implementation phase.
Voigt and her staff outlined a proposed two
‑
phase approach. Phase 1 would involve staff-led technical and administrative corrections to the Zoning Code, such as aligning access management standards with Public Works, clarifying road dedication and construction requirements, establishing clearer processes for private-to-public road conversions, standardizing traffic and stormwater study triggers, improving coordination with rural water, fire districts, and townships, and resolving inconsistencies regarding application timelines, unlisted uses, and application review criteria. These changes would still move through the Planning Commission and County Commission for public input.
Phase 2 would address broader policy issues with the help of consultant
Clarion Associates, using remaining project funds to define a scope of work that includes potential zoning district restructuring, adequate public facilities criteria,
the possible development of a countywide future land use map, and a robust public engagement plan.
Commissioners supported proceeding with Phase 1 while Clarion develops a Phase 2 framework, noting the need for staff capacity, accessible public involvement, and coordination with municipalities. Staff will return with an initiated text amendment for Phase 1 and a proposed scope for Phase 2 within approximately 60 days.
