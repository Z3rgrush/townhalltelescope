GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT
General Public Comment will be received after the Consent Agenda and before Regular Agenda items. To help facilitate an orderly and efficient meeting, Public Comment will be subject to the following rules:
(1) Each speaker will be limited to a total of three minutes of speaking time; however, we will accept public comment of any length in writing; (2) Individual speakers may not donate unused time to other speakers; (3) All comments must address topics within the jurisdiction of the County Commission; and (4) We reserve the right to mute and remove any speaker whose comments are threatening, aggressively hostile, or belligerent.
As a general practice, the Commission will not discuss or make decisions on items presented during General Public Comment. At the discretion of the Commission, items may be referred to staff for follow up. All comments should be directed to the County Commission.
