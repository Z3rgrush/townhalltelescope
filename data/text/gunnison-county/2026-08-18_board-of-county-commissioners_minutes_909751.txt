1. A Resolution Approving LUC -25-00004, A Land Use Change Permit Application for Sketch Plan 
Review of a Major Impact Project for Lower Verzuh Ranch Subdivision 
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels thanked everyone for accommodating an additional w eek for research. She 
relayed that a moratorium was mentioned numerous times from the community. It was determined that a 
moratorium would not be feasible and not meet the community goals from a legal or political standpoint  
due to only being able to last legally for six months and the corridor plan would take longer than six months. 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels emphasized wanting to perform the Land Use Resolution process 
thoughtfully and respectfully. In her opinion, if approving this project, the Board needs to be sure that 
there is no adverse impact of future development in the development area. Commissioner Puckett Daniels 
highlighted LUR Standards Section 1, the groundwork of the LUR, to add to the discussion. 
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels noted Sections 7-102, 10-103,11-106, 13-102B, and 13-105 were ones she 
would like to highlight and discuss before toggling it back to the Resolution in front of them. Robust 
conversation ensued regarding the absence of the Town of Crested Butte’s three-mile plan, sewer, lot size 
and density, and connection to public services. Commissioner Houck noted the applicant’s willingness to 
explore the opportunities that are above and beyond what is needed for a sketch plan. He emphasized that 
even with potential passing to preliminary, nothing will be built, but significant resources will be used to 
proceed further and there will be additional moments for public input.  
 
In regard to lot size and density, Commissioner Smith noted that the numerical number does not deter her 
from believing they are substantially different from existing parcels. Commissioner Houck referenced  the 
density of Buckhorn and believes it is compatible  and substantially similar. Commissioner Puckett Daniels 
exuberantly disagreed and stated she finds this to be substantially different. Commissioner Houck 
elaborated that if this application came back at preliminary and nothing changed, he would have a difficult 
time approving it at that time, but where it is currently at, at a sketch plan level, he believes that things 
can be modified to address concerns. Additional concerns such as sprawl, wildlife and agriculture production 
and the clustering of the residences were discussed.  
 
The Board went through the draft resolution and discussed any conditions they would like to add before 
potentially approving the resolution. Moved by Commissioner Smith, seconded by Commissioner Houck to 
approve with conditions, LUC-25-00004, Major Impact Sketch Plan for Lower Verzuh Ranch and direct staff 
to prepare a resolution reflecting such. Commissioner Houck and Commissioner Smith voted in favor, and 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels voted in opposition.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE REFERRAL OF A BALLOT ISSUE FOR THE NOVEMBER 3, 
2026 GENERAL ELECTION AUTHORIZING THE IMPOSITION OF AN ADDITIONAL 0.50% 
COUNTYWIDE SALES TAX FOR THE PURPOSE OF SUPPORTING ROADS, BRIDGES, AND 
RELATED TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE; PRESCRIBING THE FORM OF THE BALLOT 
ISSUE; AND CERTIFYING THE BALLOT ISSUE TO THE COUNTY CLERK AND RECORDER  
 
WHEREAS, the County of Gunnison, Colorado (the “County”), is duly organized and existing under the 
Constitution (the “Constitution”) and the laws of the State of Colorado; and 
 
WHEREAS, the members of the Board of County Commissioners (the "Board") have been duly elected, 
chosen and qualified; and 
 
WHEREAS, the County currently imposes a voter -approved sales tax adopted in 1978, the proceeds of 
which are shared with municipalities within the County pursuant to the terms approved by the electors;  
 
WHEREAS, the Board finds that additional funding for county roads, bridges, and related transportation 
infrastructure is necessary to address current and future transportation needs, improve public safety, and 
benefit the residents, businesses, and taxpayers of the County; and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Sections 29 -2-103 and 29- 2-104, C.R.S., the Board is authorized to refer to the 
registered electors of the County a ballot issue proposing the imposition of a countywide sales tax; and  
 
WHEREAS, on January 3, 1978, the Board adopted Resolution 1978-1, referring to the voters a countywide 
one percent sales tax and providing for its collection and distribution; 
 
WHEREAS, the voters of Gunnison County approved the sales tax referred by Resolution 1978 -1; 
 
WHEREAS, the Board has determined that the County should refer to its registered electors a ballot issue 
seeking voter approval of a revenue change under Article X, Section 20 of the Colorado Constitution 
(“TABOR”), which would authorize an increase of the County sales tax of one half of one percent (0.50%); 
and 
 
WHEREAS, revenues derived from such additional sales tax shall be accounted for separately from revenues 
generated by the 1978 sales tax and shall be used solely for the road, bridge, and transportation purposes 
authorized by the ballot question; and 
 
WHEREAS, TABOR requires voter approval for, among other things, any new tax or tax rate increase; and  
 
WHEREAS, it is the intent of the Board that the proposed sales tax increase shall comply and be interpreted 
to comply with the requirements of Article X, Section 20 of the Constitution of the State of Colorado, and 
C.R.S. Title 29, Article 2, and all other applicable provisions of the law; and 
 
WHEREAS, TABOR requires elections on new taxes or increased taxes to be held on specified days and, 
the State general election is one of those days; and 
 
WHEREAS, the County Clerk is conducting a coordinated election on November 3, 2026 in accordance with 
the Colorado Election Code, C.R.S. Title 1, Articles 1 to 13, and other applicable laws and rules; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 29-2-202(1)(a), the Colorado Department of 
Revenue (“DOR”) administers the collection and administration of county sales taxes; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 29 -2-205 (1)(b), a county seeking to change its existing sales tax must 
provide a copy of a resolution approving ballot referral of such sales tax change to the DOR 45 days prior 
to the effective date of such change; 
 
WHEREAS, on July 21, 2026, the Board adopted Resolution 2026-27, approving the referral of a ballot issue 
for the November 3, 2026 election of an additional County sales tax for the purpose of supporting road, 
bridges, and related infrastructure; 
 
WHEREAS, the foregoing referral contained a provision that the additional sales tax would not apply to 
certain sales, such as food, medication, and residential utilities; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to the foregoing laws, County staff transmitted Resolution 2026-27 to the DOR; 
 
WHEREAS, the DOR informed County staff that it lacked the ability to collect, enforce or administer the 
exemptions to the additional tax proposed by Resolution 2026-27; 
 
WHEREAS, DOR did not inform the County of this issue until after passage of the foregoing Resolution;

WHEREAS, for the reasons stated in that Resolution and this one, the need remains to obtain additional 
funding for county roads, bridges, and related transportation infrastructure, notwithstanding the Board’s 
desire to exempt certain sales from the additional tax necessary for such funds; 
  
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF GUNNISON 
COUNTY, IN THE STATE OF COLORADO: 
 
Section 1. Ratification. All action heretofore taken (not inconsistent with the provisions of this 
Resolution) by the County, directed towards the election and the objects and purposes herein stated is, 
ratified, approved and confirmed. 
 
Section 2. Election. Pursuant to the applicable laws of the State of Colorado, at the election to be 
held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the ballot issue in substantially the form hereinafter set forth shall be 
submitted to the registered electors of the County: 
 
SHALL GUNNISON COUNTY TAXES BE INCREASED BY $3,811,363 ANNUALLY (ESTIMATED FIRST 
FISCAL YEAR DOLLAR INCREASE STARTING IN 2027), AND BY ANY ADDITIONAL AMOUNT AS 
MAY BE RAISED EACH YEAR THEREAFTER, THROUGH AN ADDITIONAL 0.50% (50 CENTS ON 100 
DOLLARS) COUNTYWIDE SALES TAX TO COMMENCE ON JANUARY 1, 2027, FOR THE PURPOSES 
OF: 
 
• IMPROVING SAFETY ON COUNTY ROADS AND BRIDGES; 
 
• REPAIRING, MAINTAINING, AND IMPROVING COUNTY ROADS, BRIDGES, AND RELATED 
TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE TO ADDRESS CURRENT AND FUTURE COUNTY 
TRANSPORTATION NEEDS; AND 
 
• PLANNING, DESIGNING, CONSTRUCTING, RECONSTRUCTING, AND IMPROVING 
TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE; 
 
SUCH SALES TAX TO BE IN ADDITION TO ALL OTHER TAXES LEVIED BY THE COUNTY, SHALL 
NOT BE ALLOCATED IN WHOLE OR IN PART TO MUNICIPALITIES, AND THE PROCEEDS OF SUCH 
SALES TAX AND INVESTMENT INCOME THEREON, REGARDLESS OF AMOUNT, TO BE COLLECTED 
AND SPENT BY THE  COUNTY AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, 
SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION? 
 
YES/FOR        NO/AGAINST 
 
Section 3. Certification of Ballot Issue to Clerk and Recorder for Election. The ballot issue set forth 
in Section 2 of this Resolution is hereby certified to the Gunnison County Clerk and Recorder and shall be 
presented at the election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Notices required by Article X, Section 
20 of the Color ado Constitution, the Colorado Election Code, C.R.S. Title 1, Articles 1 to 13; and other 
applicable law or rule shall be provided by the Clerk and Recorder. 
  
Section 4. Delivery to Colorado Department of Revenue. If approved by the registered electors and 
to the extent required by law, the County Clerk shall provide a copy of this Resolution and notice of its 
adoption and approval to the executive director of the Colorado Department of Revenue at least 45 days 
prior to the effective date of the sales tax increase created herein. 
 
Section 5. Enforcement and Interpretation. The collection, administration, and enforcement of this 
sales tax increase shall be performed by the executive director of the Colorado Department of Revenue in 
the same manner as the collection, administration, and enforcement of the Colorado state sales tax and 
the current County sales tax, as supplemented by C.R.S. Title 29 Article 2, and shall be interpreted the 
same as the current County sales tax, except where any language of this Resolution conflicts with the 
language of any Resolution adopting the  current County sales tax, the language of this Resolution shall 
control. Further, the Board may, by separate resolution or otherwise, interpret and clarify the terms and 
processes for collection, distribution, use and distribution of this sales tax increa se, so long as such 
resolution does not directly conflict with existing law or the express language of this Resolution. All revenues 
derived from the additional sales tax shall be accounted for separately from revenues generated by the 
1978 sales tax. 
 
Section 6. Direction and Authorization; Ratification. The officers and employees of the County are 
hereby authorized and directed to take all action necessary or appropriate to effectuate the provisions of 
this Resolution. Any action taken prior to this Resolution by any officer or employee of the County consistent 
with the terms of this Resolution is, to the fullest extent permitted by law, ratified by this Board as if fully 
adopted by this Resolution. 
 
Section 7. Repealer. All ordinances, regulations, policies and resolutions, or parts thereof, in conflict 
with this Resolution, including but not limited to Resolution 2026-27, are hereby repealed.
