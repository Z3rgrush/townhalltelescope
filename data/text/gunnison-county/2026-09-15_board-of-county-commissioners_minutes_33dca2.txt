GUNNISON COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH REGULAR MEETING: 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Commissioner Puckett Daniels called the meeting to order at 9:53 am. 
 
HEALTH COALITION UPDATE : Community Health Manager Margaret Wacker  and Health Coalition 
Coordinator Katherine Melland were present for discussion. 
 
CHM Wacker introduced HCC Melland to the Board. HCC Melland began by introducing the mission 
statement of the Health Coalition, the leadership team organizations, and its responsibilities which included 
enhancing collaboration, be ing proactive, and to educate, advocate, monitor progress, and conduct 
stakeholder initiatives. Currently the leadership team is working on website development, local community 
health data, and a strategic plan, as well as supporting subgroups for workforce support, suicide prevention 
and a sense of belonging which also have their own projects.  
 
WEST CENTRAL PUBLIC HEALTH PARTNERSHIP (WCPHP) UPDATE : Community Health Manager 
Margaret Wacker and Regional Health Services Supervisor Erika Stoerkel were present for discussion. 
1. Proclamation; 2026 Suicide Prevention Awareness Month 
 
RHSS Stoerkel updated the Board on the current timeline through the winter of 2027  which included the 
Western Slope Wellness CredibleMind program being scheduled to  launch in the spring of 2026. CHM 
Wacker discussed the status, challenges, and opportunities for Whole Health Navigation  and Stigma 
Reduction while RHSS Stoerkel discussed the status, challenges and opportunities for the Regional Suicide 
Review Board.  
 
CHM Wacker expressed gratitude to the Board for their support and willingness to make the proclamation.  
Moved by Commissioner Houck, seconded by Commissioner Smith to adopt, sign, and promote the  
Proclamation for Suicide Prevention Awareness Month in the County of Gunnison, Colorado in September 
2026 and encourage folks within the community to recognize the importance of the issue within our 
community and the work being done to support those who s truggle and those who are left behind in the 
constant challenge of suicide, and authorize the Chair’s and both Commissioners’ signatures on the 
Proclamation. Motion carried unanimously.  
 
ADJOURN:  Commissioner Puckett Daniels adjourned the meeting of the Gunnison County Board of Health 
at 10:36 am.

GUNNISON COUNTY HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-005 
 
A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE GUNNISON COUNTY HOUSING AUTHORITY BUDGET FOR 
FISCAL YEAR 2026 AND AMENDING THE APPROPRIATION RESOLUTION.  
 
WHEREAS, at the time of the adoption of the budget for Gunnison County Housing Authority for fiscal year 
2026 certain revenues were unassured and certain expenditures were not anticipated; and  
 
WHEREAS, those revenues and expenditures can now be identified;  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of the Gunnison County Housing Authority, that a 
supplemental budget and appropriation resolution be adopted in the following respects:  
1. Gunnison County Housing Fund. The revenues are decreased in the amount of $58,048,800.15 as 
detailed by account number on Appendix A attached. The expenditures are decreased in the 
amount of $57,625,300.15 as detailed by account number on Appendix A attached.  
2. Whetstone Housing Fund. The revenues are increased in the amount of $58,048,800.15 as detailed 
by account number on Appendix A attached. The expenditures are increased in the amount of 
$57,625,300.15 as detailed by account number on Appendix A attached.  
 
The above sums of money, or as much thereof as may be authorized by law and as may be deemed 
necessary to defray the expenses and liabilities of the Gunnison County Housing Authority, are hereby 
appropriated. It is the intent of the Board to make the neces sary amendments and supplements to the 
budget adoption and appropriation resolutions - Resolution Nos. 2025 -004 - for the Gunnison County 
Housing Authority for the fiscal year beginning January 1, 2026 and ending December 31, 2026; but except 
as specifically provided for herein, to make no further changes in the budget adoption or appropriation 
resolutions adopted with respect to said fiscal year. 
 
INTRODUCED by Commissioner Houck , seconded by Commissioner Smith, and adopted this 15 th day of 
September 2026.
