1. Early Childhood Services and Universal Pre -K (UPK) – ECCC Jaegar explained the previous and 
current universal preschool enrollment . The statewide average for 4-year-olds enrolled in UPK in 
the 2025-2026 school year is 59% whereas Gunnison County is at 74%. The Summer Programing 
Scholarships allowed 94 families to be served through awards. ECCC Jaegar gave updates regarding 
the Family, Friend, Neighbor Caregiver Support , CPR Certification Training, Nurturing the Young 
Child Conference on April 25th, a Community Play Date on August 19th, Sick Leave Program, Early 
Childhood Educator EC Credential Stipend Program, and other ECE data  including new programs 
or changes within Early Childhood including new family childcare supports at housing developments 
which includes Whetstone. CM Birnie elaborated that three units are allocated for a large family 
home center as well as one unit in Sawtooth.    
2. Colorado Child Care Assistance Program (CCCAP)  – CHM Wacker gave updates regarding 
enrollment freezes, eligibility requirements, financial struggles, and expanding eligibility to people 
in a substance use disorder program. She relayed that the number of children serv ed in both 
counties and the historic numbers, children that are on the CCCAP waitlist for childcare, barriers to 
care, and their initial fiscal year 2027 budget and its reduction to meet f ederal guidelines.
