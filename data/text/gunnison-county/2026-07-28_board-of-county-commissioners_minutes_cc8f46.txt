CALL TO ORDER:  Commissioner Puckett Daniels called the meeting to order at 8:32 am. 
 
MAJOR IMPACT SKETCH PLAN; LUC -25-00004; LOWER VERZUH RANCH DELIBERATION : 
Assistant County Manager for Community and Economic Development April Kroner, Planning Director Hillary 
Seminick, and applicants Marcus Lock, Bill Lacy, and Dan Dow were present for discussion. 
1. A Resolution Approving LUC -25-00004, A Land Use Change Permit Application for Sketch Plan 
Review of a Major Impact Project for Lower Verzuh Ranch Subdivision  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels explained the process and thanked the public for their attendance and their 
due diligence in looking at the proposal. The Board did note they have received correspondence after the 
Public Hearing and asked for direction from CA Hoyt regarding it.  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels then relayed the criteria for opting for an additional Public Hearing . 
Commissioner Houck stated that he does not think an additional Public Hearing would provide any additional 
information. However, he would like to consider remanding the issue back to the Planning Commission for 
them to dig a bit deeper into the application information and the Land Use Resolution ’s (LUR) standards 
and doing so would also preserve the Board the option to have an additional Public Hearing. 
 
Commissioner Smith agreed with Commissioner Houck’s recommendation. She commented she felt the 
Planning Commission not giving a recommendation to them does not give the Board adequate information 
and would like a more refined understanding of their perspective. Commissioner Houck noted they did not 
toggle back to the LUR standards and would like to have a recommendation based on t heir decision in 
relation to the LUR.  
 
CA Hoyt informed the Board that, according to the LUR, they would need to make a decision within 60 days 
of the Planning Commission recommendation and that timeframe would need to include an additional Public 
Hearing if the Board goes that direction . Commissioner Smith asked if the missing Planning Commission 
member would be able to participate  in the vote after not being present for the Public Hearing. CA Hoyt 
noted that it is up to the member because everything is on the record . She can decide if she has enough 
information. Planning Director Hillary Seminick also noted the Planning Commission bylaws do not state 
anything regarding that issue and there will be a full recording which she can listen to.  
 
Commissioner Houck wants to have the Planning Commission state their reasoning for their view and toggle 
it to the LUR standards that their decision is based on. Commissioner Smith agreed. Commissioner Puckett 
Daniels then read out the LUR standards that state the criteria for remanding the issue back to the Planning 
Commission.  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels expressed hesitation regarding dragging this  application out further. She 
asked the Board how  the Planning Commission recommendation plays in th eir decision. Commissioner 
Smith noted she wanted to understand, in analytical detail, how they reached their decision. Commissioner 
Houck agreed with Commissioner Smith and further explained he wanted a more specific opinion of the 
Planning Commission. Furthermore, he relayed the Planning Commission will meet on August 6th, and The 
BOCC can meet on this later in August with a clearer understanding. Commissioner Houck emphasized he 
would not have wanted to take this path if the opportunity for an additional Public Hearing was not there.  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels explained that with an additional Public Hearing, she is not necessarily 
looking for more information but  rather giving more people of the public opportunity to participate. 
Commissioner Houck also noted that due to the fifth Planning Commission member having a family 
emergency ten minutes prior to the hearing, he feels if the full Planning Commission participated, there 
would be a recommendation either way. Moved by Commissioner Houck, seconded by Commissioner Smith 
to, at this moment,  choose not to have an additional Public Hearing and move to the discussion of what 
this Board would like to do with the application. Motion carried with Commissioner Houck and Commissioner 
Smith voting in favor, and Commissioner Puckett Daniels voting in opposition.
