A RESOLUTION APPROVING LUC-25-00004, A LAND USE CHANGE PERMIT APPLICATION

SUBDIVISION 
 
WHEREAS, Lacy and Dow, LLC, represented by Law of the Rockies, Design Workshop, and High Mountain 
Concepts (the “Applicant”), submitted an application for a Land Use Change Permit, designated LUC -25-
00004 (the “Application”), to Gunnison County, Colorado (the “County”), proposing a Major Impact Sketch 
Plan review of the Lower Verzuh Ranch Subdivision on Parcel No. 3257 -000-00-141, a 450- acre parcel 
located south of the Highway 135 and Buckley Drive intersection, Gunnison County, Colorado, legally 
described as 450.32 acres in Sections 7, 8, 17 & 18, Township 14 South, Range 85 West, 6th p.m. (the 
“Property”); and 
 
WHEREAS, the Applicant proposes to subdivide the Property into 338 residential lots allowing a total of 433 
residential units comprised of 254 single-family free market units and 179 deed-restricted Essential Housing 
units to be physically constructed by t he Applicant, with approximately 60% of the Property to remain in 
open space, including 45% undeveloped open space, 5.7 miles of internal trails, up to 10 acres of parks, 
10 acres of ponds, and a 50-acre hay meadow preserved in agricultural production (the “Project”); and 
 
WHEREAS, the Project is classified as a Major Impact pursuant to Land Use Resolution (LUR) Section 7 -
101, A, More Than Four Units; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Applicant has requested incentives pursuant to LUR Section 9 -604: Incentives to Provide 
Essential Housing, as 41% of the residences in the Project are Essential Housing units and because of the 
deed restriction will remain Essential Housing, w hich meets the minimum 40% threshold required for 
incentives; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Application was referred for comment to the Gunnison County Public Works Department, 
Colorado Parks and Wildlife (“CPW”), the Crested Butte Fire Protection District (“CBFPD”), the Colorado 
State Forest Service, the Colorado Department of Trans portation (“CDOT”), the Gunnison Valley Regional 
Transportation Authority (“GVRTA”), the Colorado Division of Water Resources, the East River Regional 
Sanitation District (“ERRSD”), Gunnison County Housing Authority (GCHA), GCEA, RE1 -J School District, 
MetRec and the Town of Crested Butte; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held meetings on September 4, December 4, and December 18, 2025, 
May 21, 2026, July 9, August 6, 2026 and conducted a site visit on October 2, 2025, to review and consider 
the Application; and 
 
WHEREAS, a public hearing, with notice as required by LUR Section 3-112, was held before a joint meeting 
of the Planning Commission and Board of County Commissioners (the “Board”) on July 9, 2026, at which 
time significant public testimony, staff reports, applicant testimony and all submitted documents were 
considered; and 
 
WHEREAS, The Planning Commission, at its meeting on August 6, 2026, did find the Lower Verzuh Ranch 
Subdivision Sketch Plan to broadly and conceptually comply with the Standards of Approval for Major 
Impact Projects as identified in LUR  Section 7 -102, sub ject to certain conditions of approval, and 
recommends the same to the Board; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board has considered the Application, the staff report, the applicant and public testimony, 
the Planning Commission recommendation, and the entire record in this matter; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board concurs with the Planning Commission findings as follows: 
 
1. Pursuant to LUR Section 7-102: Standards of Approval for Major Impact Projects, compliance with 
which shall be determined broadly and conceptually during Sketch Plan review , the Board 
concludes: 
 
a. A. Compliance with all Applicable Standards.  The Sketch Plan is generally consistent with 
the standards and requirements of the LUR.  The Application has addressed, and the Board 
has evaluated this submittal for, its integration of the standards of the LUR within its
