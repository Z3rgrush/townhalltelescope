COMMISSIONER ITEMS:   
 
Commissioner Smith: 
1. Early Childhood Education Providers – Commissioner Smith noted there is a survey out to get 
some substantive responses by the end of the month. She hopes to have further discussions 
regarding that data in the future.  
2. Cinthia Saenz – Commissioner Smith attended the presentation and highlighted her services to 
the community.  
3. Road and Bridge – Commissioner Smith has been continuing her public outreach. 
4. Veteran Affairs Town Hall  – Commissioner Smith attended the Town Hall and noted her 
gratitude for the work within the Veteran Community.  
5. Gunnison Regional Addiction and Substance Prevention (GRASP)  and Health C oalition Joint 
Meeting – Commissioner Smith noted this meeting had  conversations regarding feedback to 
data from a survey that puts mental and behavioral health in context with other peer mountain 
resort communities.  
6. Crystal River Coalition  – Commissioner Smith relayed there is an in-person meeting in 
November to follow up on the Wild & Scenic as well as peak and instream flows and it will be 
the first time they will all convene in over a year.  
7. Welcoming Week – Commissioner Smith attended a debrief regarding the Welcoming Week 
and relayed that they will be starting work on the strategic plan going forward to acknowledge 
year-round priorities.  
 
Commissioner Houck: 
1. Western Colorado University (WCU) President Baca  – Commissioner Houck was invited to a 
meeting with WCU President Baca, the City Mayor, himself and Senator Chris Kolker to discuss 
higher education funding in smaller communities.  
2. Region 10 – Commissioner Houck attended a robust meeting regarding a $50,000 grant for 
advanced industry assets. He relayed he will forward the report to the other Board members. 
3. Colorado Sun – Commissioner Houck noted that the Colorado Sun put out an article regarding 
methane emissions coming out of the North Fork which prompted him to reach out to reach 
out to Department of Natural Resources Director Dan Gib bs to determine responses  and if 
there is a role locally.  
4. Road and Bridge – Commissioner Houck continues public outreach. 
5. Public Lands – Commissioner Houck met with Senator Michael Bennet regarding the Gunnison 
Outdoor Resource Protection (GORP) Act. Colorado Sun reporter Jason Blevins also reported 
on Congressmen Hurd’s  position on the GORP Act.  After Commissioner Houck reached out 
directly to Congressmen Hurd, he confirmed his support and accepted his offer of public 
outreach regarding it.  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels: 
1. Council Meetings – Commissioner Puckett Daniels noted the robust conversations they’ve been 
able to have with partners.  
2. Road and Bridge – Commissioner Puckett Daniels continues her public outreach. 
3. Legislative Session  – Commissioner Puckett Daniels has been preparing for the upcoming 
legislative session. She noted interest in mobile park legislation including rent control and other 
items that have resurfaced that were previously vetoed.  
4. Colorado Communities for Climate Action (CC4CA) – Commissioner Puckett Daniels has 
participating in a climate resilience ad hoc group. She expressed interest in reducing 
greenhouse gas emissions and noted it being a systemic issue rather than a personal issue.  
5. Colorado Counties, Inc. Steering Committees (CCI) – Commissioner Puckett Daniels stated that 
Colorado Parks and Wildlife (CPW) is expediting a process to eliminate recreational take of  
prairie dogs on public lands. CCI has also created a small group regarding this concern. 
6. Wildlife Crossing Subcommittee – Commissioner Puckett Daniels noted the subcommittee has 
been meeting on a semi -regular basis and they now have a consultant helping to develop a 
community engagement plan.
