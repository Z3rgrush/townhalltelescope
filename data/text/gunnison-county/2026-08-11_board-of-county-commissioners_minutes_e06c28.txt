GUNNISON COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL MEETING: 
CALL TO ORDER:  Commissioner Puckett Daniels called the meeting to order at 11:05 am. 
 
MAJOR IMPACT SKETCH PLAN; LUC -25-00004; LOWER VERZUH RANCH DELIBERATION : 
Assistant County Manager for Community and Economic Development April Kroner and Planning Director 
Hillary Seminick were present for discussion.  
1. A Resolution Approving LUC -25-00004, A Land Use Change Permit Application for Sketch Plan 
Review of a Major Impact Project for Lower Verzuh Ranch Subdivision  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels explained that this was remanded to the Planning Commission and is now 
before the Board again, but they must first decide if another Public Hearing is desired.  
 
Commissioner Smith relayed she is not inclined to continue another Public Hearing due to not having new 
information that could come forward. She emphasized keeping project s efficient and she doesn’t believe 
the conversation would substantially change . Commissioner Puckett Daniels explained that due to the 
widespread internet outage, she was unable to access the Planning Commission meeting and would like to 
either have another Public Hearing or discuss this at a further meeting. Additionally , the level of public 
interest is very high and the highest she has seen in her time with the County. She emphasized stewarding 
a process where the public voices are heard fairly. Commissioner Houck stated that he does not believe 
there will be substantially new information, and while he agrees about being good stewards, he believes 
the draft recommendation was reflective of the information they desired by toggling back to the Land Use 
Resolution standards. He then emphasized that some questions were unable to be answered at the sketch 
plan level, and if the proposal does get to further  levels in the process , there will still be opportunity for 
the public process. Therefore, he does not believe an additional Public Hearing is necessary.  
 
Commissioner Puckett Daniels reiterated her desire to either have another Public Hearing or push the 
decision off until the next meeting to be good stewards  of the public process . Commissioner Houck 
disagreed with Commissioner Puckett Daniels on her comment regarding approval changing the north valley 
forever and explained that there are other phases within the process. Commissioner Smith stated what was 
brought to them today is what was expected from what was asked and noted it is also being a good steward 
to not extend the process. Commissioner Puckett Daniels further explained that while the recommendation 
was tethered to the standards, she is unsure of the reasoning for why  the Planning Commission vote 
changed from 2-2 to 5-0. She reiterated her previously stated desires. Commissioner Houck explained the 
process timelines and confirmed waiting until next meeting is within the timeline. Moved by Commissioner 
Houck, seconded by Commissioner Smith, that based on the discussion we had today, that the Board of 
County Commissioners  not hold an additional Public Hearing for the application LUC -25-00004. 
Commissioner Houck and Commissioner Smith voted in support, with Commissioner Puckett Daniels in 
opposition. It was decided to postpone the application decision until the next Board of County 
Commissioners Regular Meeting on August 18, 2026.  
 
ADJOURN: Commissioner Puckett Daniels adjourned the meeting at 11:49 am.
