*Public Comment Policy 
Thank you for attending tonight's meeting! If you are a member of the public and plan on speaking, please remember… 
 
 Only one speaker at the podium at a time.  
 When you come to the podium, state your name and address, and the organization you may represent. 
 You are generally limited to five minutes. 
 Your comments should relate to City business or to the agenda item of business. 
 Your comments should be directed to the Board as a whole and not to individual members or staff. 
 You may only speak one time per agenda item. 
 Once public comment on an item is closed, no further public comment will be permitted on that item. 
These policies are further described in the bylaws of the Historic Resources Board and help us run a fair, civil, and efficient 
meeting. Thank you!
