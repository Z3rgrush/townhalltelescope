E. Commissioner Comments 
 
Commissioner Von Lintel expressed appreciation to the Street Department for its work 
on Third Street. He noted that staff had been prepared in the event of flooding. 
Commissioner Fox, in response to Gary Olds’ comment, stated that his understanding was 
that the sales tax would operate on a pay -as-you-go basis. He explained that projects 
would be completed as funds were collected and would not be bonded or paid through 
debt payments. 
Commissioner McCulloh noted that there was a great deal occurring at the Flint Hills 
Discovery Center and the Sunset Zoo and encouraged residents to visit their websites to 
explore available opportunities. She also gave a shout-out to Dillons East, where she had 
cut the ribbon the previous week at the Mayor’s request. She noted that the store had 
completed significant renovations and thanked Dillons for being a strong City partner. 
City Commission Minutes October 6, 2026 
  
Mayor Adamchak acknowledged that she had attended a ribbon- cutting on October 6, 
2026, for the Manhattan Free Clinic, which had relocated to a newer and much larger 
space in Building A on the ground floor of the medical complex at the corner of College 
Avenue and Claflin Road. She noted that the clinic continued to provide the same services 
and recognized the many local volunteers and medical staff who contributed countless 
hours. She also noted that a physician traveled from Kansas City every two weeks to serve 
at the clinic and expressed appreciation for that service. 
Mayor Adamchak congratulated the Black Entrepreneurs of the Flint Hills on receiving a 
$100,000 Heritage Trust Fund grant through the Kansas Historical Society for preservation 
and rehabilitation work at the Yuma Street Cultural Center. She also recognized Parks and 
Recreation staff for attending the 2026 National Recreation and Park Association 
Conference in Philadelphia and hosting a panel discussion. She announced that WTC 
would celebrate completion of a grant tomorrow, Wednesday, October 7, 2026, totaling 
nearly $1 million to provide free Wi- Fi throughout the city. She acknowledged WTC as a 
supportive partner and expressed appreciation for its contributions to the community.
