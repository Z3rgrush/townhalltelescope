D. Commissioner Comments 
 
Commissioner Von Lintel expressed excitement for the start of K-State football. He 
also stated that he planned to attend the Yuma Cultural Festival on Sunday , 
September 6, 2026, from 2:00 to 8:00 p.m. 
 
Commissioner Fox mentioned that he had attended the plaque ceremony for Roger 
Reitz at the Train Depot. He encouraged residents to visit the plaque, noting that Dr. 
Reitz had done a great deal of work for the City and the facility and that the 
ceremony had been a nice tribute. He also congratulated Aaron Estabrook, the 
Manhattan Housing Authority, and its staff on their score from HUD.  
 
Commissioner McCulloh echoed what Commissioner Fox’s remarks. She announced 
that a discussion regarding outdoor pickleball courts in City Park would be held on 
Thursday, September 3, 2026, from 5:00 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. He also noted that the 
new CiCo park tennis courts had opened and looked fantastic.  
 
Mayor Adamchak recognized the successful opening of the CiCo Park tennis courts, 
noting that all eight cou rts were in use by Manhattan High tennis team members 
and community residents by the end of the opening. She commended Parks and 
Recreation for expanding morning hours at the Anthony Eisenhower Recreation 
Centers to accommodate students before school and provide access to recreation, 
walking tracks, activities, and quiet study areas. She also congra tulated Fleet 
Services Manager Jason Cook on his acceptance into the American Public Works 
Association Emerging Leaders Program and his Shop Manager certification, and 
recognized Fleet Service Technician II Jesse Wenham for earning his Emergency 
Vehicle Ce rtification. Mayor Adamchak welcomed Amy Lange, Chief Financial 
Officer, who joined City staff on August 31, 2026 , and stated that the City looked 
forward to her contributions in strengthening its financial systems.
