Hearing no comments, Mayor Adamchak closed the public comments. 
 
D. Commissioner Comments 
 
Commissioner Von Lintel asked the Mayor about board appointments and whether 
the City was receiving enough applications for the boards. He mentioned that he 
knew someone who was interested in serving.  
 
Mayor Adamchak  stated that the City had received several applications and 
acknowledged that she had been somewhat remiss during the previous month or 
so in reviewing them in a timely manner. She stated that she would be working on 
them. She encouraged the Commission to remind constituents that board 
participation was a valuable way for residents to become mor e engaged with City 
activities. She noted that some boards had qualifications, such as residency within 
city limits or a particular professional background, and that the City did not always 
have enough applicants who met those qualifications.  
 
Commissioner Fox  commented that it was a great time of year with students 
returning and the band being heard near campus. He stated that he was looking 
forward to football season.
