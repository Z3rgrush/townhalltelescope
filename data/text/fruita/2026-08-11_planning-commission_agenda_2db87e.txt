AGENDA TEXT:Planning Commission Water Supply and Strategic Growth Community Engagement Discussion




BACKGROUND
The State of Colorado has mandated that by December 31, 2026, municipalities with a Comprehensive Plan must adopt a Water Supply Element and Strategic Growth Element as part of that plan. The purpose of this cover sheet is to get feedback and discuss with the Planning Commission a community engagement strategy to inform the preparation of these elements. This strategy includes a draft survey planned to run through September, a map of potential flyer locations to advertise the project, and potential poster boards that the community can engage with at the Fall Fest.
