E-Bike/Motorcycle Use in Fruita: Ed Pacheco 
• Has been an officer for 7 years, has seen a significant increase in use of e-moto/bikes in the city.  
• Three Classifications for E-Bikes: All have pedals. 
o Class 1: Up to 20MPH, w/ motor less than 750 watt. Pedal assist only (required to pedal). No registration 
or license required. 
o Class 2:  Up to 20MPH, w/ motor at 750 watt. No pedal requirement, throttle will allow you to go w/o 
pedaling.  
o Class 3: Up to 28MPH, only pedal assist.  Not allowed on pedestrian paths/sidewalks.   
• Electric Motorcycle: 
o On Road: All required to have a license, registration, and are not allowed on pedestrian areas.  
o Off Road: Not allowed on roads or pedestrian areas. Designed to be off-road use only.  
• Increase of use in area in the last year or so.  
• The Police Department is trying to help with education to parents and children on classifications and appropriate 
use.   
• Use and regulations should be shared with the community.  Fruita PD is working with GJPD to align the 
communication and ensure a unified message is being shared with the community.   
• Fruita PD, GJPD and CSP are looking in to aligning an educational effort to the community prior to enacting a 
enforcement side of things.   
• E-Scooters: Little regulation on same.  Ideal to use the bike lane on the road rather than the sidewalk if they’re 
going fast however there is little regulation on same.   
• If unsafe behavior is being observed, a call to the non-emergency number is encouraged. If it is significantly 
dangerous, use 911 to call it in immediately so Fruita PD can take appropriate, timely action.
