II. REQUEST & SURROUNDING ZONING:  
The applicant has been advised to request a zoning map amendment (rezone) 
for the subject property to change the designation from the Agricultural 35-acres 
(A-35) zoning district to the Agricultural 5-acres (A-5) or the Agricultural 2.5-acres 
(A-2.5) zoning district. Rezoning the parcel to the A-5 zoning district would better 
meet the surrounding zoning districts, while the A-2.5 zoning district is closer to 
the parcel’s current acreage. The owner is intending on subdividing the parcel 
into two (2) lots.

HIGHWAY 92 ANALYSIS:   
When using aerial imagery, it appears that a good majority of the properties 
located along Highway 92      have smaller acreages and have been zoned in 
the A-5 zoning designation. Staff finds that rezoning the subject property 
would coordinate better with the surrounding properties.  
 
III. SUMMARY  
The subject property is currently zoned A-35.  The intent is to split the 2.03+ 
acres parcel into two (2) lots, creating a 0.79+/- acre lot and a 1.28+/- acre lot.  In 
order to accomplish this, the property would need to first be rezoned to A-5 or A-
2.5. The applicant will still need to apply for a Variance to lot sizes, as the 
proposed zoning would still not meet the zoning needed to subdivide.

Staff believes that A-5 zoning is a better fit for this parcel as most of the 
surrounding properties in the A-5 zone are similar in acreage. Staff does not 
support rezoning this parcel to the RES-1 zoning designation, as the parcel is not      
closely located to the Town of Hotchkiss’ infrastructure (e.g., public water, sewer, 
fire hydrants, etc.) to be in alignment with the purpose of RES-1 zoning as stated 
in Table 2.a of the Land Use Code.   
 
Rezoning the subject parcel to the A-2.5 zoning district would better the current 
property’s current acreage; however, the A-2.5 zoning could be considered spot 
zoning, as there are no A-2.5 in the vicinity of the subject property.  
 
Rezoning a parcel is subject to Public Hearings with the Planning Commission 
(PC) and Board of County Commissioners (BoCC). Chapter 8, Section 2.B. of the 
Land Use Code (LUC) establishes Rezoning Approval Standards. Staff finds that 
a rezone for this property would be consistent with these standards and the 
established use of the property. The PC recommendation on this rezoning will be 
presented to the BoCC at a noticed Public Hearing. Staff supports rezoning the 
2.03-acre parcel from A-35 to either the A-5 or the A-2.5 zoning district.

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission (PC) recommend that the 
Board of County Commissioners (BoCC): 
 
a. Find that amending the Land Use Code, rezoning the subject property 
from Agricultural “35” (A-35) to Agricultural “5” (A-5) or Agricultural 
“2.5” (A-2.5), meets the criteria of Chapter 8, Section 2.B of the Land 
Use Code; and 
b. Approve PLN26-051 rezoning 2.03-acres located north of Highway 92 
from A-20 to A-5 or A-2.5 zoning district. 
 
A draft BoCC resolution is attached to review findings and evidence regarding 
this application.

Rezoning Approval Standards 
The Land Use Code (LUC) establishes three (3) standards for rezoning: 
described as follows.   
 
1. Rezoning this property would not create or facilitate the creation of 
development rights or entitlements that would either: 
a) Reduce the level of protection for significant natural resources that 
exist on the subject property; or 
b) Expose additional people or personal property to unmitigated 
natural hazards that are present on the subject property (e.g., fire, 
flood, or geological hazards). 
This policy may be waived upon a finding by the Board of County 
Commissioners that:

2. The proposed zoning district, as applied to the subject property, is consistent
