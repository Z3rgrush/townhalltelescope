CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL 
The meeting was held in a hybrid format, with attendance in person at Avon Town Hall and virtually via 
Zoom. Mayor Tamra N. Underwood called the Regular Meeting of the Avon Town Council to order on 
October 27, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Present were Mayor Tamra N. Underwood; Mayor Pro Tern Richard Carroll; 
and Councilors Chico Thuon, Gary Brooks, Ruth Stanley, Kevin Hyatt, and Lindsay Hardy (who joined the 
meeting virtually at 5:07 p.m.). Also present were Town Manager Eric Heil, Deputy Town Manager Patty 
McKenny, Chief Administrative Officer Ineke de Jong, Town Clerk Miguel Jauregui Casanueva, Chief 
Financial Officer Paul Redmond, Financial Analyst Chase Simmons, Community Development Director 
Matt Pielsticker, Recreation Director Michael Labagh, Engineering Director Eva Wilson, Public Works 
Director Mike Jackson, IT Administrator Andrew Bare, and Police Chief Greg Daly. Town Attorney Nina P. 
Williams was absent. 
2. APPROVAL OF AGENDA 
Video Start Time: 00:00:50 
Mayor Underwood opened consideration of the Agenda. Councilor Stanley moved to approve the Agenda 
as presented. Councilor Hyatt seconded the motion, which passed unanimously by those present at that 
time, 6-0. 
3. DISCLOSURE OF ANY CONFLICTS OF INTEREST RELATED TO AGENDA ITEMS 
Video Start Time: 00:01:15 
Mayor Underwood asked whether any Council member had a conflict of interest related to any agenda 
item. No conflicts were disclosed. 
4. PUBLIC COMMENT 
Video Start Time: 00:01:25 
Mayor Underwood explained the public comment process, including the available methods for submitting 
comments and the time allotted to each speaker. Tim McMahon, an Avon resident, addressed Council in 
person and stated that members of the public had spoken against Flock cameras at six meetings, while no 
members of the public had appeared in person to speak in favor of them. He expressed concern that three 
Councilors nevertheless voted in favor of retaining the cameras and requested that Public Works remove 
the cameras if removal by Flock would be delayed, No other public comment was received, either in person 
or virtually. 
5. CONSENT AGENDA 
Video Start Time: 00:03:40 
Mayor Underwood introduced the Consent Agenda, which included: 
5.1. RESOLUTION 26.18: Adopting a Fast Track Priority Scheduling Policy for Community Housing 
Projects (Town Manager Eric Heil) 
Mayor Pro Tern Carroll moved to approve the Consent Agenda as presented. Councilor Brooks seconded 
the motion, which passed unanimously by those present at that time, 6-0.

6. BUSINESS ITEMS 
6.1. WORK SESSION: 2027 Budget Review (All Funds) (Chief Financial Officer Paul Redmond and 
Town Manager Eric Heil) 
Video Start Time: 00:04:15 
Town Manager Eric Heil and Chief Financial Officer Paul Redmond presented the proposed 2027 
budget, including the budget timeline, community and economic outlook, financial policies, revenues 
and expenditures, fund balances, reserves, debt obligations, capital improvement projects, staffing 
and personnel costs, and long-term financial projections. Staff presented a balanced General Fund 
budget and projected 2027 revenues of approximately $64.1 million across all funds, while 
discussing reserve levels and continued investment in housing, infrastructure, recreation, mobility, 
sustainability, and other community priorities. Council reviewed and provided direction regarding 
recreation cost recovery and fees, boat rental operations, grant funding, employee compensation 
and staffing, the Capital Improvement Program, and funding and policy considerations for Dancing 
in the Park, Lakeside Cinema, wildfire mitigation, commercial composting, energy and landscaping 
rebates, destination marketing, and the Comprehensive Plan. No public comment was received. 
Council provided direction to staff for preparation of the 2027 budget. 
7. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS & MEETING UPDATES 
Video Start Time: 02:35:30 
Councilor Thuon spoke of the delivery of his ballot which was misplaced, and then referred the public to get a 
replacement ballot if needed at the basement of the Eagle County Commons Building located at 410 Eagle 
Ranch Rd. 
Mayor Underwood discussed the challenges of developing public policy, noting that policy decisions are 
informed by multiple sources of information and Council deliberation rather than popularity. 
Councilor Stanley discussed her attendance at Mountain Towns 2030, which she described as informative 
and enlightening. She emphasized the importance of community resiliency and disaster recovery planning 
and noted efforts underway in Vail. She also discussed climate change and the, desire of younger generations 
to take action toward a better future. She noted that next year's Mountain Towns 2030 gathering will be held 
in Aspen. Mayor Underwood added that regional partners are increasingly focused on resiliency and 
emergency planning to prepare communities for significant potential disruptions.
