GUIDELINES FOR PUBLIC INPUT 
Public Input is a vital and important portion of every meeting and will be permitted 
throughout the meeting, but according to the following guidelines: 
a. Public input is allowed during the Agenda identified Public Input and Public
Hearing portion of the meeting.
i. If you would like to address the meeting during the appropriate times,
please raise your hand and when called upon you will be asked to come
to the podium.  Announce your name so that your statements can be
adequately captured in the meeting minutes.
ii. Please keep your comments to 3-5 minutes as others may want to
participate throughout the meeting and to insure that the subject does
not drift.
b. Throughout the meeting agenda calls for public input will be made, generally
pertaining to specific action items.  Please follow the same format as above.
c. At the conclusion of the meeting, if the meeting chair believes additional public
comment is necessary, the floor will be open.
We hope that this guideline will improve the effectiveness and order of the Town’s 
Public Meetings.  It is the intent of your publicly elected officials to stay open to your 
feelings on a variety of issues.

TOWN OF RANGELY, COLORADO 
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-08 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF RANGELY, 
COLORADO, ESTABLISHING INDEPENDENT HEARING OFFICERS TO HEAR 
AND DECIDE GRIEVANCES OF DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS AND TERMINATIONS 
IMPOSED BY THE TOWN MANAGER, APPOINTING THE HEARING OFFICERS, 
AND AMENDING THE TOWN OF RANGELY PERSONNEL RULES AND POLICIES 
ACCORDINGLY 
 
WHEREAS, the Town of Rangely (the "Town") is a statutory town organized and existing under 
the laws of the State of Colorado, and the legislative and corporate authority of the Town is vested 
in its board of trustees, known as the Town Council, pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-4-301; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Council is the legislative and policy -making body of the Town and 
establishes personnel policy through its adoption of the Town of Rangely Personnel Rules and 
Policies (the "Personnel Rules"), most recently revised January 1, 2011; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Manager is appointed by the Town Council, serves as the Chief Executive 
of the Town, appoints all other employees of the Town except as otherwise provided by state law, 
and is responsible for personnel administration in accordance with the policie s established by the 
Town Council; and 
WHEREAS, the Grievance Procedure of the Personnel Rules provides that a grievance proceeds 
from the employee's immediate supervisor to the Human Resource Director and then to the Town 
Manager, whose decision is final, and that a grievance of a dismissal or demotion  proceeds from 
the Human Resource Director to the Town Manager, whose decision is final; and 
WHEREAS, the Conduct and Discipline section of the Personnel Rules provides that, where a 
Department Head or employee reports to and is directly supervised by the Town Manager, the 
Town Manager acts as the Department Head for purposes of discipline; and 
WHEREAS, the Personnel Rules do not identify the authority that hears a grievance when the 
Town Manager is the official who imposed the disciplinary action or termination being grieved, 
with the result that the Town Manager would review his or her own decision; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that review of such actions by an impartial authority other 
than the official who imposed them furthers the Town's stated policy of striving for a prompt and 
equitable resolution of employee issues and concerns, and serves the intere sts of the Town, its 
employees, and the public; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Council further finds that, under the Town's form of government, the Town 
Council sets policy and oversees the Town Manager while the Town Manager administers 
personnel matters, and that the Town Council should not itself sit as the hearing body on individual

disciplinary actions, but should instead establish the procedure and appoint independent Hearing 
Officers to decide such grievances; and 
WHEREAS, all employment with the Town is at -will, and the Town Council intends that this 
Resolution and the procedure it establishes shall neither alter the at -will status of any employee 
nor create any contract of employment, property interest, or expectation of continued employment; 
and 
WHEREAS, the Personnel Rules provide that they may be amended or revised by action of the 
Town Council at its sole discretion. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN 
OF RANGELY, COLORADO, AS FOLLOWS: 
Section 1.  Establishment of Hearing Officers.  There is established the position of Hearing 
Officer. The Town shall at all times have not fewer than two nor more than three Hearing Officers, 
appointed by the Town Council, each of whom is individually available to fill the role as the need 
arises. The H earing Officers serve with equal authority and not as principal and alternate. One 
Hearing Officer, designated for the particular grievance as provided in Exhibit A, shall hear and 
decide each grievance of a disciplinary action or termination imposed by th e Town Manager, in 
the manner and subject to the limitations set forth in Exhibit A to this Resolution. Each Hearing 
Officer shall be a person who is not an elected official, employee, or attorney of the Town, and not 
an officer of the Town other than the Municipal Judge; who has no immediate or extended family 
relationship with the Town Manager; and who, by training or experience in law, public 
administration, human resources, or the resolution of employment disputes, is qualified to conduct 
an impartial administrative review. The Municipal Judge may be appointed Hearing Officer, and 
such appointment is separate from, and does not affect the term, compensation, or tenure of, the 
office of Municipal Judge. A Hearing Officer serves in that capacity at the ple asure of the Town 
Council and, except for a Municipal Judge so appointed, is an independent contractor and not an 
employee or officer of the Town.

EXHIBIT A 
Amendment to the Town of Rangely Personnel Rules and Policies 
The Grievance Procedure is amended by adding the following new section immediately after 
"Grievance of Dismissal or Demotion": 
 
GRIEVANCE OF DISCIPLINARY ACTION OR TERMINATION IMPOSED BY THE 
TOWN MANAGER 
 
A. Purpose and At-Will Status. This section establishes the authority that hears a grievance 
when the Town Manager, acting as Department Head or otherwise, is the official who 
imposed the disciplinary action being grieved. All employment with the Town is at -will. 
This section does not c reate a contract of employment, a property interest in continued 
employment, or any requirement that the Town establish cause for a disciplinary action, 
and it does not limit the right of the Town or any employee to end the employment 
relationship at any time, with or without cause and without advance notice. 
B. Actions Covered. This section applies to a suspension without pay, demotion, reduction 
in pay, or dismissal imposed by the Town Manager on any employee. It applies to an 
employee whose employment has been terminated by the action being grieved. It does not 
apply to: (1) an  oral or written reprimand, counseling, performance evaluation, or other 
action not listed above, which may be grieved to the Town Manager under the general 
Grievance Procedure; (2) a separation for lack of funds, curtailment of work, or reduction 
in force under the Miscellaneous section; (3) a resignation; (4) the employment of the Town 
Manager, which is governed by contract with the Town Council; or (5) an officer appointed 
by the Town Council under C.R.S. § 31-4-304, whose removal is governed by state law. 
C. Hearing Officers; Designation. The Town Council appoints by resolution not fewer than 
two nor more than three Hearing Officers, each individually available to serve as the need 
arises, who serve with equal authority. Each is an independent person who is not an elected 
official, employee, or attorney of the Town, and not an officer of the Town other than the 
Municipal Judge, appointed and compensated as the Town Council determines. A 
grievance is heard and decided by one Hearing Officer. Within two (2) working days after 
a grievance is fi led, the Mayor shall designate one of the appointed Hearing Officers to 
hear it, taking into account the availability of each Hearing Officer, the absence of any 
ground for disqualification, and the needs of the particular matter. The Town Manager shall 
have no role in the designation. The Town Clerk shall record the designation and the reason 
for it and give written notice of the designation to the employee, the Town Manager, and 
the designated Hearing Officer. If the designated Hearing Officer is unavailable or becomes 
disqualified, the Mayor shall designate another appointed Hearing Officer in the same 
manner. If no Hearing Officer is able to serve, the Town Council appoints a Hearing Officer 
for that grievance. The Town Council does not hear or decide grievances under this section 
Town of Rangely – Resolution No. 2026-08 – Page 6 
and does not review the Hearing Officer's decision. At the Hearing Officer's request, the 
Town shall provide independent legal counsel, other than the Town Attorney, to advise the 
Hearing Officer on procedure. The Town Attorney shall not advise the Hearing Officer and 
shall not act as advocate for the employee or the Town Manager in the review. 
D. Disqualification. The Hearing Officer shall not hear a grievance if he or she (1) is a 
member of the immediate or extended family of the employee or the Town Manager; (2) 
was personally involved in the events giving rise to the action; (3) has a pecuniary interest 
in the outcome; or (4) has any other relationship with the employee or the Town Manager 
that would cause a reasonable person to question his or her impartiality, including, where 
the Hearing Officer also serves as Municipal Judge, a pending or recent matter in the 
Municipal Court in which the employee was a party, witness, or prosecuting officer and 
which the Hearing Officer determines bears on his or her impartiality. The designated 
Hearing Officer shall disclose any such circumstance to the Town Clerk upon receiving the 
grievance. The employee or the Town Manager may object to the designated Hearing 
Officer on those grounds in writing to the Town Clerk within three (3) working days after 
notice of the designation; the Hearing Officer shall rule on the objection, and if the Hearing 
Officer steps aside the Mayor shall designate another appointed Hearing Officer. 
E. Filing. The employee shall file a written grievance with the Town Clerk within five (5) 
working days after the effective date of the action. The grievance shall identify the action 
grieved, the facts on which the grievance is based, the specific Town policy, rule,  
ordinance, or law alleged to have been violated or the prohibited basis alleged, and the 
remedy requested. The Town Clerk shall promptly transmit copies to the Mayor, the Town 
Manager, and the Town Attorney, and, upon designation, to the designated Hearin g 
Officer. A grievance not filed within this time limit is waived, and the action of the Town 
Manager is final. The time limit may be extended only by written agreement of the 
employee and the Hearing Officer. 
F. Scope of Review. Consistent with the at -will nature of Town employment, the review is 
limited to whether the action: (1) violated a specific provision of these Personnel Rules, a 
departmental rule or procedure approved by the Town Manager, a Town ordinance, or 
applicable state or federal law; or (2) was taken on the basis of race, color, national origin, 
gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, veteran or marital status, or in 
retaliation for a good -faith report or complaint of harassment, sexual harassment, o r 
violence, for the filing of a grievance, or for any other activity protected by these Personnel 
Rules or by law. The Hearing Officer does not decide whether the action was wise, whether 
he or she would have reached the same decision, or whether cause existed for the action. 
G. Administrative Review. Within fifteen (15) working days after the grievance is filed, the 
Hearing Officer shall set a date for an administrative review and shall give the employee 
and the Town Manager at least five (5) working days' written notice of the date, time, and 
Town of Rangely – Resolution No. 2026-08 – Page 7 
place. At the review the employee and the Town Manager may each present testimony, 
documents, and argument, and the employee may be accompanied by an attorney or 
another person of his or her choice at his or her own expense, with advance notice to the 
Town Clerk. The Town shall furnish legal counsel to represent the Town Manager at the 
review, at the Town's expense. The review is informal; the Colorado Rules of Evidence do 
not apply, and the Hearing Officer presides and may set reasonable time limits. The review 
may be conducted in person at Town Hall or another location in the Town desig nated by 
the Hearing Officer, or by videoconference using a platform arranged by the Town Clerk 
that allows all participants to see and hear one another and to present documents. The 
review shall be held by videoconference unless the employee or the Town Manager elects 
an in-person review by written notice to the Town Clerk within three (3) working days 
after notice of the review date, or the Hearing Officer determines that an in-person review 
is needed to fairly decide the grievance. The review is closed t o the public as a personnel 
matter unless the employee requests in writing that it be open. The review shall be recorded. 
The Town Clerk shall keep the grievance, the notice, the exhibits received, and the 
recording as the record of the review. 
H. Decision. Within ten (10) working days after the administrative review, the Hearing 
Officer shall issue a written decision stating findings of fact on the issues raised in the 
grievance and whether the grievance is sustained or denied. If the grievance is sustained in 
whole or in part, the Hearing Officer may rescind or modify the action and may award back 
pay and restoration of benefits for the period affected, as the Hearing Officer determines 
appropriate on the findings. The decision of the Hearing Officer is the final administrative 
action of the Town and is binding on the Town Manager. The Town Clerk shall deliver the 
decision to the employee and the Town Manager, place a copy in the employee's personnel 
file, and provide a copy to the Town Council for its information. 
I. Status Pending Review. The action of the Town Manager remains in effect while the 
grievance is pending. A dismissed employee is not an employee of the Town and does not 
receive pay or accrue benefits during the review unless and until the grievance is sustained 
and reinstatement is ordered. The Deferral of Subsequent Actions Affected by Grievance 
section applies. 
J. Failure to Act. If the Hearing Officer does not convene the administrative review or issue 
a decision within the times stated in this section, and the time has not been extended by 
written agreement of the employee and the Hearing Officer, either party may ask the Mayor 
to designate another appointed Hearing Officer, and the time limits begin again from the 
new designation. If no other Hearing Officer is available, the Town Council appoints a 
Hearing Officer for that grievance under its resolution. 
K. Confidentiality and Non -Retaliation. The grievance, the record, and the decision are 
confidential personnel records to the extent permitted by law. No employee shall be

subjected to retaliation for filing a grievance under this section or for participating in the 
review as a witness.

Conforming Amendments to the Town of Rangely Personnel Rules and Policies 
 
1.  Grievance Procedure, third step (Town Manager review). The sentence "The decision of 
the Town Manager will be final." is amended to read: 
"The decision of the Town Manager will be final, except that a grievance of a disciplinary 
action or termination imposed by the Town Manager shall be filed and decided by the 
Hearing Officer as provided in the section titled Grievance of Disciplinary Actio n or 
Termination Imposed by the Town Manager." 
2.  Grievance of Dismissal or Demotion. The first sentence is amended to read: 
"Actions involving dismissal or demotion imposed by a Department Head other than the 
Town Manager may be grieved beginning with the Human Resource Director; actions 
involving dismissal, demotion, suspension without pay, or reduction in pay imposed by the 
Town Manager are grieved to the Hearing Officer as provided in the section titled 
Grievance of Disciplinary Action or Termination Imposed by the Town Manager." 
3.  Conduct and Discipline, written confirmation of discipline. The phrase "and the right of the 
employee, except in the case of dismissal, to submit a formal grievance to the Town Manager" is 
amended to read: 
"and the right of the employee to submit a grievance as provided in the Grievance 
Procedure" 
4.  Conduct and Discipline, Town Manager acting as Department Head. The following 
sentence is added at the end of the paragraph beginning "In cases where a Department Head or 
employee reports to and is directly supervised by the Town Manager": 
"A grievance of a disciplinary action or termination imposed by the Town Manager under 
this paragraph shall be heard and decided by the Hearing Officer appointed by the Town 
Council, as provided in the Grievance Procedure, section titled Grievance of Disci plinary 
Action or Termination Imposed by the Town Manager." 
5.  Definitions. The following definition is added to the Definitions section: 
"Hearing Officer: Any of the two or three independent persons appointed by the Town 
Council by resolution, with equal authority and each individually available as the need 
arises, to hear and decide grievances of disciplinary actions and terminations impos ed by 
the Town Manager." 
6.  Table of Contents and Employee Acknowledgment. The Table of Contents is updated to 
reflect the new section, and the Employee Acknowledgment is updated to reference the current 
revision date of the Personnel Rules.
