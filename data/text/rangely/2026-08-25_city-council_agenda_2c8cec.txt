GUIDELINES FOR PUBLIC INPUT 
Public Input is a vital and important portion of every meeting and will be permitted 
throughout the meeting, but according to the following guidelines: 
a. Public input is allowed during the Agenda identified Public Input and Public
Hearing portion of the meeting.
i. If you would like to address the meeting during the appropriate times,
please raise your hand and when called upon you will be asked to come
to the podium.  Announce your name so that your statements can be
adequately captured in the meeting minutes.
ii. Please keep your comments to 3-5 minutes as others may want to
participate throughout the meeting and to insure that the subject does
not drift.
b. Throughout the meeting agenda calls for public input will be made, generally
pertaining to specific action items.  Please follow the same format as above.
c. At the conclusion of the meeting, if the meeting chair believes additional public
comment is necessary, the floor will be open.
We hope that this guideline will improve the effectiveness and order of the Town’s 
Public Meetings.  It is the intent of your publicly elected officials to stay open to your 
feelings on a variety of issues. 
Thank you, Rangely Mayor

6. Petitions and Public Input – Todd Thayn addressed the council concerning the crosswalk by Dakota Ave on 
Bell Street.  Todd stated that he was thankful for the crosswalk but stated that the crosswalk sign in the road 
was not user friendly when pulling a trailer and trying to make the turns especially if there are vehicles 
parked on the road by the park.  Todd asked if there was a possibility of another way to post the signs. 
7. Changes to the Agenda - None 
8. Public Hearings - 7:10pm  
9. Town Manager Update – Jonah reported to the council that the Town’s website was having technical issues 
with posting the agenda/board packet.  Jonah reported on the water supply status stating that water 
continues to drop but pumps are still running.  Jonah indicated that Stage 2 is still in place, but if the Town 
goes into Stage 3, the Town will ask for release of water from Kenny Reservoir.  If continued use would 
require a call on the river. Internet at the bulk water station is being worked on.  Jonah reported that the 
Town is water two times a week and flowers will be collected.  Jonah stated that the building permit process’ 
digital system is live.  Jonah indicated that budget process has started, and looking at a main street 
beatification project.  Jonah reminded the council about CIRSA training on August 25th at 6pm and that Alden 
Vanden Brink with the water conservancy would like to have a work session on August 25th at 5pm. 
5
10. Council Reports – Council had a work session with Rio Blanco County Commissioners and stated that it was a 
good start and would like to meet quarterly. 
11. Supervisor Reports 
a. Emily Peterson – Emily reported to the council that CDPHE did an inspection and indicated that the 
lagoon liners at wastewater need to be repaired.  Emily stated that they have been looking at the liners 
for each pond and stated that Pond C was the worst and needed to be fixed with a new liner as there is 
water underneath the current liner.  Emily also stated that all the other liners would need to be replaced 
at some point also, but Pond C was the most urgent. Emily indicated that the liners have about a 20-year 
life and the last pond that was re-lined was Pond A in 2017.  Emily said that she is actively looking for 
grants to help with the expense.   
12. Old Business 
13. New Business 
a. Discussion and action to approve the July 2026 Check Register – Motion made by Jimmy Dillon to 
approve the July 2026 Check Register, second by Shawn Morgan.  Motion passed. 
b. Discussion and action to delay the middle zone water tank re-coating project – Michael Dillon presented 
to the council on the middle zone tank.  Michael stated that the tanks were inspected in 2024 was 
determined that the middle zone tank was the worst.  Michael stated that the project was postponed in 
2025.  The tank re-coating project was to be started when demand decreases and the temperatures 
drop.  Michael indicated that the project would take 8 to 10 weeks to complete. Motion made by Tim 
Webber to proceed with the project, second by Shawn Morgan.  Andy Shaffer Nay, Jimmy Dillon 
abstained.  Motion passed. 
c. Discussion and action to approve new Bulk water customer (H & K Trucking, LLC); approval or denial of 
new bulk water customers is at the discretion of the council pursuant to Emergency Ordinance 707 – 
Michael Dillon indicated that H & K Trucking was needing about 15,000 gallons from bulk water to fill a 
tank for a project on Hwy 139.  Michael stated that it would not be ongoing.  Motion made by Andy 
Shaffer to deny the new Bulk water customer (H & K Trucking, LLC), second by Brenda Ahrens.  Jimmy 
Dillon and Shawn Morgan voted Nay. Motion passed. 
d. Discussion and action to approve the Energy Campus Development & Municipal Participation Study (stage 
2 of the Rangely geothermal development) – Jonah presented to the council stage 2.  Jonah stated that 
his recommendation to approve the stage 2 study engagement in the amount not to exceed $59,850 
contingent on execution of a town prepared addendum addressing the terms and authorized retention of 
an independent registered municipal advisor to review the resulting work.  Jonah stated that as we 
engage with these investment professionals and different firms, we will need our own counsel 
representation at an additional cost, but not sure what that cost would be.  Andy Shaffer asked Jonah if 
he would be able to get those estimates.  Motion made by Andy Shaffer to table until September 8th to 
give Jonah the time to get costs, second by Tim Webber.  Motion passed.
