For more information and instructions on how to join this meeting, please contact Aimee

Members Present:Martha Wilson, Catherine Lau, Marc Sisler, Jennika O’Brien Kevin 
Benitez, Breton McNamara, Jeffrey Boring
Members Absent:       None
 Staff Present: Eliberto Mendoza, Christina Pacheco, Aimee Bustamante
Agenda Item 1:Call to Order: Vice Chair Marc Sisler called the meeting to order at 5:34 
p.m.
Agenda Item 2:Approve minutes from July 9 regular meeting – Jeffrey Boring 
motioned to approve; Board Member Kevin Benitez seconded the motion. 
Agenda Item 3:Mid-year Report on HSBC and Home Steady – Eliberto Mendoza, 
Human Services Project Manager, presented on current challenges facing 
organizations funded through Human Services funding, including 
instances of residents arriving with several months of back rent due. These 
situations often arise from delays within partner organizations such as 
HOPE, Our Center, and All Roads. He also highlighted services and 
ongoing efforts; an example is HOPE, noting that they have successfully 
housed 53 individuals, transitioning them from homelessness into 
permanent housing.
Agenda Item 4:Foundant Training and Scoring – Eliberto Mendoza, Human Services 
Project Manager, provided the board with an overview of the Human 
Services funding application process, including how applications are 
scored. He explained key evaluation areas such as organizational 
collaborations, system efficiencies, and service stacking.

Agenda Item 5:Revisiting Board Interview Questions -  Eliberto Mendoza, Human

Services Project Manager, presented information from Blue Sky Bridge 
regarding the services they provide to the community and the impact on 
Longmont residents, including the availability of therapy services in 
Longmont.
