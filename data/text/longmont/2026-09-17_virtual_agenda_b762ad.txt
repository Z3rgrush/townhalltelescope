8979 Nelson Road Annexation 
Proposal: Annexation of a 0.88-acre parcel into the City of Longmont 
Project Location: 8979 Nelson Road (see vicinity map on the reverse side) 
When: September 17, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. 
Where: Via livestream at https://www.youtube.com/@cityoflongmont. There will be an opportunity for 
questions. 
Property Owner: Connection Church Longmont, Inc.  
Applicant: Ryan McBreen, Norris Design 
Background: This property is the site of an existing church. It is currently located in unincorporated 
Boulder County, and the owner seeks to be annexed into the City of Longmont. 
Future Meetings: The Longmont City Council is the decision-making body for annexations, with the 
Planning and Zoning Commission making a recommendation. If this project applies for annexation and goes 
through the full development review process, public hearings before the Planning and Zoning Commission 
and City Council will be held at future dates to be determined. 
Additional notification of all public hearings before the Planning & Zoning Commission and City Council will 
be provided, as required by City regulations. If you have questions regarding the neighborhood meeting, the 
development review process, code requirements, or other specific items, please contact the staff member 
identified on the reverse side.
