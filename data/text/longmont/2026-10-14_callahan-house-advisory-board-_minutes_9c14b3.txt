g. ArtWalk Debrief 
Karen and Brittaney shared event outcomes: 
• Participation: 16 artists (15 artists plus the band). 
• Attendance: 540 total visitors. 
• Donations: $212 plus $22 in merchandise sales. 
• Event cost: $320.80 (after sponsorships). 
• Artist satisfaction survey (9 responses): average 4.57 out of 5 (excluding an outlier score). 
• Suggestions included offering food and beverage options; a retired food and beverage provider, Wade 
Denney, offered to provide small food and beverage items at no cost to the House in the future, charging 
only visitors directly. 
• Lighting challenges in the East Garden impacted artist experience; the Board discussed potentially 
adjusting Callahan House ArtWalk hours to 3:00pm – 7:00pm to avoid late-evening darkness. 
• Garden overgrowth was noted as an aesthetic concern that aligns with garden restoration goals. 
• A thank-you card will be sent to Doug’s Garage band for donating their performance.

times listed on the schedule. Paper copies are being provided at each club visit to ensure members who 
prefer non-digital formats are included. Feedback will assist with future grant-writing for restoration 
efforts.
