TRANSPORTATION ADVISORY BOARD 
ACTION MINUTES 
August 10, 2026 
 
1. Call to Order 
 
Chair Gina McKee-Burrows called the August 10, 2026, meeting of the Transportation Advisory Board 
(TAB) to order at 6:02 p.m., Civic Center, Council Chambers, 350 Kimbark Street, Longmont, CO. 
 
2. Roll Call 
Board Members present were David McInerney, Gina McKee-Burrows, Ross Starritt, Taylor Wicklund 
and Amy Wilkins, and City Council Liaison, Matthew Popkin. Board Members Jim Paulmeno and 
Arlene Zortman were absent. 
City staff members present were Jim Angstadt, Cammie Edson, Kyle Haworth, Chris Robbie, and 
Stephanie Nguyen. 
3. Land Acknowledgement Statement 
 
Chair McKee-Burrows read the Land Acknowledgement Statement adopted by the Longmont City 
Council, July 13, 2021. 
 
4. Approval of Action Minutes of Preceding Meeting – July 13, 2026 
Board Member Starritt motioned to approve July 13, meeting minutes. Board Member McInerney 
seconded the motion. Motion carried 5-0 (Board Members Paulmeno and Zortman were absent.)  
5. Public Invited to be Heard (PITBH) 
Susan Sommers, 1418 Galilee Lane - Sommers voiced concerns regarding traffic on Sundance Drive and 
expressed frustration with the city regarding requirements for implementing traffic safety measures in 
the neighborhood. Sommers requested assistance from the board in changing the classification of 
Sundance Drive and addressing traffic issues. 
6. Action Items 
None. 
7. Informational Items 
Assistant City Attorney Chris Robbie lead a TAB overview and bylaw discussion, received input and 
answered questions from the board. (Note: written comments from Board Member Arlene Zortman were 
presented by Cammie Edson.) 
Link to Longmont Municipal Code (TAB discussed in Section 2.90): 
https://library.municode.com/co/longmont/codes/code_of_ordinances 
 
8. Staff Updates –

Kyle Haworth gave an update regarding the Neighborhood Traffic Mitigation Program and answered 
questions from the board. 
Cammie Edson provided updates regarding the Regional E-Bike Share official launch scheduled for 
August 12th and RTD service changes slated for September 2026. Edson also shared information 
regarding recently approved expansion grants and contract extensions for RIDE Longmont (new 
addition to agenda) and answered questions from the board. 
9. Comments From Board Members – 
Board Member Wilkins gave feedback regarding the Regional E-Bike program and made attendees 
aware of the Unity in the Community event scheduled for August 28th (date correction from August 
29th.) 
Board Member Starritt expressed excitement for the good news shared by staff and responded to Board 
Member Wilkins’ comments regarding the Regional E-Bike program. 
Vice Chair Wicklund expressed thanks for staff’s presentations and updates and briefly commented on 
the traffic mitigation program.  
Board Member McInerney thanked staff for presentations and updates and asked about upcoming study 
session topics. 
Chair McKee-Burrows praised staff for their presentations and updates and shared information regarding 
Electrify Longmont scheduled for October 4th. 
10. Comments From City Council Liaison 
 
Council Liaison Popkin echoed board comments regarding news presented by staff, gave feedback and 
thanks regarding the reinstatement of RTD Longmont-to-airport bus, and briefly discussed the upcoming 
Unity in the Community event. Popkin also asked staff about completion dates for the city greenway and 
119 bikeway projects, and construction start dates for the project located at Sunset Street and Highway 
119. 
 
11. Items for Upcoming Agendas (Next Scheduled Meeting scheduled for September 14, 2026) 
The board agreed to hold a regular meeting in September. Possible topics discussed - TAB bylaws draft; 
updates for Sundance [Film Festival] transportation, quiet zones, neighborhood traffic mitigation and 
Vision Zero. 
The board agreed to move the September study session to October - Roundabouts/traffic circles were 
requested as the session topic. 
Cammie Edson suggested that the 2027 TAB work plan be slated for the December TAB meeting.
