Key to agenda abbreviations:  
 
(A) Administrative- matters involving day-to-day decisions such as approving contracts, hiring 
staff and the procurement of goods and services. Administrative actions generally do not 
require formal actions by the elected body. 
 
(L)Legislative-  typically in the policy arena; legislative matters affect large areas and large 
groups of people, such as enacting dog regulations or amending the City code. Legislative 
action generally involves motions, resolutions and ordinances. 
 
(QJ)Quasi-Judicial- apply general rules to a specific interest, such as zoning change affecting 
a single piece of property, or a special use permit. Quasi-Judicial actions generally involve 
adjudication, sometimes in writing, but not a resolution or ordinance.  Decision for Quasi-
Judicial proceedings are made exclusively based upon the testimony presented on the record. 
Ex-parte communication (communication outside the official hearing) between elected officials 
and citizens is not permitted on Quasi-Judicial

NATIONAL VETERANS SMALL BUSINESS WEEK AND 
VETERANS WEEK 2026 
 
WHEREAS, the strength and prosperity of City of Woodland Park and the Pikes Peak region are enriched 
by the service, sacrifice, leadership, and entrepreneurial spirit of veterans, active-duty service members, 
National Guard and Reserve members, military spouses, and their families; and 
WHEREAS, according to the U.S. Small Business Administration, Colorado is home to nearly 49,000 
veteran-owned small businesses that contribute approximately $23 billion to the state’s economy and 
employ more than 96,000 people, demonstrating the significant role veteran entrepreneurs play in creating 
jobs, strengthening communities, and advancing Colorado’s economic prosperity; and 
WHEREAS, the United States Small Business Administration recognizes November 2–6, 2026, as National 
Veterans Small Business Week to celebrate veteran-owned businesses and highlight the resources 
available to help military-connected entrepreneurs start, grow, and strengthen their businesses; and 
WHEREAS, the Colorado SBDC Serving Pikes Peak, in partnership with the Veterans Business Outreach 
Center of Colorado hosted by Mt. Carmel Veterans Service Center, provides training, advising, resources, 
and connections that empower veterans and military spouses throughout their entrepreneurial journeys; 
and 
WHEREAS, the 19th Annual Mission Colorado, presented by Huntington Bank, will be held on 
Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at The Antlers Hotel in Colorado Springs, other activities include the 
Salute from the Springs display of 1,111 American flags in downtown Colorado Springs from November 1–
12 and the In Their Honor Veterans Day Parade on Saturday, November 7, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.; and 
WHEREAS, these observances provide an opportunity for residents, businesses, and community 
organizations to honor those who have served, support military-connected entrepreneurs, and reaffirm our 
region’s enduring commitment to the military community; 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, the City Council of Woodland Park, Colorado, does hereby proclaim  
 
November 2-6, 2026, as National Veterans Small Business Week and November 1-12, 2026, as Veterans Week 
 
 
and encourage all residents to recognize, celebrate, and support our veterans, service members, military families, 
and military-connected entrepreneurs.  
 
Dated this 15th day of October, 2026

SUBJECT: Approve on Initial Posting, Ordinance No. 1536, Series 2026, An Ordinance of the 
Woodland Park City Council Amending Section 6.06.120, 7.03.5, and Table 4.03-1 of 
the Unified Development Code Concerning the Classification of a New Use Type 
Labeled Public Safety Facilities and Set the Public Hearing for November 5, 2026 (L) 
(Presenter: Planning Director Schminke) 
     
  
BACKGROUND:  The City of Woodland Park Unified Development Code (UDC) provides minimum 
requirements for the construction, reconstruction, alteration, or use of buildings, structures, and land 
within the City of Woodland Park.  Currently, there is not a clear use type classification for emergency 
service/public safety uses.  This ordinance proposed a new use type of "Public Safety Facilities" and 
identifies the zone districts where these facilities may be sited. 
  
RECOMMENDATION:  Approve on Initial Posting, Ordinance No. 1536, Series 2026, An Ordinance 
of the Woodland Park City Council Amending Section 6.06.120, 7.03.5, and Table 4.03-1 of the 
Unified Development Code Concerning the Classification of a New Use Type Labeled Public Safety 
Facilities and Set the Public Hearing for November 5, 2026.

CITY OF WOODLAND PARK, COLORADO 
ORDINANCE NO. 1536, SERIES 2026 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE WOODLAND PARK CITY COUNCIL  
AMENDING SECTIONS 6.06.120, 7.03.5, and TABLE 4.03-1, OF THE  
UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE CONCERNING THE CLASSIFICATION OF 
A NEW USE TYPE LABELED PUBLIC SAFETY FACILITIES  
WHEREAS, the City of Woodland Park, Colorado (the “City”) is duly organized and 
validly exists as a home rule city under Article XX, Section 6 of the Colorado Constitution and the 
City Charter; and 
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 31 -15-401, the City by and through its City Council 
(“Council”), possesses the authority to adopt laws and ordinances within its police power in 
furtherance of the public health, safety and welfare; and 
WHEREAS, on March 23, 2026, City Council adopted Ordinance No. 1521, An 
Ordinance of The City of Woodland Park Repealing and Replacing Title 16, Title 17, and Title 18 
of The Woodland Park Municipal Code, Repealing Section 5.24, and Adopting a New Unified 
Development Code; and  
WHEREAS, the new Unified Development Code (“UDC”) implements the mission of the 
adopted City of Woodland Park Comprehensive Plan to protect, promote, and enhance the general 
well-being of the mountain community of Woodland Park through an unwavering commitment to 
excellence that strengthens public trust  by protecting and providing for the public health, safety, 
and general welfare of the City; and   
WHEREAS, the UDC provides minimum requirements for the construction, 
reconstruction, alteration, or use of buildings, structures, and  land within the City of Woodland 
Park; and 
WHEREAS, the UDC currently lacks a clear use type classification for emergency 
service/public safety uses; and 
WHEREAS, emergency service/public safety  providers, generally comprised of police, 
fire, paramedic, and emergency medical services (“EMT”), serve a critical role in the provision of 
emergency and lifesaving care and assistance in the immediate aftermath of a crisis; and  
WHEREAS, the expansion and construction of emergency services/public safety uses and 
facilities in Woodland Park is vital to supporting the community’s continued public safety, health, 
and general welfare needs; and  
WHEREAS, City Council finds it desirable and appropriate, and in the best interest of the 
general health, safety, and welfare of its citizens, to amend the UDC to incorporate a new use type 
classification labeled Public Safety Facilities and amend the appropriate Sections, therein.
