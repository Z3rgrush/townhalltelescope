Item 10. 1:55:22 Ordinance 2026-22; AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF 
GLENWOOD SPRINGS, COLORADO, AMENDING THE GLENWOOD 
SPRINGS MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING THE REGULATION AND 
PERMITTING OF ACCESSORY TOURIST RENTALS AND SHORT TERM 
RENTALS IN THE CITY. 
Mayor Dehm recused himself.  
Senior Planner Emery Ellingson presented on the item. 
Councilor Schmahl, Councilor Townsley, Councilor Weimer, Councilor Schachter and Councilor Smith 
asked questions. 
Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski opened the item for public comment. 
There were none.  
2:18:10 Councilor Schachter moved, seconded by Councilor Weimer, to approve Ordinance 2026-22; an 
Ordinance of the City of Glenwood Springs, Colorado, amending the Glenwood Springs Municipal Code 
regarding the regulation and permitting of accessory tourist rentals and short term rentals in the city 
amended to include a minimum of 20 nights and a minimum remittance of $150 in sales tax over a two 
year period. 
Councilor Schachter, Councilor Schmahl, Councilor Weimer, Councilor Smith and Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski 
made comments.  
2:30:40 The motion passed 4:2 with Councilor Schmahl and Councilor Smith voting against. 
Councilor Schachter and Councilor Weimer made comments.
