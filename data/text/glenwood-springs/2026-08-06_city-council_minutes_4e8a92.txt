Smith, asked questions. 
Mayor Dehm opened the item for public comment. 
City resident Ashley Stahl, city resident Erin Anderson, valley resident Mae Gray, valley resident Sally 
Boughton, city resident Grace Vaught-Smith, city resident Judy Haynes, valley resident Ellen Freedman, 
valley resident Auden Schendler, valley resident Debbie Bruell, valley resident Jess Hall, city resident

2:15:44 Councilor Schachter moved, seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski, to instruct counsel to prepare 
legal enforcement action and related sanctions as well as injunctive relief against JG Housing Solutions 
at 100 Midland Avenue related to Units 110 and 210 for code and zoning and land use violations based 
on the prior findings of P&Zs revocation of the special use permit and continued violations of land use 
code and violations and pursue that in state court.  
Councilor Smith, Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski, Councilor Schmahl, Councilor Townsley, and Mayor Dehm made 
comments. 
2:24:47 The motion passed 6:1 with Councilor Schmahl voting against.
