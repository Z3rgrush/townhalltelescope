ACTION ITEM 
 
Per Section 070.060.060(b) of the Glenwood Springs Municipal Code (Code), “[T]he 
preliminary plat procedure provides a mechanism for the City to review an overall plan for a 
proposed subdivision to ensure compliance with this Code, the Comprehensive Plan, and 
the adequate provision of facilities and services in the City.” Section 070.060.060(b)(2) 
states that “[A] preliminary plat is required if the proposed subdivision is on land that has 
not been platted.”  The subject property related to this application is unplatted, meaning 
that it has a metes and bounds legal description and is not part of an approved subdivision. 
Otherwise, the application would be eligible for a minor subdivision since it simply 
proposes a combination of lots.  
 
Per Section 070.060.060(b)(3)f.1, the Planning and Zoning Commission (Commission) 
“…shall review the preliminary plat application and recommend approval, approval with 
conditions, or denial of the preliminary plat in accordance with Subsection 070.060.030(g) 
and the approval criteria in Paragraph 4…”  After approval of a Preliminary Plat application, 
applicants must submit a Final Plat application, which is reviewed administratively by City 
staff.   
 
Action 1 – Preliminary Plat – Consideration of a Preliminary Plat application to combine 
three parcels into one. 
 
Staff Recommendation: Staff recommends approval of the application.

BACKGROUND 
 
The subject property is located at 51993 Highway 6 and 24 and consists of three separate 
parcels totaling 3.93 acres. On April 3, 2025, a Major Site and Architectural Plan Review 
application was approved by City Council for the construction of an 80-unit deeply 
affordable multifamily project referred to as Canyon Vista.  A combined building permit 
and Final Plans Review application is currently under review.   
 
It is necessary to combine the three parcels, because without the site being one combined 
lot, the buildings will cross property lines and violate setback requirements.  The 
combining of the three parcels that make up the site represents one of the last steps for 
the project prior to the applicant closing on the property and commencing construction.  
The land at 51993 Highway 6 and 24 has functioned as one lot, under the joint ownership of 
the current owners, but is actually made up of three lots with metes and bounds legal 
descriptions.  This is a common situation in Glenwood for historical properties that have 
never been subdivided.

Project Narrative – Preliminary Plat 
Project: Canyon Vista  
Project Address: 51993 Hwy 6 & 24, Glenwood Springs, CO 
Purpose: 80 units of affordable housing 
Cohen Esrey Development Group is preparing to begin construction on Canyon Vista, an 80-
unit, 100% affordable housing project in west Glenwood Springs. As part of this process, we 
are submitting application for a Preliminary Plat to the City of Glenwood Springs. The process 
will consolidate parcels, ensure adequate provision of facilities and services to them and 
create a subdivision from the metes and bounds lot descriptions.  
Canyon Vista will deliver an apartment complex consisting of 3 new buildings with 52 two-
bedroom and 21 one-bedroom apartments. There are 138 parking stalls and various amenities 
for residents throughout the site. These include a fitness center, community room with full 
kitchen, co-working lounge, playground, courtyard, onsite management office, dog run, dog 
wash, roof deck and CCTV monitoring of the entire property.   
The existing land on which the project is proposed is Parcels 1, 2A & 2B in the NW1/ 4SE / 4 of 
Section 5, Township 6 South, Range 89 West, 6th Principal Meridian, lying between county Road 
and US HWY No. 24. The property address is 51993 Hwy 6 in the City of Glenwood Springs, CO. 
The site is approximately 3.9 acres in size and is zoned Mixed Use Corridor (M1). The property is 
owned by Margaret O. Macpherson and under contract for sale to Cohen Esrey Development 
Group.  
 
Canyon Vista is also designed as a highly energy-efficient, all-electric, solar-powered, workforce 
housing community where local families can grow and thrive in the beautiful surroundings of West 
Glenwood Springs.
