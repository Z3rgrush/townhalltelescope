Item 9. 34:32 Resolution 2026-20; A resolution of the city council of the 
City of Glenwood Springs, Colorado, calling an election on November 3, 
2026, and certifying a ballot issue, setting the ballot title and content 
for the ballot issue; and providing other matters relating to the election 
City Manager Steve Boyd opened the item. 
Councilor Townsley, Councilor Smith and Mayor Dehm asked questions. 
Mayor Dehm opened the item for public comment. 
There were none. 
43:17 Councilor Smith moved, seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski, to approve Resolution 2026-20 with 
ballot language A-1 to election.  
Councilor Schachter, Councilor Smith, Councilor Schmahl, Councilor Townsley and Mayor Dehm made 
comments. 
51:21 The motion passed unanimously. 
City Attorney Karl Hanlon made comments about the Fair Campaigns Practices Act and next steps. 
Mayor Dehm, Councilor Smith and Councilor Schachter asked questions and made comments.

19; Intergovernmental Agreement with Garfield County for the 2026 Coordinated Election. 
1:06:11 The motion passed unanimously. 
Item 11. 1:06:19 Ordinance 2026-18; an ordinance of the City of 
Glenwood Springs, Colorado, amending title 070 of the Glenwood 
Springs Municipal Code regarding the regulation and permitting of 
electric vehicle charging projects within the city. 
Senior Planner Emery Ellingson presented on the item. 
Councilor Smith and Mayor Dehm asked questions. 
Mayor Dehm opened the item for public comment. 
There were none.  
1:20:24 Councilor Smith moved, seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Zalinski, to approve planning file CDA 
000050-2026 incorporating staff’s findings because the application meets the approval criteria for a 
code amendment with the findings and conditions of the staff report.  
1:21:32 The motion passed unanimously.
