5. MAYOR  AND  BOARD
OF  ALDERMEN
COMMENTS: Mayor  Spellman  wanted  to give  a shout-out  to resident  Derek  Blake  for
putting  on  the  mining  competitions  at the  Gilpin  County  Fair,  which  the
City  of  Black  Hawk  provided  the purse  money  to.  He acknowledged
City  staff  members  Michele  Martin  and  Chris  Vincent  for  their  work  on
it and  added  that  it was  nice  that  Derek's  father,  Kent  Blake,  got  to work
on it with  him.  Overall,  it was  a good  event,  and  he's  glad  that  Black
Hawk  helped  to sponsor  it.  He said  Black  Hawk  has a great  deal  of
appreciation  for  Derek  Blake  and what  he has put  on with  the mining
events.
Next,  there  are two  worthy  organizations  that  Black  Hawk  contributes  to,
and,  in  conjunction  with  the  Silver  Dollar  Metropolitan  District  (SDMD),
he's  asking  the City  to match  $2,000.00  in funding  for  the Friends  of
Charlie's  Place  Animal  Shelter.  Dostal  Alley  in Central  City  is hosting
the  fundraising  event,  and  the SDMD  is donating  $2,000.00,  which  we
would  match.
The  second  event  is hosted  by  the  Horseshoe  and  Lady  Luck  casinos  to
fill  a bus  with  school  supplies  for  Gilpin  County  students.  The  SDMD
will  also  donate  $2,000.00  to this  cause,  and he is asking  the City  to
match  this  amount  as well.
Council  was  unanimous  in  favor  of  both  contributions.
