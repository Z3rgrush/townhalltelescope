Approved  by the Voters  of  the City  is Subject  to Certain  Self-Executing
Conditions,  the Violation  of  Which  Automatically  Terminates  Said  Tax
City  Attomey  Hoffmann  introduced  the topic  for  discussion  and provided
background  on why  it came  about. He said there  had been discussion  in
the community  about the Gilpin  County  Commissioners  considering
putting  a sales tax on the November  ballot. The City  has an Education
Enhancement  Tax approved  by Black  Hawk  voters  in 2008.  He said
there is also discussion  about  what  was actually  approved  in 2008 and
wbether  the  City  has any  discretion  regarding  the  Education
Enhancement  Tax if  the conditions  were violated.  This  draft  resolution
block  quotes  the language  that  was  approved  by the voters  of  Black  Hawk
in 2008,  one of  which  is that  if  one of  the conditions  in the ballot  question
is violated  or tr:iggered,  the tax ends automatieally;  it is self-executing
Nanguage and has a date when  the tax wbu!d  terminate,  which  is either
the foilowirxg  January  1 or July I of  when  the tax is imposed.  This
resolution  attempts  to  lay  out the  process  by  whicb,  if County
Commissioners  put  a ballot  sales tax question  on the ballot,  it is approved
by the registered  electors  of  Gilpin  County,  and the county  imposes  that
sales tax, what  the impact  would  be on the Education  Enhancement  Tax.
The language  of  the ballot  question  approved  by the voters  of Black
Hawk  compels  the tax to terminate  automatically  if  that condition  is
triggered.
Mayor  Spellrnan  said they  invited  the school board president  and
superintendent  to the next meeting.  He said he also has his original
comments  from  2009,  when  the City  gave the first.check  presentation,
and from  2019, from  the 10th  anniversary  presentation.  At the next
meeting,  he will  read both  of  those.  He remembered  that  at the time,  one
of  the phrases  he used was that, since this is self-executing,  everyone
needs to understand  that  you  can't  come  and cry crocodile  tears to City
Council  and ask us to keep the 1.5%  Education  Tax intact,  because  it
automatically  terminates.  He suggested  to Council  that if  the County
Commissioners  submit  a question  to the voters  and voters  approve  it that
not  only  would  the 1.5 oA sales tax  be terminated  but  he thinks  they  would
need to lower  the City's  sales tax that  we get to keep for  the general  fund
by an additional.5%  so that  they  don't  go over  the current  sales tax rate
of  8.9%  which  includes  the state sales tax, so it would  be a direct  impact
to the City.  He believes  they  can do that,  but  Central  City  is in an entirely
different  position  where  they  too collect  a 6o/o sales tax, but they  get the
benefit  of  all of  that  sales tax, 4% goes to their  general  fund,  and 2% to
their  emergency  services,  so they  are not in- a position  to lower  their  sales
tax so they would  have a sales tax of  10.9%  if  this all were  approved.
There  are disadvantages  to both  cities  if  something  like  this  were  to be
placed  on the ballot  and passed.  He suggested  scheduling  this  resolution
for  the next  meeting  and encouraged  the school  to attend  to make  their
comments.  He said he believes  they  will  pass a companion  resolution
