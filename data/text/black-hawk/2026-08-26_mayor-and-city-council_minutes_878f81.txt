F.  Resolution  52-2026,  A Resolution  Approving  Change  Order  #3 for  the Bobtail  Mine
Improvements  Phase  2 Project  in  the  Amount  of  $447,460.00
Mayor  Spellman  read  the title.
City  Engineer  Reed  introduced  this  item  and presented  photo  slides  of
the historic  Crregory  Diggings  extension  stope.  Change  Order  #3 would
restore  it to its historic  appearance,  based  on an 1861  sketch.  There  was
another  slide  of  the portal  in 1936  that  Black  Fox  Mining  would  recreate
with  timber  sets. There  are a few  other  elements  included  as well.
4
Mayor  Spellman  said this  is  the  same  artist  who  painted  George
Washington  crossing  the  Delaware  and  the  Westward  mural  in  the  U.S.
Capitol,  which  is why  he was in the Colorado  territory  conducting
research  for  the  mural.  He  said  the  City  will  put  the  sketch  on one  of  our
history  panels  running  up the  stairs.  He  said  if  we  get  really  lucky,  and
if  the  portal  can  be accessed,  there  will  be a string  of  ore cars  left  in  there,
as was  the  story  his  Uncle  Norm  Blake,  father  to Kent  Blake,  would  tell,
that  would  be a big  bonus!
MOTION  TO
APPROVE Alderman  Bennett  MOVED  and  was  SECONDED  by  Alderman  Torres
to approve  Resolution  52-2026,  a Resolution  approving  Change  Order
#3 for  the  Bobtail  Mine  Improvements  Phase  2 Project  in  the  amount  of
$447,460.00.

operating  without  intert'uption,  the City  of  Black  Hawk  would  do so on the  following  two  conditions:
1.  The  City  of  Central  will  disconnect  the Briggs  Lot  firom  its municipal  boundaries,  and  agree  not
to object  to the City  of  Black  Hawk's  annexation  of  the Briggs  Lot  property  with  payment  for  such
disconnection by Black  Hawk  to Central  in  the amount  of  $500,000.00;  and
2. Black  Hawk  will  agree  to convey  at no cost  11.77  acres  of  land  to the  City  of  Central  in  the  area
generally  known  as the Gold  Mountain  Apartments,  located  off  of  Lake  Gulch  Road,  which  includes
the property  Central  has identified  as the  preferred  location  for  its 1-million-gallontreatedwater  storage
