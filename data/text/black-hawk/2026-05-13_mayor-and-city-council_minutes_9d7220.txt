8. PUBLIC  HEARINGS:
A. CB9,  An  Ordinance  Amending  Chapter  6 of  the  Black  Hawk  Municipal  Code  by the
Repeal  and  Reenactment  of  Article  XVIII  and  the  Repeal  of  Articles  XIX  and  XXI
Regarding  the  Licensing  of  Marijuana  Establishments  in  Black  Hawk
Mayor  Spellman  read  the title  and  opened  the public  hearing.
City  Attorney  Hoffmann  introduced  this  item  and  provided  the
background.  The  City  has no pending  licenses,  no existing  licenses,  and
the one license  it did have  has now  been surrendered.  The City  now
wishes  to repeal  those  chapters  of  the Municipal  Code  and prohibit  all
medical  and  recreational  marijuana  businesses,  facilities,  and
establishments  within  the City.
PUBLIC  HEARING: Mayor  Spellman  declared  a Public  Hearing  on CB9,  an Ordinance
amending  Chapter  6 of  the Black  Hawk  Municipal  Code  by the repeal
and  reenactment  of  Article  XVIII  and  the repeal  of  Articles  XIX  and  XXI
regarding  the licensing  of  marijuana  establishments  in Black  Hawk  open
and invited  anyone  wanting  to address  the Board  either  "for"  or "against"
the Ordinance  to come  forward.
2

9. ACTION  ITEMS:
D. Resolution  28-2026,  A Resolution  Approving  Change  Order  #2 for  the Bobtail  Mine
Improvements  Phase  2 Project  in the  Amount  of  $949,307.00
Mayor  Spellman  reconvened  City  Council  and  read  the title.
City  Engineer  Reed  said the original  contract  was set up as a time  and
materials  contract  because  they  didn't  know  what  they  were  getting  into,
and as it turns  out,  construction  has been  more  challenging  than  expected.
This  change  order  includes  the train  car loop,  a short  walking  tour  loop,
and installing  some  timbers  to create  an exhibit  space. This  change  order
is a firm  price  to finish  the project,  which  should  be completed  by
September  1.
Alderman  Midcap  and Torres  had a few  questions.  Council  went  on a
tour  yesterday,  and Alderman  Midcap  said he thought  it was excellent
and a one-of-a-kind  attraction,  to which  Alderman  Johnson  said he
thought  it would  be the main  attraction  in  Black  Hawk.  Mayor  Spellman
saidthe  exteriortourwill  be polished  and  professional,  like  aDisney  tour,
and  the interior  tour  will  be professional  but  will  certainly  be an authentic
tour  of  a 1860-1870s  gold  mine.
MOTION  TO
APPROVE Alderman  Johnson  MOVED  and was  SECONDED  by Alderman  Wiley
to approve  Resolution  28-2026,  a Resolution  approving  Change  Order
#2 for  the Bobtail  Mine  Improvements  Phase  2 Project  in the amount  of
$949,307.00.
MOTION  PASSED After  some  discussion,  the motion  PASSED  unanimously.
