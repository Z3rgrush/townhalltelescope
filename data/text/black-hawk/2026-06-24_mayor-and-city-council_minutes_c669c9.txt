C. Resolution  39-2026,  A  Resolution  Awarding  the  Bid  and  Approving  the  Contract  Between
the  City  of  Black  Hawk  and  Local  Electric,  LLC  in an Amount  Not  to Exceed  $87,153.00
for  Construction  of  the  Red  Dolly  Casino  Electrical  Service  Relocation  Project
Mayor  Spellman  read  the title.
City  Engineer  Reed  introduced  this  item  to hire  an electrician  to install
the new  underground  service  to Red  Dolly  since  Xcel  Energy  does not
install  cornrnercial  service  lines.  He said six bids  were  received,  with
Local  Electric  being  the low  bid. Xcel  will  begin  construction  in two
weeks,  and Local  Electric  will  coordinate  with  Xcel  and Red Dolly  to
complete  the work.
MOTION  TO
APPROVE Alderman  Bennett  MOVED  and was  SECONDED  by  Alderman
Johnson  to approve  Resolution  39-2026,  a Resolution  awarding  the Bid
and approving  the contract  between  the City  of  Black  Hawk  and Local
Electric,  LLC  in an amount  not  to exceed  $87,153.00  for  construction  of
the Red  Dolly  Casino  Electrical  Service  Relocation  project.
MOTION  PASSED There  was  no discussion,  and  the motion  PASSED  unanimously.
D. Resolution  40-2026,  A  Resolution  Awarding  the  Bid  and  Approving  the  Contract  Behveen
the  City  of  Black  Hawk  and  Maverick  Construction,  LLC  in an Amount  Not  to Exceed
$85,390.95  for  Construction  of  the  Black  Hawk  Commons  and  Copper  Kitchen  Pizzeria
Storage  Sheds  Project
3
Mayor  Spellman  read  the title.
City  Engineer  Reed  explained  the need  for  more  storage  for  our  tenants
at the Commons  and Copper  Kitchen  Pizzeria.  He said they  decided  to
go with  custom  sheds  rather  than  Tough  Sheds  because  they  would  look
better.  He said eight  bids  were  received,  with  Maverick  Construction
being  the lowest  bid.  They  anticipate  being  completed  by the end of
August.
MOTION  TO
APPROVE Alderman  Johnson  MOVED  and  was  SECONDED  by  Alderman  Torres
to approve  Resolution  40-2026,  a Resolution  awarding  the Bid  and
approving  the contract  between  the City  of  Black  Hawk  and Maverick
Construction, LLC in an amount  not to  exceed  $85,390.95  for
construction  of  the Black  Hawk  Commons  and Copper  Kitchen  Pizzeria
Storage  Sheds  project.
MOTION  PASSED There  was no discussion,  and  the  motion  PASSED  unanimously.
