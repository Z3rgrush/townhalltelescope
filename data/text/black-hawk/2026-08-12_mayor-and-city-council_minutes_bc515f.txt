7. PRESENTATION: Fill  the  Bus  Check  Donation  for  Gilpin  County  Students
Mayor  Spellman  introduced  the  campaign  to provide  school  supplies  for
Gilpin  County  students.  He  said  this  was  initiated  by  two  team  members
from  the Horseshoe  Casino,  Matthew  Lindberg  and  Tyler  Figge.  Joe
Marr  from  the  School  received  the  checks.  The  City  agreed  to donate
$2,000.00,  and the  Silver  Dollar  Metropolitan  District  (SDMD),
represented  by  Lynnette  Hailey,  also  presented  a check  for  $2,000.00  to
Mr.  Marr.  Mr.  Lindberg  said  this  was  all  Tyler's  idea  and  that  he did
most  of  the  heavy  lifting.  He  went  on to say that  every  seat on  the bus
2
was  filled  with  backpacks  and  every  necessary  school  supply.  It  took  two
hours  during  back-to-school  night  for  every  student  to take  their  pick.  He
said  it was  very  joyful  and  turned  out  to be a remarkable  event.  A nice
story  to go with  this,  Mr.  Lindberg  said,  was  that  Mr.  Zigge  cold-called

11. ACTION  ITEMS:
B. Resolution  47-2026,  A  Resolution  Awarding  the  Bid  and  Approving  the  Contract  Between
the  City  of  Black  Hawk  and  Roche  Constructors  in  an  Amount  Not  to Exceed  1,104,900.00
for  Construction  of  the  Ballard  House  Renovation  and  the  Barn  Shed  Addition  Project
Mayor  Spellman  read  the  title.
City  Engineer  Reed  introduced  the proposed  renovations  at the Gregory
Point  Ballard  House  and  Barn  Shed. He  presented  two  exhibits  outlining
the plans. The Ballard  House  will  be renovated  into a full-service
restaurant,  with  a connection  to the Stevenson  House  to use its ADA-
compliant  battu'ooms,  and  the  Barn  Shed  to hold  events.  Mayor  Spellman
pointed  out that although  there  will  be  a connection  between  the
buildings,  they  can still  be isolated  if  one is closed,  as the Stevenson
House  is planned  to be the new  visitor  center.  Reed  said  these  were  all
bundled  together  for  a single  bid;  seven  bids  were  received,  and Roche
Constructors  was  the low  bidder.
MOTION  TO
APPROVE Alderman  Bennett  MOVED  and  was  SECONDED  by  Alderman  Torres
to approve  Resolution  47-2026,  a Resolution  awarding  the bid and
approving  the contract  between  the City  of  Black  Hawk  and Roche
Constructors  in an amount  not  to exceed  1,104,900.00  for  construction
of  the Ballard  House  renovation  and the Barn  Shed  addition  project.
MOTION  PASSED After  some  discussion,  the  motion  PASSED  unanimously.
