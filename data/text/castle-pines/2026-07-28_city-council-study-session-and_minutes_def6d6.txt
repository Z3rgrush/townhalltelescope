• APPROVAL OF AGENDA 
• The agenda was approved as presented. 
 
 
• PUBLIC COMMENT 
• Roberta Allen, 5703 Jasper Pt., noted she could not see who was 
speaking in the Study Session, but enjoyed the energy of the room. 
Stated that she does not support the location of the coffee shop on 
Castle Pines Parkway and would like to see the roadway opened to 
provide residents with the opportunity to walk through a more opened 
Castle Pines.
