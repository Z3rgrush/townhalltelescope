• No Public Comment was presented. 
 
• Mayor Engerman closed the Public Comment at 8:12 p.m. 
 
• Motion: Mayor Engerman moved to approve Resolution 26-62, A Resolution of the 
City Council of the City of Castle Pines, Colorado, Approving the Canyonside 
Preliminary Plan No. 1, 2nd Amendment, City Case No. PFP-2026-001, with the 
following condition of approval, the Subdivision Improvement Agreement shall 
require the developer to evaluate lot and foundation drainage and to install chase 
drains, consistent with City policy, where needed for appropriate drainage, as 
determined by the City Engineer. Such evaluation and installation shall be 
completed prior to final acceptance of public improvements, and the City Engineer 
may require additional chase drains identified during the warranty period. Council 
Member Salazar seconded. The motion passed 6 to 0. 
 
• Mayor Engerman closed the Public Hearing at 8:24 p.m. 
 
 
• Resolution 26-63, A Resolution of the City Council of the City of Castle Pines, 
Colorado, Adopting an Expedited Review Process for Qualified Affordable 
Housing Development as Part of a Commitment to Proposition 123, as 
Adopted by Colorado Voters in 2022 
 
• Donna Ferguson, Community Development Director, presented the 
item. 
 
• Motion: Council Member Salazar moved to adopt Resolution 26-63, A Resolution 
of the City Council of the City of Castle Pines, Colorado, Adopting an Expedited 
Review Process for Qualified Affordable Housing Development as Part of a 
Commitment to Proposition 123, as Adopted by Colorado Voters in 2022. Council 
Member Cole seconded. The motion passed 6 to 0. 
 
 
• Resolution 26-64, A Resolution of the City Council of the City of Castle Pines, 
Colorado, Authorizing the City Manager to File an Affordable Housing 
Commitment with the Colorado Department of Local Affairs Pursuant to 
Proposition 123 and State Law and Continuing the City's Commitment to a 
Regional Partnership with Douglas County Jurisdictions to Meet Affordable 
Housing Goals 
 
• Donna Ferguson, Community Development Director, presented the 
item.
