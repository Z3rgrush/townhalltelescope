POLICY QUESTION #1
EMPLOYEE COMPENSATION
Compensation Philosophy
• Position salary ranges set to meet the identified labor market
• Ranges midpoint will be equal to average midpoint of market
• Maintain salary of high-performing employees within their position’s 
range based on compa-ratio, years of experience, and successful 
job performance
• Conduct comprehensive survey every 3 to 5 years
• Conduct informal survey annually
30
POLICY QUESTION #1
EMPLOYEE COMPENSATION
• In 2022, City Council affirmed the compensation philosophy and the 
salary ranges for 12 positions - conducted by staff
• In 2026, completed a comprehensive compensation salary study - 
conducted by a consultant, reviewed by City staff
• The City Manager proposed budget requested compensation 
funding based on the study’s determined midpoints
• Following staff’s review, the proposed request is revised downward 
• Presented and reviewed with CM Blue and CM Eubanks
