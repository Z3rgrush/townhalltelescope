• No public comment was presented. 
 
 
• PRESENTATION OF MINUTES 
• Minutes from the August 20, 2026 Parks and Recreation Advisory 
Board Meeting were approved as presented. 
 
 
• GENERAL BUSINESS 
• Trail Signage - Trail Mile Markers and Kiosks (West) 
• Marcus Graves, Parks, Recreation and Open Spaces Director, 
presented the item. 
 
• Motion: Chair Pro Tem Faber moved to support staff’s recommendation 
to adopt Option 3, trail signage at the start, midpoint, and end of each 
trail segment as the mile marker approach for the Castle Pines trail 
network. Board Member Wright seconded. The motion passed 5 to 0.
