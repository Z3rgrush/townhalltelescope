2. Discussion, Recommendation, and Possible Action Regarding Amendments to Chapter 8 of 
The Town of Palmer Lake Municipal Code Concerning Bear-Resistant Trash Containers 
The Planning Commission discussed introducing a mandatory, town-wide bear-proof container ordinance 
covering residential, commercial, and short-term rental properties in Palmer Lake. To address rising human-
bear interactions, members emphasized requiring certified bear-proof receptacles, researching CPW grant 
funding to offset costs, evaluating fine structures, and resolving waste hauler logistics. The commission 
agreed to discuss during an upcoming planning workshop before submitting a formal recommendation to the 
Town Attorney and Board of Trustees. 
 
Public Comment: 
Jennifer Rausch - Asked the commission to prioritize mandatory bear-resistant trash requirements and 
stricter license enforcement for short-term rentals, citing frequent issues with out-of-town guests leaving 
unsecured trash that habituates bears and creates neighborhood hazards. 
Marne Kendrick - Asked for a town-wide bear trash ordinance with strict enforcement, citing multiple 
dangerous encounters with habituated bears drawn to unsecured trash at nearby short-term rentals. 
Tati McAlister - Asked the commission to enact bear trash regulations immediately, explaining that local 
bears undergo torpor rather than continuous winter hibernation and will forage during warm spells 
whenever unsecured trash is available. 
Patrick McAlister - Asked the commission to immediately regulate short-term rentals as commercial 
businesses by mandating trash compliance backed by license revocation and fines, using them as an initial 
test case before rolling out broader community-wide regulations. 
Public Comment

Don Kendrick - Recommended posting informational flyers at the post office and sending direct compliance
