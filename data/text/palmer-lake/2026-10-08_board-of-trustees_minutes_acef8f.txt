Staff/Department Reports 
5. Town Administrator 
Town Administrator Patrick Davidson reviewed the reports included in the meeting packet and provided 
updates on ongoing bear management concerns and discussions regarding potential code amendments. He 
also discussed the proposed consolidation of the Water, Road/Streets, and Parks departments into a single 
Public Works Department to improve operational efficiency, maximize shared resources, and provide 
budgetary support. 
Mr. Davidson reported that legal and financial reviews are underway regarding potential participation in the 
Pikes Peak Rural Transportation Authority, which would require a TABOR-compliant ballot measure in 
November 2027. Additionally, he is researching a potential ballot measure concerning fire protection services 
involving the Town of Palmer Lake Fire Department and Monument Fire. He noted that, because these 
measures involve taxation, a special election may not be permissible, and consideration may need to be 
deferred to the next regular election. Further information will be provided as research and reviews are 
completed. 
6. Town Attorney 
Attorney James Godbold reported that the Town has retained a hearing officer and is finalizing an agreement 
with a presenting officer to begin scheduling upcoming hearing pertain to Trustee Roger Moseley. He also 
noted that Attorney Karl Kumli is expected to provide guidance to Town staff regarding a final 
reimbursement check to Buc-ee’s, which is subject to an upcoming deadline. 
Trustee Moseley requested clarification on the procedures for Board members submitting Colorado Open 
Records Act (CORA) requests. Attorney Godbold explained the distinction between requests submitted by 
individuals under the state CORA statute and requests made by Board members in their official capacity, 
which are handled separately.Trustee Beltran inquired whether any Trustees had contacted the Town 
Attorney directly by email and whether the established communication protocol was being followed. 
Attorney Godbold responded that he had not received any direct inquiries but had been included in 
correspondence between Trustees and Town staff. Trustee Beltran requested that the matter be placed on 
the next Board of Trustees meeting agenda to review the appropriate communication procedures and ensure 
fiscal responsibility regarding legal service costs. Trustee Harris requested a copy of the existing policy 
governing communications between Trustees and the Town Attorney. 
7. Town Clerk/Administration/Treasurer  
Town Clerk Erica Romero addressed questions on September code enforcement updates, including resolved 
trash complaints and unpermitted right-of-way structures at a Short-Term Rental that obstruct emergency 
access, and confirming that campaign finance reports remain available for public review by appointment and 
in compliance with state statue.  
 
8. Water Department 
Trustee Beeson asked if the Town knew if there was an end date to the water call.  Water Supervisor Charles 
Roubidoux responded that there was no firm end date. 
Trustee Beeson asked about the possibility of a tour of the water treatment facility. 
Trustee Moseley asked a question about the current status of the monument creek water levels. Water 
Supervisor Roubidoux responded that the levels are too low to be a production source currently. 
Mayor Stern asked how many entities have senior water rights to the creek.  Water Supervisor Roubidoux 
responded that there are 26 more senior rights. 
9. Public Works Department 
Town Administrator Patrick Davidson had nothing specific to report at this time. 
10. Police Department 
Trustee Moseley asked if the police department owned any drones.  Police Chief Glen Smith confirmed that 
the police department maintains a drone squad used for tasks such as locating lost children and assisting with 
fire location identification, including providing coordinates for other agencies as some examples. The police 
department's single drone was purchased through the budget rather than via a grant. 
11. Fire Department 
Fire Chief John Vincent discussed potential measures regarding fire safety inspections for short-term rentals 
(STRs). He noted that an exemption in the Town Code currently allows STRs to operate without fire safety 
inspections and shared ideas for addressing this gap to help ensure fire safety compliance and protect 
occupants. Chief Vincent also provided information regarding anticipated El Niño winter weather conditions 
and the potential for upcoming winter storms.

15. Discussion, Review, and Possible Action Regarding Adopting Town of Palmer Lake Financial 
Management Policy by Resolution 36-2026 
Town Administrator Davidson presented a proposed financial management policy establishing formal 
operational guidelines for daily municipal finance, including grant monitoring, credit card accountability rules, 
and handling misuse or lost cards. Additionally, it introduces long-term financial controls by requiring 
dedicated reserve funds and establishing depreciation schedules for the town's aging vehicle fleet. 
 
In discussion of this item, the Board agreed to only release detailed legal invoices on a case by case basis due 
to cost concerns.

16. Discussion, Review, and Possible Action Regarding Adopting the Town of Palmer Lake 
Agenda Setting Policy by Resolution 37-2026 
The Board discussed Resolution 37-2026, which establishes a formal agenda-setting policy aimed at 
streamlining meeting preparation while avoiding Open Meetings Law (OML) violations. Under the proposed 
workflow, trustees submit topics to staff for a preliminary review, after which staff circulates the item via 
email so members can individually signal support, requiring three concurrences to add an item to the agenda. 
While staff will manage scheduling based on workload bandwidth, the board supported reviewing a public 
agenda request log, preserving on-the-spot meeting amendments, 
 
Dennis Stern moved to continue this motion. Roger Moseley seconded.  
Voting Yes: Michael Beeson, Tony Beltran, Roger Moseley, Dennis Stern, Elizabeth Harris  
Voting No: None 
Abstaining: None  
  
Motion Passed (5 - 0)
