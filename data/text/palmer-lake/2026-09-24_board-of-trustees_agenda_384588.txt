 Agenda  
This agenda is subject to revision 24 hours prior to commencement of the meeting. 
The Board welcomes public comment on Town-related matters. Speakers must sign in, be recognized by 
the mayor, and state their name and address for the record. Comments are limited to three (3) minutes

disability accommodation, contact the Town at 719-481-2953 at least 48 hours before the meeting.
