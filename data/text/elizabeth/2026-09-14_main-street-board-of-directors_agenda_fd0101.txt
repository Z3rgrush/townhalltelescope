This is a meeting of the Main Street Board of Directors held in public. We welcome you here and thank you for 
your time and concerns. When you are recognized, please stand and state your name and address to the 
Board. Your comments will be limited to 3 minutes. The Main Street Board of Directors may not respond to 
your comments during this meeting. Rather, they may take your comments and suggestions under 
advisement and your questions will be directed to the appropriate person or department for follow-up. 
Personal attacks against Board Members, Administrative Staff, or Employees will not be recognized. If a 
response from Staff is requested, the President will direct Staff to have a response at the next regularly 
scheduled Board meeting.
