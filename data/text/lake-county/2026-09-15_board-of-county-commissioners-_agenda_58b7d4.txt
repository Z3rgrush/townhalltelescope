From  Alana Medeiros, Public Health 
Subject Memorandum of Understanding Agreement with LCSD PASS Team 
 
Background 
Public Health has been asked to participate in the second year of Lake County School District’s 
Prevention and School Safety (PASS) Team. As a prevention organization, this ﬁts into our mission 
to support the community, our youth, and the school district. The PASS team meets quarterly to 
evaluate current data, discuss school climate and culture, and brainstorm initiatives to improve 
the wellbeing and safety of all students in Lake County. This team allows Public Health to build 
relationships with school staK and better coordinate existing initiatives for youth prevention 
eKorts.  
Topic for Presentation  
In order to participate in this team, we are being asked to sign this MOU so that our participation in 
the team is formalized and LCSD can meet grant requirements. The MOU also formalizes the data 
sharing element of the team.  
Budget 
This is a budgeted request.

Decision item
