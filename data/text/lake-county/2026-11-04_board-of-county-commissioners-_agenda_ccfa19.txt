From  Matt Hobbs, County Attorney 
Subject Resolution Renaming the Skate Park in Honor of Donny Llama 
 
Background 
This is request was generated by Commissioner Andy Lee.  The Board is asked to consider a 
resolution renaming the County-owned skate park in Leadville, currently designated the “Huck 
Finn Skate Park,” as the “Donny Llama Skate Park.” The “Huck Finn” name has no documented 
connection to Lake County or to the history of the park. 
The current skate park replaced an earlier, informal facility built from salvaged and deteriorating 
materials that became known locally as “Tetanus Park.” In 2007, Donny Llama founded “Blaze the 
Belt,” a longboard race on the Mineral Belt Trail, to raise funds to repair those ramps. Working with 
Partners for Lake County Recreation and the Lake County Recreation Department, Donny grew the 
event into a nationally recognized fundraiser. Those efforts were instrumental in securing the Tony 
Hawk Foundation grant that built the skate park the County has today. 
Donny passed away on November 14, 2024, in Lake Charles, Louisiana. He considered Leadville 
his adopted home and its community his family. 
Summary of Resolution 
Section 1 renames the park the “Donny Llama Skate Park.” Section 2 directs the County to update 
signage, maps, websites, and other official County records. Sections 3 and 4 provide for 
severability and make the resolution effective immediately upon adoption. 
The draft contains two alternatives for one WHEREAS clause and for Section 1. The Board should 
select one alternative, which will be used in both places: 
• Alternative A names him as “Donny Llama, born Donald Conner II,” and notes that he was 
known by the name he chose for himself rather than his birth name; or 
• Alternative B omits the birth name and refers to him only as Donny Llama, the name by 
which he was known and loved in the community. 
Legal Authority

Subject File no. 26-31 Hayden Meadows PUD 
 
Background 
The Planned Unit Development for Hayden Ranch (HR-PD) proposes the preservation and 
development of the Hayden Ranch in compliance with the conservation easement that 
encumbers this land.  The primary purpose of this project is to preserve the historic structures of 
Hayden Ranch and rehabilitate identiﬁed structures.  The intended result is to provide a heritage 
tourism site for visitors while preserving the unique historical and ecological character of the 
ranch. 
Topic for Presentation  
The Planning Commission made a favorable recommendation with six (6) conditions of approval 
at the public hearing October 26, 2026.  As a rezoning proposal, the Board of County 
Commissioners is authorized to make a ﬁnal decision on the application.

To  Board of County Commissioners 
From  Matt Hobbs, County Attorney 
Subject Resignation of Coroner Alexander Krieg and Appointment to Fill Vacancy 
 
Background 
On October 30, 2026, Lake County Coroner Alexander Krieg submitted his written resignation, 
effective upon transmission at 2:11 a.m. that day. The office of Coroner is now vacant. Mr. Krieg’s 
term would otherwise have expired in January 2027. 
The Board and Mr. Krieg have agreed on a joint statement announcing the resignation, which the 
Chair should read into the record: 
Lake County Coroner Alexander Krieg has submitted his resignation, effective 
October 30, 2026. The Board of County Commissioners thanks Mr. Krieg for his 
service to Lake County. The Board will appoint a successor to serve the remainder of 
the term, which ends in January 2027. Death investigation services in Lake County 
will continue without interruption. 
Authority to Fill the Vacancy 
• Appointment. Under C.R.S. § 1-12-205, a vacancy in any county office other than county 
commissioner is ﬁlled by appointment of the Board of County Commissioners. The 
appointee serves until the vacancy is ﬁlled by election; here, until the Coroner elected on 
November 3, 2026, takes office in January 2027. 
• Qualiﬁcations. Under C.R.S. § 30-10-601.5, the Coroner must be a citizen of the United 
States, a resident of Colorado and of Lake County, and have a high school diploma or 
equivalent (or a college degree). 
• Training. Under C.R.S. § 30-10-601.8, a new Coroner must complete a 40-hour training 
course at the ﬁrst opportunity after taking office and obtain certiﬁcation in basic medical-
legal death investigation within one year, unless an alternative is approved. 
• Taking office. The appointee must take the oath of office, and ﬁle any required bond, 
before assuming the duties of the office. The appointee may appoint a deputy under C.R.S. 
§ 30-10-602. 
The incoming coroner, Jeffery Edmonds has agreed to ﬁll the vacancy for the remainder of the 
term. Appointing the incoming Coroner provides continuity and a single transition for the office.
