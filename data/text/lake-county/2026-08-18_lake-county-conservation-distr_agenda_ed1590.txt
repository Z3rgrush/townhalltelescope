Dear Lake County Soil Conservation District Members 
 
We hope everyone is staying healthy during these last couple of months of unusual times. The Soil 
Conservation District has not been able to hold regular meetings due to the pandemic. We would like to 
give everyone an opportunity to buy into the new One-Pass Pasture Aeration Seeder the Soil 
Conservation District is purchasing. It is in the process of being manufactured right now.  
The One-Pass Pasture Aeration Seeder will be 6ft wide and be mounted by a category 1&2 three point 
hitch.  It will have 6 Coulters to open the ground a with the ability of adding weight to adjust the depth 
of the coulter. It will have a 12 volt seed spreader that can be adjusted for width of pass. The seed 
spreader will be powered by a 12 volt deep cycle battery in a protected box. No 12 volt power supply is 
required by the tractor but will be need to be charged on occasion. The 6ft drag shown in the article 
attached will not be provided, most members have their own drag to cover the opened ground and 
seed. An attached article for additional information is included in the description. 
Lake County Soil Conservation District has been able to provide $2000.00 of the $4250.00 needed  to 
purchase the One-Pass Pasture Aeration Seeder. The additional $2250.00 will be acquired by members 
that want to buy in. Right now there are seven members that have chosen to buy into this seeder. The 
Lake County Soil Conservation District would like to give any Lake County Soil Conservation District 
Member the opportunity to buy into this seeder.  As of now each joining member will be sending in 
$325.00 into the Lake County Soil Conservation District if more members should want to buy into this 
One-Pass Pasture Aeration Seeder they will need to send in their money as well. The $2250.00 will be 
divided up equally between the members joining into this opportunity.   Only members buying into this 
seeder will have the ability to use the seeder. Any leftover monies will be refunded back to the joining 
members. 
 
There will be some commonsense rules to be applied to the One-Pass Pasture Aeration Seeder.  
     There is only a small window of time for planting please be respectful of time when using this seeder. 
     Be respectful of the machine, misuse will result in you paying for repairs. 
     Seed will not be provided with this machine. Have your seed mix on hand when it is your turn. 
     Basic maintenance and storage will be performed by joining members.  
 
There are definitely some details to be worked out but with spring upon us we did not want to lose the 
season. We hope this letter finds you and if you are interested please reach out to Mary Smith 
(President of the Lake County Soil Conservation District and joining member) at 719-427-7748. 
     
 Lake County Soil Conservation District and Joining Members
