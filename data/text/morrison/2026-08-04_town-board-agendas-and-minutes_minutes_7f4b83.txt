1. Call to order.  
 
Mayor Wolfe called the Regular Town Board Meeting to order at 4:30 P.M. 
 
2. Roll Call. Minute Mark 00:08 
  
Mayor Chris Wolfe, Trustees David Wirtz, Adam Way, Angela Bernhardt, and Krista Nash were 
present. John Leonard was absent. A quorum was established.   
 
Trustee Way moved that the Board of Trustees enter into executive session  for the purpose 
of receiving legal advice on specific legal questions regarding the Town’s water infrastructure 
and planning, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(b) and Town Charter Section 3.4., and;  
to determine positions relative to matters that may be subject to negotiations, develop a 
strategy for negotiations, and/or instruct negotiators concerning a potential agreement for 
extraterritorial sewer service, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(e) and Town Charter Section 
3.4., with the Town Attorney, Town Manager, and Town Clerk. Trustee Bernhardt seconded. 
All present voted aye. The motion passed. 
Mayor Wolfe reopened the regular board meeting at 6:02 PM 
 
3. Amendments to the Agenda. N/A 
 
4. Public to Address the Board. Minute Mark 1:05 
 
David Killingsworth, 403 Bear Creek Ave. Killingsworth expressed concerns about adding 
additional businesses to Town without adequate parking and believes parking requirements 
should be established for new businesses. 
 
Reza Ardehali, 215 Bear Creek Ave. Ardehali agreed with Killingsworth and stated that parking 
should be required for new businesses or that the same requirements should apply to all 
businesses.  
 
Katie Gill, 231 Red Rocks Vista Dr. Gill. requested an update on the hearing for The Farm at the 
Fort. 
 
Krista Gaasvig, 102 Market St. Gaasvig addressed the parking situation in downtown Morrison 
and expressed that new businesses should not be permitted without adequate parking. 
 
Kathleen Dichter, 109 Spring St. Dichter addressed the resignation of the Town Manager.

6. General Business. Minute Mark 22:37 
 
a. Resolution No. __ Approving an Intergovernmental Agreement with Jefferson 
County Regarding the Conduct of the Town’s Participation in the November 3, 2026 
Coordinated General Election  
 
A motion was made by Trustee Way to approve the resolution regarding the Town’s 
participation into 2026 coordinated general election and the associated Intergovernmental 
Agreement with Jefferson County. Trustee Nash seconded. All present voted aye. The 
motion carried.  
 
b. Interim Town Manager Employment Agreement 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Way approve the Interim Town Manager Employment 
Agreement effective August 14, 2026. Trustee Bernhardt seconded. All present voted aye. 
The motion carried.  
 
c. Ordinance 565 - Repealing and Reenacting Title 8 of The Morrison Municipal Code 
Regarding Water and Sewer Regulations 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Wirtz to approve Ordinance 565 repealing and reenacting 
Title 8 of the Morrison Municipal Code regarding water and sewer regulations. Trustee 
Way seconded. All present voted aye. The motion passed. 
 
d. Term Sheet for Extraterritorial Sewer Service with Green Tree Metro District  
 
Trustee Bernhardt moved to approve the term sheet for Extraterritorial Sewer Service with 
Green Metro District, with exceptions, including establishing a final takedown date for all 
taps; removing the hard deadline to follow the timeline and product schedule and moving 
that deadline to December 2029; limiting the geography not only to their district language 
but also to Rooney Valley; and ensuring Green Tree is building infrastructure for future 
tie-ins in our area.  Trustee Nash seconded. All present voted aye, except Trustees Wirtz 
and Bernhardt, who voted nay. The motion passed. 
 
7. Consent Agenda. Minute Mark 37:51 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Bernhardt to approve the Consent Agenda for June 16, 
2026. Trustee Way seconded. All present voted aye. The motion carried. 
 
8. Future Considerations. Minute Mark 38:20 
 
Trustee Nash thanked Town Manager Nassau for her service. Trustee Nash asked for more 
direction from the community on the parking issues. 
 
Trustee Bernhardt thanked the local restaurants for providing food for their community picnic.
