Written Comment Period for Ballot Issues              


The Town of Morrison is currently accepting written comments from registered voters regarding the ballot issues appearing on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot.
Registered voters may submit written comments for or against either of the Town’s ballot issues. Comments must address a specific ballot issue and include the submitter’s name, signature, and address at which they are registered to vote.
Comments may be submitted using the Town’s written comment form (QR code above), on our website www.morrisonco.us/251/Elections or by submitting a separate written statement containing the required information.
Comments must be received by 12:00 p.m. (noon) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026
If you choose not to use the online comment form, written comments may be submitted to:
