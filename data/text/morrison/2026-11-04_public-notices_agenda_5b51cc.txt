NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARINGS 
TOWN OF MORRISON 
2027 BUDGET 
NOTICE is hereby given that a proposed budget has been 
submitted to the Board of Trustees of the Town of Morrison 
for the ensuing year of 2027; a copy of such proposed budget 
has been filed in the office of the Town of Morrison and 
available for public inspection at www.morrisonco.us. Such 
proposed budget will be considered at the Regular Meeting of 
the Board of Trustees on Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 
6:00 pm, and Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 6:00 pm at the 
Morrison Town Hall, 110 Stone Street.  
Any interested elector of the Town of Morrison may inspect 
the proposed budget and file or register any objections 
thereto at any time prior to the final adoption of the budget.
For more information please contact the Town Clerk at 
303-697-8749.
Courtney Christensen
Town Clerk 
Published this 29th day of October, 2026 on the Town Website
(www.morrisonco.us) and the Canyon Courier
