TOWN OF MORRISON  
NOTICE OF MUNICIPAL ELECTION 
 
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that Town of Morrison will conduct a coordinated election with 
Jefferson County on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. 
said election will be conducted as a mail ballot election.  
 
At said election, the registered electors of the Town of Morrison will vote on the following:

MUNICIPAL BALLOT MEASURES:  
BALLOT QUESTION 2D: 
 
SHALL THE TOWN OF MORRISON'S TAXES BE INCREASED BY $125,000 IN THE 
FIRST FULL FISCAL YEAR, COMMENCING JANUARY 1, 2027, AND BY WHATEVER 
AMOUNTS RAISED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, BY ELIMINATING AN EXISTING 
EXEMPTION FROM THE TOWN’S LODGING TAX AND THEREBY EXPANDING THE 
TAX TO APPLY TO ALL LODGING FACILITIES REGARDLESS OF THE NUMBER OF 
GUEST ROOMS, WITH THE REVENUES THEREFROM TO BE SPENT ON ANY 
LAWFUL MUNICIPAL PURPOSE; AND SHALL ALL REVENUES DERIVED FROM 
SUCH TAX AND ANY EARNINGS THEREON BE COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT 
AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE NOTWITHSTANDING ANY REVENUE 
OR EXPENDITURE LIMITATIONS CONTAINED IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE 
COLORADO CONSTITUTION? 
 
BALLOT QUESTION 2E: 
 
SHALL THE TOWN OF MORRISON’S TAXES BE INCREASED BY $500,000 IN THE 
FIRST FULL FISCAL YEAR, COMMENCING JANUARY 1, 2027, AND BY WHATEVER 
AMOUNTS ARE RAISED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, BY INCREASING THE RATE OF 
THE TOWN’S EXISTING LODGING TAX FROM 6% TO 8%, WITH THE REVENUES 
THEREFROM TO BE SPENT ON ANY LAWFUL MUNICIPAL PURPOSE; AND SHALL 
ALL REVENUES DERIVED FROM SUCH TAX AND ANY EARNINGS THEREON BE 
COLLECTED, RETAINED, AND SPENT AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE 
NOTWITHSTANDING ANY REVENUE OR EXPENDITURE LIMITATIONS CONTAINED 
IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION? 
 
BALLOT QUESTION 2F: 
 
SHALL SECTION 2.4 OF THE TOWN OF MORRISON HOME RULE CHARTER BE 
AMENDED TO READ AS FOLLOWS:  
 
SECTION 2.4. TERMS OF OFFICE 
Terms of office of Board members shall be four (4) years, staggered, and without term limits.  
 
 
Ballots will be mailed to registered voters beginning October 5, 2026. The last day to register to 
vote and request a ballot to be mailed is October 26, 2026. Voted ballots may be returned to a 
ballot drop-off location, a Voter Service and Polling Center, or by mail. Ballots must be received 
by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day, November 3, 2026.
