TOWN OF MORRISON BOARD OF TRUSTEES 
MORRISON TOWN HALL, 110 STONE STREET 
REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES 
TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2026 
6:00 P.M. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Call to order.  
 
Mayor Wolfe called the Regular Town Board Meeting to order at 6:00 P.M. 
 
2. Roll Call. Minute Mark 00:14 
  
Mayor Chris Wolfe, Trustees David Wirtz, Adam Way, Angela Bernhardt, Krista Nash, and John 
Leonard were present. A quorum was established.   
 
3. Trustee Way moved that the board enter into executive session to determine 
positions relative to matters that may be subject to negotiations, develop a strategy for 
negotiations, and/or instruct negotiators concerning the east planning area pursuant to 
C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(e) and Town Charter Section 3.4. Trustee Leonard seconded. All 
present voted aye. The motion carried. 
 
4. Amendments to the Agenda. N/A 
 
5. Public to Address the Board. Minute Mark 00:15 
 
Ross Bradley, 16371 W. Morrison Rd. Bradley reviewed the proposals submitted by Bear Creek 
and THK for the East Planning Area. 
 
Katie Gill, 231 Red Rocks Vista Dr. Gill thanked the Utility Director for his response to water 
emergencies over the weekend and commented on the proposals for the East Planning Area. 
 
Andra Slavsky, 181 Red Rocks Vista Lane. Slavsky addressed the proposed employee pay 
structure and the Town Manager application process. 
 
6. Departmental Reports. Minute Mark 10:44 
 
a. Interstate Parking 
b. Accounting 
c. Town Manager 
i. 2027 Personnel Budget Considerations 
d. Town Attorney 
 
7. General Business. Minute Mark 32:04 
 
a. Ord. 567 - Abatement of a Nuisance  
 
A motion was made by Trustee Bernhardt to adopt Ordinance No. 567, an ordinance 
amending Title 5 of the Morrison Municipal Code regarding the abatement of a nuisance. 
Trustee Way seconded. All present voted aye. The motion carried.

b. Consideration of Agreements related to the East Planning Area 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Bernhardt to approve the pre-development agreement 
with Red Rocks Gateway LLC related to the East Planning Area. Trustee Nash 
seconded. All present voted aye, except Trustee Leonard who voted nay. The motion 
carried. 
 
8. Consent Agenda. Minute Mark 1:03:57 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Wirtz to approve the Consent Agenda for October 6, 2026. 
Trustee Nash seconded. All present voted aye. The motion carried. 
 
9. Future Considerations. Minute Mark 1:04:10 
 
Trustee Leonard would like the community to take better care of their trash.  
 
Trustee Bernhardt asked the board to make a consensus to begin executive sessions earlier than 
6pm for consistency sake. 
 
Trustee Wirtz inquired about an alarm near Hwy 74. 
 
10. Executive Session. Minute Mark 1:11:38 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Way to enter into executive session to consider personnel 
matters, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(f) concerning the Interim Town Manager and not 
involving: any specific employees who have requested discussion of the matter in open 
session; any member of this body or any elected official; the appointment of any person to 
fill an office of this body or of an elected official; or personnel policies that do not require 
the discussion of matters personal to particular employees with the Town Attorney, Town 
Manager, and Town Clerk. Trustee Nash seconded. All present voted aye. The motion 
carried. 
 
11. ADJOURNMENT 
 
 Mayor adjourned the regular meeting at 8:25 P.M.
