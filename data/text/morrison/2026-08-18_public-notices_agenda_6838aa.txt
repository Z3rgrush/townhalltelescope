TOWN OF MORRISON 
NOTICE OF A PUBLIC HEARING 
 
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN pursuant to Title 10 of the Morrison Municipal Code that the Board of Trustees 
of the Town of Morrison will hold a public hearing on Tuesday, August 18, 2026, at 6:00 P.M. at Morrison 
Town Hall, 110 Stone Street, Morrison, Colorado 80465 to consider an application for initial zoning to the 
Residential Estate (RE) and Mixed-Use Neighborhood (MX-N) zoning districts for property located in 
unincorporated Jefferson County, Colorado, known as the Farm at the Fort and more particularly described in 
Resolution No. 2026-03, related to annexation and initial zoning applications filed by Morrison 
Redevelopment, LP and Revesco Properties. 
 
A copy of the application is available for inspection at th e Town Clerk's office at 110 Stone St., Morrison, CO 
80465 during normal business hours and is posted on the Town Website . Interested persons are encouraged to 
attend the public hearing and provide public comment. 
 
GIVEN THIS 23rd DAY OF JULY, 2026 
Courtney Christensen, Town Clerk
