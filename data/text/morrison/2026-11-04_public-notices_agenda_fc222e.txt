NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
TOWN OF MORRISON 
2027 CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN 
NOTICE is hereby given that a proposed Capital 
Improvement Plan has been submitted to the Board of 
Trustees of the Town of Morrison for the ensuing year of 
2026; a copy of such proposed Capital Improvement Plan has 
been filed in the office of the Town of Morrison and available 
for public inspection at www.morrisonco.us. Such proposed 
Capital Improvement Plan will be considered at the Regular 
Meeting of the Board of Trustees on Wednesday, November 
4, 2026 at 6:00 pm, and Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 6:00 
pm at the Morrison Town Hall, 110 Stone Street. 
Any interested elector of the Town of Morrison may inspect 
the proposed Capital Improvement Plan and file or register 
any objections thereto at any time prior to the final adoption 
of the Capital Improvement Plan.
For more information please contact the Town Clerk at 
303-697-8749.
Courtney Christensen
Town Clerk 
Published this 29th day of October, 2026 on the Town Website
(www.morrisonco.us) and the Canyon Courier
