board of trustees
ORDINANCE NO. 562
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 3-6-2 AND 3-6-4 OF THE MORRISON MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING A LODGING TAX EXEMPTION
WHEREAS, the Town of Morrison is a Colorado home rule municipality operating under a Home Rule Charter approved by the electorate pursuant to Article XX of the Colorado Constitution and governed by its elected Board of Trustees;
WHEREAS, the Board has authority pursuant to the Home Rule Charter and C.R.S. § 31-16-101, et seq. to adopt and enforce all ordinances; 
WHEREAS, at the regular municipal election held on April 3, 2012, the Board of Trustees submitted a ballot issue to the registered electors of the Town regarding the creation of a lodging tax in the Town exempting lodging facilities of less than eight guest rooms;
WHEREAS, the voters approved the ballot issue thereby imposing a 6% tax on lodging and exempting lodging facilities of less than eight guest rooms, commencing on January 1, 2013, with the revenues to be spent on any lawful purpose;
WHEREAS, on September 17, 2013, the Board passed an ordinance exempting lodging facilities of less than 11 units from the lodging tax;
WHEREAS, aligning the guest room exemption with the adopted ballot language conforms with Colorado Supreme Court decision in MetroPCS California, LLC v. City of Lakewood, 576 P.3d 139 (Colo. 2025) and the Colorado's Taxpayer Bill of Rights, Colo. Const. art. X, § 20 ("TABOR") because voter approval was previously obtained to create a lodging tax that exempted guest rooms of fewer than eight rooms, therefore, this ordinance does not enact a new taxes, tax rate increase, expansion of a tax or tax policy change; and
WHEREAS, the Town desires to amend the Morrison Municipal Code to make the lodging tax exemption consistent with the voter approved ballot measure and to simplify the collection and administration of the lodging tax. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Board of Trustees of the Town of Morrison, Colorado:
Section 1.Section 3-6-2 of the Morrison Municipal Code is hereby amended as follows:
3-6-2:DEFINITIONS:
***
LODGING PROVIDER:  Any person in an establishment with eight (8) or more more than ten (10) guestrooms, furnishing lodging services or such provider's authorized agent.
LODGING SERVICES: The providing of hotel rooms, motel rooms, lodging rooms, motor hotel rooms, guesthouse rooms or other similar accommodations, in an establishment with eight (8) or more guestrooms, except meeting rooms, that are rented to persons for a period of less than one month or thirty (30) consecutive days, but shall not include rentals under a written agreement for occupancy for a period of at least one month or thirty (30) days.
Section 2.Section 3-6-4(D) of the Morrison Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
3-6-4:EXEMPTIONS:
***
D.Lodging services provided in an establishment with fewer than eight (8) eleven (11) guestrooms.
Section 3.Severability.  If any article, section, paragraph, sentence, clause or phrase of this Ordinance, or the standards adopted herein is held to be unconstitutional or invalid for any reason, such decision will not affect the validity or constitutionality of the remaining portions of this Ordinance.  The Board of Trustees hereby declares that it would have passed this Ordinance and each part hereof irrespective of the fact that any one part or parts are declared unconstitutional or invalid.
Section 4.Effective Date.  This Ordinance shall take effect on October 1, 2026.
