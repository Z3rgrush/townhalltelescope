5. b Public Hearing - LURC 2026-16 North lands Filing No. 1 Replat C Final Plat and Site 
Development Plan (UCHealth Hospital) 
Property Location: NW corner of the 1-25 & CO 7 Interchange 
Owner/ Applicant: UCHealth 
Chair Mickelsen opened the public hearing at 7:07 p.m. 
Branden Roe, Planning Manager, summarized the staff report and asked that the staff report be made part of 
the record. 
The applicant, Ryan Rohman, President of UCHealth Longs Peak and Broomfield Hospital, and Mike Chihoski 
with UCHealth spoke regarding the request. 
Also spoke: Jon Spencer, Civil Engineer 
Members of the public requested to speak: None 
Commissioners' comments and questions followed. 
Chair Mickelsen closed the public hearing at 7:54 p.m. 
Commissioner Peterman moved to recommend approval of LURC 2026-16 Northlands Filing No.1 Replat C 
(UCHealth Hospital). 
Commissioner Dong seconded the motion. 
Commissioner Jackson requested that the motion be amended to include the following condition: 
1. The applicant shall work with staff to revise the special exception for the signage allowance 
to bring it more in line with the actual needs of the development. 
Commissioner Peterman and Commissioner Dong had no concerns with the proposed condition to be added 
to the motion. Commissioner Peterman restated his motion with the proposed condition and Commissioner 
Dong seconded the amended motion. 
The motion passed by a vote of 6 to 0.
