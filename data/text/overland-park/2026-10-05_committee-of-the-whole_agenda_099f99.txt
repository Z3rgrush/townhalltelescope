STAFF REPORT
Committee of the Whole - October 5, 2026
ITEM TITLE:
Dedicated Sales Tax for Parks and Recreation.
(continued from September 28, 2026 Committee-of-the-Whole Meeting)
POLICY ISSUE:
To place a dedicated sales tax for Parks and Recreation on the ballot, the City Council must approve a 
Sales Tax Election Ordinance.
STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends that the Committee of the Whole direct staff to prepare ballot language, and a Sales 
Tax Election Ordinance for COW review on November 2, 2026.
PROPOSED MOTION:
I move to direct staff to prepare ballot language for the purpose of establishing a dedicated [1/8  or  1/4  
or  3/8 ]th cent sales tax for Parks and Recreation and to prepare a corresponding ordinance 
authorizing such sales tax election to be brought back to the Committee of the Whole on November 2, 
2026.
BACKGROUND:
At the September 28, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting, the final draft of Playbook OP was 
presented and recommended for approval; the City Council officially approved it later that evening.
 
During that same meeting on September 28, 2026, the Committee of the Whole evaluated the concept 
of establishing a dedicated Parks and Recreation sales tax. This strategy, along with broader funding 
strategies and sources, was initially introduced at the July 20, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting 
and revisited at the August 3 Committee of the Whole meeting.
 
Following discussion at the September 28, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting, the Committee 
requested that the item return to the Committee of the Whole on October 5, 2026, for additional 
discussion.
STRATEGIC GOALS:
