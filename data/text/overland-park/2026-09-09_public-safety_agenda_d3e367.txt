ITEM TITLE:
2027 Annual Calendar of Meetings - Public Safety Committee Meetings.  
POLICY ISSUE:
Resolution No. 5011 establishing Council and Committee Procedures, calls for the development of an 
annual calendar of meetings. Each Goal Area Committee and Board, Commission and Advisory 
Committee reviews and recommends an annual calendar of its meetings.  A comprehensive Annual 
Calendar will be reviewed by the Finance, Administration and Economic Development Committee 
which recommends a final calendar to the City Council for approval.
STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
That the Public Safety Committee approve the proposed 2027 meeting dates as presented. 
 
PROPOSED MOTION:
I move that the Public Safety Committee approve the 2027 meeting dates as presented. 
 
BACKGROUND:
Under the Kansas Open Meetings Act ("KOMA"), the City must provide notice of meetings to anyone 
who requests it. A single notice is sufficient for regularly scheduled meetings, but there is an additional 
obligation to notify of any special meetings. 
As outlined in Section 2.01 of the City Council and Committee Procedures policy, the Governing Body 
must establish an Annual Call and Notice. This notice informs the public about the date, time, and 
location of all Regular Council Meetings and is presented as an annual calendar. Individuals who have 
requested to receive this notice will receive it, and it will also be published on the City's website under 
the City Calendar + Events section.
Attached is a list of holidays and religious observances during which no meetings are scheduled.
Also attached is a recommended calendar for 2027 meetings of the Public Safety Committee.
Each Goal Area Committee and the City's Boards, Commissions, and Advisory Committees will review 
and recommend a schedule of meetings for 2027. 
In October, the Finance, Administration, and Economic Development Committee will review a 
comprehensive annual calendar and recommend its approval to the City Council for consideration on 
November 2, 2026.
