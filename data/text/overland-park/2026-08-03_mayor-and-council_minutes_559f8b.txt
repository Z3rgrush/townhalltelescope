CITY MANAGER LORI CURTIS LUTHER 
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
City Manager Lori Curtis Luther stated there are upcoming changes at the Matt Ross Community Center 
for the pool patrons. Stongate and Young pools will conclude their summer session Friday, August 7, 
2026. Tomahawk Ridge Aquatic Center will remain open Monday through Friday, from 4:00 p.m. to 7:30 
p.m. and on weekends from noon to 7:30 through Labor Day weekend. Most of the City's lifeguards are 
high school and college students who will return to school next week. The City would like to publicly thank 
them for their service and for keeping everyone safe this summer. The City is continuing work on the slide 
replacement and overall maintenance at Matt Ross Community Center. The Community Center members 
will be able to use Young's pool to swim laps and for fitness classes through Labor Day weekend, but the 
pool will not be open to the general public. Maintenance on indoor pools is expected to be complete on 
August 8, 2026.  No vote required.
