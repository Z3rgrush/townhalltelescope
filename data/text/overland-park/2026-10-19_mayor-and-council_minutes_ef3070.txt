COUNCIL PRESIDENT MELISSA CHEATHAM 
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
Council President Cheatham noted that Saturday was the Recycling Extravaganza, where 1,000 residents 
dropped off recyclable and reusable items keeping them out of the City's landfill. The City collected more 
than 3 full trucks of electronics and more than 550 pounds of fabric and sewing goods. She thanked the 
more than 200 people who made the event possible, including City staff, councilmembers, volunteers, 
vendors, and the host, Shamrock Trading Corporation. No vote required.
