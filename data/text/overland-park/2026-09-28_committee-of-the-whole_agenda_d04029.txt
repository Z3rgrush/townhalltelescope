STAFF REPORT
Committee of the Whole - September 28, 2026
ITEM TITLE:
Playbook OP Adoption 
POLICY ISSUE:
Adoption of Playbook OP, the long-range plan for the Parks and Recreation Department, requires 
approval by the City Council. 
STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends adoption of Playbook OP. 
PROPOSED MOTION:
I move to recommend City Council adoption of Playbook OP. 
BACKGROUND:
The draft of Playbook OP was presented to the Committee of the Whole on  February 16, 2026, by the 
consultant, MIG.  The Plan was unanimously recommended for approval to the Committee of the Whole 
by the Playbook OP Steering Committee and the Parks and Recreation Advisory Council on February 
3, 2026.
 
At the April 6, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting, the Committee provided direction on the process 
for further plan consideration. The direction the Committee provided was to incorporate Category 1 and 
2 revisions and to bring back, for further review,  the topics of Universal Design, Aquatics, and Trail and 
Sidewalk infrastructure near planned parks.
 
The topic of universal design was discussed at the May 4, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting.  
 
The topic of aquatics was discussed at the July 20, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting.  Discussion 
of a possible dedicated Parks and Recreation Sales Tax was also introduced at this meeting.
 
The topic of trails and sidewalks was discussed at the August 3, 2026 Committee of the Whole 
meeting.
 
Following the August 3, 2026 meeting, MIG and staff worked to incorporate the revisions discussed 
since April 6, 2026 into the plan for final consideration.
 
The Playbook OP Final Plan is presented for adoption.
STRATEGIC GOALS:
This item addresses the following Strategic Goals adopted by the City Council:
☐ Create and Sustain Safe and Healthy Neighborhoods
☒ Create and Sustain a High Quality of Life

Committee of the Whole - September 28, 2026
ITEM TITLE:
Dedicated Sales Tax for Parks and Recreation.
POLICY ISSUE:
To place a dedicated sales tax for Parks and Recreation on the ballot, the City Council must approve a 
Sales Tax Election Ordinance
STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends that the Committee of the Whole proceed under one of two options:
 
Option 1: Provide direction on sales tax and rate
 
Direct staff to prepare ballot language, instructing staff to prepare a Sales Tax Election Ordinance for 
COW review on November 2, 2026.
 
Option 2: Continue discussion
 
Hold a COW meeting on October 5 for further discussion. The additional information needed is ____.
PROPOSED MOTION:
If the Committee of the Whole is ready to provide direction on proceed with a sales tax and rate the 
following motion is recommended: 
 
I move to direct staff to prepare ballot language for the purpose of establishing a dedicated [1/8 or 
1/4 or 3/8 ]th cent sales tax for Parks and Recreation and a corresponding ordinance authorizing 
such sales tax election to be brought back to the COW on November 2, 2026.
BACKGROUND:
Playbook OP Process Background
 
The final draft of Playbook OP was presented to the Committee of the Whole on February 16, 2026, by 
the consultant, MIG. The Plan was unanimously recommended for approval to the Committee of the 
Whole by the Playbook OP Steering Committee and the Parks and Recreation Advisory Council on 
February 3, 2026. 
 
At the April 6, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting, the Committee provided direction on the process 
for further plan consideration. The direction the Committee provided was to incorporate Category 1 and 
2 revisions and to bring back, for further review, the topics of Universal Design, Aquatics, and Trail and 
Sidewalk infrastructure near planned parks. 
 
The topic of universal design was discussed at the May 4, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting. 
 
The topic of aquatics was discussed at the July 20, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting.

Identified Deficiency
 
As identified by Playbook OP, the City faces a significant capital and maintenance funding gap for 
Parks and Recreation. The total deficit identified over the next 10 years is estimated at $248 million.
 
Potential Funding Strategies and Recommendation
 
Staff evaluated the potential funding strategies identified in Playbook OP: 
 
Park impact fees and/or land dedication programs. 
Expanded grant opportunities and private contributions. 
Facility and programming fee adjustments / partnerships / arts percent contributions. 
Mill Levy Increase: 1 mill generates approximately $5.8 million annually. 
Dedicated Sales Tax: 1/8 cent generates approximately $8.8 million annually. 
 
While other funding strategies will continue to be evaluated, staff recommends pursuing a dedicated 
sales tax on the March 2, 2027 mail-in ballot. It will provide a significant revenue stream for the next 10 
years that can be budgeted to help offset the identified deficiency.
