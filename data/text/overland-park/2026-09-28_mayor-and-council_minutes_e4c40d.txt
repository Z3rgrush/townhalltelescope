COUNCIL PRESIDENT MELISSA CHEATHAM 
 
 Report from the Committee-of-the-Whole Meeting Held September 28, 2026 - Playbook OP Adoption. 
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
Council President Melissa Cheatham noted the Committee of the Whole discussed the PlaybookOP final 
plan. The version presented and discussed included revisions incorporated after direction over the course 
of four Committee-of-the-Whole meetings spanning from April through August 2026, which were 
incorporated and the plan was recommended for adoption by the Committee of the Whole. The Committee 
of the Whole discussed a potential dedicated sales tax to put to voters in March 2027, which would fund 
parks and recreation. They did not reach a resolution on the sales tax, but agreed to discuss further at the 
first October 2026 Committee-of-the-Whole meeting. 
  
Adopted Playbook OP, the long-range plan for the Parks and Recreation Department as recommended by 
the Committee of the Whole.  Moved by Grummert, seconded by Newlin.  Approved 9 – Denied 3 – 
Abstained 0.
