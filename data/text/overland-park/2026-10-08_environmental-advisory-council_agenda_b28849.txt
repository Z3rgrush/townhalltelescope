ADJOURNMENT   
  
Environmental Advisory Council Purpose and Mission 
 
1. Study environmental issues as they pertain to the City of Overland Park, as well as other cities' 
experiences, successes, and failures in dealing with these issues. 
2. Advise the City Council and the Community Development Committee on issues of concern to the 
Overland Park community and suggest action options. 
3. Support and promote existing and new City programs in areas of solid waste management and 
environmental awareness, including, without being limited to, specific city efforts such as Household 
Hazardous Waste Day, and County and Metro-wide environmental efforts. 
4. Develop additional programs, educational programs and information resources, at the City level and in 
cooperation with County level efforts, that are beneficial to individuals and businesses in our community 
with respect to environmental issues.
