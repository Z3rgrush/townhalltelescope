COUNCIL PRESIDENT MELISSA CHEATHAM 
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
Council President Melissa Cheatham stated round two of the property tax rebate program opens 
tomorrow, September 15, 2026. She encouraged those who qualify based on household income to apply 
before October 15, 2026. During round one, 341 households qualified with an average rebate of $421 and 
more than $100,000 in rebate funds remain. The recent trip to Overland Park's sister city, Bietigheim -
Bissingen, Germany, was attended by Mayor Skoog and some members of the City Council, as well as 
staff spent time strengthening this long-standing partnership through shared learning and friendship. The 
visit provided an opportunity for discussing shared challenges and learning from solutions the City may 
wish to emulate, such as the way a well-designed City Hall and public art can anchor community gathering 
spaces, and how authentic, cultural, and civic identity can drive economic development. The trip was a 
meaningful and valuable experience. Continuing this partnership reminds the City that more connects than 
divides the people both at home and abroad, and the City is grateful for the hospitality and friendship 
shown to Overland Park by friends in Bietigheim-Bissingen, Germany. No vote required.

Non-Agenda Announcement: 
City Manager Lori Curtis Luther stated earlier this month, they shared that the Public Works department 
received notification of their upcoming APWA reaccreditation. She highlighted that the Police Department 
also received a reaccreditation notice. ACLEA Accreditation is a voluntary professional credentialing 
program for Public Safety agencies that proves that they meet high standards for quality, safety, and 
efficiency. She congratulated Police Chief Doreen Jokerst and her team for this honor. The City's 
Communication Department received a first place Savvy award in the branding category for their work on 
the City's 2025 branding update from the City-County Communications and Marketing Association. One of 
the judges stated, the brand identity so effectively captures the strength and character of its community. 
The decision to leverage in-house design talent proved to be an exceptional one. She stated the designer 
certainly deserves well-earned recognition. Communications staff executed the entire project in-house with 
guidance from a team of residents familiar with branding and graphic design. She congratulated the 
communications team and the City for this recognition.
