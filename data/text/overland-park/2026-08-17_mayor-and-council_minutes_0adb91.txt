COUNCIL PRESIDENT MELISSA CHEATHAM 
 
 Report from the Committee-of-the-Whole Meeting Held August 10, 2026 - Relating to the Employment 
Agreement between the City of Overland Park and City Manager Lori Curtis Luther.  
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
Council President Cheatham provided a report from the August 10, 2026, Committee- of-the-Whole 
meeting, which was held to conduct the City Manager's annual performance review. Following the review, 
the Committee-of-the-Whole unanimously recommended that the Council direct the City Attorney to 
prepare the necessary amendment to the Employment Agreement between the City of Overland Park and 
Lori Curtis Luther to provide for a 2.5 percent base salary increase for a cost-of-living adjustment, a 2.4 
percent base salary increase for a merit adjustment, a severance provision, including a lump sum payment 
equal to 18 months of the City's portion of benefit premiums, including COBRA, a performance incentive 
payment of $30,000 to be distributed as a lump sum base salary increase, deferred compensation, or 
other compensation, and installments allocated upon approval by the City Manager and Mayor; and that 
the Mayor be authorized to execute the amendment to the agreement. Moved by Cheatham, seconded by 
Heley.  Approved 12 – Denied 0 – Abstained 0.  
 
CITY MANAGER LORI CURTIS LUTHER 
 
 ACTION AND VOTE: 
Deputy City Manager Kristy Stallings shared that the American Public Works Association evaluation team 
recommended full reaccreditation for the City's Public Works Department. Last week, Director of Public 
Works Lorraine Basalo and her team received preliminary notice of this reaccreditation. This was a year of

hard work for Mrs. Basalo and her entire team. It took an immense amount of work to complete the 
reaccreditation process, in addition to all of their other responsibilities. The American Public Works  
Association specifically recognized Overland Park's snow and ice removal practices as an industry model. 
One reviewer noted, "The snow and ice control policy and practice manual is one of the best examples of 
coordinating planning I have seen. This document shows that Overland Park understands the effort 
necessary to provide exceptional service to residents during snow events." Mrs. Stallings recognized this 
as the 20th year of APWA accreditation for the Public Works Department, which certainly highlights the 
entire Public Works team's long-standing commitment to high standards and continuous improvement. 
She congratulated and thanked Mrs. Basalo and her entire team for this accomplishment.  No vote 
required.
