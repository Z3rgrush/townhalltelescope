CALL TO ORDER & ROLL CALL 
A regular meeting of the Rifle GRIT Advisory Board began at approximately 12:01 p.m, October 13, 2026, at 
Rifle City Hall.

Representative Roberts. Board Members Young and Mackley were absent. A quorum was present.

REGULAR AGENDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT – Karah Brubaker 
Façade Grant Funding 
Staff reported that the façade grant program began with a $40,000 allocation and that $21,956.25 had 
previously been awarded, leaving $18,043.75. The two pending applications, from Pro Max and The CoWork 
would exceed the remaining balance by $1,329.88. Members discussed using savings in the City 
Beautification line, including funds left after the misters came in under budget, to cover the shortfall. The 
formal recommendation and vote are recorded under Regular Agenda Actions below. 
Downtown Wayfinding 
The selection committee received 10 proposals, narrowed the field to three finalists, and selected Clarity 
Design for a $40,000 contract. City approval and a kickoff are pending; Board members will be key 
stakeholders. Discussion covered the sign family, digital wayfinding, durable and phased installation, 
construction bid documents, and public outreach. Members asked staff to consider bilingual signage and 
community input at local events. About $30,000 is budgeted for continued work in 2027; installation timing will 
depend on funding and priorities. No further vote was taken. 
Misters and Shade Structure 
Misters were delivered for expected installation in April 2027. Members discussed a shade structure with 
potential GRIT–DDA cost sharing; GRIT included funds in its 2027 budget, and a DDA contribution may be 
possible. No allocation was decided pending design. 
GRIT Stickers and Merchandise 
GRIT stickers are selling well at participating businesses at $2 each; members discussed a higher price for a 
future edition. Staff will research magnet costs following public interest in them.

EVENTS UPDATE – Delaney Passmore 
Delaney Passmore plans to leave after Hometown Holidays, her position is posted; she is documenting 
procedures and hopes to train a successor. 
Third Thursday and Oktoberfest 
The final Third Thursday event of the season is planned as a Halloween event, with heaters from the Ute 
Theater, a DJ, costume contest, and vendors. Staff requested help at the GRIT booth. Oktoberfest reached 
approximately 380 attendees despite rain; staff will seek public and downtown business feedback about the 
events. 
Hometown Holidays 
“Think Snow” is the parade theme. Planned activities include the middle school craft fair, pet photos, 
hayrides, tree lighting, school choirs, bonfires, and parade. Staff is seeking a Grinch performer and 
volunteers and improving sound and lineup logistics. Members discussed bingo wait times and a downtown 
photo scavenger hunt for ornaments, with prize pickup at a central location. Staff is exploring reindeer options 
after a quote of approximately $9,500. 
Barks, Brews and Barbecue 
Members discussed a simpler, family-oriented summer kickoff with local barbecue vendors or food trucks, 
music, and activities such as cornhole. May 22 was considered as a possible date. Discussion leaned toward 
Centennial Park over the movie theater, though parking remained a concern. Members also discussed 
discontinuing the competition, possible beer sales, and offering Journey Home an opportunity to participate if 
beer sales are included. No final date, location, or format was approved. Members emphasized maintaining 
relationships with past vendors.

The City of Rifle is seeking funding through the Department of Local Affairs Energy and Mineral Impact

investments are intended to make travel along the corridor more accessible and dependable for the 
community.The City is seeking DOLA EIAF Traditional Grant funding to advance the project. GRIT’s support 
would reflect the Board’s commitment to a connected, accessible community and recognize the role this

Discussion and Possible Action Regarding Amendments to the 2027 GRIT Budget 
  
Presenter: 
Karah Brubaker, Economic Opportunity Specialist II  
  
Item Description: 
Discussion and possible action regarding proposed amendments to the 2027 GRIT budget, including the GRIT 
Manager cost of $22,840, the Event Coordinator cost of $9,789, and a proposed reallocation of $25,000 
budgeted for Blues, Brews & BBQ to support a participatory budgeting pilot. 
  
Recommended Action: 
Move to recommend approval to Rifle City Council of the proposed amendments to the 2027 GRIT budget as 
presented. 
  
Fiscal Impact: 
The proposed 2027 GRIT budget includes $22,840 for the GRIT Manager payment, supporting staff time to 
administer the GRIT program, coordinate projects, and manage related board and budget work. It also includes 
a $9,789 transfer to Parks and Recreation to cover the Event Coordinator’s staff costs for supporting GRIT 
events. The proposed amendment would move $25,000 from the Blues, Brews & BBQ allocation to a 
participatory budgeting program. This would shift the use of those funds within the GRIT budget without 
increasing the total budget. 
  
Operational Impact: 
The GRIT Manager will administer GRIT’s program and project work; the Parks and Recreation transfer will 
support the Event Coordinator’s work on GRIT events. Reallocating $25,000 from Blues, Brews & BBQ would 
shift GRIT’s support from that event to a participatory budgeting program, giving residents a role in choosing a 
community improvement project. 
  
Prior Board Motions: 
N/A 
  
Background Information: 
The proposed 2027 GRIT budget includes a $22,840 payment for the GRIT Manager’s work administering the 
program, supporting the board, and coordinating GRIT projects and activities. It also includes a $9,789 transfer 
to Parks and Recreation for the Event Coordinator’s staff time supporting GRIT events. The coordinator is 
housed in Parks and Recreation, so the transfer would cover the portion of that employee’s work that supports 
GRIT. 
 
The proposed budget amendments are being considered after voters did not approve the 2A lodging tax 
increase. A participatory budgeting pilot had previously been discussed as a possible use of additional Visitor 
Page 15 of 22
 
 
Improvement Fund revenue. The current proposal would instead use $25,000 already allocated to Blues, Brews 
& BBQ to fund a smaller pilot within the 2027 GRIT budget. Through participatory budgeting, residents would 
help identify and select a community improvement project for GRIT to support. The program would give 
residents a direct role in setting a funding priority and help demonstrate how lodging tax revenue supports 
projects and activities in Rifle. 
  
Executive Summary: 
The 2027 GRIT budget supports the people who help turn the board’s priorities into visible work in Rifle. The 
proposed $22,840 GRIT Manager payment supports the staff time needed to coordinate GRIT’s projects, 
events, board work, and budget. The proposed $9,789 transfer to Parks and Recreation covers the Event 
Coordinator’s time supporting GRIT events. Although the coordinator works in Parks and Recreation, this 
transfer reflects the work they do for GRIT. 
Voters did not approve the 2A lodging tax increase, so the additional funding GRIT had hoped to use for 
community improvements will not be available. GRIT had previously discussed a $50,000 participatory 
budgeting pilot if the increase passed.  
 
The current proposal is to use $25,000 already allocated to Blues, Brews & BBQ to start a smaller pilot. 
Residents would share ideas and vote on a community improvement project for GRIT to support. This gives 
people a direct voice in how some GRIT funds are used and creates a visible example of how lodging tax 
revenue benefits Rifle. The proposed change involves a tradeoff: GRIT would move its $25,000 allocation from 
Blues, Brews & BBQ to the participatory budgeting pilot. GRIT is being asked to approve these budget 
amendments.

ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT 
Façade Grant Program 
At the November 4 City Council meeting, Council approved the two pending façade grant awards. The awards 
support ProMex Bakery and Rifle CoWork. The GRIT Commercial Façade Grant Program funds have been 
exhausted for 2026.  
 
Downtown Wayfinding 
The City held an brief pre-kickoff meeting with Clarity for the Downtown Wayfinding and Signage Plan. Staff 
distributed project information at Trunk or Treat and updated the project section of the GRIT website so 
residents can follow the work and learn how to participate. 
Kristin with Clarity will be in Rifle for Hometown Holidays to introduce the project, help the public 
understand the planning process, and begin gathering feedback. The project includes an assessment of 
existing signs, public and stakeholder engagement, a wayfinding strategy, sign designs and locations, 
preliminary costs, and an implementation roadmap. GRIT will be engaged at key project milestones. 
 
Downtown Projects 
Three quotes will be obtained for the architectural design of the Third Street shade structure, in accordance 
with the City’s procurement code. The DDA will fund the design. Once it is complete, construction bids will be 
solicited separately, with construction funding included in the 2027 budget. 
 
Third Thursdays Survey Feedback 
Survey responses show strong support for Third Thursdays. Participants said they enjoy the events and do 
not want Railroad Avenue closed during them. They also identified more seating and additional shade as 
priorities for improving the event experience and having themes for every Third Thursday event. Staff will 
send a post-Third Thursdays survey to Third Street businesses to gather their feedback and ideas for future 
events.
