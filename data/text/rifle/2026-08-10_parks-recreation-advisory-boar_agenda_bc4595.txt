facilities, programs, and event information they need. Effective marketing supports the Parks and 
Recreation Departments mission by:
 • Increasing awareness of departments parks, programs, facilities, and services.
 • Encouraging participation in recreation programs and events.
 • Building trust through timely, accurate, and transparent communication.
 • Strengthening Rifle’s identity as a premier recreational community on the Western Slope.

Branding Identity
A strong brand identity helps residents immediately recognize Rifle Parks and Recreation and  
reinforces the quality, professionalism, and consistency of the services we provide. Every social 
media post, advertisement, event flyer, sign, and digital communication contributes to the public’s 
perception of the department.
Over the past several years, we have worked to establish a consistent visual identity by using unified 
colors, logos, typography, photography, and messaging across all communication platforms. This 
consistency strengthens public recognition, builds credibility, and creates a more cohesive  
experience for residents and visitors.
A recognizable brand also supports participation by making programs, events, and public  
information easier to identify and trust. As the department continues to grow, maintaining a strong 
and consistent brand identity will remain an important part of connecting with the community and 
promoting the value of Parks and Recreation.
Content Strategy
An effective social media presence depends on more than posting frequently—it requires purposeful, 
strategic communication. Our content strategy is designed to educate, inform, engage, & celebrate

Content is planned to maintain a balanced mix of program promotions, event coverage, park and

repetitive promotional messaging.
Whenever possible, content emphasizes authentic photography and video captured at Parks and

By combining timely information with engaging storytelling and consistent branding, our content 
strategy has helped expand our online reach, increase community engagement, and strengthen 
awareness of the many opportunities available through Rifle Parks and Recreation.

Paid advertising is used strategically to extend the reach of Rifle Parks and Recreation communication 
and maximize the department’s marketing investment. Rather than promoting programs that

Each campaign is tailored to a specific audience using Meta’s targeting tools, allowing

marketing resources are directed where they have the greatest impact.

Executive Summary: 
Staff is seeking direction after receiving feedback from a few citizens regarding the weather closure and 
cancellation policy for Rifle Metro Pool. Potential options to adjust the policy include leaving the current 
policy, a refund, partial credit, or a "rain check" voucher. The current policy listed on the website states, "No

Executive Summary 
Strategic planning is a process for clarifying the aspirations of a Board and Staff. It 
establishes goals to be met and identifies broad strategies to achieve them. 
Everything that happens in an organization is driven by its values. These are the 
principles and beliefs that explain why the organization exists. Values define the goals 
and boundaries of behavior for the board, staff, and volunteers in their interactions with 
each other, with clients, and with the community. 
Values are the things we believe are important. Values govern the way we behave, 
communicate, and interact with others. Values help us determine our priorities. Values 
are a small set of general guiding principles not to be compromised for short-term 
financial gain or expediency. Below are the values the Parks & Recreation Advisory 
Board and Staff set forth. 
Mission 
The mission is a summary of the means that the organization will employ to achieve its 
vision. The mission statement answers the question, “Why do we exist?” 
“To put forth the highest level of parks, programs, facilities, and  
services necessary, to establish Rifle as the premier recreational  
community on the Western Slope.” 
Vision 
The vision is a picture of the future state of the community we serve as a result of our 
work. Our vision statement is our destination. 
“We strive to enrich lives through quality parks,  
programs, facilities, and services.” 
Values 
When an organization’s values appear as guiding principles, a code of conduct, or a 
culture code that explains how the organization intends to operate in accordance with 
those values, a values statement makes a promise. Complimentary to the City’s  
values, Parks & Recreation has identified additional values that guide how they  
provide services.
