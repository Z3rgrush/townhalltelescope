MEMORANDUM 
 
 
 
 
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
Staff is seeking GRIT Board feedback regarding the potential coordination of GRIT and Downtown 
Development Authority membership and meeting schedules. The concept would allow qualified 
individuals to serve on both boards and schedule the meetings consecutively. This could reduce 
the number of separate meeting commitments, help support consistent attendance and quorum, 
and improve communication between the two boards. 
This proposal would not merge GRIT and the DDA. Each board would remain a separate public 
body with its own responsibilities, meetings, projects, funding decisions, and official actions. Staff 
is requesting guidance regarding whether the concept should be explored further and, if so, 
whether the DDA meeting should be held immediately before or after the GRIT meeting. 
BACKGROUND 
The DDA has recently experienced difficulty achieving quorum. GRIT currently includes 
individuals with strong connections to downtown businesses and community development who 
may also meet DDA membership requirements. 
Coordinating membership and meeting times could reduce the number of additional volunteers 
needed and make participation more manageable for members serving on both boards.  
SEPARATE GOVERNANCE  
GRIT and the DDA are separate public bodies with different responsibilities and funding sources. 
Sharing members or holding meetings consecutively would not combine the boards. Each board 
would maintain its own agenda, call to order, quorum, minutes, motions, votes, and adjournment. 
Projects and funding decisions would remain clearly assigned to the appropriate board, although 
the boards could coordinate when their priorities align. One meeting would formally adjourn before 
the other is called to order. 
DDA MEMEBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS  
Appointed members must qualify through a connection to the DDA district by: 
• Residing within the DDA district; 
• Owning real property within the district; 
• Leasing a business location within the district; or 
• Serving as a manager, agent, or employee of a business or entity located within the district.

MEETING SCHEDULE OPTIONS 
Staff is requesting guidance regarding the preferred order of the meetings: 
1. The DDA could meet first, formally adjourn, and then transition into the regularly scheduled 
GRIT meeting; or 
2. GRIT could meet first, formally adjourn, and the DDA meeting could begin immediately 
afterward. 
The preferred order should consider member availability, the typical length of each meeting (1 
hour or less), and which arrangement would provide the most reliable quorum. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION 
Discuss the potential coordination of DDA and GRIT board membership and meeting schedules 
and provide staff direction regarding: 
1. Whether the concept should be explored further 
2. Whether the DDA should meet before or after GRIT 
3. Which GRIT members may be interested in serving on both boards 
If the Board reaches a clear consensus in support of the concept, it may direct staff to present the 
proposal to the DDA and City Council for consideration and approval.
