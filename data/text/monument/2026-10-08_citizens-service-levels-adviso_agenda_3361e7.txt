5. Follow-up on Use Tax Ballot Initiative – Jennifer Phillips, Director of Finance 
Jennifer explained to the Committee that the ballot initiative had been focused on construction use 
tax , due to the $2.2M potential construction use tax revenue increase, and that no attention had 
been given to the motor vehicle component of use tax.  Jennifer apologized for missing the motor 
vehicle use tax and mentioned that there was no intent to ignore motor vehicle use tax.  Jennifer 
further explained that 2 different use tax ballots will be presented to Town Council on 8/17/2026 – 
one with motor vehicle and one without.  When asked if there are any In Town dealerships, Laurie 
Young informed the committee of the 2 small dealerships (by Taco Bell and next to Pin Ball Pub), 
plus the upcoming Buick/GMC dealership and Peak Equipment.  Steve King expressed his concern 
of unclear language on the ballot.  Brandy mentioned that it’s important to understand the 
potential revenue numbers.  Jennifer explained that increasing the motor vehicle use tax to 3.5 
would generate an additional $500K in use tax revenue.   
 
6. Review Preliminary 2027 Proposed Budget – Jennifer Phillips, Finance Director 
Jennifer Phillips presented a high-level overview of the preliminary 2027 budget, which will be 
presented to Town Council on 8/17/2026.  Scott Gilson asked if the revenue amounts in the 
proposed budget included an estimate of the new user fees, to which Jennifer explained no, 
because we would need to wait for the user fee resolution to be passed first.  Regarding park 
 
 
vandalism, Scott Gilson asked if the Town considered security cameras.  Madeline’s response was 
that security cameras are tricky in public spaces, plus there is currently no budget for cameras, but 
that would be something for the Town to consider in the future.  Scott also asked about the 
budgeted pavilion at Monument Lake, and if reservations would apply, to which Madeline answered 
the lake pavilion would be small and for general use.  Scott also asked if the town tracks vandalism, 
to which Andrew Archuleta answered yes, with a rough estimate of $5,000 per year in parts. 
Regarding Water Cybersecurity, Scott asked about guidelines for the Town’s cybersecurity.  
Jennifer explained that our SCADA system is protected by the Town’s firewalls and our wells are 
only set to “On” or “Off”, and are under constant monitoring by Water staff and phone apps.  
Madeline further explained that all wells are checked twice every day, and all have alarms. 
Regarding the Storm Water Impact Fee Fund and Storm Water Impact Fee study, Steve King 
suggested that Triview be responsible for their portion.  Jennifer further explained that since we 
have a plan in place, an Impact Fee Study can now be applied to Storm drainage needs. 
Steve King gave “Kudos” to Jennifer, Madeline and Dan for all of the Studies that have been 
performed for the Town, and although it is difficult to have to pay for the studies, the impacts are 
coming to fruition with increased fee revenue.  
 
7. Funding proposal from Small Business Development Center Network – Jennifer 
Phillips, Finance Director 
Regarding the proposal from the Small Business Development Center Network, John Lewis felt 
positive, Dan DeBoo expressed confusion about why they are only asking for $15K with such a large 
gap, Scott Gilson felt that roadblocks for small businesses don’t currently exist, Johan Moum felt 
that $250 fee for consulting costs is too high, and that not enough of an impact is made directly to 
the Town to delete funding to our other sponsors.  The consensus by the CSLAC committee is to 
say No to sponsorship in 2027 but consider it in the future.
