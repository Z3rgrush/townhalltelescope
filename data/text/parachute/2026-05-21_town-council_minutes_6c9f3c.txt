ADMINISTRATIVE REPORTS
 
I.1) 
Town Manager Report
Travis Elliott, Town Manager, reported the following:
 - July is the deadline for any ballot issues;
 - June 4 is the employee and family BBQ at Cottonwood Park;
 - June 15, CDOT will be presenting at Garfield County Commissioners meeting;
 - June 1 PATS is officially launching on-demand service;
 - 147 1st Street has closed and is now owned by the Town;
 - Stage 2 water restrictions remain in place;
 - Splash pad will only be open on Wednesday, Saturday, and Sunday of each week with reduced hours to be
in compliance with Stage 2 water restrictions;
 - Straight Bottom Ranch will be expanding solar project by 50 acres;
 - Town Council photos at 5:45 p.m. on Thursday, June 18th; and
 - Parachute Pickup was a great success.
