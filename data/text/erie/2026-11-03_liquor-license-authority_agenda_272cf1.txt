III. GENERAL BUSINESS/PUBLIC HEARING 
 
a. QuikTrip Corporation dba QuikTrip #4286 
1425 Highland Dr 
Erie CO, 80516 
New-Concurrent Fermented Malt Beverage and Wine Liquor License 
 
Action: Judge Bailhache opened the public hearing at 2:12 p.m. regarding the New-
Concurrent Fermented Malt Beverage and Wine (City) Liquor License application submitted 
by QuikTrip Corporation dba QuikTrip #4286. Attorney Katie Vera represented the Town.  
 
Cory Tipton, local QuikTrip Attorney, Brittany Sikorski, real estate project manager, and 
Lydia Hernandez, operations manager, appeared at the hearing today to answer any questions 
related to the application for a new-concurrent Fermented Malt Beverage and Wine Liquor 
License. Cory Tipton questioned Brittany Sikorski regarding the company structure. Ms. 
Sikorski stated that the businesses are privately owned and operated by the Corporation. The 
corporation has been operational since 1958. Next Cory Tipton questioned Lydia Hernandez 
about the operations of the stores specifically related to alcohol training and sales. Ms. 
Hernandez stated that there is always a manager or assistant manager on site in the stores so 
if there is ever a question or issue when it comes to the sale of alcohol they can help with the 
situation. Each employee goes through responsible retail training that goes over alcohol sales 
and is trained to scan all IDs and verify the photo of everyone that looks under the age of 50. 
If it is believed that the person is under 21 or visibly intoxicated the sale is denied and the 
alcohol is taken off the counter. Ms. Hernandez stated that QuikTrip does internal testing of 
Page 2 of 20
the employees. If the employee fails the test there is additional training done. If the employee 
fails a state sting operation, then that employee is terminated. Ms. Hernandez went over the 
security of the stores and stated that the doors on the beer coolers are timed to lock when 
sales of alcohol are not permitted. Ms. Tipton closed out her testimony stating that the 
QuikTrip Corporation meets all statutory needs, meets all the necessary requirements, and 
has shown that the corporation is a responsible vendor.  
 
Ms. Vera confirmed with the applicant that there have not been any violations at the 29 stores 
located in Colorado. There were no further questions by Ms. Vera.  
 
The needs and desires of the neighborhood petitioning were done by Liquor Pros. Carol 
Johnson from Liquor Pros was asked to present the results of the petition that was completed 
on June 30th and July 1st. Ms. Johnson states that the results provide sufficient evidence that 
the public is in favor of the business receiving a liquor license.  
 
No one from the public was there to speak for or against the application. Judge Bailhache 
stated the application does meet the needs and desires of the neighborhood and that QuikTrip 
Corporation appears to be of good moral character and therefore approved the application at 
2:30 pm.
