Summary/Key Points: 
• The Town is constructing a floating solar photovoltaic array at the North Water 
Reclamation Facility (NWRF) that will be complete in August 2026. 
• When complete, the Flotovoltaic Array at the North Water Reclamation Facility 
(NWRF) must interconnect to, and operate in parallel with, a United Power, Inc. 
(UP) distribution facility. 
Page 8 of 444
• The interconnection Agreement outlines the Terms and Conditions for the Town's 
solar, including the responsibilities of both the Interconnector (the Town) and the 
Utility Cooperative (UP). 
  
 
Background of Subject Matter: 
In May 2023, the Town of Erie entered into a Colorado Energy Office Energy 
Perfomance Contract with Iconergy Ltd. 
 
In June 2025, the Town amended the Energy Performance Contract with Iconergy Ltd 
(Now Iconergy CEG Solutions) to include construction of a 1.2 MW Floating Solar 
system at the North Water Reclamation Facility (NWRF).  
 
The agreement before Council for approval is a required interconnection agreement 
with United Power for the NWRF solar array. This agreement has been reviewed by 
Town staff and the Town Attorney's office and contains mutually agreed-to terms and 
conditions for both the Interconnector (the Town) and the Cooperative (United Power).

AGREEMENT FOR INTERCONNECTION AND PARALLEL OPERATION OF 
GENERATION FACILITIES LARGER THAN 25 kW
This Agreement for Interconnection and Parallel Operation of Generation Facilities Larger than 
25kW (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between United Power, Inc., a Colorado 
cooperative association (the “Cooperative”), and the Town of Erie, a Colorado municipal 
corporation (the “Interconnector”) as of the Effective Date.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Interconnector is proposing to design, construct, own, operate, and 
maintain a Generation Facility, consistent with the Interconnection Application completed by 
Interconnector on September 25, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the Interconnector desires that its Generation Facility interconnect to, and 
operate in parallel with, the Cooperative Distribution System; and
WHEREAS, the Parties desire to (a) describe the facilities to interconnect the Generation 
Facility to, and operate it in parallel with, the Cooperative Distribution System; and (b) establish 
the respective obligations and rights of the Parties with respect to the procurement, construction, 
installation, ownership, operation, and maintenance of such facilities.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and agreements 
set forth herein, the Parties hereby agree as follows:
AGREEMENT
I. SCOPE OF AGREEMENT AND LIMITATIONS
A. This Agreement governs the terms and conditions under which the Interconnector’s 
Generation Facility will interconnect to, and operate in parallel with, the Cooperative 
Distribution System. 
B. This Agreement does not constitute an agreement for the provision of electric service. 
Electric service shall be provided for separately, pursuant to the terms and conditions of the 
Cooperative’s Electric Tariffs. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to amend, 
modify, supersede, limit, or otherwise affect the terms and effects of the Electric Utility 
Franchise Agreement between the Town of Erie and United Power, Inc., dated March 29, 
2010, as amended, renewed, extended, or replaced from time to time.
C. This Agreement does not constitute an agreement to purchase the Interconnector’s power. 
Any other services that the Interconnector may require from the Cooperative shall be 
provided for separately, pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Cooperative’s Electric 
Tariffs, separate agreements, or both. 
D. Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings assigned to them in Exhibit A, 
unless otherwise noted herein.

II.   RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES
A. Interconnector General Requirements; Compliance with All Applicable Authorities 
The Interconnector shall, at the Interconnector’s sole risk and expense, design, construct, 
own, operate, and maintain the Generation Facility, Interconnection Facilities, and all 
constituent equipment in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, as 
well as all other applicable requirements and authorities pertaining to the interconnection

Reliability Requirements set forth in Exhibit E. In the event of a conflict between any of the

B. Interconnection Service
The Cooperative shall provide Interconnection Service for the Generation Facility in 
accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement and all applicable requirements 
and authorities listed in Section II.A, until such time this Agreement is terminated in 
accordance with Section III.B of this Agreement.
C. Cost Responsibility
1. The Interconnector shall pay for all costs associated with the interconnection and 
intended parallel operation of the Generation Facility in accordance with the terms and 
conditions contained herein. 
2. Costs that are estimable as of the Effective Date of this Agreement are identified in 
Exhibit C. The Parties acknowledge that (i) the Cooperative has made its best efforts to 
provide a reasonable estimate of such costs based on information available to it as of the 
Effective Date, including, but not limited to, supply chain market analyses of anticipated 
market conditions, labor rates, and material availability based on the Interconnector’s 
project construction schedule, (ii)  actual costs may be different than the estimated 
amount, and (iii) all actual costs are still the responsibility of the Interconnector.

commencing construction of any Cooperative Facilities, the Cooperative may update the 
cost estimate set forth in Exhibit C as necessary to reflect its then-current best estimates

the Cooperative an additional deposit equal to the full amount of any increase in such 
cost estimate, and the Cooperative shall have no obligation to commence construction of 
the Cooperative Facilities until it has received such payment.
4. The Interconnector shall reimburse the Cooperative for all costs that the Cooperative 
incurs under this Agreement, including, but not limited to, any costs incurred in relation 
to design, construction, ownership, operation, maintenance, or modification of the

to this Agreement. The Interconnector shall make payment to the Cooperative within 
thirty (30) calendar days of the invoice date.

Background of Subject Matter: 
In 2018, the then Board of Trustees (now Town Council) approved a site development 
plan for the Schofield Farm Open Space. The development of the plan included 
substantial public outreach, research on similar sites, interviews with former residents of 
the farm, and site analysis. This process created a framework to develop the site into a 
unique cultural and recreational resource for the benefit of the residents of the Town of 
Erie and visitors. Since 2018, staff have been working on a phased approach to 
gradually improve the site: 
• Historic Structures Assessment (State Historical Fund Grant) 
• Interim Grading & Utility Installation Project (Partial State Historical Fund Grant) 
• Farmhouse Rehabilitation (Partial State Historical Fund Grant) 
• Concept Design & Design Development of the Site Improvements 
• Phase 2, Package A of the Site Improvements (Partial Land & Water 
Conservation Fund Grant) 
• Large Barn & Loafing Shed Rehabilitation (Partial State Historical Fund Grant) 
• Restroom & Brooder House Deconstruction & Reconstruction into Site 
Restrooms 
• Phase 2, Package B of the Site Improvements 
 
Funding for the entire project is a mixture of approved funds, including the Parks 
Improvement Impact Fund, Trails and Natural Areas Fund, and the Tree Impact Fund. 
The Town is leveraging these funds with State Historical Fund grants and Land and 
Water Conservation Fund grants. The first phase of site improvements are complete, 
and the second phase will begin this summer. 
 
Staff engaged with Wenk Associates for landscape architectural services through a 
competitive RFP process. Wenk Associates completed the Concept Design, Design 
Development documents, Phase 2, Package A Construction Drawings, and is in the 
process of completing Phase 2, Package B Construction Drawings. Wenk Associates 
manages a project design team involving structural and civil engineers, electrical 
engineers, irrigation designers, geotechnical engineers, biologists, and landscape 
architects who assist with the project throughout design and construction. This 
amendment will provide continued construction observation and design services for 
Package B of the site improvements which are scheduled for completion in the fall of 
2027.

species.
