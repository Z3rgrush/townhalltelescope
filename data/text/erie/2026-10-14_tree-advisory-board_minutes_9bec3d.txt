A. Neighborhood Forest (www.neighborhoodforest.org) 
1) Program gives free trees to kids and families at Earth Day with the goal of the kids 
watching the trees grow over time 
2) Jessica partners with them and donates a portion of her book sales back to the 
program 
3) They partner with community groups and organizations to help with the distribution 
across the country. 
4) We are TAB and/or Town of Erie can become a Partner.  We would help collect 
applications, Neighborhood Forest ships trees here once application period closes 
and we would organize the distribution of trees.  
5) Neighborhood Forest decides the tree species.  Last year it was Blue Spruce.  They 
do try and distribute trees that are native or fruit bearing trees appropriate to each 
region.   
6) Trees are saplings with planting instructions. 
7) They can only increase their reach by increasing the number of partners.
