a. Continued Discussion of Camping Complaints within Town Limits: Staff 
Note: Clerk spoke with Town Attorney Jo Seavy on 7.29.2026. Camping within town 
limits is not defined in Ordinance 340, Silver Plume Land Use Code, as either an 
acceptable land use or a prohibited land use. It is therefore considered a 
nonconforming use, which is defined under Division 10 of the Town’s Land Use 
Code and applies to all legally established land use that does not conform with the 
requirements of the Land Use Code. Per the LUC, a nonconforming use may be 
continued, a nonconforming structure may continue to be occupied, and normal or 
routine maintenance of the structure shall be allowed, even if the Town passes an 
ordinance to try and address the issue. The only instance in which the 
nonconforming use is not grandfathered in is in the instance the nonconforming 
use expands. Essentially, there is not much the Town can legally do to intervene in 
this issue. Property owner should be encouraged to safeguard their own property 
and inform the Town if the area of the nonconforming use expands or grows. 
Mike Winchel joins the board and asks that the town intervenes into the
