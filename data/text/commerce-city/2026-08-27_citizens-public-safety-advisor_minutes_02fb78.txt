Reports6.
The department provided updates on recent operational activities, community 
programs, technology, and upcoming initiatives.
Patrol Division:
Patrol reported several recent operational developments. K9 deployments resulted in 
narcotics and weapons seizures. Nine field training officers are progressing into phase 
2 of the training process. Community Service Officers are now authorized to 
investigate minor traffic crashes, prepare related reports, and issue lower-level 
citations.
The Safe City initiative also contributed to an arrest involving the theft of approximately 
700 pounds of copper wire.
Support Services Division:
Investigations reported positive trends in several areas. Shootings are historically 
down, and auto thefts have decreased approximately by 26% compared to the previous 
year. Detectives continue to address sexual assault cases involving children, resulting 
in arrests being made.
Chief's Report:
The Chief provided updates regarding department programs, community engagement, 
equipment, technology, and policy initiatives.
National Night Out was successfully conducted at six locations. The Citizens Academy 
currently has approximately 24 participants enrolled in the 11- week program. The 
Youth Police Camp, held at the Grandby YMCA, was attended by approximately 38 
youth. 
The department's  Explorer Program currently has 12 active Explorers. Members 
recently participated in the annual competition and received recognition in several 
competitive events.
Chief also provided an update regarding the department's Flock camera system. 
Examples were shared regarding successful cases in which the technology assisted 
investigators, including an ebike-related investigation.
Lastly, Chief Guadnola will be presenting his Annual Crime Report to Council on 
September 21st, which is also the same day of the second reading of Ordinance 2796 
- to authorize the de-appropriation and transfer of funds in the amount of $110,000 to 
purchase a mobile command unit from the SACFD.
Community Outreach:
The group discussed promotional and outreach items for future community events.
