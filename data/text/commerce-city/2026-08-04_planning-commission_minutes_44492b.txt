Omar Yusuf, City Planner, introduced the case.
Heidi Short, Shamrock Foods, presented on behalf of the applicant.
Commissioners asked the applicant to clarify safety measures for the above-ground 
tanks.
The applicant clarified the tanks will be fully fire-code compliant not fireproof.
Omar Yusuf, City Planner, presented on behalf of staff.
Commissioners asked staff whether Sand Creek Regional Greenway was notified. 
Staff reported:
• The Greenway is not on the city’s referral list for industrial zoning cases.
• The Greenway is approximately 1,100 feet north of the site, outside the 500-foot 
noticing radius.
• Notices were published and mailed in compliance with code.
Commissioners asked about employment changes since 2021 and whether the 
company anticipated additional manufacturing uses. 
The applicant stated:
• Roughly 150-200 employees were previously located onsite.
• Approximately 30 new employees will be added with this project.
• Future expansion is planned as part of revitalizing the site into a manufacturing 
hub.
Commissioners asked about the oil processing activities. 
The applicant explained:
• Raw food oils are received and processed into finished food-grade oil products.
• No chemical refining occurs.
• No distillation or petroleum-related processes are used.
Commissioners asked whether the tanks would have enclosure walls. 
The applicant stated:
• Tanks will sit within a containment field.
• No enclosure walls are planned due to existing screening provided by building 
placement and site layout.
A motion was made by Commissioner Shecter, seconded by Commissioner 
VanHeusen, that the Planning Commission enter a finding that the requested 
I-3 (Heavy- Intensity Industrial Zoning District) Zoning for the property located at 
southwest corner of East 52 Avenue and Ivy Street contained in case 
ZON26-0004 meets the criteria of the Land Development Code and based upon 
such finding, recommend approval to City Council of the I-3 (Heavy- Intensity 
Industrial Zoning District) Zoning. VOTE:
