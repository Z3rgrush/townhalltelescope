26-099 Public Comment - Individuals may address the City Council on items 
that are not on the agenda, or on agenda items that do not appear as 
Public Hearings, during Public Comment.  Items of a personal nature 
should not be discussed; personnel-related matters shall be directed 
through the appropriate chain of command.  Please sign up prior to 
the meeting start time.  Neither Council Members nor City staff should 
be expected to engage in discussions or debate with any speaker.  
The Council may direct the City Manager to follow up with certain 
matters.  Out of respect for City Council and others in attendance, 
comments are limited to three (3) minutes or less. If a large number of 
speakers have signed up representing a group and on the same topic, 
the Mayor may ask that the group designate a representative to 
present their collective comments. Visit <http://c3gov.com/council> to 
access full meeting agendas. Click on, "Virtual Meeting and Public 
Comment Instructions," for instructions on how to provide virtual live 
and written comments. The deadline to register for virtual comments,

NCB 26-060 EAST 120TH AVENUE RESTRIPING BETWEEN SABLE 
BOULEVARD AND CHAMBERS ROAD
A motion was made by Mayor Douglas, seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Noble, to direct 
the City Manager to prioritize and coordinate with CDOT the restriping of East 120th 
Avenue between Sable Boulevard and Chambers Road, including the bridge over 
I-76, in both directions before winter, weather permitting and subject to required 
approvals. The goal is to establish two continuous travel lanes in each direction 
between Sable Boulevard and Buckley Road, including a smooth connection from the 
dual northbound Chambers Road left-turn lanes onto westbound 120th Avenue, 
subject to traffic engineering approval. Staff shall identify available funding and 
promptly report the implementation schedule or any obstacles requiring further 
Council action. 
Mayor Douglas withdrew the motion.
NCB 26-059 EVALUATE PUMP TRACKS AND BICYCLE PARKS AS 
COMMUNITY "THIRD SPACES" IN THE NORTHERN RANGE AND 
SOUTHERN COMMERCE CITY
A motion was made by Mayor Douglas, seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Noble, to direct 
the City Manager, through the Parks, Recreation and Golf Department and Parks 
Recreation Commission and in coordination with Public Works, Finance, Community 
Development, and the City Attorney’s Office as appropriate, to conduct a feasibility 
study for pump tracks, dirt jump areas, and bicycle skills parks that would serve as 
community third spaces in both the Northern Range and southern Commerce City. 
 
Mayor Douglas withdrew the motion.
