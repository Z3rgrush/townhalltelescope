 
TOWN OF DOLORES 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES WORKSHOP MINUTES 
August 24, 2026 – 5:30 P.M. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Holkestad called the workshop to order at 5:30 p.m. 
2. ROLL CALL 
Present- Mayor Holkestad, Mayor Pro tem Curry, Trustee Roan, Trustee Peterson, Trustee, 
Beckman, Trustee McCoy. Trustee Maule attempted to attend via Microsoft Teams but was 
unable to establish audio and subsequently disconnected. 
3. WORKSHOP DISCUSSION 
3.1 2027 BUDGET PLANNING 
Town Clerk/Treasurer Kelley presented the 2027 budget planning information and reviewed 
the budget to date. Trustees were encouraged to review the highlighted areas in 
preparation for further budget discussion at the October meeting. 
3.2 BOARD MEETING SCHEDULE / 3.3 PUBLIC FORUM OPTIONS 
The Board discussed options for the Town’s meeting schedule and opportunities for public 
engagement. Discussion included holding two working meetings per month and 
conducting quarterly town hall forums. 
3.4 SHERIFF’S CONTRACT 
Town Attorney Ethan Sumrall presented a draft of the Sheriff’s contract. The Board 
discussed quantitative and qualitative metrics that could be used to evaluate the value 
and level of law-enforcement services provided under the contract. 
3.5 CODE ENFORCEMENT PROCESS REVIEW 

Building Inspector Ryan Greene provided an overview of the Town’s code-enforcement 
process and discussed his role and limitations with regard to code enforcement. 
3.6 DOLORES COMMUNITY CENTER SITE REVIEW OPTIONS 
Town Manager Reeves presented the Dolores Community Center Advisory Board’s request 
for a site review. Trustee McCoy suggested that volunteers could potentially be utilized to 
assist with cleanup of the Community Center site. Ethan Sumrall noted that a volunteer 
waiver is available for DCC volunteers. 
4. FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS / BOARD PARKING LOT 
Town Manager Leigh Reeves asked whether Board members planned to attend the 
upcoming bear presentation at the Dolores Public Library in order to determine whether a 
quorum notice would be necessary. 
The following items were added to the Future Agenda Items/Board Parking Lot for future 
consideration: 
• Community Center site visit 
• Road safety 
• Holidays 
• Revenue generation 
5. ADJOURNMENT 
The workshop adjourned at 7:37 p.m. 
 
 
 
Chris Holkestad, Mayor 
 
Kelley, Town Clerk
