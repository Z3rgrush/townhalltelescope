Minutes  October 26, 2026 
 
 
TOWN OF DOLORES 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES WORKSHOP AND REGULAR MEETING MINUTES 
October 26, 2026 – 5:30 p.m. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado 
 
WORKSHOP: The Board of Trustees held a budget workshop beginning at 5:30 p.m. to 
review the proposed 2027 Town of Dolores Budget. 
The Board reviewed the 2027 Budget Notebook in its entirety, including the proposed 
revenues, expenditures, capital projects, and funding priorities for the upcoming fiscal 
year. 
The workshop concluded at 8:00 p.m. Following a brief break, the regular Board meeting 
was called to order at 8:09 p.m 
 
REGULAR MEETING: 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Chris Holkestad called the regular meeting to order at 8:09 p.m. 
2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
3. ROLL CALL 
Present: Mayor Chris Holkestad, Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry, Trustees Josh Maule, Derek 
Beckman, and Marie Roan. Absent: Trustee Billy McCoy. 
4. APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry moved to approve the agenda. Trustee Derek Beckman 
seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
 
Minutes  October 26, 2026 
5. DISCLOSURE OF CONFLICTS OF INTEREST 
No conﬂicts of interest were disclosed. 
6. CONSENT AGENDA 
Trustee Marie Roan moved to approve the Consent Agenda, including the liquor license 
transfer to El Patron Mexican Restaurant and Cantina, the October 12, 2026 meeting 
minutes, and the library board appointment. Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry seconded. Motion 
carried unanimously. 
7. ITEMS REMOVED FROM CONSENT AGENDA 
None. 
8. PUBLIC COMMENT 
• Deanna Truelson reported that attendance at senior lunches has been declining. 
• Val Truelson commented that operating the Dolores Community Center at a deﬁcit 
is comparable to the Town operating parks. 
• Jen Stark noted that the community supports seniors by making the Community 
Center available for lunches. She expressed concern that uses of the facility not 
fully reﬂected in its ﬁnancial reporting may affect its ﬁnancial position and 
expressed interest in the Advisory Committee and Board working together. 
9. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
No Trustee reports were provided. 
10. DISCUSSION / POSSIBLE ACTION ITEMS 
10.1 Resolution No. 726 – Accepting a Proposal from Durango Electrical Services for 
Electrical Service Improvements at the Dolores Water Treatment Plant. Mayor Pro Tem 
Chris Curry moved to approve Resolution No. 726. Trustee Marie Roan seconded. Motion 
carried unanimously. 
11. ADMINISTRATIVE BUSINESS 
11.1 Rescheduling Trustee Interviews and Appointment: The Board decided to 
reschedule the trustee candidate interviews and appointment to the November 23, 2026 
meeting. 
12. FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS / BOARD PARKING LOT 
No additional items were reported.

13. ADJOURNMENT 
The meeting adjourned at 8:23 p.m.
