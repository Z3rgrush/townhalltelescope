September 14, 2026 – 5:30 P.M.

1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Chris Holkestad called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

All Trustees were present.

Mayor Holkestad moved to approve the agenda. Mayor Pro Tem Curry seconded. Motion 
carried unanimously. 
5. DISCLOSURE OF CONFLICTS OF INTEREST 
No conflicts of interest were disclosed. 
6. CONSENT AGENDA 
Mayor Holkestad moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Trustee Maule seconded. Motion

The consent agenda included the August 10, 2026 minutes; August 24, 2026 workshop 
minutes; August 2026 proceedings; Special Event Permits for High Desert DEVO and 
DRBA; and the Minor Plat Amendment for the parcels at 100 and 102 N. 4th Street.  
7. ITEMS REMOVED FROM CONSENT AGENDA 
None.

Rebecca Busic introduced herself as a candidate for County Commissioner and discussed 
her platform and issues.

work at Flanders Park.

9. STAFF / COMMITTEE REPORTS 
9.1 Sheriff’s Report – Steve Nowlin 
Sheriff Nowlin reported on increased bear activity and increased traffic in the area. 
9.2 Town Manager Report – Leigh Reeves 
Manager Reeves provided updates on the Comprehensive Plan, parks project, October 19–
20 cleanup days, October 2–4 Bike and Skate Festival, Southwest Youth Corps assistance 
with trail cleanup, and progress on the water main replacement project. 
Reeves requested Board direction regarding the Community Center, including whether the 
Town should continue subsidizing the facility, whether the Board should conduct a site 
visit, and whether staff should proceed with addressing boundary issues. The Board 
directed that a Community Center site visit be placed on the Future Agenda Items/Board 
Parking Lot, that staff move forward with the boundary issue, and that Community Center 
funding be reviewed during the budget process. 
Reeves also discussed possible approaches to addressing feral cats. 
9.3 Town Attorney Report – Ethan Sumrall 
Attorney Sumrall discussed the Comnet/Everest telecommunications tower lease and 
provided a report on August Municipal Court activity. 
9.4 Treasurer Report – Kelley Unrein 
Treasurer Unrein presented current financial information and informed the Board regarding 
HB 26-1409 and its effect on marijuana tax distributions. 
9.5 Maintenance/Public Works Report – RJ Cross 
The Maintenance/Public Works report was included in the packet. 
9.6 County Commissioner Report – Jim Candelaria 
Commissioner Candelaria was unable to attend. 
9.7 Community Center Advisory Board 
Jen Stark presented the Community Center Advisory Board report and requested direction 
regarding specific items the Board would like the Advisory Board to review in preparation 
for the budget meeting.

10. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
Mayor Holkestad reported on the continuing search for a Town Attorney and expressed 
concern regarding increased speeding and failure to stop at stop signs since school 
resumed. 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry had no comments. 
Trustee Maule raised concerns regarding e-bikes on trails. 
Trustee McCoy had no comments. 
Trustee Beckman provided an update regarding fundraising for the MMA youth group. 
Trustee Roan provided a Chamber of Commerce update, reporting that Visitor Center 
renovations were nearly complete and that holiday activities were being planned. 
Trustee Peterson also discussed the feral cat issue. 
11. PUBLIC HEARINGS / QUASI-JUDICIAL 
11.1 Ordinance 592 – Rezoning 206 and 207 South Third Street 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 6:52 p.m. Sara Pritchard responded to 
questions raised at the previous meeting and was available to answer additional questions 
from staff and the Board. 
During public comment, Rachel Vass encouraged the Board to consider the “bigger 
picture” and potential future uses when making its decision. 
The public hearing was closed at 7:10 p.m. 
Mayor Holkestad moved to approve Ordinance 592 on second reading. Mayor Pro Tem 
Curry seconded. A roll-call vote was taken and the motion carried unanimously. 
The agenda identifies Ordinance 592 as rezoning 206 and 207 South Third Street from 
Downtown Mixed Use (DMU) to Neighborhood Residential-3 (NR-3).
