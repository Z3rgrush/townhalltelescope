None. 
8. PUBLIC COMMENT 
Val Truelson addressed the Board and stated that he believes things are going well within 
the Town. He noted that CDOT had recently swept portions of the highway corridor and 
informed the Board that a culvert crossing exists beneath the highway, providing access 
near the bike and skate park should services be needed in that area. 
Mike Sawyer informed the Board that comments regarding transportation concerns may be 
submitted through the CDOT website. He also expressed concerns regarding the lack of 
cleanup and maintenance occurring on vacant lots within the community. 
9. STAFF / COMMITTEE REPORTS 
9.1 Sheriff's Report – Officer Kaley Green 
Officer Greene reviewed the monthly calls for service report and noted an increase 
in bear activity within town limits. 
9.2 Town Manager Report – Leigh Reeves 
Town Manager Reeves reported that drainage concerns at the school property have 
been resolved. She highlighted the success of Riverfest and encouraged trustees 
and community members to continue reviewing and participating in the 
Comprehensive Plan update process. 
9.3 Town Attorney Report – Ethan Sumrall 
Town Attorney Sumrall provided an overview of the proposed marijuana licensing 
ordinance amendment. He reminded Board members to wait for recognition before 
speaking during meetings, to represent the interests of the Town as a whole, and to 
remain focused on agenda topics during deliberations. He further advised attendees 
that disorderly conduct during meetings would be addressed as necessary. 
9.4 Treasurer Report – Kelley Unrein 
Clerk/Treasurer Unrein reviewed the Town's ﬁnancial reports and provided 
background information regarding Resolution No. 713. 
9.5 Maintenance Report – RJ Cross 
The maintenance report was provided in the Board packet. 
9.6 County Commissioner Report – Jim Candelaria 
Commissioner Candelaria updated the Board regarding the success of recent 
community cleanup days. 
9.7 Community Center Advisory Board 
Representatives of the Community Center Advisory Board spoke regarding 
Resolution No. 711 and introduced the concept of a future Stone Soup Competition 
community event. 
10. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
10. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
10.1 Mayor Chris Holkestad 
Mayor Holkestad encouraged residents to adopt a "see something, say something" 
approach regarding potential fire hazards and emphasized the importance of 
reporting concerns that could increase fire risk. He also encouraged residents to be 
mindful of cleaning up after their dogs in public areas. 
10.2 Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry encouraged residents to participate in the Comprehensive 
Plan update process by completing the community questionnaire. In response to 
questions regarding the timeline, Town Manager Reeves indicated that responses 
submitted by August would be most beneficial to the planning process. 
10.3 Trustee Josh Maule 
Trustee Maule inquired about responsible watering practices within the Town's 
parks system. 
10.4 Trustee Bill McCoy 
Trustee McCoy thanked Town Attorney Ethan Sumrall and the Sheriff's Office for 
their work and assistance to the Town. He also noted safety concerns related to 
trees obstructing visibility of stop signs and speed limit signs.
