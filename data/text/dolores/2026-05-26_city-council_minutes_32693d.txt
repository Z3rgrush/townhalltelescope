 
 
TOWN OF DOLORES, COLORADO 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES WORKSHOP 
MAY 26, 2026 – 5:30 P.M. 
The Town of Dolores Board of Trustees held a workshop meeting on May 26, 2026, at Town 
Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado, with virtual access available via the Town 
website. 
Mayor Chris Holkestad called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m. 
ATTENDANCE 
PRESENT: Trustees Curry, Maule, McCoy, Miller, Peterson, and Roan. 
ABSENT: Trustee Beckman. 
STAFF PRESENT: Town Manager Leigh Reeves, Town Clerk Kelley Unrein, and Town 
Attorney Ethan Sumrall. 
MANAGER’S REPORT 
Town Manager Leigh Reeves provided a brief manager’s report. Reeves highlighted the 
Town’s recent award of approximately $700,000 in funding from the Colorado Department 
of Local Affairs (DOLA) for the Phase 2 Water Project. Reeves also provided an update 
regarding cleanup efforts at 102 North 11th Street. 
WORKSHOP TOPIC: COMPREHENSIVE PLAN 
Town Manager Leigh Reeves presented information regarding the Town’s ongoing 
Comprehensive Plan update process. 
Reeves reported that, at the time of the meeting, 32 out of 918 community survey 
recipients had responded. Reeves encouraged trustees to complete the online survey and 

set an example for community participation in the Comprehensive Plan update process. 
Trustees were also encouraged to continue reviewing materials and provide feedback 
throughout the process.  
Reeves reviewed preliminary information and data being compiled by Logan Simpson, the 
consultant retained to assist the Town with the Comprehensive Plan update. Discussion 
included community feedback trends, planning priorities, and the overall update process. 
Reeves also discussed anticipated next steps in the project, including public engagement 
efforts, draft plan development, and certain legal requirements associated with adoption 
of an updated Comprehensive Plan. 
ADJOURNMENT 
There being no further business, the workshop adjourned at 6:52 p.m. 
 
 
 
Chris Holkestad, Mayor
