TOWN OF DOLORES, COLORADO 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING MINUTES 
April 13, 2026 – 5:30 P.M. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
The meeting was called to order by Mayor Chris Holkestad at 5:30 p.m. 
2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
3. ROLL CALL 
Present: Trustees Wheeler, Curry, Roan, Peterson, Youngquist, and Mayor Holkestad 
Absent: Trustee Grigg 
A quorum was present. 
4. APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA 
Motion: Trustee Youngquist moved to approve the agenda as presented. 
Second: Trustee Curry 
 
Vote: Unanimous 
Result: Motion carried. 
5. DISCLOSURE OF CONFLICTS OF INTEREST 
No conflicts of interest were disclosed. 
6. CONSENT AGENDA 
Items included: 
• March 9, 2026 Board Meeting Minutes  
• March 2026 Proceedings  
• Transfer of Lindo Michoacan Liquor License  
• Montezuma Mexican Liquor License Renewal  
Motion: Trustee Curry moved to approve the Consent Agenda. 
Second: Trustee Wheeler 
Vote: Unanimous 
Result: Motion carried. 
7. PUBLIC COMMENT 
• Mike Sawyer thanked the outgoing trustees and congratulated the incoming 
trustees.  
• Emily Bud addressed a potential development project on 3rd Street and expressed 
concerns regarding parking.  
• Val Truelson noted that Riverside Avenue is 35 feet wide.  
(No action taken.) 
8. STAFF / COMMITTEE REPORTS 
• Sheriff Steve Nowlin reported that the speed limit has decreased to 35 mph at 18th 
Street, noted the first bear sighting of the year, indicated that a fire ban date is to be 
determined, and responded to concerns regarding speeding, encouraging citizens 
to file traffic complaints.  
• Town Manager Leigh Reeves provided background information for new trustees 
and updates regarding the comprehensive plan, CIRSA audit results, nuisance 
abatement and public works activities, the Daniels Fund grant, and Phase 2 water 
project funding.  
• Town Attorney Ethan Sumrall reviewed recent court proceedings and provided 
clarification regarding Resolutions 698, 700, and 703.  
• Treasurer Kelley Unrein reviewed the March financial statements.  
• County Commissioner Jim Candelaria discussed potential burn ban plans, 
community cleanup efforts, and an upcoming health fair.  
• Community Center Advisory Board (Jen Stark) reported on the success of the 
bread competition and requested that an action item be placed on a future 
workshop agenda.  
(No formal action taken.) 
9. TRUSTEE REPORTS AND ACTIONS 
• Mayor Holkestad and Trustees Curry, Roan, and Peterson expressed appreciation to 
the outgoing trustees.  
• Trustee Roan noted that the Chamber of Commerce will select a director on 
Wednesday.  
• Trustee Peterson inquired about ongoing speed limit changes.  
• Trustee Youngquist welcomed the new trustees and encouraged preparedness.  
• Trustee Wheeler reflected on her service as a trustee.  
(No formal action taken.) 
10. PUBLIC HEARING / ORDINANCES 
Public Hearing: Second Reading – Dog Licensing Ordinance 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 6:31 p.m. 
Town Attorney Sumrall presented the ordinance on second reading. 
No public or Board comments were received. 
Mayor Holkestad closed the public hearing at 6:35 p.m. 
Motion: Mayor Holkestad moved to approve the ordinance on second reading. 
Second: Trustee Peterson 
 
Vote: Unanimous 
Result: Motion carried.

Swearing-In of Newly Elected Trustees 
The Mayor and Town Clerk recognized the outgoing trustees. The Town Clerk administered 
the oath of office to the newly elected trustees and to Mayor Holkestad for his reelection. 
Election of Mayor Pro Tem (C.R.S. § 31-4-303) 
Trustee Peterson nominated Trustee Curry. 
Mayor Holkestad nominated Trustee Peterson; Trustee Peterson declined the nomination. 
Second: Trustee Maule 
Vote: Unanimous 
Result: Trustee Curry was elected Mayor Pro Tem.
