11.1 Public Hearing: Conditional Use Permit at 200 South 8th Street 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 7:11 pm.   
Planner Sarah Pritchard presented the staff report and demonstrated how the 
proposed garage met the standards and intentions of the Land Use Code and the 
Comprehensive plan.  
After the presentation there was some discussion about the appearance of the 
proposed structure, lighting and landscaping. 
 
No public comments were received. 
Mayor Holkestad closed the public hearing at 7:27 pm 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry made a motion and Trustee Roan seconded the motion to 
approve the Conditional Use Permit for 200 S 8th with the conditions listed below.  
 
Conditions: 
1. Prior to construction, the applicant shall confirm with all appropriate entities (i.e. 
Town of Dolores, Empire Energy, Atmos Energy, etc.) that the proposed garage 
placement is not within the boundaries of any legally established easements on the 
property. 
2. Prior to construction, the applicant shall obtain a building permit approval, as 
required by the Town of Dolores. 
3. At the time of construction, all exterior lighting for the proposed garage shall be 
compliant with applicable Outdoor Lighting Standards, as outlined in Article 10 of 
the Dolores Land Use Code. 
4. In the event that the principal use of the property is converted to a non-residential or 
commercial use, this approval shall be automatically revoked. Any continued or 
proposed use of the accessory structure as a non-residential or commercial use 
shall require separate review and approval in accordance with the applicable 
provisions of the Dolores Land Use Code.

11.1 Public Hearing: Conditional Use Permit at 18390 Hwy 145 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 7:29 pm. Manager Reeves presented 
the staff report for the Dolores River Experience’s proposed RV park business. 
Manager Reeves’ report demonstrated that the proposal met the minimum 
standards of the Land Use Code and the intentions of the Comprehensive Plan.  The 
report states the conditional use permit would be approved with nine conditions of 
approval listed in the report.  
Public comments. Jerry Whited, Softball Tournament administrator, expressed 
concerns about traffic hazards for vehicle turning in and pulling out at that location.  
He suggested some additional warning signage could address the dangers of traffic 
behavior. Whited also encouraged the development of the business as he is aware 
of the shortfall of lodging in Dolores. Sheila Wheeler expressed concern that the 
business would have a cleaning station for the wildlife harvested by the RV 
customers. Wheeler commented that an adjacent property resident has trouble 
with trespassers along the riverbank.  Attorney Sumrall would tell the resident to 
post “No Trespassing” signs where access is denied. 
Mayor Holkestad has canvassed his neighbors on 21st street and received positive 
feedback for the business.   No further comments were received.   
Mayor Holkestad closed the public hearing at 7:48 pm. 
Mayor Holkestad made a motion and Mayor Pro Tem Curry seconded to approve the 
Conditional Use Permit for 18390 Highway 145 for The Dolores River Experience RV 
Park based on the nine conditions of approval listed here being met. 
Recommended Conditions of Approval 
1. Development of the RV park shall comply with the Recreational Vehicle/Travel 
Trailer Park standards of Section 4.5.B.3(a)–(f) of the Dolores Land Use Code. 
2. All applicable requirements of the Dolores Land Use Code shall be met prior to 
issuance of any building or development permits. 
3. Utility connections to each RV site shall be installed in accordance with the 
Town's adopted utility standards, building codes, and all applicable state and 
local regulations. 
4.  Final engineering drawings shall be reviewed and approved by the Town Building  
Inspector prior to construction. The Town will engage their contracted engineer 
if necessary. 
5.  All grading, drainage, erosion control, and utility improvements shall comply 
with applicable Town standards. 
6.  Any required floodplain development permits shall be obtained prior to  
construction. 
7. Exterior lighting shall be downward-directed and shielded from adjacent 
properties and the Dolores River corridor. 
8. Landscaping shall be installed and maintained in accordance with Article 7 of 
the Land Use Code. 
9.  Any material changes to the approved Conditional Use Permit or proposed use 
shall require review and approval by the Town in accordance with the Dolores 
Land Use Code. 
 
Yes: 6 
No: 0 
Motion carried. Conditional Use Permit for RV park at 18390 is approved.

12.1 Ice House Presentation: Jordan Lang  and Darby Dettloff o D & L LLC  will 
present their proposed development to the Board, followed by an explanation of the 
contract and terms to the Board.  
Attorney Sumrall addressed the Board regarding a question posed by Mayor Pro 
Tem Curry concerning the town’s liability if the project defaulted on the project.  
Sumrall assured the board that the town is indemnified and held harmless and is 
designated as a pass-through entity. 
Jordan Lang and her husband Darby Dettloff  described their history of owning the 
property west of Kelly’s Kitchen and the Bike shop. They purchased the lot and used 
the 750 square foot structure, formerly a commercial ice production facility, as a 
garage.  The undeveloped front area has been used recently for Food Truck Fridays, 
a music venue, and currently, a mobile retail business and an outdoor seating area 
for Kelly’s Kitchen.  
In 2025 Ms. Lang applied for a technical assistance grant with Colorado Housing 
and Finance Authority. This grant gave Jordan and Darby access to professional 
consultants to help work their way through the planning stage for architectural 
design and determine the housing need in Montezuma County.  The end result was 
that the cost of the project is unattainable.  Then the housing and finance authority 
came back with a pilot program that helps fund small-scale affordable housing 
projects in rural communities. The couple was able to get funding of $900,000 and a 
low interest loan from CHAFA.  The Affordable Housing project needs a resolution 
from the Town Board  to act as the applicant and grantee for this affordable housing 
development.   
12.1 Resolution 714: Authorizing the Town to act as applicant and grantee for a

liability.  
Mayor Holkestad made a motion and Mayor Pro Tem Curry seconded the motion to

13.2 G. Whited request for fee waiver for Park fee for the Escalante Day softball 
tournament. 
The request for fee waiver for Park fee for the Escalante Day softball tournament 
was discussed by the Board of trustees .  There is concern that this could create a 
precedent for other organizations to follow suit with waiver requests .  Generally 
non-profit organizations are the groups in the parks with the Large Group fee 
applications. The actual costs of providing services to the park for events are 
significant in respect to wear and tear on the grounds.  Jerry Whited is requesting 
the waiver for this year only so the money can be used for the registration fees to 
become a nonprofit corporation called #Joey Strong. Jerry expects the nonprofit 
status to be active in September this year.  Attorney Sumrall listed the fees 
associated with registering as a 501C3 nonprofit. If the annual gross receipts of a 
nonprofit organization are under $50,000 the fee is $275 and other associated costs 
for the articles of incorporation. Any organization earning above $50,000 pay $600 
to register.   
Trustee Beckman suggested a solution for waiving the fee by asking the Nonprofit 
organization #Joey Strong to apply through the Dolores Community Fund, a 
donation program intended to assist organizations within the Dolores community 
who provide services to the community members. Attorney Sumrall’s  suggestion 
was  to receive payment for the event upon approval of the nonprofit.  
The approval to waive the $500 fee this year only conditional on payment in 
September following the nonprofit status of #Joey Strong was made by consensus.
