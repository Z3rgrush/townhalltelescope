Minutes  October 12, 2026 
 
TOWN OF DOLORES 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES REGULAR MEETING MINUTES 
October 12, 2026 – 5:30 P.M. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Chris Holkestad called the regular meeting to order at 5:29 p.m. 
2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
3. ROLL CALL 
All Trustees were present except Trustee Derek Beckman. 
4. APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA 
Trustee Josh Maule moved to approve the agenda. Trustee Billy McCoy seconded. Motion 
carried unanimously. 
5. DISCLOSURE OF CONFLICTS OF INTEREST 
No conflicts of interest were disclosed. 
6. CONSENT AGENDA 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry moved to approve the Consent Agenda, including the 
September 22, 2026 workshop/meeting minutes and September 28, 2026 meeting 
minutes. Trustee Maule seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
7. ITEMS REMOVED FROM CONSENT AGENDA 
None. 
8. PUBLIC COMMENT 
Sheila Wheeler shared her thoughts regarding Indigenous Peoples' Day. 
Minutes  October 12, 2026 
Mike Sawyer thanked the Board for not adopting the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code. He 
expressed concerns regarding unfinished grant projects and suggested laying railroad 
track for the Galloping Goose. 
Val Truelson expressed appreciation for the trimming of Russian olive trees and suggested 
that completing park improvements should be a priority in the 2027 Budget. 
Deanna Truelson commented positively on the improvements to the Riverside Trail. 
9. STAFF / COMMITTEE REPORTS 
9.1 Sheriff's Report – Written report provided. 
9.2 Town Manager Report – Leigh Reeves 
Manager Reeves reviewed her written report and provided updates on ongoing Town 
projects. She noted that the Parks Committee would like to assist with the GOCO project 
and informed the Board of a CDOT grant opportunity for a safety study. 
9.3 Town Attorney Report – Ethan Sumrall 
Attorney Sumrall discussed options available to the Board for addressing the Dolores 
Public Library complaint. 
9.4 Treasurer Report – Kelley Unrein 
Treasurer Unrein presented the Treasurer's Report and reviewed the Town's financial 
information. 
9.5 Maintenance/Public Works Report – Written report provided. 
9.6 County Commissioner Report – No additional report noted. 
9.7 Community Center Advisory Board – Written report provided. 
9.8 Planning and Zoning Report – Written report provided. 
10. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
10.1 Mayor Chris Holkestad 
Mayor Holkestad encouraged residents to vote in the upcoming election, reminded the 
community of the scheduled cleanup days, and expressed interest in addressing the use of 
Flock license plate reader cameras. He also noted the upcoming Turkey Trot event. 
Minutes  October 12, 2026 
10.2 Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry commented on the Comprehensive Plan, noting that it is a 
substantial project. 
10.3 Trustee Josh Maule 
Trustee Maule commented positively on the recent bike festival and expressed interest in 
improving vehicle access to the park. He hoped community volunteers would participate in 
cleanup day and expressed opposition to Flock cameras. 
10.4 Trustee Billy McCoy 
Trustee McCoy commented on Flock cameras and noted that culverts may need to be 
cleaned to help prevent flooding. 
10.5 Trustee Derek Beckman 
Absent. 
10.6 Trustee Marie Roan 
Trustee Roan provided an update on the Chamber of Commerce's plans for Christmas 
activities. 
11. QUASI-JUDICIAL / ORDINANCES 
No additional items. 
12. DISCUSSION / POSSIBLE ACTION ITEMS 
12.1 Resolution No. 723 – Accepting the Resignation of Town Attorney Ethan J. Sumrall 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry moved to approve Resolution No. 723, accepting the resignation of 
Town Attorney Ethan J. Sumrall, effective November 1, 2026. Trustee Maule seconded. 
Motion carried unanimously. 
12.2 Resolution No. 724 – Approving a Non-Exclusive Easement Agreement with 
Clearnetworx, LLC 
Trustee Maule moved to approve Resolution No. 724, approving a non-exclusive easement 
agreement with Clearnetworx, LLC, for fiber optic communications facilities at 101 
Railroad Avenue. Mayor Pro Tem Curry seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
Minutes  October 12, 2026 
12.3 Resolution No. 725 – Approving a Legal Services Agreement with Holligan 
Westendorff, PLLC 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry moved to approve Resolution No. 725, approving a legal services 
agreement with Holligan Westendorff, PLLC. Trustee McCoy seconded. Motion carried 
unanimously. 
12.4 Ordinance No. 594 – Adopting the 2024 International Codes – First Reading 
Ryan Greene presented information regarding the proposed adoption of the 2024 
International Codes and discussed the updates with the Board. 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry moved to approve Ordinance No. 594 on first reading. Trustee Roan 
seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
13. ADMINISTRATIVE BUSINESS 
13.1 Search and Rescue Presentation 
Vicki Shaffer presented information regarding Montezuma Search and Rescue and 
requested that the Town consider allowing the organization to use or lease Town-owned 
property for a staging area. 
13.2 SB24-174 Housing Needs Assessment – Region 9 EDD 
Shak Powers presented information regarding the 2026 Region 9 and Montezuma County 
Housing Needs Assessment. The Board discussed the assessment and local housing 
needs. 
13.3 Preliminary 2027 Budget 
Treasurer Kelley Unrein presented the proposed 2027 Budget and reviewed the upcoming 
meetings and public hearing associated with the budget process. 
14. FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS / BOARD PARKING LOT 
The Board identified the following items to be added to the Future Agenda Items list: 
• Culvert maintenance and cleaning 
• Flock cameras 
• Fast Track Resolution 
• Affordable housing 
• Montezuma Search and Rescue property request 
Minutes  October 12, 2026 
15. EXECUTIVE SESSION 
None. 
16. ADJOURNMENT 
The meeting adjourned at 7:15 p.m.
