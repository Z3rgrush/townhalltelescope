 
 
TOWN OF DOLORES 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES WORKSHOP & REGULAR MEETING MINUTES 
April 27, 2026 – 5:30 P.M. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, CO 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry called the workshop to order at 5:30 p.m. 
2. ROLL CALL 
Present: 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry 
Trustee Josh Maule 
Trustee Bill McCoy 
Trustee Derek Beckman 
Trustee Marie Roan 
Trustee Linnea Peterson 
Absent: 
Mayor Chris Holkestad 
3. WORKSHOP 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry opened the workshop portion of the meeting. 
Town Manager Leigh Reeves presented a PowerPoint on board governance and meeting 
practices. 
The Board of Trustees discussed meeting procedures and requested the development of 
“cheat sheets” with example phrases for use on the dais to assist with meeting 
management and participation. 
Mayor Pro Tem Curry transitioned from the workshop to the formal meeting. 
4. APPROVAL OF AGENDA 
Motion: Trustee Beckman 
Second: Trustee Roan 
Vote: Unanimous approval

Town Clerk Kelley Unrein introduced Resolution No. 705.

appointments:

• Josh Maule – Parks Committee 
• Bill McCoy – Community Center Advisory Board 
• Derek Beckman – Library 
• Marie Roan – Chamber of Commerce 
Second to Amendment: Trustee Maule 
Vote on Amendment: Unanimous approval 
Motion to Approve Resolution No. 705 as Amended: Trustee Beckman
