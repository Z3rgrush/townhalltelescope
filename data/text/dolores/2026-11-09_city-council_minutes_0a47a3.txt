TOWN OF DOLORES 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES REGULAR MEETING MINUTES 
November 9, 2026 – 5:29 P.M. 
Town Hall, 601 Central Avenue, Dolores, Colorado 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Chris Holkestad called the regular meeting to order at 5:29 p.m. 
2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
3. ROLL CALL 
Present: Mayor Chris Holkestad, Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry, Trustees Josh Maule, Derek 
Beckman, and Marie Roan. Absent: Trustee Billy McCoy. 
4. APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry moved to approve the agenda. Trustee Marie Roan seconded. 
Motion carried unanimously. 
5. DISCLOSURE OF CONFLICTS OF INTEREST 
No conflicts of interest were disclosed. 
6. CONSENT AGENDA 
Trustee Josh Maule moved to approve the Consent Agenda, including the October 26, 2026 
meeting minutes and October 2026 check register. Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry seconded. 
Motion carried unanimously. 
7. ITEMS REMOVED FROM CONSENT AGENDA 
None. 
8. PUBLIC COMMENT 
• Town Manager Leigh Reeves provided a brief update regarding the November 1–2 rain 
event. 
• Mayor Holkestad congratulated Rodney Cox on his election as county commissioner. 
• Val Truelson noted that he had seen the river reach high levels on previous occasions 
and asked about the Town’s capital plans for 2027. 

Minutes                                                    November 9, 2026 
• Rachel Ashmore and her neighbor Jay described excess water affecting their properties 
during the rain event and identified areas they believed could be improved. Photographs 
of their homes were provided to the Town Clerk for inclusion in the November 23 
meeting packet under public comment. 
• Sara Vass expressed her view that the Dolores Community Center is a community asset 
rather than a business. 
• Jen Stark thanked the maintenance team for its work during the rain event. 
• Rachel Vass separately thanked the maintenance team and asked the Board to 
consider community priorities regarding the Community Center. 
• Claire Martini recommended developing an after-action plan concerning the rain event. 
9. STAFF / COMMITTEE REPORTS 
9.1 Sheriff’s Report: The Sheriff reported that Halloween went well, reviewed the 
month’s calls for service, and recommended a message board to communicate with 
residents who do not have phones. 
9.2 Town Manager Report: Town Manager Leigh Reeves was available to answer 
questions regarding her written report. 
9.3 Town Attorney Report: Town Attorney Julie Holligan-Westendorf reported that she 
had reviewed the Sheriff’s contract and was working on the Atmos contract. 
9.4 Treasurer Report: The Treasurer’s report was presented. 
9.5 Maintenance/Public Works Report: The written report was included in the meeting 
packet. 
9.6 County Commissioner Report: Commissioner Jim Candelaria reported that the 
landfill would likely accept green waste at no charge for a period to assist with rain-
event cleanup. He also reported that Mancos remained under a boil-water order and 
that one road remained closed. 
9.7 Community Center Advisory Board: Jen Stark presented the Advisory Board 
materials. 
9.8 Planning and Zoning Report: A written report was included in the agenda packet. 
10. TRUSTEE REPORTS 
10.1 Mayor Holkestad suggested a workshop concerning the recent rain event and 
planning for future events. 
10.2 Mayor Pro Tem Curry encouraged residents to follow Planning and Zoning and the 
Comprehensive Plan process. 
10.3 Trustee Maule commented that Halloween went well and presented ideas for flood 
planning. 
10.4 Trustee McCoy was absent. 
Minutes                                                    November 9, 2026 
10.5 Trustee Beckman thanked Town staff and noted the value of providing clear 
direction to volunteers and others assisting during an emergency. 
10.6 Trustee Roan asked about Montezuma Search and Rescue’s request to use Town 
land and was informed that the organization was not ready to proceed with a building. 
She also reported on upcoming holiday activities planned by the Chamber of 
Commerce. 
11. QUASI-JUDICIAL / ORDINANCES 
11.1 Ordinance No. 594, Series 2026 — Second Reading and Public Hearing: The public 
hearing opened at 6:38 p.m. Building Official Ryan Greene reviewed the ordinance 
adopting the 2024 International Code Council codes and answered questions. During 
public comment, Val Truelson asked whether the new code would require triple-pane 
windows; the answer was no. The public hearing closed at 6:43 p.m. 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Curry moved to approve Ordinance No. 594, Series 2026, on 
second and final reading. Trustee Derek Beckman seconded. The motion passed 
unanimously by roll-call vote. 
12. DISCUSSION / POSSIBLE ACTION ITEMS 
12.1 Resolution No. 727, Series 2026 — Allocation to Montezuma County Senior 
Services: Trustee Marie Roan moved to approve Resolution No. 727. Trustee Derek 
Beckman seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
12.2 Resolution No. 728, Series 2026 — 2027 Montezuma County Sheriff’s Contract: 
Trustee Josh Maule moved to approve Resolution No. 728. Trustee Marie Roan 
seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
12.3 Resolution No. 729, Series 2026 — Appointment of Leigh Reeves to Region 9: 
Trustee Derek Beckman moved to approve Resolution No. 729. Mayor Pro Tem Chris 
Curry seconded. Motion carried unanimously. 
13. ADMINISTRATIVE BUSINESS 
13.1 Budget Update: The Board had no questions regarding the budget update. 
14. FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS / BOARD PARKING LOT 
The Board added flood planning, snow removal, and a notice board to the future agenda items 
list. Flock cameras and the Search and Rescue property request were removed. Town Manager 
Reeves stated that the Fast Track Resolution would be on the November 23, 2026 agenda. 
15. EXECUTIVE SESSION 
No executive session was held. 
16. ADJOURNMENT 
The meeting adjourned at 6:51 p.m.
