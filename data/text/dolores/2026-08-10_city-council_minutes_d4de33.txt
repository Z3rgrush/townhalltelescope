11.1. SECOND READING AND PUBLIC HEARING — ORDINANCE 591 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 7:08 p.m. No public comment was received. 
ACTION: Trustee Peterson moved to approve Ordinance 591, establishing authority for 
fees to be set by resolution; establishing procedures for license and permit renewal, 
delinquency, expiration, and reinstatement; authorizing municipal court administrative 
fees; and providing for related matters. Trustee Beckman seconded. The motion passed 
unanimously. 
The public hearing closed at 7:17 p.m. 
11.2. FIRST READING AND PUBLIC HEARING — ORDINANCE 592 
Mayor Holkestad opened the public hearing at 7:20 p.m. Attorney Sumrall advised the 
Board regarding a potential conflict of interest. 
Sara Pritchard presented the staff report. She explained that a traffic study would occur 
during review of a specific use application rather than during the zoning-map amendment. 
Trustee Peterson requested clarification regarding language in the 250-foot notice letter. 
Applicant Broc Smith stated that the requested change from Downtown Mixed Use (DMU) 
to Neighborhood Residential-3 (NR-3) was intended to help meet the community’s need for 
housing. 
Public comment: Marianne Mate renewed her earlier comments and requested that a 
traffic study be completed during the zoning process. Rachel Vass stated that changing the 
zoning could eliminate future business opportunities. Tracy Keuric stated that she did not 
want high-density housing or businesses. Greg renewed his earlier comments and 
questioned whether NR-2 zoning would constitute spot zoning. 
 
August 10, 2026 4 
During Board discussion, Trustee Peterson again asked about a traffic study. Staff 
reiterated that such a study would be addressed during review of an actual use application 
and that whether to require one during zoning review was an administrative determination. 
Trustee Beckman observed that many concerns related to possible future development 
rather than a use currently proposed. Mayor Holkestad stated that consistency was 
appropriate. Attorney Sumrall summarized the matter that would ultimately come before 
the Board for decision. 
The public hearing closed at 8:20 p.m. No vote was taken on first reading; Ordinance 592 
will be considered for action at second reading. 
12.1. CODE ENFORCEMENT REVIEW 
Manager Reeves reported that nuisance-code violators had been issued notices. Trustee 
McCoy stated that additional discussion was needed and requested a workshop on the 
subject. 
12.2. RESOLUTION 715 — PUBLIC RECORDS REQUEST POLICY AND FORM 
ACTION: The Board approved Resolution 715, approving the Public Records Request 
Administration Policy and Public Records Request Form. The action passed unanimously. 
12.3. RESOLUTION 716 — UPDATED TOWN FEE SCHEDULE 
ACTION: The Board approved Resolution 716 adopting the updated Town fee schedule as 
presented. The action passed unanimously. 
13.1. PUBLIC COMMENT AT BUSINESS AND WORKSHOP MEETINGS 
The Board tabled a decision so the matter could be discussed further at the September 
workshop. 
13.2. TRUSTEE COMMITTEE FOR TOWN ATTORNEY SEARCH 
Mayor Holkestad, Mayor Pro Tem Curry, and Trustee Beckman were selected to serve on 
the committee leading the search for a new Town Attorney. Clerk/Treasurer Unrein 
requested that the committee provide meeting dates and times so the meetings could be 
properly noticed. 
14. ADJOURNMENT 
The meeting adjourned at 8:36 p.m.
