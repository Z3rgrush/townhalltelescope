CHALLENGES
THE REGULATORY FLOODPLAIN
The major barrier to reinvestment is the regulatory floodplain, not the 
frequency or severity of flooding.
Merriam Drive is in the floodway, not just the 
floodplain. Both ways in and out of 
Downtown are.
The 50% rule caps reinvestment. 
Improvements over half a building’s value 
require full floodplain compliance, so 
buildings stay Class C.
Turkey Creek is flashy: 2.5 to 3 inches of rain 
exceeds the channel, with about a 50% 
chance in any given year.
Flood protection projects reduce risk. They 
do not eliminate it.

WHAT THE 50% RULE MEANS FOR A DOWNTOWN BUILDING
In the regulatory floodplain, federal flood insurance rules require a building to be brought fully into compliance when 
improvements or repairs reach 50% of the building's value. Renovation below that threshold — new tenant space, 
systems, doors, windows, lighting, façade work — generally does not.
