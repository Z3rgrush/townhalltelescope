2. PUD26-000001-IKEA PARKING DECK REMOVAL FINAL DEVELOPMENT PLAN
Community Development Director, Bryan Dyer, presented the request for a revised final 
development plan for IKEA’s Parking Structure, located at 6000 IKEA Way. Mr. Dyer stated 
that the request is to remove the parking structure which is approximately 65,000 square 
feet. The structure is at the end of its lifespan and a maintenance concern. The structure is 
generally not used. The applicant provided a statement that they are comfortable with the 
amount of parking spaces that will remain and are confident there will be enough parking for 
their customers’ needs.
Chairperson Bill Bailey asked if there were any issues with the surface drainage.
Mr. Dyer stated that the applicant provided an engineer’s statement that the existing 
infrastructure will be able to handle the drainage and that the removal of the structure will 
result in a small increase in greenspace.
Chairperson Bill Bailey asked if the commission had any questions for staff.
Commissioner Nida Dillion asked about the timeline on how long the project will take.
Mr. Dyer stated that would be an appropriate question for the applicant.
Commissioner Sarah Limbocker asked will there be a reduction in accessible parking with 
the removal of the parking structure.
Mr. Dyer stated that he does not believe there is any accessible parking on the upper level.

Staff Comments: 
 
The applicant and property owner, Richard Oddo; AMR Properties, is requesting approval 
of Hardman’s Resurvey of Johnson’s Subdivision Final Plat for property located at 6126 
Merriam Lane. The subject property was originally platted in 1 887 as part of Hardman’s 
Resurvey of Johnson’s Subdivision plat.  The property was rezoned from R-3 (Two-Family 
Residential) and C-2 (Retail Commercial) Districts to I-1 (Light Industrial) District in 1978 
(Ord. 738). The property is developed with two (2) existing industrial buildings . The 
eastern building was constructed prior to 1978 and the western building was constructed 
in 1978.   
 
The plat will create two (2) lots. Lot 1 will be 20,730 square feet and Lot 2 will be 20,000 
square feet.  
 
The proposed plat will allow for separate ownership of both lots . No changes or 
improvements are to either Lot are being proposed with this plat.  
 
Comments from Public Utilities 
 
As part of the review process, staff provided copies of the proposed plat to the public 
utilities and city departments. 
 
The applicant acknowledged and addressed all comments. 
 
Access 
 
The existing access point  off W. 62nd Street for Lot 1 and Lot 2  will remain.  An access 
easement is being dedicated with this plat providing a shared access point to the two lots.   
 
Floodway/Floodway Fringe (Floodplain) 
 
The subject property is adjacent to Turkey Creek and borders a parcel of land that is 
controlled by the Merriam Drainage District.  A portion of Lot 2 and the building located 
are the lot are in the floodplain. 
 
Easements/Dedication of Rights of Way 
 
This final plat dedicates an ingress/egress easement between Lot 1 and Lot 2 to provide 
access to both lots.  
 
The plat does not dedicated any additional right-of-way or easements. 
 
Lot Size and Bulk Requirements  
 
The subject property is a zon ed I-1 District . The proposed lots meet the lot size  
requirements for lots in the I-1 District. The existing lot does not meet current setbacks.
