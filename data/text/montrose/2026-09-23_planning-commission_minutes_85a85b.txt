City of Montrose Planning Commission
September 23, 2026
The Montrose City Planning Commission held a meeting on September 23, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. in the City
Council Chambers. The meeting agenda was posted in accordance with the Colorado Open Meetings Act
C.R.S. § 24-6-401, et.seq.).
Planning Commissioners Present: David Fishering ( Chair), Chad Huffman ( Vice - Chair), Phoebe Benziger,
and Ronald Cairns. Absent: Delphine Jadot, Richard Rogers, Beth McCorkle, and Steven Bail.
Staff Members Present: Gregory Stunder ( Asst. City Attorney), Jace Hochwalt ( Community Development
Director), Sierra Lucero ( Associate Planner), Scott Murphy ( City Engineer), Lisa DelPiccolo ( City Clerk).
Guests Present: Envision 2040 Consulting Team Gerald Dahl and Martin Landers.
There were two additional members of the public in attendance: Doug Glaspell and Aaron Ranstrom.
CaII to Order
Chairperson David Fishering called the meeting to order at 5:02 p.m.
Approval of Minutes
Phoebe Benziger moved to approve the minutes of the August 12, 2026, meeting as submitted. Ronald
Cairns seconded, and the motion carried unanimously.
Additions or Deletions
None.
Comprehensive Plan Updates
Jace Hochwalt gave an overview and history of the Envision 2040 comprehensive plan, and reported on
the recent state - mandated long-range planning requirements for local governments, including a housing
needs assessment, housing action plan, water supply, and strategic growth. The housing assessment is
completed, and staff is working on the remaining mandates.
Staff is working on a targeted, standard five-year technical update to the comprehensive plan, with the
goal being a shorter, more user-friendly, readable plan, as well as updating the land development
regulations, specifically code and the inclusion of state mandates.
