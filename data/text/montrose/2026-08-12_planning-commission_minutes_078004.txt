Filing No. 2, also addressed as 739-749 East Star Court. This application subdivides the townhomes,
which are under construction, into 6 individually conveyable units. The applicant is Justin Tanner with
Forza Red, LLC.

William Reis introduced this item. All public requirements have been fulfilled, and the official files and
exhibits have been entered into the record.
Applicant Presentation
Design engineer Brandon Julian, applicant's representative, approached the podium to answer any
questions.

None.
Public Comment
None.
Motion and Vote
Delphine Jadot moved to recommend to City Council approval of the Preliminary Plat application with
the following condition(s). The approval of this Preliminary Plat is expressly conditioned upon City staff

CHELSI LANE & TOWNHOMES AMENDED PD PLAN
This is a hearing for a proposed amendment to the Chelsi Replat Amended PD Plan and Preliminary Plat.
The previously approved PD plan from 1992 approved 24 units in this portion of the property. This PD
amendment reduces the number to 23. There are no further changes to the PD plan. The applicant is
Selvin Sandoval with El Chapin Investment Group, LLC.
Staff Presentation
William Reis introduced this item. All public requirements have been fulfilled, and the official files and
exhibits have been entered into the record. Scott Murphy gave an infrastructure overview, includingdrainage, sewer, sidewalks, and fire protection.
Questions for Staff
Staff responded to questions from Planning Commissioners about how the sidewalk code requirement
differences would be handled, road dimensions, paving, and number of vehicle trips per day.
Applicant Presentation
Applicant Selvin Sandoval approached the podium to answer any questions.
Questions for Applicant
None.
Public Comment
Members of the public approached the podium with concerns about lack of existing sidewalks,
pedestrian safety and noise due to potential traffic influx, water pressure, and emergency services
access. A member of the public provided a street photograph to the Commission.
Discussion
Planning Commissioners and staff discussed access, measurements, water pressure, and traffic volume.
Motion and Vote
Phoebe Benziger moved to recommend to City Council approval of the Amended Preliminary Planned
Development application with the following conditions). The approval of this Amended PreliminaryPlanned Development is expressly conditioned upon City staff ensuring that all policies, regulations,
ordinances and municipal code provisions are met and that the Applicant adequately addresses all of
staffs concerns prior to the execution of the Final Planned Development. The City staff is not authorized
by this approval to execute the Final Plat prior to all conditions being satisfied. The request meets the
Code criteria based on the evidence and testimony presented at this hearing and in the staff report.
Beth McCorkle seconded, and the motion passed unanimously.

CHELSI LANE & TOWNHOMES PRELIMINARY PLAT
This is a hearing for a proposed subdivision located on a property accessed off of Margot Court. This plat
renews the undeveloped portion of the Chelsi Replat Subdivision and proposes 23 individually
conveyable townhome lots. The applicant is Selvin Sandoval with El Chapin Investment group, LLC.
Staff Presentation
None.
Questions for Staff
Planning Commissioners and staff discussed how community priorities and standards have changed over
time. Staff confirmed the original plat had sidewalks for the cul de sac entrance, which would be
continued in this development, and that both the fire marshal and emergency 911 coordinator reviewed
and approved the plan.
Applicant Presentation
None.
Questions for Applicant
None.
Public Comments
Members of the public came forward with concerns and questions, including why the entrance is not off
a main artery, whether ingress/egress will accommodate two-way traffic, traffic projections, navigation
of City trash/recycle vehicles and other large vehicles, and if the new units will have garages.
Discussion
Planning Commissioners and staff addressed the questions posed by members of the public, including
that this is a part of the 1992 plan, which is a continuation of Margo Court as a whole. Access, traffic
volume, fire marshal review, vehicle trips per day, access point, and trash/recycle were discussed.
Motion and Vote
Chad Huffman moved to recommend to City Council approval of the Preliminary Plat application with
the following condition(s). The approval of this Preliminary Plat is expressly conditioned upon City staff
ensuring that all policies, regulations, ordinances and municipal code provisions are met and that the
Applicant adequately addresses all of staff's concerns prior to the execution of the Final Plat. The City
staff is not authorized by this approval to execute the Final Plat prior to all conditions being satisfied.
The request meets the Code criteria based on the evidence and testimony presented at this hearing and
in the staff report. Richard Rogers seconded, and the motion passed unanimously.
The application is tentatively scheduled to go before City Council on September 15th; William Reis will
confirm with applicant.
