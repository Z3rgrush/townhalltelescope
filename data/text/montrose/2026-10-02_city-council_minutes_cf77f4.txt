DISCUSSION OF COUNCIL MEMBER VACANCY
City Attorney Chris Dowsey reported that City Councilors contacted him with questions related
to the current District lV vacancy including how to expand the pool of applicants, and if more
time is possible to make the appointment. Mr. Dowsey stated that the District lV seat was vacated
on September 7, and the City Charter requires an appointment to the vacant seat within 30 days.
Mr. Dowsey reviewed possible paths forward if an appointment is not made in accordance with
the City Charter and said a specialelection would be the likely result.
City Councilor i. David Reed submitted a written proposal into the record of the meeting asking
City Council to commit to moving forward together by striving for a unanimous decision on the
appointment.
Mr. Reed proposed that City Council consider appointing Adam Woodden to the District lV seat
which would create a vacancy in the At-Large seat. The application process would reopen
allowing a diverse pool of applicants from throughout the City and providing City Council an
opportunity to identify an applicant that all four City Council members could support. Mr. Reed
stated that Mr. Woodden's current term is not extended by moving to the District lV seat.
Mr. Reed reviewed past City Council vacancy processes including a similar circumstance in 2017.
Mr. Reed said the objective is to expand the public process and provide a better opportunity for
City Council to agree on an appointment.
Mr. Reed asked that City Council confirm Mr. Woodden's willingness to resign the At-Large seat
for appointment to the District lV seat and reopen the application process. Citizens who
submitted applications for the District lV seat would not need to reapply.
Mr. Woodden confirmed that he is willing to proceed with the resignation and appointment. Mr.
Dowsey stated that this is a legally available option, and Mr. Woodden would be appointed until
the next general or special municipal election.
lnterim City Manager Ann Morgenthaler reviewed a potential timeline. Mr. Woodden would
resign the At-Large seat before October 6. The District lV seat would be filled on October 6, and
the application process would open on October 7 with a deadline of October 21. An update on
the number of applicants will be provided at the October 20 City Council meeting to determine
if a list of finalists is warranted. lnterviews would be conducted at the November 2 work session,
with appointment at the regular City Council meeting on November 2.
Applicants will be updated following this meeting and informed that they can carry their
applications forward for the At-Large seat if desired.
Mr. Dowsey stated that no action is necessary at this meeting and recommended canceling the
october 5 work session since the interviews were the only topic. The October 6 City Council
meeting agenda will be amended to include the appointment of Mr. Woodden to the District lV
seat. An oath of office will be administered following Mr. Woodden's appointment.
Mr. Reed stated that the current pool of applicants might not produce a candidate that would
receive unanimous support and suggested pursuing an opportunity to broaden the pool of
applica nts.
Mr. Dowsey stated that a letter of resignation is required from Mr. Woodden and recommended
it is submitted to the city clerk before Tuesday, October 6. Mr. Dowsey said the letter can include
an effective date.
A special meeting for an executive session was scheduled for Monday, October 5, at 10 a.m. for
mid-cycle evaluations for the municipal court judge and the city attorney. lt was recommended
that Mr. Woodden's letter state that his resignation is effective at the end of the business day on
October 5 to allow his participation in the special meeting.
ADJOURNMENT
At 11:54 a.m. the meeting adjourned with no further action taken.
