MONTROSE URBAN RENEWAL AUTHORIW EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR APPOINTMENT
City Council considered the appointment of Ann Morgenthaler as Montrose Urban Renewal
Authority (MURA) Executive Director.
City Attorney Chris Dowsey reported that this topic was discussed at the work session yesterday.
Currently, Bill Bell is the appointed executive director for MURA. When the City and Mr. Bell
mutually parted ways, a vacancy resulted in the MURA executive director position. Mr. Dowsey
stated that the City has found it beneficial to have the city manager serve as MURA executive
director which allows for streamlined and coordinated action. Pursuant to the MURA bylaws, the
executive director sha ll be appointed by City Council or the city manager. Mr. Dowsey stated that
for the sake of continuity, it is presented to City Council to appoint Ann Morgenthaler as MURA
Executive Director.

A motion was made by J. David Reed, seconded by Ed Ulibarri, to appoint Ann Morgenthaler as

RESOLUTTON 2026-14
City Council considered Resolution 2026-14 authorizing the filing of a Colorado Parks and Wildlife
Non-Motorized Trail - Planning and Design grant application for the Montrose Audubon in the
amount up to 545,000.00.
Community Development Director Jace Hochwalt reported that this item was discussed at the
work session yesterday and is expedited to meet the grant application deadline. Mr. Hochwalt
requested authorization of a grant application for planning and design of the Montrose Audubon
Project on a 25-acre city property located off of Marine Road. The City completed a conceptual
design for the site which is envisioned as a nature-based outdoor recreation and educational
destination focused on wildlife viewing, accessibility, habitat restoration, and connecting the
community with the natural environment. Mr. Hochwalt said the concept includes accessible
trails and boardwalks, bird blinds, and viewing areas, and the pro.iect also includes removal of
invasive species.
Mr. Waschbusch reviewed the bid process and said five proposals were received. Mr.
Waschbusch recommended awarding a contract to FCI Constructors which submitted the low bid
of 5467,283.00. The contract includes a 10 percent owners contingency. Mr. Waschbusch said
the project will begin mid-October after irrigation season concludes.
Mr. Hochwalt stated that the grant funding would take the conceptual design to the next level of
developing a detailed construction plan and construction drawings. The City is requesting up to
545,000.00 through the grant program, and a 25 percent match is required. The grant match of
approximately S15,000.00 is proposed within the 2027 budget. Mr. Hochwalt said that
construction-ready plans are used to determine the actual costs, refine future phasing, and
pursue future grant funding. Grant awards will be announced in March of 2027.
