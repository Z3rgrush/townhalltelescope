Re: Solid Waste Code Revisions Recommendation

● Exhibit A - Existing Chapter 3-1, Trash Collection 
● Exhibit B - Proposed Chapter 3-1, Solid Waste Collection Services 
 
Action 
Consider the approval of a comprehensive update to Chapter 3-1 Trash Collection. 
 
Background 
Public Works staff and City Attorney staff have developed a comprehensive rewrite of Chapter 
3-1 Trash Collection to make service requirements clearer and support consistent collection. 
Renamed Solid Waste Collection Services, Chapter 3-1 would establish separate residential, 
non-residential, special collection, and recycling provisions.  
Principal changes include: 
● Curbside Recycling. Retains voluntary participation without an additional collection 
charge for eligible residential customers and references Colorado producer responsibility 
funding. Specifies collection every other week and adds overfill, clearance, mechanical 
emptying, and replacement rules. Allows recycling service termination for 
noncompliance, in addition to refusing contaminated containers. 
● Trash Collection and Container Standards. Specifies weekly residential collection, 
mechanical emptying, container orientation and loading, and no residential collection on 
private property. Defines overfilling by whether the lid closes; allows refusal or an 
additional charge for certain service problems; and extends same-day trash cart removal 
to alleys and other locations. Adds customer responsibility for lost or stolen containers 
and replacement terms for normal wear and other requests. 
● Special and Bulk Services. Replaces prior approval for loose material with scheduled, 
paid bulk collection and Code Enforcement referral when unscheduled. Identifies 
construction, landscape, automotive, oversized household, and other materials excluded 
from regular service. Establishes temporary 300-gallon rentals with one included 
monthly collection, additional pickups, and discretionary premium services subject to 
available resources. 
● Hazardous Waste and Expanded Recycling. Expressly prohibits medical and 
electronic waste in regular containers and authorizes special hazardous waste events. 
Permits optional, fee-based programs for tires, televisions and monitors, bulbs, batteries, 
refrigerated appliances, green waste, and compost, plus future recycling services.

● Service Eligibility and Coverage. Adds City and private-hauler service provisions tied 
to 1994 annexation boundaries, outside-city utility customers, and limited self-hauling. 
Residential treatment for multifamily properties generally applies to four or fewer 
separately billed units; the existing code expressly includes up to eight units for curbside 
recycling. Adds screening and collection-access requirements for properties with five or 
more units, except alley locations. 
● Separates Residential from Non-Residential. Clarifies the difference between the two 
and what determines if an account is one or the other. 
● Billing and Service Terms. Establishes minimum service periods of eight months for 
residential service and three months for non-residential service; eliminates residential 
partial-month proration; and allows outside-city charges up to 1.5 times normal fees. 
Adds a monthly service fee alongside the per-gallon charge for 300-gallon non-
residential service, eliminating the purchase of the bin for new customers; Adds a 90-
gallon option with once- or twice-weekly collection. 
● Existing Billing Provisions that have been Removed or Revised. Does not carry 
forward the current vacancy exemption, multifamily opt-out provision, annual CPI 
adjustment mechanism, or container-return refund language.  
● Account Administration and Enforcement. Requires contact information updates 
within 30 days and electronic service applications; sets requested termination at the end 
of the next billing cycle. Revises termination notices to allow email and other contact 
methods, changes the Council appeal condition from a deposit to payment, and requires 
all amounts due for restoration. Adds penalties for each day of a violation and nuisance 
abatement authority while retaining owner liability and collection remedies. 
 
Fiscal Impact 
The proposed revised Solid Waste Code changes billing terms and authorizes additional 
charges but does not establish a rate increase. Dollar amounts remain in the City’s approved 
fee schedule.

Chapter 3-1 Solid Waste Collection Services
Sec 3-1-1 Applicability. 
A. All customers of city solid waste collection shall be subject to the terms and conditions 
as set out in this Chapter and shall be subject to all applicable regulations of this Code, 
the City Charter, and other ordinances, resolutions, and regulations of the City, as they 
now exist or as they may be amended in the future.
B. The owner of any property served by city solid waste collection systems shall be liable 
for all fees, charges, and penalties imposed by this Chapter and for compliance with 
other requirements of this Chapter, notwithstanding any agreement among themselves. 
All service changes shall be made under the property owner's/landlord's name. 
C. No person other than as authorized and otherwise in compliance with applicable 
statutes, rules and regulations shall collect, convey over any of the streets in the City, or 
dispose of, any refuse accumulated in the City. The City shall have the sole and 
exclusive right to serve and charge for service any and all residential customers of the 
City existing as of April 19, 1994. Areas annexed on or after April 19, 1994, may be 
served by the City or a private refuse provider in accordance with §  30-15-401, C.R.S. 
This article shall not prohibit the actual producer of refuse, or the owner or occupant of 
premises upon which refuse has accumulated, from personally collecting, conveying and 
disposing of such refuse provided such refuse is of a nature not normally collectible or is 
in addition to the amount normally collected from the producer and the disposal of such 
refuse is in compliance with the provisions of this article and with any other applicable 
ordinance(s), statute(s), rules and regulations. Hauling by the producer shall not affect 
the obligation of residential refuse producer(s) or owner(s) to pay the charges 
established by this article.
Sec 3-1-2 Residential Solid Waste Collection Service
A. A residential solid waste collection customer is defined as any customer who has solid 
waste collection service associated with a single-family dwelling or multi-family dwelling 
of four or less units. Customers living outside of City limits may be charged up to 1.5 
times the normal solid waste collection service fees.  Units in multi-dwelling buildings will 
be billed separately.  The City may, at its discretion, discontinue residential service to 
customers who do not comply with the provisions of this Chapter.  City residential water 
and sewer service customers will be charged for City solid waste collection services 
regardless of whether the customer uses this service unless the customer’s property 
was annexed into the City after April 19, 1994 and the customer provides proof that they 
are receiving service from another collection service provider.
B. All residential customers are required to provide accurate and up-to-date contact 
information (i.e. mailing address, phone number, and email address).  Contact 
information must be updated within 30 days of any changes.
Page 15 of 40
C. Residential solid waste collection service will not be provided for a period of less than 
eight continuous months.
D. The City, at its expense, will provide one 90-gallon container to each residential 
customer when collection service is initiated.  The City will determine if a residential 
customer will be required to use an individual container or a shared container. The City 
will provide and maintain shared containers at the City’s expense.  
E. A residential customer may request an additional 90-gallon container (for residential use 
only) by electronic submission using the application provided on the City’s website. 
Requests for additional containers shall be for a minimum continuous service period of 
eight months. Customers will be charged an additional residential collection fee for the 
additional container pursuant to the fee schedule.
F. All residential solid waste customers will be charged pursuant to the City’s fee schedule.  
Service fees will not be pro-rated if the service period is less than one month.
G. Residential solid waste collection shall be on a once-a-week pickup schedule.  
H. The City, at its discretion, may provide additional special solid waste collection services 
for an additional fee to be determined by the City.
I. The City, in its sole discretion, may bill a surcharge for increased landfill or disposal 
costs without updating the fee schedule.
J. Solid waste containers are owned by the City and may not be removed from the service 
address.
K. The City will only collect solid waste from City-provided solid waste containers. The City, 
in its sole discretion, may approve other types of containers for residential customers.
L. An overfilled container may not be collected depending upon circumstances and the City 
may charge an overfilled container fee if the container is collected.  A container is 
overfilled when the container lid cannot be completely closed.
M. Solid waste that is not inside the City-provided solid waste container will not be collected 
unless the customer has scheduled and agreed to pay for bulk solid waste collection. If a 
bulk solid waste collection appointment has not been scheduled, the incident will be 
reported to the City’s Code Enforcement Unit.
N. The following items are not included in regular solid waste collection service; customers 
must contact the City to confirm if any of the materials are eligible for bulk solid waste or 
recycling services: 
1. Construction materials including, but not limited to, scrap lumber, concrete, 
asphalt, roofing materials, doors/windows, piping, siding, masonry and brick.
2. Landscape materials including, but not limited to, fill dirt, rocks, fencing, 
landscape timbers and large tree branches.
3. Automotive parts including, but not limited to, tires and batteries.
4. Freight packaging including, but not limited to, pallets.
5. Large household items including, but not limited to, furniture and appliances.
6. Televisions and monitors.
O. The customer shall be responsible for cleaning and maintaining all City-provided 
containers in serviceable condition. The City may perform minor repairs to the containers 
as appropriate at the City's discretion. Replacement of a container due to customer 
negligence or neglect shall be at the customer's expense. Lost or stolen containers are

the responsibility of the customer and customers will be charged for lost or stolen 
containers pursuant to the fee schedule. A used container may be provided as a 
replacement, if available.
P. The City, in its sole discretion, may replace solid waste containers due to normal wear.  
Customer requests to replace solid waste containers for any other reason will be at the 
customer’s expense pursuant to the City’s fee schedule.
Q.  Residential customers shall fill solid waste containers in such a manner that allows for 
all solid waste to be removed when the container is mechanically lifted and inverted.  
The customer shall also position the container with the lid opening facing outward in 
order to facilitate collection.  All solid waste that cannot be removed from containers 
when the container is mechanically lifted and inverted will remain in the container.  City

or alley abutting their service address, or in such other location as may be designated by 
the City, by 6:00 a.m. on the day of collection. Containers shall be removed from the 
street, alley, or other location on the same day that the solid waste or recyclable material 
is collected.
S. Solid waste containers shall be placed in a location, on collection day, with at least three 
feet of clearance from all other objects.  Noncompliance may result in non-collection or 
an additional fee pursuant to the fee schedule.
T. The City, in its sole discretion, will resolve noncompliance with this Code.
U. City solid waste equipment operators will not collect residential solid waste on private 
property.
V. A solid waste container of any size will not be collected if the weight or distribution of its 
contents prevents the container from being safely lifted and/or emptied.  The City will 
provide notice to the customer if the container cannot be safely collected, and the 
customer shall reduce or redistribute the contents of the container to allow for safe 
collection.  The customer may request an additional collection after the customer has 
reduced or redistributed the contents of the container.  The additional collection may be 
subject to an additional fee pursuant to the fee schedule.

Sec 3-1-5 Special solid waste collection services
A. Rental solid waste container service: Any residential or non-residential customer may 
request a temporary 300-gallon solid waste container for an additional monthly rental fee 
pursuant to the fee schedule.  The monthly rental fee includes one collection per month.  
Customers shall schedule collections, but the container will be collected even if a 
collection has not been scheduled by the customer if the container is full.  The customer 
will be charged a collection fee pursuant to the fee schedule if there has been more than 
one monthly collection.
B. Additional collection service: Residential and non-residential solid waste customers may 
request additional collections which will be billed pursuant to the fee schedule. 
C. Bulk solid waste collection: Residential and non-residential solid waste customers may 
request a bulk solid waste collection for an additional fee pursuant to the fee schedule. 
Bulk solid waste pickup is defined as any solid waste pickup that does not fit into the 
City’s solid waste containers.  
D. Premium solid waste collection service: Residential and non-residential solid waste 
customers may apply for premium solid waste collection service.  This service is subject 
to available resources. Premium solid waste collection service is any service that is not 
set forth in this Chapter.  Fees for premium solid waste collection service will be 
determined solely by the City based on the services provided.

E. Refrigerated appliance disposal service: The City may offer a refrigerated appliance

F. Green waste drop-off program and compost service: The City may offer a green waste 
drop-off service and/or compost program. The City is permitted to charge fees for these

G. The City may add additional recycling services and programs as new recycling methods 
become available. If new recycling options are added, the City is permitted to charge

H. All expanded recycling services and programs may be terminated at any time without

Sec. 3-1-7. Remedies for nonpayment.
A. All charges may result in a lien on the property to which service is (or was) provided  and 
the City may foreclose on the property in accordance with law. 
B. The City may seek relief in a court of competent jurisdiction for amounts due plus 
interest. 
C. The City may certify to Montrose County all amounts due which may then be assessed 
and collected, in the same manner as real property taxes, for the property to which 
service was provided. 
D. The City may terminate service in accordance with the procedure set out below: 
1. If a customer does not pay for City solid waste and/or recycling collection 
services or fails to comply with the City of Montrose Code, the City of Montrose 
Charter or other regulations and/or laws applicable to the City solid waste and 
recycling collection service, the City may terminate collection services to the 
customer and may repossess all solid waste and recycling containers. 
2. Prior to all services to the customer being terminated, the City will provide a 
termination notice to the customer.  The notice will be sent to the mailing address 
or email address (or other contact information) provided by the customer.  The 
notice will provide the reason for the termination of service, the service 
termination date, the amount due to the City that must be paid by the customer in 
order to avoid termination (if applicable), and/or a description of the customer’s 
non-compliance (if applicable).  The termination of service date shall be no 
sooner than seven days from the date of termination notice.  The termination 
notice will be the date that the notice was mailed or emailed (or the date sent via 
some other form of service to the customer). 
3. The customer is entitled to a hearing with the City Manager or the City Manager’s 
designee at the customer’s request.  The termination notice shall advise the 
customer of their right to a hearing.  If the customer requests a hearing, the 
termination of service will be stayed until a decision is issued.  
4. If the customer requests a hearing, it shall be scheduled as soon as possible by 
the City. 
5. If the City Manager or the City Manager’s designee makes a finding adverse to 
the customer, service may be terminated immediately or on the date specified in 
the termination notice, whichever is later. 
6. If the City Manager’s or the City Manager’s designee’s finding is adverse to the 
customer, the customer may appeal to the City Council for an additional hearing.  
The hearing will be granted if the customer pays all amounts due to the City. 
7. If payment is not received by the date and time specified on the Termination 
Notice, service will be terminated.  Service will not be restored until all amounts 
due are paid to the City (including amounts billed after the date the termination 
notice was provided to the customer).  
E. Non-payment of amounts due for City solid waste and/or recycling collection services 
shall be unlawful.

Re: Stormwater Inspection Camera Purchase

● Exhibit A - Photo of Camera 
 
Action 
Consider approving the purchase of the Edge AI stormwater inspection camera at the quoted 
cost of $74,082.86.  
Background 
Public Works obtained three vendor quotations and conducted in-depth equipment 
demonstrations at the Public Works shop to evaluate camera systems for underground 
stormwater infrastructure inspections. Staff used direct quotations and hands-on demonstrations 
rather than a formal solicitation to assess how each system would perform under operational 
conditions. 
Vendor and system Quote Evaluation 
JIREH TROGLO $50,837.39 Partially meets operational needs 
Edge AI PipeDream Gooseneck $74,082.86 Met operational needs; preferred based 
on demonstrated performance. 
Neverest Envirosight ROVVER X $184,931.99 Exceeded Available Budget  
Staff considered price, quality, availability, demonstrated performance, and included services. 
Although the JIREH system met operational requirements at a lower price, staff found the Edge 
AI system’s demonstrated capabilities superior and recommends it as the best value for the 
City.  
As part of the equipment demonstrations, staff constructed a test course within a 40-foot section 
of storm pipe incorporating two different pipe types, poorly aligned joints, and varying pipe 
conditions. Staff added rocks, gravel, mud, trash, and tree branches to replicate conditions 
encountered in the City’s stormwater system. The Edge AI system was the only one to exceed 
staff’s expectations and demonstrate a clear advantage over the other systems evaluated. 
Fiscal Impact 
The 2026 Stormwater budget includes $85,000 in account 100-5120-944-000 for this purchase. 
The quoted cost of $74,082.86 is $10,917.14 below the budgeted amount.
