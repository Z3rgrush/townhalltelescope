Town of Frederick Arts Committee 
Meeting Minutes 
September 8, 2026 
 
  
Call to Order – Roll Call: At 4:33pm the Arts Committee Meeting was called to order.  
Present were Chairperson Sumner, Vice Chair Hattel, Committee Member Lewis, 
Committee Member Maxwell, and Committee Member Nyland.  
Committee Member Church was excused. Committee Member Sinclair was not 
present.  
Also Present were Deputy Town Attorney Christine Francescani and Deputy Town 
Clerk Emily Nitcher. 
Pledge of Allegiance:  
Approval of Agenda: No changes to the Agenda. 
Public Comment: No public comment. 
Consent Agenda: Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Committee Member 
Lewis to approve the consent agenda as presented. 
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes:  
Discussion Items:  
Open Meetings Law Refresher : Deputy Town Attorney Christine Francescani 
presented to the Arts Committee. 
 
 
Concert Dates for Next Year : The Arts Committee discussed having their summer 
concerts either on the First Friday or Second Thursday of June, July and August 2027. 
 
 
Upcoming Meetings : The Arts Committee discussed moving their October meeting 
from October 13th to October 27th to allow for Mural Art Selection.  
Action Agenda:  
Mural RFP: Motion by Chairperson Sumner and seconded by Committee Member 
Lewis to approve the RFP with the following changes: addition of name/ business 
name, The budget for the project is 19,000.00 to include all costs, and Submissions are 
due on Oct. 20th.   
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Placement of Wood Carving : Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Committee 
Member Lewis to approve placing the wood carving on the concrete pad in Crist Park.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanumously.  
Commissioner Reports:  
Other Business: The Arts Committee discussed hiring a balloon artist for the 
Halloween event. Deputy Town Clerk Nitcher will reach out to Town Staff to get the 
particulars of the event.

Adjournment: There being no further business of the Arts Committee the meeting 
adjourned at 5:28 pm .

Town of Frederick Arts Committee 
Meeting Minutes 
September 22, 2026 
 
  
Call to Order – Roll Call: At 5:34 pm, the meeting was called to order.  
Present were Arts Committee Member Lewis, Arts Committee Member Church, Arts 
Committee Member Nyland, Chairperson Member Sumner, Vice Chair Hattel, Arts 
Committee Member Sencalar.  
Also present were Economic Development Manager Liesha Talley and Deputy Town 
Clerk Emily Nitcher. 
Pledge of Allegiance:  
Approval of Agenda: No changes to the agenda.  
Public Comment: No public comment.  
Consent Agenda:  
Action Agenda:  
Frights on Fifth Arts Committee Involvement: Economic Development Manager Leisha 
Talley presented to the Arts Committee about the Frights on Fifth event.  
Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Committee Member Church to approve 
hiring two balloon artists for the Frights on Fifth event.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Motion by Chairperson Sumner and seconded by Vice Chair Hattel to approve up to 
$600.00 for two balloon artists.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Discussion Items:  
Commissioner Reports:  
Other Business: The Arts Committee discussed keeping the RFP at $19,000 and to 
either coordinate with Economic Development to supplement the difference for balloon 
artists, or use Maintenance budget for the Arts Committee. 
Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Committee Member Lewis.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Adjournment: There being no further business of the Arts Committee, the meeting 
adjourned at 6:42pm.
