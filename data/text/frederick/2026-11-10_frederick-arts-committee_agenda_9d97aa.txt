Town of Frederick Arts Committee 
Meeting Minutes 
October 27, 2026 
 
  
Call to Order – Roll Call: At 4:38 pm the Arts Committee Meeting was called to order. 
Roll call was requested. 
Present were Chairperson Sumner, Vice Chair Hattel, Committee Member Lewis, 
Committee Member Church, Committee Member Maxwell, and Committee Member 
Nyland. 
Also present were Deputy Town Clerk Emily Nitcher, and Economic Development 
Manager Leisha Talley. 
Pledge of Allegiance:  
Approval of Agenda: No Changes to the Agenda.  
Public Comment: No Public Comment.  
Consent Agenda: Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by Committee Member 
Lewis to approve the consent agenda as presented.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously. 
Approval of Minutes:  
Action Agenda:  
Engineering Building Mural Selection : Motion by Chairperson Sumner and Seconded 
by Vice Chair Hattel to approve Laurie Cullum as the artist for the Engineering Building 
Mural selection, with $19,000.00 of total compensation, with 50% due at contract 
signing, 25 % due at start of installation and 25, % due at completion of project.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
The Arts Committee directed staff to add to the artist contract that installation of the 
mural is not to exceed June 1, 2027.  
The arts committee has requested that the artist add 1907, Frederick and Built on What 
Matters to the art mock-ups and to have them for the committee to view at the next 
meeting. 
Summer Concert 2027 Date Selection : Motion by Vice Chair Hattel and seconded by 
Committee Member Church to have the 2027 Summer Concerts on the 2nd 
Wednesdays in June, July, and August.  
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
June 9, 2027 
July 14, 2027 
August 11, 2027 
 
 
Discussion Items:  
Commissioner Reports:  
Other Business:  
Adjournment: There being no further business of the Arts Committee the meeting 
adjourned at 6:04 pm.
