Summary Statement: 
 
The Commission will select a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson to serve until May 
2027.  
 
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
The Bylaws of the PROST Commission, more specifically Article III, Section 4, require 
the election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson. They shall serve the remainder of 
the current term until May 2027, or as soon thereafter as prescribed by the Bylaws.  
 
The Chairperson's roles include leading and representing the Commission, presiding 
over meetings, advocating for the Commission's advice and maintaining relationships 
with the Mayor, Mayor Pro Tem and Board of Trustees, as well as Staff. The Vice 
Chairperson roles include stepping in for the Chair in their absence, partnering with the 
Chair on priorities and relationship building.  
 
The Secretary will coordinate the election process, subject to the Bylaws. In summary, 
any Commissioner may nominate themselves or another Commissioner and a majority 
of the Commission (4 members) are required to seat an Officer.

Town of Frederick Parks, Recreation, Open Space and Trails Advisory Commission Bylaws – October 24, 2023 1 
 
Bylaws 
Town of Frederick, Colorado  
Parks, Recreation, Open Space and Trails Advisory (PROST) Commission  
 
Article I – Introduction 
1. The Town of Frederick’s Board of Trustees, by Resolution 23-R-45 of October 24, 2023, authorizes the creation 
of the Parks, Recreation, Open Space and Trails Commission and adopts these bylaws for the transaction of 
business.  
 
2. The Bylaws of this date have been adopted by the Town of Frederick Board of Trustees and all previously 
adopted Bylaws are deemed repealed.  
 
3. Pursuant to the enacting resolution, the Parks, Recreation, Open Space and Trails Commission, shall be 
hereinafter referred to as “the Commission” and the Town of Frederick Board of Trustees shall be hereinafter 
referred to as “the Board.”  
Article II – Purpose 
1. The Town of Frederick’s Board of Trustees, by resolution, authorizes the creation of the Parks, Recreation, Open 
Space and Trails Commission to: 
 
a. Advise Town Staff and the Board on all matters pertaining to the operation of the Town’s recreational 
assets, including but not limited to, parks, open spaces, trails and related amenities.   
 
2. These Bylaws specifically encourage the Commission to advise and make recommendations to Town Staff and 
the Board on the following matters which include, but not limited to: 
 
a. Strategic planning as it relates to Town recreational assets;  
b. Maintenance and access levels of service at all Town recreational assets;  
c. Proposed use fees for Town recreational assets;  
d. Land-use policies relating to recreational assets and public landscapes;  
e. Acquisition, disposition, or redevelopment of recreational assets including Town owned lands; 
f. Strategic partnerships with, including but not limited to Carbon Valley Parks and Recreation District, to 
leverage public resources to the highest extent possible.  
  
Article III – Membership and Officers 
1. The Commission shall be composed of members appointed by the Board in accordance with the Town of 
Frederick Municipal Code.  
 
2. The Commission shall consist of or as prescribed by the Town of Frederick Municipal Code: 
 
a. Seven (7) voting members who are residents of the Town of Frederick; 
Page 12 of 19
Town of Frederick Parks, Recreation, Open Space and Trails Advisory Commission Bylaws – October 24, 2023 2 
 
 
b. A Board of Trustee who serves as a nonvoting liason to the Commission;  
 
c. A staff representative to serve as a nonvoting liason to the Commission, who shall also serve as 
Secretary to the Commission;  
 
d. A member of the Youth Commission as a nonvoting liason to the Commission.  
 
3. Commissioners shall serve three (3) year terms or as prescribed by the Town of Frederick Municipal Code.  
 
a. Terms shall be staggered to prevent a majority of the membership being renewed at one (1) time; 
 
b. There shall be no limit to the number of terms a Commissioner may serve; 
 
c. Commissioners appointed due to a resignation or removal shall fulfill the term of the seat to which they 
are appointed.  
 
4. The Commission shall elect a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson.  
 
a. The Chairperson shall preside at all regular and special meetings of the Commission and shall decide all 
points of procedure.  
 
b. The Vice Chairperson shall assume the duties and responsibilities of the Chairperson in the 
Chairperson’s absence.  
 
c. Commission officers shall serve for a term of one (1) year.  
 
d. Officer vacancies shall be filled at the next regular meeting of the Commission by election of the 
members. Officers elected to fill vacancies shall serve until the next regular election of officers.  
 
e. Any member of the Commission may nominate another member of the Commission as an Officer.   
 
f. Election of Officers shall be administered by the Secretary and conducted by ballot, or as prescribed by 
law, and a majority of the Commission shall elect the prescribed Officer.  
 
g. Election of Officers shall occur during the May Regular Meeting of the Commission or as soon thereafter 
is practical at the discretion of the Chairperson.   
 
h. The Board may remove any Officer or member from the Commission, per the Town of Frederick 
Municipal Code.   
 
i. The Commission may remove an Officer from their office at any time by a majority vote, but may not 
remove them from the Commission. The Commission may, upon a majority vote by the commissioners, 
recommend a member for removal by the Board.

Article IV – Regular and Special Meetings 
1. The Commission shall hold a regular meeting at least monthly on a date and at a time and location designated 
by the Chairperson. These meetings may be rescheduled or cancelled by the Secretary, upon direction from the 
Chairperson.  
 
2. Members of the Commission shall notify the Chairperson and Secretary as soon as possible if they will not be 
able to attend a meeting. Members of the Commission who exceed three, consecutive unexcused absences may 
be subject to Article III’s removal provision.  
 
3. The Chairperson shall coordinate with the Secretary to prepare, or cause to be prepared, an agenda for each 
regular and special meeting of the Commission. 
 
4. The Secretary shall prepare, or cause to be prepared and posted, a notice of each regular and special meeting of 
the Commission. Such notice shall contain, at a minimum, the agenda, minutes for approval and any documents 
to be presented to the Commission by Town staff. Those matters subject to conditions of Executive Session shall 
be noticed in accordance with Colorado Law.  
 
5. Special meetings may be called by the Chairperson at any time, provided notice is given to each member of the 
Commission, at a minimum, five (5) calendar days prior to the meeting and provided the aforementioned public 
notice is posted.  
 
6. All regular and special meetings may be held in-person, virtually, or a combination of these two methods, 
provided that a link for the virtual meeting is provided in the meeting agenda.  
 
7. All regular and special meetings of the Commission shall be open to the public. Members of the public in 
attendance shall be provided up to three minutes, during a designated public comment period for general 
comment. An additional public comment period may be provided during discussion for a specific agenda item as 
determined by the Chairperson.   
 
8. Before official and formal action can be taken on any matter at any regular or special meeting of the 
Commission, a quorum shall be present and in-session. A quorum is defined for purposes of these Bylaws as any 
four (4) members.  
 
9. The Vice Chairperson shall preside in the absence of the Chairperson. Where both the Chairperson and the Vice 
Chairperson are absent, members present shall by majority vote select a member to preside over the meeting.  
 
10. All voting shall be by roll-call vote, except for the nomination of Officers.  
 
11. The order of business for regular and special meetings shall be the order as it appears on the agenda, except 
that any member of the Commission, may, by motion and majority vote of the Commission, request to 
rearrange the order of business or remove an item from the order of business.  
 
12. The Secretary shall keep, or cause to be kept, minutes and keep the same recording the date, action minutes, 
the vote of each member upon action agenda items, or those absent or failing to vote and such other details as

the Commission shall direct. The minutes of each regular and special meeting shall be provided for approval by 
the Commission at its next meeting.  
 
13. The Secretary shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, the transmittal of official and formal actions of the 
Commission to the Town Manager’s office for scheduling before the Board.  
 
14. The Commission may hold an Executive Session, subject to any and all applicable rules and regulations of the 
State of Colorado, at the request of the Chairperson or majority vote of the Commission.  
 
Article V – Study Sessions 
1. A study session may be called by the Chairperson at any time, provided notice is given to each member of the 
Commission, at a minimum, five (5) calendar days prior to the session and provided public notice is posted as 
prescribed in Article IV, Section C.  
 
2. The purpose of a study session shall be for Town staff to provide additional information on a topic or topics of 
particular interest and/or concern to the Commission.  
 
3. No formal action shall be taken during study sessions. A public comment period may be provided during 
discussion for a study session item as determined by the Chairperson.   
 
4. The Chairperson shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, an agenda for each study session of the Commission.  
 
5. The Secretary shall prepare, or cause to be prepared and posted, a notice of each study session of the 
Commission. Such notice shall contain, at a minimum, the agenda.  
 
6. At any time, during a regular or special meeting, a quorum of the Commission does not exist, the Chairperson 
may continue the meeting as a study session, provided that no official or formal action is taken.  
 
7. No quorum is required for a study session.    
 
Article VI – Committees 
1. There shall be no standing committees of the Commission.  
 
2. With a majority vote of the Commission, ad hoc committees of the Commission may be formed for the purposes 
of special study or review of a specific item of interest.  
 
Article VII – Ethics 
1. The members of this Commission shall be bound by the Colorado Code of Ethics, the Colorado Public Official 
Disclosure Law and the Town of Frederick Municipal Code Section 2-9-30.

Summary Statement: 
 
The Director's Report is intended to provide a brief summary of the Department's 
activities in the previous month, specifically to but not necessarily limited to, those 
strategic initiatives identified by the Board of Trustees or Commission. 
 
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
The Open Space and Trails Division worked to advance immediate priorities of the 
Open Space Management Plan, including re-seeding several pocket open spaces and 
portions of natural areas throughout their system. They also worked to finalize asset 
data in our asset management system relating to existing trails maintained by the 
Division, in partnership with our IT/GIS department. Finally, they met with our 
agricultural lessee at Bulrush Open Space to plan through 2027's vegetative 
management.  
 
The Parks Division began winter blowouts of irrigation systems and will work to finalize 
those into mid-November. They also worked to advance Parks Water Management 
Plan immediate priorities, specifically the internal buildout of their RainBird IQ4 platform, 
which is our irrigation central control, which will advance data collection in future years 
as well as provide for significant operational efficiencies for day to day operations.  
 
Both Parks and Open Space began installation of holiday lighting in Crist Park and 
Downtown Frederick for Festival of Lights. This process typically takes 6-8 Technicians 
about 4-6 weeks to install and another 3-4 in February to take down.  
 
The Golf Division hosted their final events of the season, including Santa Cops, which is 
a fundraiser in partnership with the Frederick, Firestone and Dacono Police 
Departments to raise money to purchase toys for children, as well as the Men's League 
Championship and the Fall Scramble. Tee times remain in high demand with the warm 
weather and staff are continuing to operate normally into the winter season.  
 
The Parks Planning, Design and Construction Division continued working to ensure 
new developments have adequate PROST amenities, most notably 'Miner's Park,' 
which is located south of Centennial Park, 'Clearview' which is located near Godding 
Hollow and Silver Birch, and 'The Shores on Plum Creek' which is located west of 
Bulrush Open Space on Highway 52. They additionally finalized the Urban Forestry 
Management Plan document and are nearing completion of the documents for the 
Parks Water Management Plan and the Golf Course Management Plan, as well as 
advancing the Asset Replacement Plan to finalize a Maintenance Program level of 
service recommendation. Finally, they worked to draft a Request for Proposals for the 
Golf Course Maintenance Facility Project, should that project receive approval to move 
Page 18 of 19
forward from the Board of Trustees in November.  
 
The Administration Division took the Department's 2027 budget request forward. The 
Department requested five additional full-time positions in 2027 - 2 Open Space & Trails 
Technicians, 1 Golf Shop Associate (which was not a new position, but took dedicated 
payroll from existing Seasonal/PTE positions and made it a full-time position), 1 Park 
Planner and 1 Irrigation Technician. The Town Manager's Office did not advance the 
Park Planner or Irrigation Technician forward for Board approval, stating financial 
sustainability as the primary factor for denial. The Department's operations and 
maintenance budget and capital budget were otherwise moved forward for approval as 
proposed. The Director is also coordinating with the TMO to finalize the updated Board 
Strategic Plan for 2027-2031 with more updates coming to PROST this winter.  
 
Finally, the Parks, OST, PDC and Admin Divisions began moving to the Frederick 
Municipal Service Center in November.
