Action Type 
 
2) Administrative: Actions of routine nature that implement policies/directions or 
effectuate the functioning of government; such as contract approvals/granting of 
licenses and permits/etc.  
 
Strategic Plan Alignment: 
 
 Strategic Priority 5: Reliable & Sustainable Infrastructure  
– Frederick will invest in and maintain reliable infrastructure that supports a 
growing community, protects public resources, and ensures high-quality 
service for residents today and in the future.   
 
Summary Statement: 
 
The Town is providing an easement to United Power so that UP can provide electricity 
to operate an irrigation pump for the Wyndham Hill subdivision's HOA managed public 
lands, including parks, common areas, and landscaping. 
 
Page 16 of 57
Detail of Issue/Request: 
 
Further development of the non-potable irrigation pond at Wyndham Hill necessitates 
installation of a pump so that water from the pond may be effectively utilized. This 
easement provides an area for extension of United Power's underground utilities to 
provide electricity for the pump. Town Staff, including the Town Attorney's Office, Parks 
and Open Space, and Public Works and Engineering have reviewed this easement and 
support its implementation. 
 
Legal Comments: 
 
The Town Attorney's Office has reviewed and commented on this easement. 
 
Alternatives/Options 
 
Approve the grant of easement to United Power. 
 
Deny grant of easement to United Power. 
 
Financial Considerations 
 
There are no direct Town appropriations or revenues for this easement dedication as all 
work completed is a requirement of other entities, per established agreements. 
 
Staff Recommendation 
 
Staff recommends that the Board approve the grant of easement. 
 
Community Impact 
 
Installation of electrical utilities on the easement granted to United Power will assist in 
providing less expensive water to current residents of Wyndham Hill by reducing 
reliance on costly, treated drinking water for irrigation purposes and will indirectly serve 
the intended purposes of the Town's adopted Water Conservation Plan goals by 
utilizing untreated water for irrigation.

RESOLUTION NO. 26-R-44 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE TOWN OF FREDERICK, COLORADO APPROVING 
AN EASEMENT AGREEMENT WITH UNITED POWER 
 
 WHEREAS, further development of the non-potable irrigation pond at Wyndham 
Hill necessitates installation of a pump by United Power so that water from the pond may 
be effectively utilized; and, 
 
WHEREAS, the Town owns the property that United Power must utilize for an 
extension of United Power’s underground utilities to provide electricity for the pump; and,  
 
WHEREAS, the Town wishes to enter into an Easement Agreement with United 
Power to allow such extension. 
 
 NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE 
TOWN OF FREDERICK, COLORADO, AS FOLLOWS: 
 
Section 1.  Easement Agreement Approval. The Board of Trustees approves the 
Easement Agreement with United Power, attached hereto as Exhibit A, 
allowing for extension of United Power’s underground utilities, and 
authorizes the Mayor to sign the Easement Agreement.  
 
Section 2. Effective Date. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon 
adoption. 
 
Section 3. Repealer.  All resolutions, or parts thereof, in conflict with this resolution 
are hereby repealed, provided that such repealer shall not repeal the 
repealer clauses of such resolution nor revive any resolution thereby. 
 
Section 4. Certification. The Town Clerk shall certify the passage of this resolution and 
make not less than one copy of the adopted resolution available for 
inspection by the public during regular business hours.
