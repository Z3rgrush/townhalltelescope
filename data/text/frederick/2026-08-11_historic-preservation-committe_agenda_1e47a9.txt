Meeting Minutes 
July 14, 2026 
   
Call to Order – Roll Call: At 2:33pm, Chairperson Smith called the meeting to order 
and requested roll call.  
Present were Committee Member Cave, Committee Member March, Vice Chair Simek, 
Committee Member Grubb, Chairperson Smith, Committee Member McDonald. 
Committee Member McDonald arrived at 2:48 pm.  
Also, present were Trustee Hickman, Planner III Brien Schumacher, and Deputy Town 
Clerk Emily Nitcher. 
Approval of Agenda: No changes to the agenda.  
Public Comment: No Public Comment.  
Consent Agenda: Motion by Chairperson Smith and seconded by Committee Member 
Cave to approve the consent agenda. 
Upon roll call vote, motion passed unanimously.  
Committee Member McDonald arrived after the vote, and therefore was not included.  
June 9, 2026 Minutes:  
Action Agenda:  
Discussion Items:  
Museum Friday open dates: The Historic Preservation Committee discussed upcoming 
Museum hours and agreed not to open the Museum on August 7, as it will be open 
during the Music in the Park event on August 12. Chairperson Smith and Vice Chair 
Simek will staff the Museum on August 12, with Committee Members Grubb and March 
potentially assisting. The Committee will continue with the first Friday Museum opening 
schedule for the remainder of the year. 
Upcoming community events: Downtown walking Tour: Sierra Fedder presented to the 
Committee.  
Start of a downtown walking tour to kick off Chainsaws and Chuckwagons.  
Any information that would be helpful to include in the tour from the Historic 
Preservation Committee, please send to Ms. Fedder.  
Chairperson Smith and Committee Member Cave will join the tour.  
Meet at Centennial Park by the Bathrooms. 
Commissioner Reports: Committee Member Cave shared information about his visit 
to the Burlington History Museum and thanked members of the public for their 
contributions to the Museum. 
 
Chairperson Smith and Vice Chair Simek discussed cleaning responsibilities for the 
Miners’ Museum and requested clarification regarding regular cleaning and the 
condition of the windows and front doors. Mr. Schumacher will contact Facilities 
regarding regular cleaning and window and door maintenance.

Other Business: Next meeting: August 11, 2026, Regular Meeting.

Committee, Chairperson Smith adjourned the meeting at 2:57 pm.
