VII. Presentations 
G. Coca-Cola Mural Sign Update 
Brigid Keating reviewed the background of the sign, including the 2016 restoration and the 
2024 evaluation, responses from the request for qualifications for restoration and conservation 
services, board and commission input and common themes, involvement of the Lafayette Urban 
Renewal Authority, and potential options, including (1) restoring the sign and transferring 
ownership to the current building owner, and (2) restoring the Hi-Way Bar banner and creating a 
rotating mural program. 
Council asked about the mural being relocated to an alley or indoor location, the feasibility of 
placing it on a non-south facing location for better protection, the history of the sign and similar 
advertisements, protection treatments, Louisville’s Coca-Cola sign, deterioration of the sign and 
substrate, restoration locations and considerations around moving the mural, and timing of next 
steps.  
Council discussed the two options, the significance and historical value of the advertisement 
versus the banner, landmarking options, honoring the community members who funded the 
restoration, and historical interpretations of the sign. 
Council directed staff to proceed with restoring the Hi-Way Bar banner and creating a rotating 
mural program, suggesting that the Cultural Arts Board should advise on the content of the first 
mural, with a nod to the present Coca-Cola sign.

I. Resolution No. 2026-19 / Submitting to the Electorate of the City of Lafayette at the November 
3, 2026, Coordinated Election a Certain Question Authorizing an Extension of an Expiring 
Property Tax for LaMont Does Park Purposes; Specifying the Form of the Ballot and Other 
Election Procedures; and Setting Forth Related Details 
Heather Balser and Melissa Hisel presented background on the 2014 ballot measure, LaMont 
Does and Great Outdoors Water Park projected capital, operations, and maintenance expenses, 
proposed ballot language modeled on a 20-year time frame, and next steps. 
Council asked about interest payments, adding language regarding financial assistance for 
qualified residents to the ballot title, and certificates of participation. 
Maddie Prodanovic and Dalton Kelley answered questions.  
Council discussed whether the use of this particular tax was the best way to fund maintenance 
and operations. 
A motion was made by Councilor Beaulieu and seconded by Councilor Gallegos to approve 
Resolution No. 2026-19, submitting to the electorate of the City of Lafayette at the November 
3, 2026, coordinated election a certain question authorizing an extension of an expiring 
property tax for LaMont Does Park purposes; specifying the form of the ballot and other 
election procedures; and setting forth related details. The motion passed 6-0.
