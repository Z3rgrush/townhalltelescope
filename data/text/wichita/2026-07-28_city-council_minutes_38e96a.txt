Mayor Wu stated Consent Agenda item number ten is the Wichita Airport Authority's public exigency ratification for purchase of replacement equipment for the airfield electrical vault at Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National Airport. City Manager, can you just let us know any information regarding this item that will expedite this as soon as possible?
City Manager Dennis Marstall stated yes, thank you, Mayor, for bringing attention to that. Actually, we have Nate Hinkel from the airport, the audience will ask him to come forward first and give a quick update. We did have a fire issue, water issue. I'm sorry, water issue for him to outline. And I do want to take a moment to thank our partners at the state because other people have reached out who can provide some assistance for us. We do have it identified for a solution and I'll let Nate talk about that. But your effort today will help us expedite some of these things so we can ensure the continued operation. I think we'll take care of that.
Nathaniel Hinkel, Airport, stated thank you, Manager. And good morning, Mayor, Council. Nathaniel Hinkel, Assistant Director of Airports. Just for some quick background information. There was a flooding event on June 25
th
, the same night that affected much of the City with high water at ICT. We received about 10.3 inches of rain overnight and with the low levels of the airfield, there was some flooding in our underground regulator vault. That vault is controlled with some sump pumps and has some elevated equipment in there, but the flooding was significant. We had about eighteen inches of water in the vault that required us to clear out in the morning. And did affect some of our regulators. We quickly got to work clearing out the water, assessing the damage, and bringing regulators back online. In that process, we found about eight of our twenty five regulators that failed. And, we quickly went to work, trying to restore lighting to the field by connecting jumper cables and so forth to different regulators. We're fortunate to have some excess capacity due to some recent construction projects where we have been changing over halogen incandescent lights to LED. So that provided some extra capacity on those regulators. In the process, we consulted an electrical engineer, and on July 8th, we had a recommendation from them on replacement regulators that would best fit our current needs. We went forth and we obtained three quotes or attempted to obtain three quotes. Two declined, and we did have an acceptable bid from Cooper Crouse-Hinds, and that amount was slightly under one hundred four thousand. And with that, they provided approximately an eight week lead time, and we should have a ship date of September eighteenth on those regulators. And in the meantime, we've been able to keep operations as normal day and night, being able to change over some of those regulators. And, this process will assure that we're able to get our airfield lighting system back up to normal capacity.
Mayor Wu stated thank you very much, Nate, and your entire team. I just wanted to highlight that there have been some misinformation regarding what has happened. And we are resting assured right now that regular operations are still as is, and you guys are doing everything even until the parts come in in September. So, thank you for making sure that the airport continues to operate normally and safely. I see no further questions. With that, we'll open it up for public comment. I see none. I'll bring it back.
Lily Wu moved to approve and ratify the public exigency for the ratification for purchase for Consent Agenda item number 10.
Motion carried 7 to 0
