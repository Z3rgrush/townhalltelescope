26-1766 A letter dated November 12, 2026, from Nicole C. Doheny, Manager of 
Finance, pursuant to Section 20-92 of the Denver Revised Municipal Code, advising 
City Council that the Department of Finance has retained the following firms to 
provide financial advisory services to the City:
1) Stifel, Nicolaus & Company, Incorporated, which will serve as the City’s 
Independent Registered Municipal Advisor (IRMA) for General Administration and 
Investor Relations Municipal Advisory Services and may also provide municipal 
advisory services for non-Airport debt and credit issuances. General Administration 
services include advice on the City’s debt portfolio and policies, refunding and 
restructuring opportunities, rating agency and investor relations, continuing 
disclosure and post-issuance matters, regulatory developments, special taxing 
districts, tax increment financings and, as needed, conduit and private activity bond 
activities. Transaction services may include General Obligation Bonds, Excise Tax 
Revenue Bonds, Certificates of Participation, Wastewater and Golf Enterprise 
Revenue Bonds, and other direct financial obligations of the City and its non-Airport 
Enterprises;
2) Frasca & Associates, LLC, which will serve as municipal advisor solely for 
Airport-related matters in connection with financial obligations for and on behalf of 
the Airport Enterprise. Services include Airport debt portfolio administration, 
financing and underwriter proposal review, rating agency and investor relations, 
continuing disclosure and post-issuance matters, credit and liquidity facilities, debt 
planning and financial analysis, and transaction-specific support for Airport debt and 
derivative transactions, including structure, pricing, method of sale, financing 
documents and qualified independent representative services.
All firms above were selected through a competitive RFP process in June 2026. 
These firms will work under the  direction of the Manager of Finance and/or their 
designee(s), in close coordination with the Capital Management and Financing 
Division within the Department of Finance.
