The public is welcome to attend Business Meetings held in the East Hearing Room at the 
Arapahoe County Administration Building, 5334 South Prince Street, Littleton, CO 80120. 
Business meetings also can be viewed online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Virtual public comment 
is allowed on general issues or specific agenda items by calling 1-855-436-3656 and, when 
prompted to, by pressing *3 on the telephone keypad to be entered in the speaking queue.
The Board of County Commissioners typically holds Public Hearings at 9:30 a.m. the second and fourth 
Tuesday of each month. Items listed on the consent agenda are adopted with one vote. Items listed 
under regular business are considered individually. 
The public may comment during designated times as directed by the Chair of the Board. Each speaker 
will be limited to one three-minute period of comment, unless modified by the Chair of the Board.
Meeting agendas are available at arapahoe.legistar.com. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 
795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303-795-4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

6. CONSENT AGENDA
Items on the consent agenda are generally non-controversial, do not require much, if any, 
discussion, and may be voted on as an entire group with a single motion. (The motion is “to 
approve the items on the consent agenda.”) A Commissioner may request an item from the 
consent agenda be removed for separate discussion. This enables the item to be considered 
and voted upon separately.

7.a. Submission of 2027 Recommended Budget
AC Decision Support Framework
Board Summary Report
Presentation
2027 Recommended Budget
Proposed Motion
Resolution
Attachments:
John Christofferson, Acting County Attorney, established jurisdiction for the Board to 
consider this matter.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff read the preamble establishing the budget is balanced and conforms with Colorado law. 
2027 Budget quick facts and figures were listed. 
The 2027 Budget Revenue was presented. A chart was displayed showing Total Actual 
Value and New Construction Trend. Other charts displaying Total Assessed Value and 
Revenue Trend and Estimate of Property Tax Revenue - Post 1A were reviewed. 
The 2027 Budget Expenditures was presented. Charts were displayed and reviewed reflecting 
the General Fund Staffing and the General Fund Operating Budge. 
Staff presented the 2027 budget and how it is structurally balanced. The revenue and 
expenditures were compared to show a current surplus. The General Fund is structurally 
balanced with a set-aside for future years. The County continues to make major investments 
in key areas of essential service delivery with priorities enabled by Issue 1A. Long-term 
commitments to capital improvements have been thoughtfully considered for the future.
The Budget Package Requests for General Fund was presented. Staff reviewed the 2027 
requested budget, the 2027 recommended budget, and the variance between the two.
A chart was displayed reflecting the 2027 Budget Fund Balance from the 2025 Actual 
Budget and the 2031 Projected Budget. 
Future dates and plans were listed.
Commissioner thanked staff for their work. 
The public comment period was opened.
No one spoke.
The public comment period was closed.
