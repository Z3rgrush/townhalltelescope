The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

1:00 PM *2027 Open Space Grant Cycle Funding
Board Summary Report
Presentation
OSTAB Recommendation
OS Grant Program Policies and Procedures
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was staff presented recommendations for the 2027 Open Space Grants 
cycle and requested approval by the Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) for the funding 
allocation.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
The 2027 grant cycle allocation was discussed. Staff recommend keeping the total allocation of $ 5.5 
million, increasing the maximum award from $ 600,000 to $750,000 for standard grants, and to keep the 
maximum award for small and planning grants at $150,000. 
Staff shared the 2026 grantee survey feedback. 
Commissioner inquired on the grant allocation amounts and what the impact would be for small grants . 
Commissioner also asked if any of the feedback that was received would be considered for future 
recommendations. 
Commissioner congratulated staff regarding the positive feedback that was received. 
4-0 in favor of the recommendations. Commissioner Warren-Gully absent and excused.

1:30 PM *Waiver per Purchasing Policy for TruePoint Solutions
Board Summary Report
Waiver per Purchasing Policies - Truepoint
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to add $ 25,000 to the waiver for TruePoint Solutions to cover the 
final implementation and support invoices for the Accela project. This increase will exceed the $ 100K 
limit and is necessary to ensure completion of the project as planned.
The background of the request was presented. 
Staff provided insight surrounding the business needs that have changed over the years, and new 
programs that have been implemented causing a backlog of enhancements accrue. An additional 
$25,000 is being requested. The funds are already within budget; therefore, staff are requesting for Chair 
signature to utilize the funds. 
Commissioner asked regarding the projects that were done causing the increase. 
4-0 in favor. Commissioner Warren-Gully absent and excused.
