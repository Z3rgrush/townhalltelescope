The Board engaged in discussion amongst themselves and with Presenter Steve Chevalier, 
Environmental Health Supervisor Connor Gerken, and County Attorney Monica Kovaci. The 
Homeowner of 23900 E. Ohio Ave. was not present in person or virtually for this hearing. 
Director Burrows vocalized concerns about possibility of criminal charges and scope of the 
sentencing guidelines permitting incarceration. County Atty. Kovaci clarified the intent to 
partner with other county departments such as Judicial, Public Works, and Law Enforcement to 
gain compliance through civil courts and did not intend to pursue criminal action against the 
homeowner. There was discussion speculating on the homeowners ability/desire to remedy the 
concerning onsite wastewater systems but The Board consistently returned to their duty to 
protect public health being paramount to their decision. Several directors expressed compassion 
towards the Homeowner and discussed various options to gain compliance, including the county 
paying for installation of fencing, whether the homeowner could continue using the septic 
system without further deterioration, and reducing the civil penalties amount to a judgment lien 
on the property.
A motion was made by Davis and seconded by Walker to approve the Public Health Order to 
Cease and Desist to be issued to Fernando Avina Garcia for the OWTS at 23900 E. Ohio Ave., 
Aurora CO 80018.
