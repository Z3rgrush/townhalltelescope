The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

The purpose of this study session was to seek approval from the BOCC to apply for the 2026 Coverdell 
Formula Grants Program. If awarded, the grant will support the Unified Forensic Laboratory (UFL) 
through the purchase of new instrumentation to increase the available technology in the Latent Print 
Unit, as well as provide training and overtime funding for staff at the UFL. Grant award amounts vary 
from partial to full funding based on application evaluation and available funds.
A background of the requests for the following grants was presented. Sheriff’s Office are requesting the 
opportunity to apply for the grants prior to the deadlines. 
Grant opportunity: BJA FY 2026 Paul Coverdell Forensic Science Improvement Formula Grants 
Program. The grant would support the Unified Forensic Laboratory (UFL) through the purchase of new 
instrumentation to increase the available technology in the Lanent Print Unit, as well as provide training 
and overtime funding for staff at the UFL. 
Grant opportunity: BJA FY 2026 Paul Coverdell Forensic Science Improvement Competitive Grants 
Program. The grant would support the UFL through the purchase of new instrumentation to increase the 
available technology in the Seized Drug/Chemistry Unit. It would also expand the current technology as 
well as cover costs for validation and implementation of the technology. Staff provided further 
information on the equipment included. 
Grant opportunity: BJA FY 2026 DNA Capacity Enhancement for Backlog Reduction Competitive 
Grants Program. The grant would support DNA analysis at the UFL through the purchase of new 
instrumentation to simplify DNA extraction on sexual assault cases, as well as cover the cost for 
validation and implementation of the technology. 
Grant Opportunity: BJA FY 2026 DNA Capacity Enhancement for Backlog Reduction Formula Grants 
Program. The grant would support DNA analysis at the UFL through overtime funds, training funds, and 
software maintenance costs. 
Commissioner thanked the staff for finding grants and asked for clarification for what occurs internally 
for staff and confirmed that federal funding will not be an issue. Commissioner asked finance staff 
regarding the ability to track these grants moving forward. 
Commissioner asked regarding the longevity about the equipment that is going to be purchased through 
the grants.  
Commissioner thanked Sheriff’s Office staff for finding the grants to apply for and noted the importance 
for having the most up-to-date equipment. 
4-0 in favor of the requests. Commissioner Campbell absent and excused.

The purpose of this study session was to present 2026 Fair recap and recommendations for key 
operational changes to the 2027 Fair. Staff requested the Board’s feedback on recommended changes.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
The goals and objectives for the 2027 Fair were shared. 
Key performance indicators were listed and reviewed. 
A recap of the 2026 Fair was discussed. A list of improvements that took place was presented. 
Staff discussed the potential reasons for decreased attendance for the 2026 Fair. These included local 
event saturation, change in approach to marketing and sponsorship, demographics, weather, ticket price 
increase, and budget. 
A chart was displayed showing the revenue, expenditures, and sponsorships from 2025 and 2026.
Graphs were shared displaying the 2026 Fair Revenue as of 10/12/2026 and the Expenses Projected to 
Year-end.
Commissioner asked regarding the 4-H expenses and where the 3% comes from. 
Charts were displayed showing the CSU Extension 4-H Program/Livestock Sale statistics from 2025 
and 2026. 
Commissioner asked staff which counties host their fairs during the same timeframe that Arapahoe 
County does. Commissioner also asked regarding attendance for the Fall for You Festival and the 
marketing for the event.
Commissioner made comment surrounding the Heritage Hall events. Commissioner asked what the 
BOCC can do moving forward to influence the marketing for events.
Commissioner asked regarding ticket prices for Arapahoe County in comparison to other counties that 
have minimal ticket prices for their fairs.
