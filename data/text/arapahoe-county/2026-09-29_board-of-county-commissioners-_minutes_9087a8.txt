The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

The purpose of this study session was to provide the Board with an annual update from their economic 
development partner, Aurora Economic Development Council.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff shared agenda for the presentation. 
Aurora Economic Development Council’s mission was shared. The organization metrics were 
discussed. Between 2025-2026 3,183 primary jobs were created /retained, and $ 408.2 million capital 
investments were made. 
Staff provided insight on deals made from 2025-YTD 2026. These included deals in distribution, data 
center, constructions and manufacturing, and hospitality. 
A map was displayed showing the development and activity areas within the area. Staff highlighted four 
projects including Scannell, QTS Phase II, Amazon, and Project Spruce. 
Redevelopment at the CCCS Lowry Campus was discussed. 
Data Center news and updates were reviewed. 
Commissioner asked regarding the film school that was located at the Lowry Campus. 
A pie chart was presented showing Aurora Economic Development Council’s leveraged investments 
from Arapahoe County, Adams County, Aurora, Other Gov’t, and Private sectors. 
Arapahoe County collaboration and events were discussed. 
Successes and challenges were reviewed. State and Federal policy efforts and priorities were 
highlighted. 
Staff shared how AEDC is contributing to economic resilience and stability as well as workforce and 
community prosperity to align with Arapahoe Forward Strategic Plan. 
Upcoming event dates were discussed. 
Commissioner asked regarding Xcel Energy cost and utilization for consumers if data centers are to 
support their own energy needs. Further discussion was held to determine if any increases will occur for 
consumers in future years. 
Presentation concluded without vote.

majority of the Payment Error Rate, ( 4) advocate for fiscal notes to reflect the impact on County 
operations, (5) work with the incoming Governor’s Office to understand their agenda, ( 6) legislation 
that improves the long-term fiscal health of the state, ( 7) implementation of the new Colorado Benefits

Board Summary Report
Draft Lieutenant Shift Pay Policy (2026)
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to provide updated information and receive direction on 
implementing shift pay for Lieutenants who take on additional shifts doing deputy or sergeant level 
work.
A background of the request for Lieutenant shift pay was presented. At times, Lieutenants are paid less 
then sergeants because of their exempt status. 
The proposition is to include a flat rate shift differential. The requirements for the shift rate were listed . 
The rates would average the hourly rate of deputies. The highest possible fiscal impact would be 
$500,000 but is not expected to get reach that level.
An example was provided for the added shifts and opportunities. 
Commissioner asked about the hours included in the shift and types of shifts. Commissioner also asked 
regarding the use of the recommended system in other departments. 
Commissioner requested information for options to see if the system is sustainable. Commissioner also 
asked about the possibility of a cap. 
Commissioner asked if staff is accepting of the model.
Commissioner inquired on the data and if the system would alleviate overtime. 
BOCC approved shift pay. 
Commissioner also asked if the pay shift would be included in the 2027 budget.
