The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

STUDY SESSION TOPICS
1:30 PM *MOU approval with Colorado State Patrol for CMV Inspections
Board Summary Report
Draft MOU
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was the Arapahoe County Sheriff’s Office sought approval from the 
BOCC for the Sheriff to sign the Memorandum of Understanding between the Colorado State Patrol and 
ACSO to allow Deputies with the Sheriff’s Office to perform Commercial Motor Vehicle Inspections.
A background of the request was presented. 
The MOU would allow Deputies access to the training classes as well as the State guidelines in order to 
be authorized to conduct commercial motor vehicle inspections. 
Commissioner asked regarding violations and who would be cited for the violations. Commissioner also 
asked for clarification surrounding the consequences for whomever is cited. Commissioner inquired if 
this MOU is related to recent news surrounding immigration issues. 
Commissioner asked about the expansion aspect of the MOU and the insight for what the demands 
would be and how it would improve public safety. 
Commissioner asked regarding fees associated with violations. 
Commissioner asked surrounding the fiscal impacts and the equipment needed to get started . 
Commissioner clarified the process on when an inspection would be required. 
Discussion was made regarding the impact to Sheriff’s Office.
Staff recommend signing the MOU and obtaining the necessary training and certifications. 
4-0 in favor. Commissioner Summey absent and excused. 
1:45 PM *Potential Payment Error Rate Costs
Board Summary Report
PER Policy and QA Comparison
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was DHS provided information to the Board of County 
Commissioners (BOCC) on the current state of the SFY 2026-27 Payment Error Rate (PER) and 
potential costs associated with HR- 1 penalties for Arapahoe County, the PER level for the State of 
Colorado and a potential cost sharing proposal for counties and the state.

Staff provided background information regarding the PER and potential costs associated with HR- 1 
penalties. 
Colorado will receive approximately $ 140 million less in the SNAP allocation from the federal 
government as the state’s PER is 10%. 
The state’s plan is to have the state cover 60% of the reduction in allocation with the counties covering 
the remaining 40%. This percentage would represent approximately $ 56 million from the counties . 
Arapahoe County makes up approximately 11% of the state’s total caseload which would represent the 
approximate penalty of $ 6.1 million. Staff informed BOCC that these numbers may change depending 
on pending federal legislation. 
Staff presented a document showing the comparison of State Policy versus State QA, a copy of which 
has been retained for the record. 
Commissioner noted the issue with self-attestation. 
Commissioner asked staff regarding the QA that the State uses and if the Federal Government also 
utilized the same QA. 
Discussion was made surrounding the PER errors within Arapahoe County. Staff provided examples of 
instances where the errors occurred and noted the discrepancies between the State Policy vs. State QA. 
Further discussion was held regarding what can and cannot be verified when someone provides 
information when applying. 
Commissioner asked if a person who provides erroneous information is held responsible. 
Discussion was made on how Arapahoe County DHS could potentially lower the number of errors with 
the limited access using the State’s Policy vs. State’s QA. 
Commissioner asked about the consequences if all resources available were utilized without State 
permission. Staff mentioned considerations that were being held within the department. 
Presentation concluded without vote. Commissioner Summey absent and excused. 
The meeting was adjourned.
