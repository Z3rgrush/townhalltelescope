The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually.

study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The

meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If

Meeting Minute Summaries October 13, 2026
The purpose of this study session was county staff requested Board approval to authorize the submission 
of Arapahoe County’s 2027-2029 Proposition 123 Affordable Housing Commitment to the Colorado 
Department of Local Affairs in accordance with applicable State requirements.
A background of the request was presented. 
HB26-1313 changed the calculations for growth. The calculation for Arapahoe County would be 513 
average annual permits × 15% (median job growth) × 3 years = 231 affordable housing units. 
Commissioner asked for clarification on the permits being for new construction only and what would 
qualify as affordable housing units. 
5-0 in favor of request. Commissioner Campbell and Commissioner Warren-Gully appeared virtually.

3:30 PM *Enterprise Network Firewall Replacement Funding and Fixed Asset Numbers
Board Summary Report
Cost Analysis
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was the IT Department was requesting Board of County 
Commissioners (BoCC) approval to utilize existing network firewall fixed-asset funds to replace the 
County's current Check Point enterprise firewall infrastructure with Fortinet FortiGate next-generation 
firewalls. The IT Department is requesting 7 new fixed asset numbers and appropriation in Fund 70 of 
the $223,692.
The IT Director presented background for the request to replace the county firewall.
Staff reviewed the competitive research for the firewalls and the cost savings. Staff felt that it was 
prudent to search for other firewall providers because of the cybersecurity environment. The providers 
were compared by different criteria. 
Staff recommended going forward with a provider and provide cost savings.
Commissioner asked about the RFP process. Commissioner clarified the need for the asset numbers. 
Commissioner asked regarding the length of the contract that would be entered into. 
5-0 in favor of staff recommendations. Commissioner Campbell and Commissioner Warren-Gully 
appeared virtually.
The meeting was adjourned.
