The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

Local Plan - A/D Works!

The purpose of this study session was to request approval from the Board of County Commissioners to 
submit an updated local plan for Arapahoe Douglas Works!.
Staff presented background updates to the Arapahoe Douglas Works Plan. Current updates and 
opportunities were made to ensure plans continue to match. 
The local plan is for program years 2024-2027 and changes to the local plan were listed. Discussion was 
held regarding some of the highlights and benefits of the updates.
Commissioner asked about the number of programs and providers available. 
Commissioner inquired as to the burden for individuals in need that don’t have the social security 
number. 
4-0 in favor to approve the plan. Commissioner Baker absent and excused. Commissioner Campbell 
appeared virtually.

Study Session
Meeting Minute Summaries September 22, 2026
The purpose of this study session was to provide the Board with foundational information about the data 
center industry in advance of the Sept. 25 data center tour.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Background and information of data centers were presented. A quick history of data centers and current 
data centers were reviewed. 
The four types of data centers were listed to include hyperscale, colocation, enterprise, and edge /micro. 
The characteristics of each were presented.
The different water and cooling systems were discussed. Further discussion was held as to the different 
types of cooling and the impacts. 
The benefits of Data Centers an economic development drives and major infrastructure were listed . 
Commissioner asked about benefits and incentives for Data Center development. 
Staff presented what other communities are doing related to Data Center, both federally and locally . 
Commissioners asked about infrastructure and water concerns.
A map of the local area and indicated data centers was displayed. There are 57 different data centers in 
Colorado. Staff elaborated on each type of Data Centers in Colorado. 
The BOCC will be going on a tour of Data Centers, so policy considerations were presented . 
Commissioner included a public educational consideration. The details of the tour were explained. 
Commissioner asked about what to expect at the follow-up study session. Discussion was held 
surrounding the future needs and decision making for the county. 
Presentation concluded without vote. Commissioner Baker absent and excused. Commissioner 
Campbell appeared virtually. 
The meeting was adjourned.
