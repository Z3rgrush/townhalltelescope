Local Adoption of the Colorado Swimming Pools and Mineral Baths Regulations 
Purpose and Request: 
Presentation
Resolution
Attachments:
Arapahoe County Public Health (ACPH) requests that the Board of Health adopt a resolution 
locally adopting by reference the Colorado Swimming Pools and Mineral Baths Regulations, 
5 CCR 1003-5 (Regulations), for administration and enforcement within ACPH’s 
jurisdiction.
ACPH currently administers and enforces the Regulations for public and semi-public aquatic 
venues. The proposed resolution would formally establish the existing state code as the local

regulatory baseline for the program. It would not change the standards currently applied to 
regulated facilities.
The resolution would also direct ACPH to begin a separate, stakeholder-informed process to 
develop proposed local aquatic venue regulations for future consideration by the Board. That 
future process will include evaluation of the standards in the Centers for Disease Control and 
Prevention’s (CDC) Model Aquatic Health Code (MAHC), along with potential permitting 
or licensing requirements, fees, and associated enforcement procedures.
The resolution does not adopt the MAHC, establish a licensing program, approve new fees, 
or impose new technical requirements at this time.
