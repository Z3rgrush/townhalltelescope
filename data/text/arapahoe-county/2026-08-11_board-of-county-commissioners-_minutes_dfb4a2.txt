The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

The purpose of this study session was the County’s Strategic Plan - Arapahoe Forward - emphasized 
Economic Resilience and Stability as a key focus area to strengthen the county’s economic foundation 
by ensuring financial sustainability, housing security, and business growth. Investing in economic 
development activities can help advance the County’s strategic plan by offering higher quality public 
services and more job opportunities for residents. This meeting provided an overview of the County’s 
current economic development program and tools, as well as a review of how economic development is 
structured in neighboring counties. Staff requests that the Board provide feedback on the tools available 
to counties and identify any areas where they would like additional information.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff provided the purpose and outline of the presentation. 
A background and overview of the Economic Development Program was discussed. 
The objectives of Economic Development were reviewed. These objectives were previously identified 
by the Board and included to attract, expand, and retain primary employers, to rebuild and /or enhance 
county tax base post-COVID, to strengthen job talent pipeline, to increase awareness of county role in 
supporting businesses, and to chart cohesive and collaborative approaches that would improve focus and 
reduce overlap among partners. 
Staff discussed the county approach. The county leverages Arapahoe /Douglas Works! resources to 
support businesses and job seekers. The county also partners with economic development organizations 
across multiple focus areas to support work throughout the county. 
Commissioner asked staff regarding the small business development centers. 
The Strategic Plan was discussed. Within the plan economic resilience and stability as well as workforce 
and community prosperity were highlighted as the areas of importance.
Current tools and resources were listed and reviewed. These included Arapahoe /Douglas Works!, South 
Metro Enterprise Zone, County Tax Incentive Program, and Revitalization Areas. 
Commissioner asked staff about the six businesses within the County Tax Incentive Program . 
Commissioner noted interest in seeing demographic data. 
The Regional Economic Development Structure was presented. Staff highlighted internal vs. external 
staffing, funding investment, location within county structure, and available tools and resources. 
Staff presented the Environmental Scan data for the County Economic Development Structure. The 
counties that were discussed were Adams County, Boulder County, Douglas County, Jefferson County, 
and Larimer County.

11:45 AM *Grant Opportunity: State Historical Fund
Board Summary Report
Grant Evaluation Form
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to for the Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) to consider 
approving the electronic submission of a grant application to History Colorado’s State Historical Fund 
(SHF) for up to $250,000 to help fund preservation and restoration work at 17 Mile House.
A background of the request was presented. 
Staff are seeking the BOCC approval to submit a grant application to SHF to support continued 
preservation and rehabilitation work at the 17 Mile House. The grant request would fund up to $250,000 
or up to 75% of the project cost. Matching funds would come from the Open Space fund. 
Staff discussed the scope of work for the project. 
Commissioner asked staff regarding the replacements of the windows and if there are plans to reuse the 
original windows in another way. 
5-0 in favor of the request.

The purpose of this study session was to provide information and receive directions for 2027 Total 
Compensation Benefits package. This includes specific direction for the following benefits: increasing 
the County’s contribution to the 401a Defined Benefit plan by .25%; reviewing the renewal and setting 
rates for the 2027 medical plans; use of the dental reserve to manage premium impact; change to 
Vacation carryover policy; and approval on the 2027 holiday schedules. It is also to provide 
compensation information for: market updates and 2027 salary structure adjustments and 
reclassifications; collective bargained compensation proposals; increase to one-time award maximum; 
and DA hiring rates and compression request.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
The agenda was laid out for the presentation.
The County’s total compensation philosophy was presented. The five components are compensations, 
benefits, work-life success, performance recognition, and development & career opportunity. 
Staff reviewed benefits and wellbeing in comparison with the market and listed benefits that leads, at, or 
below market. The County’s retirement contribution is currently a 10% match, and staff are 
recommending increasing the contribution to 10.25%. 
The net value of the 2026 DHMO was reviewed by a calculation compared to the market. Negations 
took place regarding medical renewal for 2027 for a renewal of 9.5%. The premium cost share for 
DHMO, Choice PPO, and overall was presented. A biweekly price comparison was displayed. The 
impact of the increase was explained with two examples of different types of employees. The strategy 
for the medical renewal was presented to the BOCC for consideration.
Commissioner asked about the timing and schedule of the negotiations. 
The dental renewal for 2027 was presented. The renewal was an increase of .3% and a dental 
administrative fee increase of 2.9%. Staff recommended reducing the employees’ contribution and 
having the cost come from the reserve. 
Commissioner asked about the possibility of a rate increase.
5-0 in favor of the dental renewal. 
The Life, AD&D, Disability and Medical Gap RFP program was mentioned. Staff went to market to 
match or enhance the current Life and AD&D, Short-term Disability, Longterm Disability, Accident, 
Critical Illness, and Hospital Indemnity plans.
The well-being programs of the county were listed, and the statistics of each program was reviewed.
The vacation carry over was presented. Staff proposed a carry over policy that creates three accrual tiers 
based on years of service.
