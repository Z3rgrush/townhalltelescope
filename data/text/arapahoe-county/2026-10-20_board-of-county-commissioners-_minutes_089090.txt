The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

Board Summary ReportAttachments:
The purpose of this study session was Arapahoe County Public Health requested a change in the scope 
for the build-out of Arapahoe Plaza. They would like to open a new Vital Records office to service the 
area and are seeking direction from the BOCC on how to proceed.
A background of the request was presented. 
ACPH are requesting to redirect $ 40,135 of Contingency Funds from the project to accommodate the 
new scope of work to change the Harm reduction space into a Vital Records office. The required design 
modifications were discussed and are necessary to comply with state standards and regulations 
associated with operating a vital office. Discussion was held regarding the departments that would be 
moving into the Vital Records office
Commissioner asked if the Vital Records Office would be open to the public state-wide not just 
Arapahoe County residence. 
Commissioner asked regarding the fiscal impact on the project and where the funds would be utilized 
from. Commissioner also clarified how much was set aside in contingency funds for the project and how 
much has been utilized so far. 
5-0 in favor of the request. 
Commissioner noted the importance of maintaining proper budget process moving forward.

1:00 PM *Executive Budget Committee Presentation of the 2027 Recommended Budget
Board Summary Report
AC Decision Support Framework
Presentation
2027 Recommended Budget
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to present the recommendations of the Executive Budget 
Committee for the 2027 budget to the full Board of County Commissioners. All of the recommendations 
presented regarding the budget are the basis for the 2027 Recommended Budget document that was 
presented to the Board during a public hearing on October 13, 2026.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff shared 2027 budget quick facts and figures regarding total revenues, total expenditures, total 
staffing, estimated ending 2027 General Fund balance, total county net assessed value, total county mill 
levy, and Capital Improvement Program budget. 
Revenue trends were reviewed. A graph was displayed showing the General Fund Revenue Growth 
Trends from 2018-2027.
A chart was displayed showing Total Actual Value and New Construction Trend. Other charts 
displaying Total Assessed Value and Revenue Trend and Estimate of Property Tax Revenue - Post 1A 
were reviewed. Staff also presented charts showing the comparison of Property Tax and Sales Tax from 
Arapahoe County, Adams County, Douglas County, and Jefferson County.
Commissioner asked regarding the reduction of the mill levy for ACWW PID. 
EBC goals and objectives were listed and reviewed. Main goals were for the General Fund to be 
structurally balanced with a set-aside for future years, to continue to make major investments in key 
areas of essential service delivery-priorities enabled by Issue 1A, and to plan thoughtfully for the future 
through long-term commitments to capital improvements. 
Staff provided insight on the importance to structurally balancing the General Fund operating budget 
and listed how it would benefit the County. A graph was displayed showing the Requested Budget 
Packages from the General Fund.
