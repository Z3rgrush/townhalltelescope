The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

1:30 PM *Federal Lobbyist Update
Board Summary Report
Presentation
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to provide an update on federal advocacy activities to the Board 
of County Commissioners.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.  
FY2027 Continuing Resolution (CR) was discussed. Topics that are included within the CR were listed 
and reviewed. 
Staff presented the update on OMB Federal-Grant Rule. The Senate temporarily blocked the OMB from 
finalizing or implementing changes to the federal-grant rule until December 11th. 
Commissioner noted the provision that was put in place indicating that funding could end at any point 
and asked staff for other major concerns. 
Discussion was held regarding the different agencies that would be affected by the OMB Federal-Grant 
Rule. 
Discussion was made surrounding where the FY 2027 Appropriations stand as of Sept. 14, 2026. The 
House Status and the Senate Status for the Committee Progress, Floor Action, Defense Bill, Next Steps, 
and Fiscal Year were reviewed. 
An update on Reconciliation 3.0 was presented. The resolution would allow for tens of billions in 
spending for military, farm, and election-related priorities. 
The Safeguard American Voter Eligibility (SAVE) Act was reviewed. This Act would require proof of 
citizenship to register to vote in all federal elections. Discussion was held regarding Colorado 
continuing with mail-in ballots for the mid-term election. 
House Resolution 1490 and 1499 were reviewed and discussed. HR 1499 combined the debate rules for 
multiple distinct pieces of legislation into a single procedural vote. HR 1490 would condemn socialism 
in all its forms, oppose socialist politics, and would call for enacting the SAVE America Act regarding 
citizen-only voting.

FY27 National Defense Authorization Act ( NDAA) was presented. The Act sets forth policies and 
authorizes the Department of Defense program, activities, and military constructions. It also includes 
national security programs for the Department of Energy. 
Commissioner asked regarding the NDAA and if the Senate would be able to get the SAVE Act 
excluded. 
Child Vaccine Recommendations Executive Order was reviewed. Within the EO, vaccines are listed in 
three different categories. These include immunizations recommended for all children, immunizations 
recommended for certain high-risk groups, and immunizations based on shared clinical decision 
making. It is still advised to follow doctor’s guidelines for child vaccines as there is not a lot of 
enforcement behind the EO. 
The House Session discussion items were listed and reviewed. The dates that the House is in Session are 
August 31st-September 3rd, and September 14th-17th. The Senate Session discussion items were listed 
and discussed. The dates that the Senate is in Session are September 14th-October 2nd.   
Commissioner asked regarding the delay with SNAP penalties and work requirements. 
Staff shared the post-election agenda. Discussion was made regarding what would
happen if Democrats would take majority of the House.
Discussion was held regarding Commissioner Summey’s agenda for DC trip. 
Presentation concluded without vote. Commissioner Fields absent and excused.
