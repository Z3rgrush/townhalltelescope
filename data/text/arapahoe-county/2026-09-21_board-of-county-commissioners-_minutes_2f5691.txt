The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

The purpose of this study session was an informational update to the Board of County Commissioners 
from the E-470 Public Highway Authority.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff presented background of E- 470 and listed facts about the board and E- 470 usage. A history of 
E-470 was reviewed with impactful years such as 1988, 1991, 2003, 2009, 2025, and 2026. 
Commissioner asked staff regarding the westside tollway (C- 470) and clarified that it is managed 
through the state. 
The governance of E- 470 consists of 8 voting members and 9 non-voting members from different 
districts and agencies. 
Staff discussed the partnerships both federally and locally. Staff elaborated on the different ways that 
E-470 interacts with the county and projects.
A line graph was displayed showing the total toll transaction growth from 2023-2026. 2026 YTD 
statistics for E- 470 were presented. E- 470 is currently undergoing a rebrand to better reflect their 
strategy. 
Commissioner Fields left at 2:02 PM.
Announcements were made such as an updated website and new plazas along E-470 to expand customer 
services. 
National Interoperability was reviewed. Multiple states partner to use transponder and send money back 
to original state. 
Recent and upcoming projects were listed. 
Current Colorado toll roads were discussed, including CTIO wanted to separate from E- 470. 
Commissioners asked for clarification related to possible confusion because of the inclusion of other 
transponders. 
A map of the current Denver Metro toll roads was displayed. 
Discussion was held as to the toll rates. 
Presentation concluded without a vote. Commissioner Fields absent and excused.

Arapahoe County Water Supply Study
BOCC Meeting Nov. 12, 2024
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to present a proposed update to the 2018 Arapahoe County 
Comprehensive Plan specifically incorporating a new water supply element as mandated by recent state 
law. The proposed amendment builds on recommendations from the 2024 Water Supply Study and if 
adopted, would meet statutory requirements for a Water Supply Element to the Comprehensive Plan . 
Staff is seeking Board comments on the proposed Water Supply Element chapter and support for 
proceeding with the proposed amendment.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff presented background to the water study and the purpose of the inclusion of the Water Supply 
Element.
Senate Bill 24-174 was reviewed for its effects on affordable housing and water supply. A water supply 
study was conducted to plan for future water needs. 9 out of 10 recommendations were listed. The study 
also established two growth scenarios. 
A rendering and modeling of the Denver Basin Aquifers were displayed. A graph was displayed to show 
the availability of the aquifers. 
Chapter V for the water supply element is organized into seven sections. These include Introduction and 
Statutory Compliance, Current Water Landscape, Growth Scenarios and Projected Water Demand (2050 
Horizon), Water Supply Management and Groundwater Protection, Goals, Policies, and Strategies, 
Water Conservation and Efficiency, Water Collaborative Policies and Actions, and Water Supply 
Element Implementation.
The next steps for the plan amendment were presented. 
Commissioner thanked staff for their work since water will be county wide issue. 
Commissioner asked for clarification on the groundwater analysis chart. 
Presentation concluded without a vote. Commissioner Fields appeared virtually.
