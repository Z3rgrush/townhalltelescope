The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

2:00 PM *Approve vehicle retention policy for county fleet management operations
Board Summary Report
Draft Policy
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to seek Board concurrence to advance a vehicle retention policy 
to the next business meeting consent agenda for approval.
Staff presented the purpose of the item for a vehicle retention policy. The current process is regarding 
purchasing and replacing vehicles that exceed their lifecycle when specific business needs exist.
The amendments would establish a buyback from auction.
Commissioner mentioned the regulatory process for compliance for FFM. Commissioner also asked 
about exemptions for the replacement lifecycle. 
Commissioner inquired about retaining vehicles and how those would appear on the asset list. 
Commissioner asked about the retention for parts. 
5-0 in favor to move to consent agenda. 
2:15 PM *Request to transfer unallocated CIP funds to the Detention Facility Medical Expansion 
Project
Board Summary ReportAttachments:
The purpose of this study session was to request $ 250,000 of unallocated capital improvement fund 
monies to be transferred to the Detention Facility Medical Expansion to address unforeseen life-safety 
smoke control system requirements and complete the project as current funds are insufficient to address 
these mechanical modifications and remaining project closeout tasks.
Item was heard after “Approval to replace a heavy-duty vehicle lift for fleet services”.
Delays were caused by a smoke control system. The steps for the approval process were reviewed for a 
detention facility compared to other building types.

2:30 PM *Approval to replace a heavy-duty vehicle lift for fleet services
Board Summary ReportAttachments:
The purpose of this study session was to seek approval to proceed with the procurement and installation 
of a new vehicle lift to ensure continued safe fleet maintenance operations.
Item was heard before “Request to transfer unallocated CIP funds to the Detention Facility Medical 
Expansion Project”. 
Staff presented the need for a replacement of a vehicle lift. The vehicle lift is currently out of service 
and if the vendor was to fix the issue, the county would lose the safety certification for the lift.  
Staff recommended to replace the lift. Funds will be borrowed to purchase and will be moved in Second 
Quarter Budget Amendments. 
5-0 in favor to move forward with recommendations.

A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff presented background for the strategic plan. The timeline and milestones were reviewed. In 2025 a

2026 steps for finding opportunities are being conducted. The data is utilized to better define outcomes

The county scorecard integration was discussed. The County scorecard measures outcome measures and
