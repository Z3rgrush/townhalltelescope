The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

2:00 PM *I-70 REAP Annual Update
Board Summary Report

REAP Byers Drone Academy 2026
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was to provide the Board with an annual update from their economic 
development partner, I-70 REAP.
Executive Director of I-70 REAP provided background, scope of work, and mission of REAP. 
Commissioner Campbell arrived at 2:17PM.
Discussion was held regarding the rezoning occurring in Byers and that others are now interested in RV 
resort developments. 
Further discussion was made surrounding educational programs that are offered to students in rural 
areas of Eastern Colorado. These programs are being funded through REAP and continue to grow 
resources and opportunities for the community. 
An overview of the work the REAP has done throughout 2026 was reviewed. Quarterly meetings 
surrounding transportation, energy, and data centers have been held. 
I-70 REAP are requesting larger funding from Arapahoe County for 2027. The reasonings for the 
request were discussed. The work that REAP intends to accomplish in 2027 was discussed. 
Commissioner asked for clarification regarding the funding request. 
Commissioner noted the opportunities that are needed for the rural communities in Arapahoe County. 
Commissioner discussed the drone resources that the Sheriff’s Office could contribute to REAP. 
Presentation concluded without vote.

A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
President & CEO of South Metro Denver Chamber of Commerce shared the history and background of 
the County’s relationship with the Chamber and why it so important for communication, understanding, 
and collaborations to strengthen relationships, encourage meaningful conversations, and bring people 
together to identify practical solutions that benefit businesses, communities, and the entire region. 
The Chamber’s purpose and mission were shared.  
How the Chamber supports the community and Arapahoe County was discussed. 
The three pillars were listed. Those include economic development, legislative advocacy, and business 
engagement. 
Discussion was held regarding the importance of educating businesses and the communities.  
BOCC was asked if there are anything that the Chamber is missing from their scope of work that BOCC 
wish to see happen. 
Commissioner noted the fluctuation of memberships for the other Chambers in the region . 
Commissioner wishes to know the challenges that the Chamber is facing and how the County can assist 
to provide support. 
Discussion surrounding 2027 funding was held. The Chamber is requesting $ 35,000 from the County 
and reviewed how those funds would be utilized.  
Commissioner clarified the funding request.  
Presentation concluded without vote.
