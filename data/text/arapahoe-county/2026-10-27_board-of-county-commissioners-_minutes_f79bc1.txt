The public is welcome to attend Business Meetings held in the East Hearing Room at the Arapahoe 
County Administration Building, 5334 South Prince Street, Littleton, CO 80120. Business meetings also 
can be viewed online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Virtual public comment is allowed on general issues or 
specific agenda items by calling 1-855-436-3656 and, when prompted to, by pressing *3 on the 
telephone keypad to be entered in the speaking queue.
The Board of County Commissioners typically holds Public Hearings at 9:30 a.m. the second and fourth 
Tuesday of each month. Items listed on the consent agenda are adopted with one vote. Items listed 
under regular business are considered individually. 
The public may comment during designated times as directed by the Chair of the Board. Each speaker 
will be limited to one three-minute period of comment, unless modified by the Chair of the Board.
Meeting agendas are available at arapahoe.legistar.com. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 
795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303-795-4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

5.a. Proclamation Recognizing October 2026 as Domestic Violence Awareness Month
ProclamationAttachments:
The proclamation recognizing October 2026 as Domestic Violence Awareness Month was 
read. 
Commissioner Campbell arrived at 10:04AM.
Commissioner made comment surrounding the importance to recognize Domestic Violence 
Awareness and to ensure children are protected and families are supported. Commissioner 
commented about the changes happening at the federal level and the financial hardships that 
can occur after Domestic Violence. 
Commissioner made comment regarding the concern of mass shootings in relation to 
Domestic Violence and the dangers of having firearms in the home and the importance in 
recognizing signs of Domestic Violence and act before fatal situations occur. Commissioner 
thanked DA Office for their work and efforts to support those in these situations. 
Commissioner highlighted the importance of protecting children within homes that are 
experiencing Domestic Violence and how to break the cycle of generational trauma. 
Executive Director from Three Birds Alliance spoke about the partnership between Three 
Birds Alliance and Arapahoe County. Director further spoke regarding statistics of Domestic 
Violence throughout the years. The history and the work that staff do at Three Birds Alliance 
was shared. 
The 18th JD District Attorney further spoke about the work that the DA Office does for 
Domestic Violence cases. DA shared priorities and the importance of holding Domestic 
Violence offenders accountable. 
A motion was made by Chair Pro Tem Baker, seconded by Commissioner Fields, that this 
Proclamation be adopted. The motion carried by the following vote:
Commissioner Warren-Gully, Chair Pro Tem Baker, Chairperson Summey, 
and Commissioner Fields

6. CONSENT AGENDA

discussion, and may be voted on as an entire group with a single motion. (The motion is “to

consent agenda be removed for separate discussion. This enables the item to be considered

7.b. Public Hearing on the 2027 Recommended Budget
Board Summary Report
AC Decision Support Framework
Presentation
2027 Recommended Budget
Proposed Motion
Resolution
Attachments:
John Christofferson, Acting County Attorney, established jurisdiction for the Board to 
consider this matter. 
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff shared 2027 budget quick facts and figures regarding total revenues, total expenditures, 
total staffing, estimated ending 2027 General Fund balance, total county net assessed value, 
total county mill levy, and Capital Improvement Program budget.  
Revenue trends were reviewed. A graph was displayed showing the General Fund Revenue 
Growth Trends from 2018-2027.
A chart was displayed showing Total Actual Value and New Construction Trend. Other 
charts displaying Total Assessed Value and Revenue Trend and Estimate of Property Tax 
Revenue - Post 1A were reviewed. Staff also presented charts showing the comparison of 
Property Tax and Sales Tax from Arapahoe County, Adams County, Douglas County, and 
Jefferson County.
Commissioner asked for clarification regarding property tax totals and the reason for the 
decrease. 
EBC goals and objectives were listed and reviewed. Main goals were for the General Fund to 
be structurally balanced with a set-aside for future years, to continue to make major 
investments in key areas of essential service delivery-priorities enabled by Issue 1A, and to 
plan thoughtfully for the future through long-term commitments to capital improvements.  
Staff provided insight on the importance to structurally balancing the General Fund operating 
budget and listed how it would benefit the County. A graph was displayed showing the 
Requested Budget Packages from the General Fund.
Commissioner asked regarding the $ 19 million set aside funding that would be considered 
“ongoing” funding and clarified how those funds could be utilized. 
The General Fund was discussed. A graph was displayed showing the General Fund 
Operating Budget from 2018-2027.  
Total Compensation budget was reviewed. The EBC recommendations were discussed.

Recommended budget packages from the General Fund and Other Funds were listed and 
reviewed.  
The recommended staffing level for 2027 additions was discussed. Graphs were displayed 
showing the total budgeted staffing from 2018-2027, and the total FTE Changes by 
Department. A chart was shared providing the total FTEs and the impact on the General 
Fund, and the ALEA Fund.  
Commissioner commended Finance staff on how the County’s priorities are being supported 
through the budget recommendations from the General Fund and highlighted 
recommendations for Community Resources and the DA Office. 
The budget requests that were not recommended were discussed. A graph was presented 
showing the total FTE not recommended based on the departments. A chart was shared 
displaying the departments making the requests, total amounts requested from the General 
Fund and all other funds, the total amounts EBC recommended, and the amounts not 
recommended. Staff also shared the notable budget package requests that were not 
recommended.  
Staff discussed the Capital Improvement Program and the projects being recommended for 
funding. A graph was displayed showing the Capital Expenditure Fund Budgeted Revenue 
Sources from 2020-2027.  
Major investments in key areas of essential service delivery were reviewed. These included 
investments in infrastructure and road maintenance, public safety, and housing and protective 
services.  
A review of the General Fund was presented. A graph showing the fund balance trend and 
forecast from 2024-2031 was displayed.  
Charts were displayed showing significant fund budgets and other budgeted funds. The 
significant funds that were discussed included the Social Services Fund, the Law 
Enforcement Fund, the Road and Bridge Fund, the Capital Expenditure Fund, the Recreation 
District Fund, and the ACWW PID Fund. The other budgeted funds that were highlighted 
were the Arapahoe/Douglas Works Fund, the Grant Fund, the Open Space Sales Tax Fund, 
the Infrastructure Fund, the Central Services Fund, and the Public Health Fund. 
 
Future dates and plans were listed.
Commissioners thanked staff for their work. 
The public comment period was opened.
One person spoke.
