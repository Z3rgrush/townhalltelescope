The Arapahoe County Board of Commissioners typically holds weekly study sessions on Monday and 
Tuesday. Study sessions (except for executive sessions) are open to the public and agendas are available 
online at arapahoe.legistar.com. Meetings marked with an asterisk (*) can be attended virtually via 
arapahoe.legistar.com while non-asteriked (*) sessions are open to in-person attendance only. The 
members of the Board of County Commissioners may choose to attend study sessions virtually. 
The Board of County Commissioners may go into executive session during or at the conclusion of a 
study session or administrative meeting as necessary to receive legal advice or discuss other 
confidential matters, and if they do so, the public will be excluded from that portion of the meeting. The 
Board may alter the times of the meetings throughout the day, as well as cancel or reschedule noticed 
meetings. Contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or kdavis2@arapahoegov.com with 
questions about the agenda.
Arapahoe County is committed to making its public meetings accessible to persons with disabilities. If 
you need special accommodations, please contact the Commissioners’ Office at 303 795 4630 or Relay 
Colorado 711 at least 3 days in advance to make arrangements.

The purpose of this study session was to update the Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) on the 
status of the 2026 budget, to review the fiscal status of Arapahoe County, and identify issues that may 
arise in the near future. Various supplemental requests will be reviewed by the Executive Budget 
Committee (EBC) and recommended for approval by the Board. These supplemental requests will be 
presented, and the Board will be asked to review these proposals and decide which should go forward to 
a public hearing on September 8, 2026.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
The economic outlook was shared. A data table was shared showing the economic indicators including 
Real GDP Growth, Unemployment Rate, Personal Income Growth, Wage and Salary Growth, Inflation, 
Housing Permit Growth, and Nonresidential Building Growth from 2024-2028.
Graphs were displayed showing the projected assumptions for the total actual value and new 
construction trend, and the total assessed value and revenue trend from 2011-2031.
A graph showing the key expenditure growth assumptions from 2021-2031 was reviewed. 
The General Fund was discussed. A table showing the total revenue, total expenditures, beginning fund 
balance, and the ending fund balance from 2025-2026 were presented and discussed. A table showing 
the General Fund data from 2025-2031 was also shared and reviewed.

inquired if next year is the last year that implications will be seen from that work. Commissioner also 
asked regarding commercial property taxes. 
Commissioner asked for clarification of the 1A funding for ongoing costs until 2030. 
The Capital Expenditure Fund was discussed. A table showing the total revenue, total expenditures, 
beginning fund balance, the ending fund balance, and the funds available for appropriation from 
2025-2026 were presented and discussed. A table showing the Capital Expenditure Fund data from 
2025-2031 was also shared and reviewed.

2:30 PM *Proposed Rewrite of Land Dedication/Cash-in-lieu Code Provisions (Land Development 
Code Section 4.2-5, Land Dedication Standards)
Board Summary Report
Presentation
Arapahoe County Land Dedication Standards Report
Current Arapahoe County Land Dedication Standards
Draft Public Land Dedication Revisions
Attachments:
The purpose of this study session was staff was seeking feedback on a proposed rewrite of the County’s 
Land Dedication /Cash in Lieu Code. If the Board supports this proposed rewrite, staff will request 
approval to solicit public feedback on the proposed rewrite.
A PowerPoint presentation was presented, a copy of which has been retained for the record.
Staff presented background for the request to amend the Cash- in-Lieu Code. A previous study was 
conducted to update dedication standards. An example of the current code and the proposed amendment 
was reviewed for park land and schools. 
The new provisions that needed to be addressed were listed. The new provisions include subdivision v . 
zoning changes, land sustainability for parks and school, removal of “Public Purpose” provision, and 
waivers. 
Provisions that have been presented before but have been revised since were presented. These include 
Commercial & industrial land dedication and Market Appraisal & Assumed Value approach. 
The provisions that will remain the same were listed.
Staff presented the next steps for the amendment process. 
County attorney staff further presented public land dedication standards and authority. The provisions 
that would be removed were listed. 
Staff discussed the use of DOLA’s template code and redline it for Arapahoe County’s needs. New 
materials that were added by the County Attorney’s Office were reviewed. Language was also added to 
account for future changes. 
Commissioner asked about an example of a property that raised concerns. 
Commissioner asked for clarification of a provision and the county’s authority. 
Commissioner inquired as to the purpose of the amendments. 
Discussion was held as to the cash- in-lieu of residential and schools compared to industrial properties
