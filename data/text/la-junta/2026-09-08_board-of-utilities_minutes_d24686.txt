Pueblo Sewage Spill 
Since this spill occurred, the river has been monitored more closely. The CDPHE has 
now put up a website where it shows all their different testing sites for e-coli and other 
contaminants. The test is measured in CFUs or colony-forming units. The Colorado 
Swim Beach Safe level is 235 CFUs. The sample from La Junta taken on September 
6th showed 770.1 CFUs. To clear this spill basically comes down to sunlight, air and 
gravity. A natural process that normally breaks down most of your harmful bacterias that 
do not like UV light. This spill will basically just take its time. It is suggested to follow all 
normal guidelines that is being put forth by the CDPHE, Pueblo County Health 
Department and Otero County Health Department. Don't swim, wade, kayak, or do 
anything in the river. Definitely do not drink from the river or allow your animals to drink 
from the river. Don't use river water or untreated shallow well water for cooking, bathing 
or other consumption. Don't use for cleaning vegetables or preparing meals. It's an 
unfortunate event. It was a spill where it was an older pipe that had a plug that popped 
out, and they didn't notice it for about thirty-six hours.
