Marueen Rikhof – This was our second project request under the new application 
format. In the contract, it does say 180 days and we are around 52 days past that. 
There are four major components of that second aspect. Those would be fire 
suppression, HVAC, plumbing and exterior doors. Fire suppression is currently being 
installed and they are two days away from the inspection. Once that is complete, we will 
then install the insulation, finish the walls, install the sprinkler heads for the system and 
then they are good to go. They are days away from being completed. HVAC is done, 
except it is assembled off-site, which is why we didn’t install all of the components yet. 
With plumbing, the DWV (Drain, Waste, & Venting) is finished in the basement and 
most of the first floor is finished; now moving up to the second floor. That was the 
portion in this application process. The “Little ‘Nut” (garage behind the Chestnut 
Apartments) is completely plumbed. As for the doors, they were delayed because there 
was an engineering issue regarding the balconies outside with the door installation. All 
in all, we are probably four to five weeks away from completing this scope of work.
