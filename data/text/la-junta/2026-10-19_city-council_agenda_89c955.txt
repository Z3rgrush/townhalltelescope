C. City Manager Report 
• Police Department: For the month of September, 1,710 calls, 34 warrant arrests, 
83 arrests, 111 traffic stops, 10 traffic collisions.  
• Code Enforcement: 232 calls, 116 of them being muni-code violations, another 
47 for code enforcement issues, and 69 animal complaints. 
• Fire Department: A total of 177 calls, 117 EMS calls, 16 motor vehicle collisions, 
two structure fires, two mutual aids, two search and rescues and then 38 other 
calls for service. 
• This is Fire Prevention Week. They will see over 700 kids. Schools included will 
be Manzanola, Swink, La Junta, Cheraw and McClave. 
• The City has received a grant up to about $30 million. It's from the Emergency 
Contaminants in Small and Disadvantaged Communities. It will be about $10 
million over the next three years to help upgrade and improve our R.O. Plant. 
This will help out with the PFAS, manganese and selenium. 
• The airport runway is complete. They'll be doing chip sealing on the taxiway 
sometime this month. 
• Street Department: Ongoing street repairs on 9th Street between San Juan and 
Belleview, then 1st and 3rd on Belleview and Lincoln. After the Christmas 
parade, they'll start on the intersection of 5th and Colorado. 
• Parks: Still waiting on scheduling for a plumber to get the toilets installed. 
D. Community Events/Council Report 
• AI Town Hall is happening right now. 
• Cyber Security Defense Lunch & Learn tomorrow from noon to 1:00 p.m. 
• Tarantula Fest was awesome, and it's appreciated what it's done for the town. 
• Tour de Santa Fe from the Valley Regional Medical Center was another 
successful event. 
• The Art Project, celebrating its fifth anniversary. 
• The Plaza Block Building is being used, and they had their first Friday event 
there. 
• This Saturday, CASA has their Boots and Bling fundraiser at the Trading Post. 
• It is La Junta High School's homecoming week. Their theater just wrapped up 
their play "The House." 
• Colorado Trust generally deals with health equity within Colorado. They were the 
title sponsor for the second year for the Tour de Santa Fe. 
• Some other past events were the Sangre de Cristo and Casa del Sol Cream Can 
Dinner and the Chuckwagon at the museum.

THIRD AMENDMENT TO THE CONSOLIDATION AGREEMENT 
This Third Amendment to the Consolidation Agreement (this "Amendment") is entered 
into by and between the CITY OF LA JUNTA, acting through its duly elected City Council (the 
"City"), and the ARKANSAS VALLEY REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER, a Colorado nonprofit 
corporation ("AVRMC"). The City and AVRMC are referred to individually as a "Party" and 
collectively as the "Parties." 
RECITALS 
WHEREAS, the City, AVRMC, and the Mennonite Mission Network, formerly known as 
the Mennonite Board of Missions ("MMN"), are parties to that certain Consolidation Agreement 
executed on or about October 27, 1997, and recorded July 2, 1998 at Reception No. 563944 of the 
Otero County, Colorado records, as amended by the First Amendment to the Consolidation 
Agreement effective March 25, 2008 and the Second Amendment to the Consolidation Agreement 
dated May 26, 2026 (collectively, the "Agreement"); and 
WHEREAS, by letter dated January 29, 2026, MMN confirmed that amendments to the 
Agreement mutually approved by the City and AVRMC do not require the further approval or 
consent of MMN; and 
WHEREAS, since its inception, AVRMC has been a nonprofit hospital recognized under 
Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code and, as such, operates for charitable purposes that 
include providing care to patients regardless of their ability to pay; and 
WHEREAS, as a hospital located in the state of Colorado, AVRMC has continuously 
provided health care services to low income residents of the City of La Junta and its surrounding 
service area who are unable to pay for such services, consistent with its obligations under Colorado 
law and associated Provider Agreement, first through the Colorado Indigent Care Program and the 
current Colorado Hospital Discounted Care Act, C.R.S. § 25.5-3-501 et seq.; and 
WHEREAS, AVRMC has maintained various policies related to its nondiscrimination 
based on a patient’s ability to pay, including its EMTALA Policy and its Financial Assistance 
Policy; and 
WHEREAS, AVRMC desires to enter into this Amendment memorialize AVRMC's 
commitment to provide such care  through an arrangement that is not dependent on annual 
applications or changes to Colorado law , and the Parties therefore wish to amend the Agreement 
to document this longstanding arrangement for recordkeeping purposes; 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals, which are incorporated 
by reference, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which 
are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
1. Covenant to Provide Care to Low Income Residents 
AVRMC covenants and agrees that it shall continue to provide health care services to low 
income residents of the City of La Junta and its service area who are not entitled to benefits under

2 
Title XVIII of the Social Security Act (Medicare) and not eligible for medical assistance under the 
Colorado state plan (Medicaid), regardless of such residents' ability to pay, in accordance with (a) 
AVRMC's Financial Assistance Policy, as it may be amended from time to time, and (b) the 
Colorado Hospital Discounted Care Act, C.R.S. § 25.5-3-501 et seq., or successor laws.  
AVRMC further covenants that it shall continue to prepare a community health needs 
assessment for its service area in a manner consistent with applicable law and shall share the results

as amended by this Amendment.

In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Amendment and the Agreement, the 
terms of this Amendment shall control solely with respect to the matters expressly addressed 
herein. 
5. Defined Terms 
Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Amendment shall have the 
meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.

original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Signatures
