NEW BUSINESS  
A. A Resolution of the City Council of La Junta, Colorado, Authorizing the 
Conveyance of Certain Real Property Located at 322 Harriet Avenue to Otero 
College (City Attorney) (Action) 
MOTION TO APPROVE A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF LA JUNTA, 
COLORADO, AUTHORIZING THE CONVEYANCE OF CERTAIN REAL PROPERTY 
LOCATED AT 322 HARRIET AVENUE TO OTERO COLLEGE: Rikhof 
SECOND: Velasquez 
DISCUSSION: Michael Hart – This is essentially a junk property that the city has and 
has had for a while. This is a great opportunity for us to get rid of a piece of property 
that we don’t need and have been paying roughly $2,500 per year to maintain. It is also 
an opportunity for the City of La Junta to partner with Otero College, and their intention 
for this property is to utilize their construction management students to go in and rehab 
the dwelling. 
Mayor Joe Ayala – This is something that the college has tried to start for some time 
now. 
City Manager Michael Hart – They are all ready to get this thing going to get their 
students in and rehab the property. 
Council Member Rikhof – It is part of a larger grant, called the “Copper Grant” and it’s 
through the attorney general’s office. Other communities have participated in this. It is 
kind of an exciting first step in what hopefully will be a very productive cycle. 
• Council Member Velasquez – They don’t have to have a prospective buyer prior to the 
construction beginning? 
• Council Member Rikhof – No. 
Council Member Rikhof – A question regarding properties: are there other properties of 
this nature that are also owned by the City of La Junta? 
• City Manager Michael Hart – There are; according to Brock Hinkhouse, we have at 
least four.  
• Council Member Rikhof – Do we have a plan on what we are doing with those four 
properties? 
• City Manager Michael Hart – Not yet, no. 
• Mayor Joe Ayala – When they get to this stage, we either have to pay to get the 
asbestos abated on that property, or we have something cool like this Otero program 
that can do that. 
• City Manager Michael Hart – Correct; Otero College is taking care of all the asbestos 
abatement, etc. 
Mayor Joe Ayala – Just ballpark: what does it cost when we have to get properties like 
these torn down with asbestos abatement? 
• City Manager Michael Hart – Not including asbestos abatement, the last one that 
Martin Montoya did was in the $10,000-$12,000 range. That house was a hoarder's 
house, so there was potentially more stuff in there than a vacant house would have. 
• Mayor Joe Ayala – Sometimes places get lucky and earn grants from the EPA to abate 
asbestos. Similarly, the building that caught fire across from City Hall was all done by 
the EPA and did not cost the City of La Junta a dime. 
• City Manager Michael Hart – There was emergency funding going on with the property 
across the street from City Hall and that was through Ayers. They aided us with filling 
out and filing the applications, and they got it done in a very timely manner. 
VOTE: The motion carried 5-0 
ABSTAIN: Copley 
ABSENT: Pantoya
