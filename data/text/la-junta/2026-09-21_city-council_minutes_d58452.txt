INCIDENT  
Brian Lovato and Brad Davidson explained that Code Red is no longer their reverse 911. This 
company went out of business. At the beginning of this year or end of last year, they found a 
new system called RAVE QR. The Rocky Ford Communication Center sent out a shelter in 
place within a three-block radius. Then, when the all clear was sent out, it went out to 
everybody instead of just the three-block radius. There are some nuances in the new program 
that is being worked out. This is a hot topic, and they are working on getting it figured out and 
making sure everybody is trained.

INFORMATION  
A. Honoring a La Junta Baseball Legend 
Duane Grasmick, La Junta High School athletic director: Wrote a letter last week about 
Jim Sullivan. He was a La Junta native, moved away, came back, and then lead the 
school district in many different forms for thirty years. They are not requesting to 
rename Potter Park but instead name the field Jim Sullivan Field at Potter Park. If 
allowed, they're going to have a big celebration and dedicate the field the night of their 
first game in the Jim Sullivan Classic. As part of their relationship and partnership with 
the city and the high school, they will take care of the signage and the dedication part. 
City Manager Michael Hart did some research and there is a process to do this. In 
Chapter 2.95.110, entitled Procedure for Community or Citizen Request to Name or 
Rename a Facility, there are seven steps for this process. Mr. Hart would be more than 
happy to assist in the process.

NEW BUSINESS  
A. Intergovernmental Agreement Establishing the Arkansas Valley Emergency 
Communications Authority (Brad Davidson) (Action) 
Brad Davidson: Dispatches were combined earlier this year, and they've been in the 
process of working and reorganizing everything to where this group will not be under 
one agency. They were directed to come up with the Arkansas Valley Emergency 
Communication Authority. That way, this group works underneath a board and not one 
specific town or jurisdiction. A specialist came in and drafted this IGA, which is the 
intergovernmental agreement just for all the towns and agencies that are going to be 
involved in this authority. There's a fee on everyone's cell phone in the state of Colorado 
that goes towards 911. A portion of that will help fund this board. (Brad continued to 
answer questions of the board.)

B. First Reading/AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF LA JUNTA ESTABLISHING AN 
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BOARD (City Attorney) (Action) 
Pam Denahy and Michael Hart, along with a few others from the old LJCI board, were 
given the task of getting the economic development arm back up and going. This is 
what the board had put together and sent to the city attorney. The only difference 
between the two codes is the way that the former La Junta Capital was written is it was 
a non-profit. That's where the issue with the IRS comes from. It was discussed to have it 
be more like tourism, where it's an arm of the city so that the budget can be under the 
city and can be audited. The other thing that was dug into was the membership to 
identify representatives that should be on the board. It was kind of open in the last one, 
and it was felt that it would be helpful to line out the members that would have 
representation on the board.
