A. Conflict of Interest Document (City Attorney) (Action) 
City Attorney Buchner: A resolution was passed that adopted a conflict of interest 
disclosure form that was discussed at the last regular meeting that encapsulates the two 
different regimes that govern the statutory code of ethics and conflicts of interest for 
government officials, which is Title 24, Article 18, and Article 129, and then 41 of the 
Colorado Constitution. There's a host of conflicts, from gifts to family to contract interest, 
etc., but it would be more productive of a conversation if we discussed the specific 
points of issues that Council Member Rikhof wanted to discuss and other council 
members wanted to discuss. The broader issue is, if and when the board adopts the 
more robust code of ethics proposed, if there was a conflict and one of the council 
members disagreed, they would then de facto be put in front of the board of ethics for 
an investigation and review. My initial thought would be to allow me to do objective 
analysis and talk individually with the council members. My suggestion is that we are 
allowed to address it informally, whether it's done through a verbal or written disclosure 
form, and then, if a council member insists that there is no conflict, even if they've been 
advised to the contrary, then we could use the soon-to-be adopted or some version of 
procedure for complaints, because at that point it would typically be a complaint that 
there is unethical behavior on the part of a council member. (There was lengthy 
discussion regarding informal process, ethics board, elected officials, employees and 
procedures.)

C. City Manager Report 
• Police Department: 1,800 calls for service for the month of July, 82 arrests, 146 
traffic stops, and 20 traffic collisions. 
• Code Enforcement: 147 calls for service, 12 were junk vehicles, 49 weed 
complaints, nine trash complaints, 57 animal complaints, and 20 miscellaneous 
weed code violations. 
• The PD is hosting a P.O.S.T. approved handgun red dot site instructor school 
this week here in La Junta. There will be about 15 to 20 different students from 
different agencies across the state. 
• Fire Department: 199 calls total. 146 were for EMS. There were 10 accidents and 
39 calls for service. This included four mutual aid responses, two public assists, 
and two severe weather-related calls. They also had three staff members 
complete their wild land fire course.

through door hangers, utility bills, or online.
