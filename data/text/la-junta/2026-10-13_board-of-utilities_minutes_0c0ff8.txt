REPORTS   
A. ARPA Board (Gary Cranson) 
B. Water & Wastewater Treatment (Tom Seaba) 
R.O. Plant 
•    Routine maintenance: PM all 3 degassifiers, serviced RO conductivity probes, tank 
inspections, replaced mixing pump for chlorine tank and replace overpressure line.  
•    Changed out cartridge filters. 
•    South booster generator struck by lightning. Installed new control board and HMI, 
waiting on delivery of a few additional control components.  
•    Well #13 certified and back in service after meter repairs.  
•    Airbase booster control valve #1 needs replaced – not repairable.  
•    Additional facilities and grounds maintenance; meter reading.  
Distribution 
•    Continued on system expansion on 24th Street between Carson and San Juan to 
include a new hydrant and pressure testing/chlorinating new line. 
•    Installed new hydrant at 5th & Colorado in an alternative spot for upcoming street 
project. 
•    Mowed and prepared 25 acres for fall planting.  
•    Meter Reading and utility billing office generated work orders, equipment 
maintenance. 
Wastewater 
•    Treated 26.448 million gallons. Removed 58ft3 of screenings and 53ft3 of grit.  
•    220,000 gallons of sludge was pressed.  
•    RO concentrate discharge to plant was 17.348 million gallons.  
•    Jetted 5,767 of collection line for 5 emergency calls and 26 manhole locate 
requests.  
•    Diagnosed bad sensor on UV system. Waiting on replacement sensor. 
•    Fuses blown on lift stations by lightening. Fuses replaced, manual pumpdown, floats 
adjusted.   
•    Additional facilities and grounds maintenance.  
Other Notables: 
•    Signed contract for unregulated contaminants grant. Project will begin ASAP with 
continued planning, design engineering, and land acquisition.  
•    Regulatory and permit reviews.   
•    Conservation program planning for 2027. 
•    Arkansas Basin Roundtable meeting and subsequent committee meetings with 
discussion involving the Pueblo sewer spill.  
 
 
C. Electric Department (Chris Arguello) 
In addition to daily reads, disconnects, and maintenance, the crew performed these 
additional tasks: 
• Replaced suspension insulators behind Valley Plumbing. 
• Completed extensive maintenance at the Old Boys Ranch. 
• Set up all necessary electrical services for the Tarantula Fest. 
• Installed two 40-foot streetlight poles for Lewis Bolt & Nut at the Anchor plant. 
• Assisted in installing an A/C unit for both the library and police department 
buildings. 
• Installed a lift pole for a secondary line on 10th and Grace. 
• Reinforced a riser pole at the well field in North La Junta. 
• Ran a new overhead service line for the streetlights on Hwy 50. 
• Replaced broken cross arm braces. 
• Tree trimming on the east side of town. 
 
There is no new system update. 
In September, a total of 11,602 kWh was returned to the grid by solar. 
D. Sanitation (Martin Montoya) 
• The month of October is the citywide cleanup month. The community is 
encouraged to bring their trash and items like TVs, tires, appliances, 
refrigerators, etc. This is for residents only and not for commercial use. 
• Still waiting for CAA to approve the contract to be able to offer free curbside 
recycling to the community. Hopefully it will happen before the end of the year, 
but if not, it will be at the beginning of the year.
