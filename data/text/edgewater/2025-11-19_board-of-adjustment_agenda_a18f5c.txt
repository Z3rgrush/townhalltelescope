REQUEST SUMMARY: 
The Applicant intends to construct a new single -story 576 square foot detached 
garage in the rear yard. The p roposed structure is 18’ 2” tall when measured from 
ground level and 20’ 2” tall when measured from the rear property line  (due to the 
slope of the property), as required by the Code  (see Figure 1).

A variance is being sought to allow the detached garage to exceed the height 
limitation by five feet, two inches.  
 
The proposed detached garage appears to meet * all other applicable code 
requirements.   
 
Note*: If variance is granted, the next step would be to apply for a  building permit. 
The detached garage would be reviewed against all applicable Code regulations. 
 
 
 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The Property has been zoned R-1 since at least 2003, according to City historical 
documents. The Applicant initially applied for a building permit in May 2025 and 
was informed that the proposed height exceeded what is allowed in the Code. The 
Applicant applied for a variance on October 20, 2025 . 
 
The Property slopes from front to back, similarly to many west -facing properties in 
Edgewater. Without this slope, the structure would exceed the height limit by three 
feet, two inches.

APPLICABLE CODE SECTIONS REVIEW: 
 
Sec. 16-1-50. - Definitions 
For accessory structures located entirely within the rear yard , building height 
means the vertical distance measured from the center point of the rear property 
line to the highest point of the roof surface.  
 
Sec. 16-5-20. - Height 
No principal building shall exceed twenty -five (25) feet in height, excepting, 
however, that portion of a principal building with a minimum roof pitch of two over 
twelve (2/12), which portion of the building shall not exceed twenty -eight (28) feet 
in height. No accessory building shall exceed fifteen (15) feet in  height. 
 
The Applicant is requesting relief from th e above regulation to allow an accessory 
structure that is 20’ 2” in height . This would require a variance of five feet , two 
inches to the rear yard accessory structure height  regulation. 
 
BOA REVIEW: 
 
Eligibility: Per Code Section 16-24-60(a), a Variance request from the 
requirements of Chapter 16 of the Code  shall only be granted through the Variance 
process by the Board of Adjustment when the circumstances and  conditions of a 
property are exceptional or extraordinary such that they do not apply to property 
generally within the City and the denial of an application for relief from the 
requirements of Chapter 16 would result in an inability to reasonably utilize the 
property. 
 
Burden: The burden of establishing that all criteria are met is upon the Applicant. 
Code Sec. 16-24-60(a).

BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT ACTION:   
 
After the conclusion of the public hearing, the Board of Adjustment may : (1) 
Approve the Variance application if you find  that it meets all the criteria listed 
above; (2) Approve the Variance application with conditions if necessary to alleviate 
or mitigate the potential adverse impacts of the variance  – conditions must relate to 
the Property and may relate to specific person(s) who are directly associated with 
the use contemplated by the application ; (3) Deny the application if you find that it 
does not meet the standards listed above; or (4) continue the matter . If you 
continue the matter because you wish to receive additional information (evidence), 
the hearing should not be closed – instead, it must be continued.  
 
The Board of Adjustment’s decision must be rendered by adoption of a Resolution. 
Staff has provided resolutions for approval and denial.  It is up to the Board of 
Adjustment for final decision-making.

RESOLUTION NO. 2025 - 03 
A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN APPLICATION FOR A VARIANCE FROM 
EDGEWATER MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 16-5-20 TO ALLOW AN ACCESSORY 
BUILDING TO EXCEED THE CODE’S 15 FOOT HEIGHT LIMIT  FOR ACCESSORY 
BUILDINGS 
 
WHEREAS, the Edgewater Board of Adjustment (the “Board”) has received an  
application for a variance and related documents (collectively, the “Application”) from  
Stepan and Lida Jalovec, owners (collectively, the “Applicant”) of that property located at 
2458 Gray Street,  Edgewater, Colorado (the “Property”); 
 
WHEREAS, the Property is located in the Residential 1 (R-1) zone district;  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Edgewater Municipal Code (“Code”) Section 16-5-
20, an accessory structure cannot exceed 15 feet in height;  
 
WHEREAS, the Application seeks a variance from  the aforementioned Code 
section to allow the construction of an accessory structure more than 15 feet in height;  
 
WHEREAS, after due and proper notice, the Board conducted a public hearing  on 
the Application on November 19, 2025, at which time the Applicant and all other interested 
parties were given the opportunity to be heard and to present evidence, as  reflected in 
the record of those proceedings; 
 
WHEREAS, Code Section 16-24-60(b) sets forth variance approval criteria; and  
 
WHEREAS, based upon all the testimony and evidence received at the November 
19, 2025, public hearing, the Board finds that the Application meets each of the   variance 
approval criteria set forth in Code Section 16-24-60(b), as further set forth below, and thus 
the Application is hereby approved by the Board. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE I T  RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
OF THE CITY OF EDGEWATER, COLORADO, THAT: 
 
Section 1 . Findings: The Application filed by Applicants as owners of the 
Property for a variance from Code section 16 -5-20 to allow the construction of an 
accessory structure that exceeds 15 feet in height meets the variance approval criteria of 
Code Section 16-24-60(b), as follows: 
 
(1) The property has extraordinary or exceptional physical conditions that do not 
generally exist in nearby properties in the same zoning district; 
 
Finding: The Property slopes from front to back. It is therefore physically different

2  
than nearby properties in the same district , particularly those which do not face 
West.  
 
(2) The extraordinary or exceptional physical condition of the property will not  allow 
reasonable use of the property in its current zone in the absence of relief; 
 
Finding: Reasonable use of property within the R-1 zone district is a reasonably sized 
single-family home  and garage . Given the slope of the property, a reasonably tall 
detached garage cannot be built without a variance. 
 
(3) The granting of the variance will not have an adverse impact on the            surrounding 
properties, the neighborhood or the community as a whole; 
 
Finding: Granting this variance request will not have an adverse impact on the

structures that would be impacted by a taller detached garage. 
 
(4) The granting of the variance will not be detrimental to public health, safety     and

property constitutes an extraordinary or exceptional physical condition.

use of the Property. Granting the variance would allow a detached garage that 
could be used for a camper van.

EDGEWATER BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT  
RESOLUTION NO. 2025 - 03 
A RESOLUTION DENYING AN APPLICATION FOR A VARIANCE FROM EDGEWATER 
MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 16-5-20 TO ALLOW AN ACCESSORY BUILDING TO 
EXCEED THE CODE’S 15 FOOT HEIGHT LIMIT FOR ACCESSORY BUILDING 
 
WHEREAS, the Edgewater Board of Adjustment (the “Board”) has received an  
application for a variance and related documents (collectively, the “Application”) from 
Stepan and Lida Jalovec owners (collectively, the “Applicant s”) of that property located 
at 2458 Gray Street,  Edgewater, Colorado (the “Property”); 
 
WHEREAS, the Property is located in the Residential 1 (R-1) zone district;  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Edgewater Municipal Code (“Code”) Section 16-5-
20, an accessory structure cannot exceed 15 feet in height;  
 
WHEREAS, the Application seeks approval to violate the aforementioned Code 
section to allow the construction of an accessory structure more than 15 feet in height;  
 
WHEREAS, after due and proper notice, the Board conducted a public hearing  on 
the Application on November 19, 2025, at which time the Applicant and all other interested 
parties were given the opportunity to be heard and to present evidence, as  reflected in 
the record of those proceedings; 
 
WHEREAS, Code Section 16-24-60(b) sets forth variance approval criteria; and  
 
WHEREAS, based upon all of the testimony and evidence received at the March 
19, 2025, public hearing, the Board finds that the Application does not meet each of the    
variance approval criteria set forth in Code Section 16-24-60(b), as further set forth below 
, and thus the Application is hereby denied by the Board. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
OF THE CITY OF EDGEWATER, COLORADO, THAT: 
 
Section 1 . Findings: The Application filed by Applicants as owners of the 
Property for a variance from Code Section 16 -5-20 to allow the construction of an 
accessory structure that exceeds 15 feet in height does not meet one or more of the 
variance approval criteria of Code Section 16-24-60(b), as follows: 
 
(1) The property has extraordinary or exceptional physical conditions that do not 
generally exist in nearby properties in the same zoning district; 
 
Finding: Other properties within the R-1 district are similarly sloped. This criterion is 
not met.  
Page 14 of 24
5  
 
(2) The extraordinary or exceptional physical condition of the property will not allow 
reasonable use of the property in its current zone in the absence of relief; 
 
Finding: Because the Property is not uniquely sloped, this criterion cannot be met. 
But even if the Property were uniquely sloped, reasonable use could still be fit upon 
the Property – for example, a house with an attached garage, or no garage. This 
criterion is not met. 
 
(3) The granting of the variance will not have an adverse impact on the            surrounding 
properties, the neighborhood or the community as a whole; 
 
Finding: This criterion may be met. 
 
(4) The granting of the variance will not be detrimental to public health, safety     and 
welfare or injurious to surrounding property values and neighborhood character; 
 
Finding: This criterion may be met. 
 
(5) The granting of the variance shall not be substantially inconsistent with any plans 
adopted by the City; 
 
Finding: This criterion may be met. 
 
(6) The granting of the variance shall not materially weaken the general purpose of this 
Chapter or any other zoning regulations of the City. 
 
Finding: The granting of a variance for the height of an accessory structure when a 
shorter accessory structure could be built defeats the purpose of that section of code. 
This criterion is not met. 
 
(7) The variance, if granted, shall only be to the extent necessary to afford a  reasonable 
use of the property; 
 
Finding: Reasonable use of the Property could be made without these variances. An 
attached garage could be built, or uncovered parking could be used. This criterion is 
not met. 
 
(8) The unique conditions of the property under which the variance is sought were not 
created by the owner of the property or his or her agent. 
 
Finding: This criterion is met. 
 
Section 2. Decision: Based on the findings set forth in Section 1 above, the Board 
hereby DENIES the Application.
