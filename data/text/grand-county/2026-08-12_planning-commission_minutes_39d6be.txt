Staff Presentation & Project Overview
Planner I Hannah Mahorney presented a request two Outright Exemptions to subdivide a
44.33-acre parcel into two separate lots. Lot 1 is proposed to be 8.82 acres and is configured to 
accommodate the existing lodge and associated improvements. Lot 2 is proposed to be 32.60 
acres is currently undeveloped. This parcel would have the ability in the future to accommodate
one additional single-family home.
While this request initially resembles a Subdivision Exemption, it does not meet the eligibility 
criteria and must be processed as two Outright Exemption lots. Standard Subdivision 
Exemptions are limited to creating up to three lots with only one single-family residential unit 
on each. The presence of four existing structures on proposed Lot 1 exceeds this threshold, 
making an Outright Exemption the appropriate land use review process.
Conditions of approval
Staff recommends approval of the Devils Thumb Ranch High Lonesome Lodge Outright 
Exemptions with the following conditions to be met prior to any meeting before the BOCC:
1. The title of the plat shall read “Devil’s Thumb Ranch High Lonesome Lodge 
Outright Exemptions”. Conveyance to the subdivider at Reception No. 
2017009132 is noted as well.
2. Easements of not less than twenty feet (20') in width shall be provided along rear
and/or side lot lines for utilities. Such easements may be provided by ten foot
(10') easements on each of adjoining lots.
3. Easements of not less than twenty feet (20') in width shall be provided along rear
and/or side lot lines for utilities. Such easements may be provided by ten foot
(10') easements on each of adjoining lots.

them and the lot lines on Lot 1 should be bolded for clarity.

Agenda Item 2: Good to Go portable toilet storage Special Use Permit
Project Name: Good to Go portable toilet storage Special Use Permit
Applicants: Christopher R. Bergquist Sr Revocable Trust and Good to Go Inc
Location: Bergquist Outright Exemption/ 9565 County Rd 5
Zoning: Forestry and Open (Outside Growth Boundary)
Staff Recommendation: Conditional Approval
Staff Presentation & Project Overview
The Christopher R. Bergquist Sr. Revocable Trust, represented by Christopher Bergquist, is the 
current owner of the Bergquist Outright Exemption. This request is to allow for a Special Use 
Permit (SUP) for the storage of up to two hundred (200) clean and empty portable toilets for 
Good to Go Inc. 
Good to Go Inc. has been operating in Grand County since 2015, primarily based out of 
Tabernash, where they provide portable toilets and sinks for various events and construction 
sites. Around 2022, the owners began using the Bergquist property to store clean, empty units. 
County Staff received no complaints until June 2026. Upon receipt of the Notice of Violation, 
the Applicant met with Staff to discuss necessary corrective measures.
With the above in mind, the applicant has submitted a request for a Special Use Permit to allow
for portable toilet storage in the Forestry and Open Zone District. 
Conditions of approval
Staff recommends the Planning Commission recommend approval of the Good to Go inc.
Portable Toilet Dry Storage Special Use Permit with the following conditions for a term of one 
year, expiring August 12, 2027: 
1. The Applicant shall provide an executed lease agreement for the Bergquist property 
prior to any issuance of any Special Use Permit.  If said lease agreement is 
terminated for any reason, then any approved Special Use Permit shall become null 
and void.
2. No expansion of the business beyond what is listed as part of this Special Use Permit 
request may occur if said permit is issued.  If expansion of the business is ever 
desired, an amendment to the Special Use Permit will be required.
3. The toilets and trailers shall be moved to the new approved location by August 12, 
2026. The storage area shall be adequately screened from all adjacent property 
owners. This can include trees, fences, berms, or buildings. A full screening plan has
