
Grand County Historic Preservation Board
Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, September 1, 20266:30 p.m.Grand County Road and Bridge Offices467 Topaz Ave., Granby, Colorado
Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 6:36 p.m.
Members Present: Jim Miller, Timothy Harvey, Dennis Humphries, Scott Munn, B. Travis Wright, Graham Johnson, and Dave Lively.
Member Absent: Kent Wehmeyer.
Vacant: Winter Park representative.
Others Present: Members of the public, including Shanna Ganne, Executive Director, Grand County Historical Association.
A quorum was present.
Board members introduced themselves and briefly described their backgrounds, community representation, and experience related to historic preservation.
Election of Officers
Without objection, the Board took up the election of officers out of agenda order.
Dave Lively nominated Graham Johnson for Chair. With no further nominations, Johnson was elected unanimously.
Dave Lively nominated B. Travis Wright for Vice Chair. Wright was elected unanimously.
Dennis Humphries moved to elect Scott Munn as Secretary. Jim Miller seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously.
Dennis Humphries moved to elect Dave Lively as Treasurer. Jim Miller seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously.
The officers will serve one-year terms and may serve successive terms pursuant to the Board’s governing resolution.
Board Organization and Governing Resolution
The Board reviewed the recently amended resolution governing the Grand County Historic Preservation Board and discussed its designation procedures, review responsibilities, meeting requirements, and relationship with the Board of County Commissioners.
Members discussed future consideration of staggered Board terms, coordination with Grand County staff and Planning and Zoning, procedures concerning historic designation and the alteration, relocation, or demolition of designated historic properties, administrative support, and consultation with the County Attorney as Board procedures are developed.
The Board also discussed its potential advisory role when historic resources may be affected by federal projects, including participation in Section 106 review.
No formal action was taken on these matters.
Certified Local Government Status
The Board identified restoration of Grand County’s Certified Local Government (CLG) status as an immediate priority.
Chair Johnson agreed to contact History Colorado to confirm the current CLG reinstatement requirements, application process, and next steps before substantial application work is undertaken.
Preservation Priorities and Outreach
The Board discussed longer-term priorities, including historic resource inventories, local landmark and historic district designations, public education, grant opportunities, and development of a countywide historic preservation plan.
Members also discussed supporting History Colorado’s upcoming presentation concerning historic preservation and preservation tax credits, scheduled for September 16, 2026.
The Board discussed the availability of contracted staff support under its governing resolution. Further consideration was deferred until the Board’s priorities and staffing needs are more fully developed.
Public Comment
A member of the public asked what types of properties and resources may qualify for historic designation, including whether historic sites and roads may be eligible.
The Board discussed the designation criteria contained in its governing resolution and noted that eligible historic resources are not limited to buildings and may include landmarks, sites, districts, and cultural landscapes associated with Grand County’s history, provided the applicable designation criteria are met.
Next Meeting
By consensus, the Board tentatively selected Saturday, October 17, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. for its next meeting, with the location to be confirmed.
Members also discussed establishing a regular quarterly weekday meeting schedule for future meetings.
Adjournment
Scott Munn moved to adjourn. Dave Lively seconded the motion. The motion carried.
The meeting adjourned at 7:45 p.m.
