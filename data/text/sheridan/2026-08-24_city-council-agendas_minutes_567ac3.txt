13. Mayor's Choice 
Mayor Daigle canvassed the neighborhood to promote Sheridan Celebrates and attended the VFW 
pig roast, which featured pig races and water balloons tossed at the new commander. 
14. Council Members' Choice 
Council Member Clark - After eight months on Council, he has been speaking with residents, 
many of whom raised concerns about roads. He noted that Council’s authority is more limited 
than some residents realize, so he has been directing them to the appropriate resources. He 
continues to learn and thanked everyone for their guidance. He also asked whether police officers 
feel safe and noted that residents feel positive about safety in the City. 
 
Council Member Whittaker - Reported that DRCOG wants to move forward with approving 
measures to reduce roadway congestion and expand E-470.
