11. Mayor's Choice 
Mayor Daigle shared that she attended the Boots Not Suits event at the Arapahoe 
County Fairgrounds. She also attended the City of Littleton’s Illumination event,

survey that was recently distributed.

appreciation for the City newsletter and hopes to continue contributing articles.
