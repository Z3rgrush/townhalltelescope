b. Preliminary and Final Subdivision for Property Located at 2200 W. Radcliff Ave. 
Public Hearing Packet - 2200 W. Radcliff Ave.pdf 
 
Mayor Daigle called the Public Hearing to order at 7:18pm. The purpose of this public hearing will 
be to review an application for a preliminary and final subdivision for property located at 2200 
West Radcliff Avenue. 
 
Mayor Daigle Invited the representative of the applicant, Michelle Kuehl to the podium to 
introduce herself. Mrs. Kuehl was present representing Iron Woman.  
 
Mayor Daigle - disclosed that she also serves on the Colorado Association of School Board 
Executive Board of Directors with the co-owner of Iron Woman Anne Egan. That relationship will 
have no effect on this decision.

Mayor Daigle invited Planner II, Ari Snow, to deliver the staff presentation. Mr. Snow outlined the 
relevant criteria, factors, adjacent zoning, and land use considerations. 
Staff presented the preliminary and final subdivision plats for 2200 West Radcliff Avenue. 
The four industrial parcels have never been legally subdivided, and platting is required 
before building permits can be issued; no development application is currently proposed.  
Staff found the subdivision consistent with the Sheridan Subdivision Code, Zoning Code, 
Comprehensive Plan, and the 2017 South Santa Fe Business Park Report. The property’s 
existing utilities, drainage, parcel configuration, and access are adequate for its current 
material-storage use, although future redevelopment could require additional 
infrastructure and environmental remediation. 
As a condition of approval, the applicant would dedicate an additional 19 feet of right-of-
way along West Radcliff Avenue to support the City’s long-term goal of creating a 68-foot 
corridor.  
Required public notice was completed, and the Planning Commission unanimously 
recommended approval of both plats. Staff recommended City Council approval subject to 
the right-of-way dedication and completion of remaining technical corrections. 
Michelle Kuehl shared for the applicant 
The applicant explained that the platting is an administrative step needed to formally 
organize four parcels and resolve unused easements. No development is currently 
planned, but completing the subdivision process will make it possible to evaluate and 
determine future uses for the property. 
Public Comment 
Alice Corns, representing Colorado Auto and Parts, asked the City to clarify its long-term 
plans for West Radcliff Avenue. Although she did not oppose the subdivision application, 
she questioned the basis for a proposed 68-foot corridor, the City’s current right-of-way 
calculations, and how affected property owners would be notified and involved before 
additional land is requested. 
Docusign Envelope ID: B0CA5D69-5B7F-82B6-83B1-317AF8D3580F
Written Comments were also provided by Alice Corns 
Applicant response to public comment  
The applicant acknowledged the concerns but explained that the current request is limited 
to legally subdividing the property. Any future redevelopment or City actions would require 
separate discussions to address the needs of property owners and the broader community. 
The Public Hearing was closed at 7:37 p.m. and regular meeting of the City Council of the City of 
Sheridan was reconvened.
