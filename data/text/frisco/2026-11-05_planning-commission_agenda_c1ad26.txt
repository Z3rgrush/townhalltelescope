Public Comments: 
Citizens making comments during Public Comments on items not on the agenda shall state 
their names and addresses for the record, be topicspecific, and limit comments to no longer 
than three minutes. No Planning Commission action is taken on public comments. The 
Commission will take all comments under advisement and if a response is appropriate the 
individual making the comment will receive a formal response from the town at a later date.
