Re:  2025 Audit 
Item #: 1 
Background 
Colorado law requires municipalities to have their annual financial statements audited by an 
independent certified public accounting firm. The Town's 2025 audit has been completed by 
Chad K Chavies, CPA, TIM CHAVIES & ASSOCIATES, INC., CPA'S and will be presented to 
the Board of Trustees. 
The purpose of the audit is to provide an independent opinion on whether the Town's financial 
statements are presented fairly, in all material respects, in accordance with generally accepted 
accounting principles (GAAP). In addition to the financial statements, the audit includes a 
review of internal controls and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. 
During this meeting, Mr. Chavies will present the results of the audit, discuss the Town's 
financial position for the year ending December 31, 2025, review any findings or 
recommendations, and answer questions from the Board.

MEMORANDUM 
 
Date:  July 28, 2026 
To:   Town Board 
From:  Stacy Brown, Town Manager 
Re:  Community Center Staffing Discussion 
Item #: 2 
Background 
Following the resignation of Community Center Coordinator Jami Heiss, staff has evaluated the 
current operational needs of the Kersey Community Center and how those responsibilities are 
being managed. 
At this time, Community Center operations continue uninterrupted.  A temporary employee has 
been assisting with daily operations, while other Town staff have absorbed portions of the 
position's responsibilities.  This arrangement has allowed the Community Center to continue 
providing services while staff evaluates the long-term staffing needs of the facility. 
During Jami's informal exit interview and subsequent discussions, several topics were identified 
regarding the role and the future direction of the Community Center.  When the expanded 
position was originally presented to the Board October 28, 2025 one of the primary objectives 
was to expand community programming, increase participation, and strengthen community 
engagement through additional events and activities. 
While Jami made a sincere effort to achieve those goals, she shared that generating consistent 
community participation proved to be more challenging than anticipated.  Despite outreach 
efforts and new programming opportunities, attendance and engagement were often lower than 
expected or simply non-existent.  Her feedback has prompted staff to evaluate whether the 
original scope of the position continues to align with the community's current level of 
participation and the Town's operational priorities. 
Discussion 
Staff is seeking the Board's direction regarding the future of the Community Center Coordinator 
position. 
One option would be to refill the position as originally designed, with an emphasis on 
community programming, special events, outreach, and expanding participation. 
A second option would be to redefine the position to focus primarily on the Community Center's 
core functions, including: 
• Coordinating senior programming and activities. 
• Managing Community Center rentals and scheduling. 
• Providing customer service to facility users.

• Supporting routine Community Center operations and administrative functions. 
Under this approach, existing community-wide events would continue to be coordinated 
collaboratively by Town departments, as appropriate, rather than being the primary responsibility 
of a single employee. 
Recommendation from Staff or Commission 
Rather than structuring the position around expanding community-wide events, staff 
recommends returning the focus of the Community Center position to its primary purpose of 
managing the facility, coordinating senior activities, and overseeing rentals.  Community events 
would continue to be a collaborative effort among Town departments, as appropriate, instead of 
being assigned solely to the Community Center Coordinator.  This approach provides greater 
organizational resiliency while ensuring the Community Center's core services remain the 
priority 
 
Financial Impact 
Cost of position could decline under staff recommended proposal, due to reduced staff 
hours.
