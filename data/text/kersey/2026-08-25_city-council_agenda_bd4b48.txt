Re:  Berryman Farms – Subdivision Signage 
Item #: 1.a 
Background 
Several streets within the Berryman Farms subdivision share names with streets located in other 
areas of the Town of Kersey, including Klein, Clark, and McIntyre. 
The duplication and similarity of these street names can create confusion when identifying a 
specific location. In particular, it may not be immediately clear from an address whether the 
responding party should be traveling to a street or location east or west of 1st Street. 
While this can be inconvenient for residents, visitors, deliveries, and service providers, the 
Town's primary concern is public safety and emergency response. When law enforcement, fire, 
or emergency medical personnel are dispatched to an address, clear and unambiguous street 
identification is important. Any uncertainty regarding the correct location has the potential to 
delay an emergency response. 
Proposed Solution 
Staff recommends that the affected streets east of 1st Street in the Berryman Farms subdivision 
be completely renamed, rather than adding directional designations such as "East," "West," or 
similar prefixes or suffixes. 
Assigning entirely new and distinct street names would provide greater clarity and create a long-
term solution to the existing duplication. New names should be selected that are clearly 
distinguishable from existing street names within the Town and surrounding emergency response 
area. 
Prior to implementation, staff would coordinate as necessary with affected residents (3-5) and 
property owners, Weld County, emergency dispatch, law enforcement, fire protection and 
emergency medical services, the U.S. Postal Service, utility providers, mapping/GIS systems, 
and other affected agencies to ensure address records are updated appropriately. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 2 
 
Public Safety Considerations 
Renaming the affected streets would help: 
• Reduce confusion when emergency calls are dispatched; 
• Provide clearer location information for police, fire, and EMS personnel; 
• Reduce the possibility of responders being directed to the wrong area; 
• Improve consistency within addressing, mapping, and navigation systems; and 
• Provide clearer addresses for residents, visitors, deliveries, utilities, and other service 
providers. 
Although changing a street name will require affected residents and property owners to update 
their addresses with various agencies and service providers, staff believes the long-term public 
safety benefit of having unique and easily identifiable street names warrants consideration. 
Recommendation from Staff or Commission 
Staff requests that the Town Board authorize staff to proceed with the process of renaming 
the affected streets in the Berryman Farms subdivision east of 1st Street, including 
identifying appropriate replacement street names, coordinating with affected residents and 
agencies, and bringing any required ordinance, resolution, or other formal action back to the 
Board for consideration. 
The goal is to establish street names that are unique, easily understood by emergency dispatch 
and responders, and unlikely to create future confusion within the Town of Kersey.

Re:  Kersey Holiday Festival  
Item #:  2 
Background 
The Kersey Holiday Festival is a community event held annually on the first Saturday in 
December. Main features of this event in past years have been a parade, chili supper 
and raffle provided by the Kersey Area Chamber of Commerce, pictures with Santa, 
children’s crafts, carolers, and other Christmas-themed activities.  
Community feedback received through the RTAP workshop showed a high priority on 
downtown activation (Action 3.2) and synchronization of Town and PVRE-7 
communication and events (Action 4.1). This has caused the Kersey Community 
Experience Department to review the features of our planned events and outreach to 
ensure they meet community goals and expectations. 
Financial Impact 
Holiday Festival is not a revenue-generating event. It is paid for using the department’s 
expenditure line item 01-16-00-6736 (Winter Events) and the generation donations of 
the Kersey Area Chamber of Commerce and other sponsors. Planning changes to the 
event will not have a significant financial impact. 
Recommendation from Staff or Commission 
Staff are asking for Town Board feedback regarding changes to the Kersey Holiday 
Festival, particularly the parade, and recommending closer alignment to the community 
feedback referenced above.
