o Aspen Airport Closure Opportunity and Visitor Activation: Members 
discussed the upcoming Aspen Airport closure, the anticipated increase in 
traffic at Eagle Airport, and strategies to capitalize on this opportunity to 
attract visitors to Eagle through marketing, partnerships, and data analysis. 
▪ Marketing Initiatives at Eagle Airport: Melissa reported that the staff 
is updating a promotional video to run above baggage claim at Eagle 
Airport for the next year, targeting increased visitor traffic resulting 
from the Aspen Airport closure. 
▪ Data Monitoring and Activation Strategies: The committee 
discussed using Placer.ai data to monitor visitor flows and discussed 
the need for creative activation mechanisms such as coupons, 
incentives, and partnerships with hotels, rental car agencies, and 
limousine companies to encourage visitors to explore Eagle. 
▪ Timing and Messaging Considerations: Members noted the 
importance of reaching travelers before their arrival, as many make 
plans in advance, and proposed ideas such as greeters or targeted 
advertising in hotel rooms and rental cars to plant the idea of visiting 
Eagle. 
▪ Private Jet Center and Affluent Visitors: Scott highlighted the 
potential to engage private jet travelers and limousine companies, 
suggesting the distribution of promotional materials to this high-value 
audience during their transfer to Aspen. 
 
• EVC-MEAC Merger Planning and Coordination: The group discussed the 
upcoming joint meeting between the Economic Vitality Committee (EVC) and 
Marketing & Events Advisory Committee (MEAC), outlining the distinct roles of each 
group, areas of potential overlap, and strategies for effective integration. 
o Distinct Roles and Overlap: Nikki clarified that while MEAC focuses on 
marketing, events, and sports tourism funded by lodging tax dollars, and EVC 
centers on business vitality and redevelopment, there are complementary 
areas such as civic branding, sports tourism, and data analysis where 
collaboration is beneficial. 
o Joint Meeting Objectives: The joint meeting, scheduled for September 10th, 
aims to identify natural overlaps, maintain necessary distinctions, and set
