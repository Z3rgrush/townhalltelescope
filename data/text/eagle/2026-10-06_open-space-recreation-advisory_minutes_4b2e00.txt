9:30 a.m. (40 min) School House to Kill Bill Trail Proposal  
• Staff reviewed the BLM alignment, Town-focused alignment, and No New Trail option.

• Staff recommended the Town-focused alignment. 
• Comments from OSRAC included: 
- Support for both the Town-focused alignment and No New Trail option. 
- Questions about enforcement, seasonal closures, off-leash dogs, and social trails. 
- Questions about neighborhood outreach and the BLM review process. 
 
• Public comment was received regarding enforcement, off-leash dogs, wildlife impacts, and social 
trails. 
• OSRAC continued discussing education, outreach, enforcement, and restoration. 
• Additional public comment was received regarding the previously contemplated seasonal 
ranger/enforcement program. 
• Ernest Saeger made motion to “recommend that Town Council support the Town-Focused 
Alignment for the School House Rock to Kill Bill Connector with an increase in resources for 
education, outreach, enforcement, and social trail closure and restoration to effectively manage 
and protect open space, wildlife, and wildlife habitat.” 
• Jessica Foulis seconded the motion. 
- Ernest Saeger (motion) — Yes   
- Jessica Foulis (second) — Yes   
- Roger Mitchell — No   
- Chris Cohen — Yes   
- Ryan Gilmer — No   
- Phillip Kirkman — Yes
