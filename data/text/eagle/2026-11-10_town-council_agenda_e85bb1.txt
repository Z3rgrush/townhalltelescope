TOWN OF EAGLE 
OFFICIAL PROCLAMATION 
IN RECOGNITION OF COLORADO GIVES DAY, DECEMBER 8, 2026 
 
 
WHEREAS, the Town of Eagle recognizes the vital role that nonprofit organizations play in 
strengthening communities, enriching lives, and addressing critical needs through 
charitable, educational, cultural, environmental, health, and human service programs; and 
WHEREAS, Colorado Gives Day, held annually each December, has become one of the 
largest statewide movements of generosity in the nation, inspiring thousands of 
individuals, businesses, and organizations to support the causes and communities they 
value most; and 
WHEREAS, Colorado Gives Day unites residents across Colorado in a shared spirit of 
philanthropy, demonstrating the power of collective giving to create lasting and meaningful 
impact throughout the state; and 
WHEREAS, the nonprofit organizations serving the Vail Valley and Eagle County provide 
essential services, foster community connections, promote arts and culture, protect 
natural resources, support education, and enhance the quality of life for residents and 
visitors alike; and 
WHEREAS, charitable giving strengthens the social and economic fabric of the community, 
enabling nonprofit organizations to expand their reach, respond to emerging challenges, 
and continue their important work on behalf of those they serve; and 
WHEREAS, Colorado Gives Day celebrates the generosity, compassion, and civic 
engagement of donors, volunteers, nonprofit professionals, and community leaders whose 
commitment to service helps build a stronger and more resilient Colorado; and 
WHEREAS, the Town of Eagle encourages residents, businesses, and visitors to participate 
in Colorado Gives Day by supporting local nonprofit organizations and investing in the well-
being and future of the community; 
Page 4 of 281
NOW, THEREFORE, the Mayor and Town Council of the Town of Eagle do hereby proclaim 
December 8, 2026, as COLORADO GIVES DAY in the Town of Eagle and encourage all 
members of the community to recognize and celebrate the profound impact of charitable 
giving and volunteerism. Through acts of generosity, both large and small, we strengthen 
our community, support those in need, and ensure a vibrant future for the Vail Valley and 
the State of Colorado. 
Dated this 10th day of November, 2026.

Agenda Item: Community Impact Award 
  
REQUEST: The Mayor and Town Council are requested to announce and honor this year's 2026 Community Impact 
Award winner. 
  
BACKGROUND: The Town has been awarding the Community Impact Award to community members since 2014 in an 
effort to recognize those that have a positive impact on our community. 
  
ANALYSIS: Solicitations for nominations took place in September of this year and we received five nominations. The 
individuals and businesses nominated were: Natalie Neuwirth and Emily Carney with Vail Valley Mountain Trail Alliance, 
Celebrate the Beat, Alex Smiley, Zach Gilliam with Eagle River Jamboree, and the Golden Eagle Senior Center.  
 
Past winners: 2025 Becky Burgess, 2024 Kim Fritzler, TJ Davis, Kelly Moser; 2023 Ernest Saeger and Raenette Johnson; 
2022 Kyle Foster; 2021 Jennifer Filipowski; 2020 Andy Clark; 2019 Jay Lucas, Tara Novak, and Kat Conner; 2018 Alexis 
and Stan Kensinger; 2017 Rosie Shearwood; 2016 Mick Daly; 2015 John Shipp; and 2014 Scott Turnipseed and Chris 
Cook.  
 
The Council was provided with the nominations and asked to make their selections via a ballot poll. The Mayor has been 
given the winner's name to announce during the meeting tonight. All nominees and their sponsors were invited to 
attend tonight's meeting. 
  
COMMUNITY INPUT: A request for nominations was made on our website, via social media, through Eagle Today, and 
through various partner agencies seeking nominations. 
  
BUDGET / STAFF IMPACT: This is a budgeted item and planned annual activity for staff. 
  
STRATEGIC PLAN ALIGNMENT / STANDARDS ACHIEVED: This program was developed prior to the adoption of the 
Strategic Plan. However, the intent of the Community Impact Award does highlight the Foundation of the Town's values 
of integrity, respect, commitment, responsibility, collaboration, and leadership. 
  
RECOMMENDED ACTION OR PROPOSED MOTION: The Mayor is requested to honor all nominees and announce the 
2026 Community Impact Award winner. 
  
ATTACHMENTS: None

Date:  November 10, 2026 
Agenda Item:  Resolution 62, Series 2026, A Resolution of the Town Council of the Town of  
Eagle, Colorado Approving Amendments to the Eagle Downtown Development Authority Rules 
of Procedure Concerning Alternate Members 
REQUEST: Staff requests the Town Council consider Resolution 62, Series 2026, approving 
amendments to the Eagle Downtown Development Authority (DDA) Rules of Procedure 
concerning alternate members. 
 
BACKGROUND: In June 2026, the DDA Board requested that the Town Council authorize up to 
two alternate members to improve meeting continuity and help maintain a consistent quorum. 
The Town Council subsequently adopted Ordinance 12, Series 2026, retaining seven regular 
members and authorizing the appointment of up to two alternates. 
Ordinance 12-2026 provides that alternates may participate in Board discussions and may serve 
and vote when a regular member is absent, recused, or otherwise disqualified. It also authorizes 
the DDA Board to establish procedures for designating and using alternates through its Rules of 
Procedure, provided those procedures remain consistent with the ordinance and applicable law. 
 
ANALYSIS: The proposed amendments incorporate alternate members throughout the Rules of 
Procedure and establish a predictable process for their participation. The amendments: 
• confirm that the Board consists of seven regular members and up to two alternate 
members appointed by the Town Council; 
• apply the same eligibility, conflict-of-interest, ethics, compensation, term, vacancy, and 
removal provisions to alternates; 
• encourage alternates to attend meetings and allow them to participate in discussion 
whether or not they are serving as a voting member; 
• allow an alternate to vote and count toward a quorum only when serving in place of an 
absent, recused, or disqualified regular member; and 
• ensure that no more than seven members vote on any matter.

RULES OF PROCEDURE OF THE EAGLE DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY 
 
ARTICLE 1.000 
1.100 Establishment. Under Ordinance No. 25, Series 2020 the Town Council of the Town of 
Eagle, Colorado established a Downtown Development Authority known as the “Eagle 
Downtown Development Authority” (the “Authority”) as a body corporate.  
1.200 Purpose. The Authority is established to halt and prevent deterioration of property values 
within its district and to assist in the development and redevelopment of its district and to use 
its power to promote the general welfare of the district by the use of its direct and 
supplemental powers. 
1.300 Powers. By ordinance of the Town of Eagle (the “Town”), the Authority has all the powers 
authorized by Part 8 of Article 25 of Title 31, Colorado Revised Statutes, and all additional and 
supplemental powers necessary or convenient to carry out and effectuate the purposes and 
provisions of said Part 8. 
1.400 Seal. The Authority shall have a seal, which shall be circular in form and shall have 
inscribed thereon the name of the Authority and the word, “SEAL”. 
1.500 Offices. The Authority shall have the power to maintain an office within the boundaries of 
the Town.  
1.600 Annual Budget. Each year the DDA Board shall consider and approve a budget pursuant 
to Section 3.540. After such approval, the budget shall be submitted to the Eagle Town Council 
for its approval.  
 
ARTICLE 2.000 
2.100 Board Members. The Chair and the Members of the DDA Board shall manage the affairs 
of the Authority.  
2.110 Membership. The Board shall consist of seven regular members and up to two alternate 
members, all appointed by the Town Council in accordance with Ordinance No. 25, Series 2020, 
as amended, and C.R.S. §§ 31-25-805 and 31-25-806, as amended. One regular member shall be 
a member of the Town Council and shall serve at the pleasure of the Town Council. 
2.120 Alternate Members. Alternate members shall satisfy the same statutory qualifications 
and eligibility requirements applicable to regular members. Unless otherwise stated in these 
Rules, provisions applicable to a DDA Board Member also apply to an alternate member. 
2.130 Terms. Regular and alternate members shall serve staggered terms and shall serve until 
their terms end, and a successor is appointed and qualified. A regular or alternate member may 
be reappointed to serve a new four-year term. 
Page 40 of 281
2.140 Eligibility. Each DDA Board Member, except the representative from the Eagle Town 
Council, shall be appointed in accordance with eligibility requirements as stated in C.R.S. § 31-
25-806, as amended. No officer or employee of the Town, other than an appointee from the 
Eagle Town Council, shall be eligible for appointment to the DDA Board.  
2.150 Compensation. All regular and alternate members, including the Chair, shall serve 
without compensation, but they may be reimbursed for actual and necessary expenses incurred 
on behalf of the Authority.  
2.200 Vacancies. In the event of a vacancy or the expiration of a regular or alternate member’s 
term, the Authority may, at a regular or specially called DDA Board Meeting, nominate a 
replacement or recommend reappointment for consideration by the Town Council.   
2.210 Nominations. The Authority may nominate a prospective regular or alternate member to 
the Town Council at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of an existing member’s term. 
When a vacancy occurs, the Authority shall make a nomination promptly after it receives notice 
of the vacancy. A regular or alternate member should use their best effort to give at least ninety 
(90) days’ advance notice of an intent not to renew their position or of a resignation.  
2.220 Voting. A regular or alternate member whose term or appointment is under 
consideration shall not vote on a recommendation concerning the extension or replacement of 
that member’s own position.  
2.230 Appointment. The Eagle Town Council is not obligated to appoint a nominee 
recommended by the Authority and may appoint a qualified individual of its own selection. If 
the Town Council has not appointed a successor when a member’s term expires, the member 
may continue to serve until a successor has been appointed and qualified. 
2.240 Removal. After notice and a public opportunity to be heard, the DDA Board may request 
that the Town Council remove a regular or alternate member for cause. A regular or alternate 
member may be removed by the Town Council for misfeasance, malfeasance, nonfeasance, 
violation of the public trust, or other lawful cause. 
2.300 Conflicts of Interest. If any person who is a DDA Board member or staff of the Authority is 
aware that the Authority is about to enter into any business transaction directly or indirectly 
with themselves, any member of their family, or any entity in which they have any legal, 
equitable or fiduciary interest or position, including without limitation, as a director, officer, 
shareholder, partner, beneficiary or trustee, such person shall (a) immediately inform those 
charged with approving the transaction on behalf of the Authority of their interest or position, 
(b) aid the persons charged with making the decision by disclosing any material facts within 
their knowledge that bear on the advisability of such transaction from the standpoint of the 
Authority, and (c) not be entitled to vote on the decision to enter into such transaction.  
2.310 Annual Conflict of Interests Statement. Each regular member, alternate member, and 
staff shall annually complete and submit a Conflict-of-Interest statement declaring any potential 
conflict of interest with regard to Authority business.  
Page 41 of 281
2.320 Code of Ethics. Each regular member, alternate member, and staff shall comply with the 
Town Code of Ethics, as amended. 
 
ARTICLE 3.000 – OFFICERS OF THE DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY 
3.100 Board Chair. The DDA Board Chair shall preside at all meetings of the Authority except as 
otherwise authorized by resolution of the Authority. The Chair shall sign all contracts, deeds, 
and other instruments made by the Authority. At each meeting, the Chair shall submit such 
recommendations and information as deemed necessary for the proper administration of the 
business affairs and policies of the Authority. In the absence or disability of a Director, the DDA 
Board Chair shall assume all Director duties and responsibilities unless directed otherwise by 
DDA Board vote. 
3.110 Election. At the first regularly scheduled meeting of the DDA Board in July of each year, a 
Chair shall be elected to preside at the meetings of the Authority with the full power to vote on 
any issue, except as otherwise provided herein. The Chair shall serve until election of a new 
Chair at the first regular meeting the following July, at which time the Chair may be 
renominated or a new Chair may be elected. 
3.200 Vice Chair. The Vice Chair shall perform the duties of the Chair in the absence, incapacity 
of the Chair, or resignation or death of the Chair. The Vice Chair shall perform the duties of the 
Chair until such time as the Authority shall select a new Chair. The Vice Chair shall be elected in 
the same manner and at the same time as the Chair. An Interim Vice Chair may be elected to 
perform the duties of the Vice Chair in the absence or incapacity of the Vice Chair, if desired by 
the DDA Board. 
3.300 Treasurer. The Treasurer shall keep the financial records of the Authority and, together 
with the Director, shall approve all vouchers for the expenditure of funds of the Authority. The 
Treasurer shall prepare and submit a monthly report stating the assets, liabilities and year-to-
date and month-to-date expenditures of the Authority.  
3.400 Secretary. The Secretary shall maintain custody of the official Seal and of all records, 
documents, or other papers not required to be maintained by the Treasurer. The Secretary shall 
attend all meetings of the DDA Board and keep a record of its proceedings, file minutes with the 
Town Clerk of all regular and special meetings and shall perform such other duties as required 
by law, agreement with Town Council or as may be delegated to them by the Director. The 
Secretary shall have power to affix the Authority’s Seal to and attest all contracts and 
instruments to be executed by the Authority.  
3.500 Director. Pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-25-815, the Authority shall employ a Director. In the 
absence or disability of the Director, the DDA Board may designate a qualified person to 
perform the duties of the office of acting Director. The Director is an at-will employee and shall 
serve at the pleasure of the DDA Board. The DDA Board shall periodically, no less frequently 
than annually, review the Director’s performance. The compensation to be paid to the Director 
shall be established by the DDA Board and budgeted accordingly.  
Page 42 of 281
3.510 Responsibility. The Director shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority and shall 
have general supervision over and be responsible for the performance of the functions of the 
Authority. Subject to and in accordance with these Rules and direction by the DDA Board, the 
Director may expend funds in accordance with standards, generally accepted governmental 
accounting and fiscal management practices. The signature of the Director and the Treasurer 
shall be required for the payment of all expenses.  
3.520 Staff. The Director shall be responsible for hiring and supervising a subordinate staff and 
for providing reports to the DDA Board. A staffing report shall be submitted monthly, which 
report shall include, without limitation, recommendations for staff hiring, separations, salaries 
and assigned duties of each staff member. Staff shall serve under the direction of the Director.  
3.530 Bond. The DDA Board may require a bond from the Director, or, by resolution, waive the 
bond requirement.  
3.540 Budget. The Director, working with the Treasurer, shall annually prepare a budget and 
submit it to the DDA Board for its approval, in accordance with applicable State law and 
Financial Management Rules for the Town. The budget shall indicate the amount of 
compensation to be paid staff and the amounts to be devoted to specific Authority projects.  
3.550 Committees. The DDA Board, by resolution, may designate and appoint one or more 
committees, each of which shall consist of no more than two (2) DDA Board Members and may 
contain members of the public. Committees shall have and exercise such authority as granted to 
them by such resolution; provided, however, no such committee shall have the power or 
authority to adopt an agreement of merger or consolidation or an agreement for the sale, lease 
or exchange of all, or substantially all, of the Authority’s property and assets, dissolve the 
Authority, or amend the rules of the Authority. Any member of a committee may be removed by 
the DDA Board, following notice and an opportunity to be heard, whenever in DDA Board’s 
judgement the best interests of the Authority shall be served by such removal.  
 
ARTICLE 4.000 – MEETINGS 
4.100 Regular Meetings. The regular meetings of the DDA Board shall be at such time and place 
as determined by the DDA Board. Regular meetings may be recessed and continued on another 
date. The Director shall send notice by electronic mail (e-mail) to each regular and alternate 
member at least two (2) days in advance of each meeting stating the time and location within 
the Town where the meeting is to be held. By resolution, the DDA Board may direct that a 
regular meeting not be held, but in no event shall more than two (2) consecutive scheduled 
meetings be cancelled. Regular meetings may be conducted in all or in part by electronic means 
by one or all DDA Board Members.  
4.110 Attendance. Attendance expectations apply to both regular and alternate members. In 
the event of three (3) consecutive absences from regular meetings, a letter may be written by 
the Director, at the direction of the Board, to the Eagle Town Council, requesting removal of that 
member pursuant to Article 2.240 of these rules.  
Page 43 of 281
4.120 Participation and Designation of Alternate Members. Alternate members may 
participate in Board discussions and deliberations at all meetings but may vote and count 
toward a quorum only when designated to serve in place of an absent, recused, or otherwise 
disqualified regular member. The Town Council may designate appointees as Alternate 1 and 
Alternate 2. If the Town Council has not assigned that order, the Board shall do so by motion. 
Alternate 1 shall serve first when one substitute is needed; Alternate 2 shall serve when 
Alternate 1 is unavailable or disqualified or when two substitutes are needed. The Chair shall 
announce each designation on the record. A designation based on a regular member’s absence 
ends when that regular member becomes present and qualified to participate. A designation 
based on recusal or other matter-specific disqualification applies only to that matter. No more 
than seven members may vote on any matter. 
4.200 Special Meetings. Special meetings of the Authority may be called by the Chair or in the 
Chair’s absence, the Vice Chair, at a convenient place and time. The Director shall send notice by 
electronic mail (e-mail) to each regular and alternate member at least two (2) days in advance 
of each meeting stating the time and location within the Town where the meeting is to be held. 
4.300 Open Meetings. All meetings of the DDA Board shall be open to the public, except those 
that may lawfully be closed by law. Notice of all meetings of the DDA Board shall be posted on 
the Town’s website at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance, in compliance with the Colorado 
Open Meetings Law, C.R.S. § 24-6-401, et seq. All meeting notices shall include agenda 
information where possible.  
4.400 Quorum. A quorum consists of a majority of the seven authorized regular positions. An 
alternate member designated under Section 4.120 shall be counted toward the quorum while 
serving in place of a regular member. An alternate who has not been designated shall not be 
counted toward the quorum. 
4.500 Voting. All regular business matters shall be decided by a majority of the quorum unless 
otherwise provided by these Rules or by law. An alternate member may vote only while 
designated to serve under Section 4.120. 
4.600 Rules of Order. The Chair (or the Vice Chair in the Chair’s absence) will preside at 
meetings, unless otherwise approved by the DDA Board. Unless otherwise required by law, 
action of the DDA Board will be by motion or resolution duly made, seconded, and approved by 
a majority of the quorum present at a public meeting. A motion or resolution may be revised 
before adoption with the prior approval of the maker and seconder. DDA Board Members will 
maintain respect and decorum at meetings. The public may speak at a meeting as allowed by 
the DDA Board or as required by law.

5.100 Contracts. The DDA Board may authorize, by resolution, the Chair or the Director, 
consistent with the authority stated by the resolution, to enter into any contract or execute any 
instrument in the name of an on behalf of the Authority.

5.230 Loans. No loans shall be contracted for or on behalf of the Authority and no evidence of

6.100 Amendments. These Rules may be amended at any regular or special meeting by a two-

ARTICLE 7.000 – INDEMNIFICATION  
7.100 Indemnification. Any of the Authority’s officers, Directors and other employees may be 
indemnified or reimbursed by the Authority for reasonable expenses (including without 
limitation attorney fees, judgements and payments in settlement) actually incurred in 
connection with any action, suit or proceeding, civil or criminal, actual or threatened, to which 
they are made a party by reason of his being or having been, or by reason of any actual or 
alleged acts performed or omitted to be performed in connection with his being or having been 
a DDA Board Member, officer or employee of the Authority; provided, however, that no person 
shall be so indemnified or reimbursed in relation to any matter in such action, suit or 
proceeding as to which they shall finally be adjudged to have been guilty or liable for gross 
negligence or willful misconduct or criminal acts in the performance of his duties to the

7.200 Insurance. The DDA Board may, upon affirmative vote of its Board of Directors, purchase
