REQUEST: Staﬀ requests Council direction regarding the highest-priority capital improvement projects for 
inclusion in the draft 2027 budget. Staﬀ also seeks Council guidance on the allocation and use of MEAC 
and Community Grant funding for 2027. 
 
BACKGROUND: Staﬀ have collaborated across departments to develop the Town’s operating and capital 
budgets. In addition, Council members have identiﬁed speciﬁc projects for consideration and requested 
a review of MEAC and Community Grant Fund allocations. As staﬀ ﬁnalize the preliminary budget and 
prepare for upcoming grant cycles, Council’s collective direction is needed to prioritize capital 
investments and ensure strategic use of available funding resources. 
 
Capital Project Priorities for 2027 
You can ﬁnd the 2026-2035 CIP here. This plan is actively being updated as staﬀ prepare the 2027 
budget. The anticipated year-end fund balances for the Capital Improvements Fund (CIF), Sales Tax 
Capital Improvements Fund (STCIF), and Conservation Trust Fund (CTF) are listed below. Staﬀ are seeking 
prioritization input on projects in these funds for the 2027 budget year. Please keep in mind that these 
are not the only capital projects being proposed for next year.  
 
The fund balances listed assume all budgeted CIP projects are completed this year, and staﬀ anticipate 
these balances being adjusted based on the progress of projects through the end of 2026. Historically, 
projects not completed in a budgeted year are rolled forward to the next year. Updated year-end 
projected balances will be presented to Council at the Capital Budget Work Session on September 29. 
 
As a reminder, the CIF is funded through a transfer of excess fund balance from the year prior as well as 
use tax and impact fees. As we adjust operating budgets to more accurately reﬂect annual revenues and 
expenses, the amount available for transfer will be reduced. The STCIF is funded through sales tax, and 
the CTF is funded through lottery proceeds, with usage restricted for conservation sites or recreational 
purposes on public sites.

Future Capital Investments Needed: 
The CIP is a 10-year planning document with a heavy focus on the next 5 years. As Council considers 
2027 priorities, it is important to preserve suﬃcient fund balance capacity to address projects scheduled 
in future years and anticipated needs not yet reﬂected in the CIP . One notable example is the future 
redevelopment of the St. Mary’s property. Planning eﬀorts, including cost estimates, are underway and 
will continue through 2027, with site-related improvements anticipated no earlier than 2028 following 
full property vacancy. 
 
Council should also consider the organizational capacity required to successfully deliver capital projects. 
Capital initiatives are implemented alongside ongoing service delivery and require signiﬁcant staﬀ 
involvement, even when consultants or contractors are utilized. In recent years, operating budget 
constraints have aﬀected the Town’s ability to keep pace with routine maintenance of facilities, streets, 
sidewalks, and landscaping. Staﬀ recommends balancing investments in new amenities and initiatives 
with the resources necessary to maintain existing infrastructure and service levels. 
 
MEAC (Marketing & Events Advisory Committee) Funding 
MEAC has provided funding recommendations for various events in Eagle for many years. Over the last 
two years, MEAC has been allocated $50,000 to award, in addition to providing $50,000 for Showdown 
Town annually. MEAC objectives are to enhance the sense of community for Eagle residents, balancing 
the qualify of life with vitality for the business community, and supporting events and programs that 
showcase Eagle’s unique character and promote cultural diversity.  
 
MEAC funding is supported by a 6% lodging tax that was passed by voters in 2021. The lodging tax 
revenue is split 50/50 between the Open Space Fund and Town marketing and events. MEAC has an 
established grant program where event producers and community partners apply for event funding each 
year. MEAC members review applications and make award recommendations that are then approved by 
Town Council.

MEAC has requested additional feedback and direction from Council about the type of events to invest in 
for Eagle. The common question is whether MEAC should be focused on events that draw in visitors and 
put “heads in beds” from a lodging standpoint, or support more community-focused events that are 
loved by locals. Each year, MEAC attempts to strike a balance. As costs continue to rise for producing 
events, however, the question remains about the most impactful use of the MEAC funds.  
 
The 2026 Strategic Plan includes an initiative to evaluate staﬃng costs support of larger events. These 
costs include Public Works staﬀ for event infrastructure set up and tear down and any required road 
closures. The costs can also include extra duty police as needed, depending on the event size and type. 
Staﬀ are compiling this data and want to note that the cost is not the only barrier to providing more 
event support. Adequate staﬀ capacity to work extra shifts or alter regular work schedules to support 
evening and weekend events is also necessary. We will have more information in the coming weeks 
associated with the presentation of the operating budget. For the purpose of the Work Session 
discussion, staﬀ encourage Council to consider the balance between additional larger events and the 
support that would be requested from Public Works and the Police Department.  
 
Community Grant Funding 
Community Funding resources are awarded by Town Council annually through a grant application 
process. The funds are used to support programs that foster a sense of community and enhance the 
quality of life for residents and visitors, promote leadership or provide cultural and positive health and 
well-being opportunities for the community.  You can review the program information and 2025 
application on our website here (scroll past the event funding section).  
 
Historically, Council has voted on support levels via a spreadsheet and staﬀ calculate recommend award 
amounts based on Council’s priorities. This tends to result in smaller amounts of funding but spread 
across many awardees. There is signiﬁcant administrative time spent processing the applications and 
calculating, dispersing and tracking the awards.  Additionally, the applicants invest signiﬁcant time 
preparing applications. Some applicants request upwards of $10,000 but they may only receive an award 
of a few hundred dollars.  2026 Community Grant Fund Awards.  
 
In 2026, the total amount of allocated funds was reduced from $50,000 to $25,000 to assist with 
balancing the General Fund operating budget. For 2027, staﬀ have identiﬁed $25,000 for Community 
Grant funding. However, staﬀ recommend that Council consider reallocating these funds to support 
increasing General Fund operating costs, including contract services, software expenditures, animal 
control services, dispatch services, employee health insurance, and compensation adjustments. Should 
Community Grant funding be restored in future years, staﬀ recommend a more competitive award 
structure with fewer grants at higher funding levels to maximize community impact while reducing 
administrative burden.

Quarterly Capital Projects Report
BACKGROUND: The purpose of this quarterly report is to provide consistent and transparent updates on the progress of Town capital projects. Our goal is to
enhance communication with Town Council and foster trust and confidence within the community. This collaborative effort includes regular updates from all
departments and enables us to address challenges related to project management, budgeting, and communication. This report includes capital projects budgeted
for the current year as well as prior year projects that were not completed before year-end and have been rolled into 2026 via the supplemental budget process.
GUIDELINES:
1. Staff will provide a quarterly report to Council that includes the following information:
          •     All Town Capital Projects by Fund and Department
          •     Staff Lead for each project
          •     Original and revised budgets for each project
          •     Year to date (YTD) actual amounts for each project and budget to actual variances
          •     Current status of each project as defined in the "Project Status List"
          •     Estimated completion date for the current status of the project
          •     Estimated completion date for the entire project
          •     Notes to Council regarding project status, including delays and changes in scope
2. Finance Role & Responsibilities:
          •     Update YTD budget and actual project spend, on a quarterly basis.
          •     Collaborate with Staff Leads to provide necessary status updates as outlined below.
          •     Finalize the report and submit it to the Council Packet.
3. Project Staff Lead Role & Responsibilities:
          •     Review and validate budget and actual amounts provided by Finance.
          •     Provide the following information to Finance:
               •     Project Status: Identify the current status of each project as of quarter end. There will be a new column for each quarter so that staff, Council, and the 
                     Community can see the progression of each project. Project Statuses are shown below.
               •     Estimated Status Completion Date: Identify the estimated completion date for the current status of the project. If the project status has changed from
                     the prior quarter, identify a new estimated completion date for the current status of the project.
               •     Estimated Project Completion Date: At the beginning of the project, an "Estimated Project Completion Date" will be identified. Should this estimate
                     change, it must be updated in the report.
               •     Notes: Staff does not need to provide notes every quarter unless there is something that Council should be aware of; however, if an estimated 
                     completion date has changed, you must include the reasoning why. Please be concise. If Staff need to provide extensive information regarding a
                     particular project, that can be accomplished in the monthly Council Staff Reports, or through a regular business item on the agenda.

Marketing & Events Advisory Committee (MEAC) 
 
The Marketing & Advisory Committee (MEAC) works to strengthen the sense of community among Eagle 
residents by balancing a high quality of life with a vibrant local business environment.  
MEAC Objectives: 
• Enhance the sense of community for Eagle residents by providing opportunities for connection. 
• Balance the quality of life for residents with the need for vitality in our business community. 
• Support events and programs that showcase and protect our unique character, traditions, and 
resources. 
• Support cultural events and programs that promote diversity. 
 
Ordinance No. 24 (2020) 
WHEREAS, at the coordinated election held on November 3, 2020, the Town's electors approved Ballot 
Issue 2A, which permits the Town to increase and change the computation method of the Town's lodging 
tax from $4.00 per day per occupied lodging room or accommodation to 6% of the cost of the rental fee, 
price or other consideration paid or received for the occupied lodging room or accommodation, 
commencing January 1, 2021. 
 
The Town of Eagle occupation tax is levied on the provision of lodging upon every person or business that 
furnishes any hotel room, motel room, lodging room, motor hotel room, guest house room or other 
similar accommodation for consideration for less than 1 month or 30 consecutive days within the Town 
of Eagle in the amount of 6% of net Lodging Sales. 
  
Lodging occupancy tax has a dual purpose. Fifty percent of taxes collected is allocated to the Town’s 
Open Space Fund to preserve and protect natural open space areas and wildlife habitat, while also 
providing outdoor recreation opportunities for Eagle citizens and visitors. The other fifty percent is 
allocated to fund Town marketing and events that bring visitors to the Town of Eagle.
