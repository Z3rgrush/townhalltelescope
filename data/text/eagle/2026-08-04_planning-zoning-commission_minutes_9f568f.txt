DISCUSSION / BUSINESS ITEMS  
 
 Trail Wayfinding Signage 
• Staff presented prototype trail signs and continued the Committee's discussion of sign content, 
placement, materials, and durability. 
• The Committee generally supported: 
• A hierarchy with detailed information at trailheads, maps or orientation at major trail hubs, and 
simple trail names and directional arrows at individual junctions. 
• Including permitted-use, regulatory, or land-manager information only where it is relevant. 
• Wood posts for their natural appearance, while exploring metal ground sleeves, post caps, and 
other treatments that could extend their service life. 
• Coordinating fonts, colors, and other design elements with the Town's broader wayfinding 
system. 
• Using signs at land-management transitions when needed to explain changes in regulations. 
• Carsonite posts were discussed as a lower-cost option but were not preferred as the primary 
Town standard. 
• Next Steps: Staff will compare material costs and expected service life, coordinate with town 
staff on coordinated manufacturer pricing, and continue refining the sign standard.  
 
Open Space Fund Operating Budget Check-in 
• Staff reported that the 2026 operating budget is generally on track and presented proposed 
adjustments for 2027. 
• Major proposed changes include: 
o Increasing restoration supplies by $8,000 for seed, ground cover, signs, soil 
amendments, and related materials. 
o Reducing the legal-services budget to reflect actual use. 
o Reducing trail repair and maintenance because Bike Park maintenance is 
expected to be funded through the Sales Tax Fund and some work can be 
supported through staff and volunteers. 
o Adding $1,000 for volunteer recognition. 
• The proposed budget continues to fund the manager and technician positions. Staff will consult 
with Finance about the feasibility of converting the technician position to a full-time specialist 
position. 
• The Committee discussed Haymeadow real-estate-transfer revenue, the Open Space Fund 
balance, and maintaining the Town's ability to contribute matching funds toward future land 
acquisitions. 
• The Committee expressed general support for the proposed operating budget.

OSRAC Vacancy and Recruitment 
• The Committee supported coordinating the current vacancy with the regular fall recruitment for 
terms expiring at the end of 2026, provided the approach is consistent with the bylaws. 
• Members were generally comfortable continuing to use the existing application and interview 
process. 
• Next Steps: Staff will confirm the vacancy requirements, begin recruitment in the fall, and 
anticipate appointments in early 2027. No formal action was taken.
