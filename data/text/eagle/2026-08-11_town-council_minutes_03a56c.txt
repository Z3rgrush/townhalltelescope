Ordinance 13, Series 2026 - An Ordinance of the Town Council of the Town of Eagle, Colorado, Miscellaneous 
1. Amendments to Title 4, Specifically Chapters 17 and 20 (Variances) of the Eagle Land Use and Development Code 
Related to Development Standards and Processes 
2. 
Community Development Director Jessica Johnsen presented comprehensive amendments to the variance 
process, including a two-tiered system of mandatory and discretionary criteria, a new definition of undue 
hardship, and clarification that financial hardship alone is not sufficient for a variance. 
Council debated the call-up procedure, with some members advocating for retaining Council oversight and 
others supporting a staff-initiated appeal mechanism. Council members discussed the implications of removing 
the call-up provision, the need for accountability of P&Z decisions, and the importance of harmonizing appeal 
procedures across the code. After discussion, Council deleted the subsection prohibiting the call-up procedure 
and approved the remainder of the ordinance. Council directed staff to draft language that specifically addresses 
the call-up procedure. 
MOTION: Council Member Andrew Atkins motioned to amend Ordinance 13, Series 2026, An 
Ordinance of the Town Council of the Town of Eagle, Colorado, Miscellaneous Amendments to Title 4, 
Specifically Chapters 17 and 20 of the Eagle Land Use and Development Code Related to 
Development Standards and Processes, amending subsection 2 by deleting subsection 4 of section 2. 
The motion was seconded and passed with a vote of 5 in favor (Glowacki, Mccrackin, Atkins, Woods, 
Woodworth Foral) and 2 opposed (Grimmer, Schreiner). 
MOTION: Council Member Andrew Atkins motioned to approve the amended Ordinance 13, Series 
2026, An Ordinance of the Town Council of the Town of Eagle, Colorado, Miscellaneous Amendments

Ordinance 14, Series 2026 - An Ordinance of the Town of Eagle, Colorado Authorizing the Purchase of Real 
Property Located at 215 Capitol Street in Eagle, Colorado, Approving the Buy and Sell Agreement for the Property 
and Authorizing the Town Manager to Sign Documents on Behalf of the Town that are Necessary to Consummate 
the Purchase of the Property 
Mayor Bryan Woods stepped out of the meeting. 
Council discussed and approved the purchase of 215 Capitol Street (St. Mary's Church) for $3.15 million, 
including a leaseback to the church until January 2028. Council members highlighted the property's prime 
location, flexibility for future uses (including police facilities, parking, or potential public-private partnerships), 
and the opportunity to address long-term municipal needs without removing a revenue-generating asset from

COUNCIL DISCUSSION AND FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS 
Council requested finance staff to present options for allocating additional funds to the Energy Smart 
Program. 
Council agreed to include a discussion on the status, obstacles, and future direction for Brush Creek Road, 
with input from Public Works and other departments at the next meeting. 
Town Manager Melissa Daruna wanted to clarify that the Open House on August 25th is intended to gather 
broad public feedback on multiple site options that both include and don't include pickleball. She and Council 
emphasized the importance of clear, data-driven communication, direct follow-up with residents who have 
raised concerns, and ensuring that the event is not perceived as advocating for a particular outcome. Once 
the Open House is complete and feedback has been compiled and distributed, it is ultimately up to Eagle 
County to make the final decision and seek funding from the Town of Eagle. Mayor Woods wants to clearly 
communicate that the discussion should focus on what should be built at the geothermal build site, not what 
the best location for pickleball courts would be. 
Due to the Pickleball Open House the August 25th Town Council Meeting will begin at 6:30 PM.
