Minutes for the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting September 1, 2026
3.
PUBLIC PARTICIPATION -
Individuals may not comment on items that are on this Agenda during the Public Participation time.  There is no limit to the number of speakers, although public comments will be held to an overall time limit of 30 minutes. (Speakers have a time limit of three (3) minutes per person, may only speak once, and may not cede time to another commenter.)
4.
PUBLIC HEARINGS
a.
Planning & Zoning Resolution No. 9 Series 2026
The Planning and Zoning Commission will review Resolution No. 9 Series 2026, and will hold a public hearing for a possible recommendation for multiple revisions to the Cortez Land Use Code for historic designation.
b.
Planning and Zoning Resolution No. 7, Series 2026
Resolution No. 7, Series 2026 is a formal recommendation from the Planning and Zoning Commission to the Cortez City Council requesting that the Council adopt an ordinance amending the City Code to authorize Commission members to participate in Commission meetings remotely via videoconferencing or other electronic means ("Electronic Participation").
