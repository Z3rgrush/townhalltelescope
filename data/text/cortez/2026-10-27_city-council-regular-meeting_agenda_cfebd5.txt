ADJOURNMENT
PURSUANT TO RESOLUTION NO. 10, SERIES 2025, PUBLIC COMMENT RULES:
-- During the general Public Participation sections of City Council Meetings, individuals may not comment regarding items on the Council agenda, but may comment on any other topic they wish to address the City Council about, including items discussed in a previous Council Workshop or previous Council Meeting.
--Those persons wishing to comment must register by completing an "Intent to Speak" card (located outside of the Council chambers). Completed cards will be collected at the start of the meeting and delivered to the Mayor or other presiding officer, who will call each speaker to the podium at the appropriate time.
--Comments specific to agenda items scheduled for this agenda should be reserved and delivered during the public comment portion of that specific agenda item.
--Courtesy, civility, and respect for others is expected. All commenters should only speak from the podium using the microphone, and comments should be addressed directly to the Council and not to staff or other members of the public. Commenters who are called upon by the Mayor, or other presiding officer, to speak are the only persons allowed to speak during the allotted time. Comments, or other distractions from the audience intended for commenters or others are not permitted. The Mayor, or other presiding officer, as the chairperson for the meeting, retains the discretion to deviate from the format described below.
--There are two general opportunities for the public to address the Council:
For the first opportunity (which will occur toward the start of the meeting) there is no limit to the number of speakers, although public comments will be held to an overall time limit of 30 minutes so City business may proceed. Speakers have a time limit of 3 minutes per person, may only speak once, and may not cede time to other commenters.
For the second opportunity (which will occur toward the end of the meeting) there is no limit to the number of speakers, and no overall time limit. Speakers have a time limit of 3 minutes per person, may only speak once, and may not cede time to other commenters.
--Other Opportunities to Participate
The public may also participate via email if addressed to councilcomments@cortezco.gov. Comments received by 3:00 pm the day of a Council meeting will be delivered to Council the same day and entered into the meeting minutes. The public may also send letters to the Council by addressing them to "City Council" 123 Roger Smith Ave., Cortez, CO 81321. In-person deliveries are also accepted.
