ORDINANCE NO. 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE UNIFIED GOVERNMENT OF WYANDOTTE COUNTY/ KANSAS 
CITY, KANSAS, SELECTING A MEMBER OF THE UNIFIED GOVERNMENT BOARD OF 
COMMISSIONERS AS AN APPOINTING AUTHORITY FOR PURPOSES OF REVIEWING 
THE CRIMINAL HISTORY RECORD CHECK OF A SELF-APPOINTED MAYORAL 
MEMBER OF THE KANSAS SPORTS FACILITIES AUTHORITY PURSUANT TO 2026 
HOUSE BILL NO. 2466 
 
WHEREAS, during the 2026 session, the Kansas Legislature enacted House Bill No. 
2466, which, among other things, establishes the Kansas Sports Facilities Authority Act (New 
Sections 1 through 8 of House Bill No. 2466) creating the Kansas Sports Facilities Authority (the 
“Authority”) to provide for the construction, financing, and management of certain sports facilities 
and sports facility infrastructure; and 
WHEREAS, New Section 3(b) of House Bill No. 2466 provides that the Authority shall 
consist of eleven voting members, including, as provided by New Section 3(d), up to two members 
appointed by the mayors of cities where a sports facility of the Authority is located; and 
WHEREAS, New Section 3(d)(1) of House Bill No. 2466 permits the mayor of such a city 
to appoint a member of the Authority who may be the mayor themselves, subject to the conditions 
set forth in that section; and 
WHEREAS, the Mayor/CEO of the Unified Government of Wyandotte County/Kansas 
City, Kansas has made a self-appointment to the Authority pursuant to New Section 3(d) of House 
Bill No. 2466; and 
WHEREAS, New Section 3(a)(2) of House Bill No. 2466 requires that, before the 
appointment of any person as a member of the Authority, the appointing authority shall require 
such person to be fingerprinted and submit to a state and national criminal history record check in 
accordance with K.S.A. 2025 Supp. 22-4714, as amended; and 
WHEREAS, New Section 3(d)(3) of House Bill No. 2466 provides that, in the event of 
the self-appointment by a mayor to the Authority, the governing body of such mayor’s city shall 
select a member of such governing body as the appointing authority for purposes of review of the 
criminal history record check required by New Section 3(a)(2); and 
WHEREAS, the Unified Government Board of Commissioners desires to select and 
designate a member of the Commission to serve as the appointing authority for the limited purpose 
of reviewing the criminal history record check information pertaining to the Mayor/CEO’s self-
appointment to the Authority, as required by New Section 3(d)(3) of House Bill No. 2466. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE UNIFIED 
GOVERNMENT OF WYANDOTTE COUNTY/ KANSAS CITY, KANSAS: 
SECTION ONE: Designation of Appointing Authority. Pursuant to New Section 3(d)(3) 
of House Bill No. 2466, the Unified Government Board of Commissioners hereby selects and 
designates Commissioner Melissa Bynum as the appointing authority for purposes of reviewing 
the state and national criminal history 
record check required by New Section 3(a)(2) of House Bill No. 2466 in connection with the 
self-appointment of the Mayor/CEO as a member of the Authority. 
SECTION TWO: Scope of Designation. The designation made in Section One is 
limited solely to the review of the criminal history record check information obtained 
pursuant to New Section 3(a)(2) and (a)(3) of House Bill No. 2466 with respect to the 
Mayor/CEO’s self-appointment to the Authority, and confers no other appointing, 
supervisory, or decision-making authority upon Commissioner Bynum with the respect to 
the Authority or Mayor/CEO’s membership on it. 
SECTION THREE: Confidentiality. Commissioner Bynum shall maintain the 
confidentiality of any criminal history record check information received in connection 
with this designation, consistent with New Section 3(a)(3) of House Bill No. 2466, which 
provides that the Kansas Bureau of Investigation shall remain in custody and control of 
such information and shall allow only the appointing authority and certain legislative 
leaders to view it. 
SECTION FOUR: Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately 
upon its adoption.
