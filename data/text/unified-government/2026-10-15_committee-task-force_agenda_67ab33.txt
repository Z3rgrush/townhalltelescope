5. New Business: 
a. Ethics FAQ for website—The Commission reviewed the FAQ, proposed additional edits and tabled 
the matter.  The revisions were reviewed and approved, and two more suggestions were made to 
add in a reference to admonishment and to rename they hyperlink. Additional edits were 
discussed for the flow chart—remove the green box stating “UG Ethics Code Applies?” and to add 
“for additional information see FAQ.” There was a motion to approve the FAQ and flow chart 
which was seconded and approved unanimously. The changes will be posted to the website, and 
Reed will advise the PIO that we have updated the website so it can be noted in the newsletter 
and on social media. 
   
b. Proposed revisions to the Ethics Code--The Commission reviewed the proposed revisions.  
i. The Commission discussed revised Section 2-261(b) and agreed the newly proposed 
language would provide clarification. 
   
ii. The Commission discussed proposed revisions regarding honoraria and setting a $1,000 
limit on honorarium and requiring disclosure to the Ethics Commission. Any amount over 
$1000 is to be turned over to the Unified Government general fund. There was discussion

iii. A discussion about whether or not to amend the Ethics Code to require annual ethics 
training vs. the current requirements for a refresher every three (3) years ensued.  Reed 
discussed the plan to use Workday to provide Ethic refresher training once an online 
training module was completed.   Ethics training has been provided to the fire department 
and now appointed employees which takes time, and Reed recommended  that the Ethics 
Commission push back changes to the Code for annual ethics  refresher until electronic 
training is ready to be unrolled. The Commission discussed ways to develop an online 
refresher training program including asking colleges if a student is looking for an 
internship who can assist.  Commissioner Wittman and Jenkins expressed a wish to push 
back changing to an annual training at this time, and the Commission agreed. 
 
 
iv. Commissioner Wittman moved to approve the proposed changes to the UG Ethics Code 
with the changes discussed and identified on page 9 and to forward to the Unified 
Government Board of Commissioners.  Commissioner Clark seconded the motion, and the 
Ethics Commission approved the motion unanimously.  
 
c. Questions from Ethics Commission:  Chairwoman Ford asked whether public comment was being 
promoted. Reed advised that public comment is noted on every agenda that is distributed. 
Chairwoman Ford advised that the Commission had addressed the goals that had been 
established and asked whether the Commission would like to take a summer break and reconvene 
in September, unless a meeting was needed sooner. The Commission agreed a break was in order.
