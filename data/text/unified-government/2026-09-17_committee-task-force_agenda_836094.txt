4.  Old Business: 
a. March 19, 2026 minutes approve unanimously. 
b. Review of Monthly report of Administrator: 
• The Commission was briefed on the training provided. 
• Two new requests for advisory opinions were discussed. 
➢ Three matters are pending investigation.  
 
 
5. New Business: 
a. Ethics FAQ for website—The Commission reviewed the FAQ and proposed additional edits.  
Regarding the images, the 2nd image was preferred.  M. Jenkins had some edits she would like to 
proposed to the Ethics Administrator. This matter was tabled until the May meeting for additional 
revisions.  
b. Proposed revisions to the Ethics Code--The Commission walked through the proposed revisions. 
Formatting change were suggested to the definition sections, and the Legislative Auditor 
requested that the Ethics Commission consider holding on mandating annual training for all 
employees until an online training module is complete due to feasibility of staffing that training.  
The Commission discussed Section 2-261(b) and the need to try and clarify the language to make 
it more easily understood.   The Commission discussed setting a $1,000 limit on honorarium 
ensuring it was clear that any amount over $1000 was to be turned over to the general fund. 
There was additional discussion about prior sections of the Code that could use clarification as 
well.  
c. Annual goals for the Ethics Commission—The Chair noted that the Commission has been actively 
working to address the annual goals.  The Commission is working to update the Ethics Code, 
working with HR on updating Ethics training, the training schedule and the website. A discussion 
ensued about whether the Ethics Commission wanted to have periodic meetings with HR.
