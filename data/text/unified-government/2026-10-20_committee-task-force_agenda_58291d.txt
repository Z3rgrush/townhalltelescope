BYLAWS OF THE WYANDOTTE COUNTY LIBRARY BOARD 
 
Adopted on _____________, 2026 
 
 
ARTICLE I -- PURPOSE 
 
The purpose of this library board shall be to: 
 
A. Fairly distribute funds generated by the Wyandotte County Library mill 
levy for the Library District. 
 
B. Work closely with the Kansas City, Kansas Public Library and the Bonner 
Springs City Library all public libraries in the county to promote joint 
countywide planning. 
 
C. Develop and promote any inter-local agreements between USD 
500/Kansas City, Kansas Public Library, the Unified Government of 
Wyandotte County/Kansas City, Kansas, Edwardsville Public Library 
and Bonner Springs City Library to provide library service to all 
residents of Wyandotte County. 
 
 
ARTICLE II -- ORGANIZATION 
 
The Library Board is established by Charter Resolution No. CR-01-99 CR-01-26, 
adopted by the Unified Government Board of Commissioners on August 5, 1999 
August 6, 2026, and taking effect on November 1, 2026, and is composed of 
eleven eight members. One member is appointed by each Unified Government 
Commissioner and one member is appointed by the Mayor. 
Appointments are as follows: Two members from USO 202 (Turner), two from 
USO 203 (Piper), two from the City of Edwardsville, four from USO 500 and one 
from Bonner Springs. The appointment of members and terms Terms of office for 
Board members are as set out in Unified Government Charter Resolution No. CR-
01-99 CR-01-26. 
 
 
ARTICLE Ill -- POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE BOARD 
 
The Library Board has the following powers and duties: 
 
A. Determine the amount of the tax levy for the Wyandotte County Library 
library district. 
 
B. Develop a plan for allocation of the funds raised from the tax levy. 
 
C. Contract with any library to furnish library service to the Wyandotte County

ARTICLE IV -- MEETINGS 
 
A. The Board shall hold meetings as determined by the Board. The meetings 
shall be held in the various libraries of Wyandotte County. There shall be 
a preference for holding the meetings in the various public libraries of 
Wyandotte County; except that a meeting may be scheduled at City Hall 
or another alternative site when audio-visual capabilities at a public 
library, including microphones and recording equipment, are not adequate 
or available. 
 
B. Special meetings of the Board may be called by the chairperson if deemed 
necessary at any time by giving suitable notice of time and place of such 
meeting to all members. A meeting may also be called upon the request 
of any three two members. They should direct their request to the 
chairperson. 
 
C. A quorum shall consist of a majority of the members of the Board 
appointed and qualified at any given time. 
 
D. All meetings should shall be open to the public as required by the Kansas 
Open Meetings Act, K.S.A. 45-201 75-4317 et seq., and conducted in 
accordance with Robert's Rules of Order except as modified by these by-
laws and the rules and procedures as adopted by the Board.

ARTICLE VI -- AMENDMENTS TO BYLAWS 
 
The Board may, by a two-thirds majority vote of all its members, amend these 
bylaws or any provisions or sections thereof at any time when the same is not in 
conflict with any laws of the State of Kansas, Charter Resolution No. CR-01-26, 
or the UG Code of Ordinances, or applicable local law thereof. A notice of the 
proposed amendments will be furnished to the Board members not less than ten 
days prior to the meeting at which the amendments will be considered.
