Land Use Consent Calendar
5.a.
FINAL PLAT - HARDY ROAD PROPERTIES SUBDIVISION - A request by Hardy Road Properties, LLC, for approval of a 19.34-acre Final Plat creating two single-family lots and one tract. The property is zoned RR-5 (Residential Rural) and is located approximately 1.75 miles east of North Meridian Road, at 10545 Hardy Road.  The requested lots have a minimum lot size of 5 acres and are anticipated to be served by individual wells and on-site septic systems. The item was heard as a consent item at the September 17, 2026, Planning Commission meeting, and was recommended for approval with a vote of 8-0. (Parcel No. 5114000046) (Commissioner District No. 1) (SF-26-005)
662/2026
5.b.
PRELIMINARY PLAN - TRIPLE H RANCH PRELIMINARY PLAN - A request by P760 Land, LLC for approval of a 752.68-acre Preliminary Plan creating 244 single-family lots, 40.57-acres of tract dedication, and 67.92-acres of right-of-way dedication. The properties are within the RR-2.5 zoning district and are located north of Jones Road, 0.23 miles east of the intersection of Murr Road and Jones Road, and 0.49 miles west of the intersection of North Peyton Highway and Jones Road. The item was heard as a consent item at the October 1, 2026, Planning Commission meeting, and was recommended for approval with a vote of 7-0. (Parcel Nos. 3300000168 & 3300000388) (Commissioner District No. 2) (SP-25-004)
