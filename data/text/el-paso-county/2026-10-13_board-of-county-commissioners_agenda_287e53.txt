14.a.
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(b) and (e), the County Attorney’s Office is requesting an Executive Session regarding potential litigation stemming from a rear-end auto accident involving Daniel Krawczyk and former Assessor employee Sherri Gonzales. The purposes of the Executive Session include (1) conference with the County Attorney’s Office for the purpose of receiving legal advice on specific legal questions related to a potential litigation matter; and (2) Determining positions relative to matters that may be subject to negotiations; developing strategy for negotiations; and instructing negotiators.
