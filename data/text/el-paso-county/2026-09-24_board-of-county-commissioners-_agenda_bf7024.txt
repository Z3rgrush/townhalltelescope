MAP AMENDMENT (REZONING) - HILLPOINTE APARTMENTS REZONE - A request by Hillpointe, LLC for approval of a Map Amendment (Rezoning) of 14.09 acres from CR (Commercial Regional) and CAD-O (Commercial Airport Overlay District) to RM-30 (Residential Multi-Dwelling) and CAD-O (Commercial Airport Overlay District).
