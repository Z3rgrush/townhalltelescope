MINOR SUBDIVISION - BRADLEY POINT FILING NO. 1 AMENDMENT - A request by M&S Civil Consultants, Inc and Bradley Point, LLC for approval of an amendment to the previously approved Bradley Point Filing No.1 Minor Subdivision (PCD File No. MS212) to establish a water sufficiency finding. The 9.736-acre property is zoned M (Industrial) and is located on the east side of East Las Vegas Street/CanAm Highway, at the southbound exit ramp of South Academy Road turning northwest onto East Las Vegas Street/CanAm Highway. The water sufficiency finding request is not required to be heard by the Planning Commission. (Parcel ID nos. 6503403002 and 6503403003) (Commissioner District No. 4) (MS-21-002)

5.b.
MAP AMENDMENT (REZONING) - SIESTA GROVE REZONING RR-5 TO RR-2.5 - A request by Quentin Dassonville for approval of a Map Amendment (Rezoning) of 35.08 acres from RR-5 (Residential Rural) to RR-2.5 (Residential Rural). The property to be rezoned is located at 15850 Siesta Grove, and is approximately 1.63 miles southwest of the intersection of Black Forest Road and Hodgen Road. The item was heard on the consent agenda at the October 15, 2026 Planning Commission meeting, and was recommended for approval with a vote of 7-0. (Parcel No. 6100000292) (Commissioner District No. 1) (P-26-010)
729/2026
5.c.
MAP AMENDMENT (REZONING) - THE MARKETS AT BENT GRASS REZONE (I-2 TO CR) - A request by Cythia DeYoung and Lena Gail Case for approval of a Map Amendment (Rezoning) of a 17.17-acre property from the I-2 (Industrial) to the CR (Commercial Regional) zoning district. The property is located along Woodmen Frontage Road and Bent Grass Meadows Drive and 0.64-miles west of the intersection of Woodmen Road and Meridian Road. The item was heard as a called up consent item at the October 15, 2026 Planning Commission meeting, and was recommended for approval with a vote of 7-0.  (Parcel Nos. 5301000016) (Commissioner District No. 2) (CR-26-001)

PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT/PRELIMINARY PLAN - MAYBERRY PHASE 2 - A request by Mayberry Communities, LLC, for approval of a Map Amendment (Rezoning) of 18.70 acres from a conceptual PUD (Planned Unit Development) to a site-specific PUD (Planned Unit Development) with approval of a Preliminary Plan illustrating 92 single-family attached lots, 6 single-family detached lots, 1 clubhouse lot, for a total of 99 lots, 8 tracts, including 5.99 acres of open space provisions and 2.33 acres of land dedicated for public right-of-way. The property is located at the southeast corner of the intersection of State Highway 94 and Mayberry Drive. The item was heard on the regular agenda at the October 15, 2026, Planning Commission meeting. There was significant discussion about Ellicott Fire Protection District comments and their lack of adequate response related to fire protection and the District's input on if the project meets codes and fire regulations. The Planning Commission recommended approval and the motion failed with a vote of 3-4. (Parcel Nos. 3400000472, 3400000473, 3414201024, 3414205001, and 3414206001) (Commissioner District No. 4) (PUDSP-25-001)
712/2026
