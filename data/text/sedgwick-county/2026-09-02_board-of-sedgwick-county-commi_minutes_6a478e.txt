PROCLAMATION
WHEREAS, Sedgwick County is honored to be home to a vibrant Latino/Hispanic community, whose roots span across Latin America, bringing a rich tapestry of cultures, languages and traditions that contribute to the diversity of our county; and
WHEREAS, Latinos/Hispanics have played a pivotal role in shaping our social, cultural and economic landscape, contributing to every sector, from arts and education to business, civic leadership and beyond; and
WHEREAS, Sedgwick County takes pride in its strong partnership with the Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, which tirelessly advocates for the economic advancement and empowerment of Hispanic businesses, fostering a more inclusive and prosperous community for all; and
WHEREAS, Hispanic Heritage is an integral part of our community’s identity, enhancing it with unique traditions, culinary delights, artistic expressions and cultural celebrations, thereby enriching the lives of all residents.

“HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH”
