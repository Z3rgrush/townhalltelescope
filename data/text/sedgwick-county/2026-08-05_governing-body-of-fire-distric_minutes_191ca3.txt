B.
REPORT OF THE BOARD OF BIDS AND CONTRACTS’ REGULAR MEETING ON JULY 30, 2026.
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Good morning, Joe Thomas.”
Mr. Joseph Thomas
, Purchasing Director, greeted the Commissioners and said, “The Board of Bids and Contracts that was sitting for Fire District One. We have one item for you this morning.”
VISUAL PRESENTATION
VARIOUS FIRE APPARATUSES -- SEDGWICK COUNTY FIRE DISTRICT 1
“The recommendation is to utilize the HGACBuy Contract, which is Number FS12-23A, and that’s with Conrad Fire Equipment. It’s in the amount of $3,325,950.60. I’ll be happy to try to answer any questions you may have and recommend approval.”
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Okay. Commissioners, do you have any question about the item?”
Commissioner Howell
said, “Just one question here.”
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Commissioner Howell.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “Thank you, Chairman. Just was wondering, what’s the delivery time on these trucks?”
Mr. Thomas
said, “I believe it’s 20…”
Commissioner Howell
said, “Do we have an estimate?”
Mr. Thomas
said, “Yes, Chief Williams will.”
Mr. Douglas Williams
, Fire Chief, greeted the Commissioners and said, “The estimated delivery time on the two pumpers is 49 months, and the delivery time on the tender is 17.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “So I’m just curious: will these be 2027 models or 2030 models?”
Chief Williams
said, “They’ll be 2030 or 2031.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “Interesting. All right. So again, I’m just, again, I don’t know how long it’s been going on like this, but to have to order something for four years from now takes a lot of planning and a lot of. I’ll just say this, that you don’t get these, there’s no lot with these for sale. They build them for our specs, right?”
Chief Williams
said, “Yes.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “These are expensive, and they are a 49-month delivery. That is crazy. Thank you for clarifying that. That’s all I want to say.”
Chief Williams
said, “Yeah, and they’re actually, we had hoped that that was going to come down. We had heard stories that it might come down, but it actually got worse. So these are technically on the replacement schedule in ‘29. So they’re actually going to be at least a year behind schedule anyway.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “I guess I have one more question. These are so expensive. Are these cash-funded or are they? How are they, what’s the funding behind the scenes?”
Chief Williams
said, “As a general rule, to maintain the constant budget hit every single year, they generally finance these on a seven-year basis lease purchase.”
Commissioner Howell
said, “All right, thank you so much.”
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Okay, any other questions? All right. Thank you, Chief.”
