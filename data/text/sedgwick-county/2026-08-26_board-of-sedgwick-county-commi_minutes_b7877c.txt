What Revenue Neutral Rate means is that the amount of ad valorem property taxes that a taxing subdivision receives would need to be the same or less to be Revenue Neutral Rate than the prior budget year, and so it does not allow taxing subdivisions to, you’re not Revenue Neutral Rate if you even get $1 more than you got the prior year. Does not allow for exceptions for new properties that may have been constructed. Does not allow for exceptions for property improvements or anything along those. Just if there’s $1 more received in your budget than the prior year, you’re not Revenue Neutral Rate. Under those Revenue Neutral Rate statutes, taxing subdivisions like the county and the Fire District must indicate their potential intent to exceed Revenue Neutral Rate on or before July 20th of each year. The Board of County Commissioners (BoCC) did this on July 15.”
