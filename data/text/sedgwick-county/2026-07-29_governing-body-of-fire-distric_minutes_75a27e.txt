BOCC Regular Meeting
MEETING OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF FIRE DISTRICT NO. 1
SPECIAL MEETING
July 29, 2026
The Special Meeting of the Board of the County Commissioners of Sedgwick County, Kansas, was called to order at 6:19 p.m. on July 29, 2026 in the County Commission Meeting Room in the Lower Level, Ruffin Building, 100 N. Broadway, in Wichita, Kansas, by Chairman Jeff Blubaugh, with the following present: Chair Pro-Tem Commissioner Stephanie Wise; Commissioner Peter F. Meitzner; Commissioner Ryan Baty; Commissioner James M. Howell; Mr. Russell Leeds, Assistant County Manager; Mr. Justin Waggoner, County Counselor; Ms. Lucy Axmann, Deputy County Clerk.
ROLL CALL
The Clerk reported, after calling roll, that all Commissioners were present.
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Next item.”
NEW BUSINESS
A.
PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING THE 2027 FIRE DISTRICT 1 BUDGET.
Mr. Russell Leeds,
Assistant County Manager, greeted the Commissioners and said, “Very good.”
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Thank you, Rusty.”
Mr. Leeds
said, “We will repeat the same process. This evening is the first public hearing on the 2027 recommended budget for Fire District Number One. It totals $33,499,131. This budget will be supported with a property tax levy of $25,438,662, which will require a mill levy of approximately 16.754 mills based on the estimated assessed valuation and subject to technical adjustments. I recommend that you open the public hearing, receive comments, and close the public hearing once it is over.”
Chairman Blubaugh
said, “Okay, we do not have. Commissioners, do you have any questions? Okay. At this time, I do not have anyone signed up to speak on the Fire Agenda. Would anyone like to come to the podium and speak? If so, you have five minutes. Okay, bring it back to the bench. Commissioners, do you have any questions or comments? Okay.”
MOTION
Chairman Blubaugh moved to take the recommended action to open the public hearing, receive public comments, and close the public hearing.
Commissioner Wise seconded the motion.
There was no discussion on the motion, the vote was called.
