Meitzner; Commissioner Ryan Baty; Commissioner James M. We’ve sold out of our VIP (Very Important Person) tickets, so that is very exciting news, and we’re right on track with our regular ticket sales that we’re getting. So this is a big deal for the zoo. This is a good percentage of our annual operating budget, so roughly a $1,000,000 gets raised for the zoo in just that one night. So we’re excited. It’s a big night, and we hope to see everybody there.”

VISUAL PRESENTATION
Ms. Gibbs
continued, “The office will operate on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and then have a lunch break and will open again from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. Appointments are now available for scheduling if you want to go online. In addition to the Sedgwick County Tag Office at Douglas, Sedgwick County residents can complete their tag business at multiple satellite locations. For those addresses and more Tag Office information, visit our website, sedgwickcounty.org. Have you been negatively impacted by the ongoing drought? If so, you may be eligible for drought disaster loans from the Small Business Administration. This working capital loan helps eligible small businesses, agricultural cooperatives, aquaculture businesses, and private non-profit organizations pay ordinary and necessary expenses they cannot cover because of the disaster. Applications are open now and available until March 10th of 2027. You can also find this information and how to apply online at our website.”
Ms. Gibbs
continued, “Reminder that Sedgwick County Fire District One, its member cities, and multiple cities around the county have implemented an indefinite burn ban. This is necessary due to the dry conditions, high temperatures, strong winds, and potential dangers to area fire services should a grass fire occur. No outdoor burning is allowed, and all active burn permits are suspended until further notice. Residents are encouraged to contact their respective fire departments with questions about their burning status. The Sedgwick County Election Office is actively looking for Election Workers for the upcoming 2026 General Election on November 3rd. Election Workers are essential to maintain the integrity of our elections. For more information and to apply, visit sedgwickcounty.org/elections. That’s all I have for today, and I’ll stand for any questions.”
