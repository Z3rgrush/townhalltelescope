Making public comments during the beginning of the meeting or for a specific agenda item can be made in person, virtually or in writing. The 
option to speak virtually is available with preregistration. Learn more and access the link to submit public comment in writing or in person at 
jocogov.org/BOCCmeetings.
A live broadcast is available at boccmeetings.jocogov.org and on Youtube. The PUBLIC COMMENT period provides residents a forum to 
address the Board of County Commissioners on matters that pertain to or involve the Johnson County Government, but which do not appear on 
the printed agenda. Speakers are encouraged to be professional, concise, courteous, and respectful. Comments should be addressed to the Board 
of County Commissioners as a whole. Speakers are encouraged to not make personal attacks. Speakers should refrain from obscene, profane, 
slanderous, insulting, or threatening remarks. Speakers should avoid repeating prior speakers. Speakers who agree with or wish to make the same 
comments as a previous speaker should simply state their agreement with the comments of that previous speaker. Due to the use of county 
equipment, funds, and supplies, political campaigning and commercial advertisements are not allowed. Violations are subject to audio streaming 
interruptions. 
Public comments at the Board of County Commissioners meeting can be given either in-person or virtually. Virtual speakers must pre-register via 
the form before noon on Wednesday before the Thursday meeting. In-person speakers have the option of using the form or signing up in-person

may limit the number of speakers for general public comments or for a specific agenda item, if the Chairman so choses.

Welcome to this meeting of the Johnson County Board of Commissioners.
The Board of County Commissioners encourages citizen participation in local governance processes. To that end, and in compliance 
with the Kansas Open Meetings Act (KSA 75-4317), you are invited to participate in today’s meeting. The following rules have been 
established to facilitate the transaction of business during the meeting; please take a moment to review these rules before the meeting 
begins.
▪TURN ALL CELLULAR TELEPHONES, PAGERS, BEEPERS, AND OTHER NOISE-MAKING DEVICES OFF OR TO “SILENT MODE” BEFORE 
THE MEETING BEGINS.
▪Always speak from the podium, using the microphone. This ensures that all remarks are accurately and properly recorded. Only 
one speaker should be at the podium at a time. Speakers are requested to state their full name, address, and group affiliation, if any, 
before delivering any remarks. Speakers should restate their name each time subsequent remarks are made.
▪Speakers should limit their comments so as not to exceed two minutes. In the event of extensive public participation, the Chairman 
may further limit speaking times to assure as many people as possible have an opportunity to address the Board of County 
Commissioners.
▪If you have documentation or other materials to present to the Board of County Commissioners, please provide them to the Clerk 
for distribution and filing with the official record of the meeting. If possible, a total of fifteen (15) copies of any document should be 
provided. 
▪The PUBLIC COMMENT period provides citizens a forum to address the Board of County Commissioners on matters that pertain to or 
involve the Johnson County Government, but which do not appear on the printed agenda.
Speakers are encouraged to be professional, concise, courteous, and respectful.  Comments should be addressed to the 
Board of County Commissioners as a whole. Speakers are encouraged to not make personal attacks.  Speakers should 
refrain from obscene, profane, slanderous, insulting or threatening remarks. Speakers should avoid repeating prior 
speakers.  Speakers who agree with or wish to make the same comments as a previous speaker should simply state their 
agreement with the comments of that previous speaker. Due to the use of County equipment, funds, and supplies, political 
campaigning and commercial advertisements are not allowed.
▪The CONSENT AGENDA is a single agenda item that can include multiple items previously reviewed and determined to be of a 
routine business nature. Items that are included as part of the CONSENT AGENDA will be voted upon under a single motion, unless 
separate discussion on any single item is requested by a Member of the Board of County Commissioners or a member of the public. 
If separate discussion on any item is requested, it will be set aside for further consideration as part of the ACTION AGENDA.
Agendas can be accessed via the Internet at: http://boccmeetings.jocogov.org, or by contacting the Clerk of the Board of County 
Commissioners at (913) 715-0430. An annotated agenda detailing the results of all votes taken during each regular meeting is posted to 
the Internet after adjournment.
