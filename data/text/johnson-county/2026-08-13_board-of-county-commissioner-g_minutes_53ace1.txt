8. (Added Agenda Item approved by Vice-Chair at the 8/6/2026 BOCC Meeting) 
Consideration of the Fifth District Commissioner’s request to consider adopting 
Resolution No. 115-26, finding that the City of De Soto’s proposed TIF redevelopment 
district (De Soto Storage, LLC / assisted living and memory care project) will have an 
adverse effect on Johnson County and disapproving its establishment, pursuant to K.S.A. 
12-1771(d); or, in the alternative, decline to exercise the Board’s disapproval authority 
under that statute. This item appears on the agenda to preserve the Board’s statutory 
window to act, which closes on or about August 15, 2026 (30 days after the City’s July 
16, 2026, public hearing).
Chairman Kelly recused himself.
Commissioner Allenbrand moved to decline to exercise the Board’s disapproval authority under 
K.S.A. 12-1771(d) as to the City of De Soto’s proposed TIF redevelopment district, and direct 
staff to continue negotiating a cost-recovery agreement with the City and Midwest Health. There 
was no second, therefore the motion. Lacking a proper second, the Vice-Chair declared the motion 
Lost.
 Failed: 
Commissioner Meyers moved to decline to adopt the Resolution and direct staff to continue 
negotiating an interlocal or cost-recovery agreement with the City and Developer to offset 
MED-ACT’s projected costs, allowing the Project to proceed under the TIF district as proposed.           
Commissioner Brewer seconded the motion.
 Failed: 2-4-0
Commissioner Ashcraft moved to Adopt Resolution No. 115-26, finding that the City of De 
Soto’s proposed TIF redevelopment district will have an adverse effect on Johnson County and 
disapproving its establishment, pursuant to K.S.A. 12-1771(d). Lacking a proper second, the Vice-
Chair declared the motion Lost.
 Failed:
Chairman Kelly moved that the board direct staff to work with us and develop a policy on 
incentive guidance, both those under K.S.A. 12-177 1D as well as incentive requests for projects 
in the unincorporated and that it be considered at a Committee of the Whole before the end of 
this calendar year. Commissioner Allenbrand seconded the motion.
 Passed: 7-0-0
