2 Resolution No. 2026-2. Sitting as the Governing Body of Johnson County Consolidated Fire 
District No. 1, hold a public hearing on the proposed budget and to exceed the revenue neutral 
rate and consider adopting Resolution No. 2026-2, authorizing a levy of property taxes in 
support of the budget for Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 for the Fiscal Year 
(FY) 2027 budget year that requires a property tax rate exceeding the revenue neutral rates of 
12.374 mills for the Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 General Fund, 1.088 mills 
for FD1 Debt Service 2019A, and 0.688 mills for FD2 Debt Service 2021A (I). The RNR is not 
requested to be exceeded for the following two debt service funds: 1.072 mills RNR for FD1 
Debt Service 2019B and 0.484 mills RNR for FD2 Debt Service 2015A (H).
Commissioner Ashcraft moved to, sitting as the Governing Body of Johnson County 
Consolidated Fire District No. 1, approve and adopt Resolution No. 2026-2 authorizing a levy 
of property taxes in support of the budget for Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 
for the Fiscal Year 2027 budget year that requires a property tax rate exceeding the revenue 
neutral rates of 12.374 mills for the Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 General 
Fund, 1.088 mills for FD1 Debt Service 2019A, and 0.688 mills for FD2 Debt Service 2021A 
(I), and that does not exceed the revenue neutral rate of 1.072 mills for FD1 Debt Service 
2019B and 0.484 mills for FD2 Debt Service 2015A (H). Commissioner Brewer seconded the 
motion.
 Passed: 7-0-0
The Chair called for anyone wanting to address the Commission on the budget. The Chair called for further 
comments and, hearing none, closed the public hearing at 1:41 p.m.
3 Resolution No. 2026-3. Sitting as the Governing Body of Johnson County Consolidated Fire 
District No. 1., Consider approving and adopting Resolution No. 2026-3 to approve the Fiscal 
Year (FY) 2027 budget for Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 as proposed.
Commissioner Brewer moved to, sitting as the Governing Body of Johnson County 
Consolidated Fire District No. 1., approve and adopt Resolution No. 2026-3 to approve the 
Fiscal Year 2027 budget for Johnson County Consolidated Fire District No. 1 as proposed. 
Commissioner Allenbrand seconded the motion.
 Passed: 7-0-0
