RESOLUTION 2026-16: Implement Fees for Sewer Pretreatment Program 
Special Projects Coordinator Dennis Lytle presented Resolution 2026-16, which establishes a 
formal, enforceable fee structure for the Sewer Pretreatment Compliance Program. 
 
The Board of Trustees discussed: 
• $355 per month for the “do nothing” option could be far less than the capital investment 
of installing an interceptor — which could potentially disincentivize compliance 
•  Restaurant tenants who do not own their building could be a conflict with the 
landlord/property owner. 
• Enforcement consists of Non-payment of surcharges resulting in a lien placed on the 
property.  
• The fee schedule is not permanent — if too many businesses opt for Option 1, the 
surcharge rate can be raised in a future Resolution.
