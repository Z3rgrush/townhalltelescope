Will Colon, owner of Colon Orchards, appeared today to draw attention to the alarming growth of the 
homeless population. He’s used to some homeless people being present, but his rights as a property owner

and county jurisdiction begins and ends. Miscommunications arise when one agency issues a trespassing

assist, because he is not willing to let people trespass or threaten him with assault.  
 
Commissioner Bell agreed that inaction explained as “Well, it's coming from Denver” is unacceptable 
and has not heard this statement herself. Fremont County does not accept damage to or trespassing on 
personal property and agrees to investigate such incidents.

1. Proclamation naming August 19, 2026, as Richard Baker Day in Fremont County 
 
Commissioner Bell read the proclamation, noting that Mr. Baker's first flight was on October 31st, 
1968. He has played many roles throughout his career as a pilot, including working for various local 
companies, serving as an airport manager, and serving as a technical expert and consultant. He received 
the Wright Brothers Master Pilot Award, the highest honor for certified pilots, for achieving 50 years of 
safe flight operations. He also received the Charles Taylor Master Mechanic Award for 50 years of 
exemplary service, technical proficiency, and dedication to the field. Only 38 people in Colorado have 
received both awards. The proclamation can be viewed at the bottom of these minutes. 
 
To review the proclamation in its entirety, please visit fremontcountyco.gov under the Commissioner 
agendas and minutes tab.  
 
Wes Brandt, the Fremont County Airport Manager, approached the podium to say he has known Mr. 
Baker for 22 years; he hired him at the airport, and they worked together for 15 years. Mr. Baker taught 
Mr. Brandt a great deal, and he wanted to publicly thank him because he believed he would not be here 
today if it weren’t for Mr. Baker.  
 
Commissioner McFall moved to accept the proclamation recognizing Richard Baker for lifetime 
achievement in aviation and designate August 19th, 2026, as Richard Baker Day in Fremont County, 
Colorado. Commissioner Grantham seconded the motion. Upon Vote: Commissioner McFall, aye; 
Commissioner Grantham, aye; Commissioner Bell, aye. The motion carried by unanimous consent.

2. Proclamation naming August 11, 2026, as Bruce McCandless Veterans Community 
Living Center at Florence Day in Fremont County 
Representative: Rick Gutierrez, Director, Fremont County Department of Public Health and 
Environment 
 
Public Health Director Gutierrez explained that before firefighters gained control of the north side of 
the Aspen Acres Fire, the Bruce McCandless Veterans Community Living Center was in a very 
vulnerable position. Although the center was outside the evacuation zones, the staff still took time to 
plan, recognizing how quickly conditions can change. He said that, as someone with a background in 
emergency preparedness, he understood the magnitude of the coordination required. The staff has been 
working together to plan and practice for a potential evacuation ever since the 24 Fire. Public Health 
Director Gutierrez then recited the proclamation, which can be found in its entirety at the bottom of 
these minutes. 
 
 
Barbara Moore, Administrator for Bruce McCandless Veteran Community Living Center, wanted to 
comment that it was very scary to have a fire so close, but she was very grateful for the tremendous help 
they received from the Colorado Department of Human Services. She described how all their sister 
facilities coordinated and received assistance through their transportation partnerships to get everyone to 
their temporary locations.  
 
Commissioner Grantham moved to approve the proclamation, proclaiming August 11th of 2026 as 
Bruce McCandless Veterans Community Living Center at Florence Day here in Fremont County. 
Commissioner McFall seconded the motion. Upon Vote: Commissioner Grantham, aye; Commissioner 
McFall, aye; Commissioner Bell, aye. The motion carried by unanimous consent.

3. MS 26-001 Free Eleven Minor Subdivision 
Requesting approval for a minor subdivision that will create a total of two (2) lots. Located at 
1115 Fremont Street, in Penrose. 
 
 Planning and Zoning Director Victoria stated that the planning commission held a hearing on this 
minor subdivision on July 7th. The applicant, Travis Jenkins, is requesting approval of a two-lot minor 
subdivision at 115 Fremont Street in Penrose. Both lots exceed the one-half-acre minimum. However, 
the depth is barely more than twice the lot length, and the applicant requested a deferral of the drainage 
plan until the time of development. The Fremont County Building Department reported that there are no 
violations on the property and that septic permits have been issued. Colorado DOT stated that the 
applicant must obtain an access permit from Fremont Street. Director Victoria said the planning and 
zoning department recommends approval, with the following: approval of waiver requests 1 and 2. 
Number one is the drainage plan and report. Waiver two is Appendix One, Section B, number 10: the 
length-versus-width requirement. As a contingency item, the applicant must have the surveyor correct a 
few red lines on the plat and add a note stating that the drainage plans for lots one and two will be 
deferred until the time of development. 
 
Commissioner Bell noted that the commissioners have reviewed the entire 71-page packet, which is 
straightforward. 
 
Commissioner Grnatham said that the depth is only two feet out of compliance. The rule is intended to 
prevent people from splitting lots into 50-foot-wide by a-mile-deep parcels.  
 
Commissioner McFall moved to approve minor subdivision 26-001, Free Eleven Minor Subdivision, 
with the following waivers approved. Waiver number one is a drainage plan and report. Waiver number 
two is Appendix One, Section B, number 10, which is a waiver for depth. A registered surveyor must 
correct the additional red line, and a surveyor must note on the plat that the drainage plan for lots one 
and two may differ until the time of development. One condition: the applicant must also apply for and 
receive an access permit from CDOT at Fremont Street. Commissioner Grantham seconded the motion. 
Upon vote: Commissioner McFall, aye; Commissioner Grantham, aye; Commissioner Bell, aye. The 
motion carried by unanimous consent.
