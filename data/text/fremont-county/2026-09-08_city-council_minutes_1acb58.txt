3. Requesting the issuance of a Hotel & Restaurant Liquor License for 
CC & J Enterprises LLC dba Chelsey's at 
103 Broadway Unit 11, Penrose, CO 81240. 
Representative: James Hawks 
 
Mr. Hawks said he wants to expand the Chelsey’s brand, and this seemed like the logical next step; they 
intend to serve the same hamburgers and wings. 
 
BOCC Clerk Sammy Ryan began the staff report by saying Mr. Hawks submitted the new liquor 
license as a hotel and restaurant on July 22nd, 2026. He submitted all supporting documents, including 
the background check, financial paperwork, and proof of legal possession of the premises. The location 
had a current liquor license, but the landlord and Mr. Hawks provided documents showing the property 
was abandoned, which voided the previous license and made this application a new license rather than a 
simple transfer. The Sheriff and Public Health reports were sent out on July 22nd, returning within 10 
days, and both departments recommended approval. The file was then given to County Attorney Bellas 
on August 11th; after his thorough review, Attorney Bellas recommended approval. The applicant is then 
required to publish notification of the new license in the Daily Record at least 10 days before the 
meeting today. Proof was on August 26th, along with proof of the poster at the establishment to notify 
the public. After completing the application process, the Clerk’s office recommends approval.  
 
Commissioner McFall moved to approve the issuance of a Hotel & Restaurant Liquor License for CC & 
J Enterprises LLC dba Chelsey’s at 103 Broadway, Unit 11 in Penrose. Commissioner Bell seconded the 
motion. Upon Vote: Commissioner McFall, aye; Commissioner Bell, aye . The motion carried by 
unanimous consent. 
 
 
Ref. Fremont County BOCC Meeting 34:49 
 
4. Amendment #1 CUP 11-001 Cañon Dolomite Quarry 
Requesting to amend a condition of Conditional Use Permit (CUP) 11-001 Cañon Dolomite 
Quarry, Resolution 12, Series of 2012, Section K, Recording #894566. Amrize is requesting that 
the current truck traffic condition (Section K) be amended to include the “Annual Average Daily 
Traffic” verbiage as stated in the original CDOT approval. 
Owner: Amrize West Central, Inc (f/k/a Holcim -WCR, Inc,) Location: 50814 US Highway 50, 
Canon City, CO 81212 Representative: Kurt Thurmann, Land Manager, Amrize  
 
Mr. Thurmann said the amendment to CUP 11 -001 requests an adjustment to Section K language for 
truck traffic to include the words average annual daily trips. He explained the language change reflects a 
recent surge in sales at this quarry. The quarry operates only a day or two each  month. They are not 
proposing to operate more days, but to accommodate random surges during those days of operation so 
they can exceed the allotted 48 trips or 24 trucks a day.  
 
Commissioner Bell asked Mr. Thurmann whether they anticipate any changes in the foreseeable future 
to operating only one to two days per month. 
 
Mr. Thurmann answered no; it really depends on customer demand. Sometimes in December or January 
they do not open at all. 
 
Commissioner McFall requested clarity on why they can't simply operate more days instead of operating 
just one or two days and requesting more trips in a day. 
 
John Themig, Area operations manager for Southern Colorado, said the language limits them to 48 
trips per day, whereas the state permit says an average of 48. Since they are only open for a couple of days 
a month, they would like to increase trips on those days so they do not exceed the permit limit. 
 
Commissioner McFall agreed that what he said is factually true, but it opens the possibility that Amrize 
could not operate for two months, hit a sales surge, and then combine those averages to make 1,400 trucks 
in a day. That is where the concern lies with amending the verbiage to average, regardless of what they 
agree to today; that could happen in the future, and the commissioners are not willing to take that risk.  
 
Commissioner Bell asked if they had that many truck trips to do in a day; the question would then change 
to why Amrize can't open operations four days a week. 
 
Mr. Themig answered that even though they are doing sales, they are not exactly operating by crushing 
rock; on those days, they pull other workers to make these trips . Ultimately, when they are selling rock, 
those workers cannot perform their normal positions. The most they expect to need in a day is 50 trucks 
or 100 trips. 
 
Planning and Zoning Director Victoria said Amrize is requesting the current truck traffic condition 
section K be amended to include annual average daily traffic verbiage as stated in the original  CDOT 
approval. Resolution No. 12 series of 2012  states in Section K that haul truck trips shall not exceed 48 
 
trips per day and 10 employee trips. A trip is considered a single or one-direction vehicle movement with 
either the origin or destination ex iting or entering inside the subject property . Colorado state highway 
access permit number 211052 states that annual average daily traffic volumes associated with the gravel 
quarry use shall not exceed 10 employee trips and 48 trips for hauling operations. These figures do not 
include trips generated by the single -family residence. This amendment to the existing conditions will 
provide Amrize more operational flexibility as market conditions continue to change and the material from 
the Canon Dolomite Quarry becomes more desirable  across Fremont County. Currently, the pit operates 
from 7:00 am to 7:00 pm,  Monday through Friday. Amrize submitted all required materials, and the 
Planning and Zoning staff thoroughly reviewed the application and recommended approval. 
 
Clerk Grantham asked whether the employees' trip could remain the same, since the applicants stated 
they have extra employees on those days. 
 
Mr. Themig answered no; the Canon City Quarry is directly South and connected by one of their private 
roads, so they do not need to use highway access. 
 
Commissioner Bell asked County Attorney Bellas whether they must give a yes-or-no answer or whether 
the board is authorized to change the number. 
 
County Attorney Bellas answered yes; theythe Commissioners can approve the change with a new 
condition when changing conditions on a CUP.  
 
Commissioner Bell proposed changing the verbiage to double the allowed number of trips instead of 
adding the word average. 
 
Mr. Themig said they would accept the 96 trips a day because it would help , and considering 
Commissioner McFall’s point, if more is needed,  they can open and operate another day.  He said he 
understands the concern with adding average; it just did not cross his mind before that as a possible 
interpretation. 
 
Commissioner McFall moved to approve the amendment for CUP 11 -001 for Canon Dolomite Quarry 
to change the words from 48 trips per day to 96, which is 48 truckloads per day,  and it still includes the 
original 10 trips for employees ., and excludes traffic generated by the nearby single -family residence. 
Commissioner Bell seconded the motion. Upon Vote: Commissioner McFall, aye; Commissioner Bell, 
aye. The motion carried by unanimous consent.
