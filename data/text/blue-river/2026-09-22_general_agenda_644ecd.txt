BLUE RIVER: MAXIMUM RENTAL-DAY POLICY
Single-license recommendation using 41% average unit occupancy (~150 nights/year) and 25% STR housing share
RECOMMENDATION
120–150 nights / year Recommended maximum range for every STR license — one license type, with no unlimited category
30–37.5 townwide nights At 25% of housing stock, the range represents 30-37.5 potential STR-occupied nights per total housing unit per year. 
WHY THIS FITS BLUE RIVER
Current occupancy implies about 150 rented nights per STR annually. A 150-night cap primarily constrains the highest-frequency operators, while a 120-night cap 
would reduce average potential activity by about 20%.
Because licensed STRs represent approximately 25% of housing stock, frequent rental activity can affect community character even when average unit occupancy is 
approximately 41%.
A universal 120–150-night maximum is easier to explain, administer, audit, and enforce than multiple ownership-based license tiers.

Recommendation basis: one license type; current STR occupancy of 41% implies approximately 150 occupied nights per licensed unit annually; STRs represent 25% of Town housing. A 150-night 
maximum targets frequent renters with minimal effect on the average operator, while 120 nights would produce an estimated 20% reduction from current average activity.
Neumo is unable to verify compliance based on the maximum number of bookings in a year. The number of individual nights rented may serve as an effective proxy; 
reconcile those nights to quarterly lodging-tax filings and self-certification forms and require booking records upon request.
As the renewal window approches, compare occupied nights, complaints, violations, and license count. Adjust the maximum number of rental nights based on the 
impacts of licensed STRs on residential character.
Count each occupied rental night, not bookings or advertised availability. A multi-night stay consumes one night for each night occupied.
Apply the same selected maximum within the 120–150-night range to every licensed STR; do not create an unlimited or ownership-based tier.
Apply the maximum at the next renewal period, with no permanent grandfathered exemption.

Town of Blue River 
Code Compliance Administrator 
Department: Administration 
Reports To: Town Manager 
FLSA Status: Non-Exempt 
Supervisory Responsibilities: None 
Position Type: Full-Time 
Work Location: Town of Blue River, Colorado 
Position Summary and General Description of Duties 
The Code Compliance Administrator reports directly to the Town Manager and serves as 
the Town's primary license and violation compliance, inspection, and enforcement official. 
The position administers and enforces the Municipal Code, Land Use Code, Short-Term 
Rental Regulations, and other applicable Town ordinances and regulations. 
The position administers the nuisance abatement and short-term rental licensing and 
compliance programs, documents violations, coordinates corrective actions, maintains 
enforcement records, and supports administrative hearings and Municipal Court 
proceedings. 
The Code Compliance Administrator works closely with residents, property owners, 
contractors, management companies, the Police Department, Deputy Clerk, Neumo, legal 
counsel, and other governmental agencies. The position requires sound judgment, 
professional communication, strong documentation practices, and consistent application 
of Town regulations. 
 
Essential Duties and Responsibilities 
Code Compliance and Enforcement 
• Administer and enforce provisions of the Municipal Code, Land Use Code, Short-
Term Rental Regulations, permit conditions, and other applicable Town regulations. 

• Receive, document, investigate, and resolve complaints regarding code violations 
and regulatory noncompliance. 
• Conduct ﬁeld investigations to determine compliance with Town regulations. 
• Coordinate follow-up on Police Department referrals, including reported code 
violations, incident reports, corrective-action tracking, and subsequent action. 
• Issue warnings, notices of violation, correction notices, administrative citations, 
and penalty assessments as authorized by Town regulations. 
• Establish compliance deadlines, monitor corrective action requirements, and 
conduct follow-up inspections as needed. 
• Identify repeat offenders and recommend appropriate further enforcement actions. 
• Coordinate enforcement efforts with Town legal counsel, Municipal Court 
personnel, consultants, and other agencies. 
• Conduct community-wide compliance inspections and proactive enforcement 
initiatives as directed by the Town Manager. 
Short-Term Rental Compliance Administration 
• Coordinate administration and enforcement of the Town's Short-Term Rental 
Program, including licensing, compliance monitoring, and enforcement activities. 
 
• Assist applicants, owners, managers, and members of the public with identifying, 
understanding, and complying with applicable short-term rental code provisions. 
 
• Receive, investigate, research, and resolve STR-related questions, violations, and 
compliance issues involving licensing, advertisements, bookings, operations, 
occupancy, parking, noise, trash, and local emergency contact requirements. 
 
• Review short-term rental applications, renewals, responsible-agent information, 
attestations, and documentation for compliance with Town requirements. 
• Conduct required biennial inspections of licensed short-term rental properties to 
verify compliance with operational, life-safety, parking, and permit requirements. 
• Review compliance reports and monitoring information generated by software 
platforms, and third-party compliance vendors, including Neumo, and use this 
information for licensing, enforcement, auditing, and compliance follow-up. 
 
• Coordinate with the Deputy Clerk, Town administration, and Neumo on STR tax and 
license administration, lodging tax remittance, payment records, booking and 
occupancy records, compliance data, and missing or overdue materials. 
 
• Review Neumo discovery, recovery, and compliance audit information related to 
unlicensed, delinquent, or noncompliant STR activity; coordinate follow-up on prior-
period revenues, penalties, interest, late charges, audit overages, and other 
identiﬁed revenue or compliance issues. 
 
• Monitor online booking platforms and STR listings for evidence of unlicensed 
activity, noncompliance, or inaccurate license information. 
• Assess and administer warnings, penalties, suspensions, and other enforcement 
actions in accordance with adopted Town regulations and penalty schedules. 
• Coordinate public-facing short-term rental compliance communications, including 
guidance on licensing obligations, avoidance of violations, corrective actions, 
complaint procedures, and responsible rental operations. 
 
Land Use Compliance and Permit Administration 
• Monitor approved developments, permits, and land use approvals for compliance 
with Town regulations and conditions of approval imposed by the Planning & Zoning 
Commission, Board of Trustees, and Town staff. 
• Investigate and document violations of permit conditions and land use 
requirements. 
• Assist with administrative reviews of Type B and Type C permit applications and 
other planning-related reviews as assigned. 
 
Case Management and Record Administration 
• Develop, maintain, and administer comprehensive enforcement case ﬁles and 
compliance records. 
• Track inspections, complaints, warnings, citations, penalties, corrective actions, 
compliance deadlines, permit violations, and enforcement outcomes. 
• Maintain evidence and documentation necessary to support enforcement 
proceedings and legal actions. 
• Utilize compliance software, databases, and reporting systems to administer 
enforcement activities and regulatory programs. 
• Prepare periodic reports regarding compliance trends, enforcement actions, and 
violation statistics for the Town Manager and Board of Trustees. 
• Monitor enforcement metrics and recommend process improvements and policy 
modiﬁcations. 
 
Public Education and Customer Service 
• Serve as the Town's primary point of contact for questions, complaints, and 
concerns regarding code compliance, regulatory issues, and licensing matters. 
• Work collaboratively with community members to achieve voluntary compliance 
whenever practical while maintaining consistent enforcement standards. 
• Develop educational materials, notices, guidance documents, and outreach 
resources related to compliance programs. 
Hearings and Legal Support 
• Prepare reports, affidavits, exhibits, evidence packages, and enforcement 
documentation for administrative hearings and Municipal Court proceedings. 
• Coordinate enforcement matters with the Town Attorney, Municipal Prosecutor, 
consultants, and applicable agencies. 
• Testify before Municipal Court, administrative hearing officers, the Board of 
Trustees, the Planning Commission, and other regulatory bodies as required. 
 
Program Development and Special Projects 
• Assist the Town Manager with development, implementation, and administration of 
compliance programs, enforcement procedures, and regulatory initiatives. 
• Participate in review of proposed ordinance amendments and provide 
recommendations regarding enforceability and compliance impacts. 
• Develop policies, procedures, forms, and best practices to improve regulatory 
compliance and enforcement efficiency. 
• Complete monthly Town Park inspections and provide periodic weed and trash 
clean up of Town property as necessary. 
 
Other Duties 
• Performs other related duties as assigned by the Town Manager. 
 
Delegated Authority 
Acting under authority delegated by the Town Manager and applicable Town regulations, 
the Code Compliance Administrator may: 
• Conduct inspections, investigations, re-inspections, and compliance reviews as 
authorized by law. 
 
• Issue warnings, notices of violation, correction orders, administrative citations, and 
penalty assessments. 
 
• Establish compliance deadlines and require corrective actions. 
 
• Recommend permit suspensions, license suspensions, permit revocations, permit 
denials, and other enforcement actions authorized by Town regulations. 
 
• Coordinate directly with Town departments, law enforcement personnel, 
contractors, consultants, and legal counsel regarding compliance matters. 
 
• Represent the Town in Municipal Court proceedings, administrative hearings, and 
enforcement actions. 
 
Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities 
• Ability to read, interpret, explain, and apply municipal codes, ordinances, permit 
conditions, license requirements, written procedures, and regulatory requirements. 
 
• Ability to conduct ﬁeld inspections, document observations, take photographs, 
prepare written reports, maintain case ﬁles, and track follow-up requirements. 
 
• Ability to prepare reports, maintain legal records, organize evidence, and present 
information in administrative hearings, Municipal Court proceedings, or other 
formal settings as needed. 
 
• Strong customer service, conﬂict resolution, de-escalation, and public 
communication skills, including the ability to communicate professionally. 
 
• Ability to handle complaints, conﬂict, and difficult public interactions with sound 
judgment, professionalism, and discretion. 
 
• Basic proﬁciency with computers, email, document preparation, spreadsheets, 
records systems, permitting or compliance software, and related office technology. 
 
Minimum Qualiﬁcations 
• High school diploma or GED required. 
 
• Minimum of one year of relevant experience in code enforcement, planning and 
zoning, permitting, inspections, law enforcement, public administration, short-term 
rental administration, records administration, or a closely related ﬁeld. 
 
• Valid Colorado driver's license required or ability to obtain one after hire. 
 
• Ability to successfully complete any Town-required background check. 
 
• Equivalent combination of education, training, and experience may be considered. 
The Town of Blue River recognizes that applicants may not possess direct 
experience in every area of code enforcement, short-term rental compliance, 
inspections, licensing, tax administration, and vendor coordination at the time of 
hire; however, applicants must demonstrate the judgment, communication skills, 
technical aptitude, and ability to learn and apply Town regulations necessary to 
perform the essential functions of the position. 
 
Preferred Qualiﬁcations 
• Associate's or bachelor's degree in public administration, planning, criminal justice, 
construction management, code enforcement, or a related ﬁeld. 
 
• Two or more years of progressively responsible experience in municipal code 
compliance and enforcement, planning and zoning administration, community 
development, law enforcement, short-term rental compliance, or a related ﬁeld. 
 
• Colorado Association of Code Enforcement certiﬁcation or other recognized code 
enforcement training. 
 
• Experience administering short-term rental compliance programs and utilizing 
compliance monitoring software and permitting systems. 
 
• Experience testifying in administrative hearings or Municipal Court proceedings. 
 
Work Environment and Physical Requirements 
• Work is performed in both office and ﬁeld environments, including Town facilities, 
residential areas, public rights-of-way, and short-term rental properties. 
 
• Position requires driving, walking over uneven terrain, standing, taking photographs, 
conducting outdoor inspections, and working in varying weather conditions. 
 
• Position may require occasional evening, weekend, holiday, or after-hours work to 
respond to complaints, short-term rental issues, public meetings, hearings, 
inspections, or enforcement needs.
