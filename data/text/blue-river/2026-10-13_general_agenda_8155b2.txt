Executive Session

amended during the Work Session.

Reading of Ordinance No. 2026-11 
 
1. Reading and Consideration of Ordinance No. 2026-11 An Ordinance of the Board of 
the Trustees of the Town of Blue River, Colorado, Amending the Blue River Municipal 
Code by: 
Conditionally Repealing Currently Effective Article 1 of Chapter 6 Titled Short-Term 
Rental Licensing and Operation on April 30, 2027, Except to the Extent Currently 
Effective Article 1 of Chapter 6 Must Remain Effective for the Purpose of Enforcing 
Against Any Violations and Imposing Penalties for any Violations which Occurred in 
2026 and Occurred During the Period of January 1, 2027, to April 30, 2027 
AND 
Conditionally Adopting a New Article 1 of Chapter 6 to be Titled Short-Term Rentals 
to be Effective on December 15, 2026, for the Purpose of Processing Short-Term 
Rental Applications and Issuing Licenses to be Effective on May 1, 2027 and, 
Following License Issuance, for the Ongoing Regulation of Short-Term Rentals after 
May 1, 2027.
