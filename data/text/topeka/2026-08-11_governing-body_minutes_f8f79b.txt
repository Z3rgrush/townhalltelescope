 DISCUSSION of AN ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez, 
establishing utility rates for 2027 through 2030, amending Topeka Municipal Code Sections 
13.05.20 through 13.05.060 and repealing original sections, was presented.  
 Dr. Robert M. Perez, City Manager, stated when discussions began June 2, 2026, Staff 
provided the Governing Body with two fee structure scenarios. Based upon feedback, the rate 
recommendations that he supports include a higher rate increase on commercial industrial 
customers who currently pay a lower per unit cost than residential customers. Proposed 
adjustments to the base charge will be discussed and Staff intends to continue to receive input from 
this discussion for any final fee schedule adjustments before presenting the ordinance for action on 
August 18, 2026.     
 Sylvia Davis, Director of Utilities, stated they have all heard the concerns raised by the 
community related to water, wastewater and storm water safety and service impacts from potential 
data center developments. She stated the utility rate process discussions started long before the data 
center discussions and the concerns that have been expressed including safety and adequacy of the 
City’s infrastructure – are items the City’s Water Department worries about daily. The focus and 
priority of the Water Department is to protect the health and safety of the community and support 
utility rates that support those efforts. City Staff have been tasked with operating and maintaining 
the community’s water, wastewater, and storm water systems, a total of $6.3 billion worth of 
infrastructure on a 24-hour basis. Current utility rates support a $120 million operating budget and 
approved $40.6 million capital budget. Staff have tried to stress that many of Topeka’s assets are at 
or beyond their useful life and have been plagued by deferred maintenance and historic 
08-11-26 
303 
 
underfunding. They can no longer afford to ignore or downplay the situation and there needs to be 
an increased investment in those assets. As a result from receiving feedback from the community 
and the Governing Body, Staff has focused on the goal to keep residential impacts as low as 
possible while increasing capital investment. The proposal provided to the Governing Body on 
June 16, 2026, reflects an additional $148 million capital investment increase over the next four 
years. To support this effort and address the desire to reduce impacts on residential customers, a 
stairstep approach to increasing rates for commercial and industrial, industrial two, and multifamily 
customers was taken. Additionally, they increased multipliers to wholesale and irrigation customers 
and maintained the current 175% outside city multiplier rate. This does not include any increases to 
base charges on less than 2-inch meters; however, it does increase volume charges, which means 
99.5% of residential customers and 76% of commercial customers will not see a base rate change 
on their monthly bills. While the proposed rate package does not solve all of the existing 
challenges, it does show commitment to the community through improvement of replacement 
levels by bringing over $260 million in improvements over the next four years. Providing Staff the 
ability to address immediate infrastructure needs, support other infrastructure improvements, and 
advance efforts towards ideal rehab and replacement schedules while allowing them to maintain 
daily cash on hand and required debt service coverage. Monthly Residential Average Impacts were 
highlighted (Attachment B). It was noted; there was a trade-off with this approach. Low residential 
impacts come at the cost of the largest users who have historically benefited from a lower 
consumption rate. Potential increases with industrial partners have been discussed, and those 
customers have shared their concerns that those increases will impact operations and the 
importance of understanding steady rate increases moving forward. Staff proposes adopting a 
multi-year rate adjustment that provides stable, predicting, fundable funding for customers and
