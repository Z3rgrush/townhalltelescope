Potential Consideration of an Ordinance Restricting Utility Connections for Data Centers based on the approval to suspend Governing Body Rules and Procedures, Rule of Order 6.3(f)(2) - Wait Period.
POTENTIAL CONSIDERATION
OF THIS ORDINANCE IS BASED ON THE APPROVAL BY THE GOVERNING BODY TO SUSPEND THE GOVERNING BODY RULES AND PROCEDURES, RULE OF ORDER 6.3(f)(2) - WAIT PERIOD.
Suspension of Rules Voting Requirement
: Action requires seven (7) votes of the Governing Body.
AN ORDINANCE introduced by Councilmembers Murray McGee, Christina Valdivia-Alcala and Michelle Bradberry implementing a temporary moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and approval of applications or
utility connections
for data center developments
Ordinance Voting Requirement
: Action requires at least six (6) votes of the Governing Body.
(Approval would amend the language to restrict utility connections both inside and outside of city limits for data centers. This is necessary to prevent administrative exceptions that would allow staff to extend utility services outside of city limits to serve a data center as defined in the ordinance.)

Discussion with Possible Action - Ordinance Adopting 2026 Uniform Public Offense Code
DISCUSSION with POSSIBLE ACTION of an ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez, adopting the 2026 edition of the Uniform Public Offense Code, amending Section 9.05.080 of the Topeka Municipal Code and repealing original section.
Voting Requirement
: Action requires five (5) votes of the City Council. The mayor does not vote. The proposed ordinance involves a matter of home rule on which the mayor has veto authority.
(Approval will adopt the most current version of the UPOC with current statutory changes.)
