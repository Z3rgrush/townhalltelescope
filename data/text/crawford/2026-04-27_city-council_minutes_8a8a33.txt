OLD BUSINESS
Report from the Water Rate Committee
Committee members: Sara Taylor, Gill Saunders, Bruce Bair, Grant Knowles, Chana Clawson, Cindy Jones.
Key points:
Committee has met twice and is gathering financial, operational, and usage data.
Goal: develop a fair, sustainable rate structure that covers operating expenses and builds reserves.
Consultant Rita Trick (RCAC) recommended a $125/month water rate.
Committee is analyzing customer categories, usage patterns, and infrastructure priorities.
Wastewater rates will also be reviewed.
No recommendations yet; committee will return when ready.
