NEW BUSINESS
Variance Request – 604 Dogwood A real estate sale requires the mobile home title to be purged. The buyer installed a stateapproved XI2 tiedown system, inspected and approved. The Town was asked to sign a certificate verifying permanent foundation compliance. Bruce and Grant inspected the tiedowns and confirmed they match the stateapproved installation. However, the home is nonconforming under the Town’s 2020 Mobile Home Code, which requires specific foundation pads, skirting, and landings/porches at all entries. Only one usable exit currently exists. Council discussed the age of the home, the longstanding placement on the property, and the difference between state and Town requirements. The buyer indicated willingness to restore the second door if required. Motion by Gill, seconded by Tim, to direct Public Works to conduct a full inspection of the property for compliance with the Town’s Mobile Home Code and report findings to Council.Motion carried unanimously. No variance was granted at this meeting.
