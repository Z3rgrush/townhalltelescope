NEW BUSINESS
Committee for Water/Sewer Rate Increase
The Clerk’s Office recommended forming a committee (two trustees, staff, public works, and possibly a community member) to review water/sewer rates, special categories, and ordinance structure.
Discussion highlights:
Jim Crook opposed forming a committee, preferring full-board work sessions.
Concern about delays affecting loan repayment.
Staff emphasized complexity: 
Special rates (school, state park, grandfathered taps)
Ordinance revisions
Usage tiers
Budget impacts
Attorney Nerlin suggested a structured work session with clear agenda.
Outcome:No formal action taken. Staff will compile data (budget, rate categories, ordinance sections, consultant updates) for council review at the next meeting, after which the board will decide whether to form a committee or proceed as a full board.
