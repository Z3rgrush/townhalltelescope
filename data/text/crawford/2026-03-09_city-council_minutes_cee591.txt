NEW BUSINESS
Presentation: Michelle Haynes, Region 10
Michelle Haynes, Executive Director, provided an overview of Region 10’s services, including:
Regional planning & economic development strategy
Broadband infrastructure, including new fiber connections to Denver and Salt Lake City
Small Business Development Center consulting and training
Business loan fund (typical loans $30–40k; up to $200k with job creation)
Senior services, including meals, transportation, in-home support, and caregiver assistance. Crawford received 36-37,000 funding for seniors last year.
Enterprise Zone tax credits, with $19,000 in credits reinvested locally last year
Council discussed potential collaboration on:
A campus master plan for Town-owned property
A roof structure over the basketball court
Grant opportunities (DOLA, USDA Community Facilities Program)
Enterprise Zone contribution project eligibility
Michelle expressed Region 10’s willingness to assist with planning, grants, and coordination.
