6.2 NC 540 (R-2829) Bonus Allocation Programming Update
Alex Rickard, MPO Staff
Requested Action: Receive as information  
Staff Report
R-2829 Bonus Allocation Letter
Attachments:
Mr. Rickard provided an overview of the Strategic Transportation Investments law (STI) 
and the $100 million bonus allocation received for the tolling of NC 540 (R-2829), noting 
that the funds must be allocated by June 30, 2029. He commented that there are 
different caps on spending for the different tiers and that our maximum for division needs 
is $33.8 million. The bonus allocation must be spent on the same area tolled. He spoke 
regarding the selection process and the 16 projects selected. Since last presented, Staff 
has worked with NCDOT Division 5 to go through each project on the original candidate 
list and identify their actual scopes and determine updated costs. He highlighted several 
projects and summarized them and provided further details on the recommendations. The 
recommended projects are planned to be included in Amendment #3 to the FY26-35 TIP, 
which is the next item on the Agenda. A public hearing will be held in May 2026. 
Chair Butch Lawter commented that, although these funds cannot be used in Johnston 
County, he likes the fact that many of the projects will still benefit Johnston County.
Responding to Board Member Darryl Moss’s question regarding how the NC 50 project 
relates, Mr. Rickard stated that the project relates to the Timber Drive to NC 540 section 
of NC 50, not the northern Wake County portion of NC 50.
In response to Board Member Susan Evans’ inquiry regarding the area south of Ten-Ten 
Road, Mr. Rickard provided a summary of the project area, noting that it includes 
widening a section to four lanes. It will not improve all of Benson Road, just the choke 
points. Eventually the rest of the corridor will become more competitive in the SPOT 
process and there are will also be future opportunities for private development. This 
project includes the most expensive parts of the improvements.
Responding to Board Member Deans Eatman’s question regarding the inclusion of 
pedestrian access on the Poole Road Bridge, Mr. Rickard stated that the greenway 
bridge is north of the existing bridge. The road bridge would include sidewalks on the 
south side of the bridge. The project will create a sidewalk gap to Barwell Road; however, 
right-of-way acquisition costs blow the budget and we can’t afford it. He noted that there 
will be crosswalks at Barwell and Poole Roads and other options for people to cross. In 
the long-term, there will be other opportunities to continue the work as additional 
properties are developed.
This item was received as information only.

6.3 Amendment #3 to FY 2026-2035 Transportation Improvement Program 
(TIP)
Alex Rickard, MPO Staff
Requested Action: Receive as information
Staff Report
TIP Amendment #3
Attachments:
Mr. Rickard provided an overview the quarterly TIP amendment, noting that it includes the 
NCDOT STIP changes made from January-March 2026 and LAPP, transit, and RDU 
adjustments. He provided the following updates and noted that the public hearing will be 
held in May 20206:
   •  I-5966 had a $20 million increase due to right-of-way and construction costs. 
   •  AO-0004 is the new RDU project for a partial interchange at Aviation Boulevard and 
National Guard Drive, meant to reduce delays at the intersection. It will be 100% funded 
by RDU, but they may also seek federal grant funding for it.
   •  BL-172 is a FY 2026 LAPP project in the Town of Cary that is not able to proceed 
and is being removed at the request of the Town.
   •  HL-0169 is a FY 2026 road project previously identified as W-5805E. This was just 
an administrative change in project identification number.
   •  TD-5314 is a GoTriangle Park & Ride project funded by Wake Transit. The funds are 
being reallocated to other GoTriangle project costs, and the Project needs to be removed 
from the TIP.

6.4 Wake Transit Conference Committee Update
Ben Howell, CAMPO Staff
Requested Action: Receive as Information
Staff Report
March 26, 2026 Conference Committee Presentation-GoTriangle - 
Distributed at Meeting
Attachments:
Mr. Howell provided an overview of the February 23, 2026, Conference Committee 
meeting and the decision to allocate $0 of vehicle rental tax (VRT) revenues in the FY27 
Wake Transit Work Plan and notate that the financial assumptions for FY28 and beyond 
are “TBD” for future VRT allocations. He then provided a summary of the March 26, 2026, 
Committee meeting where GoTriangle presented financial outlook/long-term budget 
projections, information on how their bus services compare in the Triangle, and options 
for long-term resolution and analysis of tradeoffs. He noted that CAMPO Staff provided 
information on the impacts to the Wake Transit Financial Model resulting from the 
removal of VRT revenue and examples of what that VRT revenue could buy. Wake 
County Staff presented an option for Wake Transit to continue receiving VRT revenue, 
but fund additional GoTriangle projects equaling the VRT revenue in cost; however, there 
seemed to be no interest in that option. He stated that the meeting went well and that the 
Committee members all commented that the meeting was one of the best held to date. 
The next Committee meeting will be on May 19, 2026, where a long-term VRT decision 
will be discussed and potentially voted on.
Chair Butch Lawter commented that the last Committee meeting was the best so far.
Deans Eatman commented that he learned a lot at the meeting.
Chair Lawter noted that there was a desire from all to bring the matter to a conclusion, 
sooner rather than later. He recognized Brian Smith’s leadership at GoTriangle and stated 
that it meant a lot to the discussion.
This item was received as information only.
