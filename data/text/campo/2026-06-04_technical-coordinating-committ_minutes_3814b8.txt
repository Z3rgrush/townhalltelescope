5.6 SPOT 8 Update - Statewide Programming & Regional Impact Target 
Modal Mixes
Daniel Spruill, MPO Staff
Requested Action: Approve the Regional Impact target modal mix
Staff ReportAttachments:
Mr. Spruill provided an overview of the SPOT program and the evolution of the 
prioritization process, the approval schedule, STI Programming Process tiers, Division 
and region maps, STIP funding distributions, the revised P8 schedule and where we are 
at, draft Statewide programming, project development influence, local input points, and 
funding availabilities for the STI funding categories.
Chris Lukasina commented that last year there were seven more negative numbers on 
the Draft STIP Estimated Funding Availability List and that there had been a lot of 
discussion in the State about them.
Mr. Spruill continued by providing a summary of the recommended P8 Modal Allocation, 
non-highway funding competition, CAMPO’s adopted methodology for assigning local 
input points and maximizing funding potential, STI-Region C projected funding versus 
submitted need, and the Regional impact target modal mixes.
 
Responding to Bret Martin, Mr. Spruill stated that the regional impact scores became 
available last Thursday and that Staff is still reviewing them and working with NCDOT.
Chris Lukasina added that the scores will be posted on CAMPO’s website.
Responding to David Keilson’s inquiry as to whether 300 public transportation points and 
600 rail points were enough for the regional tier, Mr. Spruill stated that CAMPO limits 
project submissions to those most likely to be funded, no matter how excellent a project 
is.
Chris Lukasina added that this item is only recommending the target modal mixes. Later, 
with more discussion, we may recommend different point allocations. The recommended 
mixes will be used as a guide as we assess projects.
Mr. Spruill also added that we are still waiting for the non-highway category funding 
availabilities, expected in mid-June, which can also impact the final mix.
Responding to Het Patel, Mr. Lukasina stated that the recommended mix percentages 
are not the same as other rounds, but they create a good starting point.
Matt Day made a motion to recommend Executive Board approval of the 
Regional Impact target modal mix. Thanh Schado seconded the motion. The 
motion carried 35-0.
