5. Financial Report 
 Dean presented the financial report, explaining that the operation remains largely unchanged 
from previous years. Money is transferred from the capital improvement fund each year to 
make loan payments to the USDA. The remaining balance on the USDA loan at the end of 
2024 was $2,044,539. Payments are set up as direct ACH transactions. 
 
Dean also noted they maintain a money market investment at Bank of Colorado. The USDA 
initially required setting aside one year of debt service ($145,000), which has earned interest 
over time. 
 
Dean explained that maintenance of the building is handled by town staff at no additional cost 
to the Building Authority. He clarified that recent renovations to the building were paid for with
