D. COMMUNICATIONS 
1. Review of Raw Water Dedication 
Water Resource Manager L. Lesoing shared a brief update regarding the Town's raw water 
dedication policy, and information in the Windsor Municipal Code pertaining to the policy. 
 
2. Homeschool Day on the Farm Program September 10 10AM to 2 PM at the Historic Halfway 
Homestead 
The Parks, Recreation and Culture team is hosting the annual "Homeschool Day on the Farm" 
program on September 10, 2026 (10:00am - 2:00pm), at the Historic Halfway Homestead 
(335 McNeil Dr, Windsor, CO, 80550). This program is geared towards homeschooled 
children (grades K-12) and encourages them to engage with the history of agriculture in 
Northern Colorado. The Town's Museums Education team has invited the Water and Sewer 
Board and Water Efficiency team to participate with an educational activity for the children that 
relates to agricultural life and the roles of water resources in the area. 
 
Board members G. Bielawski, J. Willey, and K. Jacobsen volunteered to attend. "Leak 
Detective" activity used at Skyview Elementary STEM Fair earlier this year will likely be used 
for this event. This activity demonstrates how a small leak can add up very fast, and the 
importance of efficient water use. 
 
3. Alternative turfgrass demonstration  
Board members and staff visited the newly installed Alternative Turfgrass Demonstration area 
on the north side of the Community Development building. Five plots of struggling Kentucky 
Bluegrass have been replaced with Tahoma 31, Dog Tuff, and Buffalo grasses, as well as a 
plot of wildflower mix, and a plot of ground cover alternatives. Members of the Water 
Efficiency and Communications teams are working on monitoring the installation, promoting 
the Town's Coloradoscape Initiative rebate program, and creating signage and informational 
materials to promote the conversion of Kentucky Bluegrass to more water efficient 
alternatives, better suited for Colorado's climate.
