Background / Discussion: 
As part of the annual budget process, staff will present the requested operations budget for 
2027. Presented expenditures are funded from a variety of sources. The majority of department 
requests are funded via the General Fund, which is supported primarily by sales tax, property 
tax, intergovernmental revenue, franchise fees, user fees and permit fees.  Remaining requests, 
occurring in the Town’s enterprise and special revenue funds, are primarily funded by user fees 
alone or via a combination of user fees and sales tax.

Medical Premium Renewal
• The Town was placed in Utilization Category 3, resulting in a 10% medical premium increase.
• If current employer/employee premium splits and enrollment remained unchanged, the projected increase would be 
approximately $484,300.
Benefits Committee Action
The Benefits Committee approved changes to premium contributions and plan offerings to reduce the financial impact:
Result: These changes reduce the projected Town premium increase from $484,300 to approximately $336,600.
Additional Plan Options
• Four additional medical plans will be added to the Town's offerings.
• Each new plan has lower premiums for both the employee and the Town compared with existing plan options.
• Any employee enrollment in the new lower-cost options will further reduce the overall premium impact.
