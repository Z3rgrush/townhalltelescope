C. BOARD ACTION  
1. Approval of Revisions to the Public Art Policy – The board read the newly revised 
policy. There were questions on Reimagine Windsor and Exhibit B Section 2. There was 
discussion on the use of rendering and changing an AI image. The one issue that the 
board wanted revised was in Exhibit B Section 7. There needed to be clear consequences 
laid out on what happens if AI art is shown to be used. It was stated that there needed to 
be language at the end of Exhibit B that states the consequences to “all of the above” A 
motion to approve the revisions with the changes to Exhibit B made by Marisha Pelnar, 
Second by Carrie Ferris, Unanimously Approved 
2. Approval of the mural design for Halfway Homestead, Red Barn Mural by Katie Curcio. 
– The board was shown the latest mockup for the Red Barn Mural. The responses were 
overwhelmingly positive. There were a couple of areas that they wanted to see changed. 
One was the section the inside of the letter “I” and the inside of the letter “D” was the 
other. The Boardwalk Park Pavilion is in the water in the design and is in the grass in 
real life. They propose using water activities such as paddleboarding, fishing or tubing. 
They also wanted the fish to be a bit smaller as the perspective of the images seems off 
with an abnormally large fish. Chris Bates will communicate with Katie about the 
proposed changes. Motion to approve the design with changes to the “I” and “D” made 
by Carrie Ferris, Second by Marisha Pelnar, Unanimously Approved.

1. Sol of Windsor: Sculpture on Loan Unveiling Update: Chris Bates – The SoL of Windsor unveiling 
was a very successful event. There were 2 community members that came to the season 1 kickoff. 
This year we had over 40 people show up to take the tour. Big thanks to Carrie and Marisha for 
keeping people safe, wrangling tarps and making sure the unveiling went smoothly. Go check out 
the pieces and especially the one on the jetty at night! 
 
2. Project Updates: 1. CRC Signage- The sign is fabricated and ready to be installed. Fusion 
Fabrication will be starting the work to put it up the week of September 21. They estimate that the 
install will take 2-3 days. 2. Noblewins Benches – The benches will be shipped out on 9/16 and will 
be arriving on 9/18 or 9/21. There will be 4 benches painting this year, as Dara Weyna opted out of 
painting hers this year. One of Chris or Laura will be touring Vestas in early October. This will be an 
opportunity to make some in roads with them and hopefully work towards sponsorship in the future. 
3. Ray King – Ray is fabricating his piece and the last update that we had was him working to get 
everything stamped from a local engineering firm. 
  
2.SWAG 2027! – Brainstorm session to come up with ideas for give away items for next year. 
Shopping bags were a big hit, more of those, different style. Postcards, magnets, scarfs, chip clips, 
measuring tape, pens, ChapStick, flashlight, handheld fans. There was also a desire to use some of 
the artwork and projects to make some beautiful swag.

Hello Windsor Art Commission!  
I hope you’re doing well. My name is Erica Wharton, and I’m the Volunteer Coordinator for the Town of 
Windsor. I’m excited to connect with you and to support the important work you do on our Boards and 
Commissions. 
As we continue strengthening community engagement, we’re integrating all Boards & Commissions volunteers 
into the Windsor Volunteer Program. Doing this will help us streamline communication, provide consistent 
support and recognition, and ensure that all volunteers—across every department—have access to the same 
resources and opportunities. 
It will also allow us to better understand the full picture of volunteerism in Windsor. By having everyone in one 
system, we’ll be able to tell a more complete story about how many people generously give their time and 
talents to make our town such a great place to live. Your service is a meaningful part of that story, and we want 
to make sure it’s recognized. 
To help us get started, we’re asking all Board & Commission volunteers to complete a brief online volunteer 
application. This ensures we have accurate information for communication, training, and support. You can fill 
out the application here. 
Thank you so much for the dedication and care you bring to Windsor. I’m looking forward to working with you 
and supporting you in your role. 
Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions or need anything along the way.
