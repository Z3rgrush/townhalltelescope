MINUTES OF MISSION WOODS CITY COUNCIL 
Tuesday, September 1, 2026 
The City Council of Mission Woods, Kansas met in regular session on Tuesday, September 1 , 
2026, in person at Westwood City Hall. 
Budget Meeting  
Mayor Hartley opened the budget hearing meeting to the public  at 6:01 p.m. John Martin, City 
Treasurer, shared this year’s budget changes/additions, City’s Revenue Neutral Rate (12.962) and 
Mill Levy ra te, with the City NOT opting to exceed the RNR. Mr. Martin stated this was 
comparable to the prior year. Public meeting was closed.   
Mayor Hartley asked if there were any questions for Mr. Martin. With no additional questions 
motion to conclude the hearing was made by Councilmember  Mays and seconded by 
Councilmember Hartley. A voice vote was had, and the motion was  approved, 4-0, 
Councilmember Mohsen was absent.

Unfinished Business 
• Consider formally appointing City Council to act as Tree Board 
o Propose workshop in September/October for the purpose of establishing protocols for 
maintaining city tree inventory + health reports, creating a removal/replacement policy, 
establishing budgets/funding, and resident outreach 
Mayor Hartley shared after the discussion at the last meeting regarding the Tree Board, she 
wanted to formally appoint City Council as the acting Tree Board. She asked Mr. Deane 
what needs to happen to officially appoint the City Council as the Tree Board. 
Councilmember Hartley shared some suggestions on what the Board would be responsible 
for.  Mr. Deane stated that the Council has not yet officially created or given duties to the 
Tree Board. He discussed this with the Council on creating the ordinance and their ability 
to amend in the future if needed. There was additional discussion. It was decided to create 
an ordinance to organize a Tree Board and have a work session. Mr. Deane stated that 
sincere there will be a quorum at a work session it would have be noticed – date, time and 
place – stating what the session is for and that there will be no voting.   
New Business 
• Approval of 2027 Budget. 
Mayor Hartley stated that the Council needed to approve the 2027 budget. At the hearing, 
there were no questions so she asked for a m otion to approve the 2027 budget. Motion to 
approve 2027 budget was made by Councilmember Carto and seconded by Councilmember 
Hartley. A roll call vote was had; Councilmembers Mays, Carto, Hartley, and Sweeney 
voted in the affirmative and the motion was approved, 4-0. Councilmembers Mohsen were 
absent.   
• Consideration for city-wide curbside compost pick up (Paul Hartley)  
Councilmember Hartley investigated the costs for compost pickup. He shared his findings 
with the Council. He stated he did not believe the City was big enough for a single compost 
drop off area. There were other options, like biweekly pickup.  Mayor Hartley stated that 
they would need to know how much residential interest. She stated she also believed they 
should get some research and education on why composting would be beneficial before 
presenting it to the residents. After Council discussion, it was decided to educate and do a 
city-wide survey.
