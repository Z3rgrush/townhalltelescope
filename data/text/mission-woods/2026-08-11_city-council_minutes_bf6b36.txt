• Request from resident at 5314 Mission Woods Terr (Brockmeier) for reimbursement 
of tree trimming in city right-of-way 

2 
Mayor Hartley stated that Jessica Brockmeier , resident of Mission Woods was in 
attendance to discuss reimbursement of payment made regarding a large tree limb which 
had fallen in her yard  and driveway . Mayor Hartley shared the photographs on her 
computer screen. Ms. Brockmeier stated this tree was abutting the curb. She also stated she 
believed this tree needed to be taken down due to low hanging limbs that were hit by traffic. 
The Council responded to her reciting the new tree ordinance, and that they were in the 
process of creating a tree board. Mayor Hartley stated to Ms. Brockmeier that this situation 
was the reason they were in the process of creating the tree board. She went on to explain 
that the City did not have a tree ordinance and last year the City had significant cost 
involving tree situations. She stated that the ordinance the City adopted was the same as 
the state statute . She also stated that the trees int eh City  are over 75-years and in the 
upcoming years could mean a lot of trees being removed or replaced. Mayor Hartley asked 
the Council to discuss these matters.  
Councilmember Carto asked how they would decide these matters. Mayor Hartley stated 
they could decide if they wanted the Council to also serve as the tree board since they meet 
regularly.  
Councilmember Hartley shared they had the inventory of the trees back from the Kansas 
Forestry service. It showed 350 trees, only about 80 within the definition of the right of 
way. Out of 350 trees, only 4 were dead or dying.  
Councilmember Mohsen stated  he believed two things were needed: (1) a  process for 
residents to petition the City for reimbursement of money paid for a tree situation; (2) 
budget and how it is allocated in any given year.  
Discussions by the Council regarding reviewing certain tree situations and how they would 
be handled.  
Councilmember Carto left the meeting at 6:43.  
The Council stated it will continue to discuss protocols for tree removals, reimbursement 
etc. in the upcoming meetings.  
A Motion to approve reimbursing Ms. Brockmeier $300 for the cost of the tree disposal 
was made by Councilmember Mohsen and seconded by Councilmember Hartley. A voice 
vote was had, and the motion was approved, 4-0. Councilmember Carto was absent.
