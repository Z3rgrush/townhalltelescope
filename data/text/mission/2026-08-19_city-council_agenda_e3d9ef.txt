RE: Resolution Establishing the 2027 Solid Waste and Stormwater Utility Rates 
 
RECOMMENDATION: Adopt the Resolution establishing the 2027 Solid Waste Utility 
and Stormwater Utility rates for the City of Mission for certification to Johnson County. 
 
DETAILS: Johnson County requires all utility rates to be certified no later than August 
25 each year so they can be incorporated into the annual property tax and special 
assessment/utility billing process. This certification deadline occurs in advance of the 
City's overall budget adoption deadline of October 1, requiring the Governing Body to 
formally establish these rates before the remainder of the 2027 Budget is adopted. 
The proposed rates were reviewed as part of the Council's 2027 July 29 Budget Work 
Session, during which the Governing Body discussed the assumptions supporting both 
utilities and expressed general consensus with the proposed rates. Adoption of this 
Resolution formalizes those rates for the 2027 Budget and authorizes staff to certify 
them to Johnson County by the required deadline. 
The proposed 2027 utility rates are: 
• Stormwater Utility: $28.00 per Equivalent Residential Unit (ERU) per month.  
• Solid Waste Utility: $211.08 annually for single-family residential properties.  
Approval of this Resolution satisfies the County's certification requirement while 
allowing the remainder of the City's 2027 Budget to continue through the normal 
budget adoption process, culminating with final budget approval later this fall. 
 
CFAA CONSIDERATIONS/IMPACTS:  Establishing utility rates provides sustainable, 
dedicated funding for essential infrastructure and public services that support safe, well-
maintained neighborhoods and a high quality of life for residents of all ages and 
abilities.

RE: Declaration of Surplus Equipment  
 
RECOMMENDATION: Approve the Resolution providing for the sale/disposal of surplus 
equipment from various Departments. 
 
DETAILS: City Council Policy No. 111 defines the process and procedure for the sale 
and disposal of real and personal property by the City of Mission, which is also outlined 
in K.S.A. 12-101. This policy was updated in October 2025 to increase the threshold to 
$5,000 in estimated property value which would require City Council approval prior to 
disposition. Property valued at less than $5,000 will be reviewed and approved for 
disposal by the City Administrator. Property and equipment valued over $5,000 and 
identified as surplus has been included as Attachment A to the Resolution. 
 
Each Department, in consultation with the Deputy City Administrator, will be responsible 
for determining the best method for disposal in accordance with Council Policy and 
State law. 
 
Items to be declared as surplus include five parcels of City-owned property. Two of 
these parcels were discussed at the July 8th Finance and Administration Committee 
Meeting including 5534 Broadmoor St and the parcel directly to the southeast (Parcel 
KP02600000 0008).  
 
The three remaining parcels to be declared surplus are the Mission transit lot parcels 
which Encore Land LLC (Sunflower Development Group) is purchasing for $250,000 as 
part of the Encore Project Redevelopment Agreement approved at the May 2026 City 
Council meeting.  
 
CFAA CONSIDERATIONS/IMPACTS: The disposal of surplus City-owned lots 
supports Mission's commitment to being a Community for All Ages by returning 
underutilized property to productive use. Redevelopment of these sites for potential 
housing can expand housing opportunities, strengthen established neighborhoods, 
increase the tax base, and promote long-term community vitality while ensuring City-
owned land is managed in the best interest of residents.
