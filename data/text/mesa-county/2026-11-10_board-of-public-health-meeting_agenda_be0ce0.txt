Rules of Decorum:
Rules of decorum shall be strictly observed during the meeting. Visit the Mesa County Website to view the Rules of 
Decorum. A copy of such is placed on the bulletin board outside the Mesa County Public Health Administration office 
at 510 29 ½ Rd, Grand Junction, Colorado 81504. 
First Public Comment Period (Agenda Items Only):
The First Public Comment Period on the agenda provides the public an opportunity to speak to the Board of Public 
Health about issues that are listed on the main agenda. Testimony relating to non-agenda items will only be allowed 
during the second public comment period. 
The Rules of Decorum will be strictly enforced. Outbursts and interruptions from any person in the hearing room, 
including shouting, clapping, booing, laughing, or other reactions to the proceedings are prohibited. Any comments 
from the public shall only be made by persons who are residents of Mesa County, who have been recognized by the 
Chair, and only after they have identified themselves on the record.
Each person will have 3 minutes to comment. No speaker may yield part or all of his/her time to another speaker. If a 
speaker is reading from prepared notes and runs out of time before reading all their notes, the speaker MAY leave the 
printed notes with the Board, but speakers may NOT extend their time by having someone else finish reading their 
comments. Comments shall only be made to the Board. No comments or questions shall be directed to staff or other 
members of the public.
