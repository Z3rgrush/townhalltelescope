Study Session
The Salina City Commission meets from time to time in study session. These sessions may be held before or after a
regular City Commission meeting, or at a separate date and time. The purpose of a study session is to allow adequate
time for staff reports and Commission discussion and review of specific public policies and issues. All meetings are open
to the public and news media representatives. No formal action is taken at a study session, but matters reviewed at a
study session may be acted on at a later formal City Commission meeting.
