Discussion on Belleview Avenue and Franklin Street Safety Measure Proposal (CDOT 
Representative) 
   
To:   Honorable Mayor and City Council 
Through:   John Jackson, City Manager 
Shawn Cordsen, Deputy City Manager 
Sarah Geiger, City Attorney 
From:   Jacob James, Public Works Director  
  
Executive Summary: 
  
The Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) will provide an update to the effort of analyzing the 
Belleview Avenue and Franklin Street Intersection for safety improvements.  This update provides a 
recommended alternative of improvements to the intersection from CDOT following a collaborative effort of 
analysis between the cities of Greenwood Village and Cherry Hills Village and CDOT. 
  
Recommendation: 
  
For information only. 
  
Attachments: 
  
None 
  
Discussion 
  
Over the course of the past year, staff from the City of Greenwood Village, City of Cherry Hills Village, and the 
Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) have reviewed safety concerns and crashes at the Belleview 
Avenue and Franklin Street intersection.  In addition, through a consultant hired by CDOT, several alternatives 
were reviewed with the goal of improving the safety of vehicles and pedestrians at the intersection.  Belleview 
Avenue is considered a State Highway and is under the jurisdiction of CDOT.  Since the road is State Right-of-
Way, CDOT provides the ultimate recommendation for the improvements.  This was based on narrowing down 
the larger list of alternatives utilizing scoring criteria agreed upon by the entire project team.  CDOT will 
present the recommended alternative to City Council and also provide information on next steps for the project 
which include design and construction.  CDOT will also speak to funding and long term operations and 
maintenance considerations for the project.

Executive Summary: 
  
The Capital Improvement Program (CIP) includes a project for replacement of the rails on the Green Oaks 
Drive Bridge over the Highline Canal (Green Oaks Bridge) and the Orchard Road Bridge over Greenwood 
Gulch (Orchard Bridge) to bring them into conformance with current standards.  In addition, the CIP also 
includes a project to repair the delamination of the asphalt pavement on the Orchard Bridge.  Combined bids 
for the project were received, and all exceed the projected budget.  A supplemental budget appropriation of 
$387,996 is necessary to award the construction contract. Staff is soliciting feedback from City Council to 
determine if a budget amendment is supported. If there is support for the project, staff will prepare the budget 
amendment for approval at a future City Council meeting. 
  
Recommendation: 
  
For Council consideration. 
  
Attachments: 
  
None 
  
Discussion 
  
Background 
Every two years, CDOT conducts a rating of all roadway bridges on state and local transportation networks to 
review their adequacy.  In recent years these reviews have identified that the Orchard Road bridge and the 
Green Oaks bridge require rail upgrades to meet current Industry standards.  
 
In addition to the bridge rail replacement, the asphalt on Orchard Road Bridge is failing and in need of 
repair.  This work was originally programmed as a separate project; however, it was included with the 
construction of the rail project to take advantage of construction efficiencies. 
 
Additional Improvements Identified 
During design, staff identified additional necessary improvements that were not included in the scope of the 
project when originally programmed in the CIP budget. The deteriorated sidewalk on the Orchard Bridge is 
integral to the bridge structure and must be replaced in conjunction with the installation of the new bridge rail. 
Also, the guardrail along the south side of Orchard Road adjacent to Palos Verdes Park must be extended to 
protect vehicles from the steep slope south of Orchard Road. Both improvements are necessary to assure 
compliance with current safety standards and were therefore included in the design and the construction bid 
package. 
 
Outreach to affected residents, acquisition of the required easements, and design to address both the bridge 
rail and pavement replacement has been completed.
