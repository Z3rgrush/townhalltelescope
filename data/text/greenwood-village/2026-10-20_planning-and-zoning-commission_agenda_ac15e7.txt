Executive Summary: 
  
The Planning and Zoning Commission requested that Staff organize study sessions to provide instruction on the 
Land Development Code. Staff and the Commission determined that Staff would incorporate introductory 
instruction into meetings where the Commission conducts regular business. The Planning and Zoning Chair and 
the Planning Manager discussed starting these sessions sooner and have repurposed the Oct 20, 2026 meeting 
for the sole purpose of a study session. The topics were selected in coordination with the Planning Chair and 
the Planning Manager. 
This session covers the Development Activity Map (DAM), the Municipal Code, and the Land Development 
Code (LDC). The session’s learning objectives are to: 
Explain the purpose and function of the DAM and its relationship to public noticing. 
Review the structure and searchability of the Municipal Code, including how it organizes the laws and 
requirements governing the City. 
Locate the LDC and familiarize the Commission with its organization. 
These sessions are intended to promote a deeper understanding of the Land Development Code, and questions 
are encouraged.

Discussion  
 
In 2022, at City Council’s request, the City formed an Ad Hoc Committee to revise the Land Development 
Code. The committee met for two years and included four (4) City Council members, the Planning and Zoning 
Chair or their representative, and the BoAA Chair or their representative. Its work produced comprehensive 
revisions that many stakeholders found beneficial. 
In spring 2026, the Planning and Zoning Commission requested, and City Council affirmed, that the 
Commission begin study sessions on the Land Development Code. City Council wished to extend these

The Development Activity Map is available on the City’s website at Greenwoodvillage.com/development 
(Attachment 1: Development Activity Map). It highlights development applications and shows their current 
status. This map coordinates development cases with the noticing requirements of Sec. 16-2-270. Noticing will 
be discussed separately; however, property signs required for application notices include a QR code linking to 
the map. Applicant letters mailed to property owners also include a QR code to the map. 
Selecting an item on the map opens a pop-up containing the case status, potential hearing dates, and an image 
of the proposed development or project. The map is color-coded and shape-based by application type; for 
example, green represents variances. This resource can help Board members identify upcoming cases and 
determine whether they have been scheduled. 
 
The Municipal Code is available on the City’s website. It is located at https://library.municode 
.com/co/greenwood_village/codes/municipal_code (Attachment 2: Municipal Code). A municipal code is the 
organized collection of a city's local laws, ordinances, and regulations. It serves as the legal rulebook for how a 
municipality operates and how activities within its boundaries are regulated.  
Typical topics found in a municipal code include: 
• Zoning and land use 
• Building and construction standards 
• Business licensing 
• Sign regulations 
• Property maintenance 
• Public safety requirements 
• Planning and development procedures 
• Local government administration  
This municipal code website is searchable. Staff will go over how to search the code and the various 
components of the code relating to its structure. The topics include Article X Boards and Commissions, Chapter 
2, Article 12 Board of Adjustment and Appeals, Chapter 2, Article 14 Planning and Zoning Commission, and 
Chapter 7 Health, Sanitation and Animals. The purpose is to allow stakeholders to gain basic skills in finding 
and researching items in the Municipal Code independently based on their curiosity.

The Land Development Code (LDC) is Chapter 16 of the Municipal Code. This is the area of the municipal 
code where most Planning and Zoning Commission and BoAA items are codified (Attachment 3). These are 
the rules that the Planning Department uses to evaluate any application. This includes Article 2 Procedures, 
Division 1 Types of Applications, Article 2 Division 2 – Review Procedures, Staff will describe the intent 
portions of the code, Sec. 16-1-100 Definitions, 16-2-270 Public Notice, 16-2-290 Public Hearings, and the 
Zoning District Sections of the LDC. The intent is to introduce the LDC and have this be a primer for further 
discussion. Staff will also go over the Calendars on the web, the remaining year’s schedule, and the schedule 
for 2027 (Attachment 4: Calendars). The next topics for the study session will be a reintroduction to the Bylaws 
of the Planning and Zoning Commission, Zoning districts, and how they relate to the Planned Unit 
Developments (PUDs) and/or Site Development Plans (SDPs), along with Master Development Plans (MDPs). 
Finally, the Staff will discuss the role of HOAs in the City.
