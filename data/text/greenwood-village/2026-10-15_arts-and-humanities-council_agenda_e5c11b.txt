BYLAWS OF 
THE GREENWOOD VILLAGE  
ARTS AND HUMANITIES COUNCIL 
(as amended 4/18/2019) 
 
Article I Name of the Council  
 
The name of the Council shall be the Greenwood Village Arts and Humanities 
Council (GVAHC). 
 
Article II Mission, Vision and Duties 
 
Section 1: Mission:  The Greenwood Village Arts and Humanities Council’s 
mission is to nurture and support arts and humanities activities in Greenwood 
Village to enrich the cultural environment and help create a vibrant, thriving and 
successful community in which to live, work and do business. 
 
 
Section 2: Vision:  The Greenwood Village Arts and Humanities Council 
envisions a Greenwood Village where: 
 
- High quality experiences in the arts and humanities are locally available to 
support and inspire the cultural and creative lives of all members of our 
community. 
 
- All Individuals and organizations within the community: 
 
• are aware of the importance of these cultural and arts experiences; 
 
• have the opportunity to be engaged in these experiences as planners, 
participants, sponsors and attendees; 
 
• are inspired to make the arts part of their everyday lives. 
 
Section 3: Duties and Responsibilities: The duty and responsibility of the 
GVAHC is to contribute leadership and provide recommendations to the City 
Council for the purpose of enhancing Greenwood Village’s cultural identity; 
implement the Village’s public arts policy and provide recommendations to City 
Council regarding the same; promoting the arts and art education in the Village 
and surrounding areas; encourage the production, presentation, exhibition, 
advancement, or preservation of art, music, theater, dance, literature, zoology, 
botany, anthropology, cultural history, or natural history through programs and 
events that add to those already available through the Curtis Arts & Humanities 
Center; and initiate and produce fundraising events for the benefit of the 
production, presentation, exhibition, advancement or preservation of art, music, 
Page 5 
theater, dance, literature, zoology, botany, anthropology, cultural history, or 
natural history, and for the acquisition of public art in the city. 
  
 
Article III Authorization 
   
The GVAHC shall make recommendations to City Council as necessary.  
 
Article IV Membership and Term of Office 
   
Members:  The GVAHC shall consist of nine members: one adult member from 
each of the four Council Districts (“district members”), four At-large members 
and one At-Large Youth Member.  Each member shall have one vote. 
 
At-large Youth Member:  The At-large Youth member shall be a young adult 
between the ages of sixteen and eighteen or a junior or senior in high school who 
has resided in the City for at least one (1) year immediately preceding the date of 
appointment.  The At-large Youth member shall be a resident of the City 
throughout his or her term of office.  The At-large Youth member shall be 
appointed by the City Council, but only after filing a written application and 
having an interview with City Council.  The term of the At-large Youth member 
shall be two years (2.52.030 City Code).  
 
 
Appointment:  Each member shall be appointed by the City Council.   
On the date of appointment, each member shall be a registered elector of the 
Village and shall have resided within the Village for at least one year immediately 
preceding the date of appointment.  Each member shall be a resident of the district 
on the date of appointment and throughout his or her term of office.   
 
The City Council shall make appointments to fill vacancies for unexpired terms.  
In the event a person is appointed to fill a vacancy for an unexpired term and the 
time remaining to serve under the expired term is less than one-half of the entire 
term of office, the period of time the new member serves to fill out the unexpired 
term shall not constitute a term of the new member and the new member shall be 
eligible to serve three consecutive full terms thereafter. 
 
Term of Office:  The term of each member shall be two (2) years.  No 
Councilmember shall serve more than four (4) consecutive terms of office.  If a 
past GVAHC member who has served the maximum amount of tenure permitted 
desires to recommence service on the GVAHC, the past member may be 
reappointed only after an absence of at least two years from the GVAHC. 
 
Article V Election of Officers 
 
Page 6 
The members of the GVAHC shall annually elect for a one-year term from their 
membership a Chair and a Vice Chair as the first item of business at the first 
meeting of the year. The vote is to be by written ballot. 
 
1. Upon proper nomination and second by any GVAHC member, a nomination 
for Chair shall be considered.  A majority vote of members present shall be 
required to elect a Chair. 
 
2. In the election of officers, each GVAHC member shall be entitled to only one 
vote. All candidates for elective office shall be present. Voting by written 
proxy is not allowed. 
 
3. In the event that the duly elected Chair becomes unable to complete his/her 
term of office, the Vice Chair shall automatically assume the position of Chair 
until the completion of the term, and a new Vice Chair shall be elected at the 
next regularly scheduled meeting in accordance with the provisions of the 
Bylaws. 
 
4. If neither the Chair nor the Vice Chair is present, any member in attendance 
shall call the meeting to order, and the GVAHC shall immediately elect by 
majority vote a Chair Pro Tem to preside at the meeting.  The entrance of the 
Chair or Vice Chair terminates such office. 
 
Article VI Duties of Officers 
 
Chair:  It is the responsibility of the Chair to conduct formal GVAHC meetings 
in accordance with accepted principles of decorum and the rules of order adopted 
in these Bylaws.  The Chair shall be a voting member of the GVAHC and is 
accorded the same rights and privileges accorded other members of the GVAHC.  
The Chair shall be entitled to enter into all discussions of the Council and to vote 
on all questions before the Council, but cannot make or second any motion 
(Section 2-15-40 Municipal Code). 
 
Vice Chair:  In the event that the Chair is temporarily unavailable to act due to 
absence from the City, illness, personal interest in any matter coming before the 
GVAHC, or any other cause, the Vice Chair shall act in his or her place.  In this 
instance, the Vice Chair shall be accorded the same privileges and responsibilities 
as the Chair. 
   
Article VII Compensation 
 
Members of the GVAHC  shall not be compensated but shall be reimbursed for 
prior authorized expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties upon 
submission of a proper claim to the Director of Parks, Trails and Recreation. No 
member of the Council or immediate family shall receive compensation or be 
eligible for grants or scholarships sponsored by the Council or the City.  Council 
Page 7 
members and their immediate family may participate in art exhibits sponsored by 
the City or the Council. Council members are prohibited from receiving cash 
awards for their work of art submitted for any art show sponsored by the City or 
the Council (Section 2-15-40 Municipal Code).  
 
 
Article VIII Quorum 
 
A quorum of the GVAHC shall be present at any meeting in which official 
recommendations are made.  A quorum shall consist of four members.  The 
GVAHC may elect to cancel a meeting due to a lack of a quorum.  Voting by 
written proxy not allowed. 
 
Article IX Removal of Members 
 
If any member of the GVACH fails to attend seven (7) out of every ten (10) 
consecutive meetings, City Council shall consider whether such member should 
be removed from the Council (Section 2-2-330(c) Municipal Code). 
 
 
 
Article X Meetings 
 
Regular meetings of the GVAHC shall be held on a monthly basis and shall be 
open to the public in accordance with applicable law. To the extent practicable, 
regular meetings shall be scheduled for the third Thursday of each month 
at 5:30 p.m. at the Curtis Center for the Arts. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the 
GVAHC reserves the right to modify the date, time, and/or location of any regular 
meeting as deemed necessary, provided that notice of such change is given in 
accordance with applicable noticing requirements. 
  
Regular meetings shall be conducted when there are matters requiring 
consideration or action. The GVAHC may cancel, postpone, or reschedule any 
regular meeting in the absence of agenda items, subject to compliance with 
applicable notice provisions. 
  
At any meeting or hearing of the GVAHC, any interested person may appear in 
person or be represented by an agent or attorney and shall be afforded the 
opportunity to present relevant evidence or testimony, subject to such reasonable 
rules of procedure as the GVAHC may adopt. 
 
The GVAHC shall keep minutes of its proceedings and those minutes shall be 
filed in the office of the Parks, Trails and Recreation Department (Section 2-15-
40 Municipal Code).  All such minutes shall be available for public inspection. 
The City Attorney and other City officials shall advise and assist the GVAHC in 
the performance of its duties as requested by the GVAHC. (Prior code 2.53.040; 
Page 8 
Ord. 26 §1, 2011; Ord. 32 §1, 2011; Ord. 19 §3, 2012).  The GVAHC may 
establish and appoint committees as it deems necessary to discharge its duties and 
responsibilities (Section 2-13-40 Municipal Code). 
 
Article XI Correspondence 
 
All notices that are required by these Bylaws and all other correspondence 
between staff and GVAHC members, or between GVAHC members, in respect to 
administrative matters shall be made through the staff representative (Cultural 
Arts Coordinator).   GVAHC members shall receive an agenda and packet of 
corresponding materials, if any, four days prior to a regularly scheduled meeting. 
 
Article XII Funding and Budget     
 
Monies required for the operation of the Council shall come from donations, 
proceeds of fundraising events and grants secured by the Council and held within 
the Arts and Humanities Special Revenue Fund (Section 2-15-60 Municipal 
Code).  The City Manager shall submit for consideration by the City Council an 
annual budget within the Arts and Humanities Special Revenue Fund for the 
reasonable expenses of the Council, which shall also contain an estimate of 
anticipated revenues for the next calendar year. (Prior code 2.53.060; Ord. 26 §1, 
2011; Ord. 32 §1, 2011) 
 
Article XIII Order for Business 
 
The following is the order of business conducted by the GVAHC at its meetings: 
 
1. The Chair calls the meeting to order and a roll call is conducted indicating the 
presence of the GVAHC. 
 
2. The Chair calls for consideration of the minutes of the previous meeting.  The 
minutes may be adopted as written, or corrected, by a majority vote of the 
GVAHC members at that meeting.  The GVAHC will be provided a copy of 
the minutes to be approved in agenda packets.  The minutes are to be 
reviewed by the GVAHC prior to the meeting.  Minutes shall be taken by a 
staff member or in the absence of a staff member an agreed upon GVAHC 
member may take the minutes. 
 
3. The Chair shall call for public comment as presented on the agenda or in the 
order the Chair deems appropriate. 
 
4. The Chair shall conduct the business as presented on the agenda or in the 
order the Chair deems appropriate. 
 
5. Adjournment.

Article XIV Rules and Procedure for Review of Business 
 
The following is the order of review of business conducted by the GVAHC at its 
meetings when public comment is on the agenda: 
 
1. The Chair shall request that the applicant approach the GVAHC and identify 
himself/herself and his/her complete address. 
 
2. The Chair shall ask the applicant if he/she desires to provide a brief 
description of his/her request or response to the GVAHC’s recommendation. 
 
3. The Chair shall then open the meeting for public input, and ask for those 
speaking in favor of the request or in opposition to the request.  Those persons 
desiring to address the GVAHC must first complete a form prior to the 
meeting giving their name, address, phone number and issue to which they are 
speaking.  Those speaking in favor shall be heard first, followed by those in 
opposition.  All speakers will give their name and address prior to giving their 
input for the purpose of public record. 
 
4. The Chair shall close the public input portion of the meeting. 
 
5. The Chair shall ask each GVAHC member for any questions relative to the 
request.  These questions may be directed to the applicant or to the staff. 
 
6. After all speakers have presented their input, the Chair shall ask either the 
applicant or staff to respond, as applicable.  In no case shall an applicant be 
prevented from the opportunity to rebut unfavorable input.  Dialogue between 
the applicant and those members of the public addressing the GVAHC shall 
not be permitted except upon approval of the Chair. 
 
7. The Chair shall ask if any member of the public has any further statements to 
make on any new evidence presented by the applicant in rebuttal. 
 
8. The Chair shall ask each GVAHC member for his/her comments and final 
questions relative to the input taken.  Questions may be addressed to the 
applicant, other GVAHC members, the staff, or members of the public. 
 
9. After all discussion has taken place, the Chair shall ask for a motion from the 
GVAHC.  Any GVAHC member or staff member may be recognized for 
comments on the motion.  Upon proper motion and second, the Chair shall 
conduct a vote either by roll call or voice vote.   
 
10. After the vote is taken, the Chair shall reiterate to the application the vote and 
inform the applicant of the GVAHC’s recommendation to City Council. 
 
Page 10 
11. The adoption of any resolution or motion shall require the affirmative vote of 
a majority of those members of the Council present at the meeting at which 
the vote is taken (Section 2-15-40 Municipal Code).  When the vote on a 
motion consists of an equal number of affirmative and negative votes, the 
motion shall be defeated. 
 
12. Any GVAHC member who actually or potentially has a conflict of interest 
with regard to any business before the GVAHC on or whose participation 
would otherwise violate the appearance of fairness shall disqualify 
himself/herself from voting or participating in the discussion and in any 
motion concerning such business.  When a GVAHC member has so 
disqualified himself/herself, the member shall neither sit on nor preside over 
the GVAHC during the discussion and voting on the matter. 
 
13. A potential conflict of interest or violation of the appearance of fairness exists 
when a GVAHC member has a direct or indirect interest in the outcome of the 
discussion or vote beyond that of the general public. 
 
14. The recommendations rendered by the GVAHC members shall be based 
solely on information which has been presented to the GVAHC by an 
applicant, staff, the city attorney, consultants hired by the Village, or other 
city officials (Section 2-6-50 Municipal Code). 
 
15. Any question or issue as to the proper procedure for conducting business at 
any GVAHC meeting which is not resolved by the above rules and order of 
business shall be resolved by reference to the relevant portions of Robert’s 
Rules of Order. 
 
Article XV Definitions 
 
1. Applicant:  That person or firm who proposes action to be taken by the 
GVAHC. 
 
2. GVAHC:  Greenwood Village Arts and Humanities Council. 
 
3. GVAHC Member:  A person appointed by the City Council to serve on the 
Greenwood Village Arts and Humanities Council. 
 
4. City:  City of Greenwood Village. 
 
5. Motion:  A statement of proposed action to be taken by the GVAHC by a 
GVAHC member. 
 
6. Motion, Amendment to:  A proposal by a GVAHC member, other than the 
maker of a motion, to amend the maker’s motion.  A “friendly” amendment 
may be offered, to which the maker and seconder of a motion may respond by 
Page 11 
voluntarily including the suggested amendment in the motion.  A “formal” 
amendment requires a second apart from the second to the original motion, 
and must be voted upon prior to a vote on the original motion. 
 
7. Public Input:  That portion of GVAHC meeting during which public comment 
is solicited. 
 
8. Second:  An acknowledgment by one other member of the GVAHC that a 
motion should be considered. 
 
9. Staff:  Employees of the City of Greenwood Village. 
 
10. Objector:  That person or entity requesting that the GVAHC deny or place 
conditions upon the request of an applicant. 
 
11. Proponent:  That person or entity requesting that the GVAHC approve the 
request of an applicant.  
 
Article XVI Amendments to Bylaws 
 
Notice of an amendment to these Bylaws is required at least two weeks prior to a 
regular meeting.  Bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote, a quorum being 
present.
