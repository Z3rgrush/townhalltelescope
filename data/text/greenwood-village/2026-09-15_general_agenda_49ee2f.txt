Executive Summary 
 
The Applicant is requesting approval of the Site Development Plan (SDP) 
to allow for an additional two (2) electric vehicle charging stations on the 
subject property.  A proposal to reduce an approved SDP’s open space 
and parking by less than 5% is considered a minor modification and 
requires review and approval from the Planning and Zoning Commission 
(PZC).

Background 
  
• The subject property was annexed into the City of Greenwood Village in 1976.  
• The original final development plan was approved in October of 1980.  
• Existing historical final development plans (FDPs) are now processed as site development plans (SDPs) pursuant 
to Chapter 16 – Land Development Code.  
• In 2023, the SDP was amended to allow for the construction of an additional drive-through ATM. 
• In 2024, the 3nd Amendment was approved to allow for two (2) EV chargers, a transformer, a switchboard, and 
vegetation screening. 
• In 2025, a recording error with the County resulted in a building permit being issued by the City for the existing 
conditions, with two (2) additional HION EV chargers being installed along with an additional mechanical cabinet 
and an electrical meter. A light pole was also relocated.  
• This proposed plan of the existing condition would be the 4th Amendment.

Analysis 
 
Per the Land Development Code (LDC) Sec. 16-2-50(f)(2), if an applicant wishes to modify an approved SDP, the 
applicant shall submit a request for such modification to the Community Development Department Director. The 
Director shall determine whether the proposed modification is a major, minor, or administrative modification. A 
modification is considered a minor modification if open space and parking requirements are reduced by no more than 
five percent (5%), there is no increase in density, height, or floor area ratio, nor a decrease in any approved setbacks.

the B-1 PUD zone district vs. existing development.

Review Criteria 
In considering an application for an SDP minor modification, the Planning and Zoning Commission (PZC) shall consider the 
following criteria as outlined in LDC Section 16-2-50. PZC is to provide the final decision on the proposed SDP minor 
amendment. The approval criteria and Staff’s findings are presented below. 
Staff has determined that the proposed modification aligns with the goals outlined in the Comprehensive Plan (attachment 
6) for this area. The development meets the Interstate Corridor Planning Area’s objective of providing high-quality open 
space and landscaping. Additionally, staff considers the reduction in open space to be minimal (0.048%) and unlikely to 
impact the quality of the existing open space and landscaping. The modification is compatible with surrounding land uses, 
as a fueling station with EV chargers is located diagonally across from the property and on the east side of the site. The 
proposed SDP modification effectively mitigates any potential adverse impacts, with sufficient screening for the new 
transformer and switchboard. The EV charging-only spaces are installed and previously approved.
