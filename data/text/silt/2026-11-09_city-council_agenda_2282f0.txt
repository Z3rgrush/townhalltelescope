"The Town of Silt is dedicated to providing the highest quality of services to its growing population of over 3,800 citizens.  Silt serves as a "Gateway to the Outdoors", offering its citizens the activities that those who live in larger cities desire.  The Town of Silt Board of Trustees extends a welcoming hand to all its long term residents, newcomers and visitors.  They promise to continue their work of keeping Silt an excellent place to live, a safe environment to raise children, and an area with a high quality of life."
