b) February 2026 Disbursements 
Mayor Ed Pianalto pointed out that the Clerk did not send out the Budget v. Actual Report,

February 2026 Disbursements by Trustee Cory Nasso. The motion was seconded by Trustee

c) Gunnison Met Rec / Western Slope Towers Lease Agreement for marketing of tower services 
located on Town Property 
Mayor Ed Pianalto went over the details of the agreement as it is at this time. Chris Stryker 
joined us on the Zoom meeting to provide information and answer questions. Trustee Cory 
Nasso asked about the emergency services they are providing in CB South. Chris Stryker said 
they are looking in the more remote areas to see if they can help fill a need there. They are 
also working with internet providers and things like that. He thinks within 9 months they will 
have a good idea of who/what might be interested in renting the equipment. Mayor Ed 
Pianalto asked Chris Stryker what he sees happening with over-the-air tv across the area. 
Chris said he sees the customer base has dropped significantly, making it expensive and 
outdated. Trustee Jerra Garetson said she has attended several MetRec meetings over the 
past months and she has heard the exact same things. Town Attorney Hilary Graham 
wonders if the term of the agreement can be backdated to when MetRec acquired the tower. 
Chris Stryker says they would be open to that as long as we don’t take a long time to make an 
agreement. The topic was opened up to Public Comment. No Public Comments. Mayor Ed 
Pianalto proposes postponing this issue to the March Board Meeting.  
 
 
 
d) Liquor License Renewal for the Silver Plume General Store 
Trustee Cory Nasso left the meeting to avoid any conflict of interest.  Mayor Ed Pianalto went 
over the renewal and asked the Board if there are any questions. Trustee Tom Gibb mentions 
that he has not heard any negative feedback on this and he is neutral on the matter. Trustee 
Jerra Garetson has also not heard any negative feedback. Mayor Ed Pianalto has had the 
same experience. Open to public comment – there were none. Trustee Jerra Garetson made 
a motion to accept the liquor license renewal for the Silver Plume General Store. The motion 
was seconded by Mayor Ed Pianalto. All in favor, motion carried 3-0. 
 
e) AN ORDINANCE OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF PITKIN, COLORADO, 
ADOPTING THE TOWN OF PITKIN, COLORADO, ZONING CODE 2026   
Trustee Tom Gibb started by telling us the long, meticulous process of revising the 
Zoning Code the last time. He does not believe we should be considering passing this 
revision until the same amount of care and meticulousness can be put into it this time 
as well. He does not feel this is ready to be passed yet. Specifically, the RV section. 
Trustee Jerra Garetson has no problem with not including the RV section at all. Trustee 
Cory Nasso has heard some vegetive feedback from towns people and he is also fine 
with removing the RV section as well. Mayor Ed Pianalto has also heard some of the 
negative opinions and he made a point of stating the RV Park was only going to be 
allowed in the business district(s), it would not be allowed in residential areas at all. 
He also pointed out that, because of spacing guidelines and restrictions, there would 
only be 2-4 RV’s allowed. Mayor Ed Pianalto wants everyone to remember that the 
idea of adding RV Parks to the list could help to restrict or limit the camping 
restrictions throughout town. And, we will still have “accessory uses” to personal 
property. That being said, he is open to removing it from the Zoning Code if that is 
what everyone thinks we should do.  
Mayor Ed Pianalto opened the topic up to Public Comments: 
Jesse Garetson made a public comment. 
Molly Seigert made a public comment via Zoom. 
Ramon Reed made a public comment. 
End Public Comments. 
Trustee Cory Nasso thinks we should just remove the RV Parks from the Zoning Code 
completely. Town Attorney Hilary Graham will remove the RV Park section from the 
business district and anything else it affects.  
Mayor Ed Pianalto brought up that he has heard some negative feedback regarding the 
Planned Unit Development. But he wants to be clear that there would be stringent 
rules and plans that would be specific for each PUD and there would be a process that 
would include voting. 
Open to Public Comments: 
Pete Olsen made a public comment. 
Jesse Garetson made a public comment. 
Public comments closed. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto asked the Board if they want to leave the Planned Development 
section or remove it. Trustee Cory Nasso doesn’t have a strong opinion. Trustee Jerra 
Garetson is fine with taking out the “mixed uses” but she doesn’t want us to tie up the 
future with so many regulations that we remove all our freedom or wiggle room. 
Trustee Tom Gibb likes developing but he thinks the “mixed use” might create more 
confusion rather than clarification. Trustee Cory Nasso thinks it would be a good idea 
to remove the terminology “mixed uses” from the Planned Development section of 
the Zoning Code. The Board agrees to strike the “mixed uses” verbiage from the 
Zoning Code. However, Town Attorney Hilary Graham thinks that could complicate 
things more.  
Public Comment by Pete Olsen. 
The idea for Town Attorney Hilary Graham is to remove the “mixed uses” and change it 
to say residential PD and business PD. 
Public Comment by Bob Taylor. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto asked the Board if they want to revisit or make any changes to the 
sign section, per Bob Taylor’s request. Trustee Tom Gibb asked if we can pass the other 
Ordinances, required by the State, separate from the Zoning Code. Mayor Ed Pianalto 
said they can be passed separate from the Zoning Code. Trustee Jerra Garetson thinks 
all signs are tacky but she thinks we should leave the sign ordinance as it is for now. 
Pete Olsen made a public comment.  
 
f) First Reading: AN ORDINANCE OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF PITKIN, 
COLORADO ADOPTING BY REFERENCE TO THE 2025 COLORADO WILDFIRE RESILIENCY 
CODE AND THE 2018 INTERNATIONAL RESIDENTIAL CODE AND PROVIDING PENALTIES 
FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF  
The Board of Trustees will also consider setting a public hearing for further 
consideration of this ordinance before second reading  
Mayor Ed Pianalto did the first reading. This ordinance has to be passed/adopted next 
month, otherwise the County will take this over from the Town of Pitkin, which we do 
not want to happen. It has to be passed by April 1st 2026. Motion to acknowledge the 
first reading made by Trustee Tom Gibb. Seconded by Trustee Jerra Garetson. All in 
favor, motion carried 4-0.  
Motion to have the Public Hearing with the second reading at the March 16, 2026 
Regular Board Meeting by Trustee Jerra Garetson. The motion was seconded by 
Trustee Tom Gibb. All in favor, motion carried 4-0. 
g) First Reading: AN ORDINANCE OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF PITKIN, 
COLORADO ADOPTING BY REFERENCE THE 2023 COLORADO ELECTRIC READY AND 
SOLAR READY CODE AND THE 2021 INTERNATIONAL ENERGY CONSERVATION CODE AND 
PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF 
The Board of Trustees will also consider setting a public hearing for further 
consideration of this ordinance before second reading.  
Mayor Ed Pianalto did the first reading. This ordinance has to be adopted by July 1, 
2026, otherwise we will be forced to adopt the 2024 Low Carbon Energy Code, which 
is all electric and would not be great for Pitkin.  
Trustee Tom Gibb clarified this applies to all building permits issued after the date of 
June 1, 2026. Motion that we had the first reading by Mayor Ed Pianalto. The morion 
was seconded by Trustee Tom Gibb. All are in favor, motion carried 4-0. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto motions to have the second reading and a public hearing on March 
16, 2026 at the Regular Board Meeting. The motion was seconded by Trustee Jerra 
Garetson. All  in favor, motion carried 4-0.
