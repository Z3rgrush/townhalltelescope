• Discuss with possible action: Gunnison Met Rec / Western Slope Towers Lease Agreement for 
marketing of tower services located on Town Property. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto gave a short summary of what this lease agreement entails. He has a few 
concerns with the current lease agreement. Western Slope wants 18 months at no charge to 
market it, then they will pay the town a certain amount of payment each month, but Mayor 
Ed Pianalto feels that amount is too low. The town attorney, Hilary Graham, feels that the 
payments do seem low and the 18 months with no payments seems like too much. Her 
recommendation is to ask for a little bit more. Trustee Tom Gibb asked how much land is 
there where the tower is, in the lease agreement is shows 2,500 square feet. Trustee Cory 
Nasso agrees 18 months seems like a long time with no rent being paid, but he doesn’t want 
to see us turn it down because of that if it can become a help to the Town. Trustee Jerra 
Garetson mentioned that we should be sure to include clean up when the site gets 
decommissioned, Mayor Ed Pianalto said MetRec will be on the hook for the clean up if 
there ends up being no tenant.  
Open to public comments: 
Ramon Reed made a public comment. 
Jesse Garetson made a public comment. 
Pete Olsen made a public comment.  
Trustee Cory Nasso asked Town Attorney Hilary Graham to clarify on the lease agreement, page 1 
under section 2: Options – She said the first 18 months would be completely free, but then if the 
Western Slope needs to extend another 18 months because they don’t have a tenant yet, then they 
would pay $1,000 for that second “option period”. Mayor Ed Pianalto made a point that we want to 
be sure and add to the contract that Western Slope will clean up the site if/when there is no tenant. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto proposes that we change the lease from 18 months to 12 months free of charge, 
after that time the first $1,000 payment would be made if no tenant has been found at that time. 
Trustee Cory Nasso wants to make sure the Town is not getting taken advantage of while also not 
asking too much and creating a situation where Western Slope just removes the equipment and 
moves out.  
Open back up to public comments: 
Pete Olsen made a public comment.  
Town attorney Hilary Graham mentioned we should back date the lease to November or December, 
whenever they took control of the equipment lease. Trustee Jerra Garetson thinks we need to 
investigate this more. Trustee Cory Nasso will confer with a friend of his who has experience with 
this type of stuff and Mayor Ed Pianalto will speak with Chris Stryker to get more information so we 
will hold off on a vote on this for now and bring it back up for a vote in the next meeting in February. 
Town Attorney Hilary Graham thinks that sounds like a good plan. 
 
• Discuss with possible action: Zoning Code updates.  THIS ITEM CONTAINS A PUBLIC HEARING 
FOR ORDINANCE ADOPTION OF THE 2026 ZONING CODE. 
Went over red line items that changed at the last meeting before open to public hearing.  
There was a public comment by Pete Olsen.  
Town attorney Hilary Graham clarified that those who were out of compliance with the previous 
zoning code will continue to be out of compliance – the updated code will not grandfather-in those 
who are out of compliance at the time of adoption of the new code.  
 
Public Hearing opened up at 8:19pm  
Comments will be limited to 3 minutes each.  
 
Molly Seigert made a public comment. 
Pete Olsen made a public comment. 
Ramon Reed made a public comment. 
Jesse Garetson made a public comment.  
Public Hearing is closed at 8:42pm. 
 
Regarding the CCR-8-10-10-9 that Pete Olsen brought up in his public comment, Mayor Ed Pianalto 
asked town attorney Hilary Graham if that is applicable. She would like more time to look at this and 
will get back to us, we may want to wait to adopt this until she has had more time to review, 
although she does feel the new zoning code meets all minimum requirements.  
 
Rachel New made a public comment. 
 
Trustee Tom Gibb thinks this creates more confusion than clarification. He does not believe that the 
people in the Town of Pitkin want an RV Park, so he suggests we leave the RV Park issue up to a 
public vote. Regarding the CCR-10-10-9, he says the requirements therein make it unfeasible to have 
an RV park here because it would just be too small a space to be able to camp on or do anything 
with. He thinks we need to do more work on this and it is not passable as it is currently written. 
Mayor Ed Pianalto added that, because of space restrictions, an RV Park would likely only be able to 
hold 2-3 RV’s, so he agrees it will be limited space. Trustee Jerra Garetson is willing to work on this 
more and is also willing to put it up to a public vote. Trustee Cory Nasso would like to research some 
of the topics brought up tonight and not vote on this until February’s Meeting. Motion to table this 
discussion for the 2026 Zoning Code until February Town Council Meeting by Trustee Jerra Garetson. 
Motion seconded by Trustee Cory Nasso.  
Pete Olsen made a public comment. 
All are in favor; motion is carried 4-0.
