i) Consideration of entering a Partnership or Cost-Share Agreement to perform Operations and 
Maintenance with the USFS for the Limekiln Reservoir (Pitkin Kiddie Pond) 
Mayor Ed Pianalto was contacted by Shannon at the USFS, saying the Forest service is trying 
to get out of the pond and lake business, and she asked if we would be interested in doing 
something like that. We would be signing an Operations and Maintenance agreement, where 
we take over the maintenance but the liability will still be with the Forest Service, not the 
Town. They want to do a 5 year agreement. CPW would still be stocking the pond, nothing 
would change there. The inlet to the pond may eventually need some repair, as one example.  
Mayor Ed Pianalto has had some feedback that there may be some danger of them draining 
the pond and just dumping the water directly into the ditch. He, of course, would not want to 
see that happen, but he also doesn’t necessarily want to take over the pond maintenance. 
Trustee Cory Nasso would like more information, he is skeptical of working with the forest 
service on this. Trustee Jerra Garetson would like to point out that she doesn’t think she’s 
ever seen the Forest Service doing maintenance on the pond in all of her 51 years here in 
Pitkin. 
Public Comment from Jesse Garetson. 
We will let this sit and wait for another reach out from Shannon at the USFS. No further 
action is needed.
