• Discuss with possible action: Resolution – 6  providing that certain elected and/or appointed 
officials in The Town of Pitkin shall be deemed not to be “Employees” within the meaning of 
the workers’ compensation laws. 
Trustee Tom Gibb would like clarification on who is excluded – Mayor Ed Pianalto explained 
those who would be excluded. We were hoping the change to paying some commissioners 
and such, Town Attorney Hilary Graham explained that simply paying them makes them an 
“employee” for WC purposes, so if we pay them, we must cover them with the WC 
Insurance. 
Open for Public Comments. 
Public Comment made by Jesse Garetson. 
Motion to pass the Resolution as it is written regarding WC with CIRSA by Trustee Cory 
Nasso, Seconded by Trustee Jerra Garetson, Motion carried 4-0.  
 
• Discuss with possible action: Appointing Josh Ackerman to the position of Environmental 
Health Agent. 
Joshua Ackerman stepped up and spoke as to his interest in the EHA position for the Town 
of Pitkin. He also requests to be reimbursed for the required training. There are no 
questions from the Board at this time.  
Mayor Ed Pianalto opened it up to public comment. 
Jesse Garetson made a public comment. 
Public comment over Zoom by Pete Olsen. 
Motion to appoint Joshua Ackerman as the new Town of Pitkin Environmental Health Agent 
was made by Mayor Ed Pianalto. Seconded by Trustee Cory Nasso. Motion carried 4-0.
