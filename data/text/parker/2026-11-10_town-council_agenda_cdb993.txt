MEMORANDUM

Bryce Matthews, Assistant Director - Planning 
Chris Hudson, Deputy Director of Engineering 
    
DATE: November 10, 2026 
    
SUBJECT: Parker Road-Mainstreet Conceptual Design Project Update 
     
  
ITEM OVERVIEW:  
The Parker Road (CO-83) and Mainstreet intersection requires operational improvements to address 
current and future mobility, safety, and connectivity needs while supporting downtown Parker’s 
continued growth and vitality. 
 
Since the April 2026 Town Council study session update, the Parker Road (CO-83) and Mainstreet 
Operational Improvements 10% Conceptual Design project has advanced through additional technical 
analysis, stakeholder coordination, and public outreach. Based on the extensive evaluation of the three 
initial alternatives and input received, the project team has identified the Dual Quadrant Roadway 
(DQR) as the Preliminarily Identified Preferred Alternative (PIPA). 
 
The DQR alternative offers several key benefits: 
 
•    Improves intersection operations and traffic circulation through additional roadway 
     connections 
•    Enhances pedestrian safety, comfort, and east–west connectivity across Parker Road 
•    Supports downtown accessibility, walkability, and future redevelopment opportunities 
•    Minimize impacts to existing buildings and O'Brien Park while providing opportunities 
     for improved park access 
•    Offers a more financially feasible path toward implementation through lower 
     anticipated costs and phased opportunities – while recognizing that cost was not a 
     primary factor in its selection 
 
Staff and project consultant, Kimley-Horn, request Council’s input and direction to continue advancing 
the Preliminarily Identified Preferred Alternative, complete the conceptual design study, and guide future 
planning and implementation efforts. 
  
BACKGROUND AND ANALYSIS:  
Since the April 2026 Town Council update, the project team has: 
 
•    Conducted outreach with stakeholders such as Town departments, CDOT, property / 
     business owners, residents, and other stakeholders 
•    Refined the three alternatives  

Page 2 
•    Completed additional traffic and environmental analyses  
•    Further screening against the project’s Purpose and Need statement - the ‘North Star’ 
     guiding project decisions 
 
The Level 2 Alternatives Analysis (final screening) evaluated each alternative against the Purpose and 
Need statement, considering traffic operations, safety, connectivity, property impacts, downtown 
character, economic development, environmental considerations, and cost. 
 
The DQR emerged as the preliminarily preferred alternative based on its overall performance and ability 
to balance transportation improvements with the Town's long-term vision for a connected, walkable, and 
vibrant downtown. 
 
The preferred alternative remains conceptual. Future planning and design phases will require additional 
coordination with CDOT, property owners, and other stakeholders to determine roadway alignments, 
address access and redevelopment considerations, integrate O'Brien Park planning, and identify 
funding and phasing opportunities. 
 
The project team anticipates completing the 10% conceptual design study by the end of 2026, with 
subsequent design and implementation phases dependent on future funding and agency coordination. 
  
FINANCIAL AND POLICY IMPACTS:  
None. 
 
COUNCIL DISCUSSION:  
Staff and the project consultant, Kimley-Horn, request Town Council input on the Preliminarily Identified 
Preferred Alternative and general support for advancing the Dual Quadrant Roadway as the 
Preliminarily Identified Preferred Alternative for completion of the 10% conceptual design study.
