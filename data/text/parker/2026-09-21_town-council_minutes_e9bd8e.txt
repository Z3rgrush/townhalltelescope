Comment
I would like to request that the Town of Parker conduct a Traffic
Study along Mainstreet between Jordan Road and Twenty Mile
to address speed, and traffic calming possibilities to help
increase safety for our communities. The Clarke Farms HOA
recently received a violation warning for noncompliance in the
landscaping of the median between Motsenbocker Road and
Twenty Mile Road. We have now experienced two fatal
crashes in as many years, approximately 15 feet away from
each other, where young drivers were traveling at excessive
speeds and lost control of their vehicles. Both drivers crashed
into our median trees resulting in death. While we understand
that speed was a factor in both crashes, we also believe that
there are measures that the Town can implement to increase
safety in this area. We are sending a formal request to the
Town to consider additional traffic calming measures along this
stretch of Mainstreet. As you know, this corridor has
experienced a significant increase in pedestrian and vehicular
traffic in the last 40 years. In fact, no fewer than 10 new
subdivisions have been built on the western side of Parker
since Clarke Farms was first built. New commercial
developments continue to grow along Jordan Road and
Mainstreet, including restaurants, gas stations, shopping
centers, schools, and healthcare facilities. We also now serve
two elementary schools in which our children walk across the
intersection two times a day. This significant increase in traffic
deserves a second look as capacity and speed continue to
grow in the more than half- mile stretch of roadway between the
intersections of Motsenbocker and Twenty Mile. With the
upcoming Mainstreet improvements and other significant
developments along the east and west side of the corridor, we
believe that this stretch of Mainstreet deserves another look
into traffic calming, safety infrastructure, and built
improvements to encourage slower, safer speeds along this
roadway. We all know that the owness of safety cannot reliably
exist only for drivers - it must also be on municipalities and
agencies that build and design roads that visually or physically
restrict speeds to safe levels. In the 20 years of my residency
3
in Clarke Farms, I have witness many incidents of excessive
speeds along this corridor - not just among our young drivers. 
As the Town threatens to fine Clarke Farms for cleaning up the
visual appearance of this median, we are faced with the need
to remove now TWO makeshift memorials for deceased drivers
less than 20 feet away from each other. We are also now
required to replace the trees, irrigation, and landscaping that
has been damaged each time someone has died on these
medians. In fairness and in the interest of keeping our
community safe and protected, we are requesting that the
Town of Parker invest our tax dollars into not just new
developments, but into increasing safety and infrastructure
improvements for already built communities that exist here. 
Please consider an in-depth traffic study along this corridor and
seriously consider mutually beneficial infrastructure that
encourages safer driving along this more than 1/2 mile corridor
into downtown Parker. We, as an HOA, cannot continue to
replant trees for young people to die on. We believe that the
Town cannot in good conscience ask our HOA to remove
memorial after memorial in our neighborhood while also not
trying to prevent them in the first place. Thank you.
