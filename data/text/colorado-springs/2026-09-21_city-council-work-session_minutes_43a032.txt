5.A. Colorado Springs Chamber and EDC - 2026 mid-year Lodgers and 
Auto Rental Tax (LART) contract update to City Council
  Presenter:  
Dani Barger, Chief Operating Officer
Colorado Springs Chamber and EDC
Colorado Springs Chamber + EDC LART Council pres - Sept 2026Attachments:
Dani Barger, Chief Operating Officer Colorado Springs Chamber and 
EDC introduced the 2026 mid-year Lodgers and Auto Rental Tax (LART) 
contract update and provided an overview of: LART Powered by Tourism, 
Economic Development on the Road, National Earned Media Results, and 
Familiarization Tour Business Meeting Planners OCT 27-29, 2026. She 
reviewed the results of the national messaging and branding for Colorado 
Springs and noted the launch of the national website. 
Councilmember Donelson asked what negative stories are out there about 
Colorado Springs. Ms. Barger stated the negative stories are not 
specifically about Colorado Springs, but Colorado as a whole and the 
intent of this tour is to be able to tell the story of Colorado Springs in our 
own voice.  
Ms. Barger stated Colorado Springs has earned 55 national, trade & local 
placements and 213.8 million unique visitors reached. 
Councilmember Donelson asked how much media coverage the Pikes 
Peak Ascent and marathon gets and stated the event would be a good one 
to focus on. Ms. Barger stated that specific event was not pitched but they 
are always looking for unique events we host for reporters to come out and 
cover. 
Councilmember Donelson asked what the amount the Chamber and EDC 
received from LART. Ms. Barger stated it is $750,000.

6.A. Quarterly Financial Report
  Presenter:  
Charae McDaniel, Chief Financial Officer
Quarterly Financial Update - 09-21-26Attachments:
Charae McDaniel, Chief Financial Officer, introduced the Quarterly 
Financial Report and provided an overview of the Sales & Use Tax 
Overview General Fund, LART and RMJ Tax Overview, 2026 Revenue over 
Budget, 2026 Expenditure Savings, City Turnover Rates, 2027 Budget and 
Key Dates. 
Councilmember Leinweber asked about a ballpark figure where the City 
will end up at the end of the year. Ms. McDaniel stated the year end 
estimate is coming in higher than budget estimates and that revenue has 
been pulled into the 2027 city budget estimates. 
Councilmember Donelson jokingly asked if the City would be receiving 
200% year over year growth on Retail Marijuana taxes from now on . Ms. 
McDaniel stated it would not.
Councilmember Donelson asked about the removal of the November 25th 
furlough day and how it would affect employees already having planned for 
that day off. Ms. McDaniel stated that is the next item on the agenda and 
should be discussed then. 
President Pro Tem Risley asked for clarification on the 911 Authority 
budgeting cycle. Ms. McDaniel stated since the budgeting request came in 
at such a different amount than the previous year, and in conversations with 
the police department, they did not feel the number would be approved and 
were cautious on the outlook and was not included in the budget. 
Councilmember Henjum stated there have been numerous City building 
roof replacements due to hail happening all over the city and asked if they 
can be covered by insurance. Ms. McDaniel stated they are not related to 
the most recent hail damage and have been on the repair list for a while 
now. 
Councilmember Donelson stated the 2.275 million comes from the 911 
fund and asked if it is coming out of the reserve or the revenue from this 
year. Ms. McDaniel stated the 911 charge from calls goes to the authority 
and there was a change in practice how much per call center employee

7.A. An Ordinance Amending Ordinance 25-95 (2026 Budget Appropriation 
Ordinance) for a Supplemental Appropriation to multiple funds for the 
elimination of the November 25, 2026, Scheduled Furlough Day
  Presenter:  
Wayne Williams, Chief of Staff
Charae McDaniel, Chief Financial Officer
Ordinance - Nov furlough day elimination
Furlough day cancellation for Nov 25 - presentation
Attachments:
Wayne Williams, Chief of Staff presented the ordinance Amending 
Ordinance 25-95 (2026 Budget Appropriation Ordinance) For A 
Supplemental Appropriation To Multiple Funds For The Elimination Of The 
November 25, 2026, Scheduled Furlough Day. 
He stated revenues have exceeded 2026 projections and would like to use 
the excess of $330,800 of general funds to eliminate the November 25th 
furlough day. 
Councilmember Donelson stated he supports this. 
President Pro Tem Risley asked for clarification on the options employees 
could use to take November 25th off if they had already planned it off. Mr. 
Williams stated that employees could use a vacation day, sick day, or take 
an unpaid day off if they do not have enough time off accrued. 
President Pro Tem Risley asked about salary saving the city has 
recognized year to date on budgeted positions that have gone vacant. Ms. 
McDaniel stated the salary savings were already accounted for in the 
budgets for each department.
