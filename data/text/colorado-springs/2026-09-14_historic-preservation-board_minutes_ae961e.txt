3.  Communications
William Gray - Senior Planner
Wiliam Gray, Senior Planner, explained that the City had requested the 
Board consider moving its meeting time earlier in the day to align with 
other boards and commissions, most of which meet during standard 
business hours. He invited Board discussion on potential changes to the 
meeting start time or day. Board members shared availability and 
perspectives. Some were flexible and others raised concerns about 
attendance if meetings began earlier than the current late-afternoon 
schedule. Members also discussed the impact on applicants and members 
of the public, who may need to take time away from work to present their 
cases. After weighing preferences, including morning versus afternoon 
options, the majority of members supported retaining Mondays but shifting 
the meeting to 3:30 PM, with the understanding that the change could 
be revisited if it proved unworkable.
Mr. Gray updated the Board on implementation of the recently adopted 
Historic Resources Survey Plan. He noted difficulty advancing 
prioritization discussions due to sporadic attendance and emphasized the 
need for full participation at a future meeting. The survey planning topic will 
return as a later agenda item.
Mr. Gray reported strong interest from both the Historic Neighborhoods 
Partnership and Historic Preservation Alliance in revising the City’s 
preservation ordinance. He outlined three broad components under 
consideration, establishing a clearer process for designating individual 
landmarks; reviewing procedures for creating landmarks and preservation

Mr. Gray stated that staff had been directed by the Planning Director to 
pursue this effort. He said the City intends to prepare one or more scopes

intends to bring draft scopes and RFP language to the Board for review.
Mr. Gray reported that staff are developing a complete redesign of the 
historic preservation webpage, incorporating prior recommendations from 
Board Members Judy and Sarah. A draft mock-up will be shared with the 
Board at a future meeting.
Mr. Gray advised that, due to the workload and lack of design review items, 
there would likely be no October meeting. The Board should expect a 
November meeting including one Report of Acceptability and several 
discussion items.
Mr. Gray stated that at the November meeting the Board would elect a new 
Chair and Vice Chair, noting that current Chair Christine intends to step 
down after several years of service.
Sarah Brewen, Citty Attorney’s Office, introduced herself as the Board’s 
new legal advisor, succeeding Young Shin. She explained general voting 
rules related to meeting minutes and clarified that members may vote to 
approve minutes regardless of attendance at the prior meeting, provided 
they have reviewed them.
