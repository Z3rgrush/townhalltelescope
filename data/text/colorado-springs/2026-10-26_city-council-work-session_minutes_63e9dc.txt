Alexandru Kovacs, Director of Aviation, Colorado Springs Airport, 
presented the Resolution recognizing a partial donation of approximately 
174 acres of land to the Colorado Springs Municipal Airport. He provided 
an overview of the site location, partial donations, phased closings, and 
staff recommendation.
President Pro Tem Risley asked what the land is slated to be used for. Mr. 
Kovacs stated it does not have a specific designation as of right now, but it 
was intended to be used for future development and protecting the airport 
overall.
Councilmember Williams requested additional background information 
regarding the development of this area. Wayne Williams, Chief of Staff, 
stated the expansion of the airport business park started in May 2023, with 
the leasing of land to provide money to assist with ongoing operations, and 
being able to control what goes next to an airport is an important aspect of 
preserving the airport’s viability.

Department, presented the Ordinance amending Section 304 (Accessory 
Uses) of Part 3 (Use-Specific Standards) of Article 3 (Use Regulations) of 
Chapter 7 (Unified Development Code UDC) of the City Code pertaining to 
height allowances for Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs). He provided a 
brief overview of the background of ADUs, project timeline, application 
review criteria, and optional motions.
Councilmember Henjum asked if the Planning Commission recommended

Councilmember Henjum asked what concerns citizens had with this 
proposed Ordinance. Mr. Malpica stated at the Planning Commission 
meeting there was not anyone who signed up to speak in opposition to the 
Ordinance however, there was a letter provided which had concerns 
regarding an ADU being attached to a garage rather than above the 
garage, but the Planning Department wanted to keep some degree of 
flexibility.
Councilmember Casey asked if an ADU would be permitted to be taller 
than the principal residence. Mr. Malpica stated it would not be because 
the building height limit is twenty-five feet.

reasonable next step for ADUs and he will be supporting it.
