Nadine Hensler - Planning Commission Chair

historically set for June but delayed to fall for several reasons. She stated

importance of careful review, noting some past transcription inaccuracies.

with an electrical demand estimated above 100 MW, was proceeding 
through a development plan review. He said this project had significant

Mr. Walker also mentioned the updated City Planning Dashboard, which 
had been distributed in person and was posted online. He reviewed 
ongoing work related to the AnnexCOs, Housing Action Plan, UDC 
updates, and comprehensive plan sections on growth and water. Mr. 
Walker noted that statutory deadlines would not be met and that work would 
extend into next year.

centers. Mr. Walker explained that research had been completed, including 
examining definitions and noise considerations. He stated that next steps 
depended partly on political leadership and the upcoming April 
election. He anticipated future public input opportunities and possibly 
reviewing mayor issued standards that were not binding to the

Draft CPC Minutes 9.9.26 - Lowell Commons
Staff Presentation_Lowell Commons
Signed Ordinance No. 26-58
Attachments:
William Gray, Senior Planner, presented the Lowell Commons application, 
covering 1.20 acres at 202, 251, and 252 East Las Animas Street. The 
request sought to amend the existing PDZ (approved 1999; amended 
2001) to support a dense mixed-use, affordable-housing-focused project 
within the historic Lowell neighborhood framework. Mr. Gray described the 
highly urban context, existing mixed uses, and transit accessibility. The 
applicant sought up to 184 dwelling units per acre, producing 
approximately 250 residential units, along with ground-floor commercial 
space and a maximum building height of 65 feet. The project would adhere 
to the established Lowell Urban and Architectural Design Standards.
Mr. Gray reported both support and opposition in public comments. 
Supporters cited the project as aligned with original neighborhood vision 
and affordable housing goals. Opponents expressed concerns typical for
