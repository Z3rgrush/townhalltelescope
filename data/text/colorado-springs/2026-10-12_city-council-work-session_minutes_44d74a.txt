City Clerk General Business License Fees - Exhibit A - 10-12-26

Sarah B. Johnson, City Clerk, presented the Resolution setting the fees for 
the general business licenses issued by the City Clerk’s Office. She stated 
the Resolution would set the fees for most of the general business licenses 
which have not been increased since January 1, 2019. Ms. Johnson stated 
the proposed 2027 fee schedule would change the business license fees, 
with the exception of liquor and marijuana licenses, to a three-year 
phase-in, beginning January 1, 2027, designed to reach ninety percent 
cost recovery by 2029. She stated fees  for 2030 will include a one-time 
“catch-up” adjustment based on the total cumulative increase in the 
Denver/Boulder/Greeley CPI from 2027 through 2030, ensuring the fee fully 
reflects inflation over the initial phase-in period. Ms. Johnson stated 
beginning in 2031, annual fee adjustments will reflect the needed 
adjustment, however any future fee amounts will not exceed the overall 
costs of the City Clerk’s licensing service.
Councilmember Henjum asked why the City does not implement one 
hundred percent recovery. Ms. Johnson stated it is due to the City’s choice 
to use its home rule status to put these licenses and regulations in place
