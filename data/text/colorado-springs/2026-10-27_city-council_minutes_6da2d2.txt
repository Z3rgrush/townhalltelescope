6.  Mayor's Business
Wayne Williams, Chief of Staff, stated they were able to welcome the 
Colorado Stampede to the City on Sunday, October 25, 2026, and 
highlighted recent recognitions received by the community, including 
Colorado Springs being named the best City in America to start a small 
business and the Colorado Springs Airport being ranked the eighth-best 
small airport in the United States by USA Today. He also announced that 
the City received the 2026 Department of the Air Force Community 
Partnership Award, presented at the Association of Defense Communities 
forum in Tampa, Florida which recognizes the City's partnership with the 
U.S. Air Force Academy on stormwater management through an 
Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) previously approved by City Council. 
He congratulated the Department of Public Works for its role in receiving 
the award and noted that the recognition reflects the City's ongoing support 
of the military community. Mr. Williams also reminded the public about the 
upcoming Veterans Parade, which will take place on Tejon Street from St. 
Vrain Street to Vermijo Avenue and announced the conclusion of the 2C 
paving season and invited the public to a celebration of the season's 
completion at the intersection of Stanton Street and Cragmor Road. He 
expressed appreciation to the Public Works Department, contractors, and 
partners for their work in delivering on the commitments made to voters 
through the 2C program.
