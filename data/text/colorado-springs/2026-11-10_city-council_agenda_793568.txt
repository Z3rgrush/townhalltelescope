PUBLIC ACCESS & PARTICIATION
HOW TO VIEW THE MEETING
You can watch the meeting live on SpringsTV 
at coloradosprings.gov/springstv. The meeting 
will also be streamed on Facebook Live 
through the Colorado Springs City Council 
page @coscitycouncil.
If you prefer to watch on television, the 
meeting is broadcast on Springs TV on 
Comcast Channel 18 or 880 (HD). Stratus IQ 
customers can view the broadcast on 
Channels 76 or 99 through their streaming 
service.
HOW TO COMMENT ON AGENDA ITEMS
If you would like to sign up to speak and 
provide Public Comment on any agenda item, 
please contact allcouncil@coloradosprings.gov 
or 719-385-5480. You may also email 
comments to prior to the meeting
at which an item appears.
Topics shall be relevant and germane to City 
Council business and shall be related to items 
over which the City has jurisdiction and those 
items which are not pending quasi-judicial 
matters.
GENERAL INFORMATION
Estimated times for agenda items are provided 
for planning purposes only and do not guarantee 
when items will be heard. Items may take more 
or less time than listed. City Council may also 
adjust the order of items during the meeting at 
any time.
ADA ACCOMODATIONS
In accordance with the ADA and Title VI, anyone 
requiring an accomodation or languages services 
to participate in the City Council Meeting should 
make the request as soon as possible but not 
later than 48 hours before the scheduled 
meeting.
Please contact Legislative Services at 
719-385-5986 to coordinate.
For accessibility assistance please contact 
Human Resources or use Relay Colorado by 
dialing: 711 or 800-659-3656
