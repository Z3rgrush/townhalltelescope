  Presenter:

Ordinance - RMJ funding South Nevada Security
Memo-SNevadaAvSurveyArea_Final051426

Signed Ordinance 26-36
Attachments:
Jariah Walker, Executive Director, Colorado Springs Urban Renewal 
Authority (CSURA), presented the Ordinance for a supplemental 
appropriation to the Retail Marijuana Tax Fund in the amount of $458,000 
to fund a contract with the CSURA to provide security services in the South 
Nevada corridor. 
Councilmember Donelson stated he will be voting in support of this item 
because the Chief of the Colorado Springs Police Department stated that 
even with this funding, they would not be able to hire the number of police 
officers that they would like to have in the City.
Councilmember Henjum stated she believes the marijuana retailers from 
where this money originates should have been invited to be a part of this 
discussion.

she believes the money should toward housing people experiencing 
homelessness.
Motion by Councilmember Williams, seconded by Councilmember Rainey 
Jr., that the Ordinance approving the supplemental appropriation to the 
Retail Marijuana Tax Fund in the amount of $458,000 to fund a contract 
with the Colorado Springs Urban Renewal Authority to provide security 
services in the South Nevada corridor be finally passed. The motion 
passed by a vote of 7-2-0-0
