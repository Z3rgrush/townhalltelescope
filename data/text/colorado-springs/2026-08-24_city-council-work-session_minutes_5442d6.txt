5.  Presentations for General Information
5.A. Visit COS - 2026 mid-year Lodgers and Auto Rental Tax (LART) 
contract update to City Council
  Presenter:  
Doug Price, President and CEO, Visit COS
26-314
2026 Q1 & Q2 VCOS KPI_City Council PresentationAttachments:
April Perez, Director of Finance, President and CEO, VisitCOS, presented 
the 2026 mid-year Lodgers and Auto Rental Tax (LART) contract update. 
She provided an overview of the vendor collections through May 2026, 
revenue per available room (RevPAR), trade shows attended, total room 
nights booked for group travel and estimated economic impact. 
Councilmember Donelson asked what a domestic tour is. Ms. Perez stated 
the majority of domestic tours are motor coach operators and the biggest 
party they work with is American Bus Association (ABA) which provide 
historical tours.
Melissa Williams, Director of Marketing, VisitCOS, went over the 
VisitCOS.com website Key Performance Indicators (KPI), social media 
activity, new followers, and social media traffic to website.
Councilmember Henjum asked how they are addressing Artificial 
Intelligence (AI) and implementing other ways to measure website clicks. 
Ms. Williams stated they have been investing time, energy, and resources 
to determine what these tools look for.
Councilmember Donelson asked what the reason is behind the increase in 
YouTube views. Ms. Williams stated they have been prioritizing YouTube 
Shorts.
Councilmember Donelson asked what the primary message is they are 
sending out via all the platforms. Ms. Williams stated it is inviting people to 
come and explore Colorado Springs.
Councilmember Donelson asked if they are highlighting anything in 
particular in 2026. Ms. Williams stated they are working on a music tourism 
strategy with an organization called Mission to Market.
Councilmember Donelson asked why the ABA did not come to the City this 
year. Ms. Perez stated she will provide that information.

Councilmember Donelson asked if there is a goal rate that the hoteliers 
have for the RevPAR. Ms. Perez stated she will provide that information.
Councilmember Donelson asked if tourism is doing great this year. Ms. 
Williams confirmed it is. Alexia Veneration, Director of Communication, 
VisitCOS, stated they do not have the metrics for the summer and the fires 
within the state did impact domestic tourism and outdoor attractions.
Councilmember Casey asked if the 2025 numbers reflect the whole year or

Region (COPPeR), presented the 2026 mid-year Lodgers and Auto Rental
