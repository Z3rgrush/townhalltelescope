Tristan Gearhart, Chief Planning and Financial Officer, Colorado Springs 
Utilities (CSU), presented the Resolution authorizing a term for a lease of 
CSU’s communications network assets. He provided an overview of the 
lease extension economic benefits, cumulative lease value, and net 
present value increase.
Councilmember Donelson stated he supports this item due to longer 
revenue certainty, greater cumulative cash flow, and higher net present 
value and he believes this will result in even better service for customers.
Motion by Councilmember Donelson, seconded by Councilmember Gold, 
that the Resolution authorizing a term for a lease of CSU’s 
communications network assets be adopted. The motion passed by a 
vote of 8-0-1-0
