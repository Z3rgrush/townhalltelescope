2.  Executive Session
2.A. In accord with City Charter art. III, § 3-60(d) and its incorporated 
Colorado Open Meetings Act, C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(a), (b) and (e), the 
City Council, in Open Session, is to determine whether it will hold a 
Closed Executive Session. The issues to be discussed involve 
consultation with the City Attorney for the purposes of (i) receiving legal 
advice and determining positions relative to negotiation regarding a real 
property interest in a portion of Wilson Ranch Park and (ii) receiving 
legal advice regarding refunds of sales and use tax to qualified entities 
for construction projects.
