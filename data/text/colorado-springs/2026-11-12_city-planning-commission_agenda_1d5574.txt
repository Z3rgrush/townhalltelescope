10. Executive Session
If the City Planning Commission requests legal advice from its attorney, an executive 
session may be needed:
 
In accord with City Charter art. III, § 3-60(d) and its incorporated Colorado Open 
Meetings Act, C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4) (b), the City Planning Commission, in Open 
Session, is to determine whether it will hold a Closed Executive Session. The issue to 
be discussed involves consultation with the City Attorney for the purpose of receiving 
legal advice regarding the Special Appeal Hearing for Corporate Ridge Filing No. 1 Lot 
4.
The Chair of the Planning Commission shall poll the Commissioners, and, upon 
consent of two-thirds of the members present, may conduct a Closed Executive 
Session. In the event any Commissioner is participating electronically or telephonically 
in the Closed Executive Session, each Commissioner participating in the Closed 
Executive Session shall affirmatively state for the record that no other member of the 
public not authorized to participate in the Closed Executive Session is present or able 
to hear the matters discussed as part of the Closed Executive Session. If consent to 
the Closed Executive Session is not given, the item may be discussed in Open Session 
or withdrawn from consideration.
