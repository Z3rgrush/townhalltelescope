Applicant Rebuttal
Ms. Quander clarified that the item under review is a specific Development 
Plan modification, not a rezoning, and not a broader referendum on data 
centers, the proposal involves the adaptive reuse of an existing industrial 
building in a long-established industrial corridor that has been zoned to 
allow data centers as a permitted use for approximately thirty years. She 
stated the Development Plan includes thirty generators and thirty-six 
chillers, as shown in the plan on record, the generators do not operate 
regularly; they run only during power outages and undergo periodic testing 
to ensure functionality, therefore, ongoing noise from generators should not 
be expected. Ms. Quander stated multiple iterations of the sound study 
were completed in response to community feedback, including concerns 
regarding dBC, the applicant incorporated additional evaluations and 
mitigation measures into the final study, and the Planning Commission 
added a monitoring and reporting condition, which the applicant accepted. 
She explained that Raeden followed the required development review 
procedures, confirmed that data centers are a permitted use, submitted a 
modification to the Development Plan, responded to Staff comments 
through several rounds of revisions, held community information meetings, 
and made changes based on public concerns which included relocating 
generators, adding a sound wall, and performing additional studies. The 
final plan was reviewed for compliance with the code and was approved. 
She emphasized that the modification is consistent with the Colorado 
Springs Comprehensive Plan and the intent of the BP Zone District, in 
2019, the City identified the Garden of the Gods industrial corridor as an 
appropriate area for industrial uses, data centers are specifically 
referenced in the corridor’s vision as desirable development, and the 
project represents an adaptive reuse of an existing industrial building, 
aligning with both PlanCOS and City Code. Ms. Quander stated that the 
applicant intends to operate transparently, return the building to productive 
use with the necessary power and water infrastructure, a Development 
Agreement and bond are in place to ensure accountability, and as the use 
is permitted by right and the Development Plan meets all applicable 
criteria, applicant's counsel asked City Council not to impose the 
Planning Commission's power condition, and requested that the 
Planning Director’s approval be upheld.
