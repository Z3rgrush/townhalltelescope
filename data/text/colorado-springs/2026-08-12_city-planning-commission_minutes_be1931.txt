at 2001 West Colorado Avenue, located at the intersection of 20th Street

a single-family residence into a restaurant. The variance seeks approval for 
only one off-street parking space where seven spaces would normally be 
required for restaurant use. He noted that the applicant intends to demolish 
the existing detached garage and replace it with a single ADA-accessible 
parking space off the alley. Mr. Gray provided context about the mixed-use 
character of West Colorado Avenue and described site conditions, 
including available on-street parking. He also summarized public

variance.

requesting approval of a non-use variance to reduce the required on-site 
parking for a proposed café that would reuse their family’s historic 1901

but the small lot, the home’s historic features, and their desire to preserve 
the property make adding that many spaces impossible without paving 
over nearly the entire yard. They proposed instead to provide one 
ADA-accessible space off the alley and rely on adjacent on-street parking,

the remaining demand. The applicants emphasized the home’s

parking would destroy features like the cherry tree, the historic well, and the

streetscape design along Colorado Avenue. They concluded by stating that
