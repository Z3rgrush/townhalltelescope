New Castle Climate & Environment Commission Minutes 
July 16, 2026 
 
Attendees: Commissioner Ryan Deyarmond, Commissioner Sarah Benson, Commissioner Kendall Bakich, Chair 
Sharon Morris, and Commissioner Nancy Barnes 
 
Chair Morris called the meeting to order at 5:04 p.m. 
 
Introduction of New Commissioner Sarah Benson 
Chair Morris welcomed Sarah Benson as the newest commissioner. Commissioner Benson is a Roaring Fork 
native and cares deeply about the environment and volunteer work.  
 
Consider New Castle Community Market Participation and Bag Fee Money 
City Clerk Andis informed the commissioners that the Town needs an implementation plan for the 
composting and recycling program at the New Castle Community Market to comply with state audit 
requirements. The CEC will also need to submit a final report documenting the program’s impact. 
 
Chair Morris contacted Rec Center Director Kelley Cox regarding the project plan. Andis confirmed that the 
CEC may proceed with ordering materials. Cox recommended placing recycling and composting bins in two 
designated areas. Commissioners Benson and Deyarmond will transport the materials to the dump after the 
event. Commissioners should arrive at Burning Mountain Park at 4 p.m. for setup. 
 
Action: Chair Morris will procure the 9 -oz compostable cups, two hundred per event, purchase the recycling 
and composting bins, and draft the final report. Commissioner Deyarmond will price metal bins, stencil, and 
paint to paint recycling and composing logos on the bins. Commissioner Bakich will create signs for the bins.  
 
Consider Participation in Burning Mountain Festival Parade 
Commissioner Deyarmond and Chair Morris met to draft and submit the parade application. Commissioners 
discussed ideas for the parade float.  
 
Action: Commissioners Deyarmond and Benson will decorate the float.  
 
Consider New Castle Dump Day Cleanup with Bag Fee Money 
Andis explained that the town can use the plastic bag fee money to help fund the cost of recycling and 
disposal of electronics, tires, paints, and oils at the Town’s October clean-up day. There is approximately 
$12,000 remaining in the bag fee fund.  
 
Motion: Commissioner Deyarmond moved to allocate $5,000 for 2026 and up to $5,000 for 2027 to support 
the town’s annual clean-up day. Commissioner Bakich seconded. Motion passed unanimously.

Budget Update 
Chair Morris reported that there is approximately $12,000 remaining in the bag fee fund.  
 
Consider Business Award and Recognition 
Commissioner Barnes suggested recognizing a New Castle business with a “Guardian of Nature” business 
award for environmental stewardship and sustainability. Commissioners agreed to a “Guardian of 
Nature” spotlight in the quarterly Town newsletter. The commission agreed to feature Salon West in the 
fall, New Castle Coffee in the winter, and West Canyon Nursery in the spring.  
 
Motion: Commissioner Bakich moved to recognize an environmentally sustainable local business as the 
Business of the Season in the Town’s newsletter. Commissioner Deyarmond seconded. Motion passed 
unanimously.  
 
Action: Chair Morris and Commissioner Barnes will interview the business and draft the spotlight.  
 
Approval of June 18 Minutes 
Motion: Commissioner Bakich moved to approve the minutes with the noted changes. Commissioner Benson 
seconded. Motion passed unanimously.  
 
Commissioner Comments 
Commissioner Deyarmond recommended creating a community-created mural event rather than using a solo 
artist to create the mural. Deyarmond suggested a mounted mural on wood rather than painted on a brick. 
Chair Morris suggested a work session to discuss the goals of the mural project.  
 
Adjourn 
The meeting adjourned at 6:22 p.m.
