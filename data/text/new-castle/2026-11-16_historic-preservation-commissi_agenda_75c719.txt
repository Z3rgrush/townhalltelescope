Items for Consideration 
 
Discussion of Videography Project 
Chair Riddile said the project was going well. Chair Riddile said Charles and Angela Ryden 
told more stories after the camera stopped filming. Chair Riddile said she wanted to move 
forward with the next videography subjects.  Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said she would 
schedule with Joe Gamble at Colorado Mountain College (CMC) for Spring of 2027. 
Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said the students at CMC are very respectful and engaged in 
the project.  
Chair Riddile asked if the projects were on the website. Professional Assistant Huster said 
the consent forms were still needed. Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said she would drop 
them off at Town Hall that week.  
Consider Purchasing a Gift Card for Joseph Gamble at CMC.  
Chair Riddile explained to the commission that Joseph Gamble was the professor leading 
the videography project. Commissioner Ruth Fletcher suggested purchasing a gift card for 
one hundred and fifty dollars to the Asian Bistro to thank him for his work.

MOTION: Chair Riddile made a motion to purchase a gift card for Joesph Gamble 
in the sum of one hundred and fifty dollars to the Asian Bistro. Commission 
alternate, Tom Fuller seconded the motion and it passed unanimously. 
Consider applying for Grants to Survey of Historical Properties from Sandborn 
Maps  
Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said the commission should survey previously unsurveyed 
properties deemed eligible by historic maps of New Castle and Sandborn Fire Insurance 
maps. Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said Mayor Hazelton’s maps were be helpful. Chair 
Riddile asked Professional Assistant Huster to confirm the mayor’s availability to meet in a 
work session before November 16, 2026, meeting. Commissioner Fletcher said the grant 
was due mid-January but did not know if they needed to have the properties picked out 
before applying for the grant.  
Commissioner Fletcher asked Professional Assistant Huster if they were required to use 
Pinon Survey again. Professional Assistant Huster said they needed to use one that was 
on the list provided by History Colorado. Tom Fuller said that there were other local 
companies, but Pinon did a good job with the prior survey.  
 
Consider Draft of the CLG (Certified Local Government) Audit 
Commissioner Ruth Fletcher said there was a typo on page eight.  
MOTION: Commissioner Ruth Fletcher made a motion to approve the CLG Audit 
for 2026. Commissioner Tom Fuller seconded the motion and it passed 
unanimously.

Commissioner Comments 
Chair Riddile said the HPC Commission was doing projects outside of the bylaws. Chair 
Riddile said the bylaws state the commission’s role is to approve applicants for historical 
designation. Chair Riddile said the commission would consider discussing the projects and 
goals with Town Council. Professional Assistant Huster said she would mention this to 
Town Clerk Mindy Andis. Chair Riddile proposed cancelling the December 14, 2026, 
meeting.  
 
MOTION: Chair Riddile made a motion to not meet on December 14, 2026. 
Commissioner Richard Fletcher seconded the motion and it passed unanimously. 
Consider Approval of September 21, 2026, Minutes

unanimously.
