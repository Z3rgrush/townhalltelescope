Items for Consideration 
 
Consider Review of Designation Plaque 
Chair Riddile explained that the wrong year was placed on the plaque for the dedication of 
the cemetery. The year that was placed on the plaque was the year 1889, which was the 
first burial. The cemetery was established in 1888. Chair Riddile asked how the budget 
looked for getting a new plaque made. Administrator Reynolds said he has looked at the 
budget and there is money in the budget for a new plaque. 
The commission agreed to have a new plaque made with the year 1888. 
MOTION: Chair Riddile made a motion to approve a new plaque with the year 
1888. Commissioner Richard Fletcher seconded the motion, and it passed 
unanimously. 
Consider Future Videography Subjects 
Chair Riddile said the videography is dome through CMC class. At this time CMC is not 
sure if they are going to have a class or not. Chair Riddile asked the commission to come 
up with some ideas who they would like to interview. She said she would contact Mayor 
Grady Hazelton for some ideas and Commissioner Ruth Flethcer said she would reach out
