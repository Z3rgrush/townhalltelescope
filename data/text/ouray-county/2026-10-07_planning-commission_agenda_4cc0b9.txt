ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT (ADU) . A structure - requiring and receiving a certificate

A. Parcels less than 3 -acres in size may include an ADU with a maximum size of

B. Parcels 3-acres or more in size may include an ADU, with a maximum size of

underlying zoning are limited to one dwelling unit and no ADU is allowed.

Re: Section 2 Definitions Draft Revisions 
 
Dear Commissioners: 
 
First, many thanks for all the hard work and time you and staff have devoted to revising Section 2 as 
requested by the BOCC. Overall, I would agree with the substance of your  efforts in addressing the 
identified issues.  
 
General Comments.  
 
As a general comment, it would be helpful at some point to include the definitions which are not being 
revised with those that are s o that all could  be reviewed together to make sure that they are all 
consistent and make sense together. Also, there will clearly be a need to revise other Sections of the 
Land Use Code in light of the proposed definition changes. For example, the revised definitions provide 
for a  Primary Dwelling Unit, an Accessory Dwelling Unit, and an Accessory Dwelling Long- Term 
Dwelling Unit (i.e. duplex). I assume that not all would be permitted on one lot at  the same time – 
otherwise each lot could contain four livi ng units. The draft definitions  don’t address this issue and 
presumably other issues, so I would suggest that  contemplated changes to the Land Use Code be 
presented sooner rather than later so that the effects of the revised definitions can be best understood. 
 
Specific Comments: 
 
Accessory Dwelling Long- Term Rental Unit (ADLRU). This would allow a  duplex “structure” 
containing two dwelling units. The “ structure” cannot exceed 2000 square feet and can contain 1 to 3 
bedrooms. Shouldn’t it read “ each dwelling unit” can contain 1 to 3 bedrooms? It can’ t be a duplex if 
the “structure” contains only one bedroom since there would only be one dwelling unit, not two. 
Relatedly, is it your intent  that the entire structure –  or each dwelling unit -  not exceed 2000 square 
feet? 
 
Bed and Breakfast. Maximum occupancy could not exceed 20 persons. Should it be clarified that the 
20 person limit includes the owner and family since the four rental bedrooms can be occupied by up to 
four persons per bedroom? 
 
Commercial Outdoor Recreation. Why are overnight stays limited to two nights? Especially since this 
use can be applied for in conjunction with a Guest Lodge, which does not have that limit.  
 
Guest Lodge Tier 2. Meal service would not be required unlike Guest Lodge Tier  1 and B ed and 
Breakfast. This means that guests will be likely make more offsite trips during the day for meals, adding 
to external impacts on roads and neighbors. Thank you for considering these comments.

Scott Williams
