  d. Consideration of a new Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) between 
Weld County and the Town of Milliken for the Community Development 
Block Grant (CDBG) Program 
Page 9 of 103
Caree Rinebarger, Town Clerk  
Caree Rinebarger, Town Clerk, asked the Board of Trustees for their consideration, approval 
and execution of a new Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) between Weld County and the 
Town of Milliken to continue participation in the Weld County Community Development 
Block Grant (CDBG) Program for the upcoming Urban County qualification period. 
 
The Town of Milliken has actively participated in the Weld County CDBG Program since its 
inception. This program provides crucial funding for local community development projects. 
 
Every three years, Weld County must re-qualify with the U.S. Department of Housing and 
Urban Development (HUD) as an "Urban County" to continue receiving these federal CDBG 
funds. As part of this standard three-year re-qualification cycle, HUD reviews and updates its 
mandatory regulatory compliance requirements for agreements between the County (the 
Grantee) and participating local municipalities. 
 
The attached new IGA updates and replaces the Town's current agreement by consolidating the 
original IGA, the first amendment, and the latest HUD federal regulatory requirements. 
 
Key Changes in the New IGA: 
• Term Limitations: The primary change eliminates the current agreement's automatic 
renewal clause, which carried an indeterminate end date. Moving forward, automatic 
renewals will be strictly limited to a maximum of two (2) consecutive three-year terms 
before a full renewal is required. 
• Federal Regulatory Updates: Additional text updates have been incorporated to 
ensure absolute compliance with changing federal regulations mandated by HUD. 
 
Weld County is on a compressed timeline for this HUD re-qualification period. The County has 
requested that the fully executed agreement be returned via email no later than August 20, 
2026. Following the Town Board’s approval and signature, the agreement will be transmitted to 
the Weld County Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) for final execution by the BOCC 
Chair. 
The Town has received grant funding in the amount of $154,000.00 that assisted the Town in 
upgrading 13 handicap ramps in the old town area in 2023.   
Trustee Amanda Nix motioned to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement between Weld

  a. Special Meeting Called by Trustee Cummins 
BJ Cummins, Trustee  
SPECIAL MEETING ITEM – BOARD CONSIDERATION & DISCUSSION 
 
Trustee BJ Cummins introduced the items requested for consideration during the Special 
Meeting. The Board of Trustees engaged in formal discussion regarding municipal 
administrative procedures, technical livestreaming safeguards, elected official conduct, and 
agenda-setting processes. 
 
1. Executive Session Livestream Incident and Technical Safeguards: Interim Town 
Administrator Krolczyk and IT Specialist Mundy provided a detailed overview of the technical 
failure occurring on July 22nd, where an executive session was inadvertently live-recorded and 
published. 
Page 14 of 103
• Cause & Timeline: The failure stemmed from a legacy software configuration involving 
OBS, TriCaster, and Zoom, where pausing standard recording did not terminate the 
active OBS live stream. Old Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) established during 
May training failed to account for this specific routing configuration. 
• Immediate Safeguards & SOP Revisions: Staff confirmed the video was removed 
immediately upon discovery. The manual SOP has been updated with explicit, 
highlighted protocols for executive sessions: manually pausing the stream in CivicPlus, 
removing Zoom participants, and pausing active recordings. 
• Retraining & Equipment Upgrades: Staff, including Town Clerk Caree Rinebarger 
and administrative personnel, are undergoing retraining on the updated SOPs. Staff will 
evaluate simplified AV equipment options and present cost estimates during the 
upcoming budget cycle to streamline future operations. 
 
2. Clarification of Candidate Statements & Statement Regarding Town Clerk:  The Board 
discussed candidate correspondence and unverified statements raised during the Town 
Administrator selection process regarding Town Clerk Caree Rinebarger and candidate Mr. 
Brinck. 
• Public Correction & Statement: Trustee Cummins presented a statement clarifying that 
rumors alleging a personal relationship between Clerk Rinebarger and Mr. Brinck were 
thoroughly investigated, determined to be false, and were not relied upon by the Board in 
any hiring decisions. 
• Board Consensus: Trustees Cummins and Mayor Pro Tem Orcutt confirmed approval 
of the draft response; the Board concurred that no executive session was necessary. 
Trustees expressed concerns regarding staff integrity being publicly questioned based on 
unverified rumors and emphasized that administrative inquiries must be vetted through 
the Town Administrator. 
• Trustee Statements & Mayor's Remarks: Trustee Wakeman offered a formal personal 
apology to Town Clerk Rinebarger for the distress caused, affirming that final hiring

PURPOSE 
To authorize the Mayor to sign the Intergovernmental Agreement on behalf of the Town 
for initial Engineering and Design Services, Support Costs and Expenses related to the 
Regional Water Authority 
BACKGROUND 
The Town of Milliken along with Central Weld County Water District and other 
municipalities, are exploring the formation of a Regional Water Authority and possible 
Water System Projects.  The Intergovernmental Agreement is for initial engineering and 
design services, support costs and expenses related to this possible Water Authority 
formation. 
BUDGET IMPLICATIONS 
The Town’s proportionate share of these engineering services is $50,000, which has 
been appropriated in the Town's 2026 Capital Budget. 
STAFF RECOMMENDATIONS 
Staff recommends the approval of the Intergovernmental Agreement Authorizing Initial 
Engineering and Design Services related to the Regional Water Authority.

INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT 
 
 THIS AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is made and entered into effective July 1 st, 2026, by and 
among CENTRAL WELD COUNTY WATER DISTRICT, a quasi-municipal corporation and 
political subdivision of the State of Colorado (“Central Weld”), The TOWN OF BERTHOUD, 
COLORADO, a municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of Colorado 
(“Berthoud”), the CITY OF DACONO, COLORADO, a home rule municipal corporation and 
political subdivision of the State of Colorado (“Dacono”), the TOWN OF FREDERICK, 
COLORADO, a municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of Colorado 
(“Frederick”), the TOWN OF JOHNSTOWN, COLORADO a municipal corporation and political 
subdivision of the State of Colorado (“Johnstown”), the TOWN OF MILLIKEN, COLORADO a 
municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of Colorado (“Milliken”), LEFT HAND 
WATER DISTRICT, a quasi-municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of Colorado 
(“Left Hand”), LITTLE THOMPSON WATER DISTRICT, a quasi-municipal corporation and 
political subdivision of the State of Colorado and a duly organized and existing special district pursuant 
to Title 32, Article 1, Colorado Revised Statutes (“Little Thompson”). Central Weld, Berthoud, 
Dacono, Frederick, Johnstown, Milliken, Left Hand and Little Thompson may herein be collectively 
called the “Parties” or each individually a “Party.” 
 
BACKGROUND and RECITALS 
 
 A. The Parties are encouraged and empowered to agree about the subject matter of this 
Agreement under the terms of Title 29, Article 1, Part 2, of the Colorado Revised Statutes. This 
Agreement is made under the terms of Section 29-1-203, Colorado Revised Statutes. 
 
 B. Each Party owns and operates a separate potable water system to serve its respective 
residents, customers or users and the Parties are exploring possible projects, possible contracts, 
possible joint cooperation and/or possible cost sharing matters regarding such water systems 
(“Possible Water System Projects”). 
 
C. The Parties recognize that water quality analysis, treatment engineering and 
design services, and support costs and expenses  will be incurred in exploring the formation of a 
Regional Water Authority, and Possible Water System Projects and agree to equally share in such costs 
and expenses. 
 
D. The Parties currently estimate that such initial engineering and design services and 
support costs and expenses will not exceed four hundred thousand dollars ($400,000.00) (“Initial 
Engineering Costs and Expenses”) and the Parties desire to confirm their equal sharing of such Initial 
Costs and Expenses in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement. The scope of 
this work is outlined in the attached “Exhibit 1 (To Intergovernmental Agreement)”, a Master Services 
Agreement and Task Order #101 for Engineering Services, between Central Weld and Jacobs 
Engineering Group Inc. dated June 1st, 2026.

AGREEMENT: 
 
Now, therefore, in consideration of the foregoing Background and Recitals and the mutual 
promises and agreements made in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the 
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties hereby agree as follows: 
 
1. Background and Recitals . The foregoing Background and Recitals constitute 
substantive terms of this Agreement and are incorporated into this Agreement by this reference. 
 
2. Equal Sharing of Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses .  The Parties hereby 
agree and acknowledge that they shall equally share the Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses. The 
Parties further agree that Central Weld shall pay the various contractors, vendors and others for the 
costs and expenses comprising the Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses and Central Weld will 
notify the other Parties of the amounts paid by Central Weld and the equal share amount due from 
each Party. Central Weld will provide to each Party copies of all invoices, billings or other documents 
containing the Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses.  Each Party shall forthwith pay Central Weld 
for such Party’s equal share of the Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses. 
 
3. Additional Costs and Expenses . If costs and expenses exceed the Initial 
Engineering Costs and Expenses, the Parties shall mutually discuss the status of the Regional Water 
Authority and Possible Water System Projects and determine whether to proceed. If any of the Parties 
determine to proceed, such Parties shall mutually execute an amendment to this Agreement regarding 
additional costs and expenses or execute additional agreements or document(s) regarding additional 
costs and expenses to be incurred by the Parties prior to incurring any costs and expenses above the 
Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses. If any of the Parties determine not to proceed, such 
amendment will remove such Parties from this Agreement. If any of the Parties determine not to 
proceed, such Party shall remain obligated to pay for such Party’s equal share of any outstanding or 
unpaid Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses pursuant to the terms of this Agreement. 
 
4. Default. In addition to any other remedies allowed by law, the provisions of the 
Agreement may be specifically enforced in a court of competent jurisdiction, and in any such judicial 
action, the unsuccessful party shall pay all costs and expenses incurred by the successful Party or 
Parties including reasonable attorneys fees. Venue for any disputes concerning this Agreement shall 
be in the Weld County, Colorado, District Court. 
 
5. Miscellaneous. 
a. No Joint Venture. The sole purpose of this Agreement is to provide for the 
cooperation of the Parties regarding the sharing of the Initial Engineering Costs and Expenses 
regarding the formation of a Regional Water Treatment Authority and Possible Water System 
Projects. Nothing contained in this Agreement is intended to create a partnership or joint 
venture between the Parties, and any implication to the contrary is hereby expressly disavowed. 
It is understood and agreed that this Agreement does not provide for the joint exercise by the 
Parties of any activity, function or service, nor does it create a joint enterprise or any other 
separate legal entity, nor does it authorize any Party hereto to act as an agent for another Party 
hereto for any purpose whatsoever. 
b. Nonwaiver of Immunity.  This Agreement shall not be construed to waive any 
of the privileges, defenses, or immunities of any Party hereto including but not limited to the
