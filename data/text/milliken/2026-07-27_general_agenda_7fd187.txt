1.   2026 RHTP - Letter of Support - Milliken (002) 
PURPOSE 
To consider approval of a Letter of Support for Sunrise Community Health's application 
to the Colorado Department of Health Care Policy and Financing's Rural Health 
Transformation Program (RHTP). 
BACKGROUND 
The Town recently received a request from Sunrise Community Health seeking a Letter 
of Support for its Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) grant application. Due 
to the State's recent release of the final program requirements, Sunrise was only 
recently determined to be eligible to apply, resulting in an abbreviated application 
timeline. The completed application, including letters of support, must be submitted by 
August 1, 2026. 
 
Sunrise Community Health is a nonprofit community health center that has served 
northern Colorado since 1973 and currently provides medical, dental, and behavioral 
health services to more than 45,000 residents through clinics located in Evans, 
Greeley, Windsor, and Loveland. 
 
According to Sunrise Community Health, many Milliken residents currently travel to 
Evans or Loveland to receive health care services. Recognizing transportation 
challenges and barriers to accessing care, Sunrise has identified Milliken and the 
surrounding rural communities as an area of focus for expanding healthcare access. 
 
The proposed project would initially utilize mobile medical and dental clinics to bring 
Page 5 of 7
primary care services directly into the Milliken area while Sunrise evaluates the 
feasibility of establishing a permanent brick-and-mortar health clinic offering integrated 
medical, dental, and behavioral health services. 
 
The requested Letter of Support is intended to demonstrate community support for 
Sunrise Community Health's grant application. The draft letter provided by Sunrise 
states that the Town supports the organization's efforts to improve healthcare access 
within rural Weld County and acknowledges the potential benefits of expanding 
medical, dental, and behavioral health services in underserved communities. 
 
The draft letter further identifies several potential areas of collaboration, including: 
• Participation in regional planning and coordination efforts; 
• Supporting community engagement with stakeholders; 
• Participation in advisory meetings or project planning; and 
• Working collaboratively to improve healthcare access, equity, and health 
outcomes.  
 
The proposed letter does not commit the Town to providing funding, property, staffing 
or entering int any contractual agreement.  Rather, it expresses general support for 
Sunrise Community Health's efforts to pursue grant funding and improve healthcare 
accessibility for residents of Milliken and surrounding rural communities. 
 
Supporting grant applications of this nature is common practice among local 
governments when proposed projects have the potential to benefit the community 
without creating financial or legal obligations for the municipality.

RE: Letter of Support for Rural Health Transformation Program Application  
 
 
To RHTP Review Committee:  
 
On behalf of the Town of Milliken , I am pleased to provide this Letter of Support for the 
Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) application being submitted by  Sunrise 
Community Health (Sunrise). The RHTP grant will have a significant positive impact on 
our communities in rural counties which are in special need of strengthening health care 
access, improving health outcomes, and expanding workforce capacity.   
 
With this grant funding, Sunrise will increase access to primary care in rural zip codes in 
Weld County, beginning with a mobile medical  van and mobile dental van to reach the 
Milliken and surrounding rural areas . Sunrise will soon follow with the planning and 
development of a brick and mortar clinic to establish long-term, sustainable primary care 
access with integrated medical,  dental, and behavioral health services.  
 
We represent a significant presence in our communities and support this RHTP grant to 
advance sustainable rural /frontier healthcare transformation and improve access to 
services for our community. We are excited to working with Sunrise Community Health in 
one or more of the following ways:  
 
• Participate in regional planning and coordination activities 
• Support community engagement with key stakeholders 
• Participate in advisory meetings or project planning 
• Improve health care equity, access, and outcomes for the people we serve while 
saving Coloradans money on health care and driving value for Colorado.  
  
We appreciate the opportunity to support this important initiative and look forward to 
continued collaboration to improve healthcare delivery systems throughout rural 
Colorado.
