These minutes have been taken remotely via Zoom. Some County staff, representatives from other governments and

A. Call to Order 
  The special meeting of the Board of Commissioners was held on August 10, 2026, at the Chaffee 
County Courthouse, 104 Crestone Ave., Salida. Board members present were Chair Gina Lucrezi, 
Commissioner Dave Armstrong, and Commissioner P. T. Wood. 
  
Others present in person were County Administrator Beth Helmke, Director of Finance Dan Short (items 
A. through B.1. only), County Attorney Kathleen Sullivan, Deputy Public Health Director Emily 
Anderson (items A. through C.1. only), and Chief Building Official Chad Chadwick (items A. through 
C.1. only).  
Present remotely was Deputy County Clerk Barbara Tidd. 
    
  1. Pledge of Allegiance 
    Following the Pledge of Allegiance, Chair Lucrezi called the meeting to order at 1:01 p.m.  
  2. Approve Agenda 
    Commissioner Wood moved to approve the Agenda. Commissioner Armstrong seconded the 
motion. The motion carried unanimously.  
      
B. Consent Agenda 
  Commissioner Wood moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Commissioner Armstrong seconded the 
motion. The motion carried unanimously.   
  1. Approve County serving as applicant and fiscal sponsor for grant to Great Outdoors Colorado 
(GOCO) for Conservation Service Corps' trails maintenance project with Colorado Youth Corps, 
via partnership with Salida Mountain Trails - Community Planning Natural Resources Department
