POLICIES AND PROCEDURES 
 
 
 
 
TITLE:  Policy Adoption Process Approval Date: proposed 
August –,.2026 
PURPOSE: To establish the process for the Board of County 
Commissioners’ adoption, implementation, and 
maintenance of policy 
Revision Date(s): new 
 Adoption Date: 
 
The Board develops policies and puts them in writing to provide for the successful, consistent 
and efficient operation of the County. The Board’s policies shall be interpreted in accordance 
with state and federal laws and regulations.  
 
1. Applicability 
 
A Policy is the written description of a direction set by the Board of County Commissioners 
(BOCC) to guide the present and future actions of the various Departments/Divisions within 
Chaffee County. A Policy shall be established when directed by the BOCC or when required in 
accordance with state or federal law.  
 
Procedures may be developed by staff to implement a policy, though not all policies will require 
a procedure.  
 
2. Process 
 
All Policies shall be adopted by the BOCC at a public meeting, after an opportunity for public 
comment.   
 
All Policies, excluding Ordinances, may be revised administratively to make format changes, 
corrections to reflect organizational changes, or similar nonmaterial changes without BOCC 
review.  
 
When a new policy or an amendment to an existing policy is necessary, the County Attorney 
shall work with the County Administration and relevant department(s) to develop such policy or 
amendment for consideration by the BOCC.  
 
All adopted Policies shall be maintained in the County Policy Manual. 
 
The BOCC will review its policies regularly and as needed to address any legislative changes. 
 
Departments may develop procedures to promote efficiency and consistency in policy
