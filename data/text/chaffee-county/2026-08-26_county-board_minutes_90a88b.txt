Airport Advisory Board Meeting Minutes 
August 26, 2026 
9:00 a.m. 
 
1. Call to Order 
Steve Bush called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m.  Those present in person were  
Airport Manager Zech Papp, Steve Bush, Clifton Brown, Randall Cone, Jim 
Oglesby, Phil Phillips, Robert Christiansen, Michael Marino, Tara Miller, Salida 
Councilman Aaron Stephens, County Attorney Kathleen Sullivan and Airport 
employee Ethan Smith.   
Participating in the meeting via zoom were Cory Bieganek and Commissioner 
P.T. Wood. 
 
2. Minutes for Approval 
Clifton Brown moved to approve the July 22nd, 2026 Meeting Minutes.  Jim 
Oglesby seconded the motion. All approved.   
 
3. Public Comments 
Ray & Ann Plumb were present but did not make any comments during the public 
comment portion of the meeting.   
 
4. New Business  
a. Airport Capital Improvement Plan – Zech prepares the Capital 
Improvement Plan (CIP) annually with Dibble Engineering.  If a project is 
not on the CIP, the Airport cannot obtain funding for it from discretional 
grants.  No new projects have been added to the CIP for 2027.   
b. 2027 Airport Budget – The main push for 2027 is to tighten up the budget 
because funding is very limited for both the city and the county.  The 
larger items on the 2027 budget are the old fuel farm removal, a well for 
the area that will soon be developed, the master drainage plan and the 
Taxiway A rehabilitation design.  The old fuel farm removal is eligible for 
state oil funding for $2 per gallon capacity, which should end up being 
around $48,000.  Ray Plumb made public comment on this item.   
 
5. Old Business 
a. Land Lease Discussion – The new land lease was discussed at length.  
Board members had the opportunity to make comments on the new lease, 
and those comments were shared with County Attorney Kathleen Sullivan.  
 
Kathleen is meeting with Zech after the meeting regarding the lease.  Ray 
& Ann Plumb made public comment during this discussion.   
b. 2026 Projects – The new Fuel Farm and Taxiway A Extension projects are 
nearly closed out.  The master drainage plan proposal is in process with 
Dibble Engineering.   
 
6. Manager’s Report – Activities during last month; statistics of prior months; 
updates; other projects – Airport traffic has picked up over the last month, but 
the afternoon weather has been slowing things down.   
 
The discussion shifted back to the land lease prior to the meeting adjourning, and 
it was discussed at length again. 
 
7. Adjourn – There being no further meeting business, Steve Bush moved to 
adjourn at 10:12 a.m.  Phil Phillips seconded the motion and the meeting 
adjourned.   
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Tara Miller 
Secretary to the Airport Board
