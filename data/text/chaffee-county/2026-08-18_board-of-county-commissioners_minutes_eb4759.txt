A. Call to Order

County Public Safety Complex, 200 Steele Dr., Buena Vista. Board members present were Chair Gina

• Review Potential MOU with SPOT for maintenance of potential trail subject to FLAP Grant 
- Garry Baker 
    Manager Baker addressed the Board to consider an application to the 2026 Federal Lands Access 
Program (FLAP) and to review a potential MOU with Salida Parks Open Space and Trails (SPOT) 
for maintenance of the potential trail subject to the FLAP Grant.

the 2026 Federal Lands Access Program (FLAP) for a grant for the potential trail. Commissioner 
Armstrong seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously.

Public Hearing
