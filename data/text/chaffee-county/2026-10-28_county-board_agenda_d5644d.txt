Airport Advisory Board Meeting Minutes 
September 23, 2026 
9:00 a.m. 
 
1. Call to Order 
Steve Bush called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m.  Those present in person were  
Airport Manager Zech Papp, Steve Bush, Cory Bieganek, Clifton Brown, Randall 
Cone, Jim Oglesby, Phil Phillips, Michael Marino, Shawn O’Day, Commissioner 
P.T. Wood, Salida Councilman Aaron Stephens, and Airport employee Ethan 
Smith.  There were no participants attending via zoom. 
 
2. Minutes for Approval 
Clifton Brown moved to approve the August 26, 2026 Meeting Minutes.  Steve 
Bush seconded the motion.  All approved.   
 
3. Public Comments 
Ray and Ann Plumb, and Barry Cole were present, but did not make any 
comments during the public comment portion of the meeting. 
 
4. New Business  
a. Nathan Jordan lease name change to Antero Aviation LLC – Nathan 
Jordan currently has a hangar lease for Site G2.  He would like to change 
the name on the lease from his name to his LLC name, Antero Aviation 
LLC.  Steve Bush moved to approve the change.  Phil Phillips seconded 
the motion.  All approved.   
 
5. Old Business 
a. Land Lease Discussion – Zech is meeting with County Attorney Kathleen 
Sullivan next week to continue work on the land lease.  It is expected that 
the lease will be presented to the board at the next meeting.   
b. 2026 Projects – The taxiway project has been done for quite some time, 
but finalizing all the paperwork for that project is still in process and 
should be done within the next few weeks.   
The terminal roof has been leaking during rainstorms.  United Roofing 
will be reroofing the building.   
Zech has received the annual inspections for the old fuel farm, therefore 
the process of getting those removed can begin.  Zech will file for the 
 
temporary closure of the old fuel farm and then collect bids for its removal 
next year.   
The Airport has a proposal from Dibble Engineering for the master 
drainage plan and Zech is hopeful that the project will be underway next 
year.   
c. Airport CIP Meeting – The Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) meeting took 
place on Monday, August 21st.  The same projects have been on the 
Airport’s CIP for the last four years.   
d. 2027 Budget – The 2027 budget is still in process with the City and 
County.  The federally funded project for the year will be the rehab of 
Taxiway A from A1 to A2, which is an area with extensive cracking.  The 
master drainage plan, the removal of the old fuel farm, and augmenting a 
well in the new development area are all requests in the 2027 budget.   
A lengthy discussion ensued regarding the new development area.  Ray 
Plumb and Barry Cole commented during the discussion.   
 
6. Manager’s Report – Activities during last month; statistics of prior months; 
updates; other projects – Fuel sales over the last month were good and 
operations have been up lately since the weather has been so nice.  Staff has been 
busy preparing for winter.   
 
7. Adjourn – There being no further meeting business, the meeting adjourned at 
9:46 a.m. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Tara Miller 
Secretary to the Airport Board
