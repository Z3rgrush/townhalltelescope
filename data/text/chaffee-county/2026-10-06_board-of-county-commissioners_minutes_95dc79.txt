A. Call to Order 
   
The regular meeting of the Board of Commissioners was held on October 6, 2026, at the Chaffee County 
Courthouse, 104 Crestone Ave., Salida. Board members present were Chair Gina Lucrezi, 
Commissioner Dave Armstrong, and Commissioner P. T. Wood. 
  
Others present in person were County Administrator Beth Helmke, Director of Finance Dan Short, 
County Attorney Kathleen Sullivan, Community Planning & Natural Resources Director Miles Cottom, 
Sheriff Andy Rohrich (item G.3. only), and Undersheriff Alex Walker (item G.3. only). 
Others present remotely were Planner Brandon Wolff, Chief Building Official Chad Chadwick, and 
Deputy County Clerks Kit Kuester, Julia Rivera, and Barbara Tidd.

    Following the Pledge of Allegiance, Chair Lucrezi called the meeting to order at 9:05 a.m.
