A. Call to Order 
  The regular meeting of the Board of Commissioners was held on September 1, 2026, at the Chaffee 
County Courthouse, 104 Crestone Ave., Salida. Board members present were Chair Gina Lucrezi and 
Commissioner Dave Armstrong. Commissioner P. T. Wood was absent and excused. 
  
Others present in person were County Administrator Beth Helmke, Director of Finance Dan Short, 
County Attorney Kathleen Sullivan, and Community Planning & Natural Resources Director Miles 
Cottom. 
Others present remotely were Planner Brandon Wolff and Deputy County Clerks Kit Kuester, Julia 
Rivera, and Barbara Tidd.

  2. Consider Grant Agreement and IGA with Colorado Energy Office (CEO) for acceptance of the 
Local IMPACT Accelerator grant award supporting County Waste Diversion and Facilities

Administrator Helmke addressed the Board to consider a Grant Agreement and IGA with the

Proposition 123 Commitments. 
 
Discussion ensued during which Director Cottom spoke. 
 
Commissioner Armstrong moved to approve the draft MOU with the Chaffee Housing Authority, 
Poncha Springs, Buena Vista, and Salida for Sharing Regional Proposition 123 Commitments as

the motion. The motion carried unanimously.  
F. Regular Agenda Items 
  
 
  1. Consider Resolution 2026-31 In Support of Ballot Measure 308 - Commissioners 
    Chair Lucrezi addressed the Board to consider RESOLUTION 2026-31 IN SUPPORT OF 
BALLOT MEASURE 308. 
 
Discussion ensued during which Chair Lucrezi suggested the following revisions: 
-Change Ballot Measure 308 to Proposition 137 throughout the document because the ballot 
measure officially qualified for the November ballot as Proposition 137. 
-Change paragraph 1, "WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Chaffee County, 
Colorado desires to formally support Colorado ballot Initiative 308, Designating Sporting Goods 
Sales Tax Revenue for Conservation, a statewide ballot measure that would provide a dedicated 
funding source for protecting Colorado's forests, watersheds, water resources, wildlife habitat, and 
outdoor recreation opportunities; and"
