  1. Continued from July 7, 2026 and August 18, 2026 
Public Hearing 
Project Name: Corrigan Rezone 
Applicant: Shane Corrigan 
Project Address: 22420 Hwy 50 (Parcels #368727403171 & 368727403176), 22435 Hwy 50 
(Parcel #368727408169), 22585 W Hwy 50 (Parcel #368727408026), and the currently 
unaddressed parcels numbered #368727403168, #368727403172, and #368727408024 
Existing and Proposed Zoning: Residential Rural (RR) to Mixed Use Rural (MUR) 
Brief Summary of the Proposal: To rezone several parcels in the historic townsite of Garfield 
from the Residential Rural zone to the Mixed Use Rural zone 
Planner: Brandon Wolff / 719-530-5570 / bwolff@chaffeecounty.org 
     
Planner Wolff addressed the Board to open the continued public hearing to consider the Corrigan 
Rezone for Applicant Shane Corrigan for the project described above. 
 
Wolff responded to Commissioners' questions. 
 
Applicant Shane Corrigan addressed the Board and responded to Commissioners' questions. 
 
The public comment portion of the hearing opened at 9:54 a.m.  
Public comments were given by:  
Mieke Bushhouse, 22425 W. US Hwy. 50 (adjacent to subject property), Salida. 
Greg Totten, 22425 W. US Hwy. 50, Salida. 
Logan Opsahl, 22457 Hanson St., Salida (Garfield). 
 
The public comment portion of the hearing closed at 10:05 a.m. 
Wolff responded to Commissioners' questions. 
 
Corrigan responded to Commissioner's question. 
 
Wolff and Commissioner Wood responded to Commissioner Lucrezi's question. 
 
Commissioners deliberated during which Director Cottom and Planner Wolff responded to 
Commissioners' questions. 
 
Commissioner Armstrong moved to support this Corrigan Rezone project for Applicant Shane 
Corrigan converting the zoning of the subject parcels to Mixed-Use Rural (MUR). 
Commissioner Lucrezi seconded the motion.  
Discussion ensued. 
 
The motion carried with Commissioners Armstrong and Lucrezi voting FOR the motion and 
Commissioner Wood voting AGAINST the motion. 
 
Director Cottom responded to Commissioner's question.
