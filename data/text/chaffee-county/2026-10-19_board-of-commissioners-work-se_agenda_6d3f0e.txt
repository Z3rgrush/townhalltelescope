Day-To-Day Oversight and Supervision: From time to time throughout the year, two or more Commissioners may meet to discuss day-to-day 
oversight of County property and/or supervision of employees. Such meetings are held in accordance with C.R.S. § 24-6-402(2)(f) and, therefore, 
prior notice may not be posted. No policies, positions, resolutions, rules or regulations are adopted at day-to-day business meetings, nor is any formal 
action taken. Accordingly, no official minutes of day-to-day business meetings may be kept.

Dear Chaffee County Board of Health/Board of County Commissioners: 
 
Chaffee County Public Health (CCPH) Environmental Health recently completed an audit of other county 
environmental health programs throughout the state of Colorado concerning their special event protocols. We 
updated our fee structures to be more in line with comparable counties in Colorado. Due to the increased amount of 
events in Chaffee County and the time allotted for processing and evaluating the Retail Food Establishments (RFE) 
involved in these special events, we request the following fee changes to meet the current need: 
 
The Event Coordinator Fee will change from $50 to $75 (No fee required for non-profit or charitable 
organizations). Event coordinator applications are requested 30 days prior to the event but no later than 14 days prior 
to the event. A late fee of $50 will be added to the total event invoice if this packet is submitted within 2 weeks 
of the event. 
Event Coordinators are responsible for completing the Event Coordinator Packet and compiling the Vendor 
Application forms and payments. The Event Coordinator will receive an invoice for all RFE fees and services after the 
event. Vendors are not to send separate payments to Chaffee County Public Health.  
There will be no additional vendor fee if the Temporary Food Vendor is licensed and local to Chaffee County. If the 
Vendor is licensed in Colorado - but not local - there will be a $50 fee for each booth. If the vendor is from out of 
state or is not licensed there will be a fee of $100 for each booth.  
An inspection or plan review fee of $45/hour MAY be requested from the county inspector. Inspector fees are 
determined on a case by case basis and will be invoiced with the vendor fees after the event.  
_____________________________________________________________________________________ 
CCPH has created a Tent Vendor Permit to satisfy our Retail Vendors working out of the new definition of a Mobile 
Food Establishment due to changes in the state statute section related to Mobile Food Establishments: 
“(10.5) “Mobile food establishment” has the meaning set forth in section 29-11.6-102 (6). 29-11.6-102 (6): “Mobile 
food establishment” means a retail food establishment that: (a) Is operated from a movable, motor-driven, or propelled 
vehicle, portable structure, or watercraft; (b) Can change location; and (c) Is intended to physically report to and 
operate from a commissary for servicing, restocking, and maintenance.” 
This new permit will help those vendors that do not fall within that definition. Vendors must submit $250 fee, provide a 
proposed menu, drawing of the tent layout, commissary agreement, fill out the application and sign the Conditional 
Employee or Food Employee Reporting Agreement.

Future Medical Diagnosis: 
Whenever diagnosed as being ill with Norovirus, typhoid fever (Salmonella Typhi), shigellosis (Shigella spp.
infection), Escherichia coli O157:H7 or other STEC infection, nontyphoidal Salmonella or hepatitis A (hepatitis A
virus infection) 
Future Exposure to Foodborne Pathogens: 
1. Exposure to or suspicion of causing any conﬁrmed disease outbreak of Norovirus, typhoid fever, 
shigellosis, E. coli O157:H7 or other STEC infection, or hepatitis A.
2. A household member diagnosed with Norovirus, typhoid fever, shigellosis, illness due to STEC, or hepatitis A.
3. A household member attending or working in a setting experiencing a conﬁrmed disease outbreak of Norovirus,
typhoid fever, shigellosis, E. coli O157:H7 or other STEC infection, or hepatitis A.
I have read (or had explained to me) and understand the requirements concerning my responsibilities under the
Food Code and this agreement to comply with: 
1. Reporting requirements speciﬁed above involving symptoms, diagnoses,and exposure speciﬁed;
2. Work restrictions or exclusions that are imposed upon me; and
3. Good hygienic practices.
Preventing Transmission of Diseases through Food by Infected Conditional Employees or Food Employees with
Emphasis on Illness due to Norovirus, Salmonella Typhi, Shigella spp., or Shiga Toxin-producing Escherichia coli
(STEC), nontyphoidal Salmonella or Hepatitis A Virus 
The purpose of this agreement is to inform conditional employees or food employees of their responsibility to
notify the person in charge when they experience any of the conditions listed so that the person in charge can
take appropriate steps to preclude the transmission of foodborne illness. 
I agree to report to the person in charge: 
Any Onset of the Following Symptoms, Either While at Work or Outside of Work, Including the Date of Onset: 
1. Diarrhea
2. Vomiting
3. Jaundice
4. Sore throat with fever
5. Infected cuts or wounds, or lesions containing pus on the hand, wrist, an exposed body part,or other body part
and the cuts, wounds, or lesions are not properly covered
(such as boils and infected wounds, however small)
I understand that failure to comply with the terms of this agreement could lead to action by the food
establishment or the food regulatory authority that may jeopardize my employment and may involve legal action
against me.

Special Event Protocol
Event Coordinator Packet: 
Event coordinator is responsible for:
$75 Event Coordinator fee (an exemption will be made if the sole purpose is a charitable 
organization ). 
Late fee of $50 if packet, Vendor Applications and licenses are submitted less than 2 
weeks the event.
Completing the Event Coordinator Packet and compiling the Vendor Application forms 
and payments. 
The Event Coordinator will receive an invoice for all RFE fees and services after the event. 
An inspection fee of $45/hour MAY be requested from the county inspector. Inspector 
fees are determined on a case by case basis and will be invoiced with the vendor fees 
after the event. 
Vendor Application:
If they are a local licensed RFE = No Fee as long as the menu does not deviate from the 
current menu to higher risk foods,  and RFE will still need to ﬁll out Vender Application Form. 
If they are a Colorado Licensed RFE but not local = $50 and ﬁll out the Vendor Application 
Form.
If they are not licensed or are from out of state - $100 and ﬁll out the Vendor Application 
Form. 
Potlucks: Events will need to display a sign stating "These products are made in a home 
kitchen."
