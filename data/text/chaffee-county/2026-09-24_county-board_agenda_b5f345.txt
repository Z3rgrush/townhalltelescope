ESF 8 – Health & Medical Services 
Emily Anderson (Public Health)- Monitoring Cyclospora outbreak that’s happening in the 
Midwest as of now Colorado doesn’t seem to be affected so we’re not advising anyone on what 
to eat. In Midwest we can advise avoiding shredded lettuce, or if you’re high risk some other 
foods where the parasite can hide. Measles is on track to set a new 33-year record. Reached 
measles elimination in status in year 2000 very possible we will lose status in November when 
cases are reviewed. What this means is we expect measles to be transmitted throughout the 
US. At this time if we were to get a measles case in Chaffee County because we haven’t 
officially considered it endemic the Public Health response will be extensive with intention of 
stopping it. In 15 years, we’ve never had so many issues with bats. Bats are known to have 
rabies in Chaffee County and Colorado. If a bat is found in a room where you slept there is no 
certainty that you weren’t bitten. Bats can land on you and bite you without knowing. Next 
steps would be to get rabies shots if they have the bat, it can be sent into the lab to be tested. 
Smoke was an issue that had a lot of calls for air quality, gave masks out and directed to purple 
air monitors. Changes day to day hopefully will be offering indoor air quality monitors. Thinking
