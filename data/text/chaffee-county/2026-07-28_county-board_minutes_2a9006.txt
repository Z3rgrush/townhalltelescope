Multi-Year Grant Disbursement Consideration 
 
13:20 – The committee considers recommending the disbursement of year 3 of 4 of the 
National Forest Foundation’s Sawatch Range Restoration Project. NFF has 
submitted their latest progress report and showed the need for the disbursement as 
they have planned expenditures to make progress on the project.  
  
 C Matthews notes she has been having issues seeing reports and CCCF will work 
with her to correct access issues. 
 
13:25 – Joe S asks if the committee should recommend disbursing less than the full 
amount and disbursing the rest at a later date. CCCF and County Staff insist that it 
is too administratively burdensome to make a partial disbursement. 
 
13:28 – The committee votes unanimously to recommend the disbursement of$150,000 to 
NFF . 
 
Multi-Year Grant Presentations – Central Colorado Conservancy Hands for Lands 
 
13:30 – Suzanne C discusses the Hands for Lands Program which organizes volunteer 
conservation events on private property across the county including irrigation ditch 
maintenance, partner with other organizations on restoration projects, and provide 
public education. This program is in year 3 of 3 of their grant. 
 
 In 2025 there were 6 events totaling 150 volunteer hours and cleaning 14 miles of 
ditches. In 2026 they have so far had 3 event totaling 114 volunteer hours and 
cleaning 8 miles if ditches. More events are expected tot take place in 2026 and 
2027. 
 
County Staff Updates 
 
 Chaffee County Councils 
 
13:45 - Gabe H shares that the Rec Council has been having coordination meetings for 
trainings and grants. Stakeholders are working on collaborating on a grant to fund 
paid trail crews in the County.  He has created a shared form which allows 
stakeholders to identify what trainings are a common need and work together to 
provide trainings to their staff and volunteers. 
 
 The Forest Health Council has met to discuss the update to the CWPP . The 
Colorado Forest Restoration Institute presented on updated modeling that may be 
able to be included in the update, including updated PODS which provide smaller 
areas of delineation. There is discussion of shifting the deadline for the update to 
spring of 2027 to accommodate the timeline of land managers that are busy 
managing wildfires.  
 
13:49 – Caroline M asks if the Upper Arkansas Conservation District (UACD) will host Ag 
Council. Gabe H explains that they are tasked with exploring the viability of an Ag 
Council through their Working Lands Resilience Coordinator. 
 
 CAC Vacancies 
 
13:53 – The CAC currently has 5 members and 2 vacancies. After two rounds of requests 
for letters of interest with no responses. the County will wait until late in the year to 
issue another request since they are moving into grant reviews soon and as long as 
the committee has 4 members it can still function. 
 
Grant Process Updates
