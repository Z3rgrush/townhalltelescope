 Lyons Sundance Film Festival Task Force — Meeting Recap 
 Friday, August 21, 2026 | 1:30pm | Lyons Community Library, Effie Banta Room 
 Attendees: Betsy, Gil, Chrystal, Sarah, Alaina, Jen, Dee 
 Recap 
 The meeting focused on planning for the Sundance Info Session on August 25th, the housing 
 Info Session hosted by Fox Properties on September 23rd, and creating a website to promote 
 Lyons as a destination. The group discussed potential bus routes to Pearl Street and the 
 importance of transportation options, particularly during inclement weather. Chrystal mentioned 
 that Sundance Institute would provide banners and branded materials for downtown activation, 
 with the first phase targeted for December 1st to coincide with the tree lighting. Betsy shared 
 connections with key contacts at Sundance, including Ruthie Johnson from Sundance's Senior 
 Management of Guest Operations, and mentioned meeting someone from Amazon Prime who 
 handles film acquisitions. Betsy learned that parties start getting booked after the films are 
 announced in Nov/Dec; she believes that all the work we’ve been doing is a great foundation as 
 a lot of the right people know about Lyons. 
 The latter focus was on creating a website to showcase Lyons as a destination for Sundance 
 visitors, with discussions about whether it should be hosted by (possibly) the Chamber of 
 Commerce rather than the town website due to limitations around promoting specific 
 businesses. The group considered using local resources like high school or college students for 
 website development, and discussed incorporating information about outdoor activities, hiking, 
 biking, and other attractions that could appeal to visitors during both summer and winter 
 months.
