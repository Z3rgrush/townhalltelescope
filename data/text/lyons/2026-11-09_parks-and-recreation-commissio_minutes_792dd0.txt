 Board presentation follow-up: Dog Waste Pilot Project and post-Pilot Initiative – 
Eva Guenther
i. Presentation regarding the successful dog waste pilot program at the dog 
park was well received by BOT. The program will continue. 
ii. The expansion may be to other smaller dispensers in town, but that could 
be another pilot and may cause initial confusion.
iii. An easy win would be to coordinate and integrate dog waste composting 
with town clean-up efforts (especially the Spring River cleanup) where 
volunteers can pick up waste and dispose of the dog waste in the dog 
park compostable container.
iv. Dog waste program is incorporated in the budget for 2027. 
v. PRC discussed the continued low dog licensure in town at 27%, which is 
difficult to enforce.

i. Staff and budget-related: The need to review the comprehensive plan,

iii. DRBOP advances comprehensive plan goals.
 Dr. Bop Plan Annual Review from 2017 – Process and Timeline- discussion with 
Town Planner Lisa Ritchie
i. Dr. Bop plan was adopted 9 years ago and requires a 10-year review in 
2027. There is not money budgeted in 2027 for a consultant, so the staff 
would have to review. 
ii. The commission agreed to look at the plan's status. PRC can help review 
in 2027 or 2028, starting with a background and diving into what has and 
has not been done, including what has been implemented and what 
needs changes.
iii. Public engagement is an important component of the future updates, and 
a strategy for community engagement would be guided by the town.
 Wildland fire ordinance- As it relates to parks and operations-
i. BOT is addressing a mandatory wildfire draft ordinance, noting that 
survey results show high awareness of wildfire risk but limited support for 
mandatory requirements. 
ii. Wildfire and insurance don’t have a lot of overlapping or incentives. 
Mandatory disclosure went into effect in October on how they assessed 
your risk needs to be disclosed.  
 BOT Liaison – Greg Lowell BOT Update
 Liaison updates
i. BOT Passed Ordinance 1227 to change the meaning on the municipal 
code, on second reading, that restricts individual board members from 
taking official direction from single trustees (including the Mayor) without 
explicit board authorization.
1. Currently Staff checks with BOT for approval. 
2. PRC suggestion to add Ordinance 1227 to the bottom of the 
agenda.
3. Perhaps a formalized process for what to work on. Add it to every 
agenda, BOT liaison and Staff to enforce it.
4. Highlight there is a parallel accountability of the individuals on the 
commissions. 
ii. Northern foothills bike pathway- Boulder County transportation tax, trying 
for federal funding. Four problem areas, (Lyons, Boulder, Greenbriar, 
Neva Road). Wildlife crossings are getting looked at as well.
