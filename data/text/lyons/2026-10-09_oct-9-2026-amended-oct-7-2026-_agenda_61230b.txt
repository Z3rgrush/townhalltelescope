 2.  Task Force Structure — What's Next 
 Now that the Town's core ask has been accomplished (the SELL ordinance enabling 
 festival housing), does it still make sense for the group to remain a town-sponsored 
 commission, with the associated rules and sunshine-law requirements? 
 Discussion of transitioning to operate under a local nonprofit — Chamber of Commerce 
 and Creative District both seem like natural fits. This decision also determines who takes 
 ownership of a few unfinished workstreams: the Lyons elevator pitch and brochure, the 
 outdoor/adventure programming inventory, and the Sundance destination website the 
 Chamber had been considering hosting. 
 3.  Beautification Funding — Director's Brunch Prep 
 The Town has directed staff to find $5,000 this year for beautification along the route into 
 town ahead of the January 26th Director's Brunch at River Bend (~225 attendees 
 arriving mid-morning by bus). 
 Discussion: what will make the biggest impression on a drive-by — removing eyesores, 
 fixing up tired spots, signage, festive touches — and how to stretch the funding as far as 
 possible. Quick check: status of Sundance Institute's downtown banners/branded 
 materials, originally targeted for December 1st alongside the tree lighting. 
 4.  SELL Update

 September 23rd community info session, and any open questions.
