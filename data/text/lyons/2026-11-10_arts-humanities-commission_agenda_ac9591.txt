2. Land Acknowledgement          
 The LAHC acknowledges the town of Lyons is on the ancestral homelands and 
unceded territory of Indigenous Peoples who have traversed, lived in and stewarded 
lands in the Boulder Valley since time immemorial.  Those Indigenous Nations include 
the Arapahoe, Cheyenne, Comanche, Kiowa, Sioux, Ute and others.  Our benefit from 
this land came at a dire cost to these people and we give thanks to them.
