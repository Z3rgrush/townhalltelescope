 ●  New Business 
 a.  Aaron Caplan: Flagstone Culvert Investigation & Proposed Repairs -  Review the 
 Flagstone Culvert Investigation and discuss planned repairs with Town staff. Staff is 
 seeking HPC input on the proposed work from a historic preservation perspective. 
 i.  Requested Action:  Review the investigation and provide  feedback on historic 
 preservation considerations related to the planned culvert repairs. 
 ii.  https://www.townoflyons.com/DocumentCenter/View/3166/Lyons-Flagston 
 e-Culvert_Final-Report_Jul_16_2025 
 Staff sought HPC input for DOLA and History Colorado on proposed repairs funded by an 
 approximately $482,000 DOLA grant. 
 Bryan Heisinger noted that the full 2,490-foot culvert (site 5BL9093, constructed post-1891) was 
 documented by CDOT in 2002 and recommended by the recorder as eligible for the National 
 Register of Historic Places (Criteria A and C, locally quarried sandstone) and as potentially 
 contributing to the Lyons Sandstone Buildings Historic District (5BL.241). This was a 
 recommendation only; the culvert is not listed or designated. A visible segment runs between 
 4th and 5th Streets. 
 Section 106 review was initially raised. On further research, Bryan believes the project is likely 
 not a federal Section 106 undertaking but may require a History Colorado determination of effect 
 tied to DOLA funding. Slip-lining of the subterranean portions and in-kind replacement of the 
 flagstone lids were discussed, though these appeared less feasible. 
 HPC consensus: 
 -  The Commission has no strong position on repairs to the non-visible, subterranean 
 portions of the culvert. 
 -  Structural and public-safety needs come first. 
 Persons needing accommodations or special assistance should contact the Town at 
 hr@townoflyons.com  as soon as possible, but no later  than 72 hours before the scheduled meeting.
