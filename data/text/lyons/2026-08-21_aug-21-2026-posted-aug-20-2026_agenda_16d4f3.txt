 Lyons Sundance Film Festival Task Force — Meeting Recap 
 Monday, July 6, 2026 | 3:00–4:30 PM | Lyons Community Library, Effie Banta Room 
 Attendees: Betsy, Gil, Chrystal, Tanya, Catherine (+ Rene via Zoom) 
 Strategic Direction — The Most Important Decision of the Meeting 
 The group made a meaningful shift in strategy. Rather than attempting to host film screenings 
 during Sundance — which would put Lyons in competition with the festival and risk damaging 
 relationships — the task force agreed to pivot toward  private events, curated experiences, 
 music, and entertainment  at local venues. 
 Betsy flagged a useful framing: the Mountain Film on Tour will be in Boulder in September, 
 which is a concrete reason to step back from film programming and let Lyons play a different, 
 complementary role. 
 The direction for Lyons programming is now: 
 ●  Indoor farm dinners and storytelling dinners (both private and public formats) 
 ●  Live music 
 ●  Wellness experiences 
 ●  Curated outdoor adventures 
 The vision: position Lyons as a retreat and recovery destination during the festival window — 
 somewhere filmmakers, industry professionals, and visitors come to decompress, connect, and 
 experience something authentically Colorado. 
 1. SELL Ordinance Update 
 The Board of Trustees voted unanimously on June 29 to adopt Ordinance 1222, creating the 
 Special Event Lodging License. Key details: 
 ●  Applications open  August 1 
 ●  $125 for year one, $50 annual renewal 
 ●  29-day annual cap, during designated event windows only 
 ●  $1,000,000 liability insurance required — platform coverage (Airbnb, VRBO) does not 
 qualify 
 ●  ADUs, second homes, and non-primary residences are now eligible 
 ●  RVs permitted on Town-owned and privately owned non-residential commercial property 
 only 
 ●  Tenant protections included — landlords cannot displace tenants to obtain a SELL 
 The Sundance Film Festival will be formally designated as a Special Qualifying Event at the 
 July 20 BOT meeting  . 
 Note: Airbnb's "  Host Your Home  " program was flagged as a useful resource for property owners 
 learning how to list their homes.
