#6 Richard & Mary Sloss* 6646 Wenonga Terrace 
The Slosses are proposing to replace the wood fence along the rear and side of their property. 
Continued from the July 14th meeting so that a survey locating the trees and the City Arborist’s review of the 
trees could be provided.  
Summary of Property: 
• Character Area: Neighborhood Estates 
• Location of Common Green Space: Front & Side 
• Any Special Frontages: Corner Lot 
Summary of Project: 
The proposed rear fence will bridge the gap between their neighbor’s rear fence and the Slosses’ side fence.  
The gap was formed when the BZA denied a variance for the neighbor’s fence (installed without a building 
permit) in approximately the same location.  The Slosses propose to inset the fence 3 inches.  
The side fence that currently runs along Tomahawk Road was installed in a location not approved by the 
ARB or the Board of Zoning Appeals. 
Ordinance Compliance: 
The new fences are/will be in violation of a platted setback.  A variance of 30 feet is required.  The fences 
are also in violation of City Code Section 5-135.D.1 which requires that a solid fence extend no further from 
the principal building than 30% of the distance from the street (back of curb) to the point of the principal 
building that is closest to the street, and be no taller than 5 ft.  A variance of 18 ft. is required for the 
location, and a variance of 1 ft. is required for the height. 
Design Guideline Review: 
Section 2.7.3.C on page 109 of the Design Guidelines recommends that fences be located behind platted 
setbacks in conformance with the Mission Hills Zoning ordinances.  This recommendation has not been 
met.

July 28, 2026 
Section 2.7.2.A recommends that “Detached garages within Detached Accessory Buildings located in 
the side and rear yards are allowed when the lot is large enough to allow that garage to be minimally 
visible from the street and appropriately set back from neighbors per the Guidelines in this chapter.  
While the ARB will determine whether they believe the proposed structure is “minimally visible from the 
street”, staff considered the following: 
• The proposed structure is visible from two properties across the street.  Visibility for vehicles 
driving down the street is limited.  See attached diagram. 
• A portion of the structure will be located directly behind the existing garage. 
• The Design Guidelines allow a detached accessory building to have a 720 sq. ft. footprint.  The 
proposed structure is 530 sq. ft. 
• The Design Guidelines allow a detached accessory building in this location to have a 10 ft. eave 
height.  The proposed eave height is 8’11”.   
• The rear corner of the proposed structure is 10 ft. off the property line which is as close as it can 
be to the rear property line without a variance. 
• Potential new Design Guideline recommendation regarding maximum length/width of a 
Detached Accessory Building is 30 ft.  The proposed structure is 28 ft. wide. 
The garage doors as well as the pavement of the adjacent back-out area should be oriented into the 
property rather than toward neighbors whenever possible. Care should be taken to ensure that the 
driveway pavement approaching the garage does not dominate views from neighboring lots.  No more 
than one double-car garage or two single-car garage doors should face the street at the front yard.  A 
double-car garage door should be no wider than 18 ft.  A single-car garage door should be no wider 
than 8 ft.  The height of front facing garage doors should not exceed 8 ft.”  The proposed garage doors 
face the street.  The width of the garage door is 16 ft.  The height of the garage door is 8 ft.  These 
recommendations have been met. 
Page 34 of the Design Guidelines states that detached accessory buildings in Mission Hills “are always 
subordinate in height and mass to the main house, and are consistent with the architectural style of the 
Main House.” This recommendation has been met.
