Section 2.3 on pages 64 through 67 of the Design Guidelines provides specific recommendations for 
the Neighborhood Estates character area. 
Subsection A recommends that the main mass of the house should be no more than 50% of the lot 
width or 70 ft. whichever is greater. This section goes on to recommend that the depth of the main mass 
should not exceed 40 ft.  With the modifications, the main mass is approximately 30 feet wide and 30 
feet deep. The main mass height should be no more than 2 ½ stories and 35 ft.  At 39’-5” tall this

main mass should be located entirely within the Primary Building Area.

width.  The depth should not be greater than the width. The height is limited to 2 stories and should be 
at least 3 ft. shorter than the main mass.  These recommendations have been met.

been met.
