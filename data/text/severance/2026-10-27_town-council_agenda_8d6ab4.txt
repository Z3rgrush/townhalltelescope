  ACTION REQUESTED 
Finance and Management ask that the Town Council review and discuss 
the proposed 2027 Budget, the proposed 2027 Town of Severance Fee 
Schedule, and the proposed 2027 increases in water, wastewater, 
stormwater, and transportation rates. 
Discussion 
 
 BRIEF HISTORY OF ITEM 
 
 
  BRIEF SUMMARY 
This discussion item will present the proposed 2027 Budget, along with the proposed 2027 utility 
rates and Fee Schedule. Finance and Management are proposing a 1.5% increase in the water rate 
fee, a 6% increase in the wastewater rate fee, a $0 increase in the stormwater rate fee, and a $0 
increase in the transportation utility fee. 
 
Finance and Management are recommending the following changes to the 2027 Fee Schedule: 
• Water Rates (Proposed 1.5% Rate Increase) 
• Wastewater Rates (Proposed 6% Rate Increase) 
• Decreased Impact fees 
• New Sign Storage Fee 
• Increased PD Body Cam Fee 
 
 
  PUBLIC SUPPORT/CONCERN 
None at this time. 
 
  ANALYSIS AND RECOMMENDATION 
Finance and Management recommend that the Town Council consider the proposed rate increases 
and accept all proposed changes to the 2027 Fee Schedule, so that staff can bring these forward as 
an action item in November. If there is consensus among the Town Council, Management, and 
Finance will make any requested changes to the 2027 Town of Severance Budget and present it at 
the November 10th Council Meeting.
