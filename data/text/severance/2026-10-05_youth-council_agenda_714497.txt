 The purpose of the Public Comment is for members of the public to speak to the Youth Council

  ACTION REQUESTED 
Town Management asks that the Youth Council discuss the appointment of 
a Chair and take action. 
• Actions that may be taken: 
o Move to appoint a Chair for a one-year term 
 
Action Requested 
 
 BRIEF HISTORY OF ITEM 
  
  BRIEF SUMMARY 
Per section 2.7.60 Officers of the Severance Municipal Code: 
 
The Youth Council shall annually elect a Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, and Vice Secretary 
from among its members. The Vice Chair shall perform the duties of the Chair in the Chair's 
absence or inability to act, and the Vice Secretary shall perform the duties of the Secretary in 
the Secretary's absence or inability to act. 
  
  PUBLIC SUPPORT/CONCERN 
None at this time.  
  ANALYSIS AND RECOMMENDATION 
Town Management recommends that the Youth Council take action and move to appoint a Chair for a 
one-year term.
