History: Severance High School opened in the fall of 2019 with only Sophomore and Freshmen classes, adding a new 
class each year. The SHS After prom committee began as a very small group of parents interested in providing a safe 
alternative for activities after the dance ended and was run by one parent for 2 years but not affiliated with the school. 
At the graduation of that parent’s student, the event was at risk of not continuing. Fortunately, a group of 5 junior class 
parents stepped up in February of that school year and were able to raise enough funds with the help of the towns of 
Severance and Windsor providing half of the cost. At this time, the organization was still not affiliated with the school 
and at risk of not continuing. Working to establish an ongoing structure and relationship to support the students at SHS 
for many years to come, one member drafted bylaws and with the support of the remaining senior parents, the group 
became recognized as a parent organization with Weld re4 School District status. Although the senior students 
graduated, a few of those parents have been able to continue on the committee as they have younger students at SHS 
and are providing guidance and leadership. The continuation of the After Prom event at SHS has become a tradition and 
expectation by the student body and school. Through much time, dedication, and work, this will be the 7th After Prom 
Event!

1. Grant Request Summary
Requesting organization: Severance Chamber of Commerce
Federal tax classification: IRC Section 501(c)(6) chamber of commerce / business league (not a 501(c)(3))
Amount requested: $2,000
Project: New Severance Chamber digital community platform / website
Project timing: Target completion within approximately 2-3 months of award
The Chamber is requesting $2,000 to help build a modern public-facing digital platform that makes it easier for 
residents, businesses, employers, job seekers, and community partners to find and use local business information 
in one place. The project is intended to improve business visibility, community access to Chamber resources, and 
the usefulness of the Chamber as a connector between the Town, local businesses, schools, and residents.
Proposed public-facing capabilities
 Searchable local business directory and member profiles.
 Centralized events and community calendar.
 Jobs and workforce opportunity listings.
 Local deals, offers, and business promotions.
 Business assistance, education, and local resource information.
 Improved search visibility, accessibility, mobile usability, and analytics.
Chamber contribution
The Chamber will contribute development oversight, technology administration, content coordination, ongoing 
maintenance, and future expansion beyond the $2,000 grant request. The grant is intended to accelerate the initial 
public platform build rather than create an ongoing Town operating obligation.
Application field: IRS 501(c)(3)?
Answer: NO. The Severance Chamber is recognized as tax-exempt under IRC Section 501(c)(6).
2. Organization Description, Mission and Leadership
The Severance Chamber of Commerce is a Colorado nonprofit chamber of commerce serving businesses and the 
broader Severance business community. The Chamber works to strengthen the local business environment 
through connections, visibility, education, advocacy, community collaboration, and practical resources for 
businesses at different stages of growth.
Mission
The Severance Chamber of Commerce leads with integrity to champion local businesses and economic 
vitality. As a trusted hub of resources and connections, we create opportunities for growth, foster lasting 
partnerships, and invest in the success of our town. Through advocacy, education, and collaboration, we 
empower businesses to leave lasting legacies as partners in shaping the unique character and quality of life 
that define Severance, Colorado.
Current leadership and governance
The Chamber currently operates under a sole-director governance structure. Dustin Mark Peyser serves as 
President and Sole Director. Program members, ambassadors, advisors, liaisons, officers, contractors, and 
volunteers do not become voting Directors solely by holding those roles. The Severance Chamber remains a 
separate legal and financial entity from the Timnath Chamber of Commerce.
Community role
 Connect local businesses with customers, peer businesses, and community partners.
 Create networking and educational opportunities, including collaboration with Clearview Library District and 
regional business-resource partners.
 Support workforce and career-readiness connections with Weld RE-4 schools and local employers.

Severance is growing, but local business information is still fragmented across websites, social profiles, email lists,

 Residents can more easily discover and support local businesses.
 Businesses gain a reliable place to publish profiles, events, jobs, and offers.
 Job seekers and students gain another path to local employment and workforce opportunities.
 Community partners gain a clearer place to point businesses for education and support resources.
 The Chamber can measure usage and continuously improve what information businesses and residents 
actually use.
