FROM: BRIAN HUTCHINSON, PUBLIC WORKS SUPERVISOR
DATE: AUGUST 10, 2026 
SUBJECT:   EDWARDSVILLE TRAIL SEALING 
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
City Council to consider awarding a bid to McConnell & Associates for the purpose of 
repairing and sealing the City trail system for $42,200 and authorizing the City Manager
to enter into an agreement with for said services. 
FINANCIAL IMPACT
This project was bid by McConnell & Associates at $42,200. There are funds available in 
Special Highway Fund. 
BACKGROUND 
City staff reached out to five (5) local companies for bids for this trail seal project. Two (2) 
of those companies did not respond; two (2) of the companies submitted bids, and one 
(1) company gave a no-bid.  
The trail sealing will consist of cleaning the pavement with high-velocity air blowers used 
in conjunction with stiff brooms. Then they will seal the pavement with 2 coats of high 
solids MAC Pro-Blend pavement sealer. Sealer will be in slurry form with the addition of 
four (4) pounds of silica sand per 1 gallon. The first coat will be applied by squeegee; the 
second coat will be applied by spray. Squeegee application provides 20% to 25% more 
sealer than a spray application. The cracks shall be cleaned and filled with hot-pour 
rubber-asphalt sealer. All cracks 1/4" or greater will be filled with the exception of 
alligatored areas. The joints between the asphalt and curb will not be filled.  
 
Ongoing maintenance of existing infrastructure is crucial. The trail system is well-used 
and is an important component of Edwardsville’s quality of life. The project will take 
roughly two (2) days to complete. The seal should last between 3 and 6 years.  
 
The public works department has worked closely with McConnell & Associates in the past. 
They have provided quality services and have a good working relationship with staff. 
Therefore, City Council to consider awarding a bid to McConnell & Associates for the 
purpose of repairing and sealing the City trail system for $42,200 and authorizing the City 
Manager to enter into an agreement with for said services.

Proposed Scope of Services 
The City of Edwardsville, KS (Edwardsville) has requested a proposal for consulting services 
to support its compensation program and advance key initiatives.  
 
In 2024 Edwardsville completed a compensation study which included market pricing, 
competitive pay assessment, and salary structure build.  Since the study the city has 
received feedback that current progression through pay range pace is unrewarding for 
employees.  They would like to design a step structure with more sizeable increases tied to 
time in job anniversaries.  They envision providing guaranteed increases at 3, 5, and 10 
years in job, as well as continued steps past 10 years.  The frequency of subsequent steps is 
yet to be determined. 
 
This study will include updating scopes on previous job matches, competitive pay 
assessment, and a new salary structure build with financial analysis of various options. 
 
The scope and objectives presented below define the focus of the consulting engagement 
and may be refined or adjusted based on additional input from Edwardsville. 
 
 
1. Salary Structure Design  
Develop comprehensive, data-driven salary structures that are competitive, 
equitable, and aligned with both internal goals and external market trends. 
 
Approach and Deliverables 
Our approach to every engagement combines proven technology, rigorous methodology, 
and experienced consulting professionals committed to delivering high-quality, data-
driven results. The following details the specific services proposed for this engagement, 
beginning with an initial discovery and data review that establishes the foundation for all 
subsequent work. 
Discovery and Data Review 
Each consulting engagement begins with a structured discovery process before any 
analytical work commences. The discovery phase is where we develop organizational 
understanding that makes all downstream analysis meaningful. We take time to learn not 
just what the data shows, but the context surrounding it: how the organization has 
historically approached compensation, where current practices are working well, where 
tensions exist, and what leadership is trying to accomplish through this engagement. 
The formal kickoff meeting establishes shared expectations on scope, timeline, decision 
rights, and communication protocols. We confirm who is empowered to make decisions at 
 
 
 5 
each stage, which stakeholder groups will need to be engaged, and how deliverables will 
be reviewed and approved. This alignment prevents delays later and ensures the entire 
project team is oriented around the same objectives from day one. 
Following kickoff, we conduct an in-depth review of the labor markets in which 
Edwardsville competes for talent, alongside a thorough assessment of existing 
compensation structures, HR policies, job documentation, and organizational design. We 
collect and validate all relevant employee and position data, identify gaps that must be 
resolved before analysis begins, and develop a clear picture of the current state that 
informs every phase of the work ahead. 
01 Salary Structure Design 
Salary structures are a critical component of a well-designed compensation system, 
establishing clear pay ranges for employees and ensuring internal equity, market 
competitiveness, and alignment with organizational goals. Developing a tailored salary 
structure helps organizations attract, retain, and motivate talent while fostering fair and 
consistent pay practices. By establishing defined pay ranges based on job levels, market 
data, and business objectives, salary structures serve as a foundation for managing 
compensation practices efficiently and transparently across the workforce. 
 
Our approach to developing salary structures is comprehensive, data-driven, and tailored 
to the unique needs and strategic goals of the organization. 
Edwardsville would like to move to a step structure approach with more sizeable 
guaranteed increases at defined intervals.  The project will model up to three options and 
will be coupled with financial analysis to determine ongoing budgetary feasibility. 
Summary of Approach 
Initial Assessment of Current Compensation Practices 
• Review the current compensation structure (pay grades, ranges, and 
job categories) to understand existing frameworks and identify any 
gaps or inconsistencies. 
Design Salary Structure(s) and Ranges 
• Advise on key design elements of structure(s), including structure 
types, number of steps, midpoint differentials, and range spreads. 
• Based on job levels and market data, design salary ranges that reflect 
both competitive pay trends and organizational goals.  
• Model structure options in alignment with organizational objectives 
and external competitiveness (market-aligned and mission-centric).  
Deliverables

differentials, job assignments, and financial impact of implementation.
