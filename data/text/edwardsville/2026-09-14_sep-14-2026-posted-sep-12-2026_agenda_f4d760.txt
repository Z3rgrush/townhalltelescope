 TO: MAYOR & CITY COUNCIL 
 FROM: CHANTAL FRIERSON, CITY CLERK 
    DATE: SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 
 SUBJECT: SPECIAL EVENT PERMIT – BK5K RUN 
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
City Council to consider Special Event permit application from Fastenal for the BK5K 
Run/Walk. 
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
The application fee for special events is $50.00 with a $25.00 per day fee each day of 
the event.  
 
BACKGROUND 
 
Fastenal Distribution Center has requested to be the operator of a 5K run to take place 
on the morning of September 19th. 
 
The BK5K run was established by Fastenal as a mechanism to give back and provide aid 
to the communities where the company has facilities. It was established to honor Fastenal 
founder Bob Kierlin, who was a lifelong supporter of youth programs. This would be the 
fifth year the race would be held in Edwardsville.  Fastenal will also bring back its grant 
opportunities for local agencies involved in active lifestyles and healthy programming for 
young people. 
 
The race will begin at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday and is expected to conclude by 11:30 a.m. 
The proposed route has not changed and as with previous races, EPD will provide 
additional support at the crossings at Blake and 9th streets. All other race logistics will be 
handled by Fastenal staff. 
 
Staff recommends the City Council approve the special event permit for the BK5K run to 
take place on September 19, 2026. 

                                                                                                                                      ITEM #4D 
 TO: MAYOR & CITY COUNCIL 
 FROM: SETH MOTES, INTERN 
    DATE: SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 
 SUBJECT: TECHNICAL REVIEW COMMITTEE APPOINTMENT 
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
City Council to consider the mayoral appointment of Robert Niffen from the City Planning 
Commission to serve on the Stormwater Runoff Technical Review Committee (SRTC). 
The SRTC plays a quasi-adjudicative role in accordance with City Code §3.11.010, which 
allows aggrieved persons to appeal stormwater -related decisions made by the City 
Engineer.  
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
NONE 
 
BACKGROUND 
City Code §3.11.010 establishes a formal appeals process whereby any person 
aggrieved by a final determination of the City Engineer concerning stormwater runoff may 
first appeal in writing to the Technical Review Committee, and subsequently, if necessary, 
to a court of competent jurisdiction.  
 
The ordinance mandates that a hearing may be requested within ten (10) days of the City 
Engineer’s final decision. The Technical Review Committee is responsible for reviewing 
any relevant evidence presented by the appellant, offering a recommendation—reversal, 
modification, or confirmation—to the City Engineer. The Engineer, who participates in the 
hearing, retains final administrative discretion under the ordinance.  
 
To fulfill this responsibility, the Council must formally constitute the Technical Review 
Committee. City Code §3.11.010 establishes that five (5) members of the Planning 
Commission must sit on the Technical Review Committee. There are currently four (4) 
members who sit on the Technical Review Committee who were appointed on 
06/09/2025. The current members are John Altevogt, Tim Sweeten, Ian Abbott, and Ryan 
Parker. 
 
Mayor Caiharr wishes to appoint Robert Niffen to the Stormwater Runoff Technical 
Review Committee.

 FROM: DOUG GERBER, INTERIM CITY MANAGER 
 DATE: SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 
 SUBJECT: BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING, BUDGET CERTIFICATION 
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
Governing Body to conduct a public hearing to consider certifying the FY27 Annual Budget and 
consider the certification of the FY27 Annual Budget. 
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
The maximum budget authority  for all funds  for the proposed FY27  Annual Budget as 
summarized in the Notice of Budget Hearing is $16,101,071. The published maximum mil levy 
is 36.425 mills and the revenue neutral rate is 34.346 mills. 
 
BACKGROUND 
 
The City Council will conduct a  public hearing related to the review and adoption of the FY26  
Annual Budget. The City Council will also certify the FY2 7 Annual Budget. The Council has 
already adopted a not to exceed mil levy rate, so the final step in the budget process is to certify 
the FY27 Annual Budget. 
 
The purpose of the public hearing is to hear resident comments regarding the certification of 
the final FY27  Budget. Notice of this hearing was provided  in accordance with state statute. 
The FY27 Budget has been an agenda topic at City Council meetings since May, where the 
Governing Body established some general goals for the FY27  Budget, including tax relief,  
taking care of employees, and making tangible investments in the community. To this end, the 
Governing Body has discussed numerous budgetary options. The recommended budget option 
is broadly summarized in the table on the following page and should accomplish the Governing 
Body’s budget goals. 
 
 
36.425 total mill levy (approximate); No change to Debt Service Mill 
Balanced General Fund Budget. Revenues exceed Expenditures 
$9,516,290 General Fund expenditures; $9,517,184 General Fund revenues 
Increased Library budget in 2027 
  
The overall City budget for FY2 7 is slightly higher  than the FY26 budget  across all funds . 
Changes to the General Fund revenues and expenditures for FY2 7 are consistent with the 5-
year average for the General Fund.  
 
Staff recommends the City Council follow this procedure when moving through the actions of 
the City Council meeting: 
 
1. Open the public hearing regarding the certification of the FY27 Annual Budget 
2. Close the budget public hearing and consider certification of the FY27 Annual Budget.
