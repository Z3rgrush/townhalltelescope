 FROM: DOUG GERBER, INTERIM CITY MANAGER

 SUBJECT: COMPENSATION STUDY  
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
City Council to allow the City Manager to enter in a contract with Salary.com for a 
compensation and classification study in an amount not to exceed $9,600.   
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
The proposed fee amount is a not to exceed cost of $9,600. If actual project hours exceed 48 
hours, additional fees will be billed at the rate of $200 an hour.
 
BACKGROUND 
 
The City of Edwardsville, KS (Edwardsville) requested a proposal for consulting services to 
support its compensation program and advance key initiatives. In 2024, Edwardsville 
completed a compensation study which included market pricing, competitive pay 
assessment, and salary structure build. This study was not implemented and the Governing 
Body formed a committee to examine alternatives. They would like to design a step structure 
with more sizeable increases tied to time in job anniversaries. The Governing Body envisions
providing guaranteed increases at 3, 5, and 10 years in job, as well as continued steps past 
10 years. The frequency of subsequent steps is yet to be determined. This study will include 
updating scopes on previous job matches, competitive pay assessment, and a new salary 
structure build with financial analysis of various options.
 
Salary.com was asked to conduct a benefit comparison as well. However, they do not have a 
reliable system to compare benefits. Therefore, it makes sense to proceed with the 
compensation portion of the study at this point. The ideal scenario for the study is to be able 
to have information in late 2026. This timeframe will allow us to incorporate appropriate 
changes into the 2027 pay structure. Therefore, we recommend that the City Council allow 
the City Manager to enter in a contract with Salary.com for a compensation and classification 
study in an amount not to exceed $9,600.

ITEM # 8 
 TO: MAYOR & CITY COUNCIL 
 FROM: DOUG GERBER, INTERIM CITY MANAGER 
 DATE: AUGUST 28, 2026 
 SUBJECT: BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
Governing Body to consider setting a public hearing date of September 14, 2026 for the FY27 
Annual Budget. 
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
The revenue neutral rate for the FY25 budget is 34.346 mills. The final expenditure authority 
and mill levy will depend on Council direction stemming from the noticed public hearing. 
 
BACKGROUND 
 
Every year, cities are required to have a formal public hearing related to comments, questions, 
concerns of the annual operating budget. Notice of this hearing will be provided in accordance 
with state statute. The FY27 Budget has been an agenda topic at City Council meetings since 
April, where the Governing Body established some general goals for the FY2 7 Budget, 
including tax relief, improving the health of the general fund, and making tangible investments 
in the community. To this end, the Governing Body has discussed numerous potential 
scenarios. The Governing Body also established a maximum mill levy of 3 6.425 mils by 
adopting Resolution 2026- 13 at the July 13, 2026 City Council meeting. This Resolution 
declared their intent to (potentially) exceed the revenue neutral rate. The scenario that was set 
by the Governing Body  broadly summarized  below and  should accomplish the Governing 
Body’s budget goals. 
 
1 Mill Cut 
36.425 total mill levy 
No change to Debt Service Mill 
Balanced General Fund Budget. Revenues exceed Expenditures 
No new employees 
Investment in equipment and infrastructure 
  
This is the latest date corresponding with the regular meeting schedule that a public hearing 
can be hel d. Additionally, this date mirrors the date of the revenue neutral public hearing. 
Therefore, Staff recommends that the Governing Body set a public hearing date of September 
14, 2026 for the FY27 Annual Budget.
