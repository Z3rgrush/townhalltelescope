CONSENT AGENDA
(The items listed under “Consent Agenda" are a group of items to be acted on with a single motion and vote. This agenda is designed to expedite the handling of limited routine matters by the Board. The Board has received the information on these matters in their Board packets prior to the meeting. The Mayor will ask if any citizen wishes to have, any specific item discussed. A Board member may request that an item be removed from the Consent Agenda on behalf of a citizen or himself/herself and state when the item will be discussed in the meeting or tabled to another meeting. The removal of an item will require a second and a majority vote of the Board to be removed. Items on the consent agenda are then voted on by a single motion, second, and non-roll call vote).
