PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES
• West Colfax LRA tax increment financing (TIF) expires in 2030
• Create a strategy to apply funding within the Corridor to further LRA and City goals
– Infrastructure upgrades
– Land acquisition and assembly
– Encourage new development
• Objective to impact catalytic change with a focus on economic development
– Projects that create influential change

CORRIDOR ANALYSIS
• Evaluated quantitative and qualitative data to determine opportunity sites and nodes 
for redevelopment
• Mapped recent on planned investment
– Planned/proposed development
– Recent development (built since 2015)
– Infrastructure projects
 West Colfax Transportation Safety Project
 North Dry Gulch Project
• Opportunity Sites
– Redevelopment Parcels: property with vacant buildings and safety concerns
– Vacant Parcels: property with no building improvement
– Public ownership: property owned by public entity

INITIAL FINDINGS
• Central Section – Pierce Street to Dover Street
– North Dry Gulch will solve floodplain issues
– Safety Projects focus on narrowing Colfax from 3 lanes to 2 lanes in each direction, 
installing medians and crosswalks, and lighting
– These investments alleviate significant infrastructure costs and improve safety, and are 
expected to attract private development
• Eastern Section – Pierce Street to Sheridan Boulevard
– Attracted recent development but also has longstanding development issues
– Opportunity node
• Western Section – Dover Street to Simms Street
– Attracted recent development but also has longstanding development issues
– Opportunity node

RECOMMENDATIONS
• Prioritize RTD property as catalytic driver for the western section of the corridor
– Initiate conversations with RTD
– Balance of TOD and economic development uses
• Prioritize Grand Mile scenario implementation
– Significant economic impact
– Identify available state funding to support business attraction
• Explore potential for King Soopers to relocate to or near the RTD site
– Grocer as retail anchor and attract supporting retail
– Opens current Quail Street Center for redevelopment
• Establish public private partnerships (P3)
– Necessary for project feasibility
