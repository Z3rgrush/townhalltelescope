Fixing a flatFixing a flatFixing a flat
1.Prepare bike
a.Open rim brake (if applicable)
b.If removing rear wheel, shift into the smallest
cog on your gears/cassette
2.Flip Bike Over
3.Remove wheel
4.Take tire partially off to access and remove
punctured tube
5.Inspect tire for damage and remove debris
6.Patch tube and slightly re-inflate; or slightly inflate
fresh spare tube to give it some shape
7.Reinstall tube and tuck tube into tire
8.Put tire back on, tucking tire bead back into the rim 
9.Inflate to pressure indicated on tire sidewall
10.Inspect tire to confirm the tire bead is properly
seated all the way around the rim
11.Reinstall wheel
12.Flip bike rightside up
13.Reconnect brake (if applicable)
14.Ride On!

Air
Are your tires properly inflated? If one feels low, re-inflate the tire. If your
tire was full yesterday and flat today, chances are you need to add sealant or
install a new tube. Sealant can help prevent flats from most small punctures.
Brakes
Check the function of each brake with a squeeze. Only ride if both work!
Chain
Is your chain lubed? This simple step can improve bicycle performance.
