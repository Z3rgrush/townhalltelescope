DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The Law Department provides legal services to City Council, the 
Mayor, all city departments, and boards and commissions.  
It reviews all contracts and provides legal advice on issues that arise 
from the daily operations of the City.
It prosecutes violations of the Pueblo Municipal Code in Municipal 
Court and represents the City in all appeals from Municipal Court.
It institutes and defends civil litigation on behalf of the City.
