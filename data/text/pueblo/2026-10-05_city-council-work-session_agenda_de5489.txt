Background - The 2019 Approved Initiative
Voters said “YES” in 
2019
● Voters allowed the Library 
District to use a mill levy to 
fund library operations. 
● This existing mill levy had 
previously been used to 
finance the construction of the 
Rawlings Library.
What did this vote do?
● Created no new taxes, thi 
initiative redirected existing 
funds to support all 
operations at PCCLD. 
● This allowed continued 
funding of library materials, 
services, and facility 
renovations.

What Changed 
– HB24B-1001
In 2024, the Colorado 
legislature passed HB24B-1001
This law imposes a 5.25% cap on 
year-over-year property tax 
growth for local governments and 
districts.
Even though local voters already 
approved the library’s funding 
structure, the state cap now 
overrides the local decision.

Educating the Public on this Initiative
If this measure 
passes
● The Tax rate stays 
exactly the same. 
● The Library keeps the 
revenue voters already 
approved in 2019. 
● No new financial 
burden on taxpayers. 
If this measure fails
● The Library cannot follow through on 
the promises made to the community in 
2019. 
● Potential impacts include: 
○ Pause to library branch renovations
○ Less library materials made available
○ Reduced hours of operations
● It creates an unpredictable budget, not 
allowing the Library District to keep up 
with cost of living and inflation.
