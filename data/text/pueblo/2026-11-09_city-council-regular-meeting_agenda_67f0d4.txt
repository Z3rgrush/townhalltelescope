and restates the schedule of permit, inspection and plan review fees charged by the 
Fire Department to conform it to the 2021 Edition of the International Fire Code, 
adopted by Ordinance No. 11209. The Resolution also establishes the two permit fees 
required by Sections 3-2-12(b) and 3-2-13(c) of the Pueblo Municipal Code, which are 
being adopted by companion Ordinance. 
PREVIOUS COUNCIL ACTION: 
On June 22, 2026, City Council adopted the 2021 International Fire Code (I.F.C.) by 
Ordinance No. 11209. That Ordinance repealed Chapter 2 of Title III in its entirety and 
enacted a new Chapter 2. 
 
On November 25, 2024, City Council passed Resolution No. 15828, amending 
Resolution No. 11711, which restated Section 1 in full and replaced the fee schedule 
with the schedule now in use. 
On September 14, 2009, City Council passed an ordinance amending Chapter 2 of Title 
III of the Municipal Code relating to the Fire Code, modifying the 2009 International Fire 
Code, as adopted and amended and establishing permit fees for required operational 
and construction permits. 
On September 28, 2009 City Council passed Resolution No. 11711 which established 
Page 38 of 585
the Plan Review Fee for reviewing construction plans for compliance with the 
International Fire Code. Resolution No. 11711 also set inspection and re-inspection 
fees required for special inspections for new businesses and sales tax, etc. 
BACKGROUND: 
Section 107.2.1 of the I.F.C., as adopted and amended by Ordinance No. 11209, 
requires that fees for permits and other Fire Department services be established by 
resolution of the City Council. 
 
Updating the fee schedule is necessary to reflect the new edition of the I.F.C. now in 
use. 
Three corrections accompany the update. Subsection A of the schedule identifies each 
construction permit by its subsection number in Section 105.7 of the International Fire 
Code. Construction permits are located at Section 105.6 in the 2021 Edition. A 
subsection for operational permit fees, which appeared in Resolution No. 11711 and 
was not carried forward in Resolution No. 15828, is restored. And Section 1 as restated 
expressly supersedes Section 1 of both prior Resolutions, so that only one schedule is 
in force. 
FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS: 
The financial impact is based on the fees for service for inspections/tasks performed. 
No fee amount carried forward from Resolution No. 15828 is changed by this 
Resolution.
