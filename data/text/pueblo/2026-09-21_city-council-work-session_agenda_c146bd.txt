Draft Steering Committee Framework · Pueblo Regional Transportation Authority 2
Pikes Peak Model → PueRTA
A practical structure for building regional consensus before the RTA ballot question.
PPRTA Feature Observed Model Pueblo RTA Adaptation
Formation Coalition Local governments, business groups, and community organizations shaped the original package. Use a broad steering committee instead of a purely elected-official working group.
Participating Governments PPRTA is a collaborative effort among six regional governments. Reserve seats for Pueblo County, City of Pueblo, Pueblo West, Colorado City, Rye, and Boone.
Citizen Advisory Structure PPRTA uses a 17-member Citizens Advisory Committee with government and at-large seats. Include at-large City, County, and rural resident seats from the beginning.
Technical Support RTA work requires project, funding, transit, and governance detail. Place CDOT, legal, finance, and program support at the table as non-voting advisors.
Formation Output The governing framework needs an IGA, project list, revenue structure, and ballot language. Organize the committee around governance, projects, finance, and public outreach work groups.
Model sources: PPRTA History; PPRTA Citizens Advisory Committee materials; PPRTA IGA materials.

Non-Voting Advisors + Work Groups (AS NEEDED)
Advisors should support technical accuracy without controlling the policy decision.
Non-Voting Advisors
Seat Representation Purpose
CDOT Region 2 Representative State transportation Coordinate state-interface issues and project eligibility.
RTA Counsel Formation / CRS compliance Draft IGA, governance structure, and ballot language.
Finance / Bond Advisor Revenue and bonding Model tax scenarios, bonding capacity, and allocation formulas.
Program / PM Support Administration Maintain schedule, minutes, decision log, and public-facing materials.
Recommended Work Groups
Work Group Primary Charge Expected Output
Governance + IGA Boundary, board structure, member commitments, voting rules. Draft IGA and governance framework.
Projects + Prioritization Collect, screen, and rank capital, maintenance, and transit projects. Prioritized project list and cost ranges.
Finance + Ballot Structure Evaluate sales tax level, sunset period, bonding, and allocations. Revenue package and ballot assumptions.
Public Outreach + Coalition Build public understanding and stakeholder support. Outreach calendar and messaging plan.
End product: boundary, project list, revenue structure, allocation formula, draft IGA, and ballot language.
