B) Items for Discussion 
 1. AVIS Speed and Red Light Cameras and Designation of Speed Corridors 
 
The purpose of this item is to provide an update on the performance of the Automated 
Vehicle Identification System (AVIS) corridors adopted under Ordinance No. 098, 
2024, (formerly known as Red Light Camera Radar) and recommends targeted City 
Code amendments to align local practices with Colorado law. The analysis considers 
reported crashes, officer-issued speeding and reckless-driving citations, and newly 
available fixed-camera and transportable-system activity.  
 2. Surveillance Technology Governance Policy Update 
 
City of Fort Collins City Council Agenda 
The purpose of this item is to seek feedback from Council on the draft Surveillance 
Technology Governance Policy. This policy establishes a structured, transparent 
framework to govern how City departments acquire, deploy, and manage the use of 
surveillance while protecting community privacy, civil rights, and public trust. 
 3. Our Climate Future Progress Update: Goals, Actions, and Impact 
 
The purpose of this item is to provide an update to Council on progress towards the 
Our Climate Future (OCF) 2030 goals, highlighting trends and planned actions over 
the next 2–4 years to continue progress towards the goals. Those goals are: 
• Reduce community greenhouse gas emissions by 80% below 2005 levels, 
• Provide 100% renewable electricity, and 
• Achieve zero waste through 100% landfill diversion. 
The presentation will highlight examples of work completed in 2025 and 2026 
supporting OCF goals and outcomes and share next steps for staff to further develop 
metrics and indicators to track progress. 
 4. Zero Waste Progress Update: Current Initiatives and Future Infrastructure 
Pathways 
 
The purpose of this item is to provide updates on the City's Zero Waste progress 
across three areas: the 2025 Community diversion rate; past, current, and emerging 
initiatives; and pathways toward community-scale infrastructure for recycling organics 
(yard trimmings and food scraps) and construction and demolition (C&D) materials.
