BE A PART OF HISTORY!                                                        
2026 TIME CAPSULE AT THE OVERLAND TRAIL MUSEUM 
In honor of the Sesquisemiquincentennial — America 250 and Colorado 150, the Overland Trail 
Museum is creating a 2026 Time Capsule!  The Time Capsule will be sealed on December 31, 
2026, with instructions for it to be opened 50 years later, in 2076. 
The Time Capsule itself is a piece of Logan County history—a historic safe once used at the 
Logan County Sheriff’s Office. Inside, we hope to preserve the stories, experiences and everyday 
life of the people of Logan County in 2026. 
We are looking for ideas and contributions from individuals, families, churches, schools, clubs, 
organizations and businesses to help us capture what life is like in our community today. 
What will life in Logan County be like in 2026? 
What would you want people in 2076 to know? 
• What did things cost? Restaurant menus, grocery prices, gas prices, etc. 
• How were our sports teams doing?  What kind of music were we listening to? 
• What was happening in our community?  What were our schools like? 
• What was happening in our churches and organizations? 
• What businesses were part of our community? 
• What were people celebrating, worrying about or excited about? 
• What everyday objects, photographs, newspapers or other items might help tell the story of 
2026? 
Be creative! We want the Time Capsule to reflect the real people and everyday experiences of 
Logan County—not just the major events.  Our hope is that when the Time Capsule is opened 50 
years from now, in 2076, the people of the future will be able to open that historic safe and 
discover what life was really like in Logan County in 2026.  How can you be a part of 
history? 
If you have an idea, would like to contribute something, or would like to learn more about the project, 
please contact the Overland Trail Museum.
