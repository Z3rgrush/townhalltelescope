Subject: Ordinance amending the Liquor Licensing School Distance Restriction Exemption 
 
Background: At the July 28, 2026, City Council Work Session, the City Clerk provided Council 
with information regarding the School Distance Restriction Exemption currently included in the 
City Code. Council directed Staff to bring forward an amendment to eliminate the language 
stating that the exemption applies “only if granted for restaurant purposes” for the Northeastern 
Junior College campus.  
 
Removing this language would allow Hotel & Restaurant, Beer and Wine, and now Fermented 
Malt Beverage and Wine licenses near the Northeastern Junior College campus, provided all 
other applicable requirements under state law and City Code are met. This change will allow a 
Fermented Malt Beverage and Wine license to potentially be issued to other types of 
businesses, such as a small convenience store or market, rather than limiting the exemption to 
restaurants. 
 
This amendment would apply only to the Northeastern Junior College campus. All other school 
distance restrictions currently established in the City Code would remain in effect for other 
schools and educational institutions. 
 
Recommendation: Staff recommends approval of this Ordinance.
