S
ubject: First Reading – Ordinance Amending Zoning Code for Veterinary Services in MS Zone 
BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE 
This is the first reading of an Ordinance amending the Municipal Zoning Code to permit small 
animal veterinary clinics within the Medical Services (MS) zone district. The amendment is 
strictly limited to the treatment of small domestic pets, such as dogs and cats, and does not 
permit large animal or agricultural veterinary care. 
ANALYSIS 
Small animal veterinary practices operate similarly to human outpatient medical offices, with 
minimal noise and light traffic. To ensure full compatibility with neighboring medical uses, the 
proposed code language mandates this use in MS zones by conditional use permit and for 
only small animal veterinary services. Adding small animal care to the MS district provides 
modern animal healthcare options while utilizing existing professional office spaces. 
RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommends approval of the Ordinance on First Reading and scheduling the public 
hearing and second reading for the next regular City Council meeting.
