Subject: Second Reading – Ordinance Amending Zoning Code for Veterinary Services in MS Zone 
 
BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE 
This is the second reading of an Ordinance amending the Municipal Zoning Code to permit 
animal veterinary clinics within the Medical Services (MS) zone district.  
ANALYSIS 
Animal veterinary practices operate similarly to human outpatient medical offices, with minimal 
noise and light traffic. To ensure full compatibility with neighboring medical uses, the proposed 
code language mandates this use in MS zones by conditional use permit. Adding animal care 
to the MS district provides modern animal healthcare options while utilizing existing 
professional office spaces. 
RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommends approval of the Ordinance.

Subject: Resolution setting the ballot language for a 1% Sales and Use tax increase for the 
November 3, 2026, Election 
 
Background: At the August 11, 2026, City Council meeting, Council discussed the upcoming 
ballot question, including the proposed term length for the tax. Council expressed concern that 
a 10-year term may be too long and could negatively impact voter support. Council requested 
that Staff bring forward alternative ballot language incorporating a 5-year term for further 
discussion and consideration at this meeting. 
 
A 5-year term would provide the City an opportunity to demonstrate to citizens how the funds 
are being utilized and the benefits of the investment before considering a longer term in the 
future. 
 
The ballot language must be finalized at this meeting in order to meet the required timelines for 
submission to Logan County for inclusion in the coordinated election. 
 
If Council wishes to consider a different term length, Staff recommends selecting an odd 
number of years. This would allow the question to be considered during a regular municipal 
election rather than requiring the City to call another special election. 
 
Recommendation: Staff recommends approval of this Resolution.
