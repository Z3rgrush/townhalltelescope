Subject: Ordinance 11 – Criminal penalties 
 
Summary 
 
This proposed ordinance amends Chapter 12, Section 12- 23 of the Sterling City Code to 
automatically align and cap municipal criminal penalties with state statutory limits for comparable 
misdemeanors and petty offenses as required under the Colorado Supreme Court cases People 
v. Camp and People v. Simons . This serves as an interim measure while the City works on a 
comprehensive overhaul and rewrite of Chapter 12's offense and penalty structures 
 
Recommendation: Staff recommends approval of this Resolution. 
 
Attachments:  
Ordinance 11 
Ordinance No. 11 
Series of 2026 
 
An Ordinance amending Chapter 12, Section 12- 23 of the City Code of Sterling, 
Colorado, relating to penalties for violations to align municipal criminal penalties 
with Colorado Revised Statutes. 
 
Introduced by Councilmember ______________________________________________ 
 
Whereas, the City of Sterling is a home rule municipality; and  
Whereas, in order to be in compliance with recent Colorado Supreme Court cases   
People v. Camp and  People v. Simons  the criminal penalties section of the City Code 
requires amendments and; 
Whereas, the City of Sterling is currently engaged in a comprehensive review and 
rewrite of Chapter 12 of the Sterling City Code to establish an updated, modernized, and 
comprehensive municipal offense and penalty structure; and 
Whereas, this ordinance is intended as an interim measure to cap municipal 
criminal penalties in accordance with the Colorado Revised Statutes  and Colorado 
Supreme Court rulings cited above,  while the complete revision of Chapter 12 is being 
finalized, and; 
Whereas, the City Council is required to amend the C ity Code to align to the 
Colorado law.  
Now, Therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Sterling, 
Colorado: 
Chapter 12 – Offenses, Section 12-23 – Penalty, of the City Code of Sterling, Colorado, 
is hereby amended to read in full as follows: 
 Sec. 12-23. - Penalty. 
The penalty for a violation of this chapter shall be the same as that specifically provided 
in section 2-243, except that where the person charged is under the age of eighteen 
(18) years, there shall be no jail sentence imposed as a penalty and such other 
restrictions on the penalty as may be set forth in section 1-15. In the event that a 
violation occurs pursuant to this section and a specific penalty is not set forth in section 
2-243, the penalty shall be at the court's discretion. It shall, however, still be subject to 
section 1-15. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Code, the maximum penalty for 
any violation of a municipal criminal offense shall automatically reflect and be capped by 
the state statutory penalty structure for comparable or identical misdemeanors and petty 
offenses as set forth in the Colorado Revised Statutes, as amended, which state limits 
shall control over any conflicting local provision.  
This ordinance shall be in full force and effect ten days after its passage.
