OTHER ITEMS 
  
IK Bar Parcel and Youth Power 
 
Mike Imhof, President and CEO of the Vail Valley Foundation, thanked the Town Council for its 
continued discussions regarding the parcel. He was joined by Juan Fernandez, Executive Director 
of Youth Power, and Yann Benjamin, Vice President of Public Relations and Marketing, in 
presenting the proposed indoor sports facility. 
 
Discussion followed, and one question was addressed by Sarah Clubine, Senior Vice President. 
 
Vail Valley Foundation Executive Committee members Gary Woodworth and Steve Coyer also 
spoke in support of the project. 
 
Additional speakers, including Jamie Gonzalez, Linda Mossman, Torrey Kaddatz, and Veyra 
Gamboa, shared personal experiences highlighting the positive impact of Youth Power's programs 
on the community. 
 
Joan Hursey, a Gypsum resident who lives near the proposed facility, expressed concerns 
regarding the size of the building. 
  
State of Colorado Proposition 123 Commitment 2027-2029 Funding Cycle  
 
Director Bryce explained the benefits and potential downside of committing to the 2027-2029 
funding cycle under Proposition 123 for affordable housing. 
 
Mayor Pro Tem Chris Huffman moved to authorize Director Bryce to enroll the Town of Gypsum in 
the Proposition 123 affordable housing commitment for the 2027-2029 funding cycle. 
 
After consideration, the motion passed by the following vote: AYES: Tom Edwards, Scott Green, 
Chris Huffman, Jay Oyler, Adam Trujillo. NAYS: None. ABSTAINERS: None. ABSENT: Kathleen 
Brendza, Marisa Sato.
