CITIZENS BEFORE COUNCIL 
Eric Medina - Town Staff Concerns 
Eric Medina, a Gypsum resident, requested an independent investigation into the Town regarding 
license plate reader cameras and potential legal claims. 
Alexa Martinez - Devil Duels 
Alexa Martinez, an executive production manager for the Devil Duels, appeared with fellow 
executive board members to explain the program and ask the Council to maintain its support. 
Lisa Vasquez - Shop with a Cop 
Deputy Vasquez expressed appreciation to the Council for their ongoing support of Shop with a 
Cop and asked that it be considered during the upcoming budget discussions. 
Derrick Bretta - Wildlife Protection & Trash Containment Concerns 
Derrick Bretta, a Gypsum resident, advocated for the Town to adopt bear-resistant trash cans. He 
was joined by Courtney Harris of Colorado Parks and Wildlife, who provided statistics on bear 
encounters this year. 
Discussion followed about the availability and cost of bear-resistant cans.
