PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
Mayor Green explained the changes to public comment. 
  
Bart Ewing - Buckhorn Valley Striping Concern 
 
Bart and Mary Kate Ewing, Gypsum residents, addressed the council regarding concerns that the 
white striping on Buckhorn Valley Boulevard would negatively affect their trucking operations. 
 
Eileen Aldeen, also of Gypsum, raised issues related to the locations of school zone lights near the 
high school.

Resident Michele Gieg expressed frustration with truck traffic on Buckhorn Valley Boulevard and 
urged the town to reinstate the enhanced striping previously in place. 
 
Mike Allen of Eagle commented on balancing neighborhood safety with the trucking company’s 
liability considerations. 
 
Juan Pena of Gypsum encouraged support for ballot measure 5B. 
 
Rex Roberts of Denver requested that the council consider a proposed settlement between the 
Town and JPJG related to the Siena Lake project. 
 
Claire Noble of Edwards introduced herself as a candidate for Eagle County Coroner. 
 
Eric Medina of Gypsum voiced concerns that the Eagle County Sheriff’s Office has not fulfilled his 
records request regarding automated license plate reader cameras.

Yes, on 5B - Yes for Eagle County School District 
3 
Prepared by:

Wendy Rimel, President and Executive Director of the Education Foundation of Eagle County, and 
Tessa Kirchner, Vice President, gave a presentation and asked council to consider a resolution 
supporting ballot issue 5B. 
 
Discussion ensued, and council members expressed their appreciation for local schools and 
teachers.  
 
The council chose not to pass a resolution and to leave the matter to the voters. 
  
Presentation of Bids and Selection of Contractor for the Cooley Mesa Road Pump Station 
Generator Project 
 
Engineer Rivera explained the need for a backup generator to support a critical water pump. 
 
Council Member Tom Edwards moved to award the contract for the Cooley Mesa Pump Station 
Generator project to Pinnacle Electric in the amount of $131,183.68. 
 
After consideration, the motion passed by the following vote: AYES: Kathleen Brendza, Tom 
Edwards, Scott Green, Chris Huffman, Marisa Sato, Adam Trujillo. NAYS: None. ABSTAINERS: 
None. ABSENT: Jay Oyler.
