SUMMARY  
Federal Realty, the owners of Leawood Town Center Plaza and Town Center Crossing, have plans 
to improve the Town Center property. At the work session, they will share their plans and timeline. 
As a backdrop to the discussion, it is important to note that Leawood Town Center Plaza and 
Crossing bring in a combined sales of approximately $300 million annually, amounting to over 30% 
of the City of Leawood's entire sales tax base. 
  
In total, Federal Realty plans to undertake an approximately $59.8 million redevelopment project to 
improve Leawood Town Center Plaza. This would include utility upgrades, parking lot resurfacing 
and sidewalks, roof replacement, tenant improvements, common area signage improvements, 
exterior building improvements, artwork, site furnishings, a development of a new pad site on the 
north side of the property, the development of an outdoor plaza, and LED lighting improvements.   
  
In order to assist the project, they are requesting the establishment of a Community Improvement 
District (CID), which would place an extra one cent sales tax upon sales within the shopping centers 
to help fund the improvements. The CID would last up to 22 years, or until such time the agreed-
upon revenue has been generated. In this case, Federal is requesting approximately $35.6 million 
from CID-generated revenue over 22 years. The ratio of private funding to CID funding would be 
roughly 40%/60%.  
  
Federal Realty estimates that the redevelopment investments will yield an additional $19.1 million in 
new incremental sales tax revenue to the City over the 22-year term.

City of Leawood, 
On behalf of Federal Realty OP LP (“Owner”), we submit this supplemental letter in support of 
discussing a Town Center Community Improvement District (“CID”) for the redevelopment and 
revitalization of the Town Center properties located respectively at 5000 W. 119th Street and 
4555 W. 119th Street in Leawood, KS (collectively, the “Property” or “Project”). This letter is 
provided at the request of City Staff to address Sections 4 and 5 of the City’s CID Policy, as well 
as to provide the estimated economic benefit to the City of Leawood that the Project is expected 
to generate based on future incremental projected sales productivity. 
Section 4 – Eligibility Criteria 
Pursuant to Section 4(3), the Project is expected to promote material economic development 
within the City of Leawood. Town Center Plaza is showing visible signs of aging as it was 
originally constructed in 1996 and has very limited investment or updating by prior Owners over 
the subsequent 30 years. The goal of the Project’s property improvement plan is to modernize 
and refresh Town Center Plaza and its shopping experience, update merchandising with a 
complementary mix of national, regional, and local tenants, drive traffic to the Property with 
enhanced marketing and onsite customer experience, maintain and expand the Property’s 
shopping destination dominance in the Kansas City area, and increase the long-term 
productivity of the Property. When complete, we believe the Project upgrades will ultimately 
increase sales productivity from existing tenants, new tenant openings, and future relevant and 
more productive tenants at Town Center Plaza which, in total, is projected to generate 
approximately $19.1 million1 in incremental City sales tax revenue over the 22-year term of the 
CID, as reflected in Exhibit A. This sustained growth in sales tax collections will provide a 
substantial and lasting fiscal benefit to the City well beyond the term of the CID. In addition to 
the fiscal benefits, the Project will support construction jobs, as well as permanent employment 
for ongoing operations and maintenance at the Property, and staffing for retailers, restaurants, 
and service providers. 
Pursuant to Section 4(4), the Project incorporates first class design, architectural, and 
construction standards for the exterior storefronts, facades, common areas, streetscape, and 
placemaking improvements that exceed the minimum requirements of the Leawood 
Development Ordinance, as described further below. 
Pursuant to Section 4(5), the Project encourages the redevelopment, renovation, and 
rehabilitation of the Property. Town Center Plaza has not undergone a comprehensive

renovation or material investment into the Property since its original construction in 1996, and 
the Project will reposition the center for long-term competitiveness and sustained economic

Pursuant to Section 4(6), the Project incorporates the construction of public-facing common 
area infrastructure, including the activation of the eastern pedestrian cut-through — renamed 
“Cedar Street” — along with new common area signage, wayfinding, and directories that will 
benefit the broader shopping center and surrounding community. 
Section 5 – General Plan and Use of CID Funds 
CID revenues will be used solely for eligible costs associated with the exterior and site 
improvements to the Property, including: (i) architectural upgrades to existing building exteriors, 
ranging from repainting and updated light fixtures to more substantial façade modernization; (ii) 
landscape architectural improvements, including new planting beds, shade trees, site lighting, 
and common area seating; (iii) placemaking improvements along Cedar Street, including 
plantings, shade elements, murals, hardscape upgrades, and seating, as well as approximately 
5,000 square feet of new building GLA; (iv) new common area signage, including monument 
signs, wayfinding, and directories; (v) the construction of a new ~7,000 square foot commercial 
pad building sited to activate the north end of Cedar Street and anchor that portion of the 
Property; and (vi) the reconstruction of deferred-maintenance site infrastructure, including 
parking lot asphalt and concrete. 
The proposed CID Sales Tax will be levied at a rate of 1.0% and will be used solely to 
reimburse the approved and verified Costs of the Project on a pay-as-you-go basis, in 
accordance with the City’s CID Policy. 
We appreciate the City’s consideration and look forward to continuing to work with the City on 
the redevelopment and revitalization of Town Center in Leawood, Kansas.
