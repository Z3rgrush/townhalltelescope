Proclamation
WHEREAS, Leawood, Kansas has continued to address the rising demand for mental 
health and substance use services; and
WHEREAS, mental health and substance use challenges can lead to homelessness, 
incarceration and emergency room visits, which are more intrusive and costly for communities 
than earlier interventions and connections to behavioral health services; and
WHEREAS, Leawood, Kansas has recognized the need for additional resources for 
individuals experiencing mental health crises and identified Crisis Intervention Team training, 
Mental Health First-Aid training and Johnson County Mental Health Co-responders as emerging, 
effective practices; and
WHEREAS, the Leawood, Kansas Police Department, having recognized the importance 
of advanced and ongoing training in mental health awareness, now has more than 95% of its first 
responders with such training; and 
WHEREAS, the Leawood, Kansas Police and Johnson County Mental Health Co-
Responder Program began a collaborative effort in 2019 to embed a mental health clinician with 
law enforcement to respond to individuals in crisis, provide the right intervention at the right 
time to avoid arrests, emergency room visits, and repeat 911 calls by connecting to appropriate 
resources; and
WHEREAS, the City of Leawood currently maintains two staffed Co-Responder 
positions, including an additional role that was authorized by the Governing Body in 2022;  
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Mayor Marc Elkins, Mayor of the City of Leawood, on behalf 
of the Citizens of Leawood, do hereby proclaim September 13th – 19th, 2026 as  
CO-RESPONDER AND CRISIS RESPONDER WEEK
IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the Seal of the City of 
Leawood to be affixed this 8th day of September 2026.

AGENDA ITEM  
Conduct a public hearing and consider a Resolution approving the levy of a property tax rate 
exceeding the Revenue Neutral Rate for the City of Leawood, Kansas (FN) [ROLL CALL VOTE 
REQUIRED BY STATE LAW] 
 
SUMMARY  
In March 2021, the Kansas Legislature passed Senate Bill 13 (SB13), which requires all taxing 
jurisdictions (Counties, Cities, Schools, Libraries, etc.) to hold a public hearing if they intend to 
exceed the Revenue Neutral Rate (RNR). The RNR is the rate that would collect the same amount 
of revenue as the previous year.  
 
Staff received Leawood’s RNR of 22.390 from the County in mid-June. On July 6, 2026 , the 
Governing Body adopted Resolution 6547, stating its intent to levy a property tax exceeding the 
RNR and scheduling a Public Hearing for Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at 7:00 P.M., or as soon 
thereafter as may be heard. The required notice of hearing to exceed the RNR and Budget hearing 
was published on the City's website and in the Johnson County Post on July 13, 2026. Information 
about the 2027 proposed budget and RNR is also published on the City's website. 
 
The City Clerk informed the County Clerk of the City’s intent to exceed the RNR in July. The 
proposed mill levy rate for the 2027 budget is 23.500. The City Council has not increased the mill 
levy since 2003. 
 
In early August, the County sent estimated tax notices to all Leawood property owners, notifying 
them of the RNR, the proposed Mill Levy for 2027, and the date, time, and place for the public 
hearing. 
 
Per SB13, a resolution adopted by a roll call vote is required to exceed the RNR.

Staff recommends the following: 
1. Open the public hearing and accept comments regarding the RNR; 
2. Close the public hearing; and 
3. Consider a Resolution approving the levy of a property tax rate exceeding the RNR. [Roll Call 
Vote]

In March 2021, the Kansas Legislature passed KSA 79-2988 (formerly 
Senate Bill 13) to establish limitation on ad valorem property tax levies 
by taxing subdivisions without an additional notice and hearing prior to 
budget adoption.  This  bill repealed the tax lid law and computed tax 
limits imposed by KSA 79-2925b and KSA 70-2925c.  
Taxing subdivisions cannot levy above the RNR without holding a

Expenditure authority on state budget forms includes any carry-forward balances available in the 
funds at the end of the budget year. This allows the Governing Body to increase expenditures from 
the fund

during a budget year, the established expenditure limit for each fund is considered budgeted 
expenditures, and any increases to that amount require the Governing Body's approval. The 
attached table summarizes budgeted expenditures against statutory budget authority for the 2027 
budget.
