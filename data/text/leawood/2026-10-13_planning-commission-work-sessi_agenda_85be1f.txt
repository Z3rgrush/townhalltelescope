16-2-6.4(D) – Bulk Regulations (Required Use Ratios)
MXD (Mixed-Use Development District) and MXD-4 (Mixed-Use Development 
District with Four Story Maximum)
Not less than 15% of the total floor area within the development shall be designated for 
residential use; not less than 5% of the total floor area shall be designated for retail use; 
not less than 5% of the total floor area shall be designated for office use; and the total 
percentage of commercial use shall not be less than 20% of the total floor area 20% of 
the total floor area shall be designated for commercial use, including office and/or retail 
uses.  For the purposes of this calculation, a portion of hotel square footage may be 
counted as residential as set forth in subsection (B) above.
Infill Development: Not less than 5% of the total floor area within the development shall 
be designated for residential use; not less than 5% of the total floor area shall be 
designated for retail use; and not less than 5% of the total floor area shall be designated 
for office use 10% of the total floor area shall be designated for commercial use, 
including office and/or retail uses. Infill development is defined as either vacant or 
underdeveloped land that is 15 acres or less, not within an existing development, but 
within an otherwise developed area that shares common property lines with two or more 
fully constructed developments.
