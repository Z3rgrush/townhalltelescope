3. New Business  
 A. Discuss the Maccabi Games and visit from the Gezer delegation 
 
Taly gave a presentation on the Maccabi Games and the Gezer delegation trip. 
  
For the first time since 1997, Kansas City will be host to The JCC Maccabi Games. 
It is anticipated that 1,000 local and visiting athlete participants, representing over 
30 Jewish communities from North America and beyond, will attend. 
  
There will be 36 teen athletes from Jewish Kansas City’s three sister communities 
– the Gezer Region in Israel, Romania, and Bulgaria joining as part of team 
Kansas City. There will be 24 teen athletes from the Gezer Region and 4 adult

chaperones (representing boys' baseball and boys' basketball). The delegation 
includes Gezer Region Mayor Rotem Yadlin & CEO, Carmel Tal. 
  
The pre-Maccabi experience runs from July 29 to August 2, and Maccabi week 
runs from August 3 to August 7. The pre-experience includes tours of the 
Kauffman Stadium, a Sporting KC game, and opportunities to engage with the City 
of Leawood and the Gezer Committee. Maccabi week includes the opening 
ceremony on August 3, athletic competitions, volunteer projects, teen and host 
family night, and closing ceremonies on August 6. 
  
The Gezer Committee is planning a brunch at Gezer Park with Mayor Yadlin, 
Carmel, and the entire Gezer delegation, including athletes and host families. This 
event will include an abbreviated tour of the park.  
  
The Gezer Committee is planning several other events with Mayor Yadlin and 
Carmel, including a tour of the City, meetings with Mayor Elkins and Diane 
Stoddard. Other engagement opportunities may be available will be and 
announced as they are planned. 
  
The Maccabi Games Opening Ceremony is on Monday, August 3, in the evening. 
This is also an evening City Council meeting. There was consensus among the 
Committee to request that the Governing Body reschedule this meeting so that the 
Mayor and City Council could attend the opening ceremony. 
  
Taly reviewed the registration requirements to attend events as a spectator or 
volunteer. This is required as security is a top priority for the Maccabi games. 
  
More information about the brunch on August 2 will be emailed to the committee 
planning progress.
