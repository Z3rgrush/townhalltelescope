applications for large data centers in the Town". Trustee Grace seconded the motion. In a

Trustee Murphy reported on the interviews for the Tree Advisory Committee and noted that there 
is still a vacancy on the committee.

Daniels to the Tree Advisory Committee for terms to expire September 30, 2028. Mayor

Trustee Anderson moved to appoint Carri Grimditch, Cheryl Szydlo, and Leslie Moore to

2028. Trustee Wing seconded the motion. In a 7-0 voice vote, the motion Passed.

 3. Tree Advisory Committee Annual Report (20 Min) 
Richard Kurcab, Chair of the Tree Advisory Comittee, gave a presentation on the Committee's 
activities over the past year, their community outreach efforts, and their goals and ideas for the 
upcoming year. 
 
Town Forester Paul Furnas provided additional information on tree plantings along Mountain 
Avenue and in Fickel Park. 
 4. Approval of Funding Request for the Berthoud Veterans Club (15 Min) 
Mike Brooks and Adam Stephen, representing the American Legion and the Veterans of Foreign 
Wars, presented a funding request to assist with rent for the organization's building. They 
provided information about the building and the activities of the Berthoud Veterans Club. 
 
The Board inquired whether the requested funding would cover rent for the entire year and 
encouraged the group to pursue fundraising efforts.  
 
Trustee Wing moved to approve the funding request for the Berthoud Veterans Club in the 
amount of $11,500. Trustee Anderson seconded the motion. In a 7-0 roll call vote, the 
motion Passed. 
 5. Public Hearing: Approval of Neighborhood Master Plan for the 
Property known as Heron Lakes Marina (Heron Lakes 22nd Filing) - 
Resolution 2026-25 
(30 Min) 
Community Development Director Anne Johnson provided information regarding the 
Neighborhood Master Plan for Heron Lakes Marina, including the history and zoning of the 
property. Johnson noted that the Planning Commission recommended approval with the 
condition that the water provider be determined and provided with the preliminary plat. 
 
Engineering Supervisor Luke Seeber provided additional information regarding the traffic study 
that was completed for the project. 
 
The Board inquired about applicable code changes, trails throughout the property, and the 
schools that would serve future residents. 
 
Applicant, Jim Birdsall, provided additional information regarding the property's annexation 
process, challenges associated with developing a marina at that location, and the proposed trail 
system.

Mayor Karspeck opened the public comment portion of the Public Hearing. 
No one spoke during public comment. 
Mayor Karspeck closed public comment.  
 
The Board had discussion regarding the water provider for the property and expressed concerns 
regarding the metro district. 
 
Trustee Wing moved to continue the Public Hearing for the Heron Lakes Marina 
Neighborhood Master Plan to October 27, 2026. Trustee Anderson seconded the motion. In 
a 5-1 roll call vote, the motion Passed, with Mayor Pro-Tem Soricelli-Albrecht having the 
dissenting vote, and Trustee Murphy abstaining. 
 6. Elected Official Reports (20 Min) 
 a. Town Board -   
Trustee Anderson reported on the recent Library Literary Festival and upcoming events 
including Oktoberfest on October 3, the Historic Preservation Advisory Committee (HPAC) 
Walking Tour on October 3, and the Habitat for Humanity Hoedown on October 10. 
 
Trustee Murphy reported on the recent Tree Advisory Committee meeting, including discussions 
on the upcoming Oktoberfest event and the Committee's annual report to the Town Board. 
Murphy also reported on HPAC's opposition to proposed changes to Section 106 and stated 
support for a Town Board resolution opposing the changes. At least three trustees expressed 
support for placing a resolution on the October 13 meeting agenda. Murphy further reported that 
the Tree Advisory Committee would like to combine its next annual report with the Arbor Day 
proclamation and a "State of the Trees" address.  
 
Trustee Grace reported on the Parks, Open Space, Recreation, and Trails (PORT) Committee 
meeting, including discussions regarding the new committee ordinance, onboarding of new 
members and development of bylaws. Grace also reported on a Berthoud Main Street community 
meeting, which included discussion of upcoming Town events including the Homecoming 
Parade, Oktoberfest, Scarecrow Contest, Trick or Treat Street, Small Business Saturday, and A 
Very Merry Berthoud. Grace inquired about scheduling a study session regarding Flock cameras. 
With at least three trustees expressing support, Administrator Kirk suggested the November 17 
Study Session for this discussion. 
 
Trustee Wing commended the Board for speaking up for their beliefs and advocating on behalf 
of citizens. Wing also requested an update regarding the businesses located at Highway 287 and 
Berthoud Parkway.  
 
Mayor Pro-Tem Soricelli-Albrecht inquired about budgeted funds for improvements to the 
cemetery gates and commented on the Board's work on behalf of the Town. Soricelli-Albrecht 
also reported on the Civic Academy.

 b. Mayor -   
Mayor Karspeck reported on a meeting with Loveland Lake and Ditch and upcoming events 
including the Colorado Municipal League (CML) Fall District Meeting on September 24 and 
The House of Neighborly Service Gala on September 25. Karspeck reported on the placement of 
a new crosswalk at Welch Avenue and 8th Street. Karspeck inquired about the Legacy Park 
vested rights agreement approved at the previous Board meeting and about the sidewalk 
medallions in downtown. 
 c. Staff - Town Administrator, Attorney  
Administrator Kirk reported on the Civic Academy and the upcoming Study Session on 
September 29, which will cover data centers, the Capital Improvements Plan, updates to the 
purchasing policy, and meeting management.

Background:  
The Federal Advisory Council on Historic Preservation (ACHP) issues regulations to guide the 
Section 106 review process.  The current Section 106 process requires federal agencies to 
collaborate with local communities when a federal project may impact local historic resources. 
 Section 106 provides local control and input.  ACHP voted to change Section 106 on July 24, 
2026.  The change would make collaboration with local communities optional rather than 
required.  Any project receiving federal funding would not be required to collaborate with the 
Town should the change go into effect. 
 
The Berthoud Historic Preservation Advisory Committee (HPAC) approached the Town Board 
regarding concerns with the proposed change.  The Town Board indicated to HPAC that they 
would entertain submitting a letter indicating concerns with the proposed change to Section 106. 
 Attached to this Information Memo is Resolution 20-2026 which provides authorization to the 
Mayor to sign the attached letter voicing concerns regarding the lack of local control in federal 
decision-making regarding local historic resources. 
 
Update/Next Steps: 
Upon approval of Resolution 20-2026, the Mayor will sign the Resolution as well as the letter, 
both of which are attached.  Staff will submit the letter as soon as public comment is opened. 
 The rationale for requesting the letter now is to be able to react quickly once public comment is 
opened.
