5. Public Hearing: Comprehensive Plan update, Strategic   
A Public Hearing to consider the inclusion of a Strategic Growth element to the 2021 
Comprehensive plan as required by SB-174 prior to December 31, 2026.  
a. Planner Lakin Dickson provided a staff presentation beginning at 6:06 p.m and ending at 
6:14 p.m. 
b. Public testimony opened at 6:15 p.m. and closed at 6:15 p.m. No members of the public 
provided testimony. 
c. Planning Commissioners deliberated and discussed the proposed update. Discussion 
opened at 6:16 p.m. and closed at 6:29 p.m. 
d. Commissioner Semedalas moved to approve the update as proposed. A second to this 
motion was made by Commissioner Buttice. The motion passed unanimously. 
e. The public hearing closed at 6:30 p.m.

6. Public Hearing: Legacy Park Final Plat 
Public Hearing to consider a request for approval of a Final Plat known as Legacy Park 
a. Planning Manager Tawn Hillenbrand provided a staff presentation at 6:31 p.m. 
b. The applicant was represented by Don Hinson and Kristin Turner. They provided a 
presentation beginning at 6:43 p.m. and ending at 7:18 p.m. 
c. Public testimony opened at 7:19 p.m. and closed at 7:27 p.m. The following members of 
the public provided testimony: Amy Barnard, Mark Morris. 
d. Planning Commissioners deliberated and discussed the proposed Final Plat. Discussion 
opened at 7:27 p.m. and closed at 7:54 p.m.  
e. Commissioner Pond moved to approve the Final Plat Request.  A second to this motion 
was made by Commissioner Buttice. The motion passed unanimously. 
f. The public hearing closed at 7:54 p.m. 
 
7. Public Hearing: 597 Meadowlark Replat 
Public Hearing to consider a request for a Replat, for the property known as 597 Meadowlark 
Drive 
a. Planning Manager Tawn Hillenbrand provided a staff presentation at beginning at 8:01 
p.m. and ending at 8:08 p.m. 
b. The applicant was represented by Doug Jones. They provided a presentation beginning 
at 8:08 p.m. and ending at 8:14 p.m. 
c. Public testimony opened at 8:14 p.m. and closed at 8:14 p.m. No members of the public 
provided testimony. 
d. Planning Commissioners deliberated and discussed the proposed Replat. Discussion 
opened at 8:15 p.m. and closed at 8:15 p.m.  
e. Commissioner Terrazas moved to send a recommendation of approval to the Town Board 
of Trustees of the Replat request. A second to this motion was made by Commissioner 
Semedalas. The motion passed unanimously. 
f. The public hearing closed at 8:16 p.m. 
 
8. Public Hearing: Heron Lakes Marina Neighborhood Master Plan 
Public Hearing to consider a request for a Neighborhood Master Plan for the property known as 
Heron Lakes Marina (Heron Lakes 22nd Filing) 
a. Planner Bella Manzo provided a staff presentation at 8:17 p.m. 
b. The applicant was represented by Jim Birdsall. They provided a presentation beginning at 
8:26 p.m. and ending at 8:51 p.m. 
c. Public testimony opened at 8:52 p.m. and closed at 9:01 p.m. The following members of 
the public provided testimony: Jeromy Ginther, Anita Ballinger. 
d. Planning Commissioners deliberated and discussed the proposed Neighborhood Master 
Plan. Discussion opened at 9:01 p.m. and closed at 9:18 p.m.  
e. Commissioner Terrazas moved to send a recommendation of approval to the Town Board 
of Trustees of the Neighborhood Master Plan request. A second to this motion was made 
by Commissioner Hofmans. The motion passed unanimously. 
f. The public hearing closed at 9:19 p.m. 
 
9. Report by Staff:  
a. Planning Manager Tawn Hillenbrand provided an update on the Public Art Master Plan 
process. Staff has received a final draft from the consultants, NINEdotARTS. 
b. Planner Bella Manzo provided an update on the Historic Preservation Advisory 
Committee’s work toward the goals outlined in the recently adopted Historic Preservation 
Plan. They are planning a fall walking tour to take place at 11:00 am on Saturday, 
October 3rd beginning at Fickel Park and touring landmarked homes in Berthoud.  
c. Planner Bella Manzo reported that the Housing Consortium for the Town of Berthoud
