5. Public Hearing: Public Art Master Plan 
A Public Hearing to consider adoption of the Public Art Master Plan was presented.  
a. Planner Lakin Dickson provided a staff presentation at 6:04 p.m. 
b. Planning Commission provided questions to Planner Dickson at 6:21 p.m. 
c. Public testimony opened at 6:41p.m. and closed at 6:42 p.m. The public was not in 
attendance at this meeting.   
d. Planning Commissioners deliberated and discussed the proposed Master Plan. 
Discussion opened at 6:42 p.m. and closed at 6:42 p.m. 
e. Commissioner Terrazas moved to approve Resolution PC-03 and send a 
recommendation of approval to the Town Board.  A second to this motion was made by 
Commissioner Buttice. 
f. The public hearing closed at 6:42 p.m.
