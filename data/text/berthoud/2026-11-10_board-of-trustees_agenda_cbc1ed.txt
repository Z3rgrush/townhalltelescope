Trustee Wing moved to approve the meeting agenda for the October 27, 2026 Town Board

Meeting. Trustee Anderson seconded the motion. In a 6-0 voice vote, the motion Passed. 
V.  Citizen Participation 
 
Sherry Watts, Brian Watts, Peg Rumaine, Linda Pryor, Jon Evans, and Charles McConnell, all 
Town of Berthoud residents, spoke regarding concerns about metro districts, specifically in 
Heron Lakes. Topics included the alleged mismanagement of funds, developer investments in 
properties, tax benefits available to metro districts but not to homeowners under TABOR, and a 
request for the Board of Trustees to review the state’s audit of metro districts.

Trustee Murphy requested item "a. October 13, 2026 Meeting Minutes" be removed from

Trustee Wing moved to combine and approve items on the Consent Agenda minus item

Marina 
(30 Min) 
Community Development Director Anne Johnson presented the Heron Lakes Marina 
Neighborhood Master Plan and provided a summary of the public hearing process conducted at

The Town Board discussed the Metro District boundaries, including the district’s operations and 
oversight. The Board reviewed the application and approval criteria for a Neighborhood Master 
Plan.

Trustee Anderson moved to approve Resolution 2026-25, a Resolution of the Town of 
Berthoud Approving a Neighborhood Master Plan for a Property Known as Heron Lakes 
Marina, with the condition that the determination of the water provider must be provided 
with any subsequent Preliminary Plat application for this development. Mayor Pro-Tem 
Soricelli-Albrecht seconded the motion. In a 4-2 roll call vote, the motion Passed, with 
Trustee Olbricht and Trustee Wing having the dissenting vote(s). 
 3. Establish an Interview Committee to Interview Applicants for the Public 
Art Advisory Committee 
(10 Min) 
Town Clerk Christian Samora provided information regarding the interview date for the Public 
Art Advisory Committee. 
 
Trustee Anderson, Mayor Pro-Tem Soricelli-Albrecht, and Trustee Olbricht expressed interest in 
serving on the interview committee. The Board determined that Trustee Olbricht would act as a 
backup interviewer. 
 
Trustee Murphy moved to appoint Mayor Pro-Tem Soricelli-Albrecht and Trustee 
Anderson to an Interview Committee for the purpose of interviewing and making 
recommendations for the appointment of seven Public Art Advisory Committee Members. 
Trustee Olbricht seconded the motion. In a 6-0 roll call vote, the motion Passed. 
 4. Elected Official Reports (20 Min) 
 a. Town Board -   
Trustee Wing discussed adding an agenda item to a future meeting regarding metropolitan 
districts. The Board was supportive of reviewing the Town's Special District policies at a Study 
Session on December 15, 2026. Trustee Wing also discussed Berthoud Main Street's Trick or 
Treat event. 
 
Trustee Anderson reported on the HPAC meeting, including discussions regarding newly 
adopted Ordinance 1388, Section 6 of the National Historic Preservation Act, and Historic 
Walking Tours and coloring books for 2027. 
 
Trustee Murphy reported on a meeting with CDOT in regard to the Mountain Avenue and 9th 
Street crossing. Murphy reported that construction is currently scheduled to be completed before 
the beginning of the 2027 school year. Trustee Murphy re-stated his position of support for state 
legislation on Metro Districts. 
 
Mayor Pro-Tem Soricelli-Albrecht reported on Berthoud Main Street's Trick or Treat event and 
the scarecrows in downtown, as well as the recent population growth in Town. Soricelli-Albrecht 
reported on the Berthoud Main Street strategic planning session. Soricelli-Albrecht inquired 
about golf cart use in Berthoud, including the permitting process and public outreach regarding 
golf cart safety and instruction.

Waggener Parking lot, and the new side of Pioneer park. These trees will be monitored over the coming years to
assess if they are good candidates for Colorado’s climate.

 If Modifications to the process should be allowed:

I.  SCOPE AND ADMINISTRATION  
This policy shall apply to all capital projects of an estimated value greater than $2M. Maintenance and 
repair projects of such values may follow this policy as is necessary to be completed effectively. It is the 
intent for all projects to follow directly the provisions of this policy unless modiﬁcations are warranted due 
to project complexity, emergency conditions, or regulatory requirements. Any modiﬁcations must be 
approved by the Board of Trustees and Town Administrator. 
 
II.  PURPOSE  
The purpose of this policy is to establish a consistent process for planning, evaluating, designing, 
approving, bidding, and constructing capital improvement projects that: 
 
• Aligns projects with Town strategic goals, master plans, and improvement plans. 
• Improves budget accuracy and schedule predictability. 
• Incorporates value engineering prior to bidding. 
• Incorporates Advisory Committee recommendations and public input. 
• Provides Board of Trustees oversight at key decision intervals. 
• Ensure project scopes are effectively developed prior to design development.
