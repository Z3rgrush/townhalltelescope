TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
September 15, 2026  
 
Mayor Huelskamp called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Mayor Dave Huelskamp, Betsy McCain, Ed Icenogle, Jeff Sahr, Willi 
Taylor, and Cinamon Watson 
Also present: Lee Schiller, J.D. McCrumb, and Bret Cottrell 
* participated virtually 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: There was no public comment. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Sahr and a second by Trustee Watson, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved the consent agenda which consisted of 
the August 18, 2026 minutes. 
REPORTS 
A. Mayor Huelskamp updated the Trustees a salary survey that is underway and notified them 
that the Town has vacancies on the Planning and Zoning Commission, he is seeking 
suggestions for candidates. 
B. There were no additional Trustee reports. 
C. Mr. McCrumb presented the financials as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
D. Mr. McCrumb reviewed his report as presented in the Trustees Packet.  
E. Chief Cottrell reviewed his report as presented in the Trustee Packet.  
 
PRESENTATIONS: 
A. First Draft – 2027 Town Budget: Mr. McCrumb presented the first draft of the 2027 
budget, including several special projects and initiatives.  Also presented was the budget 
process through final adoption in December.  The Trustees asked clarifying questions and 
discussed several of the projects but did not offer any direction or changes at this time. 
B. Housing Needs Assessment: Mr. McCrumb offered the Trustees an update on the 
Housing Needs Assessment.  The Community survey is underway, and staff expects the 
response rate to exceed the 20% target by the time it closes.  A draft presentation will be 
offered at the October meeting. 
 
OLD BUSINESS: 
A. Resolution #12 – 2026: Mr. Schiller presented the resolution concerning an agreement 
with Forged Fiber.  There was one sentence that was awaiting final review by the Town 
Attorney. The Trustees asked questions and discussed the resolution. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee Sahr, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Resolution #12 and authorized 
the mayor to sign upon the Town Attorneys review of a final draft. 
 
B. Platte Canyon Sidewalk Update: Mr. McCrumb advised the Trustees that the proposed 
Platte Canyon sidewalk would not be proceeding as the design had changed too 
significantly and was no longer meeting the intended purpose. The Trustees supported that 
decision. 
 
2 
 
 
NEW BUSINESS: There was no new business. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 
8:27 p.m. 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
