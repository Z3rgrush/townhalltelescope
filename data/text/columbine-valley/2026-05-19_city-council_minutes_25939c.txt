TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
May 19, 2026  
 
Mayor Huelskamp called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Mayor Dave Huelskamp, Betsy McCain, Mike Giesen, Ed Icenogle, 
Willi Taylor, and Cinamon Watson 
Also present: Lee Schiller, J.D. McCrumb, Kelly Anton, and Bret Cottrell 
* participated virtually 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: There was no public comment. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Watson and a second by Trustee Giesen, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved the consent agenda which consisted of 
the April 21, 2026 minutes. 
REPORTS 
A. Mayor Huelskamp updated the Trustees on his Mayoral transition with Bill Dotson and 
specifically mentioned the Watson Lane and Pickleball discussions. 
B. Trustee Taylor mentioned that he was approached by a resident of the Christensen Lane 
Estates HOA about annexing into the Town. 
C. Mr. McCrumb presented the financials as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
D. Mr. McCrumb reviewed his report as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
E. Chief Cottrell reviewed his report as presented in the Trustee Packet. He highlighted some 
staffing updates and reported that the new dashboard cameras had been installed. 
 
PRESENTATIONS: Mrs. Anton presented a departmental overview of the Communications and 
Event functions of the Town. 
 
OLD BUSINESS:  
A. PUBLIC HEARING – Trustee Bill #5: Mayor Huelskamp opened the public hearing at 7:05 
p.m. Mr. Schiller presented the ordinance on second reading. 
 
There was no public comment. Mayor Dotson closed the Public Hearing at 7:06 p.m. 
 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Taylor and a second by Trustee Giesen, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #5 on second reading. 
 
NEW BUSINESS: 
A. Trustee Bill #6 -2026, Adoption of 2024 Fire Code (1st Reading) Mr. Schiller presented 
the ordinance. The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the ordinance. No 
changes or amendments were made. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Giesen and a second by Trustee McCain, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #6 – 2026 on first 
reading and set a Public Hearing for June 16, 2026. 
 
B. Trustee Bill #7 -2026, 2026 E-Bikes and E-Scooters (1st Reading). Mr. Schiller presented 
the ordinance. The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the ordinance. 
Direction was offered for several minor changes. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Taylor and a second by Trustee Giesen, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #7 – 2026, revised as 
discussed, on first reading. 
 
B. Resolution #8-2026, Mr. McCrumb presented the Salary Policy. The Trustees asked 
clarifying questions and discussed the policy, offering minor changes. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee Giesen, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the resolution as revised. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 
8:20 p.m. 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
