TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
March 17, 2026  
 
Mayor Dotson called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Bill Dotson, Dave Huelskamp, Mike Giesen, Ed Icenogle, Betsy 
McCain, and Jeff Sahr 
Also present: Lee Schiller*, J.D. McCrumb, Diane Rodriguez*, and Bret Cottrell 
* participated virtually 
 
Mayor Dotson recognized Trustee-elect Mr. Willi Taylor. Mr. Taylor introduced himself to the Board. 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: Jared Hamilton (7 Fairway) offered the Trustees with an update to the 
concerns he expressed last month about pickleball noise. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Huelskamp and a second by Trustee Sahr, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the consent agenda.  
 
REPORTS 
A. Mayor Dotson distributed to the Trustees a letter he had sent to local state representatives 
regarding his opposition to HB 26-1308. He also shared with the Trustees recent actions 
the Town took to mitigate graffiti in a culvert under Platte Canyon Road. 
B. There were no Trustee reports. 
C. Mrs. Rodriguez presented the financials and answered Trustee questions. She noted the 
status of the bank change-over at approximately 50% with two or three months to go. 
D. Mr. McCrumb reviewed his report as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
E. Chief Cottrell reviewed his report as presented in the Trustee Packet.  
 
PRESENTATIONS: Mr. Andy Bass, new Executive Director of SSPRD, introduced himself to the 
Trustees and offered a recap of residents’ use of district amenities. Mr. Bass was accompanied 
by Mrs. Pam Eller, SSPRD Board Member and Columbine Valley resident. 
 
Mr. McCrumb presented a departmental overview of the Town Administrator and Town Clerk 
functions of the Town. 
 
OLD BUSINESS:  
A. Resolution #5-2026, Mr. McCrumb presented the draft Capital Improvement Program and 
informed the Trustees that it remained unchanged from February, with the exception of 
two additions for Tasers and a new Server.  
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee 
Huelskamp, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the resolution with the 
inclusion of the two new expenditures. 
 
B. Trustee Bill #1 -2026, Amending the Model Traffic Code: Mr. Schiller presented the 
ordinance. The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the ordinance. No 
changes or amendments were made. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Huelskamp and a second by Trustee 
McCain, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #1 – 2026 on 
second reading. 
 
C. Trustee Bill #2 -2026, Amending Municipal Code Section 1.01.080: Mr. Schiller 
presented the ordinance. The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the 
ordinance. No changes or amendments were made. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Giesen and a second by Trustee Huelskamp, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #2 – 2026 on second 
reading. 
 
D. Trustee Bill #3 -2026, Amending Muni Code re: Building Commissioner: Mayor Dotson 
updated the Trustees on the larger municipal code review/clean up that he has been 
conducting with Trustee Icenogle and Mr. Schiller.  He asked for the following motion: 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee McCain, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously agreed to table this ordinance indefinitely. 
 
NEW BUSINESS: 
A. Trustee Bill #4 -2026, Prohibiting Smoking and Vaping in the Town Park.  Mr. McCrumb 
presented the ordinance. The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the 
ordinance. No changes or amendments were made. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee McCain and a second by Trustee Sahr, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #4 – 2026 on first reading. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 
7:57 p.m. 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
