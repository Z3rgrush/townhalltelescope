TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
December 9, 2025  
 
Mayor Dotson called the Special Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the ball room at the 
Columbine Country Club, 17 Fairway Lane, Columbine Valley, CO 80123. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Bill Dotson, Mike Giesen, Dave Huelskamp, Ed Icenogle, Jeff Sahr 
and Al Timothy 
Also present: J.D. McCrumb 
* participated virtually 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT 
There was no public comment. 
 
NEW BUSINESS  
A. Resolution #5, 2025: Upon presentation and review of the 2026 Budget for the Town of 
Columbine Valley, the Board took the following action: 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee 
Giesen, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Resolution #5 as 
presented. 
 
B. Resolution #6, 2025: Upon receipt of the valuation of the property in the Town from the 
Arapahoe County Assessor’s Office, the Board took the following action: 
ACTION:  upon a motion by Trustee Timothy and a second by 
Trustee Huelskamp, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved 
Resolution #6 as presented. 
 
ADJOURNMENT:  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 6:33 p.m. 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
