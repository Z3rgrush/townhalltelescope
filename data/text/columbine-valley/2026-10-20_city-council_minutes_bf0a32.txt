TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
October 20, 2026  
 
Mayor Huelskamp called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Mayor Dave Huelskamp, Betsy McCain, Mike Giesen, Ed Icenogle, 
Jeff Sahr, Willi Taylor, and Cinamon Watson 
Also present: Lee Schiller, Kelly Anton, Bret Cottrell, Brent Kaslon, and Diane 
Rodriguez* 
* participated virtually 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: Mara Marks, 8 Driver Lane, Concern that P&Z lighting regulations on pages 
67 and 91 of packet too vague and lacking definitions (such as lumens);  
 
Jared Hamilton, 7 Fairway Lane, expressing for Tim Vandel that Old Town HOA vigorously 
approves moratorium on lighting;  
 
Jared Hamilton, 7 Fairway Lane, expressing for himself appreciation for P&Z work on 
lighting and requests clear process for sharing plans and input. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Giesen and a second by Trustee Icenogle, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the consent agenda which consisted 
of the September 15, 2026, minutes, setting a public hearing for the 2027 Budget, 
and setting a public hearing for Housing Needs Assessment feedback. 
REPORTS 
A. Mayor Huelskamp updated the Trustees that he has identified Planning & Zoning 
commission candidates, little concern from residents about fiber installation, and 310 
trucks of dirt will be transported to the Par 3 golf course.  
B. There were no additional Trustee reports. 
C. Diane Rodriguez presented the financials as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
D. The Town Administrators report was included in the packet.  Mr. McCrumb was not at the 
meeting due to his attendance at the ICMA Conference. 
E. Chief Cottrell reviewed his report as presented in the Trustee Packet and noted that 
resident Dennis Norris has passed away.  
 
PRESENTATIONS: 
A. First Draft — Housing Needs Assessment: Mr. Kaslon presented a high-level summary of 
the report, answered Trustee questions, and listed next steps. 
B. Second Draft — 2027 Town Budget: Ms. Rodriguez summarized the budget for the 
upcoming year.  
 
OLD BUSINESS: There was no old business. 
 
NEW BUSINESS:  
A. Trustee Bill #9 — 2026 Concerning Permit Fees: Mr. Schiller presented the bill on first reading. 
The Trustees asked questions and discussed the ordinance. 
 
2 
 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Taylor and a second by Trustee Watson, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #9 on first reading. 
 
B. Trustee Bill #10: Mr. Schiller presented the amendment to the zoning code on first reading. The 
Trustees asked questions and discussed the amendment. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee Watson, 
the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the recommendations of the 
Planning and Zoning Commission concerning the amendments to the Zoning 
Code, with the inclusion, rather than the exclusion, of paragraph 2, concerning 
tennis lighting and paragraph 3 concerning recreational lighting, under the 
Lighting section of the Zoning Code, Article II, Section 3, Country Club (CC) 
District, of the Zoning Code and approves the provision recommended by the 
Town attorney  concerning revisions and amendments to Articles I-V of the 
Zoning Code, and to set a public hearing and 2nd Reading for Tuesday, November 
17, 2026 
 
C. Trustee Bill #11: Mr. Schiller presented the bill regarding annexation of 8 Hunter Run Lane on 
first reading. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Sahr and a second by Trustee McCain, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #11 on first reading. 
 
EXECUTIVE SESSION: Upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle, second by Trustee Sahr, the Board of Trustees 
unanimously approved going into an executive session to confer with legal counsel regarding US District 
Court case, Chrisanna   Elser v. Flock Group, Inc., Town of Columbine Valley et. al., pursuant to  CRS 
Section24-6-402(b) at 7:55 p.m. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately
