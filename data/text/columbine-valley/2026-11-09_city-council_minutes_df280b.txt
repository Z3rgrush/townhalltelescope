TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
November 9, 2026  
 
Mayor Huelskamp called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 1:00 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Mayor Dave Huelskamp, Betsy McCain, Mike Giesen, Jeff Sahr, and 
Willi Taylor 
Also present: Lee Schiller and J.D. McCrumb 
* participated virtually 
 
STUDY SESSION: The trustees conducted a study session to discuss recreational lighting and a 
possible moratorium of recreational lighting. They directed staff to pursue lighting restrictions on 
recreation, sports, and parking lighting within the town.  They further directed staff to draft an 
ordinance for consideration at their November meeting regarding a moratorium on such lighting 
through July 1, 2027. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 
1:48 p.m. 
 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
