TOWN OF COLUMBINE VALLEY  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Minutes 
November 18, 2025  
 
Mayor Dotson called the Regular Meeting of the Trustees to order at 6:30 p.m., in the Conference 
Room at the Town Hall at 2 Middlefield Road, Columbine Valley, Colorado. Roll call found the 
following present: 
 
Trustees: Bill Dotson, Mike Giesen, Dave Huelskamp, Ed Icenogle, Betsy 
McCain, Jeff Sahr, and Al Timothy 
Also present: Lee Schiller, J.D. McCrumb, Diane Rodriguez, and Bret Cottrell 
* participated virtually 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: There was no public comment. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
ACTION:  upon a motion by Trustee Huelskamp and a second by Trustee 
Giesen, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved the consent agenda.  
 
REPORTS 
A. Mayor Dotson offered no report. 
B. The Trustees had no report. 
C. Mrs. Rodriguez presented the financials and answered Trustee questions.  
D. Mr. McCrumb reviewed his report as presented in the Trustees Packet. 
E. Chief Cottrell reviewed his report as presented in the Trustee Packet.  
 
PRESENTATIONS: There were no presentation items.  
 
OLD BUSINESS 
A. Trustee Bill #7-2025 Building Codes. Mr. McCrumb presented the ordinance on second reading. 
 
B. PUBLIC HEARING – Trustee Bill #7: Mayor Dotson opened the public hearing at 7:05 p.m. 
 
There was no public comment. Mayor Dotson closed the Public Hearing at 7:05p.m. 
 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee 
Huelskamp, the Board of Trustees unanimously approved Trustee Bill #7 on 
second reading. 
 
C. Mrs. Rodriguez presented the updated 2026 Town Budget Draft. The Trustees discussed 
and asked clarifying questions. 
 
D. PUBLIC HEARING – 2026 Town Budget: Mayor Dotson opened the public hearing at 
7:28 p.m. 
 
There was no public comment. Mayor Dotson closed the Public Hearing at 7:28p.m. 
 
NEW BUSINESS: 
A. Mrs. Rodriguez presented a proposal to change the Towns primary bank from BMO to 
Inbank.  The Trustees asked clarifying questions and discussed the proposal. 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Sahr and a second by Trustee Giesen, the 
Board of Trustees unanimously approved the change in banks. 
 
EXECUTIVE SESSION: Mayor Dotson called for an executive session to discuss personnel matters, 
involving performance and compensation, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(f). 
ACTION: upon a motion by Trustee Icenogle and a second by Trustee Timothy, 
the Board of Trustees entered into an executive session at 7:47 p.m. 
 
  The Trustees exited the executive session at approximately 9:00 p.m. 
 
ADJOURNMENT:  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 
9:00 p.m. 
 
Submitted by, 
J.D. McCrumb, Town Administrator
