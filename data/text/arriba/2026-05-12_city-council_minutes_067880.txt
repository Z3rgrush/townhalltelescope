TOWN OF ARRIBA 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING 
Tuesday, May 12, 2026 
Arriba Town Hall, 711 Front Street, Arriba, CO  80804 
Call to Order Mayor Marilyn Lightsey called the meeting to order at 6:00. Trustees in 
attendance included Carolyn Steinsiek, Bob Rush, and Troy McCue. Ben VanderWerf was 
absent. 
Employees in attendance included Josie Hart and Mike Becker. 
Public Audience Kevin Harmon and Emily Baylie. 
Hospital Update  Erika Saffer gave her hospital report to the Board.  
Approval of Agenda Trustee McCue moved to approve the Agenda as written. Trustee Rush 
seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes  
Trustee Steinsiek moved to approve the minutes of the April 14 regular meeting as written. 
Trustee McCue seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Accounts Receivable and Payable  
Trustee McCue moved to approve the receivables and payables for April. Trustee Steinsiek 
seconded, and the motion passed unanimously. 
Swear in New Board Members 
Clerk Hart gave the Oath of Office to newly elected trustees Kevin Harmon and Emily Baylie. 
Appointments 
Mayor Pro-tem 
Trustee McCue moved to appoint Bob Rush as the Mayor Pro-tem. Trustee Steinsiek seconded, 
and the motion passed unanimously. 
Clerk 
Trustee Steinsiek moved to appoint Josie Hart as the Town Clerk. Trustee McCue seconded and 
the motion passed unanimously. 
Treasurer 
Trustee McCue moved to appoint Pam Koehn as the treasurer. Trustee Rush seconded and the 
motion passed unanimously. 
Attorney 
Trustee Steinsiek moved to appoint Corey Hoffman with Hoffmann, Parker, Wilson, & Carberry, 
PC as the Town’s attorney. Trustee McCue seconded, and the motion passed unanimously.

Municipal Judge 
Trustee Steinsiek moved to appoint Stephen Ruddick as the Town’s Municipal Judge. Trustee 
McCue seconded, and the motion passed unanimously. 
Approval of Bills  
There was discussion on Schaal Drilling having to replace the motor in the north intown well. 
It’s been 2 years since the motor has been replaced on that well. 
Trustee Steinsiek moved to approve the May bills for payment. Trustee McCue seconded and the 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
Time Capsule Event Clerk Hart said that the Town received a $2500 grant from the Tourism 
Board. David Becker donated $5,000 for the event, asking that $2500 be used for the free meal 
and $2500 be used to get the park ready for the event. 
Clerk Hart said that Rob’s Septic Service will deliver 3 port-a-potties and 6 polycarts for the 
event.  
Clerk Hart asked the Board if they had anything they wanted to include in the new time capsule, 
it was suggested that a picture of the current Board be included, and maybe a few of the local 
newspapers.  
Clerk Hart said that the new time capsule plaque will say: The spirit of 76, half century time 
capsule, to be opened July 4th, 2076, sealed during the celebration of the Colorado 150 America 
250.  
Clerk Hart said that the food truck wants $6,000 2 weeks before the event, the Board asked that 
she negotiate paying a portion of that in advance with the remainder being paid after the event.  
Clerk Hart said that High Plains Brass will be playing at the event. 
Clerk Hart said that she hasn’t been able to get a photographer for the event. 
 
Wildfire Resiliency Code Ordinance The Board agreed to wait on adopting this.  
Wastewater Permit Regulations  Trustee Rush said that GMS has not quite finished the 
wastewater report but that they did tell him that Arriba has an infiltration problem. 
He noted that there might be a miniature mechanical treatment option for the wastewater facility. 
Add/Remove Names on Bank Account Trustee Baylie said she would be willing to be on the 
bank accounts. Trustee Rush moved to remove Ashley Smithburg from the Town’s bank 
accounts and to add Emily Baylie. Trustee McCue seconded and the motion passed unanimously. 
ECCOG / Senior Meals Clerk Hart explained that ECCOG has a budget shortfall and is needing 
to cut down on senior meals in May and June. She said that meal expenses are $21 per person, 
she did not have a count of how many people attend the meals in Arriba. The Board asked her to 
find out how many people attend in Arriba, based on that number the Town might sponsor 
Tuesday’s meals in June. 
Maintenance Report Mike Becker said that he got paint for the park building. He said that 
Brian Mohr has been helping mow the park.

Clerks Report Clerk Hart said that the Dog Clinic will be held on Wednesday June 10 from 
4pm to 6pm at Townhall.  
She said that there is a CML meeting in Stratton on May 19th.

Treasurer's Report Good.

Mayors Report Mayor Lightsey said she would be needing up to $300 again on the community

Adjournment Mayor Lightsey adjourned the meeting at 7:55pm.
