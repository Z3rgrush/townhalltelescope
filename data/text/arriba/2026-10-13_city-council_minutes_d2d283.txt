TOWN OF ARRIBA 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING 
Tuesday, October 13, 2026 
Arriba Town Hall, 711 Front Street, Arriba, CO  80804 
Call to Order Mayor Marilyn Lightsey called the meeting to order at 6:00. Trustees in 
attendance included Ben VanderWerf, Troy McCue, Bob Rush and Emily Baylie. Carolyn 
Steinsiek was absent. 
Employees in attendance included Josie Hart. 
Public Audience David Ruiz, Brian Mohr, and Josie Jones. 
Hospital Update  Erika Saffer was not present. 
Approval of Agenda Trustee VanderWerf moved to approve the Agenda as written. Trustee 
Baylie seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes  
Trustee Bailey moved to approve the minutes of the September 15 regular meeting as written. 
Trustee Rush seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Accounts Receivable and Payable  
Trustee Rush moved to approve the receivables and payables for September. Trustee 
VanderWerf seconded, and the motion passed unanimously. 
Approval of Bills  
Clerk Hart explained that she has not received a bill from Rob’s Trash Service yet, the one listed 
in the bill details is the amount from last month. 
Trustee Baylie moved to approve the October bills for payment with approval for paying Rob’s 
Trash bill when it is received. Trustee Rush seconded and the motion passed unanimously. 
Wastewater Permit Regulations  Nothing new on this. 
Speeding Bumps / Speed Signs Clerk Hart said that a set of speed bumps cost $258. She said 
the average price for speed radar signs is $3500. Trustee VanderWerf said he found a speed radar 
sign on Amazon for $650. 
There was discussion on different ideas for slowing down traffic on College Ave and Lincoln 
Ave, Clerk Hart suggested putting in stop signs at each intersection.  
Trustee VanderWerf moved to purchase 3 sets of speed bumps and 2 signs saying, ‘Speed 
Bumps Ahead’ all to be placed on College Ave. Trustee Baylie seconded the motion and the 
motion passed unanimously. 
Trustee Rush cautioned the Board that speed bumps on the truck route might cause some push 
back from people that use College Ave regularly.

Vacant Trustee Position Clerk Hart said the Brian Mohr and Beverly Langley had expressed 
interest in the open trustee position. Beverly Langley was not in attendance. Brian Mohr said he 
would like to be considered for the trustee position. 
Trustee Rush moved to appoint Brian Mohr to the vacant trustee position. Trustee Baylie 
seconded the motion and the motion passed unanimously. Clerk Hart swore in Brian Mohr as 
trustee. 
2027 Budget The Board reviewed the proposed 2027 Budget. Clerk Hart noted that a cost-of-
living increase for employees will be figured for 2027. There was discussion on increasing the 
Town insurance line item for 2027. Clerk Hart noted that the water fund’s 2025 final ending 
balance is -$5,969.89. The Board instructed her to ask the auditor about this and how it will 
affect the Town. 
A budget hearing is scheduled for Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at 6:00pm. 
Update Old Meters Trustee Rush explained that digital meters have a battery in them that 
eventually dies, there’s no way to replace the battery, the whole meter must be replaced. There 
was discussion on purchasing new meters to replace the older meters in town. Mike Becker said 
that the Town has 4 new meters in the shop. Clerk Hart suggested buying 2 meters every time 
one needs replaced so that there is always a new meter on hand. 
There was a suggestion to charge a non-refundable meter fee to new customers to pay for new 
meters. 
Trustee Rush moved to table this issue. Trustee McCue seconded and the motion passed 
unanimously. 
Maintenance Report none. 
Clerks Report Clerk Hart said that Jay Spurling had maintained College Ave with his 
maintainer. 
Clerk Hart said she had someone ask her if the trash service and fees would be re-evaluated. The 
Board suggested sending out surveys to customers and review the feedback in December. 
Treasurer's Report Good. 
Trustees Report none. 
LCEDC Report  Trustee McCue gave his LCEDC report. 
Mayors Report none. 
Executive Session  
Trustee Baylie moved to go into Executive Session to discuss maintenance personnel concerns. 
Trustee Mohr seconded. The Board went into Executive Session at 7:30pm. 
The Board came out of Executive Session at 7:45pm. 
Mike Becker says he hopes to be cleared for work soon. Trustee Mohr and Trustee VanderWerf 
offered to help with what they can.
