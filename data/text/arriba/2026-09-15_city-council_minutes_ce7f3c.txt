TOWN OF ARRIBA 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING 
Tuesday, September 15, 2026 
Arriba Town Hall, 711 Front Street, Arriba, CO  80804 
Call to Order Mayor Marilyn Lightsey called the meeting to order at 6:00. Trustees in 
attendance included Carolyn Steinsiek, Ben VanderWerf, Troy McCue, Kevin Harmon, Bob 
Rush and Emily Baylie. 
Employees in attendance included Josie Hart. 
Public Audience Beverly Langley, Brian Mohr. 
Beverly Langley asked the Board to review the water and sewer rates for RV’s and 
campgrounds. 
Brian Mohr asked the Board if anything could be done to slow trucks down on College Ave, the 
truck route. The Board discussed putting speed bumps on College Ave and Lincoln Ave to 
prevent speeding. Trustee Bailey moved to add this under new business. Trustee Rush seconded 
and the motion passed unanimously.  
Hospital Update  Erika Saffer gave her hospital report. She noted the annual hospital benefit 
gala. 
Approval of Agenda Trustee McCue moved to approve the Agenda as amended. Trustee 
VanderWerf seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes  
Trustee Bailey moved to approve the minutes of the August 11 regular meeting as written. 
Trustee Steinsiek seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Accounts Receivable and Payable  
Trustee McCue moved to approve the receivables and payables for August. Trustee Steinsiek 
seconded, and the motion passed unanimously. 
Approval of Bills  
Trustee McCue moved to approve the September bills for payment. Trustee Steinsiek seconded 
and the motion passed unanimously. 
 
Wastewater Permit Regulations  Mark Morton with GMS Inc gave an update on these issues. 
He explained that CDPHE has allowed the Town to revert to its old permit compliance, he said 
that GMS is still working on the reports for the new permit regulations but are taking it slow. He 
said the Town’s permit expires in 2027. He explained that CDPHE has 2 different study groups 
researching permit improvements and feasibility studies on if and how towns can implement the 
new regulations.

McCue RV Permit Clerk Hart explained that under the RV Ordinance, tap fees are not required 
for RV hookups, so Troy McCue will not be charged a water tap fee, he will just be charged the 
RV permit fee and for the use of the backhoe. 
Trustee Steinsiek moved to approve the McCue RV permit. Trustee VanderWerf seconded and 
the motion passed unanimously. 
Park Sprinkler System There was discussion on Mike Becker and Brian Mohr putting in the 
sprinkler system. There was discussion on renting a trencher for this. 
Speeding issue on College Ave and Lincoln Ave There was discussion on putting in speed 
bumps and / or radar signs with lights. Clerk Hart said she would get prices on these. 
Maintenance Report Clerk Hart said that Mike Becker has been dealing with some health 
issues. Clerk Hart requested to go into Executive Session to discuss maintenance personnel 
concerns. 
Clerks Report Clerk Hart said that she had renewed the Town’s support of the Pet 
Overpopulation Fund for up to $210. The Town will cover the $15 copay for Arriba residents 
that qualify. 
Clerk Hart said that next year she will need to update the office computer and printer, she said 
that Quickbooks is ending the desktop support and will be moving to Quickbooks Online only. 
Treasurer's Report Good. 
Trustees Report Emily Bailey said that she had been given some items for the time capsule. 
Trustee Rush said he had ordered parts for the new meter for Josh Sauer. He said he plans to put 
this in for Josh and will have a Town of Flagler employee help him. The Town of Arriba will 
need to pay the Flagler employee $27.50 per hour for his time. 
Trustee Harmon said that he is resigning from the Board, this will be his last meeting as he is 
moving. 
Trustee VanderWerf said that there is a stop sign missing on Grant and Lincoln, or it might be 
hidden behind an overgrown tree. 
LCEDC Report none. 
Mayors Report none. 
Executive Session  
Trustee Steinsiek moved to go into Executive Session to discuss maintenance personnel 
concerns. Trustee Bailey seconded. The Board went into Executive Session at 7:27pm. 
The Board came out of Executive Session at 7:46pm. 
The Board instructed Mayor Lightsey to request Mike Becker get a doctor’s release to work. 
The Board would also like to see an accountability report every month from each employee.
