Review Trash Service Ordinance  
The Board reviewed the trash ordinance. The Ordinance applies to all occupied residential 
households. 
 
There will be 3 trash service options for residents: 
 
1. 2 poly carts provided by Rob’s, dumped once a week, $40 per month. 
2. Resident’s own 3yd dumpster that is approved by Rob’s, dumped once a month, $45 per 
month. 
3. Renting a 3yd dumpster from Rob’s, dumped once a month, $50 per month. 
 
Trustee McCue moved to adopt the trash service ordinance. Trustee Rush seconded and the 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
Wildfire Resiliency Code Ordinance Trustee McCue moved to table the ordinance and get 
more information on enforcement and how other municipalities will be handling this Code. 
Trustee Smithburg seconded and the motion passed unanimously. 
 
Exemption from Audit Clerk Hart reported that Lauer, Szabo and Associates has completed the 
2025 Exemption from Audit. A resolution needs approved to accept the exemption from audit 
and to file it with the State. 
Trustee Rush moved to approve the resolution and accept the 2025 Exemption from Audit. 
Trustee McCue seconded and the motion passed unanimously. 
 
Time Capsule Opening Clerk Hart reported that a few classes at the Arriba-Flagler School 
would like to contribute to the new time capsule, she asked the Board what the regulations 
should be. The Board agreed to allow items that will fit into a shoe box. 
There was discussion on what the time capsule should be made off. There was a suggestion to 
have sheet metal bent and welded together.  
Clerk Hart asked about purchasing items like caps, shirts, etc.. The Board agreed that only a 
small amount of each should be ordered. 
 
2026 Election Clerk Hart said she would be mailing out ballots on Monday, March 16. 
Wastewater Permit Regulations  Trustee VanderWerf said he has been communicating with 
Gillian Laycock in Akron regarding new technology for wastewater systems. Gillian is currently 
researching different solutions that could be less expensive and keep wastewater systems in 
compliance with State regulations. 
Maintenance Report Mike Becker reported that he helped Rob with polycarts and dumpsters,

He said he got a couple of loads of gravel for the roads and alleys. 
He asked the Board about either fixing the town pickup or buying a ¾ ton flatbed with a 
snowplow on it. The Board asked him to get prices on a pickup he thinks would work for the 
Town.
