TOWN OF ARRIBA 
BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING 
Tuesday, June 9, 2026 
Arriba Town Hall, 711 Front Street, Arriba, CO  80804 
Call to Order Mayor Marilyn Lightsey called the meeting to order at 6:00. Trustees in 
attendance included Carolyn Steinsiek, Bob Rush, Troy McCue, Kevin Harmon, and Ben 
VanderWerf. Emily Baylie joined via telephone. 
Employees in attendance included Josie Hart. 
Public Audience none. 
Hospital Update  Erika Saffer gave her hospital report to the Board.  
Approval of Agenda Trustee McCue moved to approve the Agenda as written. Trustee Rush 
seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Minutes  
Trustee Rush moved to approve the minutes of the May 12 regular meeting as written. Trustee 
McCue seconded and the motion passed unanimously.  
Approval of Accounts Receivable and Payable  
Trustee McCue moved to approve the receivables and payables for May. Trustee Steinsiek 
seconded, and the motion passed unanimously. 
Approval of Bills  
Trustee McCue moved to approve the June bills for payment. Trustee VanderWerf seconded and 
the motion passed unanimously. 
 
Time Capsule Event Clerk Hart said that Eastern Sloper Rural Telephone Association donated 
$500 for the event. Clerk Hart said that Wendy Pottorf says the Arriba Wind Farm will be 
donating $2,500. 
Clerk Hart said that she’s been having trouble getting the SEP for the beer garden approved by 
the State, but she will continue working on it. 
Clerk Hart said she paid the food truck $6,000, the Town will pay them the remaining balance of 
$1,181.46 at the time of the event. 
Clerk Hart said the planning committee will meet on Thursday and she will have more 
information for the Board at that time. 
 
Wildfire Resiliency Code Ordinance The Board agreed to wait on adopting this.  
Wastewater Permit Regulations  nothing new on this. 
Maintenance Report none.
