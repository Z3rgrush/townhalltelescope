Executive Session to negotiate contract with Rob’s Trash Service 
Trustee Siobhan Steinsiek moved to hold an executive session to review and negotiate the 
contract with Rob and Jennifer Fager with Rob’s Trash Service. Trustee Carolyn Steinsiek 
seconded and the motion passed unanimously. The public audience was excused from the 
meeting. The Board went into executive session at 6:10pm. 
 
The Board came out of executive session at 6:34pm. The public was allowed back into the 
meeting. 
 
Review Proposed Trash Service Ordinance  
The Board reviewed the proposed trash ordinance. Trustee Siobhan Stiensiek suggested 
amending the ordinance under Section 2. Definitions “Household”  to say “any ‘occupied’ 
residential dwelling...” Trustee Rush moved to adopt the trash ordinance, with the suggested 
amendment, requiring every occupied residential household to have trash service and to be 
Page 2 of 3 
 
charged for trash on their utility bill. Trustee Smithburg seconded and the motion passed 
unanimously. 
 
There will be 3 trash service options for residents: 
 
1. 2 poly carts provided by Rob’s, dumped once a week, $40 per month. 
2. Resident’s own 3yd dumpster that is approved by Rob’s, dumped once a month, $45 per 
month. 
3. Renting a 3yd dumpster from Rob’s, dumped once a month, $50 per month. 
 
Trustee Rush moved to accept the contract with Rob’s Trash service with the understanding that 
the contract can be re-negotiated at the end of 2026. Trustee VanderWerf seconded and the 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
Time Capsule Opening Clerk Hart informed the Board that the America 250 – Colorado 150 
Community has awarded the Town with a $7,500 grant for the event. She asked the Board if they 
wanted to have the basketball court repainted for $5,000. The Board agreed to not to have it 
repainted. Clerk Hart said she will be working on getting liability insurance for the event. She 
reported that the planning committee would like to have a beer garden so she will be working on 
getting the special event permit for this. 
Clerk Hart asked the Board if the Town should put in another time capsule, the cost would start 
at $1500. The Board agreed that if additional grant funds are awarded then a new time capsule 
should be put in.  Clerk Hart reported that the planning committee would like to have a 
community lunch. The Board agreed that having several food trucks at the event would be a 
good idea. 
 
2026 Election -Draw names for Ballot order Clerk Hart reported that Marilyn Lightsey is the 
only candidate running for mayor. She said 4 people are running for trustee, she put the names of 
each candidate in a hat. They were drawn in the following order: 
Bob Rush, Troy McCue, Emily Baylie, Kevin Harmon.
