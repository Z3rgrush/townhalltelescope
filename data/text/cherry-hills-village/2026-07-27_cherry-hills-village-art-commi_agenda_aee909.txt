DISCUSSION 
The Rules of Procedure for the Cherry Hills Village Art Commission provide for the annual 
election of a Chair and Vice Chair or two Cochairs following the third Tuesday in May. 
 
The Commission’s regularly scheduled May 2026 meeting fell on Memorial Day, and an 
alternate meeting date with a quorum could not be identified. Because the annual officer election 
has not yet occurred, the Commission should conduct the election at its July 27 meeting. 
 
The relevant excerpt from the Rules of Procedure appears below: 
 
“ARTICLE 3 Officers  
 
Section 1. The Officers shall consist of the Chair and Vice Chair, or two Cochairs. 
 
Section 2. The election of the Chair and the Vice Chair, or two Cochairs, by the 
Commission shall be held annually following the third Tuesday in May. Upon 
nomination and second by any Commission member, a nomination for Chair shall be 
considered. A majority voice vote of members present and constituting a quorum shall be 
required to elect an Officer.” 
 
The Commission should first determine whether it wishes to retain the two-Cochair structure or 
elect a Chair and Vice Chair. The Commission may then receive nominations and seconds for 
each position. Each Officer must be elected by a majority voice vote of the members present and 
constituting a quorum.

Día de los Muertos Community Event at City Hall – October   
Event Background and Prior Commission Direction 
The Commission has discussed the Día de los Muertos display and community event over several 
meetings. Earlier concepts included displaying calaveras and a Catrina gazebo outdoors at John 
Meade Park and holding an evening event with food trucks, a movie and children’s activities. 
Because of the cost and potential weather impacts associated with an outdoor display, the 
Commission subsequently directed staff to pursue an indoor display at City Hall consisting of eight 
calaveras without the gazebo. 
 
At its June 29 meeting, the Commission considered whether the October 24 event should be oriented 
toward adults and participating artists or toward children and families. Following discussion, the 
Commission agreed to: 
 
• Display eight calaveras inside City Hall;  
• Hold a family-centered community event on Saturday, October 24;  
• Change the event time from the evening to 2–4 p.m.;  
• Collect advance RSVPs; and  
• Consider including an artist component if the event budget permits.  
 
The Commission also unanimously recommended that City Council approve the loan of the eight 
calaveras from art curator Alexis Newton. 
 
Staff have since worked with Ms. Newton on a draft art-loan agreement, which is currently under 
review by the City Attorney and may be revised based on that review. The current draft provides for 
the eight hand-painted papier-mâché calaveras and their bases to be displayed at City Hall from 
October 19 through November 2, 2026, at a total cost of $10,000, including delivery, installation and 
removal. 
 
CHVAC representatives are scheduled to attend the August 18 City Council meeting to present the 
Commission’s 2027 Work Plan and seek approval of the calavera loan. A save-the-date 
announcement for the display and October 24 event has also been prepared for the August Village 
Crier. 
 
 
 
 
 
Event Planning

Commission should now finalize the program, refreshments and use of space so staff can begin 
making arrangements.

• A children’s book reading about Día de los Muertos;  
• A hands-on art activity, such as tissue-paper flowers, skull painting or decorating, or coloring 
pages;  
• A showing of Coco (runtime 1 hour and 46 minutes) in Council Chambers, subject to

• Participation by one or more of the artists who created the calaveras; and  
• A separate artist talk or an informal opportunity for artists to speak with attendees during 
another activity.  
 
The Commission should consider how the selected activities will work together and how space in 
City Hall will be used. For example, showing a movie in Council Chambers may limit the space 
available for a book reading, artist presentation or hands-on activity. The event’s maximum 
attendance and RSVP process should also be considered as the program is developed.

approximately $1,200, before optional additions.

SUBJECT: REGULAR MEETING SCHEDULE 
 
DATE: JULY 27, 2026 
 
 
ISSUE 
Reviewing the Commission’s regular meeting schedule. 
 
DISCUSSION 
The Cherry Hills Village Art Commission currently meets at 10 a.m. on the last Monday of each 
month. While a consistent recurring schedule is necessary, the current schedule presents several 
scheduling considerations. 
 
The May meeting regularly falls on Memorial Day. Most recently, the Commission’s May 2026 
meeting fell on Memorial Day, and an alternate date that provided a quorum could not be identified. 
 
Meeting during the final week of each month creates additional challenges later in the year. The 
November and December meetings occur near Thanksgiving, Christmas and common vacation 
periods, increasing the likelihood of quorum concerns or meeting cancellations.  
 
Retaining the current schedule would preserve a familiar and predictable meeting pattern around 
which Commissioners and staff may have already arranged other commitments. It would also 
avoid creating new conflicts for members who can reliably attend at the current day and time.  
 
Alternatively, establishing a different recurring day, time or week of the month could: 
 
• Reduce predictable holiday and vacation conflicts;  
• Improve the likelihood of maintaining a quorum;  
• Reduce the need for scheduling polls and rescheduling efforts; 
• Provide Commissioners, staff and the public with a more reliable annual schedule;  
• Support consistent packet preparation and meeting-notice timelines; and  
• Allow Commission projects, event planning, budget discussions and recommendations to 
City Council to proceed more efficiently.  
 
 
The Commission should consider whether the current schedule remains workable or whether a 
different day, time or week of the month would provide a more consistent schedule. The relevant 
excerpts from Article 5 of the CHVAC Rules of Procedure are below: 
 
“ARTICLE 5 Meetings  
 
Section 1. Regular meetings shall be held once a month at the Village Center. The day 
and time of regular meetings can only be changed by a unanimous vote of the entire 
membership of the Commission. 
 
Section 2. Per the Board, Commission and Committee Recruitment, Appointment and 
Removal Policy, regular meetings can only be rescheduled when the meeting day falls on 
a holiday.” 
 
Accordingly, the Commission may change its regular meeting day and time without City Council 
approval, but any change requires a unanimous vote of the Commission’s entire membership. 
 
DECISIONS NEEDED 
The Commission should determine whether to: 
 
• Retain the current schedule of 10 a.m. on the last Monday of each month; or  
• Adopt a different regular meeting schedule.  
 
If adopting a change, the Commission should determine: 
 
• The preferred day of the week;  
• The preferred occurrence within the month, such as the second or third week;  
• The preferred meeting time; and  
• The effective date of the new schedule. 
 
STAFF RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommends that the Commission discuss whether its current regular meeting schedule 
continues to meet the needs of the Commission, staff and the public.  
 
RECOMMENDED MOTION 
If the Commission wishes to change its regular meeting schedule: 
 
“I move to change the Cherry Hills Village Art Commission’s regular meeting schedule to the 
[FIRST/SECOND/THIRD] [DAY] of each month at [TIME], beginning [DATE].” 
 
No motion is necessary if the Commission decides to retain its current regular meeting schedule.
