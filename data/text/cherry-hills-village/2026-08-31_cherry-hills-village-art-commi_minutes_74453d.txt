Item 5a 2026 Event Planning
Coordinator White reviewed planning for the October 24 DIa de los Muertos community
event, which will be held from 2-4 p.m. in conjunction with the Calaveras en Mi Ciudad
exhibit. She reported that City Council had approved the art-loan agreement on August 18
and that staff was awaiting the consultant’s signature.
Coordinator White summarized the Commission’s prior direction to offer two children’s
book readings, tissue-paper flower making, sugar-skull coloring pages, themed
refreshments, informal interaction with participating artists and an Art Commission
information and donation table. Members also discussed commissioner availability for
setup, the event and cleanup.
Cochair Fedorowicz supported making the event a polished and memorable experience for
residents and families. Commissioner Blackledge shared a catering recommendation,
Commissioner Mirr cautioned against over-ordering food during the midafternoon time
frame, and Commissioner Dossier asked the Commission to reconsider a food truck.
Manager Black explained that City Code does not allow a food truck to sell directly from
the public way; the City would need to pay in advance and provide the food to attendees.
She also reminded the Commission that unspent funds remain in the City’s General Fund
and should not be viewed as use-it-or-lose-it funding. Staff will research a range of food
options and return with pricing and menu information before the Commission makes a final
selection.

Commissioner Dossier suggested music, festive decorations and a photo opportunity using

available, they will be invited to mingle with attendees during the family event instead of

NEW BUSINESS
Item 6a 2027 Work Plan Follow-Up and Art in Public Places Master Plan Update
Cochair Fedorowicz reported that City Council approved the Commission’s 2027 Work
Plan on August 18 and responded positively to the planned family-oriented DIa de los
Muertos event. She said Council’s only substantive question concerned why the snow
plows are repainted each year; the painted surfaces are exposed to winter use and the
artwork deteriorates.
Coordinator White referred members to the in-progress Art in Public Places Master Plan
update included in the packet. Because of the time and the immediate priority of the
October event, the Commission agreed to resume the master-plan discussion after the Dia
de los Muertos event.
Item 6b 2026 Snow Plow Painting Program
Members discussed inviting three local schools to participate in the annual Snow Plow
Painting Program. Because St. Mary’s Academy had opted not participated in recent
years, the Commission supported inviting Brave Academy along with Cherry Hills Village
Elementary School and Kent Denver School. Commissioner Dossier volunteered to
contact Cherry Hills Village Elementary School and Kent Denver School, and
Commissioner Iverson volunteered to contact Brave Academy.
Manager Black explained that Public Works would prime and deliver the plows as soon as
possible. The plows must be placed in school parking lots so they can be safely removed
from and returned to City trucks. Schools should complete the artwork by the end of
September so the plows can be returned to service before early-season snow.
