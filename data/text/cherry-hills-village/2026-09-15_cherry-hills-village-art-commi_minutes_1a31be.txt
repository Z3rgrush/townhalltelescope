UNFINISHED BUSINESS
Item 5a Calaveras en Mi Ciudad - DIa de los Muertos October 24 Event
Coordinator White reviewed the remaining decisions for the October 24 event, including
entertainment, children’s programming, face painting, food, decorations and promotion.
Members discussed the proposed appearance by children’s author Jolene Gutiérrez and
the logistics of a scheduled reading during the open-house-style event. The Commission
supported prioritizing programming that would be available throughout the afternoon and
reached consensus not to pursue the paid author appearance for this event. Members
discussed retaining a children’s-book element in another form and left that aspect for
follow-up.
Cochair Fedorowicz presented an option for a classical guitarist to perform throughout the
two-hour event for $500. Members supported the guitarist as an activity that could provide
atmosphere throughout the event and interact with children and families. Cochair
Fedorowicz agreed to reconnect with the guitarist and copy staff so payment and
arrangements could be coordinated.
The Commission discussed face painting. Because detailed sugar-skull designs would limit
the number of children who could participate, members supported pursuing two face
painters and obtaining pricing for a mix of detailed and simpler designs. Staff will return
with updated pricing and availability.
Members decided not to pursue the approximately $600 balloon arch. Instead, they
supported creating a photo area using the Commission’s existing branded backdrop, large
letters, marigolds and inexpensive DIa de los Muertos-themed props.

NEW BUSINESS
Item 6a 2027 Sculpture-on-Loan Process and Art Consultant Timing
The Commission discussed beginning research for the 2027 Sculpture-on-Loan cycle
earlier than in previous years. Members noted that earlier research could provide more
time to identify artists and available works before engaging the Commission’s art
consultant.
Members supported individually researching potential artists and exterior sculptures during
the fall and bringing ideas forward in November. The Commission discussed inviting the
art consultant to a December meeting to review the initial research and help develop the
project brief. Staff will request an estimate for the consultant’s participation.
Item 6b Commission Logo Color
The Commission discussed the existing dark-red logo color and whether a brighter cherry
red would better reflect the Commission’s identity. Staff noted that any revised color would
need to be evaluated for accessibility to be compliant with Colorado State law before
adoption.
Members supported developing accessible color alternatives for consideration at the
September 28 meeting. Existing stickers and other materials using the current logo will
continue to be used rather than discarded; future materials could complement and
transition to a revised color if one is adopted.
Item 6c Branded Promotional and Collateral Materials
Commissioner Iverson presented updated pricing for potential branded materials, including
a retractable banner, notepads, napkins, name tags, pens, hats, shirts and patches.
Members discussed cost, usefulness and which items would be appropriate for the
October event and future Commission activities.
Members agreed that existing magnetic name-tag holders could be reused with inserts
printed in-house. The Commission did not support purchasing branded napkins specifically
for the DIa de los Muertos event and instead preferred inexpensive colorful or themed
napkins. Members expressed interest in pens and notepads. Commissioner Blackledge
offered to bring a graph-paper-style notepad sample to the September 28 meeting, and
Commissioner Iverson will obtain additional pricing for selected soft goods.
The Commission agreed to defer final decisions on most branded materials until after the
logo-color discussion at the September 28 meeting.
