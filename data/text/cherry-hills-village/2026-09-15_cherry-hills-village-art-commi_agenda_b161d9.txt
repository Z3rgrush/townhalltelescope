ISSUE 
Whether to retain the approved red used in the Commission’s logo during its first year of use or direct 
a limited review of alternatives. 
DISCUSSION 
Background and Prior Action 
The Commission’s final 2026 Work Plan, approved August 25, 2025, identified a redesigned 
Commission logo as a 2026 deliverable for use across events and materials. Cochair Benjamin Ortiz-
Hill designed the CHVAC logo. From May through November 2025, the Commission considered 
eight concepts and variations, refined selected designs and unanimously reached agreement to 
approve the current logo as presented, including its red color treatment. The logo was launched in 
2026, completing the work plan deliverable, and Commission collateral has since been purchased or 
produced using the approved design and color. 
          
  
Approved CHVAC logo variations currently in use 
 
At the August 31 meeting, a brighter cherry red was suggested. No alternate color specification or 
production sample is available for this meeting. The Commission may retain the approved treatment 
or direct development of specific alternatives for consideration at a future meeting.

Accessibility and Production Considerations 
Regardless of the color selected, logo files should include exact Pantone, CMYK, RGB and HEX 
specifications and full-color, black, white and reversed versions. Any future alternative should be 
evaluated on light and dark backgrounds, at small sizes, in grayscale and color-vision simulations, 
and on representative office and commercial printers. A brighter red is not necessarily more 
accessible and may provide less contrast against white. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 
(WCAG) exempt text within a logo from minimum contrast requirements, but that exception does not 
apply to surrounding digital content; color also should not be the sole means of conveying 
information. 
BUDGET AND IMPLEMENTATION 
Retaining the approved color has no direct cost. A limited color review may require staff or design 
time, revised specifications and production proofs; any external cost should be identified before it is 
incurred. Adopting a different red would require updated files and vendor specifications. Replacing 
existing materials would add cost and could strand usable inventory. 
If the Commission directs further review, it should also determine whether purchases considered 
under Item 5c of this packet will proceed using the currently approved color or be held until a final 
color decision is made. Existing collateral will remain in use unless the Commission specifically 
directs replacement. 
 
 
STAFF RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommend retaining the approved CHVAC logo and current color treatment during its initial 
implementation. This recommendation reflects continuity, fiscal stewardship and focus—not a staff 
preference among shades of red. The Commission completed an extensive review, unanimously 
approved the design and has begun using it on public materials. Maintaining that direction supports a 
consistent identity, protects existing investments and keeps attention on current Commission 
priorities. 
This does not preclude future reconsideration. Staff recommend documenting the brighter-red 
suggestion for a future planned brand review or reconsidering the color if a demonstrated production, 
accessibility or operational need arises.

Commission action. Existing collateral will remain in use. New branded-material purchases 
under Item 5c will [proceed using the currently approved color/be held pending the final color 
decision]. Any proposed external cost will be presented to the Commission for approval before 
it is incurred.”
