SUBJECT: COMMISSION LOGO COLOR  
DATE: SEPTEMBER 15, 2026 
 
ISSUE 
Continue discussion of the CHVAC logo color and determine whether additional color review is 
needed before ordering new branded materials. 
 
DISCUSSION 
Background and Prior Action 
At its September 15 special meeting, the Commission continued discussion of the red used in the 
approved CHVAC logo. Members expressed interest in considering a brighter “cherry red” for future 
applications and discussed developing possible alternatives for review at the September 28 meeting. 
The Commission also agreed that existing materials using the current logo should continue to be used 
rather than discarded and that most new branded-material purchases should wait until the logo-color 
discussion is resolved. 
The current logo and dark-red treatment were approved following the Commission's 2025 design 
process and launched in 2026. Existing Commission collateral has since been produced using that 
treatment. 
    
  
Approved CHVART logs currently in use 
 
 
Color Considerations 
Color examples, if available, will be shared at the meeting, the Commission can compare them with 
the currently approved treatment and determine whether it wishes to adopt an alternative. 
Any proposed color should ultimately include defined Pantone, CMYK, RGB and HEX 
specifications so the logo can be reproduced consistently across print and digital applications. 
Alternatives should also be reviewed on light and dark backgrounds, at smaller sizes and in typical 
print and digital uses. 
A brighter red is not necessarily more accessible solely because it is brighter; contrast depends on the 
specific color, background and application. 
 
EXISTING MATERIALS 
Regardless of whether the Commission retains the current color or adopts a new treatment for future 
use, existing collateral bearing the approved logo can continue to be used until the inventory is 
exhausted. The desire is that the new color complements the current one so that they can be used 
side-by-side during this time. 
This avoids unnecessary replacement costs and allows any future color transition to occur as new 
materials are produced. The September 15 memo similarly anticipated continued use of existing 
collateral unless the Commission specifically directed otherwise. 
 
COMMISSION DIRECTION 
The Commission should consider whether to: 
1. Retain the currently approved logo color and proceed with future branded materials using 
that treatment; 
2. Select an alternative color, if sufficient examples and specifications are available for 
consideration; or 
3. Continue the color review and provide direction on any additional examples or 
specifications needed before final selection. 
If a final color is selected, staff can use that direction in implementing the Commission's decisions 
regarding branded promotional and collateral materials under the following agenda item. 
 
RECOMMENDED MOTION 
If the Commission is prepared to finalize the color: 
“I move to approve the [current/proposed] color treatment for the CHVAC logo as the standard for 
future Commission materials, with existing collateral remaining in use.” 
If the Commission wants additional review, no motion is necessary; staff can document the direction 
provided.
