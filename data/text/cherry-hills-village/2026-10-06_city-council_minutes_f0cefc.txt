Minutes of the City Council of the City of Cherry Hills Village, Colorado 
held on Tuesday, October 6, 2026, at 6:30 PM 
at City Hall, 2450 East Quincy Avenue, Cherry Hills Village, Colorado 80113 
CALL TO ORDER 
Mayor Brown called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. 
ROLL CALL OF MEMBERS 
Mayor Katy Brown, Mayor Pro Tern Earl Hoellen, Councilors Doug Robinson, Dave 
Heller, Susan Maguire, Karen Fisher, and Robert Eber were present on roll call. Also 
present were City Manager Chris Cramer, City Attorney Kathie Guckenberger, 
Community Development Director Paul Workman, Finance Director Kelly Newman, 
Police Commander Brennan Leininger, and City Clerk Laura Gillespie. 
Absent: none. 
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Council conducted the Pledge of Allegiance. 
AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION PERIOD 
Doug Tisdale, CHV resident, expressed his appreciation for the municipal property tax 
holiday Council had proposed for 2027 and for Council's continued efforts to improve 
cell phone coverage in the City. He recalled a resolution passed by Council in 2001 . He 
stated he would be submitting to Council a plan for a municipal satellite resiliency 
initiative.

Village Art Commission

Clerk Gillespie presented Resolution 32, Series 2026 for Council consideration. She

reappointing the two members to partial terms and leaving the third position vacant until

be October 20, which would only be two regular P&Z meetings before January 5.

REPORTS 
Mayor 
Mayor Brown noted ballots had been mailed and asked people to vote. 
Members of City Council 
Councilor Heller reported he was working with staff to notify the public about an open 
notice and comment rulemaking for a noise plan at Centennial Airport. 
Councilor Eber reported he, Mayor Brown, and Manager Black had attended the 
Arapahoe County Open Space Shareback breakfast. 
Mayor Pro Tern Hoellen noted tomorrow was the third anniversary of Hamas's attack on 
Israel, and he reaffirmed the City's commitment to countering antisemitism and, more 
broadly, hate in all its forms. He also cautioned Council not to make allowances for time 
zone differences for electronic participants unless Council expressly decided that is their 
policy. 
Councilor Maguire relayed the concerns of residents in her district regarding a potential 
light at Belleview and Franklin, including the narrowness of Franklin, the amount of 
construction traffic, the lack of sidewalks, and the use of the street by residents for 
walking and biking. 
Councilor Robinson reported on the death of two young students at the University of 
Mississippi, one of whom graduated from Cherry Creek High School, and the national 
attention brought to the unregulated sale of unsafe drugs. 
Councilor Fisher reported attending the High Line Canal Collaborative Forum meeting. 
She reminded everyone that the High Line Canal Conservancy is a regional 
management structure that partners with the public. The High Line Canal Collaborative 
Forum has members from each jurisdiction along the Canal who meet to hear updates 
about improvements along the Canal. Arapahoe County reported they continue to 
transition ownership from Denver Water, and are actively working on streamlining the 
project approval process. There was a brief discussion among Forum members about 
e-bike rules along the Canal. She reminded Council that in winter 2025/2026 the City

City Manager, City Staff, City Attorney 
Director Workman reported staff received 111 new building permit submittals in 
September, which is the second highest number of new submittals in a single month 
since they started tracking three years ago, only topped by June of this year with 113 
submittals. He noted if activity did not slow down staff would have to look at decreasing

Commander Leininger reported the Police Department is tracking another bear in the 
City. Mayor Brown asked for a public safety related program to present at the Metro 
Mayor's Caucus tomorrow. City Manager Cramer suggested the KultureCity Sensory 
Inclusive Certification. 
Director Newman asked Council to review the draft budget and let staff know about any 
questions. 
Clerk Gillespie asked for two Council members to review applications, conduct 
interviews, and make recommendations for appointments to the P&Z. Councilors Eber 
and Heller volunteered to continue in this role. She asked for Council's direction on 
adding Passover to the list of holidays considered when scheduling future City 
meetings, and only rescheduling City meetings when they fall on the first night of 
Hanukkah rather than on any day of Hanukkah. Council decided to add the first and 
second nights of Passover to the list of holidays and reschedule City meetings that fa ll 
on the first or last nights of Hanukkah. 
City Manager Cramer reported other communities were having conversations about 
increasing regulations fore-bikes on trails. He reported Arium Networks, formerly Crown

installation of cellular equipment, which would involve placing steel poles next to 
existing wooden poles, but represented a substantial step in the right direction.

Mayor Pro Tern Hoellen moved, seconded by Councilor Eber, to go into Executive

the federal Fair Housing Act in concert with the Cherry Hills Village Municipal Code 
and for the purpose of receiving legal advice regarding the potential expansion of 
municipal parks within the City, and to adjourn the City Council meeting immediately 
at the conclusion of the executive session.
