Overview 
 
AMR is in full compliance with the base contract signed in 2016 (C207 -2016) as well as all of the 
amendments made to such contract as listed below:  
 
Contract C207-2016 Base Contract from 2015-2016 RFP Processes 
Contract C558-2020 Full Implementation of BLS Response 
Contract C488-2021 Five Years Extension of Contract through 2026 
Contract C71-2023 Nurse Navigation, Third Dispatcher, and etc. 
Contract C184-2025 Nurse Navigation Extension 
 
All of the requirements set forth for AMR to meet are found in the supplemental Requirements 
document issued by the Ambulance Compliance Officer and available on the website:  
  
 https://www.snco.gov/em/ambulance_meeting_info.php    
 
In order to report compliance with the contract, the Ambulance Compliance Officer undertakes 
many different activities. These activities involve an audit of the communication systems, audit of 
the hospital reporting, audit of patient reports, audit of data  provided by AMR, specific field 
observation of AMR units; as well as receiving information through the various stakeholder task 
forces, groups, and committees. There is also a regular examination of the Nurse Navigation Pilot 
Program that the BOCC continued through 2025.  
 
In accordance with Resolution 2023-14, the Ambulance Compliance Officer will continue to identify 
gaps and challenges as well as formulate goals, strategies, and objectives in the Shawnee County 
EMS system. The Ambulance Compliance Officer continues to kee p good relationships with First 
Responder organizations and AMR. Due to work loads and personnel shortages, attendance and 
membership by organizations in the various stakeholder task forces, groups, and committees 
dropped down in 2025. Even in  the face of having less meetings,  a number of  strategies and 
objectives were outlined in reports from the Ambulance Compliance Officer.  
 
 
Accomplishments and Happenings during the Fiscal 2025 (Jan to Jan) 
 
Shawnee County EMS personnel worked through two large winter storms that 
impacted Shawnee County and much of NEKS. This increased the number of 
exemptions requested and approved.  
 
During 2025 the city, county, and state had large construction projects that 
impacted numerous areas of the jurisdiction. The EMS system saw increased 
response times in specific areas. As noted in every meeting and documents, 
traffic and construction exemp tions are not automatic. The compliance officer 
assesses each one to ensure there were no other factors that made the call late, 
before granting an exemption.
