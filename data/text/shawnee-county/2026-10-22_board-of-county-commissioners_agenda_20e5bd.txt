RE:   Independent Contractor Agreement for Services – Innovative  
   
Purpose: Consider approval of Independent Contractor Agreement for Services (ICAS) C2026xxx 
between Shawnee County and Innovative Impacts for the delivery of STEAM learning camps, 
programs, and classes through Shawnee County Parks + Recreation. 
 
Justification: This agreement allows Shawnee County Parks + Recreation to offer STEAM 
learning camps, programs, and classes through Innovative Impacts. The County will pay the 
contractor 55% of paid registrations and provide LEGO® kits for scheduled activities, while 
Innovative Impacts is responsible for its own operating expenses. This arrangement expands youth 
educational programming without requiring the County to directly employ the instructor.

Dear Commissioners, 
 
I am formally writing to request your final authorization and signature to execute the revised license 
agreement between Shawnee County and Pro Hockey Topeka, covering the five-year term effective 
July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2031. Rich Eckert has already reviewed the revised contract, and 
now would like to request a final signature from the Shawnee County Commissioners to fully 
execute and proceed.  
 
Executive Summary & Key Terms 
Approval of this revised contract will release the terms of the existing license agreement and 
establish a streamlined settlement framework designed to simplify venue operations and financial 
tracking: 
1. All-In Rental Pricing: Replaces game-by-game variable labor charges with a predictable, 
all-inclusive rental rate per game, establishing fixed operating expectations and eliminating 
post-game labor reconciliation friction. 
2. Shared Facility Fee:  Establishes a joint facility fee structure shared between the venue 
and Pro Hockey Topeka. 
3. Late Night Skate Revenue Share: Grants the team a 40% share of net revenue generated 
from co-hosted Late Night Skate events. 
4. Premium Seating Allocation:  Reserves three (3) party deck tables per game for the 
hockey team’s direct use and promotion.

Procedural Sequence 
Execution of this agreement will supersede current operational terms. A separate document 
formally releasing the legacy license agreement will be presented to the Board in the near future. 
 
Requested Action 
I respectfully request that the Board of County Commissioners approve the agreement and 
authorize the Board Chair to execute the revised license agreement on behalf of Shawnee County. 
Thank you for your consideration. 
Sincerely, 
 
 
Please add this request to the Shawnee County Commission agenda on Thursday, October 
22, 2026. The owner of the Pro Hockey Topeka, Don Lewis, will be in attendance with 
some hockey players to celebrate this extension.  
 
 
Thank you for your consideration of this request.
