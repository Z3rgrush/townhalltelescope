BARBEQUE BUCKS VENDOR PROCEDURES 
The following procedures may be provided to participating vendors as part of the Smoke on the 
Water Barbeque Bucks program: 
1. Patron Purchases: Patrons purchase Barbeque Bucks from SCP+R at designated sales
locations.
2. Vendor Sales: Vendors accept Barbeque Bucks for approved food and merchandise
purchases.
3. No Direct Payments: Vendors may not accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, checks, or
other direct payments from patrons at the vending location during the Event.
4. Pricing: Vendor shall post prices clearly and charge the same posted price to all patrons.
5. No Cash Change: Vendors may not provide cash change to patrons.
6. Collection: Vendors should maintain Barbeque Bucks in a secure location throughout the
Event.
7. End-of-Event Reconciliation: Vendors will bring all collected Barbeque Bucks to the
designated SCP+R reconciliation location at the conclusion of the Event.
8. Payment: SCP+R will verify the Barbeque Bucks and issue payment to Vendor for the
verified dollar value minus any shared revenue.
9. Invalid Bucks: Counterfeit, altered, duplicated, or otherwise unauthorized Barbeque
Bucks will not be eligible for payment.
10. Questions/Disputes: Any questions concerning the Barbeque Bucks program or
reconciliation should be directed to the SCP+R Event Coordinator or designated SCP+R
representative.

VENDOR PARTICIPATION 
This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is entered into by and between Shawnee County 
Parks + Recreation (“SCP+R”) and 
(“Vendor”) for the purpose of establishing the terms and conditions under which Vendor will 
participate as a food vendor in the Smoke on the Water BBQ Competition (“Event”). 
 
The Event is  anticipated to be held on October 9, 2026 at Lake Shawnee, Topeka, Kansas. 
 
1. PURPOSE 
The purpose of this MOU is to establish a cashless food and beverage purchasing system for the 
Event through the use of Barbeque Bucks issued and administered by SCP+R. 
 
Under this system, Event patrons will purchase Barbeque Bucks directly from SCP+R and use 
the Barbeque Bucks to purchase eligible food and other approved items from participating 
vendors. Vendor agrees to accept Barbeque Bucks as the sole form of payment from Event 
patrons at Vendor's vending location. 
 
SCP+R will subsequently redeem the Barbeque Bucks collected by Vendor for cash payment to 
Vendor in accordance with the terms of this MOU. 
 
2. BARBEQUE BUCKS 
SCP+R will establish and administer the Barbeque Bucks system, including the sale, distribution, 
design, denomination, and redemption procedures for the Barbeque Bucks. 
 
Barbeque Bucks will have a designated monetary value established by SCP+R and will be 
redeemable only at participating Event vendors for approved purchases. 
 
Vendor acknowledges that Barbeque Bucks are not legal tender and may not be redeemed for 
cash directly by Event patrons at Vendor's vending location. 
 
3. VENDOR ACCEPTANCE OF BARBEQUE BUCKS 
Vendor agrees to accept Barbeque Bucks as payment for approved food and other items sold by 
Vendor at the Event. 
 
Vendor shall not accept cash, checks, credit cards, debit cards, mobile payments, or any 
other form of payment directly from Event patrons at the vending location during the 
Event.
