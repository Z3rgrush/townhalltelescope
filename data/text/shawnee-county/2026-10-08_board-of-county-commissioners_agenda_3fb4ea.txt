Purpose:  Consider approval of the attached Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) C2026xxx 
with El Jeepney Grill which establishes details of the Barbeque Bucks program at the 2026 Smoke 
on the Water barbeque competition to be held at Lake Shawnee October 9.  
 
Justification: The establishment of the Barbeque (BBQ) Bucks program allows patrons to 
purchase and use BBQ Bucks within the event to purchase eligible food and event items from 
participating vendors instead of cash . The program allows for c ashless exchange for purchases, 
provides greater tracking of services utilized, and provides a basis for revenue sharing between 
vendors and the department. Participating vendors will turn in  the accepted BBQ Bucks for 
reconciliation by Parks + Recreation at the conclusion of the event and will be reimbursed by ACH 
for the cash equivalent of the BBQ Bucks turned in minus any shared revenue . The BBQ Bucks 
program is popular and common for barbeque competitions.
