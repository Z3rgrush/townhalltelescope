SHAWNEE COUNTY CONTRACT NO. C2026   
 
FACILITY MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT 
 
This Facility Management Agreement is between the Board of County Commissioners 
of the County of Shawnee, Kansas, (hereafter referred to as “County”) and YBR Properties WG 
Topeka LLC (hereafter referred to as “Manager”), (collectively referred to as “Parties”). 
 
WHEREAS, the County is the owner of a venue known as the Maner Conference Center 
generally located at 1717 SW Topeka Blvd, Topeka, KS 66612 (hereafter referred to as 
“Facility”); and 
 
WHEREAS, the County desires to engage the Manager to provide quality  management 
services for the express purpose of enhancing the public use and enjoyment of the Facility; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Manager  has the necessary experience in the business of providing 
quality management services. The County will rely upon the management services of the 
Manager for the Facility. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promise and covenants contained 
herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is 
hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
 
Article 1 - Appointment of Manager 
 
1.1 E ngagement of Manager: 
The County hereby engages the Manager and authorizes it to operate, manage and 
maintain the Facility. The Manager hereby accepts the engagement and authorization 
and agrees to us e skills and best efforts as the Manager to effectively operate, manage 
and maintain the Facility.  
 
1.2 P ortion of Facility leased to Manager : 
The Manager shall be entitled to lease all areas of the Facility shown on the attached site 
plan attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.  
 
1.3 P erformance  of Manager Duties: 
The Manager agrees to render capable and competent services and to exercise due care 
in accordance with the standard practices acceptable and common in the industry. The 
Manager agrees to  manage, operate, and maintain the Facility  in a diligent and 
professional manner consistent with industry standards. The Manager agrees to promote 
business, ensure security, and protect the Facility’s  physical condition. At all times, the 
Manager shall be obligated to act in the County’s best interest. 
 
1.4 I ndependent Contractor Status: 
It is acknowledged that the expressed intent of the County to the  Manager is that this

Article 2 - Duties of Manager 
 
2.1 Manager: 
At Manager’s cost, the Manager shall take such actions and perform such duties, as the 
Manager deems necessary and desirable, subject to the provisions hereof, of the Facility. 
 
2.2 Management of Renovation of Manor Conference Center: 
In order to ensure consistency and compatibility with the adjoining Wyndham Grand,

2.3 Employment of Personnel: 
(a) During the management term and the renewal term, if any, the Manager, at the 
Manager’s sole cost, shall select, train, schedule and employ at the Facility such number 
of employees, as the Manager deems necessary or appropriate to satisfy its responsibilities 
hereunder. The Manager shall have authority to hire, terminate, discipline and discharge 
any and all personnel working for the Manager at the Facility. 
 
(b) The Manager employees at the Facility shall not be considered to be employees of 
the County, and the Manager shall be solely responsible for their supervision and daily  
direction and control. The Manager shall be solely responsible for paying, processing 
and disbursing payroll checks to the Managers’ employees. 
 
2.4 Event Bookings and Rentals: 
The Manager shall be solely responsible for calendaring, scheduling and event bookings 
at the Facility.  
 
2.5 Scheduling: 
The Manager shall develop and maintain all schedules for events held at the Facility. All 
scheduling shall be accomplished in a manner so as to maximize the use of the Facility 
and make  the Facility more accessible to the community. The Parties understand and 
agree that the Manager shall be empowered to negotiate Event Rental Agreements, 
which may deviate from the standard rate when such negotiation is deemed by the 
Manager to be appropriate. 
 
2.6 Property Security for Managers’ Events: 
The Manager shall be responsible for hiring and supervising all security services for 
events at the leased portion of the Facility. The cost of such security will be paid by the 
Manager.

2.7 Cleaning for Managers’ Events: 
The Manager shall be responsible for providing and supervising all cleaning services to 
the leased portion of the Facility related to Managers’ Events. 
 
2.8 Workers’ Compensation Insurance: 
The Manager shall provide the County with a certificate that the Managers’ employees 
are covered by Workers’ Compensation Insurance. 
2.9 Food Services: 
(a) The Manager shall have the exclusive right to operate or contract for the 
operation of the food services, concession services, vending Services and the service and 
sale of alcoholic beverages, at the Facility. 
 
(b) The Manager  shall comply with and observe all federal, state, and  local laws, 
ordinances, and regulations  as to sanitation and the purity of food and beverages or 
otherwise relating to its operations. 
 
(c)  Except as set forth herein in Article 5, a ll profit or loss derived by said food 
services will be property of the Manager. 
 
2.10 Negotiate with Third Parties: 
The Manager may exclusively negotiate, execute, deliver and administer any and all 
licenses, occupancy agreements, rental agreements, booking commitments, advertising 
and sponsorship agreements, catering and concession agreements, decorating 
agreements, supplier agreements, service contracts and all other contracts and agreements 
in connection with the entertainment management, promotion and promotion at the   
Facility. 
 
2.11 Use of County Equipment/Assets: 
The Manager shall be granted authority from the County  to utilize all County  assets at 
the Facility for each event including, but not limited to, tables and chairs, audio visual 
equipment, communications/internet equipment or other County assets as deemed 
necessary to perform entertainment management duties herein. 
 
2.12 Nondiscrimination: 
(a) The Manager agrees that it shall not discriminate against any patron/potential 
patron, entity or person interested in renting the facility, employee or applicant for 
employment because of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, or any 
other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local laws. The Manager 
agrees to ensure equal access and fair treatment for all patrons and employees and to 
assist the County with making reasonable accommodations for individuals with 
disabilities in accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and other 
applicable laws.  
 
(b) In the event that any individual requests an accommodation for a disability in

Article 3 - Duties of County 
 
3.1 Utilities: 
The County shall be responsible for the day to day operational costs associated with the 
Facility, including, but not limited to, utilities expenses. All utilities shall remain in the name 
of the County. 
 
3.2 Cleaning, Repairs and Maintenance: 
The Manager is responsible for all day to day cleaning and maintenance of the Facility. 
Except for repair of damages to the Facility resulting from Manager’s Events, all 
expenses for any maintenance or repair will be the responsibility of the County. 
 
3.3 Outdoor Maintenance/Landscaping: 
The County shall remain responsible for outside maintenance of the Facility, including 
but not limited to landscaping, mowing and snow removal. The Manager will coordinate 
with the County to insure that outdoor maintenance activities do not occur at times when 
they would disrupt the Manager’s Events (ex. no mowing during outdoor wedding 
ceremony). 
 
3.4 Access to Facility/Keys and Security Cameras: 
The County will provide the Manager with all keys/access codes needed to access the 
Facility and operate the security system. The County will not issue new keys or codes to 
the Facility without coordinating with the Manager.  The County will provide the 
Manager with access to view the security cameras at the Facility. 
 
Article 4 – Term 
 
4.1 The term of the Agreement shall commence on August 1, 2026 f or a 10 -year period 
ending July 31st, 2036; renewable for two (5) year additional  terms to July 31 st, 2046.  
This Agreement may only be further renewed or extended by written agreement of the 
Parties. 
 
Article 5 – Fiscal Matters 
 
5.1 Rent: 
(a) Manager will pay no rent during the period the facility is under renovation until such 
time that a certificate of occupancy is obtained and facility bookings can commence. 
(b) Manager will pay a fixed rent of $125,000 in first year escalating to $150,000 in year 
two, and escalating to $175,000 in year three, at which time further rent adjustment will 
be made quarterly based on CPI.

(c) Rent will be paid quarterly. 
(d) Participation Payments to County. I f net event and catering revenue exceeds the 
following benchmark amounts, the County will receive an additional year -end payment 
from the Manager equal to 6% of net revenues above the benchmark threshold, payable

 Year 1 - $1,000,000 net event and catering revenue

For the purposes of calculating gross revenue, the parties agree that every sales contract 
includes a surcharge to cover gratuity for the staff that works the event. The gratuity 
surcharge shall not be included in the calculation of gross revenue. The parties agree that 
Gross Revenue includes revenue from conference -related events, catering, banquet 
activity, and associated revenues tied to Conference Center bookings regardless where the 
food and beverages are prepared.     
For the purposes of calculating whether net event and catering revenue has exceeded the 
benchmark amounts, the following Authorized Expenditures shall be subtracted from 
gross revenues: 
Authorized Expenditures are expenditures which are reasonable, necessary, and directly 
incurred in operating the Facility and that are consistent with generally accepted 
accounting principals. Specifically, such expenditures shall include the direct cost of 
food and beverages, event specific labor, event supplies, and other dir ect operating 
expenses attributable to Facility events. Authorized Expenditures shall not include 
corporate or regional overhead, management fees, franchise fees, marketing or 
advertising not directly attributable to a specific Facility event, depreciation , 
amortization, financing costs, interest, taxes, utilities, insurance, capital expenditures, 
reserves or other indirect or allocated expenses unless approved in writing by the 
County. 
(e) The County shall pay for all utilities currently in service at the Facility. However, in 
order to incentivize the Manager to economically operate the Facility, a baseline amount 
of $125,000 per year in utility costs shall be established. In the event the actual total 
utility use for any year this Agreement is in effect is  less than the baseline amount of 
$125,000 any savings shall be equally divided between the Manager and the County. 
 
5.2 Capital Equipment and Capital Improvements: 
The obligation to pay for, and authority to perform, direct and supervise capital 
equipment and capital improvement  purchases shall remain with the County. The 
Manager may submit written recommendations to the County for capital improvements 
no later than April 15 of every calendar year. The County shall retain the discretion to

5.4  Naming Rights: 
Any agreement for naming rights to the Facility will be negotiated by the County and 
revenue generated by the same will be retained by the County.

(c) The indemnity obligations of the Parties hereunder shall survive the expiration or 
termination of this Agreement. N either Party shall be liable or responsible for any lost 
profits, indirect, incidental, punitive or special damages, whether based  upon breach of 
contract or warranty, negligence, strict tort liability or otherwise, and each Party’s 
liability for damages or losses hereunder shall be strictly limited to direct damages that 
are actually incurred by the Party. 
 
6.2 The County shall be required to maintain, at its cost, Property Insurance covering the 
Facility structure and County-owned personal property located in the Facility. The Parties 
acknowledge that the County is self-insured for liability purposes and tort claims against 
the County are limited by the Kansas Tort Claims Act.

shall include automobile, employee fidelity, and such other coverage as may be applicable

premiums and deductibles are to be paid by  the Manager. Copies of the insurance policy

Until such relocation is completed, the County shall retain possession and control of the 
Extension Space, and the Extension Office may continue to occupy and use the Extension 
Space for its governmental purposes. The County agrees, however, that the Extension 
Office's use of the Extension Space shall be coordinated through a reservation and 
scheduling process established by the County after consultation with Lessee. The County 
shall use commercially reasonable efforts to schedule Extension Office activities in a

due diligence, design, surveying, environmental review, utility investigations, pre -
construction activities, construction, inspections, and all other activities reasonably 
necessary to complete the renovation of the Conference Center. Except in the event of an 
emergency or unless otherwise agreed by the County, Lessee shall provide the County 
with not less than forty -eight (48) hours' prior written notice before accessing occupied 
portions of the Extension Space. The County shall reasonably cooperate in providing such 
access and coordinating Extension Office operations so as not to unreasonably delay or 
interfere with the renovation schedule.

Article 7 – Termination 
 
7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon a default by the other Party hereunder. 
A Party shall be in default hereunder if such Party fails in any material respect to perform 
or comply with any of the other terms, covenants, agreements or conditions hereof and 
such failure continues for more than ninety (90) days after written notice thereof from the 
other Party. Bankruptcy by Manager is grounds for immediate termination. 
 
7.2 The Parties may mutually agree to terminate this Agreement without cause. 
 
7.3 In the event of expiration of this agreement, or termination with or without cause, the 
Manager shall remove any personal property from the facility, repair any damage caused 
to the Facility by the Manager and/or its patrons (reasonable wear and tear accepted) and 
remit any payments due to the County within 30 days.

RE:  Topeka Food Truck  Festival 
Park Services Division – Event  
 
Purpose: Consider approval of the attached Topeka Food Truck Festival LLC Agreement to 
provide a food truck event on August 30, 2026 at Gage Park. Organizer shall pay a special event

SHAWNEE COUNTY CONTRACT NO. C 2026___________ 
 
FOOD TRUCK FESTIVAL CONTRACT 
 
THIS CONTRACT is entered into this _____ day _____ of 2026, by and between 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF THE COUNTY OF SHAWNEE, 
KANSAS, hereinafter referred to as “County” and Topeka Food Truck Festival LLC, herein 
referred to as “Contractor” and together referred to as the “Parties.” 
WHEREAS, Shawnee County Parks + Recreation hereinafter referred to as the 
“Department” shall be the responsible party and representative of the County; and, 
NOW, THEREFORE, for the reasons set forth above, and in consideration of the 
mutual covenants and agreements hereinafter, the Parties agree as follows: 
1. EFFECTIVE DATE.  This Contract is effective upon the signing of the parties 
listed hereunder.  The Food Truck Festival shall be held on August 30, 2026 and is subject to 
postponement as indicated herein. 
2. TERMS.  The County and Contractor agree as follows; 
a. Contractor agrees to provide and manage a Food Truck Festival  in accordance 
with applicable Federal, State, and Local laws.   
b. C ounty agrees to provide the Westlake Shelter, amphitheater, bathrooms, and 
parkland in functional and stocked condition for the Event. 
c. No illegal drugs or alcohol shall be permitted. 
d. All food and inflatable vendors are required to have valid insurance on file with 
Contractor. The Contractor shall provide the County receive d copies of all vendor 
insurance documents prior to August 30, 2026. Any vendor that fails to provide the 
required insurance will not be allowed to participate in the Event. 
e. If the City of Topeka or Shawnee County necessitate a traffic plan, Organizer 
is responsible for obtaining and financing a traffic plan from the City of Topeka 
assuring all traffic blockages or barriers are in compliance with city guidelines. 
f. County will provide additional temporary handicap parking by the Westlake 
Shelter for the Event. 
g. County will move, add, or replace tables, trashcans, and existing dumpster 
according to Attachment A and organizational plans agreed upon with Contractor.

h. Contractor shall maintain current park cleanliness and is responsible for post -
event cleanup. The County may assess additional fees for damages or extra cleanup 
required to restore parkland to pre-event conditions. 
3. TIME OF EVENT .  Contractor shall manage the Event  on August 30, 2026 
with setup time starting at 8:00 a.m., Event open to the public at 11:00 a.m., closing time to 
the public at 5:00 p.m., with teardown and vacate of the premises by 8 p.m. unless Parties agree 
in writing to alter dates or times. This is a rain-or-shine event and it is agreed and understood 
by the Parties that the event will be held on August 30, 2026 regardless of inclement weather.   
 4.   FINANCIALS.  It is agreed and understood that the total contract fee for the 
event shall be Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00).  Contractor may utilize the Big Gage Shelter 
for an additional One Hundred Eighty-Five Dollars ($185.00) by providing County a notice of 
intent to utilize the shelter by August 6, 2026. 
 5. INSURANCE AND LICENSING.  Contractor shall provide a certificate o f 
liability insurance in the amount of O ne Million Dollars and No/100 ( $1,000,000.00) to the 
Department on or before August 16, 2026. 
a. Contractor is responsible for assuring all mobile food vendors and 
entertainment vendors have required vehicle and liability insurance. Contractor will 
collect all necessary insurance forms prior to allowing vendor to operate in the 
Event.   
b. Contractor shall be responsible for assuring all mobile vendors have current 
KDA licensing required to operate. Vendors that cannot provide proper 
documentation will not be allowed to operate in the Event. 
 6.    INDEMNIFICATION.  The  Contractor agrees to indemnify a nd hold 
harmless the Board of Commissioners of the County of Shawnee, Kansas, its officers, 
employees, contractors, subcontractors, agents, and insurers from and against all liability, 
claims, or demands, on account of injury, loss, or damage, of any kind whatsoever, which may 
arise out of or are in any manner connected with the event, if such injury, loss, or damage is or 
is claimed to be caused in whole or in part by the act, omission, or fault of the Contractor.  The 
Contractor agrees to investigate, handle, respond to and to provide a defense for any liability, 
claims, or demands at the sole expense of the Contractor, and agrees to bear any of the costs 
and expenses related hereto, including court costs and attorney fees, whether or not such 
liability, claims, or demands are groundless, false, or fraudulent as it applies to the Event.
