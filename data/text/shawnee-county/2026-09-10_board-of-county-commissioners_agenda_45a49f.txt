RE: Approval to Donate Items to PCs for People 
 
The Information Technology D epartment is requesting approval to donate several hard drives  
and laptops to PCs for People.  These items no longer have any valuable life left for use within 
Shawnee County. 
 
In 2022, the County Commissioners approved the Information Technolo gy Department to enter 
an agreement with PCs for People.  PCs for People provides e -waste pickup and recycling of 
electronic equipment.  They will sanitize all equipment using an AAA NAID Certified data 
sanitization policy and will provide an inventory log detailing the data sanitation results.  PCs for 
People then offers the recycled and refurbished equipment to qualifying families, individuals, or 
nonprofits in need. 
 
The Shawnee County Property Disposal Form and a spreadsheet is included and properly 
identifies the equipment we wish to donate.  This request has been reviewed and approved by 
the Purchasing Director. 
 
I am happy to answer any questions you may have about this request.

PRESENT USE:   Residential with an animal hospital operating under an approved Special Use 
Permit. The total parcel contains approximately 5.18 acres, of which approximately 3.78 acres are 
covered by the Special Use Permit. 
 
BACKGROUND: The Shawnee County Planning Commission initially reviewed this matter on 
Monday, July 13, 2026. During the hearing, staff presented their report and recommendation. 
Following the meeting, staff determined that the legal description was incorrect. As a result, revised 
notification and vicinity maps were required, along with the issuance of new notification letters. 
Therefore, the matter is before the Commission again for further consideration. 
 
On August 13, 1980, the Shawnee County Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No.  80-
123, approving a Special Use Permit for the establishment of an animal hospital on the subject 
property. As part of the approval, the Board imposed a condition limiting the permit to the existing 
ownership and requiring Board of County Commissioners approval for any transfer or assignment 
of the permit. The County's current Conditional Use Permit process serves substantially the same 
purpose as the Special Use Permit process in place at the time of the original approval. 
 
PROPOSED USE: The existing animal hospital use will remain unchanged. The request is limited 
to the removal of Condition No. 2 of Resolution No. 80- 123, thereby eliminating the ownership 
restriction and the requirement for Board of County Commissioners approval prior to the transfer or 
assignment of the permit.
