RE: Presentation – Kossover Tennis Center 
 
Purpose: Recognition  of Kossover Tennis Center as one of 41 winners in the USTA’s annual 
Outstanding Facility Awards program. 
  
Justification:  Kossover Tennis Center was recognized for an award for Large Tennis Center/Public 
Courts by USTA’s Outstanding Facility Awards program due to the highly effective public-private 
partnership between Shawnee County Parks + Recreation, the Topeka Tennis Association, and 
Racquet and Paddle. With the addition of 4 new courts, surfacing, lighting, and amenities, the 
improvements have elevated Kossover as the largest facility in Kansas and one of the most premier 
facilities in the Midwest. 
 
Nominated facilities were voted on in the following categories: Public Courts that are either small 
tennis centers with 2-11 courts or large tennis centers with 12 or more courts; Private Facilities 
that offer both USTA and public programming designed to help grow tennis (small: 2-11 courts; 
large: 12 or more courts); Educational Institutions such as colleges, universities, public and 
private grade schools, middle schools, or high schools; and Innovation, which recognize facilities 
that have been particularly innovative when it comes to delivering tennis. All 2026 award winners 
will receive a wall plaque and an all-weather sign, which can be mounted at the facility.
