Town of Haxtun 
December 1, 2025 
REGULAR MEETING 
Minutes   
 
The Haxtun Town Council met for their regularly scheduled meeting at 7:00 p.m. The meeting 
was opened by Mayor Mike Ensminger at the Haxtun Town Hall. Roll call was taken with the 
following council members present; Bob Cionek, Dave Green, Ryan Horton, Lori Lundgren and 
Jon Brammer. Tana Pope was absent. Mayor Ensminger led the group in the Pledge of 
Allegiance.  
 
Staff members present were Darrell Smith, Chief of Police, Ron Carpenter, Town 
Superintendent, Kelsey Harms, Town Clerk/Treasurer, and Shea Lightsey newly appointed 
Town Clerk/Treasurer.  
 
Approval of Agenda: 
A motion was made by L. Lundgren and seconded by D. Green to approve the agenda.  All 
present voted yes, the motion carried.  
 
Consent Agenda: 
A motion was made by D. Green and seconded by B. Cionek to approve the consent agenda, 
which included the regular meeting minutes of November 3, 2025, meeting minutes, the monthly 
invoices, and transfers of cash. All present voted yes, and the motion carried.

Regular Business: 
 
A public hearing was held for the 2026 Proposed Budget. Clerk Harms presented a copy of the 
budget to all present members. R. Carpenter brought up items on the budget to discuss, such as 
a crack-filled machine for the streets. The final property assessment was used to finalize the mill 
levy, with a slight increase from the estimated. K. Harms talked about the supplemental fund for 
the community center. D. Green asked about new chairs and tables for the community center, but 
it was decided that that will happen once the generator project price is complete.  The hearing 
started at 7:02 pm and was closed at 7:10 pm after no public comment.  
 
A motion was made by D. Green and seconded by J. Brammer to approve Resolution 2025-9 to 
Adopt 2025 Budget. All present voted in favor, motion passed.

Resolution 2025-11 
 
REOLUTION TO SET MILL LEVIES 
 
A RESOLUTION LEVYING GENERAL PROPERTY TAXES FOR THE YEAR 2026 TO HELP 
DEFRAY THE COSTS OF GOVERNMENT FOR THE TOWN OF HAXTUN, COLORADO, FOR THE 
2026 BUDGET YEAR.  
 
 WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the Town of Haxtun have adopted a budget in accordance 
with Local Government Budget Law, on December 1, 2025. 
 
 WHEREAS, the amount of money necessary to balance the budget for general operating expenses 
is $205,591 and; 
 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF 
HAXTUN, COLORADO.  
 
 Section 1. That the purpose of meeting all general operating expenses of the Town of Haxtun during 
the 2026 Budget Year, there is hereby levied a tax of 20.111 mills upon each dollar of the total valuation for 
assessment of all taxable property within the Town for the year 2026. 
 
 Section 2. That the Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to immediately certify to the County 
Commissioners of Phillips County, Colorado, the mill levies for the Town of Haxtun as herein above 
determined and set.
