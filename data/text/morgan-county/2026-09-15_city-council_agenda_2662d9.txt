Section 1. Accumulation of Junk and Rubbish and Inoperable Vehicles 
A. Purpose 
The purpose of this Section  is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, to the extent possible, the accumulation of rubbish within the County, 
which, if not eliminated or controlled, c an result in visual blight, attractive 
nuisances, incompatibility with existing land uses, hazardous or toxic substances 
being deposited in unapproved sites, attraction of disease-infested rodents and other 
vermin, fire or other hazards, and air, noise and water pollution. 
B. Applicability 
1. This Section shall apply to all land within unincorporated Morgan County, 
including all individual properties and any public right of way immediately 
adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caused by the property 
owner or occupant or originated from the property, except agricultural land 
currently in use as agricultural land as that term is defined in C.R.S. § 39 -
1-102(1.6).   
2. This Section does not apply to identified and operating junk yards currently 
allowed pursuant to the County’s Zoning Regulations. 
C. Definitions 
1. Agricultural land means any parcel of land presently classified as 
agricultural use by the Morgan County Assessor’s Office, as defined by and 
subject to the restrictions contained in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6). 
2. Inoperable vehicle means any vehicle in which the engine, wheels or other 
essential parts have been removed, altered, damaged or allowed to 
deteriorate so that the vehicle cannot be driven, or a vehicle which is not 
currently licensed or registered or otherwise may not be legally operated on 
highways of the State. 
3. Junk and Rubbish means any trash, garbage, litter, refuse, debris, lawn or 
garden waste, newspapers, magazines, rubber, glass, scrap metal or alloys, 
used lumber or crates, used pipe or pipe fittings, bottles, plastic or paper 
containers or packaging, rags or cloth, remna nt construction or demolition 
materials, vehicle parts, derelict vehicles, abandoned or junk equipment, 
used tires, used appliances or furniture, oil, carcasses of dead animals, or 
any object otherwise likely to injure any person or create a traffic hazard, or 
any other object which has been discarded or abandoned or is or obsolete as 
to make it unusable in its existing condition. 
4. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
3  
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record, and any occupant 
or tenant of the premises or property. 
D. Rubbish Containers  
Rubbish containers shall be provided by the owner of the premises.  Rubbish 
containers shall be of a kind suitable to contain all trash and garbage collected and 
shall have a tight-fitting lid.  Collection of garbage in plastic garbage bags of twenty 
(20) gallon capacity or larger is allowed during the thirty (30) day accumulation 
period prior to collection by a licensed waste hauler and/or disposal at the County 
landfill. 
E. Inoperable Vehicles 
A maximum of one (1) inoperative vehicle per parcel is allowed in residential, 
manufactured home and agriculture (parcels twenty ( 20) acres or less) zones.  A 
maximum of three (3) inoperative vehicles is allowed in the Agriculture Zone 
(parcels larger than twenty (20) acres), A/B, C, LI and HI zones unless otherwise 
allowed or restricted by a conditional or special use permit approved and issued by 
the County.  Vehicles in excess of the limitations in this subsection are not allowed 
unless they are stored in a private enclosed garage or storage shed and fully 
screened so as to prevent such vehicles from being viewed from public property, 
including any public road.  Vehicles in excess of the allowed numbers, which are 
unscreened, may be allowed if the Planning Administrator determines that they are 
economically necessary for a bona fide farming or ranching operation and the 
Planning Administrator may limit the number of unscreened inoperable vehicles in 
excess of the limitations contained in this subsection. 
F. Unlawful Acts 
1. It is unlawful for any owner to cause or permit junk or rubbish to be left or 
accumulated in or upon any yard, garden, lawn, outbuilding or premises 
upon any parcel or property, or any public right of way immediately 
adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caused by the owner 
or originated from the property  in the County unless otherwise specified 
herein. 
2. It is unlawful for any person to discard or abandon any junk or rubbish upon 
premises not owned or occupied by the person without the consent of the 
owner thereof, and the junk and rubbish deposited without consent shall be 
deemed to have been discarded and abandoned. 
3. It is unlawful for any owner to maintain inoperable vehicles on any property 
in excess of the allowed numbers contained in this Ordinance.  
Section 2. Accumulation of Weeds and Brush 
A. Purpose 
4  
The purpose of this  Section is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, to the extent possible, the growth and proliferation of weeds and brush 
within the County, which, if not elimina ted or controlled, can result in negative 
visual impact, incompatibility with existing land uses, fire and traffic hazards, the 
clogging of drainage ways, obstructions of public access and other detrimental 
health and safety impacts. 
B. Applicability 
The Section shall apply to land within unincorporated Morgan County, including 
all individual properties and any public right of way immediately adjacent to the 
property to the extent the condition is caused by the property owner or occupant or 
originated from the property , except agricultural land currently in use as 
agricultural land as that term is defined in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6). 
C. Definitions 
1. Agricultural land means any parcel of land presently classified as 
agricultural use by the Morgan County Assessor’s Office, as defined by and 
subject to the restrictions contained in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6). 
2. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record, and any occupant 
of the premises or property. 
3. Weeds and brush means: 
a. Any plant or vegetation which: 
i. Ordinarily grows without cultivation; 
ii. Is not growth for the purpose of landscaping or food 
production; and 
iii. Attains a growth of six (6) inches in or more in height, or 
grows or accumulates so as to become a fire, traffic, 
pedestrian, or other public health or safety hazard; or 
b. Any plant which has been designated as a noxious weed by the 
Board of County Commissioners, including without limitation: 
i. Leafy Spurge (Euphorbia esula L.); 
ii. Russian Knapweed (Centaurea repens L.); 
iii. Spotted Knapweed (Centaurea maculosa Lam.); 
iv. Diffuse Knapweed (Centaurea diffusa Lam.); 
v. Canada Thistle (Cirsium arvense [L.] Scop.); 
vi. Musk Thistle (Carduus nutans L.); 
vii. Field Bindweed (Convolvulus arvensis L.); 
5  
viii. Volunteer Rye (Secale cereale L.); and 
ix. Jointed Goatgrass (Aegilops Cyindrica Host.). 
D. Compliance 
All owners shall take any necessary action to suppress weeds and brush to comply 
with this Ordinance.  
E. Unlawful Acts 
It is unlawful for any owner to allow the accumulation and/or outgrowth of weeds 
and brush in violation of this Ordinance  on property under such owner’s control, 
possession or ownership, or upon any public right of way immediately adjacent to 
the property to the extent the condition is caused by the owner or originated from 
the property unless otherwise specified herein.   
Section 3. Unsafe Buildings and Structures 
A. Purpose 
The purpose of this Section  is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, the existence of unsafe or abandoned buildings which, if not repaired, 
secured, or removed, can result in fire hazards, attractive nuisances, rodent 
infestations, create an uninhabitable environment for occupants, that becomes a 
place used by trespassers, and other detrimental health and safety impacts. 
B. Applicability 
The Section shall apply to land within unincorporated Morgan County, except for 
buildings or structures located on affected land as that term is defined in the 
Colorado Mined Land Reclamation Act, C.R.S. § 34-32-103(1.5) and C.R.S. § 34-
32.5-103(1), and lands subject to the Colorado Surface Coal Mining Reclamation 
Act, C.R.S. § 34-33-101, et seq. 
C. Definitions 
1. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record. 
2. Unsafe building or structure means: 
a. Any building or structure where the condition of which presents a 
substantial danger or hazard to public health, safety, or welfare; or 
b. Any building which is materially dilapidated and unused by the 
owner or uninhabited because of deterioration or decay; and 
6  
i. Which condition constitutes a substantial fire hazard; or 
ii. Which condition subjects adjoining property owners to 
danger of damage by storm, soil erosion, or rodent 
infestation; or 
iii. Which becomes a place frequented by trespassers or 
transients seeking a temporary hideout or shelter. 
D. Compliance 
All property owners shall take all necessary actions, including but not limited to, 
maintaining, repairing, securing and demolishing any building or structure which 
violates this Ordinance.  
E. Unlawful Acts 
It is unlawful for any owner to maintain, permit, or fail to repair, secure or remove 
any unsafe building or structure on their property.   
Section 4. Enforcement 
A. Notice of Violation  
1. If probable cause exists to believe that a violation of this  Ordinance has 
occurred, a notice of violation shall be personally served and sent via first -
class mail, return receipt requested, to the property owner's mailing address 
as listed in the records of the Morgan County Assessor's Office and to the 
mailing address of the property in question, if one exists  and is different 
than the property owner's mailing address .  If personal service is not 
successful, the notice of violation may be posted on the property.  
2. Any owner shall have a total of ten (10) days upon the date of the notice of 
violation to remedy and abate the violation.  This time may be reduced to 
as few as three (3) days if the accumulation presents an immediate danger 
to health or safety of the public. 
3. The notice of violation shall refer to the section or sections of the Ordinance 
that are alleged to be violated, the facts that constitute the violation, and 
shall specify the remedies available to the County in cases where the owner 
fails or refuses to remedy and abate the violation before the time specified 
in the notice, including any possible liens due to abatement costs. 
B. Administrative Entry and Seizure Warrant 
Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section 
following notice pursuant to subsection A of this Section and the time allowed for 
remedy and abatement of the violation by the owner has passed, in event of failure 
7  
or refusal of any owner to remedy and abate the violation as described in the notice 
of violation, the County may seek an administrative entry and seizure warrant from 
the Morgan County Court or District Court. The application for an administrative 
entry and seizure warrant shall consist of: 
1. A copy of the written notice to the property owner, including evidence that 
the owner received such notice, such as a post office return receipt or an 
affidavit of personal service; 
2. Evidence that the owner has not complied with the notice; 
3. A copy of this Ordinance; and 
4. A sworn or affirmed affidavit stating: 
a. The factual basis for the warrant; 
b. A description of the location of the property; 
c. A general list of the actions necessary to remedy and abate the 
violation; 
d. As applicable,  a general list of any junk, rubbish or inoperable 
vehicles to be removed from the property; and 
e. The proposed disposal or temporary impoundment o f any items 
removed from the property, as applicable. 
Within thirty (30) days following the date of the issuance of the administrative entry 
and seizure warrant, the warrant shall be executed in accordance with the issuing 
court’s directions, and a copy of the issued warrant shall be provided or mailed to 
the owner.  Following execution of the warrant, proof of the execution shall be 
submitted to the issuing court.  Such proof shall include a written inventory of any 
property impounded. 
C. Imposition of Fines 
Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section, County 
code enforcement officers shall have the authority to issue citations or summonses 
and complaints for the purpose of enforcing this Ordinance .  Any person who 
violates this Ordinance commits a civil infraction, and, upon conviction thereof, 
shall be punished by a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars ($250) per day for the 
first action leading to conviction, five hundred dollars ($500) per day for the second 
action leading to conviction, and one thousand dollars ($1,000) per day for the third 
action leading to conviction and all subsequent actions.  For purposes of calculating 
the fine, each day the violation exists shall constitute a separate offense.  This 
graduated fine schedule shall apply to repeat offenses committed by the same owner 
with respect to the same lot, parcel, or tract of land. 
8  
In addition to the fine prescribed by this Section, persons convicted of a violation 
of this Ordinance are subject to a surcharge in an amount fixed by statute that shall 
be paid to the clerk of the court by the defendant, as provided by C.R.S. § 30- 15-
402(2)(a). 
D. Civil Penalty and Abatement Order 
1. Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section, 
following notice pursuant to s ubsection A of this Section and the time 
allowed for remedy and abatement of the violation by the owner has passed, 
in event of failure or refusal of any owner to remedy and abate the violation 
as described in the notice of violation , the County shall have the authority 
to request from the Morgan County Court or District Court an order 
imposing a civil penalty.  The penalty shall be not less than one hundred 
dollars ($100) or more than two thousand six hundred fifty dollars ($2,650) 
per day of the violation, subject to the conditions and presumptive 
maximums set forth in C.R.S. § 30 -15-401(2)(d)(III).  Each day that a 
violation occurs is deemed a separate offense and the penalty continues until 
the violation is remedied.   No  civil penalty shall be imposed against any 
owner which is a State agency. 
Until paid, any civil penalty imposed shall be recorded with the Morgan 
County Clerk and Recorder as a lien against the real property.  If the 
assessment is not paid within thirty (30) days, said assessment, together 
with ten percent (10%) added for the cost of collection, shall be certified by 
the County Clerk to the Morgan County Treasurer for collection. 
2. In addition to any civil penalties ordered by a court, the County shall have 
the authority to bring an action in the Morgan County Court or District 
Court for an order enjoining the violation, ordering the violation's restraint, 
removal, termination, or abatement by the owner, and authorizing the 
abatement by the County or its agent.  In bringing said action, the County 
Attorney shall file a verified complaint.  The County Attorney shall serve 
the hearing summons and complaint on the owner in accordance wit h the 
applicable rules of civil procedure and pursuant to C.R.S. § 30- 15-
401(2)(d)(IV) at least ten (10) days prior to the hearing. 
3. If the County abates the violation pursuant to an order of the court as set 
forth in this subsection and subsection B, the County has authority to impose 
the entire cost of abatement, plus ten percent (10%) for inspection and 
expense will be assessed against said real property in violation of this 
Section.  The assessment is a lien against the real property until paid.  If the 
assessment is not paid within thirty (30) days, said assessment, together 
with ten percent (10%) added for the cost of collection, shall be certified by 
the County Clerk to the Morgan County Treasurer for collection. 
Section 5. Additional Remedies.  The remedies provided in this Ordinance  shall be 
9  
cumulative and in addition to any other federal, state or local remedy, criminal or civil, which may 
be available.  Nothing contained herein shall be construed to preclude prosecution under any other 
applicable statute, ordinance, rule, order or regulation.

declared to be invalid.

Section 2. Prohibition on Accumulation of Weeds and Brush 
A. Purpose 
The purpose of this Section is  to protect the public hea lth, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan  County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, to the extent possible, the growth and proliferation of weeds and brush 
within the County, which, if not eliminated  or controlled, can result in negative 
visual impact, incompatibility with existi ng land uses, fire and traffic hazards, the 
clogging of drainage ways, obstructions of public access and other detrimental 
health and safety impacts. 
B. Applicability 
The Section shall apply to land within  unincorporated Morgan County, including 
all individual properties and any public right of way immediately adjacent to the 
property to the extent the condition is caused by the property owner or occupant or 
originated from the propertyadjacent alleys or rights-of-way , except agricultural 
land currently in use as agricultural land as that term is defined in C.R.S. § 39-1-
102(1.6). 
C. Definitions 
1. Agricultural land  means any parcel of land presently classified as 
agricultural use by the Morgan County Assessor’s Office, as defined by and

corporation, governmental agency, or other associat ion of persons, or any

a. Any plant or vegetation which:

approved the  creation of  a home rule charter commission and elect ed eleven  charter 
commissioners;  
 
 WHEREAS, through public meetings conducted in April, May, June, July and August, the 
Morgan County Home Rule Charter Commission reviewed the structure of the government of 
Morgan County and determine d the ways in which government operations may be improved or 
reorganized;  
 
 WHEREAS, the Home Rule Charter Commission held three public hea rings as required 
by and in accordance with C.R.S. § 30-11-504(1);  
 
 WHEREAS, the Home Rule Charter Commission unanimously voted in favor of the 
proposed home rule charter at its meeting on August 27, 2026, and forwarded the proposed home 
rule charter to the Board of County Commissioners;  
 
 WHEREAS, on September 1, 2026, the Board of County Commissioners, upon receipt of 
the proposed home rule charter, called for a special election to refer to the registered electors the 
question of the adoption of the proposed home rule charter at the November 3, 2026 election;  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 1- 45-117(1)(b)(III)(A), the Board of County 
Commissioners is authorized to pass a resolution taking a position of advocacy on matters of this 
nature; 
 
 WHEREAS, the Colorado Constitution provides the authority for the voters of a Colorado 
county to choose to become a home rule county;  
 
 WHEREAS, becoming home rule provides the citizens of Morgan County  with 
opportunities to exercise greater local control and a me chanism to oppose state laws and actions 
which do not align with the values of the citizens of Morgan County;  
 
WHEREAS, home rule will allow the Board of County Commissioners to address the 
needs of Morgan County based on specific community circumstances  rather remain subject to  
uniform regulations passed by the Colorado legislature; 
 
 WHEREAS, the proposed home rule  charter retains the structure of Morgan County 
government with the exception of the County Surveyor which will become an appointed position, 
 2 
and will allow the voters of Morgan County to determine  and control the structure of County 
government in the future;  
 
 WHEREAS, the County's Right to Farm Policy is integrated into the proposed home rule 
charter along with the preservation and protection of agricultural activities and uses which are a 
key component of the Morgan County economy and history;  
 
 WHEREAS, the proposed home rule charter preserves the protections provided to citizens 
of Morgan County under the Taxpayer Bill of Rights ("TABOR") even if TABOR is repealed by 
Colorado voters;  
 
 WHEREAS, the proposed home rule charter expands the County's legislative process by 
increasing opportunities for citizen engagement with Board of County Commissioner action and 
granting voters the rights to initiative and referendum which County voters do not currently have;  
 
 WHEREAS, the proposed charter recognizes the value of water rights in Morgan County 
to its economy and communities;  
 
 WHEREAS, the proposed charter places in the will of the voters of Morgan County at the 
forefront and provides a tool for local self-government to meet the needs and desires of the citizens 
of Morgan County; and 
 
  WHEREAS, home rule would provide  for the decisions affecting Morgan County  to be 
made by the people who live here.

2-502 – Continuing Jurisdiction 
The County retains continuing jurisdiction over any permit granted under these Zoning Regula tions to 
ensure compliance with the permits, any conditions and the Zoning Regulations.  Approvals are conditioned 
on compliance with all information and representation contained in the applications and presented during 
any public hearings.  If any representations or information presented by the applicant or owner during the 
application process are found to be erroneous, lacking a factual basis or otherwise inaccurate, the County 
may institute enforcement proceedings, including issuing an order to show cause to the applicant or owner.  
If there is conflict regarding the interpretation of a condition, the Board may, subject to notice and hearing, 
amend, add, or remove any conditions.  
6-108 Enforcement Procedures  
This Section sets forth the enforcement options available and the presumed progression of enforcement 
actions that may be taken by th e County to address violations. This Section sets forth the processes and  
penalties for violations of th ese Regulations, permits issued under these Regulations, and any agreement 
which a condition and requirement of a permit i ssued under these Regulations.   The following remedies 
for enforcement shall be cumulative and shall in no way limit the County's authority to seek any other 
remedy available at law for violations of these Regulations. Nothing in this Section shall prevent the County 
from exercising di scretion to pursue the remedies or any combination thereof, as set forth below or as 
provided by Colorado law. 
(A) Notice of Violation  
(1) If probable causecompetent evidence exists to believe that a violation has occurred, a notice 
of violation shall be personally served on the owner, if possible, or occupant of the property.  
The notice of violation shall also be posted on the property, if possible.  A notice shall also 
be sent via first class mail to the owner's mailing address as listed in the records of the 
Morgan County Assessor's Office and to the mailing address of the property in question, if 
different than the owner's address and if one exists.  
(2) The notice of violation shall specifically describe the nature of the violation and shall 
require that the violation be corrected at least ten (10) days after the date of the notice.  The 
notice of violation shall provide that the alleged violator may request additional time to 
correct the violation. 
(3) If the violation is not corrected within the time period established in the notice of violation 
or approved extension of time, the County may determine to pursue remedies under 
subsections (B) and/or (C) of this Section. 
(4) The notice described in this Section is not required for violations of permits issued pursuant 
to these Regulations, including violation of any condition or agreement as a result of the 
issuance of a permit.  However, the County may issue a notice of violation pursuant to this 
Section for a violation of a permit or permit condition.  
(B) Revocation of Approvals and PermitsRemedies 
(1) Revocation of Permits, Amendment of Conditions, and Administrative Penalties.  
(a) If an alleged violator is in violation of the conditions of a land use approval or 
permit that was issued by the County or if the approval or the permit was obtained 
by false representation or issued in error, the Board may revoke the approval or 
permit, and/or amend conditions imposed on the approval or permit that are related 
to the violation or impose administrative penalties.  Amendment of the terms and 
conditions shall include the addition of new conditions and the removal of 
conditions.  Nothing herein shall be deemed to limit the County's authority to 
impose any combination of the remedies in this Section.  
(b) The Pla nning Administrator shall first consider the alleged violation(s) and

(c) The Board shall conduct the show cause hearing, and the violator and/or owner 
may present evidence as to why the County should not revoke the land use 
approval or permit; or amend the conditions on the approval or permit; or impose 
administrative penalties .  The Board may also hear from individuals directly 
aggrieved by the alleged violation.  No other individuals have the right to be heard 
at the hearing.  The Board shall  determine whether to revoke the permit ,  and/or 
amend conditions or  impose administrative penalties  within fourteen (14) days 
after the conclusion of the hearing.  The Board shall notify the alleged violator 
and/or owner in writing of its decision.  
(d) Administrative Penalties.  Administrative penalties may not exceed Two Thousand 
Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500) per violation per day the violatio n occurs and 
continues.  When imposing administrative penalties, the Board shall take into 
consideration the following factors: 
(i) the impact of the violation on public health, safety, and welfare; 
(ii) the impact of the violation on neighboring properties or public property; 
(iii) the violator's willingness to comply with or demonstrated disregard for the 
applicable laws; 
(iv) the violator's ability to comply with the applicable laws; and 
(v) whether multiple or prior violations have occurred.
