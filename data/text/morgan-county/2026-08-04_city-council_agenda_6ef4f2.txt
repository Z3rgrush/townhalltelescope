ROAD USE AGREEMENT 
 
THIS ROAD USE AGREEMENT (hereinafter "Agreement") is  made this _____day of 
_______, 20      ("Effective Date"), by and between Morgan County, Colorado, whose address is 218 
W. Kiowa Avenue, Fort Morgan, Colorado 80701 (hereinafter "County") and [ Permittee], whose 
address is [Address], its successors and assigns (the "Permittee"). 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, Permittee applied for and obtained a [permit] to construct ("Permit") and operate 
a [use] in Morgan County (the "Project");  
 
WHEREAS, Permittee’s agents, employees, affiliates, contractors, subcontractors, workforce 
and related service companies may utilize equipment and heavy vehicles on Morgan County roads and 
Appurtenances in or around such roads (collectively, the “County Roads”), as identified in Exhibit A, 
in connection with development of the Project; 
 
WHEREAS, Permittee’s use of County Roads may cause impacts which require mitigation and 
repair to ensure the public's continued ability to use County Roads; and  
 
WHEREAS, as a condition of Permit, the County requires Permittee  to enter into this  
Agreement to mitigate the impact on the County Roads due to the Project. 
 
NOW THEREFORE , in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions contained 
herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the County and Permittee agree as follows: 
 
1. Purpose of Agreement.  The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms and conditions 
under which Permittee will pay for the cost of repair ing any damage arising from the use of 
County Roads by Permittee and any contractors or subcontractors of Permittee  (“Contracting 
Companies”).  Permittee has indicated that it intends to use approximately ____ miles of 
County Roads, identified on Exhibit A, of which approximately _______ miles are asphalt-
paved and________ miles are gravel surfaced.  This Agreement is intended to mitigate the 
damage to County Roads , minimize interruptions to the traveling public , and compensate the 
County for the costs of repairing any damage to the County Roads and any increased 
maintenance costs resulting from the Project. 
 
2. Definitions. 
 
A. Appurtenance means a ditch, culvert, or any type of wall, fence, guardrail, pavement 
marking, traffic control device, illumination device, mailbox or barrier adjacent to or 
in, along or on a road, or any construction, obstruction, erection or any situation, 
arrangement or disposition of any earth, rock, tree or other material or thing adjacent 
to or in, along or on a road that is not on the traveled portion of the road. 
 
B. County Roads means a roadway under the direction, control and management of the 
County, including: 
 
 
2  
i. A developed road on which improvements such as grading or surfacing have 
been made for the purpose of public access and included any Appurtenances, 
and includes a bridge forming part of a public road and any structure incidental 
to a public road; and/or 
 
ii. An undeveloped road or right of way under the County's jurisdiction. 
 
C. Estimated Costs are included as part of Exhibit C and are the cost of any potential road 
repairs and/or post construction restoration based upon the condition of the road and 
the anticipated traffic, including volume and weight of transport vehicles.  The cost 
estimate must be signed by a Colorado licensed engineer.  
 
D. Mitigation Plan means a plan, approved by the County Planning and Public Works 
Departments, detailing County Roads will be used for Project traffic  as approved in 
Exhibit A, the route of Project traffic, a schedule of when County Roads will be used 
(including daily hours of use), and plans for how impacts to the County Roads, 
neighboring properties, and traffic will be minimized.  The Mitigation Plan must also 
include detailed information regarding how the operator will ensure that the correct haul 
route is used and examples of information that will be given to trucking companies and 
truck drivers to ensure the correct haul routes are used.  The Mitigation Plan must also 
include the names of all trucking companies to be used during construction.   The draft 
Mitigation Plan does not need to include the Estimated Costs.  
 
3. Pre-Construction Requirements .  Once the haul route, designated in Exhibit A to this 
Agreement and a draft Mitigation Plan , has been approved by the Board of County 
Commissioners, Permittee must submit the following to the County Public Works Department 
prior to obtaining a construction permit: 
 
A. A pre-construction baseline survey of County Roads in a form required by the Public 
Works Department. Upon approval by the Public Works Director, the pre-construction 
baseline survey shall become Exhibit B to this Agreement.  
 
B. A final Mitigation Plan including but not limited to Estimated Costs as described herein. 
The Public Works Director may request review by any third- party consultant of the 
estimated costs.  Upon approval by the Planning Administrator and Public Works 
Director, the Mitigation Plan shall become Exhibit C to this Agreement.  
 
C. Submission of the security described in Section 5. 
 
4. Obligations During Construction. 
 
A. During construction, the operator must designate a person to be the point of contact for 
the County for communication.  Contact information, including but not limited to, a 
phone number where the individual may be reached at any time, must be provided prior 
to the issuance of a construction permit and updated, if necessary, throughout the 
project.  All calls from the County must be returned within two (2) hours.  
 
B. The list of trucking companies in the Mitigation Plan must be kept updated throughout 
 
3  
the period of construction.  Notification of a new trucking company must be provided 
to the Public Works Director and the Planning Administrator prior to that company 
hauling any materials or products to the project site. 
 
5. Repair and Maintenance Obligations. 
 
A. Permittee and Contracting Companies shall comply with the Mitigation Plan at all 
times.  Failure to comply with the Mitigation Plan shall be grounds for the County to 
suspend the Permit until such time as the Permittee provides sufficient assurances to 
the County that Permittee will comply with the Mitigation Plan. 
 
B. Permittee shall be responsible for all costs and expenses required to restore County 
Roads used by Permittee or its Contracting Companies to conduct operations for the 
Project.  This obligation shall require Permittee to restore roads to the same condition 
as shown in the pre-construction baseline survey. 
 
C. During the time when Permittee , or any of its Contracting Companies, are  engaged 
in the use of a County Road for access to conduct operations for the Project, Permittee 
shall be responsible for road damage resulting from such use, including but not 
limited to Examples of Road Damage (defined below) in this  Agreement, to keep 
County Roads in safe condition for the public.  
 
D. All restoration of County Roads shall be completed within _________ months of the 
completion of the installation of the Project.  
 
6. Security.  
 
A. To secure the maintenance and restoration of the County Roads, Permittee shall furnish 
the County, at Permittee's expense and prior to commencement of installation and 
construction of the Project described herein, cash or an irrevocable letter (or letters) of 
credit in which the County is designated as beneficiary in an amount equal to one 
hundred fifteen percent (115%) of the Estimated Costs  of maintenance and restoration 
of the County Roads as described in this Agreement (the "Performance Guarantee").  
 
B. The Performance Guarantee shall be in a form approved by the County in its sole 
discretion.   
 
C. The purpose of the Estimated Costs is solely to determine the amount of security.  No 
representations are made as to the accuracy of these estimates, and Permittee agrees to 
pay all costs of the maintenance and restoration for which it is legally obligated, 
regardless of the Estimated Costs.   
 
D. The Estimated Costs may increase in the future.  Accordingly, the County reserves the 
right to review and adjust the Estimated Costs at any time, prior to the restoration of the 
County Roads.  Adjustments shall be made according to changes in the Construct ion 
Costs Index as published by the Engineering News Record.  If the County adjusts the 
Estimated Costs, the County shall give written notice to Permittee . Permittee shall, 
within thirty (30) days after receipt of said written notice, provide the County wi th a 
 
4  
new or amended Performance Guarantee in the amount of the adjusted Estimated Costs.  
If Permittee fails to provide a new or amended Performance Guarantee, the County may 
exercise the remedies provided for in Section 11 hereof. 
 
E. If the County Roads are not restored within the period of time specified by Section 4  
above, the County may draw on the Performance Guarantee to restore the County 
Roads.  If the Performance Guarantee is to expire within fourteen (14) calendar days 
and Permittee has not yet provided a satisfactory replacement, the County may draw on 
the Performance Guarantee and either hold such funds as security for performance of 
this Agreement or spend such funds to restore the County Roads. 
 
F. Upon preliminary acceptance of the County Roads, the Performance Guarantee shall be 
reduced to the amount of fifteen percent (15%) of the total actual cost of restoration of 
the County Roads.  The reduced Performance Guarantee shall be held by the County 
until expiration of the two (2) year warranty period.   
 
G. If no restoration is required at the time of end of the warranty period, the Performance 
Guarantee shall be released by the County.  
 
H. The County may draw upon the Performance Guarantee to recoup unpaid administrative 
penalties imposed by the Board of County Commissioners.  
 
7. Post-Construction Survey and Inspections.    
 
A. Upon completion of the construction of the Project, Permittee  shall submit a post -
construction survey in the same form as the pre-construction baseline survey.  
 
B. Inspections of County Roads identified in this Agreement may be carried out after the 
post-construction survey  is completed  and submitted to the County and then again, 
following the restoration of the County Roads .  Inspections will be conducted by  
designees of both the County and Permittee at a time set by the County or the County's 
official designee if so agreed to by Permittee. 
 
8. Road Damage. 
 
A. Examples of Road Damage include but are not limited to the following: 
 
i. Potholes or wheel -depressed areas after the roads have been maintained or 
graded. 
 
ii. Damage to shoulders due to heavy vehicles running off the edge of the road. 
 
iii. Damage to ditches due to heavy vehicles squeezing the ditches closed by 
running on the shoulders of the road. 
 
iv. Damage to culverts crushed by heavy hauling activities or being "plugged" by 
sediment from closed ditches. 
 
 
5  
v. Damage to road surface causing the re-cycling of the surface for the purpose of 
proper roadway drainage. 
 
vi. Damage to the existing base by heavy traffic. 
 
vii. Damage to the profile of the road and the loss of aggregate on the driving 
surface. 
 
9. Emergencies.  The County may, in emergency situations, and acting reasonably, and without 
giving any notice to Permittee as required elsewhere in this Agreement, take immediate and all 
action necessary to complete repairs to County Roads that the County deems necessary for 
public safety.

NOW THEREFORE , in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions contained 
herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the County and Permittee agree as follows: 
 
1. Purpose of Agreement.  The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms and conditions 
under which Permittee will pay for the cost of repairing any damage arising from the use of 
County Roads by Permittee and any contractors or  subcontractors of Permittee (“Contracting 
Companies”).  Permittee has indicated that it  intends to use approxi mately ____ miles of 
County Roads, identified on Exhibit A, of  which approximately _______ miles are asphalt-
paved and________ miles are gravel surfaced.  This  Agreement is intended to mitigate the 
damage to County Roads, minimiz e interruptions to the traveling public, and compensate the 
County for the costs of repairing any dama ge to the County Roads and any increased 
maintenance costs resulting from the Project. 
 
2. Definitions. 
 
A. Appurtenance means a ditch, culvert, or any type of wa ll, fence, guardrail, pavement 
marking, traffic control device, illumination device, mailbox or barrier adjacent to or 
in, along or on a road, or any construction, obstruction, erection or an y situation, 
arrangement or disposition of any earth, rock, tree or other material or thing adjacent 
to or in, along or on a road that is not on the traveled portion of the road. 
 
B. County Roads means a roadway under the dire ction, control and management of the 
County, including: 
 
 
2  
i. A developed road on which improvements such as grading or  surfacing have 
been made for the purpose of public access and included any Appurtenances, 
and includes a bridge forming part of a public road and any structure incidental 
to a public road; and/or 
 
ii. An undeveloped road or right of way under the County's jurisdiction. 
 
C. Estimated Costs are included as part of Exhibit C and are the cost of any potential road 
repairs and/or post construction restorati on based upon the conditi on of the road and 
the anticipated traffic, including volume and weight of transport vehicles.  The cost 
estimate must be signed by a Colorado licensed engineer.  
 
D. Mitigation Plan means a plan, approved by the County Planning and Public Works 
Departments, detailing County Roads will be used for Project traffic as approved in 
Exhibit A, the route of Projec t traffic, a schedule of when  County Roads will be used 
(including daily hours of us e), and plans for how impac ts to the County Roads, 
neighboring properties, and tr affic will be minimized.  The Mitigation Plan must also 
include detailed information regarding how the operator will ensure that the correct haul 
route is used and examples of information that will be given to trucking companies and 
truck drivers to ensure the correct haul routes are used.  The Mitigation Plan must also 
include the names of all trucking companies to be used during construction.  The draft 
Mitigation Plan does not need to include the Estimated Costs.  
 
3. Pre-Construction Requirements.   Once the haul route, desi gnated in Exhibit A to this 
Agreement and a draft Mitigation Plan , has been approved by the Board of County 
Commissioners, Permittee must submit the following to the County Public Works Department 
prior to obtaining a construction permit: 
 
A. A pre-construction baseline survey of Count y Roads in a form required by the Public 
Works Department. Upon approval by the Public Works Director, the pre-construction 
baseline survey shall become Exhibit B to this Agreement.  
 
B. A final Mitigation Plan including but not limited to Estimated Costs as described herein. 
The Public Works Director may request revi ew by any third-party consultant of the 
estimated costs.  Upon approval by the Planning Administrator and Public Works 
Director, the Mitigation Plan shall become Exhibit C to this Agreement.  
 
C. Submission of the security described in Section 5. 
 
4. Obligations During Construction. 
 
A. During construction, the operator must designate a person to be the point of contact for 
the County for communication.  Contact informa tion, including but not limited to, a 
phone number where the individual may be reached at any time, must be provided prior 
to the issuance of a construction permit and updated, if necessary, throughout the 
project.  All calls from the County must be returned within two (2) hours.  
 
B. The list of trucking companies in the Mitigation Plan must be kept updated throughout 
 
3  
the period of construction.  Notification of  a new trucking company must be provided 
to the Public Works Director and the Planning Administrator prior to that company 
hauling any materials or products to the project site. 
 
4.5. Repair and Maintenance Obligations. 
 
A. Permittee and Contracting Companies shall comply with the Mitigation Plan at all 
times.  Failure to comply with the Mitigation Plan shall be grounds for the County to 
suspend the Permit until such time as the Permittee provides sufficient assurances to 
the County that Permittee will comply with the Mitigation Plan. 
 
B. Permittee shall be responsibl e for all costs and expenses required to restore County 
Roads used by Permittee or its Contracting Companies to conduct operations for the 
Project.  This obligation shall require Permittee to restore roads to the same condition 
as shown in the pre-construction baseline survey. 
 
C. During the time when Permittee, or any of  its Contracting Companies, are engaged 
in the use of a County Road for access to conduct operations for the Project, Permittee 
shall be responsible for road damage re sulting from such use, including but not 
limited to Examples of Road Damage (defin ed below) in this Agreement, to keep 
County Roads in safe condition for the public.  
 
D. All restoration of County Roads shall be completed within _________ months of the 
completion of the installation of the Project.  
 
5.6. Security.  
 
A. To secure the maintenance and restoration of the County Roads, Permittee shall furnish 
the County, at Permittee's expense and pr ior to commencement of installation and 
construction of the Project described herein, cash or an irrevocable letter (or letters) of 
credit in which the County is designated as beneficiary in an  amount equal to one 
hundred fifteen percent (115%) of the Estimated Costs of maintenance and restoration 
of the County Roads as described in this Agreement (the "Performance Guarantee").  
 
B. The Performance Guarantee shall be in a form approved by the County in its sole 
discretion.   
 
C. The purpose of the Estimated Costs is solely to determine the amount of security.  No 
representations are made as to the accuracy of these estimates, and Permittee agrees to 
pay all costs of the maintenance and rest oration for which it is legally obligated, 
regardless of the Estimated Costs.   
 
D. The Estimated Costs may increase in the future.  Accordingly, the County reserves the 
right to review and adjust the Estimated Costs at any time, prior to the restoration of the 
County Roads.  Adjustments sh all be made according to changes in th e Construction 
Costs Index as published by the Engineering News Record.  If th e County adjusts the 
Estimated Costs, the County shall give writ ten notice to Permitte e. Permittee shall, 
within thirty (30) days afte r receipt of said written notic e, provide the County with a 
 
4  
new or amended Performance Guarantee in the amount of the adjusted Estimated Costs.  
If Permittee fails to provide a new or amended Performance Guarantee, the County may 
exercise the remedies provided for in Section 11 hereof. 
 
E. If the County Roads are not re stored within the period of  time specified by Section 4 
above, the County may draw on the Perform ance Guarantee to restore the County 
Roads.  If the Performance Guarantee is to e xpire within fourteen  (14) calendar days 
and Permittee has not yet provided a satisfactory replacement, the County may draw on 
the Performance Guarantee and either hold such  funds as security for performance of 
this Agreement or spend such funds to restore the County Roads. 
 
F. Upon preliminary acceptance of the County Roads, the Performance Guarantee shall be 
reduced to the amount of fifteen percent (15%) of the total actual cost of restoration of 
the County Roads.  The reduced Performan ce Guarantee shall be held by the County 
until expiration of the two (2) year warranty period.   
 
G. If no restoration is required at the time of end of the warranty period, the Performance 
Guarantee shall be released by the County.  
 
G.H. The County may draw upon the Performance Guarantee to recoup unpaid administrative 
penalties imposed by the Board of County Commissioners.  
 
6.7. Post-Construction Survey and Inspections.    
 
A. Upon completion of the construction of th e Project, Permittee shall submit a post-
construction survey in the same form as the pre-construction baseline survey.  
 
B. Inspections of County Roads identified in this Agreement may be carried out after the 
post-construction survey is completed and submitted to the County and then again, 
following the restoration of the County Roads.  Inspections will be conducted by 
designees of both the County and Permittee at a time set by the County or the County's 
official designee if so agreed to by Permittee. 
 
7.8. Road Damage. 
 
A. Examples of Road Damage include but are not limited to the following: 
 
i. Potholes or wheel-depressed areas after the roads have been maintained or 
graded. 
 
ii. Damage to shoulders due to heavy vehicles running off the edge of the road. 
 
iii. Damage to ditches due to heavy vehi cles squeezing the ditches closed by 
running on the shoulders of the road. 
 
iv. Damage to culverts crushed by heavy hauling activities or being "plugged" by 
sediment from closed ditches. 
 
 
5  
v. Damage to road surface causing the re-cycling of the surface for the purpose of 
proper roadway drainage. 
 
vi. Damage to the existing base by heavy traffic. 
 
vii. Damage to the profile of the road a nd the loss of aggregate on the driving 
surface. 
 
8.9. Emergencies.  The County may, in emergency situations, and acting reasonably, and without 
giving any notice to Permittee as required elsewhere in this Agreement, take immediate and all 
action necessary to complete repairs to Count y Roads that the County deems necessary for 
public safety. 
 
9.10. Indemnification.  Permittee shall indemnify, release and hold harmless the County and its 
officers, agents, employees, successors and assignees from any and all actions, proceedings 
causes of action, claims, demands and/or costs attributable to, whether directly and indirectly, 
damages or injuries arising out of or resulting from acts or omissions by Permittee or otherwise 
arising out of the performance  under this Agreement by Permittee, its employees, agents, 
contractors or subcontractors, or its Contracting Companies, but such indemnity shall not apply 
to the intentional acts or neg ligence of the County, its officers , agents, employees, successors 
and assignees. 
 
10.11. Assignment.  Except as otherwise provided herein, or except as may be hereafter determined 
by the parties, no party to this Agreement may sell, assign, partially assign or transfer its 
interest in this Agreement, or  any of its rights, duties or ob ligations hereunder, without the 
prior written consent of the other party.  Whenever consent or the approval of a party is required 
herein, such party shall not unreasonably withhold, delay, or deny such consent or approval. 
 
11.12. Breach.   
 
A. If Permittee breaches this Agreement, the County may take such action as permitted or 
authorized by law or this Agreement as the County deems necessary to protect the 
public health, safety and welfare.  The remedies include, but are not limited to: 
 
i. A suspension or revocation of the Permit;  
 
ii. Draw on the security to remedy the breach;  
 
ii.iii. Impose administrative penalties of up to $2,500 per breach and per day a breach 
continues; and 
 
iii.iv. Any other remedy available at law or in equity. 
 
B. Unless necessary to protect the immediate health, safety and welfare of the County, its 
citizens or its property , or to protect the interest of the County with regard to security 
given for the restoration of the County Roads, the County shall provide Permittee thirty 
(30) days'ten (10) days written notice of its intent to take any action under this Section, 
during which Permittee may cure the breach and prevent further action by the County.  
 
6  
Revocation and the imposition of administrative penalties will require a hearing before 
the Board of County Commissioners.  If a he aring is required, the notice in this 
subsection shall include the date, time, and location of the hearing.  The Permittee must 
appear before the Board of County Commissioners at the designated time and 
demonstrate why such remedies should not  be imposed.  If th e Board of County 
Commissioners imposes administrative penalties after the hearing, such penalties shall 
be paid in full within ten (10) calendar days  of the Board's decision.  Failure to timely 
pay such penalties will permit the County to draw upon the Performance Guarantee as 
described in Section 4 without further notice to the Permittee.  If necessary to protect 
the immediate health, safety and welfare of the County, its citizens or its property, or to 
protect the interest of the County with regard to security given for the restoration of the 
County Roads, the Public Works Director or Planning Administ rator may summarily 
suspend the Project pending a hearing befo re the Board under th is subsection. Such 
suspension must be provided in writing. Failure to comply with a suspension is a breach 
of this agreement.  
 
C. The rights and remedies of the County under this Agreement are in addition to any other 
rights and remedies provided by law.  The expiration of this Agreement shall in no way 
limit the County's legal or equi table remedies, or the period  in which such remedies 
may be asserted, for work negligently or defectively performed. 
 
D. Should this Agreement become the subject of litigation to resolve a claim of breach by 
Permittee and a court of competent jurisdiction determines that Permittee was in breach 
of this Agreement, Permittee shall pay the attorney fees, expenses and court costs of the 
County. 
 
12.13. Nuisance Conditions.  Permittee shall prevent the existe nce of any nuisances by way of its 
maintenance or restoration of County Roads unde r this Agreement. If the County determines 
that a nuisance exists, it shall notify Permittee that such nuisance exists.  If the nuisance is not 
abated or an abatement plan is not submitted to the satisfaction of the County, the County may, 
upon thirty (30) days' written notice, draw upon the Performance Guarantee to pay the cost of 
abating the nuisance.  The decision to draw on the Performance Guarantee shall be within the 
sole discretion of the County. 
 
13.14. Approval of Haul Route and Construction Permit.   If a construction permit has not been 
issued to the Permittee for the Project wi thin one year after the Board of County 
Commissioners' approval of the haul route, as shown in Exhibit A to this Agreement, such 
approval shall be void without further action of the Board of County Commissioners.  
 
14.15. Conditions of Approval. Nothing in this Agreement shall be deemed to modify or rescind any 
finding or condition in the resolution approving the Permit.  
 
15.16. Waivers. 
 
A. Failure by either party to this Agreement to enforce or insist upon compliance with any 
of the terms or conditions of this Agreement shall not constitute a general waiver or 
relinquishment of any such terms or conditions, but the same shall be and remain at all 
times in full force and effect.

17.18. Severability.  If any provision of this Agreement shall be held invalid, illegal or unenforceable

18.19. Venue and Jurisdiction.  This Agreement shall be interpreted in accordance with the laws of 
the State of Colorado.  Venue of any suit or cause of action under this Agreement shall lie 
exclusively in Morgan County, Colorado. 
 
19.20. Entire Agreement.  This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement and understanding 
between the parties and supersedes any prior agreement or understanding relating to the subject
