RESOLUTION 2026 BCC 33 
 
A RESOLUTION APPROVING  A USE BY SPECIAL REVIEW PERMIT 
ISSUED TO ALLOW A SMALL FARM STAND LOCATED IN A PART OF 
THE N½SE¼ OF SECTION 7, TOWNSHIP 3 NORTH, RANGE 57 WEST 
OF THE 6TH P.M., MORGAN COUNTY, COLORADO. 
 
WHEREAS, Brian Butterfield and Rikki Marie Butterfield  (the “Owners”) own a parcel 
of land located in a part of the N½SE¼ of Section 7, Township 3 North, Range 57 West of the 6th 
P.M., Morgan County, Colorado (“Property”) in the Estate Residential Zone District;  
 
 WHEREAS, Rikki Butterfield (the “Applicant”), on behalf of and with authorization of 
the Owner s, has applied for a special use permit to allow  a small farm stand in the Estate 
Residential Zone District (the “Application”) to be located on the Property;  
 
 WHEREAS, on July 13, 2026, after holding a duly noticed public hearing, the Morgan 
County Planning Commission recommended that the Application be approved; 
 
WHEREAS, on August 4, 2026, the Board of County Commissioners ( “Board”) held a 
duly noticed public hearing on the Application; 
 
 WHEREAS, during the public hearing, the Board recei ved testimony and evidence from  
Morgan County staff, the Applicant and the public; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Board desires to approve the Application.  
 
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY 
COMMISSIONERS OF MORGAN COUNTY, COLORADO: 
 
1. APPROVAL 
 
a.  The Application is hereby granted subject to the conditions and terms contained in this 
Resolution. This Resolution shall constitute the special use permit (hereinafter referred 
to as this “Resolution” or the “Permit”).   
 
2. FINDINGS OF FACT 
 
The Board, having reviewed the application, all information provided, and testimony heard, 
finds that:

a. The use and its location as proposed are in conformance with the Morgan County 
Comprehensive Plan. Specifically, in this area Comprehensive Plan goals include: 
Preserve existing agricultural lands that grow food for the community, and support the 
efficient expansion of productive agricultural uses, farms, ranches and facilities by 
protecting prime agricultural areas from impacts of developments and commercial 
recreation by upholding farmers’ and ranchers’ rights to utilize their land for 
agricultural uses. Although the property is not zoned agricultural, the Comprehensive 
Plan encourages the use. 
 
b. The application documents are complete and present a clear picture of how the use is 
to be arranged on the site. 
 
c. The requirement of a special use map was waived by the Planning Administrator. The 
site plan provided included sufficient information for the proposed use. 
 
d. All on and off-site impacts have been satisfactorily mitigated either through agreement, 
public improvements, site plan requirements or other mitigation measures . There is 
access to established public infrastructure  and operation impacts are expected to be 
minimal. 
 
e. The special use proposed has been made compatible with the surrounding uses and is 
adequately buffered as determined by the County . Adjacent uses are residential. The 
use is compatible as low -impact secondary use on the property which contains 
residential use. 
 
f. The special use poses no or minimal risk to the public health, safety and welfare. 
 
g. The special use proposed is located on a conforming parcel. 
 
h. The Applicant represents that a small family-operated farm stand will benefit the public 
by offering locally grown and handmade products to the community . All pertinent 
technical information, adequate financial resources to implement it, and has paid all 
fees and review costs levied by the County for application processing and review. 
 
i. Water is not required for the proposed use. 
 
3. GENERAL PROVISIONS. 
 
a. The Board of County Commissioners retains continuing jurisdiction over this Permit 
to ensure compliance with this Permit and the Morgan County Zoning Regulations. 
County Representatives are authorized to inspect the Property at any reasonable time 
upon notice to the Applicant.  This approval is conditioned on compliance with all 
information and representations contained in the Application and presented by the 
Owners, which are incorporated into this Permit. If any representations or information

Building Authorization Pilot Program 
 
I. Purpose. To incentive economic development within the Innovation Zone, the Board adopts the 
Building Authorization Pilot Program to streamline building permit process. The Board adopts this 
program pursuant to C.R.S. §§ 30-11-101, 30-11-107 and 30-28-201, and the applicable building 
code.  
 
II. Applicability. This program is only available for buildings to be constructed on property within 
the Innovation Zone. No applications for this program will be accepted until the property is zoned for 
the Innovation Zone.  
 
III. Procedure.  
 
A. With a complete application for a building permit, the applicant shall submit a complete 
sealed certification of code conformance by a Colorado-licensed architect or engineer. 
Detailed building plans must be supplied with the building permit and the certification 
from the architect or engineer must be based upon these plans.  
 
B. Except as provided herein, upon submission of a complete application, the building 
official shall review and confirm completeness and issue the permit no event later than 
ten (10) business days from receipt. If the application shall deficiencies, the building 
official shall notify the applicant within ten (10) business days. If the building official 
does not issue the permit or deliver written notice of a specific deficiencies within ten 
(10) business days, the permit is deemed issued.  
 
C. Exception as provided herein, the certification is the basis for the building official’s 
conformance determination under C.R.S. § 30-28-205. The issuance of the building 
permit is subject to the official’s retained authority to require full review for high-risk 
(Group H) category of buildings and to audit, inspect, and order correction or stop work.  
 
D. Notwithstanding the certification, the County building official retains authority to review 
and inspect certain health/safety elements of the applicable building code (e.g., 
sprinklers, ingress, and egress). The initial review of these elements shall occur during 
the review of the building permit application and is subject to the timeframe in subsection 
B above.  
 
E. Upon completion of the building, the applicant may request a certificate of occupancy 
from the County building official. The request must be accompanied by confirmation 
from the Colorado licensed architect or engineer that the building as built complies with 
the code and plans and executed indemnification and release agreements described 
herein. Upon a complete submittal, the building official will issue a certificate of 
occupancy.  
 
F. Indemnification and release agreements must be executed by the Colorado licensed 
architect or engineer certifying the plans and code compliance, the property owner, lessee 
(if applicable) and operator (if different than owner or lessee). All indemnification and 
release agreements must be in form provided by and acceptable to the County.
