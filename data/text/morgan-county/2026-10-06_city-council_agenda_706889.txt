THIS SECOND AMENDMENT TO LEASE AND EASEMENT AGREEMENT FOR 
SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEM (this “Amendment”) is made as of this 6th day of October, 2026 
(the “Effective Date”), by and between Morgan County, Colorado (“Lessor”), and Fort 
Morgan Solar, LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company (“Lessee”).  Lessee and Lessor are 
sometimes referred to herein individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”. 
RECITALS: 
A. Lessor and Lessee entered into that certain Lease and Easement Agreement for Solar 
Energy System dated on or about June 4, 2024 (the “Agreement”). 
B. That certain Memorandum of Lease and Easement Agreement for Solar Energy System 
was recorded on June 6, 2024, as Reception No. 951609, in the real estate records of 
Morgan County, Colorado (the “Memorandum”). 
C. The Agreement and Memorandum encumber those certain premises defined and legally 
described in Exhibit “1-A” to the Agreement (the “Real Property”). 
D. Lessor and Lessee entered into that certain First Amendment to Lease and Easement 
Agreement for Solar Energy System and Memorandum of Lease and Easement 
Agreement for Solar Energy System dated December 16, 2025 (the “First 
Amendment”), pursuant to which S ection 2.5 of the Agreement was repealed in its 
entirety and replaced to provide that the Commercial Operations Date shall occur no 
sooner than June 1, 2026, and no later than November 1, 2026. 
E. Pursuant to Section 2.4 of the Agreement, Lessee obtained a survey of the Real 
Property identifying the boundaries of the Leased Premises, prepared by Blew & 
Associates, PA (the “Survey”).  The Leased Premises, as shown on the Survey, contains 
approximately 16.968 acres (739,146 square feet), more or less, and is a portion of the 
real property identified by Morgan County Assessor’s Parcel No. 1041-363-00-901. 
F. The Parties wish to (i) incorporate the legal description of the Leased Premises shown 
on the Survey as Exhibit “1- B” to the Agreement, as contemplated by Section 2.4 of 
the Agreement, and (ii) further amend the Commercial Operations Date set forth in 
Section 2.5 of the Agreement, as previously amended by the First Amendment. 
NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of 
which is hereby acknowledged, Lessor and Lessee hereby agree to amend the Agreement and 
Memorandum as follows: 
1. Recitals; Definitions. Lessor and Lessee hereby agree that the Recitals set forth 
above are true and correct and are incorporated herein by this reference.  Capitalized terms not 
otherwise defined in this Amendment shall have the meanings assigned to them in the 
Agreement. 
2. Leased Premises; Exhibit “1 -B”. The legal description attached hereto as 
Exhibit “1-B” is hereby inserted into and incorporated into the Agreement as Exhibit “1- B” 
 
thereto, pursuant to Section 2.4 of the Agreement.  The Leased Premises consists of 
approximately 16.968 acres, more or less, as more particularly described in Exhibit “1-B,” and 
is a portion of the real property identified by Morgan County Assessor’s Parcel No. 1041-363-
00-901. 
3. Commercial Operations Date. The Parties hereby repeal Section 2.5 of the 
Agreement, as previously amended by the First Amendment, in its entirety, and replace it with 
the following: 
Commercial Operations Date.  Lessee intends to install Solar Power Facilities on the 
Leased Premises consisting of such facilities, equipment and related improvements 
permitted under this Lease.  The precise location on the Leased Premises and timing of 
such installation shall be d etermined by Lessee.  The date of commencement of 
Operations as measured by the date the Project is interconnected to the electric utility 
grid at its fully rated capacity and transmits energy to the grid for commercial sale (not 
including test energy) shall be referred to herein as the “Commercial Operations Date.”  
The Commercial Operations Date shall mean the date of commencement of Operations 
as measured by the date the Solar Energy Facilities are interconnected to the electric  
utility grid at its fully rated capacity and transmits energy to the grid for commercial 
sale (not including test energy).  Lessee represents and warrants that, unless otherwise 
agreed to in writing between Lessor and Lessee, the Commercial Operations Date shall 
occur no sooner than May 31, 2027, and no later than August 31, 2027. 
4. Access Easement. The Parties hereby repeal Section 3.3 of the Agreement, in its 
entirety, and replace it with the following: 
 Access Easement. Lessor grants to Lessee an easement for ingress to and egress from 
the Solar Energy Facilities over and across the Real Property by existing access road located 
on the west side the Real Property. Such access easement is non -exclusive and benefits third 
parties. Lessor shall not limit use of the access easement. 
5. Memorandum of Amendment . Lessor and Lessee shall execute, in recordable 
form, and Lessee shall record in the real estate records of Morgan County, Colorado, a 
memorandum of this Amendment in substantially the form attached as Exhibit “A,” which 
memorandum shall provide construct ive notice of the Leased Premises legal description set 
forth in Exhibit “1-B” and restate the Commercial Operations Date as amended hereby. 
6. Remaining Provisions. Except as specifically set forth in this Amendment, all 
terms and conditions in the Agreement and Memorandum shall remain in full force and effect 
as written. 
7. Ratification. Except as expressly modified herein, all terms and conditions of the 
Agreement remain in full force and effect. 
8. Binding Effect. This Amendment shall inure to the benefit of and be binding upon 
the Parties and their respective successors and assigns and the rights granted hereunder shall 
run with the land and burden the Leased Premises. 
9. Governing Law. The laws of the State of Colorado shall govern this Amendment. 
 
10. No Default . Lessor acknowledges that as of the Effective Date of this 
Amendment there has been no default under the Agreement by Lessee. 
11. Multiple Counterparts . This Amendment may be executed in any number of 
counterparts, all which taken together shall constitute one and the same instrument, and any of 
the Parties hereto may execute this Amendment by signing any such counterpart. 
12. Entire Agreement. This Amendment reflects the Parties’ entire understanding, 
intent, expectations, and representations with respect to the subject matter hereof. 
13. Conflicting Provisions. To the extent the Agreement conflicts with any provision 
of this Amendment, this Amendment shall govern.

ROAD USE AGREEMENT
THIS ROAD USE AGREEMENT (hereinafter "Agreement") is made this 6th day of 
October, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between Morgan County, Colorado, whose address is 218 
W. Kiowa Avenue, Fort Morgan, Colorado 80701 (hereinafter "County") and Glacier Construction, 
whose address is 9801 E Easter AVE, Centennial, Colorado 80112, its successors and assigns (the 
"Glacier").
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Glacier is contracted with the City of Fort Morgan ("City") to update and 
expand the City of Fort Morgan’s existing wastewater treatment plant; 
WHEREAS, the City applied for and obtained an Amendment to a Use by Special Review 
Permit ("Permit"); 
WHEREAS, the City’s agents, employees, affiliates, contractors, subcontractors, workforce 
and related service companies may utilize equipment and heavy vehicles on Morgan County roads 
and Appurtenances in or around such roads (collectively, the “County Roads”), as identified in 
Exhibit A, in connection with development of the Project;
WHEREAS, the City’s use of County Roads may cause impacts which require mitigation and 
repair to ensure the public's continued ability to use County Roads; and 
WHEREAS, as a condition of Permit, the County requires the City or its contractor to enter 
into this Agreement to mitigate the impact on the County Roads due to the Project.
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions contained 
herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the County and Glacier agree as follows:
1. Purpose of Agreement.  The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms and conditions 
under which Glacier will pay for the cost of repairing any damage arising from the use of 
County Roads by Glacier and any contractors or subcontractors of Glacier (“Contracting 
Companies”).  Glacier has indicated that it intends to use approximately .77 miles of 
County Roads, identified on Exhibit A, of which approximately .77 miles are gravel surfaced.  
This Agreement is intended to mitigate the damage to County Roads, minimize interruptions 
to the traveling public, and compensate the County for the costs of repairing any damage to 
the County Roads and any increased maintenance costs resulting from the Project.
2. Definitions.
A. Appurtenance means a ditch, culvert, or any type of wall, fence, guardrail, pavement 
marking, traffic control device, illumination device, mailbox or barrier adjacent to or 
in, along or on a road, or any construction, obstruction, erection or any situation, 
arrangement or disposition of any earth, rock, tree or other material or thing adjacent 
to or in, along or on a road that is not on the traveled portion of the road.
B. County Roads means a roadway under the direction, control and management of the 
2
County, including:
i. A developed road on which improvements such as grading or surfacing have 
been made for the purpose of public access and included any Appurtenances, 
and includes a bridge forming part of a public road and any structure 
incidental to a public road; and/or
ii. An undeveloped road or right of way under the County's jurisdiction.
C. Estimated Costs are included as part of Exhibit C and are the cost of any potential 
road repairs and/or post construction restoration based upon the condition of the road 
and the anticipated traffic, including volume and weight of transport vehicles.  The 
cost estimate must be signed by a Colorado licensed engineer. 
D. Mitigation Plan means a plan, approved by the County Planning and Public Works 
Departments, detailing County Roads will be used for Project traffic as approved in 
Exhibit A, the route of Project traffic, a schedule of when County Roads will be used 
(including daily hours of use), and plans for how impacts to the County Roads, 
neighboring properties, and traffic will be minimized.
3. Pre-Construction Requirements.  Once the haul route, designated in Exhibit A to this 
Agreement, has been approved by the Board of County Commissioners, Glacier must submit 
the following to the County Public Works Department prior to obtaining a construction 
permit:
A. A pre-construction baseline survey of County Roads in a form required by the Public 
Works Department. Upon approval by the Public Works Director, the pre-construction 
baseline survey shall become Exhibit B to this Agreement. 
B. A Mitigation Plan including but not limited to Estimated Costs as described herein. 
The Public Works Director may request review by any third-party consultant of the 
estimated costs.  Upon approval by the Planning Administrator and Public Works 
Director, the Mitigation Plan shall become Exhibit C to this Agreement. 
C. Submission of the security described in Section 5.
4. Repair and Maintenance Obligations.
A. Glacier and Contracting Companies shall comply with the Mitigation Plan at all 
times.  Failure to comply with the Mitigation Plan shall be grounds for the County 
to suspend the Permit until such time as the Glacier provides sufficient assurances 
to the County that Glacier will comply with the Mitigation Plan.
B. Glacier shall be responsible for all costs and expenses required to restore County 
Roads used by Glacier or its Contracting Companies to conduct operations for the 
Project.  This obligation shall require Glacier to restore roads to the same condition 
as shown in the pre-construction baseline survey.
3

D. All restoration of County Roads shall be completed within two (2) months of the

5. Security. 
A. To secure the maintenance and restoration of the County Roads, Glacier shall furnish 
the County, at Glacier's expense and prior to commencement of installation and 
construction of the Project described herein, cash or an irrevocable letter (or letters) of 
credit in which the County is designated as beneficiary in an amount equal to one 
hundred fifteen percent (115%) of the Estimated Costs of maintenance and restoration 
of the County Roads as described in this Agreement (the "Performance Guarantee"). 
B. The Performance Guarantee shall be in a form approved by the County in its sole 
discretion.  
C. The purpose of the Estimated Costs is solely to determine the amount of security.  No 
representations are made as to the accuracy of these estimates, and Glacier agrees to 
pay all costs of the maintenance and restoration for which it is legally obligated, 
regardless of the Estimated Costs.  
D. The Estimated Costs may increase in the future.  Accordingly, the County reserves the 
right to review and adjust the Estimated Costs at any time, prior to the restoration of 
the County Roads.  Adjustments shall be made according to changes in the 
Construction Costs Index as published by the Engineering News Record.  If the 
County adjusts the Estimated Costs, the County shall give written notice to Glacier. 
Glacier shall, within thirty (30) days after receipt of said written notice, provide the 
County with a new or amended Performance Guarantee in the amount of the adjusted 
Estimated Costs.  If Glacier fails to provide a new or amended Performance 
Guarantee, the County may exercise the remedies provided for in Section 11 hereof.
E. If the County Roads are not restored within the period of time specified by Section 4 
above, the County may draw on the Performance Guarantee to restore the County 
Roads.  If the Performance Guarantee is to expire within fourteen (14) calendar days 
and Glacier has not yet provided a satisfactory replacement, the County may draw on 
the Performance Guarantee and either hold such funds as security for performance of 
this Agreement or spend such funds to restore the County Roads.
F. Upon preliminary acceptance of the County Roads, the Performance Guarantee shall 
be reduced to the amount of fifteen percent (15%) of the total actual cost of 
restoration of the County Roads.  The reduced Performance Guarantee shall be held 
by the County until expiration of the two (2) year warranty period.

following the restoration of the County Roads.  Inspections will be conducted by 
designees of both the County and Glacier at a time set by the County or the County's 
official designee if so agreed to by Glacier.
7. Road Damage.
A. Examples of Road Damage include but are not limited to the following:
i. Potholes or wheel-depressed areas after the roads have been maintained or 
graded.
ii. Damage to shoulders due to heavy vehicles running off the edge of the road.
iii. Damage to ditches due to heavy vehicles squeezing the ditches closed by 
running on the shoulders of the road.
iv. Damage to culverts crushed by heavy hauling activities or being "plugged" by 
sediment from closed ditches.
v. Damage to road surface causing the re-cycling of the surface for the purpose 
of proper roadway drainage.
vi. Damage to the existing base by heavy traffic.
vii. Damage to the profile of the road and the loss of aggregate on the driving 
surface.
