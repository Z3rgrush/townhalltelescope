This application is for a Special Use Permit to allow for temporary residential use of RVs while 
residences are being developed, use of RVs as storage containment, the storage of RVs, the storage of 
inoperable vehicles and junk and/or storage yard and the ability to commercially work on vehicles if 
needed. The proposed permitted area is located in the E½ NE¼ of Section 31, Township 4 North, Range 
60 West of the 6th P.M., Morgan County, Colorado north of Highway 34 and also known as 18975 
County Road 1, Wiggins, Colorado 80654. The property is zoned Agricultural Production and is in the 
Wiggins Fire District. 
 
Currently, the property uses are as follows: 
• Four RVs are being used as residences  
• RVs are being used as storage 
• RVs are being stored 
• Semi-trailers and Conex boxes are being used as storage 
• Several inoperable vehicles are being stored in relation to the  business operations of one of the 
residents on the property.  The business operations occur  at a different location and not on this 
property.  
• Junk is being stored as the property is cleaned up from its previous use as a rest stop.  
 
The special use is being requested to bring the property into compliance with the County’s zoning 
regulations regarding those specific uses.  
By November 5, 2026, the applicant has already agreed to obtain two manufactured home placement 
permits for homes to be placed on property to the east and south of the property subject to the application 
and also owned by Michael and Joan Michael Miller. Once completed, the RVs will no longer be permitted 
as residences.  Two other manufactured home placement permits will be obtained for homes to be placed 
on the subject property and south of the subject property. Completion of the third and fourth homes will be 
by the end of 2028.

A. The use and its location as proposed are in conformance with the Morgan County Comprehensive

Planning Commission Meeting 
 
This application was considered by the Planning Commission at a public hearing on July 13, 2026.  
 
Several landowners had concerns regarding property values, the property not currently being cleaned-up, 
safety, hazards, and a specific timeframe for the permit.

addition of the following conditions: 
 
1. All customer vehicles need to be removed from the property within 6 months of the date of the 
permit.

made. The Planning Commission failed to make a recommendation.

We are asking for a Special Use to encompass the following activities on our land: Temporary residential use of 
RVs, using RVs as storage, storage of RVs, the allowance of inoperable vehicles for an auto repair business and 
junkyard or storage yard. The ability to commercially work on a vehicle if needed on the property.   We ask for 
the ability to live in our 5th wheels while we develop our residences on three parcels of land with four different 
houses for our family.  
 
Our family made the decision to move out to Morgan County to oversee and ensure that Michael and Joan 
Miller are always cared for, and that they would never be put into a nursing home. The house that they were 
previously living in had become a prison for Joan. Due to her medical conditions, she was incapable of going 
down the stairs to go outside without a great deal of assistance. The fifth wheel does have a few stairs, but Joan 
has been able to figure out how to adapt to using them to go in and out on her own, we find that sense of 
‘independence’ to be invaluable. She has still fallen and must use a walker to be outside, but she is no longer 
locked indoors. The two of them need assistance with medication intake, meal preparation, and some daily 
needs and care. 
 
The property has been owned by a member of this family since May 17. 1946. Then when the State ended the 
lease and closed the rest stop everything was stolen. The gate stopping access was taken, and no matter how 
many no trespassing signs were put up everyone kept accessing and taking what they wanted. This is still an on 
going matter; we had a semi-trailer that was stolen in August of 2025. Without the presence of us on this parcel 
it is a legitimate fear that everything would be stolen. Michael insists that he must be here to watch over the 
belongings.  
 
The condition of the dirt on all the other parcels is less than to be desired, this is due to the other parcels have 
had no kind of work done on them. It is often that vehicles and equipment get stuck and must use four-wheel 
drive and even at times be pulled to get out of the sandy conditions. If there is any kind of wet weather, it makes 
it almost impossible for some vehicles to get in or out. This makes it impossible for those parcels to be 
inhabited or capable of any type of storage at the moment.  
 
Our timeline for the Special Use Permit coincides with the modular home permits for the two houses currently 
located at 18978 County Road 1, Wiggins, CO 80654, which must be pulled by November 5, 2026. The dirt 
work and driveways at both 18978 and 18795 County Road 1 must be completed prior to construction of the 
homes and are expected to be finished by the end of November 2026. The tentative construction timeline for the 
last two of the four homes is expected to begin shortly thereafter the first two homes are completed and 
continue through 2028. 
 
Our proposed mitigation is that the rest stop area, which has not been maintained since its closure, will be 
significantly improved and cleaned up. The buildings will be painted, dead trees will be removed, and we hope 
the grass and landscaping will be restored over time. Although we are requesting approval for storage and “junk 
yard” use within our permit, we are not proposing to keep trash or garbage on the property. The intent is to 
maintain the area in a clean, organized, and well-managed manner. 
 
We are striving to have as little environmental impact on the property as possible, especially given our intent to 
live on and responsibly maintain the property. 
 
We currently have a large number of items on the property due to temporary storage needs. Our household 
consists of 11 people, and all of our clothing, personal belongings, and household items are being stored in 
semi-trailers and small connex containers. Additionally, my son, Ashton, owns and operates a diesel mechanic, 
fabrication, and welding business. Much of the equipment stored in the trailers and containers belongs to his 
business. The vehicles on the property are primarily customer vehicles that he is actively working on as part of 
his business operations. 
 
Since he works on larger equipment, motors, and vehicles the tools are larger and heavier. He has three 
toolboxes that hold things like sockets, and wretches for these large repairs these things alone are very space 
consuming. He has another two toolboxes for regular size tools, the kind of tools that fit on automobiles. He 
also has a welder, machines, metal lathe, and part bins, these are just other examples of tools and equipment that 
are stored from his shop. Another Connex has wood working equipment in it, such as a table saw, drill and 
wood lathe. In addition, the business has several trailers. Examples of them are a welding trailer, a Hotsy 
pressure washing trailer, an enclosed trailer, and hauling trailers.  
 
The business has rented a shop space in Wiggins, he also has trucks for mobile service. The shop was rented so 
that most of the work is being performed off the property. The rented space, however, is small meaning he 
cannot store the majority of his tools or any extra vehicles at that location. We ask that he be allowed to work on 
a few items on the property for situations that fall under an ‘exception’, understanding the majority of his work 
will not be performed on the property.

Response to DWR Referral 
The current well permit is not being utilized because the existing site conditions do not meet the 
permit requirements regarding the number and type of residences served. At this time, the 
property is occupied by four fifth-wheel RV trailers, which do not qualify as permanent 
residences under the terms of the permit. 
For this reason, construction of the permitted well has not yet begun. Instead, water for the 
current occupants and property needs is being supplied by hauled water. This approach was 
taken to ensure compliance with the conditions of the existing well permit and to avoid any 
potential violations. 
Once the permanent residences are completed and the temporary use of the fifth-wheel RV 
trailers is no longer necessary, construction of the permitted well will be scheduled and 
completed in accordance with the permit requirements. 
The status of the well permit and our decision not to utilize it under the current conditions were 
discussed with the Zoning and Planning Department and the County Attorney during our 
meeting on April 23, 2026. This matter was also discussed with the Board of County 
Commissioners. Throughout these discussions, we explained that we are not utilizing the existing 
permit because the current occupancy does not meet the permit conditions and that hauled water 
is being used until the property is eligible for construction and use of the permitted well.
