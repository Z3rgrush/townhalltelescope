RESOLUTION NO. 2026 BCC 42 
A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE MORGAN COUNTY ZONING REGULATIONS 
CONCERNING ENFORCEMENT PROCESSES 
WHEREAS, the County desires to amend the Morgan County Zoning Regulations to clean 
up zoning regulations concerning enforcement processes. 
WHEREAS, on September 14, 2026, the Planning Commission held a duly noticed public 
hearing on the proposed amendments and recommended approval of the amendments; 
WHEREAS, on September 15, 2026, the Board of County Commissioners held a  duly 
noticed public hearing on the proposed amendments; 
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has complied with all relevant 
provisions for amending the Morgan County Zoning Regulations; and 
WHEREAS, after considering public testimony received and the recommendation of the 
Planning Commission, the Board of County Commissioners finds these amendments to be in the 
best interest of the citizens of Morgan County. 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Morgan County  Board of County 
Commissioners as follows: 
Section 1 .  The following new section shall be added to the Application and 
Implementation of Regulations in Chapter 2 of the Morgan County Zoning Regulations to read as 
follows: 
2-502  Continuing Jurisdiction 
 
The County retains continuing jurisdiction over any permit granted under these Zoning 
Regulations to ensure compliance with the permits, any conditions and the Zoning 
Regulations.  Approvals are conditioned on compliance with all information and 
representation contained in the applications and presented during any public hearings.  If 
any representations or information presented by the applicant or owner during the 
application process are found to be erroneous, lacking a factual basis or otherwise 
inaccurate, the County may institute enforcement proceedings, including issuing an order 
to show cause to the applicant or owner.  If there is conflict regarding the interpretation of 
a condition, the Board may, subject to notice and hearing, amend, add, or remove any 
conditions.  
 
Section 2.   Section 6-108 of the Morgan County Zoning Regulations is amended to read 
as follows:

6-108 Enforcement Procedures  
 
This Section sets forth the processes and penalties for violations of these Regulations, 
permits issued under these Regulations, and any agreement which a condition and 
requirement of a permit issued under these Regulations.  The following remedies for 
enforcement shall be cumulative and shall in no way limit the County's authority to seek 
any other remedy available at law for violations of these Regulations.  
 
(A) Notice of Violation  
 
(1) If competent evidence exists to believe that a violation has occurred, a 
notice of violation shall be personally served on the owner, if possible, or 
occupant of the property.  The notice of violation shall also be posted on the 
property, if possible.  A notice shall also be sent via first class mail to the 
owner's mailing addres s as listed in the records of the Morgan County 
Assessor's Office and to the mailing address of the property in question, if 
different than the owner's address and if one exists.  
 
(2) The notice of violation shall specifically describe the nature of the violation 
and shall require that the violation be corrected at least ten (10) days after 
the date of the notice.  The notice of violation shall provide that the alleged 
violator may request additional time to correct the violation. 
 
(3) If the violation is not corr ected within the time period established in the 
notice of violation or approved extension of time, the County may 
determine to pursue remedies under subsection (B) of this Section. 
 
(4) The notice described in this Section is not required for violations of permits 
issued pursuant to these Regulations, including violation of any condition 
or agreement as a result of the issuance of a permit.  However, the County 
may issue a notice of viola tion pursuant to this Section for a violation of a 
permit or permit condition.  
 
(B) Remedies 
 
(1) Revocation of Permits, Amendment of Conditions, and Administrative 
Penalties. 
  
(a) If an alleged violator is in violation of the conditions of a land use 
approval or permit that was issued by the County or if the approval 
or the permit was obtained by false representation or issued in error, 
the Board may revoke the approval of permit, amend conditions 
imposed on the approval or permit that are related to the violation or 
impose administrative penalties.  Amendment of the terms and 
conditions shall include the addition of new conditions and the 
removal of conditions.  Nothing herein shall be deemed to limit the

(b) The Planning Administrator shall first consider the alleged 
violation(s) and determine whether there is sufficient evidence that 
a violation(s) has occurred or is occurring.  Upon a determination 
that sufficient evidence exists, the Planning Administrator shall 
issue an order to show cause to the alleged violator, directing the 
alleged violator to appear before the Board and show why the 
approval and/or permit should not be revoked or amended, and/or 
administrative penalties not imposed.  The order to show cause shall 
set a time and date for hearing on the violation to be held at least ten 
(10) days from the date of the order.  The order to show cause shall 
be personally served, posted on the property subject to the permit, 
or sent cer tified mail to the alleged violator and the owner, if 
different.  The show cause order may also be sent via electronic mail 
to the alleged violator if the Planning Administrator has a reasonable 
belief that the electronic mail address is correct and one of the other 
service options listed above is also attempted.  
 
(c) The Board shall conduct the show cause hearing, and the violator 
and/or owner may present evidence as to why the County should not 
revoke the land use approval or permit; or amend the conditions on 
the approval or permit; or impose administrative penalties.  The 
Board may also hear from individuals directly aggrieved by the 
alleged violation.  No other individuals have the right to be heard at 
the hearing.  The Board shall determine whether to rev oke the 
permit, amend conditions or impose administrative penalties within 
fourteen (14) days after the conclusion of the hearing.  The Board 
shall notify the alleged violator and/or owner in writing of its 
decision.  
 
(d) Administrative Penalties.  Administrative penalties may not exceed 
Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500) per violation per day 
the violation occurs and continues.  When imposing administrative 
penalties, the Board shall take into consideration the following 
factors: 
 
(i) the impact of the vi olation on public health, safety, and 
welfare; 
 
(ii) the impact of the violation on neighboring properties or 
public property; 
 
(iii) the violator's willingness to comply with or demonstrated 
disregard for the applicable laws;
