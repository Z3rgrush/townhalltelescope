APPLICANTS and LANDOWNERS: Charles and Marcia Bledsoe 
 
This application is to combine Lots 130 and 131 of the First Replat of Morgan Heights Winslow 
Second Subdivision, being a replat of Lots 52 through 59 and Lots 126 through 136 of Morgan 
Heights, Winslow Second Subdivision, being a part of the N½NW¼ of Section 25, Township 4 North, 
Range 58 West of the 6th P.M., Morgan County, Colorado.  
 
The lots are in the Moderate Density Residential (MDR) zone and in the Fort Morgan Fire District. Lot 
130 currently has a residence.  
 
In reviewing an application for an amended plat to vacate interior lot lines in a previously approved 
subdivision, the Board of County Commissioners shall apply the criteria as listed from Section 10-
200(G)(1) of the Morgan County Subdivision Regulations. 
 
a) No additional lots will be created by the vacation. 
 
The application satisfies this criteria. 
 
b) The vacation is keeping with the purpose and intent of these Subdivision Regulations. 
 
The application satisfies this criteria. 
 
c) The vacation will not create a nonconforming setback for any existing buildings. 
 
The lot line vacation will not create a nonconforming setback. 
 
d) The resultant lot will meet the requirements of the applicable zone district, including those 
requirements in Appendix B of the County’s Zoning Regulations. 
 
The resultant lot meets the requirements of the MDR zone. 
 
e) The vacation will not adversely affect access, drainage or utility easements or rights-of-way 
serving the property or other properties in the area.

After reviewing all submitted bids, I determined that only two bids appear to fully meet the requirements outlined in our request: Bid #1, Buchanan Construction & Specialty Services, in the amount of
$168,997.08, and Bid #7, High Pointe Companies, LLC, in the amount of $116,145.39.
The remaining bids did not fully meet the requested specifications and requirements based on my review. Therefore, I believe it is appropriate to focus our consideration on the two bids that most closely
align with the project requirements.To ensure an accurate comparison of the total project costs, the County requested clarification and a revised proposal from High Pointe Companies, LLC. The request
required a 90-day pricing guarantee and clarification of exclusions, allowances, and potential additional costs necessary to complete the project.
Based on the submitted pricing and compliance with the requested scope of work, I recommend proceeding with High Pointe Companies, LLC,. Its revised proposed total of $120,094.33, excluding any
applicable additional costs not included in the proposal, remains lower than Buchanan Construction & Specialty Services’ bid of $168,997.08.
