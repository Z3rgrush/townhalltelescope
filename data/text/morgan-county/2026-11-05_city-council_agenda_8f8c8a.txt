RE: Opposition to Proposed Regulations Banning Recreation Shooting of Prairie Dogs on Public 
Land 
Dear Members of the Colorado Parks & Wildlife Commission, 
 
We are writing to express our strong concern regarding the proposed regulations to ban 
recreational shooting of certain species of prairie dogs on public land. While we appreciated the 
dedication of CPW staff in managing our state’s wildlife resources we are deeply troubled by 
the expedited rulemaking timeline, the exclusion of local county governments from the process, 
and the sever operational and economic consequences proposal poses for local communities.  
The rapid progression of this regulatory plan—advancing from a citizen petition to a statewide 
management proposal and formal Commission submission in just six months—has severely 
limited meaningful public and intergovernmental engagement. Stakeholder outreach began 
only weeks before the Commission's final meetings to deliberate on rule approval. By 
formulating statewide regulatory language without direct input from county governments, CPW 
missed a vital opportunity to partner with local leaders who directly manage the land-use, 
economic, and agricultural impacts of these decisions. 
 
Key Issues with the Proposed Regula�ons: 
1. Impacts on Surrounding Private Lands: Prairie dogs do not recognize public-private land 
boundaries. Banning management tools on public acreage will lead to unchecked 
population growth that inevitably spills over onto adjacent private properties. 
2. Agricultural and Ecological Degradation: Uncontrolled prairie dog populations inflict 
devastating damage to prairie ecosystems, grasslands, grazing pasture, and productive 
agricultural operations. 
3. Increased Financial and Operational Burden on Counties: Counties already expend 
substantial taxpayer dollars and staff resources annually to mitigate prairie dog 
encroachment and weed growth. Restricting management on public lands will directly 
increase county workloads and mitigation expenses 
 
 

Morgan County Government -  218 West Kiowa Ave -  PO Box 596 -  Fort Morgan, CO  80701 
Telephone (970) 542-3500         Fax (970) 542-3556 
 
To address these outstanding concerns and ensure a balanced, cooperative approach to 
wildlife management, Morgan County Board of County Commissioners respectfully 
requests that the Commission take one of the following actions: 
 
1. Delay the rulemaking process to allow for meaningful, structured collaboration with 
county governments and local agricultural stakeholders. 
2. Halt the proposed regulation until a comprehensive impact study and county 
consultation process can be completed. 
3. Amend the proposed language to include local exemptions, county consultation 
requirements, and specific management flexibility for public lands bordering agricultural 
or private properties. 
Thank you for your consideration of local county perspectives on this critical issue. 
Sincerely, 
 
Morgan County Board of County Commissioners

ORDINANCE NO. 2, SERIES 2026  
AN ORDINANCE CONCERNING THE COUNTY'S PROHIBITIONS ON THE  
ACCUMULATION OF JUNK AND RUBBISH, WEEDS AND BRUSH, AND  
INOPERABLE VEHICLES AND REQUIRING REPAIR, REMOVAL, OR SECURING 
OF UNSAFE BUILDINGS IN MORGAN COUNTY  
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 30- 15-401, the Board of County Commissioners of 
Morgan County has the general enabling power to adopt ordinances for the cont rol or licensing 
of those matters of purely local concern, and to do all acts and make all regulations which may 
be necessary or expedient for the promotion of health or the suppression of disease;  
WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30-15-401(1)(a)(I) authorizes the County to adopt ordinances and 
regulations to compel the removal of rubbish, including trash, junk, and garbage from property 
within the unincorporated areas of Morgan County, except for agricultural land currently in 
agricultural use as the term agricultural land is defined in C.R.S. § 39- 1-102(1.6), and from any 
public right of way immediately adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caused by 
the property owner or occupant or originated from the property;  
WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30- 15-401(1)(a)(I.5) authorizes the County to adopt ordinances 
and regulations to compel the removal of weeds and brush from property within the 
unincorporated areas of Morgan County, except for agricultural land currently in agricultural 
use as the term agricultural land is defined in C.R.S. § 39- 1-102(1.6), and from any public right 
of way immediately adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caused by the 
property owner or occupant or originated from the property;  
WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30- 15-401(1)(q) authorizes the County to adopt ordinances and 
regulations to compel the removal or securing of unsafe buildings and structures within the 
unincorporated areas of the Morgan County;  
WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30- 15-401(2)(d) provides additional enforcement options for 
violations of the County's rubbish, weeds and brush, and removal or securing of unsafe 
buildings and structures;  
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 30- 15-406, this ordinance was introduced and read 
before the Board of County Commissioners in a first reading on Septem ber 15, 2026, and was 
published in full in the Morgan County Times on September 24, 2026, at least ten days prior to 
adoption; and  
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners find that this ordinance is necessary to 
preserve the public health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Morgan County.  
  2  
            NOW THEREFORE  be it ordained by the Morgan County Board of County 
Commissioners as follows:  
 
 Section 1.  Accumulation of Junk and Rubbish and Inoperable Vehicles  
 A.  Purpose  
The purpose of this Section is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, to the extent possible, the accumulation of rubbish within the County, 
which, if not eliminated or controlled, can result in visual blight, attractive 
nuisances, incompatibility with existing land uses, hazardous or toxic substances 
being deposited in unapproved sites, attraction of disease -infested rodents and 
other vermin, fire or other hazards, and air, noise and water pollution.  
 B.  Applicability  
1. This Section shall apply to all land within unincorporated Morgan County, 
including all individual properties and any public right of way 
immediately adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caus ed 
by the property owner or occupant or originated from the property, except 
agricultural land currently in use as agricultural land as that term is defined 
in C.R.S. § 391-102(1.6).    
2. This Section does not apply to identified and operating junk yards 
currently allowed pursuant to the County’s Zoning Regulations.  
 C.  Definitions  
1. Agricultural land means any parcel of land presently classified as 
agricultural use by the Morgan County Assessor’s Office, as defined by 
and subject to the restrictions contained in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6).  
2. Inoperable vehicle means any vehicle in which the engine, wheels or other 
essential parts have been removed, altered, damaged or allowed to 
deteriorate so that the vehicle cannot be driven, or a vehicle which is not 
currently licensed or registered or otherwise may not be legally operated 
on highways of the State.  
3. Junk and Rubbish means any trash, garbage, litter, refuse, debris, lawn or 
garden waste, newspapers, magazines, rubber, glass, scrap metal or alloys, 
used lumber or crates, used pipe or pipe fittings, bottles, plastic or paper 
containers or packaging, rags or cloth, remnant construction or demolition 
materials, vehicle parts, derelict vehicles, abandoned or junk equipment, 
used tires, used appliances or furniture, oil, carcasses of dead animals, or 
any object otherwise likely to injure any person or create a tr affic hazard, 
  3  
or any other object which has been discarded or abandoned or is or 
obsolete as to make it unusable in its existing condition.  
4. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record, and any 
occupant or tenant of the premises or property.  
 D.  Rubbish Containers   
Rubbish containers shall be provided by the owner of the premises.  Rubbish 
containers shall be of a kind suitable to contain all trash and garbage collected and 
shall have a tight -fitting lid.  Collection of garbage in plastic garbage bags of 
twenty (20) gallon capacity or larger is allowed during the thirty (30) day 
accumulation period prior to collection by a licensed waste hauler and/or disposal 
at the County landfill.  
 E.  Inoperable Vehicles  
A maximum of one (1) inoperative vehicle per parcel is all owed in residential, 
manufactured home and agriculture (parcels twenty (20) acres or less) zones.  A 
maximum of three (3) inoperative vehicles is allowed in the Agriculture Zone 
(parcels larger than twenty (20) acres), A/B, C, LI and HI zones unless otherw ise 
allowed or restricted by a conditional or special use permit approved and issued 
by the County.  Vehicles in excess of the limitations in this subsection are not 
allowed unless they are stored in a private enclosed garage or storage shed and 
fully scre ened so as to prevent such vehicles from being viewed from public 
property, including any public road.  Vehicles in excess of the allowed numbers, 
which are unscreened, may be allowed if the Planning Administrator determines 
that they are economically nece ssary for a bona fide farming or ranching 
operation and the Planning Administrator may limit the number of unscreened 
inoperable vehicles in excess of the limitations contained in this subsection.  
 F.  Unlawful Acts  
1. It is unlawful for any owner to cause or permit junk or rubbish to be left or 
accumulated in or upon any yard, garden, lawn, outbuilding or premises 
upon any parcel or property, or any public right of way immediately 
adjacent to the property to the extent the condition is caused by the owner 
or originated from the property in the County unless otherwise specified 
herein.  
2. It is unlawful for any person to discard or abandon any junk or rubbish 
upon premises not owned or occupied by the person without the consent of 
the owner thereof, and the junk and rubbish deposited without consent 
shall be deemed to have been discarded and abandoned.  
  4  
3. It is unlawful for any owner to maintain inoperable vehicles on any 
property in excess of the allowed numbers contained in this Ordinance.   
 Section 2.  Accumulation of Weeds and Brush  
 A.  Purpose  
The purpose of this Section is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, to the extent possible, the growth and prolif eration of weeds and 
brush within the County, which, if not eliminated or controlled, can result in 
negative visual impact, incompatibility with existing land uses, fire and traffic 
hazards, the clogging of drainage ways, obstructions of public access and other 
detrimental health and safety impacts.  
 B.  Applicability  
The Section shall apply to land within unincorporated Morgan County, including 
all individual properties and any public right of way immediately adjacent to the 
property to the extent the condition is caused by the property owner or occupant 
or originated from the property, except agricultural land currently in use as 
agricultural land as that term is defined in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6).  
 C.  Definitions  
1. Agricultural land means any parcel of land presently classified as 
agricultural use by the Morgan County  Assessor’s Office, as defined by 
and subject to the restrictions contained in C.R.S. § 39-1-102(1.6).  
2. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record, and any 
occupant of the premises or property.  
3. Weeds and brush means:  
 a.  Any plant or vegetation which:  
 i.  Ordinarily grows without cultivation;  
ii. Is not growth for the purpose of landscaping or food 
production; and  
iii. Attains a growth of six (6) inches in or more in height, or 
grows or accumulates so as to become a fire, traffic, 
pedestrian, or other public health or safety hazard; or  
  5  
b. Any plant which has been designated as a noxious weed by the Board of 
County Commissioners, including without limitation:  
i.  Leafy Spurge (Euphorbia esula L.); ii. 
 Russian Knapweed (Centaurea repens L.); iii. 
 Spotted Knapweed (Centaurea maculosa Lam.); 
iv.  Diffuse Knapweed (Centaurea diffusa Lam.); v. 
 Canada Thistle (Cirsium arvense [L.] Scop.); 
vi.  Musk Thistle (Carduus nutans L.); vii. 
 Field Bindweed (Convolvulus arvensis L.);  
viii.  Volunteer Rye (Secale cereale L.); and ix.  
 Jointed Goatgrass (Aegilops Cyindrica Host.).  
 D.  Compliance  
All owners shall take any necessary action to suppress weeds and brush to comply 
with this Ordinance.   
 E.  Unlawful Acts  
It is unlawful for any owner to allow the accumulation and/or outgrowth of weeds 
and brush in violation of this Ordinance on property under such owner’s control, 
possession or ownership, or upon any public right of way immediately adjacent to 
the property to the extent the condition is caused by the owner or originated from 
the property unless otherwise specified herein.    
 Section 3.  Unsafe Buildings and Structures  
 A.  Purpose  
The purpose of this Section is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of 
the citizens and residents of Morgan County, Colorado, by eliminating and 
controlling, the existence of unsafe or abandoned buildings which, if not repaired, 
secured, or removed, can result in fire hazards, attractive nuisances, rodent 
infestations, create an uninhabitable environment for occupants, that becomes a 
place used by trespassers, and other detrimental health and safety impacts.  
 B.  Applicability  
The Section shall apply to land within unincorporated Morgan County, except for 
buildings or structures located on affected land as that term is defined in the 
Colorado Mined Land Reclamation Act, C.R.S. § 34- 32-103(1.5) and C.R.S. § 
3432.5-103(1), and lan ds subject to the Colorado Surface Coal Mining 
Reclamation Act, C.R.S. § 34-33-101, et seq.  
  6  
 C.  Definitions  
1. Owner means the owner of record, whether person, partnership, firm, 
corporation, governmental agency, or other association of persons, or any 
authorized agent or representative of the owner of record.  
2. Unsafe building or structure means:  
a. Any building or structure where the condition of which presents a 
substantial danger or hazard to public health, safety, or welfare; or  
b. Any building which is mat erially dilapidated and unused by the 
owner or uninhabited because of deterioration or decay; and  
i. Which condition constitutes a substantial fire hazard; or  
ii. Which condition subjects adjoining property owners to 
danger of damage by storm, soil erosion, or rodent  
infestation; or  
iii. Which becomes a place frequented by trespassers or 
transients seeking a temporary hideout or shelter.  
 D.  Compliance  
All property owners shall take all necessary actions, including but not limited to, 
maintaining, repairing, securing and demolishing any building or structure which 
violates this Ordinance.   
 E.  Unlawful Acts  
It is unlawful for any owner to maintain, permit, or fail to repair, secure or remove 
any unsafe building or structure on their property.    
 Section 4.  Enforcement  
 A.  Notice of Violation   
1. If probable cause exists to believe that a violation of this Ordinance has 
occurred, a notice of violation shall be personally served and sent via first-
class mail, return receipt requested, to the property owner's mailing 
address as listed in the records of the Morgan County Assessor's Office 
and to the mailing address of the property in question, if one exis ts and is 
different than the property owner's mailing address.  If personal service is 
not successful, the notice of violation may be posted on the property.   
  7  
2. Any owner shall have a total of ten (10) days upon the date of the notice of 
violation to remedy and abate the violation.  This time may be reduced to 
as few as three (3) days if the accumulation presents an immediate danger 
to health or safety of the public.  
3. The notice of violation shall refer to the section or sections of the 
Ordinance that are al leged to be violated, the facts that constitute the 
violation, and shall specify the remedies available to the County in cases 
where the owner fails or refuses to remedy and abate the violation before 
the time specified in the notice, including any possibl e liens due to 
abatement costs.  
 B.  Administrative Entry and Seizure Warrant  
Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section 
following notice pursuant to subsection A of this Section and the time allowed for 
remedy and abatement of the violation by the owner has passed, in event of failure 
or refusal of an y owner to remedy and abate the violation as described in the 
notice of violation, the County may seek an administrative entry and seizure 
warrant from the Morgan County Court or District Court. The application for an 
administrative entry and seizure warrant shall consist of:  
1. A copy of the written notice to the property owner, including evidence that 
the owner received such notice, such as a post office return receipt or an 
affidavit of personal service;  
2. Evidence that the owner has not complied with the notice;  
3. A copy of this Ordinance; and  
4. A sworn or affirmed affidavit stating:  
a. The factual basis for the warrant;  
b. A description of the location of the property;  
c. A general list of the actions necessary to remedy and abate the  
violation;  
d. As applicable, a general list of any junk, rubbish or inoperable 
vehicles to be removed from the property; and  
e. The proposed disposal or temporary impoundment of any items 
removed from the property, as applicable.  
 
  8  
Within thirty (30) days following the da te of the issuance of the administrative 
entry and seizure warrant, the warrant shall be executed in accordance with the 
issuing court’s directions, and a copy of the issued warrant shall be provided or 
mailed to the owner.  Following execution of the warr ant, proof of the execution 
shall be submitted to the issuing court.  Such proof shall include a written 
inventory of any property impounded.  
 C.  Imposition of Fines  
Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section, 
County code enforcement officers shall have the authority to issue citations or 
summonses and complaints for the purpose of enforcing this Ordinance.  Any 
person who violates this Ordinance commits a civil infraction, and, upon 
conviction thereof, shall be punished by  a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars 
($250) per day for the first action leading to conviction, five hundred dollars 
($500) per day for the second action leading to conviction, and one thousand 
dollars ($1,000) per day for the third action leading to c onviction and all 
subsequent actions.  For purposes of calculating the fine, each day the violation 
exists shall constitute a separate offense.  This graduated fine schedule shall apply 
to repeat offenses committed by the same owner with respect to the sam e lot, 
parcel, or tract of land.  
In addition to the fine prescribed by this Section, persons convicted of a violation 
of this Ordinance are subject to a surcharge in an amount fixed by statute that 
shall be paid to the clerk of the court by the defendant, as provided by C.R.S. § 
30-15402(2)(a).  
 D.  Civil Penalty and Abatement Order  
1. Nonexclusive of other remedies available to the County under this Section, 
following notice pursuant to subsection A of this Section and the time 
allowed for remedy and abatement of the violation by the owner  has 
passed, in event of failure or refusal of any owner to remedy and abate the 
violation as described in the notice of violation, the County shall have the 
authority to request from the Morgan County Court or District Court an 
order imposing a civil pena lty.  The penalty shall be not less than one 
hundred dollars ($100) or more than two thousand six hundred fifty dollars 
($2,650) per day of the violation, subject to the conditions and 
presumptive maximums set forth in C.R.S. § 30- 15-401(2)(d)(III).  Each 
day that a violation occurs is deemed a separate offense and the penalty 
continues until the violation is remedied.  No civil penalty shall be 
imposed against any owner which is a State agency.  
 
 
  9  
Until paid, any civil penalty imposed shall be recorded wit h the Morgan 
County Clerk and Recorder as a lien against the real property.  If the 
assessment is not paid within thirty (30) days, said assessment, together 
with ten percent (10%) added for the cost of collection, shall be certified 
by the County Clerk to the Morgan County Treasurer for collection.  
2. In addition to any civil penalties ordered by a court, the County shall have 
the authority to bring an action in the Morgan County Court or District 
Court for an order enjoining the violation, ordering the violation's restraint, 
removal, termination, or abatement by the owner, and authorizing the 
abatement by the County or its agent.  In bringing said action, the County 
Attorney shall file a verified complaint.  The County Attorney shall serve 
the hearing summons and complaint on the owner in accordance with the 
applicable rules of civil procedure and pursuant to C.R.S. § 30 -
15401(2)(d)(IV) at least ten (10) days prior to the hearing.  
3. If the County abates the violation pursuant to an order of the court as set 
forth in this subsection and subsection B, the County has authority to 
impose the entire cost of abatement, plus ten percent (10%) for inspection 
and expense will be assessed against said real property in violation of this 
Section.  The assessment is a lien against the real property until paid.  If 
the assessment is not paid within thirty (30) days, said assessment, 
together with ten percent (10%) added for the cost of collection, shall be 
certified by the County Clerk to the Morgan County Treasurer for 
collection.  
Section 5.  Additional Remedies.  The remedies provided in this Ordinance  shall be 
cumulative and in addition to any other federal, state or local remedy, criminal or civil, 
which may be available.  Nothing contained herein shall be construed to pr eclude 
prosecution under any other applicable statute, ordinance, rule, order or regulation.  
Section 6. Severability.  Should any section, clause, sentence, or part of this ordinance 
be adjudged by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutiona l or invalid, the same 
shall not affect, impair or invalidate the ordinance as a whole or any part thereof other than the 
part so declared to be invalid.  
Section 7 . Safety Clause .  The Board of County Commissioners hereby finds, 
determines and declares th at this ordinance is necessary for the immediate preservation of the 
public welfare, health and safety.  
Section 8. Repeal of Ordinance No. 1, Series 2020.  Ordinance No. 1, Series 2020, An 
Ordinance Prohibiting the Accumulation of Junk and Rubbish, Weeds and Brush, and Requiring 
Removal of Unsafe Buildings in Morgan County, is hereby repealed in its entirety.  
Section 9. Effective Date.  Pursuant to C.R.S. § 30- 15-405, this ordinance shall become 
effective thirty days after publication following adoption.
