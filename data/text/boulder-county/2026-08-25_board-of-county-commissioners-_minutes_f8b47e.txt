5.e
Human Services - 2027 IDD - A&I Avenues - Funding Approval Memo
1.
2027_idd_aiavenues_funding_approval_memo.pdf
Boulder County Human Services (BCHS) requests approval of $3,444,952 in Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities (IDD) Mill Levy funding for A&I Avenues for the 2027 contract term, reflecting a 3% cost-of-living adjustment from the 2026 amount. A&I Avenues is designated by the State of Colorado as the sole single-entry point responsible for conflict-free case management services in Boulder County, making competitive solicitation inappropriate for this funding. Services provided include crisis management, family support, autism spectrum disorder services, and Medicaid waiver coordination, all at no cost to participants. Beginning in 2027, BCHS is transitioning from a procurement contract to a funding agreement to better reflect the nature of the relationship between the County and A&I Avenues. BCHS anticipates submitting a similar annual funding request to the Board each year until a new State procurement process is expected around July 1, 2031.
Staff Contact(s):
Whitney Wilcox, Human Services; Georgina Becerril, Human Services

2026_08_20_MFD_Memo(1).pdf
