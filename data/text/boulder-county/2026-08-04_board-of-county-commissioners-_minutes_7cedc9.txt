Parks and Open Space - Zweck Amendment to Option Agreement
1.
Zweck_First_Amendment_to_Option_Agreement_landowner_signed.pdf
On November 10, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners approved an option agreement with Zweck LLC for the purchase of 210 acres of land and associated water rights at 11009 Airport Road, Unincorporated Boulder County, just west of incorporated Longmont. That option agreement was signed on May 30, 2006. For the past 20 years, the county has made option payments to Zweck LLC and on May 30, 2026, the county exercised its option to purchase the property with the closing scheduled for August 27, 2026. To reflect current conditions and circumstances, an amendment to the option agreement is necessary to replace a perpetual agricultural leaseback provision with a termed 3-year agricultural lease with the current tenant, and to update several administrative provisions.
