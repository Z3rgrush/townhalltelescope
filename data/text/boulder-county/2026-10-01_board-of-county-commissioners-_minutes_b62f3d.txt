Board of County Commissioners - Regular Meeting - October 01, 2026
1.
Call to Order
The Board of County Commissioners of Boulder County met in Regular Meeting in full conformity with the law and resolution of said Board at the regular place of hybrid meetings, 1325 Pearl Street, Boulder and on Zoom Webinar, on October 1, 2026.
The meeting was called to order at approximately 9:04 a.m. by Chair Claire Levy.
2.
9:00 a.m. Morning Session
2.a
Public Comment
This meeting will be to take public comment for any matter not being discussed in another current public hearing process. Each speaker (up to 10 people) will be able to give public comment to the commissioners for 3 minutes each. No pooled speaking is available for this meeting. Participants will not be able to screen-share but can bring printed materials in person.
This public comment session was called to order at 9:04 a.m. and adjourned at 9:26 a.m.
Public Speaker(s):
Edward Yagi, Allenspark
John Bolton, Longmont
Christel Markevich, Nederland
Michael Bauer, Boulder County
Laura Gonzalez, Boulder
2.b
Community Planning and Permitting - SU-25-0002: Eldora Resort Cell Tower
1.
SU_25_0002_BOCC_staff_rec_red.pdf
Community Planning and Permitting Department: Docket SU-25-0002: Eldora Resort Cell Tower; Special Use Review to permit construction of a 100-foot-tall Macro-Cell Telecommunications Facility (cell tower) on a 46-acre parcel in the Forestry zoning district. The application is submitted by Eldora Enterprises LLC (owners/applicants) and Ryan Sagar of CSAi (Agent). The proposal is in the Forestry (F) zoning district at 2890 Lake Eldora Ski Road, approximately 2.5 miles west of the intersection of Lake Eldora Ski Road (aka Shelf Road) and Eldora Road in Section 29, Township 1S, Range 73W.
This public hearing was called to order at 9:26 a.m.
Commissioner Loachamin was excused for Item No. 2.b.
Staff Presenter(s):
Sam Walker, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Other Presenter(s):
Ryan Sagar, Application, Verizon Wireless
John Perry, Eldora Ski Resort
County Attorneys Present:
Erica Rogers
Public Speaker(s):
Laura Gonzalez, Boulder
The commissioners went into recess from 11:04-11:15 p.m.
The commissioners' public hearing adjourned at 11:18 a.m.
Moved by
Commissioner Stolzmann
Seconded by
Commissioner Levy
to conditionally approve Docket SU-25-0002 in accordance with staff recommendations, commitments of record by applicant, and with additional conditions as discussed and contained within the official record.
Carried

Community Planning and Permitting Department Docket LU-25-0005: Colorado Lookout LLC Use of Community Significance; Limited Impact Special Review to recognize existing nonconforming use as a Use of Community Significance to permit a multiple dwelling units on an approximately 2.5-acre parcel at 182 County Road 84W. The application is submitted by Colorado Lookout LLC c/o Ronald Leigh Williams Jr. (owners/applicants). The subject property is in the Forestry (F) zoning district at 182 County Road 84W, a 2.5-acre parcel located approximately 1 mile south of the intersection of County Road 84W and County Road 115, in section 23, Township 3N, Range 73W. No public testimony will be taken.
