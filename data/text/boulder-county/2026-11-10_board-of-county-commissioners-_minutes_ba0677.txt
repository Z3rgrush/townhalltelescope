Community Services - Contract Amendment with Aramark Services Inc for Joe Pelle Center Food Services
1.
b418f83e_891f_86be_8144_161fd17a1b14_signed.pdf
The Board of County Commissioners is asked to approve an amendment to Contract No. 304443 with Aramark Services Inc for food services at the Joe Pelle Center. The amendment extends the contract term by one year, from November 1, 2026 through October 31, 2027, and adds funds to support facility operation at full capacity. The total contract value for this amendment is $1,559,401, funded through the General Fund. Aramark provides food services meeting nutritional health standards for adults and juveniles housed at the facility, including culinary vocational training for clients participating in kitchen and educational programs.

Decision

7.d
Parks and Open Space - PVREA Agreement for Hall Ranch Power Line
1.
PVREA_BOCC_11_10.pdf
Poudre Valley Rural Electric Association (“PVREA”) will be conducting a large project to underground overhead power lines that cross Hall Ranch open space. This work will take place across three phases through Winter 2029. PVREA’s existing power line sits within an existing implied easement that will be formalized once the work is complete. Upon completion of the underground line, the easement will be defined and recorded based on the as-built underground alignment. The conveyance of the express easement will define and clarify the scope of PVREA’s existing implied easement, and therefore no value will be attributed to the as-built easement.
Staff Contact(s):
Aaron Clark, Parks & Open Space
