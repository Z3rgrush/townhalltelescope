Human Resources department is requesting a contract with Unum Insurance Company for group life and disability insurance for Boulder County employees. Unum shall provide fully insured Life Insurance and Short- and Long-Term Disability Insurance products, along with administrative support services, to ensure accurate enrollment, eligibility management, claims processing, and member support for Client employees. The term of this contract is from 01/01/2027-12/31/2029. The cost for these services shall not exceed $813,034.00 per year, for a total not-to-exceed amount of $2,439,102.00.

Parks and Open Space - Russell-2026 Restrictive Covenant
1.
Russell_Restrictive_Cov_Grantor_Signed.pdf
2.
BOCC_Business_Mtg_Memo_Russell_Restrictive_Covenant_10_20_26_initialed.pdf
The landowners of the 50-acre Russell conservation easement, located north of the Town of Lyons, would like to tie it together with their adjacent 5-acre house lot. A restrictive covenant would tie the parcels together, requiring them to always be conveyed together and preventing annexation of the house lot. Staff recommends approval of this request. This document is needed to complete the transaction.

Melissa Arnold, Parks and Open Space

Sheriff's Office - RESOLUTION 2026-069 RESCINDING STAGE 1 FIRE RESTRICTIONS IN THE MOUNTAIN AREA OF BOULDER COUNTY
1.
81b2eb31_5a3c_87d0_80ab_4eac67486554_signed.pdf
Effective October 15, 2026m Stage 1 Fire Restrictions for the mountain area of Boulder County are hereby rescinded.
The mountain area of Boulder County includes any and all unincorporated areas of Boulder County west of Colorado Highway 93 from its intersection with the southern boundary of Boulder County up to and including its intersection with Colorado Highway 119; west of Broadway Avenue in the City of Boulder from its intersection with Colorado Highway 119 up to and including its intersection with United States Highway 36; west of United States Highway 36 from its intersection with Broadway Avenue up to its intersection with the northern boundary of Boulder County; and west of the western boundary of Rabbit Mountain Open Space up to and including United States Highway 36.
Action Requested:
Decision
Staff Contact(s):
Seth McKinney, Sheriff's Office

Community Services - Mental and Behavioral Health Tax Recommended Grant Awards
1.
BOCC_MBH_Tax_Grant_Awards_Recommendation_Memo_10_9_26.pdf
2.
1B_Evaluation_Metrics_Table.pdf
Boulder County is implementing the voter‑approved 2025 Mental and Behavioral Health Tax to provide dedicated funding that strengthens the behavioral health continuum through prevention, peer recovery, treatment, and equitable access to services. This competitive grant cycle will make strategic investments aligned with voter intent, Board of County Commissioners’ priorities, and identified community needs and disparities, with an emphasis on producing measurable outcomes and tracking key performance indicators to improve access, engagement, recovery, prevention, and treatment results. Community Services recommends grant awards totaling $4,088,901.60 annually across 2026–2028, structured as an integrated portfolio across Prevention, Peer Recovery, and Treatment.
