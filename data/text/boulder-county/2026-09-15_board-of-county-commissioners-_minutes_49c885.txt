Community Services - Funding Agreement for AAHT Grant to Boulder County Housing Authority
1.
Affordable_Housing_Grant_Program_Funding_Agreement_2025_BCHA_Willoughby.pdf
The Board of County Commissioners awarded $1,084,000 in Boulder County Affordable and Attainable Housing Grant funds to the Boulder County Housing Authority (Contract No. 800283) to support development of Willoughby Corner Phases II and III at 928 Canterbury Drive in Lafayette. Phase IIA will deliver 32 rental units serving households with intellectual and developmental disabilities at an average of 48% AMI, with a Certificate of Occupancy required by December 31, 2027. Phase IIB will provide 48 multifamily rental units at 60–80% AMI, with a Certificate of Occupancy required by December 31, 2028; both phases must maintain affordability requirements for a minimum of 15 years following completion. Phase III will add 73 affordable for-sale units to be made available through the Boulder Regional Affordable Homeownership Program within 12 months of the expenditure date. The contract term runs from December 1, 2025, through December 31, 2031.

Michelle Alexander, Housing
4.b
Community Services - U.S. Department of Housing & Urban Development Continuum of Care Grant Agreement
Renewal agreement for the Continuum of Care Rapid Re-housing Program in the amount of $956,189. There is a 25% match allocation ($239,047); Boulder County Housing Authority will use its Housing Stabilization Program Affordable and Attainable Housing Tax (Fund 152) dollars to meet the match requirement. The Continuum of Care Rapid Re-housing program fills the gap in Boulder County’s current housing continuum by offering a Housing and Supports resource for two targeted vulnerable populations: (1) families experiencing literal homelessness and (2) transition-aged youth experiencing literal homelessness. Boulder County Housing Authority partners with the Metro Denver Housing Initiative (MDHI) and use their coordinated entry system, OneHome, to prioritize support. The goal of the program is: (1) to stabilize housing for these populations for at least 6 months after program exit; (2) ensure enrollment in high level programs like SNAP, Medicaid, and TANF; and (3) ensure linkages to job training and education opportunity with the goal of increasing household income.

Gwen Mossman, Housing

Community Services - USAging Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute Comparative Study Opportunity Grant Application
1.
BOCC_USAging_CSD_AAA_PCORI_Grant_Application.pdf
The Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute funded initiative is a five year comparative study evaluating two online dementia caregiver support programs. Led by USAging and the University of Minnesota, the project aims to build evidence for effective caregiver tools and inform service strategies across the Aging Network. The Community Services Department requests approval to apply for the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute funded comparative study opportunity, administered by USAging and the University of Minnesota School of Public Health. Participating Area Agencies on Aging will recruit caregivers of individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease or related dementias and support their use of one of two online caregiver support platforms. This work will strengthen local caregiver resources and contribute practical, actionable findings to the national Aging Network. Applying to receive $25,000 annually and intend on applying for inclusion in the Advisory Group, which provides an additional $2,000 per year.
Staff Contact(s):
Carol Cross, Community Services

Human Services - 2027 IDD ACL Funding Approval Memo
1.
2027_idd_acl_funding_approval_memo.pdf
Boulder County Human Services (BCHS) is requesting Board approval of $262,078 in Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities (IDD) Mill Levy funding for the Association for Community Living (ACL) for the 2027 contract term. The amount reflects a 3% cost-of-living adjustment over the 2026 contract and will be structured as a funding agreement rather than a procurement contract. ACL is the sole provider of specialized, community-based IDD advocacy services in Boulder County and has served in this role since 1964 as an official chapter of The Arc. Because no other agency in Boulder County provides this service, BCHS is requesting approval to award the funding outside of a competitive solicitation process. Individual advocacy services were identified as a top priority in the IDD Needs Assessment, which guided this funding request. BCHS will conduct an annual review of eligible agencies and return to the Board each year with a similar funding request.

Georgina Becerril, Human Services; Whitney Wilcox, Human Services
