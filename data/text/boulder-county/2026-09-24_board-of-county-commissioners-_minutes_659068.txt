Board of County Commissioners - Regular Meeting - September 24, 2026
1.
Call to Order
The Board of County Commissioners of Boulder County met in Regular Meeting in full conformity with the law and resolution of said Board at the regular place of hybrid meetings, 1325 Pearl Street, Boulder and on Zoom Webinar, on September 24, 2026.
The meeting was called to order at approximately 9:01 a.m. by Chair Claire Levy.
Commissioners Levy and Loachamin attended virtually.

9:00 a.m. Morning Session

Public Hearing on Hummel Farmstead Historic Landmark Designation

Community Planning and Permitting Department Docket HP-26-0004: Hummel Farmstead Historic Landmark Designation; Boulder County Historic Landmark Designation of a site. The application is submitted by the City of Boulder (applicant/owner), the subject property is in the Rural Residential (RR) zoning district at 3902 N 63rd Street, in Section 14, T1N, R70W of the 6th Principal Meridian.
This public hearing was called to order at 9:01 a.m.
Staff Presenter(s):
Jessica McKinnon, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Other Presenter(s):
Julie Johnson, City of Boulder
Christian Driver, City of Boulder
Public Speaker(s):
No one gave comment during the public hearing portion of this docket.

Moved by
Commissioner Stolzmann
Seconded by
Commissioner Loachamin
to conditionally approve Docket HP-26-0004 in accordance with staff recommendations, commitments of record by applicant, and with additional conditions as discussed and contained within the official record.
