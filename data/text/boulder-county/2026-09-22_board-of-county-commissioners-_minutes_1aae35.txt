5.a
Community Services - Community Action Programs (CAP) Grant Continuation Application to Department of Local Affairs (DOLA)
1.
Department_of_Local_Affairs_CSD_CAP_Grant.pdf
The Boulder County Community Services Department is seeking Board approval to submit a renewal application to the Colorado Department of Local Affairs (DOLA) for continuation of the Community Services Block Grant (CSBG), which funds the Community Action Programs (CAP) division. The grant period would run from October 1, 2026, through October 1, 2029, with a total three-year request of approximately $1,027,999. The first-year funding request is $341,181, covering 4.0 FTE personnel and operating costs, with no required county match. CAP uses these funds to promote self-sufficiency for low-income individuals and families through three program areas: Personal Investment Enterprise (PIE), People Engaged in Raising Leaders (PERL), and the Cultural Broker Program. Notable changes from the prior grant cycle include elimination of the CAP Division Manager position as part of 2027 budget reductions and a pending countywide expansion of the Cultural Broker Program using existing grant dollars.
Staff Contact(s):
Robin Bohannan, Community Services

Sheriff's Office - CO Dept of Public Safety VOCA Grant Agreement (2026-VA-27-535-20)
1.
Dept of Public Safety VOCA Grant Agreement (2026-VA-27-535-20).pdf
Consideration of approval of a renewal grant agreement (2026-VA-27-535-20) with the Colorado Department of Public Safety, Division of Criminal Justice, under the federal Victims of Crime Act (VOCA) Program. The grant award totals $110,000 in federal funding from the U.S. Department of Justice, covering the period of January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027. Funds will support Boulder County's Multi-Agency Victim Assistance Program, which deploys volunteer victim advocates around the clock to provide trauma-informed services to victims of crime and other traumatic events, including accidents, house fires, unattended deaths, and medical emergencies.
