This is a public meeting where the Board of County Commissioners receives information, reviews proposals, and discusses topics in greater detail often prior to making a decision in a later public meeting. These sessions allow commissioners and staff to explore issues, ask questions, and consider options in a collaborative setting. The commissioners do not generally take formal action or vote during a work session.
No public testimony will be taken
. The meeting recording will act as the minutes.
