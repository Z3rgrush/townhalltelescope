County Commissioners’ Town Hall (Lafayette/East County)
Residents are invited to join the Boulder County Commissioners for a Town Hall on Tuesday, Sept. 15, from 5:30 to 7 p.m.
In recognition of September as Disaster Preparedness Month, the Town Hall will feature a brief presentation from the Boulder Office of Disaster Management on practical tips and information to help residents prepare for emergencies and unexpected events. The commissioners will then take questions on this topic and any other county-related topics brought up by residents.
Please note:
there will not be a virtual option for this Town Hall, however, all residents who cannot attend are always welcome to email
commissioners@bouldercounty.gov
with questions or to request materials shared at the event.
