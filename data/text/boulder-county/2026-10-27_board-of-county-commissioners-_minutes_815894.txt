1.
2026_10_21_Viramontes_v_Cook_County.pdf
Boulder County is requesting ratification of its participation along with other jurisdictions in an amicus brief in Viramontes v. Cook County, pending before the U.S. Supreme Court. The amicus brief supports Cook County, Illinois’s ban on certain semiautomatic firearms, including AR-15-style rifles. The Court’s decision could affect the decision in Rocky Mountain Gun Owners v. Town of Superior, et. al, Civil Action No. 22-cv-02680-NYW-TPO, in the plaintiffs have challenged Boulder County’s assault weapons ordinance.

6.c
Office of Sustainability and Climate Resilience - Applications for State Electric Vehicle Charging Grants
1.
BOCC_Memo_Applications_State_EV_Charging_Grants.pdf
OSCAR seeks Board approval for three grant applications to the state through the Charge Ahead Colorado program and the Direct Current Fast Charging Plazas program: $203,000 for Level 2 EV charging at seven multifamily properties; $112,000 for Level 2 EV charging at four public municipal properties; and $1.4 million for L3 EV charging at one public municipal property and two retail properties. Boulder County will coordinate the grant applications but will not own or operate the charging stations or have an ownership interest in them. The required matching funds for this grant will be provided by the owners of the charging stations (i.e. EV charging vendor or property owners). OSCAR also plans to contribute funding in 2027 to support the charging infrastructure through the sustainability tax. These funds are already included in OSCAR's 2027 budget request; no additional county funds are requested.
Staff Contact(s):
Zac Swank,
Office of Sustainability and Climate Resilience

6.f
Parks and Open Space - Dowe Flats-CEMEX Operations Parcels Acquisition
1.
BOCC_OpsParcel_Memo_102726.pdf
2.
CEMEX_Operations_Parcel_Exercise_Letter_docx.pdf
Staff recommend that Boulder County exercise its option to acquire from CEMEX, Inc. 613 acres referred to as the “Operations Parcel.” The property is located approximately three-tenths a mile north of Highway 66 on North 53rd Street east of Lyons. If the county exercises its option and pays the required $1,500,000 for the parcel, CEMEX is required to convey the 613-acre property to the county. Boulder County’s option to acquire the 613-acre Operations Parcel was established by a 2002 agreement. The county has been holding this option for more than 20 years in anticipation of eventually owning the property. Acquiring it will augment county open space associated with the Rabbit Mountain B1 High Biodiversity Area.

Tina Burghardt, Parks and Open Space
6.g
Sheriff's Office - DOLA CDBG-DR Mitigation Evacuation Study Grant Application
1.
DOLA_CDBG_DR_Mitigation_Evacuation_Study_Grant_Application.pdf
The grant will fund the purchase of Ladris Software allowing ODM staff, first responders and planners to test scenarios directly rather than commissioning a new study for each problem set or evacuation question. The software contains road network data, lane counts, road type, one-way and contraflow status and construction or road blockage can be introduced to a scenario, so clearance-time results reflect conditions. The software evacuation model has been reviewed through a research partnership with Old Dominion University and publication by the Transportation Research Board of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine and this approach is already in use for evacuation and wildfire planning elsewhere in Colorado. The education and capacity building component of the program is intended to leave the County with more than a document. The clearance-time analysis produced by the polygon risk assessment and feasibility and analysis extend directly into a standing local capability. Evacuation clearance-time thresholds generate evacuation-order recommendations as an incident unfolds and flagging which zones cannot clear before the fire arrival. This is the capacity-building deliverable of the gran, and durable, locally maintained response capability built directly on the funded planning work.
Staff Contact(s):
Mike Chard, Sheriff's Office
6.h
Sheriff's Office - DOLA Wildfire Initiated Community Conflagration Grant Application
1.
DOLA_Wildfire_Initiated_Community_Conflagration_Grant_Application.pdf
Grant funding will provide for Curriculum Contractor to develop Wildfire Initiated Community Conflagration curriculum for training first responders. The developed curriculum will cover the following capability areas: Understanding the science, hazard, risk and vulnerabilities that are associated with the devastating WICC incidents, Alert and Warning systems, law enforcement management of urban evacuations, effective fire control methodologies and practices of WICC incidents, C-5 Practices, mission command and leaders intent, and EOC Operation implementing effective operational support, coordination and managing multi-jurisdictional and agencies during fast moving urban conflagrations.
Staff Contact(s):
Mike Chard, Sheriff's Office
