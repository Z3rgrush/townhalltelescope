6.c
Community Services - Contract with Boulder Valley School District for Mental and Behavioral Health Tax Direct Funding
1.
7000037_BVSD_CSD_SI_New_Contract_1B_MBH.pdf
Consideration for an $800,000 contract with the Boulder Valley School District (BVSD) to fund school-based mental health services, effective June 16 through December 31, 2026. Funding is sourced from the Boulder County 1B Mental and Behavioral Health Tax and will support Wellness Centers at six high schools and one pilot middle school location, providing students with tiered mental health support ranging from universal access to intensive referral-based services. The contract also funds two Mental Health Advocates serving K–12 students with more intensive behavioral health needs across up to four Boulder County schools, as well as a Student Support Services Coordinator to provide district-wide leadership and coordination. All services will be delivered in a culturally and linguistically appropriate manner and will not supplant existing Medicaid, Medicare, or private insurance-funded services.
Staff Contact(s):
Marnie Huffman-Green, Community Services

County Administrator's Office - Longmont Urban Renewal Authority Tax Increment Funding Agreement
1.
TIF Sharing IGA for Longmont URA re 1st_Main (002).pdf
Tax Increment Funding agreement between Boulder County and Longmont Urban Renewal Authority. This agreement is intended to fund the redevelopment of an area the City desires to designate as an urban renewal area, namely the First and Main Urban Renewal Plan, to be redeveloped by one or more developers and/or property owner(s) as a mixed-use development(s) that will eliminate existing blighted conditions which constitute threats to the health, safety and welfare of the community and barriers to development. The City desires to approve the First and Main Urban Renewal Plan in order to authorize the utilization of tax increment financing in accordance with the Colorado Urban Renewal Law, Part 1 of Article 25 of Title 31, C.R.S., to pay Eligible Costs of the Improvements.

6.g
Human Resources - Marathon Health Bid Waiver
1.
Bid Waiver for Marathon.pdf
Human Resources is requesting a two-year waiver of bid requirements allowing contracting with Marathon Health from Jan. 1, 2027 -Dec. 31, 2028, for a not-to-exceed amount of $2,203,374 which we anticipate will be less than the amount of money we’ll save from having claims run through Aetna, ultimately resulting in savings rather than additional expenditure. A bid waiver is requested due to the unique nature of Marathon’s clinic model and their broad network of clinics across Colorado. In a year where we are looking at significant increases to medical premiums, Marathon clinics will offer a free option for our members to seek care. This is in no way a replacement for utilizing our Aetna insurance with other providers, it is simply another cost-effective option for our members to utilize.
Staff Contact(s):
Kurtis Alberi, County Administrator's Office
