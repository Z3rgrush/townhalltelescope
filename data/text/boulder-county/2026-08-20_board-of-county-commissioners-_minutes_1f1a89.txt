Board of County Commissioners - Regular Meeting - August 20, 2026
1.
Call to Order
The Board of County Commissioners of Boulder County met in Regular Meeting in full conformity with the law and resolution of said Board at the regular place of hybrid meetings, 1325 Pearl Street, Boulder and on Zoom Webinar, on August 20, 2026.
The meeting was called to order at approximately 9:05 a.m. by Chair Claire Levy.
Commissioners Stolzmann was excused from the Public Hearings on Thursday, August 20, 2026.
2.
9:00 a.m. Morning Session
2.a
Public Hearing on Osterholm-DiGiulian Accessory Dwelling Unit and Residential Additions
1.
LU-26-0005-SPR-26-0015-staff-recommendation-packet-red.pdf
Community Planning and Permitting Department Docket LU-26-0005/SPR-26-0015: Osterholm-DiGiulian Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) and Residential Additions; Limited Impact Special Review to permit construction of a family care unit ADU, and Site Plan Review to permit a one-time 165-square-foot residential addition on a 4-acre parcel in the Forestry zoning district. The application is submitted by Scott Giles (applicant/agent) and Sasha DiGiulian and Erik Osterholm (owners). The subject property is in the Forestry (F) zoning district at 274 S. Cedar Brook Road, approximately 0.25 miles south of the intersection of S. Cedar Brook Road and Linden Avenue, in Section 23, Township 1N, Range 71W.
This public hearing was called to order at 9:05 a.m.
Staff Presenter(s):
Sam Walker, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Other Presenter(s):
Erik Osterholm, Owner
Scott Giles, Agenda
County Attorneys Present:
Erica Rogers
Public Speaker(s):
No one gave comment during the public hearing portion of this docket.
The commissioners went into executive session from 9:51-10:02 a.m.
The commissioners' public hearing adjourned at 10:19 a.m.
Moved by
Commissioner Levy
Seconded by
Commissioner Loachamin
to go into an Executive Session with County Attorneys, pursuant to CRS 24-6-402(4)(b) to discuss legal matters related to Docket LU-26-0005/SPR-26-0015 on August 20, 2026, at approximately 9:51 a.m.
Carried
Moved by
Commissioner Loachamin
Seconded by
Commissioner Levy
to deny Docket LU-26-0005/SPR-26-0015 as discussed and contained within the official record.
Carried
3.
1:00 p.m. Afternoon Session
3.a
Public Hearing on Riverside Cabins Lodge and Boundary Line Adjustment Revocation
1.
SU-23-0013 BOCC Staff Report for August 20, 2026.pdf
Community Planning and Permitting Department Docket SU-23-0013: Riverside Cabins Lodge and Boundary Line Adjustment Revocation; Public Hearing to consider revocation of the Special Review approval of docket SU-23-0013 (Resolution 2026-10) due to the violation of conditions of approval, pursuant to Article 4-601.B of the Boulder County Land Use Code. The property owner is Rockmont Investments LLC. The subject properties are located at 16188,16190, 16194, and 16202 St. Vrain Drive in the Forestry zoning district.
This public hearing was called to order at 1:03 p.m.
Staff Presenter(s):
Jesse Rounds, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Sam Walker, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Summer Frederick, Community Planning and Permitting Department
Other Presenter(s):
Micah Kohls, Representative
County Attorneys Present:
Liana Larremore
Public Speaker(s):
Russ Landow, Golden
Cat Oehlman, Lyons
Sarah Legh, Lyons
Steve Pischke, Lyons Fire Protection District
The commissioners' public hearing adjourned at 2:43 p.m.
Moved by
Commissioner Loachamin
regarding SU-23-0013, to not revoke at this time, but continue this hearing in the event there is any future violation with the acknowledgement that the applicant has a conditioned approval so if they meet those conditions they would notify staff and they would then have a use.
Not carried
Moved by
Commissioner Levy
Seconded by
Commissioner Loachamin
to not accept the recommendation of revocation of the special use and instead continue this hearing to January 7, 2027, at 1 p.m. for purposes of determining whether to revoke the special use permit. In the meantime, if the applicants are able to complete all the work and satisfy all the conditions of approval and submit satisfactory evidence of that and receive written confirmation from Community Planning and Permitting that all the conditions of the special use have been met, the applicants may revive their website and make use of the special use that previously granted.
Carried
