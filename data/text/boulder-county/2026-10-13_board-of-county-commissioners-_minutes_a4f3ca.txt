Consideration of an $800,000 contract (No. 700038) with St. Vrain Valley School District to provide mental and behavioral health services in Boulder County schools. Funded by the Boulder County 1B Mental and Behavioral Health Tax, the contract runs from June 18, 2026 through December 31, 2026. Under the agreement, St. Vrain Valley School District will deploy approximately 7.0 FTE behavior specialists to provide classroom-based supports, assess root causes of student behavioral health needs, and design evidence-based interventions. Services will prioritize Boulder County schools where counselor staffing has been reduced, while remaining available to any Boulder County school in need.

4.g
Human Resources - Marathon Health Bid Waiver
1.
Bid_Waiver_for_Marathon.pdf
Human Resources is requesting a two-year waiver of bid requirements allowing contracting with Marathon Health from Jan. 1, 2027 -Dec. 31, 2028, for a not-to-exceed amount of $2,203,374 which we anticipate will be less than the amount of money we’ll save from having claims run through Aetna, ultimately resulting in savings rather than additional expenditure. A bid waiver is requested due to the unique nature of Marathon’s clinic model and their broad network of clinics across Colorado. In a year where we are looking at significant increases to medical premiums, Marathon clinics will offer a free option for our members to seek care. This is in no way a replacement for utilizing our Aetna insurance with other providers, it is simply another cost-effective option for our members to utilize.
4.h
Human Resources - Progyny Bid Waiver
1.
Bid_Waiver_for_Progyny.pdf
Human Resources is requesting a one-year waiver of bid requirements allowing contracting with Progyny from Jan. 1-Dec. 31, 2027, for a not-to-exceed amount of $560,000. A bid waiver is requested due to the County’s established program with Progyny and the critical continuity‑of‑care needs for current members. A number of our plan members are presently in the midst of complex fertility treatment cycles or transfer processes that cannot be interrupted without medical, financial, and emotional harm. Changing vendors during active treatment would risk loss of established clinical care teams, an interruption in authorized Smart Cycle services, potential delays in medication access, and treatment plan changes that could negatively impact outcomes.
4.i
Human Resources - ZERO Health Bid Waiver
1.
2027_Bid_Waiver_for_ZERO.pdf
Human Resources is requesting a one-year waiver of bid requirements allowing contracting with ZERO Health from Jan. 1-Dec. 31, 2027, for a not-to-exceed amount of $610,000. ZERO is unique in that it offers reduced-cost imaging and other lower dollar items like lab work, in addition to surgeries. ZERO is focused on smaller regions, Colorado being one of a few, and they are also uniquely suited in Northern Colorado where we often face less competition with providers. ZERO’s offering covered more procedures, had a larger provider network, and offered far greater savings over Cigna’s Condition Specific Care program. We have been closely monitoring the vendor landscape in the medical claims bundling space, and there still isn’t another vendor that offers the breadth of services and providers that ZERO is able to offer in Colorado.

5.b
Community Planning and Permitting - Proposition 123 Expedited Review Process Compliance
1.
cpp_bocc_fast_track_packet.pdf
By December 31, 2026, local governments must demonstrate that they have an expedited review process in place for housing projects where at least half of the units are affordable to remain eligible for Proposition 123 funding in the next 3-year cycle (2027-2029). Staff request approval from the Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) regarding the proposed compliance action for Proposition 123’s expedited review requirement, also referred to as “Fast Track.” Staff propose that Boulder County adopt a resolution which acknowledges that Boulder County’s current review processes already meet the 90-day review requirement and establishes a policy for an expedited review process for housing projects where at least half of the units are affordable. If the BOCC approves the proposed compliance action, staff have submitted a resolution that the BOCC can approve documenting this decision.

6.a
Parks & Open Space - Xcel Energy Easement
1.
Jafay_GPID_Board_Memo_10_1_26_Final_js.pdf
Xcel Energy proposes to acquire a permanent non-exclusive easement over 1.2 acres of the Jafay and Heatherwood Notch open space properties (“Property”) for installation of underground power lines. The Property is located near the intersections of 75th Street and Lookout Road. Xcel is working on a program of undergrounding power lines in areas where fire danger is high during wind events. The Property is in an Xcel priority area for undergrounding because the lines on the Property are frequently depowered during PSPS (Public Safety Power Shutoff) events. Xcel is requesting 20 feet of right-of-way on the eastern side of 75th Street for the easement to comply with setback requirements and to avoid the gutter that runs through the existing right-of-way on the eastern side of 75th.
The Board of County Commissioners sat as the Gunbarrel Public Improvement District Board of Directors for Item No. 6.a and then reconvened as the Board of County Commissioners.

Public Hearing on Parks and Open Space Real Estate Acquisitions
1.
Sunshine_FPD_BOCC_Memo_10_1_26.pdf
Sunshine Fire Protection District proposes to acquire a permanent non-exclusive easement over 0.07 acres of the Bald Mountain open space property for installation of a 40,000-gallon cistern that will store water to fight fires in the area. The Property is located at 4111 Sunshine Canyon Drive near the intersections of Sunshine Canyon Drive and County Road 83. This request from SFPD is part of a project to install several cisterns in the Sunshine Fire Protection District's service area to assist with firefighting in areas that do not have easy access to the volumes of water required to combat a fire.
