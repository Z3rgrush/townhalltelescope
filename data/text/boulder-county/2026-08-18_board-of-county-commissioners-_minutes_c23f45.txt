This iteration of the Core Services Plan is for the third year of a three-year plan for the use of Core funds. Colorado counties receive centralized state-administered funding to implement the Core Services Program, which is designed to keep children safe in the least restrictive environment. The Core Services themselves consist of ten short-term, family-driven interventions (such as home-based intervention, intensive family therapy, life skills training, and emergency economic assistance) aimed at strengthening families and maintaining child safety. Boulder County must submit a Core Plan each year to explain how these funds will be used.

Human Services - 2026-2028 Nurturing Futures Direct Cash Assistance Program with AidKit
1.
2026_27_aidkit_bchs_direct_cash_assistance_exhibit_b_304839.pdf
2.
BC_Exhibit_A_AidKit_Nurturing_Futures_Direct_Cash_Assistance_Program_2729.pdf
The Board of County Commissioners is asked to approve an original contract with AidKit, Inc. (Contract No. 304839) in the amount of $3,770,409.40 to administer the Nurturing Futures Direct Cash Assistance Program, running August 18, 2026 through March 31, 2028. The Program will provide monthly cash assistance to eligible low-income Boulder County households with children ages birth through three, funded through American Rescue Plan Act interest dollars. During a Bridge Period (October–December 2026), up to 528 currently enrolled households will receive $300 per month; during an Extension Period (January–December 2027), up to 410 households will receive $600 per month. AidKit will also maintain the existing program application platform through December 2027 to support data collection and program evaluation. This contract follows the Direct Cash Assistance Pilot Program (Contract No. 303453) and is intended to provide a seamless continuation of cash assistance without a gap in disbursements.

On November 10, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners approved an option agreement with Zweck LLC for the purchase of 210 acres of land and associated water rights at 11009 Airport Road, Unincorporated Boulder County, just west of incorporated Longmont. That option agreement was signed on May 30, 2006 and amended on August 4, 2026. For the past 20 years, the county has made option payments to Zweck LLC and on May 30, 2026, the county exercised its option to purchase the property with the closing scheduled for August 27, 2026. This document will provide the County access to the southern part of the Zweck property and is necessary for closing.
