County Attorney's Office - Employee Legal Representation Agreement for Henry Trujillo
1.
Moscow239 - LRA Trujillo, Henry.pdf
David Moscow a former detainee in the Boulder County Jail has filed a lawsuit alleging claims for state civil rights violations against Commander Henry Trujillo. Among other claims, Mr. Moscow alleges Commander Trujillo denied him a Kosher diet during his detention. Based on the information currently available, Commander Trujillo acted within the scope and course of their employment and are entitled to representation by the County Attorney.

Kevin McCaffrey, County Attorney's Office
