I. Call to Order 
a. The meeting was called to order by the Director at 2:01PM 
II. Roll Call: 
a. All Board Members listed below were present 
i. Steve Indrehus, Tommyknocker Brewery  
ii. Tara Worley, Marigolds Flowers  
iii. Jennie Kim, 6&40 Motel  
iv. Lana Hearne, Club Hotel-  
v. Andy Marsh, City Administrator  
III. Approval of the Agenda 
a. Lana Hearne made a motion to approve the agenda of the July 20th meeting 
b. Steve Indrehus, 2nd, all in favor, motion passes 
IV. Approval of the Minutes of June 15th 2026 
a. Steve Indrehus made a motion to approve the minutes of the June 15th 2026, meeting 
b. Lana Hearne, 2nd, all in favor, motion passes 
V. Public Comments  
a. A representative of the Libertarian Party of Clear Creek County requested that the Business & 
Community Promotions Board consider making a recommendation to City Council to include 
vehicles displaying Disabled Veteran license plates in the managed parking program's free 
parking provisions. The representative noted that vehicles displaying handicap placards and 
plates currently receive free parking and requested that Disabled Veteran plates receive the same 
consideration. 
VI. Conflict of Interest: None reported 
VII. Director Report 
a. Summer Tourism & Marketing Momentum: Marketing efforts continue to generate strong 
results, with significant growth across social media, successful influencer partnerships, and 
continued promotion of Idaho Springs through regional media and the Social Media Co-Op. 
b. Economic Impact Dashboard: The Board received a presentation on the new Colorado School 
of Mines Economic Impact Dashboard, which will provide data-driven insights into visitation, 
sales tax, lodging, recreation, and tourism trends to support future decision-making. 
c. Fall Event Planning: Planning continues for several major fall events, including RapidGrass at 
the Mighty ARGO, the inaugural Taste of Idaho Springs & Music Festival, Halloween 
programming. 
d. Visitor Management & Downtown Planning: Updates were provided on the Visitor 
Management Plan, Crisis Communications planning, Downtown Master Plan, parking 
improvements, and discussions surrounding Historic District design standards to guide future 
downtown enhancements. 
 
 
 
 
 
Page 4 of 5
e. Board & Community Engagement: The Board was informed of Lindsay Valdez's resignation, 
the upcoming Board recruitment process, and continued outreach efforts through community 
conversations, business engagement, and the new "What We Do" guide highlighting BCPB 
programs and services. 
VIII. Round Table Updates from Businesses 
a. Board members and local businesses shared updates on the summer season. Tommyknocker 
Brewery reported a busy summer, performing at approximately the same level as last year, while 
noting that parking capacity continues to be a challenge during peak periods. Club Hotel reported 
continued year-over-year growth, and several retailers indicated sales are exceeding last year's 
performance. Several restaurants shared that business has been slower during the early part of the 
day and particularly in the evenings. Additionally, the owner of the Free Mountain Press, a 
nonprofit community newspaper, introduced himself to the Board and shared information about 
advertising opportunities available for local businesses. 
IX. Presentations: None 
X. Action Items: None 
XI. Closing Remarks 
a. Next regular Meeting Monday August 17th 
b. Meeting adjourned at 3:04pm
