Executive Session
The Open Space Commission Will Move into Executive Session for the Following 
Purposes:
1. Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24 -6-402(4)(f), for the Open Space Commission to discuss 
personnel matters -- specifically to discuss the public lands manager position and 
associated issues. 
2. Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24 -6-402(4)(b), for the Open Space Commission to confer 
with the Assistant County Attorney for the purpose of receiving legal advice on 
specific legal questions related to adding a gate to an access road for the Sheep 
Keep Open Space property.
