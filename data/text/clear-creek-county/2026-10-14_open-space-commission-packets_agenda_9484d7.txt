Executive Session
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(a) and (e), for staff to meet with the Open 
Space Commission in executive session for the purposes of: 1) receiving 
direction related to providing funding to the Town of Georgetown for the 
purchase, acquisition, lease, transfer, or sale of any real, personal, or other 
property interest in Parcel No. 2091-034-00-645 in unincorporated CCC; 
and 2) determining positions relative to matters that may be subject to 
negotiations; or developing strategy for negotiations; or instructing 
negotiators with regard to the aforementioned property.
Action Items
Recommendation to the Board of County Commissioners related to providing funding to 
the Town of Georgetown for the purchase, acquisition, lease, transfer, or sale of any real, 
personal, or other property interest in Parcel No. 2091-034-00-645 in unincorporated Clear 
Creek County.
