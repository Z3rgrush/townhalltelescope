New Business – F Street Mosaic Artist Contracts: Approval of Professional Services Agreements with Jeffrey Erwin, 
Reven Swanson (China Cat Sunflower, LLC), Hannah Tidechild, and Anne Walker for the F Street Planter Mosaic public 
art project 
B
ackground 
The F Street Planter Mosaic project will add permanent mosaic artwork to six large, multi -sided concrete planters with 
integrated seating along F Street, the City's primary downtown corridor and Creative Arts District. Each planter averages 
approximately 110 square feet of surface area. The project is managed by the Arts, Parks, Recreation & Culture (APRC) 
Department with the Salida Arts and Culture Advisory Commission (ACAC). This item asks Council to approve the 
Professional Services Agreements with the artists selected through a competitive call for artists.  
Call for Artists. ACAC member Reed Govert drafted the call for artists, and staff added the City's standard language 
(Exhibit A). The call was posted nationally on CaFÉ (callforentry.org) and promoted through the City's social media, 
website, and newsletter. Proposals were due by 11:59 p.m. MST on August 14, 2026. Artists could apply for one or more 
of the six planters. The call required an artist statement, contact information including county of residence, 1 –4 concept 
images, and 2–4 work samples. Artwork could not include words, advertising, religious, sexual, violent, or politically 
partisan content, and had to be durable, weather-resistant, vandal- and graffiti-resistant, and safe for the public. 
Local preference. The call stated that artists based in the City of Salida would receive preference within a 5% price 
difference, and artists based in Chaffee County within a 3% price difference. Because the City set a fixed fee of $10,000 
per planter, there was no price difference to apply. The jury applied the local preference through the scoring rubric 
instead, as a +10 point bonus for artists located within 30 miles of the Salida city center (Exhibit B).  
Submissions received. Fifteen artists or artist teams submitted proposals. Applicant locations, based on the mailing 
addresses submitted in CaFÉ (Exhibit E), are summarized below. Two applicants (13%) are from Chaffee County, and 13 
of the 15 (87%) are from Colorado.

Key contract terms. Each agreement pays a fixed fee of $10,000 per planter, covering all design, materials, fabrication,

substantial completion; and 10% at final acceptance. The schedule sets a Notice to Proceed of November 2, 2026, 60% 
design packages due November 30, 2026, 90% design packages due December 30, 2026, installation between March 1 
and June 5, 2027 (when nighttime temperatures are above 45°F), and substantial completion by May 28, 2027. A Review 
Committee of Reed Govert, Lindsey Ernst, and Tina Gramann, with Diesel Post as final authority, will review design and 
installation progress at each milestone. Artists must carry $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate general 
liability insurance and automobile liability insurance.

City of Salida Arts & Culture – Call for Artists: F Steet Planters Mosaics 
The City of Salida Arts & Culture Department, with the Salida Arts and Culture Advisory 
Commission (ACAC), invites artists to submit proposals for mosaic artwork to be installed 
on one or more of six, approximately 110 sq ft, large multi-sided outdoor concrete 
planters, with integrated seating, located along F Street, the City's primary downtown 
corridor and Creative Arts District. The city seeks mosaic, tile, or glass designs that 
celebrate and reﬂect Salida's unique culture while enhancing the public's realm. Selected 
artists or artist-led teams will install their work directly onto the existing planters, creating 
vibrant, engaging public art that enriches the pedestrian experience. Artists will be selected 
based on artistic excellence, demonstrated craftsmanship, relevant experience, and the 
creativity, originality, and strength of their proposed concept.   
Submitted designs must be original works of the artist and in compliance with all existing 
copyright laws. Due to the public nature, artwork may not contain words, advertising, 
religious art, sexual, violent or other content unsuitable for all ages and cannot convey 
political partisanship.  
All submissions will be reviewed by the ACAC, assessing compliance with the 
requirements, creativity, and overall quality of the work being submitted. The ACAC will 
consider each submission for its ability to enliven the public area and bring a unique 
concept to the Creative District.  
Located at an elevation of 7,000 feet in the Upper Arkansas River Valley of south-central 
Colorado, Salida is known as the "Heart of the Rockies. " The community is recognized for 
its vibrant arts scene, diverse creative enterprises, rich cultural offerings, and abundant 
outdoor recreation, making it one of Colorado's premier destinations for residents and
