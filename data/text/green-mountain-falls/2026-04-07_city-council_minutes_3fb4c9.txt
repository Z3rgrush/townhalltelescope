1. Call to Order/Roll Call/Pledge of Allegiance 
Mayor Dixon called the  regular meeting to order at  7:00 pm. The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
All Trustees were present except for Trustee King and Trustee Ives. 
 
2. Additions, Deletions, or Corrections to the Agenda 
Mayor Dixon made a motion to accept the agenda as is.  Motion was seconded by Trustee  Bell.  All 
was in favor.  Motion carried.    
 
3. Consent Agenda 
Minutes from the 2026-03-03 and 2026-03-31.  Motion to approve  both set of minutes by Trustee 
Bell and seconded by Trustee Moralez.  All was in favor.  Motion carried.  
 
4. Reports 
Mayor Dixon repo rted that on April 8th the Wildfire Resiliency code will be published in the paper, 
which means 15 days after that a Public Hearing can be held  which puts that on April 23 rd.  Public 
Works Supervisor Kelley Hakes reported that 50 tons of road base was set out, the asphalt work dates 
was posted to the public and 5 potholes were filled.  Town Marshal Sean Goings reported that the 
calls of service were up to 54 from the prior month of 37.  Did receive 5 complaints about speeding 
near pool park, which Marshal Goings reported that another speed camera will be added to that 
general location.  Town Clerk Nicole Woods reported that the STR renewals are over, and 3 spots are 
officially opened.  Currently there is a waitlist of 7 people.  Town Manager Caleb Patterson reported 
that he would like to have an executive session held regarding the El Paso Trail and gave out some 
dates that would work.  The audit has begun and we are doing some testing, which has gone well.

None 
 
 
 
 
6. BUSINESS ITEMS 
a. Special Events Permit - 4th of July –  Jesse Stroope presented the information rega rding 
the Green Mountain Falls July 4th Celebration.  Trustee Bell moved that the special event 
application for the 4th of July gets approved.  Trustee Moralez seconded.  All was in favor.  
Motion carried.  It was later brought up regarding the cost for traffic control for the 
parade.  Mayor Dixon made a motion to modify the approval for the special events permit 
application for Historic Green Mountain Falls Foundation  4th of July Celebration to add 
the fee of $600.00.  Trustee Moralez seconded.  All was in favor.  Motion carried.   
b. Special Events Permit – Green Box – Jesse Stroope presented dates that Green Box would 
be utilizing the pool.   Trustee Bell moved to accept the application wi th the $1200 pool 
fee.  Seconded by Trustee Moralez.  All was in favor.  Motion carried.  
c. Resolution 2026-06 Appointment to Outside Agency - Motion was made by Trustee Bell 
to accept Mr. McMillian’s application to be a member of the Community Development 
Advisory Board, representing Green Mountain Falls.  Seconded by Trustee Moralez .  All 
was in favor.  Motion carried.  
d. Ordinance 2026-04 BoT Meeting Time Change  - Trustee Bell moved to accept the BoT 
Meeting time change from 7:00p.m. to 6:30p.m .  Seconded by Trustee Moralez.  All was 
in favor.  Motion carried.  
e. Ute Pass Elementary School Proposal for Dog Waste Dispensers – Motion to approve the 
proposal for the installation of the dog waste receptacles was made by Trustee Moralez.  
Seconded by Trustee Bell.  All was in favor.  Motion carried.  
f. Resolution 2026- 05 – Declaring April as Child Abuse Prevention Month – Motion to 
accept the Resolution 2026-05 was made by Trustee Bell.  Seconded by Trustee Moralez.  
All was in favor.  Motion carried.  
g. 2026 Ballot Items- Mayor Dixon made a motion to put on the ballot a sales tax increase 
of .05% for four years  to go into the Capital Improvement Fund .  Seconde d by Trustee 
Moralez.  The motion was modified by Mayor Dixon to  move for half a percent for four 
years to be used in Capital Improvement Funds, with the priority being the old Marshall’s 
Office and other items that are on the capital improvement plan.  Seconded by Trustee 
Bell.  All was in favor.  Motion carried.
