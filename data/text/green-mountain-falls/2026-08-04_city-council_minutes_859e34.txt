1. Call to Order/Roll Call/Pledge of Allegiance 
Mayor Dixon called the  regular meeting to order at  6:30 pm. The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
All Trustees were present. 
 
2. Additions, Deletions, or Corrections to the Agenda 
Mayor Dixon made a deletion to the agenda for item 6A .  Trustee Moralez made a motion to accept 
the agenda as is  with the deletion of 6A.  Motion was seconded by Trustee  Bell.  All was in favor.  
Motion carried.    
 
3. Consent Agenda 
Minutes from the 2026-06-02, 2026-07-07 and 2026-07-17.  Motion to approve all minutes with the 
corrections to July 7th minutes was made by Mayor Dixon and seconded by Trustee Moralez.  All was 
in favor.  Motion carried.  
 
4. Reports 
Trustee Bell mentioned how they have been working on the mushrooms and ¾ of the acre is done.  
Mayor Dixon reported that all of the events in July were very successful and went smoothly.  Marshal 
Goings reported that there were 53 service calls answered.  The speed camera issued 815 tickets and 
of those 473 were paid.  Public Works Supervisor Kelley Hakes reported that all backflows had been 
checked, oil changes in the vehicles, and the shop and yard had been cleaned up.  Town Clerk Nicole 
Woods reported that the transition from QuickBooks to Caselle was  still a work in progress, but 
getting closer to having all the information that was transferred over be in the correct accounts.  The 
Columbine Pour House got their liquor license approved.  Town Manager Caleb  Patterson reported 
that he was in the process of applying for a grant on behalf of the Marshal’s office to replace a patrol 
vehicle and add a drone.   The draft copy of the audit is completed and will see that within the next 
month.

None
