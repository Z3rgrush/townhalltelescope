4. Reports 
Trustee Bell mentioned how Fire Mitigation has had a very busy month.  Trustee Bell also brought up 
how other states and counties are starting to implement an early fire detection system to help catch 
fires earlier on.  Mayor Dixon reported that the Board of Health voted 7 to 1 to remove the transfer 
of title resolution for El Paso, which will take effective July 11th.  Town Marshal Goings reported that 
there were the same number of calls as the previous month; 48. Through the Meta Traffic Cameras 
472 citations were issued in May and 357 have been paid  as of now.  The Marshall’s office is d own 
one patrol car.  Public Works Supervisor Kelley Hakes reported  that the new pavement project was 
completed, totaling 78 parking spots available in the downtown area.   Town Clerk Nicole Woods 
reported that the transition from QuickBooks to Caselle was in progress, which was a lot of 
information that had to be transferred over.  (Cantina Verde renewed their liquor license and there 
are currently 2 STR spots available).  Town Manager Caleb Patterson reported that the audit will start 
soon and if everything goes well, we should be on time.  The swimming pool now has staff,  and 
training will be starting on the 8th of June for the opening date of June 18th.  The days and hours for 
the pool will be Thursday through Sunday 11am-5pm.

7) DISCUSSION ITEMS 
           a) Attorney Memo Regarding Annexation of Enclaves - A Memo was  presented from the 
Town’s attorney regarding this.  A discussion was held regarding what the next steps would be.  The 
direction that was given was to reassign the annexation to the Planning Commission  to begin 
annexation of the property and to notify the owners.   
           b) Attorney Memo  regarding Rezoning Town Property from Open Space to Park - Mayor 
Dixon mentioned how when we did our last major rezoning map it was listed as a park .  Town 
Manager Patterson read the summary from the memo that the Town Attorney wrote stating , “The 
key point here is that rezoning the property to park coul d make it more difficult to sell without an 
election, but it will not provide absolute protection.  A future board of trustees will be able to take 
measurers that could eliminate the applicability of the statute and the election  requirement.”  
Mayor Dixon stated and asked if at this time are we making a bigger deal out of this, right now there 
is no buyer, also the Town is not interested in selling especially any of the big parcels. Mayor Dixon 
also stated how to provide the most protection would be to write a Conservation easement.  In the 
end it was concluded to bring this to July’s Board meeting as a discussion item.
