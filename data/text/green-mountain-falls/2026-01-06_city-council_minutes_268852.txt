6. BUSINESS ITEMS 
a. Highway Sign for GMF entrance archway – Caleb Patterson presented 5 different sign 
options that would be representation for the Town of Green Mountain Falls. Motion was 
made by Mayor Dixon to proceed with the concept of the general location, including the ideas 
for the sign to have Green Mountain Falls, Gem of the Rockies and the Gazebo.  Motion was 
seconded by Trustee Bell.  All was in favor.  
b. Water Attorney Services for Water Rights – Motion was made my Mayor Dixon to table this 
item to try to determine the volume of water that is coming down the two creeks.  Motion 
was seconded by trustee Bell.  All was in favor.   
c. Reappointment of Planning Commission Members  – Mayor Dixon made a motion to 
reappoint Commissioner Bonwell and Commissioner Blasi for another 2 years.  Motion was 
seconded by Trustee Bell.  All was in favor.   
d. Committee Member Appointments for FMAC – Mayor Dixon made a motion to approve 
Fire Mitigation Committee member appointments to Paul Yingli ng, Li sa Huizenga and 
Kelly Hunter.  Motion was seconded by Trustee Bell.  All was in favor.   
e. Appointment of BoT Member to FMAC & PRTA C - Mayor Dixon is the ex officio for the  
Planning Commission and will stay on.  Trustee Bell stated he would like to become the 
ex officio for FMAC.  Mayor Dixon made a motion to appoint Trustee Bell as the ex officio 
for FMAC.  Motion was seconded by Trustee Ives.  All was in favor.
