SUBJECT:  2027 - 2028 Proposed City Manager's Budget Presentation 
   
Report in Brief 
  
The City adopts a two-year budget with this being the first year of a two-year budget.  This will be the presentation on the 
proposed 2027-2028 Operating and Capital Budget.  The City team will present an overview of the City Manager's 
recommended budget by Worksystem and Department inlcuding the pay plan. 
  
Background 
  
This workshop will present the proposed 2027-2028 budget, focused on the City Council's Strategic Plan.  On August 25, 2026, 
members from the Infrastructure Worksystem along with the Finance and CMO teams presented final recommendations for 
funding the City's water, wastewater and stormwater infrastructure.  This was the last presentation in the series of presentations 
that have gone back over the past year.   In early September 2026, the budget document was provided to the Council and posted 
to the City's website. The September 22 presentation will focus on what is being proposed for funding and what areas need to 
be addressed in the upcoming years.  The City Manager's Office, Finance teams and Worksystem leaders will facilitate the 
presentation.  The proposed 2027 pay plan will also be presented by representatives from Human Resources. 
  
Strategic Alignment 
  
The workshop supports the Organizational and Service Effectiveness Priority Area within the City Council Strategic 
Plan.  Specifically, consideration of the 2027-2028 proposed Operating and Capital Budget aligns with the following Priority 
Area Principle:  
 
Provides a well-managed, fiscally responsible government that operates effectively and efficiently while identifying revenue 
requirements to sustain current service levels and achieve future requested levels of service. 
  
Next Steps 
  
The first reading of the 2027-2028 Proposed Budget will occur on October 6, 2026 and second reading on October 20, 2026.

2027 Proposed Pay Plan 
Guided by Our Total Rewards Philosophy
● Employer of choice | Competitive, market-based pay | Fiscally responsible
Informed by Data
Employment conditions:  3.9% unemployment | 66% labor participation | 1 job for every available worker
Arvada workforce metrics:  Strong attraction & retention 
● 47 new hires | 36 promotions |  88% successful recruitments from first job posting
● 94% staffed | 6.6% turnover with 1.3% retirements
● Higher health care costs @ 15.9% increase 
Market: 3.4% Employment Cost Index | 2% local salary range movement | 3.6% average budget for pay increases in 2027 
What this means:  Arvada has a stable and productive workforce, but continues to compete for talent in a competitive 
labor market. The team is also experiencing higher health care increases. A 2% market adjustment, combined with 
step increases for eligible employees, results in a 3.8% overall pay increase budget, while balancing resources for 
higher health care costs. 

2027 Pay Plan Recommendations 
MRA: All employees will receive the 2% market adjustment in January 
Steps: Eligible employees will receive step increases on their step anniversary date
● 70% of employees eligible 
● Step increases range from 2% to 3.75% for most employees, and 7.25% for police officers. 
   
Reclassifications: 32 employees are eligible for reclassifications 
● Due to significant changes to roles and market value.   
Total Impact to Payroll ~ 3.86% 
Individual employee increases will range from from 2% to 9.75%, depending on their step eligibility and position.  
Increases from reclassifications will vary.   
Looking Ahead
The City will conduct a comprehensive compensation study in 2027, providing an opportunity to evaluate compensation 
best practices, update our philosophy and strategy, and modernize our pay plan structures to ensure our approach to 
pay remains competitive, financially sustainable, and aligned with the needs of the City and our employees. Council will 
be kept informed throughout the process.
