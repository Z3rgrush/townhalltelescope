SUBJECT:  Comprehensive Plan Updates 
   
Report in Brief 
  
The project team has completed Phase 1 of the Comprehensive Plan process and will present on the existing conditions 
analysis, community engagement efforts, and provide a summary of the early themes. 
  
Background 
  
On March 3, 2026, the City executed a contract with Metta Urban Design to assist with the Comprehensive Plan update.  
 
The consultant's team has broken up the project into four phases that will build upon each other as the project progresses. 
Extensive community engagement will occur throughout each phase. Phase 1 focuses on understanding the community's 
evolution and context. Phase 2 focuses on establishing a shared understanding and emphasizes community engagement 
opportunities. Phase 3 explores where the City wants to go by examining place types and scenario-based planning. Phase 4 
details how we will get to our shared vision by finalizing the plan and preparing for implementation.  
 
It is anticipated the project will span through the next year, with a target adoption date in the 3rd Quarter of 2027. 
  
Strategic Alignment 
  
It is anticipated that the Comprehensive Plan will address all topics contained within the City Council Strategic Plan. 
  
Next Steps 
  
The project team will commence Phase 2 of the Comprehensive Plan update.

SUBJECT:  2027 Water, Wastewater and Stormwater Rates/Fees 
   
Report in Brief 
  
The Infrastructure Work System is returning to City Council to provide responses to questions posed at the July 28 workshop, 
and present additional draft rates and fee scenarios for the water, sewer and stormwater funds. 
 
The City team has historically held workshops with City Council ahead of rate/fee adoption requests to provide foundational 
information and receive questions and concerns from City Council. In this workshop, the City team will continue the discussion 
with City Council about how to address the City's water, wastewater and stormwater infrastructure condition and capacity 
challenges ahead of rate/fee adoption requests later this fall. These rate and fee revenues will help to close existing resource 
gaps and sustainable utilities level of service delivery to our residents and businesses. 
  
Background 
  
The City’s Infrastructure team continues to prepare for investments in operations and infrastructure as part of our ongoing 
commitment to continue to provide reliable water, sewer, and stormwater drainage services to our community.  Over the past 
few years, the team has provided information and updates to the Council, including annual budgets and workshops related to 
the water, sewer, and stormwater master planning efforts.   
 
The focus of this presentation is to respond to City Council's questions from the July workshop and provide some additional 
rate and fee scenarios for City Council to consider. The City team is holding time to return to City Council for another 
workshop in September, if requested, to provide follow-up information from any requests at the August workshop. The City
