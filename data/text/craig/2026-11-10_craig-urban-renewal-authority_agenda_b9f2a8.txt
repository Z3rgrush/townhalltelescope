November 10, 2026 
 
Chairman and Commissioners of the Board 
Urban Renewal Authority of the City of Craig 
 
SECTION 1: Introduction 
The proposed budget beginning January 1, 2027 and ending on December 31, 2027 is hereby submitted. 
As required by Colorado statutes, this proposed budget is in balance with anticipated and existing 
revenues equal to or greater than the 2027 proposed expenditures. 
 
SECTION 2: Authority and District Details 
The URA Board consists of eleven members: (7) the elected members of the City Council of the City of 
Craig; (1) a member appointed by the Moffat County Board of County Commissioners; (1) a board 
member of a special district selected by agreement of the special districts levying a mill levy within the 
boundaries of the Authority area (Craig Rural Fire District); (1) an elected member of a board of education 
of a school district levying a mill levy within the boundaries of the Authority area selected by agreement of 
the school districts levying a mill levy within the boundaries of the Authority area; and (1) a member 
appointed by the Mayor of the City of Craig, confirmed by City Council who is a member of the Moffat 
County Affiliated Junior College District ("CNCC"). 
 
The objective of the Authority is the acquisition, clearance, rehabilitation, conservation, development, or 
redevelopment of slum and blighted areas within the City. The authority was adopted on January 26, 
2021. 
 
Two separate plans and districts are included within the Urban Renewal Authority Fund. 
 
Urban Renewal Area #1 (Mall/WVictory Area) was created July 13, 2021. The base amount 
is $559,981 of sales tax each year. The portion of municipal sales taxes in excess of such base shall be 
allocated and paid into the special fund of the Authority to be allocated to approved projects within the 
Urban Renewal Area. Incremental property taxes from City of Craig, Moffat County, Moffat County School 
District, Craig Rural Fire District, and CNCC will also be paid to the Authority. The Authority will allocate 
the increment based on the varying agreements back to the districts and reserve for use on approved 
projects within the area. 
 
Urban Renewal Area #2 (Downtown/Industrial Area) was created September 28, 2021. The base amount 
is $457,217 of sales tax each year. The portion of municipal sales taxes in excess of such base shall be 
allocated and paid into the special fund of the Authority to be allocated to approved projects within the 
Urban Renewal Area. Incremental property taxes from City of Craig, Moffat County, Moffat County School 
District, Craig Rural Fire District, and CNCC will also be paid to the Authority. The Authority will allocate 
the increment based on the varying agreements back to the districts and reserve for use on approved 
projects within the area. 
 
Section 3: The 2027 Budget Process 
The proposed 2027 budget expenditures total $80,750. URA revenues for 2027 are estimated at 
$190,300. The proposed budget is scheduled for a public hearing on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. Following 
the November 10, 2026 public hearing, the URA budget is scheduled for adoption. 
 
The URA does not have authorized staff. 
 
Mindy E. Elliott, Treasurer of the Board
