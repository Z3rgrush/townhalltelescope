1. Pledge of Allegiance 
Mayor Nichols brought to order at 5:30 p.m. the first regular meeting of the Craig City 
Council for the month of October 2026.  In addition to Mayor Nichols, those present were 
Councilmembers Jnl Linsacum, Derek Duran, Randy Looper, Joe Herod, and Luke Tucker.

Attorney Garry Rhoden, Finance Director Mindy Elliott, City Clerk Katie Carmody, Police
