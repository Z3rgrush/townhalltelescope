9. Limited Public Comment 
Regular City Council meeting agendas and council packets are posted on the City’s website to keep City 
residents informed of City Council actions and deliberations that affect the community. Limited Public 
Comment time is set aside for citizens to address the City Council on matters posted on the agenda and 
within the jurisdiction of the city. Speakers must provide their full name, spelling, if requested, and have 
signed up on the Public Comment Sign-up Form. Each speaker is allocated no more than 3.0 minutes to 
speak regardless of the number of agenda items being addressed. Speakers may not cede their time to 
another speaker. Speakers who fail to follow the rules of procedure or rules of decorum for public 
comment will be requested to stop their public comment by the presiding officer. If you have documents 
to present to the City Council, please provide a minimum of 8 copies.  
 
Alternatively, you can submit your comments in writing by dropping your written comments at the front 
business office window to be delivered to the Clerk’s office or by emailing to cityclerk@cityofcraig.org 
no later than 12:00 p.m. on the day of the meeting. Please ensure that your full name, address, and phone 
number are included with the written comment.  
 
For participation in public comment virtually, please email the City Clerk at the following email address: 
cityclerk@cityofcraig.org no later than 12:00 p.m. on the regularly scheduled Council meeting day with 
your full name and home address.

Public Works Update 
November 10, 2026 
 
The following are updates for October 2026 from the Public Works team consisting of Road and Bridge, 
Solid Waste, Community Development, and Water/Wastewater.  
 
Grant Efforts: 
1. The Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) Division of Transit & Rail has issued a notice 
of funding availability calling for application for 2026 for capital projects using Federal Transit 
Administration (FTA) and FASTER Transit funds. Eligible projects include transit vehicles and 
capital infrastructure in support of the transit service. The Public Works Director is evaluating 
project candidates and considering applying for bus shelters at existing bus stops. The program is 
an 80% (federal) and 20% (local) funding structure. The application deadline is December 11.  
2. A call for applications has been announced for CDOT’s Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP) 
and the Safe Routes to School (SRTS) which are both federal grant programs administered by 
CDOT. TAP provides funding for community transportation projects that expand walking, biking, 
non-driver mobility, environmental protection, scenic activities and historic preservation. SRTS 
funds programs and active transportation improvements within 2 miles of an elementary, middle 
or high school. The Public Works Director is evaluating project candidates and considering 
applying for missing sidewalks in school areas and/or design and engineering for a regional trail 
serving Loudy Simpson Park and eventually River Park.  
3. Work has begun on the USDA Rural Business Development Grant project, and a Request for 
Proposals (RPF) is in draft to solicit proposals from engineering and surveying consultants to 
support phase two engineering for the Craig Business Industrial Park project which will include 
engineering for the public infrastructure and subdividing the property.  
4. The City has received a CDOT Safe Routes Activating Communities mini grant in the amount of 
$10k and does not require a local match. This is a new grant program, and the City is one of six 
communities who were picked to be included in a cohort. The program is designed to enhance the 
capacity of small and lower-resourced communities to develop safer walking, biking and rolling 
routes to everyday destinations.  
5. The Craig Master Plan Update project which is funded by a grant received from DOLA’s Local 
Planning Capacity program in the amount of $120k, is coming into focus with the SE Group being 
selected as the consultant who will lead the work. With city council’s recent approval to award the 
work to SE Group we are now ﬁne tuning the scope of the project and have begun the contracting 
process. We anticipate a project kickoff meeting in early December.

program. There is $15m funding available statewide with the source of funds coming from the 
Federal Mineral Lease (FML) Direct Distribution. Staff has contacted Moffat County to discuss 
coordination of applications. Another call of applications is anticipated in the spring 2027.  
Intergovernmental:

of Victory Way and Colorado Street.

even while sometimes not having a full staff.
