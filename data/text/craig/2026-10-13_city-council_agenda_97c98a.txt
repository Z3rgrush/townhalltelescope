9. Limited Public Comment 
Regular City Council meeting agendas and council packets are posted on the City’s website to keep City 
residents informed of City Council actions and deliberations that affect the community. Limited Public 
Comment time is set aside for citizens to address the City Council on matters posted on the agenda and 
within the jurisdiction of the city. Speakers must provide their full name, spelling, if requested, and have 
signed up on the Public Comment Sign-up Form. Each speaker is allocated no more than 3.0 minutes to 
speak regardless of the number of agenda items being addressed. Speakers may not cede their time to 
another speaker. Speakers who fail to follow the rules of procedure or rules of decorum for public 
comment will be requested to stop their public comment by the presiding officer. If you have documents 
to present to the City Council, please provide a minimum of 8 copies.  
 
Alternatively, you can submit your comments in writing by dropping your written comments at the front 
business office window to be delivered to the Clerk’s office or by emailing to cityclerk@cityofcraig.org 
no later than 12:00 p.m. on the day of the meeting. Please ensure that your full name, address, and phone 
number are included with the written comment.  
 
For participation in public comment virtually, please email the City Clerk at the following email address: 
cityclerk@cityofcraig.org no later than 12:00 p.m. on the regularly scheduled Council meeting day with 
your full name and home address.

Public Works Update 
October 13, 2026 
 
The following are updates for September 2026 from the Public Works team consisting of Road and Bridge, 
Solid Waste, Community Development, and Water/Wastewater.  
 
Major Movements: 
1. Work continued at the East Highway 40 sidewalk project. The project is over halfway completed 
from the Boys & Girls Club to Emerson Street and including the new pedestrian ramps at Victory 
Way and Colorado Street. Moffat County Road & Bridge has been an excellent partner completing 
the earthwork and improving nearby drainage ditches. City Road and Bridge has done a 
tremendous job installing the timber edgers and paving. Due to delays associated with weather 
and the upcoming closure of the asphalt plant the project will be suspended over the winter 
months and resume in the spring.  
2. Twelve proposals were received for the Craig Master Plan Update. A review and selection 
committee was formed and has selected three ﬁnalists to move onto interviews that will be held 
the week of October 12th. Staff anticipates bringing a recommendation to award to city council at 
its next meeting.  
3. A kickoff meeting was held with city staff and the consultant for the Craig Rail Station Area Plan 
which is a study that will examine alternatives for creating a passenger train station within city 
limits. The outcome will be identiﬁcation of a preferred location and a conceptual level design and 
estimate of cost. Staff and the consultant are working to form a stakeholder advisory committee 
and has requested councilor Looper to represent city council at those meetings. Staff will provide 
monthly updates on the progress of the project and will schedule a workshop or presentation for 
all of council when it is necessary.  
4. I applied to CDOT’s Safe Routes Activating Communities program for a $10,000 mini grant which 
requires no match commitment from the city. If awarded the funds will be used to hire a 
consultant to create a Safe Routes Action Plan for activate transportation projects (sidewalks and 
bicycle facilities). Grant awards will be announced at the end of October.  
5. The State of Colorado Division of Natural Resources is nearing completion to update ﬂoodplain 
mapping within the city and other areas in Moffat County. A resilience meeting will be scheduled 
with stakeholders to review the updates and implications for landowners. More information will be 
provided to council following that meeting.  
6. The new stormwater quality vault near the intersection of W. 1st Street and Woodbury Drive was 
substantially completed. The vault has already proven its effectiveness to remove leaves, debris, 
and trash from the stormwater stream.

Successes: 
1. City departments participated in the Fall Consignment auction which included several pieces of 
city owned equipment and ﬂeet. Everything the city included was sold totaling $55,400 of revenue.

anticipated.

completion on October 28th.

Waste programs in advance of holding a workshop with council on September 23rd to discuss 
potential rate increases.

traffic study to inform potential off-site improvements, geotechnical investigation, and wetlands 
survey.  
10. The Public Works Director attended the Colorado Association of Stormwater and Floodplain 
Managers (CASFM) annual conference.  
11. Arrangements have been made with a ﬁle scanning company to digitize the Public Works hard
