 
 
Regular City Council Meeting 
September 8, 2026 
Minutes 
 
1. Pledge of Allegiance 
 
Mayor Nichols brought to order at 5:30 p.m. the first regular meeting of the Craig City 
Council for the month of September 2026. In addition to Mayor Nichols, those present were 
Councilmembers Michelle Gottschall, Jnl Linsacum, Joe Herod, and Luke Tucker. Staff 
present were City Manager KC Hume, City Attorney Heather Cannon, Finance Director 
Mindy Elliott, City Clerk Katie Carmody, Police Chief Michael Cochran, Public Works 
Director Danny Paul, Parks and Recreation Director Ryan Dennison, and GIS/Technology 
Tech John Meinhart.  Joining via Zoom was Councilman Randy Looper.

absence of Councilman Duran. Ayes: 6. Nays: 0. Motion carried.
