CRAIG PLANNING & ZONING COMMISSION MINUTES 
May 18, 2026 
 
 
The Craig Planning and Zoning Commission of the City of Craig, County of Moffat, State of  
Colorado, met in the City Council Chambers, 300 West 4th Street, Craig Colorado, 81625, at the 
hour of 6:00 p.m. There being present the following Commissioners: Mike Tucci, Tom Gilchrist, 
Justin Jenison, Mason Updike, Savana Crow. City Staff present were Building Official Marlin 
Eckhoff, Public Works Director Danny Paul, and Assistant Sierra Arellano. 
 
Vice Chairman Tucci called the meeting to order at 6:00 PM and began with Commission roll 
call. Those present were Commission members Mike Tucci, Tom Gilchrist, Justin Jenison, 
Mason Updike, and Savana Crow. Chairman Kloss was absent. 
 
Commissioner Gilchrist made a motion, seconded by Commissioner Updike, to approve the 
minutes from March 30, 2026. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 
Official Eckhoff presented the Commission with the Craig Business and Industrial Park Major 
Subdivision Sketch Plan at TBD West 1st Street, Craig, CO 81625, Parcel #085501200008. Ryan 
Peterson of the Antero Group was present via Zoom to give the Commission some background 
of the project and present the sketch plan. Eckhoff noted to the Commission that the sketch plan 
is a broad concept and the first step of many to meet the City’s Land Use Code requirements. 
Peterson discussed with the Commission that there is much potential with the project as a whole 
along with the possible economic impacts of adding office spaces, warehouses, large industry, 
etc. Eckhoff discussed his memo with the Commission noting that all aspects of the sketch plan 
comply with the City’s Land Use Code except the sidewalks which are required to be on both 
sides of the primary access street and the emergency access loop. Vice Chairman Tucci asked 
Eckhoff if a variance would be required to keep the sidewalks as presented, which Eckhoff noted 
would be required if the Commission and City Council deemed the additional sidewalk as 
necessary. Commissioner Jenison noted the sketch plan being vague leading to questions of how 
to plan sizing of buildings, infrastructure capacity, etc. Peterson noted there are limitations but 
their intent was to design the concept to maximize each parcel for maximum build.  Heavy 
discussion from the Commission regarding concerns about feasibility and what the 
Commission’s role is in approving the sketch plan. Eckhoff and Paul discussed with the 
Commission that their the Commissions role is to determine if the sketch plan complies with 
Title 16 of the City’s Land Use Code while City Council will need to determine feasibility. No 
further discussion. Vice Chairman Tucci made a motion, seconded by Commissioner Updike, to 
recommend City Council to approve the sketch plan as presented with the condition that either 
sidewalks are added to bring the plan into compliance with the City of Craig’s Land Use Code or 
a variance is achieved. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried.  
 
No Commission Reports given.  
 
For Staff Reports, Commissioner Gilchrist asked Eckhoff for an update regarding the Colorado 
Wildfire Resiliency Code (CWRC). Eckhoff noted that the City of Craig has adopted the CWRC 
Page 2 of 20
which will go into effect on July 1st, 2026. Eckhoff noted that he along with the Building 
Inspector, Chad Yeager, had attended several classes to gain perspectives on the interpretation of 
the CWRC as well as hosted a workshop for local contractors and homeowners. Paul gave the 
Commission a brief overview of potential projects that may come before the Commission 
including a site plan and two annexations. 
 
There being no further business, Vice Chairman Tucci made a motion, seconded by 
Commissioner Updike to adjourn the meeting. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried.

Subject         Site plan from Mike Thompson to build a personal hobby shop at 791 Industrial 
Ave. Green Tree Business Park Lot: 6, Block: 1 
 
   
   
BACKGROUND / DISCUSSION 
Mike Thompson is requesting approval of a site plan for an industrial building at 791 Industrial 
Ave. The project will consist of a 3,672 sq. ft. single story wood framed building which he 
intends on using as a storage and hobby shop for personal use.  
 
The following is an outline of the Land Use Code requirements for this project: 
 
Zoning: 
This lot is zoned Light Industrial (I-1). The I-1 zone allows for workshops, while this won’t be 
open to the public as a service or retail business at this time, it will be designed with ADA 
access, an ADA restroom, and an office space so it will be compliant in case it is open to the 
public in the future.   
  
Floor Plan: 
The building will have a basic floor plan consisting of: 
• 120 sq. ft. office space 
• 324 sq. ft. Hobby space 
• 96 sq. ft. restroom 
• 3,132 sq. ft. shop area 
 
Setbacks:                        Required                                    Proposed                                     . 
Front:                                    25’                                               48’                
Rear:                                     15’                                               15’ 
Sides:                                    10’                                               24’ and 42’ 
 
Access:  
The site will have one 20ft wide driveway access off Industrial Ave, and one 40ft wide driveway 
access off Stout Street. The access widths and locations have been approved by the Public Works 
and Road & Bridge Departments.

Parking: 
With the shop being used for personal use, limited parking is required. However, they have 
included one van accessible parking space and an area for two employee parking spaces for 
future use if need be. All driveways and parking spaces will be concrete to comply with the 
city’s hard surface requirement.  
 
Utilities: 
City water and sewer are available and will be tied into Industrial Ave, YVEA and Atmos 
Energy have supplied Will Serve Letters for the property.  
 
Landscaping: 
Required landscaping based on the lot size of 18,022 sq. ft. is outlined below:  
                                          Required                              Proposed                                        . 
Landscaped area: 4%                721 sq. ft.                              947 sq. ft.

Note: Due to increasing drought conditions, the applicant is proposing to supply two types of 
decorative rock and 6 shrubs with a dripline irrigation system to comply with the 721 sq. ft. of 
required landscaping instead of using turf. The remaining areas of the site will be seeded with a 
mixture of native drought tolerate grass. (See Sheet A104 of the Site Plan for location of 
landscaping.) 
 
Architectural: 
The building will consist of vertical metal siding with accent colored wainscotting on all sides, 
and metal roofing. The Land Use Code prohibits blank windowless walls over 50ft long. The 
east wall and north wall are over 50ft long, however both these walls will have windows and 
wainscotting, and they both face other industrial lots or developments.   
 
Sidewalks: 
5ft wide sidewalks already exist along Industrial Ave and Stout Street which meet the city’s 
requirements.  
  
I have included the following items for your review: 
1. 5-page site plan  
2. Floor plan 
3. Exterior elevation drawings of the exterior of the building 
4. Will Serve letters from Atmos Energy and YVEA 
5. Letter from the applicant describing the intended use for the building 
  
  
Note: Letters were sent to all property owners within 300ft of the subject property on 
September 4th complying with the city’s notification requirements. The letters included the 
site plan and notified property owners of the P&Z and city council meeting dates, along 
with letting them know they can contact the Community Development Department with 
any comments or concerns.  
As of 9/15/2026, I have received no formal complaints or comments, however I have been 
contacted by one homeowner that lives on Stout Street. The homeowner reached out by 
phone and had questions about how much traffic the proposed building might generate.

• To table the item until further/revised information is presented. 
• Deny the site plan. (Note: If a site plan id denied the same or similar request may not be
