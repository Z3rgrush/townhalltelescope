 
 
Regular City Council Meeting 
September 22, 2026 
Minutes 
 
1. Pledge of Allegiance 
Mayor Chris Nichols brought to order at 5:30 p.m. the second regular meeting of the Craig 
City Council for the month of September 2026.  In addition to Mayor Nichols, those present 
were Councilmembers Michelle Gottschall, Jnl Linsacum, Derek Duran, and Luke Tucker. 
 Staff present were City Manager KC Hume, City Attorney Heather Cannon, Assistant City 
Attorney Garry Rhoden, Finance Director Mindy Elliott, City Clerk Katie Carmody, and 
GIS/Technology Tech John Meinhart.  
 
All participated in the pledge of allegiance. 
2. Roll Call 
City Clerk Katie Carmody took Councilmember roll call.  Those present were Mayor 
Nichols and Councilmembers Michelle Gottschall, Jnl Linsacum, Derek Duran, and Luke 
Tucker.   
 
Councilman Tucker moved with a second by Councilman Duran to excuse the absences of 
Councilmembers Randy Looper and Joe Herod. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
3. Call to Order 
4. Consent Agenda 
 A. Approval of minutes from September 8, 2026, regular council meeting.  
 B. Approval of bills through September 17, 2026, in the amount of $799,365.94.  
 C. Approval of Payroll and Benefits for September 11, 2026, in the amount of 
$463,329.99. 
 
Councilman Duran moved with a second by Councilwoman Gottschall to approve the 
consent agenda as presented, which included the approval of the minutes from the 
regular council meeting on September 8, 2026, the approval of bills through 
September 17, 2026, in the amount of $799,365.94, and the approval of payroll and 
benefits for September 11, 2026, in the amount of $463,329.99. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. 
Motion carried. 
5. Agenda 
 A. Approval of the agenda for the September 22, 2026, regular council meeting. 
 
Councilwoman Linsacum moved with a second by Councilman Duran to approve the 
agenda as presented. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried.  
 B. Disclosure of Potential Conflicts of Interest regarding Agenda Items. 
 
Councilwoman Gottschall disclosed a potential conflict of interest with Item #11.A.v., 
the Economic Development Advisory Committee (EDAC) recommendation regarding 
the Local Marketing District (LMD) Dissolution of funds request from Powder 
Mountain Ventures / Powderbound Adventure.   
6. Presentations 
There were no presentations scheduled. 
7. City Manager Report 
City Manager KC Hume updated the group on current and future meetings and projects. 
8. Staff Reports 
 A. Finance Director Mindy Elliott to present the Financial Report for August 2026. 
 
Finance Director Mindy Elliott gave the Finance Report for August 2026.  
9. Limited Public Comment 
There was no limited public comment given. 
10. Public Hearing 
There was no Public Hearing scheduled. 
11. Other Business 
 A. City Manager KC Hume to present for discussion and approval of the Economic 
Development Advisory Committee (EDAC) recommendations for Local Marketing 
District (LMD) Dissoluton of funds. 
 i. Craig Community Works LLC. / Missoula Children's Theater. 
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the EDAC 
recommendation for the Craig Community Works LLC / Missoula Children's Theater 
LMD dissolution fund request.  After further discussion, Councilman Duran moved 
with a second by Councilman Tucker to approve the EDAC recommendation for the 
Craig Community Works LLC / Missoula Children's Theater LMD dissolution fund 
request in the amount of $3,500.00. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 ii. 2026 Moffat County Balloon Festival. 
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the EDAC 
recommendation for the 2026 Moffat County Balloon Festival LMD dissolution fund 
request.  After further discussion, Councilwoman Gottschall moved with a second by 
Councilwoman Linsacum to approve the EDAC recommendation for the 2026 Moffat 
County Balloon Festival LMD dissolution fund request in the amount of $15,000.00. 
Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 iii. Bear River Young Life 2026 Car Show. 
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the EDAC 
recommendation for the Bear River Young Life 2026 Car Show LMD dissolution fund 
request.  After further discussion, Councilman Duran moved with a second by 
Councilman Tucker to approve the EDAC recommendation for the Bear River Young 
Life 2026 Car Show LMD dissolution fund request in the amount of $4,000.00. Ayes: 
5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 iv. Downtown Business Association Rock Fest. 
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the EDAC 
recommendation for the Downtown Business Association Rock Fest LMD dissolution 
fund request.  After further discussion, Councilwoman Gottschall moved with a second 
by Councilman Tucker to approve the EDAC recommendation for the Downtown 
Business Association Rock Fest LMD dissolution fund request in the amount of 
$1,800.00. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 v. Powder Mountain Ventures / Powderbound Adventure.  
 
Councilwoman Michelle Gottschall recused herself and exited the dais due to a 
conflict of interest with this next agenda item.   
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the EDAC 
recommendation for the Powder Mountain Ventures / Powderbound Adventure LMD 
dissolution fund request.  After further discussion, Councilman Tucker moved with a 
second by Councilman Duran to approve the Powder Mountain Ventures / 
Powderbound Adventure LMD dissolution fund request in the amount of $15,000.00. 
Ayes: 0. Nays: 4. Abstained: 1. Motion failed.  
 
Councilwoman Michelle Gottschall entered the chamber and took her seat on the dais. 
 vi. City Business Plan Competition.  
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval the Craig Business 
Plan Competition LMD dissolution fund request.  After further 
discussion, Councilman Tucker moved with a second by Councilman Duran to 
approve the Craig Business Plan Competition LMD dissolution fund request in the 
amount of $20,000.00. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
 B. City Manager KC Hume to present for discussion and approval of a Letter of Support 
for the Yampa Valley Regional Transportation Authority (Yampa Valley RTA) federal 
funding request through the FY 2026 Grants for Buses and Bus Facilities 
Infrastructure Programs. 
 
City Manager KC Hume presented for discussion and approval a letter of Support for 
the Yampa Valley Regional Transportation Authority (Yampa Valley RTA) federal 
funding request through the FY 2026 Grants for Buses and Bus Facilities 
Infrastructure Programs. After further discussion, Councilman Duran moved with a 
second by Councilwoman Gottschall to approve the letter of Support for the Yampa 
Valley Regional Transportation Authority (Yampa Valley RTA) federal funding 
request through the FY 2026 Grants for Buses and Bus Facilities Infrastructure 
Programs. Ayes: 5. Nays: 0. Motion carried. 
12. Council Reports 
City Councilmembers discussed various meetings and events in which they participated in 
or will participate in soon. 
13. General Public Comment 
George Rinker gave a general public comment.  
14. Closing Discussion and Informational Items 
There was discussion related to a previous City Council presentation related to a potential 
housing development.  There were no requests for future agenda items. 
15. Adjourn 
Being no further business, Councilwoman Gottschall moved with a second by Councilman 
Tucker to adjourn the September 22, 2026, regular city council meeting at 6:26 p.m. Ayes: 
5. Nays: 0. Motion carried.
