9. Limited Public Comment 
Regular City Council meeting agendas and council packets are posted on the City’s website to keep City 
residents informed of City Council actions and deliberations that affect the community. Limited Public 
Comment time is set aside for citizens to address the City Council on matters posted on the agenda and 
within the jurisdiction of the city. Speakers must provide their full name, spelling, if requested, and have 
signed up on the Public Comment Sign-up Form. Each speaker is allocated no more than 3.0 minutes to 
speak regardless of the number of agenda items being addressed. Speakers may not cede their time to 
another speaker. Speakers who fail to follow the rules of procedure or rules of decorum for public 
comment will be requested to stop their public comment by the presiding officer. If you have documents 
to present to the City Council, please provide a minimum of 8 copies.  
 
Alternatively, you can submit your comments in writing by dropping your written comments at the front 
business office window to be delivered to the Clerk’s office or by emailing to cityclerk@cityofcraig.org 
no later than 12:00 p.m. on the day of the meeting. Please ensure that your full name, address, and phone 
number are included with the written comment.  
 
For participation in public comment virtually, please email the City Clerk at the following email address: 
cityclerk@cityofcraig.org no later than 12:00 p.m. on the regularly scheduled Council meeting day with 
your full name and home address.
