Hybrid Meeting Information
The Board of Adjustment meeting will be held in person at City Hall, Smith Chambers, at 949 East 2nd 
Avenue and virtually. Public participation during the meeting will be accessible through this platform. If 
attending virtually, the public will be able to view the meeting on a computer tablet or smartphone. 
Links and instructions on how to join the meeting are available below. Community members wishing to 
submit comments regarding an agenda item can also submit an email with their comments no later than 
12:00 pm on Friday, prior to the Board of Adjustment meeting to planning@durangoco.gov.
 
City Councilors may attend the meeting; however they will not make any decisions nor will they 
discuss City business.
 
1. Call to Order
2. Land Acknowledgment
3. Approval of Minutes
3.1. Approval of the August 31, 2026 Board of Adjustment Meeting Minutes
4. Variances/Waivers/Appeals
5. Reports/Comments
6. Adjournment
