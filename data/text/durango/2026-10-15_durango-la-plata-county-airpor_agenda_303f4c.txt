Airport Commission meetings are accessible to the public both in-person and virtually. The public may view the meeting

wishing to submit comments may do so by sending an email to airport@durangoco.gov no later than noon the day before 
the meeting. Each email should contain the corresponding agenda item in the subject line of the email if there is one. The 
sender’s full name and address should be included for the record.
