SUBJECT:  Town Manager Report 
 
 
• The Town Board held a special meeting on Thursday, October 29 th to discuss the construction and 
funding details of the new police facility. The 19,000 sq foot facility is to be located at the Municipal 
Facilities site on WCR 7 east of Liberty Ranch subdivision. Construction is anticipated through fourth 
quarter of 2027. The Town has suspended construction activity on the site pending the outcome of 
the zoning hearing to be heard at the November 30th Board of Trustees meeting.  Please see attached 
notice to suspend work and Police Facility Fact Sheet. 
• Town of Mead Municipal Court Judge David J. Thrower was awarded the 2026 Distinguished Jurist 
Award – Small or Part-time Court from the Colorado Municipal Judges Association. He has served as 
the appointed municipal court judge in Mead since 2018. In addition to Mead, he serves as Presiding 
Municipal Judge in Superior, Estes Park, Nederland, Louisville, Morrison and Dacono. 
• Mead’s regular election was held on Tuesday November 3, 2026, in coordination with Weld County. 
Preliminary results released on November 4th indicate that Stephan Hucal was elected Mayor and the 
following were elected as Trustees: Kim Heard, Ryan Hathorn, Jeff Morgan and Christine Smith. Final 
results are expected to be released by Weld County in the next two weeks. Elected officials are 
expected to be sworn in on November 30, 2026. 
• The 2027 Proposed Budget was approved by the Board of Trustees after a public hearing held on 
October 26th. The budget will be finalized after the final certificate of assessed values is received from 
Weld County on or before December 10th. The proposed budget is posted to the Town’s public records 
portal (https://records.townofmead.org/WebLink/).  
• Mead’s High Plains Library is located at 13815 Chaparral St, just on the east side of the Liberty Ranch 
subdivision of WCR 7. The library is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., 
Friday and Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.  
• The High Plains Blvd (HPB) road construction project is complete and the roadway is open.  
• There is quite a bit of activity going on at the Municipal facilities site located just east of Liberty Ranch 
and south of SH 66 on CR 7. This Town owned site is home to Mead’s first library, a new Mountain 
View Fire Station, and a Police headquarters. The library is completed and open to the public. 
Construction of the fire station is nearing completion and will open later this year. The police facility 
site is being prepared for construction. 
• Staff has attended several position-specific training courses and conferences. The community 
development team attended the annual American Planning Association Conference. Chief Newbanks 
and Commander Smith attended the International Association of Chiefs of Police Conference.  
• A pedestrian and bicycle underpass that will go under SH 66 and connect the pedestrian trail that 
runs along CR 7 is under design and right of way acquisition. Construction is anticipated to begin on 
this project by late this year. 
• Mayor Colleen Whitlow convenes weekly office hours at Mead Town Hall. The office hours are on 
Mondays from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Please contact the front office for an appointment. Walk ins 
will be taken as time allows.  
• Coffee with the Mayor is scheduled for Saturday, November 7 th from 8:00 – 10:00 a.m. at the 
Community Center Peak Room. Mayor Colleen Whitlow hosts this monthly event to provide updates 
on active development in Mead and Town projects, along with answering questions and general 
discussion. Coffee will be provided by local roaster, Redemption Road Coffee.  
• Mead Municipal Court for November is scheduled for November 17 th at 6:00 p.m. at Town Hall. 
Currently there are 69 cases on the docket for review hearings and arraignments. The docket is larger 
than average due to the early October court date.  Municipal Court is generally scheduled for the third 
Tuesday of each month at Town Hall. Starting in January 2027 court will be held monthly on Thursday

Community Development  
• The Certificate of Occupancy for High Plains Library District’s new library was issued on October 16th. 
• Mountain View Fire Rescue’s new fire station continues exterior construction of cladding and roofing.  
• The State of Colorado Department of Local Affairs (DOLA) awarded $105,000 in grant funds (with a 
Town match of $35,000 or 25%) to support the Town’s completion of SB24-174’s required Strategic 
Growth and Water Supply Elements of the Comprehensive Plan and a Housing Action Plan. 
• The Town issued a Request for Proposals (RFP) soliciting consultant teams to assist the Town in 
preparing a Housing Action Plan as well as the Strategic Growth and Water Supply elements of the 
Comprehensive Plan. The RFP was issued on September 25 th, an Addendum with responses to all 
submitted RFP questions was issued on October 16th, and proposals were due back on October 30th. 
The Town received seven proposals which are under initial review. 
• The Land Use Code Amendment Project continues. Town staff completed review of the six draft 
modules and the Town Attorney’s Office is reviewing. Steps to follow include preparing a final draft for 
the Planning Commission’s and Board of Trustees’ consideration. The update ensures that the LUC 
complies with state laws, best practices and efficiencies in the development processes. 
 
Public Works and Engineering

16th. 
• CR 34 Bridge Replacement – work continues on the waterline relocations. Bridge demolition is 
scheduled for mid-November. CR 34 remains closed west of CR 5 through March 2027.

• Mead Recreation kicked off its winter youth basketball season with practices for the 3rd/4th Grade age

PROCLAMATION 
Veterans Day  
November 11, 2026 
 
WHEREAS, on November 11, 1918, the armistice ending World War I was signed, marking the 
cessation of hostilities and allowing the people of the United States to resume peaceful relations 
with other nations, a peace we hope may never again be broken; and 
WHEREAS, on October 8, 1954, President Dwight D. Eisenhower issued the first "Veterans Day 
Proclamation," stating: “In order to insure proper and widespread observance of this 
anniversary, all veterans, all veterans' organizations, and the entire citizenry will wish to join 
hands in the common purpose”; and 
WHEREAS, Veterans Day continues to be observed annually on November 11, regardless of 
the day of the week on which it falls. Restoring the observance of Veterans Day to November 11 
not only preserves the historical significance of the date, but also emphasizes the important 
purpose of honoring our veterans; and 
WHEREAS, Veterans Day is a celebration to honor America’s veterans for their patriotism, love 
of country, and willingness to serve and sacrifice for the common good. As we continue the 
struggle for liberty and universal human rights, let us draw strength from the moment when free 
nations overcame oppression and gave new hope to the world;  
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Colleen Whitlow, Mayor of the Town of Mead, do hereby proclaim 
NOVEMBER 11, 2026 as VETERANS DAY and encourage all citizens to recognize the valor 
and sacrifice of our nation’s veterans. 
Given under my hand and Seal of the Town of Mead, Colorado 
On this 9th day of November 2026

Presented by: Marcus McAskin, Town Attorney 
  
Summary: 
Before the Board of Trustees (“Board”) for consideration is a new electric utility franchise 
agreement (“Franchise”) between the Town and United Power, Inc., a Colorado not-for-profit 
electric cooperative (“United Power”).   
 
A franchise agreement is a long-term contract between a municipality and a utility provider that 
details how that provider may use the municipality’s public rights-of-way and other property to 
install, maintain and repair infrastructure and facilities to deliver utility services to the municipal 
community.  As compensation to the municipality for the right to use public rights-of way and 
other public property, the utility provider pays the municipality a franchise fee, usually in the 
form of a percentage of the provider’s revenue derived from charges for providing the service 
using established rates.  
 
The existing franchise agreement with United Power, dated May 11, 2011, is currently in effect. 
 That agreement, which was set to expire on May 11, 2026, was extended this year via letter 
agreements between the Town and United Power.  As set forth in the executed letter 
agreements, the existing franchise agreement remains in effect until the earlier of the approval 
of a new electric utility franchise agreement or January 31, 2027.   
 
The Franchise provided for the Board’s consideration via the above-referenced Ordinance 
grants United Power: (1) an exclusive right to furnish, sell, and distribute electricity within the 
Town; (2) a non-exclusive right to use rights-of-way and certain other Town property to acquire, 
construct, install, locate, maintain, operate, and to access such property for the purpose of 
providing electrical service within the Town; and (3) a non-exclusive right to provide electrical 
service to street light and traffic signals within the Town, for which the Town will pay United 
Power. If adopted, the Franchise will be effective for a term of twenty (20) years from the 
effective date of the Ordinance.   
 
The Board is authorized to consider the Franchise by Article 1 of Chapter 5 of the Mead 
Municipal Code (“MMC”) and C.R.S. §§ 31-32-101 et seq. The MMC provides that franchises 
Page 49 of 202
shall be approved by ordinance in accordance with state law, which requires two readings and 
advance publication in a newspaper of general circulation within the Town.  C.R.S. §§ 31-32-
101 et seq. Notice of United Power’s application for the Franchise was published on October 23, 
October 30, and November 6, 2026 in the Longmont Times-Call, in accordance with the 
requirements of C.R.S. § 31-32-102.  A copy of the publisher’s affidavit is on file with the Town 
Clerk.  The Board has jurisdiction to consider the Franchise on first reading.  If approved on first 
reading, the “further consideration” notice required by C.R.S. § 31-32-103 will also be published 
in the Longmont Times-Call.  Second reading of the Ordinance will be scheduled for November 
30, 2026. 
 
 
United Power representatives are scheduled to present the Franchise to the Board during an 
“informational item” at the November 9th regular meeting.  
Financial Considerations: 
Under the Franchise, United Power makes monthly payments to the Town in an amount equal 
to three percent (3%) of United Power’s “Revenues”, as that term is defined in the Franchise.

 TOWN OF MEAD, COLORADO 
ORDINANCE NO. 1122 
 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF MEAD, COLORADO, GRANTING AN 
ELECTRIC FRANCHISE TO UNITED POWER, INC., ITS AFFILIATES, 
SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS, THE RIGHT TO USE THE STREETS, 
ALLEYS, AVENUES, ROADS, HIGHWAYS, EASEMENTS, RIGHTS-OF-WAY, 
AND OTHER PUBLIC PROPERTY WITHIN THE TOWN TO FURNISH, SELL, 
TRANSPORT, TRANSMIT, AND DISTRIBUTE ELECTRICITY TO THE TOWN 
AND ITS RESIDENTS; GRANTING THE RIGHT TO ACQUIRE, CONSTRUCT, 
INSTALL, LOCATE, MAINTAIN, OPERATE, REPAIR, REPLACE, AND 
EXTEND FACILITIES REASONABLY NECESSARY TO PROVIDE ELECTRIC 
SERVICE WITHIN, INTO, AND THROUGH THE TOWN; ESTABLISHING THE 
TERMS AND CONDITIONS THEREOF; AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OR 
MAYOR PRO TEM AND TOWN CLERK TO EXECUTE THE FRANCHISE 
AGREEMENT ON BEHALF OF THE TOWN 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the Town of Mead (“Board”) is authorized to approve 
utility franchise agreements pursuant to the provisions of Article 1 of Chapter 5 of the Mead 
Municipal Code (“MMC”) and C.R.S. §§ 31-32-101 et seq.; and  
 
WHEREAS,  United Power, Inc., a Colorado not-for-profit electric cooperative (“United 
Power”) desires to continue to operate an electric system within the Town and has applied to the 
Town to secure a 20-year exclusive electric utility franchise; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Town and United Power have negotiated and prepared the Electric Utility 
Franchise Agreement (the “Franchise” or “Franchise Agreement”) setting forth the terms and 
conditions under which the Town is willing to grant United Power an exclusive franchise to make 
reasonable and lawful use of the Town’s public rights-of-way and other Town-owned property to 
construct, operate, maintain, reconstruct, or rebuild an electric system for the purpose of providing 
electric service to customers within the Town of Mead, a copy of which is attached to this 
Ordinance as Exhibit A and is incorporated herein by this reference; and 
 
WHEREAS, as set forth in the Franchise Agreement, the Franchise shall be effective as 
of the effective date of this Ordinance and shall supersede any prior franchise grants to United 
Power by the Town of Mead; and  
 
WHEREAS, as set forth in the Franchise Agreement, the term of the Franchise shall be 
twenty (20) years; and  
 
WHEREAS, following publication of notice in accordance with the requirements of C.R.S. 
§ 31-32-102, the Board of Trustees of the Town of Mead (“Board”) considered this Ordinance on 
first reading on November 9, 2026; and  
 
WHEREAS, following approval of this Ordinance on first reading, the Board ordered 
publication of the Ordinance in the Longmont Times-Call, a newspaper of general circulation 
within the Town, in accordance with the requirements of C.R.S. § 31-32-103; and 
 
Page 52 of 202
 
 
 
WHEREAS, publisher affidavits evidencing compliance with the applicable notice and 
publication requirements of C.R.S. §§ 31-32-102 and -103 are on file with the Town Clerk; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Board finds and declares that the Town’s grant of the Franchise to United 
Power pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in the Franchise Agreement will reasonably 
meet the future electricity-related needs of the community, and that the approval of this Ordinance 
will promote the public health, safety and welfare of the community; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Board further finds and declares that it serves the public interest of the 
citizens of the Town of Mead to approve the proposed Franchise and therefore desires to delegate 
authority to the Mayor or Mayor Pro Tem to execute the Franchise when in final form and following 
the date on which United Power has executed same.  
  
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Board of Trustees of the Town of Mead, 
Weld County, Colorado, that: 
 
Section 1. Recitals Incorporated.  The recitals contained above are incorporated 
herein by reference and are adopted as findings and determinations of the Board of Trustees. 
 
Section 2. Franchise Agreement Approved.  The Board of Trustees hereby: (1) 
approves the Franchise Agreement by and between the Town of Mead and United Power, in 
substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit A; (2) authorizes the Town Attorney, in 
consultation with the Town Manager, to make any non-material changes to the Franchise 
Agreement, as may be necessary that do not increase the obligations of the Town prior to final 
execution by the Town; and (3) authorizes the Mayor or Mayor Pro Tem to execute the Franchise 
Agreement on behalf of the Town and the Town Clerk to attest the signature of the Mayor or 
Mayor Pro Tem when in final form, subject to the requirement that United Power shall have first 
executed the same.  
 
Section 3. Effective Date.  This ordinance shall be published and become effective 
as provided by law. 
  
Section 4. Severability.  If any part, section, subsection, sentence, clause or phrase 
of this ordinance is for any reason held to be invalid, such invalidity shall not affect the validity of 
the remaining sections of the Ordinance.  The Board of Trustees hereby declares that it would 
have passed the ordinance including each part, section, subsection, sentence, clause or phrase 
thereof, irrespective of the fact that one or more part, section, subsection, sentence, clause or 
phrase is declared invalid. 
 
Section 5. Repealer. All ordinances or resolutions, or parts thereof, in conflict with this 
ordinance are hereby repealed, provided that such repealer shall not repeal the repealer clauses 
of such ordinance nor revive any ordinance thereby. 
  
Section 6. Certification.  The Town Clerk shall certify to the passage of this ordinance 
and make not less than one copy of the adopted Code available for inspection by the public during 
regular business hours.  The Town Clerk shall maintain a copy of the Franchise Agreement on 
file with the Town, and shall additionally ensure that a copy of the Franchise Agreement is 
available on the Town’s website, as required by Sec. 5-1-80 of the MMC.

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS 
1.1 Short Title. This agreement shall be known as the Town of Mead/United Power Electric 
Franchise Agreement (“Franchise”, “Franchise Agreement”, or “Agreement”). 
1.2 Definitions. For the purpose of this Franchise, the following words and phrases shall have 
the meaning given in this article.  When not inconsistent with the context, words used in the 
present tense include the future tense, words in the plural number include the singular number, 
and words in the singular number include the plural number.  The words “shall” or “will” are 
mandatory and “may” is permissive. Words not defined in this article shall be given their common 
and ordinary meaning. 
“Board” or “Board of Trustees” means the governing body of the Town of Mead. 
“Commission” or “CPUC” means the Colorado Public Utilities Commission.  
“Electricity” and “Electric Service” means all electric energy and electric service provided 
to Members located within the Town, including Street Lighting Service and Traffic Signal 
Lighting Service. 
“Electric Distribution Facility” means that portion of United Power’s electric system that 
delivers electricity from the substation breakers to United Power’s meters , including all 
devices connected to that system.  
“Emergency” means an event that directly influences the ability to provide service or that 
threatens life or property. 
“Energy Conservation” means the decrease in energy requirements of specific Members 
during any selected time period, with end-use services of such Members held constant. 
“Energy Efficiency”  means methods of energy conservation, reduced demand or 
improved load factors resulting from hardware, equipment, devices, or practices that are 
installed or instituted at a Member facility.  
“Facilities” means all physical components of United Power that are deemed necessary 
by United Power to provide electricity within and through the Town for transportation, 
distribution and sale of electricity and include, but are not limited to, plants, works, 
systems, substations, transmission and distribution structu res, lines, street lighting 
fixtures, equipment, conduit, transformers, underground lines, meter reading devices, 
communications and data transfer equipment, wireless facilities and equipment  for 
supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA)  wires, cables, poles, and building 
structures, which may be referred to individually as a Facility. 
“Member” refers to a person or legal entity that receives or uses United Power’s electric 
service and agrees to follow United Power’s governing documents, including its Bylaws. A
