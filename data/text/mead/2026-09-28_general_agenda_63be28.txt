Agenda Item Summary  
  
Agenda Date: 9/28/2026  
Subject: Resolution No. 01-H66-2026 - A Resolution of the Town of Mead 
Highway 66 and I-25 General Improvement District Approving an 
Intergovernmental Agreement Between the Town of Mead, 
Colorado and the Town of Mead Highway 66 and I-25 General 
Improvement District  
Presented by: Marcus McAskin, Town Attorney 
  
Summary: 
The Town of Mead Highway 66 and I-25 General Improvement District (the “District”) is a public 
or quasi-municipal corporation of the State of Colorado and a body corporate under Part 6, 
Article 25, Title 31, Colorado Revised Statutes (the “Act”). Pursuant to Section 31-25-607(4)(c) 
of the Act, the Town of Mead (the “Town”) Board of Trustees previously declared the District 
organized by Ordinance No. 1071, dated November 25, 2024.  
 
The District was established to reimburse costs and expenses of installation and construction of 
public improvements within or serving development within the boundaries of the District, and to 
assist with defraying the costs associated with administering the District and operating and 
maintaining said improvements. 
 
Section 12-4-60 of the Mead Municipal Code (“MMC”) requires each general improvement 
district (“GID”) to execute an intergovernmental agreement with the Town to memorialize the 
terms and obligations of the GID petition for organization. Staff recommends entering into an 
intergovernmental agreement with the Town to satisfy the requirement set forth Section 12-4-60 
of the MMC (the “IGA”).  
 
Resolution 01-H66-2026 (the “Resolution”) approves the IGA and authorizes the GID 
Chairperson to execute the IGA on behalf of the District when in final form. 
 
  
Financial Considerations: 
The IGA establishes that District revenue, not Town general revenue, will fund District-related 
costs. Administrative and O&M levy revenue is remitted to the Town to offset its staff, legal, 
administrative, and eligible maintenance costs. Contract-payment and debt-service levy 
revenues must be held in separate Town-administered funds and released only for approved, 
documented payments or reimbursements. 
  
Staff Recommendation / Actions Required: 
Staff recommends adopting the Resolution, approving the IGA. A motion to approve the 
September 28, 2026, consent agenda will approve the Resolution.  If this item is removed from 
the consent agenda for discussion, Staff recommends the following motion:

2. District use of Town resources.  
a) At the direction of the Town Manager, the Town agrees to perform operation 
and maintenance associated with  public improvements within or without the 
boundaries of the District and which directly or indirectly serve development 
within the boundaries of the District. Pursuant to the Petition, the Town also 
agrees to amend its snow plowing routes to give priority to the streets located 
within the boundaries of the District. 
b) At the direction  of the Town Manager, the Town agrees to provide Town 
employees (collectively, “Town Staff”) to the District on an as -needed basis 
to perform administrative services of the District including, but not limited to, 
accounting, treasury, budgeting, auditing, management, and procurement 
services. Town Staff working on behalf of the District are authorized to utilize 
Town office space, furnishings, and equipment, including telephones, 
printers, photocopiers, computers, office supplies, and similar equipment, to 
conduct the District’s business.  
c) As authorized by Section 12 -4-80(b) of the Town GID Regulations , at the 
direction of the Town Manager, the Town Attorney may provide legal advice 
and services to the District . T he cost of any such legal services shall be 
allocated to the District at a rate approved by the Town Manager but not in 
excess of the rate charged to the Town for similar legal services.
