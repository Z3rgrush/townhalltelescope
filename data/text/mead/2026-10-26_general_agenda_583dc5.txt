Agenda Date: 10/26/2026  
Subject: Resolution No. 02-H66-2026 - A Resolution of the Town of Mead 
Highway 66 and I-25 General Improvement District (GID) Board of 
Directors Summarizing Expenditures and Revenues, Adopting a 
Budget, Setting the Mill Levy, and Appropriating Funds for the GID 
for the Calendar Year Beginning on the First Day of January 2027 
and Ending on the Last Day of December 2027  
Presented by: Mary Strutt, Administrative Services Director 
  
Summary: 
The Town of Mead Highway 66 and I-25 General Improvement District (GID) was created by 
Town Ordinance No. 1071 dated November 25, 2024. The proposed budget for 2027 was 
presented to the Board of Directors (Board) of the GID on September 28, 2026. The October 26, 
2026 public hearing for the proposed 2027 GID budget is for final review, public input and 
adoption of the budget. Changes to the budget since the initial presentation include a 
recalculation of the mill levy for revenue collection and for payment of the Operations and 
Maintenance obligation to the Town and an increase to the administrative overhead. According 
to the organizational election, the mill levy shall be adjusted for changes to the method of 
calculating assessed value occurring after January 1, 2023. The adjustment would increase the 
mill levy from 7.00 mills to approximately 7.500 mills. The final mill levy will be calculated and 
certified after receipt of final assessed values are received from Weld County. In addition, 
according to Mead Municipal Code, the administrative overhead amount of $7500 may be 
adjusted by CPI each January 1st.  
 
For the public hearing, staff will review the budget, followed by an opportunity for public input 
and questions from the Board. The attached Resolution adopts the 2027 budget, sets the 2026 
mill levy (for collection in 2027) and appropriates funds.  
Financial Considerations: 
The draft budget includes revenues for FY 2027 of $38,150. Expenditures are $36,300. 
Staff Recommendation / Actions Required: 
Staff recommends approval of the 2027 budget as presented.

Subject: Resolution No. 02-E25-2026 - A Resolution of the Town of Mead

Summarizing Expenditures and Revenues, Adopting a Budget, 
Setting the Mill Levy, and Appropriating Funds for the GID for the 
Calendar Year Beginning on the First Day of January 2027 and 
Ending on the Last Day of December 2027  
Presented by: Mary Strutt, Administrative Services Director 
  
Summary: 
The Town of Mead Elevation 25 General Improvement District (GID) was created by Town 
Ordinance No. 1019 dated January 9, 2023. The proposed budget for 2027 was presented to 
the Board of Directors (Board) of the GID on September 28, 2026. The October 26, 2026 public 
hearing for the proposed 2027 GID budget is for final review, public input and adoption of the 
budget. Changes to the budget since the initial presentation include a recalculation of the mill 
levy for revenue collection and for payment of the Operations and Maintenance obligation to the 
Town and an increase to the administrative overhead. According to the organizational election, 
the mill levy shall be adjusted for changes to the method of calculating assessed value 
occurring after January 1, 2023. The adjustment would increase the mill levy from 5.00 mills to 
approximately 5.340 mills. The final mill levy will be calculated and certified after receipt of final 
assessed values are received from Weld County. In addition, according to Mead Municipal 
Code, the administrative overhead minimum amount of $7500 may be adjusted by CPI each 
January 1st.  
 
For the public hearing, staff will review the budget, followed by an opportunity for public input 
and questions from the Board. The attached Resolution adopts the 2027 budget, sets the 2026 
mill levy (for collection in 2027) and appropriates funds.  
Financial Considerations: 
The draft budget includes revenues for FY 2027 of $51,900. Expenditures are $41,250. 
Staff Recommendation / Actions Required: 
Staff recommends approval of the 2027 budget as presented.
