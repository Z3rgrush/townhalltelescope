PARCEL NO. 36896"? (L) 
Date: 10/5/2026 
 
 
Background/Analysis: 
In March of this year, the Town submitted the attached letter to Park County asking to acquire two 
parcels of land owned by them. The parcel behind 501 Main was deeded to the Town in August, 
and Park County then suggested the Town and County consider and exchange of parcels for the 
property that sits at the NWC of 5th and Clark. Andy Grand, Park County Director of Development 
Services, has obtained approval of the exchange from the Board of Commissioners in September 
and now the Board of Trustees is being requested to approve the same. 
 
Town Attorney Joe Rivera has reviewed the deeds and provided Ordinance No. 5 which authorizes 
the land exchange with Park County as follows - In exchange for a vacant residential lot, 17 Kiwi 
Court (Park County Parcel No. 32670), the Town will acquire Park County’s former Public Works 
maintenance building site located at 5th and Clark (Park County Parcel No. 36896). The parcels are 
being conveyed via the attached quit claim deeds. 
 
Recommendation: 
I move to approve adoption of Ordinance No. 5, AN ORDINANCE BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF 
THE TOWN OF FAIRPLAY, COLORADO AUTHORIZING A LAND SWAP WITH THE PARK COUNTY 
BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS WHEREBY THE TOWN CONVEYS TO PARK COUNTY REAL

LEGISLATIVE HEARING 
 
The Board acts in its legislative capacity when it considers a modification to the Town’s code (adding 
to or revising the Town’s code) or takes a specific action which require s an ordinance. During 
legislative hearings, the Board seeks to provide a reasonable opportunity for all interested parties  
and the public to offer an opinion on the proposed legislative action, so long as the shared opinion is 
reasonably related to the legislative action at issue.  
 
The Board will generally conduct a legislative hearing using the following format:  
 
(1) Town staff presentation and Board questions for Town staff.  
 
(2) Public questions and public comment  (for and against ) with additional Board 
questions. 
 
(3) Town staff follow up to public questions/comments with additional Board questions.  
 
(4) Board only deliberation. At this point, it is best to announce that the Board is 
beginning Board only deliberations. However, at the discretion of the presiding 
officer, the presiding officer may re-open the legislative hearing and accept additional 
public questions/comments. If the public comment is re -opened, after the second 
round of public comment is concluded, it is best to make a second announcement that 
the Board is re-entering Board only deliberation.  
 
(5) Voting: All Board members in attendance during the legislative hearing may 
participate in and vote on the legislative action at issue. 
 
(a) Move to approve.  
 
(b) Move to revise/amend.  
 
(c) Move to deny. 
 
(d) Move to continue to a date certain. If final action is not taken at the legislative 
hearing and the Board wishes to revisit this Ordinance at a future time, it is a 
best practice to continue the legislative hearing to a date certain. An appropriate 
motion would be “I move to continue the Board’s consideration of Ordinance

RE:  Town of Fairplay Request to Acquire Park County Owned Parcels R0036896 and R0000472  
 
Honorable Board of Park County Commissioners: 
 
On behalf of the Town of Fairplay Board of Trustees, we would like to express our appreciation for the County’s 
continued partnership and collaboration in supporting the needs of our shared community. We are writing to 
formally express the Town’s interest in acquiring two vacant parcels currently owned by Park County for public 
benefit purposes. 
 
The first parcel, identified as Parcel No. R0036896, presents a critical opportunity to address the growing need 
for affordable workforce housing within the Town of Fairplay. As you are aware, the Town has opted in to 
Proposition 123 and is actively pursuing funding opportunities associated with that program. Acquisition of this 
parcel would allow the Town to move forward with the development of much-needed workforce housing units, 
helping to support local employees, retain essential services, and strengthen the long-term sustainability of our 
community. 
 
The second parcel, identified as Parcel No. R0000472, is located within the Town’s downtown commercial core. 
The Town intends to incorporate this property into a broader infrastructure improvement effort that includes 
paving enhancements and the installation of electric vehicle charging units. In addition to supporting these 
improvements, the parcel would provide valuable events space as well as parking capacity for downtown 
businesses, thereby contributing to economic vitality and improving access for residents and visitors alike. 
 
Both of these projects align with shared goals of enhancing livability, supporting economic development, and 
investing in critical infrastructure within Park County. We believe that transferring these parcels to the Town 
would ensure their use in a manner that maximizes public benefit and serves current and future community 
needs. 
 
We respectfully request the opportunity to discuss the potential acquisition of these parcels, including terms, 
timelines, and any considerations the County may have. The Town is committed to working collaboratively with 
the County to ensure a smooth and mutually beneficial process. Thank you for your time and consideration. We 
look forward to continuing our strong partnership and to discussing this opportunity further. 
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
Frank Just, Mayor     Janell Sciacca, Town Administrator

WHEREAS, the Town of Fairplay, Colorado (“Town”) is a statutory town and political 
subdivision of the State of Colorado and acts through its elected Board of Trustees (“Board”); 
and 
WHEREAS, under C.R.S. § 31-15-101, et seq., the Town may contract with private 
parties and may acquire, hold, lease, or dispose of real property; and 
 
WHEREAS, under C.R.S. § 31-15-713, et seq., by ordinance, the Town may sell and 
dispose of any real estate not used or held for park purposes or any governmental purpose; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board approves the conveyance of Park County Parcel No. 32670, 
AKA 17 Kiwi Court, to Park County through a quit claim deed; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Town acquired Park County Parcel No. 32670, a vacant residential lot, 
in 2009, and the property remains a vacant residential lot; and 
 
WHEREAS, in exchange for Park County Parcel No. 32670, the Park County Board of 
Commissioners will convey Park County Parcel No. 36896 to the Town through a quit claim 
deed; and  
 
WHEREAS, at present, Park County uses Park County Parcel No. 36896 as a vehicle 
service garage.  
 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF 
THE TOWN OF FAIRPLAY, COLORADO: 
Section 1. The recitals contained above are incorporated herein by reference and are 
adopted as the Board’s findings and determinations. 
 
Section 2. The Board (A) approves the conveyance of Park County Parcel No. 
32670, AKA 17 Kiwi Court, to Park County in exchange for Park County Parcel No. 36896 
and, (B) in a form approved by the Town Attorney, authorizes the Town’s Mayor to execute all 
related documents necessary for the above described land swap, and (C) the Town Clerk is 
hereby authorized and directed to take such other ministerial actions necessary to implement 
this decision of the Board of Trustees via this ordinance, including, but not limited to, filing any 
necessary property records with the Clerk and Recorder of Park County, Colorado.
