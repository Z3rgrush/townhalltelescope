RE: AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF FAIRPLAY , COLORADO REPEALING 
AND RE-ENACTING SECTION 8-72 OF THE FAIRPLAY MUNICIPAL CODE 
TO ADJUST THE PENALTIES FOR SPEEDING VIOLATIONS DETECTED BY 
THE TOWN’S AUTOMATED VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM 
DATE: November 2, 2026 
 
 
The Colorado General Assembly intended the enforcement of AV I S citations to be uniform 
across Colorado. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(1) (“The general assembly hereby finds and declares that 
the enforcement of traffic laws through the use of automated vehicle identification systems under 
this section is a matter of statewide concern and is an area in which uniform state standards 
are necessary.”  
 
The Town must publish the number of A VIS citations and amount of revenue collected 
through A VIS citations. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(2)(g)(IV) (“The state, a county, a city and county, 
or a municipality implementing an automated vehicle identification corridor pursuant to 
subsection (2)(g)(I) of this section shall annually publish a report on its website disclosing the 
number of citations and amount of revenue generated by the automated vehicle 
identification corridor.”). The Town has two (2) A VIS corridors. See below.  
 
Under A VIS, the pursuit of the collection of fines requires personal service. C.R.S. § 42-4-
110.5(2)(a)(IX) (“The state, a county, a city and county, or a municipality shall not initiate or 
pursue a collection action against a registered owner of a motor vehicle for a debt resulting from 
an unpaid penalty assessed pursuant to this section unless the registered owner is personally 
served the notice of violation or the final order of liability.”). Because A VIS violators are not 
stopped by Fairplay PD, A VIS violators are not personally served.  
 
Colorado law establishes a uniform penalty scheme. AV I S  penalties are determined by the 
vehicle’s speed, number of previous A VIS citations, and whether the offense occurred in a 
School Zone or Construction Zone.

• penalties for traveling less than six MPH over speed limit 
 
If a vehicle travels less than 6 miles per hour over the posted speed limit, then the 
Town may mail the registered owner a warning regarding the violation. C.R.S. § 42-
4-110.5(4)(a)(I). 
 
If a vehicle travels less than 6 miles per hour over the posted speed limit, and the 
violation occurs in a School Zone or a Construction Zone, then the Town may impose 
a penalty of up to $40, including any surcharge. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(a)(I). 
 
• penalties for traveling six to nine MPH over speed limit 
 
If a vehicle is traveling at least six and less than ten miles per hour over the posted 
speed limit, and the violation is the first speeding violation by the registered owner 
that the Town has detected using an automated vehicle identification system, the 
Town shall mail the registered owner a warning regarding the violation and shall not 
impose a penalty or surcharge for such first violation. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(a)(II). 
 
If a vehicle travels at least six and less than ten miles per hour over the posted speed 
limit, and the violation is a second or subsequent speeding violation, then the Town 
may impose a penalty of up to $40, including any surcharge.  C.R.S. § 42-4-
110.5(4)(b)(I)(A). 
 
If a vehicle travels at least six and less than ten miles per hour over the posted speed 
limit, and the violation occurs in a School Zone or a Construction Zone, then the 
Town may impose a penalty of up to $40, including any surcharge. C.R.S. § 42-4-
110.5(4)(a)(III). 
 
If a vehicle is traveling at least six and less than ten miles per hour over the posted 
speed limit, and the violation is a second or subsequent speeding violation, and the 
violation occurs in a School Zone or a Construction Zone, then the Town may 
impose a penalty of up to $80, including any surcharge. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(b)(II).  
 
• penalties for traveling ten to twenty-four MPH over speed limit 
 
If a vehicle travels at least ten and less than twenty-five miles per hour over the 
posted speed limit, then the Town may impose a penalty of up to $40, including any 
surcharge. C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(b)(I)(A).  
 
If a vehicle travels at least ten and less than twenty-five miles per hour over the 
posted speed limit, and the violation occurs in a School Zone or a Construction 
Zone, then the Town may impose a penalty of up to $80, including any surcharge. 
C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(b)(II).

TOWN OF FAIRPLAY, COLORADO 
 
ORDINANCE NO. 6 
(Series of 2026) 
 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF FAIRPLAY, COLORADO REPEALING 
AND RE-ENACTING SECTION 8-72 OF THE FAIRPLAY MUNICIPAL CODE 
TO ADJUST THE PENALTIES FOR SPEEDING VIOLATIONS DETECTED BY 
THE TOWN’S AUTOMATED VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM 
WHEREAS, the Town of Fairplay, Colorado ("Town") is a statutory town, duly organized and 
existing under the laws of the State of Colorado; and 
WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-15-401, the Town by and through its Board of Trustees 
("Board"), possesses the authority to adopt laws and ordinances within its police power in 
furtherance of the public health, safety and welfare; and 
WHEREAS, pursuant to this authority, the Board previously adopted Article 4 of Chapter 8 of 
the Fairplay Municipal Code (“Code”) and established an automated vehicle identification 
system (“AVIS”) enforcement system within the Town to detect certain traffic violations; and 
WHEREAS, subsequent to the Board’s adoption of Article 4, Chapter 8, the State of Colorado 
updated the penalties and rules relating to enforcement of AVIS detected speeding violations; 
and 
WHEREAS, the Board finds, determines, and declares that the adoption of updated penalties 
for AVIS detected speeding violations is in the best interests of the citizens of the Town and 
furthers the protection of the public health, safety, and welfare. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE 
TOWN OF FAIRPLAY, COLORADO AS FOLLOWS: 
Section 1. Section 8-72 of the Code, concerning speeding violations detected by automated 
vehicle identification systems, is hereby repealed and replaced to read in its entirety as follows: 
Sec. 8-72 – Speeding. 
(a) Violations and Civil Penalties. The Town is hereby authorized to detect 
speeding violations and issue various civil penalties for same in accordance 
with the limitations and authorizations provided by C.R.S. § 42-4-110.5(4)(a)–
(c). 
(b) Signage. The Town shall place an appropriate temporary or permanent sign 
in conspicuous place not fewer than 300 feet before the area in which the 
automated vehicle identification system is to be used to notify the public that 
an automated vehicle identification system is in use immediately ahead.

responding to these requests.

SCOPE OF SERVICES 
The Contractor agrees to remove / plow snow from parking lots and sidewalks within the Town 
of Fairplay, Colorado, as shown in Exhibit B attached hereto.  
. 
For purposes of this Agreement, to “remove snow” or “plow shall mean that the Contractor uses 
vehicles and equipment specifically designed for the purpose of snow and ice removal and that 
the road and sidewalks are made reasonably passable for the common passenger vehicle and 
pedestrians. 
The Contractor shall use its best efforts to avoid the pushing and piling of snow into or onto 
residents' driveways in a manner that will prevent a common passenger vehicle from exiting or 
entering the driveway without additional efforts to remove the accumulation of snow. The 
Contractor shall also use its best efforts to keep from damaging trees and other property along 
the roadways and turnarounds. 
The Contractor shall use its best efforts to avoid the pushing and piling of snow into or onto or 
Town or State-owned streets, alleys and rights of way. The Contractor will receive instruction 
from the Public Works Supervisor on specific locations as to where snow and ice can be pushed 
or piled. 
If, during spring melt or warm weather, in the reasonable opinion of the Town Administrator or 
Public Works Supervisor, the accumulation of slush renders any lot or sidewalk impassable to 
normal vehicular traffic, or creates a driving or walking hazard, slush removal operations will be 
required. Slush removal will be required only during the period that slush removal operations are 
effective, normally between 10 AM and 4:00 PM MDT 
Obstruction by Vehicles. In the event any lot is partially or completely obstructed by a parked, 
stalled or abandoned vehicle, the Contractor shall make every effort to plow around the vehicle 
without damaging the vehicle. If this effort will create additional hazards, maintenance problems 
and/or safety problems, the Contractor shall not be required to plow past the obstruction, 
provided, however, the Contractor shall notify, first the Town Police Department or Town 
Administrator, as soon as possible, but not later than two (2) hours from Contractor’s discovery 
of the situation so that it can be remedied. If the Town requests that the Contractor return to plow 
areas where vehicles have been moved, the Contractor shall be compensated at the hourly rate set 
forth on Exhibit “C”.  
Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary, the Town shall not indemnify or hold the 
Contractor harmless for any Contractor caused damage to vehicles during the performance of the 
services under this Agreement.

Staff Memorandum  
To: Board of Trustees 
From: Janell Sciacca, Town Administrator 
Joe Rivera, Town Attorney 
RE: Discussion regarding fees for Park County's use of Town Water for outside of Town 
parcels related to Landfill contamination. 
Date: 11/2/2026 
 
 
Background/Analysis: 
As the Board is aware, the Town discovered that Park County has been taking Town water without 
permission or agreement since October 2025 and providing it to homeowners in the County whose 
wells have been contaminated by polluted groundwater.  
 
Staff has been in contact with Park County Public Works and Administration regarding this matter 
and advised that the Town was amenable to helping them out in the spirit of intergovernmental 
cooperation. However, we needed to know how much water is going to be needed and how the 
water is being obtained.  Public Works Director Nick Bredsnajder indicated last month that the total 
since October 2025 is approximately 25,000, but he has not provided any information on how or 
where the water is being obtained. Staff has advised that the Town is very concerned about cross 
contamination / backflow, yet there still is no cooperation on their part in providing the requested 
information. 
 
In a letter provided to Assistant County Manager Brandon Heacock dated September 23, 2026, this 
matter was addressed and the following Fairplay Municiipal Code provisions was shared advising 
that Park County's actions were a violation and could result in action by the Town: 
 
Sec. 13-1-170. - Supplying water to others. 
The Town furnishes water for a certain specified sum. If, therefore, a consumer furnishes water 
to other users, or permits it to be taken without the consent or knowledge of the Town, such 
conduct shall be deemed a violation of this Article and the consumer shall be required to pay 
double the price of the water so used. The Town may also shut off the supply for abuse of water 
privileges. When the water has been turned off for violation of this Section, the water will not be 
turned on again until all water charges and penalties have been paid, including an additional 
sum in an amount to be set and determined by the Board of Trustees by resolution. 
 
To date, there has been no response and Staff is now seeking specific direction from the Board of 
Trustees on dealing with this matter.  While the Board previously gave verbal agreement that the 
Town would be willing to continue providing water in the spirit of intergovernmental cooperation, a 
Page 53 of 54
formal agreement needs to be approved by both entities and the Town must protect the Town’s 
water system. 
 
 
Recommendation: 
Discuss the situation and provide direction to Staff for formation of a letter and/or proposed 
agreement to be presented to Park County to formalize the Town's commitment to provide water to 
affected property owners and determine any associated fees to be assessed if any.
