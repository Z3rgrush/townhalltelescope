to the north, a drive-through bank to the east, and then once again, the Cosmopolitan 
first phase building there to the south.  
 
[Comprehensive Plan and Zoning slide] 
The Future Land Use shows mixed-use.  And then the current zoning, once again, is 
Townsquare.  You do have some R-1 to the west and to the north.

COMPREHENSIVE PLAN AND ZONING 
 
Future Land Use Framework 
The subject site is designated as Traditional Residential Neighborhood within the Achieve Shawnee 
Future Land Use Framework. The Traditional Residential Neighborhood District supports single 
family, detached residential uses, typically within a subdivision. Lots are connected to Local Roads 
and Minor Residential Collectors, with development entries connected to Collectors and Arterials. 
They should have sidewalks and other mobility  amenities such as shared used paths. While 
connectivity should be provided to private amenities, such as neighborhood pools, the 
neighborhoods should also provide connections to public parks, open space, schools, and the 
broader community in some way.

Considerations 
Johnson Country records show that the property has been developed with a single-family house 
since 1900. In 2019 , a rezoning from AG (Agricultural) to R -1 (Single Family Residential) was 
approved for all the property except the northwest 5. 6 acres around the existing house and 
accessory farm structures. The intent of the rezoning was to allow for the development of a 24-lot 
subdivision known as Beaver’s Farms. The first phase of Beaver’s Farms included 6 lots along the 
east side of the property adjacent to Belmont Drive. Since that time the remainder of the property 
has sat idle and was subsequently sold for the development of a new single-family house. 
 
The applicant intends to construct a new single-family house and convert the existing house to an 
accessory structure. The underlying R-1 zoning district allows for a maximum of one accessory 
structure. The AG zoning district does not limit the amount or size of accessory structures. A 
rezoning to the AG zoning district will eliminate a split zoning on the property and ensure that a 
non-conforming situation is not created with the construction of the new house and conversion of 
the existing house to an accessory structure. 
 
The Future Land Use Map identifies this property as appropriate for Traditional Residential 
developments. The Traditional Residential District supports single family residential uses; although 
typically these include subdivisions with supporting infrastructure.  
 
Per Chapter 17.92.030.D. of the Shawnee Municipal Code, the Planning Commission should 
consider the following factors when reviewing a request for rezoning: 
 
1. Existing uses and zoning classifications within the general area of the property in question; 
2. Suitability of the property to uses permitted under the existing zoning classification; 
3. The character of the neighborhood;  
4. The length of time the subject property has remained vacant as zoned;  
5. The relative gain to the public health, safety, and welfare by the destruction of the value of 
the owners' property as compared to the hardship imposed upon individual landowners;  
6. The extent to which the removal of the restriction will detrimentally affect nearby property; 
7. The recommendations of professional staff;  and 
8. Conformance of the requested change to  the adopted Comprehensive Plan.
