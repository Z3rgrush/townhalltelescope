MR. ALLMON:  Good evening, everyone.  The rezoning and preliminary site plan for 
this request proposes a 277-unit multi-family apartment building.  It is four to five 
stories in height with a two-story below grade parking garage.  The subject site is 
designated as Attached Residential Neighborhood within the Shawnee Future Land 
Use Framework.  The Attached Residential district supports mainly multi-family 
attached housing.  The proposed use is in conformance with the Attached Residential 
District in the Achieve Shawnee Comprehensive Plan, Future Land Use Framework.   
 
The property has been vacant under an Office Commercial zoning designation since 
1998.  The site sits in a valley that drops over 50 feet in elevation from southwest to the 
northwest -- northeast corner.  This creates a significant site constraint and limits the 
types of development that may be feasible.   
 
The adjacent properties include Park Lanes Family Fun Center, Shawnee Mission 
Park, Federated Rural Insurance Exchange building, and I-435 to the east.  
 
A rezoning to RHR (Residential High Rise) will not have a detrimental effect on 
surrounding areas, nor will it have a negative impact on the health, welfare or safety of 
the community.   
 
A public hearing was held on this item at the, excuse me, July 20th, 2026, Planning 
Commission meeting.  No one from the public spoke in regard to the item.  The 
Commissioners did have a little discussion about parking and access to the site.  But 
after hearing from the applicant and from staff, they were comfortable that the 
application that was presented was appropriate for the site.  
 
This does require an ordinance, so five votes are required to approve the request in 
terms of the recommendation of the Planning Commission, or six votes to deny the 
request by overriding the recommendation of the Planning Commission.  The Planning 
Commission, by a unanimous vote, did recommend that the Governing Body approve 
RZ26-01, subject to the conditions listed in the staff report.  And with that, I would stop 
my comments and invite the Rec Centric team to go over some photos and slides.
