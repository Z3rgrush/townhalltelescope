SUBJECT: Midland Entertainment District Area Plan 
 
BACKGROUND: 
On May 11, 2026, the Governing Body approved an ordinance passing a Tax Increment 
Financing District (TIF) and establishing a redevelopment district as required by state statute 
within the Midland Entertainment District (MED) area, generally located at the intersection of 
435 and Midland Drive. The Redevelopment District contains approximately 25 developed 
commercial parcels with diverse ownership, along with multiple undeveloped parcels. The 
Redevelopment District also contains various parcels that are either currently under 
development, or may be later developed, for residential, multi-family, and other uses. 
  
The district has seen limited development progress in recent years, which raises concerns 
about declining value, potential vacant buildings, a lack of new investment, and a general 
disinterest in the available parcels. In order to attract additional residential, hospitality, retail, and 
other in investment to TIF the support City the uses, development commercial created 
infrastructure necessary for redevelopment.  
  
The TIF ordinance allows for the diversion of incremental property tax revenues within a 
TIF district. Within the TIF district, multiple project plans may considered to address 
needs throughout. Project Plans can be brought forth by the City or private developers.

Midland Entertainment District Area Plan
Preliminary Project Outline – September 2026
Scope:
•Address bicycle and pedestrian connectivity to the new Midland Drive improvements, 
within the district, and to Shawnee Mission Park.
•Enhance the identity of the district through branding and physical place-making 
improvements.
•Incorporate green infrastructure stormwater management.
•Complete an analysis of underdeveloped and undeveloped sites to include highest and 
best use recommendations.
•Robust community engagement, including but not limited to an appointed, external 
stakeholder advisory committee, public project website, collaborative workshops or 
roundtables, public meetings, pop-ups at City events, and other digital media tools.
