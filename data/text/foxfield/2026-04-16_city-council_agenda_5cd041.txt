5. For Discussion
a. Ordinance 2026-01 An Ordinance Setting Compensation for the Mayor and
Board of Trustees Pursuant to C.R.S § 31-4-405 and Section 2-2-50 of the Town
of Foxfield Municipal Code
Ms. Proctor informed the board that she is proposing an increase for all Trustees.
She explained Attorney Hoffmann’s direction on how we can proceed forward with
this. Trustee Schultz asked for the reason why this was being proposed now. Ms.
Proctor said the amount hasn’t changed in 25 years. Trustee Hodge said we are
considering future Trustees. Trustee Thompson questioned if the mayor should be
paid more due to increased responsibility over other Trustees. Trustees agreed to an
increased amount for the Mayor. Mayor Jones moved to approve Ordinance 2026-
01 Setting Compensation for the Mayor and Board of Trustees pursuant to C.R.S 31-
4-405 and Section 2-2-50 of the Town of Foxfield Municipal Code as amended with
the Mayor receiving $400 per month, seconded by Trustee Cockrell. Passed
unanimously.
Item #3a
 
b. Resolution 2026-05 Revising the Rules of Procedure 
Mayor Jones explained what has changed since the last time this was reviewed. 
Trustee Cockrell asked for clarification on whether we are taking comments after 
staff presentation or right after Trustee discussion. Trustees discussed and agreed 
that comments should come after Trustee discussion. Mayor Jones moved to 
approve, seconded by Trustee Hodge. Passed unanimously.

RE
: Land Use Code  Public Hearing  
DISCUSSION: 
The project to update the Town of Foxfield Land Use Code began about 3 years ago.  The scope 
of the project only included Chapter 16 of the code.  The goal was to make the Land Use Code 
more user friendly with better organization and the use of some tables.  In addition, a legal 
review was carried out and a comprehensive overhaul of current regulations to conform with 
best practice was done.

Chapter 16: Land Use Code 
Article 1: General Provisions 
1. Applicability1 
a. The provisions of this Article shall apply to and may be enforced within 
all areas of the Town. 
b. Except with respect to approved official development plans and final 
development plans for land zoned Planned Development (PD), whenever a 
provision of this Chapter and any other provision of this Chapter, other 
law ordinance, resolution, rule or regulation of any kind contains any 
restrictions which cover the same subject matter, the more restrictive 
requirements shall govern. 
2. Purpose2 
This Chapter shall be for the purpose of promoting the health, safety, 
convenience, order, prosperity and welfare of the present and future residents of 
the Town by lessening of congestion on the streets and roads or reducing the 
waste of excessive amounts of roads, securing safety from fire and other dangers, 
providing adequate light and air, classifying land uses and the distribution of land 
development and utilization, protecting and enhancing the tax base, securing 
economy in governmental expenditures, protecting urban and non-urban 
development and providing for the implementation of the goals and policies of the  
Master Plan or other policies approved by the Board of Trustees.  
3. Title and Short Title3 
This Chapter, as amended, shall be known and may be cited as the Town of 
Foxfield Land Use Code. It may also be referred to within this document as the 
"Land Use Code," "LUC," "Chapter 16," or "this Chapter."

establishing land use classifications, dividing the Town into districts, 
imposing regulations, prohibitions, procedures and restrictions, governing 
the use of land for residential and nonresidential purposes, regulating and 
limiting lot occupancy, determining the size of yards and other open 
spaces, establishing standards of performance and design, adopting a map 
of said zone districts, prescribing procedures for changes and 
modifications of districts, uses by special review, variances and other 
permits, allowing for nonconforming uses, structures and land, providing 
regulations for accessory uses and buildings, providing for the adjustment, 
amendment and enforcement thereof, defining certain terms, providing a

9. Transition Rules14 
a. Development Approvals 
i. Any development approved under regulations in effect prior to the 
effective date of this Chapter may be carried out under the terms 
and conditions of the approval and the development standards in 
effect at the time of approval, provided the approval has not 
expired and the development complies with any applicable 
standards of this Chapter regarding ongoing operations and 
maintenance.  
ii. If the prior approval expires, is revoked, or otherwise becomes 
invalid, any subsequent development of the site shall be subject to 
the procedures and standards of this Chapter.  
b. Pending Applications 
A development application that has been determined and documented to 
be complete pursuant to the Town Planner, prior to the effective date of 
this Chapter may be decided under the regulations in effect when the 
application was determined to be complete or may be reviewed and 
decided under this Chapter at the request of the applicant. Applications 
shall not be processed under a combination of prior regulations and this 
Chapter. 
c. Prior Violations 
i. If a development or activity in violation of the prior development 
regulations fully complies with this Chapter, it shall no longer be 
deemed a violation.  
ii. Unpaid fees and/or penalties from prior enforcement of violations 
are still valid and shall remain the responsibility of the violator 
under the prior regulations, unless otherwise waived by the Town.

5. Purpose53 
The intent of this Article is to encourage the creation of safe, adequate and 
attractive facilities and to minimize views of unattractive uses or activities 
through use of sound site design principles and the establishment of minimum 
requirements. The standards set forth herein are recognized as enhancing the 
compatibility of dissimilar uses and promoting stable property values.

not be retroactive on existing uses. However, these standards shall apply 
to all uses in all zoning districts under the following circumstances54: 
i. New buildings and uses of land that require a permit, license, plot 
plan or site development plan.  
ii. Additions involving expansion of the gross floor area by twenty 
percent (20%) or more above that in existence prior to the effective 
date of this Chapter. 
iii. A change in the use of the building or land which requires a 
change in the zoning district or a special review use permit. 
iv. A change in the occupancy of a building or land, which requires a 
new sign or other site improvements addressed in this Article.

granting of a change in use in any zoning district for any property, the 
applicant shall demonstrate that the property complies with all the 
applicable regulations.

noted in the general regulations and development standards in this Article 
shall be constructed and installed in accordance with the approved plans 
prior to issuance of a certificate of occupancy for the building or use. 
i. The Town Planner may allow certain improvements to be 
constructed or installed within an agreed-upon time, allowing for 
seasonal changes55.

use, or temporary use not listed in this Chapter, provided that such

13. Grading and Drainage75 
a. Purpose. The purpose of this Section is to provide regulations to monitor 
and control the high rates of erosion and sedimentation due to land 
disturbance caused by grading (cut or fill). This Section is further intended 
to ensure the protection of existing drainageways as well as private and/or 
public property adjacent to any disturbed area regulated by this Article. 
b. Overlot Grading Permit Required. An overlot grading permit is required if 
any one of the following conditions occurs: 
i. The area of grading is greater than 10,000 square feet. 
ii. More than 100 cubic yards of fill material is imported or placed 
from on site cut; 
iii. The proposed grading increases or changes the historical flow of 
surface water to adjacent lots or the 
iv. Town rights-of-way; or 
v. The disturbed area is within 75 feet of an existing drainageway, 
floodplain or wetlands as determined by the Town Engineer.

f. Locational considerations in the siting of new development: 
i. Integrated structures and roadways into the surrounding natural 
landscape and topography.  
ii. Avoid visible construction cuts and permanent scarring. 
iii. Orient lots toward views and vistas. 
iv. Locate lots at right angles to contour lines.

v. Place development in less sensitive areas of the site. 
vi. Minimize disturbance of natural features, such as slopes, drainage 
areas, flood prone areas and open spaces. 
vii. Engineer grade cuts and fills to imitate natural slope changes with 
rounded tops of cuts and gradual transitions at the toes of fills. 
viii. Fit structures into the existing topography of the site. Buildings

minimize site disturbance, it will also create architectural interest

g. Grading and site specifications 
Prior to submission of a grading plan, the applicant shall meet with the 
Town Planner to develop a grading plan which adequately addresses these 
standards, engineering standards and other applicable ordinances and 
regulations. 
i. All slopes shall be landform graded. Landform grading is defined 
as a grading method that creates artificial slopes with curves and 
varying slope ratios. Landform grading is designed to simulate the 
appearance of surrounding natural terrain. 
ii. At the intersections of manufactured and natural slopes, abrupt 
angular intersections shall be avoided. Contours shall be curved to 
blend with the natural slope. 
iii. Grading for pads shall follow the contours of the existing 
underlying landform. 
iv. Standard prepared building pads resulting in grading outside of the 
building footprint and driveway area is discouraged. 
v. To minimize grading and protect natural contours, roads shall 
follow the natural contours.  
vi. All newly graded slopes shall be planted with appropriate erosion 
control plant materials. 
vii. Retaining walls located in public rights-of-way or visible from 
public spaces shall be faced with river rock or natural stone or 
constructed of interlocking blocks in earth tones and designed as 
an architectural extension of the primary structure. 
viii. Areas that will not be graded shall be delineated in the field with 
fences during construction to ensure that these areas remain 
undisturbed.

retaining walls shall provide benches to allow for landscaping and
