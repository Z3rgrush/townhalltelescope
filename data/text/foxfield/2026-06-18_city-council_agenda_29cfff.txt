BOARD OF TRUSTEES MEETING MINUTES 
June 4th, 2026 
Call to Order  
The meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. via Microsoft Teams. 
1. Pledge of Allegiance
2. Roll Call
The following Trustees were present in person: Mayor Pro-Tem Thompson, Trustee
Cockrell, Trustee Hodge, Trustee Lawrence, and Trustee Pakanati.
A quorum was present. 
3. Audience Participation
none
4. Consent Agenda
Mayor Pro-Tem Thompson moved to approve the minutes, seconded by Trustee Hodge.
Passed unanimously.
5. For Approval
a. Engineering Fee Estimate for 2026 Pavement Repair and Surface Treatment
Program
Ms. Proctor informed the board of the annual estimate for road maintenance.
Mayor Pro-Tem Thompson asked specifics about crack seal tonnage, communication
if material is short, the contingency put in place, and shoulder work not being
included. SEH provided details and agreed to address all the board’s concerns. Ms.
Proctor informed the board of Terracare’ s desire to bid for this work and asked the
board if they see any problems with that. Scott with SEH explained the standing
relationship with Vance and how that would be different for Terracare for this bid.
Ms. Proctor mentioned that she will work with Scott regarding a bid from Terracare.
Trustee discussion with SEH included crack seal material, cracks existing in town and
their condition, and the solution for different types of cracks. Mayor Pro-Tem
Thompson requested that if budget needs to be adjusted after SHE’s review in town,
to please communicate that to Ms. Proctor before work commences.
Mayor Pro-Tem Thompson moved to approve the SEH Fee Estimate for 2026
Pavement Repair and Surface Treatment, seconded Trustee Lawrence. Passes
unanimously.
Item #4a
6. Reports
a. Members of Town Board
i. Trustee Cockell informed the board about the meeting regarding the
fence issue with SafeBuilt and staff. Trustees agreed to take no action
and brainstormed ideas on how to address this in the future. Trustee
Cockrell investigated different fence types but recommended that it may
be suitable to ask residents to suggest the fence types they would like.
Ms. Proctor mentioned that our code needs to be more clear, and that
Jana, the Town Planner, was going to investigate what other jurisdictions
have. Trustee Cockrell provided an update on the trail study and that the
RFP will be submitted soon.
ii. Trustee Hodge mentioned how sales tax has dropped significantly over
the last four years. He asked about sales tax revenue and how we can
incentivize FVC to get more tenants. Trustees asked about the property
owners’ contact information which Ms. Torres provided.
iii. Trustee Lawrence asked if we had contacted Airbnb about the listing that
is violation of our Ordinance and providing them with a copy of it. Ms.
Torres will report the listing and provide the Ordinance. Trustee
Lawrence asked about light pole update. Ms. Proctor informed the board
that it has been ordered and explained the delays. She mentioned that
the contractor recommends replacing the whole fixture to be LED for the
lights that are not working.
iv
. Trustee Pakanati interpreted the speed data for the board. Trustees
discussed percentiles, how the data has changed, and what the data
needs to reflect to move forward. Trustee Pakanati mentioned the
number of cars that are speeding is concerning. Trustees discussion
included perception of speeding, different methods of gathering data,
engineer guidelines, patrol cars, reporting speeders to the Town, the
parameters of the data gathered, and different ways to address speeding.
Trustees asked staff to investigate the price of a solar powered flashing
sign that remits data and another that does not to compare.
v
. Mayor Pro-Tem Thompson updated the board on the 4th of July meeting.
She asked board members to bring a bag of candy for the parade. Trustee
Thompson provided an update on clean-up day.
b. Staff
i. Town Clerk Torres
1. Ms. Torres provided the board with an update on the Home Rule
article on the website. She confirmed with the board on cancelling
the July 2nd meeting and having the Wildfire Public Hearing on the
July 18th. Ms. Torres asked the board for the next steps for the
LUC. She provided the board with a notice for her time out of
office.

DISCUSSION: 
Town Attorney Hoffmann has advised the Town to adopt the Wildfire Resiliency Code by 
reference as instructed by the State of Colorado to remain in compliance with regulations. The 
purpose of this code, as established by the state, is to have minimum regulations for the 
safeguarding of life and for property protection. Regulations in this code are intended to 
mitigate the risk to life and structures from intrusion of fire from wildland fire exposures and 
fire exposures from adjacent structures, in addition to mitigate structure fires from spreading 
to wildland fuels. Town Attorney, Corey Hoffman, will be available to answer questions 
surrounding this code and questions related to the Town. The full Wild Fire Code can be found 
here > https://dfpc.colorado.gov/colorado-wildfire-resiliency-code.

No motion at this meeting. First reading for the Public Hearing will be at the July 16th meeting.

Chapter 16: Land Use Code 
Article 1: General Provisions 
1. Applicability1 
a. The provisions of this Article shall apply to and may be enforced within 
all areas of the Town. 
b. Except with respect to approved official development plans and final 
development plans for land zoned Planned Development (PD), whenever a 
provision of this Chapter and any other provision of this Chapter, other 
law ordinance, resolution, rule or regulation of any kind contains any 
restrictions which cover the same subject matter, the more restrictive 
requirements shall govern. 
2. Purpose2 
This Chapter shall be for the purpose of promoting the health, safety, 
convenience, order, prosperity and welfare of the present and future residents of 
the Town by lessening of congestion on the streets and roads or reducing the 
waste of excessive amounts of roads, securing safety from fire and other dangers, 
providing adequate light and air, classifying land uses and the distribution of land 
development and utilization, protecting and enhancing the tax base, securing 
economy in governmental expenditures, protecting urban and non-urban 
development and providing for the implementation of the goals and policies of the  
Master Plan or other policies approved by the Board of Trustees.  
3. Title and Short Title3 
This Chapter, as amended, shall be known and may be cited as the Town of 
Foxfield Land Use Code. It may also be referred to within this document as the 
"Land Use Code," "LUC," "Chapter 16," or "this Chapter." 
4. Authority4 
a. An ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Foxfield, Colorado, 
establishing land use classifications, dividing the Town into districts, 
imposing regulations, prohibitions, procedures and restrictions, governing 
the use of land for residential and nonresidential purposes, regulating and 
limiting lot occupancy, determining the size of yards and other open 
spaces, establishing standards of performance and design, adopting a map 
of said zone districts, prescribing procedures for changes and 
modifications of districts, uses by special review, variances and other

Chapter.

(1) Purposes. The purposes of this Section are: to allow the
