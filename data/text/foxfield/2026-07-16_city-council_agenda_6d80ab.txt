June 18, 2026 
 
 
Call to Order 
The meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. via Microsoft Teams. 
1. Pledge of Allegiance 
 
2. Roll Call of Board Members 
The following Trustees were present in person: Mayor Jones, Trustee Thompson, Trustee 
Cockrell, Trustee Hodge, Trustee Lawrence, Trustee Schultz and Trustee Pakanati. 
A quorum was present.  
3. Audience Participation 
No audience comments were made. 
4. Consent Agenda 
Mayor Jones moved to approve the Consent Agenda, including the minutes and financials, 
seconded by Trustee Cockrell.  Motion passed unanimously.  
                                                                                                                                                                                
5. For Discussion 
a. Ordinance 2026-03 Amending Chapter 18 of the Town of Foxfield Municipal Code by the 
Addition Thereto of a New Article 13 Adopting the Wildfire Resiliency Code by Reference  
The Board discussed the State-required Wildfire Resiliency Code, insurance implications, 
requirements for new construction and substantial remodels, vegetation management, and 
enforcement through building and fire code officials.  Attorney Hoffmann recommended we 
discuss this with South Metro Fire.  He stated most municipalities are adopting this code. 
 
b. Solar Powered Radar Speed Signs 
Town Administrator Proctor presented information regarding solar-powered radar speed 
signs. Trustees discussed effectiveness, costs, data collection capabilities, installation 
locations, and future enforcement opportunities. Town Attorney Hoffmann suggested 
looking into AVIS speeding system that is used by other municipalities. The Board would like 
to purchase four (4) signs with the tracking software to be installed on Hindsdale and Waco, 
one in each direction. Staff was directed to obtain additional cost and installation 
information and bring the item back for further consideration.

RE: Pulic Hearing – Wildfire Resiliency Code  
 
DISCUSSION: 
Town Attorney Hoffmann has advised the Town to adopt the Wildfire Resiliency Code by 
reference as instructed by the State of Colorado to remain in compliance with regulations. The 
purpose of this code, as established by the state, is to have minimum regulations for the 
safeguarding of life and for property protection. Regulations in this code are intended to 
mitigate the risk to life and structures from intrusion of fire from wildland fire exposures and 
fire exposures from adjacent structures, in addition to mitigate structure fires from spreading 
to wildland fuels. The full Wild Fire Code can be found here > 
https://dfpc.colorado.gov/colorado-wildfire-resiliency-code.

(1) Purposes. The purposes of this Section are: to allow the 
location of a wireless service facility ("WSF") in the Town 
while protecting the public health, safety, and general

(b) If a telecommunications competitor attempts to co -

to be completed at the expense of either or both 
parties to determine the feasibility of co-location. 
(4) Consent given to a telecommunications provider or 
broadband provider to erect or construct any poles, or to 
locate or co -locate communications and WSF on vertical 
structures in a right -of-way, does not extend to the co -
location of new facilities or to the erection or construction of 
new poles in a right - of-way not specifically referenced in 
the grant of consent.

6. Applicability 
a. The general regulations and development standards of this Article shall 
not be retroactive on existing uses. However, these standards shall apply 
to all uses in all zoning districts under the following circumstances54: 
i. New buildings and uses of land that require a permit, license, plot 
plan or site development plan.  
ii. Additions involving expansion of the gross floor area by twenty 
percent (20%) or more above that in existence prior to the effective 
date of this Chapter. 
iii. A change in the use of the building or land which requires a 
change in the zoning district or a special review use permit. 
iv. A change in the occupancy of a building or land, which requires a 
new sign or other site improvements addressed in this Article. 
b. Prior to issuance of any permit, license or special review use permit, or 
granting of a change in use in any zoning district for any property, the 
applicant shall demonstrate that the property complies with all the 
applicable regulations. 
c. All buildings, parking areas, landscaping, signs and other improvements 
noted in the general regulations and development standards in this Article 
shall be constructed and installed in accordance with the approved plans 
prior to issuance of a certificate of occupancy for the building or use. 
i. The Town Planner may allow certain improvements to be 
constructed or installed within an agreed-upon time, allowing for

7. Subdivision Standards 
a. Applicability56 
This Section shall apply to all subdivisions and land divisions located 
wholly or partially within the Town unless specifically exempted 
otherwise. 
b. Subdivision Name57 
The proposed name of a subdivision shall be approved by the Town 
Planner and shall not use a word that is the same as, similar to, or 
pronounced the same as a word in the name of any other subdivision in the 
Town except for common locational terms like hills, court, etc. 
c. Street Layout58 
i. Streets shall be located with appropriate regard for topography, 
creeks, wooded areas and other natural features which would 
enhance attractive development. 
ii. Existing streets, including preliminary platted streets, in adjoining 
territory shall be continued at equal or greater width and in similar 
alignment by streets proposed in the subdivision, unless variations 
are approved by the Board of Trustees. 
iii. Streets within subdivisions shall be designed as a system of 
circulation routes, so that the use of local streets by through traffic 
will be discouraged. 
iv. Where a subdivision borders on or contains a state highway right-
of-way, the Board of Trustees shall require provisions for 
reduction of noise. Masonry walls, landscaped berms, a parallel 
street, landscaping or screening easement, greater lot depth and 
increased rear yard setbacks, among others, are recommended 
solutions. 
v. Streets shall intersect at or as close to a 90 degree angle as 
possible.59 
vi. When a tract is subdivided into larger than normal building lots or 
parcels, such lots or parcels shall be so arranged as to permit the 
logical location and opening of future streets and appropriate 
resubdivision, with provision for adequate utility easements and 
connectors for such resubdivision.

tangent at least 100 feet in length.

The Town of Foxfield Trails Plan for development of an off-street system

in the Town of Foxfield Trails Plan. In addition, the subdivider will be

so that safe access to public recreational easements will be provided to lots 
within the subdivision.
