Hayden Town Council Meeting Minutes 
October 15, 2026 
 
 
Call to Order/Roll Call 
 
Mayor Banks called the regular meeting of the Hayden Town Council to order at 5:30 p.m. Also calling to order 
NWCO GID, and HMHA as all three meetings ran in tandem for the 2027 budget discussion.   
Mayor Banks Offered a moment of Silence  
Mayor Banks Led the Pledge of Allegiance 
 
Mayor Banks, Mayor Pro Tem Gann, Councilmembers Carlson, Bell, Haight, Hayden (Zoom), and Hicks were 
present. Also, present were Town Manager Mathew Mendisco, Chief Scott Scurlock, Finance Director Andrea 
Salazar, Intern Nathan Dettelbach, and Town Clerk Barbara Binetti.  
 
Councilmember and Staff Reports and Updates – Mayor Banks recognized Councilmember Hicks and thanked 
her for her service to the Town of Hayden since 2024. This is her last council meeting.  
 
Study Session – There was no study session. 
 
Proclamations/Presentations –  
The draft 2027 – 2029 budget was presented by Mathew Mendisco. He asked for a direction from the Council 
on the issues of the Insurance benefit costs, the COLA, and the merit increases for the Town employees. Council 
gave direction and asked for numbers to be brought back for review. 
 
Public Comment – There was none. 
 
Consent Agenda Items -  
1. Consideration of minutes for the Regular Meeting of October 1, 2026      
2. Consideration to Review and Approve payments dated 10/08/2026 in the amount of $760,107.01  
3. Consideration to Review and Approve Hayden Merc payments dated 10/09/2026 in the amount of 
$2,534.05 
 
Mayor Pro Tem Gann moved to approve all consent items, seconded by Councilmember Carlson. A roll 
call vote was held. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
Old Business –   
The Council reviewed and considered  approval of RESOLUTION 2026-16 Endorsing the Referral of Ballot 
Measure 7A, A Half -Percent Sales Tax to Fund the Yampa Valley Regional Transportation Authority, to 
Voters for Approval 
Mayor Banks moved to approve Resolution 2026-16, seconded by Councilmember Haight. A roll call vote was 
held and the motion passed 6 to 1 with Councilmember Hayden voting nay.

New Business. 
Council reviewed and considered for approval to continue to support the Western Resilience Center 
(formerly the Yampa Valley Sustainability Council) to carry on the work of the Routt County Climate 
Action Plan Collaborative for 2027. Mathew explained that the Town of Hayden is the fiscal agent for the 
wester Resilience Center.  
 
Mayor Banks moved, seconded by Councilmember Carlson. A roll call vote was held. Mayor Banks, aye, 
Mayor Pro Tem Gann, nay, Councilmember Bell, nay, Haight, aye Hicks, aye, Carlson, aye, Hayden, nay. 
The motion passed 4 to 3.  
 
Staff and Councilmember Reports and Updates –  
Mathew Mendisco presented a 10-Year Review of the Town of Hayden (The item was moved from 
proclamations and presentations) coinciding with his 10th year as Hayden Town Manager. He highlighted 
the Town’s many accomplishments over the last 10 years. Mathew started his role as Town Manager in 
December 2017. Less than 2% of Town Managers have his tenure.  
 
 
Executive Session – Mayor Banks moved to executive session at 8:20 p.m. for discussion of a personnel matter 
C.R.S. Section 24-6-402(4)(f)(I)(II) and not involving: any specific employees who have requested discussion of 
the matter in open session; any member of this body or any elected official; the appointment of any person to 
fill an office of this body or of an elected official; or personnel policies that do not require the discussion of 
matters personal to particular employees; specifically, the town manager’s annual evaluation.  
Mayor Banks closed the executive session at 9:43 p.m. and returned to open session.  
 
In open session, Mayor Banks moved to approve Town Manager Mathew Mendisco’s contract for November 1, 
2026 through October 31, 2027. Councilmember Haight seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
 
 
Adjournment: - Mayor Banks adjourned the meeting at 9:45 p.m.

BACKGROUND REVIEW:   
Routt County Office of Emergency Management led the five-year update of the multi-jurisdictional 
Routt County Hazard Mitigation Plan (HMP), which builds on the County's 2010 and 2020 plans. The 
Town of Hayden was one of twelve participating local governments, along with Routt County, the 
City of Steamboat Springs, the Towns of Oak Creek and Yampa, five fire protection districts, Mt. 
Werner Water and Sanitation District, and the Upper Yampa Water Conservancy District. The 
planning process began in March 2025 and included three Steering Committee workshops between 
March and August 2025, two rounds of individual coordination meetings with each participant, and 
public outreach including an interactive booth at the Routt County Fairgrounds in Hayden. Town staff 
participated throughout and developed the Town's risk information, capability assessment, and 
mitigation actions. The final plan is dated September 14, 2026. 
The HMP was prepared under the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act as 
amended by the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000 and FEMA regulations at 44 CFR 201.6. FEMA 
requires each participating jurisdiction to formally adopt the plan through its governing body. 
Adoption keeps the Town eligible for FEMA Hazard Mitigation Assistance grant programs, including 
the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program, for the next five years. The HMP is a strategic planning 
document and is not regulatory; it does not obligate Town funds or change the Municipal Code. 
The plan profiles eleven hazards: drought; earthquake; flood/dam and levee incident; landslide; severe 
weather; severe winter weather; soil instability; wildfire; public health and ecological hazards; hazardous 
materials release; and wildlife-vehicle collision. Key findings for Hayden include flood risk from the Yampa 
River and Dry Creek, the Hayden West Levee, drought impacts to the Town's single Yampa River water 
source, power outage impacts to water and wastewater infrastructure, wildfire risk at the edges of town 
where new residential development is occurring, and hazardous materials along the US 40 corridor and at 
the power plant. The plan also recognizes the Town's acquisition and renovation of the Hayden Center, 
which served as the Town's emergency shelter during the 2023 spring flood. 
30
The plan includes twelve Town of Hayden mitigation actions: 
• 2026-045 Alternate Water Source feasibility study (Medium, 2028) 
• 2026-046 Drought Monitoring and water shortage staging plan (Medium, 2029) 
• 2026-047 SCADA System Upgrades at the airport and Dry Creek (High, 2026) 
• 2026-048 Bridge, culvert, and access road Infrastructure Assessment (Low, 2027) 
• 2026-049 New Flood Insurance Rate Maps (Medium, 2028) 
• 2026-050 Water/Wastewater Backup Generators, e.g., Washington St. Lift Station (Medium, 2029) 
• 2026-051 Generator for the Hayden Center Warming Shelter (High, 2027) 
• 2026-052 Public Soil Instability Mitigation Education (Low, 2030) 
• 2026-053 Adopt a Wildfire Code (High, 2026) 
• 2026-054 Hayden Center Shelter HVAC Upgrades (Medium, 2030) 
• 2026-055 Green Infrastructure Incentives through code updates (Medium, 2028) 
• 2026-148 Seismic Assessments of municipal facilities and water/wastewater infrastructure (Low, 2030) 
 
Each action is identified for implementation subject to annual budget appropriation and the 
availability of grant funding. The Town also committed to integrating the mitigation strategy and risk 
assessment into the in-progress Environmental Resiliency Plan and future Master Plan updates, 
developing an Emergency Operations Plan, and reporting annually to Council at a public meeting on 
the status of the Town's mitigation actions in coordination with the County's plan maintenance 
process. 
Fiscal Impact: None from adoption. Individual projects will be brought forward through the annual 
budget and grant processes.

RESOLUTION 2026-17 
A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE 2026 ROUTT COUNTY, COLORADO HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN. 
RECITALS 
WHEREAS, the Town Council of the Town of Hayden, Routt County, Colorado recognizes the threat 
that natural hazards pose to people and property within the Town of Hayden; and 
WHEREAS, the Town of Hayden, in partnership with Routt County and the other participating local 
governments, has prepared a multi-hazard mitigation plan, hereby known as the 2026 Routt County, 
Colorado Hazard Mitigation Plan, in accordance with federal laws, including the Robert T. Stafford 
Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act, as amended; the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968, 
as amended; and the National Dam Safety Program Act, as amended; and FEMA requirements at 44 
C.F.R. 201.6; and 
WHEREAS, the 2026 Routt County, Colorado Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies mitigation goals and 
actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and property in the Town of Hayden from the 
impacts of future hazards and disasters; and 
WHEREAS, adoption by the Town Council of the Town of Hayden demonstrates its commitment to 
hazard mitigation and achieving the goals outlined in the 2026 Routt County, Colorado Hazard 
Mitigation Plan. 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF 
HAYDEN COLORADO THAT: 
In accordance with Colorado law and the Town of Hayden Municipal Code, the Town Council of the 
Town of Hayden adopts the 2026 Routt County, Colorado Hazard Mitigation Plan, as approved by 
FEMA. Subsequent plan updates following the approval period for this  plan will require separate 
adoption resolutions.
