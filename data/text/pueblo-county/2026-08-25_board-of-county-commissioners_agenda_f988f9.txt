THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
AMENDING THE 2026 ELECTION INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO, ON BEHALF OF THE PUEBLO 
COUNTY CLERK AND RECORDER, AND EDISON FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT 
AND CLARIFYING THE APPLICABILITY OF THE UNIFORM ELECTION CODE  
 
          WHEREAS, Colorado Constitution Article XIV, Section 18 (2)(a), and C.R.S. § 29-1-
203, as amended, authorize the County Clerk and Edison Fire Protection District (“District”) to 
cooperate or contract with each other to provide any function, service, or facility lawfully 
authorized to each, and any such contract may provide for the sharing of costs; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the District has determined that it is in the best interests of the District to 
conduct its election, to be held on November 3, 2026, as a coordinated election and mail ballot 
election in accordance with the procedures and requirements of the Uniform Election Code of 
1992, Articles 1 to 13 of Title 1, C.R.S. (“Code”); and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 1-7-116, the Code requires entry into and performance 
of intergovernmental agreements where political subdivisions and the county clerk and recorder 
cooperate in the conduct and financing of the election; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the County Clerk and District entered into an intergovernmental agreement 
on August 20, 2026 and now desire to amend the Agreement to clarify that the election will be 
conducted pursuant to the Uniform Election Code and not pursuant to any contrary provisions of 
Article 13.5, Title 1, C.R.S.; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County, on behalf of the Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder, further 
desires to formally resolve under C.R.S. § 1-13.5-106(1) that it will utilize all provisions of the 
Uniform Election Code (Articles 1 through 13, Title 1, C.R.S.) rather than any contrary 
provisions of Article 13.5. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVEDby the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
I. For the 2026 November Election and pursuant to C.R.S. § 1-13.5-106(1), Pueblo County 
and the Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder shall utilize all provisions of the Uniform 
Election Code (Articles 1 through 13, Title 1, C.R.S.) rather than any contrary provisions 
of Article 13.5, Title 1, C.R.S. 
II. The Amendment to the Intergovernmental Agreement between Pueblo County and the 
Edison Fire Protection District dated August 20, 2026 is hereby approved and the chair 
and the Clerk and Recorder are authorized to execute the Amendment as necessary.  
III. In the event the Edison Fire Protection District fails to approve the Amendment, Section

I of this Resolution shall remain in full force and effect.

AMENDMENT TO INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT  
 
This Amendment is made and entered into this ___  day of ______________________, 
2026, between Pueblo County, a political subdivision of the State of Colorado, on behalf of the 
Clerk and Recorder of Pueblo County, hereinafter referred to as the "County Clerk," and Edison 
Fire Protection District, hereinafter referred to as "District” or “Political Subdivision” for purposes 
of this agreement. 
 
WITNESSETH: 
 
WHEREAS, the Parties entered into an Intergovernmental Agreement regarding the 2026 
General Election on August 20, 2026; and 
 
WHEREAS, to comply with the requirements of C.R.S. § 1-7-116, involving the Clerks’ 
duties in conducting November elections, the Parties have decided to amend the Intergovernmental 
Agreement dated August 20, 2026 to clarify the nature of the election; 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual covenants and promises 
contained herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows: 
 
I. Amendment. Subsection D of Section I General Matters is deleted and replaced with 
the following provision.  
 
D.   Nature of Election: 
  
 The parties agree that C.R.S. § 1-7-116 requires the County Clerk to assist in 
conducting the District’s election as part of the 2026 Coordinated Election. 
Furthermore, the Parties acknowledge that C.R.S. § 1-13.5-106(1) permits political 
subdivision otherwise subject to the provisions of Article 13.5 of Title 1, C.R.S., to 
utilize all or part of the requirements Articles 1 through 13 of Title 1, C.R.S. (the 
“Uniform Election Code”). Under this provision, the Parties agree that the County 
Clerk will assist in conducting the District’s election by utilizing all relevant 
provisions of the Uniform Election Code and not those provisions of Article 13.5 
that might otherwise apply. 
 
II. Construction of Other Provisions. Any provisions specifically referring to Article 
13.5 shall be read as referring to the substantially analogous provisions of the Uniform 
Elections Code. 
 
III. Other Terms not Affected.  Except as provided above, the Intergovernmental 
Agreement dated August 20, 2026 shall remain in full force and effect.  
 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have entered into this Amendment effective the day 
and year first written above.
