RESOLUTION NO.  
 
THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PUEBLO COUNTY CAPITAL 
CONSTRUCTION CORPORATION  
 
AUTHORIZING THE TERMINATION OF THE MASTER LEASE AND THE 
TRANSFER OF REAL PROPERTY TO PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO  
 
          WHEREAS,  the Pueblo County Capital Construction Corporation (the “Corporation”) 
was incorporated on December 18, 1989 at the direction of the Pueblo County Board of County 
Commissioners and for the purpose of assisting Pueblo County, Colorado, a political subdivision 
of the state of Colorado (the “County”) in the acquisition of real property, including by leasing 
such property to County to facilitate financing arrangements; and  
 
          WHEREAS, under Section III(a) of the Articles of Incorporation, the Corporation is 
empowered to lease and to convey real property to the County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Corporation holds title to real property acquired on behalf of the County 
and used by the County in connection with the Master Lease Purchase Agreement between the 
County and the Corporation effective December 1, 1989 and executed December 26 and 27, 
1989 (the “Lease”); and 
 
          WHEREAS, such real property (the “Property”) is located at 206-212 W. 12th St., 
Pueblo, CO 81003, also known by Assessor Schedule No. 525427010, more particularly 
described as follows:  
 
Lots 1 through 16, inclusive, of Block 12, together with the vacated alley in Block 12 and the 
north half of the adjacent vacated portion of West 11th Street, County Subdivision to the City of 
Pueblo, Amended Platt, County of Pueblo, State of Colorado 
 
          WHEREAS, the Lease was executed to allow the County to finance the purchase of the 
Property and obligates the County to provide rental payments to the Corporation beginning in 
December of 1989 and ending in December of 1994; and   
 
          WHEREAS, in addition to the Lease, the Corporation also entered into the Mortgage and 
Indenture of Trust with the Pueblo Bank and Trust Company (“PB&T”) effective December 1, 
1989 (the “Mortgage”) under which the Corporation agreed to make principal and interest 
payments on a loan for the purchase of real property, with such payments beginning in 
December of 1989 and ending in December of 1994; and 
 
          WHEREAS, under Section 12 of the Lease and Section VI of the Mortgage, the 
Corporation and PB&T agreed to take necessary steps to convey the Property to the County 
upon completion of payments under both the Lease and the Mortgage; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the County and the Corporation made all required payments with the final 
Page 2 of 4
payment transmitted to PB&T on November 28, 1994 but no document conveying the Property 
was ever obtained by the County or recorded in the Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder’s Office; 
and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Corporation recognizes that the Property should have been conveyed to 
the County at the end of 1994 once it completed its payments under the Lease.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Directors of the Pueblo 
County Capital Construction Corporation, that: 
1. The County has made all rental payments outlined in Exhibit D of the Lease. 
2. To the extent necessary, the Corporation terminates, or authorizes the termination of, the 
Lease. 
3. Under Section 12 of the Lease, the Corporation will convey the Property to the County 
through the execution of a quitclaim deed. 
4. If necessary, the Corporation will cooperate with PB&T to effectuate the transfer of the 
Property to the County, including through the execution of any agreements relating to the 
Mortgage or Lease. 
5. Director Robert Choi is authorized and directed to execute any agreements, deeds or 
other documents necessary to effectuate the terms and purpose of this Resolution and to 
take all other actions reasonably necessary to effectuate such terms and purpose.   
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 16, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
