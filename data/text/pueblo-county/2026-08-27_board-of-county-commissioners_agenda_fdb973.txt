A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS,the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 4th, 13th, 20th 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued 
Authorization of the Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to 
Page 4 of 454
affected individuals, business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
 
1.    The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided for 
herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further action is taken 
by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until the declaration expires 
pursuant to state statute; and 
 
2.    The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said Declaration 
on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 27, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

AGREEMENT FOR INSTALLATION OF PLAYGROUND EQUIPMENT 
 
 This Agreement is made and entered into this ____ day of August 2026, by and between Playcore 
Wisconsin, Inc. dba GameTime, a corporation doing business in Alabama,  (hereinafter referred to as 
“Contractor”) and Pueblo County, Colorado, a political subdivision of the State of Colorado, (hereinafter 
referred to as “County”). 
 
 WITNESSETH: 
 
 WHEREAS, the County needs new playground equipment and related products and services to be 
installed at Fulton Heights Community Center, 1331 Santa Rosa St., Pueblo, Colorado.   
 
 WHEREAS, Contractor is a participating Contractor with OMNIA Partners, under Public Sector 
Contract #2017001134 to provide playground and outdoor fitness equipment, site accessories, surfacing 
and related products and services and has submitted a proposal to the County to provide the labor and 
materials for the installation of the playground equipment and related products for the Fulton Heights 
Community Center; and 
 
 WHEREAS, County desires to have Contractor to provide the labor and materials for the 
installation of the playground equipment and related products for Fulton Heights located in Pueblo County, 
Colorado. 
 
 NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, 
the parties agree as follows: 
 
I.   OBLIGATION OF CONTRACTOR: 
 
As consideration for payment of the charges set forth herein by County, Contractor agrees to provide the 
labor and materials for the installation of the playground equipment and related products for Fulton Heights 
Community Center located in Pueblo County, Colorado, as per the Quote provided by GameTime,  dated 
07/28/2026, which is incorporated herein by this reference as “Exhibit A”. 
   
II.   PROJECT COST: 
 
The Contractor shall provide the labor and materials for the installation of the playground equipment and 
related products for Fulton Heights Community Center  located in Pueblo County, Colorado, as per the 
Quote provided by GameTime, dated 07/28/2026, which is incorporated herein by this reference as “Exhibit 
A” at a total project cost not to exceed $102,466.47.

amount;
