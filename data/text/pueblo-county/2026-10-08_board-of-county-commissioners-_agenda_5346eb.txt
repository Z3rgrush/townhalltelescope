SUBJECT: Zoning Variance ZV-26-6 
Samantha Cordova (Applicant and Landowner) 
1174 30th Ln, Pueblo CO 81006 
 
PURPOSE 
 
The applicant requests a zoning variance to allow a 10’ 4” side setback for a proposed 26’ X 32’ 
addition on the east side of the residence, in lieu of the required 15’ side setback (Exhibits 1 and 2). 
 
LOCATION 
 
The subject property is north of Hillside Rd and east of 30th Ln addressed at 1174 30th Ln. The 
property is legally described as N 134.03 FT of S 402.09 of W 325 FT of Lot 3 Blk 78 St Charles Mesa 
Pueblo County, Colorado. (Exhibit 3). 
 
BACKGROUND 
 
The Unified Development Code sets forth standards in the Small Agriculture (A3) zoning district. 
Specifically, Sec. 17.02.050(d) requires that the Principal Building Setback (minimum) side setback be 
15 feet. Since the applicant is proposing a new 26’ X 32’ addition on the east side of the residence, 
the zoning requires a 15-foot side setback. 
 
The proposed zoning variance requests a 10’ 4” side yard setback in lieu of the 15-foot side setback 
as required above for a principal building. 
 
The applicant is requesting this variance because the property has two existing decks attached to the 
north and east side of the home with a cistern tank underneath the deck and a well to the side of the 
deck. There is also an existing garage to the north of the house. The location of the cistern tank and 
well would disallow the proposed addition to meet the required 15’ side setback. Per the applicant, 
locating the proposed addition on the eastern portion of her home would allow the addition to align 
with her current home and avoid building the addition near the cistern tank and well.   
 
As such, the most probable location for the addition is on the eastern portion of the home, south of the 
cistern tank and well, within the required 15’ side setback. The proposed 10’ 4” setback would not 
create a substantial impact, as the addition aligns with the existing eastern wall, which is already set 
back 10’ 4” from the side setback.

LAND USE AND ZONING 
 
The subject property is in the A3 Zone District and is used as a residence. All immediately adjacent 
properties are also zoned A3 and are generally used for residential purposes. The Land Use and 
Zoning Map is attached as Exhibit 3.   
 
PUEBLO REGIONAL COMPREHENSIVE PLAN 
 
The adopted 2022 Pueblo Regional Comprehensive Plan depicts the location of the subject property 
as “Agricultural Neighborhood”, which is described as primarily “Crop and food production”, with 
supporting land uses including “Single-family detached homes, accessory dwelling units, farm stands, 
equestrian uses, low-intensity agriculture, limited commercial services, associated outbuildings, agri-
tourism, and other complementary uses.” Staff finds the requested variance to be associated with a 
supporting use that meets the intent of the Agricultural Neighborhood designation. 
 
ACCESS AND DRAINAGE 
 
The subject property access is from 30th Ln, which is a County maintained road. Pueblo County does 
require a Road Access Permit for this property. 
 
AGENCY COMMENTS 
 
Staff requested comments from various review agencies including Pueblo County Department of 
Engineering and Public Works (PCDEPW), Pueblo Rural Fire District (PRFD), St. Charles Mesa 
Water District (SCMWD), and Pueblo Department of Public Health and Environment (PDPHE) 
 
PCDEPW comments were received and they did not have an opposition to the variance.  
 
 STAFF COMMENTS 
 
 
1. Chapter 17.06, Administration and Procedures, 17.06.110 Flexibility and Relief Procedures, 
17.06.110(c) Variances of the Pueblo County Unified Development Code sets fourth criteria the 
Zoning Board of Appeals must take into consideration to grant approval of a zoning variance. The 
following is a list of those standards, in bold face type, with staff’s comments thereafter: 
 
a) The Variance, if granted, will grant only those uses listed as a use permitted in the zone 
district in which the parcel is located. 
 
The variance application does not propose unpermitted land uses in the A3, Small Agriculture 
zoning district. The variance will only be in effect if it is approved by the Board of County 
Commissioners. 
 
b) The parcel for which the Variance appeal is made suffers unique or singular 
disadvantages such as, but not limited to, size, shape, topography, location, or 
surroundings not shared by other parcels in the neighborhood. 
 
There are site-specific operational constraints that influence the proposed addition location, 
namely, the location of the existing cistern tank and well (under and beside the existing decks) 
and the garage. Other properties in the vicinity which are similar in size do not appear to also 
have existing structures and/or rights-of-way which would prevent similar structures from 
meeting setbacks on those respective properties.

c) The Variance will not grant privileges inconsistent with limitations shared by other 
parcels in the zone district. 
 
Granting this variance would not bestow an unfair privilege upon the property, as the proposed 
addition location is constrained by the cistern tank and well, two existing decks, and a garage. 
The proposed addition's encroachment and property constraints qualify for a zoning variance, 
whereas other properties in the same zone district have more feasible locations to construct 
an addition without obtaining a variance.  
 
d) The Variance will not have an injurious effect on the existing or future use of adjacent 
parcels. 
 
The granting of this variance will not have any adverse impact on the existing or future use of 
adjacent parcels; they can all continue to be developed in accordance with the standards of 
the A3 zone district. 
 
e) The Variance will not injure or adversely alter the general character of the 
neighborhood in which the Variance is sought. 
 
Staff believes the granting of this variance will not adversely alter the general character of the 
neighborhood.  
 
f) The Variance appeal is in harmony with the intent, purpose, and spirit of this resolution. 
 
Staff finds the requested variance to be consistent with the intent, purpose, and spirit of the 
Pueblo County Unified Development Code. 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
Staff recommends the Board of County Commissioners, acting as the Zoning Board of Appeals, 
APPROVE Zoning Variance ZV-26-6 based on the above listed criteria per the Pueblo County Unified 
Development Code, Chapter 17.06, Administration and Procedures, 17.06.110 Flexibility and Relief 
Procedures, 17.06.110(c) Variances, and the stated conditions: 
 
                                                                   
 
CONDITIONS 
 
Should the Board of County Commissioners, sitting as the Zoning Board of Appeals, approve the 
above variance request, staff have the following conditions: 
 
1. The approval of Zoning Variance ZV-26-6 shall be to a 10’ 4” side setback for a proposed 26’ X 
32’ addition on the east side of the residence, in lieu of the required 15’ side setback. 
 
2. All future development on the parcel must comply with the development standards of the A3 Small

3. The applicant shall submit a Road Access Permit (RAP) to register their driveway with the County 
Opengov database.  
 
4. The site plan displaying the new building addition must show the existing horseshoe driveway in 
relation to the existing property lines.
