A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th, and September 3rd, 2026, and again 
Page 2 of 3
desires to enact this Continued Authorization of the Emergency Declaration to ensure that 
financial aid is made available to affected individuals, business and governmental entities as 
applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 10, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
