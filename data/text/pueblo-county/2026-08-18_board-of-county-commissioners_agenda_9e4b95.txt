APPROVING THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN SUMMIT LOGGING, LLC AND 
PUEBLO COUNTY FOR THE RYE MOUNTAIN PARK FOREST THINNING AND 
FUELS REDUCTION PROJECT  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County owns and operates Rye Mountain Park located in Pueblo 
County, Colorado; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 26-122, Pueblo County Parks and Recreation entered into 
Financial Assistance Agreements to implement a large-scale forest health and wildfire 
mitigation project at Rye Mountain Park through funding provided by the Colorado State Forest 
Service (CSFS); and 
 
          WHEREAS, the project targets approximately 64.2 acres of overly dense ponderosa pine 
trees that currently present elevated wildfire risk and declining forest health conditions; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Parks and Recreation, acting by and through the Pueblo County Purchasing 
Department, sought, received, opened, and reviewed original bid proposals for Invitation for Bid 
No. 26-17 Rye Mountain Park Forest Thinning and Fuels Reduction Project; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Parks and Recreation recommends that Pueblo County accept the bid 
submitted by Summit Logging, LLC and that Pueblo County enter into an Agreement with 
Summitt for said purpose in the total amount of $127,437.00; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board concurs with recommendation and desires to enter into an 
Agreement with Summit, as stated above, and to utilize the Financial Assistance Program from 
the Colorado State Forest Service, subject to appropriation of funds for this purpose. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Agreement between Summit Logging, LLC and Pueblo 
County for the Rye Mountain Park Forest Thinning and Fuels Reduction Project is hereby 
approved and that the Chair of the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, 
Colorado, is hereby authorized to execute said Agreement on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 18, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
