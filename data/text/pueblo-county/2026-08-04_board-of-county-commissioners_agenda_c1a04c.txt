          WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 39-8-102(1), C.R.S., the County Board of Equalization

require, it shall raise, lower, or adjust any valuation for assessment appearing in the assessment 
roll to the end that all valuations for assessment of property are just and equalized within the 
County; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 39-8-106, C.R.S., the County Board of Equalization 
shall receive and hear petitions from any person whose objections or protests have been refused 
or denied by the County Assessor, which hearings may be conducted by independent referees 
appointed by the County Board of Equalization pursuant to Section 39-8-102(2), C.R.S.; and 
 
          WHEREAS, based upon the evidence and testimony presented to the Board on this date, 
the Board has determined to take the following action. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, acting as the Pueblo County Board of Equalization, as follows: 
 
1.    The Board hereby accepts the findings and recommendations of the appointed independent 
referees, pursuant to Section 39-8-102(2)(i), C.R.S., and adopts said recommendations as the 
final actions of the Pueblo County Board of Equalization, with the understanding that the Board 
hereby authorizes the Clerk or the Deputy Clerk to the Pueblo County Board of Equalization to 
make minor administrative modifications to said referees recommendations to correct any 
calculation error or other similar mistake prior the Clerk’s notification to the property owner 
and/or their representatives.  
 
2.    The attached hearing report and orders of the referees are incorporated into this Resolution 
by this reference.  
 
3.    The Clerk and Recorder is hereby directed to forward a copy of this Resolution to the 
Pueblo County Assessor for further action as may be required by law.            
 
4.    The Board of County Commissioners respectfully requests the Pueblo County Assessor to

          WHEREAS, under C.R.S. § 38-30-141, this Board is authorized to appoint a

authorized to sell, convey, and execute all documents necessary to effectuate the sale of the

ESTABLISHING A REPORTING STRUCTURE FOR CERTAIN PUEBLO COUNTY 
FACILITIES AND PUBLIC WORKS EMPLOYEES  
 
          WHEREAS, this Board, by Resolution No. 26-210, approved July 16, 2026, revised the 
organization of Pueblo County facilities functions and placed those functions under the 
supervision of a Deputy Director of Facilities who reports to the Chief Administrative Officer 
(CAO); and  
 
          WHEREAS, this Board recognizes that the movement of Pueblo County Facilities from 
the Public Works Department into a separate Department ultimately supervised by the CAO has 
required an adjustment of some supervisory responsibilities in both Facilities and Public Works; 
and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board believes that clear lines of reporting and accountability for 
Facilities and Public Works employees should be formally established to enhance efficiency and 
County services. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
 
1. The following Facilities position will report directly to the Deputy Director of 
Facilities (Cliff Torres): 
a. Executive Administrative Assistant (Stacey Keller). 
 
2. The following Facilities positions will report directly to the Facilities Executive 
Administrative Assistant (Stacey Keller): 
a. Administrative Assistant IV (Dahr Villegas). 
b. Administrative Assistant III (Brittany Moore). 
3. The following Public Works (Road and Bridge) position will report directly to the 
Director of Public Works (Greg George): 
a. Administrative Assistant IV (Valerie Ramirez).

4. The following Public Works (Road and Bridge) positions will report directly to 
Administrative Assistant IV (Valerie Ramirez): 
a. Administrative Assistant III (Jessica Villafon). 
b. Administrative Assistant III (Rebekah Reed). 
c. Accounts Technician III (Susan McDaniel) 
 
5. The direct reporting mechanisms described herein are effective upon execution of

APPROVING THE EMERGENCY FACILITIES & LAND USE AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN PUEBLO COUNTY AND THE USDA FOREST SERVICE  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, the Board of County Commissioners 
authorized a declaration of Local Disaster Emergency by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2, 2026; 
and  
 
          WHEREAS, USDA Forest Service, At-Incident Management Support Branch (AIMS), 
needs a temporary slash disposal site for cleanup efforts of the burn sites left from the Aspen 
Acres Area Fire; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the County recognizes that emergency management operations continue 
beyond the immediate response phase and desire to enter into the Emergency Facilities and 
Land Use Agreement with the USDA Forest Service for the use of county property for a 
temporary slash disposal site. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Emergency Facilities & Land Use Agreement between the 
USDA Forest Service and Pueblo County for use of county property, the terms and conditions of 
which are incorporated herein by this reference, is hereby approved and that the Chair of the 
Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, Colorado, is authorized to execute said 
Agreement on behalf of Pueblo County. 
  
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 4, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
