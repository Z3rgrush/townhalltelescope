A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th, and September 3rd, and 10th 2026, and 
Page 4 of 30
again desires to enact this Continued Authorization of the Emergency Declaration to ensure that 
financial aid is made available to affected individuals, business and governmental entities as 
applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVEDby the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 17, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

MEMORANDUM 
To: Counties  Subscribing  to HSConnects  
From: Philip Savino,  Director  & CIO, Arapahoe  County 
Cc: Dan Makelky,  Human Services  Director;  Todd  Weaver,  Finance  Director 
Re: 2027 HSConnects  Subscription  Fee 
Date: June 9, 2026 
Dear Customer,  
Arapahoe  County charges  a subscription  fee for HSConnects  based on the average number of  users per 
subscribing county each month. This fee supports the operating costs of the cloud environment, along 
with a portion of Arapahoe County’s development and  support investment.  
In August  2025 we officially  began the HSConnects  Modernization  effort,  starting  with an evaluation  by 
Gartner.  Based on their recommendations,  we contracted  with a vendor to perform  a full cybersecurity 
audit and assess the development required to transition HSConnects to a more modern, sustainable 
code platform. We also needed to ensure compliance with House Bill 21 -1110 and Senate Bill 23 -244 
related to Digital Accessibility Laws.  
Our development  team has been working  closely  with the vendor to build the modernized  version  of 
HSConnects, incorporating key cybersecurity recommendations and ensuring full accessibility. In  
parallel,  our Business  Partners  in Human  Services  have dedicated  a team to thoroughly  test new features 
and updates to  support a smooth  rollout in  Fall 2026.  They have  also been actively maintaining the 
legacy application and ensuring any new state requirements are being made.  
This modernization  represents  a signiﬁcant  ﬁnancial  investment  for Arapahoe  County—one that will 
beneﬁt all HSConnects subscribers.  Therefore,  beginning in 2027 the  quarterly subscription fee  will 
increase from $101.59 to $111.74 per user ($446.96 annually). This adjustment reﬂects ongoing 
investment in HSConnects.  
We appreciate  your continued  support.  If you have any questions  about the new fee, please feel free to 
contact me.  
Sincerely,

A RESOLUTION DECLARING THE INITIAL NEED FOR A COUNTY 
REVITALIZATION AUTHORITY IN PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO PURSUANT 
TO C.R.S. § 30-31-104(1)(A)  
 
          WHEREAS, the County Revitalization law, codified in part as Article 31 of Title 30 of 
the Colorado Revised Statutes, became effective August 7, 2024; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Colorado General Assembly found and declared as part of the County 
Revitalization Law that there exist in counties of the State of Colorado certain unincorporated 
areas that would benefit from revitalization and economic investment that will not occur without 
additional funding; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Colorado General Assembly further found and declared as part of the 
County Revitalization law that the existence of these areas impairs successful development and 
redevelopment within counties and harms the welfare of county residents in ways that harm the 
surrounding communities, and the revitalization of these areas is a matter of public policy and 
statewide concern; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the County Revitalization Law creates a process for the establishment of a 
County Revitalization Authority (“CRA”), which is a corporate body that can, among other 
things, leverage Tax Increment Financing (“TIF”) and private financing to address underutilized 
or deteriorating areas within counties that could benefit from strategic economic investment by 
conducting a county revitalization project in a revitalization area in accordance with a county 
revitalization plan; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to the County Revitalization Law, the Board of County 
Commissioners may adopt a resolution setting forth that there is a need for a CRA as a first step 
in the process of determining whether to create a CRA; and  
 
          WHEREAS, there exists areas within Pueblo County that could potentially substantially 
promote the sound growth of the County, improve the economic and social conditions, and 
further the health, safety, and well-being of the public if subject to further economic investment. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, pursuant to C.R.S. 30-31-104(1)(a), that there is a need for a CRA in 
Pueblo County; and  
 
          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that when a CRA is ready to be brought forward to a 
public hearing, County staff is authorized and directed to provide notice of the time, place, and 
purpose of the public hearing where the Board of County Commissioners will further determine 
Page 29 of 30
the need for a CRA in Pueblo County, Colorado pursuant to all applicable provisions within 
C.R.S. 30-31-104(1)(b) and (c), including notice which shall include a general description of the 
land proposed for inclusion within the initial county revitalization area; ii) shall be sent to every 
municipality within three miles of the proposed CRA; and iii) shall otherwise comply with 
C.R.S. 30-31-104(1)(b)(I)-(II). 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 17, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
