SUBJECT: Use-Specific Standards for Data Centers 
 
PURPOSE 
 
The Pueblo County Unified Development Code (UDC) provides for Data Centers as a Special Use 
in the LI, HI, and PP zone districts. However, the UDC does not prescribe use-specific standards 
for Data Centers, which can be an intensive use. The purpose of this work session is to provide 
draft standards to the Planning Commission and receive feedback on same, with the end goal of 
adopting suitable use-specific standards into the UDC. 
BACKGROUND  
 
Staff brought draft use-specific standards for Data Centers to the Planning Commission in a Work 
Session format at the August 19, 2026 meeting for feedback. At this meeting, staff heard 
comments on several sections from the Planning Commission. Staff has since incorporated those 
comments and revised the draft standards accordingly, and these changes are outlined below. The 
full text of use-specific standards is attached as Exhibit 1. Changes made after the August work 
session are indicated by red text.  
 
SELF-SUFFICIENCY 
 
One comment made by the Planning Commission concerned the need for stronger standards 
ensuring Data Center uses would “pay their own way” and not cause financial impacts to utility 
service providers. As such, staff has incorporated an additional section which would require 
developers to provide robust information regarding what infrastructure improvements may be 
necessitated by the data center and how it will pay for its fair share of said improvements. Staff is 
proposing the “fair share” language instead of requiring the developer to pay every cost, because 
in some instances improvements may benefit the larger community or utility system, so it may not 
be appropriate to require the developer to pay 100% of those costs. 
 
GENERATORS 
 
The Planning Commission mentioned enacting specific environmental standards for backup 
generators. In response, staff solicited help from the Colorado Department of Public Health and 
Environment (CDPHE)’s Air Pollution Control Division who provided some resources which staff

DECOMMISSIONING 
 
The Planning Commission voiced concern that the language requiring all equipment “not essential 
for the functionality of the building” be removed as part of the decommissioning process would be 
difficult to enforce. Rather than attempt to define which equipment is and is not essential for 
building functionality, staff has removed that language and simply required that all exterior 
equipment related to the Data Center use such as generators and batteries be removed. 
 
The Commission also indicated that a 12-month decommissioning timeline would not be sufficient. 
However, the draft standards provide for a timeline of 12 months “or per the schedule as approved 
within the Decommissioning Plan.” This allows the developer to request a longer timeline for 
decommissioning should they believe it to be necessary.  
 
CONSTRUCTION TRAFFIC 
 
Planning staff worked with Public Works staff to draft requirements for a Road Use Agreement for 
construction traffic. This language was not ready in time for the August work session but has since 
been incorporated. 
 
CONCLUSION 
 
The purpose of this work session is to provide the Planning Commission with revised draft 
standards for data center uses, based on direction received at the previous work session. Staff 
would like receive direction as to whether these standards should be proposed in a formal Text 
Amendment for Planning Commission recommendation and Board of County Commissioners 
approval.

SUBJECT: Amendments to the Pueblo Regional Comprehensive Plan 
 
PURPOSE 
 
Pueblo County Planning staff refers to the Pueblo Regional Comprehensive Plan (the 
“Comprehensive Plan”) whenever they made a land-use decision or recommendation to a 
decision-making body, in order to ensure the decision is aligned with the plan. Over years of 
working with this document, staff has identified several areas where the Future Land Use (“FLU”)  
designations of certain areas are inconsistent with land use and development patterns. The 
purpose of this work session is to provide draft changes to certain FLU designations to the 
Planning Commission and receive feedback on same, with the end goal of adopting these 
changes. 
 
COMMENTS AND OUTREACH 
 
Staff brought Comprehensive Plan amendments to the Planning Commission in a Work Session 
format at the August 19, 2026 meeting for feedback. At this meeting, staff was advised by the 
Planning Commission to perform outreach to the public and affected governmental agencies 
including the City of Pueblo and Colorado City.  
 
On August 24, county staff contacted City of Pueblo staff to solicit feedback on the proposed 
Comprehensive Plan amendments. City Staff indicated that they would not support the Transit-
Dependent Industry designation along Interstate 25 north of the City. They indicated they believed 
the more appropriate designation would be Employment (E) for all that area, save for three (3) 
parcels immediately adjacent to the City, located near the intersection of Elizabeth St and Drew Dix 
Pkwy. For these parcels the City requested a designation of Commercial Mixed-Use (CMU) to 
enable the continued development of commercial uses adjacent to the existing retail, restaurant, 
and fuel station uses in the vicinity. Their full comments can be seen in Exhibit 1. County staff is 
not opposed to the changes suggested by City staff, as the E and CMU designations will still 
enable the growth of interstate-serving commercial uses, and light industrial uses, along the north 
I-25 corridor. 
 
On September 2, staff presented the proposed amendment lying partially within the Colorado City 
Metro District boundaries to Colorado City staff and board members at the regular quarterly

amendments as a result, will be provided to the Planning Commission at the Work Session. 
 
CHANGES 
 
As a result of the comments received from the City of Pueblo, staff is changing the proposed 
Future Land Use designation for the properties shown in Exhibit 2 to Employment (E) and for the 
properties shown in Exhibit 3 to Commercial Mixed-Use (CMU). All other proposed FLU 
designation changes are the same as presented in the August Work Session memo. 
 
CONCLUSION 
 
The purpose of this work session is to provide the Planning Commission with draft changes to 
certain Future Land Use designations and updates regarding outreach done. Staff would like 
receive direction as to whether these standards should be proposed in a formal Comprehensive 
Plan Amendment for Planning Commission approval.
