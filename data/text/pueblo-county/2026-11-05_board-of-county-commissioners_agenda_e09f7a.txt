A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
Page 4 of 321
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th, September 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th, October 
1st, 8th and 15th, 22nd, and 29th 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued Authorization 
of the Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to affected 
individuals, business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo 
County, that:  
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this November 5, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PUEBLO COUNTY TREASURER TO 
LIQUIDATE SECURITIES  
 
          WHEREAS, the County Treasurer is responsible for the investment of county funds in 
accordance with state law and county investment policies, with the goal of ensuring safety, 
liquidity, and reasonable yield; and 
 
          WHEREAS, certain investments are purchased with specific maturity dates to align with 
the County’s cash flow needs and financial objectives; and 
 
          WHEREAS, on occasion maturity dates of certain investments do not align with the 
County’s cash flow needs, creating a need to redeem an investment prior to its maturity date; 
and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. Section 30-10-708 a treasurer may sell securities held by 
a county if the board of county commissioners passes a resolution approving the sale and 
designating the bank in which such proceeds shall be deposited; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo Board of County Commissioners desires to liquidate securities 
including those that are not mature to the extent necessary to pay the County’s operations and 
expenses for the months of November 2026 through December 2027 to be deposited into Vectra 
Bank.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that Kim Archuletta, Pueblo County Treasurer, is directed to liquidate 
securities including those that are not mature to the extent necessary to pay County bills and 
expenses for the months of November 2026 through December, 2027 to be deposited into Vectra 
Bank and/or other similarly appropriate operating account(s). 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this November 5, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

A RESOLUTION FORMALLY DECLARING NO OUTSIDE FUNDING REQUESTS 
FOR FISCAL YEAR 2027  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, Colorado (the 
“Board”), is charged with the responsible stewardship of County resources and the orderly 
administration of the County’s annual budget process; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board has determined that, for Fiscal Year 2027, the County’s budgetary 
priorities and available resources require a focused approach to internal fiscal planning and the 
preservation of existing funding streams; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the acceptance and processing of outside funding requests during Fiscal 
Year 2027 would divert staff time and resources from core County functions and would create 
unanticipated fiscal obligations unable to be accommodated; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board finds it to be in the best interest of the citizens of Pueblo County 
to formally establish that no outside funding requests will be accepted, considered, or approved 
for Fiscal Year 2027 except for the specific mission critical purposes described herein. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
 
For Fiscal Year 2027, the County shall not accept, process, or approve any outside funding 
requests from individuals, organizations, agencies, or other entities seeking County financial 
support, grants, contributions, or similar forms of funding paid for by the County general fund 
except that funding to the Humane Society of the Pikes Peak Region and the Pueblo Department 
of Public Health Environment shall be exempt from this Resolution and nothing herein shall 
preclude the Board from considering emergency or extraordinary circumstances by separate 
action if deemed necessary in the public interest. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this November 5, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

Tablet Damage and Replacement Policy 
 
A. Overview: The purpose of this Policy is to provide an operational framework to support a ‘Spare 
Tablet Program’ to help ensure uninterrupted access to communication and entertainment 
services for incarcerated people (“User”). ICS acknowledges the rigorous use environment of 
correctional facilities and provides a Spare Tablet Program to County with an allowance of spare 
tablets equal to 25% of the total tablets offered. Under ordinary use conditions, the Spare Tablet 
Program includes repair or replacement as needed at no cost to County. However, Tablets that 
are physically damaged, lost, stolen, tampered with, altered, or otherwise fall within the 
exclusions set forth below shall not be covered under the Program. All tablets are required to be 
returned upon termination of the Agreement.  Termination of the Agreement prior to the 
completion of the Initial Term will require the County to reimburse ICS for the cost of all 
unrecovered costs for the tablets, including but not limited to unrecovered depreciation and the 
set up and installation of services used through the tablets (e.g., phone service), prorated for each 
month that would have otherwise remained in the Initial Term. 
B. Exclusions from Free Replacement Coverage: While ICS is committed to supporting County 
in maintaining operational efficiency and User satisfaction, it is understood that the Spare Tablet 
Program does not extend coverage to tablets that have been physically damaged, which includes 
but not limited to the following:  
   - Screens that are shattered, cracked, or otherwise compromised. 
   - Audio jack damage 
   - Tablets with missing pieces or parts, indicating tampering or attempts to disassemble. 
   - Damage resulting from attempts to open the tablet casing unauthorizedly. 
   - Any form of physical alterations that deviate from the tablet's original condition as supplied. 
   - Lost or stolen tablets while under the County’s supervision. 
C. Procedure for Reporting Damages: County shall report any damages to tablets within a 
reasonable timeframe from the occurrence of the damage or, at minimum, monthly. The report 
should include a detailed description of the condition of the damaged tablet, accompanied by 
photographic evidence when applicable. County shall return damaged tablets to ICS, or its 
designee, within thirty (30) calendar days of becoming damaged or prior to the County 
accumulating more than ten (10) damaged tablets, whichever is earlier.  
D. Assessment and Determination: Upon receiving the tablets ICS will assess the condition 
against the exclusions listed in paragraph B, above. Tablets deemed by ICS, in its reasonable 
discretion, to have been damaged due to reasons covered under the exclusions will not be eligible 
for free replacement under the Spare Tablet Program. In such event, County may opt to purchase 
replacement tablets at the cost in effect at the time. 
E. Responsibility and Care: County agrees to educate and enforce proper care and use of the 
tablets among the User population to minimize incidents of deliberate damage. ICS will provide 
County with care and handling guidelines to be communicated to Users as needed. At the 
discretion of ICS, Users may be required to agree and / or accept terms and conditions of tablet 
usage (“Terms of Use”). 
F. Amendments and Exceptions: ICS reserves the right to review and reasonably adjust this 
Policy as needed to ensure fairness and sustainability of the Spare Tablet Program.

2027 (SFY27) to the County.
