AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 26-209, NOMINATING MEMBERS AND 
ALTERNATE MEMBERS TO THE MONETARY MITIGATION FUND ADVISORY 
COMMITTEE CREATED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE FOUNTAIN 
CREEK WATERSHED FLOOD CONTROL AND GREENWAY DISTRICT 
 
 
          WHEREAS, the Governor of the State of Colorado signed Senate Bill 09-141 on April 
30, 2009, which created the Fountain Creek Watershed, Flood Control, and Greenway District; 
and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 32-11.5-203, the Fountain Creek Watershed, Flood 
Control, and Greenway District is governed by a Board of Directors that consists of nine (9) 
Directors; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to Article X of the District Bylaws, the Board of Directors has 
authority to appoint a Monetary Mitigation Fund Advisory Committee (“Committee”) and to 
consult with the Committee regarding public policy or expenditure of Monetary Mitigation 
funds for the benefit of the Fountain Creek Corridor; and 
 
          WHEREAS, according to the Monetary Mitigation Fund Advisory Committee Bylaws, 
two (2) Pueblo County staff members are to be nominated by the Pueblo Board of County 
Commissioners with two (2) alternates in case of unavailability and/or conflict of the primary 
member; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 26-209, this Board nominated nominate Aaron Brice as a 
primary member with Dominga Garcia-Jimenez as his alternate and Jonathan Griffen as a 
primary member with Dana Moses as his alternate to the Monetary Mitigation Fund Advisory 
Committee for one (1) year terms; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Aaron Brice is no longer with Pueblo County and this Board desires to 
Page 3 of 472
nominate Steven Bauguess to replace Aaron Brice as a primary member. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that Resolution No. 26-209, is hereby amended, Steven Bauguess to 
replace Aaron Brice as a primary member with Dominga Garcia-Jimenez as his alternate are 
hereby nominated to the Monetary Mitigation Fund Advisory Committee to be appointed by the 
Board of Directors of the Fountain Creek Watershed, Flood Control and Greenway District to 
serve a one (1) year term.

APPROVING THE STORMWATER FACILITY MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN PUEBLO COUNTY AND HIGHLAND SOLAR I, LLC 
 
 
          WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30-15-401, et seq., enables counties to adopt ordinances for the 
control or licensing of those matters of purely local concern, and to do all acts and make all 
regulations which may be necessary or expedient for the promotion of health or the suppression 
of disease; and 
 
          WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30-15-401 (11)(a)(I), specifically authorizes counties that have 
been issued Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System Permits (MS4 permits) pursuant to Part 5 
of Article 8 of Title 25, C.R.S., to adopt a stormwater ordinance to develop, implement, and 
enforce the stormwater management program required by the permit; and 
 
          WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 30-15-40 l ( I I )(a)(ii)(A), authorizes counties to provide for and 
compel the abatement of any condition that causes or contributes to a violation of a MS4 permit 
or requirement from any property located within the unincorporated portion of a county at such 
time, upon such notice, and in such manner consistent with the terms of the MS4 permit as the 
Board of County Commissioners prescribed by Ordinance No. 20, Chapter 8.26 of the Pueblo 
County Code; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to Chapter 8.26 of the Pueblo County Code, the Pueblo County 
Department of Public Works has approved a Stormwater Facilities Management Plan submitted 
by Highland Solar I, LLC, the property owner of a commercial solar facility located on Tax 
Parcel Nos: 91-000-00-0086, 010, 038, 041 and 042 / 81-000-00-033 Legal Description: A 
PORTION OF SECTION 33, TOWNSHIP 18 SOUTH, RANGE 61 WEST AND A PORTION 
OF SECTIONS 4, 5, 8, 9 AND 10, TOWNSHIP 19 SOUTH, RANGE 61 WEST, ALL THE 
6TH P.M., County of Pueblo, State of Colorado, and has issued a Stormwater Facilities 
Maintenance Agreement between Highland Solar I, LLC., and Pueblo County by and through 
the Public Works Department.

          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Stormwater Facilities Maintenance Agreement between 
Highland Solar I, LLC., and Pueblo County by and through the Public Works Department, 
which is incorporated herein by this reference, is hereby approved.

ACCEPTING THE DEDICATION OF EASEMENT AND RIGHT-OF-WAY FOR A 
DRAINAGE FACILITY ACCESS BETWEEN PUEBLO COUNTY AND HIGHLAND 
SOLAR I LLC., FOR DRAINAGE IMPROVEMENTS 
 
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has the lawful authority and jurisdiction to provide for the 
acceptance and maintenance of real property for public right-of-way and easement purposes in 
Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, Highland Solar I LLC, (Grantor) has executed a Drainage Easement and 
Right-of-way document dedicating real property to Pueblo County for drainage facility access 
located on Grantor’s property; and  
 
          WHEREAS, it is the custom and practice of the Board of County Commissioners to enter 
a resolution formally accepting such conveyance and it is the recommendation of the Pueblo 
County Public Works Department that this Board accept the conveyance of the real property 
already conveyed by easement document; and  
 
          WHEREAS, this Board, having determined that such property is necessary for the needs 
of Pueblo County, concurs with such recommendation.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the conveyance of an easement for a Storm Water Detention 
Right-of-way and easement for Drainage and Utilities, which is incorporated herein by this 
reference, is hereby accepted by Pueblo County and nothing in this resolution shall limit or 
restrict the right of Pueblo County to vacate the same according to law when such action is 
deemed by the Board of County Commissioners to be in the public interest.
