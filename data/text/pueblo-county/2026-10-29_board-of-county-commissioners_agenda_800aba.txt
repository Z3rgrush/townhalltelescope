A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
Page 3 of 119
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th, September 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th, October 
1st, 8th, 15th, and 29th, 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued Authorization of the 
Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to affected individuals, 
business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVEDby the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 29, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS,by Resolution No. 26-294, this Board approved the Grant Award and the 
Agreement between the U.S. Department of Agriculture, Natural Resources Conservation 
Service for assistance, planning, oversite and necessary construction to help in the repair and 
recovery in the wake of the burn scar flooding for a total project amount of $18,336,812.00; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works issued a Request for Qualifications from 
reputable Engineering Consultants and Land Surveyors and received a Statement of 
Qualifications from Wilson and Company; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo County Department of Public Works recommends that Pueblo 
County accept the Statement submitted by Wilson and Company and enter into an Agreement 
with Wilson and Company, for the Post Wildfire Watershed Engineering Design Services for the 
Aspen Acres Fire Flood Recovery Project, in the amount of $300,000.00; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board concurs with the recommendation and desires to enter into an 
Agreement with Wilson and Company for said Post Wildfire Watershed Engineering Design 
Services, subject to the appropriation of funds for this project. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Agreement between Wilson and Company and Pueblo 
County, for Post Wildfire Watershed Engineering Design Services for the Aspen Acres Fire 
Flood Recovery Project, the terms and conditions of which are incorporated herein by this 
reference, is hereby approved, nunc pro tunc, September 21, 2026, and the Chair of the Board of 
County Commissioners and/or Director of the Pueblo County Department of Public Works is 
hereby authorized to execute the same on behalf of Pueblo County.

1. Statutory Authority & Objective 
This Statement of Work (SOW) defines the professional engineering and technical services required by the Pueblo County 
Public Works Department for Emergency Watershed Protection (EWP) recovery measures following the Aspen Acres 
Wildfire. Under Cooperative Agreement Award No. NR268B05XXXXC008 and DSR 08_03_26_5099_001, the Engineer shall 
deliver comprehensive surveying, 2D hydraulic modeling, geotechnical design, construction drawing production, and 
engineering inspection across Middle Creek, North Creek (North & South segments), and Siloam Road reaches. 
2. Professional Engineering Scope of Work 
Task 1: Topographic & Drone LiDAR Field Surveys 
• Execute high-resolution drone LiDAR and RTK GPS topographic cross-section surveys along 6.5 channel miles of Middle 
Creek, North Creek, and Siloam Road. 
• Establish geodetic control monuments tied to NAD83 Colorado State Plane South and NAVD88 vertical datum. 
Task 2: Hydrologic & 2D Hydraulic (H&H) Modeling 
• Develop post-wildfire 2D HEC-RAS hydraulic models utilizing 10-year and 100-year peak flow hydrographs accounting 
for burned soil hydrophobic response. 
• Analyze shear stress, velocity vectors, and sediment transport capacity to establish stable channel cross-sections and 
riprap D50 gradations. 
Task 3: Engineering Design & Plan Preparation 
• Design grade control structures to arrest head cut migration and stabilize channel inverts. 
• Design Large Erosion Barrier (LEB) sediment traps, HESCO barrier alignments, and rock riprap bank protection. 
• Submit formal 30%, 60%, 90%, and 100% PE-sealed construction drawing packages and technical specifications for 
NRCS concurrence. 
• Submit detailed and Colorado compliant sediment and erosion control maps for inclusion in site Stormwater 
Management Plans
