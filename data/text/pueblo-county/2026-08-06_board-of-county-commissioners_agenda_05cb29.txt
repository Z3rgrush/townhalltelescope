A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke; and  
 
          WHEREAS, as of August 4th, 2026, approximately 101,921 acres within the County 
have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 homes and 9 businesses have been 
destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 1900 fire personnel from across the 
County at various times; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, and 30th 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued Authorization of 
the Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to affected individuals, 
Page 2 of 5
business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law.  
  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVEDby the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 6, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
ADOPTING A STAGE-2 OPEN FIRE BAN  
 
          WHEREAS, this Board by Resolution 26-201, dated July 2nd, 2026, ratified and Adopted 
a Stage-2 Open Fire Ban for a period of 30 days; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. 30-15-401, and applicable under Chapter 8.24 of the 
Pueblo County Code, both this Board and the Pueblo County Sheriff are authorized to impose an 
open fire ban whenever the danger of forest and grass fires is found to be high; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board is of the opinion that danger continues to be high based on recent 
weather conditions and an active wildfire in the southwest portion of Pueblo County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, for those reasons, and pursuant to Section 8.24.100 of the Pueblo County 
Code, Pueblo County hereby imposes a Stage-2 open fire ban (“Open Fire Ban”), prohibiting the 
following activities within unincorporated Pueblo County: 
 
1.    Building, maintaining, attending or using a fire, campfire, or stove fire. This includes 
charcoal grills and barbecues; coal and wood burning stoves and sheepherder's stoves and 
includes use in developed camping and picnic grounds. 
 
EXCEPT: Devices using pressurized liquid or gas ovens, grills or lanterns) that include shutoff 
valves are permitted when used in an area at least three feet or more from flammable material 
such as grasses or pine needles.  
 
2.    Smoking. EXCEPT: Within enclosed vehicle, trailer or building. 
 
3.   The sale or use of fireworks 
 
4.   Welding or operating acetylene or other torch with open flame. 
 
5.    Operating or using any internal combustion engine (e.g. chainsaw, generator, ATV) without 
a spark arresting device properly installed, maintained and in effective working order meeting 
either: 
 
Operating a chainsaw without an approved spark arresting device as described in Prohibition #4, 
a chemical pressurized fire extinguisher (8 oz. capacity  by weight or larger and kept with the 
operator)  
or 
A round point shovel with an overall length of at least 35 inches readily available for use.

6.    Using an explosive. This includes but is not limited to fuses or blasting caps, fireworks, 
rockets, exploding targets, and tracers or incendiary.      
 
7.    Possessing or using a motor vehicle off established roads, motorized trails or established 
parking areas, except when parking in an area devoid or vegetation within 10 feet of the vehicle. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the adoption of a Stage-2 open Fire Ban is hereby imposed as 
described above and effective August 6, 2026.  
 
          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that that the Sheriff of Pueblo County is hereby directed 
to enforce the provisions of this resolution until such time as it is rescinded. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 6, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
