A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
Page 3 of 50
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th, September 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th, October 
1st,8th and 15th, 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued Authorization of the 
Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to affected individuals, 
business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided 
for herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further 
action is taken by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until 
the declaration expires pursuant to state statute; and 
2. The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said 
Declaration on behalf of Pueblo County 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 22, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
BETWEEN THE PUEBLO CONSERVANCY DISTRICT AND PUEBLO COUNTY TO 
PROVIDE ASSISTANCE FOR THE ASPEN ACRES FIRE MITIGATION PROJECTS  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has also suffered significant damage and hardship to land 
and property within Pueblo County due to the Aspen Acres Fire; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the severity of the Aspen Acres Fire drastically altered the biological 
landscape of affected areas within Pueblo County and created catastrophic debris flows, rapid 
runoff, and flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo Conservancy District agrees to provide the County up to 
$500,000 to assist in mitigating fire flooding and erosion currently experienced in affected areas 
throughout Pueblo County, Colorado; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board desires to accept said funding for mitigation assistance of the 
Aspen Acres Fire damage and to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Pueblo 
Conservancy District, setting the terms and conditions between the parties. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Memorandum of Understanding between the Pueblo 
Conservancy District and Pueblo County for the purpose of mitigation assistance with the Aspen 
Acres Fire disaster and damage, which is attached hereto and incorporated herein, is hereby 
approved and shall be effective September 28, 2026, nunc pro tunc; and that the Chair of the 
Board of County Commissioners and/or Chief Administrative Officer are hereby authorized to 
execute said Memorandum of Understanding on behalf of Pueblo County.  
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 22, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING A LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE RUNYON 
SPORTS COMPLEX COMMISSION, INC. AND PUEBLO COUNTY  
 
          WHEREAS, the Runyon Sports Complex Commission, Inc., is a nonprofit corporation, 
under the provisions of the Colorado Non-Profit Corporation Act, contained in C.R.S. § 7-40-
101, et seq., the purpose of which is to develop, promote, and coordinate recreation programs 
utilizing the Runyon Field facility; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County is the owner of the Runyon Field Sports Complex and 
desires that such facility be utilized to the greatest extent possible for the benefit, use and 
enjoyment of the citizens of Pueblo County and is responsible for maintaining the Runyon Field 
Sports Complex; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Runyon Sports Complex Commission, Inc., has special expertise with 
coordinating, promoting and developing organized recreation programs, including but not 
limited to baseball and softball programs, for Pueblo youth; it has performed these functions in 
the past; and it desires to continue to coordinate, promote and develop organized recreation 
programs at the Runyon Field Sports Complex; and   
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County desires to lease the Runyon Field Sports Complex to the 
Runyon Sport Complex Commission, Inc. and both parties desire to enter into this Lease 
Agreement under which the County shall lease such property to the Commission, subject to the 
terms and conditions contained in this Agreement in a manner that delivers a more efficient 
delivery of services to the Pueblo Community.    
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Lease Agreement between the Runyon Sport Complex 
Commission, Inc. and Pueblo County, the terms and conditions of which are incorporated herein 
by this reference, is hereby approved, and that the Chair is hereby authorized to execute the same 
on behalf of Pueblo County.
