A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE 
AND SUBSEQUENT FLOODING  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from the fire that started in 
Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County and further damage 
from flooding in the area of the burn scar; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke and flash flooding; and  
 
          WHEREAS, over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times, and with the continuing and 
developing risk of flash flooding in the area of the burn scar; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on

Information:  
In FY 2026-2027 the Medicaid County Performance Program (County Incentives 
Contract), each county human/social services department with a signed contract 
can earn one incentive payment at the end of the fiscal year. To qualify for the 
payment, the county must meet specific targets for each incentive or submit the 
necessary deliverables for the incentive. 
The Medicaid County Performance Program aims to improve timeliness, 
accuracy, and customer service. The incentive contract also helps counties 
follow rule 10 CCR 2505-5 1.020.3.4, which requires county directors to 
establish internal controls. These controls ensure that the department 
provides timely, respectful, and culturally appropriate service to Medical 
Assistance applicants and members. 
The Department has statutory responsibility for the supervision of local 
administration of the Medical Assistance Program (which includes Medicaid and 
Child Health Plan Plus); it is also obligated under federal law, specifically 2 CFR 
Part 200 Uniform Guidance, to establish performance measures and targets for The 
Department and counties to continuously strive to achieve to meet the program 
objectives as established by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services. 
Further regulatory requirements at 10 CCR 2505-5 1.020.5 require the Eligibility 
Site Director to establish adequate internal controls, including organizing 
operations and staff functions to support performance management. 
 
This memo provides an overview of the Medicaid County Performance Standard 
Incentive Program, focusing on the following key component: 
1. Performance Compliance Performance Incentive Standard 
The following sections will provide details on the Performance Compliance Performance 
Incentive Standard to help guide and support counties in meeting program 
expectations 
Performance Compliance Performance Incentive Standard 
The Performance Compliance Performance Incentive Standard consists of the 
following performance measures: Timeliness measures, Exceeding Processing 
Guidelines measures, Accuracy measures, and Customer Service. The target is to 
increase timeliness percentages, decrease exceeding processing 
guideline(backlog) cases, meet accuracy targets, and provide exceptional 
customer service. The key element of this incentive standard is to meet federal

and state performance standards, to address counties’ internal performance 
management processes, and to comply with the Medical Assistance Performance 
(MAP) 2.0 Dashboard program. MAP 2.0 Dashboards are the established

with performance standards. 
Earning Performance Compliance Performance Incentive 
The Performance Compliance Incentive accounts for 100% of the total County 
Incentives funding for the fiscal year. The Department will utilize MAP 2.0 data to 
evaluate county performance across three key reporting periods. Status Report 1 
(Timeliness & EPG Targets) will cover July 2026 through December 2026, while Status 
Report 2 (Timeliness & EPG Targets) will span January 2027 through June 2027. 
Additionally, Status Report 2 (Accuracy Targets) will use data from April 2026 to 
March 2027 and Customer Service Tier ASA data from July 2026 to June 2027. The 
MAP 2.0 dashboard will be used to score and calculate eligible earnings under the 
Performance Compliance Performance Incentive Standard. The targets for each 
reporting period are outlined below. 
Counties can earn the Performance Incentive for Reporting Period 1 and 2 by 
doing the following: 
● They will receive 100% of the Performance Incentive Payment if they meet 
the targets for both status reports as shown below within the target and 
deliverable section. 
● The payment will be divided as follows: 50% will be awarded for meeting 
status report 1 and 50 % will be awarded for meeting status report 2. 
○ If only one status report period is met, then partial payment 
will be adjusted accordingly.
