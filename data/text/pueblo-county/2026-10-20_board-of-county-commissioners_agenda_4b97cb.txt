October 13, 2026 
Re: Letter of Support for Crossroads' Turning Points, Inc.  
To Whom it May Concern: 
The Board of County Commissioners for Pueblo County is pleased to write this letter in support of 
Crossroads' Turning Points, Inc. (CTP) and their efforts to address the opioid epidemic and overdose rates 
in southern and southeastern Colorado through substance use disorder (SUD) services for prevention, 
treatment, recovery, harm prevention and diversion from criminal justice. Funding CTP's proposal will 
increase resources available for SUD services and support. 
Southern Colorado and Pueblo County have experienced significant workforce, family and general 
livelihood disruption from substance use disorders and the opioid epidemic. The incidence of 
substance use triggers generational impact that not only diverts community resources but  also frays 
the critical social connections of our families and neighborhoods.  
The Board of County Commissioners for Pueblo County supports the efforts of CTP as a 
sophisticated and responsive  agency that works with community  leaders, members of the public, 
law enforcement, health care and other community -based agencies which has encouraged  its 
evolution into becoming one of the region's leading providers of behavioral health programming. 
CTP provides services in twenty -one counties throughout southern and southeastern Colorado, 
including Pueblo County. CTP provides a full continuum of services ranging  from prevention 
through recovery and includes specialized services for men and women involved with the criminal 
justice system, pregnant and postpartum women and women with children. CTP's individual clients, 
along with the communities  in which they live, will benefit greatly from enhanced SUD services and 
support. 
For all these reasons, the Board of County Commissioners for Pueblo County, Colorado, strongly 
supports Crossroads' Turning Points, Inc and their request for funding. 
Thank you, 
 
Board of County Commissioners for Pueblo County, Colorado

          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that: 
 
SECTION 1.  Regular Part-Time Employees who are scheduled to work on a Holiday may 
receive Holiday Pay for two of the Holidays of their choice as approved by their Elected Official 
or Department Director consistent with mission needs 
 
SECTION 2.  Unless the annual resolution states otherwise: when a holiday falls on Saturday, 
the County observes the holiday the Friday before; when a holiday falls on a Sunday, the County 
Page 8 of 12
observes the holiday the Monday after.  
 
SECTION 3.  Personal Leave shall be considered a paid time off chosen by the employee and 
approved by their Elected Official and/or Chief/Department Director (as applicable) and shall 
not roll over from year to year. Paid leave may be utilized in increments of 30 minutes or more 
at the discretion of the Elected Official and/or Chief/Department Director (as applicable) 
consistent with mission needs. Personal hours shall be added to employee Personal Leave 
balances in Workday and shall be considered an annual “use or lose” benefit.  
 
SECTION 4.  Early releases that are not contemplated herein shall be at the discretion of the 
Elected Official and Chief/Department Director (as applicable) based on the operational needs of 
their office/department. 
 
SECTION 5.  Employees who need to work on a County recognized holiday shall be paid as 
normal work hours. Employees may request the holiday hours worked to be added to the 
employee’s Personal Leave balance.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING PUEBLO COUNTY’S 2027 EMPLOYEE MEDICAL, 
DENTAL, AND VISION BENEFIT OFFERINGS AND PREMIUM STRUCTURE  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, Colorado, desires to 
establish employee medical, dental, and vision benefit offerings for the year 2027; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo County Human Resources Department presented seven (7) 
medical benefit plan options to the Board of County Commissioners for consideration in 
determining the County’s 2027 employee benefit offerings; and 
 
          WHEREAS, after reviewing the seven (7) options presented by Human Resources, the 
Board of County Commissioners identified its top four (4) options for further consideration; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the top four (4) options selected by the Board of County Commissioners 
were presented to the Pueblo County Benefits Committee, whose members were provided an 
opportunity to review the options and provide feedback through a survey; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has taken into consideration the 
feedback received from the Benefits Committee, the budgetary constraints facing Pueblo 
County, and the financial investment associated with each of the proposed benefit plans; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to implement a four-tier 
premium and benefit election structure for 2027, replacing the previous three-tier structure. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that after taking into consideration all of the factors outlined above, 
and subject to final annual budget appropriation for fiscal year 2027 by the Board of County 
Commissioners, the following medical, dental, and vision benefit offerings and premium 
structure for the calendar year 2027 shall be offered to County employees:
