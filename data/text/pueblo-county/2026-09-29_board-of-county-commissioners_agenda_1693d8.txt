          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to regulate County roads, 
public easements, and rights-of-ways, and to authorize the use of such street and public ways 
provided such uses may be revoked by the Board of County Commissioners at its pleasure; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Larry A. Davis, being the owner of that property located at 27840 Hillside 
Road described as Parcel# 1411-003-007 Subdivision Lot 8 Valdez Park 1st, hereinafter referred 
to as the Permittee, has requested an Agreement for Revocable Permit for Use of the Public 
Right-of-Way; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Revocable Permit is for the use of a carport, with a portion of Toltec 
George Ln right-of-way which is adjacent to Parcel# 1411-003-007 Subdivision Lot 8 Valdez 
Park 1st; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Agreement for the revocable permit shall extend to and be binding upon 
the owner of said Lot 2, his heirs, administrators, executors, successors, and legal 
representatives, and shall constitute an agreement running with the land until released by 
resolution of the Board of County Commissioners; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to the terms of the permit agreement, the owner has agreed to 
indemnify and hold harmless the County and any utility having the right to use this right-of-way 
and their respective agents and employees, from any and all claims, expenses, liabilities, loss, or 
damage to property or injury to or death of persons, caused by or arising out of the construction, 
reconstruction, reinstallation, maintenance, or use of the land and property covered by and used 
in connection with this permit. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Board of County Commissioners hereby approves the 
Agreement for Revocable Permit for Use of Pueblo Right-of-Way as summarized above and 
which shall be subject to all of the terms and conditions of the Agreement and that the Chair of 
the Board of County Commissioners is hereby authorized to sign the Agreement for Revocable 
Permit on behalf of the County of Pueblo, Colorado. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 29, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

          WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 14-222, dated September 8, 2014, the Board of County 
Commissioners of Pueblo County, Colorado, repealed Resolution 10-122 and established a new 
structure of the Pueblo County Community Corrections Board; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by the same Resolution, the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo 
County, Colorado, established the membership structure of the Pueblo County Community 
Corrections Board; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo County Community Corrections Board has terms expiring for 
seven (7) permanent agency designees on the board and Brian Gomez, Community Corrections 
Director, has reached out to corresponding agencies and received the following ex-officio 
designees; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board, has reviewed the recommendation of the Community 
Corrections Board to appoint Deputy District Attorney Kendall Pearson, 10th Judicial District 
Attorney’s Office Designee, Captain Santino Oliva, Pueblo Police Chief Designee, Bureau Chief 
Paul Toth, Pueblo County Sheriff Designee, District Judge Amiel Markenson, 10th Judicial 
District Court Chief Judge Designee, Probation Supervisor Dana Montoya, 10th Judicial District 
Chief Probation Officer Designee, Deputy Public Defender Griffin Hay, Colorado State Public 
Defender Office, Pueblo Designee and Parole Manager Brian Musso, Colorado Department of 
Corrections, Adult Parole Designee, as members to serve on the Pueblo County Community 
Corrections Board for three (3) year ending October 1, 2029.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the above named agency designees are hereby appointed to the 
Pueblo County Community Corrections Board. Said appointees shall serve at the pleasure of the 
Board of County Commissioners, and the staff to the Board is authorized to provide members of 
the Pueblo County Community Correction Board with notice of said reappointments by 
providing them with a copy of this Resolution.

AMENDING RESOLUTION NOS. 25-284 AND 25-285, ESTABLISHING BUDGET AND 
APPROPRIATIONS BY FUND FOR PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO, OPERATIONS 
FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2026 AND AUTHORIZING A SUPPLEMENTAL 
APPROPRIATION OF $548,882.58 IN UNANTICIPATED INSURANCE PROCEEDS 
FOR REPAIR OF WATER DAMAGE AT THE PUEBLO COUNTY COURTHOUSE  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. §§ 30-11-101, 30-11-103, and 30-11-107, the Board of 
County Commissioners exercises the corporate and administrative powers of Pueblo County, 
including authority over the County's property, accounts, receipts, and expenditures; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Local Government Budget Law of Colorado, C.R.S. §§ 29-1-101 et seq., 
requires the Board to adopt an annual budget and to appropriate County funds, and the Board 
adopted the County's 2026 budget and appropriated funds for fiscal year 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board, by Resolution Nos. 25-284 and 25-285, dated December 15, 
2025, established the budget and appropriations by fund for Pueblo County, Colorado, 
operations in calendar year 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, C.R.S. §29-1-109 requires that any changes to the budget due to any 
transfer, supplemental appropriation, or revised appropriation be made only by ordinance or 
resolution and in accordance with the notice requirements of C.R.S. §29-1-106; and 
 
          WHEREAS, in 2025 a water supply line broke and flooded areas of the Pueblo County 
Courthouse, 215 West 10th Street, and the water damaged building finishes, other property, and 
furniture; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the County submitted insurance claims for that damage and received two 
insurance payments in 2026: $431,652.58 on February 2, 2026, on the initial Courthouse claim, 
and $117,230.00 on June 2, 2026, on a supplemental claim for the damaged furniture, for a 
combined total of $548,882.58; and 
 
          WHEREAS, per County financial records, County staff recorded both payments as 
revenue in General Fund account 101.301000.364450, Insurance Proceed Revenue, and the 
adopted 2026 budget did not anticipate this revenue; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the adopted 2026 budget appropriated no funds in General Fund 
expenditure account 101.419400.436602, R&M Bldg Property - Insurance Claims, the Facilities 
Department account from which the County pays for this repair and replacement work; and 
 
          WHEREAS, under C.R.S. § 29-1-109(1)(b), the Board may authorize the expenditure of 
Page 11 of 39
unanticipated revenues, received from a source other than the County's property tax mill levy 
and not assured at the time the budget was adopted, by enacting a supplemental budget and 
appropriation. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
 
Section 1.  
The Board amends the 2026 Pueblo County budget and increases the estimated revenues of the 
General Fund for fiscal year 2026 by $548,882.58 in account 101.301000.364450, Insurance 
Proceed Revenue. 
 
Section 2.  
The Board enacts a supplemental budget and appropriation and increases the General Fund 
appropriation for fiscal year 2026 by $548,882.58, appropriated to Facilities Department account 
101.419400.436602, R&M Bldg Property - Insurance Claims, which increases from $0.00 to 
$548,882.58, to pay the costs of repairing the water damage at the Pueblo County Courthouse 
and replacing the damaged property and furniture. The General Fund shall make this 
supplemental expenditure through the Facilities Department as the spending agency. 
 
Section 3.  
The Clerk to the Board shall file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Division of Local 
Government in the Colorado Department of Local Affairs, as C.R.S. § 29-1-109(2)(c) requires.  
 
Section 4.  
This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption by the Board of County 
Commissioners.  
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 29, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

for Region II and now the Parties desire to enter into Contract Amendment #2 to provide 
increased funding; and 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Water Heater Replacement Services Contract Amendment #2 
between the Parties, the terms and conditions of which are incorporated herein by this reference, 
are hereby approved and the Director of the Pueblo County Department of Human Services (or 
designee) is authorized to sign said Contract on behalf of Pueblo County.
