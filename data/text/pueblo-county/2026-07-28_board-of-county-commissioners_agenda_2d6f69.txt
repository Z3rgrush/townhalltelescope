Scope of Work 
“Scope of Work” and notations within are based on the stop work eMail from Pueblo County and verbal request to switch 
the installation of the previously ordered and received 200-ton chiller intended for installation at the Pueblo County Jail to

Contract Changes 
• Section II: Increase contract value from $1,972,367.00 to $2,177,380.00 
• Section V. Project Time and Liquidated Damages 
o Change 180 days to 694 days 
o This is based upon a 22-week lead time for the cooling tower 
o This will shift the completion date of the contract to April 30th, 2027 
• Section XII: Superintendent of the Work 
o Our team will have a superintendent on the job as necessary, as determined by Trane 
 
General Scope Modifications 
• The 200-ton Chiller ordered for the Jail will no longer be installed at the jail, and this scope is completely removed 
from the contract (the eliminated scope is shown below). 
• The 200-ton chiller ordered for the Jail will now be installed at the Pueblo County Department of Human Services 
Building (320 W 10th St, Pueblo, CO 81003), replacing the existing 150-ton chiller. 
• Shifting the completion date and associated costs into 2026 (materials and labor). 
 
GSC Cooling Tower Scope Clarification 
• The existing MCC serving the existing cooling tower is difficult to find replacement components. The pricing within 
this change order assumes a replacement, compatible breaker can be located, or the cooling tower can be fed by 
separate feeds that fit into the existing MCC. If either of these options are not viable, a change order will be 
presented.

o Full access to the building June 14th, 2025 – June 29th, 2025

General 
• Like, kind, and quality replacement

Mechanical 
• Preparation of existing chiller for disposal to include removal of refrigerants and electronics.  
• Provide crane services for removal of (1) existing chiller. Crane service will include certified crane operators, 
signalmen, and riggers. It will also include a pick plan. 
• The old chiller will be removed from site and disposed of properly at a local scrap yard. We have a signed 
agreement with Dionisio to only dispose of approved components at their yard.  
• Relocate (1) small mini split condenser closer to south and east wall. We may opt to place condenser on wide 
stance support bracket with roof protection pads under each of the (4) legs.  
• The new chiller is expected to be received at Duffy’s crane yard in Pueblo so that it can be loaded at the proper 
date and time for installation. It will be loaded onto a trailer and brought out with the crane team the morning of 
the installation.  
• We will have a safety manager onsite the day of the installation. The same individual will also review the pick plan 
and comment if needed. Certifications of operators, riggers, and signalmen will match the crew on site. Crane 
certificates will be provided.  
• Chiller will be placed by crane operator and crew with assistance from our staff. Coordination of this placement 
will be critical to make sure the building in this area is unoccupied.  
• Once the chiller is set, crane operations will demobilize.  
• Provide and install new piping from existing butterfly valves on roof to the new chiller pipe connection points. 
Includes all fittings, adapters, high point vents, automatic air vents, and P&T ports.  
• Pressure test piping at 1.5 times system pressure in preparation for system pressurization.  
• Insulate all new piping and fittings and provide metal jacketing the entire length.  
• Fill new portion of the system with water and glycol. Bleed air from the system.  
• Provide on site assistance during system startup 
• We will provide lifts and ladders as needed for our scope of work.

What is a Core Services Plan II?   
Counties that were over-spent in their Core Allocation during the previous fiscal year, and wish to request 
additional funds beyond the current year allocation, have the option to complete a Core Services Plan II.  
 
This Core Services Plan II should focus exclusively on outlining the additional or expanded services planned, 
contingent upon the availability of extra funding. If these funds are intended for a County Designed service or 
program not already detailed in the current Core Services Plan, supplemental information must be included.

3. What is the name of the service or program? 7.303.1 Definitions Supervised Family Time Program 
4. Describe the service and components; define the goals of the program 7.303.11 Program Goals 
The Pueblo County Center for Supported Family Time is a neutral, family friendly setting that provides a 
safe, family friendly environment for families to engage in monitored contact with their child(ren). During 
Family Time sessions workers maintain the safety of the child while educating, mentoring, and training 
parents to encourage bonding and healthy parenting. Family Time workers also provide role modeling to 
foster healthy parent-child interactions. As a result, clients are able to make positive changes in the quality 
of each parenting interactions with their child(ren) and ensure safety of the family. 
 
 Program Goals:  
a) To increase family stability and positive parent-child interactions that will enable the child to safely return 
home and remain home without further incidents of abuse or neglect.  
a. 96% of children involved in Supervised Family Time Services will return to their family home or kin within 
6 months of completing Family Time services.  
b. 98% of families actively participating in the Supervised Family Time Program, whose children have been 
returned home, will have no subsequent confirmed incidents of abuse or neglect.
