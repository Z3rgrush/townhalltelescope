RESOLUTION NO.  
 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
APPROVING THE SECURE TRANSPORTATION SERVICES LICENSE AND 
VEHICLE PERMIT FOR ROCKY TRIP, LLC PER THE PUEBLO COUNTY POLICY 
AND PROCEDURES REGARDING SECURE TRANSPORTATION SERVICES  
 
          WHEREAS, House Bill 21-1085, adopted in June 2021, created a regulatory and service 
system to provide public or private secure transportation services to individuals experiencing a 
behavioral health crisis; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Bill directed each county’s Board of County Commissioners to 
implement a program, including issuing licenses and vehicle permits for such transports, 
processing complaints and enforcement of the rules associated with the program by January 1, 
2023; and  
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 22-291, dated December 22, 2022, the Board of County 
Commissioners adopted the Pueblo County Policy and Procedures regarding Secure 
Transportation Services; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Rocky Trip, LLC, has submitted the complete application form with 
documentation and necessary fees to provide Secure Transportation Services in Pueblo County 
and obtained completed vehicle inspections all in compliance with the adopted Policy. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Secure Transportation Services License Number 2026-02 and 
associated Vehicle Permit 2026-02, Class B, for Rocky Trip, LLC, which are incorporated 
herein by this reference, are hereby approved and the Chair of the Board of County 
Commissioners of Pueblo County, Colorado, is hereby authorized to sign said License and 
Vehicle Permit on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this November 3, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado. 
 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO
