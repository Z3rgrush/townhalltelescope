RESOLUTION NO.  
 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
APPOINTING DIRECTORS TO THE BOARD OF THE PUEBLO COUNTY CAPITAL 
CONSTRUCTION CORPORATION  
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo County Capital Construction Corporation (the “Corporation”) 
was incorporated on December 18, 1989 at the direction of this Board and for the purpose of 
assisting Pueblo County in the acquisition of real property, including by leasing such property to 
Pueblo County to facilitate financing arrangements; and  
 
          WHEREAS, under Section V of the Articles of Incorporation, as amended, the 
Corporation is managed by a board of two directors appointed by this Board and subject to 
removal by this Board at any time; and  
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 20-223, this Board appointed Sabina Genesio, County 
Manager, and Cynthia Mitchell, County Attorney, to serve as directors of the Corporation; and  
 
          WHEREAS, neither director is currently employed by Pueblo County, and so the Board 
has resolved to remove the directors from the board of the Corporation and to appoint new 
directors; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the role of the Corporation is limited in nature and its directors will meet 
infrequently, and as such the Board believes that it is in the best interests of Pueblo County to 
appoint two current employees of the County to serve as directors of the Corporation.  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that Sabina Genesio and Cynthia Mitchell are removed as directors of 
the Corporation. 
 
          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Craig McBrain, Pueblo County Chief Financial 
Officer, and Robert Choi, Pueblo County Chief Administrative Officer, are appointed to serve as 
the directors of the Corporation, with their appointments effective immediately upon adoption of 
this Resolution. Consistent with Article V of the Articles of Incorporation, the term of each 
director is perpetual; except that if either director ceases to be employed by Pueblo County, then 
the director’s term will expire and such director shall be removed automatically, without the 
need for further action of this Board, at the time of his separation from Pueblo County.  
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 16, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
