 3. Planned Unit Development 
HPUD-16-4 RESCISSION 
Phoenix Eye Consulting, LLC (Applicant) 
NATT CO Holdings, LLC (Landowner) 
129 E. Enterprise Dr., Pueblo West 
 
The applicant is requesting RESCISSION of Planned Unit Development HPUD-16-4 
(fka PUD 2016-003) that was originally approved on June 22, 2016. The PUD lists 
specific uses on the subject property to allow for development of marijuana and light 
industrial uses, which the current property owner no longer wishes to use. The 
rescission of the PUD will reinstate the previous zoning of LI, Light Industrial, for the 
subject property. 
(1 minute)
