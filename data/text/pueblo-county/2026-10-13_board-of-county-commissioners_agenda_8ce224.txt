A RESOLUTION AMENDING RESOLUTION 26-316   
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo County government is in a budgetary situation that requires 
significant reductions in spending, including spending on personnel costs; and  
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-316, dated October 8, 2026, this Board approved the 
elimination of the Board of County Commissioners Receptionist Position as a whole and ended 
the employment of the current employees in that position; and  
 
          WHEREAS, this Board wishes to amend the effective date to December 19, 2026, to 
reflect the Pueblo County payroll cycle; and  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
1. The position of BOCC Receptionist is hereby eliminated effective December 19, 2026. 
2. Those County employees holding the position of BOCC Receptionist shall be separated 
from County employment effective December 19, 2026. 
3. County staff are directed to take all necessary steps to implement this Resolution and to 
assist the affected employees with their out processing.  
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 13, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING PUEBLO COUNTY’S 2027 PAY PLAN GRADES  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of Pueblo County Commissioners makes regular adjustments to 
the Pueblo County Pay Plan to ensure a competitive edge with recruitment, retention efforts, and 
to comply with federal and state minimum wage laws; and 
 
          WHEREAS, to address the current financial status of the County, and to comply with 
federal and state law, the BOCC seeks to approve revised pay plan grades for 2027; and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board has determined that it would be in the best interest of the County 
that the 2027 Pay Plan Grades be effective for the January 2027 payroll (beginning 12/20/2026). 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners on 
behalf of Pueblo County, Colorado, that the 2027 Pay Plan Grades, attached hereto and 
incorporated herein and made a part of this Resolution, are hereby approved, and shall be 
effective for the January 2027 Payroll (beginning 12/20/26); and this Resolution shall supersede 
and control over any conflicting County Policy or Resolution. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 13, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND DESIGNATING PUEBLO COUNTY’S 2027 
OBSERVED HOLIDAYS  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. §30-10-109, the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, has the authority to designate County holidays; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to designate the holidays on 
which County offices shall be closed for the transaction of County business in the year 2027. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the following dates be observed as holidays by Pueblo County 
Government during the calendar year 2027: 
 
HOLIDAY DATE OBSERVED

Presidents’ Day Monday, February 15th  
Memorial Day Monday, May 31st  
Juneteenth Day (observed) Friday, June 18th  
Independence Day (observed) Monday, July 5th

Personal Leave (16 hours total) All full-time County employees will receive 16 hours 
of Personal Leave in 2027, regardless of position, 
schedule, department, or office. Use of Personal 
Leave is selected by the employee and subject to 
approval by their Chief Officer, Department 
Director, or Elected Official. 
 
 
 
          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that: 
 
Page 8 of 9
SECTION 1. Regular Part-Time Employees who are scheduled to work on a Holiday may 
receive Holiday Pay for two of the Holidays of their choice as approved by their Elected Official 
or Department Director. 
 
SECTION 2. When a holiday falls on Saturday, Friday will be celebrated as the County holiday   
  
When a holiday falls on Sunday, Monday will be celebrated as the County holiday 
 
SECTION 3. Personal Leave shall be considered a paid day off chosen by the employee and 
approved by their Elected Official and Chief/Department Director (as applicable) and shall not 
roll over from year to year.  
 
SECTION 4. Early releases that are not contemplated herein shall be at the discretion of the 
Elected Official and Chief/Department Director (as applicable) based on the operational needs of 
their office/department. 
 
SECTION 5. All time off taken by County employees for the days designated as Early Releases 
shall be considered holiday time off for purposes of “worked time” in determining Overtime in 
accordance with the Fair Labor Standards Act.  
 
SECTION 6. Employees who need to work on a County recognized holiday shall be paid as 
normal work hours. Employees may request the holiday hours worked to be added to the 
employee’s Personal Leave balance.   
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this October 13, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
