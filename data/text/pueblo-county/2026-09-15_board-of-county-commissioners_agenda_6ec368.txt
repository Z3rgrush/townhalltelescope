RESOLUTION NO.  
 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
APPROVING THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN COLORADO 
STATE UNIVERSITY GLOBAL AND PUEBLO COUNTY TO PROVIDE ONLINE 
EDUCATION PROGRAMS FOR COUNTY EMPLOYEES  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desire to provide educational 
opportunities to its employees to support career development; and  
 
          WHEREAS, Colorado State University Global (CSU-Global) is a public institution of 
higher education offering online undergraduate, graduate, and certificate programs; and  
 
          WHEREAS, CSU-Global wishes to collaborate with the County to provide educational 
opportunities at a discounted rate to employees and establish CSU Global as a preferred 
University for the County employees; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is in the best interests of Pueblo County 
Government and its employees to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with CSU-
Global. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that the Memorandum of Understanding between the Colorado State 
University Global and Pueblo County, the terms and conditions of which are incorporated herein 
by this reference, is hereby approved, and that the Chair of the Board of County Commissioners 
or the Director of Human Resources are hereby authorized to sign said Agreement on behalf of 
Pueblo County. 
 
 
    PASSED AND ADOPTED this September 15, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
Between 
Colorado State University Global  
and 
 Pueblo County Government  
THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) is entered into and effective the ____ day of 
________________ 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and between Colorado State University Global 
(CSU Global) and Pueblo County Government (County), collectively referred to as the “Parties” and 
individually as a “Party”. 
WHEREAS, CSU Global is a public institution of higher education offering online undergraduate, 
graduate, and certificate programs; and  
WHEREAS, the County desires to provide educational opportunities to its employees to support 
career development; and  
WHEREAS, the Parties wish to collaborate to provide educational opportunities at a discounted rate 
to employees and establish CSU Global as a preferred University for the County employees.  
NOWTHEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the 
Parties agree as follows: 
 
A. Mission 
Pueblo County Government and CSU Global are aligned with the mission of advancing 
academic and professional success for working adults in a global society through access to 
dynamic education characterized by excellence, innovative delivery technologies, industry 
relevance, and strong stakeholder engagement. 
B. Purpose 
This MOU establishes the process for Pueblo County Government employees to matriculate to 
CSU Global.    
It further outlines (C) admissions process; (D) pricing structure and billing process; (E) partner 
contact information for notices; (F) term; and (G) additional provisions. 
C. Admissions 
i. Admission to the University is not guaranteed; all interested students shall apply for 
admissions, enroll, and register as students in accordance with the University's 
admissions and registration requirements and processes.  
ii. Admissions and applicable discounts shall be considered regardless of race, 
ethnicity, religion, sex, disability, color, age, marital status, national origin or other 
non-merit factors.  
iii. The Parties expressly agree County shall have no duty or obligation to enroll, 
register, or otherwise assist an employee during the admissions process. It is 
understood by the Parties that County employees shall provide proof of current 
County employment to CSU Global. Pueblo County shall in no way be liable or 
responsible for the representation made by its employees, or other person(s) not a 
party hereto, during the enrollment process. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the 
County agrees to verify current employment upon CSU Global’s request. 
 
D. Pricing Structure and Billing Process 
a. Pricing and Payment Structure

i. Tuition for all undergraduate-level degrees and certificates will be offered at a rate of 
10% off of current undergraduate tuition per credit. 
ii. Tuition for all graduate-level degrees and certificates will be offered at a rate of 10% 
off of current graduate tuition per credit. 
iii. Individual students are responsible for payment on or prior to the course start date 
unless they have made alternative payment arrangements through the Student 
Financial Services Office, or where a billing process is established. The Parties 
expressly understand and agree the County shall in no way be liable for any potential 
nonpayment of a County employee under this MOU. 
b. Billing Process 
i. Billing will be handled through CSU Global’s standard process, and invoices will be 
issued directly to the student. Invoices will include tuition charges for any 
students who have confirmed their intent to participate in their course(s) 
through the attendance verification process that occurs in the first week of 
each course term.  
E. Notices 
Any notice or communication required or permitted to be given under this MOU and Financial 
Provisions shall be in writing and shall be emailed with read receipt requested, served 
personally, or sent by United States registered or certified mail, postage prepaid with 
return receipt requested.  In the event that a notice is emailed and a read receipt or 
personal email reply is not received within 5 business days, the sending Party shall 
serve personally or send via US mail, as outlined in this paragraph.  Notices shall be 
addressed to the other Party as follows:

F. Term 
a. The term of this MOU shall take effect as of the Effective Date first written above and 
automatically terminate on the second calendar year anniversary from the Effective 
Date, unless sooner terminated as provided for herein.. 
b. The MOU may be terminated at any time and for any reason by either party upon not 
less than thirty (30) days’ prior written notice to the other party; provided, however, 
that if such notice is issued after the start of an academic term, this MOU shall remain 
in effect until the conclusion of that academic term.

G. Additional Provisions 
a. This MOU may be modified only in writing signed by both parties hereto. 
b. This MOU is Intended as the complete integration of all understandings between the 
parties. No prior or contemporaneous addition, deletions, or other amendment hereto 
shall have any force or effect whatsoever, unless embodied in a written agreement 
executed and approved. 
c. In the event one or more provision of this MOU are found to be void, invalid, illegal, or 
unenforceable in any way, the validity, legality and enforceability of the remaining 
provisions shall remain in full force and effect. 
d. Nothing in this MOU shall authorize the exchange of any funds between the Parties.   
e. The Parties explicitly understand and agree nothing in this MOU shall impose any 
duty or obligation between the Parties. Notwithstanding the foregoing, nothing in this 
MOU shall be construed as a waiver of County of any protections afforded to County 
under the Colorado Governmental Immunity Act. 
f. Engagement that occurs as a result of this MOU will be reviewed by all parties on a 
(minimum) quarterly basis to determine whether or not the goals of both the partner 
and the University are being met. Evaluation criteria will include trimester-to-trimester 
retention rates, academic success rates, participation rates, and collaborative 
marketing effectiveness. 
g. Nothing in this MOU shall create an employer-employee relationship. 
h. All marketing materials used by either Pueblo County Government or CSU Global 
must receive approval by the other institution prior to use. 
i. This MOU shall be governed in all respects by the laws of the State of Colorado. 
j. Nothing in this MOU shall imply exclusivity on the part of either Party. Either party 
may enter into similar partnerships with other entities. 
k. A separate training and articulation agreement will be issued for employer-sponsored 
coursework that occurs outside of CSU Global. 
Acknowledged and Agreed: 
Pueblo County Government   Colorado State University Global
