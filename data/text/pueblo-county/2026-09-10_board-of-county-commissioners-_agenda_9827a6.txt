WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has reviewed and taken administrative notice of the

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners does

consisting of a 150’ tower and 400 sf. ground support building within a 10,000 sf. lease area in an A1 Large 
Agriculture zone district, subject to the following conditions of approval and Directive to Staff: 
 
 
CONDITIONS 
  
 
1. The approval of Special Use Permit SUP-26-8 is limited to a 150’ broadcast tower and 400 sf. ground 
support building within a 10,000 sf. lease area. 
 
2. This special use permit shall not constitute an exemption from zoning or any applicable regulations. 
Issuance of this special use permit is subject to approval by any regulatory agency where required by 
regulation or statute. 
 
3. The tower shall be constructed and made available to permit collocation of, at minimum, two additional 
functionally equivalent service providers. 
 
4. Prior to commencement of construction, the applicant shall obtain all required permits from the Pueblo 
County Building Division for the approved tower, ground support building, and any associated 
improvements requiring permits. 
 
5. Prior to establishing the proposed use on the subject property, the applicant shall provide the Department of 
Planning and Development documentation demonstrating legal access to the lease area, including recorded 
copies of any access easements or agreements necessary to establish such access.

17.12.030 Definitions 
Boarding or Rooming House 
A residential structure that is the operator’s personal primary dwelling where lodging is provided, w
ith or 
without meals, for compensation. A boarding house is not a bed and breakfast or short-term rental. 
Agricultural Processing 
The processing and/or packaging of agricultural products, excluding the processing of fish, meat, 
or game. Examples include but are not limited to the making of food or herbal products, 
beverages, wreaths, woolen products, cheese, wine, and candles. Agricultural processing may 
include the accessory sales or wholesale of agricultural products grown or produced on the site. 
Agricultural Tourism 
Activities conducted on a parcel with a primary agricultural production or processing use and 
offered to the public for the purpose of recreation, education, or active tourism related 
involvement in the agricultural use. These activities shall be incidental to the primary agricultural 
use on the site or related to natural resources present on the property. This term includes guest 
ranches, site tours, hayrides, corn mazes, classes related to agricultural products or skills, farm-
to-table dining, and picnic and party facilities offered in conjunction with the above. Agricultural 
tourism does not include accommodation uses or rRetail sales shall be incidental and limited to 
the sale of products grown or processed onsite. 
 
  
Craft Alcohol Facility 
A small brewery, winery, meadery, or distillery operated separately or in conjunction with a 
drinking establishment or restaurant, provided the beer, wine, mead or liquor is sold for 
consumption onsite or off the premises and is not sold to other drinking establishments, 
restaurants, or wholesalers. Accessory uses include tasting rooms at which product tasting 
Page 33 of 176
 
 
Page 32 of 41 
 
occurs. A craft alcohol facility does not include alcoholic beverage production accessory to an 
agricultural production or processing use. 
Data Center 
A use where the primary purpose is the storage, management, processing, and/or transmission 
of digital data, and where the majority of the space is occupied by computers, servers, 
telecommunications equipment, and related supporting infrastructure. This definition does not 
include server rooms or information technology infrastructure that are accessory or incidental to 
a principal use. 
Parcel of Record 
A lot that is part of a subdivision, the plat of which was legally approved and recorded with the 
County Clerk and Recorder prior to the effective date of this UDC. 
A lot or parcel demonstrated through competent evidence acceptable to the County to have been 
created prior to the adoption of Pueblo County subdivision regulations. 
Personal Use Operation (Agricultural) 
The keeping, raising, feeding, or confinement of livestock or poultry by a property owner or 
occupant for personal or household consumption, education, or enjoyment, where the primary 
purpose is not the commercial sale of animals or animal products. Sales may occur provided 
they are occasional and subordinate to the primary personal use of the operation or are 
conducted in compliance with applicable Colorado Department of Agriculture or federal 
exemptions for small-scale agricultural producers. A Personal Use Operation does not include 
operations conducted as a commercial enterprise or primary source of income. 
 
  
Door Sidewalk Sign 
A manual changeable copy portable temporary  sign typically located near the entrance of a 
business and intended to be seen from the street, such as menu boards, sandwich boards, or A -
Frame signs. Sidewalk signs may contain manual changeable copy. 
Single-Room Occupancy 
A residential structure where individual lodging rooms are provided, with or without meals, for 
compensation, generally for an extended duration. The term includes a boarding house and a 
rooming house but does not include a bed and breakfast, short-term rental, or short-term lodging 
facilities open to public or overnight guests.

A use that may be permitted in a zone district upon favorable action by the Planning

A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF PUEBLO COUNTY, 
COLORADO, CONFIRMING THE CONTINUED VALIDITY OF 1041 PERMIT NO. 2014-003 
(STAGECOACH SOLAR PROJECT), ACKNOWLEDGING THE PERMITTEE, AND ADOPTING 
UPDATED CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL 
 
WHEREAS, the Pueblo Board of County Commissioners is the Permit Authority authorized by House 
Bill (HB) 1041 (1974), as amended, to regulate areas and activities of State and local interest; and 
  
WHEREAS, on February 28, 2017, the Board of County Commissioners, acting as the Pueblo County 
1041 Permit Authority, adopted Resolution No. P&D 17-017 approving 1041 Permit No. 2014-003 for the 
Stagecoach Solar Project, a utility-scale solar energy facility with a generating capacity of up to 400 megawatts, 
subject to conditions of approval; and 
  
WHEREAS, the Stagecoach Solar Project has not yet commenced construction and remains subject to 
the terms and conditions established in Resolution No. P&D 17-017; and 
  
WHEREAS, since approval of Resolution No. P&D 17-017, Pueblo County has adopted a new Unified 
Development Code and updated standards applicable to utility-scale solar energy facilities; and 
  
WHEREAS, in order to ensure that the Stagecoach Solar Project is constructed and operated consistent 
with current Pueblo County requirements, the Department of Planning and Development evaluated the existing 
conditions of approval and identified additional and updated requirements necessary to address current 
regulations and best management practices; and 
  
WHEREAS, by correspondence dated January 30, 2026, Leeward Renewable Energy Development, 
LLC, on behalf of Stagecoach Solar, LLC, acknowledged and agreed to comply with the updated County 
requirements, including preparation of updated construction management, emergency management, 
decommissioning and reclamation, wildlife mitigation, lighting, road use, drainage, and other plans and 
requirements prior to construction; and 
  
WHEREAS, by correspondence dated February 28, 2025, Leeward Renewable Energy represented that 
Stagecoach Solar, LLC remains the original permittee under Resolution No. P&D 17-017, that ownership 
changes occurred only at the parent company level, and that no transfer of the 1041 Permit has occurred; and 
  
WHEREAS, the Board finds that adopting updated conditions of approval is necessary to protect the 
public health, safety, and welfare, ensure compliance with current County regulations, and provide clear 
direction for implementation of the previously approved project. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Pueblo Board of County Commissioners, acting as 
the Pueblo County 1041Permit Authority, that: 
1. Resolution No. P&D 17-017 and 1041 Permit No. 2014-003 remain in full force and effect, except as

Page 34 of 41 
 
modified by this Resolution. 
2. The Board finds that Stagecoach Solar, LLC remains the holder of 1041 Permit No. 2014-003. Based 
upon the information presented, the acquisition of First Solar's renewable energy development platform 
by Leeward Renewable Energy Development, LLC did not constitute a transfer of the 1041 Permit 
requiring approval by the Board of County Commissioners. 
3. The following conditions are hereby adopted and shall supplement the original Conditions of Approval 
contained in Resolution No. P&D 17-017. In the event of a conflict between the original conditions and 
the following conditions, the provisions of this Resolution shall govern. 
Updated Conditions of Approval 
1. Construction Management and Communication 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall submit a Construction Management and 
Mitigation Plan (CMMP) for review and approval by the Department of Planning and Development. At 
a minimum, the CMMP shall identify construction laydown areas, lighting, employee parking, 
designated staging areas, a project manager with 24-hour contact information, and procedures for 
responding to public inquiries and complaints. 
2. Emergency Management 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall submit a Final Emergency Management 
Plan acceptable to Pueblo County and the applicable emergency response agencies. 
3. Decommissioning and Reclamation 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall submit a revised Decommissioning and 
Reclamation Plan meeting current Pueblo County requirements. The Applicant shall provide financial 
assurance equal to one hundred fifteen percent (115%) of the estimated cost of decommissioning and 
reclamation, excluding salvage value. Financial assurance shall be provided no later than fifteen (15) 
years after the Commercial Operation Date and shall be updated every five (5) years thereafter. 
Acceptable forms of financial assurance include an irrevocable standby letter of credit, performance 
bond, or other form approved by Pueblo County. 
4. Wildlife Protection 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall submit updated Wildlife Mitigation, Habitat 
Assessment, and Wildlife Fencing Plans consistent with current County requirements and Colorado 
Parks and Wildlife recommendations. 
5. Development Plan 
The Applicant shall update the Preliminary Site Plan and Development Plan to depict final project 
layout, wildlife corridors where applicable, and all required setbacks, staging areas, and project 
improvements. 
6. Lighting 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall submit a Lighting Plan demonstrating 
compliance with the Pueblo County Unified Development Code. 
7. Construction Schedule and Status Reports 
Construction shall commence within three (3) years of adoption of this Resolution unless extended by 
the Board. The Applicant shall submit annual written status reports until commercial operation begins. 
8. Truck Identification 
Construction vehicles shall display project identification and comply with all approved staging and 
parking restrictions. 
9.  Staging Setbacks 
No laydown yard, staging area, construction trailer, or similar facility shall be located within two 
hundred (200) feet of an existing residential dwelling. 
10. Herbicide and Noxious Weed Management 
Prior to construction, the Applicant shall submit a Noxious Weed Management Plan addressing 
herbicide use, inspection schedules, and weed control measures consistent with County requirements. 
11. Permit Coordination 
Copies of all applicable permits and approvals issued by other regulatory agencies shall be submitted to 
the Department of Planning and Development within sixty (60) days of receipt. 
12. Reporting Requirements:

Page 35 of 41 
 
  
The permit holder shall submit local worker hiring data to the Zoning Administrator at the 
commencement of construction and every three (3) months thereafter until the initial commercial 
operating date of the solar facility. Local worker and union worker hiring data shall include the 
following: 
1. The total number of workers employed on the project site during the reporting period. 
2. The number and percentage of total workers who are Pueblo County residents with a primary 
residence within Pueblo County. 
3. The number and percentage of total workers who are members of a labor union. 
Public Works Requirements 
The Applicant shall obtain all applicable Public Works approvals, including a Road Use Agreement, Access 
Permit(s), Excavation Permit(s), Pueblo County Stormwater Construction Discharge Permit, Stormwater 
Management Plan approval, Drainage Report, Grading Plan, and Erosion Control Plan prior to commencement 
of construction. 
13. Road Use and Construction Traffic 
Prior to commencement of construction, the Applicant shall execute a Road Use Agreement with Pueblo 
County and obtain all required Public Works permits. 
The Applicant shall provide: 
1. An accurate estimate of construction traffic, including vehicle types and anticipated frequency of 
travel; 
2. The number of permitted oversized deliveries authorized by the Colorado Department of 
Transportation pursuant to C.R.S. § 42-4-510(11); 
3. The total area of development; 
4. A designated haul route map identifying all roads proposed for construction traffic; and 
5. A construction schedule identifying all phases of construction. 
The Applicant may document existing roadway conditions for its records prior to construction. Road 
maintenance and repairs shall be administered in accordance with the executed Road Use Agreement 
and applicable Pueblo County Public Works requirements. 
6. No hauling will be allowed on 36th Lane. 
7. The applicant shall coordinate with the City of Pueblo for their use of Lime Road in City maintained 
sections and to coordinate with the City for the proposed Lime Road reconstruction. 
 
14. Stormwater and Revegetation Financial Assurance 
The Applicant shall provide financial assurance of $3,500 per disturbed acre for stormwater stabilization 
and revegetation in accordance with Pueblo County Public Works requirements. 
15. Final Stabilization 
Stormwater facilities and associated financial assurance shall remain in place until final stabilization has 
been achieved in accordance with applicable CDPHE requirements and confirmed by Pueblo County 
Public Works. 
16. Successors and Assigns 
These conditions shall be binding upon the Applicant and any successor or approved permit holder. 
 
Except as expressly modified herein, all findings, conditions, and requirements contained in Resolution No. 
P&D 17-017 shall remain unchanged and in full force and effect. 
 
 
PASSED AND ADOPTED this 14th day of July 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

1/4 South of Highway 50, Pueblo County, Colorado; and 
 
WHEREAS, Section 17.140.160 of the Pueblo County Code provides for a variance for parcels that 
suffer a unique or singular disadvantage not shared by other parcels in the neighborhood, that will not have an 
injurious effect upon the existing or future use of adjacent parcels, and will not adversely alter the general 
character of the neighborhood; and 
  
WHEREAS, a public hearing, preceded by proper public notice was held by the Board of County 
Commissioners, sitting as the Zoning Board of Appeals on June 11, 2026, and, at which time, all those present 
at the hearing who desired to be heard were heard and their testimony recorded; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Zoning Board of Appeals has reviewed and taken administrative notice of the application, 
documents, evidence presented at the hearing, and the contents of the Planning Director’s file, and finds as 
follows: 
 
1. The Variance, if granted, will grant only those uses listed as a use permitted in the zone district in which 
the parcel is located. 
 
2. The parcel for which the Variance appeal is made suffers unique or singular disadvantages such as, but 
not limited to, size, shape, topography, location, or surroundings not shared by other parcels in the 
neighborhood. 
 
a. The Variance will not grant privileges inconsistent with limitations shared by other parcels in the 
zone district. 
 
b. The Variance will not have an injurious effect on the existing or future use of adjacent parcels. 
 
5. The Variance will not injure or adversely alter the general character of the neighborhood in which the 
Variance is sought. 
 
6. The Variance appeal is in harmony with the intent, purpose, and spirit of this resolution. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of County Commissioners sitting as the 
Zoning Board of Appeals of Pueblo County, Colorado, by a vote of 3 to 0, does hereby APPROVE Zoning 
Variance ZV-26-2 with the following conditions: 
 
1. The approval of Zoning Variance ZV-26-2 shall be to allow a front yard setback of 57 feet in lieu of the 150-
foot front yard setback required for a new Wireless Communications Tower. 
 
2. All future development on the parcel must comply with the development standards of the A1 Large 
Agriculture zone district.

agricultural outbuildings, and a private school allowed by special use permit HSUP-08-11. 
 
In addition to the permitted residential and school uses on the property, the applicants now request 
approval to operate a commercial business selling, repairing, and renting equipment. This use falls 
under the Unified Development Code’s definition of Vehicle Sales, Rental, and Leasing, Heavy 
which includes sales or rental of heavy trucks, recreational vehicles, boats, trailers, tractors, 
construction equipment, agricultural implements, manufactured homes, or similar heavy equipment 
including storage, maintenance, and servicing. This use is listed as a special use in the A1 Large 
Agriculture zone district.
