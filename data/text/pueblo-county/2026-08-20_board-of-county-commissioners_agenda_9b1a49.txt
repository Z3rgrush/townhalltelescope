A RESOLUTION CONTINUING THE AUTHORIZATION OF A DECLARATION OF 
LOCAL DISASTER EMERGENCY REGARDING THE ASPEN ACRES AREA FIRE  
 
          WHEREAS, Pueblo County has sustained severe damage from an ongoing fire that 
started in Aspen Acres area on the border between Pueblo County and Custer County; and  
 
          WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026, the fire necessitated the evacuation of people and 
livestock from the Aspen Acres Area, Beulah, San Isabel, Rye, and surrounding areas, all 
located wholly or partially within Pueblo County, Colorado; and  
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709, which provides that a local disaster may 
be declared” only by the principal executive officer of a political subdivision,” the Chair of the 
Pueblo County Board of County Commissioners issued a Disaster/Emergency Declaration on 
June 29th, 2026; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 26-202, dated July 2nd, 2026, this board authorized a 
declaration of Local Disaster Emergency; and 
 
          WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-709(1), the declaration of a local disaster “shall 
not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven days except by or with the consent 
of the governing board of the political subdivision”; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the cost and magnitude of responding to and recovering from the impacts of 
this event continues to be far in excess of Pueblo County’s available resources; and 
 
          WHEREAS, an imminent threat continues to exist for Pueblo County citizens due to fire 
and smoke; and  
 
          WHEREAS, as over 101,965 acres have been affected by the fire, and approximately 240 
homes and 9 businesses have been destroyed in Pueblo County requiring the support of over 
1900 fire personnel from across the County at various times; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the purpose of the Continued Authorization of Declaration is to proceed 
with the response and recovery aspects of any and all applicable disaster emergency plans and to 
authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners enacted the continued resolution on 
July 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th, August 4th, and 13th, 2026, and again desires to enact this Continued 
Authorization of the Emergency Declaration to ensure that financial aid is made available to 
affected individuals, business and governmental entities as applicable and pursuant to state law. 
Page 3 of 104
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that: 
 
1.    The Declaration Continuing Authorization of the Local Disaster Emergency, as provided for 
herein, is hereby approved, and shall remain in full force and effect until further action is taken 
by the Chair or the Board to terminate or modify the Declaration or until the declaration expires 
pursuant to state statute; and 
 
2.    The Chair of the Board of County Commissioners is authorized to execute said Declaration 
on behalf of Pueblo County. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 20, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.

          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVEDthat either the Chair, the Pueblo County Emergency 
Management Coordinator, or their designee may execute the Application and Related Forms on 
behalf of the County.

PROJECT UNDERSTANDING 
Pueblo County (County) is seeking to carry out the complete design for the Connecting Communities Trail Project (the 
project), compliant with all Federal, State, and Local requirements. No federal funding has been identified for this project. 
 
Medal of Honor Boulevard Trail – This 3.2-mile portion of trail on the north side of Medal of Honor Boulevard has been 
designed, advertised, and constructed to a temporary condition by the newly completed Medal of Honor Extension 
Roadway project, which included rough grading, drainage, and ABC placement. Remaining improvements include 4" 
concrete pavement along the trail, base course recompaction, ADA ramps, pedestrian buttons, safety rail, and lighting. A 
CDOT Access Permit and Environmental clearance will be required. 
 
Colorado State Highway 45 Trail – This 1.2-mile portion of the trail runs parallel to CO 45 on the east side of the highway. 
This trail includes a crossing of the UPRR on the existing railroad overpass. Pedestrian improvements at the intersection 
of 11th Street and CO 45 will include pedestrian ramps and modifications to the existing push-button signals. A CDOT 
Access Permit and Environmental clearance will be required. 
Trailhead – A new trailhead access point near Purcell Boulevard with parking, as well as fully 
accessible, multimodal transit bus stops serving the Detention Center and the existing trailhead at the southwest 
corner of Pueblo Boulevard and 11th Street will be designed to support seamless integration of 
pedestrian, bicycle, and transit modes and to comply with applicable ADA accessibility requirements. 
The three project elements will be combined into a single construction package to be advertised for bidding to construct. 
The Consultant will be responsible for the complete survey, permit identification, permitting, cost estimates, scheduling, 
specifications, and the production of all associated design plans, right-of-way plans, the corresponding PS&E plans, and 
finally, the construction package for the Construction RFP. Design services during construction are also included in this 
scope of work.

WITNESSETH: 
 
WHEREAS, Colorado Constitution Article XIV, Section 18(2)(a), and C.R.S. § 29-1-203, 
as amended, authorize the County Clerk and the District to cooperate or contract with each other 
to provide any function, service, or facility lawfully authorized to each; and 
 
WHEREAS, the County Clerk has the facilities to perform the functions required in the 
administration of those elections and is authorized to assist in conducting all or part of election-
related duties on behalf of political subdivisions under C.R.S. § 1-1-111(2); and 
 
WHEREAS, C.R.S. § 1-7-116, requires entry into and performance of intergovernmental 
agreements where political subdivisions and the county clerk and recorder cooperate in the conduct 
and financing of the election and provide for reasonable sharing of costs; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Constitution of Colorado, Article X, Section 20 and the Code, require the 
production of a mailed Notice ("TABOR Notice") concerning certain ballot issues that may be 
submitted to the electors of the District; and  
 
WHEREAS, the TABOR Notices of several jurisdictions are to be sent as a package where 
jurisdictions overlap ("TABOR Notice Package"); and 
 
WHEREAS, the need to produce the TABOR Notice Package requires that there be 
County-wide coordination of the production and mailing of the TABOR Notice Package to 
effectuate the purposes of said constitutional section; and 
 
WHEREAS, the County Clerk and Recorder of Pueblo County and the District desire to 
set forth their respective responsibilities in the production and mailing of the TABOR Notice 
Package for elections conducted pursuant to this Intergovernmental Agreement; and 
 
WHEREAS, the County Clerk and the District have determined that it is in the best interests 
of the County, the District, and their respective inhabitants and landowners to cooperate and 
contract concerning the District’s election on November 3, 2026 upon the terms and conditions 
contained herein. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual covenants and promises 
contained herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows: 
 
I. GENERAL MATTERS: 
 
A. Purpose:   
 
 The purpose of this Agreement is to set forth the tasks to be completed by the 
County Clerk and the District in the conduct and financing of the November 3,

B.  Designated Election Official and Other Election Personnel: 
 
  Except as otherwise provided in this Section, the County Clerk shall act as the 
Designated Election Official for the conduct of the election for the District for all 
matters in the Code which require action by the Designated Election Official, 
except to the extent such action must be performed by the District Clerk or 
District’s Board of Elections pursuant to Charter requirements and except as 
otherwise provided in this Agreement. 
 
  The District designates Mandi Kirk, whose email address is 
mandi@fritschelaw.com as its "Contact Officer" who shall be the primary liaison

2026 through September 12, 2026 between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. 
 
C. Jurisdictional Limitation: 
  
 The Edison Fire Protection District encompasses only territory within Pueblo 
County. This Agreement shall be construed to apply only to Edison Fire Protection 
District territory within Pueblo County. 
 
D.   Nature of Election: 
  
 The parties agree that the County Clerk is assisting the District in the conduct of its 
own election and that the election is not a coordinated election. Rather the District 
is contracting with the County Clerk to perform part of its required duties under 
C.R.S. § 1-1-111-(2). Accordingly, the parties agree that article 13.5 of title 1, 
C.R.S., shall control the conduct of the election. To the extent provisions of the 
Uniform Election Code would apply to this election by default under C.R.S. § 1-
13.5-106(2), the District specifically acknowledges that the audit requirements of 
that Code shall not apply and County Clerk has no duty to perform them. 
 
E.  Scope of County Clerk’s Role: 
 
  Under this Agreement, the County Clerk is charged solely with assisting the District 
in the conduct of its election as specified in this Agreement. The County Clerk is 
not responsible for ensuring the election is lawful under state law or the District’s 
rules. The County Clerk shall not be liable for any issues or claims related to the 
legal effect of actions taken as part of the election. It is the District's responsibility 
to ensure that all steps necessary to effectuate the intent of the election are taken

the Designated Election Official on behalf of the District, the County Clerk agrees 
to act only as directed by the District or in a manner necessary to carry out the 
specific terms of this Agreement. 
 
II. DUTIES OF THE COUNTY CLERK: 
 
The County Clerk shall perform the following duties for the District in connection with the 
conduct of the election to the extent required: 
 
A.        Combine the text of the TABOR Notice produced by the District, if any, with those 
of other jurisdictions to produce the TABOR Notice Package.  The County Clerk 
may determine the order of the TABOR Notice submitted by the District and those 
of other jurisdictions to be included in the TABOR Notice Package, provided, 
however, that the materials supplied by the District shall be kept together as a group 
and in the order supplied by the District.  
 
B. Address the TABOR Notice package to "all registered voters" at each address of 
one or more active registered electors of the District.  Nothing herein shall preclude 
the County Clerk from sending the TABOR Notice or Notice Package to persons 
other than electors of the District if such sending arises from the County Clerk's 
efforts to mail the TABOR Notice Package at "least cost". 
 
C. Mail the TABOR Notice Package, as required by the Code. 
 
D. Provide the final layout of the mail ballots and official ballots to the District in order 
to permit the District to review, proofread, and approve before the County Clerk 
authorizes the printing of the ballots. Within four (4) hours of receipt of ballots 
from County Clerk, the District shall return the ballots following review and 
proofreading. 
 
E. Arrange for the printing, and have printed, all mail ballots and official ballots.  
 
F. Mail to all active registered electors eligible to vote in the District’s November 3, 
2026 election, at the last mailing address appearing in the County Clerk’s records, 
a mail ballot packet, as required by law.

number of qualified election judges to adequately serve the number of electors 
anticipated to vote in the election. The election judges for the District election may 
be the same persons as are employed for elections held by other jurisdictions on the 
same day and at the same VSPC(s) or Drop Off Locations.  
 
H. Conduct such formal training sessions for the election judges as deemed reasonably 
necessary by the County Clerk.

RESOLUTION NO.  
 
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
 
APPOINTING A MEMBER TO THE PUEBLO WEST ROADS SALES TAX 
OVERSIGHT COMMITTEE  
 
          WHEREAS, on November 8, 2022, the Pueblo West Metropolitan District voters 
approved Ballot Question 6C, which allows for a temporary 1% sales tax to fund roads 
maintenance in Pueblo West, as well as transfer responsibility of Pueblo West roads 
maintenance from the Pueblo West Metropolitan District to Pueblo County Government; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Pueblo West 2022 Ballot Question 6C also created an independent 
Oversight Committee composed of District residents to ensure accountability and annual review 
and report to the public on the use of the revenues collected; and 
 
          WHEREAS, by Resolution 24-175, the County and Pueblo West Metropolitan District 
adopted Bylaws for the Oversight Committee to provide further clarification to the members 
regarding their role and responsibilities; and  
 
          WHEREAS, the Oversight Committee shall consist of five (5) members, two (2) shall be 
appointed by the BOCC, two (2) shall be appointed by the BOD and one (1) shall be a joint 
appointment; and  
 
          WHEREAS, one (1) vacancy exists due to a resignation and Public Notices soliciting 
applications from persons interested in serving on the Pueblo West Road Sale Oversight 
Committee to fill said vacancy was published in The Pueblo Chieftain, on the Government 
Channel, Pueblo County Website and Social Media, and two (2) applications were submitted; 
and 
 
          WHEREAS, this Board desires to appoint _______ to the Pueblo West Road Sale Tax 
Oversight Committee to a term expiring June 30, 2027. 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of 
Pueblo County, Colorado, that ______ is hereby appointed as a County member to the Pueblo 
West Road Sale Tax Oversight Committee to fulfill the term expiring on June 30, 2027 and that 
the staff to the Board is authorized and directed to notify the aforementioned of their 
appointments by providing them with a copy of this Resolution. 
 
 
 
          PASSED AND ADOPTED this August 20, 2026, in Pueblo County, Colorado.
