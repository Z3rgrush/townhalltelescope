A RESOLUTION APPROVING MAP AMENDMENT MA-26-1 REZONING LOT 88 FOREST HILL 
FROM AN A2, MEDIUM AGRICULTURE ZONE DISTRICT TO AN A3, SMALL AGRICULTURE

Page 3 of 8 
 
ZONE DISTRICT AND LOT 95 FOREST HILL FROM AN A2, MEDIUM AGRICULTURE ZONE 
DISTRICT TO AN SR1, SUBURBAN RESIDENTIAL, LOW ZONE DISTRICT TO APPLY 
CONFORMING ZONE DISTRICTS TO EXISTING PLATTED LOTS. THE PROPERTIES ARE 
LEGALLY DESCRIBED LOT 88, FOREST HILL, PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO AND LOT 95, 
FOREST HILL, PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO. 
  
 WHEREAS, an application for a map amendment was filed on March 22, 2026, by Dennis Vallejos, Vallejos 
Revocable Trust, to rezone Lot 88 Forest Hill from the A2 Medium Agriculture zone district to the A3 Small 
Agriculture zone district and Lot 95 Forest Hill from the A2 Medium Agriculture to the SR1 Suburban 
Residential Low zone district to apply conforming zone districts to existing platted lots; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the properties are on Zorn Road and are legally described as Lot 88, Forest Hill, Pueblo County, 
Colorado and Lot 95, Forest Hill, Pueblo County, Colorado; and 
 
WHEREAS, zoning for the property was established on August 8, 1963, with an S-1, Public Use zone 
designation, which was amended to A-2, Agriculture (now A2 Medium Agriculture) via Map Amendment 
HMA-05-8 in 2005; and  
  
WHEREAS, the requested A3 and SR1 districts are necessary to enable the applicant to pursue a Lot 
Consolidation Vacation; and 
 
WHEREAS, a public meeting, preceded by proper public notice, was held by the Pueblo County 
Planning Commission on May 20, 2026, at the conclusion of which they voted to forward a recommendation 
of APPROVAL of Map Amendment MA-26-1 to the Board of county Commissioners. The recommendation 
included three (3) findings of fact based upon Chapter 17.06 Administration and Procedures, Section 17.06.070 
Zoning Procedures, Section 17.06.070(c) Zoning Map Amendment (Rezoning) of the Unified Development 
Code (UDC). Those findings are considered controlling factors in the Board of County Commissioners’ 
decision and are as follows: 
 
 FINDINGS 
  
a. The record demonstrates the proposed A3, Small Agriculture and SR1, Suburban Residential Low zone 
districts are compatible with the recommendations of the Pueblo Regional Comprehensive Plan for this property 
and its location; and 
 
b. The proposal is not expected to impact the health, safety, or general welfare of the public; and 
 
c. The subject property is not bounded by the City or any other municipality having land-use authority.  
  
 
WHEREAS, a public hearing was held by the Board of County Commissioners on June 11, 2026, and, 
at which time, all those present at the hearing who desired to be heard were heard and their testimony recorded; 
and 
  
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has reviewed and taken administrative notice of the 
three (3) findings of fact the Planning Commission considered in reaching its decision, the recommendation of 
the Planning Commission and the testimony, application, evidence, and documents submitted at the hearing, 
and the contents of the Planning Director’s file. 
  
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, 
Colorado, does hereby APPROVE Map Amendment MA-26-1 rezoning Lot 88 Forest Hill from the A2 
Medium Agriculture zone district to the A3 Small Agriculture zone district and Lot 95 Forest Hill from the A2 
Medium Agriculture to the SR1 Suburban Residential Low zone district to apply conforming zone districts to 
existing platted lots.

RESOLUTION NO. P&D 26-018 
  
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
OF PUEBLO COUNTY, COLORADO 
  
A RESOLUTION RATIFYING THE PUEBLO COUNTY BUILDING BOARD OF REVIEW BYLAWS 
AND RULES OF PROCEDURE 
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 25-304, the Board of County Commissioners established the Pueblo 
County Building Board of Review ("Board of Review") to hear appeals and other matters authorized under 
Pueblo County's adopted building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, fuel gas, and energy codes; and 
 
WHEREAS, Resolution No. 25-304 provides that the Board of Review shall adopt Bylaws and Rules of 
Procedure governing its organization, meetings, hearing procedures, ethical standards, and appeal processes, 
subject to review and approval by the Board of County Commissioners; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of Review considered and approved its Bylaws and Rules of Procedure on April 15, 
2026; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners finds that the Bylaws and Rules of Procedure establish 
appropriate standards to promote fairness, transparency, consistency, due process, and compliance with 
applicable laws and County-adopted codes. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of Pueblo County, 
Colorado, as follows: 
 
1. Ratification. 
The Pueblo County Building Board of Review Bylaws and Rules of Procedure, as approved by the Board of 
Review on April 15, 2026, and attached hereto as Exhibit A, are hereby ratified and approved. 
2. Authority to Amend. 
The Board of Review may recommend future amendments to its Bylaws and Rules of Procedure consistent with 
applicable law and County-adopted codes; provided, however, that any such amendments shall not become 
effective until ratified by the Board of County Commissioners. 
3. Severability. 
If any provision of this Resolution is determined to be invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such 
determination shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions of this Resolution. 
4. Effective Date. 
This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption. 
PASSED AND ADOPTED this 11th day of June 2026.

REQUEST 
 
The applicant requests a rezoning of a 0.55± parcel from the CC, Community Commercial, Zone 
District to the SR1, Suburban Residential, Low, Zone District.  The property contains an existing 
residence (constructed in 1929) and one (1) accessory structure.  The property, as a whole, is 
developed with a single-family residence within the CC, Community Commercial, Zone District. The 
zoning change will better reflect the use as residential and bring the parcel and use into 
compliance with the Pueblo County Unified Development Code (UDC). 
 
LOCATION 
 
The property is located at the northwest corner of the intersection of Gunnison Street and Santa Fe 
Dr. in the Blende area.  It is legally described as: Beginning at the Northwest corner of Lot 11, 
thence South 199.2 feet, thence East 75 feet, thence North 13.5 feet, thence East 52 feet, thence 
North to the North line of Lot 11, thence West 125 feet to the point of beginning, in Highland 
Gardens. 
 
OPPOSITION  
 
There was no known opposition at the time of this report. 
 
PLANNING COMMISSION ACTION 
 
Based upon staff’s review of all the information provided and acknowledging comments received,

BACKGROUND 
The Pueblo Rural Fire Protection District has requested $8,628.16 for a fire hydrant to be installed by the St. 
Charles Mesa Water District at Pueblo Rural Fire Station 2. The District states that the area currently lacks 
adequate water access and that the hydrant will support fire protection within the District's service area. 
The District recently acquired a 3,000-gallon water tender for areas with limited water supply and for mutual-aid 
response. According to the District, the tender will be filled at Station 2 and deployed throughout the Pueblo 
Rural Fire Protection District, including properties within the area where the impact fees were collected. The 
hydrant will also support safe operations at the District's regional live-burn training facility. 
ANALYSIS 
Section 17.04.050(h)(2)(iii)(d) of the Pueblo County Unified Development Code provides that fire protection 
impact fees are intended to provide fire protection within the fire service area in which the property paying the 
fee is located. Where a water distribution system exists or is planned for extension, the fees may be used for the 
purchase and associated installation costs of fire hydrants, but not for water-line extensions or hydrant 
operation, maintenance, or repair. 
The UDC further requires a hydrant disbursement request to include a map of existing and proposed hydrants, 
purchase and installation costs, an installation schedule, and a commitment of any additional funds needed to 
complete the project. The Board may disburse only fees collected from properties that can reasonably be served 
by the proposed hydrant. 
Page 37 of 97
The District has submitted a request letter, a proposed hydrant location map, and a Core & Main cost proposal. The cost 
proposal includes 200 feet of PVC pipe. Because the UDC prohibits using fire protection impact fees for the extension of 
water lines, the Pueblo County Department of Planning and Development confirmed with the District that the pipe is 
necessary for the associated installation of the hydrant and does not constitute an extension of the water-distribution

SUMMARY   
 
On February 5, 2025, The Department of Planning and Development received a complaint of a 
recreational vehicle being used for residential purposes, and that human waste had not been removed to 
a disposable area (Exhibit #1).  
 
On February 7, 2025, an on-site inspection was completed from the public right-of-way (Aspen Drive), in 
which the Land Use Inspector observed the violation. (Exhibit #2).  
 
On February 11, 2025, a Notice of Violation letter was sent to the property owner (address listed on the 
Pueblo County Assessor’s website) by certified mail outlining the violation(s) and the corrective and/or 
remedial alternatives available to address the issues. The certified Notice was eventually returned to the 
Department as undeliverable (Exhibit #3).  
 
On February 13, 2025, a copy of the Notice of Violation was sent to the Sheriff’s Office for service at the 
property location. The Notice was posted to the property by the Pueblo County Sheriff’s Office on March 
6, 2025 (Exhibit #4). 
 
On April 7, 2025, an (aerial) on-site inspection was completed from the public right-of-way in which the 
violation appeared to remain. A second Notice of Violation was served to the property owner on April 11, 
2025 by the Pueblo County Sheriff’s Office (Exhibits #5 and #6 respectively).
