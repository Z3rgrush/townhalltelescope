3. Staff Updates 
a. Q4 2026 budget report 
JG stated that the remaining balance in the Programing budget can be used for the CAPS 
December work session dinner meeting. Anything left over will default back to the Town’s 
general fund. The balance in the Public Art Acquisition budget is $2,300 which might be needed 
for contingency purposes per the Noblewins purchase or shipping costs.  Per BFFs remaining 
expenses, the licensing for the November film was paid for last year, so what we’re seeing on 
this report is mostly up to date.  “Pending items”  in the Public Art budget go before Council for 
approval next week. 
 
b. Mural festival admin and debrief 
JG and Mikaela had a post-festival meeting with Street Wise to discuss what worked and what 
didn’t, including the heavy ask of staff time and  the lengthy jurying process. JG suggested to Street 
Wise that we use the Town’s Café account in the future as it is more streamlined. Overall, it was an 
amazing event and working with Street Wise was a positive experience. The planning for 2028 
Mural Festival will start as early as February 2027. All feedback from the community has been 
positive. 
Of note, repairs to the A.J. Davis mural were delayed but the work has now been completed.  
 

General Updates, Debriefing, Upcoming Events, Other  
a. Updates 
i. Marshall Fire Remembrance Project 
Staff and the subcommittee recently had a very positive overview meeting with leading 
fundraising consultant candidate, “Make Philanthropy Work.” Pending Council 
approval, they will immediately begin building the fundraising plan. Also pending 
Council approval, Colorado Gives will be the Town’s fiscal partner and fundraising 
repository.  Louisville will be contributing $25,000 towards the Project, and the final 
design may be modified slightly to be more inclusive of the entire impacted region. 
The 5-year Marshall Fire Remembrance will be on Friday December 18th.  A 
presentation of the project will be done and information about the fundraising will be 
available. 
 
ii. Artist Spotlight slideshow exhibits 
Andrew shared that there were 11 entries and due to the overall great quality, all were 
accepted. The exhibits will start in January with two screens in the Community Center 
and one at the Civic Space on three-month rotations throughout 2027.  A 
congratulatory email was sent to each applicant which also let them know their exhibit 
time slot.  Andrew, Ardyce and JG will be working on turning the submissions into 
videos.

4. Work Plan Check-In 
a. ARTery 
The proposed use of the 2026 budget for ARTery purchases will be presented to the Council on 
Monday, October  26th.  Claire reported the need to update the plan because most of the utility 
boxes are not Town owned so instead  an additional Noblewins swing-bench will be purchased.  
One bench will be installed at Circle Park. The locations of the other benches have yet to be 
determined. Although we are buying the benches this year, fabrication and shipping could take 
up to six months. JG is assisting with getting maps of irrigation and other light boxes that are 
Town owned and could be wrapped in the future, as there are 13 light boxes alone that are 
available. JG will also inquire about the possibility of wrapping Xcel utility boxes as there is a 
possibility that Greeley was granted permission. 
 
5. Discussion Items 
a. 2027 Work Plan 
Ideas discussed: 
• Xochi suggested a visiting poet to help find voice in writing, and have workshops about 
Colorado.  Chris suggested a high school Poet Laureate program. Xochi will head a working 
group. 
• Liza proposed having stickers 3-4 sticker designs made from the characters in the murals to 
hand out at town events. She also suggested having a graffiti artist event in the inner two 
tunnels near Autrey Park. The idea included inviting graffiti artists to create art in the 
tunnels as part of an organized event, and/or to utilize the tunnels by having a mural artist 
teach the mural painting process. JG and Leslie stated that the inner tunnels are not 
intended for public access and could be a liability, however the outer two tunnels could be 
utilized. A conversation ensued about how underpass lighting has been improved after 
murals go in  but Leslie said the there’s actually a lighting improvement plan scheduled for 
the Autrey tunnels.  
• The 2028 Mural Festival planning will begin in 2027 
• Adding art in the new pool area such as possibly adding a mosaic wall or some other art 
form. 
• Sherry mentioned that ceramic totem workshops could be added to the 2027 plan in order 
to complete the totem pole project from prior years. It would be a community involved 
projects to contribute to the ARTery. 
• Film series, including education. Stephanie suggested increasing the budget and expanding 
the Film portion, to compliment the interest in Sundance; however, Chris pointed out that 
he cannot cover all of that programming by himself and JG reminded the group that the 
programming budget is $5,000 and cannot be increased. The other CAPS budget is strictly 
for public art acquisitions.  
• Providing more merch items for the town to sell. CAPS can only do it as give away.  
Discussed the formation of a 501(c)(3) to bring money into the town and CAPS budget.  
• Define the ARTery route and commission and/or purchase new artworks.  Start earlier in 
the year with a call-for-art or develop a catalog of existing completed artworks which could 
be purchased outright.  
• Musical performance by the Boulder Symphony (or other symphony).  
• Potential art for the new RCP/Coal Creek roundabout. 
• Find new location to install the Coyote Hawk sculpture. 
 
Points made: 
• JG iterated that CAPS should have a goal for all major purchases to be completed by the  
end of September in 2027 since there are far less Council meetings in the fourth quarter of 
any given year.  
• Andrew mentioned that the town should always discuss the incremental cost of adding art 
to any project. 
• Stephanie does not feel that we are providing the enrichment events that the community 
wants. 
• CAPS can be in the position to recommend programs to the Town to provide at the 
Community Center, Civic Space or park.  
• The remaining budget will go to the ARTery. 
• Andrew will put the ideas into a plan for review and finalization next month. 
• The December meeting will be a working session.
