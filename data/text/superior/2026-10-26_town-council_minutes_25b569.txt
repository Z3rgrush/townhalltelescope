EXHIBIT A 
SCOPE OF SERVICES 
COUNTY WILL: 
1. Conduct relevant background research into affordable housing development, review codes, 
policies and best management practices, including: 
• Existing conditions for Town staff review processes, Code requirements and development 
standards; and 
• Peer municipal codes from around the region.   
2. Solicit feedback about the process and potential impact through stakeholder engagement with 
local developers, affordable housing professionals and others as mutually agreed upon with 
Town staff.   
3. Develop an expedited application process and operational guide for staff, including without 
limitation:   
• Pre-application protocol; 
• Review of milestones with detailed timelines for compliance; 
• Options for coordinating extenuating circumstances at individual properties; and 
• Outcomes for application approvals and withdrawals.   
4. Assist Town staff in developing a draft ordinance that implements Prop 123 (the "Ordinance") 
and considering its applicability and compliance with Prop 123 legislation, including without 
limitation:   
• Types of applications and development projects to which the Ordinance applies; and 
• Circumstances under which the Ordinance applies.   
5. Coordinate with staff to assemble presentations for Planning Commission and Town Council to 
provide feedback.   
6. Deliver the following items to the Town  within any timeframes reasonably established by the 
Town:   
• Report on existing conditions, best management practices and peer-code studies;   
• Report on stakeholder engagement outcomes;   
• Prop 123 Affordable Housing Fast Track Review Process and Policy Guide (the "Prop 123 
Guide"); and   
• Two Planning Commission and two Town Council presentations, with one to present initial 
findings and discuss options for developing the Prop 123 Guide and one to present the draft 
final Prop 123 Guide for review and approval.   
 
 
TOWN OF SUPERIOR 
RESOLUTION NO. R-91 
SERIES 2026 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF SUPERIOR 
APPROVING AN INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT WITH BOULDER 
COUNTY FOR ASSISTANCE IN THE ADOPTION OF A STREAMLINED 
(FASTTRACK)PROCESS FOR AFFORDABLE HOUSING DEVELOPMENT TO 
IMPLEMENT PROPOSITION 123 
WHEREAS, Boulder County is the Grantee under that certain 
State of Colorado Intergovernmental Grant Agreement effective as 
of June 21, 2024 (“State Grant”), pursuant to which the State 
awarded Grant Funds to the County to be used in connection with a 
multijurisdictional effort to coordinate a countywide approach to 
streamline land use development codes and permitting processes to 
expedite review of affordable housing projects, create a countywide 
dashboard of affordable housing data and Prop 123 implementation, 
and other planning efforts that generally advance affordable 
housing goals across Boulder County; 
WHEREAS, Boulder County, has agreed to use a portion of these 
Grant Funds to assist the Town in the implementation of a 
streamlined development (Fast Track) process for affordable housing 
projects as described in the Intergovernmental Agreement; and 
WHEREAS, the total cost to the Town for the Project in 2027 
is $10,000.00.  
 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN 
OF SUPERIOR, COLORADO, as follows: 
 
Section 1.  The Town Council hereby approves the 
Intergovernmental Agreement with Boulder County in substantially 
the form attached hereto. Upon such approval, the Mayor is 
authorized to execute the Amendment on behalf of the Town. 
 
ADOPTED this 26th day of October, 2026.
