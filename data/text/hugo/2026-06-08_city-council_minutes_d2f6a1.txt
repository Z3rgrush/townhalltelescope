 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
June 8, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on June 8, 2026, at 
6:02 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Piper, Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen , Trustee Ehlers  and 
Trustee Mohan. Trustee Boyd arrived via Zoom at 6:12 P.M., left Zoom at 6:29 P.M. and arrived in person at 
7:10P.M. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Valinda Eberle, Chris Rosado Sr., Troy McCue, and via Zoom Stephanie Zwick and Koby 
Anderson. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Piper and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the agenda.  THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

ACTION ITEMS: 
Millan Asphalt & Crushing LLC and Rock ’M Inc. are available by telephone for additional questions regarding 
their submittals. GMS, our Engineer of Record, suggested that we only repair potholes for now and have testing 
done to the streets to determine roadway material, pavement thickness and any underlying structural issues of our 
streets to determine a proper maintenance plan. A motion was made by Trustee Mohan and a second was made 
by Trustee Thelen to approve the Millan Asphalt & Crushing LLC Pothole proposal. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
Koby Anderson with TextMyGov answered questions that texts are counted both ways, coming into the town and 
going out. Unused texts roll over and additional texts can be purchased at cost. He was able to discount the set-
up fee from $1,000 to $500 . It is a locked in 3-year contract. A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a 
second was made by Trustee  Ehlers to approve the TextMyGov proposal. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
The color options to paint Town Hall were not available.

07, a Resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Establishing Expedited Review Policies 
for Affordable Housing Projects. Those voting in favor were Trustee Boyd, Trustee Ehlers, Trustee Emmerling,

STAFF REPORTS: 
For the Maintenance Supervisor’s report, Trustee Thelen asked if we were still removing weeds from the st reet 
and using the street sweeper. It was reported that we continue to work on both as time allows. 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster reviewed the Clerk Administrator report and reported that if we receive a call that 
someone is violating water restrictions she calls them to inform them of the restrictions, water usage has not 
decreased by much and we will calculate a projected average at the end of June , with the projected average staff 
can make recommendations for water restrictions, the water lost at the water tower leak does count our total usage, 
the geotechnical report came back to include using the roto mill CDOT gave the Town and the subcontractor is 
rebidding using that material, Mark Foxworthy can be our ORC while he lives in a different state, we are waiting 
on approvals to move forward with the sidewalk project, we are working through expectations and suggestions  
from the Judge and Attorney while rebuilding our code enforcement program.
