 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS  
Hugo, Colorado 
January 12, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on January 12, 2026, 
at 6:00 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Ehlers, Trustee Piper, Trustee Thelen, and 
Trustee Messer. Trustee Mohan is absent. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board 
present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster and Maintenance Supervisor Kirby. 
 
Guests present were Troy McCue, Kevin Stansbury, and Audrey Sayles and via Zoom Stephanie Zwick. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Emmerling to approve the agenda.  
THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
GUEST REPORTS:  
Kevin Stansbury  with Lincoln Community Health presented the monthly report highlighting  consulting your 
doctor for vaccines you may need, Senator Hickenlooper’s visit next week, the Rural Health Transformation 
Program, the Board Spotlight, new billing and extended payment options, and new specialists for urology and 
gynecology. 
Audrey Sayles with Some Girls and a Mural discussed possibilities for a mural on Town Hall. 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Rural 
Philanthropy Days July 8-10, 2026, Opportunity Zone 2.0, affordable housing with a possible “mini-center” for 
child care, and shout-out Facebooks posts for The Main Boutique and Waite Custom Saddles.

PUBLIC HEARING: Smith Minor Subdivision Replat Application 
Mayor Emmerling opened the Public Hearing for the Smith Minor Subdivision Repalt Application at 6:52 P.M. 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster presented the application. Discussion was had on the use of the property and 
where water, where sewer lines were located and if the application met the criteria under the Hugo Land 
Development Code. 
Mayor Emmerling asked for public comment. There were no public comments. 
The public comment portion of the public hearing closed at 7:05P.M. 
Mayor Emmerling asked for Board comment. There were no Board comments. 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Thelen to deny the Smith Minor 
Subdivision Replat application request based on a finding that the application does not meet the requirement of 
the review criteria under section 404 of the Hugo Land Development Code . Those voting in favor were Trustee 
Emmerling, Trustee Piper and Trustee Thelen. Those voting opposed were Trustee Messer and Trustee Ehlers.  
THE MOTION CARRIED.  
The Public Hearing was closed at 7:08 P.M. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Messer to approve the December 8, 
2025 minutes. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Piper to accept the December 
financial report. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.
