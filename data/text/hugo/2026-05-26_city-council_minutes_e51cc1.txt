 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
May 26, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on May 11, 2026, at 
6:00 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Piper, Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen , and Trustee Mohan. 
Trustee Boyd was on Zoom. Trustee Ehlers was absent. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of 
the Board present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
No guests were present. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the agenda with 
the amendment of removing item 6.3. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
ACTION ITEMS: 
A motion was made by Trustee Thelen and a second was made by Trustee Emmerling to table the Pothole and 
Crack Sealing RFP to gather more information. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Mohan and a second was made by Trustee Boyd to approve Ordinance 300, An 
Ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Repealing and Replacing Ordinance 298 
Authorizing Restrictions on Water Usage. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
MAYOR AND TRUSTEE REPORTS: 
Trustee Mohan would like the playground equipment to be on the agenda at the June 8 Regular Meeting. 
 
The Meeting was adjourned.
