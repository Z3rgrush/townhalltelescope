 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
July 20, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on July 20, 2026, at 
6:05 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Piper, Trustee Messer, Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen , and 
Trustee Mohan. Trustee Boyd was on Zoom. Trustee Ehlers was absent . Mayor Emmerling declared that there 
was a quorum of the Board present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Tom Lee. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Mohan and a second was made by Trustee Emmerling to approve the agenda.  
THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
Tom Lee spoke about not changing the water restrictions to water three days in a row. The soil here packs and we 
are already fighting the heat and wind. 
 
ACTION ITEMS: 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Thelen to approve Ordinance 302, 
An Emergency Ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Amending the Permitted 
Hours and Days for Watering During a Level One Restricted Water Usage Period . Those voting in favor were 
Trustee Emmerling. Those voting opposed were Trustee Boyd, Trustee Thelen, Trustee Piper, Trustee Ehlers, and 
Trustee Mohan. THE MOTION FAILED. 
 
 
MAYOR AND TRUSTEE REPORTS: 
Trustee Emmerling believes we should post the water usage after we have July’s reading. 
Trustee Piper disagrees with Trustee Emmerling.
