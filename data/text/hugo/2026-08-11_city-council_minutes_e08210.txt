 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
August 11, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on August 11, 2026, 
at 6:03 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call: Trustee Boyd, Trustee Piper, Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen, Trustee 
Ehlers and Trustee Mohan. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board present for the 
transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Zach Shoenfeld, Kevin Stansbury, Troy McCue, Carl Dutro, Terry Graham, Derek Isom, 
Stacey Pythian, Brian Parmer, April Hollowell, and via Zoom Stephanie Zwick, Scott Barber, Valinda Eberley, 
Victor Martinez, Peter Huisman and Scott Thomas. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the agenda with 
the addition of 7.9 GMS CDBG Draw Dated August 2026. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
Scott Thomas and Peter Huisman with Colorado Rural Water Association (CRWA) reviewed the state Backflow 
Prevention and Cross-Connection Control (BPCCC) Program and compliance and the Town’s violations on the 
2025 Sanitary Survey in regard to  the BPCCC Program. Resurveying was  done because the sanitary survey  
identified many businesses were not inventoried. They stated that the program has been in effect since 2016 and 
the Town of Hugo has not been properly following the rule as is defined in the 2025 Sanitary Survey. The Town’s 
Operator of Res ponsible Charge (ORC) is responsible for implementation of the BPCCC Program. Scott will 
present the rate study at the September meeting. 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
Stacey Pythian stated that she has the backflow test scheduled for tomorrow for her building. She is still working 
on having the device installed at the RV Park. It will be more difficult. 
Carl Dutro suggested that when we do water restrictions in the future, we give businesses and residents with 
sprinkler systems earlier watering times. The evaporation rate is lower early morning. He foresees water 
restrictions ongoing for years. 
Terry Graham suggested we include more information in the Backflow letters.
