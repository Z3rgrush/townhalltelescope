 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
October 13, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on October 13, 2026, 
at 6:00 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call: Trustee Piper, Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen, Trustee Ehlers and 
Trustee Mohan. Trustee Boyd arrived via Zoom at 6:30P.M., left Zoom at 6:29 P.M. and arrived in person at 
7:02P.M. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Destiny Aberle, Sheri Gaskins, Michael Gaskins, Chris Rosado Sr., Kevin Stansbury, Troy 
McCue, Dawn Morton, Dannah Koeniger, Lucas Hopper and via Zoom Becky Osterwald, Stephanie Zwick, Phil 
Howerton, Bill Hall and Scott Thomas. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the agenda. 
THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
GUEST REPORTS: 
Kevin Stansbury with Lincoln Community Health presented the monthly report highlighting the Denim & 
Diamonds Gala had nearly 400 guests, Lincoln Health was awarded a grant through Colorado’s Rural Health 
Transformation Program, these monies cannot be used for new capital projects and will be used for equipment 
and technology, the Rural Healthcare Fact of the Month is about Breast Cancer Awareness Month, flu shots are 
recommended for flu prevention, Lincoln Health applied for a DOLA Housing Grant for planning the 
infrastructure to Lincoln Health’s housing. 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Hugo Main 
Street meeting on October 21, Opportunity Zone 2.0 of which part of Lincoln County is in the map, waiting on 
the final capital stack from contractors on possible new housing, they are working on new ventures with a few 
Hugo businesses and one for expansion, Hugo UP Roundhouse will host a memorial service for Chris Fox on 
October 21, SBDC is still in transition, Hugo Fall Festival had a successful Strutt Your Mutt event, the Wonder 
View Tower is closed for the season, Sentinel Clean Power, Arriba Wind Farm and Ebba Solar continue to move 
forward, Big Ben’s RV in Arriba opened The Shack as a new community space and reviewed the Value Ticker. 
Sheri Gaskins with the Lincoln County Historical Society reviewed the year of events at the Hedlund House 
highlighting the Memorial Day Ice Cream Social, 4th of July Tom Sawyer, Huck Finn and Becky Thatcher Day, 
Homecoming event and upcoming Holly Jolly Hedlund Holiday, discussed the new volunteers this year, reported 
on a project that with the Estate of Maye Gene Lee that with result in donations to the Lincoln County Historical 
Society, they have received an $8,000 donation form the descendants of John and Bessie Shaw, the Historical 
Society does not carry insurance on their collection, they had 92 visitors in 2025 and 89 so far in 2026, asked for 
the Museum sign to be moved, asked if the Town staff to paint the buildings,  and Destiny Aberle and Sheri 
Gaskins attended Rural Philanthropy days to connect with organizations that may be able to support the Historical 
Society. 
Scott Thomas with Colorado Rural Water Association (CRWA) presented the Rate Study. He pointed out that the 
Town is currently spending out of savings to service their debt in both the Water Enterprise and Sewer Enterprise. 
It is estimated that a 40% increase to rates in the Water Enterprise and a 28% increase to rates in the Sewer 
Enterprise, as well as annual rate increases for inflation using the Consumer Price Index suggested form DOLA, 
would be needed to cover operational costs and debt service for current and future projects. 
 
ACTION ITEMS:

GMS Update  
A Solomon Addition/Roundhouse Estates infrastructure presentation was given with options on how to repair the 
existing infrastructure and build out of future infrastructure. 
Water restrictions helped conserve water for the Town. We are on track to stay under our allotted shares. 
The rate increase for the CDBG Sewer Project was reduced after the 2025 audit was factored in. The loan closing 
will happen in the next few months. 
The Water System Improvements Project bid will be advertised in December with construction to begin in the 
spring. 
The Drainage Study can be broken into three sections. It is suggested to do all three sections together to save on 
costs. 
 
 
GMS received the information provided by the Town after the pothole patching. More information will be needed 
throughout in regard to pavement depth and materials. 
We are working with the ORC to find an inspector for the overflow tank at the main lift station. The inspection 
report will enable us to recommend repairs and estimated costs. 
GMS would like for a Geotechnical Engineer to confirm the stability of the hill the Town used to pull sand from 
as it relates to the electric poles at the top of the hill.

the Town Of Hugo, County of Lincoln, State of Colorado, Beginning January 1, 2027, Per Month, with an Open 
Water Account with corrections. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.
