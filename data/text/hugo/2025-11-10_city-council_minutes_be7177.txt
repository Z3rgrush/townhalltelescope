 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
November 10, 2025, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on November 10, 
2025, at 6:00 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in 
unison. The following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Ehlers, Trustee Piper, Trustee 
Thelen, Trustee Mohan, and Trustee Messer. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board 
present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Troy McCue, Chris Rosado, Sr., John LaBourde, Rachel Carson, and via Zoom Ron Farmer, 
Andrea Buller, and Stephanie Zwick. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the agenda.  THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
2024 AUDIT PRESENTATION: 
Ron Farmer with rfarmer, LLC presented the 2024 audit. 
A motion was made by Trustee Thelen and a second was made by Trustee Messer to accept the 2024 audit. THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
GUEST REPORTS: 
Rachel Carson with Lincoln Community Health presented the monthly report highlighting the new orthopedic 
specialist, the Board Spotlight featuring Dave Stone, An Attitude of Gratitude LCH is celebrating, flu shots, covid 
shots and gave a Rural Futures overview. 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Rural 
Philanthropy Dyas July 8-10, 2026, Opportunity Zone 2.0, affordable housing with a possible “mini-center” for 
child care and the possibility of an H&R Block office in Hugo. 
 
ACTION ITEMS: 
A motion was made by Trustee Thelen and a second was made by Trustee Emmerling to approve the Community 
Preservation Specialist, Inc 2026 contract.  Those voting in favor were Trustee Thelen and Trustee Ehlers. Those 
voting opposed were Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Mohan, Trustee Messer and Trustee Piper. THE MOTION 
FAILED. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Messer to reopen the matured 
Sewer Fund 6 Month CD at First National Bank of Hugo -Limon for 6 months. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Emmerling to reopen the matured 
Sewer Fund Debt Reserve 6 Month CD at First National Bank of Hugo -Limon for 6 months. THE MOTION 
CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to reopen the matured Water 
Fund 6 Month CD at First National Bank of Hugo -Limon for  6 months. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Messer to reopen the matured Cemetery 
Fund 6 Month CD at First National Bank of Hugo -Limon for 6 months. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Thelen to reopen the matured Sales & 
Use Tax Fund 24 Month CD at First National Bank of Hugo -Limon for 6 months. THE MOTION CARRIED 
UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Messer to approve GMS, Inc. moving 
forward with the collection system replacement between 5 th St and 6 th St and between 4 th St and 5 th St. THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Thelen to approve of GMS, Inc. to 
conduct a study of the Roundhouse Estates and in conducting a tap fee study for the Town . THE MOTION 
CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Thelen and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the donation of a 
burial space to the Lincoln County Coroner for use in the burial of unclaimed remains and waiving burial fees for 
that burial space with no other fees waived at this time. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve October 14, 2025, 
minutes with corrections. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
OLD BUSINESS: 
We have a community meeting tomorrow night to possibly add a 1% sales tax for the pool and Culture and 
Recreation. We will not look further into combined elections with Lincoln County.  
 
STAFF REPORTS: 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster reviewed the Clerk Administrator report. 
 
MAYOR AND TRUSTEE REPORTS: 
Trustee Piper discussed that we need to discuss a dog catcher during the next budget meeting. 
 
The Meeting was adjourned.
