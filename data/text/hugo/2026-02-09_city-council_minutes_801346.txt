 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
February 9, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on February 9, 2026, 
at 6:00 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call: Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Ehlers, Trustee Piper, Trustee Thelen, Trustee 
Messer, and Trustee Mohan. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board present for the 
transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Tiffany Stewart, Zach Schoenfeld, Rachel Carson, and Troy McCue, and via Zoom Stephanie 
Zwick, Mark Leasure, and Audrey Sayles. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Piper and a second was made by Trustee Messer to approve the agenda with the 
addition of 6.2 GMS Draw Dated February 6, 2026. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to approve the Korinek 
Konstruction Change Order Estimate for paving 5 th Avenue option 4.5” HMA over 8” Base Course. THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
GUEST REPORTS: 
Rachel Carson with Lincoln Community Health presented the monthly report highlighting  the importance of 
getting lab work done, the state will decide how to disperse the Rural Health Transformation Program money, the 
Board Spotlight, Kevin is in DC working on a bill going in front of the legislature this week,  billing efforts to 
improve accuracy and communication, there is a new payment program called HELP and extended payment 
options, and exploring cash payment options for patients without health insurance. 
Audrey Sayles with Some Girls and a Mural reviewed the mural options for Town Hall and gave possible grant 
funding sources. 
Zach Schoenfeld with the Hugo Housing Authority spoke about their plumbing repair needs. They are looking at 
grant funding and may need the Town to partner with as a pass through. 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Rural 
Philanthropy Days July 8-10, 2026, Opportunity Zone 2.0, daycare meeting with Genoa-Hugo School, the new 
Stockman’s Bank operations, and the LCEDC Annual Meeting is April 22 at the Fairgrounds. 
 
ACTION ITEMS: 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Ehlers to approve the January 6, 2026 
minutes. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Thelen to accept the December 
financial report. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
OLD BUSINESS: 
We are still looking for playground equipment.  
We have a meeting with a few community members that are interested in being on the Hugo Main Street Board  
on Tuesday, February 10. 
 
STAFF REPORTS: 
Trustee Ehlers thanked staff for the Maintenance Supervisor’s report stating that this is what we have been looking 
for. Clerk Administrator Lancaster added that the sensor for the backup generat or at the Solomon addition lift 
station had been installed. Trustees Ehlers stated that KC Electric will continue to mow the old substation area. 
Mayor Emmerling wanted to make sure the pot hole at 2nd Ave and 8th St is on our list. 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster reviewed the Clerk Administrator report and stated that all but 4 of the tires have 
been removed from  the town property by Ha ss Recycling, Rocky Mountain Recycling will be bringing out a 
dumpster soon, Richard is researching a SIPA grant to see if we can get one for codification of our ordinances, 
we are looking into what equipment can qualify for a $10,000 CDPE grant, our Attorney is reviewing the contract 
for the Photo Radar system, and reviewed the draft Part-Time Code Enforcement job description. 
 
MAYOR AND TRUSTEE REPORTS: 
Mayor Emmerling discussed bringing the ordinances that Trustees want changed starting at the March meeting 
for discussion and that at the daycare meeting Mr. Melendrez mentioned a possible donation of land from the 
Town to the School.  
 
 
Trustee Emmerling was unaware the Wildfire Resiliency Code would affect us until right before it needed to be 
passed and Farrel is spelled incorrectly on the Clerk Administrator Report. 
Trustee Thelen commented that the activity at Farrel-Hines park is picking up. There were even people playing 
tennis. 
 
The Meeting was adjourned.
