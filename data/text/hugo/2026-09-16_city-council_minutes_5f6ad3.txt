 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
September 16, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on August 11, 2026, 
at 6:01 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call:  Trustee Boyd, Trustee Piper , Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Thelen  and 
Trustee Mohan. Trustee Ehlers was absent Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board 
present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were  Clerk Administrator Lancaster and Public Works Maintenance Supervisor 
Kirby. 
 
Guests present were Peter Botsonis, Troy McCue, and via Zoom Stephanie Zwick, Chris Cobbley, Michael Wolfe, 
Victor Martinez, Fred Dunning and Brad Schlepp. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Emmerling and a second was made by Trustee Piper to approve the agenda. THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
GUEST REPORTS: 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Hugo Main 
Street meeting on September 21, Opportunity Zone 2.0 of which part of Lincoln County is in the map, waiting on 
numbers from contractors on possible new housing, there is new funding through DOLA Housing for demo, they 
are working on new ventures with a few Hugo businesses, the Hugo RP Roundhouse is working on bat mitigation, 
SBDC is still in transition, Hugo Fall Festival is September 26 starting with Strutt Your Mutt at 7:00am, the 
Wonder View Tower has a consistent flow of traffic, Sentinel Clean Power, Arriba Wind Farm and Ebba Solar 
continue to move forward, and reviewed the Value Ticker.

OLD BUSINESS: 
We have a steering committee meeting with Colorado Main Street on September 21 at 5pm. At this meeting the 
steering committee will discuss Bylaws, Board Roles and the Hugo Main Street Strategic Priorities. Anyone is 
welcome to attend. 
We will apply for a DOLA Division of Housing Local Planning Capacity Grant to hire a consultant with 
abandoned and dilapidated building ordinances. We will work with Willdan on other ordinance updates. 
Ellie is working on the Rules of Order and Procedure and Code of Ethics revision. 
The Strategic Plan draft will be reviewed more at the first budget workshop. 
 
STAFF REPORTS: 
There were no comments on the Maintenance Supervisor’s report.  
Trustee Thelen asked about the smell from the sewer lagoons. It was explained that depending on the wind 
direction, odors from the lagoons may carry into town. It was stated that a resident did call Town Hall and ask 
about the odor. 
Trustee Piper asked about the survey flags at the Railroad Park. It was explained those were there to flag our 
sprinkler heads for aeration. We seeded and hand watered after aeration. 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster reviewed the Clerk Administrator report and noted that we need to do her review, 
the CML District Meeting is Tuesday and we will leave Town Hall around 4:00pm, discussed lower water usage 
for September and stated that we have converted five common water shares to two preferred shares for the 2027 
fiscal year and will look to lease one preferred share for 2027 which would give us three total preferred shares for 
2027, asked everyone to review the draft rate increase ordinance, and discussed TextMyGov user numbers. 
Trustee Thelen stated that she is very proud of Clerk Administrator Lancaster for completing her CMCA 
Certification and completing her training. 
 
MAYOR AND TRUSTEE REPORTS: 
Mayor Emmerling reminded everyone of the CML District Meeting Tuesday, stated that there would be a grand 
opening for the new Farrel Hines Park playground equipment Friday, October 2 from 1-2:30pm with Stockmens 
Bank providing ice cream and drinks and asked what dates were best for a budget workshop. 
Trustee Thelen thanked all Town staff for their hard work. 
 
The Meeting was adjourned.
