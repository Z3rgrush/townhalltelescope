 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
March 9, 2026, 6:00 PM 
Hugo Town Hall Board Room 
 
The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on March 9, 2026, at 
6:04 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in unison. The 
following Trustees answered roll call: Trustee Ehlers, Trustee Piper, Trustee Messer, and Trustee Mohan. Trustee 
Emmerling was on Zoom. Trustee Thelen was absent. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the 
Board present for the transaction of business.  
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
Guests present were Kevin Stansbury, Scott Poss, Mark Allis , Troy McCue, and Valinda Eberle and via Zoom 
Stephanie Zwick. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Piper and a second was made by Trustee Messer to approve the agenda . THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
GUEST REPORTS: 
Kevin Stansbury  with Lincoln Community Health presented the monthly report highlighting  that respiratory 
illness is on the rise, thanking the community for the response at the Lincoln C ounty Courthouse, there is a new 
payment program called HELP Financing, there are some possible federal policy changes that may help Lincoln 
Health, the Provider Spotlight, a new Urology Specialist will be coming in April and a Urogynecology Specialist 
will be here soon. 
Scott Poss and Mark Allis presented the proposed plan for the new firehouse . They reviewed the current and 
future property maps. 
Troy McCue with Lincoln County Economic Development presented the monthly report highlighting Rural 
Philanthropy Days July 8-10, 2026, Opportunity Zone 2.0 of which part of Lincoln County is in the map, possible 
housing by the old courthouse property, he will be helping facilitate ribbon cuttings with the Limon Chamber of 
Commerce and new Hugo Businesses, the World Wonder View Grand Opening will be May 1, there is a Genoa 
Rail Transfer in planning and the LCEDC Annual Meeting is April 22 at the Fairgrounds. 
 
ACTION ITEMS: 
PUBLIC HEARING: Smith Minor Subdivision Replat Minor Application  
Mayor Emmerling opened the Public Hearing for the Smith Minor Subdivision Replat Application at 6:50P.M. 
Clerk Administrator Lancaster reported that the notice of hearing was posted and no comments from the public 
were received. 
Mayor Emmerling asked for public comment. There were no public comments. 
The public comment portion of the public hearing closed at 6:52P.M. 
A motion was made by Trustee Ehlers and a second was made by Trustee Messer to approve the Smith Minor 
Subdivision Replat Application request based on a finding that it meets the requirements of the review criteria 
under Section 404 of the Hugo Land Development Code. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.  
The public hearing closed at 6:56P.M. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Piper to table Ordinance 295, a n 
Ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Adopting by Reference the 2025 Wildfire 
Resiliency Code. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Mohan and a second was made by Trustee Piper to approve Ordinance 296, an 
Ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Establishing an Automated Vehicle 
Identification System. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Piper and a second was made by Trustee Ehlers to approve Resolution 2026-04, 
a Resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hugo, Colorado Approving an Agreement with DacraTech, 
LLC for Speeding Enforcer System Services. Those voting in favor were Trustee Messer, Trustee Ehlers, Trustee 
Piper and Trustee Mohan. Those voting opposed were Trustee Emmerling. THE MOTION CARRIED. 
 
We are waiting for quotes on playground equipment. We are exploring grants and other funding sources. Trustee
