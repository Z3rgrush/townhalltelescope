RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
Hugo, Colorado 
November 17, 2025, 6:00 PM 
Special Meeting of the Board 
Hugo Board Room 
 
 
The special meeting of the Board of Trustees, Town of Hugo, Colorado was called to order on November 17, 
2025, at 6:01 P.M. by Mayor Emmerling. The Pledge of Allegiance to the United States Flag was recited in 
unison. The following Trustees answered roll call: Trustee Emmerling, Trustee Piper, Trustee Messer, and Trustee 
Mohan. Trustee Ehlers was absent. Mayor Emmerling declared that there was a quorum of the Board present for 
the transaction of business. 
 
No guests were present. 
 
Staff present at this meeting were Clerk Administrator Lancaster. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Mohan to adopt the agenda.  THE 
MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
A motion was made by Trustee Messer and a second was made by Trustee Ehlers to deny adding the Hugo Fire 
Department driveway back into the CDOT Sidewalk Project. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY. 
 
 
The meeting was adjourned.
