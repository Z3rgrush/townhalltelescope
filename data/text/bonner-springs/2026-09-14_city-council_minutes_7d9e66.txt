WORKSHOP - 7:00 P.M. 
Council Present:  Mayor Stephens, Councilmembers Shannon, Gurley, Wood, Blanks, Long and Reeves.

Council Present:  Mayor Stephens, Councilmembers Shannon, Gurley, Wood, Blanks, Long and Reeves.  
Councilmembers Kipp and McMahan were absent.

PRESENTATIONS – 
 
Proclamation - National POW/MIA Recognition Day - Mayor Stephens presented a proclamation to members of 
the VFW recognizing National POW/MIA Recognition Day.

Small Business Administration Office of Disaster Recovery, offered to share information about a disaster

storms.

presented a guitar signed by Will Mosley, as a thank you for the City’s assistance and support with Tiblow Days 
this year.

OLD BUSINESS – None Presented 
NEW BUSINESS -   
1. Public Hearing for 2027 Budget and 2026 Budget Amendments –  
Shannon moved and Blanks seconded to open a Public Hearing for the 2027 Budget and the 2026 Budget 
Amendments beginning at 7:47 p.m. Unanimous approval. 
Gurley moved and Shannon seconded to close the public hearing at 8:10 p.m. Unanimous approval. 
 
2. Approve 2027 Budget and 2026 Budget Amendments - Blanks moved and Long seconded to Make a motion 
to approve the 2027 Budgeted Expenditures of $37,636,123 and the 2026 Ad Valorem Tax for the 2027 
Budget in the amount of $5,626,781 as reflected on the State Budget Certificate and the 2026 Budget 
Amendments as reflected on the Budget Hearing Notice. Unanimous approval. 
 
3. 2027-2031 Capital Improvement and Equipment Plans – Shannon moved and Gurley seconded to approve 
the 2027-2031 Capital Improvement and Equipment Plans as presented. Unanimous approval. 
 
4. Preliminary Plat — PP-02-26; Mission Prairie Farms - 400 S. 118th Street - Shannon moved and Wood 
seconded to approve the requested preliminary plat with the stipulations listed in the staff report. 
Unanimous approval.  
 
5. Ordinance Authorizing Issuance of Industrial Revenue Bonds for Bonner Springs Senior Villas - Blanks 
moved and Reeves seconded to adopt an ordinance authorizing the City to issue Industrial Revenue Bonds 
and authorize the execution of documents in connection with the issuance of the bonds. Unanimous 
approval. Assigned Ordinance No. 2625. 
 
6. Amendment to Kansas Housing Resources Corporation Grant Funding - 120 on Oak - Gurley moved and 
Blanks seconded to authorize the mayor to sign an amendment to the Moderate-Income Housing (ARPA) 
grant agreement with the Kansas Housing Resources Corporation. Unanimous approval.
