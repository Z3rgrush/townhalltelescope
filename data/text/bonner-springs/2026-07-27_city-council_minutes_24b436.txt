OLD BUSINESS – None presented.  
 
NEW BUSINESS -   
Award Bid - 2026 Street Resurfacing Program - Shannon moved and Blanks seconded to award the bid for the 
2026 Street Resurfacing Project to McAnany Construction in the amount of $535,829.85. Unanimous approval. 
Award Bid - 2026 Concrete Project - Gurley moved and Wood seconded to award the bid for the 2026 Concrete 
Project to New Beginning, LLC in the amount of $138,212. Unanimous approval. 
Sandstone Townhomes Public Infrastructure Acceptance - Gurley moved and Blanks seconded to accept the 
remaining public infrastructure improvements within the Sandstone Townhome development. Unanimous 
approval. 
Resolution to Determine a Need for Housing and Proposed Rural Housing Incentive District - Shannon moved 
and Reeves seconded to adopt a resolution determining a need for housing and a proposed Rural Housing 
Incentive District. Unanimous approval. Assigned Resolution No. 2026-09. 
Lynn Valley Reinvestment Housing Incentive District Funding Agreement - Reeves moved and Shannon 
seconded to approve a funding agreement with Circle H Land Development, LLC, and authorize the mayor to 
execute the agreement. Unanimous approval. Roman Haehn, Circle H Land Development, LLC, thanked the 
Council for consideration and addressed the Council about the benefits of the RHID for infrastructure, if 
approved. 
REPORTS  
 
City Manager's Report - The City Manager reported the council members received Mayor's Banquet invitations 
and requested to please let the City Clerk know by August 11th if they are able to attend. 
Advanced voting for the primary is available at the Bonner Springs library. 
The Placer AI report shows 6,700 attendees at Bonner Blast. This was an increase from 2025 attendance of 6,600. 
City Council Items  
• Reeves stated the Economic Development Manager made history with 100% occupancy on Oak St. 
• Blanks stated she has seen that chambers of commerce and tourism departments are in decline and she 
is happy Bonner Springs is not following that trend. She believes cities need the support of these groups. 
Blanks is excited to see a plan for motorized bikes. She is excited to hear the Public Works Director's 
opinion on flooding complaints and how to remedy them. The early voting drop box at the library is open 
from 7am-7pm. 
• McMahan stated that the downtown is coming along. The City Band meets on Thursday. He also 
commended the Rotary Club for a successful ice cream social and their presentation of a donation to 
Vaughn Trent Community Services. 
• Gurley reminded everyone that Long celebrated her one-year anniversary of being in business in Bonner 
Springs. 
• Shannon thanked the Lynn Valley developer for his interest in the community. 
Mayor's Report- Mayor Stephens stated voting is important and encouraged everyone to vote. The voting drop 
box at the library is monitored by security cameras, and open until 7pm on election day. 
ADJOURNMENT – Blanks moved and Reeves seconded to adjourn the City Council meeting at 8:35 p.m. 
Unanimous approval.
