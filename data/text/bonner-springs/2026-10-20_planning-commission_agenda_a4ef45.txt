2.  Replat — RP-01-26 — Coleman Estates No. 2, 13600 Riverview — Consider a 
request for a replat to combine a portion of Lot 3 of Coleman Estates and an 
unplatted portion of the north adjacent parcel, consisting of 15.47± acres.  
 Action Make a motion to approve the replat with the conditions listed in the 
staff report.  
 Recommendation Staff recommends APPROVAL of the replat for Coleman Estates 
No. 2, located at 13600 Riverview Avenue, with the following 
conditions: 
1.  The replat shall be filed within one (1) year after approval. 
2. The applicant shall adhere to the requirements of submittal 
for a replat, after approval and contingent upon, any 
recommendations from the Planning Commission. 
3. All comments made by the Design Review Team shall be 
addressed prior to execution of the replat. 
4. The applicant shall be responsible for 
     a. All fees associated with building permits and; 
     b. The extension/installation of all utilities to provide 
services to the development. 
5. The developer/property owner agrees to abide by all 
regulations contained in the Bonner Springs Unified 
Development Ordinance.

3.  Replat — RP-02-26 — Maple’s Addition to Tiblow, 313 E Front Street — 
Consider a request for a replat to divide one lot into four lots, with three of 
the four lots to be developed for single-family residential housing, 
consisting of 3.74± acres.  
 Action Make a motion to approve the replat with the conditions listed in the 
staff report.  
 Recommendation Staff recommends APPROVAL of the replat for Maple’s Addition to 
Tiblow, located at 313 E. Front Street, subject to the following 
conditions:  
1. The replat shall be filed within one (1) year following 
approval. 
2. The applicant shall adhere to the requirements of submittal 
for a replat, after approval and contingent upon, any 
recommendations from the Planning Commission. 
3. All comments made by the Design Review Team shall be 
addressed prior to execution of the replat.  
4. The applicant shall be responsible for 
     a. All fees associated with building permits, and; 
     b. The extension/installation of all utilities to provide 
services for the development. 
5. All construction documents referencing streets, stormwater, 
sanitary sewer, and other required utilities shall be submitted 
and approved by the City prior to construction 
commencement.  
6. Prior to construction of any street or utility infrastructure to be 
maintained by the City, the applicant shall enter into a 
Privately Funded Public Improvements Construction 
Agreement approved by all parties. 
7. Once construction of the public infrastructure is completed, a 
set of drawings shall be submitted; they shall be stamped 
“As-Built” or “Record” drawings shall be submitted to the City 
as an official copy. 
8. The developer/property owner agrees to abide by all 
regulations contained in the Bonner Springs Unified 
Development Ordinance.
