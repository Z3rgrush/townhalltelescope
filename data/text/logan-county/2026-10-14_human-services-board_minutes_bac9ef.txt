 
 
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
LOGAN COUNTY BOARD OF HUMAN SERVICES 
October 14, 2026 
 
 
 
Call to Order 
The Logan County Board of Human Services convened on October 14, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. at the 
Logan County Central Services Building. Chairman Mike Brownell, Logan County Commissioner, 
called the meeting to order. 
 
Members and Staff Present 
• Mike Brownell, Chairman, Logan County Commissioner 
• Jim C. Santomaso, Logan County Commissioner 
• James T. Yahn, Logan County Commissioner 
• Janice M. Rice, Director, Logan County Department of Human Services 
• Peggy Kircher, Recording Secretary, Logan County Department of Human Services 
• Karen Milner, Accounting/Finance Manager, Logan County Department of Human 
Services 
• Shannon Graves, Assistant Accounting/Finance Manager, Logan County Department of 
Human Services 
• Debbie Unrein, Logan County Finance Director 
Approval of Previous Meeting Minutes 
The minutes of the September 9, 2026, meeting of the Logan County Board of Human Services was 
presented for review. Commissioner Santomaso moved to approve the minutes as presented. 
Commissioner Yahn seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously. 
 
Financial Reports and Updates 
Ms. Milner presented the following reports for the Board’s review: 
• Expenditure Summary Report dated September 30, 2026 
• Summary Allocation Tracking Report for August 2026 
• TANF Reserve Balances Report for August, State Fiscal Year 2026–2027 
Presentation of the Proposed LCDHS Budget 
Ms. Milner and Ms. Graves provided the Board with detailed information regarding the proposed 
2027 Logan County Department of Human Services (LCDHS) budget. 
 
At 9:51 a.m., Ms. Milner, Ms. Unrein, and Ms. Graves excused themselves from the meeting. 
 
County Collaboration Agreements 
Director Rice provided information regarding the Colorado Department of Health Care Policy and 
Financing (HCPF) County Collaboration Agreements. The Board agreed that collaboration with 
local entities is already in place. Commissioner Yahn moved to opt out of the county collaboration 
agreements. Commissioner Santomaso seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously. 
 
CCCAP Intergovernmental Agreements with Baca County and Washington County 
Director Rice presented the intergovernmental agreement (IGA) between Baca County and Logan 
County for processing applications and overseeing the cases for the Colorado Child Care Assistance 
Program (CCCAP). Commissioner Yahn moved to enter into an intergovernmental agreement with 
Baca County for CCCAP services. Commissioner Santomaso seconded the motion. The motion 
carried unanimously. 
 
Director Rice also provided information regarding the current intergovernmental agreement (IGA) 
with Washington County for child care services. Commissioner Yahn moved to amend the current 
IGA with Washington County to include the addendum for CCCAP provider services.  
Commissioner Santomaso seconded the motion.  The motion carried unanimously.  
 
Briefing Highlights 
Director Rice discussed the 2012 Dodge Grand Caravan, which is experiencing mechanical issues 
that may require an expensive engine repair. The vehicle had previously been declared a total loss 
following a hailstorm. The Department may keep the insurance proceeds associated with the total 
loss and get rid of the vehicle; a decision will be made at a later date. 
 
 
 
 
Director Rice reported that she attended an implementation work group meeting where North 
Highlands, a third-party vendor, suggested a state-run system using a cohort model. 
 
Director Rice provided information regarding House Bill 26-1429, which restructures the 
administration of public assistance programs. She reported that the informational dashboard is nearly 
complete and that performance-based standards are still being developed. 
 
Director Rice advised the Board that the Bravo Christmas party will be held on December 9th. She 
requested that the office close for two hours so all staff can attend the party and lunch. The Board 
approved closing the office for staff to participate. Clients with urgent matters will be assisted during 
the closure. 
 
Director Rice informed the Board that some employees were experiencing life-threatening health 
concerns. The Board agreed that the office may close for staff funerals; employees attending funerals 
will be required to use paid time off (PTO). 
 
Director Rice announced that an all-staff meeting will be held on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 8:05 
a.m. 
 
Adjournment 
 
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:20 a.m. 
 
The next meeting of the Logan County Board of Human Services is scheduled for Thursday, 
November 12, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. at the Logan County Central Services Building.
