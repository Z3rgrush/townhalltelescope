Commissioner Yahn reported discussions with Carmen Rossi, a Northrop Grumman communications 
representative. Northrop Grumman is the lead contractor for the Sentinel missile project and is moving forward 
with a separate group handling construction. Trials are underway in Nebraska to identify the best methods for 
installing communications cables and other components.   
 
A test vault was built in Promontory, Utah, to verify that all components worked properly. Constructed from precast 
curved panels stacked in an excavated hole, the vault is larger than those used for Minuteman Missiles. Kimball, 
Nebraska, will serve as the hub for the project. About one -third of the missiles will be located in Wyoming, one-
third in Nebraska, and one-third in northeastern Colorado’s Logan County. Installation is expected to begin in 
Wyoming within the next few years. 
 
Commissioner Brownell asked if the project is still discussing removing some here missile sites in Colorado due to 
the proximity to wind towers. Commissioner Yahn said that 15 missile sites will likely be removed near Peetz due 
to their proximity to wind towers. The re will be another meeting in Peetz sometime after the first of the year to 
update to the public on what is taking place.
