Department Updates and Briefing Highlights 
Director Rice presented an overview of the units’ master report summaries. The Board discussed 
cases involving fraud investigations. There was discussion around the centralization of program 
integrity that will begin in January 2027.   
 
Director Rice provided the Board with information regarding the Farm Bill/SNAP Cost Share. 
The Board discussed the 60/40 cost share. Director Rice explained that 15% of the penalty 
associated with the error rate comes from HR-1, the State’s cost share is 60%, and the County’s 
cost share is 40%. 
 
Director Rice informed the Board that Jessica Rivera resigned from her position in the TANF 
unit. The vacancy will be announced and advertised soon. 
 
Director Rice reported that Logan County DHS is a volunteer county for several pilot programs 
in order to have input regarding upcoming changes. Two child welfare pilots include acuity and 
motivational interviewing. Rhonda Chrisp is participating in the SNAP Accuracy Model 
workgroup. This AI program assists with follow-up and helps obtain information needed on 
cases to help lower the PER. 
Director Rice discussed the Colorado Works case comment template pilot, which is intended to 
allow case comments to be entered and processed more efficiently. Logan County DHS also 
participated in the document management system pilot. 
Director Rice is also serving on the Data and Performance workgroup, which is responsible for 
developing public-facing reports. 
 
The Department recently received two scholarships for staff to attend the Elder Justice Summit.   
An application was also submitted for a scholarship to attend the National Adult Protective 
Services Association Conference in Austin, TX. 
 
Director Rice reported that the Trump Accounts for Kids will apply to children in foster care; 
however, several details regarding these accounts still need to be defined.
