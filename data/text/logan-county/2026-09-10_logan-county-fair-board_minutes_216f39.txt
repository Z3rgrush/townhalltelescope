Committee ReportsAaron said for livestock, kids are getting pictures, signatures and thank you’s right now as well as picking up checks.  He feels it is going well. Rick explained he wants to get ideas on the rabbit hutch and feedback on a better setup. New Business:Annabelle found out more information on ticket sales. She found that they had lost about $22,000 due to Saffire fees. She asked if they want to continue to absorb those ticket prices, or if they want to increase the ticket price on the customers end so the board does not pay for the fees. Guy asked about the law that was passed regarding ticket prices and disclosing those fees. Diana said they do have to be disclosed, but the way it was set up in the past was we ended up absorbing those fees instead of charging the consumer even though they were disclosed. Annabelle explained that the ticket price we set was $14, but this year we included the fees, so we only made $10 off of the ticket, but the board can change that if they want to where they make $14 off of every ticket, and the fees are on top of the $14. Diana brought up the credit card fee which was a separate fee. She also explained that all ticket prices change whether they are bought online or in the box office. Janie asked how we would advertise that.  If we advertise as $14 and when they checkout fees are applied or if we advertise as a higher price where fees are included and the price stays the same at checkout. Annabelle said to the customer it would look like we raised our ticket prices. Aaron and Guy said they think they set the concert ticket prices like that, where the price went up so we don’t lose so much money, which Annabelle said she can check and see how those were set up in Saffire. Guy asked when we would need to have this pricing set, and Diana explained the process of how they would set this up and explained last year due to trying to sign a new contract with a credit card company, their ticket sales and prices were all mixed around. This also delayed the season ticket holder process, and when they could buy their tickets. The season ticket holders’ seats were still on hold, but from their end they saw that everyone could buy tickets before they even got theirs. Diana summarized that they could start selling once they are ready and not rush it, which is what happened in 2026. Guy and Aaron agreed, they need to get everything confirmed here soon. Diana stated that no one realized they were eating that many fees, Guy jumped in and said he had realized this and needed to look at the ticket structure and how they were set up. Diana explained that because of all the different fees based on how much or where you buy them you can’t get an exact total. Janie asked if there is a way where it shows at checkout how much they are paying in fees, which Diana said with the credit card that is how it works, but the Saffire fee has to be built into the price. Janie felt this must be frustrating due to not being able to accurately track this because of all of the fees. Guy mentioned that they used to be able to not disclose fees but that changed now. Everyone agreed to table it for now and think on it.
