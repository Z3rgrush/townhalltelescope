Logan County Commissioners Work Session 
October 13, 2026 
 
Present: Mike Brownell, Jim Yahn, Jim Santomaso, Jeremy Leisure, Dennis Aulston, Annabelle Mills, Marilee 
Johnson, Debbie Unrein, Mike Burri, Callie Jones and Jennifer Crow.    
 
Chairman Brownell called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. 
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM PREVIOUS SESSION – Commissioner Yahn moved to approve the Work 
Session minutes of October 6, 2026. Commissioner Santomaso seconded, and the motion carried 3-0.   
 
REVISIONS TO THE WORK SESSION AGENDA – Commissioner Yahn reported that Trae Miller, Executive 
Director of Logan County Economic Development, was hosting several visitors from Israel who were interested in 
agricultural innovation and local water resources. Because the South Platte Basin Roundtable was meeting in 
Sterling rather than its usual location in Longmont, Commissioner Yahn invited the visitors to join a local tour 
scheduled for today. 
   
Commissioner Yahn also reported on the Courthouse Christmas Lighting Ceremony. He had talked about moving 
the ceremony to Friday, November 20, a week earlier. It has now been changed back to the traditional date, which 
is the day after Thanksgiving, Friday, November 27th.    
 
The Board will be meeting with the railroad on Thursday near Peetz concerning closing a crossing at Highway 138 
and CR 76 and State Highway 138. The meeting will start at the CR 74 crossing and then move to the CR 76 
crossing and then to the CR 78 crossing. Mike Burri w ill bring enough hard-hats and vests for the Board. The 
railroad is also considering closing two crossings in Nebraska.  
 
SHERIFF’S OFFICE HAIL DAMAGE REIMBURSEMENT  – Undersheriff Dennis Aulston and Lieutenant 
Jeremy Leisure met with the Board to discuss replacing a pickup that was totaled earlier this year in an accident for 
which the deputy was not at fault . The Sheriff’s Department received more than $32,000 from the insurance 
company and can salvage the truck’s equipment . Undersheriff Aulston located a Ford Explorer at Varsity and 
compared it with similar vehicles at Korf and Transwest. The Varsity Explorer has 36,000 miles and is priced at 
$23,200, well below the insurance reimbursement. 
 
They also found a 2018 Ford Explorer at Korf with 86,000 miles for $20,000 and a 2024 GMC Terrain, with double 
the mileage for $23,000. They have looked at the Ford Explorer. It is very nice and the one they like best out of all 
of them. It would be used as an investigator vehicle. The equipment would be salvaged out of the truck, radio and 
lighting to put into this car . Cody Adams will do the transfer of the equipment for them and does it for a very 
reasonable amount. The SUVs work much better for the investigators with all their equipment.  
 
Insurance estimates for the hail damage have been received. The Sheriff’s Department will not repair several 
vehicles scheduled for removal from the fleet. For example, although the Dodge Charger may have brought only 
$3,000 at auction, the insurance company valued the damage at $14,000. The department will begin  looking for 
another patrol car but  will wait until year -end to learn whether Lake County will provide  the two patrol trucks 
previously discussed. Undersheriff Aulston and Lieutenant  Leisure will return to the Board near  the end of the 
year—or sooner once a decision is made—to discuss whether to purchase another investigator vehicle or apply the 
insurance proceeds toward next year’s  patrol car purchases . Lieutenant Leisure noted during the budget hearing 
that when a fully equipped truck or patrol car is lost, the department does not receive enough funding to replace it. 
Using those funds for other needs has significantly affected the patrol fleet.   
 
The insurance adjuster also declared another vehicle a total loss due to hail damage, but the Sheriff’s Office will 
retain it. The investigator vehicle has only 85,000 miles, sustained relatively minor damage, and remains

ROAD AND BRIDGE VEHICLES - Mike Burri also discussed hail-damaged vehicles with the Board. The Road 
and Bridge Department is expected to receive $233,000 for vehicle hail damage. The Board has discussed upgrading 
the department’s fleet. Most trucks have already received repairs needed for safe operation, including windshields, 
turn signal lenses, and headlights. Colton’s 1990s Peterbilt received $15,000 for repairs from the insurance 
company. It still runs well as a rock truck, and repairing the hail damage is not necessary. 
 
All older pickups were declared total losses. The Board asked Mr. Burri to develop and present a fleet replacement 
plan. Mr. Burri said the only pickup he would repair hail damage on is Rob Kasper’s because it is just three months 
old.  
 
COMMISSIONERS PROCEEDINGS FOR SEPTEMBER 2026 – Commissioner Yahn moved to approve the

carried 3-0.
