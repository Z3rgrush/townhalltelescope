14. Staff Reports 
 a. Town Administrator 
Cheryl Campbell advised that while she and Lindsay Shoemaker were gone, Maria and 
Kenzie did an amazing job with court. ADP sent incorrect W-2s so Cheryl is working on 
getting them corrected. The Town CPA has advised Cheryl to start looking for a new 
payroll company. Officer Bird's tuition reimbursement will be on the next agenda.  
 b. Police Chief  
Chief Black advised the Board there will be another Change Order credit coming from 
Mark Young. The PD is completely roughed in and things are moving smoothly. The 
project should be complete in April. Officer Lamb retired on December 29th, 2025. The 
department has hired another Part-Time Officer, Christopher Watterud. He will start his 
training soon. Violent crime has fallen but nuisance crime is up. There has been more 
compliance with the bars in town.  
 c. Town Attorney  
Ms. Penfold addressed the Board about two Supreme Court Cases that may affect the 
Town. She will have an ordinance in the near future to address the decisions of the 
court.
