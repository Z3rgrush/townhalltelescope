 YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
 July 30, 2026 
 Minutes1 
 On July 30, 2026 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session. Chairman 
 Scott Weaver, Commissioner Adam Gates, Commissioner Mike Leerar, and Administrator Brandi Ritchey 
 were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts payable and 
 discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
 Liguor License 
 County Administrator, Brandi Ritchey joined the Commissioners at 8:15 a.m. to present the Special 
 Events Permit for Vernon Volunteer Fire Department. 
 Commissioner Gates moved to ratify Commissioner Weaver's signature on the State Application for 
 a Special Events Permit for the Vernon Volunteer Fire Department. Commissioner Leerar seconded 
 the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Commissioner Gates moved to approve the County Special Events Permit for the Vernon Volunteer 
 Fire Department. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Road and Bridge 
 Road & Bridge Supervisor David Lee and Blade Supervisor Justin Lorimer joined the meeting at 8:30 a.m. 
 to provide department updates. 
 Road Report   Lee and Lorimer discussed blade area coverage with the Commissioners. Because the 
 department is short-staffed, each blade operator covers a larger area, which means roads may take 
 longer to be bladed. The group discussed improving coverage by keeping more spare blades available 
 for problem areas and adding more blade operator positions to the 2027 budget. Crews are also 
 continuing to repair problem areas caused by rainstorms in the southern part of the county. The 
 County Road 39 project is underway. Simon started at County Road G and progressed to County Road 
 A. Lee would like to rent a machine to finish the shoulders to prevent further damage to the newly
 paved roads. The cost of this equipment rental is $2,800.00. The road and bridge department will
 stripe the road once work is completed.
 Department Updates   Lee reported hiring a mechanic, a parts clerk, two maintenance employees,
 and an area man. He also reviewed current salaries and presented merit increases for four employees.
 The lights at the Armel shop have been repaired and are working well. Ritchey drafted a Right of Entry 
 form, which was approved by the Colorado Division of Mining and Reclamation, and sent it to the 
 Dreher Gravel Pit landowners for signature. Once the signed form is returned, Lee and Ritchey will 
 submit it to the state so crews can begin reclaiming and closing the Dreher gravel pit. 
 Commission Gates moved to approve the Parts Clerk Salary Authorization at $19.50/ hour. 
 Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Commission Gates moved to approve the Maintenance Position Salary Authorization at $19.50/ 
 hour. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Commission Gates moved to approve the Blade Operator Salary Authorization at $21.00/ hour. 
 Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Commission Gates moved to approve the Blade Operator Salary Authorization at $21.10/ hour. 
 Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
 Minutes prepared by Brandi Ritchey are a summary of discussions, not a verbatim account.

Department Reports - The Commissioners received several department reports, including updates on the 
Eastern Transportation Planning Region and the 911 Authority Board. Ritchey reported that only one 
letter of interest had been received for the Economic Development Board, the Commissioners agreed to 5 
continue advertising the opening. She also reported that surveying had begun on the prospective property 
in Wauneta. The group then discussed the new VIP seating at the fairgrounds and agreed to have an 
engineer conduct a routine inspection of all stands, both new and existing, after the fair to confirm they 
remain safe for public use following years of normal wear and tear.

Yuma County Treasurer 
At 11:30 a.m., Yuma County Treasurer Chrystal Hammond met with the Commissioners and presented 
the January June 2026 investment report. She reported that the County's investments are well positioned 
for the remainder of 2026. Her office will continue coordinating with the Finance Office to ensure funds 
are available for upcoming budgeted expenses.
