YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
January 29, 2026 
Minutes1 
On January 29, 2026 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session.   
Chairman Scott Weaver, Commissioner Adam Gates, Commissioner Mike Leerar, and Administrator   
Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts   
payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
Administrator's Report 
Administrator Brandi Ritchey reviewed calls, correspondence, and presented the following for discussion and   
action at 8:30 a.m.: 
Minutes-The Commissioners reviewed the minutes from the previous BOCC meetings. 
• Commissioner Gates moved to approve the minutes from the January 15,2026 regular meeting of 
the Board of County Commissioners. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion, which passed 
unanimously. 
• Commissioner Gates moved to approve the Yuma County Accounts payable check sequence 84947-
85012 in the amount of $559,819.57 ran on 01/16/2026 and sequence 85013-85055 in the amount 
of $628,987.00 ran on 01/23/2026. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed 
unanimously. 
Resolutions/Ordinances/Signatures - 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the South YW Ambulance Service License. 
Commissioner Gates seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to ratify the approval of two printer contracts between Yuma 
County and Journal Office Supply for the Road and Bridge and Land Use/OEM Departments. 
Commissioner Gates seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to ratify the signature on the 2026 Steering Committees 
Designation of Proxy form for CCI appointing Brandi Ritchey as Proxy. Commissioner Gates 
seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
Department Reports -The Commissioners received several department reports, which included an update   
on the administration of the Eastern Transportation Planning Region and a discussion of activities   
conducted by the 911 Authority Board. Ritchey presented the 2026 Fair Adventure Zone Contract for   
approval, along with a salary authorization for a certified appraiser position in the Assessor's Office. The   
Commissioners also reviewed the City of Wray's 2025 fourth-quarter reimbursement request.   
Additionally, Ritchey presented bids for the Chiller Pump replacement from Glassman's Corporation and   
Wern Air for the Commissioners' consideration. She informed the Commissioners about the transition of   
legal representation; Yuma County will need to contact Brandon Dittman for any attorney-related   
correspondence. Finally, the board reviewed letters of interest for appointments to the Eckley Cemetery   
and Washington-Yuma Combined Communications Boards. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the Adventure Zone Contract for the 2026 Eair. 
Commissioner Gates seconded the motion which passed unanimously.

Human Services 
The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services   
Director Kara Hoover, income Maintenance Supervisor Daiene Rogers, and Office Manager Lori Summers   
were present. The Department of Human Services maintains detailed minutes of the Board of Social   
Services separately. The Board of County Commissioners reconvened at 10:30 a.m. 
County Treasurer, Chrystal Hammond 
At 10:30 a.m., Yuma County Treasurer Chrystal Hammond met with the Commissioners to deliver updates   
on her department's activities. During the meeting, Hammond presented a revised Yuma County   
investment Policy for their review. The group also discussed the investment report for the period of July   
to December 2025. Yuma County will see an overall increase in yield for 2025, due to the high number of   
low yield investments that matured in 2025, However, projections for 2026 indicate a decline in the rates   
available on investments. Yuma County's partnership with 3+1 is expected to help keep all county cash in   
a strategic position for liquidity, safety and interest income. 
County Clerk, Haleigh Studer 
At 11:00 a.m., Yuma County Clerk Haleigh Studer provided the Commissioners with a brief update on her   
office's activities. Recently, she attended the Colorado Clerk's Conference. Currently, her office is actively   
preparing for the upcoming election. Additionally, one of Studer's staff members is set to retire at the end   
of February. With the planned motor vehicle system upgrade coming down from the state, she has   
decided to postpone hiring a replacement until after the upgrade is complete. 
Economic Development 
At 11:30 a.m., Yuma County Economic Development Director Darlene Carpio met with the Commissioners   
to share important updates, accompanied by Economic Development board members, City of Yuma   
Manager Eric Duthie and the City of Yuma Mayor Pro-tern Nathan O'Neil. Recently, Carpio wrapped up a   
leadership event in collaboration with Wray and Yuma High Schools and Ogallala Commons. The group   
discussed the creation of a YCEDC investor group, which would feature different investment levels. Carpio   
expressed her desire to initiate a campaign in February 2026 aiming to educate the community and   
generate interest in the project.

Road and Bridge 
Road & Bridge Supervisor Tom Andersen and Blade Forman Justin Lorimer joined the meeting at 2:00 p.m.   
to provide department updates. 
• County Road MM-Andersen plans to talk to landowners about building up the road on MM and 
15. Doing so will allow crews to pull dirt up to create ditches then they will top it with material 
from one of the pits. 
• Pioneer Ditch - The pioneer ditch rider requested that Yuma County Road and Bridge evaluate the 
bridge spanning the ditch on Road 35.5. They are considering either redecking the bridge or 
installing a culvert as potential solutions. 
• Efficiencies/Department Reports - The group assessed the grader operator's efficiency report. 
Andersen presented a salary authorization for a truck driving position for approval. 
• Commissioner Gates moved to approve the Truck Driver salary authorization at 
$19.50/hour. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion, which passed unanimously. 
• Road Report/Mowing - Currently crews have recently completed their work on County Road AA. 
They transported materials to 32.5 East of County Road FF to repair damage. Crews are currently 
hauling materials to the area of County Road JJ, North of County Road 52, as well as to the Y1 
area. They are also evaluating the installation of a culvert on the road near the Mek pit.
