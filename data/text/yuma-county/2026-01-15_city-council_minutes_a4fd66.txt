 YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
 January 15, 2026  
 Minutes1 
 On January 15, 2026 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session.  
 Chairman Scott Weaver, Commissioner Mike Leerar, Commissioner Adam Gates, and Administrator  
 Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts  
 payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
 Public Comment 
 At 8:15 a.m. Larry Hilbert and Rick Vanvickle joined the Commissioners to offer a prayer for their guidance  
 and for a prosperous year for Yuma County. 
 Road and Bridge 
 Road & Bridge Supervisor Blade Supervisor Justin Lorimer and Fleet Forman Matt Martin joined the  
 meeting at 8:30 a.m. to provide department updates.
 •  2026 Capital Equipment - Lorimer reported that the new blades are expected to arrive between
 late March and early April. Once they are delivered, he will visit 4Rivers Equipment to show where
 he would like specific attachments mounted. The budgeted fleet pickup has also been delivered.
 •  Road Report - Crews have been transporting materials to County Road 9, County Road KK, and
 County Road FF near the Wauneta Shop. They also have been working on the removal of rock
 shelves on County Road AA while delivering new material to that road as wed.
 •  Fleet Programming - Martin stated that Pubworks will be transitioning to G-Works, a software
 program that the Road and Bridge department uses to monitor fuel consumption, equipment
 repairs, inventory and various other functions. He mentioned that Pubworks would credit Yuma
 County for the remaining balance and apply it towards the amount due for G-Works in 2026.
 Ritchey recommended researching other fleet management programs to ensure that Yuma
 County is receiving fair value for the services provided by G-Works.
 Human Services 
 The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services  
 Director Kara Hoover, Adult Protection Supervisor Hollie Hillman, Office Manager Lori Summers and Legal  
 Tech I Andrea Valenzuela were present. The Department of Human Services maintains detailed minutes  
 of the Board of Social Services separately. The Board of County Commissioners reconvened at 11:00 a.m. 
 CEBT 
 At 11:00 a.m., Terry Rook, Director at CEBT (Colorado Employee Benefit Trust), joined the commissioners  
 to discuss various options available to entities partnering with CEBT. He explained the differences  
 between Fully insured, Self-Funded, and Pooling insurance models, noting the advantages of pooling over  
 shopping the insurance market. The group reviewed the 2025 utilization chart, where Rook stated that  
 maintaining a utilization rate of around 91.5% is important to avoid significant premium increases. Rook  
 plans to meet with Ritchey quarterly in 2026 to review the utilization.

 Office of Emergency Management 
 At 1:00 p.m., the Commissioners received an update from Yuma County Emergency Manager Jake Rockwell.  
 Key topics included fire safety, weather conditions, and public health information. The group discussed YW  
 Electric's plan to operate with enhanced poweriine safety settings in response to the heightened fire danger  
 from anticipated high wind speeds. Rockwell shared information from Tanya Fell from NRCS, Yuma  
 Conservation District who informed him about a grant opportunity that can assist individuals with burnt tree  
 rows. Rockwell is pursuing a grant to help provide generators for communities who would benefit from them,  
 including the Town of Eckley. 
 County Treasurer, Chrystal Hammond 
 At 1:30 PM, Yuma County Treasurer Chrystal Hammond met with the Commissioners to provide departmental  
 updates. Hammond met with 3+1 in December, noting that Yuma County's Cashvest Score is 91, up from 89  
 in September. The Treasurer's office is finalizing 2025 tax statements. Yuma County is 99.86% collected for  
 the 2024 taxes. She reported that there are currently 10 active deed applications and 4 ongoing foreclosures,  
 including one in bankruptcy, with a new case opened this week. Additionally, she noted that the conversion 
 to Harris software is going well. Staff from Harris recently visited the Treasurer's Office to help with balancing  
 the tax roll from the Assessor's Office. Hammond presented various reports for the Commissioners to review 
 including the Uncollectible Tax Resolution, the Public Trustee Report, the Annual Banking Resolution and the  
 Annual Investment Resolution. 
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve signature on the 2025 Fourth Quarter Public Trustee report
 as presented. Commissioner Gates seconded the motions, which passed by unanimous vote.
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 01-15-2026 A, a resolution to approve the
 removal of uncollectible tax from the tax rolls. Commissioner Gates seconded the motion, which
 passed by unanimous vote.
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 01-15-2026 B, a resolution to approve the
 authorized signers for accounts held at First Pioneer National Bank, Wray State Bank and Bank of
 Colorado. Commissioner Gates seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 01-15-2026 C, a resolution approving the
 County Treasurer to deposit and invest funds. Commissioner Gates seconded the motion. The
 motion passed unanimously.
 Fairgrounds Manager

 seconded the motion which passed unanimously.
