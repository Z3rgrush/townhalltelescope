YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS   
December 15, 2025   
Minutes1 
On December 15, 2025 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session.   
Chairman Adam Gates, Commissioner Scott Weaver, Commissioner Mike Leerar, and Administrator   
Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts   
payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
Road and Bridge 
Road & Bridge Supervisor Tom Andersen, Blade Supervisor Justin Lorimer and Fleet Foreman Matt Martin   
joined the meeting at 8:30 a.m. to provide department updates. 
• Contracted Trucks - Andersen presented data comparing the cost of operating county owned 
trucks versus contracted truck drivers. Andersen based the county's rates on loaded wages, parts 
and maintenance, miies driven, fuel usage and other factors. The contracted trucks came in at a 
higher rate. The group weighed the advantages and disadvantages of using contracted trucks 
noting how beneficial it is to utilize them to haul to area blades to continue normal operations 
while big projects were underway in other areas of the county. 
• Easement Repairs - Andersen is still waiting for a response from BNSF Railroad regarding the 
necessary repairs that need to be made to County Road M, North of the railroad tracks. The 
commissioners would like Andersen to confirm whether the railroad company plans to charge for 
the flaggers they stated they would provide. If BNSF plans to charge for flaggers, the county will 
provide their own. 
• Road Report - Crews have been hauling material and making repairs to County Road N, South of 
County Road 54 and making repairs to a two mile stretch on County Road 46. 
• Fuel-Andersen noted that he made one last adjustment to his 2026 budget by increasing the fuel 
line. 
Human Services 
The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services   
Director Kara Hoover, Income Maintenance Supervisor Dalene Rogers, Adult Protection Supervisor Hollie   
Hiilman, Office Manager Lori Summers and IV-D Administrator Ana Moran. The Department of Human   
Services maintains detailed minutes of the Board of Social Services separately. The Board of County   
Commissioners reconvened at 11:00 a.m. 
NCRETAC 
At 11:00 a.m., Nick Knudeil, the Director of NCRETAC, joined the commissioners to provide departmental   
updates and present an operating agreement between NCRETAC and Yuma County. The Northeast   
Colorado Regional Emergency Medical and Trauma Services Advisory Council serves as a collaborative   
network for both hospital and pre-hospital providers, supporting ail emergency medical and trauma   
service organizations within our region to ensure equitable patient care. The group discussed the funding   
sources for NCRETAC. NCRETAC is primarily funded by the Colorado Department of Public Health and   
Environment, which receives support from the Highway User Tax Fund.

• Commissioner Weaver moved to approve the Listing Contract for the Idalia House.

Yuma County Water Authority Public Improvement District 
At 2:30 p.m. the Commissioners adjourned and convened as the Yuma County Water Authority Public   
Improvement District Board. Minutes for the Yuma County Water Authority Public Improvement District   
are kept separately. 
2025 Budget Hearing 
At 2:45 p.m., Chairman Gates opened the hearing to accept public comment on the proposed 2026   
budgets for Yuma County, Yuma County Landfill, Yuma County Water Authority. The Commissioners   
reviewed the budget documents. No member of the public was present to make comments. The hearing   
was closed at approximately 2:55 p.m. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the 2026 Salaries. Commissioner Weaver seconded the 
motion which passed by unanimous vote. 
• Commissioner Weaver moved to approve Resolution 12-15-2025 A to adopt the 2026 budget 
which summarizes expenditures and revenues for each fund and adopts a budget for the County 
of Yuma, Colorado, for the calendar year, beginning the first day of January 2026 and ending on 
the last day of December 2026. Total Expenditures approved $31,774,944.96. Commissioner 
Leerar seconded the motion, which passed unanimously. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 12-15-2025 B which appropriates sums of 
money to the various Yuma County funds and spending agencies for the 2026 budget year. 
Commissioner Weaver seconded. The motion passed by unanimous vote. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 12-15-2025 C setting Yuma County's Mill 
Levy for the year 2025 to defray costs for the 2026 budget year. The total Yuma County Mill 
Levy is set at 21.714 mills with the General Fund receiving 16.685 mills, with no abatement of 
mills. Road and Bridge is to receive 2.529 mills; Human Services 1.000 mill; Insurance Fund 1.250 
mills; and Recreation Fund 0.250 mills. The total tax revenue expected to be collected for all 
County Funds is $7,362,858.68. The 2025 County Assessed Valuation, as certified with the 
County Assessor is $339,083,480. Commissioner Weaver seconded the motion, which passed by 
unanimous vote.
