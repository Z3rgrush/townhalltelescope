 YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
 December 30, 2025  
 Minutes1 
 On December 30, 2025 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session.  
 Chairman Adam Gates, Commissioner Scott Weaver, Commissioner Mike Leerar, and Administrator  
 Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts  
 payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
 Road and Bridge 
 Road & Bridge Blade Foreman Justin Lorimer joined the meeting at 8:30 a.m. to provide department  
 updates. 
 •  December Fires - Andersen presented the overtime hours accrued by the crew who assisted in
 fighting the recent fires. During the discussion, the group discussed the damage incurred by one
 of the graders, which had damage from an electric wire fence. Ritchey stated that, if the damage
 were submitted to insurance, the repair costs would amount to approximately $5,000 after the
 deductible. The group opted against filing a claim with insurance.
 •  Budget/Efficiencies ■■ The group assessed the grader operators efficiency report as well as the
 current 2025 budget. Commissioner Weaver requested clarification regarding the Tax
 Apportionment revenue line. Finance Officer Sarah Carwin explained that this account tracks the
 incoming and outgoing allocated tax apportionments for the cities and towns, ultimately
 balancing to zero by the end of the year. Additionally, Andersen presented a salary authorization
 for the new Blade Operator.
 •  Commissioner Weaver moved to approve the Blade Operator salary authorization at
 $18.50/hour. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion, which passed unanimously.
 •  Road Report/Mowing - Currently, crews are top coating County Road 9 and County Road KK in the
 Armel area with gravel. Lorimer is keeping a close eye on the burn scar area from the fire where
 sand has been blowing onto the roads. Lorimer mentioned that one employee is set to retire in
 February. Until then, he plans to have this employee share his expertise with other blade
 operators throughout the county. The maintenance crew plans to be finished mowing this week
 and then plans to help clean up a shed that blew over at the fairgrounds during the windstorm.
 Human Services 
 The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services  
 Director Kara Hoover, Bookkeeper Kim Langley, and Office Manager Lori Summers were present. The  
 Department of Human Services maintains detailed minutes of the Board of Social Services separately. The  
 Board of County Commissioners reconvened at 10:30 a.m.

 Administrator Brandi Ritchey reviewed calls, correspondence, and presented the following for discussion and

 Resolutions/Ordinances/Signatures - 
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 12/30/2025 A, a Resolution Organizing
 County Operations. Commissioner Weaver seconded the motion which passed unanimously.

 Department Reports - The Commissioners received various department reports including an update on  
 the administration of the Eastern Transportation Planning Region and discussion of business undertaken  
 by the 911 Authority Board. Ritchey noted that it is time to review the IGA between Washington-Yuma  
 Combined Communications and Yuma County, noting that there are necessary updates needed to address  
 the Human Resource and Finance services that Yuma County provides. The group discussed the need to  
 replace the boilers in the courthouse as well as a storage request for the Fair Queens in Yuma. Ritchey  
 stated that Fairgrounds Manager, Carmen Murray will clean out the old CDL training storage shed and  
 make it available for the Fair Queens. 
 •  Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the bid submitted by Glassman's, having them order
 the necessary items and hold off on repairs for the meantime. Commissioner Weaver seconded
 the motion which passed unanimously.
