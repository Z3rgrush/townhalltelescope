YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
February 13, 2026 
Minutes' 
On February 13, 2026 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session. 
Chairman Scott Weaver, Commissioner Mike Leerar, Commissioner Adam Gates, and Administrator 
Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts 
payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
Liquor License 
County Administrator, Brandi Ritchey joined the Commissioners at 8:15 a.m. to present for signature the 
State Retail Liquor License Renewal Application and the County Retail Liquor License Renewal for Jo's 
Liquor LLC DBA Jo's Liquor. 
• Commissioner Gates moved to approve signature on the State Retail Liquor License Renewal 
Application and the County Retail Liquor License Renewal for Jo's Liquor LLC DBA Jo's Liquor 
Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed by unanimous vote. 
Road and Bridge 
Road & Bridge Supervisor Tom Andersen and Blade Supervisor Justin Lorimer joined the meeting at 8:30 
a.m. to provide department updates. 
• Equipment Acquisition/Surplus -Andersen purchased a 15,000-gallon tank at a sale and plans to use 
it for fuel storage at the Yuma shop. If the tank will not fit in the bunker, he plans to use it for water 
storage at the Idalia shop. Andersen and Martin walked through the fleet pickups and have five that 
can go to auction. 
• Budget - The group reviewed the status of the budget. Cutting edges were anticipated to increase 
25% in 2026, so the budget for cutting edges was increased accordingly. Lorimer stated that the 
cutting edges went up another 25%. The company supplying these blades sold 18 units to Road and 
Bridge at the previous price, honoring their price before the second increase. Andersen hired a new 
employee for the mining position and presented a salary authorization for approval. 
• Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the Mining Salary Authorization at $18.50/hour. 
Commissioner Gates seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
• Road Report - Lorimer delivered an update on road work across multiple areas. Crews have been 
transporting materials from the Smith Pit to County Roads 23, 24, and 17 in Area Yl, as well as County 
Roads 33 and 34 in Area VS. Additionally, Lorimer has been overseeing a culvert project at the 
entrance of the Mek Pit along County Road E. 
• Scraper-Andersen was approached by the Yuma County Landfill Supervisor regarding the possibility 
of borrowing a scraper to move cover dirt, as the landfill's scraper is currently experiencing 
operational issues. Andersen suggested charging a fair rental rate for the equipment, aiming to cover 
wear and tear rather than generate profit. The group agreed that a rate of $125 per hour would be 
reasonable.

Human Services 
The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services 
Director Kara Hoover, Adult Protection Supervisor Hollie Hillman, Office Manager Lori Summers, Income 
Maintenance Supervisor Dalene Rogers and IV-D Administrator Ana Moran were present The 
Department of Human Services maintains detailed minutes of the Board of Social Services separately. The 
Board of County Commissioners reconvened at 11:00 a.m. 
Media Logic Radio 
At 11:00 a.m., Lindsay Bruntz and Judy Perry presented a proposal to the commissioners for a "Salute to 
Agriculture" Series. The commissioners emphasized the importance of highlighting smaller businesses 
within the county. The series will be divided into two campaigns. The first will span four months, starting 
in March and concluding in June. It will kick off with a "Big on Beef" campaign, followed by a Dairy 
Campaign and a focus on Women in Agriculture. Once this series wraps up, a Fair Campaign will take place 
in June and July. 
Carmen Murray 
At 11:30 p.m., Carmen Murray joined the commissioners to discuss the findings of the inspection report 
for the Idalia House. Andersen inspected the water heater and it just needed to be lit and was working as 
it was supposed to. 
Commissioner Gates moved to take $2,500 dollars of the purchase price. Commissioner Leerar 
seconded the motion which passes unanimously. 
County Treasurer 
At 1:00 p.m., Yuma County Treasurer Chrystal Hammond joined the commissioners to provide office 
updates. Hammond met with Ritchey to discuss upcoming projects the county has planned to ensure 
there is enough liquidity to cover upcoming expenses. Tax statements were sent out January 30th She 
stated that postage fees will be higher due to the new format of the statements There is a Treasure's 
Deed auction scheduled for February 25th for 3 mineral interests. There is currently 4 active foreclosures 
with no changes from last month. Hammonds software conversion is moving along well Harris has begun 
processing historical data and her staff is becoming more comfortable with the software. 
Land Use 
At 1:25 p.m., Land Use Administrator Jodi Brady joined the commissioners to provide office updates. 
At 1:29 p.m. Chairman Weaver opened the public hearing for the first reading of ORDINANCE NO. 2026-
01, updating the Yuma County Land Use Code. The public hearing closed at 1:32 p.m. No members of 
the public were present. 
Commissioner Gates moved to approve Ordinance 2026-01. Commissioner Leerar seconded the 
motion which passed by unanimous vote.

permit fee process Additionally, Jodi shared two more letters from the public that addressed concerns
