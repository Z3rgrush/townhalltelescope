YUMA COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
August 13, 2026 
Minutes1 
On August 13, 2026 at 8:00 a.m., the Yuma County Board of Commissioners met in regular session. 
Chairman Scott Weaver, Commissioner Mike Leerar, Commissioner Adam Gates, and Administrator 
Brandi Ritchey were present throughout the day. The Commissioners reviewed and signed accounts 
payable and discussed various meetings attended throughout the prior month. 
Road and Bridge 
Road & Bridge Supervisor David Lee joined the meeting at 8:30 a.m. to provide department updates. 
Road Report - Crews are applying topcoat to roads throughout the county, pulling ditches, and 
rebuilding County Road 52 between County Roads J and K. Lee found exposed fiber along County Road 
18 and requested a utility locate so crews can safely cover it and prevent damage. Crews are also 
removing sand that blew onto County Road 29 following the wildfires. 
Department Reports - Lee stated that his department has had success hiring a few employees. The 
Road and Bridge Department is still looking for another blade operator and maintenance employees. 
County Road 39 Project - Paving is complete from County Road G to County Road M. Crews will begin 
grinding from Highway 59 to County Road G and inspect the subgrade before paving is completed. 
Lee presented a bid for repairs on County Road M. 
Commissioner Leerar moved to approve the Estimate from Simon Construction for removal and 
replacement of asphalt on County Road M in the amount of $410,050.00. Commissioner Gates 
seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
Gravel Pits - Lee stated that Ritchey had received the signed Right of Entry form by the Dreher Pit 
Landowners and the problem citation for Failure to Maintain Legal Right of Entry from the April 
28,2026 inspection for the Dreher Pit (M2012-051) has been marked as abated. 
Human Services 
The Board of County Commissioners convened as the Board of Social Services at 9:30 a.m. Human Services 
Director Kara Hoover, Adult Protection Supervisor Hollie Hillman, Office Manager Lori Summers, Child 
Support Legal Tech Andrea Valenzuela and Child Welfare Case Aide Kerri Horton were present. The Board 
of County Commissioners reconvened at 11:00 a.m. 
Administrator's Report 
At 11:00 a.m., Administrator Brandi Ritchey reviewed calls, correspondence, and presented the following 
for discussion and action: 
Minutes - The Commissioners reviewed the minutes from the previous BOCC meetings. 
Commissioner Gates moved to approve the minutes from the July 30, 2026 regular meeting of the 
Board of County Commissioners. Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion, which passed 
unanimously. 
Minutes prepared by Brandi Ritchey are a summary of discussions, not a verbatim account.

Resolutions/Ordinances/Signatures/Ratifications: 
Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 08-13-2026B A Resolution Supporting 
Initiative #175. Commissioner Gates seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
Department Reports - The Commissioners reviewed department reports, including updates on the 
Eastern Transportation Planning Region and the 911 Authority Board. The group discussed the Colorado 
Urban & Community Foresty Grant Program that is due September 21, 2026. Ritchey noted that only one 
bid was received for the surplus Tahoe-s. One of the Wray Volunteer Fire Department members reached 
out to Ritchey to see if the county had any vehicles that were not operable for a region wide extrication 
training that will be held at the end of August. The group also discussed a request to sign a resolution for 
Initiative #175 that would re-direct revenue for Colorado roads in FYH2027-FY2040. 
Commissioner Gates moved to donate the non-operational Dodge Charger to the Wray 
Volunteer Fire Department for region wide Extrication Training. Commissioner Leerar seconded 
the motion which passed unanimously. 
Office of Emergency Management 
At 1:00 p.m., Yuma County Emergency Manager Jake Rockwell provided an update to the Commissioners. 
He discussed current fire safety concerns, recent and upcoming weather conditions, and public health 
information relevant to the county. 96% of Yuma County is in some classification of drought on the 
drought monitor. Jake will begin working with ARCGIS to create a fire map. Jake is waiting for FEMA 
approval on the HMP Plan. 
Yuma County Treasurer 
At 1:30 p.m., Yuma County Treasurer Chrystal Hammond met with the Commissioners and provided 
updates from her office. Hammond reported that the county has collected 95% of its tax revenue. Of the 
county's 348 tax liens, 140 are currently eligible for deed applications. An additional 59 mineral liens will 
become eligible for deed applications this year and must be researched back to the original land patents. 
The Treasurer's Office is finishing its conversion to new tax software and expects to complete the project 
soon. Hammond will also prepare a resolution to extinguish several county-held liens. 
Yuma County Royalty 
At 2:30 p.m. the commissioners were joined by the Yuma County Royalty Committee members including 
Kaci Porter, Michelle Smith, David Schaffner and Justin Crossland. The group discussed the proposed 
resolution 08-13-2026A, a Resolution Establishing the Yuma County Fair and Rodeo Royalty Program. The 
group discussed the structure of how the committee would run. 
Commissioner Leerar moved to approve Resolution 08-13-2026A A Resolution Establishing the 
Yuma County Fair and Rodeo Royalty Program, Defining its Structure, Committee, Duties and 
Expectations. Commissioner Gates seconded the motion which passed unanimously. 
Commissioner Gates moved to appoint Kaci Porter, Michelle Smith, Justin Crossland, David
Schaffner and Scott Weaver as committee members to the Yuma County Royalty Committee. 
Commissioner Leerar seconded the motion which passed unanimously.

Grassroots Community Center 
At 3:00 p.m., Alicia Sears and Nonie Winslow joined the Commissioners to provide an update on the 
Grassroots Community Center. Sears has applied for several grants to build a play space that may include 
water features as well as ADA accommodations. The group also discussed the possibility of Yuma County 
serving as the fiscal agent for the grant funds. 
City or Wray 
At 3:30 p.m., Wray City Manager James DePue joined the Commissioners. DePue reported that the City 
of Wray received a new ambulance and plans to apply for another grant to replace an ambulance in 2028. 
He requested that the Commissioners reserve the budgeted $60,000 in both 2027 and 2028 to help cover 
the remaining cost. The Commissioners explained that uncertainty about future revenue prevents them 
from committing funds beyond the current budget year. The group also discussed a new law requiring 
cities and counties to reduce speed limits in school zones. Yuma County Road and Bridge Supervisor David 
Lee later joined the meeting and said he would order the signs and coordinate with the schools to place 
them in the new locatións. 
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:15 p.m. The next regular meeting of the 
Board of Couny Commissioners will be on August 27, 2026.
