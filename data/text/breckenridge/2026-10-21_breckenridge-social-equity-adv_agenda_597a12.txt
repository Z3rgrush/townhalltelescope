II. Staff Summary 
i. Runway 1.2 Lottery Update 
Staff reported on upcoming October lottery timelines and improvements made based on lessons 
learned from the previous lottery, including clearer date communication for school district 
employees versus general applicants, separate timelines, and accessibility considerations. 
Confusion around dual eligibility was clarified through new flyers. Commissioners emphasized 
the importance of ADA accessibility for all outreach events, noting community feedback.

ii. Pedal Together Event Summary
Staff shared a brief recap of this year’s Pedal Together program, noting its continued popularity
and strong positive reception from participants. Commissioners highlighted that community
members consistently express appreciation for the event, demonstrating growing recognition
and sustained interest. Staff suggested beginning planning earlier, ideally in January, to allow for
expanded programming throughout the summer season and ensure the event can be maximized
for broader engagement. Overall, both staff and commissioners agreed that Pedal Together
remains a meaningful and well‑loved initiative, and expressed enthusiasm for continuing and
growing the program in future years
III. Presenters
IV. Kris Walsh, Executive Artistic Director, Celebrate the Beat
Kris Walsh, along with Celebrate the Beat staff and Upper Blue Elementary students, presented 
an overview of the organization’s mission, curriculum, and impact. The group provided a live 
student performance, showcasing the outcomes of the bilingual program partially funded with 
the mini–Social Equity Advisory Commission grant.
Walsh highlighted Celebrate the Beat’s statewide reach and 26‑year history, emphasizing its 
unique blend of live music, social‑emotional learning, bilingual instruction, and leadership skill 
development. Several parents also shared heartfelt reflections, noting that the program 
significantly boosted their children’s confidence, joy, and sense of belonging.
Commissioners expressed appreciation for seeing firsthand the community impact of grant-
funded programming and looked forward to similar evaluation methods to better understand 
impact.

Subject:     Staff Summary – Comprehensive Plan 3D Immersion Events  
C
omprehensive Plan 3D Immersion Events   
The Community Development team successfully hosted another community engagement initiative as part of the 
ongoing Comprehensive Plan update process. The event was held over two days and featured an immersive 3D 
experience that allowed participants to explore potential future scenarios for the City Market/Parkway Center site, 
the F Lot, and the Tiger Dredge Lot. Participation was exceptionally strong, with 286 attendees across all sessions, 
bringing most sessions close to capacity. Of those participants, 45 identified as Spanish-speaking, representing 
approximately 16% of total attendance. This level of participation reflects continued community interest in the 
planning process and the importance of providing inclusive engagement opportunities. The consultant team and 
Town staff are currently reviewing and analyzing the feedback and data collected during the events. Additional 
findings and engagement outcomes will be shared as the analysis is completed.

Eventos de Inmersión 3D del Plan Integral   
El equipo de Desarrollo Comunitario organizó con éxito otra iniciativa de participación comunitaria como parte del 
proceso continuo de actualización del Plan Integral. El evento se celebró durante dos días y ofreció una experiencia 
3D inmersiva que permitió a los participantes explorar posibles escenarios futuros para el City Market/Parkway 
Center, el F Lot y el Tiger Dredge Lot. La participación fue excepcionalmente fuerte, con 286 asistentes en todas las

Feedback Loop Tracking and Reporting Discussion 
The Commission will discuss opportunities to better track community feedback and communicate how community 
input is reflected in the Commission's work, recommendations, and Town initiatives. As the Commission continues 
to expand its outreach and engagement efforts, establishing a clear process for documenting, evaluating, and 
reporting on community feedback can help strengthen transparency, accountability, and public trust. The 
discussion will help inform future strategies to ensure community members can see the connection between their 
participation and the work undertaken by the Commission and the Town of Breckenridge. 
 
ESPAÑOL: 
Debate sobre el seguimiento y la notificación del bucle de retroalimentación 
La Comisión discutirá oportunidades para hacer un mejor seguimiento de los comentarios de la comunidad y 
comunicar cómo la participación de la comunidad se refleja en el trabajo, las recomendaciones y las iniciativas 
municipales de la Comisión. A medida que la Comisión continúa ampliando sus esfuerzos de divulgación y 
participación, establecer un proceso claro para documentar, evaluar e informar sobre los comentarios de la 
comunidad puede ayudar a fortalecer la transparencia, la rendición de cuentas y la confianza pública. La discusión 
ayudará a informar futuras estrategias para asegurar que los miembros de la comunidad puedan ver la conexión 
entre su participación y el trabajo realizado por la Comisión y el Ayuntamiento de Breckenridge.

Subcommittee Updates:  
 
Equity, Rights & Access Subcommittee 
Met: October 6, 2026  
Meeting Summary:  
The meeting focused on discussing options for making subcommittee meeting recordings more public, with four 
potential approaches considered: maintaining current private notes only, providing private YouTube links upon 
request, including links in agenda packets, or making recordings fully public on the Town’s YouTube channel like 
some other commission meetings. The group also reviewed grant reporting progress, noting that only $12,500 of 
the $25,000 allocated for 2026 had been distributed, with most recipients yet to submit required reports. Town 
discussed plans to present a training on "targeted universalism" to the Town Council, which explores strategies for 
creating inclusive policies while addressing specific community needs.  
 
 
Community Voice & Belonging Subcommittee  
Met: October 14, 2026  
Meeting Summary:  
The subcommittee discussed the October 19th Cafecito event at Town Hall, which will focus on housing resources 
and the runway lottery application process. They debated making subcommittee meeting recordings more public, 
ultimately deciding to maintain the current system where recordings are only available through Colorado Open 
Records Act requests to ensure a safe space for community members to share concerns. The group explored 
potential collaborations with Building Hope and Elevated Community Health for January 14th health insurance 
enrollment sessions, deciding to partner for a 5:30-6:30 PM extension of the regular enrollment event. The 
commission also addressed concerns about a recent community dinner event that was perceived as exclusive and 
not inclusive of diverse community members, discussing the importance of using the Town's equity lens in future 
planning by all partnering organizations. Additional topics included welcoming NRO representation to future 
meetings and addressing community feedback about unsightly conditions in the McCain parcel area.
