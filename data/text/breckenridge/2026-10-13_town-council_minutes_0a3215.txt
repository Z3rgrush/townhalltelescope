IV) COMMUNICATIONS TO COUNCIL 
A) PUBLIC COMMENT (NON-AGENDA ITEMS ONLY; 3-MINUTE TIME LIMIT) 
Mayor Owens opened public comment. 
Tracy Ochoa, a Breckenridge resident, stated that October is National Domestic Violence 
Awareness Month and expressed disappointment that the Town had not publicly 
acknowledged it. Referring to her comments to Council the previous October, she 
emphasized the need for greater training, education, prevention, and community 
engagement. Ochoa asked whether the police department's policies and training comply 
with Colorado law, requested information about officers' training curriculum, and called 
for public disclosure ofthe department's domestic violence standards, policies, 
procedures, and methods for evaluating community effectiveness. She stressed that 
awareness must be accompanied by intentional institutional action. 
With no additional public comments, Mayor Owens closed public comment. 
B) BRECKENRIDGE TOURISM OFFICE (BTO) UPDATE 
Lucy Kay, President ofthe Breckenridge Tourism Office (BTO), thanked the Council for 
its support ofthe Global events. She stated that the goal ofthe events was to strengthen 
Breckenridge's culinary brand. The high-quality collection of chefs generated significant 
media exposure, including coverage in Food Magazine and Travel+ Leisure. Kay noted 
that preliminary return-on-investment data should be available next month. 
Kay also stated that the Saturday Global concert and community dinner were successful 
events. She provided updates on the success of Oktoberfest and the Governor's 
Conference. She noted that the Governor's Conference and Oktoberfest contributed to an 
increase in room nights for the month. 
Kay reviewed winter room nights and lodging revenue, noting that lodging revenue was 
down. She also stated that the BTO has begun discussing potential changes to its 
strategies and structure for event planning in 2028.

2) RESOLUTION NO. 21, SERIES 2026-A RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF 
STATEWIDE BALLOT MEASURE PROPOSITION NN, CONCERNING STATE 
INVESTMENT IN K-12 PUBLIC EDUCATION FUNDING 
Mayor Owens read the title into the record 
Shannon Haynes, Town Manager, stated this resolution would support proposition NN, 
a ballot initiative brought forward by the Colorado General Assembly through Senate Bill 
26-135. Town Manager Haynes noted the proposition asks voters to increase K-12 
public education funding by 2% each year for 10 years without raising any taxes. The 
additional funding comes from raising the existing TABOR cap. 
Council Member Page moved to approve RESOLUTION NO. 21, SERIES 2026-A 
RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF STATEWIDE BALLOT MEASURE PROPOSITION 
NN, CONCERNING STATE INVESTMENT IN K-12 PUBLIC EDUCATION 
FUNDING. Council Member Beckerman seconded the motion. 
The motion passed 6 - 0. Council Member Gerard was absent.
