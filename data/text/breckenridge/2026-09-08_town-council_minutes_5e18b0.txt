IV) COMMUNICATIONS TO COUNCIL 
A) PUBLIC COMMENT (NON-AGENDA ITEMS ONLY; 3-MINUTE TIME LIMIT) 
Mayor Kelly Owens opened public comment. 
JB Katz, a local resident, expressed concerns regarding the updated accessory dwelling 
unit definition. Katz noted that removing the restriction preventing accessory dwelling 
units from being used as accommodation units could allow additional bedrooms to be 
added to short-term rental licenses. She stated this may lead to increased calls for service 
and shared her broader concerns about potential impacts on the community. 
With no additional public comments, Mayor Owens closed public comment. 
B) BRECKENRIDGE TOURISM OFFICE UPDATE 
Lucy Kay, President of the Breckenridge Tourism Office, provided an update on lodging 
and upcoming events. She reported that summer room nights are up approximately 2%, 
with revenue down slightly. Early winter bookings show room nights down about 9% and 
revenue down 17% ADR. Kay noted Intercept survey results remain high, with an overall 
MPS score of 89.4% and strong welcoming scores. Top visitor markets continue to 
include Texas, Kansas, and Missouri. 
Kay also highlighted several upcoming events, including the Michelin Awards, GovCon, 
Oktoberfest, Global at the Mine/Global on Ridge, and associated concerts and activities. 
Staff will coordinate with Peyton to provide a one-page overview of event opportunities 
for council and staff attendance. Kay expressed appreciation for the work of the tourism 
team and event partners.

B) RESOLUTIONS, SERIES 2026 
C) OTHER 
1) RUNWAY PHASE 2 DECISION 
Laurie Best, Housing Department Director, requested that Council formally delay the 
decision on Runway Phase 2 until next fall. Best noted that both staff and the developer 
believe it is too early to decide on whether to move forward with the second phase of the 
project; however, the current development agreement required a decision in September. 
Best added that delaying the timeline also better aligns with the Town's agreement with 
the Summit School District, which has a decision deadline of December 2027. 
Mayor Owens opened public comment 
With no public comments, Mayor Owens closed public comment. 
Council Member Gerard commented that delaying the Runway Phase 2 decision is not an 
abandonment of the project but a timing consideration. He expressed support for 
postponing the decision, noting uncertainty around interest rates and their impact on 
affordability for prospective residents. 
Council Member Gerard moved to defer election of beginning Phase 2 of the Runway 
Housing Project until Fall of 2027. Council Member Saade seconded the motion. 
The motion passed 6-0. Council Member Lutke was absent.

IX) REPORT OF MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBERS 
A) CAST/MMC 
Mayor Owens reported on recent CAST and MMC meetings. She highlighted 
Carbondale's partnership repurposing an elementary school for shared nonprofit and 
community uses, noting it as an inspiring model relevant to local discussions. CAST 
communities discussed geothermal projects, with many pursuing new systems and pilot 
programs, including one in Frisco. Mayor Owens also noted an innovative childcare 
special tax district in the Aspen-Parachute area and an AI compliance initiative for 
deed-restricted housing in Vail. 
During MMC, Commissioner Tamara Pogue presented on anticipated state budget 
shortfalls affecting human services programs such as Medicaid, SNAP, and TANF. 
Mayor Owens stated local jurisdictions will continue to subsidize local providers 
wherever possible; however, most programs will still face significant challenges and 
potential program reduction. The group also discussed the school district's upcoming mill 
levy override focused on salaries and new CTE positions, as well as Proposition NN on 
class size. She added that Strong Futures reauthorization has been certified for the 
November ballot and a Town Council resolution of support will be forthcoming.
