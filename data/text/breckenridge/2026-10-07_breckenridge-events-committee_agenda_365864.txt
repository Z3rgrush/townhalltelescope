The Breckenridge Events Committee evaluates events against four strategic goals: 
• Build Business - An event designed to drive revenue for greater business community. 
• Branding/Media - An event designed to draw external media (national & international) promoting the Breckenridge brand. 
• Fundraising - An event designed to raise awareness and funding for a non-profit organization's mission. 
• Resident Focused - An event designed specifically for residents vs. an event more broadly marketed to visitors and residents.
