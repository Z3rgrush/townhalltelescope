1.  City Market #430 Fuel Center (CC), 430 N. Park Avenue, PL-2026-0171 
Mr. Cross presented a proposal to construct a new City Market Fuel Center, with seven multi-product 
dispensers providing fourteen fueling positions beneath a canopy, and an employee staffed retail kiosk.  
The application also includes the future demolition and environmental remediation of the existing City 
Market fuel center, canopy, and convenience store in accordance with applicable State of Colorado 
requirements.  No redevelopment of the existing fuel center site is proposed at this time. 
 
Commissioners were asked their opinion on the following questions from Staff: 
1. Does the Commission agree that the raised center roof and cross-gables resolve the prior roof-
modulation concern and that the revised canopy and kiosk composition is architecturally 
appropriate? 
2. Does the Commission find the proposed textured masonry, manufactured stone, synthetic shakes, 
and dark metal palette to meet the mountain architecture requested at the last hearing? 
3. Does the Commission concur that the current landscaping is insufficient and support larger trees, 
stronger Park Avenue evergreen screening, trees within the southern island, and additional living 
groundcover?

Planning Commission Staff Report 
Subject:  The Iron Kart Small Vendor Cart, Class C Minor (PL-2026-0251) 
Proposal: Locate one small vendor cart on the rear patio/service area of the Gold Pan 
Saloon. The proposed cart has an 8-foot by 4-foot body (32 sq. ft.) and a vinyl 
finish designed to resemble a historic mining ore cart. The vendor cart will serve 
late night eats and treats. 
 
Date:   October 27, 2026 (for meeting of November 3, 2026) 
Project Manager: Clif Cross, Planner II 
Applicant:  Patrick Ferro, Mountain to Sea Alchemy Ltd. dba The Iron Kart 
Property Owner: Silver Heels Inn Inc 
Address:  103 N. Main Street 
Legal Description: Lots 81R and 82, Bartlett & Shock Subdivision 
Site Area:   0.143 acres (6,238 sq. ft.) 
Land Use District: 19: Commercial 
Historic District: Commercial Core 
Site Conditions: The property contains the existing business, Gold Pan Saloon, within the existing 
historic building, and a paved rear patio and service area adjoining the alleyway. 
The main business is accessed off of Main Street, but additional access is 
provided by the alleyway. The vendor cart will be a separate commercial 
business located on the rear patio behind the historic structure. 
Adjacent Uses:  North and South:  Commercial uses 
East:    Main Street right-of-way and commercial uses 
West:    Gold Pan Alley right-of-way and Sawmill parking areas 
Item Summary 
The vendor cart is proposed to operate Friday and Saturday nights from 9:00 p.m. until 2:00 a.m. the 
following morning. Setup would begin at 8:00 p.m., and the cart would be removed by 3:00 a.m. after 
each shift and stored off site out of public view. The Gold Pan Saloon is identified as the commissary for 
water, ware washing, grease, and trash. No permanent structures or utility connections are proposed. 
Staff Comments 
Pursuant to Section 9-1-5, Class C Development, subsection H, a small vendor cart is processed as a 
Class C development permit with the public notice requirements of a Class B development permit. 
Land Use (Policies 2/A & 2/R): Vendor carts are only allowed in Land Use Districts that allow or 
recommend commercial uses. This property is within Land Use District 19, which recommends 
commercial uses.   
Per the Breckenridge Land Use Guidelines, District 19:  District 19 is the community focal point and 
primary center of commercial activity, prominent for its historic character.  It is preferred that the 
District remain a center of retail trade and services, with a pedestrian orientation.  Commercial 
Page 6 of 18
activities, particularly those which contribute to the solidarity of the central business district are 
encouraged.  Ideally, this includes retail trade uses which are associated with pedestrian traffic areas.   
Staff finds this proposal meets the guidelines established for Land Use District 19. The proposed vendor 
cart is consistent with the commercial and pedestrian oriented character of LUD 19 and staff has no 
concerns. 
Parking (Policy 18/A): Staff has confirmed that the existing parking spaces in the rear of the Gold Pan 
Saloon are not required parking for the property, as this property has paid into the parking service district 
to account for their required commercial spaces. The proposed vendor cart will therefore not occupy or 
displace required off-street parking. Section 9-3-11(E) allows an off-street parking space that is not 
required by the Code to be used for any lawful purpose. Staff finds the proposed placement acceptable 
from a parking standpoint. 
Vendor Carts (Policy 49/A): This policy addresses all aspects of vendor carts, including architecture, 
materials, colors, seating, signage, extension cords, storage, etc. If items are not addressed by this policy, 
then other Development Code polices may apply. Where this policy is more restrictive, then this policy 
applies.  
The proposed vendor cart, of approximately 32 square feet, will be located in the rear patio of the Gold 
Pan Saloon at 103 N. Main Street and provide late-night food service on Friday and Saturday nights from 
9:00 p.m. until 2:00 a.m. the following morning. The applicant has identified a potential to adjust hours in 
the future once the cart is established. The cart has a stainless steel exterior and will be wrapped with 
vinyl graphics depicting iron-oxide panels, dark iron bands, and brass rivets to resemble a historic mining 
ore cart. Staff finds the cart to meet the character requirements. The cart is on wheels and will be removed 
from the property and stored out of public view after each night of operation. 
A maximum of four small vendor carts may be permitted within the Conservation District. Presently, 
there is only one permitted small vendor cart within the District. If approved, this application will make 
two of four carts permitted.   
1.  General Design Standards - Within the Conservation District: The following general design 
standards apply as indicated to large vendor carts and small vendor carts located within the 
Conservation District: 
A. All large vendor carts and small vendor carts shall be designed to blend in with the 
existing historic character. This shall be accomplished through the proper use of architecture, 
materials and site planning. In the Conservation District, large vendor carts shall complement 
the surrounding building character through the use of high quality materials and detailing. 
Placing a large vendor cart in an unfinished vacant lot with no site improvements is prohibited.  
The proposed vendor cart will be located on a developed lot behind the Gold Pan Saloon. The cart is 
designed to resemble a historic mining ore cart, which staff finds appropriate to the Town’s mining 
history and existing historic character. There is existing precedent with the Jerky Cart, a small vendor cart 
located at 100 S Main Street, that allows for vinyl wrapping of small vendor carts that mimics historic 
materials on the exterior of a small vendor cart. 
B.  All large vendor carts and small vendor carts must be constructed of professional quality for 
use as a food vending cart.  
The Iron Kart is a commercially manufactured food vending cart constructed with a steel frame and 
stainless steel exterior. Staff finds the proposed cart meets the professional quality requirement. 
C.  All large vendor carts and small vendor carts must be maintained in good working condition 
with no broken or rusty parts. All exterior materials must be kept clean and in a neat appearance. 
Page 7 of 18
No rusty or broken metal or chipped or broken wood is allowed. All exposed edges must be 
finished. All wood details and finishes must be suitable for long wear in an exterior location. All 
detailing, construction and finishing shall be done in a craftsman like manner. 
The applicant will be required to maintain the cart in good working condition and keep all exterior 
materials clean and in a neat appearance. 
Specific Design Standards for Small Vendor Carts: 
1. Small vendor carts must be located on private property. Complies. 
 
2. Small vendor carts may only sell food and beverages in forms suited for immediate consumption. 
Complies – Late night eats and treats for immediate consumption will be served from the 
cart.  
 
3. Small vendor carts must be removed from its site and properly stored out of public view each day. 
Complies - The cart will be removed from the property and stored off-site each night.  
 
4. If a small vendor cart is connected to the Town’s municipal water system, the owner must pay 
water plant investment fees. If a small vendor cart is connected to the Town’s municipal water 
system, it must also be connected to the public sanitation system. Complies - The cart is not 
connected to the water or sewer system. 
 
5. If a small vendor cart uses a commissary kitchen, the commissary kitchen must be identified on 
the vendor cart permit application. If the commissary kitchen changes during the term of the 
permit, the small vendor cart permit holder must notify the Director within 10 days of the date of 
the change. Complies - The application identifies the Gold Pan Saloon as the commissary 
kitchen. 
 
6. Umbrellas may be used on a small vendor cart. Tents on or at small vendor carts are prohibited. 
Complies - No tents or umbrellas associated with the vendor cart specifically are proposed. 
The Gold Pan Saloon, a separate commercial business, will maintain the right to have 
umbrellas or shade structures on the patio. 
 
7. All signage must be attached to small vendor cart.  Free standing signage on or for a small vendor 
cart is prohibited. The applicant must apply for a separate sign permit.  
 
8. All storage boxes, cartons, and coolers used in connection with the operation of a small vendor 
cart shall be hidden from public view. Complies - All storage is inside the vendor cart. 
 
9. No decks, tables, or outdoor seating are allowed for a small vendor cart, except one seat for the 
operator of the small vendor cart. Complies - No deck, tables, or customer seating are 
proposed for the cart. The Gold Pan Saloon, a separate commercial business, will maintain 
the right to have tables or customer seating on the patio. 
 
 
10. No external piping or plumbing is allowed. Complies - no permanent water or sewer hookup 
is proposed. 
 
11. Extension cords may be used for a small vendor cart only if the cord is not located in an area 
where the public walks, and the placement of the extension cord does not create a public safety 
Page 8 of 18
hazard. Complies - The applicant proposes a self-contained 12-volt battery power and 
propane to power the cooking appliances within the cart. No extension cord is proposed. 
Any future cord must avoid areas where the public walks and must not create a public 
safety hazard. 
 
12. Small vendor carts must be on wheels. Complies - The vendor cart is on wheels.  
 
13. Owners of small vendor carts must obtain and maintain in full force and effect throughout the 
permit a valid Town of Breckenridge business license. Complies – Applicant has been in 
discussion with staff regarding business licensing. A condition of approval requires the 
operator to obtain and maintain a valid Town business license throughout the permit term. 
 
14. All signs for a small vendor cart shall be subject to the Breckenridge Sign Code. The maximum 
allowed sign area for a small vendor cart is 66% of the linear frontage of the cart.  The concept 
drawings include lettering, logos, and a menu graphic without a complete dimensioned sign-
area calculation. A separate sign permit and compliance with Chapter 15 are required. 
 
15. The operator of a small vendor cart shall comply with all applicable health regulations with 
respect to the operation of the small vendor cart. Complies. 
Point Analysis (Section: 9-1-17-3): Staff finds no reason to assess positive or negative points for this 
application.  The application complies with all applicable absolute and relative policies. 
Staff Decision 
The Planning Department has approved The Iron Kart Small Vendor Cart, PL-2026-0251, at 103 N. Main 
Street, with the attached findings and conditions.
