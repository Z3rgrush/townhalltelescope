1) Call to Order, Roll Call 
Chair Conway called the regular meeting of Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to order at 
9:00 am. The following members answered roll call: Kelly Lovely, Garren Riechel

were absent. 
 
2) Approval of Minutes 
With no changes or corrections to the meeting minutes of July 21, 2026, Chair 
Conway declared they would stand as approved. 
 
3) Approval of Agenda 
With no changes to the agenda, Chair Conway declared the agenda would stand as 
approved. 
 
4) Consent Calendar     
a) Liquor Consent Calendar 
 
The Liquor Consent Calendar was approved as submitted. 
 
b)       Marijuana Consent Calendar - None 
            
c)       Tobacco Consent Calendar - None 
  
5)     Public Hearing; Action Items  
 a)  New License Hearing: 
  Room 202 LLC d/b/a Room 202 
100 N Main St, Unit 202 
Tavern License 
 
  Lillian Gaspar and Edith Pech, Owners, were present on behalf of the application.  
  Ms. Gaspar stated she has owned and operated Nick & Willy’s in Silverthorne for  
  four (4) years and Peppino’s in Frisco for two (2) years, that also have liquor  
  licenses. This new business will sell cocktails and some food. The Authority  
  asked when they are hoping to open. Ms. Gaspar stated they hope to open in 
  October or November. The Authority asked about hours and food. Ms. Gaspar  
  stated they are thinking their hours will be 9:00AM – 11:00PM and for food, they  
  will  have a small kitchen that will offer waffles, bagels and snack type foods like  
  tortilla chips and guacamole. The Authority asked if they have had any liquor  
  issues at their other establishments which she stated they have not and added that 
  all staff will be TIPS trained.  
 
  Authority Member Lovely made a motion to approve the new tavern liquor  
  license application. A roll call vote was taken, and the motion passed 3-0. 
  
6)  Transfer of Ownership Applications - None

8) Adjournment 
 With no further business to discuss, Chair Conway adjourned the meeting at 9:19 am.
