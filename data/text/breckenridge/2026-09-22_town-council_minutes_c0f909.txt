B) RESOLUTIONS, SERIES 2026 
1) RESOLUTION NO. 18, SERIES 2026-A RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF SUMMIT 
COUNTY'S REFERRED MEASURE IA 
Mayor Owens read the title into the record 
Shannon Haynes, Town Manager, presented a resolution in support of the Strong Futures 
ballot initiative, referred Measure IA. Haynes explained that the initiative, originally 
passed in 2018, provides funding for several countywide programs and is currently set to 
expire in 2028. She noted that the measure will appear on November's ballot and, if 
approved, would extend the funding in perpetuity without any new or increased taxes. 
Haynes stated that Council had expressed a desire to formally support the measure; 
therefore, staff prepared the proposed resolution. 
Council Member Gerard moved to approve RESOLUTION NO. 18, SERIES 2026 - A 
RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF SUMMIT COUNTY'S REFERRED MEASURE IA. 
Council Member Lutke seconded the motion. 
The motion passed 7 - 0. 
2) RESOLUTION NO. 19, SERIES 2026 -A RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF SUMMIT 
SCHOOL DISTRICT'S MILL LEVY OVERRIDGE BALLOT MEASURE 4A 
Mayor Owens read the title into the record 
Shannon Haynes, Town Manager, stated that Council heard a presentation this afternoon 
from the Summit County School District regarding Ballot Measure 4A, which would 
increase property taxes by approximately $5 .9 million per year. The funds, if approved, 
would be used to attract and retain high-quality teachers and staff to strengthen the 
District's PK-12 programs. Haynes noted that Council expressed interest in affirmatively 
supporting this ballot measure; therefore, staff prepared the proposed resolution. 
Council Member Gerard moved to RESOLUTION NO. 19, SERIES 2026 -A 
RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF SUMMIT SCHOOL DISTRICT'S MILL LEVY 
OVERRIDGE BALLOT MEASURE 4A. Council Member Carleton seconded the 
motion. 
The motion passed 7 - 0.

XI) OTHER MATTERS 
Council Member Beckerman reported that the U.S. Forest Service is proposing 
amendments to the Travel Management Plan that could undermine local decision-making

changes.
