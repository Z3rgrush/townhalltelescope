Chair Conway called the regular meeting of Tuesday, September 15, 2026, to order at

absent.

7)  Informal Discussion 
  a) Police Department Update 
There was no police update. 
 
  b)   Town Attorney Update 
   Attorney Andrew  Armstrong provided an update on a new permit recently  
   approved by the State, which allows spirituous liquor manufacturers to serve  
   alcohol beyond the products they produce. Armstrong noted that staff are   
   currently developing an application process for implementing this new permit.  
 
   Chair Conway asked whether Breckenridge Distillery would be eligible for the  
   new permit. Attorney Armstrong confirmed that they would be eligible to apply  
   once a formal process is in place.  
 
   Authority Member Lovely asked whether manufacturers would then hold two  
   licenses. Attorney Armstrong clarified that manufacturers would retain their state- 
   level manufacturing license and obtain a local permit. 
 
   Town Clerk Watson stated that she had spoken with Breckenridge Distillery  
   regarding the new permit and informed them that a local application process  
   would not be finalized until the State completes its rule‑making process.                         
 
c) Town Clerk Update 
Mae Watson provided an update regarding a complaint received from a member 
of the public about the Breckenridge Outdoor Education Center’s Breckenridge 
Wine Classic event. The complaint raised concerns about over‑service and 
insufficient security. Watson explained that the Breckenridge Wine Classic had 
submitted a complete application, including a control plan and a map outlining 
security measures. Because the application met all legal requirements, it was 
approved. She also noted that event administrators responded directly to the 
complainant, and staff determined that no additional response from the Town was 
necessary.
