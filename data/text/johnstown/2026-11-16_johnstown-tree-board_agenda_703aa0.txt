Johnstown Tree Board Agenda Communication 
Agenda Date: November 16, 2026 
Subject: Arbor Day Celebrations: Tree Give Away and Tree Planting 
Attachments: 1.   2026 Tree Give Away 
2.   2026 Arbor Day Tree Planting 
3.   Proclamation 
Presented by: Matt LeCerf, Town Manager 
Agenda Item Description: 
The Town successfully completed its annual tree voucher and Arbor Day activities. Free tree 
vouchers were distributed at Town Hall on April 4th from 9:00–10:00 AM, with additional availability 
from April 6th–10th during regular business hours (8:00 AM–5:00 PM). Vouchers were offered on a 
first-come, first-served basis while supplies lasted. 
 
As part of Arbor Day, the Town hosted a tree pickup event on April 24th starting at 1:00 PM, during 
which 200 were made available to participating residents, with 188 trees picked up during the event. 
 
The celebration concluded with the Official Tree Planting Ceremony held on October 16th at 4:00 
PM at Sunrise Park. Mayor Duncan presented an Arbor Day proclamation, commemorating the 
Town’s continued commitment to environmental stewardship. 
 
We anticipate participation at the same volume as 2026, estimating the Town will purchase 200 
trees of various types that will be made available to residents in Johnstown. The final quantity of tree 
purchases will be based on the cost per tree. This fantastic program continues to be an initiative that 
is strongly supported and appreciated by the Community. In total, we have $$69,960.00 proposed in 
FY 2027 for tree related activities.

TOWN OF JOHNSTOWN, COLORADO 
PROCLAMATION 
ARBOR DAY CELEBRATION 
 
WHEREAS, in 1872, the Nebraska Board of Agriculture established a special day to be 
 set aside for the planting of trees; and  
 
WHEREAS, this holiday, called Arbor Day, was first observed with the planting of more  
  than a million trees in Nebraska; and 
 
WHEREAS,  Arbor Day is now observed throughout the nation and the world; and 
 
WHEREAS,  trees can be a solution to combating climate change by reducing the erosion  
  of our precious topsoil by wind and water, cutting heating and cooling costs,  
  moderating the temperature, cleaning the air, producing life-giving oxygen,  
  and providing habitat for wildlife; and 
 
WHEREAS,  trees are a renewable resource giving us paper, wood for our homes, fuel for  
  our fires, and countless other wood products; and 
WHEREAS,  trees in our city increase property values, enhance the economic vitality of  
  business areas, and beautify our community; and 
WHEREAS,  trees – wherever they are planted – are a source of joy and spiritual renewal. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT PROCLAIMED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF 
JOHNSTOWN, COLORADO THAT: 
I, Michael P. Duncan, Mayor of the Town of Johnstown, on behalf of the Johnstown Town 
Council and the Johnstown Community do hereby proclaim October 16, 2026, as Arbor Day in 
the Town of Johnstown and I urge all citizens to celebrate 
Arbor Day 
And to support efforts to protect our trees and woodlands. Furthermore, I urge all citizens to 
plant trees to gladden the heart and promote the well-being of this and future generations. 
 
 
        
By: __________________________________ 
       Michael P. Duncan, Mayor

Agenda Item Description: 
The Town has made significant investments in tree maintenance and plantings in 2026. As part of 
the Town’s annual Tree City USA program application, we are required to invest a minimum of $2.00 
per capita ($44,886.00 total). While the Johnstown population is debatable based on various 
demographic estimates, we have inserted a number of 22,443 population for the current year. During 
2026, the Town participated in both an Arbor Day Celebration on October 16, 2026, as well as 
provided trees to the Community, ordering 200 trees for giveaway. This was supported by the Trees 
Across Colorado Programming. The table below illustrates the allocation of funds thus far for the 
calendar year in the various categories. 
 
 
For FY 2026, we have the following allocations made for tree maintenance: 
Tree Focused Purpose Expenditures (through October 1, 2026) 
Maintenance $34,083.00 (plus an additional $5,000 for general tree care 
at the Town Hall and Police Department by the end of 2026) 
Planting $9,491.78 
Community Tree Program $17,260.00 
Total $65,834.78
