Proclamation in Recognition of 
Small Business Saturday   
 
WHEREAS,  the Town of Johnstown celebrates our local small businesses and the 
contributions they make to our community; and 
 
WHEREAS,  small businesses are the backbone of our community, contributing significantly to 
the growth and vibrancy of Johnstown; and 
 
WHEREAS,  the Town of Johnstown supports our local businesses and recognizes the 
importance of Small Business Saturday as a time to encourage residents to shop 
locally and invest in the success of our neighbors and community; and 
 
WHEREAS,  according to the U.S. Small Business Administration, there are 33.2 million small 
businesses in the United States, and they represent a significant portion of the 
workforce and economy; and 
 
WHEREAS,  Small Business Saturday has been recognized as an annual event across the 
country to encourage residents to support the small businesses that create jobs, 
foster economic growth, and enhance the quality of life in our towns and cities; 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF 
JOHNSTOWN, COLORADO THAT:  
 
I, Michael Duncan, Mayor of the Town of Johnstown, Colorado, on the 2nd day of November 
2026, do hereby proclaim Saturday, November 28th, 2026 as: 
 
Small Business Saturday 
In the Town of Johnstown and urge the residents of our community, and communities across the 
country, to support small businesses and merchants on Small Business Saturday and throughout 
the year.

THE JOHNSTOWN VILLAGE METROPOLITAN DISTRICT NO. 3.

Colorado (the “Town Council”); and

director in §32-1-905(2.5), C.R.S. have been met.

Woranee D. Raj and Xaq O’Riordain to the Board of Directors of the District for a term to 
May 2027.

RESOLUTION NO. 2026 - 47  
A RESOLUTION OF THE TOWN COUNCIL FOR THE TOWN OF JOHNSTOWN, 
COLORADO APPROVING THE PURCHASE OF FIFTEEN (15) COLORADO-BIG 
THOMPSON (C-BT) PROJECT UNITS FROM  
JOHNSTON-TAYLOR FAMILY PROPERTIES, LLLP 
 
WHEREAS, the Town of Johnstown, Colorado, (the “Town”) is a Colorado home rule 
municipality, duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of Colorado and the Town’s 
Home Rule Charter; and  
WHEREAS, the Town Council constitutes the legislative body of the Town with authority 
to set the policies of the Town; and 
WHEREAS, the Town’s regulations and policies provide for the Town to acquire by 
purchase or by dedication from developers allotment contracts in the Northern Colorado Water 
Conservancy District; and 
WHEREAS, Town management has been negotiating the procurement of additional 
Colorado-Big Thompson (C-BT) project units to add to the Town’s water portfolio; and 
WHEREAS, Town management has come to an agreement with Johnson-Taylor Family 
Properties, LLLP, of Loveland, Colorado, to purchase fifteen (15) C-BT Project units, at the cost 
of $60,000 per unit, for a total of $900,000; and 
WHEREAS, it is in the benefit of the Town’s citizens, residents, property owners, 
businesses, developers and visitors to purchase additional water for the Town of Johnstown’s 
allotment and use; and 
WHEREAS, Town Council wishes to authorize and approve the purchase of 15 C-BT 
Project units from Johnstown-Taylor Family Properties, LLLP, for $60,000 per unit, for a total of 
$900,000; and  
WHEREAS, Town Council authorizes the Town Manager to sign the applicable Purchase 
and Sale Agreement (PSA) and to execute all necessary documents associated with said sale, 
pursuant to approval by the Town Attorney and in conjunction with the requirements of the 
Northern Colorado Water Conservancy District. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE 
TOWN OF JOHNSTOWN, COLORADO: 
Section 1.  The foregoing recitals are hereby incorporated as conclusions, determinations, 
facts, and findings of the Town Council. 
Section 2.  The Town Council hereby approves and authorizes the purchase fifteen (15) C-
BT Project units, from Johnson-Taylor Family Properties, LLLP, at the cost of 
$60,000 per unit, for a total of $900,000. 
Page 17 of 518
 
Section 3.  The Town Council authorizes the Town Manager to sign the applicable Purchase 
and Sale Agreement (PSA) associated with said transaction, pursuant to approval 
by the Town Attorney and in conjunction with the requirements of the Northern 
Colorado Water Conservancy District. 
Section 4.  The Town Council further authorizes the Town Manager to execute all additional 
necessary documents associated with the sale of said 15 C-BT Project units, 
pursuant to approval by the Town Attorney and in conjunction with the 
requirements of the Northern Colorado Water Conservancy District. 
PASSED, SIGNED, APPROVED, AND ADOPTED this 2nd day of November, 2026.

Agenda Item Description: 
Enclosed for your review and consideration is a Professional Services Agreement (PSA) with 
Connell Resources, Inc. for Construction Manager at Risk (CMAR) Design Phase Services for the 
Raw Water Transmission Project (Project). 
  
In September 2022, Town Council authorized a PSA with Civil Resources for Engineering Design 
Services for a new 11 mile, 30-inch raw water transmission main and pump station from Lonetree 
Reservoir to the Town's water treatment plant to increase the volume of raw water available for 
treatment. As the Project has progressed to its final stage of design, engaging a CMAR is essential 
to ensure cost certainty, reduce project risks, and address constructability challenges for this 
complex infrastructure project. 
 
On July 9, 2026, the Town advertised a Request for Proposals (RFP) for a CMAR to provide Design 
Phase Services and potentially Construction Phase Services for the Project. The Town received 
eight proposals. The Selection Committee evaluated each proposal using the weighted criteria 
outlined in the RFP and assigned rankings on a defined scale from 1 to 5 for each criterion. 
 
Based on independent evaluations, the Selection Committee ranked all firms and proceeded to 
interviews with the two highest-ranked firms, which demonstrated a clear separation from the 
remaining proposals. Client and key staff reference checks were completed for both firms, with 
consistently strong feedback on performance, quality, reliability, and trust. For interviews, the 
Selection Committee scored firms on a defined scale from 1 to 5 on five weighted criteria including 
Overall Presentation, Project Team, GMP Approach, Value Engineering, and Q&A. 
 
Based on their detailed strategic approach, relevant experience addressing the specific challenges 
of this project, and strong interview performance, the Selection Committee recommends awarding 
Connell Resources, Inc. the CMAR Design Phase Services Agreement. Connell—partnered with 
Moltz Construction, Inc. for the pump station scope—brings more than 400 experienced local crew 
members in Northern Colorado, proven CMAR experience with the Town, and a demonstrated ability 
Page 29 of 518
to successfully deliver complex infrastructure projects. 
 
Assuming the Town and Connell Resources, Inc. reach an agreement on a Guaranteed Maximum 
Price (GMP) and associated contract terms, the Town intends to proceed with Connell Resources, 
Inc. for construction of the proposed improvements. Construction is anticipated to begin immediately 
following the design phase with Substantial Completion planned for Spring 2029. A summary of the 
anticipated project timeline is provided below. 
 
 
Anticipated Design Phase CMAR Award November 2, 2026 
Final Design Phase Nov 2026 - Feb 2027 
GMP Preparation and Review Dec 2026 - Feb 2027 
Bid Package Preparation and Bidding Dec 2026 - Feb 2027 
Anticipated CMAR Construction Award February 15, 2027 
Early Work Packages, Equipment and Material Procurement Feb 2027 
Construction Phase Spring 2027 - Spring 2029

ITEM F - COORDINATION WITH OPERATIONAL STAFF, START-UP SUPPORT AND TRAINING
PLAN
The Team will work closely with operational staff throughout construction, commissioning, and startup to
ensure a smooth transition with minimal disruption to ongoing operations. Startup activities will be planned
and coordinated in advance with plant personnel to align with operational requirements and system
demands.
Prior to commissioning, we will conduct operator training covering system components, operating
procedures, controls, safety requirements, and routine maintenance. During startup, qualified project
personnel and equipment representatives will be onsite to provide operational support, troubleshoot
issues, verify system performance, and help operators become familiar with the new equipment. Training
documentation, O&M manuals, and as-built information will be provided to support long-term operation of
the facility. The overall goal is for operations staff to feel confident and fully prepared to operate and
maintain the new infrastructure upon project completion.
