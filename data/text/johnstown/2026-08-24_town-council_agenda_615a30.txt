1. Firm's Interest & Overall Approach 
Hoffmann, Parker, Wilson & Carberry, P.C. was formed in 1985 with the specific goal of 
providing legal services to Colorado local governments.  We currently work with over 40 different 
local governments in Colorado in various capacities, from general counsel to special counsel to 
hearing officer.  Our firm has for many years been listed in the Martindale -Hubbell Bar Register 
of Preeminent Lawyers in Municipal and Zoning Law, and the firm has maintained Martindale -
Hubbell's highest rating, AV  Preeminent, since 1985.  No member of this firm has ever been 
involved in any disciplinary proceeding.  We have worked very hard to develop a reputation for 
quality, full-service representation at reasonable rates, and we believe we have succeeded in this 
regard.  Our firm represents a wide variety of municipalities, both home rule and statutory, small 
and large, and rural and urban.  Overall, the firm has over 60 municipal clients.  Each client is 
unique, but some issues recur.   
While the entirety of the firm's resources would be available to assist, we believe it is 
important for clients to have consistent personnel.  Thus, we propose that director Hilary Graham 
and associates Nicholas Hartman and Zachary Zalesne would serve as the primary contacts for the

2. Firm Qualifications & Experience 
Hoffmann, Parker, Wilson & Carberry, P.C. includes six directors, three "of counsel " 
attorneys, two senior associates, seven associate attorneys, and four paralegals/legal assistants.  As 
a firm, we have nearly 200 years of combined legal experience.  We are a full -service local 
government law firm, meaning that we provide representation in all areas of law  and litigation 
related to local governments, other than water law and public finance.  Our services include general 
counsel services such as attending meetings of local governing bodies, planning commissions and 
boards of adjustment; advising elected officials and staff on legal issues ; drafting and reviewing 
contracts, intergovernmental agreements, ordinances, resolutions, deeds, and other legal 
documents; representing clients  in litigation before state courts and administrative bodies; 
coordinating representation with the Colorado Intergovernmental Risk Sharing Agency (CIRSA); 
advising planning commissions and staff on land use and planning matters; advising municipalities 
on real estate matters, including acquisition of property and property interests, including 
easements, rights -of-way, licenses and encroachments; review of title matters; advising 
municipalities on the operation of utilities and enterprises recognized under TABOR; advising 
municipal staff concerning nuisance abatement matters; advising on personnel and employment 
matters; and advising on elections, open records and open meetings matters. 
We are passionate about delivering exceptional legal services tailored to the needs of the 
individual client.  The size of our firm allows us to provide expertise in various practice areas 
while still delivering highly personalized services. 
Our firm has extensive experience providing general counsel services to home rule and  
statutory municipalities.  From the day -to-day questions of how to interpret a municipal code  or 
how to amend a charter, to complex constitutional issues, over the course of the firm's history we 
have worked on nearly every conceivable municipal legal issue .  But we do not rely on the past 
for our reputation.  We strive to be at the forefront of current legal issues facing municipalities and 
to continuously earn the respect of our clients and  our colleagues.  Simply put, there is no other 
firm in the State with the same level of experience and knowledge concerning representation of 
local governments.

3. Approach to Service Delivery 
We believe it is important for clients to have consistent personnel.  Thus, we propose Hilary 
Graham as Town Attorney with backup and support by Nicholas Hartman and Zachary Zalesne.  
Hilary would be the point of contact , and all three named attorneys would collaborate to deliver 
timely legal work to the Town.  Hilary would attend regular Town Council meetings in-person on 
1st and 3rd Mondays of each month and could also attend Town Council work sessions as requested.  
Résumés for Hilary, Nicholas, and Zachary, along with the firm's résumé, are included with this 
response.  The firm's résumé includes a list of clients that the firm currently represents or has 
recently represented. 
Along with the three named attorneys, the strength of this firm's expertise as a whole and 
our depth of resources are  part of our representation.  We have several attorneys with notable 
expertise in various areas of municipal law, such as election law,  police legal advising, land use, 
and open records compliance .  We would call on these attorneys as needed.  The  three named 
attorneys would be  available for meetings, training, and consultations, in -person or remotely, at 
the request of the Town Council or Town staff.  We can be present at Town Hall in less than an 
hour, if needed, and continue to believe there are benefits to interacting in-person. 
Most commonly our clients seek ongoing consultation through email.  We have a practice 
of responding to inquiries from elected officials and staff within 24 hours and typically within the 
same business day.  The initial response may not include a fully researched answer, but it will 
provide an opportunity to discuss an issue, learn about the Town's needs, and create a plan for a 
full and timely response. We encourage our clients to involve us early and often, as preventing

4. Fee Structure 
The attorneys and staff providing legal services to the Town would work as independent 
contractors.  Our firm has worked very hard to develop and maintain expertise while also 
maintaining reasonable hourly rates .  O ur clients often find that we can do work much more 
efficiently than other firms lacking our experience with local government issues.  For the Town of 
Johnstown, we would propose a  rate of $275.00 per hour for attorneys  for legal work, including 
meeting attendance, and $150.00 per hour for paralegals for legal work.  Litigation matters, if any, 
would be billed at a rate of $325.00 per hour for attorneys and $175.00 per hour for paralegal work.  
In matters for which fees are passed through to developers, the applicable attorney rate would also 
be $325.00 per hour for attorneys and per hour and $175.00 per hour for paralegal work.  We do 
not charge for legal assistant time but do charge for actual costs incurred, such as filing fees, copies, 
postage, and computerized legal research.  Travel time is billed at half the applicable rate.  We can 
commit to these rates through 2027. 
We typically send invoices on or before the tenth (10th) day of each month and payment is 
due on or before the last day of the month, within which the invoice is sent.  Our invoices detail 
the date upon which the work was accomplished, the name of the attorney, a description of the 
work, the time (in tenths of an hour) spent on the work, and the total amount due for the particular 
work performed.  At the Town's request, we will provide separate accounting for specific activities, 
such as meetings, litigation, development work, or work for particular Town departments.
