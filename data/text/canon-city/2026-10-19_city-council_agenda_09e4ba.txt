OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
PROCLAMATION
National Code Compliance Month
October 2026
WHEREAS, Code Enforcement Officers provide for the safety, health, welfare, and quality of life
of residents and visitors through the fair and consistent application of local codes and
ordinances; and
WHEREAS, Code Enforcement Officers address a wide range of community concerns, including
property maintenance, nuisance conditions, zoning violations, abandoned or inoperable vehicles, 
weeds and vegetation, animal- related concerns, and other conditions that may negatively affect
the safety and appearance of neighborhoods; and
WHEREAS, effective code enforcement emphasizes education, communication, problem- solving, 
and voluntary compliance whenever possible, while ensuring that appropriate enforcement
measures remain available when necessary to protect the community; and
WHEREAS, the work of Code Enforcement Officers contributes to safer neighborhoods, protects
property values, reduces hazardous and substandard conditions, improves the appearance of
the community, and promotes a high quality of life for all residents; and
WHEREAS, Code Enforcement Officers are dedicated and highly responsible public servants who
routinely address complex and challenging situations while striving to provide professional, 
respectful, and equitable service to the community; and
WHEREAS, communities throughout the United States recognize the month of October as
National Code Compliance Month to increase awareness of the important role of code
enforcement professionals and to recognize their contributions to the communities they serve; 
and
WHEREAS, the City of Cañon City recognizes and appreciates the dedication, professionalism, 
and commitment of its Code Enforcement personnel and their continued efforts to encourage
voluntary compliance, address community concerns, and make Cañon City a safe, clean, and
welcoming place to live, work, and visit. 
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Phil Lund, Mayor of the City of Cañon City, on behalf of the City Council
and the residents of this community, do hereby proclaim the month of October 2026 as National
Code Compliance Month in the City of Cañon City and encourage our residents, businesses, and
community partners to join us in recognizing and expressing appreciation for the dedicated
professionals who serve our community through code enforcement and compliance. 
Dated this 19th day of October, 2026. 
PhilLund, Mayor
