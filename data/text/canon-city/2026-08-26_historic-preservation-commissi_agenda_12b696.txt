HISTORIC PRESERVATION COMMISSION 
REGULAR MEETING 
CITY HALL COUNCIL CHAMBERS – A001 
Wednesday, June 27, 2026 
4:00 PM 
Minutes 
 
1. CALL TO ORDER: Commission Chair called the meeting to order at 4pm.  
 
Present: COMMISSION CHAIR LIMON, VICE CHAIR MILLARD, COMISSION MEMBERS, 
JENKINS, MCPHERSON, AND WINTZ 
Absent: MEMBERS FLICK, MADONE, AND CITY COUNCIL MEMBER MCCULLAR 
 
2. APPROVAL of MINUTES: Motion to approve the minutes from May 27, 2026. Motion  
passed with majority vote.  
 
3. SAVING PLACES “On the Road” DISCUSSION: Commission Member Jenkins presented the 
opportunity for Cañon City and the HPC to host an upcoming Saving Places “On the Road” 
conference through Colorado Preservation Incorporated. Motion to form a committee to 
review the application for hosting the conference presented. Motion passed with majority 
vote.  
 
4. 2027 PROPOSED BUDGET: Staff presented a proposed budget for the HPC. Motion to 
approve 2027 Budget of $5400 was presented and motion passed with majority vote.  
 
5. DESIGN GUIDELINES DISCUSSION & REVIEW: Staff liaison announced the adoption of the 
proposed Historic Preservation Design Guidelines by City Council vote at the last regular 
meeting.  
 
6. CLG GRANT PROJECT UPDATE : City staff presented a brief update of progress on the CLG 
Grant Funded Survey Project.  
 
7. ANNOUNCEMENTS  
City staff mentioned an upcoming CLG Webinar in August, the Farmers Market through 
September, upcoming 150/250 Tavern Talks, and the museum’s Historic Bike Tour on July 
23rd.  
 
8. PUBLIC COMMENTS : No public comments.  
 
9. ADJOURN: Meeting adjourned at 4:28 pm.  
 
Submitted by  
Lisa Studts, Museum Director & HPC Liaison
