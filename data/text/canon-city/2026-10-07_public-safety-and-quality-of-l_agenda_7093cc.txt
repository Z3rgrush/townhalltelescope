Recommendations for Future Public Restrooms
Successful Restrooms:
1.Are vandal/tamper resistant
2.Have infrequent maintenance issues and/or ease of repair and maintenance 
already built into the budget
3.Stay open when people need them — daylight hours and year round
The underlying keys to success are:
1.Frequent cleaning
2.Fast maintenance/grafﬁti removal
3.Well lit, inside and outside - (Some have used blue lighting which makes veins 
harder to see!)
4.High foot trafﬁc area
5.Open grating at top and bottom for single units and/or individual stalls
6.Exterior handwashing stations
7.High ceilings (cannot easily access overhead lighting or get on roof)
8.Walls that are hard to permanently tag or can be easily replaced such as FRP
9.Centrally located pipe chase for ease of maintenance, isolation of tamper prone 
plumbing and better weatherization options, built large enough to store tools and 
cleaning supplies.
10.Consider metal frame construction + outside paneling that is made locally 
(cheaper than CMU).
11.Consider how to prevent gutter clogging with trash and needles.
12.Consider having police open/close, and have deadbolts that don’t have the tail, 
so they can’t be locked from the inside.
13.Consider having skylight tubes for backup lighting.
14.Polished concrete ﬂoor, with a ﬂoor drain
15.Changing tables for both genders
