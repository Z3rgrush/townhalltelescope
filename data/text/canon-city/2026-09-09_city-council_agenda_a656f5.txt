Clocktower Plaza Information 
The following memo is prepared to present information in support of the Special City Council 
meeting scheduled for Wednesday, September 9, 2026, to discuss options and provide 
direction on Clocktower Plaza. 
Design Options 
There are two primary design options presented for consideration, with renderings of both 
options attached.   
The first option displayed in the attachments is the design that was prepared and solicited for 
bids in April of 2026.  It represents the final product of the planning efforts and public 
engagement for the site that commenced in 2023.  The design sent for bids was reduced from 
the original concept to deliver a project within the available budget which is discussed later.  
The concept includes full utilization of the lot as a public plaza with a pavilion/stage, 
landscaping, sunshades (Alternate 1), and a restroom (Alternate 2).  
The second option displayed in the attachments is a design that was developed in response 
to a joint Public Safety and Quality of Life / Finance, Budget, and Public Works Committee 
meeting held in August 2026.  The design roughly splits the site into two program areas, with 
the southern portion dedicated as public parking and the northern portion laid out as a public 
plaza.  The parking portion contains roughly 17 parking spaces, and the plaza portion is laid 
out with an intent to include the key elements of the first design (stage, restrooms, 
sunshades).  
Design Costs 
Estimates of design costs for the two primary options are outlined as follows: 
 
The first option displayed was solicited for bids in April 2026.  Pricing received from the two 
low bidders is as follows.  Staff have been in contact with both bidders, and both are still 
willing to honor their original bids from April, which is beyond the date required to hold bids in 
our contract documents.  Gordon Construction requested a $10,000 adjustment because of 
increased steel pricing, IS West offered to hold their original bid pricing as still valid.   
 
• Gordon Construction (TOTAL = $1,262,646.44) 
o Base Bid = $939,559.78 
o Alternate #1 = $170,095.35 
o Alternate #2 = $141,491.32 
o Adjustment $10,000 
 
• IS West (TOTAL = $1,169,037.18) 
o Base Bid = $971,290.03 
o Alternate #1 = $54,745.75 (Bidder Proposed Equal Alternative) 
o Alternate #2 = $143,001.40 
o Adjustment = $0 
 
In consideration of only awarding the Base Bid, Gordon Construction is the apparent low 
bidder.  In consideration of awarding the Base plus both Alternates IS West would be the 
apparent low bidder.   
 
Estimates for the second option were created based on utilizing the original bid pricing and 
adjusting for the changed design under two scenarios.  The first scenario being a true 
following of the alternative design concept inclusive of all elements.  Median pricing from the 
original bidding was used, with modifications as noted in the attached sheet (reduced stage 
size, reduced electrical package, elimination of landscaping, etc....).  The second option made 
similar considerations but fully eliminated the stage, restroom, and electrical package items 
from the bid leaving a more basic plaza area with shade and tables adjacent to the parking 
lot.  The method of estimating would need further refinement if this option were moved into 
full design/construction as it is based on pricing from the original design which may or may 
not be true of this alternate when it is finalized.    
 
• Alternative Design – With Stage & Bathrooms 
o Estimate = $936,805.86 
 
• Alternative Design – Without Stage & Bathroom 
o Estimate = $570,472.92 
 
A couple of additional notes for cost on the alternative option are as follows: 
 
• Selecting this option would require revising the design.  At a high level I would estimate 
this at roughly 10% of the construction cost so potentially somewhere in the range of 
$57-94K of costs to develop a bid ready design for the site based on the new concept. 
 
 
• The concept design did not include stormwater quality, which would likely be needed, 
some costs were included in the estimate to account for this, but it would likely come 
with a reduction in usable parking spaces as some spaces would need to be converted 
to stormwater use. 
 
At this conceptual stage I would estimate this portion of the project to cost us between $600-
$1M to develop a full design and construct.   
 
All cost estimate scenarios include roughly $50,000 of costs associated with 
testing/acceptance of construction materials, and funding the city is holding in reserve for 
potential environmental needs to handle a couple of underground storage tanks on site.   
 
 
Funding Scenarios 
Funding for the selected project varied based on the design option elected to move forward.  
Potential funding sources and various restrictions associated with each are listed below: 
 
• City General Fund = $600,000 
o This is a budgeted expense within the 2026 Budget and has been proposed as 
a placeholder of the same amount for inclusion in the draft 2027 Budget 
pending discussions.  These funds are 100% city dollars and could be used for 
whatever direction Council prefers. 
 
• DOLA Grant Funding = $350,000 
o This funding is the result of a grant award obtained by the City from the DOLA 
(Department of Local Affairs) issuance of an Energy and Mineral Impact Fund 
award to the city.  These funds are specific to the first design option as they 
were awarded for use in construction of a public plaza.  The funds are 
specifically prohibited for use in construction of Parking.  In the best-case 
scenario, which would require approval of a grant amendment from DOLA, we 
could potentially utilize up to 50% ($175,000) of these grant dollars for the 
second design option.   
 
• Donor Funding = $250,000 - $300,000 
o The city has received an offer from an anonymous donor to contribute between 
$250,000 to $300,000 towards the original design option.  To my knowledge 
these funds are specific to the original design intent.   
 
Pursuing the original design with full use of the general fund, grant funding, and donor funding 
would provide a project budget of up to $1,250,000 which is anticipated to be sufficient to 
cover the full cost of construction with all alternates, and the costs for material testing and 
handling of the underground storage tanks if encountered. 
 
Pursuing the alternative design with full use of the general fund, 50% of the grant funding, 
and 0% of the donor funding would provide a project budget of $775,00.  This figure is likely 
sufficient to construct a scaled down version of the alternates (no stage/pavilion, no restroom, 
 
no electrical) but is likely insufficient to allow construction of a full version of the concept 
without identifying additional funding.   
 
 
Timing Scenarios 
Timing will differ for each option.   
 
Under a scenario with construction of the original design, we will need to negotiate a new 
schedule with the selected Contractor.  Conceivably work could still be completed ahead of 
May 2027, but we would need to work with the selected vendor to negotiate that in more 
detail. 
 
Under the scenario with construction of the alternative design, we would first need to 
complete a detailed design which involves soliciting and awarding an updated design 
contract.  I would estimate that the design effort would take approximately 4-6 months.  Upon 
completion of that we would bid/award construction.  Construction would likely occur during 
the Fall 2027 – Spring 2028 window.   
 
 
Parking Impacts 
A brief analysis of parking counts in the area is included to aid in the discussion/consideration 
and to provide additional information on parking availability in the vicinity.   
 
• Original Count (2024 and Earlier) = 127 
 
• Estimated Count With Original Clocktower = 163 
 
• Estimated Count With Alternative Clocktower = 175 
 
 
Summary 
Staff desires to seek direction on which option to proceed with for the site, if any, and 
solicit any other feedback that council/public wishes to provide on this project.
