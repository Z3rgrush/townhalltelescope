CHAPTER 19.10 DRAINAGE 
 
 
1 
 
CHAPTER 19.10 DRAINAGE 
Sec. 19.10.010. Purpose and intent. 
The purpose of this chapter is to regulate maintenance of curbs, gutters, culverts, and ditches to protect 
public infrastructure, health, safety, and private property.  
Sec. 19.10.020. Definitions. 
For the purposes of this chapter, the following words and phrases shall have the meaning set forth in this 
section:  
A. "Culvert" means a buried pipe installed to convey drainage or irrigation water.  
AB. "Curb" means the concrete or asphalt vertical edge often accompanying a gutter constructed at the 
edge of a road to collect surface water and direct it away.  
BC. "Ditch" means a channel or trench or any part thereof within the City designed to manage surface 
water runoff or convey water for a specific purpose, such as for irrigation."Ditch " means a man-made 
channel or trench or any part thereof within the City designed to manage surface water runoff or 
convey water for a specific purpose, such as for irrigation.  "Ditch" means any narrow channel dug in 
the ground, typically used for drainage or irrigation alongside a road or the edge of a field.  
CD. "Gutter" means the concrete or asphalt shallow trough structure with an invert often accompanying a 
curb constructed at the edge of a road or crossing a road to collect surface water and direct it away.  
DE. "Obstruction" means any dam, wall embankment, levee, dike, pile, abutment, projection, excavation, 
channel rectification, building, fence, stockpile, refuse, material, vegetation, fill, structure, or matter in, 
along, across or projecting into any curb, gutter, or ditch which might impede , retard or change the 
direction of a flow of water, either by itself or by catching or collection debris carried by such water.  
EF. "Occupant" means and includes any person who occupies the whole or a part of a building, premises, 
or land, whether alone or with others , whether for residential or commercial purposes but does not 
own the property. "Occupant" means and includes the terms "tenant" and "lessee."  
FG. "Property Owner" means the owner of record as reflected in the records of the County Assessor for the 
property with adjacent frontage along the public right of way. "Owner" means and includes:  
1. Any owner or holder of any legal or equitable estate in real property, including a dominant or servient 
tenement, except a future or reversionary interest and except the interest of a public trustee, 
lienholder, mortgagee, or beneficiary of a deed of trust; or  
2. tThe owner of record, as reflected by the records of the office of the County Assessor.  
GH. "Private Culvert" means a buried pipe installed to convey drainage or irrigation water under private 
improvements such as, but not limited to, a private driveway  or increased yard space.  
HI. "Public Culvert" means a buried pipe installed to convey drainage or irrigation water under public 
improvements such as, but not limited to, a public roadway  or sidewalk.  
Sec. 19.10.030. Applicability. 
This chapter shall apply to all curbs and ,gutters, culverts, and ditches and ditches within the public rights-of-
way or easements and private and public culverts which are within public rights-of-way or easement or that are 
located adjacent to such public rights-of-way and easements, unless explicitly exempted by this chapter.  
 
 
2 
 
Sec. 19.10.040. Responsibility for administration. 
The Public Works Director, or his or her designee, shall administer, implement, and enforce the provisions of 
this chapter. 
Sec. 19.10.050. Curbs and gutters. 
All occupants and /owners are required to keep the curbs and gutters for drainage that may extend along 
any roadway between the center of such roadway and the property occupied by them clear of all obstructions to 
the free flow of water therein.  
Sec. 19.10.060. Ditches and culverts – Version 1. 
A. Private culverts, and any other similar improvements shall be maintained by the property owner.  
 
B. Public culverts shall be maintained by the City.  
 
C. Ditches that are utilized to convey irrigation water shall be maintained by the ditch company or lateral 
ditch shareholders, as applicable under Colorado law.   
 
D. Ditches that are utilized solely to convey stormwater shall be maintained by the property owner.   
 
E. Property owners or occupants shall not cause any obstruction of the free flow of water within private 
culverts, public culverts, ditches, curb, or gutters.  
 
F. The removal of debris obstructing the flow of water through a ditch or culvert of any type shall be the 
responsibility of the ditch company or lateral ditch shareholders, as applicable under Colorado law.  
This shall include the removal of debris from inside a private or public culvert even if the debris or 
obstruction is not caused by an act of the owner or public entity responsible for the maintenance of 
the culvert.  
 
G. It shall be unlawful for any person to deposit waste or debris of any kind or description or in any way 
dam, clog, or interfere with the water running in any ditch or drainage or cause the same to overflow 
upon the streets, alleys, or public highways of the City.  
Sec. 19.10.060. Ditches and culverts – Version 2. 
A. Private culverts and any other similar improvements shall be maintained by the property owner.   
 
B. Public culverts shall be maintained by the City. 
 
C. Ditches shall be maintained by the property owner.  
 
H. Property owners or occupants shall not cause any obstruction of the free flow of water within private 
culverts, public culverts, ditches, curb, or gutters.  
 
D. The removal of debris obstructing the flow of water through a ditch or culvert of any type shall be the 
responsibility of the property owner, or the City in the case of Public Culverts.  This shall include the 
removal of debris from inside a private or public culvert even if the debris or obstruction is not caused 
by an act of the owner or public entity responsible for the maintenance of the culvert.

therein. 
B. It shall be unlawful for any person to cause obstruction of the free flow of water within private or public 
culverts. 
Sec. 19.10.070. Corrective action by City and charge of costs. 
If an occupant or /owner violates any requirement of this chapter, the City may take corrective measures and 
charge the cost of such corrective measures to the occupant/owner, regardless of whether the owner occupies the 
property. Such costs shall include the actual cost of any work deemed necessary by the Public Works Director, plus 
reasonable administrative and inspection costs and any civil penalties imposed pursuant to Chapter 20.10 of this 
Code.  
Sec. 19.10.080. Enforcement and penalties. 
This chapter shall be subject to the enforcement provisions in Section 20.10.160.
