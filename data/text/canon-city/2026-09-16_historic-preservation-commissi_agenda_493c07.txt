What is Saving Places On the Road?
Saving Places On the Road is Colorado Preservation, Inc.’s dynamic traveling conference
designed to spotlight and celebrate historic preservation efforts in communities across the
state. This immersive, two-day event brings together local leaders, preservation
professionals, and curious visitors for hands-on training, behind-the-scenes tours, and
exclusive access to preservation projects that highlight the unique history and character of
each host community. In addition to educational sessions and site visits, the event features
a lively Taste Of celebration—an evening dedicated to showcasing the area’s local music,
cuisine, artisans, and cultural traditions. With an average attendance of 75 to 150
participants, Saving Places On the Road offers a unique opportunity for communities to
share their preservation successes, explore region-specific challenges, and foster
meaningful connections with a statewide network of advocates. Check out some of our
past On the Road programs below:
Saving Places On the Road 2026 - Buena Vista
Saving Places On the Road 2025 - Pueblo
Saving Places On the Road 2024 - Steamboat Springs
Saving Places On the Road 2023 - La Junta
Saving Places On the Road 2022 - San Luis
 
Now Accepting Applications to Host!
To expand this popular program and ensure it reflects the rich diversity of Colorado’s
communities, CPI is inviting towns and cities to apply and bid for the opportunity to host a
future On the Road event. Selected host communities will play a leading role in shaping the
event experience, working closely with CPI to plan tours, identify speakers, and coordinate
the two-day program. In addition, hosts are expected to help secure event sponsorships
and in-kind support, identify suitable venues and lodging options, and actively promote the
event to local audiences.
Hosting Saving Places On the Road is a chance to shine a spotlight on your community’s
heritage, engage local partners and businesses, and connect with preservation advocates
from across the state. If your community is passionate about its history and eager to
showcase it on a statewide stage, we encourage you to apply!
