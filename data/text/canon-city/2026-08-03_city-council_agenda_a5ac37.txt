Thus, to approve the application, all the criteria in Section 17.10.030(B) of the UDCmust be met.  
With respect to criterion (a), Section 17.10.030(A) of the UDCreads as follows: 
Although each district is primarily intended for a predominant type of use (such as
dwellings in residential districts), there are a number of uses which may or may
not be appropriate in a particular district depending upon, for example, the
location, nature of the proposed use, character of surrounding development, 
traffic capacities of adjacent streets, and potential environmental impacts. These
factors dictate that the circumstances of development should be individually
reviewed. It is the purpose of the special review use permit process to provide
review of such uses so that the community is assured that they are compatible with
their locations and surrounding land uses and willfurther the purposes of this UDC. 
This definition of “ applicability” doesn’t necessarily impose conditions, but it is establishing
relevant factors for consideration. To put this particular special review use request into context, 
the applicant is seeking to fill a currently vacant tenant space with a retail sales of small
recreational equipment and vehicles use. Thisfacilitywillcontain motorcycles for sale along with
ancillary retail. As such, while it is a change of use from the previous retail tenant, the activity
generated by motorcycle sales location would appear to be similar to retail, with similar parking
demand as to other retail shops downtown.  Thisdoes not appear to be placing more demand
on parking facilities or access roads to the property than it was designed to accommodate.  Staff
therefore finds that criteria (a) has been satisfied.
