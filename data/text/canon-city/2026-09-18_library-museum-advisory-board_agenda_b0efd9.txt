THURSDAY JULY 9, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. 
Minutes 
 
I. Kathy called the meeting to order at 11:02.    
Those present were:   Kathy Herrin, Joey the dog, Lisa Studts, Kendra McFall, Sherry Roeber, Dan 
Meeks, Adam Gonzales, Amy Schmisseur and Jody Enderle. Absent: Loren Flick and Ron Lauteret  
 
II. Minutes from March 12, 2026, were approved with Sherry Motioning and Dan 
seconding.  Minutes/Notes from May 8, 2026, were approved with Jody motioning and Sherry 
seconding that motion.      
     
III. The Service and Support Animal Policy was reviewed and approved with the addition of the tilde to 
the "N" of Canon City. Amy will provide tech support to add tildes. This motion was made by Dan 
and seconded by Jody. It passed unanimously.  
 
IV. Building issues  
a. Kendra presented that the cooler needed work and was fixed by maintenance Randy & 
Dwayne.   
b. Building issues for the museum that were presented by Lisa were:  
i. The museum chairlift is broken, and the building will not support an elevator. Their new 
grant writer is applying for state historical funds to fix that.  This will not be fixed until at 
least next year.  Adam suggested that a virtual tour might be an option in the 
meantime.  Kathy suggested that perhaps a college intern could help with this.  
ii. The Catlin Cabin roof contractors are working out a schedule to repair that.  
iii. The Museum has had to put locks on the outdoor spigots because they are getting 
tampered with at night. 
 
V. Project Updates  
a. Library project updates:  
i. Kendra presented the Comparative statistics for our library.   
ii. Adam informed the board that the Cloud library Libby is being transferred August 18th to 
Hoopla.  This affects eBook checkout. 
b. Museum project updates: 
i. There is a new display for Frontier History. 
ii. CLG African American Families research continues. 
iii. Catlin Cabin is getting refurnished with hands on activities. 
iv. Guest Curator Vickie Leigh Krudwig local wildlife artist and author will display artwork 
and present some hands-on presentations.
