Standards of Value
Replacement cost new, as applicable to insurance valuations, is the cost required to produce a
property oflike kind and materials at one time in accordance with current market prices for
materials, labor and manufactured equipment, contractors' overhead, profit and fees, but without
provisions for overtime or bonuses for labor and premiums for materials. Consultantwill not take

property or the removal of debris. Partial losses may result in higher replacement costs as partial
losses often require a substantial amount of repair in conjunction with the replacement process. 
After gathering the applicable building data, Consultant will rely on the direct cost approach to
estimate the insurable value of the buildings. Consultant valuation will utilize a variety of sources
that include the use of proprietary and third-party software, proprietary databases, engineering
manuals, technical pricing subscriptions, discussions with contractors and various publications. 
These include Cotality (Marshall & Swift/ Boeckh), Design Cost Data, Engineering News-Record, 
and R.S. Means. As needed, Consultant will also consult with local and regional architectural and
engineering firms. This process allows Consultant to make regional adjustments to costs that may
not be readily available through national publications and estimating systems.
