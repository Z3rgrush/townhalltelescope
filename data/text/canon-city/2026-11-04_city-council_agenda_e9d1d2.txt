A BILL FOR
ORDINANCE NO. 13, SERIES OF 2026
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF CAÑON CITY AMENDING
SECTIONS 3.13.070 AND 3.13.100
WHEREAS, the City desires to insert within its Code the penalties for failure to pay the
City's use tax.  
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF CAÑON CITY: 
Section 1. Section 3.13.070 of the Cañon City Municipal Code is amended to read as
follows: 
Sec. 3.13.070.  Penalties and Interest
A. Any use tax due and unpaid shall be a debt to the City and be subject to the penalties
and interest in this Chapter.  
B. Penalty for Tax Deficiency.  
1. The Finance Director shall add to the estimated amount of taxes due or not
accounted a penalty equal to the greater of: 
a. The sum of $15.00; or
b. 10% of such unpaid, unaccounted, or incorrectly accounted amount, plus 0.5% 
per month from the date when due, not exceeding 18% in the aggregate. 
2. In addition to the penalty in subsection B, the Finance Director shall add to the
estimated amount of tax due or not accounted for interest in the amount pursuant to
C.R.S. §§ 39-21-109 and 110.5, as may be amended, until the amount due is paid.  
C. Penalty for Fraud. If any part of delinquent tax or deficiency is due to fraud with the
intent to evade the tax, the penalty shall be fifty percent (50%) of the total amount of
the deficiency.  
D. The Finance Director shall provide the delinquent taxpayer notice of the estimated tax, 
penalty and interest by first-class mail, unless the taxpayer has agreed to notice via
electronic means.   The amount of the tax due, including the penalty and interest, shall
become due and payable within ten (30) days after the date of the notice of assessment
for failure to pay use tax.   
E. Nothing in this section shall prevent the City from using any other applicable penalties
or remedies for collection or enforcement of use taxes.

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF CAÑON CITY AMENDING
SECTION 2.04.160 OF THE CAÑON CITY MUNICIPAL CODE
CREATING A COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF CAÑON CITY:   
Section 1. Section 2.04.160 of the Cañon City Municipal Code is amended by the
addition thereto of a new subsection C. to read as follows:   
Sec 2.04.160. – Standing Committees
A. The Council may establish, by ordinance or resolution, standing committees
of Council. Such committees shall be created and function with regard to
matters involving the operation of City government and the provision of
City services to the public.  
B. At the time the Council establishes standing committees of Council, it shall
specify the duties of each committee and determine the number of
Councilmembers to sit upon each committee. The Mayor shall be an ex-
officio and voting member of each standing committee. 
C. The City Council further establishes the Standing Committee of the
Whole, which shall consist of the Mayor and the seven Council
Members of the City. The Committee of the Whole shall meet in work
sessions as needed, and shall be the committee to which the City
Council refers all ordinances pursuant to Article XII, Sections 2 and 3
of the City of Cañon City Home Rule Charter, unless the City Council
specifically determines to refer such ordinance to another standing
committee of the City. Nothing in this subsection C. shall be construed
to prevent the City Council from referring an ordinance pursuant to
Article XII, Sections 2 and 3 of the City of Cañon City Home Rule
Charter to both the Committee of the Whole and to another standing
committee of the City. 
Section 2. Severability. If any section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other
provision of this Ordinance for any reason is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the
invalidity or unenforceability of such section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other
provision shall not affect any of the remaining provisions of this Ordinance, the intent being
that the same are severable.   
Section 3. Effective Date. Pursuant to Article XII, Section 3 and Section 5 of
the Charter, this Ordinance shall be effective five (5) days after final publication if it is
published after adoption or if not so published, five (5) days after its adoption.

A BILL FOR
ORDINANCE NO.  19 SERIES OF 2026
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF CAÑON CITY AMENDING APPENDIX
13.08 OF THE CAÑON CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING WATER RATES
WHEREAS, based on a water rate study completed in 2024 evaluating projections of
revenues and expenses for the City’ s Water Activity Enterprise ( the " Water Rate Study"), it is
necessary for the City to incrementally increase water rates to cover increasing operational and
capital costs;  
WHEREAS, the City further recognizes the need to provide enough revenue to allow
future capital improvements to the City' s water system;  
WHEREAS, the City Council previously adopted Resolution No. 21, Series of 2025, 
reflecting the Council’ s intent to implement the cost- of-service adjustments to the City’ s water
rates through a phased- in approach, with 2027 contemplated as the second year; 
WHEREAS, the City Council is authorized to adjust such fees by Section 13.08. 250( A) 
of the Cañon City Municipal Code; and
WHEREAS, the City finds that an incremental rate increase is in the best interests of the
citizens of the City and desires to increase the water fees incrementally over the next two years
until the fee amount recommended in the Water Rate Study is reached.  
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF CAÑON CITY: 
Section 1. In accordance with the Water Rate Study, Appendix 13.08 of the Cañon
City Municipal Code is hereby repealed and replaced as attached hereto in Exhibit A.   
Section 2. Severability.  If any section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other
provision of this Ordinance for any reason is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the invalidity or
unenforceability of such section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other provision shall not affect
any of the remaining provisions of this Ordinance, the intent being that the same are severable. 
Section 3. Effective Date.  Pursuant to Article XII, Section 3 and Section 5 of the
Charter, this Ordinance shall be effective January 1, 2027.

A BILL FOR
ORDINANCE NO. 20, SERIES OF 2026
AN ORDINANCE INCREASING THE BASE RATE USED TO CALCULATE
STORMWATER MANAGEMENT FEES CHARGEABLE BY THE CITY' S
STORMWATER UTILITY ENTERPRISE. 
WHEREAS, the Council of Cañon City has determined that an increase in the "base rate" 
used to calculate Stormwater Management Fees is necessary as required by the Cañon City
Municipal Code 13.10.065; and
WHEREAS, the City finds that this inflationary rate increase of 3.6% is in the best
interests of the citizens of the City.  
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF CAÑON CITY: 
Section 1. Effective January 1, 2027, Appendix 13.10 of the Cañon City Municipal
Code is amended by the adoption of new base rate schedule, Schedule A, 2027 Base Rate
Calculation, Stormwater Management Fees, attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein
by this reference.  The new schedule shall supersede and replace the corresponding schedule of
Appendix 13.10 that was in effect at the time of the adoption of this ordinance. 
Section 2. Severability.  If any section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other
provision of this Ordinance for any reason is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the invalidity or
unenforceability of such section, subsection, paragraph, clause or other provision shall not affect
any of the remaining provisions of this Ordinance, the intent being that the same are severable. 
Section 3. Effective Date.  Pursuant to Article XII, Section 3 and Section 5 of the
Charter, this Ordinance shall be effective January 1, 2027.
