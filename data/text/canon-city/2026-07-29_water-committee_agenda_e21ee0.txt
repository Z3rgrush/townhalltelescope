CAÑON CITY | WATER DISTRIBUTION DIVISION 
MUNICIPAL POLICY AND OPERATING PROCEDURES 
Effective Date: July 27, 2026 
Jurisdiction: City of Cañon City, CO 
 
1. MUNICIPAL COMPLIANCE & SAFETY STANDARDS 
Mandatory Tank Inspection 
• Authorized review: A City of Cañon City Water Distribution employee must inspect and approve 
all utility tanks prior to the first station activation. 
• Backflow prevention: Every tank configuration must maintain a physical, unobstructed minimum 
4-inch air gap between the source pipe and the tank entry threshold. 
User-Supplied Hardware Specifications 
• Station connection requirement: Users must supply their own distribution hose equipped with 
the appropriate municipal standard fittings based on capacity: 
o Tanks 2,000 gallons or less: 3-inch female cam-lock connection. 
o Tanks over 2,000 gallons: 4-inch female cam-lock connection. 
 
2. MUNICIPAL FEES & FISCAL CONTROLS 
Schedule of Fees 
• Account activation: New users are subject to a one-time municipal setup fee of $10.00. 
• Prepaid accounting fee: A standard administrative fee of $2.00 is assessed on all individual 
account balance updates. 
Payment Processing Regulations 
• Accepted tenders: The City accepts payment exclusively via cash or check. 
• Exact change requirement: Distribution staff do not carry currency; exact payment amounts are 
required for cash transactions. 

 
• Secured after-hours depository: Payments may be deposited in the secure drop box located 
directly to the left of the main office entry. 
 
3. JURISDICTIONAL BOUNDARIES & SECURITY 
Service Territorial Limits 
• Fremont County restriction: All bulk water drawn from this municipal asset is strictly designated 
for local consumption. 
• Boundary enforcement: Delivery or transit of water hauled from this facility outside of Fremont 
County boundaries is prohibited. 
System Security & Liability Protocols 
• Credential protection: Loaning, sharing, or distributing station access credentials to 
unauthorized personnel is strictly forbidden. 
• Hardware security: The keypad housing box remains unlocked for accessibility; operators must 
fully close the protective enclosure immediately following use. 
• Aggressive actions towards other water users or staff will not be tolerated. 
• Penalties for non-compliance: Any violation of these municipal protocols will result in the 
immediate revocation of facility access and bulk water privileges. 
• Policy amendments: The City of Cañon City reserves the right to modify these operational 
regulations as localized resource or environmental conditions warrant. 
 
4. OFFICE HOURS & EMERGENCY DISPATCH 
Kiosk Availability 
• Water Kiosk Terminal: Accessible 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. 
Water Distribution Administrative Hours 
• Monday through Thursday: 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM 
• Friday: 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM 
• Daily Lunch Closure: Office closed daily from 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM. 
Urgent System Assistance & Infrastructure Failures 
• After-hours dispatch: For support regarding station malfunctions outside regular hours, contact 
the Cañon City Police Department Dispatch Line at 719-276-5600 to route an on-call water 
infrastructure technician.
