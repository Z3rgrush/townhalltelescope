MEMORANDUM 
 
To: Chair Fierstein and Basalt Affordable Community Housing 
Commission 
 
From:  James Lindt, Planning Director 
 
Date:  August 13, 2026 
 
RE: BCC and Stott’s Mill Duplex Units- Update on Town-owned Unit 
Rentals 
 
 
DETAILS:   The Town of Basalt closed on the purchase of six (6) deed -restricted 
units in the BCC Development Project on August 5, 2026 , and the second Stott’s 
Mill Duplex being constructed by the Town is ready for final inspections.  
Therefore, Town Staff has been having discussions ab out the rents that the 
Town should charge to Town employees desiring to rent these additional units 
being added to the Town’s inventory of Town -owned employee housing.   Town 
Staff deemed it appropriate to provide an update to BACH on the rents for these 
Town-owned units.  
 
BCC Units: 
The units purchased by the Town in the BCC Project consist of the following 
breakdown: 
 
      3 Studios 
      2 One-Bedroom Units 
      1 Two-Bedroom Unit 
 
Of the six units, five of them are deed-restricted as Category 1 Units and one of 
the units is a Category 3 Unit  in the development approvals.  Town Staff and the 
Town Manager intend to rent all six of the units at Category 1 rents to Town 
employees.  Category 1 rents for the BCC Project are as follows: 
 2 
    
 
       Studio: $1,824 
       One-Bedroom: $1,955 
       Two-Bedroom: $2,235 
 
The unit that is deed -restricted as a Category 3 unit is a 1 -bedroom unit, so the 
difference in monthly rent that the Town would be foregoing to rent it at a 
Category 1 level is $978 per month.   
 
Staff and the Town Manager believe that keeping all the units at the lower 
Category 1 rent level will likely allow more Town employees to take advantage of 
renting the units and could help encourage some of the Town’s current tenants in 
the Riverside Plaza BC Building to move over to the BCC project to help vacate 
some of the Riverside Plaza units so that they can be renovated by the Town in 
2027.  The BCC units will be rented based on the process in the Town -owned 
unit policy, which includes advertising t he units to Town employees for a period 
of time before advertising the units to other government entities/non -profits in the 
Basalt area. 
 
Stott’s Mill Duplex Units: 
The four lots in Stott’s Mill that the Town purchased and constructed duplexes on 
were deed -restricted for Category 2 Development.  As such, Town Staff is 
intending to rent the new duplex being completed at the Category 2 rental rates .  
The monthly Category 2 rental rate for the duplex units is $3,144 for the three -
bedroom duplex units.  The Co uncil established a rental policy for the duplex 
units in 2025 that give priority to Town employees before advertising the units to 
other government entities/non -profits in the Basalt area.  Staff anticipates that 
there will be interest from several Town employees with families.

POLICE
CURRENT FACILITY
The Basalt Police Department’s current location at 
Riverview Plaza is not private, as it is shared with other 
businesses. The facility is undersized and outdated, 
limiting the department’s ability to serve the community 
effectively, safely, and professionally.
The current space does not provide adequate room for 
core operational needs.
• Officers share limited workspace
• No interview rooms for victims or suspects
• No processing area for arrestees
• Inadequate evidence handling and storage
• No secure parking
• No space to hold emergency operations
• No investigations/detective area

PUBLIC WORKS
CURRENT FACILITY
The central office is in an aging portable building with 
limited space for staff. It also lacks meeting space, 
lockers, changing areas, and restrooms.
Equipment and materials are stored in five different 
locations across Basalt. Each site has limited space and 
aging facilities.
Many pieces of equipment must be stored outdoors, 
which exposes them to the elements and increases 
wear and tear over time. Together, these conditions 
reduce operational efficiency, create challenges for staff, 
and contribute to more frequent equipment 
replacement.

PUBLIC WORKS
A MODERN PUBLIC WORKS FACILITY
A new facility would serve the Basalt community and be 
designed to meet future needs. New and additional 
features would include:
• Centralized space for staff and day-to-day operations
• Organized site access for vehicles, equipment, 
materials, and staff
• Heated and protected storage for Town vehicles, 
equipment, and supplies
• Dedicated space for training, meetings, lockers, and 
showers
• Functional work areas that improve efficiency and 
support long-term care of Town assets
