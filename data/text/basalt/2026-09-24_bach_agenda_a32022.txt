MEMORANDUM 
 
To: Chair Fierstein and Basalt Affordable Community Housing 
Commission 
 
From:  James Lindt, Planning Director 
 
Date:  September 24, 2026 
 
RE: Block 9, Willits Town Center (HUB Building at 300 Robinson Street) 
Daycare Priority Units Tenant Renewal Policy 
 
 
DETAILS:   The Town of Basalt approved the construction of a multi -family 
building on Block 9 of Willits Town Center (known as the HUB) pursuant to 
Ordinance No. 10, Series of 2019.  It was required to include eight (8) category 
deed-restricted rental units with a daycare professional occupancy priority.  The 
Aspen Skiing Company that owns the building has asked for the Town’s 
clarification on whether a tenant(s) that was a daycare professional when they 
originally qualified to occupy a deed -restricted unit in the HUB would need to 
move out if they no longer are employed as a daycare professional.   
 
In the situation that the Property Owner is dealing with, the tenant that was a 
daycare professional is still employed in the Town’s employment area between 
Aspen and Glenwood Springs and a family member that also occupies the unit is 
employed by the Roaring Fork School District.  The deed restriction and 
Ordinance No. 10, Series of 2019 do not address  tenant requalification for the 
units. 
 
REVIEW PROCESS:  This is an item that requires the Town Staff’s interpretation 
of the Town ’s Community Housing requirements .  Staff would like to solicit 
BACH’s com ments and recommendation on Staff ’s suggested interpretation in 
order to provide guidance to the property owner/tenants.  
 
 2 
STAFF ANALYSIS AN D RECOMMENDATION:  Staff recommends that since 
the requalification provisions are not specifically addressed in the approvals for 
the HUB, that the Town’s standard tenant requalification policies should be 
applied.  The Town’s policy on requalification of tenants on deed-restricted units 
related to employment  requirements is that the Town does not require existing 
tenants to move out of a unit if they change  jobs so long as the ir new 
employment is within the employment area of Aspen to Glenwood Springs.
