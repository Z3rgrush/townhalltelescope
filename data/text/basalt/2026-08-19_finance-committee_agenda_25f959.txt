Item Number:   7D

From: Doug Pattison, Finance Director 
SUBJECT: Ordinance 21, Series 2026 – Approval of COP financing for property 
to be used by the Town in providing affordable housing. 
RECOMMENDATION:  Approval of Ordinance 21, Series of 2026 on First 
Reading 
and set the Public Hearing and Second Reading for August 25, 2026. 
DETAILS: 
Staff has
 been working with Jeff Conklin, Dalton Kelley (bond attorney from Butler 
Snow), and Bruce Kimmel (municipal advisor from Ehler’s) to move forward with 
a Certificates of Participation financing option to finance the acquisition of six 
condominium units located at Basalt Center Circle. 
The Ord
inance is structured to allow the Town to move forward on the basis of 
setting certain parameters.  Therefore, if the top bid meets the parameters 
outlined in this Ordinance, then the Town is able to proceed with the bank 
selection, after approval of the Ordinance on second reading. The Ordinance also 
approves the forms of the Site Lease Agreement, Lease Purchase Agreement and 
Indenture of Trust, which will be completed after the bank is selected.  
The COP p
roceeds will reimburse the Town for the purchase.  
The Town will utilize rent payments from the units and RETA proceeds from the 
sale of other units located at this location to service the interest and principal on 
this lease obligation.  The costs of issuance are estimated at $60,000.

Section 1. Recitals; Ratification and Approval of Prior Actions .  The 
foregoing recitals are incorporated herein as findings of Council.  All action heretofore 
taken (not inconsistent with the provisions of this ordinance) by the Council and the 
officers, agents or employees of the Council or the Town relating to the Site Lease, the 
Lease, and the financing of the Project is hereby ratified, approved and confirmed.   
Section 2. Finding of Best Interests .  The Council hereby finds and 
determines, pursuant to the Constitution and laws of the State and the Charter, that the 
financing of the Project by leasing the Leased Property to the Trustee pursuant to the Site 
Lease and subsequently leasing the Leased Property from the Trustee pursuant to the 
terms set forth in the Lease are necessary, convenient, and in furtherance of the Town’s 
purposes and are in the best interests of the inhabitants of the Town and the Council 
hereby authorizes and approves the same.   For purposes of this ordinance, “Leased 
Property” means the property known as Units 207, 208, 206, 224, 225, 302, Basalt Center 
Circle, including an undivided interest in the common elements appurtenant to each Unit, 
with the street address of 140 Basalt Center Circle, Basalt Colorado 81621.

Section 7. No General Obligation Debt .  No provision of this ordinance, the

those budgeted and appropriated for the Town’s then current fiscal year. 
Section 8. Reasonableness of Rentals.  The Council hereby determines and 
declares that the Base Rentals due under the Lease, in the maximum amounts authorized 
pursuant to Section 3 hereof, constitute the fair rental value of the Leased Property and 
do not exceed a reasonable amount so as to place the Town under an economic 
compulsion to renew the Lease or to exercise its option to purchase the Trustee’s 
leasehold interest in the Leased Property pursuant to the Lease.  The Council hereby 
determines and declares that the period during which the Town has an option to purchase 
the Trustee’s leasehold interest in the Leased Property (i.e., the entire maximum term of 
the Lease) does not exceed the useful life of the Leased Property. The Council hereby 
determines and declares that the amount of rent to be received by the Town under the 
Site Lease, as set forth in the Site Lease, is fair rental value for the Leased Property.
