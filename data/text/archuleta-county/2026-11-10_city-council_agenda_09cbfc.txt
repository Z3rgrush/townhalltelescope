Proclamation – Operation Green Light for Veterans Day 2026
Proclamation by the Board of County Commissioners (BoCC) proclaiming the week of November 4-11,
2026, as a time to salute and honor the service and sacrifices of those transitioning from active service
and that in observance of Operation Green Light, Archuleta County Community plans to show support
and appreciation for all Veterans throughout the week. Archuleta County encourages its citizens in
patriotic tradition to recognize the importance of honoring all those who made immeasurable sacrifices to
preserve freedom by displaying green lights in a window of their place of business or residence from
November 4-11, 2026.

Consideration of Approval of Secure Transportation Service License Application from Colorado
Secure Transport with Two Vehicle Permits
This is an application for a Secure Transportation Service license for Colorado Secure Transport located in
Pueblo, Colorado. Included with this agenda item are applications for two (2) vehicle permits. In
accordance with state statute and 6 CCR 1011-4, the license and permits will allow Colorado Secure
Transport to provide urgent transportation services within the boundaries of Archuleta County to
individuals experiencing a behavioral health crisis. The vehicles have passed inspection, all
documentation has been properly submitted and the proper fees have been paid. Staff recommends
approval of this secure transportation service license with two vehicle permits.
