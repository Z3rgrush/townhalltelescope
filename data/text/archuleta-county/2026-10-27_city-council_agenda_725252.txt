LAND USE HEARING
Public Hearing for Consideration of Approval of PLN-26-028, LPEA, Transmission Lines, Board
Conditional Use Permit (BCUP)
La Plata Electric Association (LPEA) has applied for approval of PLN-26-028, Transmission Lines, Board
Conditional Use Permit (BCUP) for a proposed transmission line project located at 2140 County Road 500
and along associated easements. LPEA is proposing approximately 2,700 feet of transmission line to
provide power to the approved Trujillo Substation. The project includes seven new transmission poles,
consisting of weathered steel and wood structures, with a maximum proposed height of 75 feet. The
transmission line is proposed as a double-circuit 115 kV line, initially operated at 69 kV, with a single-
circuit 12.47 kV distribution line underbuilt on the transmission structures. Additional distribution poles
are proposed within existing rights-of-way to connect to existing and future lines. The proposed alignment
generally follows an existing distribution powerline corridor before turning south along the west side of
County Road 500, with a new corridor extending south to the Trujillo Substation. The parcels where the
transmission line is proposed are zoned Agricultural Ranching (AR). This was approved at the Regular
Planning Commission Meeting on September 24, 2026.
