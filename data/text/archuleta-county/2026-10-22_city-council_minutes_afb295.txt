draft was completed. Ed confirmed this is the case because this is what the County scoped out in the request for 
proposals (RFP) as well as surveys and other online materials. Ed also added that there will be another public outreach 
meeting conducted once the draft is complete. 
 
Chair Nobles asked how long until data stops being collected, in which Ed indicated an approximation of 2 weeks. Matt 
then asked where the survey was put out. Director of Development, Eva Kruse, said the survey was sent to all County

David Laufer, 383 Rambling Pl, shared his opinion about how the comprehensive plan should be rewritten. He discussed 
how the plan should strategize for growth rather than focus on preservation. He did not believe there were sufficient 
means to meet community needs in terms of revenue. He believed incomes were too low compared to housing costs and 
noted that people were reluctant to support growth. He believed the narrative should focus on helping people 
understand that growth was necessary and needed to occur in the right way. Otherwise, the community would remain 
stuck. He noted that the community could no longer rely on snow and was losing more people while struggling to 
replenish the workforce.

B. PLN-REZ-26-050 Cloman Blvd – Administrative Rezoning 
 
Development Director Eva Kruse presented the staff report for this project. 
 
Vice Chair McAllister asked about airport easements. Eva explained that the avigation easements would not be completed 
until the property was ready for development and that they would be addressed during the site plan review stage.  
 
County Attorney Cathie stated that the easements were in progress and had been submitted to the FAA.  
 
Chair Nobles  asked whether there was a development plan or proposed development plan and expressed concerns about 
wetlands on the parcel. He also asked what the purpose of the rezone would be because a Board Conditional Use Permit 
had previously been approved for Intermountain Propane as use  in the Agriculture Ranching zoning district. 
 
Eva explained that no development plan had been proposed. She also noted that the property that Intermountain 
Propane operates on is not the only parcel being proposed for this rezoning. Additionally, she noted that one of the 
parcels proposed for rezoning had previously been denied for use as a transfer station, insinuating that this rezone would 
allow for the County to move forward with that potential use. 
 
Chair Nobles expressed doubt that the rezoning would bring businesses to the area and raised concerns about the 
potential impacts of rezoning without a specific development plan.  
 
Eva clarified that a development plan had not been requested. 
 
Chair Nobles stated that he agreed the property should be zoned Industrial  but continued expressing concern about 
moving forward with a rezone without a development plan. 
 
Vice Chair McAllister asked for confirmation that the rezoning decision was ultimately a County  decision, and Eva 
confirmed that it was. 
 
Chair Nobles opened the floor to public comment.

Suzanne Dimeff, 1266 Oren Rd, asked about the airport master plan and requested there to be integration between this 
plan and the Comprehensive Plan. 
 
Chair Nobles closed public comment. 
 
Commissioner Vecvanags also expressed concerns regarding the review criteria, particularly the statement that there 
would be no adverse effects. 
 
Chair Nobles stated that he believed the County should work with the applicant to develop a development plan, given 
that the County had a 10% ownership stake in the property. 
 
Commissioner Dillon asked whether Chair Nobles was suggesting that a development plan be prepared, and Chair Nobles 
confirmed that he was. 
 
County Manager Longinos Gonzalez, Jr. stated that he would ensure those discussions took place and would explain that a 
development plan was not required as part of a rezoning request. 
 
Chair Nobles stated that he understood, but expressed concern because this was the largest rezoning request and 
involved rezoning the property to Industrial. 
 
Vice Chair McAllister motioned to approve the rezone request. Commissioner Jones seconded. The motion passed 3 -2 
with Vice Chair McAllister, Commissioner Jones, and Commissioner Dillon voting aye, and Chair Nobles and Commissioner 
Vecvanags voting nay. 
 
C. PLN26-051 – Archuleta Air Industrial – Minor Subdivision Final Plat 
 
The staff report for the project was presented by Eva Kruse. 
 
Commissioner Dillon asked whether the larger lot could be divided and, if so, whether the resulting parcels would have

be required. 
 
Chair Nobles explained that the proposed division would only be possible if the rezoning request was approved.  
 
The floor was opened for the applicant to speak his case.  
 
Ronnie Urbanzyk of Silesia Properties plead his project’s case. 
 
Vice Chair McAllister asked whether the four-acre parcel would be sold. Ronnie explained that he intended for the four -
