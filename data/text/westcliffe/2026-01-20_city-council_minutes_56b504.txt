NEW BUSINESS
 

a.PUBLIC HEARING to discuss the split of LOT 2 HOWARD MINOR, parcel number 0010024405 into three lots. 

Mayor Pro-Tem Patterson announced that the Public Hearing is open and for public comment only.  Only one person may speak at a time, with a 3-minute maximum. 

There was no public comment.

Mayor Pro-Tem Patterson announced that the Public Hearing was closed and moved back to the regular meeting. 

b.Consideration of a request to split LOT 2 HOWARD MINOR, parcel number 0010024405 into three lots per the Planning Commission’s recommendation.  

Trustee Shy asked if there was a commitment from Round Mountain to serve three separate lots.  The landowner Mr. Troyer said he spoke with Dave Schneider already and he said that the three lots would not be an issue.  Trustee Wilhelm called Dave Schneider who confirmed that three taps were okay.  

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve splitting LOT 2 HOWARD MINOR, parcel number 0010024405 into three lots per the Planning Commission’s recommendation.  Motion carried.

c.Consideration of a request to approve a county-wide business directory website by Custer County Tourism.  

This request was withdrawn prior to the meeting, so no action was taken on this agenda item.

d.Consideration of a request to approve Resolution NO. 1-2026 adopting the 2026 Emergency Operation Plan for Custer County. 

Clerk Christie reported that this agenda item should read ‘Custer County Community Wildfire Protection Plan’ rather than ‘Emergency Operation Plan’.

Ms. Knappe reported that the County conducted a survey regarding the Protection Plan, with 163 people responding - public comment was included in the final draft.  The old plan was from 2007 so they  have been working on redoing it.  They are currently drafting an evacuation plan that will be available in March.  

ACTION:   Mr. Shy moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve adopting Resolution NO. 1-2026 adopting the 2026 Custer County Community Wildfire Protection Plan.  Motion carried. 

e.Consideration of a request to approve the final draft of the Housing Needs Assessment. 

Clerk Christie reported that the Board needs to formally approve the Housing Needs Assessment as a condition to close out the grant. 

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve the final draft of the Housing Needs Assessment.  Motion carried. 

f.Consideration of a request to keep or remove the baseball diamond at Memorial Park.  

Trustee Wilhelm reported that before the Parks Committee can move forward on their plans, they want to know if the baseball diamond will remain in the Park or be removed, as that will determine where trees and shrubs will be planted.   

ACTION:   Mr. Shy moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve removing the baseball diamond at Memorial Park and allow the Parks Committee to continue their plans.  Motion carried. 


ADDITIONS TO THE AGENDA

Mayor Pro-Tem Patterson stated that she would like a long-standing report from the Westcliffe Parks Advisory Committee on every month’s agenda moving forward.  She would also like the Parks Committee to have a plan in place for the Board to review in order to prioritize projects so the Town can be ready to apply for grants as they become available.

She also would like to get quotes for the cost of putting an emergency door in the clerk’s office, as there is no egress currently.  If a solicitation to bid is necessary, it will be put on February’s agenda. 

Trustee Wilhelm would like the Board to allow Mr. Marrs to move forward with purchasing a truck to replace the current Town trucks.  This will go on February’s agenda.
