OLD BUSINESS
       
       a.Continued discussion of parking and safety on Main Street.

There was no discussion.  A workshop will be scheduled in the future. 
NEW BUSINESS

a.Swear in Mark Dembosky as new Trustee.  

Mayor Patterson swore in Mark Dembosky as a new Trustee. 

b.Consideration of a request to put a crosswalk on Main Street between 104 Main St. and the train depot.

Ms. Handy reported that she bought the building that the Lunds used to live in.  Sunflower is moving into that building and she is worried about the lack of parking and increased traffic so she would like a crosswalk put in.  Main Street already has some, but none beyond Second Street.  

This will be tabled until next month after more research is done to determine the best location.  

c.Garden Club Statement of Appreciation.

Ms. Munden spoke on behalf of the Garden Club to thank the Town for moral and financial support.  They have planted 40 barrels of flowers this year. 

d.Consideration of a request for funds and creation of signage for parking lot this year

Ms. Trenker reported that the Tourism Board would like signage for the parking lot on Rosita to direct tourists that there is additional parking.  It would be installed at Third and Main Streets.   The signs would be 18 x 24 and each one is about $120, not including shipping and hardware.  The Board wants to ensure that any signage is CDOT compliant.  

ACTION:   Mr. Dembosky moved, and Mr. Shy seconded to approve purchasing two CDOT compliant signs, not exceeding $1,000 total.  Motion carried. 

e.Sheriff’s report.

The Sheriff was unable to attend the meeting so this will be tabled until next month. 

f.Consideration of a request to approve Resolution 2-2026 relating to the LPC Grant as recommended by the Planning Commission and required by DOLA.  

Ms. Riddle reported that this is a boiler plate resolution by DOLA to show we are committed to fulfilling the grant requirements.  Town Attorney Findlay has no concerns with it. 

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve Resolution 2-2026 relating to the LPC Grant as recommended by the Planning Commission. Motion carried.

  g.Consideration of a request to close Town Hall on Friday, July 3rd. 

ACTION:   Mr. Wilhelm moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve closing Town Hall on Friday July 3rd.  Motion carried.

h.Discussion of overnight camping ordinance.

Ms. Patterson reported that in the recent CML newsletter there was an article about towns seeing more overnight camping because of an increase in the homeless population.  She would like to revamp the Town camping ordinance and make it match Silver Cliff’s.  The Town ordinance currently says ‘parks’ but it should be changed to ‘Town property’.  The Board would like her to pursue this more and come back to a future meeting with more information. 

       i.Discussion of creating an ordinance to ban Data Centers. 

Ms. Patterson reported that some towns are banning data centers because of the pressure they put on the electrical grid and water use.  Attorney Findlay reported that the Town could put a moratorium on them which is a temporary ban, allowing time to do more research before potentially zoning them out.  He said six months to a year is the average time frame other towns have put in the moratorium.  Ms. Patterson requested that he draft one and then eventually this will go to the Planning Commission.  

       j.Discussion of people using RVs on their properties.  

Ms. Patterson reported that there is a grey area with overnight camping in RVs on people’s properties.  An issue to consider is how long a friend/family member can stay in a relative’s RV without it violating a camping ordinance.  An example used was someone whose daughter is a traveling nurse and wants to stay in between jobs.  

This agenda item will be tabled for the future as the Town does not have any code enforcement.  So before creating new ordinances, there needs to be someone to enforce them.  

       k.Discussion of Title 4, Chapter 8, Outdoor Seating for Food Service Establishments. 

Similarly, this issue will be discussed further after the Town obtains a code enforcer.  The Board did ask Attorney Findlay what the liability is for outdoor seating.  He reported that as long as the Town shows they are attempting to enforce the ordinance, liability is low and shifts to the restaurant.
