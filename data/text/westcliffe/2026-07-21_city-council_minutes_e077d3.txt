OLD BUSINESS
       
             a.Consideration of a request to place a crosswalk on Main Street between 104 Main St. and the train     depot.
A temporary crosswalk will be installed using regular paint on the streets and the yellow crosswalk signs.  An actual crosswalk with the curb cut will be budgeted for next year.   

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Shy seconded to approve placing a temporary crosswalk on Main Street between 104 Main St. and the train depot.  Motion carried. 
NEW BUSINESS

Consideration of a request for a donation to resurface the swimming pool at Altitude Fitness.

There was not a representative from Altitude Fitness present.

Mr. Wilhelm stated that this request should go through the Community Investment Grant application process.  Attorney Findlay reported that as a statutory Town we are prohibited from making a donation, it does need to go through some type of grant program.

b.Consideration of a request for funding for the holiday wreath contest and business holiday décor.

Ms. Trenker reported that lodging has been down this year, so this makes the winter tourism season even more essential.  The Tourism Board wants to expand on the holiday décor program from last year. 

ACTION:   Mr. Wilhelm moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve funding $3,500 towards the holiday wreath contest and business holiday décor to the Business Round Table in conjunction with the Tourism Board. Motion carried. 

c.Consideration of a request for funding for Highway 69 Welcome signs.  

Ms. Trenker and Ms. Austin reported that the Tourism Board would like to put signage up on Highway 69 to try and get people to stop in Town rather than just driving through to try and increase tourism money.  The sign in the packet is just a mock-up and is a continuing concept to try and lower the price (the mock-up sign is about $5,000).  They believe that because of the Aspen Acres fire tourism revenue will go down so they are hoping the signs will help draw tourists to stop and stay here.  Mayor Patterson requested clerk Christie to check with the Town auditor to see about moving funds from different line items in the budget due to the emergency of tourist revenue declining.  

ACTION:   Mr. Dembosky moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve giving $5,000 for funding for Highway 69 Welcome signs, contingent on permission from Town auditor.  Motion carried.  Mr. Shy was opposed.    

d.Sheriff’s report.

The Sheriff was not present.

e.Consideration of a request to approve the Memorandum of Understanding regarding extended Bluff Park hours at the Smokey Jack Observatory.

f.Consideration of a request to approve Ordinance No. 2-2026, Moratorium on Data Centers.

This is the first reading of the Ordinance.  It will be put on August’s agenda for a final reading and vote and then will take effect 30 days after the final reading 

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Dembosky seconded to approve the first reading of Ordinance No. 2-2026, Moratorium on Data Centers.  Motion carried.


ADDITIONS TO THE AGENDA

a.      Mr. Wilhelm expressed concern that customers from the business next to Town Hall are driving across the     dirt strip that separates the two parking lots and it is becoming dangerous.  He would like some type of barrier to be put up.

b.      Mr. Wilhelm brought up that there are parcels of property on Crestone Court that are half in Silver Cliff and half in Westcliffe so in the future the zoning for them will need to be fixed.  It would start with being on a Planning Commission agenda. 

c.      Mr. Jagow expressed concern that Smokey Jack Observatory is not ADA compliant because there is no wheelchair accommodation.  Mayor Patterson asked him to come up with a plan as to what he thinks it should look like and bring it back to the Board.  Attorney Findlay was asked about potential liability, and he said that ADA means ‘reasonable accommodations.’
