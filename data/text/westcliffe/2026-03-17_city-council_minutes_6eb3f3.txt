NEW BUSINESS

a.Fire Department report.

Mr. Coleman reported that in 2025, there were 350 total calls, 27 of which were fire-related.  The Fire Department was awarded a grant to purchase a backup generator.  They hired a Chief Aid position to help the volunteers – there are currently 22 volunteers.  For 2026, they are applying for grants to purchase a gear dryer and to replace the asphalt outside of the building.  

b.Consideration of a request to approve a special events permit. 

Ms. Lee and Ms. Smith reported that they are having a Spring Fling Fashion Show at All Aboard Westcliffe on March 21st.  The Mortar Men have partnered with them – Brian Clince was present to represent them.  They need a special event permit because they are serving sangria.  

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Shy seconded to approve a special event permit. Motion carried.

c.Discussion of the Westcliffe Parks Advisory Committee.

Ms. Christie reported that Mr. Wilhelm resigned from the Westcliffe Parks Advisory Committee (WPAC) so there are currently no Trustees on the Committee and there are supposed to be two.  

Ms. Patterson expressed concern about the Town planting trees this year because of the drought and the potential for a water shortage.  She thinks the Town should put off planting trees until next year.   Ms. Mundon reported that back in July 2025 the Board approved creating two different tree zones in Memorial Park.  Mr. Dembosky is on the Round Mountain Board and will check if they foresee issues with the water supply this year.  Mr. Fulton thinks the Town should skip planting trees this year and instead plant them next year and at that point regroup the WPAC. 

Mayor Wenke would like the tree issue to be on April’s agenda.  

Mr. Dembosky recommended that if the Town is not going to plant trees this year, it should look at purchasing shade structures for the parks.  Mayor Wenke requested that the WPAC come back to the Board with some pricing options.  

Mr. Jagow said that he would be on the WPAC and Mr. Dembosky offered to help out as a citizen.  

ACTION:   Mayor Wenke moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to appoint Mr. Jagow to be on the Westcliffe Parks Advisory Committee.  Motion carried.

d.Consideration of a request to approve ORD. 1-2026 adopting the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code. 

Ms. Patterson reported that she attended the Fire Department Meeting and they did not approve this Code, nor did Silver Cliff or the County.

Attorney Findlay explained that while the law says this goes into effect July 1, many municipalities are choosing not to adopt it, and the law says ‘shall’ adopt.   Risks for not adopting it are potential insurance issues for people seeking new builds.  Also,  it could cause delays in construction because of building code changes.  There is an online fire risk map, and Westcliffe is in the ‘moderate’ category.  

Mr. Coleman reported that the Fire Department did not adopt it because there are too many unknowns, and there is no penalty for not adopting the code.  

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Fulton seconded to deny adopting ORD. 1-2026 adopting the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code.  Motion carried. 

e.Consideration of a request to approve adding Christy Patterson as a signatory to the United Business Bank Town accounts.  

Ms. Christie reported that Christy Patterson needs to be a signatory on the bank accounts since she is Mayor Pro-Tem and soon to be Mayor.  Mr. Wilhelm expressed concern that an additional Trustee should also be a signatory, and Mr. Jagow volunteered.  

ACTION:   Mayor Wenke moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve adding Christy Patterson as a signatory to the United Business Bank Town accounts. Motion died.
Mayor Wenke withdrew the old motion.

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve adding Christy Patterson and Chuck Jagow as signatories to the United Business Bank Town accounts.
