OLD BUSINESS
        

a.Consideration of a request to move the Town CSAFE (Colorado Surplus Asset Fund) bank accounts to  CSLIP (Centennial State Liquid Investment Pool).

ACTION:   Mr. Nordkye moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to deny moving the Town CSAFE bank accounts to CSLIP.  Motion carried.

NEW BUSINESS

                a.Consideration of a request to support the Colorado Byways Traveling Photo Exhibit (marketed by Custer County Tourism and Frontier Pathways).

Ms. Adams reported that the Colorado Byways Traveling Photo Exhibit started at DIA in 2022 and has been traveling around the State since then.  The Photo Exhibit will be in Westcliffe over the Martin Luther King holiday weekend and the President’s Day holiday weekend.  The Sangre Art Guild will be hosting it.  Ms. Adams is requesting $500 from the Town to pay for the overhead costs of renting out the Art Guild, as they are normally not open on those weekends.  

ACTION:   Mayor Wenke moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve supporting the Colorado Byways Traveling Photo Exhibit by donating $500 for overhead costs.  Motion carried. 

b.Consideration of a request to approve Resolution NO. 4-2025 Supporting the Continued Operation of the Farmer’s Market on Town Property at Jess Price Park.

Town Attorney Phipps reported that he drafted the resolution, rather than an agreement, to ensure that the Town is not bound into a leasehold in the event there is an issue in Jess Price Park being used that would prevent the Farmer’s Market from renting it.  

ACTION:   Mr. Wilhelm moved, and Mr. Fulton seconded to approve Resolution NO. 4-2025 Supporting the Continued Operation of the Farmer’s Market on Town Property at Jess Price Park.  Motion carried. 

c.Consideration of a request to close Town Hall the day after Christmas and the day after New Year’s Day, both of which fall on Fridays.

Ms. Christie reported that other municipalities are doing this.  Attorney Phipps confirmed that the Town has the authority to set working hours. 


ACTION:   Mr. Nordyke moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve closing Town Hall the day after Christmas and the day after New Year’s.  Motion carried.

d.PUBLIC HEARING – 2025 BUDGET.

Mayor Wenke announced that the Public Hearing is open and for public comment only.  Only one person may speak at a time, with a 3-minute maximum. 

There was no public comment.

Mayor Wenke announced that the Public Hearing was closed and moved back to the regular meeting. 


e.Consideration of a request to approve Ordinance NO. 5-2025 Adopting the 2026 Budget.

Mayor Wenke would like to approve all three budget ordinances (agenda items e, f, and g) together in one motion.  Town Attorney Phipps reported that it is okay to do that, as long as the full language of each ordinance is read within the motion.  Mayor Wenke read aloud the three ordinances.

ACTION:   Mayor Wenke moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve Ordinance NO. 5-2025 Adopting the 2026 Budget, Ordinance NO. 6-2025 Appropriation of Funds from the 2026 Budget, and Ordinance NO. 7-2025 Set Mill Levy for 2026 Budget.  Motion carried. 

f.Consideration of a request to approve Ordinance NO. 6-2025 Appropriation of Funds from the 2026 Budget.

Was approved above under item e.

g.Consideration of a request to approve Ordinance NO. 7-2025 Set Mill Levy for 2026 Budget.

Was approved above under item e. 

h.Consideration of a request to adopt the five (5) year Capital Plan. 

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve adopting the five (5) year Capital Plan.  Motion carried.
