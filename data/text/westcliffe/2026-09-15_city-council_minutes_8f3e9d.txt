NEW BUSINESS

a.Consideration of a request to install disc golf baskets at Hermit Park. 

The citizen requesting this was not present, so this was not discussed.  

b.Report from Rotary Van Service.

Mr. Miller reported that they are requesting support from the Town again like last year through the Community Investment Grant for up to $10,000.  Silver Cliff and the County each gave $1,500 to the van service.  They have six residents from Westcliffe who use the service and 13 riders from Silver Cliff.  

c.Sheriff’s report.

Sheriff Smith went over the call statistics and reported that they have a co-responder unit of two psychologists for suicide calls.  He reviewed the State E-bike laws as requested by the Board. 

d.Consideration of a request to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement for Prop 123 cooperation. 

 Mayor Patterson reported that the voters approved Prop 123 in the State in 2022.  The Town applied in 2024.  It requires increasing affordable housing stock in a three year period.  Westcliffe did not meet the requirement, however this agreement allows them to use some of Silver Cliff’s numbers to be in compliance.  

ACTION:   Mr. Dembosky moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement for Prop 123 cooperation.  Motion carried.

STAFF & COMMITTEE REPORTS

     a.Streets/Parks Supervisor report.

Mr. Kendrick reported that mowing and weed removal continue.  They have cleaned out culverts and irrigated the parks.  Smokey Jack Observatory was newly stained.  They have started street striping on Main and will continue with the residential streets. They will do the last of the season fertilizer application in the next few weeks.
