OLD BUSINESS
       
     a.Continued discussion and vote on planting trees in 2026.
Mr. Jagow stated that purchasing shade structures would be a better option than planting trees because of the drought.  Mr. Marrs reported that he received three quotes from companies that make shade structures.  Each one is 12 feet by 12 feet and costs $3,000-$4,000 per structure.  

ACTION:   Mr. Nordyke moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to deny planting new trees in 2026.  Motion carried.  
 
ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve purchasing shade structures, spending up to $40,000.  Motion carried. 

     b.Consideration of a request to purchase a street sweeper. 

Mr. Marrs reported that the street sweeper was supposed to be here already to test drive, but it hasn’t arrived yet.  The Town is allowed to use it for a week-long trial.   Mayor Wenke asked Town attorney Findlay if it could be rented out to businesses.  Mr. Findlay reported that while it is legal to do this, it isn’t practical, as it could open the Town up to liability if another entity broke the sweeper or was injured.  

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve purchasing a street sweeper.  Motion carried. 
NEW BUSINESS

a.Swear in newly elected officials. 

Mayor Wenke swore in Mr. Kit, Mr. Nordyke, Mr. Wilhelm, and Mr. Jagow as Trustees.  He then swore in Ms. Patterson as the new Mayor. 

b.Consideration of a request to approve a Sexual Assault Awareness Month Proclamation.

Ms. Sumpter explained what Sexual Assault Awareness Month is and how signing the proclamation helps survivor services.  

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve a Sexual Assault Awareness Month Proclamation.  Motion carried. 

c.Consideration of a request to approve the 2026 Façade Grant applications.

The recommendations made by the Façade Grant Committee were approved with one amendment - awarding $3,542 instead of $5,000 to the Wildflower Hotel. 

ACTION:   Mr. Wilhelm moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve the 2026 Façade Grant applications with one amendment.  Motion carried.

d.Consideration of a request to approve a Public Statement for the expansion project South of Town. 

Ms. Patterson reported that she would like to have a statement prepared for the Trustees and employees to use when questioned by the public about the water/sewer project. 

Recommended amendments to this statement include adding when the project commences, and the sentence “Improves the possibility of the private lots being built on”, along with stating that the plans for the infrastructure are available at Town Hall.  

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Shy seconded to approve a Public Statement for the expansion project South of Town with additions/amendments made.  Motion carried. 

e.Consideration of a request to approve the new language for future Façade Grant Applications. 

Ms. Patterson reported that she would like to put in bold on the application that it is a reimbursement grant, and that the applicant must have a business license in Town limits.   The business also must be a brick-and-mortar establishment, and if a sign is included in their request, it is only one sign per business.

This will be tabled for approval until after Mr. Fulton and Mr. Wilhelm make the changes that were discussed.  

f.Consideration of a request to approve the Memorandum of Understanding between the Town and Round Mountain Water & Sanitation regarding the water and sewer infrastructure project.  

This still needs to be reviewed by Round Mountain, but the Town Trustees approved of it.  Mr. Wilhelm recused himself from the vote.  

ACTION:   Mr. Shy moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve the Memorandum of Understanding between the Town and Round Mountain Water & Sanitation regarding the water and sewer infrastructure project, subject to approval by Round Mountain.  Motion carried.
