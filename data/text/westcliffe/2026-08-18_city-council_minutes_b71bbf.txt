OLD BUSINESS
       
       None.

NEW BUSINESS

a.Consideration of a request to close Main Street for the First Responders Parade on September 11th. 

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Nordyke seconded to approve closing Main Street for the First Responders Parade on September 11th. Motion carried. 

b.Sheriff’s report.

The Sheriff was not present. 

c.PUBLIC HEARING – Ordinance. No. 2-2026, Moratorium on Data Centers.

 Mayor Pro-Tem Shy announced that the Public Hearing was open.

There was no public comment.

Mayor Pro-Tem Shy announced that the Public Hearing was closed. 

d.Consideration of a request to approve Ordinance No. 2-2026, Moratorium on Data Centers.

Attorney Findlay reported that he drafted this is an emergency ordinance so it can be effective immediately. 

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Dembosky seconded to approve Ordinance No. 2-2026, Moratorium on Data Centers.  Motion carried.

ADDITIONS TO THE AGENDA

None. 
     
STAFF & COMMITTEE REPORTS

     a.Streets/Parks Supervisor report.

Mr. Kendrick reported that Black Hills Energy has replaced damaged lights on Main Street.  The irrigation systems in the parks are complete.  They will begin cleaning out culverts soon. 
He received three bids for shade structures to put up at Memorial Park.  The Board agreed to move forward with ordering them.  

            b.Town Clerk report.

Ms. Christie reported that the budget workshop is scheduled for August 28th at 1:00.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Mr. Schneider reported that they awarded the reservoir project to Mountain Valley Excavation which will begin in September and hoping to be done by May.  The wastewater pilot study is 70% complete.
