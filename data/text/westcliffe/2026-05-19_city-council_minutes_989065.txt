OLD BUSINESS
       
a.Consideration of a request to approve the 2026 Façade Grant applications.

The changes from last month were made.  On the application under number 13 the Board wants to limit the amount of money used for signs – the maximum expenditure will be $1,000.   

ACTION:   Mr. Wilhelm moved, and Mr. Jagow seconded to approve the 2026 Façade Grant applications with the change of number 13 capping signs at $1,000.  Motion carried.  
 
NEW BUSINESS

a.Consideration of a request to paint a mural on a fence at 106 S. 2nd Street.

Ms. Roy reported that she would like to paint a flower mural on the fence located at Candy’s Coffee.  She checked with the surrounding businesses, and no one objected.  

ACTION:   Mr. Shy moved, and Mr. Fulton seconded to approve painting a mural on a fence at 106 2nd Street.  Motion carried. 

b.Sheriff’s report.

Sheriff Smith reported that call numbers are down except for thefts.  There is a new mental health program led by someone with a Ph.D. in crisis counseling.  He will come to next month’s meeting to report on the E-bike laws as requested by the Board. 
 

c.Custer County Tourism Board report.

Ms. Trenker and Ms. Austin reported that they are the new chairs for the Tourism Board.  They are going to focus on a year around economy, focusing on Fall and Winter activities.


d.Consideration of a request to appoint Mark Dembosky as a Trustee.

There is a vacant Trustee seat – no one ran in the election for it. 
 
ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Fulton seconded to approve appointing Mark Dembosky as a Trustee. Motion carried.

e.Consideration of a request to close Main Street from 2nd Street to 3rd Street during the summer beginning next year.

Mayor Patterson reported that she wants to start a discussion regarding possibly closing Main Street during the summers for safety reasons.  Main Street is becoming more dangerous with over-length vehicles and people walking across the street, not at the crosswalk.   Vehicles would use North Ave. and come to 2nd Street where she would like a three-way stop sign.  Vendors can still get through to the grocery store.   Mr. and Mrs. Martinez, who own Wet Mountain Treasures, stated that their business goes down when Main Street is closed.  They are also concerned about the older people trying to get into the library without having the handicapped parking spots.   

Mr. Wilhem expressed concern that if Main Street is closed, people will begin parking in the alleys and then service vehicles would not be able to get through.   He suggested closing Main Street just on big weekends with events like parades.  

Ms. Patterson reported that the Town needs to start directing people to park in the lot on Rosita across from All Aboard.  Budget season is approaching and money should be set aside for signage to that lot.  The parking lot may need to be paved and striped to fit the most amount of vehicles.  

Mayor Patterson would like an agenda item of ‘continued discussion of safety and parking on Main Street’ on future agendas and possibly schedule a workshop.  

f.Consideration of a request to appoint Kit Shy as Mayor Pro-Tem.

Mr. Shy recused himself from voting. 

ACTION:   Mr. Fulton moved, and Mr. Wilhelm seconded to approve appointing Kit Shy as Mayor Pro-Tem.  Motion carried.

  g.Consideration of a request to remove Paul Wenke, Kathy Reis, and George Mowry from the Town United Business Bank accounts. 
 
The bank needs a formal motion to remove previous Trustees from the bank accounts.  

ACTION:   Mr. Jagow moved, and Mr. Fulton seconded to approve removing Paul Wenke, Kathy Reis, and George Mowry from the Town United Business Bank accounts.  Motion carried.

STAFF & COMMITTEE REPORTS

 a.Streets/Parks Supervisor report.

Mr. Marrs reported that the parks have been fertilized.  The street paving project has been completed.  The new street sweeper has arrived, and they will be using it to clean the streets in preparation for the Memorial Day parade. 

      b.Town Clerk report.

Ms. Christie reported that the Colorado Municipal League District Meeting is Thursday, May 21st.  The Trustees can attend if they want to.  Westcliffe and Silver Cliff are co-hosting it at All Aboard. 

      c.Westcliffe Parks Advisory Committee report.

None.

       d.Report from Jerry Pearl regarding the infrastructure project. 

Mr. Pearl reported that the project started last Tuesday.  Things are going smoothly overall.  There have been some questions regarding Round Mountain’s specs compared to Atencio’s but those have been worked through.  He is just eyes on the job – he isn’t supervising safety stuff, just checking on the daily progress.  The excavation company has concerns about getting paid.  Mayor Patterson thinks payment should be based off of the percentage of work complete.  She does not want Ed to wait another month to get paid.  But the engineering company has not sent an invoice to the Town.  Mayor Patterson would like the attorney to read the contract – Ms. Christie will send it to him.  Mr. Fulton will call the engineering company to check on the invoice and the pay app.
