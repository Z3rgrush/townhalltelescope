Commission Comments 
 
Ford's Comments: 
 
It’s been a mess the last part of the week and weekend. The entire back half of my apartment 
flooded with last week's rain and Thursday’s deluge. Thankfully Phoenix Restoration has been 
working on it since and hopefully will have it fixed and good to go this afternoon. 
 
Thursday afternoon I listened in on the Council on Foreign Affairs webinar “Safeguarding 
Against Cyber Threats and Foreign Interference”. Jessica Brandt, Senior Fellow for 
Technology and National Security at CFR. She discussed the current cybersecurity landscape, 
including recent attack on water utilities and ways cyber threats link national security to the 
day-to-day responsibilities of state and local governments and implications for election 
security ahead of the November 3rd midterm election. Next we heard from John Israel, 
Assistant Commissioner and State Chief Information Security Officer at Minnesota IT 
Services, and he spoke to state and local realities of cybersecurity strategies, drawing on 
Minnesota’s whole of the state approach and their recent attacks on community water systems 
and what defending those systems means for officials responsible for them. It really was an 
informative conversation and a very robust question and answer session followed as well.

Thursday evening, I attended the special meeting of the Riley County Republicans to vote and 
appoint a new individual for the position of Riley County Clerk. I am happy to say our very 
own Budget and Finance Officer was elected by a wide majority to fill the role being replaced 
by Rich Vargo who of course is retiring in December.  
 
Friday I spent some time trying to catch up on personal errands and other items. Did catch part 
of the Manhattan High School game on the radio as they beat Junction City to bring back the 
Silver Trophy home.  
 
Saturday I attended the League of Women Voters Candidates Forum at the Manhattan Public 
Library. Pleasantly surprised of the turnout considering how nice it was on Saturday. They 
broke it up into two forums and I was part of the second one which started at 12:40 p.m. and 
ran till 2:00 p.m. I was with a panel of county candidates, the State Board of Education, and 
State representatives. A lot of really good thought-provoking questions were asked as well.  
 
Sunday I spent watching a little football as my Jaguars won once again on the road against 
Cincinnati 22-17 in a hard-fought game. Spent some time getting caught up on various 
correspondence and preparing for the upcoming week as well. 
 
 
McKinley's Comments: 
 
Thursday evening was the Riley County Republicans meeting to select Rich Vargo’s 
replacement. 
 
Saturday, we attended the Alzheimer's Walk in Manhattan City Park. There was a ceremony 
before the walk. The Manhattan High School band was there and the Kansas State University 
baseball team. Later that day we attended the Riley City Fall Fest. They have a barbeque 
contest and several food trucks. There was a large car show even larger than it had been. It was 
well attended and we saw a lot of our Riley friends.  
 
 
Focke's Comments: 
 
Friday morning, I met with two homeowners from Tattarax with Michael Hawthorne to 
discuss Lot 70. They had some plans for the property with them. After the meeting, I spent 
some time shopping downtown and then did other errands.  
 
Saturday, my yard person came by and mowed the grass and did a lot of tree pruning. Things 
are looking much better in the yard. I spent most of the afternoon cleaning the floors and other 
household chores that I had been putting off.  
 
Sunday I went to church and then went to a friend’s house for a discussion with some others 
about the “Central Kansas Justice Ministry”. They are doing community gatherings to discuss 
important issues and challenges of the communities to address justice networks needed.  
 
Later, I worked on some Law Board issues, reviewed today’s agenda and documents, and took 
the dog for a walk at Keats Park.
