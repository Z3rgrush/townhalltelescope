Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments: 
 
Monday and Tuesday were spent working on cleaning up and drying out my apartment after 
last week’s flood. Looks like we got everything fixed and back to normal. Huge shoutout to 
Cobalt Property Management and Phoenix Restoration for the work effort and keeping this 
from becoming a huge disaster. 
  
Tuesday and Wednesday I spent at work. Pretty normal at this point as we gear up to do the 
annual inventory at the end of the month, and other good news we have been getting some new 
employees on board which is proving to be productive as well. 
 
Tuesday afternoon I met with a group of residential and commercial business owners both in 
Manhattan and the City of Ogden to discuss some of the challenges they are having with 
moving forward on plans they have. Unfortunately, because their property is either in a 
municipal boundary or in Pottawatomie County there’s not a lot I can do to help them but did 
provide some thoughts and ideas and pointed them in the right direction to get their concerns 
hopefully addressed. 
 
Wednesday afternoon after work I had a Zoom with the Council of State Government-Justice 
Center. Michelle Rodriguez, Policy Director for the CSG, and David Amora, Senior Advisor 
for the CSG and I discussed the issues and challenges facing Community Corrections and the 
21st Judicial District and ways hopefully we can improve and make the model and ways we 
can shape a better financial formula with the State of Kansas. This is something our 
Community Corrections Director Megan Lewis is working on along which other state and 
local partners as well. I had a really dynamic conversation on the topic and I had to apologize 
for being a few minutes late as well. 
 
Later Wednesday afternoon I attended a ribbon cutting and ceremony out at the Sunset Zoo for 
WTC and their completion of the ADOPT Grant they received from the State of Kansas 
several years back to deploy free WiFi spots in critical areas of need around Manhattan and 
various other places. It’s another great example of how local companies bringing back funding 
to the local community to do very important things.  
 
Wednesday evening, I once again met with a local citizen group for Chinese food to discuss

Focke’s Comments: 
 
Monday I had a meeting with Brittany Phillips and David Adams to discuss the Health 
Department reorganization and the upcoming proposal. 
 
Tuesday I had several doctor appointments and a meeting at my church. 
 
Wednesday I attended the monthly meeting of the Military Relations Committee at 
DoubleTree Hotel. The speaker was Colonel Jason Marquart, commander of the Irwin Army 
Hospital since June of this year. He is originally from Wichita and entered the Army at age 17 
as an enlisted combat medic. He had a good presentation on the different services offered at 
the army hospital and took quite a few questions on their operations. Later that evening, I 
practiced with the church bell choir.
