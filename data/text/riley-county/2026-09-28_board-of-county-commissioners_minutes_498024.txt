 11. General Election - Rich Vargo (1-2 minutes) 
 
Vargo reported for the November 3, 2026 General Election that October 13th voter 
registration books close, October 14th advance voting by mail and in-person begins, 
October 27th is the last day to request an advance mail ballot, and November 2nd 
advance voting closes at noon by state law. He said the polls will be open on 
Tuesday, November 3rd from 7:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.  
 
Vargo stated the Riley County Election Office will be closed on Wednesday, 
September 30th from 7:30 a.m. - 1:30 p.m. to train poll workers.  
 
Vargo reported for the General Election Riley County is short 25 poll workers and 
they asking community members to go to the Riley County website and signup to 
help serve our community in a very important process. He stated poll workers are 
trained very well in Riley County and integral to our process. 
9:40 AM Michael Hawthorne, Assistant County Counselor  
 12. Tatarrax Lot 70 
 
Hawthorne discussed the public sale of the vacant Riley County-owned Tatarrax Lot 
70. He said no vote can be taken today due to the absence of Commissioner Ford as it 
must be a unanimous vote. 
 
Hawthorne stated due to the value being under $50,000, the parcel can be sold by 
public or private sale. He said the vote to sell or not would have to be unanimous. 
 
Buell clarified there was a water/sewer treatment plant on the lot which was owned 
by the Tatarrax Development. He said Riley County took over the operation of the 
plant. He stated then Manhattan City provided water and sewer services. 
 
Parker discussed his desire to purchase the lot as a recreation area to the community. 
 
Move to table the Tatarrax Lot 70 discussion until all commissioners are available. 
 
RESULT: Passed
