Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments: 
 
Sorry once again I was not here for Monday morning's meeting. Between lingering vehicle 
issues and still battling flu symptoms I wasn’t able to get in the office. Good news all seems to 
be well now, and we will keep moving on. 
 
Tuesday and Wednesday spent my usual time at work, things continue to progress there.  
 
Tuesday there was a groundbreaking at the Manhattan Area Technical College and their 
Electrical Technology program. With the local support of Schultz Construction and Mead 
Lumber, they have added a two-story Electrical Learning Lab structure that will provide hands 
on residential and commercial wiring experience for students. The investment will help the 
region’s workforce pipeline and help students prepare for high-demand, high wage careers, 
and the growing need for this skill in construction and manufacturing.  
 
Also Tuesday I attended the Official 2027 Campaign Kickoff for the Konza United Way out at 
the Shamrock Zone. Very well attended event as usual and we heard stories and testimonials 
from five different agencies that the United Way serves each year and had an activity of 
putting together First Aid Emergency bags for local student and teachers and as always it was 
a great way to network with the agencies and board members from the United Way itself. 
Always a great event to attend. 
 
Wednesday I attended the September Policy Board meeting of the Flint Hills Metropolitan 
Planning Organization at the Manhattan Public Library. After some opening welcomes and 
introductions, we got a financial update with an update on the monthly statement balance and 
major revenue and expenses. Next we had a staff update which included updates on the crash 
data from the City of Manhattan and the Fort Riley Safe Passage Program. We got a KDOT 
update from their representative followed by several action items which included approval of

session for a director's evaluation, and then several discussion items which included future 
audits and the 2027 UPWP updates. We then adjourned and will meet again in October. 
 
Also, on Wednesday afternoon there was an Edge 3.0 Project Open House at the ground floor 
event space at 1960 Kimball Avenue. It was an opportunity to learn about the overall project

by a question and answer session with the developer. 
 
 
McKinley’s Comments: 
 
Monday afternoon we had a Special Law Board meeting that did not last too long. In the 
evening I had a call with someone about the road maintenance in Grant Township. 
 
Tuesday we had the first meeting of the search committee for the new RCPD Director. Had a 
good discussion about how the search will proceed. 
 
Wednesday was the monthly meeting at the Juvenile Detention Facility. Had a good discussion 
about the high numbers at the facility and the slow process to get through the courts. Riley 
County is way over our budgeted days and will be receiving a large bill at the end of the year. 
There are three other counties also way over. We all signed a letter to the Juvenile Justice 
Committee asking to reinstate funds taken away a couple of years ago. Not optimistic but we 
are trying. One of the more unusual incidents this month was when a parent bringing in a vape 
like device to the facility. Staff knew what it was and Junction City Police were called and the 
parent was arrested and charged. 
 
 
Focke’s Comments: 
 
Monday, after our meeting, I had several medical-related appointments to determine the next 
steps in upcoming physical therapy sessions and outcomes, and got some new and on-going 
exercises.  
 
Monday afternoon we had a Special Law Board meeting here in our chambers. We heard from 
a citizen concerning executive sessions that we have been having on personnel issues. We 
have a committee established to find a new RCPD director when Brian Peete leaves at the end 
of the year.  
 
Tuesday and Wednesday I worked on Riley County Commission and Law Board business – 
lots of e-mails and phone conversations. 
 
Tuesday evening and Wednesday, I reviewed today’s agenda and documents and, on 
Wednesday evening, I practiced with my church’s bell choir.
