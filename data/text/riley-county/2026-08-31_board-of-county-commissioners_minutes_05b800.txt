Commission Comments 
 
Ford's Comments: 
 
Thursday afternoon I attended the Ribbon Cutting and Open House of the new Riley County 
Emergency Operations Center just off of Charles Little Road where the old EMS Station used 
to be. Another extremely hot day, but still a well-attended event. So great to know we have 
now completely updated and modernized our emergency service and the necessary 
infrastructure that will last and serve the community for decades to come. 
 
Also Thursday afternoon I had my monthly meeting with my bariatrics doctor. Had a good 
appointment and we are supplementing some changes that will line up better with the changes 
being made to the overall structure of my diet moving forward. 
 
Friday morning I met with Riley County Emergency Services Director David Adams and EMS 
Division Chief Josh Gering to discuss a handful of items including the Health Department and 
some possible changes on the horizon. We also discussed the overall transition for the 
Emergency Services Department as a whole and all the new infrastructure and what it means 
for the future of all the combined services as a whole. Had a really good dialog and picked up 
a lot of new information as well. 
 
Also Friday morning I met with Riley County Historical Museum Director Katharine Hensler. 
I realized it had been a while since I made it over that way, so a lot of things have changed, 
several new displays and work continues to occur on backlogs in several departments, and they 
are really making progress. I’ve been really impressed with the continued community outreach 
and programming they continue to do there at the museum and abroad throughout the county 
and with many various community partners. 
 
Weekend was rather quiet, enjoyed some college football on Saturday and remained home 
over the weekend, and worked on correspondence and getting caught up and ready for this 
upcoming week.
