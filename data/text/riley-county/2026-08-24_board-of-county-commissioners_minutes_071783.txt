Ford’s Comments:  
 
On Thursday post BOCC meeting I met with Megan Lewis, Director of Riley County 
Community Corrections to discuss things and challenges pertinent to her department in more 
depth and some ways we can engage the appropriate state agencies moving forward as well. 
We took some time to discuss the grant process, personnel, and space needs for the 
department, and had a great dialog on the recovery court process and future funding needs as 
well. Big thanks to Megan for taking the time and effort to chat with me. Later Thursday 
afternoon, I met with my nutritionist via Video Conference. We had a good dialog, and we 
have collectively decided it is time to make some wholesale changes to my diet and start 
working on a complete transformation. It will take some time and effort of course, but it’s long 
overdue. Also Thursday afternoon, I briefly attended the ribbon cutting for the new L. Eldon 
Gideon Agronomy Research & Innovation Complex up in the Edge District. This event had a 
huge turnout, and several university officials spoke on the importance of the facility and what 
it adds to the school and the community as a whole. Thursday evening, I attended the Big 1st 
Republican District “Road the Victory” tour that took place at the Manhattan Country Club. It 
was a very well-attended event and dinner with state executive and legislative officials and 
candidates in attendance as well as local and regional people. We also had Congressman Mann 
and U.S. Senator Jerry Moran in attendance too, plus officials from the county, district, and 
state parties to boot. Great time to network and chat with everyone from across the area and 
the State of Kansas. 
 
On Friday morning, I was planning on working in the morning, but due to some technical 
issues I wasn’t able to, which was great as I was able to catch up on some personal errands, 
including finally getting my 2026-2027 Fort Riley installation pass. As usual, everyone at the 
Welcome Center was extremely helpful, friendly, and professional in getting that taking care 
of. Friday afternoon I had lunch at Tallgrass Taphouse with Brian Fuemmeler from McCown 
Gordon. We discussed some of the construction projects they will be working on locally 
moving forward and some potential candidates for the County Road and Bridge Advisory 
Board as well. It is good to catch up with our area construction and building professionals and 
to look forward as well.  
 
The weekend was rather quiet and not a bad one to be out and about. Finally, I got caught up 
on some correspondence and worked on some relevant conversations and issues that will be 
coming up here shortly. Thankfully, this is the last weekend without real football till February.

 7. Commercial Income and Expense Questionnaires going out this week - Anna Burson 
(2 minutes) 
 
Burson presented during Press Conference that Income and Expense Questionnaires 
are being mailed this week. As part of the State-mandated reappraisal process, the 
Riley County Appraisers office is conducting a survey of 
multifamily/commercial/industrial properties and respectfully requests your 
participation. We are studying trends in the local real estate market regarding rents, 
occupancy, and operating expenses. Income and expense questionnaires will be 
mailed this week. The information we receive from the surveys returned will be 
maintained confidential in accordance with Kansas Law and standards of professional 
appraisal practice. The information will be used to calibrate our valuation models, 
and it WILL NOT be used to value the specific properties from which information is 
provided. It will be used in conjunction with data on other properties of similar use to 
find typical rents, occupancy, and expenses for the particular use type (retail, office, 
multifamily, etc.). We value your time. If it’s easier to print out an income and 
expense report, Rent roll, or IRS tax statement please provide that information to 
supplement the survey form. Additionally, the forms being mailed can be accessed on 
the Riley County Appraiser’s Office web page under Real Estate>Income & Expense 
Questionnaires. The completed questionnaire and/or additional documentation can be 
returned by email, mail or dropped off no later than September 18th, 2026 to our 
office.
