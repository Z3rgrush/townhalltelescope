Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments: 
 
Monday post BOCC meeting I met with Amanda Webb, Riley County Planning and Special 
Projects Director to discuss how things are happening in her department including the Data 
Center conversation, Home in The Heartland Grant, the Comprehensive and Strategic Plans 
and several other items in regard to planning and zoning.  
 
Monday afternoon I went back and watched Session One of the Kansas Department of Health 
and Environment Series “Pulse of Public Health: A Commissioner’s Guide – The Value of 
Public Health and How it Strengthens Your Community”. It was a discussion to help clarify 
what public health really is, why it matters to your communities and the critical role county 
leadership plays in protecting the health of its residents. Unlike the third installment this was a 
bit better to follow and understand and was rather informative as well. 
 
Tuesday I took the day off and watched the Primary Election returns. Obviously I was 
entrenched in the local returns and I’m thankful for all who were willing to vote for me, but I 
too was interested in state and other local returns from around Kansas as well. I also want to 
give a large shoutout to our Elections staff, workers and everyone who had a hand in making 
this round a success. I have heard nothing but compliments from everyone I’ve engaged with. 
 
Wednesday morning, I spent at work helping to get caught up on orders and product and other 
various things. 
 
Wednesday afternoon I met with Kam Hutton SFC with the USARMY 1ID to discuss some 
military relations and other items pertaining to Fort Riley as a whole. Had a very in-depth 
discussion and dialog and it really gave me some things to consider moving forward on several 
accounts. 
 
Wednesday evening, I listened in on a Telephone Townhall hosted by 2nd District U.S., House 
Representative Derek Schimdt. Even though he doesn’t serve our district he does serve part of 
the Fort Riley Installation and is on the Armed Services Committee so I was curious what he 
might say in regard to that and what questions would come up as well. With that said there was 
dialog on issues of federal spending, social security, the SAVE Act, and the farm/agriculture 
bill to name a few. Interesting conversation all around to say the least. 
 
I ended Wednesday with a trip over to Ogden to attend their city council meeting. Gave 
everybody a quick update on everything going on with Riley County and stuck around to listen 
to them finish their city business and other items.  
 
 
McKinley's Comments: 
 
No comments.

Focke’s Comments: 
 
Tuesday I attended the Worship Committee meeting at my church. We worked on the 
September schedule for services and went over our budget for 2027. After the meeting, I came 
to the office to catch up on some work.  
 
Wednesday I attended the Military Luncheon at the DoubleTree event space. The guest 
speaker was Brigadier General Matthew J. Hardman, Deputy Commanding General. He 
discussed the various deployments and re-deployments at the Fort. It’s been a real busy time. 
The schedule for fall activities is pretty full. Apple Days will be in September this year.  
 
Later that afternoon, Gregg Eyestone came by my house to look at a large evergreen tree that 
has some dying branches. Unfortunately, it wasn’t good news, but the tree may hopefully 
survive. After that, I reviewed the agenda and documents for today’s meeting.
