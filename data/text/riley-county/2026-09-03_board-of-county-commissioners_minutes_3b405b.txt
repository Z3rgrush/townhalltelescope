Commission Comments 
 
Ford's Comments: 
  
Monday afternoon I listened in on a National Association of Counties webinar titled “Did You 
Know: The Nation's Counties and its Emergency Response.” This webinar highlighted the 
counties employment of 939,000 public servants who are trained and dedicated for emergency 
response, preparedness and tasked with conducting these duties as they happen whether they 
are natural or man-made or required first response as well. Keep in mind that September is 
National Preparedness Month and a great time to recognize those who serve across the board 
in these capacities including right here in Riley County. Thanks for all you do and your work 
and effort are always greatly appreciated.  
  
Tuesday and Wednesday I spent working my normal schedule, awfully hot but all is still going 
well. 
  
Tuesday I came down for the American Red Cross Blood Drive to donate here at the BOCC 
Chambers. It once again was quick, painless, and efficient and was in and out in less than a 
half hour. With the new APP and the Rapid Pass, it’s really easy to do and these blood 
donations are critical at all times of the year and really help save lives around the country. 
  
Wednesday I listened in on a Telephone Town Hall with Congressman Tracey Mann and 
followed up with another one with Congressman Derek Schmidt. It was a great opportunity 
while the U.S. Congress is currently in session to get caught up on important legislation that 
effects Kansas in the areas of agriculture, military and veterans services, and other areas that 
are affecting the citizens of Kansas as a whole. Keeping in mind with Congressman Mann 
being on the Agriculture Committee, and Congressman Schmidt on the Armed Services 
Committee, it was important to get caught up in real time on everything that’s going on. 
  
Later Wednesday evening I attended the Ogden City Council meeting over in Ogden. Gave a 
short update on everything we are working on here at Riley County as well as some other 
challenges facing the city. Also listened in on items from pertinent city staff as well as the 
members of the council.  
  
Also working on several issues and concerns that I have had with several Riley County 
citizens over the week. Hopefully will get the right answers from the right people here shortly. 
Also working and talking to several groups and people on filling some of the open advisory 
board positions that are currently open and or will open by years end.

Focke's Comments: 
  
Monday afternoon, after the BOCC meeting, I got lunch and then went to do some water 
aerobics and swimming in a friend’s pool. Later that evening, and for the rest of this week, I 
have been taking my dog to Keats Park for walks and to follow progress on the lagoon. Work 
has been underway, with the lower part of the pedestrian trail being rocked to accommodate 
the heavy equipment traffic. The path connects to a new passage closer to the creek than where 
we had the groundbreaking. Some large dirt berms are developing on either side of the area. 
It’s been interesting to watch progress. The park always has something going on, with 
pedestrians, picnics and other activities. 
  
Tuesday morning, I did a short welcome address to the Kansas Emergency Management 
Association meeting, being held at the Hilton Garden Inn this week. They have a full schedule 
with keynote speakers, breakout sessions, and tours of our facilities, etc.  
  
Wednesday I attended the Military Luncheon at the Doubletree Hotel Event Center. The guest 
speaker was Fort Riley Garrison Commander, Col. Joseph Orr, Jr.  
  
I have a list of upcoming events, with apple pie pre-sales from August 27th – Sept. 15th. The 
Fall Apple Day Festival at Fort Riley will be held this year on September 26th. Normally, I 
attend the Westloop Business Association meeting afterwards, but wasn’t feeling well, so went 
home for several hours before going to my bell practice in the evening.  
  
I had a short time to review today’s agenda and documents, as it wasn’t available Tuesday 
evening.  
  
The RCPD Law Enforcement Board will have a special meeting this afternoon from 3:00 – 
5:00 p.m. here in the Commission Chambers. IT will have it streamed online for the Board.
