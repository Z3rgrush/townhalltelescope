Commission Comments 
 
Ford's Comments: 
 
Thursday afternoon I attended the first Teams call of the legislative year for the National 
Association of Counties Veterans and Military Services Advisory Council. After some 
opening comments from our new Chair Aaron Whitely a Commissioner in the State of Georgia 
(haven’t yet caught the county). We went through and introduced ourselves. Another large and 
strong group for the 2026-2027 for sure. We then had an overview of the council’s initiatives 
and priorities for the upcoming term, went through the results of the recent council survey that 
was provided by the council, we also went through the term schedule and possible engagement 
opportunities upcoming, also had a short open discussion as we typically do to end the 
meeting. 
 
Thursday afternoon I had my monthly appointment with my bariatrics doctor. Nothing new to 
report everything is moving along as planned currently. 
 
Friday Riley County hosted the Annual Northeast Kansas County Officials meeting and 
luncheon up at the Blue Hills room. After a morning program which included an update from 
the Kansas Association of Counties and Bruce Chladny, Executive Director, also got and 
update on the Wareham Hall project from Blade Mages we followed up with breakout session 
from the official departments and a tour of both our new Emergency Operations Center (EOC) 
and the new EMS Station Headquarters. We came back to the Blue Hills Room for a pasta 
buffet lunch, a wrap up and drawing of door prizes and other items. Overall, it went very well 
and a huge thanks to both Riley County staff and all the community partners who helped made 
this a successful event.

Saturday morning, I attended the Riley County Republican Lunch at the Sunset Zoo. Heard 
from numerous current elected officials, candidates, state party officials and other during this 
biannual event. Always a good time to talk and meet with many constituents and voters from 
here inside Riley County. Also, on Saturday I did make a late appearance at Fort Riley’s 
Annual Apple Day. A little bit soggy to be out and about but overall, it was a very pleasant day 
and all usual activities and events were taking place. It is a great way to interact with members 
from the base and see what all makes up the installation. I always come away learning 
something on the history of the base and beyond. Saturday evening, I of course watched the K-
State and Cincinnati game at home. Not quite the outcome I was expecting with the Wildcats 
losing 31-26 for their first loss of the season. 
 
Sunday I caught up on some chores and things at home. Watched the Jacksonville and New 
England game, and the Jaguars won handily 35-6 which was great and I got caught up on 
correspondence and other items for the upcoming week.  
 
Once again I apologize for my absence on Monday, it ended up being a pretty bad day with a 
video call to the doctor as well. I did go back through the livestream, congratulations to 
Andrea Rose from Public Works on being employee of the month that was certainly well 
deserved and on the upcoming Riley County proclamations as well. 
 
Tuesday and Wednesday I spent at work once again. Certainly, feeling a lot like fall these days 
too. 
 
Tuesday evening, I attended the Annual National Night Out over at CiCo Park. Kudos to 
RCPD, Riley County Rural Fire, Riley County EMS, and others for quickly being able to 
transition to a Plan B which put everything indoors due to the rain and weather. Still turned out 
to be a wonderful event. RCPD grilled up amazing hamburgers and hot dogs, and there were 
donuts available from Varsity as well. Had quite a few vendors inside, and trucks and 
equipment including a drone outside in the barns which believe it or not work out great. And 
lastly we were graced with presence of our favorite mascot Willie the Wildcat as well. There 
were bouncy houses for kids and other games and prizes. Overall, it was a great event even 
indoors. 
 
Later Tuesday evening I attended a Town Hall Forum over at the Community Center in 
Odgen. For a rainy Tuesday it was a pretty good turnout. I went through a lot of what we are 
working on here at the county currently and some plans being discussed as well. Had a lot of 
good questions and we had a very solid dialog overall as well. May want to do a follow up at 
some point as well.  
 
Also reached out to Ben Ledo at MakeMyMove to get an update on the Home in the Heartland 
grant as we have not heard anything on it as of yet. I’m told we should be hearing back from 
the State Treasurer’s office here in the next week or so. The total for all applications came in 
below the 1.5 million dollars available so we should be a lock for our requested amount when 
the announcements are made.
