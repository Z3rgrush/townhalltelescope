Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments: 
 
Thursday post BOCC meeting I met with Riley County Treasurer Shilo Heger. We discussed 
some ongoing efforts in her department, some ideas for future budgets, and the new motor 
vehicle fee structure just to name a few. Appreciated the dialog and the conversation surely 
sparked some thoughts and ideas. 
 
Friday morning, I was at Stormont Vail for an echocardiogram. The process took about an 
hour and the results were pretty good for the most part. I will need to follow up with the 
cardiologist to go through it in a little more depth at some point. 
 
Friday late morning I met with Elizabeth Ward, Riley County Human Resources Director to go 
though some things, some thoughts and ideas and various other items in regard to personnel as 
well as the personnel budget, and the budget moving forward. Lots of moving pieces but the 
dialog went well. 
 
Friday afternoon I traveled over to Alma and had lunch with Wabaunsee County Sheriff, Eric 
Kirsch. Had a great conversation and dialog on the nuisances of being a public official, and 
several over things including law enforcement in general, the press and media and some 
longer-term plans. Really enjoyed the conversation and lunch from El Corral there in Alma 
was amazing. 
 
Spent much of the weekend at home, not really wanting to deal with the excessive heat and not 
a whole lot going on. 
 
Sunday afternoon I did make it over to the Riley County Fair to once again judge in the 
Baking Competition. This year’s competition was apple pie in honor of the America 250 
Celebration. I along with Tiffany Oppelt from TheraPie did the judging and we had nine 
entries to choose from. Every single entry was delicious, but several did standout from the 
others in terms of taste as well as presentation. Thanks to all who came out for the event and 
for those who also entered the competition.

Focke’s Comments: 
 
Thursday after our BOCC meeting, Amanda Webb, John Ellerman, Vanessa Gray, and Brian 
Foster (BG Consultants) met to discuss a ribbon cutting for the Keats wastewater project. It 
will be on August 13th at 6:00 p.m. in the lagoon area west of the Keats Park. We discussed 
the best location for the ribbon cutting. Also discussed was the contractor’s request for a 
staging area in the park, which we didn’t approve, as it would take too much space from the 
usable park area for residents. The contractor will be advised to find a location in the area and 
work it out with a property owner. Vanessa Gray will work on a flyer to hand out to residents 
and at the community meal on July 29th.  
 
Friday I came down to the office to do some work, go to vote and then have a quick meeting 
with Vanessa Gray on the Keats flyer. I also had a physical therapy appointment and stopped 
by the fair briefly.  
 
The weekend was fairly quiet, as I had a lot of catching up to do from my trip to Virginia.
