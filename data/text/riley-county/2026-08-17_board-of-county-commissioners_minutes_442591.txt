Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments:  
 
Thursday evening, I attended the long-awaited groundbreaking for the Keats Sanitary Sewer 
Project. Had a great turnout considering how incredibly hot it was. After well over a decade’s 
worth of discussion and planning this project finally is getting built. Huge thanks to all the 
community partners and officials who worked diligently through the years to finally make this 
necessary rural infrastructure upgrade a reality.

Friday morning, I had a video call with the doctor. It merely was just a status quo update to see 
where the status of things are and how to keep moving forward with the plan in place. 
 
Saturday morning, I attended the Riley County Republican Coffee talk over at Hy-Vee. Had 
various local candidates and officials available and had a really good number of people in 
attendance as well. Gave an update on everything happening here at the county and answered a 
few questions along the way too. 
 
Saturday I had some friends come into town and we had lunch at Texas Roadhouse, judging by 
the traffic and activity around town I would say the majority of people are back and ready to 
go for the upcoming school year. 
 
Saturday I did watch some preseason NFL games including my Jacksonville Jaguars who 
came back to beat the New Orleans Saints 24-20.  
 
Glad once again to receive some more rain over the weekend after how hot and humid it was 
over the last week. The rest of the weekend I spent at home worked on some various things for 
the upcoming week which should prove to be busy. 
 
 
McKinley’s Comments: 
 
Thursday evening was the groundbreaking for the Keats Sanitary Sewer Project. A nice crowd 
to see this long lasting project. 
 
Friday we went to Concordia. In the afternoon we went to the Orphan Train Museum. I have a 
friend whose grandfather came out on the orphan trains. It was a real interesting education and 
I found the story of his grandfather's life. His family name is on one of the buildings. We also 
went through the Cloud County Museum. It is really large and has a lot of interesting 
materials. In the evening we went to the Tyrus show which is why we went to Concordia.  
 
Saturday, I attended the Riley County Republicans meeting for a short while. I was able to 
explain the appraisal process to a couple of people. I later attended the Fall Lawn Care class by 
Gregg Eyestone. 
 
 
Focke’s Comments: 
 
Thursday after our meeting, I had lunch downtown and then packed my car with items for the 
groundbreaking at Keats. Thursday evening, I attended the groundbreaking for the Keats 
Sanitary Sewer Project. It was well attended, but pretty hot, so we had an informal gathering in 
the shelter’s meeting room, where project managers were present to answer questions from 
attendees and residents. We provided some refreshments and it was a successful event, and 
great start to the year-long project.  
 
Friday afternoon I took items back to my office and went over the agenda for today’s meeting. 
Later I had a physical therapy session and picked up the Law Board packet for today’s 
meeting.

scheduling delays.
