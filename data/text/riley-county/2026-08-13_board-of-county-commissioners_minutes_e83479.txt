Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments: 
 
Monday pre-meeting I met with Riley County Counselor Jacob Hansen to discuss some things 
related to his department dealing with projects they are working on as well as some other legal 
and statutory issues we are currently facing, and a dialog on the upcoming and likely transition 
taking place with his department as well. Enjoyed catching up and getting some much needed 
insight. Also, on Monday I met with Cory Meyer, Riley County IT/GIS Director to discuss 
some things happening in his department as well. Some of those items included the rising cost 
in technology and how we are planning ahead for those needs, the renovation and the 
integration of both the IT and GIS Department into one and what that may look like, and 
discussion on some other county department changes and what that could look like, and maybe 
the most important cybersecurity and how it pertains to the county’s operation and AI as well. 
All necessary and needed items to discuss for sure. 
 
Worked both Tuesday and Wednesday this week, it certainly was a hot couple of days for sure, 
but all is going well on all accounts. 
 
Tuesday afternoon I met with Kent Glasscock from the Flint Hills Regional Council at 
Radina’s here downtown to discuss some things related to his organization. Some specific 
possibilities on grants that may come up with USDA, SBA, and HUD potentially, and how we 
could either collaborate or what the counties best approach would be. Got some thoughts on 
the US 24 Corridor Study that is now out and possible options to help rebuild and possibly 
replace some other county infrastructure moving forward. It was great to have the opportunity 
to chat and get some new insight on these issues and certainly appreciated the time. 
 
Wednesday afternoon I met with Dani Hallgren at the Konza Prairie Community Health 
Center to discuss services and the program they are working on and expanding services as well 
that we may be able to collaborate and work together on along with assistance at the Riley 
County Health Department in the future. Really had a great conversation with some excellent 
possible ideas for both entities moving forward. We also discussed changing trends and 
challenges in both the public and community health sectors as well.  
 
Wednesday evening, I attended the August Business After Hours hosted by the Manhattan 
Area Chamber of Commerce out at A&H Farm. Pretty hot outside to get out on the farm but 
we had a great event inside with lots of great food and conversation. I even came home with

Tuesday morning, I met with Amanda Webb and Vanessa Gray at Keats Park to determine a
