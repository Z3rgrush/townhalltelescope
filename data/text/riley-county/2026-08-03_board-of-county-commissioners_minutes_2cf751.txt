Commission Comments 
 
Ford’s Comments:  
  
Actually not a lot going on over the end of the week and over the weekend as well. Thursday 
post BOCC Meeting I met with Anna Burson, Riley County Appraiser and Amy Manges, 
Register of Deeds to discuss everything going on in their departments and discuss some 
thoughts and ideas for the future of the services they and staff provide. 
  
Also on Thursday I had a little afternoon monthly appointment with the bariatrics team. Not a 
lot to really report just kind-of staying status quo for the moment. 
  
Friday morning I met briefly with the doctor via video conference just to catch up and go 
through a couple of things as we move into fall.  
  
The weekend was rather quiet I did manage to get a couple of things done outdoors since it 
was relatively nice out for this time of year. It also marks the last weekend without real 
football as Thursday will officially start the NFL Preseason with the Cardinals vs. the Panthers 
in the Hall of Fame game. 
  
  
McKinley’s Comments: 
  
After our meeting Thursday I met with Lori Muir and then Anna Burson to go over 
miscellaneous county business. 
  
Saturday was a McKinley reunion. It included my brothers and their families and several 
cousins, about 40 total. Included people from Manhattan, Topeka, Kansas City, Kirksville- 
Missouri, and Spain. We started these when some of the grandkids said they would like to see 
their funeral cousins in a different setting. 
  
I think I have mentioned this before but my wife needs your sympathy cards. As of yesterday, 
she has been stuck with me for 51 years. The fact that she was a kindergarten teacher for years 
probably explains how she has dealt with me for all these years.
