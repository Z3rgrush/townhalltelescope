Commission Comments 
 
Ford's Comments: 
 
Hopefully everyone had a wonderful Labor Day holiday and stayed cool and well as it finally 
looks like fall may be finally coming around here in September.  
 
Friday I did speak with Ben Ledo from MakeMyMove yesterday. He said the state is still 
reviewing the second-round applications and that the good news is there were only 1.48 
million in request with the 1.5 million allocated so were basically a shoe in at this point and we 
will be fast tracked once the review is complete. So that should be coming at any point now.  
 
Friday evening, I did take some time to walk around Purple Power Play downtown. Didn’t stay 
incredibly long due to the extreme heat but it was fun and there seemed to be a lot of energy 
for the start of the season. Also brings back memories from the first couple being downtown in 
the early 1990s. 
 
Saturday I attended a Town Hall discussion at the VFW in Randolph. Had a handful of citizens 
there from the north county to discuss some of the issue and challenges facing us here in Riley 
County including the budget, the county administrator position, personnel concerns, property 
taxes and taxes in general, fairgrounds, and emergency services to name a few. Have to say it 
was a spirted conversation but well worth the hour and half to listen to everyone, discuss and 
answer some questions as well. 
 
Saturday evening, I watched the first K-State from home. Ended up being quite the game as 
the Wildcats won 71-3 over Nicholls on another hot evening. Glad to see the Collin Klien era 
getting started off on a good foot and it looked like there was plenty of energy with the team 
and with the crowd. Got a couple of 11 a.m. games coming up next. 
 
Sunday and Monday I spent watching quite a bit of college football, there were some good 
games and a couple a bad ones in the mix. 
 
Also, on Monday I came down to office to do some work and go through some information on 
several agenda items we will be working on over the next couple of meetings to get caught up.  
 
Tuesday afternoon I listened to another National Association of Counties webinar discussing

Focke's Comments:

Chambers. The meeting consisted of an executive session concerning personnel issues and the
