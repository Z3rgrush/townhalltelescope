McKinley's Comments: 
  
Last Friday I attended the retirement reception for Russel Stukey. There was a good crowd 
there including his family. 
  
In the afternoon we had the Pre-Law Board meeting. An item that I had been called about 
earlier was the closing down of Moro Street on Saturday night at 11:00 p.m. My understanding 
was the street would only be closed if problems happened. RCPD thought it was to be at all 
home games. They were advised not to repeat on Saturday. This will probably be further 
discussed at our meeting this afternoon.

Aggieville Business Association. We had a long discussion about Aggieville.  
  
Saturday evening, I went down to Aggieville at 11:00 p.m. There were a lot of people there but 
seemed to be under control and the streets were not blocked. 
  
Sunday we went to Lawrence to meet with our granddaughter. She had us eat at Ramen Bowls.
