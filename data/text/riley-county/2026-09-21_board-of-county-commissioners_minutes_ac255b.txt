Thursday afternoon I had my monthly appointment with my nutritionist, and all is well, our 
new schedule is going to plan, and we are going to stick with it and see where it goes. We may 
need to make a few tweaks here and there, but all is well on this front. 
 
Thursday afternoon I attended the September City/County/County meeting at Manhattan City 
Hall to get an update on the Highway 24 Corridor Plan that both the City of Manhattan, 
Pottawatomie County as well as Riley County have entered into. We heard from Jim Jussel, 
Traffic Team Leader/Senior Associate and Chris Harker, Transportation, Group Leader/Vice 
President, Benesch as well as others on the status currently of the joint corridor study and what 
to expect for the future as well as the timeline for which it will occur. There were leaders and 
officials from both counties and the city on hand for this presentation as well. 
 
Thursday evening, I attended the 2026 Reorganization Meeting of the Riley County 
Republicans. Had a very good turnout and the main committee is still in place, and we have 
most of the same people that were elected delegates and alternative delegates as well in 
keeping with the rules. 
 
Friday morning, I spent of the morning at the Riley County Police Department. Took a full 
tour of the corrections facility with Major Mark French and Assistant Director Erin Freidline. I 
believe this is my third tour of the facility over the last few years and probably the most eye 
opening. ADP as of Friday was 159 with 22 inmates being housed at other locations. It was 
very packed and population density seems to be causing some other challenges as well. I do 
give kudos to the fact they are doing the best they can under not so optimal circumstances. I 
also was able to have a very large dialog with Assistant Director Erin Freidline on the status of 
a lot of things law enforcement related. Had a good dialog and I would express that everybody 
should take a chance and see for themselves the status of RCPD currently. 
 
Saturday once again watched K-State get another win at home vs Tulane 31-20 to run their 
record to 3-0 on the young season. Now comes conference season and a trip to Cincinnati to 
start.  
 
Sunday watched Jacksonville fall apart in the second half to Denver to lose 20-13 to even their 
record out at 1-1. 
 
Spent some time over the weekend getting ready for the upcoming week and catching up on 
various correspondence.
