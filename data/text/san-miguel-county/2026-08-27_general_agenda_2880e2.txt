San Miguel County Weed Board Meeting 
Date: May 5th, 2026 
Time: 4:30 p.m. 
 
1. Attendance 
- CWAB Members Present: Jim Fitzmorris, Suzanne Cheavens, Sheila Grother, John Humphries 
- Staff: Janet Kask, Julie Kolb, Cait Harvey 
 
2. Call to Order 
- By Julie Kolb at 4:50 pm 
 
3. Approval of Minutes 
- Minutes approved for meetings on 3/27/2025 and 10/20/2025 
 
4. Staff Updates  
- A tamarisk removal project was completed in Dry Creek Basin covering approximately 3 linear 
miles, funded through America the Beautiful and Conservation Corps grants.  
- Caitlin (Cait) Harvey has been hired on as the Associate Vegetation Manager for Ouray & San 
Miguel counties.  
 
5. Administrative Matters  
- Bylaws for the CWAB are currently being drafted by Julie Kolb and edited by Janet Kask. Once 
completed, they will be reviewed by the county attorney within the next few weeks.  
 
6. Grant Funding 
- A $5,800 grant from the Colorado Department of Agriculture's Noxious Weed Fund has been 
secured to help defray weed management costs for private property owners in San Miguel County.  
- There is a need to establish guidelines for the allocation of grant funding. Current suggestions 
include basing allocations by acreage, and establishing a maximum funding cap per landowner.  
- Public announcements and social media blasts will be utilized to inform landowners of the 
availability of grant funding for noxious weed treatments.  
- Allocation scenarios and ideas for this grant will be drafted by Julie Kolb for future discussion.  
 
7.  County Equipment Sharing 
- Backpack sprayers are currently available for public use; however, no additional equipment is 
available for public use at this time. 
 
8.  Board Chairperson Designation 
- Suzanne Cheavens was elected as the new Board Chairperson for the CWAB.

9.  Other Matters 
- There is interest from the CWAB in contacting CDOT to see if any weed control work can be 
performed along the highways in San Miguel county, with an emphasis on the corridor between 
Placerville and Dallas Divide.  
- San Miguel county is seeking applicants for a seasonal Vegetation Management Technician.
