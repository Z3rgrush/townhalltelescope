Resolution 2026-39 
 
Whereas, it is the intent of the Board of County Commissioners of San Miguel County, 
Colorado to provide the effective direction, control, and coordination of the San Miguel County 
government during an emergency or disaster; and 
 
Whereas, pursuant to section 24-33.5-707, C.R.S., San Miguel County’s Emergency Manager is 
responsible for preparing and keeping current a local or interjurisdictional emergency plan for 
San Miguel County; and 
 
Whereas, the Board of County Commissioners adopted the Emergency Operations Plan on 
December 10, 2025, with Resolution 2025-49, Reception No. 491709; and 
 
Whereas, in Resolution 2025-49 the Board of County Commissioners delegated the supervision 
of the Emergency Manager to the Emergency Management Coordinator for the San Miguel 
County Sheriff; and 
 
Whereas, the Board of County Commissioners and the San Miguel County Sheriff have 
determined that the Emergency Management Coordinator role is no longer necessary for the 
oversight and supervision of the Emergency Manager.  
 
Now Therefore, Be It Resolved by the Board of County Commissioners of San Miguel County, 
Colorado that Resolution 2025-49 is hereby amended and superseded as follows: 
 
1. The Emergency Manager reports directly to the Board of County Commissioners, 
who delegates the supervision of the Emergency Manager to the County Manager. 
 
2. The San Miguel County Office of Emergency Management (OEM) will consist of the 
Emergency Manager and any subordinate position determined necessary to support 
the work of the Emergency Manager. OEM staff fulfill the requirements of section 
24-33.5-707, C.R.S., and have responsibility for the planning, coordination, and 
execution of pre- and post-disaster services in the County.  
 
Page 21 of 31
2 
 
3. The County Manager, or their designee, assumes the emergency duties of the OEM 
staff in the event of their absence of inability to act, with the exception of a disaster 
based on a public health emergency.  
 
4. All other provisions of Resolution 2025-49 not explicitly amended by this Resolution 
shall remain in effect.
