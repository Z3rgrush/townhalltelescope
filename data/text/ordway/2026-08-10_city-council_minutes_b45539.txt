This letter is in reference to the claim made by the Town of Ordway ("Town") under the CIRSA 
2026 Property and Crime Policy, Coverage Part II, Coverage for Crime ("policy"). The Town 
initially indicated that the factual background of the claim was contained in the Complaint filed by 
the Attorney Regulation Counsel on October 31, 2025. We later asked for additional info1mation 
from the Town but, perhaps due to the lapse of time, some of our requests for information were not 
fully responded to and some of the information we received was potentially conflicting. Although 
for reasons that CIRSA will hereinafter provide, CIRSA will grant coverage to the Town, please 
understand that this analysis indicates that there is probably no coverage for this loss under the 
CIRSA policy. 
The reasons that there is likely no coverage for this loss under the policy are multiple. CIRSA does 
not believe that the policy intended to cover something like that which occurred here and in order 
for there to be any coverage for the acts or omissions of Anthony Perko which caused the Town 
damage, he must have been guilty of"employee dishonesty." 
The policy states: 
COVERAGE A - "Employee dishonesty" - We will pay you for "ultimate net loss" for loss 
excess of the "Member's" deductible for damage to "money," "securities," and "property 
other than money and securities" resulting directly from a dishonest act committed by an 
"employee" of the "Member" acting alone or in collusion with other persons except you or 
from the failure of an "employee" to perform faithfully his duties or account for all monies 
and property received by virtue of his position of employment. 
In relevant part, an "employee" under Coverage Part II is defined as: 
1) Any natural person: a) While in your service, including council and board members, and 
for 30 days after termination of service; and b) Whom you compensate directly by 
salary, wages, or commissions or who are your uncompensated authorized volunteers; 
and c) Whom you have the right to direct and control while performing services for you;

However, the cause of the loss is that Mr. Perko amended a contract in his role as Town Attorney

Based on the information we have, as well as reasonable inferences from information that was not 
supplied, CIRSA does not believe that Mr. Perko was an employee of the Town regarding his 
actions that caused the Town's losses. As such, there is no coverage under the Employee 
Dishonesty coverage. 
There further does not appear to be any coverage for crime Coverages B, C or E under Coverage 
Part II of the policy. Under Coverage B for "Loss Inside the Premises", this coverage contemplates 
physical theft or such other damages from within, such as by way ofrobbery or other physical 
actions. "Premises" under the policy refers to the interior of a physical building used by the Town to 
conduct business. (Definition 27) Further, even if this coverage was somehow applicable, losses 
caused by the dishonest or criminal acts committed by "employees" or "authorized representatives" 
are specifically excluded. Regardless of how Mr. Perko's roles at the Town are characterized, he 
was at least an authorized representative, so coverage is excluded. 
Coverage C for "Loss Outside the Premises" refers to loss of "money" or "security" from theft. This 
coverage contemplates the physical taking of money or property from the custody of the Town or its 
agents which did not occur here. Moreover, the same exclusion that bars coverage for Coverage B 
also applies to Coverage C. 
As to Coverage E for "forgery or alteration", this coverage usually involves the Town's losses from 
someone writing or altering a fake or unauthorized check on its accounts. The coverage explicitly 
concerns a narrow set of instruments, none of which apply here. [See Coverage Eat pg. 31 of the 
policy]. Moreover, this coverage again has the same exclusions as in Coverage B and Coverage C 
which serves to exclude coverage here. 
Two other policy provisions se1ve to prevent coverage. Under IX Conditions, §E Discovery Period 
for Loss, only covered losses discovered no later than one year from the end of the policy period 
are covered. This provision precludes coverage for losses that occur lengthy periods of time after 
the policy has expired. Additionally, General Provision II Your Duties in the Event of a Loss (A) 
requires notice of a claim "as soon as practicable." Either of these provisions likely bars coverage.

party to the contract, concerning a malpractice lawsuit against Mr. Perko. The Town has indicated

SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT, RELEASE AND ASSIGNMENT 
This Settlement Agreement, Release and Assignment ("Agreement"), done the day 
indicated below, between the Town of Ordway, Colorado ("the Town") and the Colorado 
Intergovernmental Risk Sharing Agency ("CIRSA") as follows, to wit: 
WHEREAS, the Town has made a claim to CIRSA under the Coverage for Crime in 
CIRSA's 2026 Property and Crime Policy for a loss which the Town reported to CIRSA and 
which involved the under-collection of outside water rates. The Town has alleged that the loss 
arose from the alleged professional misconduct by former Town Attorney Anthony Perko, as 
detailed in the Complaint filed on October 31, 2025 by the Attorney Regulation Counsel. 
WHEREAS, the claim is identified by CIRSA as Claim No. PC6028377-l and exceeds 
the limits of coverage under the Crime Coverage in the Policy issued by CIRSA; 
WHEREAS, the Town and CIRSA intend to resolve this claim as described more 
specifically below; 
Now, therefore, the Town and CIRSA have reached the following agreement: 
CIRSA will pay to the Town under its above described claim the amount of$149,000 
("payment") which constitutes CIRSA's policy limits for its coverage for crime for the Town in 
the amount of$150,000, less the Town's deductible of$1,000. 
In exchange, the Town agrees that CIRSA has fully and completely satisfied its 
obligations under the 2026 Property and Crime Policy for Coverage for Crime and any other 
crime coverage in any other policy that may apply. CIRSA shall not be responsible for any other 
losses or claims under any Coverage for Crime in any policy issued to the Town. 
meaning of this agreement and has executed this agreement with full knowledge and 
understanding thereof. 
The Town and CIRSA both agree that this agreement is not to be construed, in any way, 
as an admission of liability, responsibility or as any admission of the existence of any coverage 
for crime in the policy for this or any other claim. 
The Town and CIRSA agree that each has participated and/or had an equal opportunity to

based upon a claim that the other party was a drafter. 
The agreement shall be construed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State 
of Colorado, without regard to Colorado's choice oflaw rules or principles.

agreement may also be executed in counterparts.
