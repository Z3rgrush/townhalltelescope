6. Updates
a. 2026 Election Update – Deputy Clerk Bourn presented the certified ballot order

b. Committee Minutes (Informational only)
7. Executive Sessions
a. Conservation Easement Litigation
For a conference with the Town Attorney for the purpose of receiving legal advice on 
specific legal questions under C.R.S. Section 24-6-402(4)(b), specifically for discussion 
of (1) the status of the conservation easement litigation and the next steps being taken 
therein by Town Attorney, and (2) to discuss the upcoming mediation in the conservation
easement litigation and which Trustees have availability to attend what authority for 
settlement they will be given.
b. St. Louis Landing Lease-up Policy and Tenant Eligibility
For a conference with the Town Attorney for the purpose of receiving legal advice on 
specific legal questions under C.R.S. Section 24-6-402(4)(b), specifically pertaining to 
the Town Attorney’s risk assessment of the proposed St. Louis Landing tiered lease-up 
policy and tenant eligibility preferences under the Fair Housing Act and Colorado law.
Trustee Soles moved and Trustee Smith seconded the motion to open the two 
executive sessions regarding Conservation Easement Litigation and St. Louis Landing 
Lease-up Policy and Tenant Eligibility at 7:00 p.m. Motion carried: 7-0.
Trustee Gregory moved and Trustee White seconded the motion to close the two 
executive sessions regarding Conservation Easement Litigation and St. Louis Landing 
Lease-up Policy and Tenant Eligibility at 9:15 p.m. Motion carried: 7-0.
8. Adjourn
Trustee Gregory moved, and Trustee White seconded the motion to adjourn. Motion 
carried: 7-0. Meeting adjourned at 10:16 p.m.
