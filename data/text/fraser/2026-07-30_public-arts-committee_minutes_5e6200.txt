1. Mural Festival 
Canvas Placement & Photo Booth 
The group discussed updated canvas placement for the festival. It was noted that only two canvases would be 
going into the traditional locations, keeping the walkway clear and avoiding interference with traffic patterns. 
Sarah offered her driveway at the yellow house as the location for Jeff Skinner's photo booth setup, noting it 

 
 
would eliminate concerns about paint on the driveway and was a safer option away from traffic near 
Sharkie's. The sauna, which had been present in prior years, was confirmed as not returning due to the heat. 
Sarah noted she would coordinate with Jeff on whether the photo booth setup would occur Thursday night or 
Friday morning, and would notify the relevant parties about parking restrictions accordingly. 
It was confirmed that Fraser River Beer Company would no longer host any canvases, as the space had 
become too tight — with newly added décor such as a lift chair — leaving no room for artists to step back and 
gain perspective on their work. Sarah indicated she would speak with Brian directly to ensure there were no 
surprises given the brewery's long-standing support of the event. With that change, three canvases would be 
placed across the street, and five canvases would remain at the 120Z location. 
Volunteers & Creation Station 
Anastasia reported that volunteer sign-ups were trickling in and that an additional push would be made during 
the week. She also shared the exciting update that Fraser Valley Arts had decided to bring the Creation 
Station to Mural Fest, following its success at Alpine Art Affair. Kelly had ordered additional shirts and was 
expected to add the Creation Station to Sign Up Genius. Anastasia noted she had not yet confirmed the exact 
placement of the Creation Station, as she had been in Denver the prior day, but suggested that proximity to 
the info tent would allow volunteers to float between both. It was also noted that one of the artists had signed 
up to volunteer alongside his wife and would be helping out at the info tent. 
Lion's Club Pancake Breakfast Tent 
Joe raised a last-minute logistical concern regarding the Lion's Club pancake breakfast tent. Upon reviewing 
the setup space, it was discovered that the tent requires large three-foot stakes to be driven into the ground, 
which could potentially strike underground gas or electrical lines. Joe confirmed he had immediately 
submitted a request for utility locates and communicated the situation to Rich, noting that approval to use that 
space could not be given until the locates were completed, expected by Monday. Joe confirmed the Lion's 
Club planned to move in on Thursday, giving some flexibility to accommodate the situation. 
Social Media — Pancake Breakfast Promotion 
Anastasia raised the point that the Lion's Club pancake breakfast should receive ongoing social media 
attention through Explore Fraser, noting that the organization did not appear to have an active social media 
presence. She offered to continue tagging them so that Sarah could easily reshare content. Sarah 
acknowledged she would make a point of sharing those posts going forward. 
Group Text & Communications 
It was agreed that a group text thread would be set up for last-minute festival communications. Anastasia 
volunteered to take care of organizing the thread, ensuring all relevant members were included, given Sarah's 
many responsibilities leading up to the event. One attendee noted they had not yet been included in prior 
group emails or texts and requested to be added. 
Electronic Sign Near Post Office 
One attendee provided an update on the electronic sign planned for installation outside the post office, noting 
that procedural and permitting requirements had caused delays. While there remained a chance the sign could 
be operational before Mural Fest, it was acknowledged to be a close call. If the sign was up in time, Mural 
Fest would be promoted on it. It was also noted that the MMBs (message boards) typically used to advertise 
the event would not be available this year, as they were needed for other town priorities including fire 
response and a road closure. 
Traffic & Pedestrian Safety 
The group briefly discussed pedestrian and traffic safety in the festival area. It was noted that the speed limit 
had been reduced to 30 mph and that lighted crosswalks had been installed, with one attendee observing that 
 
 
driver attentiveness had noticeably improved as a result. It was also noted that construction cones near the 
post office would serve as a natural psychological slow-down for drivers. The group expressed optimism that 
safety had improved overall, with no accidents reported in the past four to five years. 
Flags 
Sarah noted that she and Beth would be setting up the remaining festival flags following the meeting, having 
only completed approximately six of them before weather interrupted the effort the previous day.
