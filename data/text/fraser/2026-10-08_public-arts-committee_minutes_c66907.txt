4. Discussion 
  
1. Fire and Ice 2027 
The committee confirmed shuttle service arrangements with a transportation representative. Six 
shuttles will operate from the prior year's locations — the elementary school ball fields, rec 

Page 2 of 3 
center, and Valley Life Church — running from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM, with 9:00 PM as the hard 
cutoff. Valley Life Church will be contacted to confirm their continued participation in providing 
their own shuttle. The transportation provider also noted that taxi service is available on Friday 
and Saturday nights as an additional option for attendees. 
Regarding the event layout, the overall footprint and staging locations will remain largely the 
same as prior years. The primary change is that EMS will be repositioned to the parking lot 
behind the main building to allow for quicker egress. The fire department will stage their engine 
in that same lot, with the water tender positioned further into the festival grounds. 
The committee discussed expanding fire and lighted sculpture elements. Staff indicated they are 
in contact with Aaron, a returning fire artist, as well as a metal sculptor familiar to Aaron who 
creates large lighted installations. Staff is also speaking with Homestead Circus, which offers 
large sway poles with lighted performers. Pricing and safety logistics are pending. The fire 
department representative requested the opportunity to review all fire installations on Saturday 
prior to the event opening, and staff agreed to coordinate a walkthrough. 
The business burn-off (effigy) program was discussed. It was noted that businesses should be 
reminded not to use fossil or non-traditional materials in their constructions. The call for 
business entries will be sent out earlier this year to allow for more participation. A size constraint 
form will be reissued. Installations may be dropped off Friday with final arrangement Saturday 
morning. 
Food and beverage vendors confirmed or in discussion include Mountain Mary's (cookies), River 
Wolf (food truck), Idyllwild, Birdie Lounge, and the Rooted food truck. Birdie Lounge is also 
planning a glow-in-the-dark mini-putt attraction. Additional food truck outreach is ongoing, with 
preference given to Fraser-based businesses. 
Other items discussed included: the potential return of a sledding hill or dragon feature for kids; 
improved speaker sound near the bonfire area; coordination with KFFR radio; a possible 
temporary crosswalk with portable signals to manage pedestrian traffic on the main road 
(referencing a similar setup used for a prior Big Gigantic concert); and the addition of Clayton 
Court as a new parking area. The event timeline will be reviewed and potentially adjusted to 
tighten the gap between the bonfire lighting and fireworks. Fireworks are expected to again be 
coordinated with Ken, pending confirmation. 
The committee discussed inviting the high school choir and drum corps to perform, with a 
meeting with the school scheduled for the following month. Outreach to Shelly Peters at the 
elementary school regarding the costume contest and effigy participation was also noted. Chief 
Holsworth and Mayor Brian were identified as potential candidates to light the bonfire. 
2. Mural Winner Updates 
Steve Fitzgerald reported that two mural commissions were awarded following Mural Fest. 
Danielle Pareto was selected for the Black Tie Ski Rental/Ballet Arts sign mural. The second 
commission recipient ultimately relocated out of state without committing to a date, and the 
relationship was formally ended. Artist Yanil was asked to take on both commissions. He will be 
on-site the following week to finalize the designs. The ski building mural will feature a skier, 
while the Fraser Valley Arts sign base will depict a guitar player on one side and a dancing 
woman on the other, with Perry's in the background, rendered in the Fraser Valley Arts autumnal 
color palette. The sign is currently being used to promote Yanil's upcoming visit and is

generating notable community attention. Large boulders will be placed in front of the sign for 
protection following an incident in which a vehicle struck the wall. A camera has also been 
installed. 
 
3. Mural Fest 2027 
The committee discussed the possibility of adding wall murals as a component of Mural Fest 
2027. A form was distributed to gauge business interest in offering wall space, but responses 
have been limited. Staff will redesign the outreach post to be more visually direct and will 
include the call in upcoming business communications. The item will also be shared with the 
DDA. 
The committee discussed a hybrid model in which selected wall murals would be offered on an 
invitational basis to established artists, while the remaining festival spots would proceed through 
the standard call-for-entry process. Stipends for wall muralists would differ given that those 
works are not sold. Wall mural artists would not be eligible for prize money. The committee 
agreed to finalize the approach by the December meeting, as the general call for entry is planned 
to open January 1st. It was noted that the committee should have sufficient information by the 
November meeting to make a determination. 
It was also reported that artist Bobby completed murals on all four sides of the HTA shed, as 
well as the wall ride at the bike park, both of which were noted positively by committee 
members.
