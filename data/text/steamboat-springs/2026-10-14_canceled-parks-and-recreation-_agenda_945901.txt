C. PARKS AND RECREATION COMMISSION MISSION AND VISION
Mission Statement: The Parks & Recreation Commission serves as an 
advisory board to City Council to promote and enhance quality of life by 
offering a broad range of safe and accessible resources including 
parks, open space, trails, recreational services, and facilities which 
invite all to explore, play, learn and preserve.

2
Vision: Parks & Recreation Commission is committed to identify, 
advocate, and provide high quality and sustainable recreation, open 
spaces and cultural amenities for all citizens and visitors by balancing 
the need to maintain and preserve the resources entrusted to us.
