TITLE / SUBJECT:  Compensation Study 
 
DESCRIPTION 
Compensation Study 
 
SUMMARY 
The Town engaged Graves Consulting to complete a compensation study and provide 
recommendations regarding the Town’s pay structures and related employee pay 
adjustments. Graves Consulting is a Human Resources consulting firm specializing in 
public sector work, including compensation, employee retention, organizational culture, 
leadership development, and performance management. 
 
The compensation study supports the Town of Timnath's Strategic Plan goal to attract, 
retain, and develop a high performing, engaged, and well-equipped workforce. 
Competitive compensation is an essential tool in achieving that goal. Through this 
study, Graves Consulting evaluated the Town's compensation structure and labor 
market competitiveness and provided recommendations to ensure employee pay 
remains aligned with market conditions. The recommendations are intended to support 
the Town's ability to recruit and retain top talent, maintain internal equity, and provide 
opportunities for career progression and sustainable wage growth. 
 
 
KEY POINTS 
 The compensation study included a review and analysis of the Town’s current 
wage structure and classification system 
 The project reviewed the Town’s defined market to confirm whether it continues 
to represent the Town’s labor market. 
 Existing wage survey data was reviewed from relevant, statistically valid market 
studies.

Updated pay structures were recommended to meet the Town’s needs, including 
placement of each position within those structures. 
 Graves Consulting met with leadership to understand positions that had been 
added or changed throughout the year. 
 Town positions were matched to benchmark positions in the Town’s defined 
market using Colorado Municipal League and Pay Factors data, and the data 
was aged forward to reflect 2027 implementation. 
 The data reviewed represents actual wages being paid to individuals performing 
similar work, rather than only comparing pay ranges. 
 The data was reviewed to ensure alignment with the unique aspects of Town 
positions. 
Graves will present its findings and recommendations, and staff plan to incorporate 
recommendations in the budget.
