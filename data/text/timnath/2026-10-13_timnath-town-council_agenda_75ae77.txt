RESOLUTION NO. 61, SERIES 2026
A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR THE CONTINUED OPERATION OF
TIMNATH ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
WHEREAS, the Town Council of the Town of Timnath (“Town”), pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-15-103, has 
the power to pass resolutions; and
WHEREAS, Poudre School District has recommended closing Timnath Elementary School beginning 
with the 2027-2028 school year, and the recommendation remains under consideration by the Poudre 
School District Board of Education; and 
WHEREAS, Timnath Elementary School has served generations of Timnath students and families and 
remains an essential part of the Town's history, identity, and sense of community; and 
WHEREAS, the Timnath Elementary campus occupies a prominent location within Old Town Timnath 
and serves as an important community asset that contributes to the area's identity, vitality, and sense of 
place while providing a neighborhood setting for education and community connection; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Council recognizes and values the students, educators, staff, and families whose 
commitment has helped Timnath Elementary earn a reputation as one of Poudre School District's highest-
performing elementary school and notes that the district's review found Timnath Elementary has strong 
enrollment and high building utilization; and
WHEREAS, the Town Council is deeply concerned about the negative and permanent effects a closure 
will have on students and families, including increased travel time and distance, reduced student safety 
and well-being, and the forever lost opportunity for Timnath’s children to begin their education within 
their neighborhood community; and
WHEREAS, the closure would displace more than 400 elementary students, nearly forty percent of the 
Town’s school aged children; and
WHEREAS, Timnath continues to grow and welcome new families, including those with school-aged 
children, and developing neighborhoods served by Timnath Elementary School, making it important to 
consider both current conditions and the community’s long-term educational needs when evaluating 
future school capacity and facility decisions; and
WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that supporting the continued operation of Timnath Elementary is 
in the best interest of Timnath students, families, residents, and the community as a whole.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF 
TIMNATH, COLORADO AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Support for Timnath Elementary School
The Town Council hereby expresses its strong support for the continued operation of Timnath Elementary 
School and respectfully urges the Poudre School District Board of Education not to close the school. The 
Town Council invites the district to collaborate on solutions and encourages the district to explore and 
adopt viable long-term alternatives that both allow Timnath students to attend school in Timnath and 
address the district's enrollment, facility, and long-term planning needs.

Section 2. Communication and Community Participation
The Town Council encourages the community to remain informed and participate in Poudre School 
District’s public input process. The Town Clerk is directed to transmit a copy of this Resolution to the 
Poudre School District Board of Education and the Superintendent of Schools.
