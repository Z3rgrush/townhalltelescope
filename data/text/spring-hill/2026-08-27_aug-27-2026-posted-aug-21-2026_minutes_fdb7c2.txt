City of Spring Hill, Kansas 
Minutes of City Council Regular Session 
August 27, 2026 
 
Mayor Young called the Regular Session of the City Council to order at 7:00 p.m. on August 27, 2026, in 
the Spring Hill Civic Center, 401 N. Madison, Council Chambers, Spring Hill, Kansas. The meeting convened 
with Glenda Gerrity, City Clerk, recording. 
 
Councilmembers in attendance:  Phillip Thron, Council President 
     Kendra Crevier 
     Mike Grant 
     Ivan Delgado 
     James Savage 
 
Staff in attendance: Mike Mallon, Interim City Administrator/ Community Dev Director 
Jacob Speer, Interim Asst. City Administrator/Public Works Director 
Chief Sutterby, Police Department 
Rhonda Dunn, Finance Director 
Alysen Abel, City Engineer 
Monakisha Jones, Human Resources Director 
 
Consultants in attendance: Spencer Low, City Attorney 
 
INVOCATION 
The invocation was given by Pastor Jack Smith, Generation Bible Church. 
 
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited. 
 
ROLL CALL 
The City Clerk called the roll of the City Council. With a quorum present, the meeting commenced. 
 
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA 
The Mayor requested to add two (2) Executive Sessions. 
 
Motion by Thron, seconded by Delgado, to approve the agenda as amended. 
Motion passed 5-0-0. 
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS AND REPORTS 
Announcements and reports were given by the Governing Body and staff.

PUBLIC HEARING 
Exceed Revenue Neutral Rate and 2027 Budget Hearing 
The Mayor opened the public hearing for the Revenue Neutral Rate (RNR) and the 2027 Budget.  
 
Finance Director Dunn presented an overview of the mill levy history  and outlined the key financial 
components for the proposed 2027 Mill Levy, noting that the proposal exceeds the RNR.  
 
The Mayor opened the public comment portion of the hearing. The courtesy of the floor was extended to 
Rodolfo Arevalo, 19419 W. 200th Street, Spring Hill, Kansas 
 
The Mayor closed the public hearing. 
 
Ms. Dunn stated that there will be another opportunity for public comments at the next meeting on 
September 13, 2026. 
 
CITIZEN PARTICIPATION 
Bill Peterman, 19431 W. 200th Street, spoke about children’s cancer awareness, collecting donations for 
the Braden’s Hope Foundation, and donating blankets to Ronald McDonald House. 
 
Jason O’Kane, 18804 Skyview Lane , inquired about updates regarding a potential crosswalk across W 
191st Street at Foxwood Ranch Subdivision and the installation of a streetlight. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA 
Motion by Thron, seconded by Delgado, to approve the consent agenda. 
1. Consider Approval of Appropriation Order No. 2026-08-27 
2. Consider Approval of Resolution No. 2026 -R-16 providing for the Range of Salaries of Various 
City Officers and Employees of the City of Spring Hill, Kansas 
3. Consider Acceptance of Off -Site Sanitary Sewer Easement dedication associated with Brycliff 
Commercial development 
4. Consider Approval of a Task Order No. 2026-02 with Lamp Rynearson in relation to an 
emergency sewer line repair near 215th Street and Woodland Rd 
5. Consider a request to apply for the KDOT Innovative Technology Grant program to enhance the 
signal timing along K-7/Hwy 169 from 175th Street to 199th Street 
6. Consider a request to apply for the KDOT Cost Share Program for grant funding to construct the 
223rd Street & Webster/Harrison Roundabout improvements 
7. Consider a request to apply for the KDOT Access Management Funds for the installation of a 
new 183rd Street Traffic Signal Access Management Program – grant for the 183rd Street signal 
Motion passed 5-0-0.

DISCUSSION 
12. Council Projects and Priorities 
Interim City Administrator Mallon presented an overview of current and upcoming capital and 
infrastructure projects submitted by Department Directors and staff. The Governing Body discussed 
its priorities, including golf carts and side-by-side safety, a street maintenance program, department 
facility needs with emphasis on the Police Department, and upcoming sewer projects.
