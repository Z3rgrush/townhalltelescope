2. Public Hearing - Amendments to Planning Commission and Board of Zoning Appeals Provisions 
 
 Chair Morse opened the hearing to the public 
 
 No conflicts or ex-party contacts with commission members 
 
Mr. Low, City Attorney presented the Staff Report for the amendments to Planning Commission and   Board    of 
Zoning appeals Provisions. 
 
Commissioners provided the following comments and suggestions regarding the proposed language:  
 
Commissioners expressed concerns regarding the proposed attendance provision providing for resignation from 
the board or commission upon missing three consecutive meetings or five meetings within a calendar year. 
Commissioners questioned the use of an automatic removal or resignation provision and noted that the 
proposed language does not appear to be consistent with the terms and provisions applicable to other City of 
Spring Hill boards and commissions. 
 
Commissioners suggested that the Governing Body consider establishing an appeal process or other review 
procedure for attendance-related removals. Commissioners also suggested that the language provide 
consideration for whether absences are excused or unexcused before any removal action is taken. 
 
Commissioners further expressed concern regarding the proposed provision allowing a member to be removed 
“for any reason.”  
 
These comments were provided for consideration by the Governing Body and will be forwarded to the 
Governing Body as part of the Planning Commission's discussion of the proposed language. No action was taken 
by the Planning Commission on the proposed language. 
 
 
The Chairman opened the public comment portion of the hearing.  Thereupon, the courtesy of the floor was e   
extended to:  
 
Terra Combs, 15320 W. 191st St., addressed the Planning Commission regarding the proposed changes to the 
board membership provisions and her concern regarding the potential for the board to no longer specifically 
designate county representative. 
 
Jon Watterman, 21110 W. 186th St., spoke regarding concerns about the provisions on county representative 
and suggested that, if a county resident applies for one of the positions, a county resident should be given 
preference over a City resident when considering appointments to those positions.
