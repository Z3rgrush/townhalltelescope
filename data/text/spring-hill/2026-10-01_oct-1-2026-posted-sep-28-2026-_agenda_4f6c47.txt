Executive Summary  
Staff has prepared updating Planning Commission Bylaws, Rules and Regulations for 
consideration and adoption following amendments to the City’s regulations approved by 
the Governing Body at its September 24, 2026, meeting.  The amendments to the City’s 
regulations included changes affecting the organization, membership, quorum and 
attendance of the Planning Commission. 
 
Background 
The Spring Hill Planning Commission currently operates under Bylaws, Rules and 
Regulations adopted in 2024.  Since adoption of the current bylaws, the City's governing 
provisions relating to the Planning Commission have been updated. The City's code 
now provides for remote attendance by phone or video conference where necessary, 
establishes provisions relating to membership and nonresident members, and 
addresses officer appointments, special meetings, and attendance requirements.  
Staff have prepared revised bylaws to better align the Commission's rules and 
procedures with the City's current code. 
Proposed Changes 
The proposed bylaws update the Planning Commission’s procedures to align with the 
amended City regulations, including: 
1. Membership and officer responsibilities; 
2. Five-member quorum and remote attendance; 
3. Special meetings and attendance requirements.

SPRING HILL PLANNING 
COMMISSION BYLAWS, RULES AND 
REGULATIONS 
 
The following rules and regulations governing the procedures of the Planning Commission of Spring Hill, 
Kansas, are adopted in accordance with the planning laws of the State of Kansas.  
 
ARTICLE I- OFFICERS 
1. The Planning Commission shall organize annually at the first regular meeting in March. 
2. The Commission shall select a Chairperson and a Vice -Chairperson from among the appointed 
members at the annual organization meeting. The officers shall serve for one year. 
3. A Secretary shall be selected for an indefinite term and it is not required that person be a member 
of the Commission. 
4. The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings and public hearings of the Planning Commission; 
shall decide all points of order and procedure; shall certify subdivision plats.  
5. The Vice-Chairperson shall assume the duties of the Chairperson in  their absence. 
6. The Secretary shall be responsible for keeping the minutes of the Planning Commission; sending 
agendas to members of the Planning Commission; carrying out written correspondence; 
transmitting and maintaining the records of the Commission; and performing su ch other duties 
as the Planning Commission may require understanding that if the secretary is on the city staff, 
that such other duties are subject to appropriate City personnel policy and procedure as 
administered by the City  Administrator.  
ARTICLE II - MEETINGS 
1. The Planning Commission shall meet on the first Thursday of each month at 7:00 P.M. This date, 
time and/or place may be changed by the Chairperson due to conditions beyond control of the 
Planning Commission, i.e., holidays, scheduling conflicts, etc. (afte r consultation with the staff). 
In the absence of substantive agenda items, the Chairperson may cancel  the meeting. 
2. Special meetings of the Planning Commission may be called by the Chairperson, or Vice-Chairperson 
in Chair’s absence upon request. 
Page 3  
3. Five (5) members of the  Commission shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.  
A quorum shall be required for the Planning Commission to take any formal or official action.   If 
a quorum is not present at a regular or special meeting, those present may either adjourn the 
meeting or continue discussion of matters as are on the agenda. No action at such a meeting  
shall be taken until a quorum is present. 
4. Motions shall be restated by the Chairperson before a vote is taken. The name of the maker and 
supporter of a motion shall be recorded. 
5. An affirmative vote of a majority of a required quorum by roll call vote shall be necessary  to 
authorize any official action of the Commission on rezoning, preliminary plats, or conditional use 
permits unless otherwise specified by statute or as requested by the Chairperson. (Some official 
actions require the affirmative vote of a majority of all th e members on the total Planning 
Commission.) Where such a vote is not possible either for or against a particular proposal, the 
results of such action shall be submitted to the governing body with an explanation of the failure 
to establish an official vote on the subject in question. All members, shall have a vote and  shall 
vote "yea", "nay" or "abstain" when present; except that any member shall automatically 
disqualify himself from voting on any decision in which there might be a conflict of interest and 
should state the nature of that conflict for the  minutes. 
6. When procedural and parliamentary rules adopted by the Planning Commission do not conflict, 
"Robert's Rules of Order" shall be used as a  guide. 
ARTICLE Ill -AGENDA SUBJECTS 
1. Upon receipt of a complete written application and appropriate city staff review, the item will be 
placed on the agenda for the next scheduled Planning Commission  meeting. 
2. Where the volume of the Planning Commission subjects may require such action, the Chairperson 
of the Commission may postpone discussion of certain subjects until sufficient time is available for 
the members to give proper review to such subjects. 
3. Agendas shall ordinarily be emailed to the members of the Planning Commission so that they 
may review them prior to the Planning  Commission meeting.  
4. Subjects not requiring a roll call vote and are not listed on a Planning Commission agenda, may be 
considered for discussion. 
 
ARTICLE IV - HEARINGS 
1. Public Hearings for rezoning, preliminary plats, and conditional use permit, shall be conducted 
in conformance with state  statutes. 
Page 3  
 
ARTICLE V - COMMITTEES 
1. The Planning Commission may establish such committees, as it deems advisable and assign 
each committee specific duties or functions. 
2. The Chairperson shall designate the members of each committee and shall name the 
Chairperson of each committee. The Planning Commission Chairperson shall fill vacancies on 
committees as they are created. 
ARTICLE VI - RECORDS AND REPORTS 
1. The Commission shall keep a record of its resolutions, transactions, findings, and determinations. 
2. The Commission shall annually review the comprehensive plan to determine if any portion has 
become obsolete and shall make a report to the governing body regarding same on or before 
the first day of June of each year. 
ARTICLE VII - AMENDMENTS 
1. These rules of procedure may be amended by an affirmative vote by a majority of the planning 
Commission members provided such proposed amendment has been submitted in writing with 
a proposed copy of the amendment provided to each member of the Commission; and placed 
on the agenda. 
ARTICLE VIII -ATTENDANCE OF MEMBERS 
1. Members of the Planning Commission are appointed by the City Council and can be removed 
pursuant to Section 1-909.I of the Spring Hil Municipal Code.  
2. Members of the Planning Commission may participate in meetings remotely by telephone or video 
conference where necessary, and voting, to the extent permitted by applicable law.  
3. The Planning Commission shall consist of nine (9) members, except that the Commission may 
contain more than nine (9) members if necessary to conform to the provisions of K.S.A. 12 -744 and 
amendments thereto.  Up to two (2) members of the Planning Commission may be nonresidents of the 
City, provided that each nonresident member: 
 
a. Resides within three (3) miles of the corporate limits of the City; and 
b. Is not a resident of any other city or municipality. 
 
All members shall be appointed in accordance with the Unified Zoning Ordinance and applicable law.

Bucyrus Automotive Site Plan Review 
 
Engineering Department 
The proposed Bucyrus Automotive is located in the northwest corner of 191st Street and 
Madison Street, north of the former Meadowlark in the Country Meadows Industrial Park. 
The stormwater will be collected on-site with the proposed curb & gutter, directed to the 
curb inlets located within the parking lot.  The proposed private storm sewer will connect 
into the existing public storm system located along Madison Street.  If there are any 
modifications to the existing public storm sewer pipes or inlets, the applicant will need to 
submit a separate Public Improvement Permit, and associated engineering drawings.  The 
review of the Public Improvements Plans can occur concurrent with the Site Plan Review 
prior to permit.  The Public Improvements will need to be installed and accepted by the City 
prior to issuance of temporary certificate of occupancy. 
Ultimately, the stormwater will discharge into the existing regional detention basin located 
in the northwest corner of the Country Meadows development.  The detention basin was 
previously designed to provide stormwater detention to the development, including the 
subject development. 
The proposed Bucyrus Automotive Site Plan includes the installation of an Oil/Sand 
Separator on the north side of the building.  The privately maintained system will be tied 
into the private sanitary sewer service line and will connect into the existing public sanitary 
sewer main located along the east side of the property.  Staff will work directly with the 
developer’s engineer on the final design of the private sanitary sewer service and collection 
system.  If there are any modifications to the existing public sanitary sewer pipes or 
manholes, the applicant will need to submit a separate Public Improvement Permit, and 
associated engineering drawings.  The review of the Public Improvements Plans can occur 
concurrent with the Site Plan Review prior to permit.  The Public Improvements will need to 
be installed and accepted by the City prior to issuance of temporary certificate of 
occupancy. 
There are two retaining walls proposed with this development.  A short wall in the 
northwest corner of the lot and a longer wall along the southern portion of the lot.  
Structural drawings and calculations are necessary for any wall that exceeds 4-feet in 
height.  That information will need to be submitted with the construction plans, prior to 
issuance of a permit. 
 
The Engineering staff has reviewed the overall site plan.  Minor plan review comments have 
been provided to the application in a separate memo.  In addition to the completion of the 
plan review comments, the administrative items outlined in the stipulations will need to be 
submitted prior to issuance of a permit. 
The Engineering Department recommends approval of this application with the following 
stipulations: 
1. Address the plan review comments previously provided by the Engineering 
Department.  Engineering will review the plans for approval prior to issuance of a 
construction permit. 
2. Concurrent with the site plan submittal, submit a separate Public Improvement 
Permit and associated engineering drawings.  The public improvements must be 
installed and accepted by the City prior to the issuance of temporary certificate of 
occupancy. 
3. Concurrent with the plan submittal, provide additional details associated with the 
Oil/Sand Separator, including details, calculations, and manufacturer’s submittals. 
4. Prior to issuance of a permit, submit a copy of the Industrial Stormwater Pollution 
Prevention Plan outlining the long-term operations and maintenance of the 
industrial site. 
5. Prior to issuance of a permit, provide a copy of the signed permit from KDHE 
approving the Industrial Stormwater Permit. 
6. Prior to issuance of a permit, submit a copy of the Stormwater Pollution Prevention 
Plan outlining the erosion and sediment control requirement during the

7. Prior to issuance of a permit, provide a copy of the signed permit from KDHE for the

Background 
In March 2026, the applicant for the Ozark Ready-Mix Facility submitted and received approval 
for a Preliminary Plat, a Final Plat, and a Site Plan with Conditional Use Permit application.  
In July 2026, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. 2026-17. This ordinance repealed the “M-
1 – General Industrial District” and established the “M-1 – Light Industrial” and “M-2 – Heavy 
Industrial” districts. All properties zoned “M-1 – General Industrial” were automatically 
redesignated as “M-1 – Light Industrial” following the adoption of this ordinance.  
This ordinance also established additional “Planned District” designations as well as the ability 
to seek the addition of an otherwise prohibited land use via a Planned District with a “PO – 
Protective Overlay”.  
Lastly, this ordinance redesignated “Basic Industry” as prohibited in “M-1” and permitted as a 
conditional use in “M-2”. Concrete batch plant facilities are considered “Basic Industry” in the 
context of the adopted Unified Zoning Ordinance.  
In August 2026, the applicant for Ozark Ready-Mix submitted a revised Site Plan application. 
Analysis/Conclusion 
As a new application, the revised site plan must meet all adopted development regulations.  
Due to these new regulations, the following actions are required for a concrete batch plant 
facility to operate in what is otherwise the “M-1 – Light Industrial” district: 
1. Rezone from M-1 to MP-1 – Planned Light Industrial with a PO – Protective Overlay to 
permit the operation of Basic Industry 
2. Seek approval of a Preliminary Plan (required of all PD rezoning applications) 
3. Seek approval of a Conditional Use Permit to operate Basic Industry 
4. Seek approval of a revised Site Plan application
