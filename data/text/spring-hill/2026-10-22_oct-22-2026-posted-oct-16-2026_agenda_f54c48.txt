Formal Item Description: Consider Approval of Ordinance No. 2026-XX: Rezoning Application 
with Preliminary Plat, Z-26-4/PP-26-3, from M-1 to MP-1 (P-O), at 18430 Webster Street.  
 
Background: The applicant, Neil Jackson of Ozark Ready -Mix Company, Inc., on behalf of the 
property owner, JPKS Enterprises, LLC, has submitted a request for rezoning of the property at 
18430 Webster Street from M -1 – Light Industrial to MP -1 (P-O) – Planned Light Industrial w/ 
Protective Overlay. Per state law, a 14-day period for protest petitions to be submitted was given. 
No protest petitions were submitted. 
 
The proposed rezoning is to facilitate the development of the site with a concrete batch plant 
facility. Following the adoption of the M -1/M-2 zoning code amendment in July 2026, ‘Basic 
Industry’ including concrete batch plants were rendered prohibited in the M -1 – Light Industrial 
zoning district. Due to an intent to modify the previously approved Site Plan application to a degree 
that requires a new Site Plan application, the zoning entitlements for this property must conform 
with the adopted Unified Zoning Ordinance. This rezoning will selectively permit the operation of 
a Concrete/Cement Batch Plan in addition to those land uses otherwise permitted in the M -1 – 
Light Industrial District, with the intent of facilitating the new Site Plan application under SP-26-
4.  
 
The surrounding properties and neighborhood are generally compatible with the proposed zoning 
designation, as adjacent or nearby parcels are zoned M-1 – Light Industrial and are developed with 
a mix of what would typically be considered ‘Heavy Industrial’ land uses, including a Cement 
Batch Plan to the northeast as well as an additional concrete batch plant two parcels to the south. 
The remaining surrounding properties are a mix of light industrial and medium commercial, 
including self-storage facilities and general manufacturing.  
 
The application was  unanimously recommended for approval by the Planning Commission 
following a public hearing on October 1, 2026.  
 
Staff Recommendation : With any conditions of approval detailed in the staff report, staff 
recommend approval of the application.

Compatibility 
The proposed zoning designation and the uses permitted within it are substantively similar to

with a mix of heavy industry and medium intensity commercial, and several of these properties 
are developed with the same type of end user – cement batch plants.  
Adverse Impacts 
The uses permitted by the proposed zoning district are expected to have a level of detrimental 
off-site impacts including noise, dust, and light pollution. While some of these are to be 
expected and cannot be eliminated, such as noise and dust, adjacent properties are developed 
in a pattern that is less sensitive to these forms of nuisances.  
With respect to off-site lighting impacts, site design will be structured in a manner that ensures 
that off-site glare and lighting is minimized or eliminated. Attention will be paid towards cut-off 
shielding and mounting heights.

a platted area of restricted access.
