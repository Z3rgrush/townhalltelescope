Spring Hill Cemetery Board Meeting 
7:00 p.m. Tuesday, September 1, 2026 
Civic Center, Spring Hill, Kansas 
 
Members present: Polly Mahoney, Susan Crawford, Keith Stiles, Joy Rose, Larry Sumner, 
Ashley Thron, Christine Hecke 
President Larry Sumner called the meeting to order. 
I. Approval of Minutes 
Christine Hecke read the minutes of the August meeting. Ashley Thron made the 
motion to accept the minutes and Polly Mahoney second. Passed 6-0 
 
II. Treasurer Report 
Joy Rose gave the treasurer’s report. Polly Mahoney made the motion to accept 
the report. Susan Crawford second. Passed 6-0 
 
III. Old Business 
Ashley Thron discussed the gravel edging with the city administrator and public 
works. Public works will contact Larry Sumner. 
 
IV. New Business 
Keith Stiles is working to eliminate the tree stumps and sprouts in the south 
fence. 
 
Ashley Thron will pursue a living history program at the cemetery. Polly Mahoney 
made the motion to proceed with the living history project and Keith Stiles 
second. Passed 6-0 
 
V.  Adjourn Meeting 
Polly Mahoney made the motion to adjourn the meeting and Ashley Thron 
second. Meeting ended at 7:18 p.m.
