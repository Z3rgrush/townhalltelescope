PUBLIC HEARING
MS4 Stormwater Management Plan
The Mayor opened the public hearing for public comments.
Public Works Director Jacob Speer reported that the Kansas Department of Health and Environment
(KDHE) issued a Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) Permit in August 2025. This is in
accordance with the Federal Water Pollution Control Act and corresponding Kansas statutes. The permit
requires the City to create, implement, and enforce a Stormwater Management Program addressing the
following six (6) areas; 1) Public Outreach and Education, 2) Public lnvolvement and Participation, 3) lllicit
Discharge Detection and Elimination, 4) Construction Site Runoff Control, 5) Post-Construction
Management, and 6) Pollution Prevention/Good Housekeeping. The program must utilize Best
Management Practices in each of the six categoriesto achieve the required points in each calendaryear
beginning in2027. The next step is to submit the Stormwater Management Program to KDHE for approval.
The City will be responsible for reporting annually to KDHE to confirm the effectiveness of the program
and permit compliance.
Public Comments:
Jason O'Kane, 18804 Skyview Lane, spoke about erosion control, construction sites, and household

18. Fireworks
Police Chief Sutterby reviewed the events and calls received during the allowable times for setting off
fireworks. After discussion, the Governing Body reached a consensus to be stricter on enforcement
outside the legal timeframes. This will require overtime in the Police Department due to the need for
additional officers on duty. Depending on the situation, if an individual is acting unlawfully, officers
may confiscate their fireworks. The Governing Body also requested a review of the locations a nd times
when fireworks may be legally discharged and provide a large sign for the vendors stands that list this
information.
19.2027 Budget
The Governing Body discussed the overall goal of maintaining reserves between 30 - 50 percent. The
City's target is to keep at least 50 percent of the total expense budget in reserves. The majority of the
Governing Body expressed support for maintaining the reserve level at 50 percent. The Finance
Director suggested presenting a plan for building the reserve and clearly defining the purpose of the
fund.
