4. Public Hearing – Rezoning Application with Preliminary Plat (Z-26-2/PP-26-1) – R-R 
to RP-1 
 
Chair Morse opened the hearing to the public 
 
No conflicts or ex-party contacts with commission members 
 
Mr. Trefren presented the Staff Report for the rezoning application  equest from R-R (Rural Residential 
District) to RP-1 (Planned Single-Family District) with Preliminary Plat.  Mr. Trefren corrected a statement 
made in the Staff Report that the Applicant requested no modifications and clarified the Applicant did 
request one modification to allow accessory structures up to 1,200 square feet.  
 
Tim Tucker, with Phelps Engineering spoke on behalf of the Owners and provided additional information 
regarding the proposed rezoning and preliminary plat. 
 
The property owner addressed the Planning Commission and answered questions regarding the proposed 
development 
 
The Chairman opened the public comment portion of the hearing.  Thereupon, the courtesy of the floor 
was extended to:  
 
Steven Sanford, 19795 W. 183rd St., expressed concerns regarding existing uses in the surrounding area and 
the potential impact on future residents. 
Jon Watterman, 21110 W. 186th St., expressed concerns regarding transportation and access associated 
with the proposed development. 
Leah Langley, 18233 W. Lone Elm Road, shared concerns regarding potential environmental and site-
related conditions 
 
The Chair requested that staff address questions and concerns raised during the public hearing, including topics 
related to transportation and infrastructure. 
 
With no other comments or discussion, the Chair closed the hearing. 
 
The Commissioners deliberated amongst themselves. 
 
Motion by Ms. Squire seconded by Mr. Erhart to recommend approval to City Council as presented by staff 
report with accessory buildings included up to 1,200 square feet. 
  Voice Vote:  Squire– Aye, Burgmeier – Aye,  Erhart – Aye, Morse – Aye, Arevalo – Aye 
  Motion carried 5-0-0
