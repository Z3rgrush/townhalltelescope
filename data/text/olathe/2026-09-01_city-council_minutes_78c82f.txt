2. Report regarding proposed amendments to Title 6 of the Olathe 
Municipal Code adopting the 2025 Johnson County 
Environmental Sanitary and Aquatic Health Codes.
Mayor Bacon said he understood that these changes were 
necessary for the County. 
Bob Gallimore, Assistant City Attorney, said the City has 
adopted this code but the County updated it in 2025 and we 
need to adopt the updated version. 
Mayor Bacon asked what the impact would be on residents. 
Mr. Gallimore said the sanitary portion is mainly unchanged but 
most of the changes are reflected in the aquatic portion of the 
code. He said it would give the City more ability to enforce 
code related to public pools.
B. DISCUSSION ITEMS
1. Discussion on the Santa Fe, Ridgeview to Mur-Len, 
Improvements Project, PN 3-C-025-18.
Theresa Vink, Assistant City Engineer, provided a presentation 
to the Council regarding this project. 
Councilmember Vakas said he thought it looks great. He 
asked about bike traffic and Ms. Vink said bike traffic would be 
accommodated. He also asked if the landscaping would be 
irrigated and Ms. Vink said there would  be part that will be and 
other areas that will not. Mr. Vakas asked if enough water was 
being planned for and Ms. Vink assured him it was. He also 
Page 4 of 7

asked why this was not being called the “gateway to Olathe 
and Ms. Vink explained that 119th and 159th streets are the 
edges of Olathe.
Councilmember Schoonover expressed some concern for the 
pedestrian walkways and wondered if it was safe to encourage 
people to walk across the bridge. Ms. Vink said currently there 
are pedestrian walkways and they are not increasing those 
walkways but  providing a safe method that is more 
aesthetically pleasing than today. Ms. Vink said the brick will 
continue to Ridgeview. She also said some left turns will be 
eliminated because of safety, placing access points as far 
away as possible from the interchange.
Councilmember Deneault asked about the life span of this 
bridge and Ms. Vink said about 50 years. Mr. Deneault 
expressed his desire to accommodate additional pedestrian 
traffic. He also asked about the width of the sidewalks and Ms. 
Vink provided additional information. She also said the 
sidewalks were placed as far away as possible from the 
driving lanes.  Nate Baldwin, City Engineer, also said there are 
no plans to increase the speed limit. 
Councilmember Creighton said he liked the design and hoped 
they could mitigate unsightliness during construction. 
Councilmember Felter asked if the metal components were 
different than the ones that are chipping at other locations. Mr. 
Baldwin said they are powder coated, not painted. Dr. Felter 
also suggested the ones that are chipping should be repaired. 
She asked about changes at 119th and said he had been 
unaware of those plans. Ms. Vink said KDOT is trying to 
address the turning movement and that they were expecting a 
public meeting regarding that soon.  
Councilmember Schoonover asked about traffic studies and 
Ms. Vink provide some additional information.
