1. Consider authorizing the City Administrator to execute Change Order No. 1 related to the Unit #1 
Turbine-Generator Overhaul to address required generator rewind work, rotor repairs, 
turbine/compressor deficiencies, and the omitted 9 th Stage Hook Fit Modification

Utility Director Hunter said the increased costs are due to unexpected repairs during teardown. The contractor 
uncovered several critical issues in both the turbine and the generator systems that must be completed to return 
unit number one to service. The last major turbine repair was more than 20 years ago. When turbines and 
generators sit for long periods of time, deterioration accelerates. The problems are only visible when the unit is 
open, and compressor blades are fully open. This must be repaired for reliable operation. We have extreme 
temperatures and this unit helps to keep our city away from the volatile market. Unit 2 has already been overhauled. 
Theoretically we should be good for another twenty years after the completion of this overhaul. 
Councilmember McNeer made a motion to move to recommend that the City Administrator be authorized to execute 
Change Order No. 1 for the Unit #1 Turbine Generator Overhaul, including the generator stator rewind, rotor 
refurbishment, turbine and compressor corrective repairs, and the required 9th Stage Hook Fit Modification, in the 
total amount of $2,751,942, and to incorporate the associated project costs into the final bonding package upon 
completion of the overhaul. 
Councilmember Wiehn Seconded.   
With all of the Councilmembers voting in favor of the 
motion, the motion carried. 
2. Consider adopting a resolution authorizing the offer for public sale of certain general obligations of the 
City of Gardner, Kansas 
 
Finance Director Wolff said the city is scheduled to sell approximately $10.225M principal amount of taxable general 
obligation bonds series 2026D and approximately $46.25M of tax-exempt general obligation bonds series 2026E 
bonds on November 2 nd, at 10 am. Series 2026D is to reimburse or pay the cost of certain electrical system 
improvements including substation 4 and represents the first series of bonds planed for these improvements. Series 
2026E is for the reimbursement or to pay the costs of two different projects. Hillsdale Lake water intake structure 
improvements is approximately $22.305M and the remaining is associated with the Kill Creek Water Resource 
Recovery Facility expansion.   
Since the last debt sale in August, estimated interest rates have increased significantly. Discussions with our bond 
council and our municipal advisor determined there’s no clear indication that delaying the sale would result in lower 
borrowing costs.  
Councilmember Deaton made a motion to adopt Resolution No. 2206, a resolution authorizing the offer for public 
sale of certain general obligation bonds of the City of Gardner, Kansas. 
Councilmember Baldwin Seconded.   
With a majority of the Councilmembers voting in favor 
of the motion, the Resolution passed and was 
assigned number 2206.
