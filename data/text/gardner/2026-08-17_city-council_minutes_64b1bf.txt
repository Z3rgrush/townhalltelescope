August 17, 2026 
  
The City Council of the City of Gardner, Kansas met in regular session on August 17, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. in the 
Council Chambers at Gardner City Hall, 120 East Main Street, Gardner, Kansas, with Mayor Todd Winters presiding. 
Present were Councilmembers Mark Baldwin, Kacy Deaton, Mark Wiehn, Steve McNeer, and Kelly Johnson. City 
staff present were City Administrator Jim Pruetting; Deputy City Administrator Amy Nasta; Finance Director Matt 
Wolff; Police Chief Kelly Herron; Parks Director Jason Bruce; Community Development Director David Knopick; 
Public Works Director Kellen Headlee; Utility Director David Hunter; City Attorney Ryan Denk; Clerk Renee Rich. 
Others present included those listed on the sign-in sheet and others who did not sign in.  
There being a quorum of Councilmembers present, Mayor Winters called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 
Mayor Winters led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance. 
PRESENTATIONS 
PUBLIC HEARING 
PUBLIC COMMENT 
Eric Arnold came to continue the discussion on Flock cameras. He had an opportunity to meet with the Chief and 
Deputy Chief. He said the chief published a new policy regarding this type of technology and has restricted access 
to the system. He said the contract does allow the city to end the contract and would like the contract to be brought 
to a future agenda.  
 
Carrie Schmidt is concerned about what Flock and Axon cameras can do. Feels that with Home Rule authority, the 
city can tell the state and county to get their cameras out of Gardner. 
 
Bradley Steinmetz also discusses Flock cameras. Supports law enforcement but does not support the cameras that 
are tracking movements of people. Discusses cases across the US of officers misusing Flock camera information. 
Concerned about what outside agencies have access to the data from these cameras.

COUNCIL UPDATES 
Chief Herron thanks Mr. Arnold for meeting with the PD. PD published a new policy for ALPRs to include audits and 
retention periods. An audit was just completed. Flock has now been shut down temporarily with access only being 
provided for the Chief and a Captain. Asking to remove Flock from our ecosystem. Pruetting confirms that we do not 
have to finish out the contract and can cancel at any time. Axon cameras are only on the cars and the information is 
only in the city’s system and not shared. History was only saved for 30 days and after the retention period the 
information is gone. Flock does not have a very big footprint in this region. The data is governed by NCIC but is held 
in the cloud through Flock. Consensus by the governing body to not renew the contract and then the company will 
retrieve their equipment. Introduced Deputy Chief Pruitt.  
 
Administrator Pruetting thanks the Velocity team for being here.  
 
Councilmember Johnson thanks those who spoke and the Velocity team. Welcome to the new Deputy Chief and 
new officers we have gotten in the last few weeks. been hired to join our community. 
 
Councilmember McNeer thanks Chief for the exceptional leadership displayed in the short time you have been here. 
Thank you to Velocity team that met with us this week and welcome to our community. 
 
Council President Baldwin appreciates Chief for looking into Flock, making a quick decision and having all of the 
information ready for tonight. Welcomes Velocity to the city and looks forward to a long term partnership. 
 
Councilmember Wiehn thanks Chief for looking into Flock and making the decision to shut them down as well as the 
ability to make a decision about them tonight. Excited about the opportunity Velocity brings to the city and the area 
of town they are moving into including some walkability. Something to look into is the walkability in that area around 
Walmart and McDonalds. Also, school has started, watch out for all those kids heading to school. 
 
Council Vice-President Deaton welcomes Deputy Chief Pruitt. Thanks Chief for all the work on the Flock cameras. 
Would like to look at those cameras in our city limits that we do not own. Thanks to the whole Velocity team. Be 
vigilant for those kids walking and riding scooters and let’s have a great school year.
