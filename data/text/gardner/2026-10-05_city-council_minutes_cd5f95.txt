PROCLAMATION 
 
Domestic Violence Awareness Month – October 2026 
 
“With Survivors, Always” 
 
WHEREAS, domestic violence occurs in communities across our nation, including 
here in  the City of  Gardner, and affects people of every age, background, and 
circumstance. Its impact reaches beyond individual victims, affecting children, 
families, friends, workplaces, and the community as a whole; and 
 
WHEREAS, every person in the City of Gardner deserves to feel safe in their own 
home and to live free from violence, fear, intimidation, coercion, and control; and 
 
WHEREAS, October is recognized nationally as Domestic Violence Awareness 
Month, a time to honor survivors, remember those whose lives have been taken by 
domestic violence, raise awareness, and strengthen our collective commitment to 
preventing abuse; and 
 
WHEREAS, the 2026 Domestic Violence Awareness Month theme, “With 
Survivors, Always,” calls upon communities to move beyond awareness and toward 
action; to stand beside survivors not only in October, but every day, through safety, 
support, solidarity, and meaningful action; and 
 
WHEREAS, we recognize the survivors in the Gardner community who have found 
safety, those who are rebuilding their lives, and those who may still be living in fear 
or wondering whether help is available. To each of them, our message is clear: you 
are seen, you matter, and our community stands with you; and 
 
WHEREAS, we remember and honor those whose lives have been lost to domestic 
violence and recognize the families, friends, and loved ones who continue to carry 
their memory; and

WHEREAS, the City of Gardner recognizes the dedication of law enforcement 
officers, victim advocates, prosecutors, healthcare professionals, social service 
providers, educators, community organizations, and citizens who work to protect 
victims, support survivors, hold offenders accountable, and prevent future violence; 
and 
 
WHEREAS, ending domestic violence requires more than awareness; it requires a 
community willing to recognize abuse, speak against it, support those experiencing 
it, and ensure survivors know they do not have to face violence alone; 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, the Mayor and City Council of the City of Gardner, Kansas, 
do hereby proclaim October 2026 as Domestic Violence Awareness Month and 
stand with every survivor who found the courage to leave, every survivor still 
searching for a way out, every child and family affected by abuse, and every person 
who has yet to realize that a life without violence is possible. 
 
BE IT FURTHER PROCLAIMED, that the City of Gardner commits to standing 
“With Survivors, Always” —with those who have survived, those reclaiming their 
lives, those still living in fear, and those waiting for someone to remind them that 
they are not alone. 
 
Awareness matters. Action matters. Every survivor deserves safety, support, dignity, 
and hope. Love should not hurt. 
 
Dated this 5th day of October 2026.

Agenda Item: Consider authorizing the execution of a contract with USD 231 for three (3)

Strategic Priority: Quality of Life 
 
Department:   Police Department  
 
 
Staff Recommendation:  
Staff recommends authorizing the City Administrator to execute a contract with USD 231 for three 
(3) School Resource Officers (SROs) for the 2026 – 2027 school year. 
 
Background/Description of Item:  
The Police Department has been prov iding SRO services since 1999. USD 231 and city staff 
have mutually agreed to enter into a contract for SRO services.  
 
USD 231 school board approved this contract at the Sept 14th, 2026 school board meeting. The 
term for the contract is for the school year which officially started on July 1st, 2026, and expires 
June 30, 2027.  
 
The contract is for three (3) Police Officers to serve as School Resource Officers (SRO). 
 
Financial Impact:  
USD 231 will pay $34.42 for regular hours and $51.63 for overtime hours worked for SRO duties. 
These rates are based on the average salaries of the officers currently assigned.  When the 
officers are not working on school -related activities, they will be paid by the city at their normal 
rates of pay.

Staff Recommendation: 
Staff recommends authorizing the Mayor to execute an agreement between KDOT and the City 
of Gardner for supplemental grant funding for construction costs for CIP project AP3012, Replace 
Fuel Facility on Gardner Municipal Airport and approve the expenditure of City Airport Funds as 
detailed in the agreement. 
 
Background/Description of Item:  
In October 2023, the City of Gardner applied for grant funds from KDOT’s Kansas Airport 
Improvement Program (KAIP) for CIP project AP3012, Replace Fuel Facility on Gardner Municipal 
Airport. The project scope includes construction and construction services fees associated with 
replacing the fuel dispensers, credit card reader, and constructing an aboveground fuel tank at 
K34. Replacing the fuel dispensers, credit card reader, and constructing an above- ground fuel 
tank would bring the airport’s fuel system up-to-date. This new system would remain as self-serve, 
allowing tenants and itinerant users to safely fuel aircraft. The new 12,000 gallon above -ground 
tank also would comply with KDHE regulations concerning fuel storage. 
 
KDOT had previously awarded funding to construct the proposed improvements up to a total 
construction cost of $850,000.00 and the corresponding agreement was authorized at the July 
15, 2024 City Council meeting.  The project opened construction bids on August 13th, 2026 with 
a low bid of $965,500.00 for total construction cost.  Due to the higher-than-expected bids and 
subsequent budget shortfall, KDOT has agreed to a supplemental funding agreement that 
provides additional funding for a total of $799,850.00 in total project grant funding. The airport is 
responsible for the remaining cost of the project (estimated at $165,650.00). 
 
Financial Impact:

City of Gardner Airport Fund – remaining project cost

Policy Regarding Sexual Harassment 
WHEREAS, sexual harassment and retaliation for sexual harassment claims are unacceptable forms of discrimination that must not be 
tolerated in the workplace; and 
WHEREAS, state and federal employment discrimination laws prohibit sexual harassment and retaliation in the workplace; and 
WHEREAS, officers and employees of the State of Kansas are entitled to working conditions that are free from sexual harassment, 
discrimination, and retaliation; and 
WHEREAS, the Governor and all officers and employees of the State o f Kansas should seek  to foster a culture that does not tolerate 
sexual harassment, retaliation, and unlawful discrimination. 
NOW THEREFORE, pursuant to the authority vested in me as Governor of the State of Kansas, I hereby order as follows:  
1. All Executive Branch department and agency heads shall have available, and shall regularly review and update at least every
three years or more frequently as necessary, their sexual harassment, discrimination, and retaliation policies.  Such policie s
shall include components for confidentiality and anonymous reporting, applicability to intern positions, and training policies.
2. All Executive Branch department and agency heads shall ensure that their employees, interns , and contractors have been

PREAMBLE 
 
The Secretary of Transportation for the State of Kansas, in accordance with the provisions of Title VI of the Civil Rights Act 
of 1964 (78 Stat. 252, 42 U.S.C. § 2000d to 2000d-4) and other nondiscrimination requirements and the Regulations, hereby 
notifies all contracting parties that it will affirmatively ensure that this contract will be implemented without discrimination 
on the grounds of race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability, income-level or Limited English Proficiency (LEP). 
CLARIFICATION 
The term “Contractor” is understood to include the Contractor , the Contractor’s assignees and successors in interest, 
consultants, and all other parties to contracts or agreements with the Secretary of Transportation, Kansas Department of 
Transportation. This Attachment shall govern should this Attachment conflict with provisions of the Document to which it is 
attached. 
 
ASSURANCE APPENDIX A 
 
During the performance of this contract, the Contractor, for itself, its assignees and successors in interest, agrees as follows: 
 
1. Compliance with Regulations: The Contractor will  comply with the Acts and the R egulations relative to 
nondiscrimination in its Federally-assisted programs of the U.S. Department of Transportation, the Federal 
Highway Administration (FHWA) , the Federal Transit Administration ( FTA) or the Federal Aviation 
Administration (FAA) as they may be amended from time to time which are herein incorporated by reference 
and made a part of this contract. 
 
2. Nondiscrimination: The Contractor, with regar d to the work performed by it during the contract , will not 
discriminate on the grounds of race, color, or national origin in the selection and retention of subcontractors, 
including procurements of materials  and leases of equipment.  The Contractor will not participate directly or 
indirectly in the discrimination prohibited by the Acts and the Regulations, including employment practices when 
the contract covers any activity, project or program set forth in Appendix B of 49 CFR Part 21. 
 
3. Solicitations for Subcontractors, Including Procurements of Material and Equipment : In all solicitations, 
either by competitive bidd ing or negotiation made by the Contractor for work to be performed under a 
subcontract, including procurements of materials , or leases of  equipment, each potential subcontractor will be 
notified by the Contractor of the Contractor’s obligations under this contract and the Acts and the Regulations 
relative to nondiscrimination on the grounds of race, color, or national origin. 
 
4. Information and Reports : The Contractor will provide all information and reports required by the Acts, the 
Regulations, and directives issued pursuant thereto and will permit access to its books, records, accounts, other 
sources of information, and its facilities as may be determined by the Recipient or the FHWA, FTA, or FAA to 
be pertinent to ascertain compliance with such Acts, Regulations, and instructions.  Where any information 
required of a Contractor is in the exclusive possession of another who fails or refuses to furnish the information, 
the Contractor will so certify to the Recipient or, the FHWA, FTA, or FAA  as appropriate, and shall set forth 
what efforts it has made to obtain the information. 
 
5. Sanctions for Nonco mpliance: In the event of the Contractor’s noncompliance with the nondiscrimination 
provisions of this contract, the Recipient will impose such contract sanctions as it or the FHWA, FTA, or FAA  
may determine to be appropriate, including, but not limited to: 
 
a. withholding payments to the Contractor under the contract until the Contractor complies; and/or 
b. cancelling, terminating or suspending a contract, in whole or in part.
