7. PUBLIC HEARING AND POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF AN APPLICATION BY THE 
SOUTH ROUTT MEDICAL CENTER HEALTH SERVICE DISTRICT FOR A LAND 
USE CHANGE OF MAJOR IMPACT / CONDITIONAL USE - ADDITION OF 
880SF OF INSTITUTIONAL SPACE TO AN EXISTING FACILITY AND OTHER 
INTERIOR RENOVATIONS 
 A. Public hearing and possible approval for South Routt Medical Center Health 
Service District land use change of major impact. 
Mayor Dobbins opened the public hearing for the South Routt Medical Center Health 
Service District land use change of major impact. Louis stated the Planning Commission 
reviewed this application as a final stage and recommended the project be approved. 
Louis stated this isn't necessarily a major project as it's just a small addition to an 
existing building.Kathryn Schlatter spoke about the project. She introduced Ashley 
Duvenhage with Wold, the architectural company who helped with the project. She 
explained how they maximized the space that was already existing. She explained the 
880 square feet of expansion and renovating the existing space to optimize useable 
area. She explained it will double the operating space. The Medical Center is working 
on bringing on another Nurse to help with patient needs. She also explained that a 
portion of the building towards the back will be used as an Urgent Care area with a rear 
entrance to help emergency vehicles access patients.  
Alvin Powell spoke regarding the project and expressed his support with the project. 
Mayor Dobbins closed the public hearing and invited the Board members to ask 
questions or comment. 
Mayor Dobbins expressed her support for the project and is excited about the future of 
the Medical Center and the new opportunities that it will present the community. She 
stated it's a straight forward plan that was easy to read and understand. Kathryn 
thanked her for her support. She explained the Urgent Care area will greatly help the 
community have availability for same day appointments and walk in ability. Mayor 
Dobbins also asked if there will be imaging included in the new space. Kathryn 
explained imaging is a large expense that they aren't including in this current plan but 
that one of the expansion rooms will be set up to add imaging in the future when budget 
allows the addition. 
Kelly asked if the building will be air conditioned. Kathryn answered that yes, the 
building will have air conditioning which will help the comfort of the building along with 
safety.

8. DISCUSSION REGARDING AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF OAK CREEK, 
COLORADO ADOPTING A NEW CHAPTER 3.02 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE 
OF THE TOWN OF OAK CREEK REGARDING IMPLEMENTATION OF THE 
TOWN'S TWO PERCENT (2.0%) SALES TAX FOR THE PURPOSE OF 
CONSTRUCTION AND REHABILITATION OF CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS 
 A. Discussion regarding an ordinance adopting a new chapter of the municipal 
code. 
Louis stated this Ordinance regards the ballot question this November of adding 2% 
sales tax for Capital Improvements. Should the ballot measure not pass, this Ordinance 
would be null and void. This Ordinance would be presented to the Board to be passed if 
the ballot measure passes. The Ordinance explains what the funds could and could not 
be used for. Section 3.02 is the chapter of the code regarding funds of sales tax. There 
is no need to make any decisions on this item until the ballot measure either passes or 
fails.  
9. DISCUSSION REGARDING POSSIBLE UPGRADES TO THE BULK WATER 
DISPENSING STATION 
 A. Discussion regarding possible upgrades to the bulk water dispensing station 
Louis stated this is a recommended upgrade. There was a price quote for the most 
important part, being the control panel. The way the water station currently works is 
there is one access code that everyone uses. There's no reason for people to not share 
the code. The new system would set up different accounts which would prevent the 
sharing of codes. To date we've dispensed about 280,000 gallons at 2 cents a gallon. 
That equates to about $5,600. If the rate was raised to 5 cents a gallon that would add 
up to about $14,000. Should the Board approve this upgrade Louis recommends also 
raising the rate at the same time. He stated he will put together a study of information 
for surrounding communities and what they charge. Louis also stated Tom is getting 
quotes for a new housing structure for the station.
