ORDINANCE NO. 678 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF OAK CREEK, COLORADO  ADOPTING A NEW 
CHAPTER 3.02 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE TOWN OF OAK CREEK REGARDING 
IMPLEMENTATION OF THE TOWN'S TWO PERCENT ( 2.0%) SALES  TAX FOR THE 
PURPOSE OF CONSTRUCTION AND REHABILITATION OF CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE 
TOWN OF OAK CREEK, COLORADO THAT:  
Section 1. The Municipal Code of the Town of Oak Creek is amended by the addition 
thereto of a new Chapter 3.02 to read as follows: 
Chapter 3.02 -- Use of Two Percent (2%) Sales Tax Used Exclusively for the 
Construction and Rehabilitation of Capital Improvements 
 
3.02.010 – Purpose.    
 
It is the purpose of this Chapter to implement the sales tax of two percent ( 2%), 
commencing January 1, 2027, for the purpose of construction and rehabilitation of capital 
improvements as approved by the registered electors of the Town of Oak Creek.  The revenues 
derived from the two percent ( 2%) sales tax  are to be used solely for the construction and 
rehabilitation of capital improvements, which capital improvements are more particular described 
in this Chapter. 
 
3.02.020 – Definitions.  The following terms used in this Chapter shall have the meaning 
set forth below. 
 
(a) A capital improvement means and includes a non -recurring expenditure in 
association with the acquisition, construction, or physical improvement of land, buildings, or 
infrastructure, whereby such expenditure results in the creation of a new asset or the extension of 
an existing  asset’s useful life, value, and/or operational capacity.  The costs of the capital 
improvement is added to the basis of the asset, and depreciated over time, in contrast to repairs  
and maintenance expenditures which are recorded as operating costs in the period in which they 
occur.   
 
(b) Capital improvement sales tax means and includes the revenues received from the 
two percent (2%) sales tax as set forth in this Chapter 3.02. 
 
(c) Construction of capital improvements means and includes the design, engineering, 
and construction of new capital improvements in a manner approved by the Board of Trustees. 
 
(d) Town Administrator means and includes the Town Administrator of the Town of 
Oak Creek, and any of the Town Administrator's designees. 
 
(e) Public Facilities means and includes all municipal buildings,  facilities, and 
infrastructure of the Town of Oak Creek.   
 
3.02.030 –Two Percent (2%) Sales Tax Imposed.   
 
There shall be a two percent (2%) sales tax imposed for the purpose of construction and 
rehabilitation of capital improvements as more particularly described in this Chapter. 
 
3.02.040 – Capital Improvement Sales Tax.   
 
The revenue received from the two percent (2%) capital improvement sales tax shall be 
separately accounted for in a manner determined necessary by the Town Administrator in order to 
implement and administer the provisions of this Chapter.   
 
3.02.050 – Use of Revenues Received from the Two Percent (2%) Sales Tax.   
 
(a)   Expenditures of the revenues received from the capital improvement sales tax shall 
be used only for the construction of capital improvements  or the rehabilitation of capital assets , 
specifically including, but not limited to public facilities, public infrastructure, and water and 
sewer capital improvement projects. 
 
 (b)   The Town may pledge revenues received from the capital improvement sales tax 
and those anticipated to be collected to the retirement of the principal and interest of bonds issued 
by the Town for financing any of the activities and improvements set forth in subsection (a) of this 
Section 3.02.050. 
 
3.02.060 –Limitations on Use of Revenues Received from the Two Percent (2%) Sales 
Tax.   
 
(a)   The use of revenues from the capital improvement sales tax shall not be used for 
the following purposes:  
 
(1) Repair and maintenance expenditures which are recorded as operating costs 
in the period in which they occur; and 
 
(2) Construction of capital improvement projects not exceeding $1,000. 
 
(b)   Notwithstanding the limitations contained in subsection (a) of this Section 
3.02.060, the Town Administrator may combine projects using revenues from the capital 
improvement sales tax with projects using revenues from other sources of funding so long as th e 
revenues from the capital improvement sales tax are only used for the limited purposes authorized 
by this Ordinance. 
 
Section 2. Severability.  Should any one or more sections or provisions of this 
Ordinance be judicially determined invalid or unenforceable, such determination shall not affect, 
impair, or invalidate the remaining provisions hereof, the intention being that the various 
provisions hereof are severable. 
 Section 3. Effective Date.  This Ordinance shall be effective only if the ballot issue 
seeking voter approval of the two percent (2%) sales tax increase at the November 3, 2026, special 
election is approved.  If approved, this Ordinance shall go into effect on January 1, 2027.  If the 
registered electors do not approve the two percent (2%) sales tax increase, this Ordinance shall be 
void and of no force or effect.
