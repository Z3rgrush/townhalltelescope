7. Action Items 
a. Formation of Committee or Task Force to Research Viability of Town Park on Bridge 
Street from Discussion of July 23, 2026 
Mayor Patricia Medina told the Board that the committee to research the viability of a park 
already had Jim Jorgensen as a volunteer. Sven Edstrom said that he would volunteer with Jim to 
help research the viability and feasibility of such a park. 
Mary Hockenbery and Larry Wilkening both submitted letters that are not in favor of such a

would like to join. 
The committee will bring a report of its findings back to the Board of Trustees. 
b. Agenda Application Request – Tanner Wilson 
Tanner Wilson, of 335 E. Orchard Street, told the Board that he has four children, ages 6, 8, 11, 
and 13. All of his kids are really into skateboarding and scootering, so he hauls them around to 
all the different skate parks in the county. Tanner would like the Board to consider building a 
skate park in the Town of Hotchkiss. Tanner has many volunteers and supporters lined up to help 
with such a project. Tanner has met with the North Fork Pool, Park and Recreation District and 
will meet with the County Commissioners. 
Mayor Patricia Medina told Tanner that the Fairgrounds are owned by the county and would be a 
great place for a skate park. She told Tanner that the Town will provide a letter of support for the 
project when it gets to that point. 
Tanner said that he wondered who would take on the upkeep once the park has been built, but he 
does not want to get ahead of himself. Mayor Patricia Medina told Tanner that coming to the 
Board of Trustees was a good first step in the process. 
c. Ordinance 2026-07 Regarding Demonstrations and Special Events in Public Rights-of-
Way (First Reading) 
Mayor Patricia Medina said that Attorney Sarah Ross did a great job on this Ordinance. Daisie 
Kirk said that she read through the Ordinance twice and does not have any questions. 
Mayor Patricia Medina said that this Ordinance is to keep everyone safe and minimize any 
confrontations. 
Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom said that he tried to read this Ordinance on his phone, but it was 
difficult. He was given time to review the paper document. 
Tom Wills said that he has attended all the honk-and-wave rallies and has not seen anything that 
the attendees did to cause confrontation. Recently, a business owner had some problems with the 
rally. Tom feels that this Ordinance will fill a gap that is lacking in the Town Code. Tom 
supports the Ordinance and feels that Attorney Sarah Ross did a great job on it. 
Martha Young, a Hanson Mesa resident, told the Board that you can step off the curb according 
to state law, but you cannot impede traffic. 
Mary Hockenbery asked if the Ordinance can supersede state statute. 
Attorney Sarah Ross said that the Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) will have to 
approve the Ordinance if it is passed, since Bridge Street is a state highway. This Ordinance will 
apply to the whole town, not just the highway. 
Bob Penetta, a local business owner, said that he has participated in a few of the honk-and-waves 
with only one business owner conflict of which he is aware. 
Attorney Sarah Ross said that there is clarification under the definitions section concerning 
Special Events and other events. This clarifies when a permit is needed and when it is not. The 
definitions are in Section 2, Subsection C. Section 3 discusses what does not need a permit, such 
as spontaneous demonstrations. 
Mayor Patricia Medina asked if there were further questions or discussion about this Ordinance. 
Joel Reed said that this puts a burden on event planners. Town Administrator Kimberly States 
said that the process will need to be worked out. Kimberly said that this Ordinance came out of 
conversations held with Chief Scott Green. With no Ordinance in place, it is hard to enforce. 
Motion: Sven Edstrom moved to approve Ordinance 2026-07, an Ordinance of the Town of 
Hotchkiss, Colorado, adding Chapter 23 to the Hotchkiss Municipal Code regarding 
demonstrations and Special Events in public rights-of-way; providing content-neutral safety, 
access, and permit requirements; providing for severability, savings, and repeal; and providing 
an effective date on first reading, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all 
Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
