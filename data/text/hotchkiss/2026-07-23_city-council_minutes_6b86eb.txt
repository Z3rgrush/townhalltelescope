6. Action Items
a. Agenda Application Request-Don Foster
Mayor Patricia Medina told the Board that the Resolution that Don Foster included with his items was not included in the packet. A Resolution will be implemented if the Board determines that this is something that they would like to proceed with. There are a lot of things to consider and discuss. It is a wonderful idea that just needs to be discussed further.

Don Foster of 226 N 1st Street told the Board that he moved to the Town of Hotchkiss in 2011. His occupation is carpenter and in early June he noticed that the two lots, located at 171 E Bridge Street and 191 E Bridge Street, were for sale. In 2022 a rendition of a park was done, and he feels that a park in this location would be good for the Town.

Don said that a park is a huge undertaking, and the first step might be a working group with a couple of interested Trustees and citizens to investigate the idea and see what needs to be done and report the findings to the Board.

Don would like to see a daycare as well as a commercial kitchen in this location as well as a sink that can be used by the public.

A non-profit could become a part of this process as well. The Town would own the land, but the non-profit would be responsible for the grants, and the upkeep once it is established.

Mayor Patricia Medina said that a park is a great idea, but the Town cannot promise anything. The Board needs to discuss the possibility.

Don Foster said that a working group formation would allow the group to meet over the next couple of months for three (3) hours a week and report back to the Board.

Jim Jorgensen said it is a smart idea, and he feels a working group should be put together to bring back more facts. Right now, the idea is too vague for a decision.

The Board agreed to put this item on the agenda for the August 13, 2026, meeting when the whole Board is present to have the discussion. 

b.  Ordinance 2026-05 Franchise Agreement for the Purchase of Delta County Tele-Com, Inc Second Reading Possible Approval
Mayor Pat Medina asked if everyone had looked the Ordinance over and asked if there were any questions.

Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve Ordinance 2026-05 June 19, 2026, an Ordinance granting a Franchise by the Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado to Delta County Tele-Comm, Inc. D/B/A DCTC/Elevate, its successors and assigns, to locate, build, construct, acquire, purchase, extend, maintain and operate into, within and through the present and future corporate limits of the Town of Hotchkiss, Delta County, Colorado, Town wires, lines, poles, arms, appurtenances and fixtures for conducting business and exchange, over, under, along, across and through any and all streets, other Public ways and places in said Town of Hotchkiss, fixing the terms and conditions thereof; providing for repeal, severability, and an effective date seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.

Motion: Roger Christian moved to Approve Ordinance 2026-03 Wildlife Urban Interface Code as a first reading, seconded by Jim Jorgensen. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
