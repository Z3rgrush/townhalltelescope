Mayor Jim Wingfield called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. A verbal roll call was

PUBLIC HEARING 
ORDINANCE 2026-01 IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE 
ISSUANCE OF NEW OUT-OF-TOWN WATERTAPS; LIMITATIONS ON THE 
SALE OF TOWN WATER FROM THE TOWN STANDPIPE AND DELCARING AN 
EMERGENCY  
Mayor Wingfield mentions the fact that we are expecting an extremely drought year. And 
that now is the time to get thinking about conserving water. Pat Medina reads a public 
comment from Mary Hockenberry. She believes that we should look into the commercial 
water hauler and see where he is hauling water to. Each of its customers should have an 
account for the Standpipe so that we can track how much and where the water is going. 
Mayor Wingfield agrees with that and that we shouldn’t be supplying people in other 
towns in a drought year. Hotchkiss treats its own water and should be kept for Hotchkiss 
residents first. Sven Edstrom asked about data from the Standpipe on how much and 
where the water is going to establish a parameter. Bo Nerlin states from a staff’s 
perspective it is explicate in the ordinance that there should be the ceasing of sale from 
the water of the standpipe for commercial purposes or for construction purposes. Also, it 
sounds like there needs to be more research on the staff’s part. Mayor Wingfield asks for 
public comment Allen Todd asked, is there anyway the town knows how much water 
goes out in a month? John Marta commented that he hauled water and that water goes 
from Hanson, Powell and Redlands mesa, he wanted the board to be aware that the water 
goes all over not just within Hotchkiss. Kim Kelly is a Rogers mesa resident the town 
treats that water will there be any restrictions like Hanson mesa? Mayor Wingfield stated 
that we treat their water, but the system is separate from the town, they have their own 
rules. Marvin Jackson, the town treats the water, it isn’t town water. Mayor Wingfield 
agrees with Attorney Bo Nerlin’s recommendation of passing the emergency ordinance 
and continuing the research and having a work session. Commercial use was defined as 
the one that is using it commercially for construction not the one that is hauling water for 
residents. Addresses need to be collected, and research needs to be done. There is only 
one water hauler that is certified through the state of Colorado in our area. Another part 
of discussion was to charge more for going over a certain number of gallons. If people 
that can afford to continue to pay the higher costs continue to use more there have to be 
other ways to suppress water usage doing the drought years. What will be the boundary 
for those who can receive water? Mike West last year’s water was bad, what needs to 
happen to bring water to Hotchkiss. Mayor Wingfield mentioned that we have been 
looking into that for the last few years but have hit roadblocks and have had to switch 
directions. We are always looking but it is not easy. There was talk about trying to turn 
irrigation water into residential water. Water was so bad because it is pond water and it is 
pulling from the bottom of the pond; Hotchkiss only has reservoir water. Sven Edstrom 
moved to pass as emergency. Pat Medina seconded, a verbal vote was taken with all 
Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
