RESOLUTION 2026-04 CONCERNING DROUGHT REGULATIONS
Don Dybala asked how the water was measured at the source and asked about the amount of household water usage per house.
Public Works Director Marvin Jackson said the measurement of water is measured in acre-     feet. Don Dybala asked about the 6,000 gallons of water approximate measurement used for this Resolution. This number is what is used as the average measurement.
There were several minutes of discussion about the water rates being the lowest in the County as well as the amount charged at the bulk water station and the people that use it.
It was noted that people that haul water naturally conserve it better than residents that have access to water without having to haul it.
There was a water rate study done a couple of years ago, but the calculation did not include the cost increase of everything that the Town has done.
The Board decided that the bulk water station (standpipe) should be discussed separately. There will be a Work Session before the second Board Meeting to be held on May 28, 2026.
Interim Town Administrator cautioned the Board about keeping the water rates and regulations separated from the drought rates and regulations. Once hired, the permanent Town Administrator will dig into the water rate situation.
Sven Edstrom asked if the Work Session will be on the Bulk Water Station as well as the water rates. He said that outside of the 81419 ZIP code will go to $12.00 per thousand at the bulk water station but then what?
Pat Medina said that would be worked out at the Work Session on May 28, 2026.
Roger Christian moved to approve Resolution 2026-04, a Resolution regarding drought emergency and response declaration for the Town of Hotchkiss, seconded by Sven Edstrom.
A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.

NEW TOWN ATTORNEY RECOMMENDATIONS
Attorney Bo Nerlin told the Board that at the April 23, 2026, meeting the Board of Trustees voted to form a committee consisting of Mayor Pat Medina, Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom, Attorney Bo Nerlin and Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister to conduct the interviews for the three (3) Attorneys that responded to the request for RFPs (Request for Proposals) for the Town of Hotchkiss.
All the firms interviewed very well. The interview committee determined that Wyatt, Hamilton and Findley best meet the Town’s needs. They have several other Statutory Municipalities in the State of Colorado that they represent as well as one in Texas. The firm would provide one primary attorney for Town legal matters and a second attorney for additional support as needed. They also offer the lowest billing rate of all the lawyers that applied.
Onboarding would begin in June. Whitt Wyatt, one of the lawyers from the firm, was in attendance on Zoom.
Attorney Nerlin told the Board that they have three (3) options in the choice of this recommendation.
Engage the Firm of Wyatt, Hamilton and Findley based upon the recommendation of the hiring committee and enter into a contract with the firm at the meeting on May 28, 2026.
Redo the interview of the recommended firm that includes the whole Board.
Reject the recommendation and reschedule the interviews with all Attorneys.
Pat Medina told the Board that the interviews were extensive, and that Wyatt, Hamilton and      Findley were felt to be the best fit for the Town of the three (3) Attorney proposals. 
Sven Edstrom said that he is fully in support of this choice. Whitt Wyatt was friendly and he and his wife were both in the interview.
Pat Medina asked Whitt Wyatt to introduce himself to the Board.
Whitt said that he has been a Municipal lawyer for the last twenty (20) years. He loves what he does, and it does have challenges as well. There are ten (10) attorneys in the firm that he works for and his wife, Sarah, will be the point of contact with Whitt for the Town of Hotchkiss. Attorneys have weekly meetings to discuss client needs and how best to approach them. They are here to adapt to Hotchkiss and feel that Hotchkiss is an amazing community to be working with.
Attorney Nerlin told the Board that a motion with directions for his office is what is needed to move forward.
Roger Christian moved to direct Attorney Bo Nerlin to work with the firm Wyatt, Hamilton and Findley to come up with a Professional Services Agreement to be ready for the May 28, 2026, Second Board Meeting for the Town of Hotchkiss, seconded by Jim Jorgensen. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
