8. ACTION ITEMS 
a. GRAVES CONSULTING-PRESENTATION AND ADOPTION OF WAGE COMPENSATION STUDY 
RESULTS AND BENEFITS RECOMMENTATION 
Laurie Graves of Graves Consulting attended the meeting via Zoom. Her screen was shared 
and the slide show presentation of the ﬁndings of Graves consulting was shared with the 
Board. 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister said that there is a clean ﬁnancial impact on the 
budget if the Board decides to move forward with Laurie Graves’ recommendations and it will 
not increase the budget for 2026. This implementation would start for all employees on June 1, 
2026, if approved. 
Pat Medina said that she likes the breakdown of the presentation and likes the fact that it is 
easy to read. 
Sven Edstrom still voiced Budget questions and concerns. 
Lauren Gister stated that the Back the Badge Fund is to recruit and retain officers. By 
comparison this Fund is in good shape. Money was already budgeted for salaries for the year 
2026. Town Clerk Ginger Redden also budgeted more for the Salary Survey than it ended up 
being so there will be $12,000 to $15,000 left over. 
Pat Medina said that the last time a salary survey was done, it was not done correctly. This 
salary survey has been done correctly and has more answers to questions that have come up. 
Chief Green has worked hard on his budget to get his employees to where they need to be. 
If the salary increase is approved, Laurie Graves of Graves consulting will give a presentation 
to the staff to the employees tomorrow. 
Corporal Cody Prentice stated that the pay scale is confusing and wanted to know how the 
grade is ﬁgured. 
Laurie Graves explained how the scale was ﬁgured. 
Lauren Gister stated that the exact recommendation for each pay scale will be talked about at 
the employee presentation tomorrow if the Board approves it tonight. 
Laurie Graves discussed more about the study and explained that all the information required 
to get the correct information for a Salary Survey. The pay should be based on Market Increase 
as well as a Merit Increase so that the pay structures can move forward every year .  
The survey also considers a cost of labor increase every year for employees to keep them 
competitive in the wage market. This is now called the Cost of Labor increase instead of the 
Cost-of-Living Increase due to the preconceptions about the wording. 
Lauren Gister also addressed the Board about the vacation and insurance that is being offered 
now and improvements that can be made that will not cost a lot but be very beneﬁcial to the 
employees.

a. GRAVES CONSULTING-PRESENTATION AND ADOPTION OF WAGE COMPENSATION STUDY 
RESULTS AND BENEFITS RECOMMENDATION 
For example, right now, vacation time does not track with current averages and increasing it 
does not cost the Town anything right now. Starting a new person with two (2) weeks of 
vacation instead of one (1) will help and increase the vacation from there. 
Vacation should start with two (2) weeks per year or ten (10) days of vacation. Five (5) to ten 
(10) years should have ﬁfteen (15) days of vacation and eleven (11) years and over should 
have twenty (20) days of vacation. The vacation should increase a little each year until each 
goal is achieved. There should be a limit on the amount of vacation that can be paid out and 
vacations should be encouraged. 
Another beneﬁt that can be changed that will minimally increase the budget and will be 
beneﬁcial to the employees is the insurance. Paying the full insurance for the Employee and 
still paying 80% of the rest of the family choices will make it easier on the employee and will 
only increase the budget by $3,000 to $5,000. 
Pat Medina said that she feels that we should help the employees and this is a good way. 
Pat Medina also stated that everything in the report seems reasonable. Custer McLeod said 
he would like to hear from each employee about the impact it will have. 
Lauren Gister said that if the Board chooses to move forward with the implementation of the 
salaries and beneﬁts, she will sit and talk with each employee about it. 
Sven Edstrom feels that there is too much information to make a decision on tonight. 
Lauren Gister said that usually this is a decision made by the Town Administrator and the 
Board just gets an update. 
Sven Edstrom stated that he does not feel comfortable making a decision without 
understanding it better. 
There were several minutes of discussion about this decision and the importance of it and the 
overwhelming amount of information related to it. 
Pat Medina said that if nothing else, we can get the employees up to Market Value with their 
salaries and discuss the rest later. 
Corporal Prentice stated that he did not understand why the fair market value of the salaries 
can be shared but the rest of the information gathered cannot. 
Laurie Graves said that some of that information cannot be shared due to the law. 
Sven Edstrom said that it should be tabled and a Work Session be scheduled to discuss it. 
Jim Jorgensen said that it felt like they were beating a dead horse and that this needs to be 
approved. The facts are all there and a decision needs to be made. 
Pat Medina agreed with Jim. 
Roger Christian said that this was a good study with a lot of effort put into it. Not everyone will 
be happy no matter what is done and that a decision needs to be made tonight. The more time 
it is put off, the more confusion it will create. 
Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the adoption of the Salary and Wage Survey done by Graves

b. PRESENTATION AND DISCUSSION WITH THE BOARD REGARDING ADOPTING THE 
WILDLAND URBAN INTERFACE (WUI CODE) 
Kim McDonald of the Division of Fire Prevention and Code in Colorado gave a slide show 
presentation to the Board regarding the Wildland Urban Interface Code. This code applies to 
new construction, and more information can be found on the State Forest Service website. 
This is a stand-alone model building code, and an energy code does not need to be adopted 
with it. 
Lauren Gister said that all jurisdictions in the State of Colorado must adopt this simple code.  
Tonight is just an introduction to the code and all that it entails. There will hopefully be an 
Ordinance ready for the Board to look at and adopt at the June 11, 2026, Board of Trustee 
meeting.

d. AGENDA APPLICATION REQUEST-PAUL SCHMUCKER-WATER RIGHTS AND ADDING TWO 
(2) METERS TO ROGERS MESA DOMESTIC WATER USERS ASSOCIATION RAW WATER LINE 
Paul Schmucker addressed the Board and explained how adding two (2) additional meters 
onto the Rogers Mesa Domestic Water Users Association raw water line would save water 
and money. Paul drew a diagram for the Board on where the lines go and how these would 
beneﬁt the Town as well. 
Pat Medina asked what these meters would cost. Paul said that the cost would be around 
$2,000 per meter.
