8. DROUGHT RESTRICTIONS DISCUSSION 
Public Works Director, Marvin Jackson, said that no new accounts are being created for the bulk 
water station (Standpipe) and he feels that everything else should remain the same so that 
everyone can be treated fairly. The Town of Cedaredge just passed a drought Resolution saying 
that no swimming pools or hot tubs can be ﬁll and then approved a new subdivision. Marvin feels 
that the water rates up to the ﬁrst ten thousand (10,000) gallons of water should remain at the 
same price and then be raised over that point. Water restrictions can be tough to enforce 
whether it is watering only two (2) days a week or every other day watering on the odd and even 
days depending on the address. 
Jim Jorgensen asked what has been done in the past. Marvin said that this is the dryest year that 
the Town of Hotchkiss has had in a long time. 
Pat Medina said that a lot of people hold multiple standpipe accounts in multiple Towns and 
wondered how we can limit their use. 
Sven Edstrom suggested a rate increase or a hauling limit on the gallons a person can haul. 
Marvin Jackson said that the amounts can vary from person to person. 
Don Dybala stated that cistern sizes vary from sixteen hundred (1600) to two thousand (2000) 
gallons and people who use cisterns already are great water conservers. 
Marvin Jackson said that all the Town water rates are some of the lowest around. Paonia has a 
standpipe that is operational now but there are plans to shut it down. Crawford may shut theirs 
down as well and that leaves people to come to Hotchkiss if this is their only water resource. 
Interim Town Administrator said that Town Staff is looking for direction from the Board tonight on 
how they would like to proceed so that something can be brought back in the May 14, 2026 
meeting. 
Pat Medina asked how far our parameter should go and how do we determine that. 
Jim Jorgensen said that if they are going to use our water, they should pay the same as an in-
Town resident must pay. We have the lowest rates around. 
Marvin said that Paonia charges $3.00 per hundred gallons, Pitkin Mesa charges $2.00 per 
hundred gallons, Crawford charges $1.50 per hundred gallons, Orchard City charges $1.00 per 
hundred gallons and the Town of Hotchkiss charges $.96 per hundred gallons at the standpipe. 
After several minutes of discussion about ideas to help implement drought restrictions, Engineer 
Joanne Fagan reminded the Board that the Town of Hotchkiss did receive the $10,000 
WaterSMART grant for education. Once that is received, we can also help educate our 
community on water and its importance.

9. NEW ATTORNEY INTERVIEW AND HIRING PROCESS 
Attorney Nerlin wanted to discuss this item next since it will be shorter than the scheduled 
training. 
There were three (3) responses by Attorneys to the Town’s request for RFPs (Request for 
Proposals). The Board has three (3) options to continue with the process of hiring a new Town 
Attorney. 
1) Conduct interviews in an Open Meeting to include the full Board 
2) Conduct interviews in an Executive Session which also includes the full Board and is 
subject to Attorney/Client Privilege 
3) Appoint a Committee of Staff and Trustees to interview the applicants and make a 
recommendation to the Board. 
All these proposals came from the Front Range. There were letters sent out to the lawyers in the 
area but there was not a response received from any of them. 
 Joel Reed said he would be interested in being on the committee to conduct the interviews. 
Jim Jorgensen said he feels the committee should be more Senior Trustees conducting the 
interviews. 
Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the forming of a committee of Staff and Trustees to conduct 
interviews and make a recommendation to the Board once interviews are complete, seconded by 
Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with Jim Jorgensen, Daisie Kirk, Don Dybala, Sven 
Edstrom and Roger Christian voting in favor of the motion and Joel Reed voting against. Motion 
passed. 
Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the committee of Pat Medina, Sven Edstrom, Attorney Nerlin and 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister to interview the Attorneys and make a recommendation 
to the Board for hiring, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with Jim Jorgensen, 
Daisie Kirk, Roger Christian, Don Dybala and Sven Edstrom voting in favor and Joel Reed voting 
against. Motion passed.
