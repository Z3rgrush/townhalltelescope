
HOTCHKISS BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MAY 26, 2026, SPECIAL MEETING

CALL THE MEETING TO ORDER
Mayor Pat Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.
Present were Don Dybala, Roger Christian, Sven Edstrom, Joel Reed, Daisie Kirk and Jim Jorgensen.

Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance.

EXECUTIVE SESSION: PURSUANT TO COLORADO REVISED STATUTES 24-6-402(4)(f)(l) FOR DISCUSSION OF PERSONNEL MATTERS RELATED TO THE RECRUITMENT AND EVALUATION OF APPLICANTS FOR THE POSITION OF TOWN ADMINISTRATOR
Sven Edstrom moved to go into the above noted Executive Session, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.

The Executive Session started at 6:31 p.m.
