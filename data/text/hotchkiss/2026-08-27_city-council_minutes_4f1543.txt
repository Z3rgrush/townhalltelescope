 
Town of Hotchkiss Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes 
 
August 27, 2026 
1. Call the Meeting to Order 
 
Mayor Pat Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. 
 
• Present: Roger Christian, Jim Jorgensen, Don Dybala, and Joel Reed. 
 
• Absent: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom and Daisie Kirk 
 
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
2. Public Comment 
None. 
 
3. Trustee Comments 
None. 
 
4. Town Attorney Comments 
Attorney Sarah Ross told the Board that Ordinance 2026-07 has neutral rules about 
demonstrations and special events. It is to protect the safety of businesses and 
customers. It does not prohibit protests or regulate any person on their messages or 
causes. It makes sure that everyone is on the same page on what can and cannot be 
done legally. 
 
Mayor Patricia Medina thanked Attorney Sarah Ross for this Ordinance. 
 
5. Town Administrator Comments 
Town Administrator Kimberly States told the Board that she met with the Town of 
Cedaredge and Upper Surface Creek Domestic Water Users regarding the water 
situation. They are looking at the possibility of an agreement with all three (3) entities 
like Project Seven. This would be valuable with the ageing infrastructure and there may 
be funding available for this. Meetings with Tri County Water are also being held to get 
information. Town Administrator Kimberly States will keep the Board up to date on the 
findings. 
 
The Town received a $10,000 Water Smart Grant that will be used for education. Once 
the contract is in place we will start working with the Water Now Alliance to start to get 
education to the public. 
 
Kimberly and Engineer Joanne Fagan have been having conversations about the water 
situation and if we have a fire where our water source is, we would not be able to get 
water. Discussions are in progress to get funding for a study in that area. 
 
 
6. Action Items 
a. ORDINANCE 2026-07 Regarding demonstrations and Special Events in Public 
Rights-of-Way Possible Adoption 
Mayor Patricia Medina asked if there were further questions or comments regarding this 
Ordinance. 
  
Motion: Roger Christian moved to approve Ordinance 2026-07 an Ordinance of the 
Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado, adding Chapter 23 to the Hotchkiss Municipal Code 
regarding demonstrations and special events in public rights-of-way; and providing 
content-neutral safety, access, and permit requirements; providing for severability, 
savings, repeal and providing an effective date, seconded by Joel Reed. A verbal vote 
was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed. 
 
b.  Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)with Technical College of the Rockies 
for the Transfer of Town of Hotchkiss Police Department Vehicles in Exchange for 
Tuition 
Mayor Patricia Medina said that she has a lot of questions about this MOU. 
 
Roger Christian said that he had questions as well. Roger does not remember a 
discussion about removing the vehicles in question from service. They have low miles 
and he is curious as to why they were being removed. 
 
Chief Green stated that they were not trustworthy vehicles. The original plan was to sell 
these vehicles but to do that they would have to be decommissioned. 
 
There were several moments of discussion about the decommissioning of cars and 
what that entails. The suggestion was made that if the cages were left in vehicles, a 
security company may want to buy them.  
 
There are four (4) vehicles not in use right now. Two (2) of them are Chargers and Chief 
Green said that TCR is not interested in them since they are fast vehicles. There was 
also discussion about what will happen if one of the vehicles goes down, what the back-
up plan will be. Chief Scott Green said that the Officers would have to share until the 
vehicle is repaired. 
 
TCR is offering $10,000 for cars in the form of tuition for two (2) students which equals 
$5,265.00 per student or $10,530, and Refresher Academy for three (3) students, which 
equals $3,400.00 per student or $10,200. The vehicles are both Ford Explorers. One is 
2018 and one is 2014. 
 
The Kelly Blue Book has them listed in the value range of: 
2014 Ford Explorer ranges from $7,775.00 to $9,675.00 
2018 Ford Explorer ranges from $11,650.00 to $18,250.00  
 
More discussion was had about these vehicles being paid for with taxpayers’ money 
and the Town should get better value for trade. 
 
The Board asked what kind of contract would be in place for someone that utilizes the 
tuition for this trade. Chief Scott Green said that typically a contract was signed saying 
the Officer will work for the Town for five (5) years or pay $5,000. 
 
After several more minutes of discussion, the Board directed Chief Scott Green to go 
back to TCR (Technical College of the Rockies) and see if the contract can be amended 
to have three (3) student tuitions for a total of $15,795.00 and four (4) refresher 
academies for a total of $13,600.00. The Board would also like to see a clause stating 
that if tuition increases, that will not affect the Town, that TCR would honor the 
agreement without the increasing tuition fees so the Town will not have to pay the extra. 
 
If TCR does not accept the terms, Chief Scott Green will come back to the Board and 
decisions on how to proceed will be talked about at that time.
