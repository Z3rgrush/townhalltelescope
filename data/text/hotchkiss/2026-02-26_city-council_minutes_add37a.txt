     RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS 
 
HOTCHKISS BOARD OF TRUSTEES 
 
FEBRUARY 26, 2026, SPECIAL MEETING 
 
1. CALL THE MEETING TO ORDER 
Mayor Pro Tem Pat Medina called the meeting to order at 7:15 p.m. 
Those present were Jim Jorgensen, Randy Thalmann, Roger Christian, Sven Edstrom and Roger 
Christian. Absent was Custer McLeod. Mayor Jim Wingﬁeld resigned in the Work Session 
previously. 
 
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
2. HOTCHKISS BOARD OF TRUSTEES COMPENSATION DISCUSSION AND REQUEST FOR 
GUIDANCE 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister told the Board that the compensation paid to the Town 
of Hotchkiss Elected Officials is very low. If the Board elects to move forward with this increase of 
compensation, only the newly elected officials will receive it. Since there is an election this year it 
can go into effect before the Election if approved tonight. The next election will be in two (2) years. 
This information was also in the Memo placed in the Trustee packets. 
The Trustees had several minutes of discussion and agreed that the pay for the elected officials 
needs to be raised. 
Sven Edstrom proposed an increase to $400.00 for the mayor and $150.00 for the Trustees. 
Interim Town Manager Lauren Gister said that she will get an Ordinance together for Emergency 
Adoption for the March 12, 2026, meeting. 
 
3. HIRING OF PERMANENT TOWN ADMINISTRATOR FOR TOWN OF HOTCHKISS 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister told the Board that the person for this job would need to 
be the right ﬁt for the Town of Hotchkiss. The Board will also need to decide where this person 
would live and have input on other things that are pertinent to this position for the Town of 
Hotchkiss. If a ﬁrm is hired to help with the hiring process, they will involve the whole Town in the 
choice. 
There were several minutes of discussion by the Board including: 
a. Housing in the Town of Hotchkiss and the difficulty ﬁnding places to live 
b. The importance of ﬁnding the right ﬁt for the Town of Hotchkiss. 
c. Changes that will need to be made to the code 
d. Changes needed for the Master Plan wording 
e. Timeline for the hiring of an Administrator if passed 
f. Cost of a ﬁrm to help with the hiring 
Sven Edstrom moved to proceed with the search and hiring process for a Hotchkiss Town 
Administrator and instruct Interim Town Administrator, Lauren Gister, to prepare a Code 
Amendment, job description, and RFQ/RFP for a search ﬁrm to assist the Board in that process, 
seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with Roger Christian, Jim Jorgensen, Sven 
Edstrom voting “Yes” and Randy Thalmann voting “No” . Motion passed.

4. HIRING PROCESS FOR NEW TOWN ATTORNEY AND AUTHORIZATION FOR PREPARATION AND 
ISSUANCE OF REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS/REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS 
Attorney Bo Nerlin submitted his letter of resignation to the Board of Trustees on February 17, 
2026. Attorney Nerlin stated that he serves at the pleasure of the Board of Trustees and is happy to 
stay and assist in the hiring of a new attorney as needed. A sample of an RFP was included in the 
packet. This sample is the one that the Town of Paonia used when it entered their search for a new 
attorney. 
Roger Christian moved to ask Attorney Bo Nerlin to write a RFP (Request for Proposal) for the 
hiring of a new attorney for the Town of Hotchkiss, seconded by Jim Jorgensen. A verbal vote was 
taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed. 
 
5. EXECUTIVE SESSION: PURSUANT TO C.R.S. 24-6-402(4)(b) FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING 
LEGAL ADVICE FOLLOWING UP ON THE ASSESSMENT OF THE TOWN CONDUCTED BY THE 
DEPARTMENT OF LOCAL AFFAIRS AND TO DISCUSS A SEPARATE THIRD-PARTY INQUIRY OF 
TOWN MATTERS 
Sven Edstrom moved to go into Executive Session pursuant to C.R.S. 24-6-402(4)(b) for the 
Purpose of receiving legal advice following up on the assessment of the Town conducted by the 
Department of Local Affairs and to discuss separate third-party inquiry of Town Matters, seconded 
by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed. 
It was said that there would not be any motion after the Executive Session. The Executive Session 
was entered into at 7:48 p.m.
