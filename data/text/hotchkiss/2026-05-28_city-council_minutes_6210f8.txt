
HOTCHKISS BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MAY 28, 2026

CALL THE MEETING TO ORDER
Mayor Pat Medina called the meeting to order at 7:50 p.m.
Those present were Roger Christian, Jim Jorgensen, Daisie Kirk, Don Dybala, Joel Reed and Sven Edstrom.

Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance.

PUBLIC COMMENT
None

TRUSTEE COMMENTS
None

TOWN ATTORNEY COMMENTS
Attorney Bo Nerlin said that no contract has been delivered to his office yet. The contract will be ready for the June 11, 2026, meeting. To assist with some of the transition there will be a couple of more big items to address at the June 11, 2026, meeting. One of the items will be preparation of Town Amendments to reflect the addition of a new Town Administrator, such as the job description and responsibilities. Attorney Bo Nerlin also discussed the timeline for interviews for the Town Administrator position, finalists and an open house to introduce the finalists. June 11th will be a jam-packed meeting.
TOWN ADMINISTRATOR COMMENTS
None.

ACTION ITEMS
POSSIBLE ACTION ON AXON AFTER WORK SESSION
Attorney Bo Nerlin asked if the Board would be in favor of having a legal review of the contract and then bring it back to the Board at the June 11, 2026, Meeting.
Mayor Pat Medina said that it is a good idea to have the contract reviewed.
Chief Green said that he would send everything over to Attorney Bo Nerlin to look at.
Joel Reed moved to have Attorney Bo Nerlin review the contract and bring it back to the Board at the June 11, 2026, meeting. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.

POSSIBLE ACTION ON STANDPIPE AFTER WORK SESSION
Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the rates for the Standpipe as noted in Resolution 2026-04 discussed last month, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.

RESOLUTION 2026-05 RESOLUTION OF SIGNERS
Joel Reed moved to approve Resolution 2026-05, A Resolution of the Board of Trustees authorizing the legal signers for the Town of Hotchkiss. AUDIT APPROVAL FOR  2026
This item was tabled until June 11, 2026, meeting so that the Board has time to read through it.

PAYROLL UPDATE
Pat Medina asked if Caselle was helpful with all of the payroll changes that are being made. Deputy Town Clerk Tessa Denison said that Caselle has been very helpful and the changes to be made are almost all ready to go. Mayor Pat Medina said that she is glad that payroll is going to be biweekly since it will make things a little easier. Town Clerk Ginger Redden and Mayor Pat Medina said that Deputy Town Clerk Tessa Denison has been working hard on this project.

EXECUTIVE SESSION PURSUANT TO C.R.S. 24-6-402(4)(b) CONFERENCE WITH ATTORNEY FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING LEGAL ADVICE ON SPECIFIC LEGAL QUESTIONS.
Sven Edstrom moved to go into the above noted Executive Session, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
