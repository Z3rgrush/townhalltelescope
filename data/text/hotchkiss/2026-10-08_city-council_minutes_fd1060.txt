Town of Hotchkiss 
Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes 
October 8, 2026 
1. Call the Meeting to Order 
Mayor Patricia Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Present: Mayor Patricia Medina; Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom; Trustees Roger Christian, 
Daisie Kirk, Jim Jorgensen, and Joel Reed. 
Absent: Trustee Don Dybala. 
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance. 
2. Public Comment 
Mayor Patricia Medina read the rules for public comment. 
Wade Pridgen, of 401 Lorah Lane, addressed the Board regarding property he owns at the corner 
of 4th Street and Lorah Lane. Mr. Pridgen stated that the property is part of the Dukes Unplatted 
Subdivision and expressed concerns regarding how the Town handled the separation of the 
property on December 21, 1921. 
Mr. Pridgen stated that there is no legal plat shown on any map and that no title was issued by 
the administration at that time. He expressed concerns regarding potential litigation and damages 
if the matter were taken to court. He characterized the situation as a mistake by the Town and 
stated that he had filed a complaint with Police Chief Scott Green, whom he described as fair. 
Mr. Pridgen also proposed establishing a pocket park in the area, to be named the Train Station 
Pocket Park. He stated that he would provide funding for the proposed park and requested that 
the Board address what he believes was an error made in 1921. 
3. Consent Agenda 
The Consent Agenda included the following: 
• Minutes of September 10, 2026. 
• Minutes of September 24, 2026. 
Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom noted that the September 24, 2026, meeting minutes incorrectly 
listed the meeting date as September 22, 2026. 
Motion: Sven Edstrom moved to approve the Consent Agenda with the correction to the 
September 24, 2026, meeting minutes. Roger Christian seconded the motion. A verbal vote was 
taken, with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed. 
4. Trustee Comments 
None. 
5. Town Attorney Comments 
Town Attorney Sarah Ross introduced Attorney Rene Congdon to the Board. Attorney Congdon 
will assist Attorney Ross with legal matters involving the Town of Hotchkiss. 
Attorney Congdon stated that she was pleased to be working with the Town and looked forward 
to assisting with its legal matters. 
6. Town Administrator Comments 
None. 
7. Planning Commission Comments 
Mayor Patricia Medina informed the Board that sidewalks were currently being reviewed. 
Planning Commission Chairperson Tom Wills reported that the Planning Commission has been 
reviewing the recently adopted Town Master Plan and identifying projects assigned to the 
Commission. 
The Planning Commission has also discussed the housing shortage and potential requirements 
for accessory dwelling units (ADUs) in the R-1 zoning district. Draft recommendations resulting 
from these discussions are expected to be presented to the Board after the first of the year. 
Mayor Medina acknowledged Chairperson Wills for his contributions to the proposed changes. 
She stated that the current Board would be responsible for making the changes needed for the 
Town. 
8. Action Items 
a. Liquor License Renewal – City Market #29 
Mayor Patricia Medina stated that there were no issues with the liquor license renewal 
application. All required documentation was in order, and the applicable fees had been paid. 
Motion: Roger Christian moved to approve the liquor license renewal for City Market #29. Sven 
Edstrom seconded the motion. A verbal vote was taken, with all Trustees voting in favor. 
Motion passed. 
b. Street Closure Request – Hotchkiss Elks Lodge – Teresa Burns 
Teresa Burns addressed the Board regarding the Hotchkiss Elks Lodge's annual Trunk or Treat 
event. 
Ms. Burns stated that this would be the fourth year the Lodge has hosted the event. The street 
between Bridge Street and Main Street has been closed for the event during the previous three 
years, and participation has continued to increase. 
She also explained that this year's haunted house would be held inside the Lodge room. 
Motion: Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the street closure request for the Hotchkiss Elks 
Lodge's annual Trunk or Treat event on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Roger Christian seconded 
the motion. A verbal vote was taken, with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed. 
c. Agenda Application and Donation Request – North Fork Community Dinners – Joanne 
Shymanski 
Mayor Patricia Medina informed the Board that Joanne Shymanski would be taking over the 
coordination of North Fork Community Dinners from Larry Jakubiak. 
Ms. Shymanski stated that she moved to the area in 2017. She explained that Mr. Jakubiak has 
been preparing free community dinners for Thanksgiving and Christmas since 1993. 
Ms. Shymanski expressed her appreciation for the community tradition and her desire to 
continue providing these meals. 
The Town of Hotchkiss has historically contributed $500 annually to support the community 
dinners. 
Motion: Roger Christian moved to approve a donation of $500 to North Fork Community 
Dinners. Sven Edstrom seconded the motion. A verbal vote was taken, with all Trustees voting in 
favor. Motion passed. 
d. Presentation of the First Draft of the 2027 Budget 
Town Administrator Kimberly States distributed the first draft of the proposed 2027 Town 
budget to the Board of Trustees. 
Administrator States explained that the budget narrative includes notes identifying line items 
requiring additional attention and highlighting significant changes. The narrative is organized by 
fund to assist the Board with its review. 
She also explained that state statute requires the proposed budget for the following year to be 
submitted to the Board by October 15. 
The first budget work session is scheduled for Thursday, October 22, 2026, in conjunction with 
the second regular Board meeting of the month. 
Administrator States requested that Board members review the budget packet before the work 
session and submit any questions to her in advance. 
Mayor Patricia Medina complimented Administrator States on her work preparing the budget. 
Mayor Medina also introduced the new Public Works Director, Cory Heiniger, to the Board and 
expressed that she would be sad to see Marvin Jackson leave. 
e. Executive Session 
The Board considered entering executive session for the following matters: 
1. Confidential Legal Advice and Investigative Matters 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(b), for a conference with the Town Attorney and outside legal 
counsel to receive confidential legal advice on specific legal questions related to investigations; 
C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(c), for discussion of matters required to be kept confidential by federal or 
state law, specifically protected investigative records or documents; and 
C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(d), for discussion of specialized details of security arrangements or 
investigations. 
2. Piñon Road Alley Dispute – Threatened Litigation and Settlement Negotiations 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(b), for a conference with the Town Attorney to receive 
confidential legal advice on specific legal questions related to the dispute; and 
C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(e)(I), for determining positions relative to matters that may be subject to 
negotiations, developing negotiation strategies, and instructing negotiators. 
Motion: Daisie Kirk moved to enter executive session for the purposes stated above. Sven 
Edstrom seconded the motion. A verbal vote was taken, with all Trustees voting in favor. 
Motion passed. 
The Board entered executive session at 7:02 p.m. 
Return to Open Meeting 
The executive session concluded at 7:39 p.m., and the Board returned to the open meeting.

Mayor Medina asked whether any participant believed that substantial discussion occurred 
regarding matters not included in the motion to enter executive session, or that any improper 
action occurred in violation of the Colorado Open Meetings Law. 
No concerns were expressed. 
The Board proceeded to the next agenda item. 
9. Adjournment 
The meeting adjourned at 7:40 p.m.
