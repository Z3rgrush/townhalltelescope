 
Town of Hotchkiss Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes 
 
September 24, 2026 
1. Call the Meeting to Order 
Mayor Patricia Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Present: Roger Christian, Don Dybala, and Daisie Kirk 
Absent: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom, Jim Jorgensen, and Joel Reed 
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance. 
Mayor Medina requested that the agenda be amended to add an action item for a permit waiver 
for the North Fork Miners Homecoming Parade. 
2. Public Comment 
None. 
3. Accounts Payable 
Mayor Medina asked about the TS&L LLC invoice. The invoice was the final invoice for the 
flashing crosswalk lights. 
Motion: Roger Christian moved to pay the bills as presented, seconded by Daisie Kirk. A voice 
vote was taken, with all Trustees present voting in favor. Motion passed. 
4. Trustee Comments 
None. 
5. Town Attorney Comments 
Attorney Sarah Ross stated that she did not have any comments but asked about the amendment 
to the agenda. 
Mayor Medina stated that the amendment was for a waiver of the North Fork Miners High 
School Homecoming Parade permit. 
 
6. Town Administrator Comments 
Town Administrator Kimberly States stated that she would be attending the One Delta County 
meeting the following day to ensure that the Town of Hotchkiss Master Plan is represented. This 
will be a two-day session. 
7. Public Hearing 
2026 Updated Budget Adoption 
Resolution 2026-09 – Adopting Updated 2026 Budget 
Mayor Medina asked the Board if there were any questions regarding this item. 
Don Dybala asked about the DMEA (Delta-Montrose Electric Association) Handheld Radio 
Grant and why it was included in 2025 but not in 2026. 
Town Clerk Ginger Redden stated that the grant funds were fully expended in 2025; therefore, 
there was a zero balance for 2026. 
Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve Resolution 2026-09, a resolution amending the Town of 
Hotchkiss 2026 Annual Budget; making revised and supplemental appropriations; and providing 
an effective date, seconded by Roger Christian. A voice vote was taken, with all Trustees present 
voting in favor. Motion passed. 
Mayor Medina stated that the Town of Hotchkiss is not the first town to have to make this type 
of correction and will not be the last. She stated that fingers should not be pointed because 
everyone makes mistakes and we are all human. 
8. Action Items 
a. North Fork Miners High School Permit Waiver 
Mayor Medina stated that the waiver would be for the Homecoming Parade to be held on Friday, 
October 2, 2026. The parade is an annual event. 
Town Administrator Kimberly States stated that since the new ordinance was passed, the parade 
permit process has changed; however, there has not been sufficient time to fully implement the 
changes. She stated that Deputy Town Clerk Tessa Denison did a great job developing an 
operating procedure and application that can be put into place. 
Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve the parade permit waiver for the North Fork Miners 
High School Homecoming Parade on October 2, 2026, seconded by Daisie Kirk. A voice vote 
was taken, with all Trustees present voting in favor. Motion passed. 
b. Revised Valley High LLC Marijuana License Renewal from September 10, 
2026, Meeting 
Mayor Medina stated that this was the State’s error. The State was late in sending the renewal to 
the Town, and the error was not initially identified. Marijuana Enforcement corrected the error 
and returned the corrected application. 
Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve the amended Marijuana License Renewal for Valley 
High LLC, seconded by Roger Christian. A voice vote was taken, with all Trustees present 
voting in favor. Motion passed. 
c. Special Event Liquor License – Hotchkiss Elks Lodge 1807 – Nolan Tracy 
Nolan Tracy was not present. This is an annual event for the Hotchkiss Elks Lodge known as the 
Charity Ball. Chief Green stated that he had no concerns. 
Motion: Roger Christian moved to approve the Special Event Liquor License for the Hotchkiss 
Elks Lodge for its annual Charity Ball, to be held October 3, 2026, seconded by Don Dybala. A 
voice vote was taken, with all Trustees present voting in favor. Motion passed. 
d. Agenda Application/Donation Request – North Fork Senior Connections 
Mark Sullivant represented North Fork Senior Connections. He thanked the Board for its 
donation last year and explained the upgrades that had been completed. He stated that the 
organization has a new plan for additional improvements to the building and property. Grant 
opportunities are being explored, and North Fork Senior Connections would like the Board of 
Trustees to become more involved in its activities. The amount of the donation in 2025 was 
$500.00. 
Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve a donation to North Fork Senior Connections in the 
amount of $500.00, seconded by Roger Christian. A voice vote was taken, with all Trustees 
present voting in favor. Motion passed. 
e. Vehicle Use Policy Revisions 
Mayor Medina asked whether the Board had an opportunity to review the revisions and whether 
there were any questions. 
The Board discussed whether the revisions would affect the Public Works Department and the 
need for clarification and updates for the Police Department. It was clarified that Public Works 
has its own section and that these revisions apply only to the Police Department. 
Motion: Daisie Kirk moved to approve the Vehicle Use Policy revisions for the Police 
Department, seconded by Roger Christian. A voice vote was taken, with all Trustees present 
voting in favor. Motion passed. 
f. Ratification of Recension of Fire Ban 
Mayor Medina stated that the Town’s fire ban was rescinded consistent with the County’s action. 
Motion: Roger Christian moved to ratify the rescission of the fire ban, seconded by Don Dybala. 
A voice vote was taken, with all Trustees present voting in favor. Motion passed. 
9. Adjournment 
The meeting adjourned at 6:54 p.m. 
Respectfully submitted, 
Ginger R. Redden 
Town Clerk
