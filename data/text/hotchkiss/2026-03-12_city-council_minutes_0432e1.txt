7. ACTION ITEMS 
Donation Request-North Fork Valley Chamber of Commerce 
Connie Layton from North Fork Valley Chambers was here to request a donation 
of $500.00 to replace the lighted backing inserts that hold the letters in the town 
entrance signs. The project total is $1000.00. No other towns have been asked yet. 
Since the North Fork Valley Chamber uses the signs for all three towns but 
housed in Hotchkiss the discussion was how much should we donate, Custer 
McLeod suggested $300.00 Roger Christian made a motion to donate $300.00 
Custer McLeod seconded. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in 
favor. Motion passed.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR SEARCH UPDATE AND POTENTIAL 
TIMELINE 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister is in the process of obtaining 
proposals from search firms and has received one from a firm she has worked 
with previously. She hopes to obtain additional proposals so the board can 
interview them. 
Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister expressed concern about the timeline 
for hiring a Town Administrator based on DOLA’s statements at the last meeting 
regarding the grant process. It may not be in Hotchkiss’s best interest to wait. She 
will keep the board updated. There is no guarantee the grant will be awarded. The 
board must make the decision to wait for confirmation of the grant before hiring a 
Town Administrator or to move forward with a hiring timeline that aligns with the 
anticipated grant opening. Interim Town Administrator Lauren Gister will ensure 
the board has the information needed to decide, while also avoiding a stall in 
progress and maintaining momentum. 
Randy Thalmann inquired about the cost. At the last meeting, the estimated cost 
was between $12,000.00 and $20,000.00, which was an estimate. The proposal 
received falls within that range. The process is expected to take approximately 
four to six months. Randy Thalmann asked if the town would pay for the search 
firm; it was confirmed that the town would be responsible for this cost, as it 
would not be covered by a grant. 
Roger Christian asked if, if the process extends to July, the town would be 
without a Town Administrator for a period or if Interim Town Administrator 
Lauren Gister’s contract could be extended. She has been asked and is open to
