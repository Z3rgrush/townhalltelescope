

Town of Hotchkiss Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes
                   Town of Hotchkiss Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes
July 9, 2026
1. Call the Meeting to Order
Mayor Patricia Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.
Present: Roger Christian, Jim Jorgensen, Daisie Kirk, Don Dybala, Joel Reed, and Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom.
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance.
2. Public Comment
Mayor Pat Medina read the rules of Public Comment.
Don Foster of 226 N First Street told the Board that he submitted an Agenda Request Application to be on the agenda for July 23, 2026. Don has a proposal to develop a park across from the Elks Lodge where there are now empty lots. Don passed out pictures of the proposed park to the council and will make his presentation on July 23, 2026, at the second Board of Trustees meeting.
3. Consent Agenda
Minutes for June 10, 2026, Special Meeting
Minutes for June 11, 2026
Minutes for June 25, 2026, Second Town Board Meeting
Minutes for June 30, 2026, Emergency Special Board Meeting
Accounts Payable for July 2026
Town Clerk Ginger Redden read out additional bills.
Motion: Sven Edstrom moved to approve the Consent Agenda as presented, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
4. Trustee Comments
Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom joked about how long the meeting that he chaired was while Mayor Patricia Medina was on vacation.
5. Town Attorney Comments
Town Attorney Sarah Ross said that she was still running since she hit the ground. She met with the Court Clerk this afternoon.
6. Town Administrator Comments
Town Administrator Kimberly States agreed with Town Attorney Sarah Ross about running. An all-staff lunch was held today, and the crosswalk project contract has been approved and just needs signatures.
7. Public Hearings
Mayor Patricia Medina read the rules for Public Hearings.
1) Ordinance 2026-04: An Ordinance Establishing the Office of Town Administrator Providing for Appointment, Powers, Duties, and Personnel Authority of the Town Administrator
Staff Presentation: Mayor Patricia Medina said that this Ordinance will give new Town Administrator Kimberly States all the tools that she needs to run the departments.
Applicant Presentation: None.
Public Comment:
Opened at 6:39 p.m.
Tom Wills, 159 E. Orchard, stated that he is happy to see the new Town Administrator position and has been trying to get the Town to hire one for years.
Closed at 6:40 p.m.
Board Deliberation: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom applauded the Council for all the cooperation shown during the hiring process. Mayor Patricia Medina stated that the cooperation shows the entire team and the public that working together makes the town strong.
Motion: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom moved to approve Ordinance 2026-04, an Ordinance of the Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado, amending Chapter II of the Hotchkiss Municipal Code by adding a new Article 2.5 establishing the office of Town Administrator; providing for appointment, powers, duties, and personnel authority of the Town Administrator, seconded by Don Dybala. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
2) Ordinance 2026-03: Wildland-Urban Interface Code
Staff Presentation: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom was curious about the map of the fire risk area in the Town limits. Don Dybala said the map encompassed the whole Town.
Applicant Presentation: None.
Public Comment: Opened and closed at 6:40 p.m. with no comments.
Board Deliberation: None additional.
Motion: Jim Jorgensen moved to approve Ordinance 2026-03, an Ordinance of the Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado, adopting the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code, 2025 Edition, and the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency State Code Map by reference; adding Chapter 10, Article 5 to the Hotchkiss Municipal Code; providing for administration and enforcement; providing a transitional provision; and providing for an effective date, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
8. Action Items
a. Opening Sealed Bids for Public Works Vehicle Ad
Two (2) bids were submitted for the white 2010 Ford XL F-150 pickup that Public Works listed for sale. Mayor Patricia Medina opened the bids:
Scott Abrams: $3,277.00
Deutsch Domestic Water Company: $3,500.00
Motion: Don Dybala moved to accept the bid from Deutsch Domestic Water Company for $3,500.00, seconded by Joel Reed. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
b. Patrick Dunstone Introduction — One Delta County
Patrick Dunstone is the new Executive Director for One Delta County, replacing Greg Pope. Patrick told the Board about One Delta County and their work to bring business to Delta County as well as supporting local businesses. Patrick expressed a desire to build a strong working relationship with the Town of Hotchkiss. Town Administrator Kimberly States will serve as the representative for the Town of Hotchkiss at One Delta County meetings.
c. Special Events Liquor License — North Fork Chapter of the Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation
No representative from the Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation was present. Mayor Pat Medina told the Board that this is the yearly fundraiser for their scholarship program. Chief Scott Green was not present, but Town Clerk Ginger Redden noted she emailed Chief Green the application and he had no concerns.
Motion: Sven Edstrom moved to approve the Special Events Liquor License for the North Fork Chapter of the Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation for August 15, 2026, from 5:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m., seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
d. Special Events Liquor License — Hotchkiss Elks Lodge BPOE #1807 (Austin Hibbard)
There was no representative from the Hotchkiss Elks Lodge in attendance. Town Clerk Ginger Redden informed the Board that she emailed all information to Chief Scott Green, who had no concerns regarding the application. Don Dybala noted that it looked good. The Board briefly discussed the limit on Special Events Liquor Licenses for non-profit organizations (maximum of six per year).
Motion: Jim Jorgensen moved to approve the Special Events Liquor License for the Hotchkiss Elks Lodge for August 6th through August 8th during Fair Week from 10:00 a.m. until 2:00 a.m., seconded by Don Dybala. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
e. Agenda Application Request — Joshua Frye (Smoke and Accessories Shop)
Joshua Frye was not present. There was discussion regarding whether his tax payments were in good standing with the Town. This item was tabled until Joshua Frye can attend.
f. Agenda Application Request — Chuck Pine (524 Willow Drive re: 602 Cottonwood Lane)
Chuck Pine (524 Willow Drive) addressed the Board regarding the neighboring property at 602 Cottonwood Lane. Over the last four years, Mr. Pine has questioned the use of 602 Cottonwood Lane, noting that three sheds are on-site where supplies for Merit Electric are stored and crews start their workday loading trucks. Mr. Pine presented photos indicating commercial use in an R1 Residential zone.
Roger Christian noted he chaired the Planning Commission when temporary use for trucks and equipment was discussed with Jeremiah Weber, though he does not recall full details. Don Dybala noted Jeremiah Weber visited the Planning Commission recently to ask what could be built on the property.
Jeremiah Weber stated his business has not expanded, all trailers are licensed, and nothing is parked behind Mr. Pine’s property line. He noted he employs 15 workers servicing a large area and apologized to Mr. Pine for any disruption. Don Dybala noted sheds under 120 square feet do not require a building permit. Mayor Patricia Medina encouraged both gentlemen to attend the Planning and Zoning Commission meeting on July 22, 2026.
g. Update on Certified VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) Fees
Certified VIN inspection fees are set annually by the State around July 1st. This year the State increased the fee from $54.00 to $55.00.
Motion: Roger Christian moved to approve the fee change from $54.00 to $55.00 per Certified VIN inspection, seconded by Jim Jorgensen. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
h. Resolution 2026-07: Resolution of Signers
Mayor Patricia Medina stated this Resolution is required to add Kimberly States as an authorized signer on Town bank accounts.
Motion: Don Dybala moved to approve Resolution 2026-07, a Resolution of the Board of Trustees authorizing legal signers for the Town of Hotchkiss, seconded by Joel Reed. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
i. Ordinance 2026-05: Franchise Agreement for Delta County Tele-Comm / Elevate (First Reading)
Mayor Patricia Medina explained this agreement is due to the acquisition of Delta County Tele-Comm by Elevate.
Motion: Jim Jorgensen moved to approve Ordinance 2026-05, an Ordinance granting a Franchise by the Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado to Delta County Tele-Comm, Inc. D/B/A DCTC/Elevate, its successors and assigns, to locate, build, construct, acquire, purchase, extend, maintain, and operate into, within, and through the present and future corporate limits of the Town of Hotchkiss, Delta County, Colorado, Town wires, lines, poles, arms, appurtenances, and fixtures for conducting a telephone business and exchange, over, under, along, across, and through any and all streets, other public ways, and places in said Town of Hotchkiss, fixing the terms and conditions thereof; providing for repeal, severability, and an effective date, seconded by Roger Christian. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.   
j. Ordinance 2026-06: Adopting and Amending Regulations Addressing Open Burning, Local Fire Restrictions, and Fireworks (Emergency Adoption)
Due to drought conditions, this emergency Ordinance will help mitigate local fire risks.
Motion: Jim Jorgensen moved to approve Ordinance 2026-06, an emergency Ordinance of the Board of Trustees of the Town of Hotchkiss, Colorado, adopting a new Chapter 20 (“Fire Restrictions”) of the Hotchkiss Municipal Code; establishing fire restriction stages; regulating open burning, ignition sources, and fireworks during periods of high fire danger; authorizing the Mayor to implement emergency Stage I and Stage II Fire Restrictions subject to ratification by the Board of Trustees; and providing penalties for violations, seconded by Sven Edstrom. A verbal vote was taken with all Trustees voting in favor. Motion passed.
9. Adjournment
The meeting adjourned at 7:26 p.m.
