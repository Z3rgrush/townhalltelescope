 
Town of Hotchkiss Board of Trustees Meeting 
Minutes 
October 22, 2026 
1. Call to Order 
Mayor Patricia Medina called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Present: Trustees Don Dybala, Jim Jorgensen, Joel Reed, and Daisie Kirk. 
Arrived at 6:52 p.m.: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom. 
Absent: Trustee Roger Christian. 
Those present recited the Pledge of Allegiance. 
2. Public Comment 
None. 
3. Trustee Comments 
None. 
4. Town Attorney Comments 
None. 
5. Town Administrator Comments 
Town Administrator Kimberly States provided background information regarding the revised 
memorandum for the updated 2026 budget. 
States explained that although a quorum was present at the previous meeting, four affirmative 
votes were required under state statute to approve the updated budget. The delay allowed the 
Clerk's Office additional time to review the 2026 budget and identify further corrections. 
6. Public Hearing 
2026 Updated Budget Adoption Ratification 
Mayor Patricia Medina read the rules for the public hearing. 
Staff Presentation 
Town Administrator Kimberly States informed the Board that staff had conducted another 
review of the 2026 budget. Deputy Town Clerk Tessa Denison worked diligently on the 
spreadsheet and identified additional errors requiring correction. Several of the primary errors 
were found in a spreadsheet used to calculate salaries. 
Public Comment 
None. 
Board Deliberation 
Trustee Don Dybala commented that considerable work had gone into updating the budget. 
Trustee Jim Jorgensen commended staff for the work completed to bring the budget to its current 
form. 
Trustee Don Dybala expressed appreciation that the updated information was easy to review and 
that the corrections were clearly explained. 
Trustee Joel Reed clarified that the corrections being made should not be confused with mistakes 
being made at the present time. 
Motion: Trustee Don Dybala moved to approve the updated 2026 budget, including the revisions 
and Resolution No. 2026-10, a resolution amending the Town of Hotchkiss 2026 annual budget, 
making revised and supplemental appropriations, and providing an effective date. Trustee Joel 
Reed seconded the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
7. Action Items 
a. Bill Approval for ACH Delivery to the Water Plant 
Mayor Patricia Medina asked for the amount of the invoice. 
Town Clerk Ginger Redden informed the Board that the invoice for the ACH delivery to the 
Water Plant totaled $53,156.64. 
Mayor Medina noted that this expenditure was one of the items she and Public Works Director 
Marvin Jackson had discussed during the previous year's budget process. 
The Board discussed the need for the delivery to the Water Plant. 
Motion: Trustee Jim Jorgensen moved to approve payment of the invoice in the amount of 
$53,156.64. Trustee Joel Reed seconded the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
b. Proclamation for Crash Responders Week, November 2–6, 2026 
Mayor Patricia Medina expressed appreciation for first responders and stated that the 
proclamation was an appropriate way to recognize their service. 
Mayor Medina read the proclamation aloud. 
Motion: Trustee Don Dybala moved to approve the proclamation designating November 2–6, 
2026, as Crash Responders Week. Trustee Joel Reed seconded the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
c. Ratification of Town Administrator Duties 
Town Administrator Kimberly States informed the Board that Town Clerk Ginger Redden 
administered her oath of office on October 16, 2026. 
States explained that she had been appointed Town Administrator on June 25, 2026. Because the 
oath was administered more than ten days after her appointment, ratification was requested to 
address potential concerns regarding the validity of actions taken during the period between the 
required oath date and the date the oath was administered. 
The Board was asked to ratify her appointment and all actions taken in her capacity as Town 
Administrator through the date of the meeting. 
Motion: Trustee Joel Reed moved to ratify the appointment of Kimberly States as Town 
Administrator of the Town of Hotchkiss and to ratify all actions taken by Kimberly States as 
Town Administrator since her initial appointment to that office. Trustee Don Dybala seconded 
the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
d. Cancellation of Flock Camera Contracts 
Mayor Patricia Medina acknowledged that the potential cancellation of the Flock camera 
contracts had been anticipated. She emphasized that the cameras were originally implemented 
for public safety purposes. 
Trustee Jim Jorgensen stated that the cameras had served a useful purpose. 
Mayor Medina noted that Police Chief Scott Green had conducted extensive research before 
presenting the camera proposal to the Board. She encouraged residents to attend Board meetings 
and express their concerns regarding agenda items before decisions are made. 
Trustee Jorgensen stated that the original arrangement had been beneficial. He emphasized that 
the Board researches proposals and makes decisions based on the best information available. He 
also expressed concerns regarding the use and distribution of information collected by the 
cameras. 
Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom arrived at 6:52 p.m. 
Town Attorney Sarah Ross informed the Board that the Town had two contracts with Flock. 
The first contract, for a Flex Bundle trailer kit, expires December 11, 2026. 
The second contract, covering three cameras located throughout Town, expires December 18, 
2028. 
Attorney Ross explained that cancellation could result in a fee of $500 per Flock hardware unit. 
She further stated that the remaining financial obligation under the outstanding contract was less 
than $12,000. 
Motion: Trustee Don Dybala moved to approve cancellation of the Flock camera contracts. 
Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom seconded the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
e. Executive Session 
The Board considered entering executive session for the following matters: 
Confidential Legal Advice and Investigative Matters 
• Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(b), for a conference with the Town Attorney and 
outside legal counsel to receive confidential legal advice on specific legal questions 
related to investigations. 
• Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(c), for discussion of matters required to be kept 
confidential by federal or state law, specifically protected investigative records or 
documents. 
• Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-6-402(4)(d), for discussion of specialized details of security 
arrangements or investigations. 
Motion: Mayor Pro Tem Sven Edstrom moved to enter executive session for the purposes stated 
above. Trustee Don Dybala seconded the motion. 
A verbal vote was taken. All Trustees present voted in favor. Motion carried. 
The Board entered executive session at 7:01 p.m.
