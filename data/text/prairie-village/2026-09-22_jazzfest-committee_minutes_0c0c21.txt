Committee Chair's Report 
Prairie Village Jazz Fest 2026 was held September 12. Three of the five acts were able to perform 
although there was rain during the second act, Nick Hmeljak’s Open Channel. Lightning forced the initial 
postponement of the third act, Stephen Martin Quartet, subsequent heavy rain and the departure of 
most attendees caused the Committee to recommend that the event be halted at approximately 6:45 
pm. Net entry gate receipts totaled $3,305.50. Estimated costs to produce JazzFest 2026 are $51,500. 
Final event costs are pending receipt of several final invoices. 
Mike Polich communicated that he would be stepping down from the Committee but was happy to 
continue to help during set up. Thank you, Mike for 15 years of service! 
 
Talent  
Because the final two acts, Stephen Martin Quartet and The Freedom Affair, were unable to perform, 
the Committee suggests that both acts be invited to perform in their same lineup spots at the 2027 
JazzFest, pending their availability. 
 
F&B  
Food and beverage vendors were disappointed by the rain-shortened event. The lineup of food trucks 
was deemed excellent and the same trucks have been invited to participate in 2027. 
Wild Spirits, selling Big Grove Brewery products and other offerings reported sales net of tips and taxes 
for purposes of remitting a sales-based percentage fee to the Committee.  
The Committee had requested that Wild Spirits redeem drink tickets issued by the Committee to invited 
guests and volunteers.  
Wild Spirits reported that the net cost to provide beverages for those redeemed tickets was roughly 
equal to the sales based percentage fee. The Committee called it even and thanked Wild Spirits for their 
participation. 
 
Infrastructure 
The rented Polaris used for setup and breakdown is recommended for next year. 
Renting only chairs from AAA saved approximately $1500 in rental costs, however having to go to AAA 
to pick up rented folding chairs increased both the cost of the U-Haul by a day, plus time and effort 
spent in the loading, unloading and transport. 
 
Hospitality 
Infrastructure costs and volunteer efforts were reduced by eliminating dedicated seating. If the event 
had been in better weather and been able to finish normally it is possible that the cost of hospitality in 
the form of beverage tickets would have been higher than budgeted. 
We need to restrict the riding of bicycles and scooters in the park once the entrance gates open. 
 
Marketing  
Some issues with JoCo Post, but it appears we received what we needed. 
With the elimination of lineup fans we need to create large posters for the event site with the musical 
lineup. 
 
Merchandise Tent 
Signed Mission Road Banner awarded to raffle winner 
 
The next meeting will be held in January 2027. 
 
The meeting concluded at 6:25 p.m.
