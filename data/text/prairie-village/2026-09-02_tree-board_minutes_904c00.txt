B. ArbNet Accreditation 
• As of September 2, 2026, Prairie Village was approved as a Level I accredited arboretum through ArbNet. The 
accreditation is effective through 2031, when reapplication will be required. 
• The Google Drive includes a running list of Arbor Day honoree trees and State Champion Trees in Prairie Village 
that may be used in media publications.

C. Public Park Tree Map 
• The public park tree map is now available on the City website. Testing during the meeting showed that website 
navigation to the map is cumbersome. 
• The Board will continue stress testing the map, refine its symbology, and pursue a more intuitive location for the 
map on the website. 
• The Board discussed conducting a tree survey to gather tree-value data for entry into the enterprise asset 
management system, allowing the information to be presented to the public. 
D. Tree Board T-Shirts 
• For the proposed tree design, the Board will explore making the leaves larger. 
• The Board will revisit T-shirts at the November meeting, confirm continuing Board membership, and then 
proceed with production as appropriate. 
E. AI Subcommittee and Data Roadmap 
Dashboard: Tree Board AI Dashboard (Google Drive) 
• Mike Konovalske proposed a roadmap discussion during the break between the November and February 
meetings. The session will document data-analysis progress, determine the Board’s next major priorities, and 
develop concise data points that Council members can share during Council meetings. 
• A Tree Board Data Analysis Meeting is scheduled for December 10, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. to develop the roadmap. 
3. New Business 
A. Fall Street and Park Tree Plantings 
• A total of 159 trees are planned for fall planting, likely from late November into early December. 
• The Board discussed the planned species and the importance of maintaining a diverse planting mix.
