III. Land Acknowledgement 
We recognize that the City of Prairie Village sits on the ancestral land of the Kickapoo, Ocheti 
Sakowin, Osage and Kansa tribes. This land is of great importance as these communities are still 
living and breathing. Consistent with our values of community and inclusion, we have a 
responsibility to acknowledge and honor these tribes.  
 A. As we begin this event, we'd like to acknowledge the stewards of this land, the Kanza, Osage, 
Kickapoo and Oceti Sakowin Nations. Sovereign nations whose traditional lands, stories, and 
ancestors live within these lands.  
 
We acknowledge their unjust removal from their lands, their continued resilience and 
endurance.  
We understand we are temporary residents and promise to treat the land and the 
people that live on it with kindness and respect.
