 5.A. Sustainability Coordinator Introduction and Update 
Elyse Hottel introduced herself and gave highlights of her background along with where 
she's at working on projects she's been tasked with as TOSV's new part time 
Sustainability Coordinator.  Her focus is defined by the council's priorities of Wildfire, 
Construction & Demolition and Composting.  There is also a newly awarded CORE 
Impact Accelerator Grant coordinated by City of Aspen (as a grantee), focused on 
revising energy codes in the built environment for the numerous grantee jurisdictions 
that Snowmass is part of. The rest of the EAB initiatives from last year's 
recommendation matrix were mentioned as future topics to keep on her radar. 
In order to help the council make some decisions at the August 3 Town Council work 
session on how to motivate homeowners to undertake wildfire mitigation efforts, it was 
suggested Core, Holy Cross and SWSD could give some examples of the types of 
rebate programs that have been successful to Elyse by July 24 to include in the packet.

8. Non-Agenda Items 
Council Member Fridstein updated the board on passage of an Ordinance allowing only 
electric leaf blowers in the summer months, initially proposed as a ballot measure by 
resident John Wilkenson.  The Ordinance was written based on comments and 
concerns shared by local residents and commercial landscaping companies with the 
council.
