Recap of the Wildfire Mitigation Rebate Program item for discussion from the Town 
Council meeting on 08-03-26. 
This pilot program for 2026 focuses on rebates for the 0-5 ft zone from a permanent 
building structure with $30,000 allocated by the town for $1000 rebates for individual 
homeowners.  Since Roaring Fork Fire Rescue doesn't have the capacity to perform fire 
risk assessments the program will be based on self verification and checklists of tasks 
to be completed.  The rebate forms will be available online by the end of August and 
RFV Wildfire Collaborative will administer the program.  Homeowners who are awarded 
a rebate will likely be eligible to apply for up to three rebates if /when the program has 
future allocations.

Housing Department Comments:  
Unit Mix  
The Housing Department appreciates the 
applicant’s thoughtful approach to providing a 
robust amount of housing for the employees that 
will be servicing the new development. As the 
applicant team continues to refine the project, it 
might be helpful to consider the types of units 
that are in highest demand within the Town’s 
workforce housing program. As shown in Figure 
1, one-bedroom units are in highest demand. 
Most applicants who requested a studio also 
requested a one bedroom , so some are double 
counted between the two-unit types. (Both unit 
types are often selected to increase chances of 
getting a unit sooner .) Two-bedroom units are 
also in high demand as folks with children or 
elderly parents seek extra space . While studios 
help increase overall density, they are generally 
less desirable  for tenants. Many folks live i n 
studio units request transfers to one -bedrooms on a regular basis as they quickly outgrow the 
limited space provided by a studio. The highest turnover of units we experience in our inventory

housing that residents enjoy and stay in for many years. Housing intended for seasonal or part-

program. For this reason, the Housing Department recommends minimizing the number of studio 
units provided and instead focus on one-bedroom and two-bedroom units.

Introduction
Project Overview 
The long-term vision and character of Snowmass Village 
as a destination mountain community is at the forefront 
of the guiding documents for the Town of Snowmass 
Village, including the Comprehensive Plan, West Village 
CPA, and Destination Tourism Plan. We have used these 
documents to guide the proposal in addition to the 
feedback we have received from Town Council, the 
Planning Commission, and Town staff during the Pre-
Sketch Plan application review process. As outlined in 
the following application, the development intends to 
create experiences for both the local community and 
guests, creating an environment that is fun and inviting, 
a luxury destination for family friendly travel, a place for 
community to gather and connect, and where guests 
want to return year after year. 
Our goal is to have a completed redevelopment and 
grand opening in 2031. The redevelopment schedule is 
intended to revitalize these iconic properties to make 
the continued operation economically sound, while 
minimizing the impact on the surrounding community 
and commercial businesses. Understanding that 
the construction management of a redevelopment 
of this scale is vitality important, the design and 
development teams are committed to coordination 
and communication with the Town to limit the overall 
demolition and construction timeline and to lessen the 
impact on surrounding community uses. 
Project Proposal at a Glance
The project includes three different parcels, which 
are proposed to be combined in order to facilitate 
redevelopment. The parcels are bounded by Carriage 
Way to the west, the ski hill to the east, Elbert Lane 
and the mall to the North, and Gallun Lane to the 
south. These roads provide access to the site and to

What Makes a Resort Great?
There are specific characteristics of a world class resort 
– above all, the ability to create memorable and long-
lasting experiences, which remains a vital goal of the 
redevelopment and operator of the resort. Experiences 
at the Ritz-Carlton Snowmass will be influenced by a 
number of factors, all contributing to the combined 
vision that experience is influenced by location, comfort 
and amenities, service, quality food and beverage, and a 
commitment to the representation of the environment 
it is located within. 
In order to meet guest and community expectations 
through the redevelopment, the following 
characteristics are incorporated into the Sketch Plan 
application: 
 + The redevelopment will be connected to the 
surrounding physical and cultural environment, 
establishing the destination as a portal to world 
class skiing and summer recreation, embracing 
the mountain community character of the Town of 
Snowmass Village. 
 + The stepping of the redevelopment with grade will 
maximize views from the various lodge and amenity 
offerings, capturing the natural beauty of the 
surrounding environment. 
 + The hotel will include a mix of community facing 
publicly accessible amenities, in addition to 
private amenities reserved for hotel guests and 
fractional unit owners. Additional space dedicated 
to commercial development will include food and 
beverage outlets, retail spaces, and conference 
amenities, all unified by a high level-of-service. 
 + The redevelopment is family friendly, and supports 
Snowmass’ community character that is a fun, 
inviting, and memorable place to live and recreate, 
encouraging guests to continue to return year after 
year. 
 + The redevelopment will bring a comprehensive 
update to infrastructure and utilities, addressing 
ongoing maintenance and operational concerns, 
while encouraging energy efficiency and improved 
standards for the consumption of natural resources. 
 + The project will activate the ski frontage, 
encouraging pedestrian access and community 
engagement in a four-season environment, 
activating the West Village year-round. 
 + Offer modern and dynamic spaces increasing group, 
event and entertainment business.
