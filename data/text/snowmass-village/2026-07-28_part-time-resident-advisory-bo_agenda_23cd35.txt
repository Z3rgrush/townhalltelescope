4. Administrative Updates & Discussion 
 4.A. Summer PTRAB Community Meeting 
The board discussed logistics for the upcoming Summer PTRAB Community Meeting, 
focusing on venue selection, date, timing, and speaker/format preferences. 
 
Venue. Town Clerk Boucher confirmed that The Collective at Base Village had no 
availability in August. The board discussed several alternatives. The Limelight Hotel 
was identified as the top preference, given its full-service capabilities, AV infrastructure, 
outdoor patio, and the town's prior positive experience with the venue. Anderson Ranch 
Arts Center was named the second choice, for its atmosphere and AV capability. The 
Stew Pot's upstairs space was noted as a third option, though its lack of built-in AV was 
a drawback. Town Hall was discussed but set aside as a venue given the complications

involved in obtaining a special liquor license. Additional venues suggested included Il 
Poggio's lower-level workspace ("Romero's workspace") and the Snowmass Club. Elk 
Camp was raised but deemed cost-prohibitive. The approximate budget was confirmed 
at $2,000–$2,500, covering light apps and a drink ticket per attendee for approximately 
25-30 guests. 
 
Date and Time. The board settled on Tuesday, August 4 as the target date, with a start 
time of 5 PM. Wednesdays were ruled out due to conflict with the Planning Commission 
schedule and the weekly rodeo. Thursdays were eliminated due to the Thursday night 
concert series. Board Member Jeff Steinberg noted he would be unavailable on August 
4, but other members tentatively confirmed availability. Staff was directed to check 
venue availability for that date first, with later August dates considered less desirable 
due to families returning for the school year. 
 
Speakers and Format. The board agreed on a maximum of 4 or 5 speakers. Confirmed 
speaker areas of interest included: the Mayor, wildfire/fire safety (Angie Devlin or the 
Fire Marshal), and a Pitkin County Commissioner representative — with Greg 
Poschman specifically suggested given his knowledge and prior participation in PTRAB 
events. Tourism and public works were noted as additional possibilities. Board Member 
Hess also suggested Christy Mahon of ACES, who has climbed and skied all Colorado 
fourteeners, as a speaker to highlight the outdoor character of the community. Priority 
for topics/speakers: Airport, Wildfire, Water, Town Projects and Events. 
 
Regarding format, members proposed replacing open-ended speaker presentations 
with a moderated panel format, wherein a moderator poses pre-prepared questions to 
draw out each panelist's expertise before opening the floor to audience questions. 
 Assistant Town Manager LeBlanc agreed to draft questions in coordination with the 
chair.  
 
Next Steps. Staff will contact the Limelight, Anderson Ranch, and the Stew Pot to check 
availability for August 4, and will report back to the board. The chair will also confer with 
the Mayor regarding her preferred role and begin drafting panel questions with staff. 
5. Non-Agenda Items & Members Concerns or Ideas 
Board members commended the town and its contractor for the minimal disruption 
caused by ongoing roadway construction around the roundabout, noting that lane 
closures had been brief and traffic flow well-managed throughout the project. 
Assistant Town Manager LeBlanc provided a brief update on the town's wildfire 
mitigation program, noting that a contractor bidding process had launched and that 
proposals would close on July 2. Staff anticipated work beginning by the end of July. 
Council Member Fridstein requested that maps showing the areas to be addressed be 
shared with part-time owners and the board. Staff confirmed that maps are available 
and would be distributed.
