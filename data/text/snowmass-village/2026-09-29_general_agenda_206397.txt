 4.A. PTRAB recommendations for the Transit Center Project 
 
 4.A.a. Town Council Agenda Item from 9/14/26 - Mall Transit Center Roundtable 
recap and next steps 
Transportation Director Sam Guarino presented an overview of the proposed Mall 
Transit Center. He outlined the primary rationale for consolidating the regional (RFTA) 
and local shuttle systems into a single facility: operational efficiency, improved transfer 
connections, enhanced passenger safety, a more cohesive arrival experience, and 
increased pedestrian traffic benefiting mall businesses. Current project estimates range 
Page 2 of 5
from approximately $42M to $60M depending on scope and parking provisions. 
 
Board members broadly agreed that improvements to existing transit infrastructure are 
necessary, but expressed significant concern over the scale and cost of a grand 
consolidated facility. Key concerns raised included: 
• Cost and funding: Members questioned whether sufficient grant and partner 
funding could be secured, noting that federal funding is unlikely before 2028 and 
construction would not begin before 2031–2035 at the earliest. 
• Private contribution: Members asked whether primary beneficiaries — Ski Co., 
mall owners, and RFTA — would contribute meaningfully to construction costs. 
• Scale and design: Several members felt the current renderings produced an 
unfavorable entrance to the mall and that the project may be premature given 
pending redevelopment of the Viewline/Ritz Carlton and potential mall 
transformation. 
• Alternatives: Members noted that targeted, modest improvements to the two 
existing facilities — estimated at $3–5M — could address near-term operational 
deficiencies without committing to a large capital project. 
 
The Board reached a general consensus that some form of improvement is needed, but 
remained concerned about project scale and the distribution of financial responsibility. 
The Board agreed to schedule a special meeting on Tuesday, September 29, 2026 at 
3:30 PM to continue deliberations and work toward a formal written recommendation to 
Town Council. Members were asked to submit outstanding questions to the Chair and 
Assistant Town Manager in advance. 
 4.B. PTRAB recommendations for the Ritz Carlton Project 
 
 4.B.a. Ritz Carlton Sketch Plan 
Community Development Director Michelle Bonfils Thibeault and Senior Planner Carlton 
Henry provided an overview of the Viewline redevelopment sketch plan. Key elements 
include: replacement of the existing hotel with a multi-building configuration designed to 
create view corridors to the ski slope; reconstruction of the conference center at a 
similar or slightly reduced height; redevelopment of the Wildwood area with 
approximately 125 workforce housing units (mix to be determined); and construction of 
a 252-space subgrade parking garage. The application is at Step 1 of a 3-step process, 
with no architectural renderings yet required at this stage. 
 
Notable discussion points included the unit mix for workforce housing — with Town 
Council having expressed a preference for multi-bedroom units to support long-term 
residents over seasonal workers — and the scale of buildings relative to the existing 
structure, which is generally comparable with only minor height increases in limited 
areas. 
 
The Board consensus was to defer any formal written recommendation on the Ritz

be more thoroughly assessed.
