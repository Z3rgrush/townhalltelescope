Presentation on pending Land Use Actions:   
 A26 -0021: The applicant is requesting a CUP to establish an agritourism business with a commercial 
campground consisting of four year-round cabins and six seasonal bell tents. 
ABE Recommendation: ABE appreciates the additional information the applicants have provided 
and appreciates their willingness to make changes necessary to ensure environmental constraints 
and wildlife issues are appropriately addressed.  However, ABE is concerned that an over all vision 
for the project has not been provided that enables us to effectively evaluate this application and PZ-
2026-0125.  ABE would ask to have the applicants attend an ABE meeting to further discuss the 
applications.   
ABE recommends that CPW be consulted regarding this application to help identify wildlife species 
that will be compatible with the existing wildlife and to identify concerns/conflicts with wildlife.   
The CODEX database was utilized to further identify further wildlife and wetlands areas issues. Based 
on the data, this area is part of the migration corridors and concentration areas for Big Horn Sheep, 
Mule deer, Elk and Moose.  The area is also identified as a production area for Big Horn Sheep and 
breeding ground for Golden Eagles.  Every effort should be made to minimize impact to these 
species.  Wildlife friendly fencing should be considered.   It is further recommended that 
identification/recognition of predatory animals e.g. bear, mountain lion be made and precautions 
(fencing – bear proof containers, etc.) be considered if the cabins and seasonal bell tents are 
constructed.   
Other areas of discussion included potential concerns with grazing cattle as identified by the 
applicant.  The applicant should be aware of paddock grazing requirements should they desire to 
graze cattle on the property; a grass study may be required.  
ABE understands the wells currently on the property may not be sufficient for the needs at build-
out.  A water augmentation plan may be required. ABE also understands that Geneva Creek is 
considered a “dead creek”.  Every provision should be made to protect human life and wildlife from 
pollutants. 
 
 PZ 2026 -0125: The applicant is requesting setback variances to roads and wetlands to build their 
agritourism business with 4 year-round cabins and 6 seasonal bell tents. 
ABE Recommendation: After reviewing this application ABE recognizes the physical conditions that 
constrain the buildable area on the 161.26 acres of the property.  Every effort should be taken to 
maintain setbacks that comply with those established by Park County from wetlands, riparian 
areas and roadways.  
The applicant should/may be able to reduce the number of sites requested or find sites for the 4 
low impact agricultural housing units and the 3 bell tent pads that will not encroach into the 50’ 
wetlands setback areas.  Should consideration be given to granting the variance as requested or 
reducing the requirements from structures/bell tent sites, ABE believes that reorienting the 
building footprints would enable greater distances from wetlands and riparian areas.  
Consideration could be given to locating the access roads closest to the wetlands and the building 
sited & bell tent pads closer to the roadway. An erosion plan may be necessary to prevent 
degradation of the areas closest to the wetlands-river areas.  
ABE recommends that CPW be consulted regarding this application to help identify wildlife 
species that will be compatible with the existing wildlife and to identify concerns/conflicts with 
wildlife.

Based on the data, this area is part of the migration corridors and concentration areas for Big 
Horn Sheep, Mule deer, Elk and Moose.  The area is also identified as a production area for Big 
Horn Sheep and breeding ground for Golden Eagles.  Every effort should be made to minimize

PZ 2026-0175: The applicant is applying for a PUD Amendment to consolidate their PUD lot with an 
adjacent lot and shift some of their original plans there. The original PUD is granted for 49355 Hwy 
285, Grant and the applicant wants to combine it with 373 County Rd 62. 
ABE Recommendation: ABE has no concerns with the lot consolidation.  However, we request 
more information regarding the application. Please provide a topographical map of the tract. 
Identification of wetland or riparian areas need to be included.  
ABE recommends that CPW be consulted regarding this application to help identify wildlife 
species that will be compatible with the existing wildlife and to identify concerns/conflicts with 
wildlife.   
The CODEX database was utilized to further identify further wildlife and wetlands areas issues. 
Based on the data, this area is part of the migration corridors and concentration areas for Big 
Horn Sheep, Mule deer, Elk and Moose.  The area is also identified as a production area for Big 
Horn Sheep and breeding ground for Golden Eagles.  Every effort should be made to minimize 
impact to these species.  Wildlife friendly fencing should be considered.   It is further 
recommended that identification/recognition of predatory animals e.g. bear, mountain lion be 
made and precautions (fencing – bear proof containers, etc.) be considered if the cabins and 
seasonal bell tents are constructed.  
Additionally, a note on the plat states that the 25’ setback to Geneva Creek will be maintained.  
County setbacks require 50’ from structures to wetlands.  The setback requirements should be 
met.  
Other areas of concern include septic system setback from Geneva Creek and wetland areas need 
to be identified. OWTS system design may/should be required for the multiple A frame cabins 
being considered. Water augmentation plan should also be considered.  
 
PZ 2026-0173: The applicant is requesting a CUP to establish a low-volume retreat facility with 
camping on the 120-acre, agriculturally zoned parcel addressed as 265 Antelope Lane, Guffey. 
ABE Recommendation: Should this application be approved, a few stipulations should be required. 
The actual number of guests allowed at any given time should be identified.  The number of 
times per year that this venue is open for guests should be stipulated.  It appears that the venue 
is responsible for the food.  If so, approval/licensing from CDPHE may be required.  A basic 
wildlife awareness/encounter plan should be required.  A concern was voiced that the guests may 
not be willing to walk to the house to use the bathroom facilities at night.  Other restroom 
provisions may be necessary.  It should also be noted that CODEX identifies the area as a 
Mountain Lion and Bear conflict area as well as a Golden Eagle breeding area.  
 
PZ 2026-0179: The applicant is requesting a Minor Subdivision to split their 40-acre residentially 
zoned parcel into five 8-acre lots. The property is addressed as 1525 Venture Rd, Fairplay. 
ABE Recommendation: Ms. Migliorato recused herself from participating in the discussion.  All 
provisions identified in the Septic Suitability Report provided by 285 Engineering should be 
followed. All setback requirements from structures and wetlands should be followed.  
The wetland areas on the properties may be glacial tarns.  The CODEX Database indicated that 
Southern Rocky Mountain Boreal Toads have been identified in the glacial tarns.  This species is 
endangered, and all provisions should be made to preserve and protect the Boreal Toad and its 
habitat.   The database also identified the area as breeding range for Golden Eagles.  The Golden 
Eagles are Federally Protected, and all provisions should be made to preserve and protect the 
Golder Eagles.
