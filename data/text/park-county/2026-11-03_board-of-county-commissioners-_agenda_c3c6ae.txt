WORK SESSION CANCELLED FOR ELECTION DAY
The purpose of a work session is to provide for a free and open dialogue.  Discussions are not 
limited to those items being on the agenda. Work sessions provide the Commissioners an 
opportunity to discuss County business in a public forum as required. Public comment is not a 
guaranteed part of the Work Session. Comments will be allowed at the discretion of the Board at 
the end of the Work Session if time allows. NOTE: This WORK SESSION agenda may be 
modified with items either being added or deleted.  Please check website www.parkcountyco.gov 
for most updated agendas.  If you need further information, please contact the BOCC (Board of 
County Commissioners) office at county.administration@parkcountyco.gov or call 719-836-4201.
