6. Consideration of action items regarding the Seven Hills Lake Stormwater 
Improvement Project 
  
 
 
  
a. Resolution amending the fiscal year 2026-2030 Capital Improvement 
Program to combine the Seven Hills Lake Stormwater Improvements 
Project, Phase 1 and the Seven Hills Lake Stormwater Improvements 
Project, Phase 2 into the Seven Hills Lake Stormwater Improvements 
Project 
    Resolution 2026-055 
 
  b. Bid award to VF Anderson Builders 
    Approved 
    An August 2023 stormwater system study recommended pipe 
improvements throughout the Seven Hills Lake area with an estimated 
cost totaling $6.6 million. Due to the project's size, the project was split 
into two phases for bidding. VF Anderson Builders ("VFA") submitted the 
lowest bid for both phases and the combined bid was below the 
engineer’s estimate and within the total combined project budget. City 
staff recommends amending the fiscal year 2026-2030 Capital 
Improvement Program to combine the projects, simplifying financial 
tracking, project management, and construction inspection. The total 
budget of the two projects combined would remain unchanged. VFA bid 
$5,694,195.39 to construct the entire project. 
      
 
   
7. Consideration of action items regarding the 108th Street & Renner Boulevard 
Southbound Left-Turn Lane Project 
  
 
 
  
a. Resolution amending the fiscal year 2026-2030 Capital Improvement 
Program to add the 108th Street & Renner Boulevard Southbound Left-
Turn Lane Project 
    Resolution 2026-056 
 
  b. Approval of a construction contract with ARCO National Construction - KC, Inc. and waiving the sealed bid process 
    Approved 
    The City plans to reconstruct a southbound left-turn lane on Renner 
Boulevard at 108th Street to align with required public improvements 
associated with Lenexa Logistics Centre North Lot 9. The project needs 
to be added to the fiscal year 2026–2030 Capital Improvement Program. 
City staff recommends ARCO National Construction - KC, Inc. construct 
the project for $311,293.
