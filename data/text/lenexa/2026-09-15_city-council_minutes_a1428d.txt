4. Approval of a cooperative agreement with Johnson County, Shawnee, 
Olathe, and Overland Park for the distribution of funds under the Edward 
Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program 
  Approved 
  The grant is designed to help communities reduce local crime and violence, 
providing funding for projects, training, staff, equipment, supplies, support, 
information systems for criminal justice, and research activities. The Lenexa 
Police Department will receive $12,722 from this grant. 
    
 
   
5. Resolution amending the Volumetric Rate Calculation Form used in 
determining the fee paid for natural gas transported within the city in 
accordance with natural gas franchise ordinances 
  Resolution 2026-048 
  Franchise ordinances with natural gas providers allow the City to receive 
compensation based on the volume of natural gas transported within the city. 
These ordinances require the Volumetric Rate Calculation Form to be 
updated annually. Adoption of this resolution will allow the City to continue 
collecting this fee in 2027. 
    
 
   
6. Resolution authorizing the assignment and assumption of Resolution of 
Intent 2026-044 and the associated payment in lieu of taxes agreement 
(Lenexa Logistics Centre North - Building 8) 
  Resolution 2026-049

Project to $1.7 million 
    Resolution 2026-051

    Approved 
 
  c. Approval of an agreement with Crouch Recreation for the purchase and installation of new playground equipment for a total of $596,751 
    Approved

    Approved 
    Updated cost estimates for the Central Green Playground Project,

well as the purchase of playground and fitness equipment, increased

construct the project for $704,152.50 and approving agreements with 
Crouch Recreation and Greenfields Outdoor Fitness to purchase the 
playground and fitness equipment for $596,751.00 and $188,998.00,
