DATE:  September 9, 2026 
 
TO:  Parks and Recreation Advisory Board 
 
FROM: Logan Wagler, Parks and Recreation Director 
 
RE:   Adoption of a Tree and Plaque 
 
 
The Parks and Recreation Department has been offered the adoption of a tree at 
Black Hoof Park with a plaque. 
 
Mrs. Mari Schieber wishes to adopt a Redbud tree at Black Hoof Park in honor of 
her granddaughter, Lily. The selected tree overlooks the lake, where lily pads 
cover a portion of the water, serving as a meaningful reminder of Lily. The family 
feels the Redbud tree's beautiful spring blooms symbolize hope and renewal, 
making it a fitting tribute to Lily and a special place for Mrs. Schieber and her 
family to reflect and remember her. 
 
The total value of the adoption is $350.00, which includes the installation of the 
honey bronze plaque by Parks staff.  
 
The attached picture shows the location of the tree at Black Hoof Park.  
 
As per policy, requested wording for the plaque is: 
 
In Loving Memory of 
Lily Emerson Schieber 
Our Little Angel 
Forever Loved, Forever Missed 
 
I recommend acceptance of this adoption. Please contact me with questions or 
comments. Thank you. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
DATE:  September 9, 2026 
 
TO:  Parks and Recreation Advisory Board 
 
FROM: Logan Wagler, Parks and Recreation Director 
 
RE:  Donation of Bench and Adoption of Tree with Plaque 
 
 
The Parks and Recreation Department has been offered the donation of a bench 
and adoption of tree at Sar-Ko-Par Trails Park with plaques for each. 
 
This request is from Donna Keele to donate a new memorial bench and adopt a 
tree at Sar-Ko-Par Trails Park in honor of her son, who was a victim of a bicycle 
accident. Mrs. Keele selected Sar-Ko-Par Park as a place to reflect and 
remember the family picnics and cherished memories they shared together. The 
memorial bench is proposed to be located beneath the adopted tree along the 
trail, overlooking the woodlands and creek near 87th Street.  
 
The total value of the donation is $1,650, which includes the installation by the 
Parks Department of the bench, concrete pad, and two honey bronze plaques.  
 
The attached picture shows the bench location at Sar-Ko-Park Trails Park. 
 
As per policy, requested wording for the plaque is: 
 
Benjamin Ray O’Toole 
In Our Hearts Always 
 
I recommend acceptance of this donation. Thank you.
