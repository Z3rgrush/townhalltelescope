     B.   Programs/Project Updates 
1. Public Art

b. Justice Center 
Maura Cluthe is working on the mural at her studio. We will be 
coordinating with her this fall on installation details. 
c. Ad Astra 
The park furniture made from recycled wind turbines did not arrive, and 
the company we were to purchase the pieces from is non-responsive. 
Staff recommends that the board move on. Now that the project is 
completed and Splish has been installed the group acknowledged that 
additional art may not be necessary at this location at this time.  
 
2.  Orchestra Recap 
Orchestra team met with Richard on 8/11 to recap and plan for next year. 
 
The 10th season went very well. Attendance: Concert 1-250+70 musicians; 
Concert 2-325+70 musicians; Concert 3-260+69 musicians. 
 
Musicians are very pleased with Richard Ryan as the director, they loved 
the new T-shirts and they also enjoyed performing commissioned pieces. 
 
This year, three members of the orchestra were engaged to compose a 
piece for each of the three concerts and were paid a small honorarium of 
$500, paid for by grant funding from the Marth Lee Cain Tranby Music 
Enrichment Fund.  
 
The city’s partnership with local high schools is working very well.  
 
3.  Outdoor Concerts
