PARKS AND RECREATION STAFF UPDATE – RYAN WIEBE (AQUATICS 
MANAGER) 
 
Mr. Wiebe, Aquatics Manager, shared information about the City of Lenexa’s 
aquatics program and updates on the success of the 2026 summer pool season.  
 
 Outdoor pool membership numbers continued to increase this year from 
1,965 to over 2,300 this summer, and overall membership numbers 
increased by approximately 1,000. 
 Outdoor pool attendance increased by more than 14,000 visits compared 
to 2025. 
 The Lenexa Lazers swim and dive team grew to 209 athletes, an increase 
from roughly 180 in previous years. 
 Birthday party rental options were simplified and streamlined into one 
package, which resulted in an overall increase in birthday party rentals. 
 Pool programming is continuing to grow, with most programs near 
capacity or boasting waitlists. 
 Special events—including Indoor Movie in the Pool, Outdoor Movie in the 
Pool, Underwater Easter Egg Hunt, and Paws in the Pool—were well 
attended, with Outdoor Movie in the Pool drawing the highest number of 
registrants. 
 Concession sales went up and revenue increased from last year. 
 Ad Astra and Flat Rock pool seasons were extended by one week this 
year on an alternating schedule. While the Rec Center pool was closed for 
annual scheduled deep cleaning and repair, Rec Center members were 
invited to visit any outdoor pool, which was made possible by the season 
extensions. Both initiatives were successful. 
 Aquatics has partnered with Eva McMann, a graduate student studying 
Occupational Therapy at The University of Kansas Medical Center, where 
she will assist in designing and implementing new programs for adaptive 
and sensory needs as well as the current sensory swim program.  
 The opening day for the outdoor pool season next year will be Saturday, 
May 29th, 2027. 
 
Mr. Sics asked how Papa Murphy’s pizza is managed by the concessions. Mr. 
Wiebe explained that Papa Murphy’s delivers frozen pizza which stays fresh for 
three days. He noted that the product loss for unused pizza was low and easily 
made up by revenue. 
 
Ms. Schmidt asked why Ad Astra was not available for birthday party rentals this 
year. Mr. Wiebe explained that it was due to the pool opening later in the season, 
among other challenges. He confirmed that Ad Astra pool would be available for 
birthday party rentals during the 2027 outdoor pool season. 
 
Mr. Thomas asked about the performance of the Lenexa Lazers Swim and Dive 
team this year. Mr. Wiebe said that they are competitive, as they pull from high 
school swimmers in the area who want to focus on rec leagues, as well as having 
strong youth swim lessons which feed into the youth Lazers teams. 
 
 Ms. Lindsey shared that her family recently visited the Sar-Ko-Par Aquatic 
Center and was impressed with the quality of the lifeguard staff. She asked 
whether all outdoor pools would follow the same opening and closing schedule 
for the 2027 season. Mr. Wiebe explained that the pools will close at different 
times and transition to modified schedules toward the end of the season due to 
staffing availability. He noted that the 2027 closure schedule is expected to follow 
the same format used this season.

    Director’s Report 
 
    Mr. Wagler reported the following: 
 The Ad Astra project is wrapping up. Contractors are still working on the 
final punchlist items and are doing an excellent job. 
 Kid Zone is re-opening on September 10 th. 
 The new staircase is about 3-4 weeks out from opening and will finish on 
schedule. 
 The City Manager, Beccy Yocham, has announced her retirement. She 
intends to stay through the remainder of the year while a third-party 
company facilitates the nationwide search to fill her role. Internal 
candidates will also be considered. 
 The community garden expansions at Ad Astra and Buffalo Meadows are 
almost done. Plots will be laid out and ready by next spring. 
 The City Council approved the 2027 operating budget. The Capital 
Improvement projects are next. 
 The Parks Service Center project is slated for 2029. Mr. Wagler is also 
seeking approval to expand the budget for the 2028 Centennial Park 
Project. 
 City Council meetings will be live streamed going forward, in addition to 
the recorded meetings already available to the public. 
 The city applied for and received a $17 million build grant at the K-10 and 
Lone Elm interchange that will allow construction to move forward and 
start as early as 2027. 
 The city is working with a Johnson County - wide work group, which has 
produced a model policy for electronic mobility devices, with the hope that 
municipalities across the county will adopt these consistent regulations. 
The city is looking into requiring helmets for riders of electronic mobility 
devices who are under the age of 18. 
 There is no update on the current Park Board vacancy.
