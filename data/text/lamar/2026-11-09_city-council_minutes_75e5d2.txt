Monday October 26, 2026
The City Council met in a regular session at 7:00 p.m. in the Council Room with Mayor Crespin presiding.
Present: Joe Gonzales, Mark Headlee, Kirk Crespin, David Zavala, Brad Johnson, Bob Seay, Kristin Schwartz, Kristin Schwartz, Mitch Hammes, Lance Clark
Absent:  Gerry Jenkins
Consent Agenda
Councilmember Gonzales moved and Councilmember Zavala seconded to approve the consent agenda Items 1-4.
Item #1 –
Approval of Council Meeting Minutes – 10/12/2026
Item #2 –
Approval of Minutes for Boards and Commissions
a)    Utilities Board – 9/29/2026
Item #3 –
Payment of Bills
General Fund-Vouchers #105882 - #105958
Item #4 –
License New/Renewal
a)    Liquor Store License – Olive St. Station, 403 E. Olive St.
Voting Yes:    Gonzales, Headlee, Crespin, Zavala, Johnson, Seay
Voting No:
Mayor Crespin stated “Let the record reflect that all Councilmembers voted an affirmative, motion passes 6-0”.
Audience Participation
Devlin O’Neal, 221 Sage Dr. Lamar, spoke in regards to the data center moratorium, draft and board creation.
Donna Smith, 33427 Cty. Rd. 7 she wanted answers from Council on why she continues to get refusal from law enforcement on responding to calls she has made, she provided a formal request in writing.
Gabriel Benabides, 411 S 2nd St. asked about affordable housing and also wanted to know who brought up the data centers.  Did the City contact them or they contact the City.  Mayor Crespin stated that a company had contacted the City.
City Treasurer Report
City Treasurer Schwartz reported that they are working on closing out the 2nd assessment grant that ended June 30, 2026.  For the 3rd assessment grant that was awarded they are working on creating the RFQ to send out for a consultant.  She stated that the contract has been signed on the clean-up grant and they should be in full swing next week beginning the clean-up for the Main Café.
City Treasurer Schwartz gave a Tyler update, we are close to having all of the modules finished.  They are in the middle of Parks & Recreation, they are excited to get to utilize the system.  She rolled out Position Budgeting last week, Linda started AR’s today and still waiting on Sale Tax, Cemetery’s wants to start mid-November.  She is looking forward to being done, she also stated that this was the first month they were able to use the Aclara file without a manual upload.
City Clerk Report
City Clerk Williams gave a brief Tyler update on the Accounts Receivable, Sales Tax, and Cemetery modules.
City Administrator Report
Park & Rec Sports Swap Event
City Administrator Hammes announced that donated items can be dropped off at the Community Building November 2-12, 2026 and the Sports Swap is Friday, November 13, 2026, 6:00-8:00 p.m. and Saturday, November 14, 2026, 12:00-2:00 p.m.
Two Shot Goose Hunt
City Administrator Hammes announced that the Two Shot Goose Hunt is December 9-11, 2026.
Office Closure
City Administrator Hammes reported that the City offices will be closed Thursday and Friday, November 26-27, 2026 in observance of Thanksgiving.
Shop Small Saturday
City Administrator Hammes reported that the Shop Small Saturday is Saturday, November 28, 2026.
Holiday Shop & Stroll
City Administrator Hammes announced that the Lamar Honkers “Holiday Shop and Stroll” is Saturday, November 28, 2026.
Lamar Chamber of Commerce Parade of Lights
City Administrator Hammes announced that the Parade of Lights is Saturday, November 28, 2026.
Trunk or Treats
City Administrator Hammes provided the list below of Trunk or Treat streets.
Lamar Public Library is Friday, October 30, 2026, 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. at the City Complex parking lot.
Lamar Elks Lodge is Saturday, October 31, 2026, 6:00 to 7:30 p.m.
Grace Family Worship Center is Saturday, October 31, 2026, 6:00 to 8:00 p.m.
Miscellaneous
City Administrator Hammes announced the hiring of Abbie Castro as the new Community Programs Manager.  She will be responsible for the Main St. Program, Lamar Redevelopment Authority Board, and Community Events.
City Administrator Hammes announced the Kevin Bommer CML Executive Director will be here for work session on November 9, 2026 to do recap/review of Council meetings along with new Council training.  Begins at 5:30 with dinner provided.
City Administrator Hammes announced that the City Christmas Party is Saturday, December 5, 2026 at the Lamar Elks Lodge.
City Administrator Hammes announced that the Pueblo Chamber of Commerce has invited City of Lamar to participate in their Veterans Day Parade on Saturday, November 7, 2026.
Department Updates
City Administrator Hammes announced that Kolby Brubacher has been given the permanent position of Park, Cemetery, & Recreation Director.  Kolby along with Gilbert Longoria, Cemetery Supervisor provided an update of what is happening in their department.
Winterizing sprinkler systems and bathrooms
Wrapping up fall outdoor pool services
Working on Fairmount Expansion
Escondido Field Field seeding and re-dressing in Spring
Main St. tree trimming
Maintenance on equipment
Getting ready for snow season
Council Liaison
Senior Center Update
Councilmember Headlee reported that Judy Nordyke has stepped in to replace Sandy Zollars on the Senior Center Board.
PEP Update
Councilmember Johnson reported that normal business discussion at the PEP meeting but they did highlight opportunities for new businesses.
