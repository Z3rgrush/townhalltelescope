BACKGROUND / DISCUSSION 
A Flock camera is an automated license plate reader system that can be used by law enforcement to track 
vehicle movements, capture license plates, and log vehicle characteristics. 
 
The motion-activated, pole-mounted cameras take photos of passing vehicles, recording the license plate, make, 
model, color and unique features like bumper stickers or roof racks. Information captured by flock cameras is 
uploaded instantly to a centralized cloud network, flagging vehicles linked to active Amber Alerts, warrants, or 
stolen vehicle databases, and allowing police forces to share and search data across different jurisdictions. 
 
While rules vary at different agencies, Flock cameras are designed to temporarily hold collected data and delete 
it after a predetermined period of time has passed. 
 
Wellington program 
The Larimer County Sheriff's Office (LCSO) started using Flock cameras in Wellington in late 2022 at three 
locations. Since then, the program has expanded to include cameras at 6 locations: 
• Highway 1/County Road 9 
• Interstate 25 Frontage Road/Jefferson Avenue 
• Cleveland Ave/westbound I25 (just past the ramps) 
• 6th Street/Cleveland Avenue southbound 
• 6th Street/Cleveland Avenue northbound 
• County Road 7/County Road 64 
 
Since the start of the Flock camera program in Wellington, members of LCSO have presented to the Wellington 
Board of Trustees two times regarding the program's impact on the community: 
o April 18, 2023 BOT work session 
o May 14, 2024 BOT meeting 
Cost 
Until this year (2026), the Flock camera program was in a testing phase meaning there was no cost to the 
Town.  
 
During the 2026 budget process, LCSO advised Town staff that it had applied for grant funding to partially 
offset the cost of the Flock camera program in Wellington. LCSO was ultimately not awarded the grant, and the 
Town's first-quarter 2026 invoice included a $17,000 charge for the program. 
 
To ensure the Town was prepared if grant funding was not received, $17,000 was included as a separate line
