6. NEW BUSINESS 
 
A. Housing Needs & Affordability Assessment (HNA) Technical Memorandum Update and Discussion –  
Aidan Checkett, Planner 1, presented the item and outlined the status of the Technical 
Memorandum.  
 
Commissioner Chollet asked if the report will be updated. Checkett stated it will be updated every 
five years in line with state requirements. Chollet asked how economic trends are reflected in the 
update. Bird replied that the report is intended to evaluate consistent data sets. Checkett added 
that the economic circumstances can likely be found in the data. Chollet asked if it would be 
possible to have a further breakdown of the demographic data informing displacement risk.  
 
Commissioner Chollet asked how much weight we should give to the data if some of the recent 
update changes might be the product of statistical aberrations? Checkett said it depends on the 
data in question, but generally the high level analysis is still valuable.  Bird added that the general 
trends haven’t changed significantly and should still be pertinent. 
 
Chair Sartor asked what the long-term goal of the memorandum was? Checkett stated it will 
inform further planning documents, help with grant applications, and help prioritize planning 
decisions. Sartor asked if the Town is considering updating population projections. Bird replied that 
updating population projections is anticipated as part of a future effort, and the current 
projections are based on the best precedent the Town has. Checkett added that most of the 
recommendations of the HNA should hold true regardless of the rate of growth.  
 
Chollet asked how the tax numbers inform affordability and the gaps. Checkett said that it’s a 
proxy for economic health and vitality which is indicative of housing affordability and contributes 
to the measure of displacement risk. Staff will ask the consultant team assisting with this update 
for more information on how this might affect the community. 
 
Chollet asked what is the ratio of rental housing to owner housing units in the housing gap table?  
Checkett replied that Staff would ask the consultants if a breakdown could be included in the final 
version.

7. COMMUNICATIONS 
 
A. 3rd Quarter 2026 Residential Building Permit Activity and Lot Inventory Report 
 
Bird shared the report. Commissioner Chollet asked the status of the Sage Farms subdivision. Bird 
stated that it is still in the entitlement process and we are waiting on a submittal for final plat. 
Chollet asked if the HNA Technical Memorandum would have any impact on the subdivision. Bird 
said that the subdivision would contribute to meeting some of the housing gaps identified in the 
HNA. 
 
B.  Updates on Current and Upcoming Planning Work 
 
Bird shared an update on the current and upcoming projects the Planning Department is working 
on. Chollet asked if Staff anticipated any updates to the Land Use Code for data centers or other 
development standards. Bird replied that evaluating commercial standards and setbacks for light 
industrial uses is on Staff’s radar but an action on this topic is not anticipated in the coming 
months.
