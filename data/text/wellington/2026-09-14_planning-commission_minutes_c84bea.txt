6. NEW BUSINESS 
 
 

A. Public Hearing to Consider Amendments to the Land Use Code, Chapter 15, Article 8 – Floodplain 
Regulations 
 
Cody Bird, Planning Director, introduced Nathan Ewert, Deputy Director of Public Works and the 
Town’s Floodplain Administrator. Ewert presented the item. 
 
Commissioner Whitehouse asked if the proposed changes were intended to achieve compliance with 
external requirements for the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) and other programs. Ewert 
said yes, and that the changes were aligned with a model ordinance provided by FEMA. 
 
Chair Sartor opened the public hearing at 6:43 PM. There was no public comment Chair Sartor closed 
the public hearing at 6:43 PM.  
 
Whitehouse asked if the appeal and revision process for modifying the maps has passed. Ewert 
confirmed that it had, and that insurance companies have been regulating based on the map since its 
publication. 
 
Commissioner Whitehouse moved to adopt the Land Use Code amendments to Chapter 15, Article 8 
related to a revised floodplain map released by FEMA and for conformance with State and FEMA 
standards and forward a recommendation of approval to the Board of Trustees. Commissioner Chollet 
seconded. 
 
Yeas –Chollet, Springer, Whitehouse, McCaffrey, Sartor 
Nays-  
Abstain-  
Motion carried.
