Town ofEstes Park, Larimer County, Colorado October 23, 2026
Minutes of a Budget Study Session meeting of the Town Board of the Town of Estes
Park, Larimer County, Colorado. Meeting held at Town Hall in the Board Room in said
Town of Estes Park on the 23rd day of October, 2026.
Board: Mayor Hall, Mayor Pro Tem Hazelton, Trustees Brown,
Eshelman, Igel, Lancaster, and Mieras
Attending: Mayor Hall, Mayor Pro Tem Hazelton, Trustees Brown,
Eshelman, Lancaster, and Mieras
Also Attending: Town Administrator Machalek, Deputy Town Administrator
Damweber, and Deputy Town Clerk Beers
Absent: Trustee Igel
Mayor Hall called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m.
Community Development - Building and Planning
Staff reviewed the proposed budget for the Building and Planning Divisions within
Community Development. The Estes Park Development Code rewrite would continue
into 2027. As part of a 2027 Strategic Plan Objective, staff would evaluate the need for
a dedicated environmental planning function, responsible for ensuring new and existing
development complies with sustainability and environmental regulations and policies.
Large building projects identified included the Stanley Hotel Expansion and Film Center
and Elkhorn Lodge. Town Administrator Machalek responded to a Board question
related to staffing the Building Division with contract staff from SafeBuilt stating building
can be uneven or unpredictable. A benefit to contracting with SafeBuilt was the
opportunity to have access to additional support during years where the Town sees a
surge in permitting. Discussion ensued regarding the possibility of annexing the
Olympus Lodge property and their future interest in accessing Town water.
Internal Services
The Internal Services Department (ISD) was created in 2024 by combining Facilities,
Fleet and Information Technology. Staff reviewed the Vehicle Replacement Fund, Fleet
Maintenance Fund, Facilities, and Information Technology budgets. The Town would
purchase eight (8) replacement vehicles in 2027 and where appropriate and possible,
consider electric vehicles for replacement. The Fleet Utilization Study was anticipated
to be completed by the end of the year providing more information on how to accurately
budget this fund. Discussion ensued on the increased wear on Police Department
vehicles with the new take home car program. The Fleet Maintenance Fund supports
the maintenance, fuel, and equipment needs for the Town's fleet as well as the
maintenance for the Estes Valley Fire Protection District's (EVFPD) apparatus and
vehicles as outlined in the IGA between the two entities. In 2025, the Town billed
EVFPD for 289 hours and the 2026 budget contains billing of 350 hours. The Town has
requested EVRPD increase the billable hours to 360 to cover the anticipated
maintenance hours. Staff stated the Town has begun conversations with the Estes
Valley Recreation and Park District (EVRPD) and the Estes Park School District
regarding fleet maintenance pilot programs. Staff reviewed Facility Division
recommended decision packages including: Public Safety Facility Debt Service
Reduction Funding, convert the ISD Project Manager contract position to a 3-year
Limited Term Employee, AED Unit Lifecycle Replacement, Townwide Life Safety
Compliance and Fire Protection Renewal Program, Parking Garage Structure
Maintenance Funding, Visitor Center Log Repair and Remediation, Town Hall Rooftop
Unit HVAC Replacement (Police), Town Hall Space Needs Assessment and Facility
Reuse Master Planning Study, Town Hall Roof Replacement (Section 2 - Police), Town
Facilities Lifecycle Improvement Funding, and Brown Sand Tent Repair - Elm Road.
Information Technology decision packages included Board Room Transparency and
Communications Improvements, Technology Disaster Recovery Planning Initiative and
Facility Security Modernization and Enhancement Program.

Town Administrator's Office - Risk Management
The fund accumulates to pay for the property and liability coverages, including building
a reserve to cover unanticipated claims and deductibles. The proposed 2027 budget
was identified at $800,994. Staff anticipated an increase to property insurance which
was reflective in the budget.
Museum
Staff reviewed the proposed budget and responded to Board questions on the math
infestation mitigation which had been successful through pheromone introduction.
Monitoring would be ongoing as those insects can remain in an active egg state for
three years. The monitoring would be conducted in house largely funded through the
Museum Friends Foundation.
Public Works
Staff reviewed the proposed budget for the Parks, Streets, Stormwater, and Engineering
Divisions, Conservation Trust Fund, Open Space Fund, Streets Improvement Fund 1A
(260), Trails Improvement Fund 1A (244), Trails Improvement Fund 1A (246) and
Stormwater 1A (265). Staff highlighted funds from the Fall River Trail project would roll
into 2027 with an anticipated completion date of May 2027. Design of the concrete trail
along the north side of East Wonderview Avenue from Safeway to the roundabout at
MacGregor Avenue would be designed in-house fourth quarter of 2026 with
construction anticipated in fall 2027. The Board requested the timeline for stormwater
updates on other phases of the channel improvements downtown.

a.m.
Public Works - Transit and Parking
Staff reviewed the proposed budget for Transit and Parking. The Town would
implement CMAQ-funded pilot programs for regional transit service, expanded transit
routes, and micro-transit service, and evaluate the effectiveness of the current transit
service routes, times, and season, and consider expansion if warranted in 2027.
Trustee Lancaster commended the cooperation with outside agencies including RMNP
and Bustang for transportation services available to the public. Discussion ensued on
accessible micro-transit opportunities to expand services outside of the current areas.
Staff stated with the conclusion of the 2026 parking season, parking data would be
reviewed and more information would be provided to the Board at a future meeting.
Utilities - Power and Communications and Water
The Power and Communications and Water Division's budgets were reviewed. The
Town would continue construction of the Trailblazer Broadband system for customers in
the electric service area as a multi-year strategic plan objective; and complete the ESRI
Utility Network (UN) migration for fiber GIS to improve network visibility with Platte River
Power Authority and other owner communities. Town Administrator Machalek stated
several years ago, the Town created and funded four (4) positions in Water for capital
projects with only one (1) position filled. Since that time, funds for those three (3)
vacant positions had been carried in the Water Division budget. In 2027, only the one
(1) position filled would remain in the budget with the remaining funds for the unfilled
positions removed while staff review the workload and structure of the Division. The
Town would begin evaluating potential site design and construction of a new water
treatment plant and complete the Carriage Hills/Spruce Knob water main replacements
to reduce high-frequency leaks and stabilize distribution pressure on the Town's south
side.
Budget Summary.

budget before those scheduled public hearings. Staff recommended one (1) position
change, converting the Police Records Technician from limited-term part-time to a full-
