Visit Estes Park Operating Plan.
Sarah LeonardA/isit Estes Park CEO presented the 2027 Operating Plan comprised of
strategic objectives, departmental initiatives and performance measures. The plan
reflected priorities guiding VEP's work and responsibility to support a healthy vibrant
destination benefiting the residents, local businesses and visitors. She stated the plan
builds on feedback from residents, businesses and partners collected over the past two
(2) years through community input. The plan was organized around five (5) core areas
Brand Experience, External Affairs, Destination Stewardship, Research and Insights
and Finance and Administration. CEO Leonard stated the Sundance Film Festival
would be an opportunity for the Estes Valley and the creative arts industry through
exposure to new audiences. VEP would combine marketing, public relations and
partnerships with practical connections to the destination, including investing in a
complementary shuttle service between Estes Park and Boulder with pop-up activations
near the festival and local business offers and experiences. Plan updates have been
summarized: Continue Vacationland partnership, the official visitor guide, and increase
support of distribution to consumers; launch a new website first quarter of 2027; move
advertising programming in-house though a new Partnership Sales Manager position;
expand reporting to be inclusive of volume and engagement metrics which reflect travel
intent and planning, and VEP visibility across platforms; maintain relationships with the
Town, County, State and Federal partners; conduct resident and business sentiment
surveys through focus groups to help understand how community perceptions are
evolving and track changes over time; continue to engage on issues which could affect
visitation, local businesses and the broader tourism economy; participate in the US
Travel Association's Destination Capitol Hill event to reinforce the importance of travel
and tourism with federal elected officials and nationwide colleagues; monitor legislative
issues at the local, state and federal level and engage where needed; VEP would host
the 2027 VEP Tourism Summit scheduled for September 15th; continue conversations
related to DarkSky certification and explore a DarkSky club which encourages business,
residents and visitors to participate through incentives and education; audit information
provided through their advertising channels, continue supporting participating business
and incorporate considerations into relevant events and sponsorships; provide at least
one (1) accessibility education training or partnership in 2027; maintain partnership with
the Estes Arts District and Arts and Culture Advisory Council; develop a VEP contract
template for most vendors; examine internal expenditures for cost saving opportunities;
and engage a professional IT consultant to assess risk, liability and areas where
additional expertise may be needed. The proposed 2027 budgeted revenues were
identified at $11.68 million with $6.45 million 6E funds passed through to the Town of
Estes Park. VEP was available for questions. The Board and County Commissioners
commended VEP on their presentation and the development of the plan comments
have been summarized: support for metrics which matter for the community and ways
to track qualitative data including community sentiment and resident experience; the

2027 Workforce Housing and Childcare Funding Plan Draft.
Manager Speedlin presented the final draft of the 2027 Annual Workforce Housing and
Childcare Funding Plan for 6E Lodging Tax funds. The annual funding plan outlined the
allocation of revenue generated by Ballot Initiative 6E, approved by voters in November
2022. The plan identified projected revenue from the Local Marketing District (dba Visit
Estes Park (VEP)) and established funding allocations for workforce housing and
childcare initiatives in the Estes Valley. Under the existing intergovernmental
agreement, the plan must be submitted to the Town and Board of County
Commissioners by November 1st of each year, with changes by December 1st of each
year. The fund was projected at $6.5 million in 2027 with 20% dedicated toward
childcare and 80% toward housing. She reviewed the Childcare Priority Objectives
including tuition assistance, childcare workforce, out-of-school programs and facility
expansion and Housing Priority Objectives including land banking, development,
assistance programs and administration. She stated the Larimer County 1B Sales Tax
would impact childcare initiatives countywide effective in 2027. The Town would
continue to monitor 1B sales tax initiatives for opportunities to braid available childcare
stability initiative funds where available. The Board requested more information on how
the Town was meeting objectives and long-term impact. Staff stated more information
would be provided in more formalized reporting from the Estes Park Housing Authority
as well as the childcare provider quarterly report data which was provided to the Town
on October 1st. There were no recommended changes to the plan.
