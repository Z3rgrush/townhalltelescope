G) Actions & Presentations
1) Consideration of Ordinance Changes to Estate Zoning: Tables 16-2.
Community Development Director Bachran identified an inconsistency in Table 16-2, 
Section 16-3-30 of the Paonia Municipal Code, which establishes a three-acre 
minimum for one dwelling in the E-1 Estate Residential District but no minimum for 
two dwellings. The Planning Commission recommended six acres for two dwellings; 
however, staff recommended considering 1.5 to two acres per dwelling to support the 
Comprehensive Plan’s housing-density goals.
Board discussion included housing density, the limited number of affected properties, 
water and access limitations, and conflicts between estate zoning provisions allowing 
livestock and Municipal Code Section 7-7-30.
Public Comment:
• D. Warren comments in support of a 1,5-acre minimum.
• C. Patterson comments on the speed of process.
The Board agreed that the proposed density and livestock provisions required 
additional review at a work session and through a revised ordinance.
Trustee Hunter moves to direct staff to bring this forward, both at a first at a work 
session and then by way of ordinance for review and discussion with regard to 
potentially increasing the density in Estate Zoning per staff recommendations, as well 
as staff review of Section 7-7-30, with regard to the keeping of livestock in the estate 
zoning district. Seconded by Trustee Ostrander.
The motion carries unanimously.
2) IGA with Delta County for Asphalt Overlay
Public Works proposed an intergovernmental agreement with Delta County to place a 
two-inch asphalt overlay on West Fourth Street. Existing utilities were reported to be 
in suitable condition, and Public Works would prepare the roadway before paving. 
Due to the County’s approximately $25,000 project limit, completing the entire street 
may require work over two years.
Board discussion included the condition of the underlying utilities, roadway 
preparation and drainage, project cost, and resident notification. Staff stated that 
affected residents would receive notices once the anticipated October start date was 
confirmed.
Public Comment:
No public comments received on this topic.
Trustee Brown moves to enter into the IGA (Inter-Governmental Agreement) with 
Delta County to do the West Four Street Asphalt overlay, seconded by Trustee Czech.
The motion carries unanimously.

5) Consideration of Res 26-20 Referring the MJ Occupational tax to the November 
General Election.
Clerk Vetter explained that Resolution 26-20 would refer a ballot question to 
voters at the November General Election, asking whether the existing $5 
occupational tax per marijuana transaction should be replaced with a 5% charge 
per sale. Other marijuana ordinance requirements, including bonding 
provisions, would be considered separately and would not require voter 
approval.
August 25, 2026, Regular Board Meeting Minutes 3

Board discussion included potential revenue impacts and creating greater tax 
parity with neighboring communities, revenue under the existing $5 fee 
compared with a 5% tax. Staffs broader analysis estimated a possible annual 
revenue reduction of up to $40,000; however, trustees noted that lower 
transaction costs could keep more purchases in Paonia and potentially offset 
some or all the reduced revenue.
Public Comment:
No public comments received on this topic.
Trustee Ostrander moves to approve Resolution 26-20 on the county election 
ballot in November, asking whether to change the Marijuana Occupational Tax 
from a flat fee of $5 per sale to a 5% charge per sale. Seconded by Trustee 
Hunter.
The motion carries unanimously.

Interim Town Administrator Sund reported that the committee reviewed nine

7) Consideration of Town Reassuming Ownership of Trash Dumpsters.
Public Works Supervisor Byrge reported that all commercial dumpsters showed some 
level of deterioration, with approximately 25% considered hazardous because of holes, 
rust, damaged lids, or missing components. Reassuming ownership would allow the 
Town to manage maintenance, replacement, safety, and recordkeeping consistently.
During Board discussion, they clarified that the immediate action concerned 
ownership only; replacement costs and scheduling would be addressed during the 
budget process. Future considerations would include bear-resistant equipment, 
concrete pads, rental fees, solid-waste rates, and documented maintenance schedules. 
The Board also discussed the limited records concerning the Town’s earlier transfer of 
dumpsters to private ownership.
Public Comment:
No public comments received on this topic.
Trustee Ostrander moves that the Town of Paonia reassume ownership of the solid 
waste dumpsters in town, for the monthly rental fee to be established at a later time, 
seconded by Mayor Pro-Tern Foster.
August 25,2026, Regular Board Meeting Minutes 4

The motion carries unanimously.
