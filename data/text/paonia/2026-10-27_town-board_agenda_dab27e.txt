G) Actions & Presentations 
 
 
 1) Presentation - Recyclops and Colorado's Extended Producer Responsibility Program - Adria 
Cipra 
 
Cole Schexnayder from Recyclops attends by Zoom to talk about the (Extended Producer 
Responsibility Program  (EPR) and their part in how the program works. They hope to roll out 
the  program in Paonia on November 1, 2026 and answers question from the Board of Trustees 
and the  public 
 
Public Comment: 
 
D. Ziskin: comments on behalf of himself and C. Rice and their participation in the program and 
their concerns. 
 
Mr. Schexnayder reiterates that they want to work with local recyclers and not cut anyone out. 
 
Director Donaldson comments on her conversations with Recyclops on bear education.

 2)  Consideration of Renewing Contract with Sustainable Futures LLC for the Re-Codification 
Project – Leslie Klusmire

strongly suggests finishing so the contradictions that cause confusion can be cleared up.

C. Patterson: comments on the DOLA grant and the reason for an executive session. 
 
Clerk Vetter will send out a poll for the week of October 27th to set a work session. 
 
 3) Consideration of the Board of Trustees Appointment of New Police Chief 
 
Mayor Fischer and Trustee Hunter report that their recommendation is to promote from inside the 
department and promote Sergeant Henderson to be the new Chief of Police. 
 
Public Comment: 
 
G. Henderson: comments on his preference of Chief Laiminger staying. 
 
Trustee Hunter moves to promote Sergeant Henderson to be the new Chief of Police, seconded by Trustee 
Brown. 
 
The motion carries unanimously. 
 
 
The Board and Community thank Chief Laiminger for his service and his care of the community. 
 
Town Clerk Vetter administers the oath of office to Sergeant Garrett Henderson, officially swearing him 
in as the Town of Paonia's Chief of Police.

1. Executive Summary
Recyclops is pleased to propose a free, reliable and convenient residential curbside recycling program for
the Town of Paonia, Colorado. The program is designed to provide eligible households with collection every
other week, at no cost to residents or the Town.
Recyclops will work with the Town to obtain eligible residential service addresses, organize those records
in HubSpot, and enter approved locations into Cigo to create efficient collection routes. Recyclops also
plans to partner with Corey's Curbside Recycling, a local company, to conduct pickups, support local
business, and create local employment opportunities.
2. Program Overview
 • Free biweekly service: Pickup once every two weeks for each eligible household.
 • Automatic eligibility: Town-provided, approved addresses are included without an individual sign-up
 requirement.
• Resident supplies: Recycling bags and collection instructions distributed before launch.
 • Local collections: Corey's Curbside Recycling performs scheduled pickups.
 • Recyclops management: Customer data, scheduling, routing, oversight and service coordination.

3. Residential Address Collection and Management
Step 1 - Receive and verify addresses
The Town will provide a list of eligible residential addresses within the designated collection area.
Recyclops will review the information to organize the initial service area and identify any records requiring
clarification.
Step 2 - Establish records in HubSpot
Recyclops will enter and maintain residential service records in HubSpot to track eligibility, customer
communications, address changes, service requests and opt-outs as appropriate.
Step 3 - Create routes in Cigo
Recyclops will enter approved service locations into Cigo to map addresses, group households into
practical collection routes, assign every-other-week schedules, share route information with the collection
partner and monitor route completion.
Step 4 - Maintain the service database
Recyclops will update records as households become eligible, residents request changes, or service status
changes. Town-provided residential information will be used to administer the recycling program and
shared with the collection partner as necessary to perform pickups.
4. Collection Operations and Scheduling
Each eligible residence will receive an assigned pickup day every other week. Before launch, Recyclops will
coordinate distribution of recycling bags, schedules and educational instructions describing accepted
materials, preparation requirements, placement locations and support contacts.
On collection days, residents will set out prepared recyclable materials at their assigned collection point.
Corey's Curbside Recycling will follow the routes established through Cigo. Recyclops will oversee
scheduling, address service concerns and adjust routes when needed.
5. Partnership with Corey's Curbside Recycling
Recyclops plans to engage Corey's Curbside Recycling as the local collection partner. Corey's will carry out
scheduled curbside pickups while Recyclops provides routing, customer management, technology and
operational oversight.
This approach will direct collection work to a local company, draw on local knowledge of roads and
neighborhoods, and support existing jobs and potential new roles for drivers, collection personnel and
operations support as service demand develops. Specific collection responsibilities will be finalized in the
partnership agreement.
