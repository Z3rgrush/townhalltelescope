12.  Presentation on preliminary recommendations for Housing Action Plan  
 
Brian Points, Robyn Player and Mike Manning with Points Consulting presented an overview 
of the Town of Ridgway Housing Action Plan, noting a work session to review the plan more 
extensively will be held on August 12 th.  It was reported based on Senate Bill 24- 174, the 
Sustainable Affordable Housing Assistance Act, communities are required to adopt by 2028 
a housing action plan. The plan being prepared for the Town includes an action plan with five 
goal areas which include policies and procedures; providing future housing; life cycle housing 
provision; momentum building and workforce housing enhancement.   Following the meeting 
the Planning Commission will refine the proposed goals and develop an implementation plan 
reflecting the goals Council determines  are in the best interest of the Town.  The plan will 
address specific actions, timelines and partners for each goal.  A completed draft is expected 
to be completed in August and then presented to the Council for review. The draft plan will 
be posted for a thirty day public review period and after evaluation of public comments, the 
Commission will prepare a final draft of the plan to be presented in a publi c hearing to the 
Council for final adoption.    
 
13.  Presentation on Destination Marketing Plan

Sara Pinnix with Destination by Design presented a draft marketing strategy  for the Town 
which was “led by community input and ideas”, “to help you tell your story”.  The three core 
principles and strategic  framework of the plan is local life leads; purposeful stewardship; 
tourism that works here. She presented the first year marketing objectives which included 
stabilizing the shoulder season, guiding area visitors to  the Town core and promote 
stewardship messaging; audience segmentation and market positioning addressing heritage, 
culture and regional travelers. Focusing on the facts the Town is “unique” “welcoming” and 
has “some really great things to offer” she suggested positioning should include authenticity, 
dark sky community, outdoor recreation access, and a unique arts community.  The first 
campaign concept would be “Rooted in Ridgway” and include “rooted in earth” focus on the 
the outdoors and stewardship; “rooted in story” history, heritage and local life; “rooted in 
culture” makers and merchants; “rooted in wellness” balance, wellness and healing.  She 
suggested the campaign  could begin this fall in the tourism “shoulder season”  called 
“basecamp to the cosmos” focusing on fall foliage and dark skies. 
 
There where questions and comments from the Council.  
 
Ms. Pinnix noted the next steps are a stakeholder and community review period; delivery of 
the final marketing plan by the end of July; and at the beginning of August start to capture 
content and campaign development to be completed by the end of September.

ten percent of the units in a project to be dedicated to community housing but an alternative 
compliance method  can be offered. Requirements for administering the housing are
