Adam Krueger, Economic Development Director
On September 25, 2018, Council approved Ordinance 3494 establishing the RII-DII Business Improvement District (District).
The Colorado Revised Statutes governing business improvement districts require the District to submit to the City on an annual basis an Operating Plan and Budget for Council’s review and approval.
On September 10, 2026, the District submitted a 2027 Operating Plan and Budget, which Council is required to approve or disapprove.
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 31-25-1211, Council has the right to require the District to supplement its Operating Plan or Budget where necessary.

D.
A resolution adopting the Thornton Parks and Recreation Master Plan.
Chris Steinke, Interim Parks, Recreation and Community Programs Director
The Parks and Recreation Department, along with consultants from THK Associates and HR Green, developed the Thornton Parks and Recreation Master Plan. The Plan establishes a long-term framework to guide decisions regarding Thornton's parks, open spaces, recreation facilities, and programs as the community grows and changes.
The Plan establishes a shared 20-year vision and identifies goals and recommendations intended to guide future investment, maintenance, land acquisition, accessibility, recreation opportunities, natural-resource stewardship, and delivery of community services. The planning process combined community engagement, demographic and growth analysis, facility and park assessments, recreation demand analysis, benchmarking, and input from City Council, the Parks and Open Space Advisory Commission (POSAC), City staff, and other community stakeholders.

H.
A resolution acknowledging receipt of the Bellflower Annexation Petition and establishing November 10, 2026, as the date for consideration of the requested annexation of property generally located north of East 139th Avenue and east of Holly Street to the City (Bellflower Annexation).
Jason O'Shea, Interim City Development Director
The applicant has requested to annex approximately 39 acres of unincorporated land for a residential development. The first step in the annexation process, per the Municipal Annexation Act of 1965, Title 31, Article 12 of the Colorado Revised Statutes (State Statutes), is for Council to acknowledge receipt of the Petition for Annexation by resolution and establish the public hearing to consider the merits of annexing the land requested by the petitioner.
Acknowledging receipt of the Petition for Annexation does not bind the City to annex the unincorporated properties. It only determines that the Petition is in proper form and that the property meets the requirements contained in the State Statue in order to be considered for annexation into the City.

A.
An ordinance establishing a temporary moratorium on the submission, acceptance, processing, or approval of any applications to the City of Thornton for any permit, license, plan approval, or other type of approval related to new or expanding of data centers. (Second Reading)
Jessica Whitney, Interim City Attorney
At Council’s direction, staff has prepared a 12-month moratorium on the development of new data centers within the City. The moratorium would apply to new data centers exceeding 50,000 square feet and would also prohibit the expansion of existing data centers during the 12-month period.
At the September 8, 2026 Council meeting, this ordinance passed by a five to four majority vote of the Council.
