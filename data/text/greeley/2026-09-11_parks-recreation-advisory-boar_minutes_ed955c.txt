5. BOARD MEMBER COMMITTEE REPORTS – Art commission – Sharon Wright – 
 
• Weld Walls Mural Festival: The festival will begin on September 14, 2026, focusing on the 16th Street 
area between 9th and 23rd. The public is invited to watch artists work throughout the week, with a 
larger festival event planned for the weekend. The Art Commission is funding the event. 
• Stolen Sculpture: A sculpture was stolen from the corner of 59th and 4th Street. Local salvage yards 
have been notified. A previous sculpture that was stolen was recovered in Commerce City and has 
been successfully reinstalled. Community members are encouraged to report any missing public art. 
• Gallery Exhibit: The Tointon Gallery is featuring a local artist and collector, Alan Adler. The exhibit 
focuses on "what's in the collector's mind."

B. Natural Areas and Trails Report (Presented by Justin) 
• Poudre River Trail Award: The Poudre River Trail project, a regional partnership involving multiple 
communities and local governments, has been selected for a Colorado Open Space Alliance (COSA) 
Blue Grama Award. The award recognizes nearly 50 years of work that has connected over 45 miles of 
trail from Bellevue to Island Grove Park. The award will be presented in Crested Butte in October. 
• Trail Closures: Trails are typically closed for the winter around January 2 and reopen in late March, 
weather permitting. 
 
C. Parks Division Report (Presented by Eric) 
• Visitation Data (as of approx. August 14, 2026): 
o Total Park System Visits: Over 2 million 
o Average Visit Duration: 67 minutes 
o Highest Visited Parks: Sports complexes, Mumford, Bittersweet, and Centennial Park. 
• Activities (YTD): 
o Baseball, Softball, & Soccer Games: Over 3,500, with another ~1,000 expected in the fall 
season. 
o Forestry: Nearly 200 new trees planted, with more planned for the fall. Approximately 1,100 
trees have been trimmed by staff. 
• Wind Event Damage: A recent wind event caused four large trees at East Memorial and Balsam to be 
uprooted. The cemetery sustained only minor limb damage. 
• Water Conservation: Due to the Greeley Loveland ditch going out earlier than usual, the department is 
voluntarily cutting back watering at 13 "conditional" park sites (e.g., Levy Park, Apple Park) to conserve 
potable water for high-use facilities. Signs reading "Dry, Not Dead" are being posted to inform the 
public. This reflects a broader trend of focusing water resources on functional turf areas. 
• Upcoming Events & Projects: 
o Redtail Park Ribbon Cutting: September 17, 2026, at 4:00 PM. 
o Twin Rivers Playground Grand Reopening: Tentatively scheduled for October 28, 2026, at 4:00 
PM. The new structure is nearly three stories tall. 
o Pheasant Run Park: The project has been awarded. Installation is expected to begin in October 
and finish by mid-November. 
o Coyote Run Park: A pop-up event was held to gather citizen feedback. The project is expected 
to be completed 4-6 weeks after Pheasant Run.
