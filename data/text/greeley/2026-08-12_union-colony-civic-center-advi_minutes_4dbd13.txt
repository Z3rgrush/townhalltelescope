Council action. Purpose/mission statement remains defined by City Charter; 
may be reiterated at the top of bylaws for clarity.

5. Other:  
• Backstage Stars Report: The Board received an update on Backstage Stars, 
including its history, current membership structure, and future direction. 
Discussion included current membership benefits, changes affecting the 
organization’s fundraising role, and the need to clarify its purpose. 
The Board discussed using membership revenue to expand community access 
to Civic Center performances, particularly for youth and new theater-goers. 
Staff will coordinate with Backstage Stars to explore membership options, 
program feasibility, and potential ticket-subsidy opportunities. 
 
• Comments: The Board discussed a recent podcast reference to a past UCCC 
concert and reflected on the audience experience, venue format, and artist 
expectations. General consensus was not to issue an official response. 
Discussion focused on audience development and future engagement 
opportunities, including inviting the artist back, exploring alternative venue 
formats, partnering with local organizations, and creating a collaborative 
community invitation.
