NOTICE: 
Planning Commission meetings are held on the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays of each month. Meetings are conducted 
in a hybrid format, with a Zoom webinar in addition to the in-person meeting in Council Chambers. 
 
Members of the public may participate in Planning Commission meetings in the manner that works best for 
them. 
Watch Meetings: 
  Meetings are open to the public and can be attended in person by anyone. 
Comment in Real Time:

the record in real time.
