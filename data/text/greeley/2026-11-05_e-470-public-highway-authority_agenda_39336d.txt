NEPA RFP:  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
FHU Road 
Widening 
Contract 
Amendment 2: 
Mr. Weiss addressed the Board and reviewed E470/I-70 Interchange NEPA RFP.  He 
reported on the history of the I -70 NEPA process culminating in the current RFP.  He 
then reviewed the RFP process and the bidders, and the results of the procurement 
process.  He then provided details regarding the top 3 proposers’ rankings, with Jacobs 
Engineering Group, Inc. being the staff recommendation for contract award.  Mr. Weiss 
then reviewed the contract terms and the Task Order 1 scope.  Councilmember Diak 
inquired about the finality of this portion of the NEPA process and Mr. Weiss responded. 
 
Councilmember Diak moved that the Board approve the award of Contract No. EN-26-
I70-1 to provide environmental assessment reevaluation and preliminary design 
services to Jacobs Engineering Group, Inc. with a Task Order 1 not-to-exceed amount 
of $500,000, and to authorize the Executive Director to execute the same, subject to  
review and approval of General Counsel. The motion was seconded by Commissioner 
Baker and unanimously approved by the Board. 
 
Mr. Templeman addressed the Board and reviewed the FHU Road Widening Design 
Contract Amendment 2 . He noted the current progress for the project and forecast 
completion by 2028.  He reminded the Board of the scope of project and the substance 
of the proposed amendment.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Concrete 
Pavement 
Maintenance 
Contract:  
Commissioner Baker moved that the Board approve Amendment 2 to the FHU Contract 
EN-25-WDES-1 to provide engineering design services for the road widening project 
in the amount of $749,000, with a revised not-to-exceed contract amount of $8,202,000, 
and to authorize the Executive Di rector to execute the same, subject to review and 
approval of General Counsel.  The motion was seconded by Mayor Douglas and 
unanimously approved by the Board.  
 
Mr. Templeman then reviewed history regarding the Pavement Maintenance Contract 
RFP resulting in no proposals due to the complexity of the work and the limited scope.  
Accordingly, he reported that Flatiron was engaged to perform some emergency repairs 
and thereafter Authority staff engaged in negotiations with Flatiron to continue t o 
perform pavement maintenance.  Mr. Templeman reviewed the terms of the proposed 
contract with the Board.  Commissioner Baker inquired regarding the competitive 
bidding process and Ms. Seaver responded regarding the Authority’s processes and 
related legal matters. Mayor Douglas inquired about the war ranties and Mr. Brady 
responded.  Director Chandler commented on the engineering team’s innovative 
problem solving. 
 
Mayor Douglas moved that the Board approve the Flatiron Dragados Constructors Inc. 
Contract EN-26-CONC-1 to provide concrete pavement maintenance services, with a 
three-year not-to-exceed contract amount of  $1,500,000 and authorize the Executive 
Director to execute the same, subject to review and approval of General Counsel. The 
motion was seconded by Commissioner Baker and unanimously approved by the 
Board. 
  
Information 
Technology 
Department:  
 
ITIV Pool 
Update:  
Ms. Batenburg addressed the Board and provided background and detail regarding the 
ITIV (information technology infrastructure) Pool.  She reviewed the metrics of the 
pool and the goal to expand the vendor pool size to minimize business risk.  She 
reported regarding the recent RFQ and the evaluations of the proposers noting 10 
vendors were selected for the pool. She provided detail regarding the vendors and staff 
recommendations regarding the same.  Mayor Douglas inquired regarding the pool size 
and Ms. Batenburg responded.   
 
Commissioner Pinter moved that the Board approve (i) Adding the following vendors 
to the existing ITIV vendor pool with no increase to the 2026 not -to-exceed 
compensation amount for the ITIV vendor pool already approved by the Board of 
Directors: Akkodis, CDW Government, LongView, Optiv, Talent Acquisition L LC 
d/b/a Stand8, Tevora, and World Wide Technology;  (ii) Entering into the necessary 
master service agreements with the following ITIV vendor pool of Advanced Network 
Management, Akkodis, CDW Government, ePlus Technology, LongView, Optiv, Talent 
Acquisition LLC d/b/a Stand8, Tevora, Trace 3, and World Wide Technology for a term 
through December 31, 2029, and authorizing the Executive Director to execute the 
master services agreements subject to the review and approval of the final contract 
terms by General Counsel; and (iii) The combined total not -to-exceed compensation 
amount of  $6,000,000 for work to be performed under the various master services 
agreements within the foregoing ITIV vendor pool, for the period of January 1, 2027 
through December 31, 2027, with all future  annual total not-to-exceed compensation 
amounts to be approved by the Board of Directors. The motion was seconded by Mayor 
Douglas and unanimously approved by the Board.

Executive Director 
Report:   
Mr. Donahue addressed the Board and provided an update on the IBTTA Global Tolling 
Summit in Montreal, which he attended with Councilmember Bergan.  He noted the 
next IBTTA event is the Annual Conference to be held in Arlington, TX.  He then 
reported on hi s attendance at Member Jurisdiction governing body meetings, local 
economic development corporations, and civil organizations.   He also noted his 
legislative meetings and related discussions.  Mr. Donahue reported on the Parker 
opening for Applegreen.   M r. Donahue reported regarding the 2026 E -470 
Transportation Safety Foundation Fundraiser.  Finally, Mr. Donahue reported on being 
asked to present at CoMotion LA 2026, a global mobility and urban innovation 
conference bringing together transportation leaders, policymakers, investors, startups, 
and researchers. Mr. Donahue will be on a panel discussing the challenges and 
opportunities of procuring technology in the public sector.

Mr. McClafferty presented to the Board the proposed Finance Department 2027 Budget 
and reviewed the department’s Fiscal Services and Financial Planning and Analysis 
teams.   He reported on modernizing the financial system with an enterprise resources 
planning (ERP) system, enhancing analytics, refinement of the long-range plan, capital 
planning and funding, and strategically partnering within the organization.   Mayor 
Douglas commented on the credit card transactions and inquired about how to get the 
percentage paid for credit card transactions reduced.  Discussion ensued among the

Donahue reported on the relative level of credit card fees and associated costs of doing 
business.
