Key Staff Contact: Nathan Mosley, Budget and Policy Director  
 
Title: 
City Manager Fiscal Year 2027 Recommended Budget 
 
Background: 
This is the first of three sessions scheduled with City Council regarding the Fiscal Year 2027 budget. 
This work session will provide a high-level overview of the budget process, the challenges faced 
through that process and the recommendation for addressing the structural deficit and setting a more 
fiscally sustainable path forward. Staff will provide operational, capital and full-time employee (FTE) 
updates. 
 
This presentation is the culmination of more than 8 months of work by staff with support from City 
Council and feedback from residents. The process began in February when City Council held their 
strategic planning retreat and provided high-level, relative priority feedback to staff.  That feedback was 
utilized to develop departmental targets in order to close the anticipated $18M structural deficit. 
 
Since that time, departments developed budget proposals to meet their assigned targets. Those proposals 
were shared through a series of work sessions throughout the summer in which we answered questions 
and solicited feedback from City Council and the community. 
 
On Friday, September 4th, the City Manager's Recommended Budget was provided to City Council and 
posted on the City's website. The following Friday, September 11th, City Council hosted a full-day 
budget retreat to discuss and finalize the budget that is being formally recommended to City Council for 
consideration and adoption. 
 
The remainder of the Fiscal Year 2027 budget process is provided below:
