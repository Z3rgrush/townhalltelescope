The Greeley Art Commission

Room 201 Greeley Recreation Center 
1. The meeting was called to order by Louisa Andersen at 4:04 p.m. 
Board Present:  Molly McIntosh, Louisa Andersen, Perry Bell, Heather Utrata, 
Benson Verbel, Tom Devore, Cheryl Lee, Sharon Wright, Kendra Albee.

2. Approval of the September 2026 minutes. 
Motion to approve the September 2026 minutes made by Perry Bell and seconded 
by Heather Utrata. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
3. Citizens Communication: There was no citizen communication for this meeting. 
4. Presentation of MERGE project by CDOT and Public Works. 
• The commission reviewed a presentation on a major highway project. 
• The project proposes underpasses for 35th and 47th Avenues beneath the 
highway. 
• Earlier 2018 plans for overpasses were complicated by a cemetery and nearby 
businesses. 
• The current plan includes roundabouts and significant property impacts. 
• Construction documents are expected by July 2027. 
• The commission has about one year to develop concepts for public art 
integration. 
• The art budget may be up to $400,000, funded by the quality-of-life tax; finance 
must confirm. 
5. Approval of MERGE Public Art Outline: 
• The commission tabled approval of initial expenditures until the October 
meeting, pending CDOT financial clarification.
