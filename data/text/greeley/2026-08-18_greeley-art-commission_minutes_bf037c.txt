3. Presentation from Weld Walls Mural Festival 2026.  
Presented artist design and festival update; clarification that the $20,000 
associated with Wild Walls should be characterized as “contracted for services” 
rather than “donated” in public documentation. 
Selected Wall & Artist — Pinocchio’s Building (Ninth Ave & Sixteenth Street) 
Location: East-facing side of Pinocchio’s building (site of ~15-year-old prior mural 
by Liz, Armando, Diane Richards, and two students). 
Selected Artist: Shannon Mack (Fort Collins-based; discovered via Wild Walls pop-
up show submission). Theme: “Homecoming.” 
Medium: Exterior paint (hand-brushed/roller), some spray paint, filler scraping; 
traditional approach (underpainting, washes). 
Mockup: Collage-style mockup; final piece will be painted. 
Building Owner Approval: Owner Walter approved; independently engaged Wild 
Walls subcontractor for additional wall prep and repainting beyond contracted 
scope. 
Positive reception of design; anticipation of strong social media engagement; 
acknowledgment that the previous mural’s “dancing girl” will be missed.
