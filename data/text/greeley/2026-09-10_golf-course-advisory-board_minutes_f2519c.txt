7. New Business 
Patrick spoke with a Yamaha representative stating that Chris was provided 
with a GPS unit to evaluate, and a demo cart will be at Highland Hills for 
several weeks. Patrick was curious about his findings. 
o Chris stated that the demo cart has a more cushioned seat than the current 
carts but is otherwise similar. He expressed concern about stitched seats 
because the stitching can retain water after washing and may reduce the 
useful life of the seats. 
o Chris stated that the new carts would likely be placed at Boomerang due to 
wind conditions, particularly if windshields are added. However, staff will 
first assess which course’s carts are most in need of replacement. 
o Chris stated that the Yamaha GPS system, Yamatrack, is the preferred 
option because it is plug-and-play and does not void the Yamaha warranty. 
Other GPS systems may require splicing into the cart wiring. 
o Chris noted that the system would allow staff to enforce rules, create 
geofences, and limit carts from leaving the property. This would reduce 
the need for physical barriers, such as rope, and help address situations 
where golfers drive carts off the course or toward vehicles parked along 
the road. 
o The system can also provide pace-of-play notifications. Chris explained 
that golfers would receive notice and acknowledge when they are behind 
pace, allowing staff or marshals to address the issue more effectively. This 
could help with limited staffing needs. 
o Mike asked how many carts each course has. Chris responded that each 
course has approximately 55 carts, with a mix of 2023 and 2024 models at 
each location. 
o Mike asked whether all new carts would go to one course. Chris 
confirmed that they likely would. 
o Chris reported that golf carts generate approximately $800,000 annually. 
o Patrick mentioned a cart GPS unit he had seen that was small and mounted 
in a corner of the cart. Chris clarified that the system being considered 
includes more than basic GPS functionality. He estimated the annual cost 
for the proposed GPS program at approximately $60,000.

9. Good of order 
e Patti shared a question from a golfer regarding whether league golfers could 
use funds in their league accounts to purchase punch cards. 
o Chris responded that league account funds may not be used for punch 
cards. He explained that prize money is designated for merchandise 
purchases in the golf shop, which helps generate shop revenue. 
o Chris also provided that if he is not aware of other courses allowing 
league funds to be used for punch cards and noted that Mariana Butte 
does not allow this practice. 
o Chris then explained that leagues are not required to collect fees and 
distribute prize money in all cases. Afternoon leagues typically do so, 
while some morning leagues, including the ladies’ leagues, do not. 
Prize money is also collected and distributed for tournaments. 
o Chris thanked Patti for bringing the question forward and encouraged 
Board members and golfers to submit questions earlier when possible 
so staff can provide a timely response.

minutes rather than receive a direct reply.

soon as possible and reiterated that his door is always open 
for questions.
