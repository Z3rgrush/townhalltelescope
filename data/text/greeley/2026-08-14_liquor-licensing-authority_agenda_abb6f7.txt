Virtual Hearing via Zoom Webinar Instructions 
  
Liquor License Authority Hearings are fully remote. This means no physical 
location, including the Administrative Hearing Room at City Center South, will be 
set up for viewing or participating in this Liquor Licensing Authority Meeting.  
 
This Hearing can be viewed via live stream. When utilizing this option, there is not the ability to 
provide live input during the meeting.   
  
- Click the following link or open a web browser and enter the web address manually: 
https://greeleyco.portal.civicclerk.com/  
- Once on the page, live meetings currently being broadcast can be seen on the 
homepage, or archived videos can be searched and viewed anytime.  
  
Public comment for items appearing on this agenda as Public Hearings are  
valuable and welcome!  
 
Anyone interested in participating and sharing public comments has three 
options:  
1.  Via email – Submit to Liquor.Licensing@Greeleygov.com  
 
All emailed comments must be received no later than Friday, August 14, 2026 at 
1:00 p.m. All comments submitted via email will be read into the record during 
the Hearing. 
  
2. Via traditional Mail - Addressed to the Greeley City Clerk’s Office, 1100 10th 
Street, Greeley, CO 80631  
 
All written comments must be received no later than Friday, August 14, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. 
Written comments received by mail will be read into the record during the Hearing.  
  
3. In real time Via Zoom webinar: https://greeleygov.zoom.us/j/84756752476  
ID: 847 5675 2476 or call into the Hearing using the same Webinar ID at: +1 719 359 4580  
  
Clicking the link for the Zoom webinar places participants in a virtual waiting 
room. Virtual audience members may speak during Public Hearing items on the

you can be easily identified.  Using the video option during the Hearing is also 
preferred.
