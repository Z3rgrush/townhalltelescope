Ticket Donation Policy & Guidelines 
Draft — September 14, 2026 — for UCCC Advisory Board Monthly Review & Recommendation; Administrative 
Adoption by UCCC Staff 
Prepared for Board Chair Dan Knopf and the UCCC Advisory Board 
Why This Policy Is Being Updated 
This policy replaces the standing Ticket Donation Subcommittee and its informal practice with a single written 
policy administered through the Board's regular monthly meetings, for two reasons: 
• To eliminate the need for a separate subcommittee. Ticket donation requests are reviewed as part of the 
Board's existing monthly business, rather than requiring a dedicated subcommittee with its own 
scheduling and membership. 
• To establish one consistent, written policy. A single documented policy sets out who qualifies, why 
tickets are donated, and how requests are reviewed — so every applicant is evaluated against the same 
objective criteria, and treated fairly relative to every other applicant, rather than relying on unwritten 
practice that can vary case by case. 
1. Program Purpose & Scope 
• Program Purpose: The Union Colony Civic Center (UCCC) Ticket Donation Program is established to 
support local charitable, civic, and educational fundraising initiatives throughout Greeley and Weld 
County. Grounded in the Advisory Board's mission under Bylaws Article III, Section 1 — to strive for 
excellence in entertaining, educating and cultivating audiences of all ethnicities, ages, and socio-economic 
backgrounds, and to assist community arts organizations in accomplishing the same, the program provides 
complimentary admission tickets and performance vouchers to qualified non-profit and civic partners to 
aid their fundraising activities.1 
Program Scope & Boundaries: 
• Eligible Scope: This policy governs the allocation of City-owned admission inventory exclusively for 
formal, organized fundraising activities (silent auctions, live auctions, benefit raffles, and door prizes) 
conducted by verified 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations, educational support groups, first-responder 
relief associations, and statutory civic entities serving Weld County. 
• Excluded Scope: This program does not provide general complimentary admissions, personal gifts, 
individual volunteer/donor/staff rewards, employee incentives, artist comps, or commercial event 
promotions. It excludes crowdfunding platforms or personal online campaigns (e.g., GoFundMe), political 
action committees, political parties, 501(c)(4) social welfare or advocacy organizations, ballot-measure 
campaign committees, candidate campaigns, and legal defense funds. Tickets shall not be granted for any 
event where proceeds are used for political lobbying, campaign intervention, legislative advocacy, private 
litigation, or personal individual aid. Tickets are strictly non-cash municipal assets intended solely to 
generate charitable proceeds for local community benefit.

2. General Eligibility Criteria 
Mandatory Operational Conditions: 
• Geographic Scope: The organization must maintain an active physical office in Weld County, or certify 
that proceeds generated from the donated tickets will directly support programs, initiatives, or residents 
within Weld County. 
• Exclusive Fundraising Use: Donated tickets are intended strictly for fundraising mechanisms — a silent 
auction, live auction, benefit raffle, or door prize at a formal fundraising event. Tickets may never be 
gifted to individual volunteers, donors, board members, or staff as compensation or perks, nor used for 
employee incentives, commercial promotion, or resale. 
• Return of Unused Tickets: Tickets or vouchers not used for the stated fundraising purpose must be 
returned to the UCCC. There is no penalty for returning unused tickets or vouchers. If physical tickets 
were issued, or a voucher was redeemed for tickets to a specific performance, the UCCC would appreciate 
their return in advance of the scheduled performance. 
• Noncompliant Use: Tickets or vouchers used in a manner that violates these terms (including resale, 
personal use, or use as an incentive for staff, volunteers, donors, or board members) disqualify the 
organization from receiving any future UCCC ticket donations. 
• Frequency Limit: One approved ticket request per eligible organization (or per school group under 
Section 2.2) per UCCC season year (July 1 through June 30). 
To qualify, an applicant must satisfy at least one of the following: 
• Section 2.1 — 501(c)(3) Public Charities: Verified 501(c)(3) non-profit charitable, cultural, or educational 
organizations in good standing with the IRS and the Colorado Secretary of State. 
• Section 2.2 — Public Education Support & Youth Scholarship Organizations: 
– Independent Non-Profit Support Entities: Independent booster clubs, PTAs/PTOs, school 
foundations, or youth scholarship organizations holding their own active 501(c)(3) status. Each 
legally distinct 501(c)(3) entity is eligible to submit one request per season year. 
– School-Affiliated Unincorporated Groups: Unincorporated booster clubs, student activity clubs, or 
parent committees without independent 501(c)(3) status cannot apply directly; the building principal

3. Non-Discrimination Standard 
• In alignment with the Board's mission (Bylaws Article III, Section 1), all requests are evaluated strictly 
against the objective criteria in this policy. Reviews are conducted in a content-neutral manner without 
regard to an applicant's political viewpoint, religious affiliation, or philosophical mission.5 
4. Allocation 
Every eligible organization under Section 2 may apply for up to 2 tickets or vouchers. For a large benefit or 
gala event anticipated to draw more than 200 attendees, up to 4 tickets or vouchers may be granted. 
5. Ticket Options, Inventory Availability, & Caps 
Selection Options: Approved applicants may request either specific show tickets for an eligible, upcoming 
City-presented performance, or complimentary vouchers redeemable by the winning bidder for a qualifying 
UCCC-presented performance during the current season. 
• Seating and Inventory Disclaimer: Ticket allocations, dates, and seating locations are determined at 
UCCC management's sole administrative discretion based on available inventory. Complimentary tickets 
and vouchers are valid for select standard seating tiers only — not premium, pit, or VIP tiers. 
• Program Cap: Maximum of 200 total complimentary tickets/vouchers distributed across all organizations 
per season year. 
• Performance Restrictions: Valid only for City-presented UCCC performances. Third-party promoter 
rentals, performances projected at or exceeding 85% capacity, and requests submitted fewer than thirty 
(30) calendar days prior to a show date are excluded. 
• Zero Cash Value: Tickets carry no cash surrender value, cannot be exchanged for credits or refunds, and 
are non-transferable outside the approved fundraising event. 
6. Required Documentation & Verification 
• Section 2.1 Entities: IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter and proof of active status with the Colorado 
Secretary of State. 
• Section 2.2 Entities (Boosters/Schools): Either the independent entity's 501(c)(3) determination letter, or 
an official letter requesting tickets from the school, on school letterhead and signed by the building 
principal, certifying the activity and verifying no other request has been submitted for that school group 
during the season year.

• Section 2.3 Entities (Public Safety): An IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter, or an official letter requesting

agency head, department chief, commanding officer, or association president, certifying the legitimacy of

• Section 2.4 Entities: Copy of the enabling charter, resolution, or formal operating agreement with the City

7. Mandatory Receipt Certification & Compliance 
• Written Certification Required: Within thirty (30) calendar days following receipt of the tickets/vouchers 
(or within 14 calendar days following the event, whichever is later), an authorized representative must 
submit a written certification by email to UCCC staff. 
• Certification Terms: The email must certify that the tickets/vouchers were used solely for the stated 
fundraising purpose, and were not sold outside the event, transferred, or used as personal gifts or 
incentives for staff, volunteers, donors, or board members. For a request granted more than 2 tickets under 
Section 4, the certification must also confirm actual event attendance met the 200-attendee threshold 
claimed at the time of request. 
• Sanction for Non-Compliance: Organizations that fail to submit this certification, or that submit a 
materially false certification (including a false attendance claim under Section 4), may become 
permanently ineligible to receive future UCCC ticket donations. 
8. Board Review & Recommendation Workflow 
• Submission Timeline: Requests must be submitted through the official UCCC website (ucstars.com) at 
least thirty (30) calendar days prior to the organization's event date. 
• Staff Intake & Monthly Docket: UCCC staff compiles incoming requests, verifies submitted 
documentation against Sections 1–7, and prepares a monthly Ticket Donation Recommendation Docket. 
Monthly Board Action: 
• The Ticket Donation Docket is placed on the agenda for each regular monthly meeting of the UCCC 
Advisory Board (held the second Wednesday of each month). 
• The Board formally reviews the docket and votes to recommend approval, modification, or denial of each 
request under this policy. 
• Recommendations are forwarded to the Department of Culture, Parks and Recreation for administrative 
issuance. The Director of Culture, Parks and Recreation, or their designee (such as the UCCC Manager), 
retains final administrative authority to issue tickets consistent with this policy. 
• No-Meeting or No-Quorum Procedure: If the Board does not hold a regular meeting in a given month, or 
a scheduled meeting fails to reach quorum, UCCC staff may seek the Board's recommendation on any 
pending, time-sensitive request through written (email) input from the Board Chair (or Vice Chair, in the 
Chair's absence). The Director of Culture, Parks and Recreation, or their designee (such as the UCCC 
Manager), retains final administrative authority to act on the request, consistent with this policy, whether 
or not that recommendation was obtained. All requests acted upon under this section — including whether 
a Board recommendation was sought and what it was — shall be reported to the full Board and reviewed 
at its next regular meeting. 
• Clerical Appeals: Organizations denied due to incomplete paperwork or clerical discrepancies may submit 
updated verification to the Board for re-examination at its next regular monthly meeting. 
Subject to formal legal review by the Greeley City Attorney's Office and administrative approval by the Department 
of Culture, Parks and Recreation.
