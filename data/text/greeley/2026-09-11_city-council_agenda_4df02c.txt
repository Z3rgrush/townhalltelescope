Title: 
2027 Budget Retreat 
 
Background: 
As part of the 2027 Budget Development Process, City Council Member Deb Deboutez proposed a 
Budget Retreat as a Council Initiative and got consensus for staff to proceed with planning and 
executing the retreat. 
 
Leading up to the retreat, staff provided a number of work sessions that included budget 
recommendations for Council consideration.  Those work sessions occurred during the following 
meetings: 
May 12, 2026 Meeting - General Budget Development Update and Municipal Court  
May 26, 2026 Meeting - Finance, Budget, City Manager's Office, Police Department, Fire Department 
and Office of Emergency Management 
June 9, 2026 Meeting - Community Development, Code Compliance, Economic Development & Urban 
Renewal, Housing and Homeless Solutions 
June 16, 2026 Meeting - City Attorney's Office 
July 7, 2026 Meeting - Culture, Parks & Recreation and Communications & Engagement 
July 14, 2026 Meeting - City Clerks Office, High-Performing Government, Human Resources, 
Information Technology, Water & Sewer, Stormwater and Public Works 
July 28, 2026 Meeting - 2027 Capital Improvement Plan  
 
Since the end of July, staff has been working to incorporate the feedback from City Council and the 
community in consultation with City Manager Brian McBroom to develop the 2027 Recommended 
Budget. The recommended budget is meant to be staff's attempt to incorporate what we have heard to 
date while balancing the desire to materially change our long-term financial outlook and create a more 
sustainable financial picture. 
 
The recommended budget was delivered to City Council and made available to the community on 
Friday, September 4, 2026. For more information pertaining to the 2027 Budget please visit: Speak Up 
Greeley 2027 Budget 
 
The purpose of this retreat is to review the major components included in the 2027 Recommended 
Budget, facilitate discussion to understand any additional concerns from Council Members and get 
consensus on any remaining items that need to be adjusted in the budget.

CBAC Statement to City Council — FY2027 
Budget 
The Citizen Budget Advisory Committee (CBAC) has strong confidence in the work of City 
staff and the remarkable effort that went into developing the FY2027 budget. We believe 
the process was thoughtful, thorough, and necessary, and that Council should hono r that 
work while recognizing its responsibility to evaluate impacts and consider adjustments as 
warranted. 
CBAC encourages Council to: 
• Trust the process and the work of staff that developed the proposed budget. 
• Recognize that the burden is being shared across the organization and that 
reductions are based on relative priority to protect core services and minimize 
impact to quality of life to the greatest extent possible. 
• Reinforce that meaningful change is necessary; the city cannot continue with the 
status quo and that comes with supporting the hard decisions made in this budget 
process. 
• Exercise its fiduciary responsibility by maintaining a focus on long -term financial 
sustainability.  
• Evaluate impacts and adjust going forward when experience or new information 
demonstrates that changes are warranted. 
• Maintain fiscal discipline by not adding expenditures without identifying 
corresponding reductions or sustainable funding elsewhere.  
• Be mindful of the trust placed in staff and avoid decisions that unintentionally 
undermine the difficult work and progress made through the budget process. 
CBAC believes the FY2027 budget represents a responsible and substantial  step toward 
addressing the City’s fiscal challenges and building a more sustainable financial future.
