For reference during meetings: 
Definitions and Mission: 
Chair: Responsible for leading and facilitating the activities of the board/commission. Their 
primary role is to preside over the members, ensure effective governance, and promote 
collaboration among board/commission members. The Chair works with staff to set the agenda, 
keeps discussions focused, and ensures decisions are made in accordance with the City’s 
objectives and applicable procedure/rules/GMC. The Chair also represents the board/commission 
externally and may have additional responsibilities depending on the organization’s by-laws and 
governance structure. 
Vice-Chair: Second in command to the Chairperson. Their role is to support and assist the chair, 
Vice-Chair may assume the Chair’s duties and preside over meetings. They often collaborate 
closely with the Chair to ensure effective governance, coordinate board activities, and represents 
the board/commission when necessary. The specific roles and responsibilities of the Vice-Chair 
may vary depending on the organization and the governing documents. 
Mission - Discuss the functions of the Commission of Disabilities to incorporate into future 
meetings (if new members are present). 
The disabilities commission shall have the following functions: 
a. To serve as an advisor to the city on issues relating to citizens with disabilities; 
b. To develop educational programs to acquaint citizens with issues affecting individuals 
with disabilities; 
c. To advise the city on the accessibility and usability of city facilities, programs and 
services for citizens with disabilities.
