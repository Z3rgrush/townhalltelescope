Old Business: 
CIP Updates: The board reviewed current capital improvement projects and discussed project 
funding, safety, and upcoming work. 
(0) MERGE Project and Funding: Staff reported that design and financial planning remain 
underway. CMGC proposals were reviewed in partnership with CDOT, and selection of a 
CMGC is expected to help refine phasing and constructability. The project includes more 
than $50 million from two major federal grants, as well as state MMOF funding. RAISE 
was identified as Rebuilding American Infrastructure with Sustainability and Equity. 
16th Street Safety: Board members raised concerns about the speed-limit transition near 
the roundabout between 8th Avenue and 11th Avenue and identified additional safety and 
accessibility concerns in the corridor. Staff indicated that Steven Younkin could review 
the concerns and discuss the design considerations with the board. 
US 34 and Weld County Road 17 Safety: The board discussed recurring crashes and 
traffic queues near the intersection. Hanna Feldmann reported that the location has 
significant regional safety concerns and is being evaluated through CDOT's updated US 
34 preservation study. Staff agreed to provide available crash information and explore a
future CDOT presentation. Weld County Road 13 and US 34 was also identified as an 
intersection to watch as traffic increases. 
• CTAB Work Plan: The board continued development of its work plan and adopted two goals.
o Community Engagement and Transportation Feedback Strategy: The board adopted
a goal to strengthen CTAB's role as a trusted advisory body by improving conmmnication
with residents, transportation stakeholders, Public Works staff, and City Council.
Discussion included community events, surveys, online engagement, neighborhood
groups, and a QR-code-based form on buses that could allow riders to submit concerns or
RSVP for meetings. Staff will coordinate with the City's community engagement team on
implementation options.
o Motion: Wendi Young moved to adopt the Community Engagement and Transportation
Feedback Strategy as a CTAB work plan goal. Jim Riesberg seconded the motion, which
passed unanimously.
Mobility Services Review and Ongoing Planning: The board discussed improving its
involvement in the ongoing review and planning of mobility services. Topics included
more frequent route refinement, coordination with the new h·ansit planner, future use of
improved ridership and stop-level data, connectivity between routes, microtransit and
other mobility options, special-event h·ansportation, and opportunities for earlier board
involvement in planning discussions.
o Motion: Jim Ries berg moved to adopt the goal of improving the board's involvement in
mobility services review and ongoing planning. Wendi Young seconded the motion,
which passed unanimously.
• Virtual Meeting Clarification: Trevor Trepanier confirmed that the virtual option for future

members.
6. New Business:
• National Week Without Driving: Jim Riesberg discussed National Week Without

revised h"ansit routes, encouraging residents and elected officials to try transit or other
h·ansportation options, coordinating with Community Engagement, collecting feedback
from new riders, and exploring free or promotional transit passes during the event.
• Project Connect: Board members discussed participating in Project Connect and
encouraging attendees to use h·ansit. Transit staff will be available at the event to provide
accmate route and service information, and board members were invited to participate or
