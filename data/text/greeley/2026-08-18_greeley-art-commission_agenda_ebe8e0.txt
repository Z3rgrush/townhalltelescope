Minutes 
The Greeley Art Commission 
May 19, 2026  
Room 201 Greeley Recreation Center  
1. The meeting was called to order by Louisa Andersen at 4:00 p.m. 
Board Present:  Molly McIntosh, Perry Bell, Louisa Andersen, Michael Haren,  
Heather Utrata, Benson Verbel, Tom Devore, Tony Miller, Eddie Mirick.  
Board Absent: Sharon Wright, Kendra Albee, Cheryl Lee.  
Citizen: Greg McIntosh 
Staff Present: Kim Snyder, Will Dillon, Megan Weiler, Brecklyn Everhart. 
2. Approval of the April 2026 minutes. 
Motion to approve the April 2026 minutes made by Molly McIntosh and seconded by 
Perry Bell.  
Motion passed unanimously.  
3. Stolen Bronze Sculpture:  
• Bronze ‘Prize Catch’ Sculpture was stolen on May 12, 2026, between 
approximately 2:45 PM and 3:30 PM; Glenmere resident reports included 
grinding noise and a white Box truck vehicle.  
• The sculpture was then sold to Rocky Mountain Recycling where they contacted 
the police and the sculpture was recovered.  
• The sculpture can be fixed and reinstalled.  
• Suggested that the Art Commission write a letter of thanks to Rocky Mountain 
Recycling and The Greeley Police department.  
Motion to send a letter to Rocky Mountain Recycling and The Greeley police 
Department made by Perry Bell and seconded by Molly McIntosh.  
Motion passed unanimously.  
4. Paint the Town Suggestions:  
• There was a discussion about adding 6 more traffic cabinet wraps, suggestions 
of new spaces were given.  
• The city’s IT department would like a mural on their new building. A consensus 
was reached to defer any art project at this site, citing fiscal responsibility and 
the building's uncertain future. No formal motion was made. 
5. Updates from Culture Manager:  
• The city has capped VSAs at 80. The Culture department has had two 
acceptances (one from UCCC, one from the Museum). The exact citywide 
number of acceptances is unknown. 
• A budget overview was presented to the Mayor and Council on May 14, 2026. 
The CPRD department budget presentation is scheduled for a work session on 
June 9, 2026. The department is seeking to close a~$600,000 gap to meet a 17% 
reduction target. 
•  Public art funding remains via CIP. A discussion is underway to fully fund 
certain staff salaries via CIP (<1%), noting that staff time is not 100% CIP-eligible 
due to other duties like gallery work. 
 
6. Project Reports:  
• Water Treatment sculpture: Artist Josh has proposed minor, positive design 
enhancements, such as a more realistic waterspout with a drop. A future 
presentation is anticipated, and Water and Sewer Board members have 
expressed interest in attending. 
• Weld Walls: The commission contributed funds to redo "the girl" mural, with 
remaining funds available for additional murals, expected to be primarily on 
alley-side walls. The poor condition of the alleys was noted, with a desire for 
improvement in tandem with the Sixteenth Street updates. 
 
7. Maintenance & Repair:  
• Cloud Tree has been repaired by Reven Swanson. Will Dillon is scheduling the 
re-installation soon.  
 
8. Other:  
• Arts Picnic:  The commission will operate a booth with a pop-up tent, legacy 
banners, and a mister for cooling.  An order form was distributed for cotton polo 
shirts, with members able to note a preference for t-shirts and choose their own 
colors. 
Meeting adjourned at 4:44 p.m.  
Next meeting will be held June 16, 2026, at the Greeley Recreation Center room 201.
