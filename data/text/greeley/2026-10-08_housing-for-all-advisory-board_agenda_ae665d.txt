FY 2026 NOFA & HAAB Funding Cycle Recap 
City of Greeley Housing Solutions 
Purpose 
The FY 2026 NOFA and Annual Action Plan process represented the ﬁrst funding cycle incorporating Housing 
for All Advisory Board (HAAB) review and funding recommendations. This recap summarizes the competitive 
funding cycle, HAAB's role, subsequent City Council action, and key lessons that will inform improvements to 
the FY 2027 process. 
FY 2026 Funding Cycle at a Glance 
3 
 
• Applications evaluated for alignment with the City's 2025–2029 Consolidated Plan, community 
impact, project readiness, organizational capacity, budget, and applicable federal requirements. 
• Applications were independently reviewed and scored by Housing Solutions staff. 
• Staff developed funding recommendations based on application review, available CDBG and HOME 
resources, eligibility, readiness, and program requirements. 
• HAAB reviewed the applications, staff recommendations, scoring, available resources, and 
Consolidated Plan priorities and developed its funding recommendation. 
• HAAB's recommendations were incorporated into the City's proposed 2026 Annual Action Plan. 
• The funding recommendations and Annual Action Plan proceeded to City Council for consideration 
and approval by resolution. 
• Following Council authorization, Housing Solutions proceeded with preparation and submission of 
the Annual Action Plan to HUD. 
Important: HAAB and City Council funding recommendations represent important steps in the federal 
funding process but do not, by themselves, constitute authorization to incur costs or begin federally funded 
work. Projects remain subject to applicable HUD requirements, environmental review, contracting, 
underwriting, and other conditions prior to expenditure of funds. 
  
 Lessons Learned from the FY 2026 HAAB Process 
The ﬁrst HAAB funding cycle generated valuable discussion and identiﬁed opportunities to strengthen the 
transparency, consistency, and effectiveness of future competitive funding decisions. 
1. Clearly Deﬁne Staff and HAAB Roles 
Future cycles should distinguish between technical/regulatory review and community-impact review. 
Housing Solutions staff should evaluate: 
 eligibility, regulatory requirements, organizational capacity, ﬁnancial capacity, project readiness, compliance 
history, expenditure/timeliness performance, and regulatory risk. 
HAAB should evaluate: 
 community impact, demonstrated community need, alignment with Consolidated Plan goals and priorities, 
and the overall public beneﬁt of the proposed project. Staff should provide a copy of the current Con Plan 
goals for review with each AAP funding cycle.  
This distinction allows HAAB to focus on community priorities while Housing Solutions maintains 
responsibility for federal regulatory and administrative review. Scores from the staff and HAAB evaluations 
should be combined to create the rank order when HAAB determines funding recommendations.  
2. Strengthen Applicant Engagement 
For FY 2027, applicants should have a structured opportunity to present their proposed project or program to 
HAAB. 
Recommended format: 
10-minute applicant presentation + 5-minute HAAB Q&A 
Multiple meetings may be scheduled depending on application volume. All applicants should receive the 
presentation and HAAB review schedule when the NOFA timeline is established. 
4 
 
3. Strengthen Conﬂict-of-Interest Procedures 
HAAB members with an actual or perceived conﬂict of interest related to an applicant or project should 
disclose the conﬂict and be recused from questioning, deliberation, scoring, recommendation, and voting 
regarding that application. Applicants are invited to view the meeting virtually as funding recommendations 
are discussed, and may be allowed to contribute comments by the meeting moderator if invited by the Board 
Chairperson.  
Recusals should be documented as part of the meeting record. 
4. Establish Clear Funding Guiding Principles 
Before the FY 2027 NOFA opens, Housing Solutions should establish Funding Guiding Principles identifying 
how funding decisions will consider: 
Community Impact + Consolidated Plan Alignment + Eligibility + Readiness + Timeliness + Regulatory 
Risk + Organizational Capacity + Prior Performance + Existing Funding Pipeline + Available Resources 
Establishing these principles before applications are reviewed will provide a consistent framework for staff, 
HAAB, applicants, and City Council. Housing Solution staff is working on completing guiding principles that 
are already incorporated in the 2025-2029 Consolidated plan as well as Standard of Operating Procedure to 
ensure every reviewer is staying consistent with the criteria and their reviewer role.  
Minimum threshold should be developed to allow applications to move into the presentation stage. If 
applications are evaluated by staff to not meet the minimum threshold, or are incomplete, the applicant and 
HAAB will be notiﬁed that the application is ineligible.  
Eligible applications will be assigned to HAAB to review electronically, or board members can request a hard 
copy version be provided by the HS department prior to the application review meeting. HAAB can develop 
questions for individual applications that will then be provided to the applicant prior to their scheduled 
presentation.  
5. Improve Application and Scoring Structure 
For FY 2027: 
• Each application will be evaluated independently on its own merits, including multiple applications 
submitted by the same organization. 
• Applicants will apply for the overall project or program, rather than determining the federal funding 
source.  
• Housing Solutions will determine whether CDBG, HOME, and eligible expenses are appropriate 
based on eligibility, regulatory requirements, project structure, risk, and available funding. This may 
may occur at any time during the funding cycle up to the negotiation of the agreement.  
• Staff and HAAB scoring criteria will reﬂect their respective technical and community impact 
responsibilities. 
• Each application will receive a summary of the evaluation including staff and HAAB scores and 
comments.  
• Prior funding and project pipeline information will be considered as part of readiness and 
organizational-capacity review rather than automatically providing additional points solely because a 
project was previously unfunded. 
• Material differences between scoring results and ﬁnal funding recommendations should include a 
documented rationale. 
5 
 
6. Improve Technology and Information Access 
The FY 2026 process demonstrated the need for a grant-management system designed for competitive 
funding administration. 
Housing Solutions is evaluating EUNA and Foundant and should pursue a system capable of supporting the 
entire grant lifecycle rather than application collection alone. 
Applications provided to HAAB should be complete, consistently organized, and readable, with standardized 
staff summaries identifying funding request, project description, score, eligibility, readiness, regulatory 
considerations, and staff recommendation. 
 FY 2027 Goal 
The objective for FY 2027 is to build on the experience of the ﬁrst HAAB funding cycle by establishing a 
process that is transparent, equitable, consistent, technically sound, and understandable to applicants, 
HAAB, City Council, and the public while ensuring that CDBG and HOME resources are awarded to eligible 
projects capable of producing measurable community outcomes. 
To allow enough time for the evaluation, review, and development of recommendations to City Council, the 
following timeline is recommended:  
December 2026-Mid-January 2027: City NOFA released, application portal open for 45 days 
Mid- February-Mid- March: 7 days for staff review, 7 days for response from applicant for 
clariﬁcation/completeness check. Application packets available for review by Board members 
April HAAB Meeting- Discussion by board to determine questions/clariﬁcation requests by HAAB to 
applicants 
May- Application Presentations (multiple afternoon/evening sessions – 10 min present/5 min Q&A for each 
application)- presentations should be recorded 
June HAAB Meeting- Determine funding recommendations (extended monthly meeting time where 
necessary). Staff assembles and submits CC agenda packet/presentation 
July- City Council meeting presentation 
August- Annual Action Plan submitted to HUD with City Council approval.

Resumen del Ciclo de Financiamiento FY 2026: NOFA y HAAB 
City of Greeley Housing Solutions 
Propósito 
El ciclo de ﬁnanciamiento FY 2026 representó el primer ciclo competitivo que incorporó la 
revisión y recomendaciones del HAAB. Este resumen presenta el ciclo, el rol del HAAB, la 
acción del Concejo Municipal y las lecciones para mejorar el proceso FY 2027. 
Resumen del Ciclo FY 2026 
Solicitudes evaluadas según alineación con el Plan Consolidado 2025-2029, impacto 
comunitario, preparación del proyecto, capacidad organizativa, presupuesto y requisitos 
federales. 
Las solicitudes fueron revisadas y puntuadas por el personal de Housing Solutions. 
El personal desarrolló recomendaciones basadas en la revisión, recursos disponibles 
CDBG y HOME, elegibilidad y requisitos. 
HAAB revisó solicitudes, recomendaciones, puntajes, recursos y prioridades del Plan 
Consolidado y desarrolló su recomendación. 
Las recomendaciones del HAAB fueron incorporadas al Plan de Acción Anual 2026. 
El plan procedió al Concejo Municipal para consideración y aprobación. 
Tras autorización del Concejo, el plan fue enviado a HUD. 
Importante: Las recomendaciones del HAAB y el Concejo no constituyen autorización para 
comenzar trabajo ﬁnanciado. Los proyectos siguen sujetos a requisitos HUD, revisión 
ambiental, contratación, análisis y condiciones.

cciones Aprendidas FY 2026 
El primer ciclo generó discusiones valiosas y oportunidades para mejorar transparencia, 
consistencia y efectividad. 
1. Deﬁnir claramente roles del personal y HAAB
El personal debe evaluar elegibilidad, requisitos, capacidad, historial, desempeño y riesgo 
regulatorio. 
HAAB debe evaluar impacto comunitario, necesidad demostrada, alineación con el Plan 
Consolidado y beneﬁcio público. 
Esta distinción permite a HAAB enfocarse en prioridades comunitarias mientras el 
personal gestiona requisitos federales. 
Los puntajes deben combinarse para crear orden de recomendación. 
2. Fortalecer participación de solicitantes
Los solicitantes deben tener oportunidad de presentar ante HAAB. 
Formato recomendado: 
Presentación de 10 minutos + 5 minutos de preguntas 
Múltiples reuniones según el volumen de solicitudes. 
3. Fortalecer procedimientos de conﬂicto de interés
Miembros con conﬂicto deben abstenerse de participar. 
Las abstenciones deben documentarse. 
4. Establecer principios claros de ﬁnanciamiento
Antes del NOFA 2027 se deben establecer principios que consideren impacto, elegibilidad, 
preparación, riesgo, capacidad, desempeño y recursos. 
Estos principios proveen un marco consistente. 
Debe establecerse un umbral mínimo. 
Solicitudes elegibles serán asignadas a HAAB para revisión. 
HAAB desarrollará preguntas para cada solicitante.

HUD PROGRAM YEAR CHANGE 
Current cycle: January 1–December 31 
New cycle: PY 2027 will run July 1, 2027–June 30, 2028. 
The transition requires the 2027 funding process to move earlier so 
application review, HAAB recommendations, Annual Action Plan 
development, contracting, and project readiness can align with the 
July 1 start of the new program year. 
What this means for HAAB: The revised 2027 Board calendar is 
structured to support the earlier funding-cycle review and 
recommendation process.
