Title:  
Discussion about Commission letter to City Council regarding the Proposed Cuts to the City of 
Greeley Museums 
 
Summary:  
At the Commission meeting on July 20th, the Commission decided to send a letter to City Council in 
support of the Museums. Commissioner Clough offered to draft a letter for the Commission to 
review at a meeting. At a special meeting of the Commission on July 27th the Commission will 
review, discuss, and finalize the letter for it to be sent to Council by early August.

To the City Council" 
    We are writing you because of our serious concern about the proposed cuts to the Greeley 
Museums.  These reductions would affect the general public, and would also impact our own particular 
area of responsibility. 
    Greeley has a proud history, a history reflected in the History Museum, the Meeker House, 
Centennial Village, and the Plumb Fsrm.  Through exhibits, public lectures, archival research,  walking 
tours, and demonstrations at Centennial Villager, generations of citizens (including, especially, school 
children) have learned of our community's rich and unique heritage.  These links with the past are 
threatened by the drastic nature of the proposed budget cuts, particularly the severe reductions in staff 
that have been recommended. 
For the Historic Preservation Commission to carry out our mandated tasks, we 
depend on the city museums.  The Historic Preservation specialist, Elizabeth Kellums, uses museum 
archives to research information for her presentations to the Commission.  The register of historic 
buildings could not be maintained adequately without the assistance of professional Museum staff.  At 
least as important, the Museums are responsible for the protection and care of sixty-eight historic 
buildings owned by the city.  Geoffrey Havens, former Historic Sites Supervisor for the Greeley Museums, 
has warned our commission that the proposed cuts threaten the city's stewardship of its rich collection 
of public structures.  What message would be conveyed to private owners of fine old homes if the city 
should shirk its responsibility to its own historic buildings? 
    We urge you to consider carefully the proposal to severely reduce the budget for Greeley's 
Museums.  Care for our heritage is crucial to the integrity of our community
