13.
Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper welcomed the statewide Ponderosa Mountain Pine Beetle Task Force along with Commissioners Dan Williams from Teller County as well as Jody Shadduck-McNally from Larimer County as well as other experts in this field this past week. This task force considered about 20 recommendations and worked to further refine them to tackle this issue in the hardest hit areas, especially along the front range. Commissioner Dahlkemper noted Jefferson County is one of the hardest hit areas affected by the beetle outbreak. Commissioners had an opportunity to meet with the Arvada City Council recently to discuss issues around wildfire resiliency. Commissioner Dahlkemper discussed changes in the human services department’s organization and potential budgetary impacts. Commissioner Dahlkemper gave a shout out to Jeffco employees following last week’s Employee Excellence Awards Ceremony. Commissioner Dahlkemper wanted to thank the Foothills Regional Housing and West Metro Housing for their work on the issue of affordable housing. These organizations have collaborated with the county to provide vouchers to keep families stable and provide affordable housing. Commissioner Dahlkemper noted that Jefferson County was a leader on this issue nationally and Foothills Regional Housing Authority, led by Lori Rosendahl, has been recognized for their pace and number of housing vouchers provided. Commissioner Dahlkemper mentioned she attended a 5k at Dinosaur Ridge with Commissioner Kerr this past weekend. Commissioner Dahlkemper shared the Gateway to the Rockies Opioid Council held their monthly meeting yesterday and shared they have a new data dashboard to ensure transparency in the distribution of the opioid settlement dollars in the three counties that make up region 10. Commissioner Dahlkemper noted that the council members are taking feedback to further refine this dashboard as well. Finally, Commissioner Dahlkemper shared there is an upcoming Veterans Event on Friday that both herself and Commissioner Kerr will be attending. This event is a great opportunity for veterans in our community to find resources, assistance, and additional help that they may need. More information about this event can be obtained by emailing
[email protected]
.
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr shared he met with our Jefferson County Legislators last week to discuss priorities of the county as well as goals moving forward. Commissioner Kerr attended the Head Start Roundtable with Congresswoman Pettersen at the Arvada Head Start site along with parents, teachers, and administrators. At this event, Congresswoman Pettersen shared a letter she is submitting to the Department of Human Services in regards to a potential change to the Head Start Administration as well as requirements for families and the potential negative effects these changes could have. Commissioner Kerr attended a land dedication to Arvada’s Gold Strike Park which was attended also by Native American Officials from the Cheyenne Nations, including Governor Reggie Wassana who led a ground blessing, the Arvada City Council, and other public lands officials.  Commissioner Kerr noted we had great weather for last week’s Employee Excellence Awards where several Jefferson County employees were recognized including a 50-year employee. Commissioner Kerr discussed a recent event with the Metro Housing Authority, Foothills Regional Housing Authority, Arvada Housing Authority, local vendors, and area landlords and thanked them for their work to increase housing stability and affordability of housing in Jefferson County. Commissioner Kerr attended the Ace Boot Camp at Red Rocks Community College where local students can participate in competitions using their technical skills. Students from RRCC as well as Warren Tech participated in this event. Commissioner Kerr thanked Jeffco Open Space and the City of Lakewood for hosting a National Public Lands Day event at Bear Creek Lake Park, a Lakewood City Park and expressed the importance of protecting public lands. Commissioner Kerr shared that he attended the inaugural Stego Stampede 5k at Dinosaur Ridge along with Commissioner Dahlkemper and Dinosaur Ridge Executive Director Jeff Lamontagne as well as the Dinosaur Ridge Board Members. This 5k is a run/walk event where people can dress up in costumes and was not only attended by families, but pets as well. Commissioner Kerr shared more information on this event can be found on his and Commissioner Dahlkemper’s social media accounts. Commissioner Kerr also wanted to note that Dinosaur Ridge is one of the top destinations in Jefferson County for scientific and cultural experiences and hosts over a quarter million visitors and over 100 school groups each year. This site is nationally and internationally one of the highest ranked dinosaur track sites, and the stegosaurus was first identified from fossils and tracks found there. Commissioner Kerr shared he will be attending the Colorado Opioid Abatement Conference in Copper Mountain tomorrow. This organization was spearheaded by Attorney General Phil Weiser several years ago to handle the opioid settlement funds and Commissioner Kerr has served on this council for the past three years. Commissioner Kerr noted Commissioner Dahlkemper serves on the Gateway to the Rockies Opioid Commission, and these organizations do such a great job, Colorado is a national model for other states. Commissioner Kerr invited veterans or individuals who support veterans in need to attend the upcoming event at Red Rocks Community College this Friday.
County Manager:
County Manager Laura E. Aldrete shared that she enjoyed the opportunity to meet and recognize the Jeffco staff at the Employee Appreciation Event last week. Additionally, County Manager Aldrete had a tour of the Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport this week which was led by Erick Dahl and plans to meet with North Metro Fire Station’s Chief next week for a tour of their facilities. County Manager Aldrete shared she is open to touring additional departments within the county to gain a better understanding of their needs.

No reports.
