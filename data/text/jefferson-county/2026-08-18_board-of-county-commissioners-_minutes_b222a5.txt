1.
0521_001.pdf
The Commissioners proclaimed August 31st, 2026 International Overdose Awareness Day and spoke of local organizations such as the Jefferson County Opioid Council and the Gateway to the Rockies Opioid Council's work to advocate for mental health and addiction resources. Dr. Annette Cannon spoke on this day's 25th anniversary and the awareness that is still needed to continue to support those in our community.
4.
Proclamation
1.
0522_001.pdf
The Commissioners proclaimed September 2026 as Suicide Awareness and Prevention Month and shared local organizations such as the Jefferson Center and Second Wing Fund which are available for counseling and support to those in our community in need of these resources. Dr. Annette Cannon spoke of the toll of suicides within our community and the importance of utilizing resources available, including the 988 help line to support in the prevention of suicide.
5.
Recognition of Service
1.
Donna Walker Distinguished Service to Jeffco Proclamation.pdf
The Commissioners recognized Donna Walker's distinguished service to the Jefferson County Public Library over the past 14 years and recognized her for her service to public libraries over the past 32 years. The Commissioners thanked Donna Walker for her leadership as the Executive Director of the Jefferson County Public Library and thanked her for the legacy she has left. Donna Walker spoke and wished to recognize those in the community and the Jeffco and JCPL staff for their continued support throughout the years.

15.
Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper welcomed the Pine Beetle Task Force to Jefferson County. The Task Force is being hosted by Jefferson County in the building today and will hear how Jefferson County has been working to mitigate the damage caused by the pine beetle with presentations by Jefferson County Parks and Open Space. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Teller County Commissioner Dan Williams, Larimer County Commissioner Jody Shadduck-McNally, and Representative Lesley Smith who serve on the task force for attending this meeting today. Commissioner Dahlkemper attended the Mountain Music Festival this past weekend and enjoyed visiting with various organizations at this event including Evergreen Sustainability Alliance Foundation, the Wooden Hawk Foundation, Resilience 1220, Evergreen Chamber, and the Evergreen Middle School "Green Team." Commissioner Dahlkemper also shared she met with Conifer business owners and shared county updates including highlights from the Gateway Partnership for Early Childhood Education, wildfire resilience efforts, and about our new County Manager who will start on Monday. Commissioner Dahlkemper provided a shout out to our Head Start Early Childhood Education Team for their successful kickoff celebration and for their work with children and families within our community.
Commissioner Zenzinger:
Commissioner Zenzinger congratulated the Gateway Partnership for Early Childhood Education for their successful kickoff. Commissioner Zenzinger shared the ECC partnership is a triad partnership shared with Clear Creek and Gilpin Counties and hopes to continue to strengthen this multi-county partnership in the future. Commissioner Zenzinger thanked these neighboring counties for their support and attendance at this kickoff event. Commissioner Zenzinger shared an update on the Jefferson County Council on Aging which was originally formed to help those who are aging utilize assistance available to seniors in the county. Commissioner Zenzinger shared several statistics that were provided at this meeting including that there are 84,596 Medicare recipients in Jefferson County, 12.7% of these are 60+ years of age, 8% have disabilities, and 6,864 of the Medicare recipients include seniors in the home who are also receiving SNAP benefits. Commissioner Zenzinger also noted that 976 individuals are recipients of the Old Age Pension Fund in Jefferson County, which means they have an income below the federal poverty level which is between $500-900 monthly income. Commissioner Zenzinger thanked the Human Services staff for this very informative presentation and for all they do to support the citizens of Jefferson County.
County Manager:
Acting County Manager Kate Newman shared that the Facilities Management Team, led by Mark Danner, was selected to be featured in Blueprint Magazine later this fall and took photos for this recognition this morning.
County Attorney:
No reports.
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr shared that the Open Space Volunteer Barbeque is coming up as well as an event to recognize Donna Walker's contribution to the JCPL and honor her in her retirement. Commissioner Kerr thanked Kate Newman for filling in at the Board of County Commissioner's Hearings while the County Manager position had been vacant and noted our new County Manager, Laura E. Aldrete will be in this role beginning on Monday. Commissioner Kerr wished to remind everyone that schools are back in session and to be mindful of the increased pedestrians, bikes, and cars out and about with the many activities that come with the beginning of the school year and encouraged everyone to be safe. Finally, Commissioner Kerr took a moment to read a final proclamation into the record for SMART Week. SMART Week is August 24-28, 2026, and is an opportunity to recognize responsible gun ownership and prevention of gun violence. SMART, an acronym for the principles of this week, stands for: Secure all guns in your homes and vehicles; Model responsible behavior around guns; Ask about unsecured guns in other homes; Recognize the role of guns in suicide; and Tell your peers to Be SMART. Commissioner Kerr presented this proclamation to Pat Rottschaefer who provided public comment earlier about gun violence and provided information about the Be SMART organization.
