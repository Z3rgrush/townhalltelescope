Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper shared that she attended the Lakewood Town Hall along with Senator Cutter, Commissioner Kerr, and Brittany Pettersen to discuss the upcoming election and thanked those who attended this event. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Commissioner Zenzinger for chairing the Jefferson County Wildfire Commission, Diane Sabo for her role with the Wildfire Task Force, Senator Cutter, Director Mike Morgan of the Colorado Division of Fire Prevention & Control, Molly Mowry and Kelly Johnston from the Community Wildfire Planning Center, and other community leaders for their support. Commissioner Dahlkemper discussed the progress of these commissions over the years. Commissioner Dahlkemper also thanked Jeffco voters for approving 1A to address wildfire resiliency along with staff members Deb Churchill and Sarah Roddis for their work behind the scenes. Commissioners along with County Manager Laura Aldrete and the Jeffco EDC joined Lockheed Martin and area business leaders at the Denver Metro Chamber Site Selector event. At this event Lockheed Martin unveiled Project Emerald, a major expansion investment in operations in Jefferson and Douglas Counties. This expansion will add 1,250 new jobs in Colorado and roughly 800 of these new jobs will be in Jefferson County. Additionally, at this event Lockheed Martin announced they will invest further in the Waterton Campus. Commissioner Dahlkemper shared that she spoke at Alameda Connects to share updates on affordable housing, investments, budget updates, and Jeffco EDC’s study to strengthen economic competitiveness. Commissioner Dahlkemper wished to thank the Insurance Institute for Building & Home Safety (IBHS) for their efforts in helping reduce costs of homeowners insurance through their home wildfire resiliency research and testing. Commissioner Dahlkemper recently served on a panel with Representative Brown, Director Morgan, along with the Rocky Mountain Insurance Information Association and thanked the IBHS for hosting this panel. Finally, Commissioner Dahlkemper shared the Gateway to the Rockies Opioid Council (GROC) was recent featured as a national case study from Opioid Settlement Works. Commissioner Dahlkemper wished to recognize the work of GROC along with Jefferson County’s Cassie Heykoop, Chelsea Shore-Miller for their work with prevention and education in our communities.
Commissioner Zenzinger:
Commissioner Zenzinger share that on Friday there will be a Metro Area County Commissioners (MACC) meeting which she will assume the role of chair for this organization. At this upcoming meeting they will host the Governor and his team and discuss their office’s policies and their effects on counties. Commissioner Zenzinger joined local leaders at the Jefferson County Power of Partnership (POP) meeting to welcome community  advocates who are coming together to discuss challenges they’re facing as well as how they are addressing these challenges. Commissioner Zenzinger wished to thank Jefferson County Human Services for hosting this meeting. Commissioner Zenzinger shared that she has been appointed by Colorado Counties, Inc. (CCI) to fill a position on the Welfare Allocation Committee and she looks forward to joining them at their next meeting at the end of this month. Commissioner Zenzinger thanked Mary Berg and the Human Services team for their orientation towards this new appointment and acknowledged that she is looking forward to this opportunity. Commissioner Zenzinger joined Congresswoman Brittany Pettersen at an Election Integrity & Security event. Commissioner Zenzinger congratulated Jefferson County Clerk & Recorder Amanda Gonzalez for putting together an informative event, Q&A session, and tour of the election’s facility in Jefferson County. Commissioner Zenzinger shared that following this event Jefferson County takes election integrity seriously and has many rigorous safeguards and processes in place to ensure security is maintained. Commissioner Zenzinger shared some of those safeguards such as logic and accuracy testing, audits, bipartisan teams, as well as post-election canvass and certifications. Commissioner Zenzinger reminded everyone to turn in their ballots as soon as possible and recommended if you are mailing your ballot to mail at least 1 week prior to the election or drop it off at a ballot box. She also reminded voters not to forget to sign and date your envelope so your vote can be counted. Commissioner Zenzinger also asked that if you have any questions or issues with your ballots, please contact Jeffco Elections at
[email protected]
or call (303)271-8111. Commissioner Zenzinger noted that the Commissioners took positions on ballot initiatives, and these can be found on our website. Also, Commissioner Zenzinger shared that information on the various judges on the ballot can be found utilizing the independent Colorado Judicial Performance Commission’s evaluations which are on their website judicialperformance.colorado.gov. Finally, Commissioner Zenzinger wished to congratulate the Jefferson County Librarians on their five different awards and recognitions this year. Commissioners recently attended the Library All Staff Conference and enjoyed the opportunity to interact with our Jefferson County Librarians.
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr recently attended the annual Colorado Opioid Abatement Council Conference. This council was originally created by Attorney General Phil Weiser and has accomplished many great things. Commissioner Kerr also noted some of these positive outcomes were recognized on Last Week Tonight with John Oliver as a state that is “doing it right” when it comes to their work over the last three years with the opioid settlement and using them wisely to provide a greater benefit to all. Commissioner Kerr congratulated Cannonball Creek Brewery for winning several awards at the Great American Beer Festival this past week. Commissioner Kerr along with Congresswoman Petterson visited Conifer High School to discuss school safety with education professionals, parents, and community members. Following this event, he also attended Evergreen to discuss with local business owners about emergency management and prevention. Commissioner Kerr attended the Lockheed Martin Emerald event and wanted to note that while this expansion will mean more jobs, construction, and commuting traffic, Jefferson County will work closely with residents to ensure that this project ends up being a positive step for Jefferson County Residents. Commissioner Kerr attended the Evergreen and Conifer Chamber’s meetings as well as attended the Renegade Burrito Ribbon Cutting last Tuesday. Renegade Burrito is located at 220 Union Blvd in Lakewood. Commissioner Kerr shared he attended the Mountain Resource Center’s fundraising gala and shared the recent news that their CEO Sarah Kinzer will be retiring. Commissioner Kerr thanked Ms. Kinzer for her service to the Mountain Resource Center. Commissioner Kerr shared he will be attending the Jeffco Schools Career Expo as well as the Jeffco History Commission’s Hall of Fame annual events in the coming weeks. Commissioner Kerr wanted to remind everyone that the Unified Land Code is in the process of being revised and is still open for public comment until October 18
th
. Finally, Commissioner Kerr share that the annual Drive Clean Colorado Summit and Expo will be held at Mile High Stadium on October 22
nd
.

County Manager Aldrete attended a tour of the North Metro Fire Facility near RMMA and thanked the Fire Chief for this tour.
