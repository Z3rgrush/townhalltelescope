Evergreen HS Shooting Anniversary Recognition.pdf
Evergreen High School Shooting Recognitions
Sheriff Reggie Marinelli and David Clark, a representative from Congresswoman Brittany Pettersen, spoke on the one year anniversary of the Evergreen High School Shooting and how our community has come together since this tragic event. The Commissioners recognized the Evergreen Community as well as two students that were critically injured that day. The Commissioners recognized the students and staff that were there that day, knowing healing does not follow a timeline, the impact continues long after that day. The Commissioners are grateful for all first responders who responded, as well as the community partners and neighbors that opened for refuge to students, including Evergreen Library and Evergreen Park and Recreation District. The Commissioners stand with the Evergreen community remembering what happened, recognizing how far the community has come, and the continued work on healing.

13.
Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper stated that she was excited about the expansion of the Head Start program in Lakewood, while the program is under scrutiny across the country, Jefferson County Commissioners are fully committed to supporting the Head Start program. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked West Metro Fire District for remembering the lives lost and forever transformed 25 years ago, on the 25
th
anniversary of 9/11. The Commissioners had the opportunity visit Conifer's Chamber of Commerce meeting, which was director Beth Schneider's last meeting. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Beth Schneider for being a tremendous advocate for the mountain community and welcomed Leah Peterson, the incoming director. Commissioner Dahlkemper also attended the 2026 legislative barbecue alongside Congresswoman Brittany Pettersen as well as State legislators, candidates who are running for office, and non-profits.  Commissioner Dahlkemper mentioned they have met with the Jefferson County Counsel on Aging and shared how Jefferson County supports older adults.  Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Jefferson County Public Affairs for their recent Engage Jeffco podcast that focused on mental health and resiliency in the Evergreen community. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked the Public Affairs team, panelist, and community members who joined the in-person town hall, where they discussed wildlife urban interface codes, improved forest health and other strategies the county has put in place to build greater wildfire resiliency. Commissioner Dahlkemper attended the quarterly Municipal County meeting, where the commissioners and mayors across Jefferson County meet. Lastly Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked the Open Space Advisory Council for the great session on the Jefferson County Green Print where they discussed how Jefferson County is being good stewards of our trails, and how the county is thinking related to forest improvements. Commissioner Dahlkemper stated it was good to have the Open Space Advisory Council weigh in on several issues related to forest improvement. Commissioner Dahlkemper reminded everyone about the Conifer Town Hall and hoped to see the community there.
Commissioner Zenzinger:
Commissioner Zenzinger mentioned at the Head Start Committee now called the Parenting Community Policy Council, they discussed some rule changes proposed by the Federal administration. The counsel analyzed several proposed rule changes and is bringing them to the Board to investigate them, and with the Jefferson County Legislative Director Emily Lauck they can uplift some of the concerns expressed by the community, parents and Head Start Staff during the public comment period of the process. Commissioner Zenzinger also attended the opening of the Head Start location in Lakewood, which was added last year. Commissioner Zenzinger thanked Sheriff Marinelli and staff for hosting the Annual Legislative Dinner. This year’s dinner was focused on learning the tools used by the Sheriff’s Department that help solve crimes and keep the community safe. Commissioner Zenzinger thanked Jefferson County Open Space Committee volunteers and the good job they do. The committee had conversations about Jefferson County’s Green Print, what is working and what we can focus on more closely in the coming year. Last night Commissioner Zenzinger attended an emergency meeting for Jefferson Center for Mental Health where they discussed  the withdrawal of the federal funding and their pathway forward to continue to support our community
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr mentioned that Jefferson County had a very successful in-person town hall and thanked the staff who helped prepare for this event. The next in-person town hall is tentatively set for November 19, 2026. Commissioner Kerr thanked Beth Schnider from the Conifer Chamber of Commerce for being a great partner with the County. Commissioner Kerr noted that Thursday September 17, 2026, PorchLight is having their annual Gala. PorchLight is a nonprofit organization that works with local government and other nonprofits that helps make the process of interacting with law enforcement as a crime victim as low-stress as possible. Commissioner Kerr stated that last Thursday he took a tour of the Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport with Councilman Jack Johnson, and representatives from the City of Westminster. Commissioner Kerr attended the Colorado Librarians luncheon and awards and congratulated the retired Executive Director Donna Walker on the award she received. Commissioner Kerr attended Conifer High School football game with Sheriff Marinelli, Undersheriff Mike Prange, and several deputies. The Sheriff’s Department works with an organization meant to highlight 9/11 25 years later, while also honoring our first responders. Commissioner Kerr noted that this Sunday at Clement Park is the 25
th
annual Second Wind Fund 5k. Second Wind is a nonprofit that started when the community got together after there were 4 deaths by suicide at Green Mountain High School in one semester. Commissioner Kerr noted that in their 25 years Second Wind Fund is very successful. The Second Wind Fund make mental health services available to students here in Colorado but also has a reach nationwide. Commissioner Kerr highlighted September 26,
2026, is National Public Lands Volunteer Day. Jefferson County Open Space will have volunteer events in the morning, and a big get together that will be held at Bear Creek Lake Park for the volunteers for Jefferson County and other counties.
County Manager:
County Manager Aldrete attended many of the events and activities that have already been mentioned by the Commissioners and stated that it has been a reintroduction to Jefferson County and is helping them familiarize themselves with parts of Jefferson County they are less familiar with. County Manager Aldrete stated they will be attending the Wheat Ridge High School 5000, a 5k, this Sunday September 20, 2026.

No reports.
