14.
Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper shared about a tour of the Clear Creek watershed that occurred last week noting that Clear Creek and its tributary serve as the primary source of drinking water for more than 450,000 residents in Golden, Westminster, and Thornton. The watershed spans four counties with the upper watershed nestled in the mountains west of Golden. Commissioner Dahlkemper also shared that last week she and others met for three forest health improvement projects in Jefferson and Clear Creek Counties which are designed not only to save lives and homes, but to protect the water supply during a wildfire effort that includes thinning trees and removing woody debris. Commissioner Dahlkemper noted this project represents strategic, thoughtful collaboration that’s grounded in science among multiple partners, including our teams here at Jefferson County. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Tony Auciello with Jefferson County Parks and Open Space for a tremendous job during the tour. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked former Evergreen Chamber president, Nancy Judge, for her presentation about the impact of wildfire on our local economies. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked everyone who is involved in this partnership. Commissioner Dahlkemper shared that Jefferson County welcomed the Ponderosa Pine Beetle Task Force recently and noted they are working on the outbreak that is affecting counties along the front range areas, also noting if you drive the 285 corridor you can see the devastating impact of these beetles on our trees. Commissioner Dahlkemper shared this Task Force is going to meet again in the coming weeks to continue their efforts.
Commissioner Zenzinger:
Commissioner Zenzinger wanted to extend gratitude to Kami Welch and the JeffCo EDC team for organizing a good Pure Community Leadership Exchange (PCLE) this year in Tucson, Arizona. Commissioners got to learn quite a bit from the local business leaders there in Tucson and how they are addressing their workforce pipeline, talent development issues, and how they integrate their planning countywide. Commissioners were able to visit Pima County Community College as well as Arizona University and learn about their focus on getting their workforce primed and ready to meet the needs of their community. Commissioner Zenzinger shared it was a good opportunity to hear and listen to similar challenges we face in Jeffco. Commissioner Zenzinger also attended the workforce convening meeting last week and noted that Colorado is currently in the process of overhauling its higher education and workforce systems through a bill that was passed last session. The bill, House Bill 1317, aims to consolidate more than 20 divisions across 7 state agencies into a unified learner-centered talent development network. Commissioner Zenzinger shared the bill also created a 27-member Transition Advisory Committee (TAC) that serves as the planning body which meets this week. Commissioner Zenzinger wished to extend gratitude to the Westminster Chamber’s Government Affairs Committee for attending the Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport tour yesterday. Commissioner Zenzinger also thanked Eric Dahl and his team as well as Abel Montoya and his team for providing valuable information on this tour.
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr attended Donna Walker, the executive director of the Jefferson County Public Libraries, retirement party and shared it was great to see everyone in attendance. Commissioner Kerr attended the Jeffco Parks and Open Space Volunteer Appreciation Event as well and noted it was great to see the hundreds of volunteers together to enjoy a meal. Commissioner Kerr volunteered with Wild Aware and worked to support the wildlife by removing barbed wire along Deer Creek Road along with Open Space staff. Finally, Commissioner Kerr shared that Labor Day is a county holiday and hopes everyone takes some time on Labor Day to appreciate the work throughout history to get this holiday. Commissioner Kerr noted we will not have hearings next Tuesday but will have a Town Hall on September 9.
