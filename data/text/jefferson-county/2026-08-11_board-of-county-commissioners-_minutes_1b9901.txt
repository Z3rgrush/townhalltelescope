12.
Reports
Commissioner Dahlkemper:
Commissioner Dahlkemper recognized the life of Evergreen leader Linda Kirkpatrick who recently passed away. Commissioner Dahlkemper attended the Colorado Counties Inc Front Range District Meeting and thanked Commissioner Stolzmann and Boulder County for hosting the meeting. She also thanked the CSU extension partners for their work and CCI for facilitating this meeting. She also attended the Wheat Ridge Carnation Festival; this year’s festival marked its 57-year anniversary. She congratulated Jefferson County Treasurer Jerry DiTullio for his role as the grand marshal during the parade. Commissioner Dahlkemper recognized Lieutenant Governor Dianne Primavera for leading the creation of the new Colorado Lieutenant Government exhibit at the State Capitol. The unveiling of the exhibit was attended by the State Governor, Lieutenant Governor, and former Lieutenant Governors Donna Lynne and Joe Garcia. Commissioner Dahlkemper gave a shoutout to the Jefferson County Transportation and Road & Bridge team for their work with the Commissioners and with Shelly Cooke, the District 2 Transportation Commissioner, on the topic of increased traffic in Kittredge. Commissioner Dahlkemper attended the Evergreen Chamber of Commerce monthly breakfast where a herd of three hundred elk caused a traffic jam before the meeting. Commissioner Dahlkemper thanked Sheriff Marinelli for the Commissioner’s annual tour of the jail last week. Commissioner Dahlkemper highlighted her interactions with the two jail K9s Vision and Reba.
Commissioner Zenzinger:
Commissioner Zenzinger recognized Sheriff Marinelli for the Commissioner’s jail tour. She noted the two K9s in the Sheriff’s Office can also be utilized for searching for missing people. Commissioner Zenzinger participated in several meetings concerning the Jeffco Housing Blueprint. She noted that they are in the final stages of setting up the three-year plan. Commissioner Zenzinger attended the Wheat Ridge Urban Renewal Authority Meeting at Wheat Ridge which is also known as Renewal Wheat Ridge. During the meeting, Patrick Goff, the City Manager, gave a presentation on developments in Wheat Ridge. Commissioner Zenzinger remarked on the city's upcoming ballot measure 2J Bond that would provide funds for some of these innovative improvements. Commissioner Zenzinger encouraged the public to view these developments on the Wheat Ridge website. Commissioner Zenzinger attended the Head Start Parent and Community Advisory Board Meeting. She noted it was the first year that every preschool slot had been offered to a child before the start of the school year and she reported that the group was seeking a grant for supplemental funds for healthy eating and nutrition. Commissioner Zenzinger noted that this evening there will be a celebration held at the Arbor House at Maple Grove Park in Golden for the Early Childhood Council. Commissioner Zenzinger gave a shout out to the City of Wheat Ridge for their Carnation Festival. She congratulated Mayor Stites for his first mayor reception.
Commissioner Kerr:
Commissioner Kerr participated in the Wheat Ridge Carnation Festival Parade and shared a major highlight of the parade was seeing former mayor Bud Starker at the beginning of the route. Commissioner Kerr thanked Sheriff Marinelli and her team for taking the Commissioners on their annual jail tour. Commissioner Kerr used the Westracks free shuttle to Matthew/Winters Park this weekend. He reminded the public that the shuttle will end service after Labor Day weekend and is currently scheduled for free trips on Saturday, Sunday and Holidays. Commissioner Kerr mentioned that he ran into Ean Thomas Tafoya of GreenLatinos at Matthew/Winters Park. Ean introduced the concept for the Westracks shuttle service to Commissioner Kerr and Council member Darrell Watson. Commissioner Kerr encouraged the community to attend the Evergreen Mountain Music Festival this Sunday, August 16th. Commissioner Kerr thanked Donna Walker, Jeffco Libraries Executive Director, for her service to Jefferson County and congratulated her on her upcoming retirement. Commissioner Kerr noted that the annual Open Space Volunteers Barbecue is this weekend. Commissioner Kerr reminded everyone to be safe on the roads as Jefferson County schools start next week which will increase the overall traffic in the county. He encouraged everyone to be cautious and to adhere to the three feet law when passing bicyclist.
