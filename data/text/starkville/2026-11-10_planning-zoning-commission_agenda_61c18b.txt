The purpose of this report is to provide information regarding a rezoning request by 
Jeff Knighton on behalf of Lakeside Student Living to rezone part of an undeveloped 
lot located on the north side of Old Hwy 12 approximately 285’ west of the Carter 
Road intersection. The property is located within a C (Commercial) zoning district 
with the property number 103J-08-001.23. Please see attachments 1- 5. 
 
REASON FOR AMENDMENT 
 
Pursuant to Miss. Code Ann. § 17 -1-17 and the Unified Development Code, the 
Official Zoning District Map may be amended only when one or more of the 
following conditions prevail: 
 
A. Error. There is an error in the Code and a need to correct the error. 
B. Change in conditions. A change in the neighborhood has occurred to such 
an extent as to justify the proposed rezoning and there is a public need for 
the proposed rezoning. 
 
The subject rezoning request is based on Error. The applicant is planning a multi-
dwelling residential development involving the aggregation of a parcel adjacent to 
Carter Boulevard  with an existing parcel directly east of Lakeside Student Living 
apartments. Upon aggregation, the new lot would include split zoning , part C 
(Commercial) and part MDU -20 (Multi -Dwelling Unit Residential) , creating a 
mapping inconsistency. 
 
Section 4.3 of the Unified Development Code requires that “district boundary lines 
shall, to the greatest possible extent, follow lot lines, the centerline of streets, alleys, 
or highways, or natural boundaries.” Leaving the zoning unchanged would conflict 
with this provision, resulting in an error on the Official Zoning Map.  The proposed 
amendment corrects that error and ensures zoning boundaries align with property 
lines as required by ordinance.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION 
 
Thomas Stewart, on behalf of Acadian Driftwood LLC, requests a Special Exception to 
develop seven dwelling units on approximately 0.70 acres located directly west of 103 Ernest 
H. Jones Jr. Drive. The property is vacant, zoned TN -E (Traditional Neighborhoo d–Existing), 
and identified as parcel number 118O-00-294.00. 
 
The Use Chart in Section 13.3 of the Unified Development Code identifies “Dwelling, 
Townhouse/Rowhouse” as requiring a Special Exception in the TN- E zoning district. The 
request involves new construction and is subject to the review criteria in Section 3.4 .1, the 
applicable use standards in Section 13.5.5, and the dimensional standards in Section 6.3.4. 
 
The applicant proposes seven two -story dwellings, each containing three bedrooms, for a 
total of 21 bedrooms. The submitted site plan depicts a five -unit building and a separate 
two-unit building, with entrances facing Ernest H. Jones Jr. Drive. A shared driveway provides 
access to parking behind the buildings, and walkways connect the dwellings to the street. 
The applicant describes the proposed construction as traditionally detailed wood framing 
with lap and board-and-batten siding intended to complement surrounding houses. 
 
The property is situated among existing residential development and immediately north of 
the Greensboro Historic District boundary, as depicted in the attached maps. Staff’s review 
considers the proposed development’s relationship to surrounding properties , including 
building scale and placement, architectural design, parking, access, landscaping, and 
drainage.

STAFF ANALYSIS OF CRITERIA FOR APPROVAL 
 
1. Site suitability. The approximately 0.70 -acre property provides sufficient overall site 
area for seven dwelling units under the listed minimum of 4,000 square feet per unit. 
The proposed buildings face Ernest H. Jones Jr. Drive, with shared vehicular access 
and parking beh ind the buildings. This arrangement supports residential infill while 
maintaining a street-facing residential appearance. 
2. Traffic. The proposal consists of seven dwelling units containing a total of 21 
bedrooms. A shared driveway concentrates vehicular access at one location, and 
parking is provided behind the buildings. Walkways connect the dwellings to the 
street. Given the size an d residential nature of the development, staff does not 
anticipate an undue traffic nuisance or serious hazard, provided the driveway, sight

ABANDONMENT OR DISCONTINUANCE (Section 3.4.3.K) 
 
Any built structure or site associated with an approved special exception may continue with 
the associated use unless the use is made a nonconformity by any subsequent zoning 
ordinance n and/or action by the Board of Aldermen. All nonconformities shall be regulated 
in accordance with section 3.17. If a specific time is not set as part of the approval of a 
special exception, the special exception shall expire within 18 months if no building permit 
has been issued and/or construction activities have ceased on the site. A special exception 
for any sign type shall expire upon the abandonment or discontinuance of the use or 
business. Reapplication for a special exception for sign will be required.
