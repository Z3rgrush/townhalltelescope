The purpose of this report is to provide information regarding a phased Preliminary 
Plat request by Pritchard Engineering on behalf of Charles Morgan for the “Sterling 
Ridge” subdivision. Please see attachments 1- 7. 
 
BACKGROUND INFORMATION 
 
This is a request for phased Preliminary Plat approval for a 156-lot single dwelling unit 
subdivision. The property is located at the southwest corner of the intersection of 
Mockingbird Road and MS Hwy 182. The lot is zoned TN-N with the property number 
103B-00-003.01. Previously the applicant had applied for a Special Exception for the 
use of townhomes on the property. That request was ultimately denied and has no 
bearing on the current request.  
 
On May 19, 2026, the Board of Aldermen approved a previous version of this plat. 
After that approval the applicant requested to do the project in phases. A phased 
plat is reviewed differently than a plat without phases. Section 15.1.2 states “lots may 
be recorded and public improvements may be constructed in phases as long as 
each phase is able to stand alone by meeting all City requirements.” This was not 
reviewed as part of the original approval. Therefore, the phased preliminary plat 
must be reviewed and approved. 
 
On July 22, 2026, the Development Review Committee approved the Preliminary 
Plat with the following staff recommended conditions of approval: 
 
Engineering Department 
 
1. The Hydrologic & Hydraulic study must be updated to reflect proposed 
conditions accounting for all fill, final grading, site layout, conveyance and 
hydraulic structures, and detention systems. The study must model and display 
the 2, 10, 25, and 100-year inundation lines based on the proposed conditions. 
The study should include Josey Creek and the two internal tributaries 
traversing the property. The H&H should identify the floodway boundary.

CRITERIA FOR SPECIAL EXCEPTION REVIEW AND APPROVAL (Section 3.4.1) 
 
1. Site suitability. The proposed location of the structure and use has adequate space 
for development, adequate access to the site, fits contextually with the surrounding 
area, and has been properly designed for any environmental constraints. 
2. Traffic. There is no undue nuisance or serious hazard to pedestrian or vehicular traffic 
in the surrounding area by the proposed structure and use. 
3. Immediate neighborhood impact. The proposed structure and use is not detrimental, 
injurious, obnoxious, or offensive to other properties in the neighborhood. Negative 
impacts can include excessive trip generation, noise, vibration, dust, glare, heat, 
smoke, fumes, gas, odors, and inappropriate hours of operation. 
4. Availability of public services. The proposed structure and use is adequately served 
by sewer, water, electricity, fire protection, police protection, and provides for any 
stormwater requirements. 
5. Site Plan. A site plan shall be reviewed by the Development Review Committee prior 
to review by the Planning and Zoning Commission. This review shall be to determine 
if elements have been adequately provided on the plan. These elements can 
include, but are not limite d to: parking areas, loading areas, buffers, screening, 
landscaping, and signage. Additional approval by the Development Review 
Committee may be required for site plan approval after approval of a special 
exception. 
6. Impact on property values.  The proposed location of the structure and use will not 
cause or contribute to a decline in property values of surrounding properties. 
7. Consistency with Comprehensive Plan. The proposed special exception is consistent 
with the goals, objectives, and policies of the Comprehensive Plan. 
8. Additional Standards. All associated additional standards for the proposed building, 
sign, accessory structure, or site associated with the use have been adequately 
provided for on the site plan. 
 
REMOVAL OF ADDITIONAL STANDARD 
 
Section 13.3.2 authorizes the Board to consider removal of an Additional Standard as part 
of the Special Exception process. The Board's consideration in this case is therefore limited 
to whether sufficient evidence has been presented to justify removal of the adopted first -
floor restriction applicable to Special Event Facilities in the T -5D District. The burden of 
demonstrating that removal of the Additional Standard is appropriate and that the 
applicable Special Exception criteria are satisfied rests with the applicant. 
 
STAFF ANALYSIS OF CRITERIA FOR APPROVAL 
 
1. Site suitability. Although the minimum parking requirement can be met based on the 
approved occupancy, the applicant has not demonstrated that adequate parking 
will remain available during periods when multiple businesses within the development 
operate simultaneously or dur ing high-demand community events. Staff therefore 
cannot conclude that the site has demonstrated adequate space to 
accommodate the proposed use under all anticipated operating conditions. 
2. Traffic. The applicant has not submitted a traffic management plan demonstrating 
orderly vehicle circulation, emergency access, or parking management as required 
by Section 13.9.8.F.3. Accordingly, staff cannot determine that the proposed use will

FINDINGS OF FACT 
 
Based upon the application, supporting materials, staff review, and the requirements of the 
Unified Development Code, staff makes the following findings: 
 
1. The subject property is located at 200 South Montgomery Street, Suite C, is identified 
as Parcel No. 118P-00-384.00, and is zoned T-5D Urban Center District. 
2. The proposed use is a Special Event Facility, which is an allowed use within the T -5D 
zoning district; however, Section 13.9.8.F.12 prohibits the use on the first floor of a 
building located within the T -5D district unless the Additional Standard is remove d 
through the Special Exception process pursuant to Section 13.3.2. 
3. The request before the Planning and Zoning Commission is limited to consideration 
of removing the first- floor Additional Standard and is not a request to establish 
whether the use is otherwise permitted within the zoning district. 
4. The subject tenant space occupies the first floor of the Creamery at Central Station 
Condominium development. Residential condominium units are located directly 
above the subject tenant space. 
5. The City Planner determined, pursuant to Section 3.4.1(5), that a site plan review by 
the Development Review Committee was not required because no exterior 
improvements or site modifications are proposed as part of this request. 
6. The Fire Marshal has established a maximum occupancy of 225 persons for the space 
with tables and chairs. The applicant states an operational limit of 175 occupants but 
has not requested that this limitation be incorporated as a condition of approval. 
7. The application does not include the Traffic Management Plan, traffic control 
procedures, or parking attendant information required by Section 13.9.8.F.3, nor does 
it identify proposed operating hours demonstrating compliance with the applicable 
Additional Standards. 
8. One written letter of opposition was received expressing concerns regarding parking 
availability, traffic congestion, and impacts to existing businesses and nearby 
residents. 
9. Pursuant to Section 1.8 of the Unified Development Code, the burden of 
demonstrating that removal of the Additional Standard and the requested Special 
Exception satisfy the applicable approval criteria rests with the applicant. Staff finds 
that the informa tion submitted does not provide sufficient evidence to support all 
required affirmative findings for approval. 
 
ABANDONMENT OR DISCONTINUANCE (Section 3.4.3.K) 
 
Any built structure or site associated with an approved special exception may continue with 
the associated use unless the use is made a nonconformity by any subsequent zoning 
ordinance n and/or action by the Board of Aldermen. All nonconformities shall be regulated 
in accordance with section 3.17. If a specific time is not set as part of the approval of a 
special exception, the special exception shall expire within 18 months if no building permit 
has been issued and/or construction activities have ceased on the site. A special exception 
for any sign type shall expire upon the abandonment or discontinuance of the use or 
business. Reapplication for a special exception for sign will be required.
