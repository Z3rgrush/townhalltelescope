A. PUBLIC HEARING AND CONSIDERATION OF SE 26-10 A REQUEST 
FOR SPECIAL EXCEPTION TO ALLOW FOR A MANUFACTURED HOME 
DIRECTLY WEST OF 1487 BLUEFIELD ROAD WITHIN AN RN ZONING 
DISTRICT.
City Planner Daniel Havelin presented a request by Rodney Pope for a Special 
Exception to place one manufactured home directly west of 1487 Bluefield Road 
in the RN district. The proposed home contains two bedrooms and approximately 
790 square feet. Section 13.3 requires Special Exception review for this request. 
The parcel contains approximately 3.03 acres. Final placement must meet Section 
6.3.4, including a 30-foot front setback, 10-foot side setback and 25-foot rear 
setback. Section 13.5.8 requires one parking space per bedroom and 
compliance with the manufactured-home standards, including anchoring, siding, 
roof pitch, foundation screening and entrance porches or decks.
Mr. Sharp opened the meeting for citizen comments. Calling for and receiving no 
comments, the Commission moved to New Business.
Staff Analysis of the Review and Approval Criteria for a Special Exception
• Site suitability: Suitable residential setting, subject to final setbacks and 
access.
• Traffic: The two-bedroom dwelling is not expected to create significant 
traffic or an undue hazard on Bluefield Road.
• Neighborhood impact: Compatible residential use; no adverse impacts 
anticipated.
• Public services: City water and sewer and 4-County electricity are 
proposed. Connections and emergency access will be verified during 
permitting.
• Site plan: The City Planner did not require site plan review for this hearing. A 
site plan will be required during permitting.
• Property values: Staff identified no evidence of a negative effect on 
surrounding values.
• Comprehensive Plan: Consistent with the area’s residential character and 
continued residential development.
• Additional standards: Sections 6.3.4 and 13.5.8 remain applicable.
Mr. Sharp opened the public hearing.
Rodney Pope (applicant) spoke in favor of the request.
Glynnis Barber spoke in favor of the request.
After discussion and upon the motion of Commissioner Prather, duly seconded by 
Commissioner Smith, the motion to approve the request for SE 26-10 received 
unanimous approval by a vote of 6-0.

City Planner Daniel Havelin presented a request by Jean Collins for a Special 
Exception for an accessory dwelling unit at 104 Curtis Circle in the SD-6 district. The 
proposed footprint is approximately 812 square feet. Section 13.5.2.D.l limits an 
ADU to 50 percent of the principal dwelling footprint or 600 square feet, whichever 
is less. The proposal exceeds the 600-square-foot cap by 212 square feet. Staff 
supports modifying the size standard under Section 13.3.2 to allow up to 820 
square feet. The existing house fronts Curtis Circle, with the proposed ADU and 
porch behind it. The concrete driveway provides access and adequate parking 
for both dwellings. The plan shows 16.9 feet between the addition and house and 
10 feet between the porch and house. The required setbacks will be met.
