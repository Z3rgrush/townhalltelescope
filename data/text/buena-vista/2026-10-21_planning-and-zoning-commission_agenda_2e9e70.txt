1. Public Hearing - Boys & Girls Club Major Site Plan
Staff
Kopp presented the Boys and Girls Club Major Site Plan project, first providing an overview of
the hearing process, zoning, and proposal for development. The proposal is for the development
of a new Boys and Girls Club facility located at 710 Cedar Street. The underlying property is
owned by the Town, with a long-term ground lease for 99 years, and helps contribute to the
vision of a civic center located adjacent to recreation land, Town Hall East, the Community
Center, and the elementary school. The proposed facility is two stories, and envisions a
community recreation and youth center, which includes a gymnasium and programming spaces.
Kopp described that there has been collaboration between Town staff and the applicant team to
ensure that agreements for sharing of parking and other site improvements are in place. An
administrative height adjustment was approved in April 2026, providing the allowance for the
DRAFT
 
building to extend beyond 35’ in height to allow for screening of mechanical equipment on the 
roof. 
Staff received a public comment in support of the project from a neighboring property owner, 
which also contained a question regarding on-street parking on Cedar Street, but this comment 
was not received in time to appear in the packet.  
Staff finds that the proposal meets all four criteria for approval for Major Site Plan applications 
per code. Kopp described conditions that are recommended for approval, including resolving 
remaining technical comments and submitting final plans, executing the required encroachment 
agreements, confirming final water demand and allocation requirements at building permit, and 
maintaining compliance with the approved height adjustment. 
Applicant 
Lou Galletta, the project architect, provided an overview of the site plan, highlighting the 
building configuration, accessible route to the Community Center, and the entrances to the 
building. Galletta addressed the criteria for approval, providing justification that the proposal 
meets all dimensional standards, use specific standards, development and design standards, 
landscape standards, exterior lighting, and signage. Galletta emphasized the thoughtfully 
planned multipurpose exterior spaces, including the landscaped area.  
Galletta also described how the development meets the intent of the Comprehensive Plan, 
providing services to the youth of the community in a safe environment and contributing to 
community spirit and character through thoughtful design. He also described the focus on 
sustainability and energy conservation through the building’s construction and design, as well as 
through the amenities offered, such as EV charging stations. 
Public Comment  
None; public comment was opened at 7:52pm, closed at 7:52pm. 
Deliberation 
Commissioner Kosley asked about landscaping, specifically about deer fencing and two of the 
plant species that are proposed, and provided feedback that one of the types of trees on the 
plans may not thrive at this elevation. Commissioner T. Bennetts asked if there was a permitted 
encroachment in place for the building features that extend into the right-of-way, and Kopp 
clarified that there are permanent encroachments in place for the ramp and awnings. 
Commissioners T. Bennetts and B. Bennetts expressed their enthusiasm for the facility. Chair 
LaGreca asked if the roof would be sloped on the gymnasium portion of the building, and 
Galetta clarified that it was the parapet on the gymnasium rather than the roof itself. LaGreca 
expressed that he is also extremely enthusiastic about the facility and the benefits that it will 
provide for his children.

Commissioner T. Bennetts motioned to approve the Boys & Girls Club of Chaffee County Major 
Site Plan for 710 Cedar Street, finding that the application satisfies the approval criteria in UDC 
Section 6.5.1, subject to the conditions identified below.  
The applicant shall address any remaining Development Review Team technical 
comments and submit a final plan set acceptable to Town staff prior to final site plan 
signature and/or issuance of the applicable building permit.  
All required revocable encroachment agreements associated with improvements located 
on adjoining Town property or within public right-of-way shall be fully executed prior to 
occupancy, or earlier if required by the applicable agreement or permit.  
Final water demand shall be confirmed at building permit, and all required water 
dedication fees or other water allocation requirements shall be satisfied prior to building 
permit issuance.  
Development shall remain consistent with the April 14, 2026 administrative adjustment 
for building height. Any change exceeding the approved relief shall require additional 
review in accordance with the UDC.  
Commissioner B. Bennetts seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously.  
Staff/Commission Interaction: 
1. The next meeting on September 30 will be to discuss the Crossing Phase II PUD. Staff 
recommends that the Commission not make a final decision to recommend the PUD at the next 
meeting, as there are some elements that need to be finalized, specifically regarding the 
financing mechanism for the development, at the request of Town legal. Kopp emphasized that 
this might be beneficial anyway, since there is a lot of information to digest within the PUD 
document, and the Commissioners agreed. 
2. Commissioner Kosley asked about if there would be some discussion regarding building heights, 
or if he had misremembered a conversation at some point. Other commissioners suggested that 
he might be thinking about the R-2 OT zoning modification, and Kopp suggested that it could be 
regarding the upcoming PUD discussion with regard to height allowances in the zoning that is 
proposed.  
3. The final Housing Needs Assessment presentation will come before the Board of Trustees on the 
first meeting in November. Board of Trustees recommitted to Proposition 123 affordable housing 
goals for the next cycle at the September 22 meeting. Buena Vista has allocated excess 
affordable housing units in this cycle to Salida to help them satisfy their Prop 123 goals through a 
MOU.  
4. The Planning Department is beginning work on SB 24-174 requirements for all Comprehensive 
Plans to have a Strategic Growth Element and a Water Supply Element. Croft is working on 
developing a methodology for the infill and greenfield development pieces of the strategic 
growth element, utilizing GIS to target vacant parcels and prioritize areas for development. The 
Water Supply Element piece is largely completed through other long range planning documents, 
but will need to be compiled to ensure that it is compliant.

5. There is growing interest in development around the airport, including an active development 
application for hangar development at the airport, subdivision potential adjacent to the airport, 
and potential for annexation proposals for other parcels near the airport. Industrial land is in 
high demand, since Buena Vista has more opportunities for industrial development than other 
jurisdictions in the area, and it is exciting to see these proposals come forth.  
6. There is an upcoming Major Site Plan application that Planning is reviewing currently, which will 
create Phase II of the existing Antero Condominiums development. This application will be heard 
by the Planning Commission later this fall. 
7. The Planning Department has seen a decline in the number of building permit applications for 
review, which is unsurprising considering the end of the summer season and interest rates for 
homebuyers. 
8. Kopp thanked those that participated in the Urban Land Institute roundtable discussions, 
including Chair LaGreca and Commissioner Kosely. She stated that the event was very successful 
and provided a great deal of helpful information about steps that the Town and community 
members can take to become more water-wise. 
9. Croft provided information about the Chaffee County Safer Roadways for All effort, on which she 
has served as a steering committee member. The project is still collecting community feedback 
on the proposed recommendations for priority locations such as intersections and corridors 
within the County. There are a number of these target areas in and around Buena Vista, and 
Croft encouraged the Commissioners to participate in the feedback process.

 1. Tell us briefly about yourself: why are you interested, and what expertise 
 would you bring? 
 I have been involved in the construction and remodeling industry for approximately 
 30 years, owning and operating a construction business with extensive experience 
 in new construction, remodeling, and working with building and engineering 
 departments, as well as navigating various permitting and regulatory requirements. 
 I lived in Buena Vista for two years before moving to Summit County, where I 
 continued operating my construction business for the past 30 years. I have now 
 returned to Buena Vista and am a full-time permanent resident. 
 I am interested in serving on the Planning and Zoning Commission because I would 
 like to become more involved in my community and contribute my practical 
 experience to the planning and development process. After many years in the 
 construction industry, I have a firsthand understanding of how planning, zoning, 
 building regulations, and development decisions affect property owners, builders, 
 and the community. 
 I also have two grown sons who are no longer living at home, which gives me the 
 time and flexibility to become more involved in the community and dedicate my time 
 to serving on the Commission. 
 2. What are some of the major concerns/issues facing this Commission and 
 why? 
 I would suggest keeping this answer balanced rather than making it sound like you already have 
 a political position on local development: 
 I believe one of the major challenges facing Buena Vista is finding the right balance 
 between responsible growth and preserving the character and quality of life that 
 make the community special. 
 As Buena Vista continues to grow, thoughtful planning will be important to ensure 
 that new development is compatible with existing neighborhoods, infrastructure, 
 traffic, housing needs, and the long-term vision of the community. I believe the 
 Planning and Zoning Commission has an important role in considering both the 
 individual property owner's interests and the broader interests of the community 
 when making these decisions. 
 3. If appointed, what would you like to accomplish? 
 My primary goal would be to become a knowledgeable and constructive member of 
 the Commission and contribute to thoughtful, fair, and practical decisions regarding 
 the future growth of Buena Vista. 
 I would like to bring the perspective of someone who has spent 30 years working 
 directly in construction and dealing with building and engineering departments, 
 while also listening carefully to the concerns and priorities of local residents. 
 Ultimately, I would like to help ensure that Buena Vista can grow responsibly while 
 maintaining the character, quality of life, and sense of community that make it a 
 great place to live. 
 4. Planning and Zoning applicants: What is the Town's responsibility in 
 overseeing and regulating local development? 
 This is probably the  most important question  for you,  and I would make your answer 
 thoughtful rather than overly technical: 
 I believe the Town's responsibility is to provide a fair, consistent, and transparent 
 framework for development that balances private property rights with the long-term 
 interests of the community. 
 The Town should ensure that development follows adopted zoning regulations and 
 comprehensive planning goals, while considering impacts on infrastructure, traffic, 
 housing, neighborhoods, the environment, and the overall character of Buena Vista. 
 I also believe the process should be predictable and understandable for property 
 owners, residents, and developers. Good planning should allow for responsible 
 growth while protecting the qualities and character that make Buena Vista a 
 desirable place to live.
