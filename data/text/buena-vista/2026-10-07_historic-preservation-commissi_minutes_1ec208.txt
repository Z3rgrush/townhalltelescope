A. Retreat Recap 
Mary Therese summarized the board retreat. The board brainstormed goals for HPC but 
did not prioritize those goals. Mary Therese will be typing up those goals and will

B. Discussion: Integrity as Local Landmark Eligibility Criterion 
Mary Therese discussed the concept of integrity, which is normally a prerequisite in 
local landmarking criterion (and is always a state/federal register criterion), but is not 
 
included in the BV municipal code. The HPC discussed the pros and cons of the current 
code vs code with an integrity requirement. As homework, the HPC will review a Google 
Doc (that Katy sends) with Chapter 19 in it and share comments on what should we 
should keep, what should go, and what should be added. Once we land on a code 
amendment plan, we can vote on how best to approach any amendments.
