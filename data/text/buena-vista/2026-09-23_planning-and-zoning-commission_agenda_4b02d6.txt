Project Summary 
The Town of Buena Vista is reviewing a Major Site Plan for a new Boys & Girls Club of Chaffee County facility at 710 
Cedar Street. The 0.729-acre site is owned by the Town of Buena Vista and is currently vacant. The proposed two-
story facility will provide youth and community recreation and program space, including a gymnasium, leadership and 
program areas, and supporting site improvements. The submitted site plan identifies approximately 16,427 square 
feet of building footprint and 21,355 square feet of gross floor area. 
 
The project includes 17 vehicle parking spaces, including two accessible spaces; 10 bicycle parking spaces; pedestrian 
improvements; landscaping and irrigation; drainage improvements; emergency access; and associated utility 
improvements. The plan also coordinates shared use with the adjacent Town Community Center, including site 
circulation and parking-related functions. 
Planning and Zoning Commission Review 
A major site plan review requires noticed public hearings with the Planning and Zoning Commission for review and 
determination. The Planning Commission provides a determination based on the consideration of the Town’s Unified 
Development Code. The Unified Development Code establishes criteria for site plan approval in section 6.5.1. and 
those criteria and staff analysis are found in section VII of this staff report. 
The Role of the Planning and Zoning Commission 
• Conduct a public hearing on the major site plan proposal 
• Determine whether the Major Site Plan satisfies the approval criteria in UDC Section 6.5.1. 
• Approve, approve with conditions, or deny the Major Site Plan based on the applicable criteria and the record 
before the Commission.

III. Project Description 
The proposal is for construction of a new Boys & Girls Club facility on the northeast corner of Cedar Street and Evans 
Street. The site is zoned MU-2 (Mixed Use 2), and the proposed “civic organization, club, or lodge” use is permitted 
within the district. The facility is intended to serve youth and community recreation needs and includes a 
gymnasium, leadership and program spaces, and supporting service areas. 
 
Site Layout and Access 
The building is positioned along Cedar and Evans Streets, with vehicular parking and site access concentrated 
primarily on the east side of the building. The submitted plan provides 17 parking spaces where 15 are required, 
including two accessible spaces where one is required, and 10 bicycle parking spaces where one is required. The site 
plan also identifies emergency access and a bus drop-off area coordinated with Chaffee County Fire Protection 
District requirements. 
 
Pedestrian, Landscaping, and Drainage Improvements 
The plan includes sidewalks and accessible routes connecting the site to surrounding public improvements, along 
with landscaping and irrigation improvements. Drainage improvements are designed to manage runoff generated by 
the development. Certain improvements serving the Boys & Girls Club, such as a drainage area rain garden and 
landscape and irrigation are located on or coordinated with adjoining Town-owned property and public right-of-way 
and will be governed through applicable shared-use and revocable encroachment agreements. 
 
Building Height 
The MU-2 district permits a maximum primary structure height of 35 feet, with an additional three feet allowed for 
parapets. On April 14, 2026, the Town Administrator approved an administrative adjustment allowing up to a five-
percent height adjustment for a limited portion of the parapet associated with the gymnasium and rooftop 
mechanical screening. The approved adjustment allows a maximum parapet height of 39 feet 9 inches. The current 
site plan identifies a maximum building height of 39 feet 9 inches and notes the prior administrative adjustment. 
 
IV. Background 
The Boys & Girls Clubs of Chaffee County currently operates its Buena Vista Club from shared space at Avery Parsons 
Elementary School. As participation and programming have expanded, the organization identified the need for a 
permanent facility capable of providing dedicated youth programming and additional community recreation space. 
The Club began fundraising for a new Buena Vista facility in 2022 through its Building Great Futures capital campaign. 
  
The Town of Buena Vista has been a long-standing partner of the Boys & Girls Club and identified Town-owned 
property at 710 Cedar Street as the future location of the facility. On January 10, 2023, the Board of Trustees 
approved a ground lease agreement with the Boys & Girls Club of Chaffee County for development of the property. 
The agreement provides a 99-year ground lease, allowing the property to remain under Town ownership while 
providing the Club with a permanent location for its Buena Vista operations. The partnership also anticipates shared 
use of portions of the facility for Town recreation programming.  
 
The new facility is being funded through the Boys & Girls Club's capital campaign, combining private donations, 
community fundraising, foundation support and public grant funding. Major documented funding sources include a 
$500,000 Colorado Opioid Abatement Council infrastructure grant and additional foundation and community 
contributions. By early 2026, the Club reported raising approximately $8.7 million toward the project and has 
continued pursuing additional private and governmental funding to complete the facility.

VIII. Criteria and Findings for Approval:  
The Planning and Zoning Commission must consider if the criteria for a major site plan have been met. The criteria 
from the Unified Development Code are shown in Bold type, followed by the staff analysis. 
Criteria-Major Site Plan 
1. The site plan is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan and other adopted Town policies and plans;  
Finding: Staff finds that the criterion has been met. 
 
The proposal directly supports several priorities in the 2025 Buena Vista Comprehensive Plan. The Community Spirit 
chapter identifies the Boys & Girls Club as a community partner and records community support for the organization 
and its expansion. The Recreation & Leisure recommendations call for the Town to maintain and expand shared-use 
agreements with the Boys & Girls Club and other community institutions, expand low-cost and free recreation 
opportunities, and provide indoor facilities for youth and adult programming. The plan also identifies expanded 
facility space for teens and youth programming as a community need. The proposed facility advances these objectives 
by creating a dedicated, year-round youth and recreation facility in a central, walkable location and by coordinating 
shared use with the Town’s adjacent Community Center and Recreation Department. Staff therefore finds the 
proposal consistent with the Comprehensive Plan’s goals for community services, youth engagement, recreation 
access, and community wellbeing. 
2. The site plan is consistent with any previously approved plan, planned unit development, and/or any other 
precedent land use approval; and 
5 
 
Finding: Staff finds that the criterion is not applicable.  
 
Analysis: There is no prior site plan, planned unit development, or other precedent land use approval governing 
development of this vacant lot that would establish additional site-specific development requirements. The property 
is therefore reviewed under the current MU-2 zoning and applicable Unified Development Code standards. The 
previously approved administrative height adjustment is addressed separately under Criterion c as an authorized 
deviation from the otherwise applicable dimensional standard. 
 
3. The site plan complies with applicable standards in this UDC; 
Finding: Staff finds that the criterion has been met, subject to the previously approved administrative height 
adjustment and completion of final technical corrections. 
 
Analysis: The proposed site plan has been reviewed for compliance with applicable MU-2 and site development 
standards, including use, setbacks, parking, accessible parking, bicycle parking, landscaping, lighting, circulation, 
drainage, utilities, and site accessibility. The plan provides 17 vehicle spaces where 15 are required, two accessible 
spaces where one is required, and 10 bicycle spaces where one is required. The primary exception to the standard 
dimensional requirements is building height. On April 14, 2026, the Town Administrator approved an administrative 
adjustment for a limited portion of the building parapet associated with the gymnasium and mechanical equipment 
screening. The adjustment permits a maximum height of 39 feet 9 inches, and the submitted site plan reflects that 
approved relief. With that approved adjustment and completion of remaining technical items, staff finds the site plan 
complies with the applicable UDC standards. 
4. There is adequate water available from the Town's water system to serve the subject property pursuant to the 
Town's water allocation policy and the requirements of subsection 16.04.4.8.1. 
Finding: Staff finds that the criterion has been met. 
 
Analysis: The proposed Boys & Girls Club is a public/nonprofit/institutional facility and is eligible for water allocation 
under the Town’s adopted Water Allocation Policy. Water demand may first be served from the allocation category 
established for public, nonprofit, or institutional facilities. If additional allocation is required beyond the amount 
available in that category, the property is also located within the geographic area eligible for the infill development 
allocation category. Final water demand and the associated dedication or fee-in-lieu amount will be confirmed 
through the building permit process in accordance with subsection 16.04.4.8.1 and the Town’s Water Allocation 
Policy. Based on the applicable allocation categories, adequate water is available to serve the proposed development. 
 
 
IX. Conclusion and Staff Recommendation 
Based on the submitted Major Site Plan and staff’s review of the applicable approval criteria, staff finds that the 
proposal satisfies the criteria for Major Site Plan approval.  
 
Staff recommends approval of the Boys & Girls Club of Chaffee County Major Site Plan, subject to the following 
conditions: 
• The applicant shall address any remaining Development Review Team technical comments and submit a final 
plan set acceptable to Town staff prior to final site plan signature and/or issuance of the applicable building 
permit. 
• All required shared-use, license, or revocable encroachment agreements associated with improvements 
located on adjoining Town property or within public right-of-way shall be fully executed prior to occupancy, or 
earlier if required by the applicable agreement or permit. 
• Final water demand shall be confirmed at building permit, and all required water dedication fees or other 
water allocation requirements shall be satisfied prior to building permit issuance. 
• Development shall remain consistent with the April 14, 2026 administrative adjustment for building height. 
Any change exceeding the approved relief shall require additional review in accordance with the UDC.
