Trustees Packet, including current staffing, the BVPD mission and vision statement, and training 
mandates.

website along with how it can be navigated.

Town Administrator touch points throughout the code (noting the Town Administrator is cited 
381 times), the presentation highlighted examples of administrative approvals.  Berger 
reviewed the Town Administrator’s authority to approve temporary encroachments, certain 
types of land use applications, and administrative adjustments; as well as, some parking 
exemptions. A decision tree was reviewed and Berger explained the methodology of how 
decisions are typically considered and processed.

Chaffee County Fire Protection District Inclusion – Intent to Reduce the Town’s Property Tax 
Should the Board of Trustees adopt Resolution No. 2026-51, “CONFIRMING THEIR INTENT TO 
REDUCE THE TOWN’S PROPERTY TAX UPON A SUCCESSFUL INCLUSION OF ALL PROPERTY IN 
THE TOWN INTO THE CHAFFEE COUNTY FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT”  
 
Town Administrator Berger led the discussion explaining that the Town of Buena Vista has 
received fire protection services from the Chaffee County Fire Protection District (CCFPD) since 
May 2021 through an Intergovernmental Agreement. Under this arrangement, the Town pays 
for fire protection from the General Fund rather than through a direct Fire District property tax.  
If voters approve inclusion into the CCFPD at the November 3, 2026 election, and the inclusion 
is subsequently approved by the District Court, property owners within the Town would begin 
paying the CCFPD property tax directly. To ensure that this change is intended to be property 
tax neutral for Town taxpayers, the resolution confirms the Board's intent to reduce the Town's 
property tax by an equivalent amount beginning with the 2027 property tax year and 
continuing each year thereafter while the Town remains within the Fire District and receives 
fire protection services from the District.

Airport Annexation Survey - Agreement for Professional Services  
Should the Board of Trustees adopt Resolution No. 2026-52, “SUPPORTING AND AUTHORIZING 
REPARATORY WORK FOR ANNEXATION OF TOWN-OWNED AIRPORT PROPERTY ASSOCIATED 
WITH CENTRAL COLORADO REGIONAL AIRPORT” 
 
Airport Manager Swanson provided a brief overview of annexing the remaining Town-owned 
airport property that lies outside the Town limits describing it as a foundational step for orderly 
airport  development, investor readiness, and long-term protection of the airport. The 
annexation effort is not a project approval and does not commit the Town to any particular 
development. It creates the municipal boundary, title, map, and legal-description framework 
needed so future development can be reviewed, negotiated, permitted, leased, and protected 
under a clear Town process. 
 
After explaining the background, historically documented desire, and benefits Swanson went 
on to say the attached 2026 proposal from Landmark Surveying & Mapping provides the 
technical next step: boundary research, deed and conveyance review, a preliminary plat for title 
work, and preparation of serial annexation maps and legal descriptions. Staff recommends that 
the Board adopt the attached resolution authorizing this preparatory work and directing staff to 
return with the completed annexation materials and any required ordinance or final Board 
action.
