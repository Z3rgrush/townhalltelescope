TOWN OF OPHIR, COLORADO 
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-7 
 
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 303.1 OF THE TOWN OF OPHIR LAND USE 
CODE TO PROHIBIT SHORT TERM RENTALS 
 
WHEREAS, the Town of Ophir (“Town”) is a home-rule municipality under Article XX of the 
Colorado Constitution and political subdivision of the State of Colorado, duly organized and operating 
under the Constitution and laws of the state and the Ophir Town Charter; and  
WHEREAS, as a home rule municipality, the Town is authorized to enact land use regulations to 
address matters of local concern; and 
WHEREAS, the welfare and enjoyment of Ophir is associated with its rural character, including 
its low population, primarily owner-occupied dwellings, and geographically-limited townsite; and 
WHEREAS, the Town does not have sufficient staff, infrastructure, municipal service, or 
commercial resources to support lodging uses, including hotels or short-term rental of residential 
dwellings or other private property; and 
WHERAS, prohibiting short-term rentals within the Town will ensure compatibility with the rural 
character of the Town and its neighborhoods and minimize the impact of short-term rentals on Town staff, 
Town services, other residents (e.g., noise, traffic, parking, and other public health, safety and welfare 
impacts); and 
WHEREAS, the Town’s General Assembly finds that prohibiting short term rentals within the 
Town is in the best interests of the health, safety, and general welfare of the citizens of the Town, is 
necessary to maintain local control in a manner responsive to the unique needs of the Town, and to 
preserve its character and limited housing stock. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE TOWN 
OF OPHIR, COLORADO, THAT: 
Section 1. Recitals. The Recitals set forth above are incorporated herein as findings and declarations 
of the General Assembly. 
Section 2. LUC Amendments. Section 303.1 of the Town Land Use Code is repealed and reenacted 
to read as follows:  
§303.1. PERMITTED USES.  
A. Uses. No building shall be erected, converted, enlarged or structurally altered, nor shall any 
building or premises be used for any purpose other than permitted in the district in which such 
building or premises is located. Notwithstanding anything in this Code to the contrary,  Short Term 
Rentals are not allowed and are a prohibited use in all Zone Districts in the Town. “Short Term 
Rentals” means the rental for lodging accommodations of a dwelling unit, a room within a dwelling 
unit, a camper or mobile home, trailer, tent or yurt, vacant or unimproved land or lot area, or a 
portion of any of the foregoing, for a term or time period of less than thirty (30) consecutive days. 
 
 
3475673.1 
It is unlawful and prohibited to lease, advertise for lease, or permit the leasing of any Short-Term 
Rental. 
 
B. Conformity with Standards. No building shall be erected, enlarged, moved or structurally altered 
except in conformity with the height, yard or other regulations prescribed herein for the district in 
which such lot is located; every part of a required yard shall be open to the sky, u nobstructed, 
except as hereafter provided; the yard of any lot shall not be reduced so as to be smaller than the 
applicable district requirement.   
Section 3. Publication. After final adoption, a public notice shall be published which notice shall contain the 
number and title of this ordinance, a brief description of the ordinance, its effective date and notice that all copies 
are available for inspection at the Town Hall office.  
 
Section 4. Severability, Conflicting Ordinances Repealed.  If any section, subsection or clause of this 
Ordinance shall be deemed to be unconstitutional or otherwise invalid, the validity of the remaining 
sections, subsections and clauses shall not be affected thereby.  All other ordinances or parts of ordinances 
in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. 
 
Section 5. Effective Date. This ordinance and the codes adopted by reference and amendments thereto 
adopted by this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon publication of notice of final adoption as 
provided in Section 3, above.
