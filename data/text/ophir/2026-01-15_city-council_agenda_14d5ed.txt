a. LUC Review 
a. Items P&Z discussed: 
i. P&Z members thought building sizes per lot size in the current LUC were 
appropriate 
ii. P&Z members thought the setbacks in the current LUC were appropriate 
iii. P&Z members had questions about raise decks and whether or not there are minimum 
and maximum sizes for raised decks. Raised decks are mentioned in the LUC, but 
there don’t seem to be applicable standards governing their size and placement. 
iv. P&Z members thought it may make sense to look into whether or not the maximum 
floor area of accessory buildings as it may limit the ability to build a standalone 
garage, if the town decided that standalone garages would be beneficial to allow.

Review and Approval of wood styled cement board wildfire resistant siding 
for 917 Porphyry St 
The Callicott’s are aiming to replace their wood siding this summer and are 
looking at installing a wood styled cement board material. The material is non-
combustible and is therefore wildfire resistant.  
Details of the siding can be found here: https://www.jameshardie.com/product-
catalog/exterior-siding-products/hardie-panel-siding/select-cedarmill/statement-
collection-colors/?color=aged-pewter 
They are looking at the following colors in the Select Cedarmill texture: 
- Timber Bark 
- Rich Espresso 
- Khaki Brown 
- Monterey Taupe 
- Mountain Sage 
- Aged Pewter  
The relevant section of the LUC is 303.4 B 
 Siding. Natural stone, natural wood (stained or clear), and rusted metal. 
Materials such as stucco, synthetic stucco, brick or plaster are limited to twenty 
percent (20%) of exterior surfaces. Ophir supports the use of new, 
sustainable/green materials as long as they mimic the natural materials listed here
