§303.4 Exterior Materials. All buildings and structures shall use designated materials 
according to the following table for exterior surfaces (excluding: doors, window areas, 
antennas and non-reflective solar energy collection devices, but including garage doors). 
Exterior Materials on all new structures and additions or alterations of greater than 500 
square feet must be compliant with the material specifications of the currently adopted 
version of the CRWC. The Planning and Zoning Commission may approve or deny 
additional materials to this list after conducting a public hearing and reviewing a sufficient 
sample of the proposed material. All homeowners must submit a sample of exterior 
materials. to the Planning and Zoning Commission prior to plan approval, as well as, prior to 
installation. A picture and description of the exterior materials may be used if deemed 
sufficient by the Planning and Zoning Commission. The Planning and Zoning Commission and 
Building Official retain the ability to request a physical sample of exterior materials to be 
provided at a public hearing or any meeting where plans are being reviewed. 
A. Foundation. Natural stone, cement cast stone, brick, non-reflective metal, 
rusted metal, stucco, synthetic stucco, adobe, and plaster. 
B. Siding. Natural stone, fire-retardant-treated wood (stained or clear), and 
rusted metal. Materials such as stucco, synthetic stucco, brick or plaster are 
limited to twenty percent (20%) of exterior surfaces. Ophir supports the use 
of sustainable and fire resistant as long as they mimic the natural materials 
listed here. 
C. Skirting. Skirting may be used above the foundation for protection from 
snow piling against the side of a house. Skirting may extend a maximum of 
five (5) above the finished construction grade and shall use non-reflective 
metal, rusted metal, or other siding material defined in §303.4.B. above. 
D. Roof. Non-reflective metal, clay tile, pre-finished modular (pro-panel) 
reduced- reflective metal panels, slate, cement tiles, non-reflective solar tiles, 
and sod or turf. No galvanized or zinc metals or the like. 
E. Garage Door. Fire-retardant-treated wood (stained, clear or painted), insulated 
press-board fire-retardant-treated wood veneer (stained, clear or painted), or 
rusted metal. 
F. Decks. Non-combustible or ignition resistant wood or recycled material 
with a natural wood appearance (stained or clear). Decking materials must 
comply with the approved decking material of the currently adopted version 
of the CRWC.  
§303.5 Fences. Fences shall be constructed out of  noncombustible or ignition-resistant 
wood materials, stone, wrought iron, corrugated metal, or any combination of 
the foregoing. Fences shall not exceed six (6) feet in height from the pre-
construction grade. The Building Official can require the use of temporary 
construction fences for any duration of the project.
