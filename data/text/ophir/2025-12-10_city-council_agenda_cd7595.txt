4. BUSINESS ITEMS 
a. OEC’s Proposed Revegetation Ordinance 
a. Items P&Z discussed: 
Fencing Requirement 
Unless fencing is required for safety purposes, temporary fencing may be unattractive and 
unnecessarily restrict movement. Neighbors may also object to plastic fencing on property 
lines. 
 
Parking Plan 
Parking should be limited to Ophir Right of Ways. Landowners should be allowed to park on 
their property during construction as needed. 
 
Topsoil Stockpiling 
If the town can provide a place to store topsoil, that would be great. 
 
Ground Cover of Exposed Soil 
While aspirational, P&Z thought the requirement to cover exposed topsoil was excessive. 
 
Heavy Machinery Access 
Neighbors can impose restrictions if someone drives on their land, so P&Z did not see a need 
to mandate this requirement as it may interfere with private property rights. 
 
Approved Seed List 
There needs to be a mechanism for alternative seed mixes if the supplier changes or goes out 
of business. Suggest case-by-case approval by Manager, OEC Chair, etc. 
 
 
Erosion Matting 
“Has this requirement been adopted by other municipalities in the area?” 
 
b. OEC Proposed Dark Sky Lighting Code 
a. Items dicussed by P&Z: 
i. Regarding the lighting document, P&Z was wondering if these are the minimum 
standards the Dark Sky organization recommends? Additionally, there was some 
concern from P&Z members about the language regarding fines for non-compliance, 
as Ophir does not have a method of imposing fines due to the lack of a municipal
