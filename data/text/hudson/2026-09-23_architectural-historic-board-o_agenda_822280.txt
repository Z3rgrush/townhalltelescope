5 Baldwin Street (Historic District)
Alteration (Siding, Windows & Awning)
Submitted by Nathan Zampelli
a) Staff notes this application was continued at the September 9, 2026 AHBR 
meeting.
b) A site visit will take place on September 22, 2026.
c) Staff notes the applicant has submitted a revised west-side elevation 
depicting a more consistent window design for the Boards consideration. 
d) Appendix 1 (6) of the Secretary of Interior Standards state “deteriorated 
historic features shall be repaired rather than replaced. Where the severity 
of deterioration requires replacement of a distinctive feature, the new 
feature shall match the old in design, color, texture, and other visual 
qualities and, where possible, materials. Replacement of missing features 
shall be substantiated by documentary, physical, or pictorial evidence.”

windows for the replacement of historic materials. Staff notes that the 
proposed window series is not currently included on the AHBR-approved 
materials list for replacement windows on historic structures. Suggest the 
applicant bring a sample window to the meeting for the Board to review.
f) Question the appropriateness of the proposed transom windows on the 
historic carriage house.

26-1168
25 Clairhaven Dr
Addition (2-Car Garage)
Submitted by Casey Liptak
a) Section III-1 (a)(4) of the Architectural Design Standards states that 
attached garages shall not face the street except for new development with 
a front yard depth of 130 feet or more. Staff notes an existing front facing 
garage would be rebuilt due to storm damage. Staff has determined a 
variance is not required under Section 1206.05(f)(1) of the Land 
Development Code. The garage door width would not be expanded.
b) Section IV-3 (b)(1) of the Architectural Design Standards states that wings 
may not be larger or taller than the main body of the structure, but they 
may be the same height. Verify the proposed garage addition is not taller 
than the existing house. 
c) Section III-1 (g)(8) of the Architectural Design Standards states that large 
expanses of blank wall are to be avoided. Fenestration placement should 
be at a maximum of approximately every 12 feet. Revise elevations to 
depict additional fenestration on the south side elevation to better meet the 
requirement.
d) Section IV-4 of the Architectural Design Standards state that additions 
should be designed to be compatible with the main structure by 
incorporating materials and a foundation to match. Revise elevations to 
depict foundation material to match the existing around the proposed 
garage addition.

26-1071
1330 Barlow Rd
Alteration (Siding Application and Window Replacement)
Submitted by Shannon Casey
a) Staff notes the existing peaked dormer received administrative approval on 
September 16, 2024.
b) Upon final inspection, staff observed a deviation from the final approved 
plans. Staff notes both horizontal siding and board and batten siding were 
installed within the peaked dormer in place of the approved green shake 
siding. The applicant has requested the AHBR consider the proposed 
dormer material change. Additionally staff notes the applicant is 
proposing to install new board and batten siding material within the 
existing west-side elevation dormer and bump-out to match the existing 
peaked dormer material. 
c) Staff suggests the applicant take this meeting to discuss the proposed 
project with the Board and verify the scope of work.
d) Section IV-4 (d)(1) of the Architectural Design Standards states that the 
walls of the main body must be a dominant material. Up to two additional 
materials may be used to call attention to the composition. For example a 
different material may be used on building projections gable ends, 
entrance recesses, or to emphasize the horizontal or vertical divisions of 
the building. Staff notes that board and batten vertical siding is proposed 
within the west side gable and bump-out that would introduce a 4th 
building material on the existing structure. Suggest installing shake siding 
to better meet the requirement. 
e) Section IV-4 (d)(1) of the Architectural Design Standards states that the 
materials used in any mass must be applied consistently on that mass on 
all sides of the structure. Staff notes that the addition of the board and 
batten siding material within the west-side gable elevation would result in 
3 separate material applications within the existing dormers.

26-1224
180 Terex Rd
New House (Single-Family Dwelling)
Submitted by Chris Coblentz, Coblentz Homes
a) Verify grade line is accurately depicted on submitted elevations.
b) Section IV-3 (g)(2) of the Architectural Design Standards states that 
projections which extend out from the mass to which they are attached 
more than five feet will be treated as wings, except for open porches, and 
single-story additions off the rear of multi-story dwellings. If the roof line 
of the addition intersects the roof line of the existing dwelling, then the 
addition shall be classified as a wing. Staff notes that the proposed 
primary suite projection extends forward approximately 9 ft from the main 
mass. Revise proposed projection to meet this requirement.
c) Section IV-4 (g)(1) of the Architectural Design Standards states that roofs 
on projections should match the roof material of the building (unless both 
roofs are flat) and to the extent possible, shall be same kind of roof. 
Natural finish metals such as copper, terne coated steel, or lead may be 
substituted for any roofing material. Staff notes that a metal roofing 
material is proposed.
d) Section III-1(g)(8) of the Architectural Design Standards state that large 
expanses of blank wall are to be avoided. Fenestration placement should 
be at a maximum of approximately every 12 feet. Question if additional 
fenestration is needed on left and right-side elevations. Suggest the 
installation of gable vents to better meet the requirement. 
e) Suggest the applicant utilize a smooth-finish vinyl siding rather than a 
simulated wood-grain finish.
