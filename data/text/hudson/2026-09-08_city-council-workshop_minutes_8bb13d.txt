Correspondence and Council Comments2.
Mr. White expressed his appreciation to City staff and volunteers for their efforts in making the Community 
Picnic successful. He also shared an update from the recent HOME Committee meeting .  He further noted that 
the Park Board supports showcasing the Bird Song Project in various parks, and he will continue collaborating 
with Mayor Anzevino and Door 2 Art Studio to plan for its installation in 2027 .
Dr. Goetz also noted the great success of the Community Picnic and read a letter into the record from resident 
Mr. Todd Zedak regarding the Canterbury Meadows Development .
Dr. Weinstein provided an update on the Veterans' Commission meeting that was recently held and reminded 
interested citizens about the 9/11 stair climb that will occur on September 11, 2026 at the Hudson High School 
Memorial Stadium.
Mayor Anzevino noted several upcoming events that included the 911 stair climb and Hudson High School 
homecoming football game against North Royalton on September 11, Hudson Trike and Bike event and Hudson 
High School homecoming dance on September 12 .

B. 26-118 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A SETTLEMENT OF THE 
PRESTIGE BUILDER GROUP, LLC V. CITY OF HUDSON LAWSUIT 
AND RELATED CLAIMS SURROUNDING THE CANTERBURY 
MEADOWS DEVELOPMENT.
Brief Description:   This legislation authorizes the City Manager to execute a 
Settlement Agreement and Release with Prestige Builder Group, LLC, George 
Vizmeg, Kuchar Limited Liability Company, Canterbury-K Farms LLC, and 
Prestige-Canterbury Farms, LLC concerning the proposed development of 
approximately 94.56 acres located on Ravenna Street and Stow Road.
The Settlement Agreement resolves pending litigation and a multi-million 
dollar administrative hardship claim concerning the Property and establishes 
the terms for the now-compliant development of the property as a 32-lot 
single-family Open Space Conservation Subdivision (“OSCS”), substantially 
in accordance with the September 12, 2025 Preliminary Plan reviewed by the 
Planning Commission.
