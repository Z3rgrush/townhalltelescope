the workshop over to President of Council Bird, in accordance with Council Rules.

Correspondence and Council Comments2.
Mr. Brezovec thanked Ms. Behnke for her time with the city, and wished her well at her new position with the 
Chamber of Commerce.
Mr. White provided an update on an E-Bike roundtable meeting that was held on October 12 , 2026 with the 
schools, Police Chief and the Parks.
Mayor Anzevino expressed his appreciation to the staff at the Girl Scouts of North East Ohio STEM Center of 
Excellence for the recent tour, as well as to everyone who attended the unveiling of the “Clark Rumiser Corner” 
honorary street sign on October 9. He also noted that he will be submitting his monthly article to Hudson Life 
later this week. Additionally, he reminded residents that Hudson City Schools will be closed on Friday, October 
16, the district’s alumni induction ceremony is scheduled for October 17, and Fall Fest will take place at the 
Evaporator Works on October 17 from noon to 7:00 p .m.
