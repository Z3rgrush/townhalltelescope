Public Comments6.
Mr. Ron Brubaker, 7593 Partridge Meadows Drive East, representing TASC Force, thanked the City of Hudson 
for their support of the Veterans' Way trail and suggested other enhancements .
Mr. Anthony Ravida, 1746 Edgar Dr., stated that he would only sign a waiver stating that neither party is 
responsible for the issue regarding his fence.
Correspondence and Council Comments7.
Mr. Brezovec stated that he met with some groups who had concerns about restrictions or exceptions in District 
11 and those would be addressed.  He also noted that he requested a discussion on the process regarding 
referendums, however, this issue requires a charter amendment and would be revisited at the next Charter 
Review Commission.  
Dr. Goetz stated that she is hosting a forum on August 31 at 7:00 p .m. at Barlow Community Center regarding 
short and long term rentals and whether Code changes need to be made .
Mr. White reminded residents of the E-Bike forum on August 19, and wished everyone a happy first day of 
school.  
Ms. D'Eramo echoed comments about the E-Bike forum, as well as a reminder regarding the Plan and Play Day 
for Clinton Crossing on August 22 from 11:00 to 2:00 p .m.  
Dr. Bird said it was nice to see all the little kids at the DREAM playground .  He also addressed Mr. Ravida's 
comments about staff, the City's right-of-way, and moving forward in a productive and respectful manner .
Mayor Anzevino addressed Mr. Ravida's comments, stating that a license agreement may be a solution for him 
to keep his fence.  Regarding the E-Bike forum, he also recounted a time when some children asked him 
questions about where they were allowed to ride their scooters, and wanted to do the right thing .   Further, he 
thanked the Western Reserve Community Band for allowing him to be their narrator, congratulated Megan 
Howie as the recipient of his July kindness challenge, and asked residents to drive safely around busses and kids
