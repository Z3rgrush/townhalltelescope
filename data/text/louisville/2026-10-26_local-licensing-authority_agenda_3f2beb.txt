A. New Liquor License Application 
Dillon Peaks Hospitality LLC d/b/a Hampton Inn Boulder/Louisville 
Lodging Facility License 
912 West Dillon Road 
 
The Licensing Authority opened up the meeting and the licensee had a chance to speak 
about their business to the Authority. 
 
The Local Licensing Authority asked some questions of the applicant. 
There was discussion about the current manager listed on the application. 
Kaufman moved to approve the application with conditions that the applicant needs to 
provide documentation for a new manager as well as conduct satisfactory background 
checks; seconded by Carpenter. 
 
Aye: Kaufman, Carpenter, Cole 
Nay: None 
Absent: Carlson, Macado 
B. Unauthorized Liquor Sales - Letter and Response Update 
Tri City Elks Lodge NO2541 INC d/b/a Tri City Elks Lodge 2541 
Club License 
525 Main Street 
The Local Licensing Authority discussed the response that was received by the City 
Clerks office by Tri City Elks Lodge No 2541. 
 
The Exaulted ruler of the Tri City Elks Lodge was in attendance and spoke on behalf of 
the Tri City Elks Lodge about the Unauthorized Liquor Sales. There were none. 
 
The Local Licensing Authority and the City found there was no wrongdoing and that the 
unauthorized liquor sales were unfounded. The liquor licensee is in good standing and 
can resume business as usual.
