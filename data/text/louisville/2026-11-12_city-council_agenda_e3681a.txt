BUSINESS MATTERS  
 
Sustainability Presentation 
 
Sustainability Program Manager Hannah Millar shared a presentation on initiatives and 
programs that the Sustainability Department is putting forth, how they intersect with 
Economic Vitality, and ways that the Sustainability Department and the Economic 
Vitality Department collaborate to support businesses in Louisville and further the 
sustainability goals of the city. 
 
Councilmember Fahey asked about ways to encourage commercial properties and retail 
and restaurants to play a role in drought mitigation. Manager Millar responded that staff 
currently works with Resource Central which is targeted at residential properties and 
have explored options for engaging commercial properties but engaging commercial 
property owners and managers can be difficult.  
 
Councilmember Kern asked about how staffs' engagement and outreach was received 
and what staff has learned from connecting with businesses. Economic Vitality 
Specialist Brian Holihan responded that businesses were happy to engage with city staff 
and were receptive to the resources and information that was shared with them. 
Specialist Holihan shared that having conversations with businesses has provided staff 
with insights into additional things that the city can provide that would be supportive for 
business owners. 
 
Economic Vitality Business Attraction Update 
 
Manager Zarate presented on business attraction efforts that the Economic Vitality is 
involved in, along with feedback that staff hears from businesses that are exploring 
physically locating in Louisville.  
 
Councilmember Fahey asked if business owners have expressed any interest in 
Louisville's population increasing so that they could hire more workers or experience more 
patrons. Manager Zarate responded yes and that retail and restaurant employers have a 
harder time employing people living in Louisville because of the cost of living when 
compared to industrial employers.

Councilmember Kern asked about the state of Colorado’s competitiveness to other states 
in relation to business attraction and retention. Manager Zarate responded that largest 
impact to Colorado’s rankings when compared to other states in relation to attracting new

Mayor Leh asked about how often businesses ask about taxes when considering

Councilmember Fahey leaves meeting at 4:45 pm.

Zarate responded that no, questions regarding differences in local level sales taxes 
typically do not come up. 
 
Mayor Leh asked the percentage of time that staff spent on business retention, retraction, 
and expansion, respectively. Manager Zarate responded that it is roughly a half and half 
split between business retention and attraction and that supporting business expansion 
efforts tend to vary on a case-by-case basis.

Subject:  2027 Draft Economic Vitality Committee Work Plan 
Date: November 12, 2026 
Prepared By: Vanessa Zarate, Economic Vitality Manager 
Presented By: Vanessa Zarate, Economic Vitality Manager 
 
Summary:  
The Economic Vitality Committee has adopted a work plan to help guide meetings and 
recommendations throughout the year. This draft EVC Work Plans aligns with the 2027 
City Council Work Plan, to help EVC make recommendations and proposed policy and 
program changes to the full City Council.  
 
This is the first conversation regarding the 2027 EVC Work Plan. It is expected that the 
EVC provide feedback for adoption of a 2027 EVC Work Plan at a future date. 
 
Background / Prior Discussions: 
The EVC adopted a 2026 Work Plan at their December 11, 2025 EVC Meeting. This 
Work Plan guided EVC meetings, agenda items and policy recommendations 
throughout 2026.  
 
The 2027 Work Plan will provide guidance on EVC topics throughout 2027, with the 
knowledge that the EVC will pivot to address business, economic vitality and program 
needs as they arise.  
 
 
 
Development Proposal: 
 
 
Analysis: 
 The draft 2027 EVC Work Plan is comprised of components adopted by City Council at 
the November 2, 2026 meeting, as well as priorities identified in the 2026 Work Plan.  
 
The EVC Work plan is intended to guide EVC discussions throughout the year, with the 
understanding that unforeseen needs may arise that results in the EVC pivoting 
discussion to address economic vitality/development needs and opportunities.

adopted November 2, 2026. 
 
Fiscal Impact: 
There is no fiscal impact to creating a EVC work plan. Any fiscal impact will be 
addressed at the time of program, policy or event adoption.

Recommendation: 
Staff recommends EVC review and provide feedback.

In the following, staff provides updates on recent activity related to economic vitality

2026 State of the City 
 
In collaboration with the Louisville Chamber, the City of Louisville will host its 2026 State 
of the City event on Monday, November 9, from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m at The Simon. The 
event will begin with the Mayor's State of the City address, highlighting the City's 
accomplishments, priorities, and vision for the year ahead, followed by an update from 
the Louisville Chamber of Commerce outlining its 2027 priorities and opportunities. 
Following the presentations, attendees will have the opportunity to connect directly with 
City departments, City Council representatives, and Chamber leadership through 
informational tables featuring key initiatives. This year's format is intended to provide 
residents and community members with greater opportunities to ask questions, learn 
about upcoming projects and initiatives, share feedback, and identify ways to become 
involved in shaping the community's future. Visit the event webpage to learn more.  
 
EDCC Drive | Lead | Succeed Conference 
 
Staff recently attended the Economic Development Council of Colorados annual 
conference in Cripple Creek from September 27th to October 30th. The event unites 
statewide economic development leaders to share insights, explore solutions, and 
strengthen partnerships for Colorado’s economic growth. Panels and workshops 
provided overviews of Colorado’s economic outlook and introduced innovative tools and 
solutions for pressing issues in Colorado’s economic development sector. To explore 
what the conference had to offer, visit the EDCC event webpage.  
 
2027 Colorado Business Economic Outlook 
 
The University of Colorado Boulder Leads School of Business will be hosting its 
Colorado Business Economic Outlook and Forum on December 7th. This event provides 
a forecast of Colorado’s economy that was built in partnership with 130 public and 
private leaders across Colorado. Along with the economic outlook presentation, the 
event includes an AI workshop and industry specific breakout sessions. Registration is

Small Business Outreach with the SBDC 
 
On September 23rd, staff conducted small business outreach in collaboration with the 
newly appointed SBDC advisor for Louisville. The goal of this was to share workshops, 
tools and other offerings from the SBDC and introduce our new advisor. Staff also 
shared current programs offered by the Economic Vitality department and received 
feedback from business owners on doing business in the City of Louisville. Outreach 
with the SBDC will be continuing on a bi-weekly basis with the McCaslin Corridor next 
on the list. Please reach out to Brian Holihan with any questions.  
 
EV Day – November 16th, 2026 
 
The Economic Vitality department will be hosting its annual EV Day on Monday, 
November 16th at the Louisville Public Library. This event provides city staff and 
leadership with an opportunity to dive deeper into Economic Vitality priorities and 
initiatives, hear from community partners, and explore opportunities for further cross-
department collaboration. To learn more about this event, please contact Vanessa Zarate.   
 
Parade of Lights  
 
The Louisville Chamber of Commerce will host its annual Parade of Lights on Friday, 
December 4, 2026, along Historic Main Street. The free community event will feature 
illuminated parade entries, visits with Santa, holiday performances, local vendors, and 
refreshments. The 2025 parade attracted more than 6,000 attendees and generated 
over 200,000 media impressions. Businesses and organizations are encouraged to 
participate through parade entries, sponsorships, and volunteer opportunities. The 
parade begins at 7:00 p.m., traveling from South Street to Parkview Avenue. To learn 
more about the event, visit the Parade of Lights event webpage.
