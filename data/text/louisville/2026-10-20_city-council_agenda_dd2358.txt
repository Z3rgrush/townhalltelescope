Subject:  Resolution No. 90, Series 2026 — A Resolution Approving a 
Construction Contract for the 2026 Crack Seal Project 
Date: October 20, 2026 
Prepared By: Jordan Jefferies, Assistant City Engineer 
Presented By: Kurt Kowar, Director 
 
Summary:  
Staff recommends approval and award of the 2026 Pavement Crack Seal Project contract 
with Superior Asphalt, LC for $86,951.32 plus a 25% contingency in the amount of 
$21,737.83 for a total authorized amount of $108,689.15. 
 
Background / Prior Discussions: 
The recommended contract supports the goal of providing a safe, well-maintained, effective 
and efficient multimodal transportation system at a reasonable cost. 
 
Work includes application of pavement crack sealant to streets throughout the city that are 
exhibiting significant asphalt cracking. Crack seal is a form of preventative maintenance that 
will extend the life of the asphalt pavement. Please refer to the attached map for locations 
included with the base bid. The Crack Seal Project will commence in October and be 
completed in December. 
 
Staff advertised the 2026 Pavement Crack Seal Project on August 28, 2026. Bids were due 
on September 17, 2026. Four bids were received: 
 
Contractor Base Bid 
Superior Asphalt LC  $86,951.32 
Top-Notch Pavement Solutions $123,821.54 
A-1 Chip Seal $140,565.20 
CR Contracting LLC $194,734.20 
Engineer’s Estimate $107,328.10 
 
Development Proposal: 
N/A 
 
Analysis: 
Staff recommends low-bidder Superior Asphalt LC based upon staff review of bid line items, 
adherence to bid requirements, and contractor reference checks. The bid price is below the 
estimate prepared by staff. Staff also recommends a contingency of 25% to allow for 
Page 14 of 757
additional crack seal locations as identified by staff in the field. Additionally, some locations 
may have more significant cracking and may require more crack seal than anticipated. 
 
Staff is requesting Council’s approval of a contract with Superior Asphalt LC as required 
by the City’s purchasing policy for contracts in excess of $100,000. 
 
Council Work Plan: 
While this project is not specifically listed in the 2026 Council Work Plan, it does align 
with the Council priority of providing core services. Maintenance of the City's street 
stystem is critical to the goal of providing core services to the residents of Louisville.
