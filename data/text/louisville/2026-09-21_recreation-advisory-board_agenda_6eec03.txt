Memorandum 
To:  Louisville City Council 
From: Recreation Advisory Board  
Date:  August 26, 2026 
Re:   2027 Golf Course Fees, Annual Passes & Residents 
The R ecreation Advisory Board appreciates the opportunity to provide th ese 
recommendations to City Council for incorporation in the 2027 budget.  The RAB has 
worked over the last three months to hear from consti tuents (residents and non-
resident golfers), understand Council priorities, and analyze various financial models 
created by the finance staff. W e believe these recommendations repre sent 
competing interests in the best possible way.  
 
2027 Golf Fee Recommendation – 
RAB recognizes the i mportance of the CCGC operating in a fis cally responsible 
manner.  This includes balancing fees charged to the public with an awareness of the 
competitiveness of the local golf scene. While fee revenue should be maximized, this 
cannot come at the cost of pricing CCGC out of the market, thus impacting demand. 
Accordingly, even thou gh it does not cover  anticipated capital costs, the RAB  
recommends the following fees for the next two-year cycle.  
- Fees should b e increased in accordance wi th Model 4a. Thi s includes a 
weighted average of 13.5% and a 6.5% increase in green fees for 2027 and 
2028, respectiv ely, recognizing that some categories  (e.g., Saturday 
mornings) can bear a greater increase than other categories.   
- The cost of cart rental and range balls should remain unchanged for 2027 and 
increase slightly for 2028.  
- See Model 4a graph and chart for specific rates.  
- Green fees alone cannot generate sufficient revenue to fund all of the capital 
projects that will be needed at CCGC.   
2027 Annual Season Passes –

While sometimes considered a “loss leader”, the RAB believes that passes are an 
important offering to our most loyal customers. Pass holders, while avid golfers, are 
also great ambassadors for the CCG C. They bring full fee -paying guests to our 
course and support ancillary services such as golf shop merchandise and restaurant 
sales.  The RAB recommends continuing the current pass programs as follows: 
- Imperial - $4300.00 for the year January-December 2027 (no proration) 
- Regal - $3500.00 for the year January-December 2027 (no proration) 
- Monarch Pass - $650.00 
- Preferred Player Pass - $200.00 (consider enhancing benefits with extra 
perks such as a nominal gift card to The Turn, golf balls etc.) 
There should be no cap on the number of passes sold and no limitations to eligibility 
(e.g. current pass holder or resident). 
Residents –  
The RAB believes that it is important to recognize the value of Louisville residents to 
the long-term success of the Coal Creek Golf Course. As a municipal golf course, 
residents are at the core o f our mission. Al though actual counts are inexact , we 
estimate that approximately 3-5% of CCGC golfers are residents. We would like this 
number to be higher. Therefore, we recommend the following:  
- Resident discounts on weekday green fees, consistent with Senior discounts.  
- Early booking capabilities, similar to those offered to Imperial and  Regal 
passholders.  
- Resident Appreciation Days – ideally one in the Spring and one in the Fall. 
This could include happy hour specials, entertainment, clothing trunk shows, 
golf club demos, free range balls etc.  
Proof of residency is required to be established in adva nce in  order to take 
advantage of the resident benefits.  
 
Respectfully,  
Recreation Advisory Board Members

                 Memorandum 
 
To:  Recreation and Golf Advisory Board  
From:  Jesse DeGraw, Recreation Manager 
Kathy Martin, Recreation and Senior Services Superintendent 
Date:  09/21/26 
Subject: 2027 Fee Proposal 
 
Purpose: 
The purpose of this agenda item is to provide RAB with optional fee increase information for 
2027admission fees to the Recreation & Senior Center.  
 
Background: Included is information on the past year’s fees and prices from neighboring 
municipalities for comparison information. In 2026 resident fees were raised by 3% and non-
resident fees by 7%.  In our Financial Modeling, during the budget process, Finance 
projects ~3% increase in fees/revenues annually from 2027-2032. This % is an 
inflationary increase aimed to offset personnel and material/operating expenses. The 
CPI is used to establish that and is currently projected at 3.9%. 
 
Next Steps: 
The PROST Department will make a 2027 fee recommendation to City Manager and City Council 
as part of the continuing budget process based on feedback received from the board. Staff is 
requesting the board make a recommendation on a proposed fees for 2027
