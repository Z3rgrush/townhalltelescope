 
 
Economic Vitality Retreat 
Agenda 
Monday, November 16, 2026 
1st Floor Meeting Room - Louisville Public Library 
951 Spruce Street 
8:30 AM 
This is in person meeting only and there is will not be an 
opportunity for public comment.   
1. Introductions and Retreat Overview 
2. Fun with Numbers 
Overview of economic and business statistics for the City of Louisville. 
3. Coming Soon to a City Near You 
Discussion of City objectives and initiatives for 2027 and how that might impact your 
business/org/operation. Overview of other activities to include City Budget overview, programs and 
Sundance.  
4. In It to Win It 
Session on what feedback we receive from businesses during attraction, retention and expansion 
visits. As well as feedback surrounding housing, site selection and current economy. Will overview 
Colorado’s Competitiveness landscape and gameplan. (Business feedback, competitiveness and 
needs) 
5. Real Estate Panel 
Real Estate and commercial lending professionals will provide a market update and share thoughts 
on what to expect in 2027.  
6. U.S. 36 Collaborative Panel 
Members of the U.S 36 Collaborative will join the day to discuss how the partners work together, 
what we are working on and how collaboration continues to support economic vitality goals.  
7. Come Hang Out with Us 
Businesses are receiving many inputs, in addition to operating their businesses. Many 
organizations have witnessed stagnant or declining engagement due to the multitude of needs, 
inputs and activities. Brainstorm ways to collaborate, new ways to share messages, streamline 
messaging, etc. What is the best way to engage?  
8. You Get a Car, You Get a Car 
Come prepared to highlight wins from 2026 and well as highlight what needs are expected for 
2027. As the business landscape and economy evolve, what do you need to remain successful into 
2027? 

9. Adjourn
