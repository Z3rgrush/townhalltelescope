VII. UNFINISHED BUSINESS 
 
 
 
 1. Update on Pump Station pump replacements, installation, and inspections 
 
  Public Works Director, Nick Freelen, updated the board on the preparation work for 
SmartBall inspection. The SmartBall inspection is expected to take place between August 5 
(PS1-PS2) and August 6 (PS2-Vista). 
The pump station 1 & 2 pump submittal is still in review between RFE, Town, and Cogent.  
The diesel back-up pump and sound barriers will be removed from PS2. A back-up diesel 
pump will be staged at PS1. 
The wet wells will be lined to extend the life and a manhole on 5th Street will be lined next 
week. 
 2. McCabe Creek Sewer Project 
 
  Kyle Rickert, Project Manager, said the culvert at McCabe Creek and the Florida Street one-
way are complete, along with the curb and gutter and sidewalk on Florida Street. 
After the Lewis Street intersection has opened, TRC Construction will finish the service line 
at 203 N. 6th Street and complete the asphalt on Florida Street. Crews will be moving to 
Rosita Street to begin the replacement project in the next 2-3 weeks.
