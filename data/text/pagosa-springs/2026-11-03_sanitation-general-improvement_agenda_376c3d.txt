Gas Pockets
• Testing determined several types of trapped gas anomalies.
• Entrained Air – small bubbles that can move with flow.
• Gas Slugs – larger, combined bubbles that can be migratory or static. If static, they’re usually at a high point.
• Trapped gas can be caused by:
• H2S
• Pump inefficiencies
• Leaking air valves

Air Valves
• Gas Anomaly Solution
• Reduce H2S
• Install the correct type and number of valves at the correct locations
• Types of Air Valves
• Air release valves-release small amounts of accumulated air while pipeline is under pressure
• Air vacuum valves- release large amounts of air to escape when a pipeline is filling and enter when it is draining. 
• Combination air valves- perform both functions
• The original design of both force mains each include 9 air vacuum vaults

PROJECT: Resolution 2026-03, Certifying Delinquent Sanitation Accounts 
ACTION: Discussion and Action 
  
  
  
PURPOSE/BACKGROUND: 
The District regulations allow the certification of delinquent sewer fees to the County Treasurer for 
collection with property taxes. This process has been a great success in the District’s ability to collect 
delinquent account fees. Accounts eligible to be forwarded to the County Treasurer must be over $150 
and 6 months delinquent. There is an additional charge of more than 30% by the Treasurer when these 
accounts are applied to the tax roll. 
The 2026 ($11,026) delinquent fees are above 2025 ($10,058) due to new accounts added this year. 
There are seven accounts of over $8,100 that have been on the tax lien list for the last several years.  
Staff has strived to assist customers in setting up recurring payments through our online bill pay system. 
Approximately half of the 860 customers are set up to pay online.  
The certification to the County Treasurer guarantees the District will recoup the fees the properties have 
been charged, along with any penalties and interest.

PROJECT: Ordinance 22, First Reading, Exclusion of Miller Property from the PSSGID 
ACTION: Discussion and Action 
  
  
  
PURPOSE/BACKGROUND: 
The Millers petitioned for inclusion into the PSSGID and were included via Ordinance 19 in January 2023. 
After consulting with multiple contractors, it was determined that the cost to connect to the PSSGID 
would be exorbitant. The Millers are petitioning for exclusion because they wish to install an onsite 
sewer treatment system (septic tanks) which are not allowed by the PSSGID rules and regulations. The 
property is currently located outside of Town Limits and has never been connected to the PSSGID.
