 a. Conveyance System Update  
 
  Public Works Director Nick Freelen said the Smart Ball inspection was performed and 
completed on August 5 and 6. Preliminary results show no major leaks. Xylem said the final 
report is expected to take 8 weeks to receive. The preliminary Smart Ball results also 
indicated excess air pockets between PS-2 and Vista. This is expected due to missing air 
release valves in the second section of the pipe.  
Submittal for new pumps was returned to Xylem on 8/12. There was a minor request from 
Roaring Fork Engineering (RFE) to Xylem to revise the suction pipe to match existing pump 
stands and reduce work on installation.  
Surge tank bids are in. Roaring Fork Engineering has compiled and recommended a 
supplier for a bladder-style tank. This is the most cost-effective route to take with a 10-year 
span and the wastewater treatment plant timeline.   
Roaring Fork Engineering has also received a single quote for the broken media scrubber at 
PS-2 and are expecting a second bid.  
 
Board Member deGraaf wanted to comment for the record that this project has shown, time 
and time again, that cheaper is not always better, and he is sure that staff has done their 
research but he wanted it noted. Mr. Freelen said, in this particular case, we are trying to 
balance affordability with practicality and longevity with the plan to move away from these 
pump stations in the future. 
 b. McCabe Creek Sewer Project Update 
 
  Public Works Director Nick Freelen said McCabe Creek will have the asphalt done on 
Florida Street and on the Loma Alley in the next two weeks. Rosita Street began the 7th 
Street manhole replacement and will continue west towards 8th Street through private 
property and the Rosita Street Alley. All utilities have been marked and potholed to help 
reduce the risk of utility strikes as there are a lot of utilities within the boundaries of the 
replacement project. 
 
Board Member Martinez would like to get a full report on where we stand with category fives 
and the impact of the INI on the entire system at the next board meeting.
