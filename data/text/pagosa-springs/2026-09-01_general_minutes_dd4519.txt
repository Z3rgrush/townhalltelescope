 3. Kids Camp Update 
 
  Parks & Recreation Director Darren Lewis said the Town will discontinue operation of the 
Kids Camp Program in 2027. There is growing concerns regarding facility security, state 
regulations, operational challenges, and the overall cost of providing the program. Ms. 
Rebecca Tenpenny, Executive Director of Pagosa Community Initiative (PCI), has 
expressed interest in working to meet the needs of the community kids held at the High 
School. Council Member Williams said this camp has been a support of our early childhood 
education. Council Member Martinez said the Town may need to find a way to assist these 
forty to fifty families to fill the summer daycare needs.  
 
Council Member deGraaf moved to discontinue providing the Kids Camp Program starting in 
2027, Council Member Lindner seconded. Motion Passed with one nay.
