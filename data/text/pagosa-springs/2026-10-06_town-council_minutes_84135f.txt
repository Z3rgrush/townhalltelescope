 3. Resolution 2026-21, Adopting 2027 Budget, Fee Schedule, and Capital Plan 
 
  Resolution 2026-21 adopts the 2027 budget with expenditures of $24,536,628 and 
expenses of $30,594,569 across seven funds. The fee schedule has changes for several 
items, including building and planning, STR rentals, cemetery, plus more. The proposed mill 
levy of 1.561 will generate $173,145 in property tax. Once the final assessment is received 
from the Assessor, the Town Clerk will submit the final mill levy to the County. The 5-year 
Capital Plan proposes large purchases for the community center roof, vehicles, and park 
improvements.  
 
Council Member Lindner moved to approve Resolution 2026-21, adopting the 2027 budget, 
fee schedule, and capital plan, Council Member deGraaf seconded. Motion Passed

 1. Ordinance 1042, First Reading, Ponderosa Mountain Planned Development 
Agreement 
 
  Planning Director Dickhoff and Planning Manager O'Dell said Modern Community Builders 
LLC has applied for the approval of Ponderosa Mountain, a major subdivision, near Hwy 160 
and Gold Mine Drive. The developer is proposing to fully develop the site, in phases, with a 
mix of residential and commercial.  
Ordinance 1042 amends the official zoning map and approves a planned development 
overlay district and a planned development agreement for property known as Ponderosa 
Mountain, located near Hwy 160 and Goldmine Drive. The development will include 
commercial lodging, housing, including workforce housing (90%-130% AMI and deed-
restricted), and amenities. This development covers 70 acres and is proposed in three 
phases. A complete major subdivision must be filed within one year of the PD approval, with 
an extension to encourage development moving forward.  
Ms. Robin Nay said she is concerned about more houses and people that prevent her ability 
to evacuate in case of emergency. 
Mr. Shane Jones said he is the listing broker for the property. He said the comprehensive 
plan the Town has shows high-density on this property.  
Ms. Holly Hasenbuhler said R-6 zoning would be more palatable and said the green space 
is not usable. She said this project is creating a need for workforce housing. She said it will
