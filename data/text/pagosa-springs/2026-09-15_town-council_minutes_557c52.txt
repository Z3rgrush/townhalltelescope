IX. NEW BUSINESS 
 
 
 
 1. Public Art Future Locations 
 
  Several locations have been identified by the Arts Committee that would benefit from future 
public art installations. Locations would be incorporated into future Public Art Calls as 
opportunities arise. The proposed locations include electrical cable panel covers in Town 
parks, river center bathroom, dumpster fence screens, landscaping at Town Hall, 
Yamaguchi labyrinth, bell tower door, 6th Street bridge, several Hwy 160 traffic boxes, 
Community Center ramp, Lewis Street wall at 4th Street, South Park concrete slab, library 
children wall, and the parks and streets trash cans. 
 
Council Member Shabro moved to approve the listed locations for future art, Council 
Member Williams seconded. Motion Passed 
 2. Public hearing and consideration of Ordinance 1039, first reading, granting a 
franchise to G&I Sanitation Services to operate a refuse collection service in the 
Town and to use the streets and alleys of the Town for such purpose 
 
  Ordinance 1039 allows for a franchise agreement with G&I Sanitation for five years, with an 
additional five-year option that may be exercised; this is the maximum allowed by the town's 
charter. This agreement highlights performance standards, including hours of operation, 
appearance and maintenance of the vehicles, and the ability for the Town to enact a 
franchise fee should it decide to do so in the future, along with prescribing a methodology to 
verify and corroborate funds received.  
 
Council Member deGraaf moved to approve Ordinance 1039, first reading, granting a 
franchise to G&I Sanitation Services to operate a refuse collection service in the Town and 
to use the streets and alleys of the Town for such purpose, Council Member Martinez 
seconded. Motion Passed 
 3. A public hearing regarding proposed rates for services provided by G&I Sanitation 
 
  The town charter requires a public hearing before a franchise holder can establish new rates 
for its customers. G&I Sanitation is proposing a fee increase. Mayor Pierce opened the item 
for public hearing. No public comment was received. 
 4. Long Table Event 
 
  The Town is partnering with 4H for a Long Table event being held at Yamaguchi Park on 
October 10th from 3 pm – 6 pm. This is a free community event that will consist of a potluck 
buffet and live music. The costs for road closure plan ($100) and park reservation fees 
($150) will be waived. Council Members Williams, Bergon, and Martinez are in favor of this 
event.
