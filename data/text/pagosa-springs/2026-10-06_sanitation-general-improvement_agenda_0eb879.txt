II. BACKGROUND  
  
The Pagosa Springs Sanitation General Improvement District (PSSGID) (Owner) is beginning the process of 
building a new wastewater treatment facility (WWTF) that will accommodate up to 1 million gallons per 
day of wastewater processing. The WWTF will be designed to handle future nutrient regulations and site-
speciﬁc regulations. The site of the new facility will be approximately 12 acres in the south of Yamaguchi 
Park, adjacent to the San Juan River. The Town of Pagosa Springs  is requesting proposals to obtain a 
qualiﬁed representative to assist the Owner throughout the entire project which is estimated at 5-7 years. 
 
  
  
III. RFQ/P OBJECTIVE  
  
The purpose of this RFQ is to solicit a q ualiﬁed Owner Advisor (Consultant) to contract with the Owner 
and also work closely with the Pagosa Springs Sanitation General Improvement District  to provide 
customary owner’s advisor services for the Construction Manager at Risk (CMAR) procurement, design 
and engineering, preconstruction, and construction phases of the project.

Phase I: Procurement of a Design & Engineering Firm 
 
Phase I Procurement of a Design & Engineering Firm will begin immediately after the awarded 
Owner’s Advisor is under contract for the RFQ/P . The criteria for the Design & Engineering ﬁrm will 
be reviewed with the PSSGID and other key stakeholders. A Design & Engineering RFP/Q will be 
drafted by the Owner’s Advisor and reviewed before the advertisement for this position is released 
in late 2026/early 2027. 
 
Phase II: Design & Engineering – 30% Plans 
 
Phase II Design & Engineering through 30% Plans will develop the plans for the WWTF with the 
PSSGID and other key stakeholders based on the projected eﬄuent limits, wastewater 
characteristics, receiving-water requirements, regulatory requirements, treatment technology, 
site conditions, reliability and redundancy, and budget. The Owner’s Advisor will not be 
responsible for attending regular design meetings but will participate in meetings at key project 
milestones. This phase of the design process will also include developing a project schedule and 
identifying long-lead-time equipment and materials necessary for the WWTF with the Owner’s 
Advisor’s oversight. 
 
Phase III: Construction Manager at Risk (CMAR) Procurement 
 
Phase III Construction Manager at Risk Procurement will advertise this position with an RFQ/P 
after the 30% design plans milestone has been met. The selected Owner’s Advisor will draft this 
RFQ/P for the position and work with the PSSGID and stakeholders to reﬁne the RFQ/P before it is 
advertised. The selection process will include the Owner’s Advisor and they will help with 
contracting once the awardee is selected. 
 
Phase IV: Design & Engineering – 90% Plans 
 
Phase IV Design & Engineering through 90% Plans will bring the WWTF design almost to 
completion. The CMAR will be actively involved throughout the continued development of the 
plans, working collaboratively with the Design and Engineering Firm, PSSGID, and other project 
stakeholders. The CMAR will also be responsible for the procurement of all long-lead time 
equipment and materials to ensure all required items are procured and received in time to meet 
the construction schedule.

At the 90% plan completion milestone, the CMAR will provide a Guaranteed Maximum Price 
(GMP) for construction based on the plans, speciﬁcations, scope of work, and other project 
requirements developed to that point. The CMAR will work closely with the PSSGID and Design 
and Engineering Firm to review the 90% documents, validate quantities and costs, identify any 
remaining design or scope issues, and establish the construction price. The GMP will provide the 
PSSGID with greater cost certainty prior to advancing to ﬁnal design and construction. If the 
CMAR’s GMP is above the PSSGID’s available budget, value-engineering and scope-reconciliation 
processes can proceed until the project reaches ﬁnal design. 
 
Phase V: Design & Engineering – Final Plans and Construction Authorization 
 
Phase V Design & Engineering Final Plans and Construction Authorization will incorporate 
comments from the 90% design review. From there Plans and Speciﬁcations will be advanced to 
100% Construction Documents and all required permitting is ﬁnalized. The construction schedule 
is ﬁnalized and the CMAR prepares for mobilization and construction. 
 
Phase VI: WWTF Construction 
 
Phase VI WWTF Construction will have the CMAR take primary responsibility for managing and 
delivering construction while the PSSGID, Owner’s Advisor, and Design & Engineering Firm provide 
oversight, technical support, and quality control. The Owner’s Advisor will attend regular 
construction meetings, conduct regular site visits, and monitor progress, schedule, GMP , quality, 
and compliance. 
 
The Owner’s Advisor will review pay applications, assist with evaluating change orders and 
potential project risks, and help coordinate communication between the CMAR, Design and 
Engineering Firm, PSSGID, and other stakeholders. The Owner’s Advisor will proactively identify 
issues that could aﬀect the project’s cost, schedule, quality, or successful completion. 
  
Phase VII: WWTF Transition to Operation 
 
Phase VII WWTF Transition to Operation will include the CMAR’s coordination with completing 
punch list items, ﬁnal inspections, equipment startup and testing, commissioning, process startup, 
performance testing, operator training, and closeout documentation for construction. Following 
startup, facility performance will be monitored to verify that the WWTF is achieving the 
established design and operational requirements and to identify and resolve any issues that arise 
during the initial operating period. 
 
The Owner’s Advisor will provide oversight with project close-out documentation, deﬁciency 
tracking, regulatory coordination, ﬁnal document review, payment and retainage, and post-

housing complexes. The Town has 95 vacation rental residential units, not all within the District 
boundaries, and many more second homeowners.
