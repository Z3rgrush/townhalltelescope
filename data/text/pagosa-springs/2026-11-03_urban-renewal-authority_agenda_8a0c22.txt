RESOLUTION NO. 2026-01 
A RESOLUTION OF THE URBAN RENEWAL AUTHORITY APPROPRIATING 
FUNDS AND IDENTIFYING SPENDING AGENCIES THROUGH THE 
ADOPTION OF AN OPERATING BUDGET FOR THE YEAR 2027 
WHEREAS, the Town of Pagosa Springs Resolution 2019-18 created the Pagosa Springs 
Urban Renewal Authority (“PSURA”); and  
WHEREAS, the PSURA operates to eliminate blight and prevent the spread of blight 
within the urban renewal areas of the Town of Pagosa Springs in accordance with the Urban 
Renewal Law of Colorado; and 
WHEREAS, the Local Government Budget Law of Colorado, C.R.S. § 29-1-101 et seq. 
requires that PSURA adopt a budget for fiscal year 2027; and 
WHEREAS, the PSURA finds it necessary to appropriate funds to further the ongoing 
efforts of the authority; and  
 WHEREAS, on November 3, 2026, the PSURA Board of Commissioners held a 
properly noticed public hearing on the budget and the appropriation for fiscal year 2027 and 
interested persons were given an opportunity to register objections; and  
WHEREAS, the PSURA has prepared and submitted a recommended budget for fiscal 
year 2027 that identifies revenues that are equal to or in excess of the budgeted amount 
according to the above-referenced statute. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Pagosa Springs Urban Renewal 
Authority, that: 
Section 1. Budget Submission. The 2027 Budget with amendment(s), if any, is 
attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference as Exhibit A, is hereby approved and 
adopted in essentially the form and content accompanying this Resolution.  
Section 2. Filing.  The budget officer is hereby directed to file the approved budget 
with the Division of Local Government in conformance with the Local Budget Law for the state 
of Colorado. 
Section 3. Severability.  If any part, section, subsection, sentence, clause or phrase 
of this Resolution is for any reason held to be invalid, such invalidity shall not affect the validity 
of the remaining provisions.   
Section 4. Effective Date.  This Resolution shall take effect and be enforced 
immediately upon its approval by the Board.

Introduction 
 
The Pagosa Springs Urban Renewal Authority (PSURA) was established in the fall of 2019. The 
governing board, or Commission, was appointed in 2019 and the PSURA has adopted a guiding policy 
document and bylaws.  
 
To date, there are no budgetary resources for the PSURA.  Any activities of the PSURA, which have 
been minimal, are funded by the Town’s General Fund. In 2021, approximately $4,900 was spent from 
the Town’s General Fund on a planning consultant to assist the PSURA with project planning and 
gathering public input for future PSURA projects. No funds have been expended in 2022, 2023, 2024, 
2025, or 2026.  At such time that funds for the PSURA are established, the funds will be reported using 
an accounting method called the modified accrual accounting method. 
 
Staff for the PSURA is provided by the Town Manager and Town Clerk/Finance Director. This budget 
is provided for state reporting purposes.
