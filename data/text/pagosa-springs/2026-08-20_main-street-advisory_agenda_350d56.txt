I. OVERVIEW 
 1. The Heritage & Culture Committee continues advancing projects focused on 
preserving and sharing local history in ways that are public-facing, engaging, and 
connected to Pagosa Springs’ sense of place. Several projects are underway, but 
the priority for this work session is to determine a clear and manageable path 
forward for Color Pagosa’s Past while continuing momentum on the History Hunt 
and follow-up from Into the Vault.
