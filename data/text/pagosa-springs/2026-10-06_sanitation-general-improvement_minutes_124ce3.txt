 2. Wastewater Treatment Facility - Owner's Advisor Update 
 
  Project Manager Kyle Rickert said the development of the new Wastewater Treatment 
Facility project is moving forward, and the Request for Qualifications & Proposal for an 
Owner's Advisor was advertised on September 28, 2026, and will close on October 19, 
2026. A committee has been selected to review all proposals that are submitted, short-list 
them, and interview them October 28th-29th. A recommendation for the award of the RFQ/P 
will be brought to the PSSGID Board on November 3, 2026. Staff has started processes for 
grants and loans for the project. The $3 million loan for the 1st Street loan was approved by 
CWRPDA.  
 3. McCabe Creek Update 
 
  Project Manager Kyle Rickert said the McCabe Creek replacement project is nearing 
completion. The Rosita replacement project is anticipated to be completed within the next 
week, and a final walk-through with Roaring Fork Engineering will be conducted before the 
project is officially closed out.  
VII. NEW BUSINESS 
 
 
 
 1. Approval to Select Evolve for Pump and Surge Tank Installation 
 
  Public Works Director Nick Freelen said bids were requested from multiple contractors with 
the expertise to complete the job of installing new pumps and surge tanks at PS1 and PS2. 
Bid requests were sent to three separate contractors, but only one, Evolve Infrastructure 
Group, submitted a bid, with the others declining to bid due to scheduling conflicts. Staff will 
report back on warranty and if there is a not-to-exceed amount in the bid. The construction 
should begin in early spring.  
 
Board Member deGraaf moved to select Evolve Infrastructure Group for installation of pump 
and surge tank based on experience and availability for a cost of $1,334,113, Board 
Member Martinez seconded. Motion passed with one nay (Board President Pierce)
