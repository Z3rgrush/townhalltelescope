PUBLIC HEARING ON A REQUEST FOR A SPECIAL USE PERMIT TO ALLOW NEW 
RESIDENTIAL LIVING SPACE 
 
Honorable Mayor Schossow reviewed the protocols to be followed during the hearing, and City 
Clerk Jill Curtis confirmed proper legal notice of the hearing had been published.  City Manager 
Brent Nation reviewed the application, explaining through the downtown corridor there are special 
rules and building codes that require the request for the special use permit to allow for the upstairs 
living space to come to council for approval.  Since the business is a tattoo shop, the State health 
department requires a separate outdoor entry into the living space, and there is an existing 
outdoor entry which will work for the health department requirement.  Melis sa Bird spoke in 
support of the owners and stated they are preserving the look of outdoor appearance, and it is 
cohesive with the Main Street look.  Brent answered council questions. No public comments were 
received by the Clerk’s office.  Councilmember Jim Parks motioned to close the public hearing, 
with a second from Councilmember Urdiales, passing unanimously.   City Attorney Geoff Wilson 
advised the council of its options for action, Councilmember Parks offered a resolution to approve 
the special permit allowing a new residential living space at 305-307 Main St.  That resolution was 
seconded by Councilmember Jon Kaper and approved unanimously.
