PRSENTATION ON NEW DESIGN OF THE FORT MORGAN AQUATIC CENTER____   
 
City Council Proceedings – October 6, 2026 
 
Mr. Underwood introduced Christa and Dane with Essenza Architecture.  They gave the 
presentation and updated the council with the design of the aquatic facility.  Overall, the design 
budget and timeline are unchanged.  Excavation is set to begin this fall. 
              
FIRST READING OF ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING CERTIFICATES OF PARTICIPATION 
REGARDING THE AQUATIC FACILITY 
 
City Manager Brent Nation explained that this ordinance is necessary to obtain the funding to pay 
for the aquatic facility.  It is a financial mechanism for lease financing for large projects.  Andrew 
Ma with Piper Sandler explained to council the Certificate of Participation process.  The COP 
proceeds will be $25 million cash contribution to fund the remaining project cost.  Ma then went 
over important dates for the market, pricing and when the city will receive the funds.  Dalton with 
Butler Snow explained the length of term and pricing and mentioned that the ordinance authorizes 
the city to get into the market when it is advantageous.   
Councilmember Kaper offered a resolution to approve the first reading of the ordinance 
authorizing the certificate of participation and set the public hearing for October 20 , 2026, which 
was seconded by Parks and approved unanimously. 
              
RESOLUTION ADJUSTING THE GAS RATES OF THE NATURAL GAS DEPARTMENT AND 
SYSTEM ENTERPRISE OFTHE CITY OF FORT MORGAN 
 
Gas Superintendent Tony Behrends told the council that a rate study conducted by Raftelis, the 
city’s gas rate consultant, recommended decreasing the gas supply portion of the city’s gas rates 
due to market price increasing. The Resolution presented would decrease the supply charge from 
$0.40 to $0.34, effective October 1.   
Parks offered a resolution to approve the Resolution adjusting the gas rates of the Natural Gas 
Department and System Enterprise of the City of Fort Morgan, which was seconded by  
Councilmember Loren Boyett and approved unanimously. 
              
RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AGREEMENTS BETWEEN THE 
CITY OF FORT MORGAN AND COLORADO INTERSTATE GAS CO. FOR STROAGE AND 
TRANSPORATION OF NATURAL GAS 
 
Gas Superintendent Behrends explained that these were existing and long-term agreements that 
needed to be extended. Behrends said staff is  asking that the contracts be extended for an 
additional five-year period to ensure the gas volumes guaranteed by the agreements would be 
available to the city for that time. 
Parks offered a resolution to approve the Resolution authorizing the mayor to execute the 
agreements with Colorado Interstate Gas Co. for transportation and storage for a five-year term. 
That resolution was seconded by Kaper and approved unanimously. 
              
PUBLIC HEARING ON A REQUEST TO CONSIDER A SETBACK VARIANCE AT 111 GRANT 
STREET 
 
Honorable Mayor Schossow reviewed the protocols to be followed during the hearing, and City

shed which compressors will be housed in to keep the noise down.  Staff recommended moving 
forward with the variance request.   No public comments were received by the City Clerk’s office. 
Parks motioned to close the public hearing, with a second from Urdiales, passing unanimously.  
City Attorney Nina Williams advised the council of its options for action, Parks offered a resolution

WORK SESSION            
 
Staff met with council to discuss the options and processes related to searching for and hiring 
the next Police Chief.  Nation explained that Human Resources would be handling the hiring 
effort, since HR was under his direct supervision he needed direction on three items.  First was 
the appointment of Steve Brown as Interim Police Chief added to the October 20th council 
meeting agenda. Second was moving forward with the current job description for the Police 
Chief with a few minor edits from council.  Direction was then given to staff to move forward with 
a recruitment specialist to assist with the hiring of a new chief.
