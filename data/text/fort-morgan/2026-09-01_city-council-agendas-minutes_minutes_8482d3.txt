SECOND READING AND PUBLIC HEARING ON ORDINANCE NO. 1338 INCREASING 
ELECTRIC RATES 
 
Mayor Doug Schossow reviewed the protocols for the hearing and City Clerk Jill Curtis confirmed 
proper legal notice.  Director of Utilities Doug Linton presented the ordinance.  Cynthia Ruppel, 
resident of Fort Morgan, stated she is opposed to the increase in the electric rates.  Ruppel 
mentioned the last 6 years have been difficult, and many are scraping by.  She had some 
questions which City Manager Nation addressed. There were no comments written/oral received 
through the Clerk’s office.  Mayor Schossow addressed Ms. Ruppel and stated he understood her 
frustration.  The city purchases power and the cost is being passed on to customers, as we can’t 
give it away for free.  Councilmember Garcia moved to close the public hearing, with a second 
from Parks and approved unanimously.  City Attorney Nina Williams advised the council of its 
options for action, and Councilmember Parks offered a resolution to approve the second reading 
of Ordinance No. 1338 increasing electric rates, and Council acting as the Board of Directors of 
the City of Fort Morgan Electricity Distribution Enterprise and publish the ordinance by title only. 
That resolution was seconded by Councilmember Urdiales and approved unanimously.
