City Council Proceedings – August 4, 2026 
 
August 4, 2026 
 
The City Council of the City of Fort Morgan, Colorado, met this day in the Council Chambers of 
City Hall.  The Honorable Mayor Doug Schossow called the regular meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. 
 
The Pledge of Allegiance was given. 
 
Roll Call:   Present: Honorable Mayor Doug Schossow 
 Councilmember Loren Boyett 
 Councilmember Todd Cozad 
 Councilmember Bill Garcia 
 Councilmember Jon Kaper 
 Councilmember James Parks 
 Councilmember Brian Urdiales  
  
The meeting was also attended by City Manager Brent Nation, Police Chief Loren Sharp, City 
Treasurer Kasie Edson, Assistant City Manager/ Director of Community Services Matt 
Underwood, Director of Public Works Tom Acre, Economic Development Specialist Sandy 
Schneider-Engle, Director of HR/Risk Management Jennifer Cuckow and City Clerk Jill Curtis. 
              
PUBLIC COMMENT / AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION       
 
Rosa Beltran, of Log Lane Village spoke about the ongoing Cargill labor conflict. Ms. Beltran 
supports the Cargill employees and mentioned that their  paychecks come right back to the 
community and the City of Fort Morgan is all in this together. 
Melvin Figuera, a resident of Morgan County, also spoke in support of Cargill employees and 
stated he felt that Cargill has divided the community and is suffocating the community and the city 
of Fort Morgan. 
Christuan Lopez a resident of Fort Morgan, has worked for Cargill for 35 years and lived in Fort 
Morgan for 4 years.  Lopez felt that the union has lied to them, the two contracts they voted on 
didn’t make sense for the people to afford to live here. 
              
APPROVAL OF MINUTES – JULY 21, 2026 CITY COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING           
 
Ms. Curtis stated the minutes were presented in their packets . Councilmember James Parks  
offered a resolution to approve July 21, 2026, minutes with a second from Councilmember  Bill 
Garcia, approving unanimously.  
              
PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING AUGUST AS CHILD SUPPORT AWARENESS MONTH  
 
Mayor Doug Schossow read the proclamation. Tammie Wick with Department of Human Services 
accepted and thanked the mayor.  Ms. Wick mentioned that 3.3 million dollars were collected and 
went right back into Morgan County to assist children in 2025. 
              
RECOGNITION OF THE BUSINESS OF THE MONTH FOR MARCH     
 
Ms. Engle presented Business of the Month to Standard Glass. Engle gave the history of the 
business, from the purchase of the Standard Glass by Ray Keefe in 2014 to moving to the larger 
location on State St. Standard offers residential windows, vehicles, mirrors, and even craft glass 
replacement.  Mady Stroh, an employee at Standard Glass, thanked the mayor and council for 
the recognition and stated they look forward to serving the community for many years to come.

APPROVAL OF LOCAL  HISTORIC LANDMARK 325 MAIN STREET     
 
Ms. Engle stated the criteria for  qualifying for a local Historic landmark. Engle described the 
property at 325 Main St. referred to as the Beale Building, detailing the history and how it has 
housed numerous businesses since 1907. Councilmember Parks offered a resolution to approve 
the designation of a local historic landmark, with a second from Councilmember Garcia, approving 
unanimously. 
              
APPROVAL OF LOCAL  HISTORIC LANDMARK 327 MAIN STREET     
 
Engle described the property at 327 Main St., mentioning it was the only stone front erected in 
the downtown district.  Engle also detailed the history of the builder Lyman Baker from the 1800’s 
and the building.  Councilmember Parks offered a resolution to approve the designation of a local 
historic landmark at 327 Main St., with a second from Councilmember Garcia, approving 
unanimously. 
              
APPROVAL OF LOCAL  HISTORIC LANDMARK 329 MAIN STREET     
 
Engle mentioned this will be the 33 rd local historic district landmark if approved.  She went into 
the history of the building, mentioning it is referred to as the Times Building.  The local Spencer 
family history was also mentioned as they ran the newspaper since the early 1900’s.  The Morgan 
County Family Center is now housed in all three of the buildings.  Mary Gross, the director of the 
MCFC has been working with the Main Street Advisory Board on the remodeling process of the 
buildings.  Councilmember Parks offered a resolution to approve the designation of a local historic 
landmark, with a second from Councilmember Garcia, approving unanimously. 
              
2027 BUDGET CALENDAR PRESENTATION        
 
City Manager Brent Nation explained the budget for next year is in the table files with the changes 
shown.  After going through the General Fund in the coming weeks the city can make sure a 
reduction in force is necessary. He further explained the city is going for bottom line effect which 
will show 15% cuts in spending.  Nation highlighted the changes and decreases for the various 
departments. No action was taken. 
              
BIDS, MEETINGS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS        
 
Curtis announced upcoming meetings. 
              
EXECUTIVE SESSION            
 
Parks motioned to move to executive session at 6:37 p.m. for the purpose of determining 
positions relative to matters that may be subject to negotiations, developing strategy for 
negotiations, and/or instructing negotiators, under C.R.S. Section 24-6-402(4)(e); Economic 
Incentives.   
The executive session began at 6:42 p.m. and ended at 7:05 p.m. and were attended by Mayor 
and Councilmembers, City Manager Brent Nation, City Attorney Michaela Szilagyi, Economic 
Development Specialist Sandy Schneider-Engle, Adam Walter and Kidane Mesgena.  At the 
conclusion of the executive session, Kaper reported that the council had met in an executive 
session for the purposes stated on the agenda and discussed economic incentives.
