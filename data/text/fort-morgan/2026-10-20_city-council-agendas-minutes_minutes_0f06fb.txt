The City Council of the City of Fort Morgan, Colorado, met this day in the Council Chambers of

The Pledge of Allegiance was given. 
 
Roll Call:   Present: Honorable Mayor Doug Schossow 
 Councilmember Brian Urdialas 
 Councilmember Todd Cozad

Williams, Deputy City Clerk Lori Lorenzini and City Clerk Jill Curtis. 
              
PUBLIC COMMENT / AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION       
 
Julie Stiewig with Morgan County Waste Coalition, passed out flyers to mayor and council.  
Stiewig reviewed how the program got started, that the MCWC is a great example of what a 
community can do when they come together. They now have two recycling trailers placed at 
Columbine Elementary school and at The Block.  They wanted to thank council and the City of 
Fort Morgan and ask to spread the word about the program. Kyla Smith, also with MCWC, thanked 
the council for their time. 
Barbara Keenan reminded mayor and council that their responsibility is to represent the 
community and feels much of the community wants Sharp rehired as Chief.  Keenan listed off 
monetary costs the city will incur to search for a new chief and special election costs i f a recall 
occurs.  Keenan stated that rehiring Sharp would be responsive to her wishes. 
Lyn Deal spoke about the retirement of former Chief Sharp and feels it is an injustice to the 
community.  She complimented Sharp on his duties as Chief.  Deal stated that she is unhappy 
with the mayor. 
Jack Darnell stated that last Friday citizens met to discuss council and to get council back on track 
and on the path forward would be to rehire Sharp.  Darnell gave council an ultimatum to rehire 
Sharp by November 3, 2026 or he and a group of citizens are ready to start a recall petition.  This 
group will replace the mayor and 3 other council members with people who will bring Sharp back.  
Darnell felt that Sharp was the best chief. 
Ron Shaver spoke about his 40 years of service with the City.  He detailed the history of how the 
city was a mayor council form of government to a city manager system where the City  manager 
oversaw the Police department.  In 2019 the 2E initiative passed and the Police Department was 
moved under the mayor and city council.  Shaver explained that it creates serious chain of 
command challenges and would like the community to revisit and address 2E to move the police 
department back under the City manager.   
              
COUNCIL APPOINTMENT OF STEVE BROWN AS INTERIM CHIEF     
 
Deputy Chief Steve Brown was unanimously appointed as Interim Chief with a resolution offered 
by Councilmember James Parks and a second from Councilmember Brian Urdiales.

REQUEST FOR IN- KIND SERVICES AND FEE WAIVERS FOR THE MCC GALA AND 
REQUEST FOR APPROVAL TO HAVE ALCOHOL ON PUBLIC PROPERTY 
 
Mr. Waite reviewed the MCC Gala request stating the total cost of $3400 includes utilities and 
trash services.  Councilmember Kaper questioned how the floors in the gyms will be protected.  
Waite stated he and Makyle Mares are working together on floor protection.  Waite explained that 
given the longstanding and excellent partnership with Morgan Community College, staff 
recommends accepting the in- kind request.  Kaper offered a resolution to approve the In- kind 
request and allow alcohol on public property, with a second from Parks, approving unanimously. 
              
RESOLUTION EXTENDING THE RIVERSIDE IRRIGATION AND QUALITY WATER LEASE 
AGREEMENT  
 
City Manager Brent Nation reviewed the background and summarized the details of NISP.  The 
current lease is good for a couple more years, but the city would like to extend it for another 10.  
This lease allows the city to have water during drought years. Urdiales commented that the city 
of Fort Morgan is doing very well compared to our neighbors out east who are dealing with some 
water issues.  Parks offered a resolution to extend the riverside irrigation and quality water lease 
with a second from Urdiales, approving unanimously. 
              
RESOLUTION AMENDING AND EXTENDING THE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH 
NCHD 
 
Nation reviewed the Resolution for Northeast Colorado Health Department  that would approve 
and extend the agreement for 2 more years to help secure funding.  Trish McClain with NCHD 
spoke and stated they have received funding but are still waiting for more to come in through 
grants and it has been a slow process. Councilmember Loren Boyett offered a resolution to amend 
and extend the development agreement with a second from parks, approving unanimously. 
              
RESOLUTION AMENDING ALLOTMENT CONTRACT IN THE SOUTHERN  WATER SUPPLY 
PROJECT PIPELINE 
 
Nation reviewed the resolution has to do with the pipeline that brings in CBT water into the water 
treatment plant along with the 2 pump stations that are associated with it.  Northern water 
recognized that the way the original agreement was written 25 years ago wasn’t exactly equitable 
to all the users of the pipeline how the maintenance costs were being allocated.  This amendment 
to the agreement costs $30,000 more to the city, which is minimal.  The city will still be receiving 
the maximum diversion rate through the pipeline.  Parks offered a resoluti on to approve the 
amendment to the allotment contract in the Southern Water Supply Project Pipeline. That 
resolution was seconded by Kaper and approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING ON ORDINANCE 1339  AUTHORIZING THE CERTIFICATE OF 
PARICIPATION FOR THE AQUATIC FACILITY 
 
Mayor Schossow outlined the guidelines to be followed during the public hearing, and Deputy City 
Clerk Lori Lorenzini confirmed that proper legal notice of the hearing had been published.  Nation 
said the ordinance is unchanged since the first reading and explained that this ordinance is 
needed to secure funding for the construction of the Aquatic Facility.  There were no public 
comments received and no council comments were offered.  Urdiales made a motion to close the 
public hearing, which was seconded by Parks and approved unanimously. City Attorney Nina 
Williams advised the council of its options for action, and Kaper offered a resolution to approve 
the second and final reading of Ordinance No. 1339, authorizing the certificate of participation 
regarding the aquatic facility, and authorizing publication by title only. That resolution was 
seconded by Parks and approved unanimously.
