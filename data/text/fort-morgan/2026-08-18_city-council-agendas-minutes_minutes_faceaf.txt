August 18, 2026 
 
The City Council of the City of Fort Morgan, Colorado, met this day in the Council Chambers of 
City Hall.  The Honorable Mayor Doug Schossow called the regular meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. 
 
The Pledge of Allegiance was given. 
 
Roll Call:   Present: Honorable Mayor Doug Schossow 
 Councilmember Brian Urdialas 
 Councilmember Todd Cozad  
 Councilmember Jon Kaper 
 Councilmember Bill Garcia  
 Councilmember James Parks  
 Councilmember Loren Boyett  
  
The meeting was also attended by City Manager Brent Nation, Police Chief Loren Sharp, 
City Treasurer Kasie Edson, Assistant City Manager/ Community Services Director Matt 
Underwood, Utility Director Doug Linton , Economic Development Specialist Sandy Schneider -
Engle, and City Clerk Jill Curtis. 
              
PUBLIC COMMENT / AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION       
 
None.  
              
COUNCIL ACTING AS THE BOARD OF THE ELECTRIC DEPARTMENT AND SYSTEM 
ENTERPRISE OF THE CITY OF FORT MORGAN        
 
Director of Utilities Doug Linton reviewed the rate study that was previously presented to council 
back in May.  Linton stated the ordinance that will increase the electric rates as presented.  Linton 
stated that public notices went out to customers on July 31 with their utility bills and requests to 
schedule a public hearing for September 1. Councilmember James Parks offered a resolution to 
approve the first reading of the Ordinance and to set the Public Hearing for September 1, which 
Councilmember Brian Urdiales seconded and approved unanimously. 
              
2027 BUDGET CALENDAR PRESENTATION OF BUDGET REVENUE AND EXPENDITURES 
OF THE GENERAL FUND           
 
City Manager Brent Nation stated that since the Cargill and Teamsters agreed on a labor contract, 
the city will be adjusting the direction again and the budget slated to be brought to council will be 
tabled and brought back to council at the next scheduled regular meeting September 1.  This will 
give the departments time to adjust their budgets accordingly.  No action was taken

All matters listed under the Consent Agenda are considered to be routine business by the Council 
and will be enacted with a single motion and a single vote by roll call.   There will be no separate

REPORTS BY OFFICIALS AND STAFF               
 
City Manager Brent Nation highlighted upcoming items for the September 1 Council meeting. The 
sales tax report is presented in the packet, and the numbers are where the city was anticipating.  
The storm sewer project is completing punch list and maintenance items. Paving of Ensign, 
Gateway and perform some patchwork around town. 
 
Chief Loren Sharp  reminded the council that since the schools have been redistricted it has 
created some small traffic issues.  The SRO’s have been working in those areas to come up with 
some solutions.  All officers are completing weed enforcement.  Urdiales asked Sharp fo r a 
breakdown of July weed warning, and citations. 
              
BIDS, MEETINGS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS        
 
Curtis announced upcoming meetings 
 
              
EXECUTIVE SESSION            
 
Parks motioned to move to executive session at 6:12 p.m. for the purpose of determining 
positions relative to matters that may be subject to negotiations, developing strategy for 
negotiations, and/or instructing negotiators, under C.R.S. Section 24-6-402(4)(e); Economic 
Incentives.   
The executive session began at 6:17 p.m. and ended at 6:47 p.m. and were attended by Mayor 
and Councilmembers, City Manager Brent Nation, City Attorney Michaela Szilagyi, Economic 
Development Specialist Sandy Schneider-Engle, Sarah Whitney, Kelly Whitney and Rebecca 
Curtis.  At the conclusion of the executive session, Kaper reported that the council had met in 
an executive session for the purposes stated on the agenda and discussed economic 
incentives. 
 
 
Mayor Schossow adjourned the regular meeting at 6:50 p.m.
