City of Lone Tree
Planning Commission Regular Meeting Agenda
Tuesday, October 13, 2026
6:30 PM
Maximum occupancy in the hearing room is limited to 110 persons. Due to hearing room capacity limitations, the City encourages members of the public to coordinate their comments and designate a representative to speak on their behalf.
Public hearing comments will be limited to 2 minutes per person and 4 minutes for individuals speaking on behalf of a group. The Planning Commission may impose additional rules during the public hearing to ensure a fair and efficient hearing.
Written comments are welcome and will receive the same consideration as comments presented during public hearing. Comments received at least twenty-four hours before the hearing will be included in the record. Written comments may be emailed to Chuck Darnell at
chuck.darnell@cityoflonetree.com
.
Please note that the public hearings on the October 13, 2026 Planning Commission agenda are to consider development applications related to infrastructure improvements (roadway and a water booster station facility) that would serve the “Hillcamp” development.
The remaining Hillcamp development applications are still under review and will be scheduled for public hearing at a later date.
Meeting Location:
Lone Tree Civic Center, 8527 Lone Tree Parkway, Lone Tree, CO 80124
Meeting Procedure:
The Planning Commission will gather for dinner at 5:30 PM at the Lone Tree Civic Center. No official business will be conducted, no decisions will be made, and no minutes will be taken.
A Regular Meeting of the Lone Tree Planning Commission will be held in City Council Chambers (upstairs) commencing at
6:30 PM
.
