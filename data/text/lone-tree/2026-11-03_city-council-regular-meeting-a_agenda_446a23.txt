The Lone Tree City Council Regular Meeting will be held in person at the

Submitting Public Comment in Advance.
In addition to speaking during designated public comment periods at the meeting, members of the public may submit written comments in advance. Written comments should be sent to the Lone Tree City Council at cityclerksoffice@cityoflonetree.com.
To request accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) for participation in a public meeting, please contact the City Clerk’s Office at least 24 hours prior to the meeting by email at cityclerksoffice@cityoflonetree.com or by phone at 303-708-1818.
