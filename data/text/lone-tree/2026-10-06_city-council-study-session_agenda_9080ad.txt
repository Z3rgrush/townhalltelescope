City Council Study Session

The Lone Tree City Council Study Session will be held in person at the
Lone Tree Civic Center, 8527 Lone Tree Parkway
Public Comment at Study Sessions.
Study Sessions are intended for informational presentations and discussion only. The City Council does not take formal action or vote during these meetings. As a result, public comment is not included in Study Sessions.
To request accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) for participation in a public meeting, please contact the City Clerk’s Office at least 24 hours prior to the meeting by email at
cityclerksoffice@cityoflonetree.com
or by phone at 303-708-1818.
