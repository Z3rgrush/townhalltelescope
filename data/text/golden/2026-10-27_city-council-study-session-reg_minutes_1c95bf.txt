Public Comment 
 
Mayor Weinberg opened the public comment period at 6:32p.m. She acknowledged the 
emailed public comment that was posted on the website.  
 
Pamela Gould, Lookout View Drive, came to discuss South Golden Road Complete Street 
project. She asked that Council separate the project into two segments. One is the first section 
between Golden High School and the Johnson Rd roundabout and dubbed South Golden 
Speedway. The other section includes the 4 roundabouts and has a 25-mph speed limit. They

asked that Council direct staff to use funding on this section. She asked for these changes 
before the budget is approved in November. 
 
Annika Witt, South Golden Road, wants to address the “South Golden Speedway.” She stated 
that its dangerous to back out of her driveway. She stated she saw a bike accident in real time 
in a roundabout and it was very concerning. She mentioned the sound noise is a problem, 
especially at the peak time of the day.  
 
Carl Gould, Lookout View Drive, wanted to discuss “South Golden Speedway.” He stated it looks 
like a 4-lane highway in the middle of Golden. He wanted to remind everyone that this section 
is near Golden High School with a high level of walkers, bikers and inexperienced drivers. He 
appreciates the separate bike lanes recently installed in Golden. He understands it will take 
time and work to get it where it needs to be but, in the meantime, he wants the speed limit 
reduced to 25 mph.  
 
Jenny and Elie Erwin came to speak about South Golden Road. Elie spoke about the need to 
slow down on South Golden Road. They had a sign that 18 kids signed it and an online petition 
with 77 adult signatures. She stated she has two young children and she is worried about the 
road. She stated that they will not bike or walk on South Golden Road. She stated that is very 
difficult to turn left out of their neighborhood due to traffic. She is requesting to reduce the 
speed limit to 25mph.  
 
Mike Schwartz, South Golden Road, said that between Johnson Road and Golden High School, 
sees 18,000 vehicles a day. He stated that Golden has a good job of completing road calming 
projects on other roads in Golden but its time for South Golden Road. He obtained speed data 
and found that half the vehicles are going over the speed limit of 35 mph. He stated that 730 
homes and apartments are being added between the new complex by King Soopers and Fossil 
Trace which includes another 2,000 vehicles on the road. He stated he sees the bigger picture – 
these things take time and money, and will to prioritize and change. He thanked Council for 
their leadership and what they do for the City. 
 
Mitch Battilla, Adventure West, came to talk about the creek management. He stated that 85% 
of people renting tubes used the shuttles. He also saw more individuals using the public shuttle. 
He stated he knew trash was a big issue this year, they are enforcing no coolers on their buses 
and vans. They also have trash cans on their buses and told individuals that they can’t bring 
single use plastics to the creek. He stated that his family participates in the weekly volunteer 
trash clean up. They also participate in the Vanover park clean up. He stated they had a safe 
and productive season, no major safety issues on the creek or with the shuttle.

Eric Palmer, Jackson Street, spoke about the recent development approved on Jackson Street. 
He has lived in Golden for 17 years and wanted to express his disappointment in this decision. 
He shared his various concerns about the plan, especially for snow removal. He asked for a 
snow management solution that does not include his property or the public alley. He raised a 
broader concern about whose interest is protected when considering new development. He 
stated that growth should not come at the expense of existing residents and neighborhoods. He 
asked if their decisions reflect the vision of Golden.  
 
Eric Vera, Table Drive, came to speak about Google Fiber. He stated it was being installed 8-10” 
below grade, below the surface of the street. He said it’s far below the requirement and when 
inquired, was told it was approved by Council. He asked why are we allowing a multi-billion 
company to have this concession. He stated it was wrong to bring in another internet company. 
He also spoke about water restrictions. He said that we are asked to save water and do our 
part. But he stated we are adding more and more units and we don’t understand limits period. 
He asked Council to do better.  
 
There being no further comment, Mayor Weinberg closed the public comment period at 
6:58pm.  
 
Council/Staff Response to Public Comment 
 
Councilor Kurzinski said hi to her individuals from Ward 1. She thanked them all for coming to 
the Council meeting and encouraged them to reach out to talk to her. She told Eric that his 
voice matters and she wants to support him. She stated that she agrees that South Golden 
Road is a speedway. She agreed with their analysis. She acknowledged the difficulties around 
the Jackson St development. She said she has the contact information for Google Fiber and she 
can talk to them directly to fix the work.  
 
Councilor Cameron thanked for the branding of South Golden Speedway. He found that on 
page 747 of the budget for the breakdown between the money on the grants. He cautioned 
that reducing the speed limit, while possible, will not do much to reduce the actual speed. He 
stated there isn’t any naturally occurring obstacles to reduce the speed limit. With regards to 
Eric’s comments, he is saddened every time he comes and says he is not heard. He pushed to 
have previous complaints by Eric fixed. He said he is not that concerned about the fiber lines 
being shallow. He apologized to Eric for recusing himself for that discussion on Jackson. He 
explained that he didn’t want the project to happen, and he could not be objective.

Mayor Pro Tem Vitry thanked Elie for coming and speaking to them. She thanked Elie for all her

Council/Staff Comments/New Business 
 
Councilor Kurzinski stated she didn’t have any updates but District 1 has monthly meetings. The 
meeting dates and times are posted.  
 
Councilor Pfau shared about DRCOG, the Denver Regional Council of Governments. They are 
coming out with the Power Ahead program, a 200 million dollar grant. There is a $1,500 rebate 
when upgrade to a heat pump. Power Ahead program has also created a green force workforce 
program to encourage more trade employees. The program includes apprenticeship and 
training. One of their career hubs is in Golden. He encouraged citizens to speak with him if 
interested.  
 
Councilor Cameron shared that at GURA last week, he found out that we annexed the Junction 
with M1 and C1 zonings. Due to this, by right, they can have a data center at the Junction. He 
also shared that through the CML newsletter, he learned about the CMCA Clerks awards. He 
nominated Stacy McClure and she won the Outstanding Contribution to a Medium Municipality 
award. He went up to Estes Park on Thursday to celebrate Stacy and gave a speech. He said last 
week he went to the Water Now Summit. They talked about turf replacement and that many 
cities don’t yet have tiered rates but we do. He shared that speeding on 24th in front of Golden 
High School is a concern and needs to be addressed. He said that people drive too fast in 
Golden. He noted that two concrete bollards have been taken out on Jackson from people

Subcommittee Update Regarding 3rd Party Investigation: Councilor Knecht shared that they are 
still underway. Currently there have been 11 folks of people noticed for interviews, they are 
halfway through their list of individuals. They are working on document review. The timelines 
are being developed. The group is hoping to have more sustainable update in November.  
 
Subcommittee Update Regarding City Manager Recruitment. They met with SGR and talked at 
length about the consolidated timeline for this process. The job is currently posted and will 
close on November 2nd. They are expecting SGR to screen and select 6 semi-finalists who will 
complete a questionnaire and video responses with ranking system instructions no later than 
November 20th. Council will review all that information and utilize the ranking system to rank 
them, provide that infomation back to SGR no later than November 29th. They will have finalists 
announced on December 1st. They will have a community meet and greet for residents and 
businesses on December 11th. City Council interviews will be conducted on Saturday, December 
12th. The Management Team meet and greet will be held the same day, all times to be
