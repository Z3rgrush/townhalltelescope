Councilor Kurzinski stated that Ward 1, District 1, District 2 are still confirming location for meet 
and greets, stay tuned. 
 
Councilor Pfau provided the subcommittee update –  
They informed all firms interviewed of their decision regarding outside counsel. The 
subcommittee requested advice from city staff on who will be point contact for sharing 
information with outside legal counsel.  The City Clerk is best placed to be that point of contact. 
That advice was shared with Corey Hofman. Sandra Llanes finished negotiating the contract 
with HPWC. The recommends an executive session during the 9/22/2026 City Council meeting 
in order to provide a venue for Corey Hoffman to provide legal advice.  
 
Cameron shared that an unhoused individual, who has resided in the City of Golden for 16 
years, has found housing. The City staff were very persistent in helping the individual and he 
gave kudos to the City for that. He just heard about a vandalism incident and he is very 
frustrated. There is a crevice garden created in White Ash Park which is a rock structure that 
requires low water usage. E-moto kids tore it apart and made it into ramps. He stated that 
Ulysses has had $1,000’s of dollars of damage right after it reopened. He said it affects our 
budget and what we can do moving forward.   
 
Councilor Evans asked for an update on e-motos and enforcement. Carly stated they had an 
internal meeting today and they are going to MTAB in the next month or two to discuss the 
issues regionally and in Golden as well as potential options for next steps. After MTAB, they will 
go to Council with a Study Session. Councilor Evans asked if there was increased enforcement 
presence in specific problems areas. Carly affirmed. 
 
Mayor Weinberg stated it is National Voter Registration Day today. She encouraged folks to 
check the JeffCo website for their voter registration status as well as ballot notifications.  
 
Consent Matters 
a) Consideration of a motion to approve the Minutes of September 8, 2026 
 
Councilor Cameron MOVED to approve Consent Matters, Mayor Pro Tem Vitry seconded.  
Voting results were as follows: 
YES: Knecht, Cameron, Evans, Weinberg, Vitry, Pfau and Kurzinski    
NO: None 
Mayor Weinberg declared the motion carried.

Regular Matters  
 
Consideration of a motion to adopt - Resolution No. 3357 – A Resolution of the City 
Council of the City of Golden, Colorado, approving a contract with Hoffman Parker 
Wilson & Carberry, P.C. to assist in conducting a third-party investigation 
 
The subcommittee shared the history of this item, the relationship and the agreement before 
Council.  
Councilors asked questions and the subcommittee answered them.  
Councilor Cameron MOVED and Councilor Knecht seconded to approve Resolution No. 3357.  
Councilors provided additional comments concerning the Resolution.  
Councilor Cameron MOVED to strike 4th and 5th Where As clauses on the Resolution, Councilor 
Knecht seconded the motion.  
Councilor Cameron amended the amended motion to also strike the word “consequences” 
from the 6th Where As clause. Councilor Knecht seconded.  
Voting results were as follows:
