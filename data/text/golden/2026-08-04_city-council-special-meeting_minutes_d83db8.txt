Consideration of a motion to adopt – Resolution No. 3344 – A Resolution of the City of 
Golden, Colorado, creating and appointing a temporary Council subcommittee to 
conduct a third-party investigation 
Mayor Weinberg stated that last Tuesday, a public statement from the City Manager, was read 
by her. After that time, it became apparent that an investigation by the city was not 
appropriate. Council began looking at outside legal counsel to run a true 3rd party investigation. 
They only have the opportunity to discuss this all together when in an appropriate meeting 
which makes it difficult to move quickly. The Resolution is to set up a temporary subcommittee 
for the purpose of the independent investigation. The subcommittee would select the outside 
legal counsel and bring the recommendation to council for a vote. The subcommittee will do 
the work and provide updates to Council. 
Councilors provided questions and feedback. 
Councilors proposed both a parallel path with REDI and to expand the scope of the outside 
investigation to include internal/REDI group practices as a part of the 3rd party investigation. 
They agreed to complete an investigation with outside special counsel and supporting the 
continuation of the work REDI has already started as well as publicly saying they support and 
welcome recommendations from REDI at any point, not just at the conclusion of the 
investigation. 
Councilor Cameron MOVED and Councilor Kurzinski seconded to approve Resolution No. 3344 
Voting results were as follows: 
YES: Knecht, Cameron, Evans, Weinberg, Vitry, Pfau and Kurzinski
