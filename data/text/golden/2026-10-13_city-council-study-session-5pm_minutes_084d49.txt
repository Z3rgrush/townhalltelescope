Public Comment 
 
Mayor Weinberg opened the public comment period at 7:17 p.m.  
 
Peyton Gibson, Cheyenne St, shared that Golden is her home and utilizing ADU’s makes living 
here affordable. She asked Council to approve these types of projects to support affordable 
housing. She asked Council to approve the upcoming GURA projects.  
 
David Clarke, Constituent Services Representative for Congresswoman’s Pettersen’s office, 
provided an update on her work in DC and the district. They were excited to see the passage of 
the 21st Century Road to Housing Act. In her role on the Housing and Insurance Subcommittee, 
they worked on multiple acts that went through. He acknowledged the summer district 
wildfires, the Representative took action to respond and introduced 6 bills around wildfire. 
They recently had a Telephone Town Hall where she spoke to 18k constituents. He encouraged 
citizens to go to website to be in the loop for future events.  
 
Robert Howe, local resident who is currently unhoused, stated that the City’s Housing Navigator 
has been excessively helpful until now. He asked for a referral for assistance to Recovery Works 
and stated she has fallen off. He asked for assistance from Council.  
 
There being no further comment, Mayor Weinberg closed the public comment period at 
7:27pm. She acknowledged the emailed public comment.  
 
Council/Staff Response to Public Comment 
 
Mayor Pro Tem Vitry stated that she is confident the concern will be addressed since Bodhi’s 
supervisor is in attendance at the meeting.  
 
Councilor Cameron thanked Peyton for providing some successes with ADU’s. In regards to the 
Junction, the zoning may allow multi-family but given its isolation to walkable features that 
exist elsewhere in town, he doesn’t perceive it to be a good multi-family location.

***Subcommittee Update Regarding 3rd Party Investigation 
Councilor Knecht acknowledged that she speaks for this project but that Gerchard is very 
involved in the work. She stated that the desk audit is in progress. Information for the following 
departments have been released to ILG and is being compiled – Fire Department, Human 
Resources, City Clerk, City Manager, and City Attorney. ILG will be scheduling interviews over 
the next few weeks on a rolling basis as they get through each set of materials and will 
determined who else should be interviewed and what additional documentation may be 
needed. ILG signaled a concern that generating an analysis of historical incidents and discipline 
measures back to September of 2022 might exceed its “not to exceed” budget. We will monitor 
and bring to Council if additional funds are needed beyond the not to exceed amount.   
 
Emily presented the Rental Housing Inventory Report. Council asked questions and staff 
answered.  
 
Councilor Knecht and Councilor Kurzinski presented their New Business Policy. Mayor explained 
that a New Business Proposal’s intent is to assess interest in Council to decide if they wanted to 
take this step. Councilor Knecht explained that they are looking at creating a city-wide data 
collecting and sharing policy. Council asked questions and they were answered.  
 
Interest in moving forward with the New Business Policy:

Consideration of a motion to adopt - Resolution No. 3365 – A Resolution of the City 
Council of the City of Golden, Colorado, reversing or affirming the decision of the 
Planning Commission with respect to PC26-03, PC26-04, and PC26-05 – Grace Fleming, 
City Planner 
 
Mayor Weinberg explained the appeal process. No new information will be requested and no 
new information will be considered. It is the facts on the record. She clarified that City 
Councilors cannot participate in quasi-judicial matters if they are prejudiced and they must 
recuse themselves. She asked if anyone has a conflict of interest. Councilor Cameron recused 
himself and stepped out of the room.  
Rick Muriby, Grace Fleming and Karl Onsager presented the resolution.  
Councilors asked questions and staff answered them.  
The Appellant provided a presentation.  
Councilors discussed the item. Councilors provided additional comments concerning the 
Resolution.  
Mayor Pro Tem Vitry MOVED to reverse the Planning Commission’s decision and reaffirm the 
director’s original approval. Councilor Pfau seconded to approve Resolution No. 3365a.  
Voting results were as follows: 
YES: Weinberg, Vitry, Pfau and Kurzinski    
NO: Evans, Knecht 
Mayor Weinberg declared the motion carried.
