B. KOSI Holiday Flip the Switch Promotion – The Golden Hotel has approached staff and 
Visit Golden with an opportunity to co-sponsor a holiday promotion with KOSI 101.1 a local 
radio station that makes a switch to all holiday music on November 19th.  The opportunity 
includes promotion of a “Flip the Switch” event at the Golden Hotel and ongoing marketing 
opportunities with the station for the city from October through December.   Representatives 
from KOSI and the Golden Hotel will present the opportunity for the board’s consideration.  
The requested sponsorship from DDA is $7500. Board discussion and action is 
requested.  
 
C. Holiday Light Contract Extension -   The contract for holiday light installation and takedown 
is periodically put out to bid per the City purchasing policies.   Generally, there are no more 
than two bids.  Per the purchasing policies, the current three (3) year contract can be 
extended for an additional two (2) years allowing for the next bid process to occur in early 
2028.   Given the successful implementation under the current contract, staff is 
recommending DDA board authorization for a two-year contract extension, with SavaTree for 
$99,594.00, for 2026-2027 and $102,581.82 for 2028 for a total of $202,175.82 for the next 
two years.  Staff recommends that the board adopt a motion authorizing staff and legal 
counsel to negotiate a contract extension with SavaTree for a two-year contract 
amount not to exceed $ $202,175.82 and for the DDA Executive Director to execute the 
contract extension. 
 
D. Authorization of Fall 2026 Shade Tree Program – Per the Board direction initiated at the 
August 5th retreat and formally approved in the August board meeting, staff met with the City 
Forrester, and determined that in addition to a longer term program to plant and maintain 
shade trees in the DDA area, there is an immediate opportunity to plant approximately 12 - 
14 street trees in the newly constructed bulb outs at intersections and the parking lot exits on 
Arapahoe Street between 12th and 13th Streets.  The opportunity will be structured as a portion 
of the City’s planned fall 2026 tree planting under the City’s contract.   The estimated cost for 
this fall will be at or under $10,000.    As part of the City contract, the DDA board simply needs 
to, by motion authorize the expenditure up to $10,000 to occur this fall.  Determination 
of the 2027 program and whether it is in the City contract, or separately will be determined 
next year. 
 
E. Authorization of Contract for Refurbishment of Santa and Reindeer on the Arch – One 
of the board’s priorities for this year is an effort to repair or replace the aging Santa and 
Reindeer that appear on the downtown Welcome Arch each holiday season.  Staff spoke to 
a few companies that could have taken on the project, but were not inclined to take on the 
job.  Recently, staff secured a bid proposal from a local sign company to replicate the figures 
rather than try to repair.   The bid proposal is for $22,982 , which is less than the $40,000 we 
thought it might have cost for a new version.  Staff recommends that the board adopt a

motion authorizing staff and legal counsel to negotiate a contract with Signs by Meeks, 
LLC for an amount not to exceed $22,982, for execution by the City, and DDA 
reimbursement. 
 
F. DDA Community Engagement Event -   Staff is recommending the Board approve a 
Downtown Block Party, to be held on Saturday, October 10th, from 11am to 3pm, in the public 
parking lot on the north side of 12th Street, between Astor House and Miners Alley. The event 
would feature community activations, live music, and performances from Miner's Alley 
Performing Arts Center and the Foothills Art Center at Astor House, along with food offerings 
from local vendors. This would be an all-ages, alcohol-free event designed to give the public 
an opportunity to meet DDA Board members and learn more about the DDA's ongoing efforts 
to support a vibrant downtown Golden. Staff will be working with the subcommittee to finalize 
the informational posters and a survey that will be deployed at the event. This event will focus 
on the DDA actions to date and also ask questions about the idea of a public plaza in 
downtown Golden. Staff requests that the Board approve an expenditure of $2,500 to 
support the event, covering costs such as vendor coordination, performer fees, and 
event logistics. 
 
G. Downtown Map Design and Installation – Staff has been approached by Visit Golden and 
the communications department to contribute 50% and $10,000 to the new downtown map 
redesign and sign updates.  This work would begin this year with new map signs being 
installed in early 2027. This would also include the update of the existing map signs downtown 
as well as the digital and printed versions of the map that would be used by the Welcome 
Center and Visit Golden.  Additional budget for new map sign placements will be addressed 
by a future downtown wayfinding funding request.   Staff requests that the Board approve 
an expenditure of $10,000 to contribute 50% to the costs of these new downtown 
maps.  
 
H. DDA 2027/ 2028 Budget Introduction – As discussed annually, the DDA board is required 
to adopt a budget for the subsequent year in November, in order to be reflected in the City 
budget.  Since the City does a two year budget, the draft DDA budget is for 2027 and 2028, 
however, the formal approval (and submittal to the State of Colorado) will only be for 2027.   
Since the board saw an initial draft version in August, the September meeting board 
discussion may be only a brief update.   
 
For this introduction, there are only a few line items to point out: 
 
 The state legislature again adopted legislation essentially reducing the assessed 
valuation of real property, which will result in a slight decease in the DDA base 
property tax revenue (line 14) and a lower than previously anticipated property tax 
increment revenue (line 7).   2027 will be reassessment year, with to be determined 
effects on 2028. 
pkt pg. 4
 
4 
 
 
 Similarly, the TIF Agreement Reimbursement Line items (58 thru 64) are slightly 
reduced from the initial draft,  and from 2026 actuals. 
 
 Traditionally the amount budgeted for DDA grants has been $130,000 annually and 
actual expenses have been less.   For 2026, the major change was the Public Safety 
Power Shutoff (PSPS) grant program.   That was anticipated to be a one time event.  
The balance of grant expenses for 2026 are projected to be about $150,000.  The 
draft budget document includes $200,000 for 2027 and 2028. 
 
 The major planned expenditure in 2027 and 2028 will again be the DDA contribution 
to Joint and City projects (line 49).  The final number for 2026 is still being finalized (in 
part based on the timing of the Ford Street electric line undergrounding contribution).  
In general, the board can reasonably assume up to a total of $2,250,000 over the two 
year period and still maintain the amounts “committed” to the potential Welcome 
Center and Miners Alley Performing Arts Center capital projects, and base reserves.  
Since many projects take more than one year to plan and implement, the board may 
wish to put a higher amount in 2027 than 2028.  The determination of specific projects 
can start late this year, and continue into 2027. 
 
 The draft budget also includes $200,000 each year as contingency.   Contingency 
funds have not traditionally been used, however, in 2026, they are offsetting the extra 
grant expenses.  Between the two years, contingency funds will also help assure a 
base level of reserves.   
 
The budget discussion will continue in October with updates and board questions and 
discussions.

A. DDA Strategic Meeting Recap and Project Prioritization – 
 
Staff and the Board recapped the August 5th strategic planning session and reviewed the projects 
recommended for short term action at that discussion, and tallied rankings submitted by Board 
members across short, medium, and long-term project opportunities. The Board narrowed down 
and engaged in detailed discussion regarding several prospective priority projects, focusing on 
catalyzing the development of the "Wire Transfer" block and the "Wells Fargo" block, investigating 
longer term transit options for the RTD owned rail corridor east of Ford Street, evaluating the 
potential design for a Ford Street Underpass, and exploring the feasibility of establishing a Red 
Rocks event shuttle operating from the DDA.  
 
Ball MOVED to officially accept the short term projects discussed on August 5th, including 
shade trees, the Goosetown area study, and the 11 th Street sidewalk project, and to add 
the above projects as DDA priority projects.; Miller SECONDED. All Board Members 
present agreed.

regarding the initial Golden Hour Mashup event, commending the strong marketing efforts and 
community turnout while expressing enthusiasm for upcoming events in the series. Board 
Member Weber noted that while he was initially skeptical about the promotion, he was 
pleasantly surprised by the substantial turnout and regarded the event as a clear success.
