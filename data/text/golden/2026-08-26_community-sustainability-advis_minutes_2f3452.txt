Business and Action Items 
A. Clayworks project Sustainability Update and Amendment Request 
Representatives from the Clayworks development summarized sustainability progress to date, including all-
electric design elements, energy and water reductions, geothermal energy, on-site solar, and green building 
certification efforts. They also presented a proposed Official Development Plan (ODP) amendment to allow 
limited natural gas use for accessory amenities (pool and spa heating, fireplaces, and communal grills) to 
support multifamily building amenities and market competitiveness.  
The Board expressed concern that the proposed expanded natural gas uses are not consistent with Clayworks’ 
original sustainability commitments. There is general preference that members would prefer to see Clayworks 
retain their original commitment to net zero and to promote other existing public pool opportunities rather 
than adding a private four-season pool. If Clayworks continues to pursue the amendment, the Board 
recommended alternatives such as reduced amenity scope, shared amenities, timers or operational controls, 
electric-ready infrastructure, and additional permanent offsets or mitigation. The Board questioned the 
emissions modeling, including its assumptions about Xcel’s current grid mix and planned grid decarbonization, 
and noted that proposed solar offsets would not fully cover the new natural gas use. 
The amendment request is under staff review and will ultimately proceed through Planning Commission and 
City Council. Staff will summarize the Board’s comments to provide feedback through the review process. 
B. Budget Update 
Staff provided an overview of the proposed 2027 sustainability budget, including grant funding, professional 
services, programs, capital projects, and proposed funding for Board priority projects identified during the 
annual strategic meeting, such as construction and demolition work, micromobility, reuse program support, 
and microgrid studies. Staff also reviewed 2026 sustainability rebate program utilization and expenditures, 
following the program closure after all available funds were committed. The Board recommended increasing 
the 2027 rebate request to $200,000 and requesting a 2026 budget amendment to add $54,000 for the final 
quarter of this year. The Board noted that additional 2026 funding would be especially valuable ahead of the 
September residential battery backup workshop, given strong participation at the earlier workshop and 
demonstrated demand for residential rebates. The Board also discussed the need to better understand and 
support commercial energy users, including through targeted outreach, regional business engagement, and 
potential partnerships. 
Current Projects 
A. Staff & Board Updates 
Staff and the Board discussed Board member participation with outside organizations, including interest from 
Sustainable Golden in having CSAB members participate in its meetings. Staff clarified that Board members 
may participate in outside organizations in their personal capacity but cannot represent CSAB’s official views 
unless directed by Council. Staff also provided updates on commercial energy benchmarking, noting that staff 
are narrowing the covered buildings list and will bring a proposal to CSAB later this year. Staff also shared 
updates on grant-funded work, including planning, RFP development, initial spending, and exploration of 
additional grant opportunities related to large building decarbonization, trees, and bear-resistant carts.
