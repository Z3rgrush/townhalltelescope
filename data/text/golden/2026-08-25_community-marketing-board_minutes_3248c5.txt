2. Special Events Review Committee (SERC)
The Board discussed the need for a Community Marketing Board representative to serve on the Community Special Events Review Committee (CERC). The committee meets monthly and provides recommendations regarding new special events as well as improvements to recurring events. Board representation will provide greater transparency and ensure the Community Marketing Board’s perspective is represented in special-event discussions.
Melissa Lutz volunteered to serve as the Board’s representative. The Board expressed appreciation for her willingness to serve and discussed coordinating her attendance at the next CERC meeting.
3. Strategic Plan Review
The Board reviewed the final draft of the 2027 strategic plan. Discussion centered on the definition of a “visitor” and the role of the Community Marketing Fund in serving businesses throughout Golden.
Staff explained that the Community Marketing Fund is funded through sales tax generated by businesses throughout the community, creating a broader mandate than a traditional lodging-tax-funded destination marketing organization. Board members discussed whether the organization should view residents, day visitors, overnight visitors, and customers of businesses such as retail and restaurants as part of its broader audience.
The Board agreed that the strategic plan should reflect a balance between tourism, community marketing, resident engagement, and the visitor experience. Members discussed four primary strategic themes, including off-season visitation, changing the value and perception of tourism, strengthening the Golden brand story, and improving the visitor experience. Stewardship was identified as an important component of the plan.
The Board also discussed concerns regarding the visitor-volume figure included in the strategic plan. Members agreed that the figure (17 million) should be reviewed and potentially revised or removed if the methodology cannot be adequately supported.
4. 2027–2028 Budget Planning
Staff presented the proposed 2027–2028 budget and reviewed changes from the previous year. A significant discussion focused on the allocation between media buying, creative services, media reporting, account management, SEO, and other marketing functions.
Staff explained that the proposed structure consolidates several functions under the new creative/media agency relationship and is intended to provide more strategic targeting, management, reporting, and flexibility. The Board discussed whether the apparent reduction in direct media-buying dollars accurately reflected the total investment in marketing and agreed that the budget should be presented in greater detail so that members can clearly understand where funds are being allocated.
The Board requested greater detail and transparency within several budget categories, including media buying, co-ops and partnerships, memberships, account management, strategic plan implementation, and group sales. Staff agreed to provide a more itemized budget and narrative explanation.
DVA Contract
Staff discussed the proposed DVA contract and the upcoming City Council consideration of the media-buying and creative/media-reporting components. Staff discussed concerns that the term “destination stewardship” could be misunderstood as an effort to reduce visitation.
Members emphasized that stewardship and visitor management can complement marketing by encouraging responsible behavior while continuing to support tourism and local businesses. (Examples discussed included messaging encouraging visitors to dispose of trash appropriately and behave responsibly while recreating in Golden.)



Small Business Marketing Grant
Jen Thoemke proposed establishing a grant program to help new Golden businesses with marketing expenses. The Board discussed potentially allocating approximately $20,000 annually and developing eligibility requirements, an application process, and grant parameters.
Members discussed whether funding could come from profits associated with Golden In Golden, whether unused funds could roll forward, and how the program would interact with the Board’s existing funding authority. The Board generally supported adding a line item for small-business marketing grants, with additional work needed to establish the program structure and financial requirements.
Website, Branding and Visitor Guide
Staff reviewed several planned 2027 investments, including a new Visit Golden website, brand redesign, and visitor guide. The visitor guide will be redesigned to provide broader access to businesses rather than relying primarily on advertising sales and may eventually be produced in seasonal editions.
The Board also discussed banners, maps, printing, and the possibility of using Downtown Development Authority banner inventory during periods when nonprofit banners are not scheduled.
Partnerships and Grant Opportunities
The Board discussed moving Visit Denver and Colorado Tourism Office partnership funding into the co-ops and partnerships category. Staff explained that this structure would provide flexibility to pursue matching grants and other partnership opportunities rather than limiting funds to specific advertising placements.
5. Mashup Program
The Board concluded the meeting with a discussion of the Mashup program. Staff reported that $33,500 is currently earmarked for the program, supporting 10 Mashups involving approximately 40 businesses. The most recent Mashup was significantly affected by heavy rain, although several participating restaurants reported exceptionally strong Wednesday-night business.
The Board discussed the program's early results and agreed that it is still developing momentum. Members noted positive feedback from participating businesses and community members, including families attending the event. Staff intends to continue the program and “double down” on the September event rather than make major changes based on one weather-affected event.
Staff identified several opportunities to improve the visitor experience, including better wayfinding, maps, staff positioned at key intersections, and more visible staff apparel. The goal is to encourage attendees to explore multiple participating businesses rather than visiting only the business they initially intended to visit.
The Board also discussed improving digital promotion of Mashups through the Visit Golden website. Future plans include individual event listings, Mashup tagging, a dedicated calendar, QR codes, regular weekly social promotion, and increased promotion during the two weeks leading up to each event.
6. Next Steps / Special Meeting
The Board determined that an additional meeting would likely be necessary to finalize the 2027–2028 budget after receiving additional information regarding the DVA contract and City Council action. Staff will circulate information following the City Council meeting and develop a more detailed budget for Board review.
The meeting was adjourned at 11:29am.
