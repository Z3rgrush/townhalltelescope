Study Session Agenda - 5:30p.m.Chair: Mayor Pro Tem Vitry
5:00 p.m.- 5:20 p.m.	Wildfire Management Update	  City staff members from The Wildfire Management Group will provide an update on the progress of the "Wildfire Management and Resiliency Planning" Council Initiative and its associated key projects as well as other ongoing operations of the internal Wildfire Management Group.
Wildfire Management Update
5:20 p.m.- 5:40 p.m.	Historic Preservation Board Authority and Direction on Regulatory Pathways  Presenter: Lauren Simmons, Senior Planner 
Staff will provide an update on the City’s Historic Preservation Policy implementation and seek Council direction regarding the appropriate level of regulatory authority for the Historic Preservation Board.
Historic Preservation Policy Direction
5:40 p.m.- 6:00 p.m.	Drought Follow-up and Next Steps  Presenter: Anne Beierle, Public Works Director and Brian Tracy, Deputy Public Works Director 
Staff will provide an after-action review of Golden’s 2026 drought response, including water-use outcomes, implementation challenges, and lessons learned.
Drought Response Review

The City Clerk's Office maintains a taped recording of all regular and special council meetings for a period of six months after approval of the minutes. In the event that someone wishes a transcript of a portion of a meeting, a copy of the tapes may be purchased. Because of circumstances beyond our control, a tape may or may not adequately insure a verbatim record of the proceedings.
