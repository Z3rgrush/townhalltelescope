Public Comment 
Alex Philipson of North Ford neighborhood provided comment and expressed a need for a 
crosswalk on Golden Gate Canyon Drive at Catamount. Staff acknowledged the need and 
outlined some additional ramp changes that might be needed to accommodate.  
Alex also expressed desire to stripe parking spaces on Washington downtown. Or as an 
alternative some additional spaces signed for short-term parking for short-duration trips 
such as picking up food.
