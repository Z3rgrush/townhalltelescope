Public Comment 
Cody Ard, described challenges he experienced while unhoused, including obtaining a driver’s 
license, the impoundment of his vehicle, and subsequent encounters with law enforcement. He 
expressed concerns about alleged harassment, the ﬁnancial and employment impacts of 
ongoing court proceedings, and the treatment of unhoused individuals. He highlighted his local 
employment, bike repair business, and support for others in the unhoused community, and 
urged city and county oﬃcials to address his concerns and protect individual rights and dignity.

City Manager Recruitment Process Discussion – Mayor Weinberg 
Mayor Weinberg introduced the city manager recruitment discussion. Human Resources 
Director Kristen Meier was available to answer questions. 
Council reviewed four recruitment options and discussed costs, the scope of the search, 
applicant screening, Council and staﬀ responsibilities, and employment contract negotiations. 
Council considered conducting portions of the process internally and obtaining outside 
assistance. Discussion addressed staﬀ workload, independent contract negotiations, and the 
beneﬁts of outside expertise and public conﬁdence in the process. 
Council discussed minimum qualiﬁcations, Human Resources screening, Council review of 
qualiﬁed applicants, and lessons from the previous recruitment process. Council also 
emphasized attracting a diverse applicant pool. 
Mayor Weinberg summarized support for an internal and statewide search with an outside 
employment expert to assist Council with applicant screening, interviews, and employment
