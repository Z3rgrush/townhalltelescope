2) Updated Agreement for City Administrative Services 
GURA has an agreement which was last updated in December 2022 with the City of 
Golden for administrative services and staff to support the board. See attached current 
administrative services IGA adopted by Council Resolution 2904.      
Urban Renewal Authorities (URAs) in Colorado typically take one of two approaches for 
support staff for their authorities: 1) Dedicated Independent Staff, (Usually the case with 
larger URAs such as Denver, Fort Collins and Colorado Springs where annual Staffing 
costs often exceed $250,000 per year), and 2) City-Staffed in smaller cities (Including 
Golden, Lakewood, Lafayette and Louisville and where there is are City staff that share 
time with the URA an other duties within the city and annual support staff costs range 
from $45,000/yr to $150,000/yr depending the level of activity of the URA).   In either case 
the URA is required to have an executive director, and often requires additional support 
staff, including IT, administrative and financial management to operate effectively. In 
general, independent staffing is more costly than the city-staffed approach which is why 
URAs operating in smaller municipalities often elect to go that route.  
As presented to the board during the last budget discussion, Staff is recommending an 
increase in the annual admin service costs from the current $60,000/yr to an updated 
$80,000/yr.  The increase is driven primarily by the reality that there will soon be five 
Urban Renewal A reas to support  administratively, up from the original three Urban 
Renewal Areas GURA has supported since 2014/2015. The $80,000/yr cost is based on 
the estimated costs of service related to  staff hours across the departments the city 
deploys to support GURA.   This revised estimated cost was also used in the 2027 and 
2028 budget estimates. Total actual staffing costs for the same level of service including 
all overhead and benefits is estimated to be $126,600/yr.  See attached Admin services 
Cost estimates for details. At $80,000/yr, GURA receives good value for the  
administrative services provided.  Staff is requesting that the board vote to update the 
administrative services contract with the city be updated from the current $60,000/yr to 
pkt pg 4
the proposed $80,000/yr with automatic renewals annually, following the form of the 
existing agreement on all other matters.   This would likely be finalized and adopted by 
City Council to take effect starting in January 2027. 
Board discussion and action is requested 
3) The Junction URA Formation Update  
As mentioned in the Subcommittee Report, Staff have been engaged with the local taxing 
entities to negotiate the Tax revenue sharing agreements.  Thus far, all entities have 
committed to timetables to complete and adopt their respective agreements with GURA 
before the end of the year and before the 120 day statutory limit which expires on 
December 23, 2026, as was requested by the board and detailed below.   
However, there has been a critical timeline adjustment based on the requirement for the 
County Assessor to certify the new URA before December 10th, 2026 in order to maintain 
the 2026 Assessed Value.  This will require all revenue sharing agreements to be fully 
executed and the URA approved by City Council on or before December 9th.   
• Jefferson County – Initial negotiations have concluded with an agreement in 
principle to an 88% GURA/12% Jeffco revenue sharing agreement that was 
presented to the BCC in a study session on October 27th.  The BCC unanimously 
supported the agreement to move forward and it is on the BCC public hearing 
agenda with a target adoption date of November 17th.  
• JCSD – Draft 100% revenue sharing agreement sent for review.  May be 
addressed by board in November with Final approval expected on November 
12th.  
• JCPL- Draft 100% revenue sharing agreement sent for review.  May be addressed 
by board at a study session on 11/12 followed by a regular meeting on 11/19.  
Final adoption is anticipated at November 19th meeting.  
• MHFD – Have issued a letter agreement understanding that the development 
does not impact their operations and they have agreed to 100% of Increment to 
GURA for the Junction Plan Area.  
• City of Golden – In preparation for a city council study session on the revenue 
sharing agreement between GURA and the City on November 10 th, Staff 
requested a letter from the Golden Fire Department about the expected fiscal 
impact of the Junction Development.   A copy of this letter is attached including a 
maximum fiscal impact estimate.  Based on estimates from Pioneer Development, 
the proposed development is forecasted to generate $5,320 of sales tax increment 
pkt pg 5
that would flow through to fire budget annually, This estimate is based on the 
recent annual transfers from the General fund to the fire budget at about 7% per 
year. If the GURA Board and City wish to completely cover the annual service cost 
provided by the Interim Fire Chief, above the maximum, it could remit a portion of 
property tax increment. If we assume that the City retains half a mill (0.5 mills), 
that equals $13,700 annually. Together with the sales tax generated by the gas 
station, total estimated revenues flowing through to the Fire District equal about 
$19,000 annually, exceeding the projected maximum cost of service by $4,000 
annually. Any new sales tax uses would only increase the annual revenue coming 
from the Junction property. It is important to put into context that every mill of 
property tax revenue remitted back to a taxing entity instead of the plan reduces 
its bonding capacity by approximately $300,000. This reduction in bonding 
capacity should be weighed against any proposed revenue sharing agreement.  
The adoption of the City-GURA revenue sharing agreement as well as the URA 
adoption has been moved to the December 8th City Council Meeting.  
Finally, based on discussions from last meetings if the board would like to explicitly 
restrict a data center or server farm from being built in the proposed development, 
there is an opportunity to do so by amending the draft plan document.   Proposed 
language would be reviewed and available to the board with the adoption of the plan 
at the next meeting.

AGREEMENT 
 
For the consideration hereinafter set forth, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby 
acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
1. Term; Renewal.  The term of this Agreement shall commence as of the date of mutual 
execution by the Parties and shall continue through and including December 31, 2027.  This 
Agreement will automatically renew for additional one-year terms commencing on January 1 and 
terminating on December 31 of each subsequent year  unless terminated by the P arties in 
accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

 Provision of Administrative and Operational Services.

for GURA.

payable and receivable, and budget monitoring services for GURA.

b. GURA will fund all of its other operational, programming, and project costs out of 
its operating budget.
