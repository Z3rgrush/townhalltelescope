4. Holiday Partnership: KOSI 101 & Golden Hotel 
• Presentation: Representatives from KOSI 101 and Heather Schneider (Golden Hotel) 
presented a holiday promotional partnership.  
• Campaign Details:  
o KOSI 101 flips to 24/7 holiday music in November.  
o The official "flip" event will take place at the Golden Hotel with on-air talent and 
a winning family pushing the launch lever.  
o Reaches ~500,000 to 1,000,000 listeners across the Denver metro area.  
• Financial Investment: 
o Total sponsorship: $17,000 split ($7,500 from Visit Golden’s media budget and 
$7,500 from the Downtown Development Authority [DDA], which was approved 
by DDA, and $2,000 from Golden Hotel).  
• Deliverables & Assets: 
o Custom audio ad spots, digital dashboard/streaming banners, live remote 
appearances, social media posts, and inclusion on KOSI's index page throughout 
November and December.  
o On-air talent will highlight local businesses across Downtown, North Golden, and 
South Golden.  
5. Golden Hour / Mashup Debrief & Future Events 
• Feedback & Observations: 
o Merchants noticed slight traffic increases, but noted the concept can be 
overwhelming for shoppers.  
o Starting at 6:00 PM is too late for school nights; dark/rainy weather slowed 
evening foot traffic.  
o Small business bandwidth makes monthly event planning difficult without 
funding/grants.  
• Proposed Adjustments: 
o Rebrand the event (e.g., "Golden Night Out").  
o Shift timing to 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM.  
o Simplify participation (e.g., stamp passports/dance cards, QR-code opt-ins via 
Visit Golden portal, featured treasure hunts).  
o Consider moving future late-night shopping events to Fridays or Thursdays.  
 
 
6. Goldens in Golden 2027 Operations & Logistics 
• Parking & Shuttle Plan: 
o Due to capacity constraints at Splash/HHS, primary satellite parking will shift to 
the Martin Marietta lot (~600 spaces off Colfax & Heritage Rd) and nearby 
industrial areas (400–600 spaces).  
o Fleet plan: 4–6 buses for longer routes and 7 shuttles for closer loops.  
o Vendor/drop-off footprints will remain at Parfet Park and the rented Foss lot.  
o Off-duty Jefferson County Sheriff deputies will assist with traffic control at key 
intersections.  
• Marketing Restrictions: 
o Per City Council direction, no direct promotional marketing will be conducted. All 
communications will focus strictly on "Know Before You Go" visitor responsibility 
and logistical details.  
7. Banner Program & Wayfinding Updates 
• Light Post Banner Program: 
o Transitioning from a free/non-profit reservation model to an internal, city-
branded program managed jointly with DDA.  
o Seasonal, high-quality branded banners (e.g., responsible visitor messaging, shop 
local) will run year-round.  
• Wayfinding & Maps: 
o Consolidated wayfinding initiative across 8 city departments covering pedestrian, 
driver, and map standards.  
o Updating downtown tear-off maps with dynamic QR codes tailored to specific 
locations  
8. Tourism Data & Analytics Report (YTD through August 2026) 
• 61+ Mile Market (Overnight/Regional Visitors): 
o Total trips and visitor days remained flat year-over-year.  
o ~50/50 split between day trips and overnight stays.  
o Visitor spending showed a slight decline in mid-to-late summer, mirroring 
statewide trends.  
o Top origin markets: Colorado Springs, Grand Junction, Pueblo, Houston, Austin, 
and Chicago.  
• 7–60 Mile Market (Local/Drive-In): 
o Represents over 80% of total visitors, dominated almost entirely by day trips.  
o Top local origins include Denver, Arvada, Wheat Ridge, and surrounding metro 
communities.
