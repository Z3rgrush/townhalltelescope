Copies of ordinances are available to council and those persons in the audience. Ordinances will be published according to law. Public hearings are held in the Council Chambers of City Hall, 911 10th Street, Golden, Colorado, at a meeting beginning at 6:30 p.m.
The City Clerk's Office maintains a taped recording of all regular and special council meetings for a period of six months after approval of the minutes. In the event that someone wishes a transcript of a portion of a meeting, a copy of the tapes may be purchased. Because of circumstances beyond our control, a tape may or may not adequately insure a verbatim record of the proceedings.
