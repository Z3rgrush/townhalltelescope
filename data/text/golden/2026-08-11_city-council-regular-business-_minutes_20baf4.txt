Council discussion and possible action following the Executive Session regarding the City 
Manager personnel matter 
Mayor Weinberg shared the Council discussion in the executive session. She stated they 
received advice on Charter, Code and contract with the City Manager.  
 
Councilor Cameron stated that they received public comment before the meeting and he 
appreciated that. He stated that their conversation was only about the City Manager as 
anything outside of that is not in their legal authority.  
 
Councilor Kurzinski stated that the constraints on the discussion legally is hard for someone 
who is used to sharing their mind. But maintaining trust with the citizens and staff is critical.  
 
Councilor Knecht stated that tonight was the first time that they could have this conversation in 
executive session. They feel a shared sense of responsibility to do what is right. She takes heart 
in the fact that they can hold hands and make steps together, as a community.  
 
Councilor Cameron thanked all the people who reached out privately and trusted him to hear 
their feelings. He stated the trust issue is paramount. He also said that it important that the 
employees know that we care deeply about this issue. He is committed to helping the City heal.  
 
Mayor Pro Tem Vitry stated that this situation is unfortunate. She said that the City Manager’s 
leadership no longer meets her expectations. He was dismissive and lacked compassion. City 
needs to move on and deserves better.  
 
Councilor Evans stated that many city employees, residents and members of the Management 
Team have reached out and shared their thoughts. The way it was handled has led to a lack of 
trust and confidence in the City Manager and top leadership. They are committed to healing, 
transparency and accountability.  
 
Pfau stated that his experience with issues like this over the past 30 years has been around zero 
tolerance policy. When something like this happens, you take an immediate response. The 
event happened in March but the real response took place two weeks ago. He is on the 
subcommittee to complete the independent investigation. His preference was to wait until the 
investigation was completed to make decisions but with the legal advice they received, 
administrative leave is not an option with this particular situation. That is the background he 
has with the vote about to take place.

Mayor Weinberg acknowledged with deep disappointment, that the work has been done over 
the past 5 years, those changes were important but were superficial. The incident that 
happened and the decisions that followed exposed an underlying system that needs major 
change. She stated she was too naïve and trusting. She accepted the system as is and that her 
role was separate. She is committed now to supporting what needs to happen. She stated that 
she has a loss in trust in the current leadership. Her conclusion is that, as a City, we need a new 
city manager at this time.  
 
Cameron said that a few weeks ago when he first learned about the incident, he tried to see it 
from the City Managers perspective and give him grace. He stated that subsequent information 
was learned and lack of trust in staff and council has led him to the same conclusion as the 
Mayor. 
 
Cameron MOVED to terminate at-will employee City Manager, Scott Vargo, and authorize the 
City Attorney to negotiate a separation agreement, Councilor Knecht seconded.  
 
Weinberg acknowledged this is a challenging decision and no one has taken the responsibility 
lightly. She thanked Council for their thoughtfulness in this moment.  
 
Voting results were as follows: 
YES: Knecht, Cameron, Evans, Weinberg, Vitry, Pfau and Kurzinski    
NO: None 
Mayor Weinberg declared the motion carried. 
 
Mayor Weinberg asked for the agreement of Council to allow the Appointee Subcommittee – 
Councilor Kurzinski and Mayor Pro Tem Vitry- to work with the City Attorney and Human 
Resources on the separation agreement and bring a resolution back to council at a future 
meeting. 
Mayor Weinberg asked Council to move forward with the appointment with Carly Lorenz as the 
Interim City Manager. She directed the Appointee Subcommittee to work on an agreement 
with Carly and Human Resources to bring back to Council in the form of a Resolution.  
Councilor Kurzinski MOVED to appoint Carly Lorenz as the Interim City Manager. Councilor 
Cameron questioned the pace of the resolution moving forward. Mayor Weinberg asked to 
expedite the agreement. Councilor Cameron seconded the motion to appoint Carly Lorenz as 
Interim City Manager. Councilor Knecht voiced her support to move this expeditiously. Mayor 
Weinberg acknowledged and appreciated all that Carly has done these past three weeks.

Public Comment 
Mayor Weinberg opened the public comment period at 7:59pm.  
 
Mayor Weinberg acknowledged the emailed comment was received and placed into the packet.  
 
Saoirse Charis-Graves, Harmony Village, acknowledged that what Council did was very difficult 
and she commended them for their bravery. She thanked them for their deep consideration

There being no further comment, Mayor Weinberg closed the public comment period at 8:01

Council/Staff Response to Public Comment 
 
Councilor Knecht continued to express gratitude to the many folks who reached out with public 
comment and in other conversations. It’s hard to hear but she is grateful. 
 
Mayor Pro Tem Vitry thanked everyone who submitted public comment, thanked Saoirse for 
speaking and stated she appreciated all the voices. She stated they have asked for their voices, 
they hear them and they thank them. 
 
Councilor Kurzinski thanked all who submitted public comment. She stated it’s hard to navigate 
this and how to work to gain trust back. She stated this is surreal, but they made the decision to 
be here and support their community. She believes they will look back and know they did the 
right thing.

Councilor Evans echoed the previous comments. She stated that they have received many 
public comments on this subject. They have taken the step to start the outside investigation 
and another step to replace the City Manager. She stated that now it’s another step to build 
trust and start healing. She stated they have a lot of work left to do. She said they will continue 
to do what they can to recover from this.  
 
Councilor Cameron stated he has been struggling for years since George Floyd and as a member 
of Golden Anti-Racism Collective to see change in our community. Two years ago he took a 
cross down from South Table Mountain. He has taken down white power stickers from trail 
signs around town. He points this out for the people who might say “one bad apple” in the GFD. 
He stated that we have issues in our society that we are continuing to not face up to. The public 
comments make a difference. The world we live in is a work in progress. He wants to do the 
work. He is saddened by it.  
 
Mayor thanked everyone for the comments.  
    
Council/Staff Comments/New Business 
 
Councilor Pfau raised the event on Sunday with the release of Officer Greg Metzel from the 
hospital. It was a very emotional event. He reiterated that we need to give support to our PD 
and Fire. 
 
Councilor Cameron asked staff when the next REDI public meeting will be held. He stated that 
he hopes that the employees feel welcome tomorrow night to attend the REDI listening session 
at the GCC from 5-6:30pm. He stated we want to start healing.  
 
Councilor Knecht noted that the 3rd party investigation subcommittee has met a few times and 
created a matrix to evaluate the 4 referrals provided by CML. They are evaluating the referrals 
based on cost, timeline and availability, cultural competency and subject-matter expertise. They 
contacted all 4 firms on Sunday, August 9th and they have received responses from three firms. 
They want to interview all the referrals within the next 2-3 weeks and bring the selection 
results to Council for review. They are holding themselves accountable for this process.  
 
Mayor Weinberg stated that they will add the Council Subcommittee report on the 
investigation onto the agenda for each meeting. She mentioned that there are other things in 
the City she wanted to tell folks. There is a community meeting on the final clear creek trail 
design. It’s at the GCC from 5:30-7 on Thursday night. This weekend the Chamber of Commerce 
is having a Fine Arts Festival at Parfet Park on Saturday and Sunday. Also on Saturday and
