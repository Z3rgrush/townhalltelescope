Current Business
Call for Public Comment
No one was present for public comment. Staff acknowledged receiving a written comment by email from Brian Quarnstrom and included it in the meeting packet. Karen Oxman submitted a written public comment regarding artwork near the Golden Pond in person during the meeting.
Nathan Richie provided an update on the ACC’s plans to develop a location charrette beginning in late fall. The process will help the ACC identify and prioritize potential locations for future public artwork.
Cindy Jaye responded to Brian Quarnstrom’s public comment regarding artwork on the water tower. She noted that the project may have previously been brought before the City but stalled due to questions surrounding funding mechanisms. Kristie Brice agreed to review past PAC meeting minutes to confirm the project’s history.

Golden Welcome Center Presentation, Lora Knowlton
Lora Knowlton gave a brief presentation on the Golden Welcome Center, including its background, current offerings, and proximity to City functions.

Stories Worth Telling Presentation, Gabe Rovick
Arts and Culture grantee Gabe Rovick presented information about his organization, Stories Worth Telling, including its history, location, events, films planned for the festival, and future goals. Staff noted that Gabe also owns Studio F4D, which produces the City’s public artwork videos. Commissioners asked questions and offered suggestions regarding community involvement.

Strategic Plan Update 
Nathan led a discussion about the strategic plan, beginning with a brief overview of the planning process and the need to incorporate the Museum into the plan. He invited individual feedback, followed by an open discussion among the Commissioners. Kristie Brice noted that the planning process is particularly challenging because the ACC is still relatively new and the Museum is undergoing changes under its new department. She also emphasized that developing the strategic plan is the responsibility of the ACC. Commissioners noted that the process is also helping clarify their roles and responsibilities. Commissioners agreed that the current version is more readable than the previous draft and encompasses all of the goals of the ACC.

Staff clarified that the strategic plan does not require City Council approval, unlike the annual work plan. Rick Souders reiterated that the plan is intended to serve as a starting point and will evolve as the ACC continues to develop.
Len Matheo asked about increasing funding to support events hosted by cultural organizations. Staff explained that additional funding would require a specific request to City Council. Rick Souders noted that Gabe Rovick from Stories Worth Telling is using the arts and culture grant to support a cultural event and noted that other organizations should use their grant funding similarly.
A motion to approve the strategic plan as presented was made and passed.

Staff Report
Nathan Richie shared that he invited the Leeds School of Business, which previously conducted an arts and culture employment study for the City, to present to the ACC in November. He noted that the Leeds report relied on IRS tax codes, which may not capture the full scope of arts and culture employment.
Nathan asked Commissioners to begin identifying the metrics they would like the ACC to track and the goals those metrics would support. He also noted that establishing a mission and vision may help the ACC develop goals and, ultimately, metrics for evaluating progress.

Rick Souders suggested reconvening the Strategic Plan Subcommittee one final time to develop mission and vision statements. Nathan asked Commissioners to provide draft statements and/or key words and phrases for the mission and vision by September 8 for the subcommittee to review.
Nathan also provided updates on the Lions Park Public Artwork project, noting that the jury is larger than usual and includes significant Commissioner participation. Cindy Jaye asked whether the full ACC could review the Lions Park finalists’ proposals. Kristie Brice invited Cindy to attend the meeting, explaining that anyone may observe the process, but only members of the jury may vote.

New Business
Rick Souders clarified that more than three Commissioners may serve on a subcommittee and encouraged Commissioners to let him or staff know if they are interested in participating.

Sarah acknowledged the challenges the City is currently experiencing and outlined steps being taken to build a healthier workplace culture. She also offered to speak individually with any Commissioners who would like to discuss the matter further.

Adjournment
The meeting adjourned at 7:31 p.m.
