Business 
 
A. Pilot Trash and Recycling program Update -  Dwyer and Glueck provided an update to 
the proposed grant funded pilot program to introduce seven day a week trash and recycling 
service downtown.  City Sustainability staff are working to seek input from potential haulers 
and downtown stakeholders to determine if such a program may be feasible on an on-going 
basis.  Issues to determine include how many subscribers it would take for the service to be 
fiscally sustainable, level of service desired by different businesses, and the ramp up period 
when potential subscribers may still be under contract to other haulers.  The board 
encouraged the continued investigation.   
 
  
B. Golden Hour Mashup Promotion Update – Dwyer updated the board about the recently 
released promotion developed with the City Community Marketing staff, involving three 
monthly mid week late afternoon promotions whereby merchants would partner together and 
schedule activities in their stores  to boost local traffic and community building.  The board 
discussed the updated idea, and after the discussion Ball MOVED to approve an increase 
in the DDA budget for downtown matching grants to businesses from $10,000 total for 
the three events to $20,000. Miller SECONDED. All commissioners present agreed.
