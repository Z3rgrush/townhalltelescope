Demolition of Historic Site
In accordance with section 18.58.220, the Board must review this application 
as a certificate of appropriateness for demolition of a historic site. With 
respect to certificates of appropriateness for the demolition of historic sites, 
because such a demolition presumptively violates th e standards of 
18.58.180, the Board shall impose the stay of demolition described in section 
18.58.230. If the owner believes that the historic site no longer meets the 
criteria for designation, the owner may apply for revocation of designation as 
set forth in section 18.58.120.
Stay of Demolition
In accordance with section 18.58.230, a stay of demolition must be imposed 
by the Board because this is a designated historic site. The Board may 
impose a stay suspending action on a demolition application for a period not 
to exceed one hundred ninety (190) days from the date the permit application

During the period of a stay, the Board may take any  action that it deems 
necessary and consistent with chapter 18.58 to pres erve the building or

limitation: 
(a) Consulting with civic groups, public agencies a nd interested

or agencies;

otherwise be demolished; 
(d) Salvaging building materials; 
(e) Exploring modification of the project to preserve the property; 
(f) Exploring alternative uses of the property which would preserve it; or 
(g) Board appointment of an ad hoc committee of two board members 
to pursue the actions above. 
Outcome of the Stay
Because stays are used for properties over which the board only has non-
binding authority, it is ultimately the owners decision whether to accept any 
alternative plan for the structure. If during the period of the stay the owner 
has neither withdrawn the application for demolitio n nor modified the 
application to avoid demolition, the director shall  issue the certificate of 
appropriateness upon expiration of the stay with a finding of inappropriate.

RE: Letter of Intent  Certificate of Appropriateness Application (Demolition) : Garage Rebuild 
Proposal
Property Address: 403 18th St, Golden, CO 80401
Dear Members of the Historic Preservation Board,
We are submitting this letter of intent in support of our application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for 
the demolition of the existing detached garage and construction of a new detached garage at 403 18th 
Street. This letter describes the reason for the proposed demolition and the design intent of the 
replacement structure, consistent with the requirements of Golden Municipal Code Chapter 18.58, 
Historic Preservation.
Reason for Demolition
The primary reason for demolition is that the existing garage is structurally unsound and unsafe for 
continued use. The structure is no longer weatherproof and cannot be secured, leaving its interior and any 
contents exposed to the elements and vulnerable to unauthorized entry. Photographs documenting the 
existing condition of the garage are attached to this application, consistent with the requirements of 
Golden Municipal Code Section 18.58.140(2).
In addition to this primary safety concern, the project will provide covered parking for our vehicles, 
which the existing structure’s deteriorated condition no longer safely accommodates, and will add an 
office and bedroom space on the second level of the new structure to meet our family’s needs.
Description of Existing Garage
The City of Golden’s 2002 Historic Resources Reconnaissance Survey (State ID No. 5JF3314) describes 
the existing garage on the property as having a gabled roof with wide lap siding and double wooden 
doors. The structure has deteriorated over time to the point that it is no longer structurally sound or safe 
for continued use, as shown in the attached photographs documenting its existing condition.
Description of Proposed New Garage
The proposed replacement is a detached garage of approximately 904 square feet on the main level, with 
additional finished space on the second level. The design follows the Cape Cod architectural style of the 
primary residence, using a gabled roof form, siding, and door detailing consistent with the historic garage 
described in the city’s survey record, so that the new structure preserves the original architectural integrity 
and character of the historic property.
The second-level space will include an office and bedroom for personal family use. This space will not 
include a kitchen and will not be established, marketed, or used as a short-term or long-term rental unit or 
as a separate accessory dwelling unit. It is intended solely for the personal use of the property owners.
Architectural Compatibility
The home’s architectural style is Cape Cod, a residential form classified under the Colonial Revival Era 
per the City of Golden’s historic district design guidelines, and the new garage has been designed in the 
same Cape Cod style to match. The proposed demolition and replacement is consistent with the general 
standards for certificates of appropriateness set forth in Golden Municipal Code Section 18.58.180, and 
with the standards for demolition and new construction within historic districts set forth in Sections 
18.58.210 and 18.58.200. The massing, roof form, and materials of the new garage are designed to be 
compatible with the primary residence and the character of the East Street Historic District.
We appreciate the Board’s consideration of this application and welcome the opportunity to discuss the 
project further at the Board’s convenience.

Dear Members of the Historic Preservation Board,
We are submitting this letter of intent in support of our application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for 
a residential addition and interior renovation at 403 18th Street. This letter describes the purpose, scope, 
and design intent of the proposed project.
Purpose of the Project
The purpose of the proposed work is to increase the interior living space of the home in order to 
accommodate a revised floor plan layout that better suits our family’s needs, while preserving the historic 
character of the residence.
Description of Proposed Exterior Changes
The project consists of an addition along the West elevation of the house. The existing West wall will be 
bumped out so that it is flush with the existing West wall bump-out already present on the home, 
extending the building footprint modestly beyond its current line rather than introducing a new, separate 
projection. The roofline will be extended to cover the expanded wall area and fully enclose the new 
interior space. Roofing and siding materials for the addition will match the existing materials on the house 
in type, profile, and color.
Additional Window Modifications
In addition to the West elevation addition described above, the project includes the following window 
modifications, each designed to be consistent with the home’s Cape Cod architectural style:
On the South-facing wall, the existing window above the kitchen sink will be replaced with a bay 
window. A new basement egress window will be added on the South wall, on the West side and below the 
new bay window. The existing window on the East side of the South wall will be removed.
On the East wall, a new transom window and a smaller bathroom window will be added.
Architectural Compatibility
The home’s architectural style is Cape Cod, a residential form classified under the Colonial Revival Era 
per the City of Golden’s historic district design guidelines. The proposed addition and window 
modifications have been designed to be fully compatible with this style and with the existing structure in 
massing, proportion, materials, and detailing, consistent with the general standards for certificates of 
appropriateness set forth in Golden Municipal Code Section 18.58.180. The primary front-facing façade 
and overall historic form of the residence are preserved, and the window changes on the South and East 
walls are designed to complement the home’s existing fenestration pattern. The design intent throughout is 
to preserve the original architectural integrity of the home.
We appreciate the Board’s consideration of this application and welcome the opportunity to discuss the 
project further at the Board’s convenience.
