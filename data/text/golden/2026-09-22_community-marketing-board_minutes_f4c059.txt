4. Holiday Partnership: KOSI 101 & Golden Hotel
Presentation: Representatives from KOSI 101 and Heather Schneider (Golden Hotel) presented a holiday promotional partnership. 
Campaign Details: 
KOSI 101 flips to 24/7 holiday music in November. 
The official "flip" event will take place at the Golden Hotel with on-air talent and a winning family pushing the launch lever. 
Reaches ~500,000 to 1,000,000 listeners across the Denver metro area. 
Financial Investment:
Total sponsorship: $17,000 split ($7,500 from Visit Golden’s media budget and $7,500 from the Downtown Development Authority [DDA], which was approved by DDA, and $2,000 from Golden Hotel). 
Deliverables & Assets:
Custom audio ad spots, digital dashboard/streaming banners, live remote appearances, social media posts, and inclusion on KOSI's index page throughout November and December. 
On-air talent will highlight local businesses across Downtown, North Golden, and South Golden. 
5. Golden Hour / Mashup Debrief & Future Events
Feedback & Observations:
Merchants noticed slight traffic increases, but noted the concept can be overwhelming for shoppers. 
Starting at 6:00 PM is too late for school nights; dark/rainy weather slowed evening foot traffic. 
Small business bandwidth makes monthly event planning difficult without funding/grants. 
Proposed Adjustments:
Rebrand the event (e.g., "Golden Night Out"). 
Shift timing to 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM. 
Simplify participation (e.g., stamp passports/dance cards, QR-code opt-ins via Visit Golden portal, featured treasure hunts). 
Consider moving future late-night shopping events to Fridays or Thursdays. 


6. Goldens in Golden 2027 Operations & Logistics
Parking & Shuttle Plan:
Due to capacity constraints at Splash/HHS, primary satellite parking will shift to the Martin Marietta lot (~600 spaces off Colfax & Heritage Rd) and nearby industrial areas (400–600 spaces). 
Fleet plan: 4–6 buses for longer routes and 7 shuttles for closer loops. 
Vendor/drop-off footprints will remain at Parfet Park and the rented Foss lot. 
Off-duty Jefferson County Sheriff deputies will assist with traffic control at key intersections. 
Marketing Restrictions:
Per City Council direction, no direct promotional marketing will be conducted. All communications will focus strictly on "Know Before You Go" visitor responsibility and logistical details. 
7. Banner Program & Wayfinding Updates
Light Post Banner Program:
Transitioning from a free/non-profit reservation model to an internal, city-branded program managed jointly with DDA. 
Seasonal, high-quality branded banners (e.g., responsible visitor messaging, shop local) will run year-round. 
Wayfinding & Maps:
Consolidated wayfinding initiative across 8 city departments covering pedestrian, driver, and map standards. 
Updating downtown tear-off maps with dynamic QR codes tailored to specific locations 
8. Tourism Data & Analytics Report (YTD through August 2026)
61+ Mile Market (Overnight/Regional Visitors):
Total trips and visitor days remained flat year-over-year. 
~50/50 split between day trips and overnight stays. 
Visitor spending showed a slight decline in mid-to-late summer, mirroring statewide trends. 
Top origin markets: Colorado Springs, Grand Junction, Pueblo, Houston, Austin, and Chicago. 
7–60 Mile Market (Local/Drive-In):
Represents over 80% of total visitors, dominated almost entirely by day trips. 
Top local origins include Denver, Arvada, Wheat Ridge, and surrounding metro communities.
