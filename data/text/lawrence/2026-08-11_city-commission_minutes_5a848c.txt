OPEN PUBLIC COMMENT: 5:00 PM
The Commission heard public comment on items and issues not scheduled on the agenda.

Brandy Hurrelbrink commented on maintenance concerns and mowing practices at the cemetery.

Eric Hyde commented on the Sandbar Block Party, NBC-LEO, South Park, the soccer banner on City Hall, and unsanctioned homeless camps.
Patrick Ross commented on data centers, A Place for Everyone Plan and other issues impacting the community.
Steven Watts commented on the meeting video and the police.
