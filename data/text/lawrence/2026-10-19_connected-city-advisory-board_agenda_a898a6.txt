Recommendations  
 Provide feedback on the 2027-2031 Five Year Plan for non-motorized projects. 
  
Executive Summary  
 The City has budgeted $4,280,000 for non-motorized projects under the Sidewalk/Bike/Ped 
Improvements project (MS5-A27000 to MS5-A31000) in the approved Capital Improvement 
Plan for 2027-2031 (CIP). The Five Year Plan serves as a framework for allocating these 
funds. 
  
Projects are evaluated for scoring through the Non-motorized Projects Prioritization Policy 
(NMPPP). The scored projects are then programmed to fit into the CIP funding constraints. 
The Five Year Plan will be brought before the City Commission for consideration of approval 
after receiving feedback from the Connected City Advisory Board. 
  
This plan dedicates funding for the 2027 projects but does not guarantee the outlying year 
projects of the Five Year Plan (2028-2031) will receive funding. Each year staff presents the 
Five Year Plan for approval that takes into account changes in funding, NMPPP changes, 
grant award opportunities and City Commission direction. 
  
Changes incorporated into the NMPPP project lists this year include: 
1. Priority network changes through the update the Lawrence Bikes Plan  
2. Higher expectations for lever of comfort standards 
3. New crossing projects identified 
  
The projects of the plan can be seen in their representative dashboards: 
Bike Project Dashboard 
Pedestrian Project Dashboard

year's plan include:
