:

The City o

Defining Affordable 
Housing
 
 
In 
general, affordable housing is defined 
as housing for which the occupant(s) 
is/are paying no more than 30 percent 
of his or her income for gross housing 
costs, including utilities.
 
 
In 
2016, the Affordable Housing 
Advisory Board 
established
 
additional 
definitions of affordable housing:
 
 
Affordable Rental Housing
 
Housing units
 
with 
monthly 
rent
 
and 
u
tilities not exceed
ing
 
110
% of the 
HUD 
defined 
Fair Market Rent, as 
determined 
yearly
 
by the Lawrence Douglas County 
Housing Authority.
 
 
Affordable Ownership
 
Housing
 
Housing units for those earning up to 
80% of the Area Median Family Income, 
as established yearly by HUD for the 
Lawrence, KS MSA.
