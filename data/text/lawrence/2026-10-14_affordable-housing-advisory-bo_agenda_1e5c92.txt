Affordable Housing Advisory Board 
Meeting 
Wednesday, October 14, 2026 @ 4:30pm 
Zoom and First Floor of City Hall, 6 East 6th Street 
 
 
  
This meeting will be held in a hybrid format. The primary format for accessing or participating in this 
meeting is in person. Virtual access to view or participate in the meeting cannot be guaranteed due to 
potential technical issues. Board members will be in person. Some staff will be present in the room 
while others may participate virtually. 
  
Members of the public are invited to join the meeting in person at Lawrence Public City Hall 
Commission Room, or virtually using the Zoom link. 
  
Written public comment must be received by 12:00 p.m. on Monday, October 12 2026. Send 
correspondence electronically to lroselyn@lawrenceks.gov. If you wish to have your contact 
information withheld, indicate so in your correspondence. Comments received after the deadline will 
not be posted and there is no guarantee that such comments will be considered. 
  
Live public comment can be made in person at the meeting or virtually using the Zoom link provided
