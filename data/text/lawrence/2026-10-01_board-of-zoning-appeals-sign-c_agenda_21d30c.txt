City of Lawrence, Kansas 
 Board of Zoning Appeals 
Bylaws 
 
 
Article I 
Purpose 
 
Section 1. The functions, powers, and duties of the Board of Zoning Appeals (“BZA”) 
are established by state statute, see K.S.A. 12-759, as amended,  and by 
City Code, see City of Lawrence, Kan., Code § 20 -1601(b) (2024), as 
amended. Specifically, the BZA hears certain appeals from decisions 
rendered under the Chapter 20 of the City Code, applications for variances 
from the City’s Zoning Ordinance, and those additional functions, powers, 
and duties assigned to it by state law and Chapter 20 of the City Code.  
 
Article II 
Membership 
 
Section 1. The BZA shall be composed of seven voting Members who are appointed 
by the City’s Governing Body. 
 
Section 2.  No Member of BZA, while a Member of the BZA, shall hold any other public 
office of the City. 
 
Section 3.  A Member of the BZA shall, at all times, be a resident of the Cit y. Once a 
Member ceases to be a resident of the City, then that Member shall resign 
from the BZA. 
 
Section 4. Members of the BZA shall serve three-year terms. A Member, appointed to 
fill an unexpired term, shall complete that unexpired term. 
 
Section 5. Each Member of the BZA shall serve no more than two successive full terms. 
Completion of an unexpired term shall not count toward the term limit. 
 
Section 6. A Member of the BZA, once appointed, may be removed during a term of 
office only for cause. In the event of the death, incapacitation, resignation, 
or removal of any Member of the BZA, before the expiration of a term of 
office, the Governing Body shall appoint a successor to complete that 
unexpired term.

Article III 
Officers and Staff Liaison 
 
Section 1. Officers of the BZA shall be as follows: 
 
 a. Chair 
 
 b. Vice-chair 
 
Section 2. Officers shall serve one-year terms, commencing on the date of election 
and continuing until the succeeding year’s election.   No person may serve 
more than two consecutive one-year terms either as Chair or as Vice-chair.  
 
Section 3. The BZA shall elect Officers at the first regularly scheduled meeting  of 
October of each year. 
 
Section 4. In the event that the Office of Chair becomes vacant,  then the Vice-chair 
shall succeed to that Office for the unexpired term. In the event that the 
office of Vice-chair becomes vacant, then the BZA shall, at its next regular 
meeting, elect a Member to succeed to th e Office of Vice -chair for the 
unexpired term. For the purposes of term limits of Officers, completion of 
an unexpired term shall not count toward the term limit. 
 
Section 5. The duties of Officers shall be as follows: 
 
 a. The Chair shall: 
 
  (i) Preside at BZA meetings; and 
 
(ii) Perform all duties assigned to the office by law and by the 
City’s Governing Body.  
 
b. The Vice-chair shall: 
 
(i) Preside at BZA meetings in the absence of the Chair; and 
 
(ii) In the event the office of the Chair becomes vacant, the Vice-
Chair shall succeed to the office for the unexpired term and 
the BZA shall select a new Vice-Chair for the unexpired term. 
 
Section 6. The Director of Planning and Development Services, or designee, shall serve 
as Staff Liaison, and act as Secretary to the BZA. The Staff Liaison shall, in 
consultation with the Chair, prepare the agenda and order of business for 
each regular meeting  of the BZA . The Staff Liaison shall also ensure that

minutes of the BZA are recorded. The Staff Liaison shall also keep the BZA 
informed of all communications.  The Staff Liaison, if required, will also 
represent the BZA on all BZA matters at meetings of the Governing Body, 
and prepare or present appeals, policies, or procedures established by the 
BZA. 
 
Article IV 
Vacancies and Appointment Procedures 
 
Section 1. Upon the vacancy of a position on the BZA, the Staff Liaison shall notify the 
City Manager  of the vacancy. The City Manager shall request that the 
Governing Body of the City appoint a replacement. 
 
Section 2. Upon being notified by the City Manager of the vacancy of a position on the 
BZA, the Governing Bod y of the City, shall endeavor to nominate a 
replacement as soon as may be practicable. 
 
Article V 
Attendance 
 
Section 1. Members are expected to attend all meetings of the BZA. Without the 
attendance of its Members, the BZA cannot conduct its business. 
 
Section 2. A Member , having three or more unexcused absences or five or more 
absences, for any reason, during one calendar year. shall be deemed by the 
BZA to have involuntarily resigned the Member’s position. Failing to notify 
the Chair or the Staff Liaison  of an absence at least 24 hours prior to a 
scheduled meeting shall count as an unexcused absence. Attendance will 
be reviewed regularly to ensure compliance. 
 
Article VI 
Conduct 
 
Section 1. Members are subject to all rules and regulations as established by the State 
of Kansas, the City, and other governmental jurisdictions and agencies 
having legal authority to regulate public officials’ behavior and conduct. 
 
Section 2. No Member may use the Member’s office in public representations unless 
authorized to do so by a simple majority vote of the BZA. 
 
Section 3. Members are expected to act with  appropriate decorum while serving the 
public.

Article VII 
Meetings 
 
Section 1. Meetings shall be held monthly, typically on the first Thursday of the month 
unless otherwise designated on the official meeting calendar . The official 
meeting calendar is adopted annually in November of the preceding year. 
 
Meetings shall commence at 6:30 p.m. , unless scheduled otherwise . 
Meetings shall be held in the City  Commission Meeting Room on the first 
floor of City Hall, 6 East 6th Street, in Lawrence, unless another location is 
identified in the public notice. 
 
If a Special Meeting is necessary, the Staff Liaison shall work in cooperation 
with the Chair to schedule the Special Meeting. At least 48 -hours’ advance 
notice of Special Meeting shall be given to Members and to the public. 
 
Section 2. Notice of regular meetings and agendas, if prepared, shall be transmitted 
to all Members at least one week preceding the meeting. Notices, agendas, 
minutes, and other materials may be delivered electronically. 
 
Section 3. Applicants, or their representatives, members of the general public , or 
persons, or their representatives, who feel that they will be affected by any 
action of the BZA may appear before the BZA to present views and 
statements either for or against a particular agenda item. The public must 
address their comments or concerns  directly to the BZA, and may do so  
either in person or in writing.  
 
Unless the BZA votes to alter it for a particular item  at a meeting , 
presentations to the BZA will be subject to the following time limitations: 
 
a. Applicant: 10 minutes per item, not to exceed 30 minutes in toto. 
 
b. Members of the general public, representing themselves or a group: 
 3 minutes. 
 
c. Applicant’s rebuttal, if any: 5 minutes.  
 
Section 4. Correspondence received by Planning Staff  from applicants, City Staff, or 
the general public after the Planning Staff report packet has been originally 
posted, will be posted to the website no later than 2:00 p.m. on the day of 
the BZA meeting. Questions submitted from the general public regarding 
items on the meeting shall be provided to staff by 10:00 a.m. on the day of 
the BZA meeting in order to allow time for responses to be prepared and 
posted by the 2:00 p.m. deadline established herein.

Section 5. Additional communications may be included as part of the BZA’s packet. 
Public comments on agenda items for meetings will be accepted by Planning 
Staff until 10:00 a.m. on the day of the BZA meeting in order to provide 
time for the correspondence to be included in the BZA’s meeting materials. 
 
 In cases where an applicant wishes to introduce new information, then the 
applicant should make a timely request for deferral of the item in 
accordance with these bylaws. In the event that there is no deferral of the 
item, then the Chair shall bar introduction of the new information and the 
BZA shall consider the item without consideration of that new information. 
 
Section 6. Deferrals may only be initiated by the Applicant prior to the commencement 
of the public hearing on an item. 
 
Section 7. The BZA shall take action on each item presented at the conclusion of 
discussion of that item.  
 
Section 8. Motions before the BZA shall be seconded, shall be restated by the Chair, 
and may be discussed by the BZA before a final vote is taken. Every motion 
to approve or disapprove an item  shall set forth reasons based on the 
criteria for approval for matters appearing before the BZA as outlined by 
State law and Chapter 20 of the City Code. 
 
Section 9. It is the duty of each Member of the BZA to vote on each issue, but a 
Member may abstain from considering an issue if the Members declares a 
conflict of interest. No Member shall participate in, discuss, or vote on a 
matter in which the Member has a conflict of interest, a substantial interest 
as defined by K.S.A. 75-4301a et seq., without having filed with the County 
Election Officer a statement of substantial interest on that subject, or is 
otherwise prohibited from considering the item  by any applicable state or 
local law. Members having declared a conflict of interest  and recusing 
themselves  from participating in the consideration of items before the BZA 
shall physically leave the meeting room  for the entirety of the hearing on 
that item. 
 
Article VIII 
Quorum 
 
Section 1. Four Members of the BZA shall constitute a quorum. There must be a 
quorum present (in person or by other media) before the BZA may conduct 
any official business. In the absence of a quorum, the BZA meeting will be 
postponed until the next regularly scheduled BZA meeting unless a Special 
Meeting is necessary to complete the business of the BZA.

Article IX 
Open Meetings and Open Records 
 
Section 1. All meetings of the BZA shall be held in compliance with the Kansas Open 
Public Meetings Act of 1972, codified as amended at K.S.A. 75-4316 et seq. 
All public records of the BZA shall be subject to the provisions of the Kansas 
Open Records Act of 1984, codified as amended at K.S.A. 45-205 et seq. 
 
Article X 
Bylaws 
 
Section 1. The purpose of these bylaws is to establish rules governing the internal 
organization of the BZA and its operational procedures. 
 
Section 2. These bylaws may be amended at any time by a simple majority vote of all 
current Members of the BZA and subsequent approval by the Governing 
Body of the City. 
 
Section 3. Amendment to these bylaws may be initiated at any time, but a proposed 
amendment must be placed on the agenda prior to a meeting before it may 
be considered by the BZA. 
 
Section 4. Amendment to these bylaws shall take effect immediately after  adoption 
and approval by the Governing Body of the City.
