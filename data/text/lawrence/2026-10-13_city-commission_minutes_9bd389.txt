RESOLUTIONS:
a)
Consider adopting Resolution No. 7712, setting November 17, 2026, at 5:45 p.m., as the date and time for a public hearing to consider the establishment of a Community Improvement District (CID) and the levy of a CID sales tax for a proposed commercial development at the northeast corner of 6th Street and George Williams Way. The proposed CID is being considered following receipt of a valid petition and in accordance with the City’s Economic Development Policy. Adoption of the resolution does not approve the CID or any incentives; rather, it authorizes the formal public review process and provides an opportunity for additional analysis, public input, and Commission consideration prior to final action.
ACTION:
Adopt Resolution No. 7712, setting a public hearing for November 17, 2026, at 5:45 p.m., to consider the creation of the 6th and George Williams Way Community Improvement District and the levy of a CID sales tax within the district.
