AFFORDABLE HOUSING ADVISORY BOARD 
 5-YEAR GOALS – 2024-2029 
 
GOAL 1: 
 
Increase supply of permanently affordable 
homeownership units for households at or below 80% 
AMI  
Output goal: 100 additional affordable homeownership units in 5 
years, from 2024-2029. 
 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Fund the preservation and rehabilitation of small/modest homes through 
grants, buying blighted and vacant properties. 
 
▪ Purchase and rehabilitate blighted and vacant properties to be sold to 
income-qualified households, and put into Community Land Trust for 
permanent affordability. 
 
▪ Recommend revision to City of Lawrence Community Benefit guidelines that 
encourage and incentivize including new permanently affordable housing 
units in new private residential housing developments. 
 
▪ Provide recommendations for affordable and accessible housing friendly 
updates to Land Development Code. 
 
▪ Utilize Trust Funds to purchase land and/or convert city-owned land for 
future affordable housing development and issue RFP for development that 
would meet specific population-level housing needs. 
 

2 
 
▪ Recommend the provision of tax and process incentives for new affordable 
housing development.  
 
▪ Negotiate affordable for sale unit creation as a part of annexations, for no 
less than 10% affordable units. 
 
▪ Recommend Tenant First Right to Purchase ordinance for the City of 
Lawrence. 
 
▪ Recommend Vacant and Dilapidated Structure Ordinance for the City of 
Lawrence. 
 
▪ Identify and recommend additional revenue streams for the Affordable 
Housing Trust Fund.

GOAL 3: 
 
Increase the accessibility of affordable housing for 
persons with disabilities. 
Output goal: 50 households in 5 years, from 2024-2029. 
 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Utilize Affordable Housing Trust Funds to provide and expand the installation 
of accessibility modifications to income-qualified housing units. 
 
▪ Prioritize Affordable Housing Trust Funds support to new units supported 
with Affordable Housing Trust Funds to incorporate Universal Design 
standards for accessibility, as applicable. 
 
▪ Incentivize ADA units for all new developments supported by the Affordable 
Housing Trust Funds by awarding additional points to project applications 
that are built for ADA accessibility. 
 
▪ Provide incentives for new housing units with accessibility features in 
housing developments with four or fewer units.

GOAL 4: 
 
Repair and rehabilitate housing in poor condition for 
households at or below 80%AMI.  
Output goal: 70 units in 5 years, from 2023-2029. 
 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Fund home modifications and weatherization for low-income households 
 
▪ Identify and leverage additional funding to achieve energy efficiency for 
affordable housing 
 
 
GOAL 5: 
 
Increase housing stability for households at or below 
80%AMI experiencing housing instability. 
Output goal: 50 tenant based rental assistance vouchers in 5 years, 
from 2023-2029. 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Fund the provision of long term and permanent housing vouchers.    
 
▪ Review and support the City’s effort to create policy to enforce source of 
income nondiscrimination ordinance  
 
 
GOAL 6: 
 
Increase racial equity in housing 
Output goal: 5 racial equity housing programs in 5 years, from 
2023-2029 
 
 
 
 

5 
 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Provide and prioritize Affordable Housing Trust Fund awards for projects that 
embed racial equity 
 
▪ Provide training for the Affordable Housing Advisory Board on racial equity 
and Recognize and understand the “A Place for Everyone: A Community Plan 
to End Homelessness” community housing plan strategies as related to racial 
equity and affordable housing goals 
 
▪ Support education on historical and current segregation and discrimination 
in housing access 
 
GOAL 7: 
 
Increase community awareness and engagement on 
affordable housing  
Output goal: 5 affordable housing community engagement 
programs or projects in 5 years, from 2023-2029 
 
STRATEGIES: 
▪ Include and accept marketing and community engagement projects for 
Affordable Housing Trust Fund awards 
 
▪ Actively participate in community engagement on affordable housing events  
 
▪ Support community education and outreach on affordable housing
