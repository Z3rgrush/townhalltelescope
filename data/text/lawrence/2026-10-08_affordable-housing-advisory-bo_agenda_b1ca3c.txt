Commission Room, or virtually using the Zoom link.

correspondence electronically to lroselyn@lawrenceks.gov. If you wish to have your contact 
information withheld, indicate so in your correspondence. Comments received after the deadline will 
not be posted and there is no guarantee that such comments will be considered.

C. AGENDA: AFFORDABLE HOUSING TRUST FUND APPLICANT PRESENTATIONS 
   
The Affordable Housing Advisory Board shall receive presentations from applicants of 2027 
Affordable Housing Trust Funds. Each presenter will have up to 10 minutes to present. 
Presentations will occur in the order they appear on the agenda. Public comment will be 
invited at the end of the presentations.

D. PUBLIC COMMENT: 
  The public is allowed to speak at public comment after first being recognized by the 
Chair. Comment is limited to 3 minutes per speaker. As a general practice, the Board will 
not discuss or debate these items, nor will it make decisions on items presented during 
this time.
