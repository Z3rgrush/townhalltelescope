D. AGENDA ITEMS:
1. Review the Friends of Lawrence Area Trails (FLAT) mural proposal, Estamos 
Aquí / We Are Here. The mural is proposed to be painted on the west-facing 
wall of the Free State Brewing Company Bottling Facility, 1927 Moodie Road, 
which overlooks the Burroughs Creek Trail just south of 19th Street. Muralist 
Dave Loewenstein is the project lead.
The Cultural Arts Commission received a presentation from the artist, Dave 
Loewenstein and reviewed the proposed mural, "Estamos Aquí / We Are 
Here," to be painted on the west-facing wall of the Free State Brewing 
Company bottling facility at 1927 Moodie Road, overlooking the Burroughs 
Creek Trail just south of 19th Street. Muralist Dave Loewenstein shared 
insights and answered questions alongside Chris Tilden from the Friends of 
Lawrence Area Trails (FLAT). 
Commission members expressed unanimous enthusiasm for the project, 
highlighting: 
 The mural's highly visible location along the Burroughs Creek Trail and
from 19th Street.

 The strong artistic reputation of Dave Loewenstein.  
 The project's thoughtful community engagement process.  
 The value of adding public art to the trail system and enhancing the 
visitor experience.  
 The successful partnership between FLAT, Free State Brewing 
Company, and the City's arts grant program.  
  
Discussion included: 
 Artwork symbolism: Loewenstein explained that elements such as keys 
and earbuds represent common objects found or lost along the trail, 
serving as contemporary "fossils" alongside prehistoric imagery, 
reinforcing themes of place, time, and human interaction with the 
landscape.  
 Project funding: FLAT explained that funding comes primarily from a 
Sunflower Foundation grant and a City Community Arts Grant, with 
additional fundraising underway to cover remaining costs such as 
scaffolding, lifts, and safety requirements.  
 Multilingual text: Commissioners discussed the inclusion of English and 
Spanish in the title, "Estamos Aquí / We Are Here." Loewenstein shared 
that the Spanish reflects his family's bilingual heritage and a broader 
desire to recognize multiple languages. He also noted that he is working 
with a Kanza elder to potentially add a Kanza translation and expressed 
openness to considering additional language representation where 
appropriate.  
  
After a motion by Tim Metz and a second by Alicia Kelly, the Cultural Arts 
Commission unanimously voted to recommend approval of the mural proposal 
to the City Commission. 
  
Staff will place the mural proposal on the July 21 City Commission agenda for 
final consideration.
