 3. Review mural proposal by Mona Cliff for Midco Native Plant Park. 
 
ACTION: Local artist Mona Cliff presented a mural proposal for the 
Midco building at 644 New Hampshire Street, adjacent to the 
new Native Plant Park being developed by Midco and the 
Native Lands Restoration Collaborative. The proposed mural 
is the first phase of a larger, multi-year project that will 
eventually extend around the building and use the changing 
seasons as an overarching theme. The initial phase will 
feature native plants found in the adjacent park, along with 
imagery inspired by the Kansas River and surrounding 
waterways. The phased mural installations will be installed on 
the east and north facing walls of the Midco building. 
  
Cliff explained that the mural is intended to complement the 
park and provide an educational connection to the native 
plants growing there. She anticipates beginning the first 
phase in October following City approval. The project is 
privately funded by Midco. Commissioners expressed support 
for the design, its relationship to the surrounding landscape, 
and Cliff’s efforts to engage nearby residents. Cliff reported 
receiving more than 100 letters of support for the project.

E. MISCELLANY: 
Commissioners shared several upcoming arts and cultural activities.  
  
The Percolator, in partnership with Danielsan Electric and Somos Lawrence, will host 
the Somos Uniditos silent art auction and neighborhood block party on Saturday, 
October 12, from 5–9 p.m. at Danielsan Electric. The event will feature donated 
artwork from more than 20 artists, live music and food, with proceeds benefiting the 
annual Día de los Muertos celebration at John Taylor Park. 
  
An update was provided on the Inclusive Winter Market, which will be held at Abe & 
Jake’s this year rather than at the Lied Center. It was also confirmed that the Phoenix 
Awards ceremony will take place at Abe & Jake’s rather than the Lied Center.  
  
Staff reminded members that Phoenix Award nominations remain open, and the need 
to fill a vacancy on the Phoenix Awards ceremony committee was discussed. It was 
suggested that new commissioners complete their upcoming orientation before 
considering committee assignments. 
  
Staff provided an update on upcoming grant cycles. The Unmistakable Events Grant 
Program is expected to open in October and run through early November, pending 
City Commission approval of the budget. The Community Arts Grant Program will 
open later in December to avoid overlap between the two application periods, with the 
Community Arts Mini-Grant cycle occurring later as well. The general grant schedule 
will remain similar to the previous year. 
  
Staff agreed to send a reminder to the CAC about reviewing the Community Arts 
Grant guidelines in preparation for the committee's recommended updates. 
  
The next Cultural Arts Commission meeting is planned for October 14.
