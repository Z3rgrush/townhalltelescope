HISTORY 
 
Highland Meadows Filing No. 1 was platted and approved by the Board of County Commissioners 
and recorded by the Teller County Clerk and Recorder on November 27, 1970 at Reception No. 
207987.  Highland Meadows Filing No. 1 is zoned R-1.    
 
A Boundary Agreement and Easement Agreement between the two property owners was made on 
March 25, 2019 and recorded with Teller County on June 25, 2019 at Reception No. 717063.  The 
agreement was made due to the encroachments of the residence and other improvements on Lot 4 
onto Lot 3 (Appendix E, page 69).  The Amende d Final Plat would eliminate the Boundary 
Agreement and Easement Agreement and adjust the interior lot line between Lot 3 and Lot 4 
according to a Monumented Land Survey Plat (Appendix E, page 73).   
 
Access 
 
Lot 3 has legal access from Floresta Drive, a platted road in Highland Meadows 1, and has 
been assigned the address of 100 Floresta Drive with a driveway permit, A25629. 
 
Lot 4 has legal access from Fl oresta Place, a platted road in Highland Meadows 1, and has 
been assigned the address of 10 Floresta Place with a driveway permit, D-4367. 
 
Water Supply 
 
Lot 3 has an approved well permit, Permit No. 249947. 
 
Lot 4 has an approved well permit, Permit No. 203570. 
 
Wastewater Disposal 
 
Lot 3 has an Onsite Wastewater Treatment System, Permit A25629. 
 
Lot 4 has an Onsite Wastewater Treatment System, Permit T-4560. 
 
 
CRITICAL AREAS 
 
The request for an Amended Final Plat does no t cause a new violation nor does it exacerbate a 
legal, pre-existing, non-conformity of the requirements of Ch apter 6 Critical Areas of the Land 
Use Regulations (LUR).  Lots 3 and 4 each has a single-family dwelling. 
 
 
SITE DESCRIPTION 
 
The subject parcels are currently zoned R-1 and have no extraordinary environmental factors that 
exist.  Adjacent or adjoining lots and the surrounding parcels are zoned R-1, and the surrounding 
properties present themselves as residential in nature and character.

DISCUSSION OF MAJOR CONCERNS AND ISSUES 
 
Staff has no major concerns or issues with the request for this Amended Final Plat.  All the 
standards pursuant to the County’s Amended Final Plat process have been met.  Lot 3 has legal 
access from Floresta Drive, and Lot 4 has legal access from Floresta Place.  Both lots have an 
approved well permit and an Onsite Wastewater Treatment System.  The request will create Lot 
3R and Lot 4R of Highland Meadows Filing No. 1.  The proposed Amended Final Plat is consistent 
with the goals, objective and polices of the Teller County Land Use Regulations. 
 
 
PUBLIC COMMENTS 
 
Staff did not receive any written comment either for or against the proposed Amended Final Plat.
