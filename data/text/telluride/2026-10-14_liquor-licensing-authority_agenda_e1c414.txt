Analysis/Financial Analysis: 
Special event permits cost $100 per day if the application is received between 30-44 
days in advance of the event and $75 if the application is received 45 days or more prior 
to the event.   
Recommendation: 
Staff recommends approval of the special event permits. The applicant has held 
previous special event permits and the Marshal's Department reports no concerns.   
Motion: 
Grant:  
Based on the foregoing findings in the staff memorandum and evidence presented at 
the hearing, the Telluride Liquor Licensing Authority grants three (3) Special Event 
Permit to Palm Arts Inc for The Telluride Horror Show for use at the Palm Theatre, 721 
W. Colorado Ave., Telluride CO 81435 for the following: 
 October 16, 2026 from 4:00 pm to 11:45 pm 
 October 17, 2026 from 9:00 am to 11:45 pm 
 October 18, 2026 from 9:00 am to 11:45 pm 
  
Grounds for Denial: 
The Authority may deny the issuance of a special event permit upon the grounds that 
the issuance would be injurious to the public welfare because of the nature of the 
special event, its location within the community, or the failure of the applicant in a past 
special event to conduct the event in compliance with applicable laws.
