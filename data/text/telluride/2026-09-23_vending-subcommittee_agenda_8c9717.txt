Introduction: 
Included for Vending Subcommittee consideration and action is an application for 
vending on public property on Gondola Plaza.  
Background: 
Prior to each summer and winter vending season, the Vending Subcommittee meets to 
evaluate vending application submittals and to make selections for vending sites on 
public property. 
  
Standards to be considered in the selection of vendors are found in the Telluride 
Municipal Code Section 6, Article 4, Vending. Sites will be approved and assigned at 
the discretion of the Vending Subcommittee. 
  
Applicants returning for consecutive summer and/or winter seasons will be given priority 
unless determined to be non-compliant by the Vending Subcommittee with either the 
provisions of the Telluride Municipal Code, Vending Code, or any agreements between 
the Town and vendor as to previously permitted vending activities. The Vending 
Subcommittee is directed to consider, but are not limited to, the below criteria in 
determining which applicants shall be issued any remaining permits. The criteria are not 
weighted or prioritized and include, but are not limited to: 
  
1. Diversity (in Town and by vendors) of offered food, goods, wares, merchandise, 
or services. 
2. Town of Telluride vending history, including any applicable compliance and 
performance reviews previously conducted by the Vending Subcommittee during 
seasonal debriefs specific to the legal entity applying for the permit; and 
3. Appearance, quality, safety and attractiveness of the display apparatus and 
related accoutrements.

The applicant has been instructed to complete the application and provide the following: 
  
 A completed, signed application with $40 application fee 
 A photo and scaled drawing of the vending apparatus along with proposed

 Proof of the ability to provide liability insurance  
 A business plan with a description of the products to be sold, trash disposal, 
location of cart storage, and location of commercial kitchen has been provided, if 
applicable. 
The applicant has been advised of the date and time of this hearing and have been 
invited to attend in order to answer any questions that the Subcommittee may have. 
They have also been invited to make a brief presentation to the Vending Subcommittee 
if they choose to do so.
