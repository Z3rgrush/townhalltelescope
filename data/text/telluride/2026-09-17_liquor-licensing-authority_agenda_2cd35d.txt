MEMORANDUM 
 
Title: Consideration Of A Permit Application From FA Whining Pig Bars 
10 LLC dba FA Whining Pig Bars For A Permanent Modification 
Of Premises At 138 East Colorado Ave. Unit 105, Telluride, CO 
81435. 
To: Town of Telluride Liquor Licensing Authority  
Meeting Date: September 17, 2026 
Submitted By: Tina Rutherford, Clerks 
Attachments: DR 8442 
Current Premises 
Modification Request on Premises 
 
Introduction: 
Applicant, FA Whining Pig Bars 10 LLC holds a Hotel and Restaurant liquor license for 
their restaurant  at 138 E. Colorado Ave. Unit 105. Applicant is seeking approval of a 
permanent modification of its liquor licensed premises to amend the premises to the 
current operational needs.  
Background: 
Colorado Liquor Code 44-3-301 prohibits alteration of a liquor licensed premises that 
materially alters the premises without first obtaining approval of the local and state 
licensing authorities. Modifications requiring approval include the increase of licensed 
premises area, the relocation or enlargement of a bar, or any material change to the 
interior of the premises that would affect the basic character of the premises.  
  
 Applicant, FA Whining Pig Bars, is applying to permanently retract their existing 
premises to only include their current operational needs.  
Factors to be taken into account, but not limited to include: 
1. The reasonable requirements of the neighborhood and desires of the adult 
inhabitants.  - With regards to the reasonable requirements of the neighborhood, 
this is currently a liquor licensed establishment. 
2. Possession by the licensee of the changed premises by ownership, lease, rental 
or other arrangement. Possession document is on file, applicant owns the 
premises.  
3. Compliance with the applicable zoning laws of the municipality, city and county. - 
This has previously been established. 
Page 6 of 33
4. Compliance with the distance prohibition in regard to any pubic or parochial 
school or the principal campus of any college, university or seminary. - This has 
previously been established. 
5. The legislative declaration that the Colorado Liquor and Beer Codes are an 
exercise of the police powers of the state for the protection of the economic and 
social welfare and the health, peace and morals of the people of this state. - Staff 
finds no reason to believe the issuance of the license threatens the economic 
and social welfare or the health, peace and morals of the people. 
The application for modification of premises submitted by FA Whining Pig Bars, which 
holds a Hotel and Restaurant Liquor License is complete. There is no local fee for a 
modification of premises permit. Telluride Municipal Code Sec. 6-2-190 requires posting 
of a premises for ten (10) days prior to hearing and action by the Authority for permit 
applications for modification of 
premises consisting of substantive changes. Staff submits the proposed changes are 
substantive, therefore, the premises was posted on September 7, 2026 as evidenced by 
Applicant’s Certificate of Posting.  
  
Analysis/Financial Analysis: 
Liquor Licenses are budgeted in the Town budget at $20,000.00  
Recommendation: 
Staff recommends the licensing authority review the evidence to determine whether the 
modification of premises should be approved based on requirements set forth in 
Colorado Code 47-302, and Telluride Municipal Code 6-2-190.  
Motion: 
Approve: 
As the Hearing Officer of the Telluride Liquor Licensing Authority, and based on the 
findings presented, I hereby approve the request by FA Whining Pig Bars 10 LLC, for a 
permanent modification of premises at 138 E. Colorado Avenue Unit 105, Telluride, 
Colorado. 
 
Deny:  
As the Hearing Officer of the Telluride Liquor Licensing Authority, and based on the 
findings presented, I hereby deny the request by FA Whining Pig Bars 10 LLC, for a 
permanent modification of premises at 138 E. Colorado Avenue Unit 105, Telluride, CO. 
[NOTE: If permission to change, alter or modify the license premises is denied, the 
licensing authority shall give notice in writing and shall state grounds upon which the 
application was denied. The licensee shall be entitled to a hearing on the denial if a 
request in writing is made to the licensing authority within fifteen days after the date of 
notice.]
