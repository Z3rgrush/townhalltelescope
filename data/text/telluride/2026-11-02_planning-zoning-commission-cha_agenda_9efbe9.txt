SITE SIGNIFICANCE AND CONTEXT 
 
The property is located at the southwest corner of W Columbia Ave 
and N Townsend St. Brazy Townhouses is comprised of two 
residential dwelling units (Condominium Unit North and Condominium 
Unit South) and a Contributing shed (5SM.1835). The shed is 
historically significant for its association with Telluride's residential 
development during the early to mid-1900s. The building is also 
architecturally significant as an intact example of a wood frame 
accessory building dating from the early decades of the twentieth 
century. It displays an overall high standard of integrity. 
 
REVIEW PROCESS/STATUS 
 
The proposal is for a Minor Subdivision to vacate the internal lot line between Lots 39 and 40, Block 9, West 
Telluride Addition, and aggregate the two existing lots which are each 2,812.5 sq. ft. into one approximately 
5,625-square-foot lot to be known as Lot 40R, pursuant to LUC Sections 6-403.B and 6-406.D.2. The Brazy 
Townhouses Association (North Unit and South Unit owners) has provided unanimous consent to proceed with 
this lot line vacation. 
 
Pursuant to LUC 6-403.B., the aggregation of lots into a parcel of land of 7,500 sq. ft. or less is a Minor 
Subdivision which must be approved by the P&Z Chairperson after notice and public hearing. Upon a finding of 
conformity, the Planning Director and P&Z Chairperson shall approve the final plat pursuant to LUC 6-406.D.2.  
 
CRITERIA FOR DECISION 
 
LUC Article 6, Division 4 outlines the purpose, procedures, criteria, and requirements for Subdivisions. All 
subdivisions must comply with the Subdivision Design Criteria contained in LUC 6-408, as applicable. 
Subdivisions must also meet the standards contained in the Town’s Streetscape Design Standards and 
construction specifications, as applicable, and all other applicable requirements of the Land Use Code.  
 
STAFF FINDINGS & ANALYSIS 
 
Staff reviewed the proposed Minor Subdivision per the applicable criteria and standards. Relevant staff findings 
and analysis are provided below.  
 
When Brazy Townhomes (North Unit and South Unit) were constructed in 1987, the building and related 
improvements were built on/over the internal lot lines associated with Lots 39 and 40, Block 9, West Telluride 
Addition. The Applicant is seeking to vacate the internal lot line to combine the two lots into one. There is also 
a Contributing shed on the property that is currently bisected by the existing lot line proposed for vacation. 
 
Consistency with Subdivision Design Criteria 
 
A brief summary of compliance with the Subdivision Design Criteria (LUC 6-408), as applicable, follows:  
 
LUC 6-408.W – Prohibition on Certain Lot Line Vacations 
 
The request to vacate an interior lot line is compliant with LUC 6-408.W which reads as follows: 
 
Within the Hillside Transitional, Residential, Historic Residential, Accommodations One, or Residential/ 
Commercial Zone Districts, a lot that contains a one or two-family dwelling unit may only be consolidated or 
aggregated with one or more adjoining lots, or two or more vacant lots may only be consolidated or 
aggregated, to eliminate non-conforming lots as per Article 4; or to eliminate pre-existing building 
encroachments across lot lines, including open decks, porches, roof eaves and stoops; or by means of a PUD. 
 
The property is located within the Historic Residential Zone District. The request is to eliminate pre-existing 
building encroachments across an interior lot line, for a two-family dwelling unit that currently spans two lots.

LUC 6-408.B – Site Requirements and LUC 6-408.T – Off Street Parking 
 
Pursuant to 6-408.B.1, building lots must meet the minimum size, setback, and frontage requirements and all 
other applicable dimensional limitations of the underlying zone district and all applicable architectural review 
criteria. The Applicant is proposing to create one conforming lot consistent with the use and dimensional 
requirements outlined in LUC 3-205, Historic Residential Zone District.  
 The resulting lot width will be 50 ft., compliant with the Minimum Lot Width of 25 ft. in the Zone District. 
 The area of Lot 40R will be 5,625 sq. ft., compliant with the Minimum Lot Area of 2,500 sq. ft. 
 
The Applicant has demonstrated compliance with the Site Requirements as outlined in LUC 6-408.B, including 
minimum lot size, frontage, and other applicable dimensional limitations of the underlying Zone District, 
consistent with B.1. Lot 40R has 50 feet of frontage on, and safe access to, a public street, consistent with B.3, 
B.4, and B.5. Lot 40R has adequate space for off-street parking with appropriate intersection angles and 
grades for one parking space, consistent with B.3 and T, and the Applicant is concurrently obtaining approval 
for payment-in-lieu for the second required parking space pursuant to LUC 3-205.E.2. Lot 40R meets all 
applicable architectural review criteria. 
 
The proposed Lot 40R will be a corner lot in with 50’ of frontage on Columbia Ave within the Historic 
Residential Zone District. Per LUC Section 6-401, one of the purposes of the Subdivision regulations is to 
encourage “a safe and convenient pattern of land development 
which respects the Town’s historic development pattern and 
which is consistent with the Design Guidelines for Building in 
Telluride and the Telluride Master Plan.”   
 
One of the Site Requirements is that building lots, where 
appropriate, shall be laid out respecting existing Town patterns, 
per B.2. Vacating the lot line as shown on the Replat would be 
consistent with the development pattern in the Historic 
Residential Zone District and Treatment Area.  
 
In the near vicinity, there are examples of lots of the same size 
and orientation as those proposed by the Minor Subdivision. The 
Brazy Townhomes and Contributing shed will remain as-is on the 
property, with the shed fronting on the alley. 
 
The residential lot is shaped so as to be able to accommodate a dwelling unit within the setbacks of the Zoning 
District and the resulting Lot 40R is not an unusual configuration; it is of consistent size, shape, and orientation 
as traditional development patterns in Telluride, consistent with B7 and B8.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION 
 
The Applicant is requesting a replat to vacate one interior lot line that bisects the primary structure and the 
historic shed. Combining the two existing lots into one LUC compliant lot will eliminate pre-existing building 
encroachments across lot lines. There is no proposed increase in density or change in use. There will be no 
impact to the neighborhood. The request is consistent with the criteria for decision-making outlined above. 
 
The P&Z Chair review is taking place concurrently with: 
1. Administrative review of a parking payment-in-lieu request for one parking space. The Applicant has met all 
criteria for parking payment-in-lieu in the Historic Residential Zone District. Pending administrative approval, 
payment of fees, and recording of the associated agreement, the parking requirements will be met for Lot 
40R. (See Condition 1 of Proposed Motion) 
2. Administrative review of a proposed Condominium Map Amendment. (See Condition 2 of Proposed Motion) 
 
Staff find that the Minor Subdivision application to vacate a lot line: meets the applicable requirements, purpose, 
and intent of the Telluride Land Use Code; is consistent with the Subdivision Regulations and the Subdivision 
Design Criteria contained in LUC 6-408, as applicable; is consistent with the Streetscape Design Standards 
and construction specifications, as applicable; and is consistent with the Telluride Master Plan. 
 
The staff recommendation is contained in the proposed motion below. 
 
REQUIRED ACTION 
 
This Minor Scale subdivision requires a determination by the P&Z Chair. The P&Z Chair may make a motion to 
approve, approve with conditions, or deny.  
 
PROPOSED MOTION 
 
I APPROVE a Minor Scale Subdivision vacating one interior lot line, combining Lot 39 and Lot 40 into Lot 40R 
as shown on the Replat dated 7/16/26, based on the findings set forth in the staff memo dated November 2, 
2026, and with the following Conditions: 
 
Condition 1. This approval is conditioned upon final approval of the request for payment-in-lieu of one parking 
space, including receipt of payment-in-lieu fees and recording of the associated Agreement for Payment-in-
Lieu of Providing Off-Street Parking (“Agreement”) in the Office of the Clerk and Recorder, San Miguel County, 
Colorado.  
 
Condition 2. Prior to recording of the Final Plat, the Final Plat must be updated to demonstrate compliance 
with the parking requirements of the Land Use Code in the form of a Plat Note stating that the South Unit 
parking space requirement is satisfied on-site and the North Unit parking space requirement is satisfied by  
payment-in-lieu for one parking space with reference to the Reception Number of the recorded Agreement. 
 
Condition 3. This approval is conditioned on revising the Final Plat to reference the Reception Numbers of the 
Condominium Map Amendment and Amended Declaration which are concurrently under administrative review 
and will be concurrently recorded. 
 
This motion is based on evidence and testimony provided at a public hearing held on November 2, 
2026, with notice of such hearing provided as required by the Telluride Land Use Code.
