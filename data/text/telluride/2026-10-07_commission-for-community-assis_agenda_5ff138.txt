Introduction: 
 
Event organizers are requesting a calendar date change for their event in 2027, which 
will take place at the Ah Haa School for the Arts. 
   
Background: 
  
Event Description: The Ah Haa School for the Arts hosts the annual HAHA – a 
participatory building-wide massive, a is community event interactive and that 
immersive art installation where each space within the Silverjack Building in Telluride, 
Co is taken over by a different artist. Whether a single art piece, a full room installation, 
video, performance and new media content, or a riveting story — we celebrate each 
artist’s vision, and encourage all visitors to actively engage with the art! Designed to be 
accessible to participants of all ages, this immersive experience takes place annually 
over 3 days in July with over 2,000 participants attending the HAHA each year. Ah 
Haa’s mission is to create community and joy through art. We are committed to creating 
opportunities for a diverse group of makers to participate. HAHA is committed to 
supporting the creative freedom of local, national, and international artists. Ah Haa 
celebrates equity and inclusion and encourages all marginalized folkx to apply. HAHA 
artists are also provided outreach opportunities within the town of Telluride. Free 
participatory events and making activities lead by HAHA artists give the community an 
outlet for expression and instill a since of place. HAHA artists are given logistical 
parameters, material stipend and creative carte blanche to design and implement an 
immersive experience that fully reflects their creative practice. 
   
Analysis/Financial Analysis: 
  
The requested dates overlap with the following event currently on the CCAASE 
Calendar: Shakespeare in the Park (July 16 - 24, 2027)

REVIEW CRITERIA FOR CCAASE CALENDAR DATES AND BANNERS   
CCAASE will use the above guidelines and criteria listed below when considering calendar date  and 
banner requests. Events occurring within the Telluride Town limits have priority . The Review Criteria 
are not weighted or prioritized and include the following:  
1. Length of time in existence on the requested date or weekend. 
2. Longevity of event in Town. 
3. Longevity of organization in Town. 
4. Value to community (cultural, economic, environmental, and social). 
5. Impact on community (infrastructure, environment, and economy). 
6. Change to existing events or new events (proximity to other events; impact on other events 
with regards  to  economics,  infrastructure,  and  available  venues; similar  events;  and crowd 
size). 
7. Fulfills requirements of Town contracts and Special Event Policies.
