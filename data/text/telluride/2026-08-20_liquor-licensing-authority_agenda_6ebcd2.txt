Sec. 6-3-10. Definitions. 
The following words and phrases, when used in this Article, shall have the meanings respectively ascribed to them: 
Admission means the right of a person to enter and/or occupy a seat or area at any festival or event staged, 
produced, convened or held in Town facilities, Town property and/or public rights-of-way for which an admission 
is charged, whether entrance is granted by purchase, sale or complimentary entrance. 
Event organizer means any person, promoter or sponsor obligated to account to the Finance Director for fees 
collected or to be collected, or from whom a fee is due, under the terms of this Article. 
Fee means either the fee payable by the purchaser or the aggregate amount of fees due from a vendor or event 
organizer. 
Major festival event shall have the meaning set forth in Chapter 11, Article 4 of this Code. 
Person means a natural person who is at least five (5) years old. 
Purchase or sale means a contract for sale and includes any transaction for the furnishing by any person to any 
person of the privilege of admission. 
Purchaser means any person to whom the privilege of admission is or has been rendered. 
Vendor means a person making a sale to a purchaser of the privilege of admission. (Prior code 5.18.010; Ord. 1288, 
2008) 
Sec. 6-3-20. Purpose of fee. 
The Town Council declares that, for purposes of this Article, every person who purchases or accepts an admission 
to a festival or event is exercising a privilege of admission, and every vendor who sells an admission or provides a 
complimentary or free admission, unless excluded in Section 6-3-40 below, shall collect the fee imposed by this 
Article in the manner set forth herein. The proceeds of the fee shall be used to defray law enforcement and other 
service-related expenses associated with festivals or events incurred by the Town or by the County pursuant to 
any intergovernmental agreement, and for such other Town Park administration, operations and improvements as 
the Town Council may designate. (Prior code 5.18.020)

Pur
pose: 
Chapter 6, Article 1 of the Telluride Municipal Code (“TMC”) sets forth the requirement that all persons doing 
business in the Town obtain a business license and pay an annual business license fee. The purpose of this Policy 
is to clarify these requirements for organizers of Festival Events and their vendors who may only be doing business 
in Town incidentally but who hold a state standard retail license, and to reconcile the  Town’s requirements with 
the mandates of Colorado Senate Bill 22-032. 
Administrative Authority: 
Adopted by the Town Clerk pursuant to TMC § 6-1-60(c) which allows the Town Clerk to promulgate and enforce 
all reasonable rules and regulation necessary to the operation and enforcement of Chapter 6, Article 1 Business 
Licenses. This Policy supersedes Business License Policy of 06/98 updated 4/5/05, and Business License Policy 
2013-02. 
What is a Festival Event? 
Festival events are occasional events that bring unique social and financial benefits to the Telluride community, 
and are regulated by Chapter 11, Article 4 of the TMC . Organizers of festival events or vendors participating in 
festival events may be required to obtain a Town of Telluride business license, a sales tax license, or both a 
business license and a sales tax license as set forth in this Policy for “doing business” in the Town. 
Town of Telluride Sales Tax License Requirements: 
All organizers of festival events, and all vendors participating in festival events  where vending is conducted by 
vendors who do not hold a current Town of Telluride business license, must obtain a sales tax license. In addition, 
the festival event organizer will be responsible for filing and remitting taxes on behalf of all unlicensed Town of 
Telluride vendors. Reporting of gross sales, taxable sales and Town of Telluride taxes remitted must be provided 
to the Town of Telluride Finance Department at the time of filing. In some instances, festival event organizers 
may be required by contract to file on behalf of all vendors, licensed and unlicensed in the Town of Telluride. 
Town of Telluride Business License Requirements: 
Festival event organizers of festivals which require physical presence of employees, vendors or subcontractors of 
the organization for more than four (4) days annually (including pre-event meetings, setup, and teardown) must 
obtain a Town of Telluride business license. The only exception to this requirement is for nonprofit organizers of 
such festivals with no vending or subcontracted activity, or  with vending and subcontracted activity only  by

ARTICLE 8 
Prohibition and Ban on the Use or Sale of Certain Plastic Materials or Products 
Sec. 7-8-10. Definitions. 
The following words and/or phrases used in this Article shall be defined as follows: 
“Business” means any commercial enterprise or establishment, including sole proprietorships, joint ventures, 
partnerships, corporations or any other legal entity whether for profit or not for profit and includes all 
employees of the business and any independent contractors associated with the business. The definition of 
“business” is specifically intended to include both minor festival events and major festival events held within 
the Town of Telluride, as those terms are defined in Section 11-4-10. 
“Plastic” means a synthetic material made from linking monomers through a chemical reaction to create a 
polymer chain that can be molded or extruded at high heat into various solid forms that retain their defined 
shapes during their life cycle and after disposal. 
“Plastic beverage straw” means a tube made of plastic used to transfer a beverage or liquid from its container 
to the mouth of the drinker. A “plastic beverage straw” includes a drinking straw made with a portion of 
biodegradable plastic or compostable plastic, but does not include straws that are made from nonplastic 
materials, including, but not limited to, paper, pasta, sugar cane, wood or bamboo. 
“Plastic cutlery” means any utensil, such as a fork, spoon, spork, or knife, intended for only one-time use and
