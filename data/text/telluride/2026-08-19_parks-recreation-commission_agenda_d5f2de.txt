Recommendation: 
  
Staff is recommending the following changes for 2026: 
  
 Fee increases of $2.00-$5.00 per night per site type, or an average of 8.13% 
based on rounding to a whole dollar amount. NOTE: The proposed Campground 
fees continue to be inclusive of lodging taxes and credit card processing fees.  
The Campground fees do not include the Campspot booking fee (currently $3.50 
per booking). 
 Raising the senior age from 63 to 64.  To not exclude anyone who has already 
received the discount previously, we will continue to increase the age by 1 year / 
year until we reach 65.    
 Staff recommends adjusting shower fees beginning in 2027. The rate would 
change from $1.00 for 75 seconds of water to $1.00 for 60 seconds. With the 
proposed installation of a credit-card payment machine in 2027, this adjustment 
will also help offset the associated processing fees.

44TOWN OF TELLURIDE  Town Parks Master Plan Phasing and Implementation
CHAPTER 9.  IMPLEMENTATION PLAN,    
   PHASING, AND COST ESTIMATES
In order to help the Town of Telluride guide improvements to its Town’s parks 
properties into the next 15 years, the TPMPU includes an implementation 
plan with phasing matrix and associated cost estimate in 2020 dollars.   The 
implementation plan distributes the master plan’s recommended improvements 
over three (3) phases to be implemented over the next 15 years.   Each phase 
of implementation is approximately five (5) years.  Phase One is 2021 to 2025, 
Phase Two is 2026 to 2030, and Phase Three is 2031 to 2035.    The goal of the 
implementation plan is to organize improvements as logically as possible based 
on the locations and areas of the Town’s parks properties that are affected.  In 
some cases, improvements adjacent to or near one other are aggregated.   The 
goal is also to spread out the improvements over the 15-year period to allow 
incremental budgeting on an annual basis, and to spread out the costs over time.  
A cost estimate with order of magnitude costs for each major recommended 
improvement is included in the plan based on 2020 construction costs and 
comparable improvements in similar Western Slope mountain town communities.   
The phasing and cost matrix included in the TPMPU shows all improvements 
recommended in the plan, the phase which they have been assigned for 
implementation, and an estimate in 2020 dollars order of magnitude cost of 
each improvements.   The matrix is included in the report on page 46.  The 
improvements recommended for each phase of implementation (not listed in 
order of priority within each phase) are as follows:

Recreation Programming
Summer Programming  
Pool : Telluride Open Swim Meet
The Telluride Torpedoes resumed hosting the annual Telluride Open Swim Meet
on Saturday, July 18, 2026. We did not host in 2025 so that the Western Slope
Swim League could reschedule some meets critical to providing opportunities
for swimmers to qualify for League and State meets. In addition to hosting 19 of
our 35 enrolled Torpedoes, approximately 90 swimmers from 6 other Western
Slope communities, including Cortez, Delta, Durango, Gunnison, Meeker and
Montrose were in attendance. Torpedoes program consists of a Travel and a
Practice team and the Practice team participants do not travel for meets. These
swimmers are permitted the opportunity to swim in a real meet with officials,
timers, and the whole production supporting them for the first time. The
Telluride Open has become a great opportunity for younger swimmers to extend
their meet season during a time in which those opportunities typically become
hard to find. Big thanks to Kurt Friederich and the Recreation team, swim team
coaches Kelsey, Grant, and Thomas, and the numerous parent volunteers!
