DISCUSSION ITEMS 
1. CDOT I-70B Corridor Traffic Improvements Request for Comments 
a. Dustin Anzures introduced the item and explained that the special 
meeting was necessary in order to respond, as necessary, to CDOT’s 
request for comments regarding potential impacts on cultural and 
historic resources in the next phase of the I-70B improvements. 
b. Tim Lehrbach summarized the Section 106 report and plans provided by 
CDOT for review and invited discussion on the reported potential impacts 
to historic resources. 
c. Discussion concerned the four identified potential impacts on properties 
that may be or contain historic resources: 
i. The former Union Carbide Nuclear Company building, now 
occupied by the Landmark Baptist Church and School. The 
buildings and property are not deemed to be historic resources, 
and the impact is limited to right-of-way acquisitions distant from 
the buildings. 
ii. The possible buried streetcar tracks through the project’s area of 
impact, as mentioned in the report, which, if they exist, are 
already covered in pavement and will be further covered but not 
removed or otherwise affected. 
iii. The Sinclair fuel station and C & F Foods store at 857-859 Pitkin 
Ave. The structures and property are not deemed to be 
 
 
2 | Page  
 
historically significant resources, and the impact is limited to a 
small right-of-way acquisition. 
iv. Emerson Park. The property is not deemed to be a historic 
resource any longer due to the improvements completed over 
time. 
d. The Board discussed potential comments to CDOT: 
i. CDOT could be asked to confirm that the State Historic 
Preservation Officer has approved the Section 106 report 
methodology, including the decision not to fully evaluate 104 of 
the 108 sites against the criteria for historic resource eligibility. 
ii. CDOT could clarify what it means to provide a cultural monitor (an 
onsite monitor or somebody to be called in)  
iii. CDOT could provide the owners of the Landmark Baptist Church 
and School and the Atomic Legacy Cabin with copies of the 
building plans and information gathered for the Section 106 
report regarding the Union Carbide Nuclear Company building. 
iv. CDOT could be notified that there may be errors in naming the 
project in the plan set. 
e. The Board decided to direct staff to provide the feedback concerning the  
i. Kevin Tinsley made a motion to direct staff to provide the 
comment on the former Union Carbide Nuclear Company building 
to CDOT. Dave Fischell seconded the motion. The motion passed 
5-0.
