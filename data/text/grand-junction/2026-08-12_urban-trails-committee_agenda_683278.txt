Urban Trails Committee – Minutes – July 22, 2026 – Regular Meeting 
 
Attendees:  Brent S., Dave T., Beniah A., Breanna G., Killian B., Jason N., Cody K. 
Staff/Ex-Officio:  Eric Mocko, Andy Gingeritch,  
Guests:  Andrea B.W., Johnny M., Tammy P.  
Minutes 
Call to Order/Announcements: 
Brent called the meeting to order at 5:33 PM. 
Meeting Minutes 
1. Approval of June Minutes 
The Committee reviewed the minutes. Benaiah moves to adopt the amended June minutes. Dave seconds, 
unanimous approval. 
Action Items 
1. Vice Chair 
With the resignation of Maureen last month, a new vice chair was needed.  The committee discussed potential 
nominees.  Brent nominated Athena.  Benaiah made a motion to nominate Athena.  Dave seconded.  Unanimous 
approval with Cody abstaining.     
Discussion Items 
1. Micromobility Presentation 
Andrea and Johnny presented information on the scooter data available through the City’s vendor, Lime.   
 
2. City Council Representation on UTC 
Jason led a discussion regarding feedback he received from the City Attorney’s office.  The feedback Jason 
received indicated that it would be recommended for City Council to abstain from any items that may go to 
council for vote or approval.  Cody and Jason agreed that this would be good practice for Council 
Representatives moving forward.    
 
Brent had a question regarding the number of representatives on the committee.  The bylaws state that the 
committee should consist of 7 to 9 members, but with 2 City Council representatives, the total comes to 10.  
Brent also asked about the Chair’s responsibility of ex-officio representation on subcommittees.  There was 
further discussion and Eric stated that he would get legal clarification.      
 
Updates 
1. Walk Audit Subcommittee 
Benaiah gave an update on the proposed routes near CMU, 12th and North area.   
Potential dates of August 26th , September 23rd , and September 30th were identified.  September 30th was 
identified as the target date.   
 
Other Business  
Cody brought up the conversation of bike parking.  It would be good to come up with funds to help provide bike parking 
for existing locations that need it.  This could be a council level decision but could be good to discuss with UTC and 
provide a recommendation to council.  Brent supports this idea.
