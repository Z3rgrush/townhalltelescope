Located at 2590 Galley Lane (Parcel #2945-031-37-001) and 675 26 
Road (Parcel #2945-031-00-009)

Galley Lane (Parcel #2945-031-37-001) and 675 26 Road (Parcel #2945-031-00-009), 
City file number RZN-2025-749, on final passage and ordered final publication in

4.a.ii.    An Ordinance Vacating Portions of Inland Avenue Right-Of-Way 
Between South Westgate Drive and 25 Road 
The property owner, Martin Mendoza, represented by Kim Kerk Land Consulting & 
Development, requested the vacation of approximately 2,236 square feet of platted 
public right-of-way associated with an unconstructed cul-de-sac on Inland Avenue 
adjacent to 567 and 569 South Westgate Drive. The subject right-of-way was originally 
dedicated as part of the Sen-Bar Subdivision plat and was intended to serve as a 
turnaround for what was then Sen-Bar Avenue, later renamed Frostline Avenue and 
ultimately Inland Avenue. 
 
With the construction of Inland Avenue as a through street, the need for the cul-de-sac 
was eliminated; however, the platted right-of-way remained of record. The proposed 
vacation will allow the owner to fully utilize the adjoining property for material storage 
associated with an existing landscaping business. 
 
The proposed vacation will not impact existing or future use of Inland Avenue by the 
public, adjacent property owners, or emergency service providers. The request is 
consistent with the City’s Comprehensive Plan and transportation objectives because 
the right-of-way is no longer needed for circulation purposes and its vacation will not 
adversely affect the City's transportation network. 
Senior Planner Lily Sutton gave an in-depth presentation on this ordinance and was 
available to answer questions from Council.  Kim Kerk of Kim Kerk Consulting and 
Development was available to answer questions. 
The public hearing opened at 6:37 p.m. 
There were no comments. 
The public hearing closed at 6:37 p.m. 
Councilmember Van Dyke moved, and Councilmember Kennedy seconded, to adopt 
Ordinance No. 5342, an ordinance vacating portions of Inland Avenue Right-Of-Way 
Between South Westgate Drive and 25 Road with corrections regarding the 
amendments made by staff, on final passage and ordered final publication in pamphlet 
form.  Motion carried by a unanimous roll call vote. 
 
4.b.i.    An Ordinance for Supplemental Appropriation for the Downtown 
Development Authority 
The budget is adopted by City Council through an appropriation ordinance to authorize 
spending at a fund level based on the line-item budget. Supplemental appropriations 
are also adopted by ordinance and are required when the adopted budget is increased 
to re-appropriate funds for capital projects that began in one year and need to be 
carried forward to the current year to complete. Supplemental appropriations are also 
required to approve new projects or expenditures. 
 
This supplemental appropriation is required to complete Phase Zero of the Terminal 
Project which includes necessary infrastructure and site improvements prior to vertical 
construction.  Phase Zero improvements are underway and anticipated to be completed 
in Fall 2026. 
Chief Financial Officer Jay Valentine gave a presentation on this ordinance and was 
available to answer questions from Council. 
Downtown Development Director Brandon Stam was available to give a brief 
explanation to the requested supplemental appropriation request. 
The public hearing opened at 6:42 p.m. 
There were no comments. 
The public hearing closed at 6:42 p.m. 
Councilmember Van Dyke moved, and Councilmember Nguyen seconded, to adopt 
Ordinance No. 5343, an ordinance making supplemental appropriations to the 2026 
Budget of the City of Grand Junction, Colorado Downtown Development Authority for 
the year beginning January 1, 2026, and ending December 31, 2026, on final passage 
and ordered final publication in pamphlet form.  Motion carried by a unanimous roll call 
vote.

4.b.iii.    An Ordinance Authorizing the City Manager to Sign a Purchase and 
Sale Agreement for Approximately 3.3 Acres of City-Owned 
Property to Grand Junction Homes, LLC for Affordable Housing 
(Continued from August 14, 2026)

5.a.    A Resolution Authorizing the City Manager to Execute a Development 
and Funding Agreement with Grand Junction Homes, LLC for the 
Development of 48 For Sale Units at 450 28 Rd to Help Eligible 
Households Achieve Homeownership Within the City Limits of Grand 
Junction (Continued from August 14, 2026) 
Resolution No. 38-26 authorizes the City Manager to execute a Development and 
Funding Agreement with Grand Junction Homes, LLC related to the conveyance and 
development of 3.3 acres, a portion of Lot 4, Grand View Common Subdivision, within 
the City-owned Salt Flats property at 450 28 Road. The project will include 
approximately 48 deed-restricted, for-sale homes serving households at or below 100% 
Area Median Income, utilizing a fee-simple homeownership model without an HOA to 
reduce monthly costs and support long-term affordability. The agreement also allows the 
City to provide up to approximately $156,330.00 for the construction of public 
improvements such as roads and utilities, and employing the city's adopted Affordable 
Housing Production Incentive, an additional $593,340 for water connection fees and 
sewer plant investment fees approved in the 2026 annual budget. 
Interim City Attorney Jeremiah Boies gave a presentation on this resolution and was 
available to answer questions from Council. 
Councilmember Nguyen moved, and Councilmember Kennedy seconded, to adopt 
Resolution 38-26, a resolution authorizing the City Manager to execute a Development 
and Funding Agreement with Grand Junction Homes, LLC for the development of 48 for 
sale units at 450 28 Road to help eligible households achieve homeownership within the 
city limits of Grand Junction.  Motion carried by a unanimous voice vote. 
 
   
5.b.    A Resolution Approving a Memorandum of Understanding Between 
the City of Grand Junction and the Grand Junction Housing Authority 
Regarding Administration of the Juniper Grove Affordable Housing 
Deed Restriction Program, and Authorizing the City Manager to Sign 
The Juniper Grove affordable homeownership project will place a 40-year deed 
restriction on each home, limiting who may buy, occupy, and resell the units. Someone 
must administer those restrictions. The Memorandum of Understanding designates the 
Grand Junction Housing Authority as the Administrator following the developer's initial 
sales, sets out GJHA's duties, and establishes how administration is funded. The City 
remains a co-beneficiary and keeps its independent right to enforce. Staff recommends 
Council approve the MOU and authorize the City Manager to sign. 
Interim City Attorney Jeremiah Boies gave a presentation on this resolution and was 
available to answer questions from Council. 
Councilmember Kennedy moved, and Councilmember Nguyen seconded, to adopt 
Resolution 86-26, a resolution approving a memorandum of understanding between the 
City of Grand Junction and the Grand Junction Housing Authority regarding 
administration of the Juniper Grove Affordable Housing Deed Restriction Program and 
authorizing the City Manager to sign.  Motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.
