GRAND JUNCTION LIQUOR LICENSING AUTHORITY 
MINUTES 
November 4, 2026 
 
I. Call to Order 
 
The meeting was called to order at 2:00 p.m. Those present were Hearing Officer 
Jill Cenamo, Interim City Attorney Jeremiah Boies, and Deputy City Clerk Krystle 
Koehler.  
 
II. License Renewal Applications 
 
1. El Mescal, Inc dba Tequila’s Mexican Family Restaurant, 2454 HWY 6 & 
50 Suite 119 & 120, Grand Junction, CO 81505 - Hotel & Restaurant 
2. Los Potrillos, Inc dba Aztecas Family Mexican Restaurant, 603 Highway 
50, Grand Junction, CO 81503 - Hotel & Restaurant 
3. Blasta, LLC dba Aztecas Family Mexican Restaurant, 705 Horizon Drive, 
Grand Junction, CO 81506 - Hotel & Restaurant 
4. TJ Cruisers, LLC dba Cruisers Bar, 2692 Hwy 50, Suites O, Q & S, 
Grand Junction, CO 81503 - Tavern 
5. 314 GJ, LLC dba Dream Café, 314 Main Street, Grand Junction, CO 
81501 - Hotel & Restaurant with Outdoor Dining Lease 
6. Apple Colorado, LLC dba Applebee’s Neighborhood Grill & Bar, 711 
Horizon Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81506 - Hotel & Restaurant 
Hearing Officer Cenamo read renewals 2, 4, & 6 into the record. She verified with 
Mrs. Koehler that the applications were in order.  
Hearing Officer Cenamo asked if anyone was present to speak against any of the 
applications; there was no one.  
Renewals 2, 4, & 6 were approved as requested. 
Hearing Officer Cenamo read Renewals 1, 3, & 5 into the record and verified with 
Mrs. Koehler that the applications were in order. These establishments had fire 
code violations. 
Hearing Officer Cenamo asked if anyone was present to speak against any of the 
applications; there was no one. 
After discussion, El Mescal, Inc dba Tequila’s Mexican Family Restaurant, 
located at 2454 HWY 6 & 50 Suite 119 & 120, Grand Junction, CO 81505 - Hotel 
& Restaurant, Blasta, LLC dba Aztecas Family Mexican Restaurant, 705 Horizon 
Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81506 - Hotel & Restaurant, and 314 GJ, LLC dba 
Dream Café, 314 Main Street, Grand Junction, CO 81501 - Hotel & Restaurant 
with Outdoor Dining Lease received conditional approvals pending correction and 
successful reinspections of their fire code violations. 
III. Modification of Premises Applications 
 
1. BPOE Lodge dba Grand Junction Lodge No. 575, 249 S 4th Street, Grand 
Junction, CO 81501, Club 
Remove 3/4 wall near bar 
Hearing Officer Cenamo read the modification into the record. She verified with 
Mrs. Koehler the application was in order. 
Hearing Officer Cenamo asked if there was a representative present for BPOE 
Lodge dba Grand Junction Lodge No. 575.  Exalted Ruler Jeremy Scheetz was 
in attendance to answer questions regarding the modification. He explained the 
removal of the 3/4 wall. He stated the removal will not change the layout, 
boundaries, or approved diagram of the existing licensed area. It will simply open 
additional space to improve pedestrian flow and allow people to move through 
more easily. Officer Cenamo asked if anyone was present to speak against the 
modification; there was no one. Hearing Officer Cenamo entered Exhibits “A” 
(attached) into the record. Hearing Officer Cenamo approved the modification. 
 
IV. New License Applications - Resolutions, Findings and Decision 
1. LV Number 2, LLC dba LV Spa Nail II, 2446 Patterson Road, Grand 
Junction, CO 81505 - Beer & Wine 
 
Member: Hong Pham, 709 Fountainhead Blvd, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
Manager: Bill Dao, 2504 Buchanan Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
Member: Son Huynh, 2504 Buchanan Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
 
Member Son Huynh and Liquor Consultant Brian Turner were present to answer 
questions regarding the new license application and how the needs and desires 
survey was conducted. Liquor Consultant Brian Turner read the survey results 
into the record. 
 
Hearing Officer Cenamo verified with Mrs. Koehler the application was in order. 
She asked if anyone was present to speak against the application. There was no 
one. Hearing Officer Cenamo entered Exhibits “A” (attached) into the record.  
 
Hearing Officer Cenamo found the needs of the neighborhood are not being met 
and found no legal reason to deny the license. The new Beer & Wine license 
application for LV Number 2, LLC dba LV Spa Nail II, was approved. The City 
Clerk's Office will prepare a resolution of findings for Hearing Officer Cenamo’s 
signature. 
 
2. Victoria Nail Spa 2, LLC dba Victoria Nail Spa & Lashes, 2883 North 
Avenue, Grand Junction, CO 81501 - Beer & Wine 
 
Member: Thien Huynh, 667 Tilman Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81506 
Member: Bill Dao, 2504 Buchanan Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
Member: Son Huynh, 2504 Buchanan Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
Member: Chi Huynh, 608 Silverado Drive, Grand Junction, CO 81505 
 
Member Son Huynh and Liquor Consultant Brian Turner were present to answer 
questions regarding the new license application and how the needs and desires 
survey was conducted. Liquor Consultant Brian Turner read the survey results 
into the record. 
 
Hearing Officer Cenamo verified with Mrs. Koehler the application was in order. 
She asked if anyone was present to speak against the application. There was no 
one. Hearing Officer Cenamo entered Exhibits “A” (attached) into the record.  
 
Hearing Officer Cenamo found the needs of the neighborhood are not being met 
and found no legal reason to deny the license. The new Beer & Wine license 
application for Victoria Nail Spa 2, LLC dba Victoria Nail Spa & Lashes, was 
approved. The City Clerk's Office will prepare a resolution of findings for Hearing 
Officer Cenamo’s signature. 
 
V. Other Business 
 
There was none.  
 
VI. Adjournment 
 
The meeting adjourned at 2:57 p.m.
