REGULAR AGENDA 
5. Resolutions (not Public Hearings) 
a. A Resolution Adopting the City of Grand Junction 2026-2027 Legislative 
Policy 
This item gives the City Council an opportunity to review the City Council's Legislative 
Policy (Policy) and consider adoption of its 2026-2027 legislative priorities. The Policy is 
intended to establish the roles of staff and Council with regard to legislative tracking, 
advocacy, and communication. The Policy also includes the annual adoption of the 
Council legislative priorities to position the City for advocacy on relevant legislative 
matters. Resolution No. 84-25 was adopted by City Council in December 2025, which 
reestablished a Council Legislative Policy and established the City Council's general 
support for the CML 2025-2026 Legislative Policy Statement, which guided the City's 
legislative work through the 2026 General Assembly Regular Session. The draft 
Resolution and Policy have been updated to reflect the 2027 General Assembly Regular 
Session, but otherwise remain unchanged in form and function. 
Assistant to the City Manager Johnny McFarland presented this item and was available 
to answer questions from Council. 
Councilmember Stout moved, and Councilmember Nguyen seconded, to adopt 
Resolution No. 91-26, a resolution adopting the City of Grand Junction 2027 Legislative 
Policy.  Motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.
