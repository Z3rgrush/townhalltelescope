Grand Junction City Council 
Minutes of the Regular Meeting 
September 2, 2026 
Call to Order, Pledge of Allegiance, Moment of Silence 
The City Council of the City of Grand Junction convened into regular session on the 2nd 
day of September, at 5:30 p.m. Those present were Councilmembers Robert Ballard 
(virtual), Scott Beilfuss, Cody Kennedy, Jason Nguyen, Anna Stout, Council President 
Pro Tem Ben Van Dyke, and Council President Laurel Lutz (virtual).  
Also present were City Manager Mike Bennett, Interim City Attorney Jeremiah Boies, 
Zoning Supervisor Tim Lehrbach, Interim Utilities Director Ashley Firl, City Clerk 
Selestina Sandoval, and Deputy City Clerk Misty Williams.

in the Pledge of Allegiance, followed by a moment of silence.

West Pride's President Jake Earich, Vice President Geneva Smith and

Public comments were heard from Dave Lord, Ed Kowalski, and Vicky Vaughn.

City Manager Mike Bennett reported on the next Community Conversations event will 
be held on September 3, 2026, at the new Community Recreation Center. 
 
Boards and Commission Liaison Reports 
Councilmember Beilfuss provided updates on the Colorado Office of Economic 
Development and International Trade (OEDIT) Listening Tour at CMU and the Historical 
Preservation Group. 
Councilmember Kennedy reported on the Urban Trails walk audit, Oktoberfest at Cross 
Orchards, Las Colonias marketing, Grand Junction Economic Partnership and his 
appointment to the Colorado Basin Roundtable.  
Council President Lutz reported on the Housing Authority Strategic Planning session. 
Councilmember Stout provided an update on a Forestry Board letter that was sent to 
Council and upcoming Colorado Municipal League District 10 & 11 meetings in Rifle, 
CO and Delta, CO.

REGULAR AGENDA 
5.a.i.  An Ordinance Amending Sections § 21.07.040 and § 21.09.060(a)(1) 
of the Zoning and Development Code (Title 21 of the Grand Junction 
Municipal Code) Regarding Preservation of Significant Trees 
The Housing Affordability Code Task Force requested removing Section § 21.07.040 
along with a reference to mature trees at § 21.09.060(a)(1) of the Zoning and 
Development Code, which provides for the identification, preservation, and mitigation of 
significant trees during development and the protection of mature trees during the 
subdivision of property. 
Zoning Supervisor Tim Lehrbach gave an in-depth presentation on this ordinance and 
was available to answer questions from Council. 
Housing Affordability Code Task Force Representative Kevin Bray gave a presentation 
regarding the effects that the Significant Tree Ordinance would have on the future 
development of the community. 
Comments were heard from Councilmembers Stout, Beilfuss, Kennedy, Nguyen and 
Ballard, as well as Council President Lutz. 
Council took a short break at 7:54 p.m. 
Council resumed at 8:09 p.m. 
The public hearing opened at 8:10 p.m. 
Comments were heard from Ray Pilcher, Jim Marshall, Mollie Frelischer, Keith Ehlers, 
Tom Ziola, Tom McClasky, Hogan Pederson, Diane Schwenke, Kammie Long, Michael 
Kotee, Caymon Haltiner and Jane Quimby. 
The public hearing closed at 8:39 p.m. 
Comments were heard from Councilmembers Nguyen, Kennedy, Ballard, Beilfuss and 
Stout, as well as Council President Pro Tem Van Dyke and Council President Lutz. 
Interim City Attorney Jeremiah Boies clarified Council’s concerns and was available to 
answer their questions. 
Councilmember Kennedy moved, and Councilmember Ballard seconded, to adopt 
Ordinance No. 5340, to include striking 21.07.100 Subsection G on the associated table 
regarding significant trees, an ordinance amending Sections of the Zoning and 
Development Code (Title 21 of the Grand Junction Municipal Code) regarding 
Preservation of Significant Trees and ordered final publication in pamphlet form. Motion 
carried by a roll call vote 4-3 with Councilmembers Nguyen, Stout and Beilfuss voting 
No. 
6. Non-Scheduled Comments 
 No comments were heard. 
7. Other Business 
Council gave direction to staff to move forward with drafting an incentive program 
for significant trees and inquired on the availability of the consultants who will be 
reviewing the code and making recommendations to include this in their scope. 
Council also requested that definitions for council liaisons be revisited, to help the 
liaisons to better understand their role for the boards and commissions meetings 
that they attend. 
8. Adjournment 
The meeting adjourned at 9:32 p.m.
