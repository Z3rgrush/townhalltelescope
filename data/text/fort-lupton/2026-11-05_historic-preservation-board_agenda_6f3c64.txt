ACTION ITEM 
 
HAM2026-001 Report of Acceptability for Odd Fellows. Planner Magaly Morales presented an overview 
of the application submitted by Jessica Holbrook of the Independent Order of Odd Fellows for 
replacement of windows at the Odd Fellows Lodge (300-306 Denver Ave). Planner Morales noted that 
the work requires a building permit and that the Historic Preservation Board has the option of doing a 
conceptual review that would be returned to the Board at a future date or waive the conceptual 
review for a final review. While the proposed windows would be a higher quality than the existing 
windows that are in need of replacement, they do not directly match the existing windows in material. 
 
The Applicant gave an overview of their proposal and explained that the existing windows are not 
practical to replace from a cost perspective. They are hoping to receive a FLURA grant to help cover. 
They are looking to use the same company as Wholly Stromboli to do the work. The Odd Fellows Lodge 
is currently used by local organizations, including the Girl Scouts, and the safety and quality of the 
windows is becoming an increasing problem. The only white trimmed alternative is vinyl and it is not 
recommended for quality reasons. 
 
Chair Kvasnicka acknowledged the cost impact and inquired about something that would look more 
like the existing windows and the potential of using metal trim to do this.

Planner Morales mentioned that History Colorado understands like for like window restoration is not 
always practical and recommends making windows match as close as possible, but that they are a 
necessary replacement for the function of the building. 
 
Ginny White and Zachary Potter both acknowledged the idea of multiple panes or ways to break up the 
windows to make them look like the original while keeping the cost practical. 
 
The Applicant mentioned they will look into using multiple panes or divider elements to make them 
match more closely in style. 
 
Member White motioned for final approval based on the discussion and Alice Garboczi seconded. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
The project is planned to be brought before the FLURA Board for potential grant assistance. 
 
DISCUSSION ITEMS 
 
Planner Morales provided a Staff Report. This meeting is her last as the City Liaison for the Historic 
Preservation Board as she leaves for a different position. Planner McDermott will likely be taking her 
place. The Board and Planner Morales expressed how much they’ve enjoyed working together over the 
last few years.

Certified Local Governments 
 
 
- Local government partnership with the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) and National 
Park Service (NPS) 
- 70 CLGs in Colorado 
- 2,100 CLGs nationwide 
 
- CLG Benefits 
o CLG Grants 
o NPS Grants 
o Eligibility for state tax credits and State Historical Fund grants 
o Access to Compass 
o Training 
o Technical preservation support 
o Formal role in National Register review process 
o Comment on Federal undertakings (Section 106) 
 
- CLG Responsibilities 
o Enforce local preservation ordinance 
o Establish and maintain a commission 
o Send meeting minutes to SHPO 
o Provide for adequate public participation 
o Maintain a system of survey and inventory 
o Attend SHPO-approved training at least once per year 
o Submit an annual report 
o Evaluation (every four years) 
 
- Elements of Local Preservation Programs 
o Survey 
o Designation 
o Design Review 
o Incentives 
o Advocacy & Public Education
