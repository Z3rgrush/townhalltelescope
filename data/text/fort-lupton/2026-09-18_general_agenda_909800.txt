CRS § 24-6-402 (2) (c) Any meetings at which the adoption of any proposed policy, position, resolution, rule, regulation, or 
formal action occurs or at which a majority or quorum of the body is in attendance, or is expected to be in attendance, 
shall be held only after full and timely notice to the public. In addition to any other means of full and timely notice, a local 
public body shall be deemed to have given full and timely notice if the notice of the meeting is posted in a designated 
public place within the boundaries of the local public body no less than twenty -four hours prior to the holding of the 
meeting. The public place or places for posting such notice shall be designated annually at the local public body's first 
regular meeting of each calendar year. The posting shall include specific agenda information where possible.
