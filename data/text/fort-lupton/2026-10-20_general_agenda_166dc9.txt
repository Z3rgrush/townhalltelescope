SUBJECT FOR DISCUSSION 
 
Consideration of the Renewal Application for a Fermented Malt Beverage & Wine License by SGH Dhillon 
& 7-Eleven Inc., dba 7-Eleven Store 41243A         
      
SUMMARY STATEMENT/BACKGROUND DISCUSSION 
On September 16, 2026, SGH Dhillon & 7 -Eleven, Inc. submitted a renewal application for the Fermented 
Malt Beverage & Wine Liquor License for 7-Eleven Store 41243A, located at 1400 2nd Street. 
 
In the renewal application, the applicant disclosed a liquor code violation that occurred on October 15, 
2025, which initially resulted in a written warning. 
 
State Enforcement Action & Violations 
The City subsequently received a Stipulation, Agreement, and Order (“Order”) from the State of Colorado 
Liquor and Tobacco Enforcement Division (“Division”). The Order details an investigation during which 
Division agents alleged violations of the Colorado Liquor Code, specifically C.R.S. §§ 44 -3-901(1)(b)(I) and 
44-3-901(11). 
 
According to the Order, a 7-Eleven employee permitted the sale, serving, giving, or procuring of an alcoholic 
beverage to an 18 -year-old underage operative. Furthermore, the employee failed to verify that the 
consumer was at least 21 years of age by requesting a valid identification, despite the consumer appearing 
to be under 50 years of age. 
 
Penalties and Settlement 
The licensee has admitted to the violation. To avoid further legal proceedings, the licensee executed the 
Order, agreeing to the following terms: 
• A seven-day suspension of the establishment's liquor license. 
• A monetary fine of $500. 
 
Reason for Local Licensing Authority Review 
Per municipal and state regulations, the City Clerk’s office is restricted from administratively approving 
liquor license renewals for establishments with active or recent major violations. Because of the 
documented sale to a minor and the subsequent state disciplinary action, this renewal application must be 
formally reviewed and determined by the Local Licensing Authority. 
 
Notice and Compliance 
Public and statutory notice requirements have been fully satisfied in accordance with C.R.S. § 44-3-305(c): 
• Physical Posting: A notice of the hearing was conspicuously posted on the licensed premises for 
a minimum of 10 days. 
• Applicant Notification: Written notice of the hearing was sent to the applicant via email at least 
10 days prior to the hearing date.
