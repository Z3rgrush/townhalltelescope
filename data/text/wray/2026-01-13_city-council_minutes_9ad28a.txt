Mayor Chad Deyle called the meeting to order and the Pledge of 
Allegiance was recited. 
Roll Call 
City Clerk/Treasurer Emily Ellis conducted Roll Call. All Council 
members were present. 
ADDITION TO AND ADOPTION OF AGENDA 
City Manager James DePue informed the Council that Ordinance #655 
had been added under "New Business". Mayor Pro tern Nicole Smith made a 
motion to adopt the January 13, 2026 Council Meeting Agenda. The motion was 
seconded and passed unanimously. 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES 
Council member Ronn Akey made a motion to approve the December 9, 
2025 Council Meeting Minutes. The motion was seconded and passed 
unanimously. 
COUNCIL OATH 
City Attorney Alvin Wall administered the Wray City Council Oath to 
Ronn Akey, Jason Lorenzini, and Chris Collins. 
APPOINTMENTS 
Nomination & Appointment of Mayor 
Mayor Chad Deyle nominated Nicole Smith for Mayor of the City of 
Wray. With no additional nominations, Council member Ronn Akey made a 
motion to close nominations. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously. 
Council voted unanimously to appoint Nicole Smith as Mayor of the City of 
Wray. Mayor Pro tern Nicole Smith abstained from the vote. 
Nomination and Appointment of Mayor Pro tem 
Mayor Nicole Smith nominated Brad Rockwell for Mayor Pro tern of the 
City of Wray. With no additional nominations, Council member Chris Collins 
made a motion to close nominations. The motion was seconded and passed 
unanimously. Council voted unanimously to appoint Brad Rockwell as Mayor 
Pro tern for the City of Wray. Council member Brad Rockwell abstained from the 
vote.

Larry Hilbert led the Council in prayer. 
PROCLAMATIONS 
General Aviation Appreciation Month -January 2026 
Mayor Nicole Smith proclaimed January 2026 as General Aviation 
Appreciation Month. 
CONSENT AGENDA 
Council member Ronn Akey made a motion to approve the Consent 
Agenda consisting of: Resolution 01-2026-Designation of Public Place for 
Posting of Notices Concerning Official City Business for the City of Wray for 
2026; Resolution 02-2026-First Pioneer National Bank Signatures (I Signature 
Required); Resolution 03-2026-Mill Levy Certification; Resolution 014-2026-
Wray Amphitheater Strategic Plan; and Notice of an Increase in Electric Rates in 
the City of Wray. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously. 
NEW BUSINESS 
City of Wray Annual Financial Audit Agreement - Lauer, Szabo & 
Associates, PC 
City Clerk/Treasurer Emily Ellis informed the Council that audit fee had 
increased by $1,100.00 and recommended approval of the Financial Audit 
Agreement. Council member Ronn Akey made a motion to approve the Annual 
Financial Audit Agreement between the City of Wray and Lauer, Szabo & 
Associates, PC. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously. 
2026 City Board Appointments 
Council member Jason Lorenzini made a motion to approve the 2026 City 
Board Appointments. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously. 
Amphitheater Advisory Board Appointment 
Council member Dane Sybrant made a motion to approve the appointment 
of William Arledge to the Amphitheater Advisory Board. The motion was 
seconded and passed unanimously. 
Ordinance #655: Authorizing the Sale of Certain Real Property to PR
